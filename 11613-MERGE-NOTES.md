## ⬜ A RULE GAP TO PUT TO WILSON — a real divergence that Pattern 14 cannot express

**Chunk 0009 @1176B.** Foliot's lemma is *Oleum effusum nomen tuum* ("Thy name is oil poured
out"). Four lines into his own gloss he re-quotes it as **« Nomen tuum est ut oleum »** —
identity turned into simile — and then builds the entire oil-floats-above-every-liquid
exposition on the simile rather than on the lemma he printed. That is exactly the class
`[var:]` exists for.

**It could not take the marker, for a mechanical reason.** Pattern 14 forbids a `[var:]`
whose content appears verbatim in the Latin twin, and the received reading is printed four
lines above — as this chunk's own lemma. The check that protects the marker from being put
round our own words also blocks it here, where the divergence is internal to the work.

⚑ **The general shape: a work that quotes itself can diverge from itself, and our apparatus
assumes divergence is always from an outside authority.** Logged in the cruces instead, which
is the right fallback, but the reader of the page sees nothing. Worth a ruling — either a
narrow exception, or an explicit decision that internal divergence lives only in the cruces.
