## Notes for the pipeline

1. **The apostrophe class is not a plate question.** 13 of the 73 items (18%) are `᾽`
   (U+1FBD) vs `'` (U+2019) on elisions — `δι'`, `μετ'`, `καθ'`, `ἀπ'`, `ἐφ'`, `δ'`,
   `κατ'`. The plate prints one stroke and cannot adjudicate between two Unicode
   spellings of it. Fix the codepoint in the transcription prompt and normalise before
   diffing; otherwise this class will keep consuming an adjudication budget forever, the
   way the grave-before-comma class did in Batch 1.
2. **This fount's final sigma reads as a semicolon, and its circumflex reads as an iota
   subscript, at 400 dpi.** That single confusion produced `οὕτω`/`οὕτως` (851) and
   `τῇ;`/`τῆς` (854), and it is silent — both outputs are well-formed Greek. Re-render
   at 600 dpi (`pdftoppm -r 600`, the scan's native resolution) before judging any
   word-final glyph. The existing 400 dpi crops are lossy for exactly this class.
3. **`Πάτρες` is the edition's form, three times over** (1632, 1636, 1650), while
   `πατέρας` is set in full on 1632's own full-width line. It is not a broken sort and
   must not be repaired to `Πατέρες`. Log it in the apparatus.
4. Two further crux items for the apparatus: `ἱμάτιον` at 1633.1 (type under-inked to
   near-invisibility; fixed by the Latin *vestem*) and `κρα-` at 1636 (the ρ dropped out
   of the forme; fixed by the continuation `τῆσαι` and the Latin *amplectitur*). Both
   are physical printing failures, not variants.
5. **Full-width lines are the structural cause of most of this leaf range's noise.**
   Where Migne balances unequal columns he sets lines across the whole measure; the
   column crops cut them, and both passes then invent hyphens (`σχολά-`, `ταπε-`,
   `δευτέρ-`) or emit fragments (`ς`, `ῇ`, `α`). Neither pass flagged the truncation.
   Worse, at leaf849 both passes lost a whole clause — `« Μηδὲν θλιβῇς· σὺ οὐκ ἔχεις
   πρᾶγμα· ἀλλ'` — and the diff cannot see a loss the two passes share. Detect
   full-width lines at crop time and route them through `fullwidth-crops/`, or this class
   will keep producing silent omissions.
6. Migne **spaces the closing guillemet** (`… με. »`). All nine leaf848 "disagreements"
   were this and nothing else — A tokenised the mark free-standing, B glued it to the
   word. Normalise the tokenizer.
7. Also observed at leaf854: **Latin bleeding mid-column** into the Greek measure
   (`rationem invenisti : sic habet ut dixisti. » Ita liberatus sophista…que admisit et
   abiit.`) — the rule-4 case. Both passes correctly excluded it.
