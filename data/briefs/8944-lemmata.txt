## How the ✓ / ⚠ marks were made, and what they do NOT mean

Mechanical phrase search of the normalized span against
`sources/vulgate/clementine-flat.txt` (all 73 books, one verse per line).

- **✓ Clementine verbatim** = this exact word sequence occurs SOMEWHERE in the Clementine.
  ⛔ It does **not** mean it occurs at the verse being glossed, and it is **not** clearance.
  A lemma can match some other verse verbatim and still diverge from its own.
- **⚠ NOT in Clementine verbatim** = the sequence was not found. Most of these will be
  innocent: a fragment cut mid-phrase, an `usque ad` abridgment span, a span carrying an
  editorial `etc.`, a Migne spelling. **A ⚠ is a place to look, never a divergence.**
- Single words are not searchable this way and are marked for checking in place.

⛔ **You still walk every line.** The marks order your work; they do not do it. A `[var:]`
is a public claim about another text and must rest on the file, cited by book chapter:verse —
never on this pre-scan and never on recollection.
