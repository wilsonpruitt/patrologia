## ⭐⭐⭐ Part D, 0141B — the finding is withdrawn

The stint's Part D asserted the single most consequential claim in the work: that Migne prints
*quia **non** in eo quod credidit* and Corpus Corporum has silently dropped the negation, so that
"the English asserts something its own Latin column does not, and there is no marker able to say so."

**Gallica shows no `non`.** The words `quia in eo` sit together at normal letter spacing in the middle
of a line; there is no gap, no ghost, and nothing that could have been over-inked into or out of a
three-letter word. The archive copy's reading was an artefact, and it is worth noting that the stint
itself reasoned, correctly, that the sense *requires* no negation there ("the plate's first *non* is
very likely intrusive") — the second witness now says Migne never printed one. Our twin is right.

⛔ **No patch. The English has been reverted** (see below) and the parallel page no longer carries an
English negative with no Latin exponent.
