## Part E — Hebrew dropped by Corpus Corporum — ALL FIVE CONFIRMED (structural)

Ink cannot put type on a page that is not there; each is settled by one good read. All five are
present on Gallica and absent from our twin.

| col | chunk | Gallica prints | reading | leaf · scale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0148C | 0031 | `*Rehobot* (רחבות), *latitudines.*` | **רחבות** — clean, 5 glyphs | f74 · 9× |
| 0155B | 0033 | `verbo *ulam* vel *olam* ולאם plenae sunt` | **ולאם** ⚠ see note | f78 · 9× |
| 0157C | 0034 | `verbum Hebraicum *monim* מנים numerum` | **מנים** — clean | f79 · 9× |
| 0158A | 0034 | `in Hebraeo *theraphim* תרפים scriptum est` | **תרפים** — final mem lightly inked | f79 · 9× |
| 0159B | 0035 | `in Hebraeo *mahanaim* מהנים ; ut sciamus` | **מהנים** ⚠ see note | f80 · 9× |

⚠ **Two of the five readings need a Hebraist's sign-off before the type is set into our Latin.**
The *presence* of Hebrew at all five is not in doubt; the letters at two are.
- **0155B** reads **ולאם**, not the expected **אולם** (Jerome's *ulam/olam*). Two independent
  readings (the archive stint's and mine) agree on the order as printed, so this looks like a
  transposition in Migne's own Hebrew type rather than a misread — but it is a claim about letter
  *order*, the one thing ink cannot cause, and it should be checked against Jerome's *Quaest. Hebr.*
  before it is published.
- **0159B** reads **מהנים** with **he**, where the Masoretic form is **מחנים** with ḥet. The glyph
  shows the detached left leg of a he. Again two readings agree; again worth a specialist's eye.
- Twelve Hebrew words were known lost across cols 0104–0159; these five bring the count to **sixteen**
  once inserted (or seventeen if the 0012 stint's count is re-checked).
