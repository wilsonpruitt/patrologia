## Findings

### F1. 0003, col 0479B (§16): class 1 (polarity)
- **Latin:** *Si enim inter homines solet bonae fidei contractus nulla ratione dissolvi:*
- **English as it stands:** `For if among men a contract of good faith is wont to be dissolved for no reason:`
- **Proposed:** `For if among men a contract of good faith is wont to be dissolved on no account:`
- **Why:** *nulla ratione* = "by no means / on no ground". The English says that contracts get dissolved (for
  no reason), which reverses the premise of the a fortiori ("how much more ought this promise … not to be
  dissolved without punishment").
- **Confidence:** certain. **In the cruces?** No.

### F2. 0002, col 0468D (title list, XIII): class 3 (grammar, two alternatives collapsed)
- **Latin:** *Ut ancillae Dei velatae, si publice nubendo vel occulte se corruptioni tradiderint, non admittantur ad poenitentiam*
- **English as it stands:** `if they have given themselves up to corruption, by marrying publicly or secretly, be not admitted to penance`
- **Proposed:** `if they have given themselves up to corruption, whether publicly by marrying or secretly, be not admitted to penance`
- **Why:** *publice nubendo* and *occulte* are the two alternatives, as the decree itself says at 0479A (*si
  postea vel publice nupserint vel se clanculo corruperint*, rendered correctly there). The English attaches
  *occulte* to marrying, so it creates a "secret marriage" and loses the secret fall.
- **Confidence:** probable (near certain given §15). **In the cruces?** No.

### F3. 0005, col 0485B (Monitum §8): class 3 (*cum* read twice)
- **Latin:** *Neque jam ipsius admodum interest compertum habere tempus quo haec scripta sit, cum quibus, qua occasione, et quo consilio scripta fuerit, teneat.*
- **English as it stands:** `since he holds with whom, on what occasion, and with what design it was written.`
- **Proposed:** `since he holds to whom, on what occasion, and with what design it was written.`
- **Why:** *cum … teneat* is causal ("since he holds"), which the English already renders. *quibus* is the
  dative of address ("to whom it was written"). The English reads *cum* a second time as "with".
- **Confidence:** certain. **In the cruces?** No.

### F4. 0003, col 0478B (note g on §15): class 2 (year). The plate reads 381
- **Latin (our file):** *Anno 581, Caesaraugustanum concilium, can. 8, statuit*
- **English as it stands:** `In the year 581 the Council of Saragossa, can. 8, laid down`
- **Proposed:** `In the year 381 the Council of Saragossa, can. 8, laid down` (and correct `581`→`381` in
  `data/plate-notes/7090.tsv` and the Latin `[cn: g]`).
- **Why:** p. 244, right foot column: I read **Anno 381** at 250 dpi. The next sentence (*Idem postea et
  Majorianus imp.* [458] *et Agathense concilium* [506] *confirmarunt*) cannot follow a council of 581, and
  Saragossa I is 380/381. This is our transcription of the note, not Migne's type.
- **Confidence:** certain. **In the cruces?** Yes, logged as undecided (cruces-0000 l. 75–78: "plate looks
  like 381 … A reader with a second witness should settle them"). The page and the internal chronology settle it.

### F5. 0005, col 0487A (note c, *Symposius*): class 2 (year). The plate reads 380
- **Latin (our file):** *qui synodo Caesaraugustanae anno 580 adversus Priscillianistas habitae subscripsit*
- **English as it stands:** `held against the Priscillianists in the year 580`
- **Proposed:** `held against the Priscillianists in the year 380` (and correct the TSV/Latin `[cn: c]`).
- **Why:** p. 249, left foot column, enlarged: **anno 380**. The first digit has the same flat-topped 3 as in F4.
  The synod against the Priscillianists at Saragossa is 380, and in 580 there were no Priscillianists to
  condemn.
- **Confidence:** certain. **In the cruces?** Yes, logged as undecided (same passage). Settled here.

### F6. 0003, col 0479A (note a on §15): class 4 (the Latin column diverges from the plate; the English is right)
- **Latin (our file):** *Praefert Quesn., vel clanculo corruptae fuerint, indeque conjectat olim clanculo quasi clam in angulo dictum esse.*
- **English as it stands:** `Quesnel prefers *vel clangulo corruptae fuerint*, and thence conjectures that *clangulo* was once said as if *clam in angulo*.` (correct)
- **Proposed:** no English change. Latin `[cn: a]` / TSV: *clanculo* → **clangulo** in both places (the third
  *clanculo*, in *vel se clanculo corruperint*, is right and stays).
- **Why:** p. 245, left foot: the plate prints *clan-|gulo* and *clangu-|lo*. Quesnel's etymology (*clam in
  angulo*) needs the g. The English follows the plate, but the two columns disagree and the Latin is the wrong
  one.
- **Confidence:** certain. **In the cruces?** No.

### F7. 0003, col 0480A (note h on §16): class 4 (Latin column, two slips; the English is right)
- **Latin (our file):** *Edit Rom. cum ms. Colb. Langob., quae pactionis suis fidem minime servarerunt.* … *Apud Quesn. ut in exemplari uno Colb. et Pith.*
- **English as it stands:** `*quae pactionis suae fidem minime servarerunt*` … `In Quesnel, as in one Colb. exemplar and another Pith.` (both match the plate)
- **Proposed:** no English change. Latin/TSV: *suis* → **suae**. Also *uno Colb. et Pith.* → **uno Colb. et altero
  Pith.**
- **Why:** p. 245, right foot: the plate prints *pactionis suae* and *in exemplari uno Colb. et altero Pith.* Our
  Latin note dropped *altero*, and the English "another" has nothing opposite it.
- **Confidence:** certain. **In the cruces?** No.

### F8. 0004, col 0483B (Monitum §5 head): class 4 (a silent repair of Migne's type)
- **Latin:** *Toletani* I *concilii vulgati tres patres.*
- **English as it stands:** `*The three parts of the First Council of Toledo as published.*`
- **Proposed:** `*The three parts [sic: *patres*] of the First Council of Toledo as published.*`
- **Why:** p. 247, left col.: the plate prints **patres** (the body two words later has *tres … partes*). The sense
  is certainly *partes*, but the English repairs Migne's type without a marker, which is the failure Pattern 7a
  describes. The plate licenses the `[sic:]`.
- **Confidence:** certain (plate read). **In the cruces?** No.

### F9. 0003, col 0478B (note g, Ambrose on Marcellina): class 2 (whose sister)
- **Latin:** *sermonem inseruit, quem habuit Liberius papa, cum soror ipsius Marcellina … signaret*
- **English as it stands:** `the sermon which Pope Liberius delivered when his sister Marcellina, on the Saviour's birthday`
- **Proposed:** `the sermon which Pope Liberius delivered when Ambrose's own sister Marcellina, on the Saviour's birthday`
- **Why:** *ipsius* points back to Ambrose, the subject. Marcellina was Ambrose's sister. In the English, "his"
  falls on Liberius, the nearest antecedent, which makes her the pope's sister.
- **Confidence:** probable (the Latin is certain; the English is ambiguous rather than flatly wrong). **In the cruces?** No.

### F10. 0004, col 0482B (Monitum §2, Sirmond quoted): class 1 (the English reads as a negation)
- **Latin:** *etsi ambigi non injuria posse intelligebam*
- **English as it stands:** `although I understood that it could not unjustly be doubted`
- **Proposed:** `although I understood that it could with good reason be doubted`
- **Why:** *non injuria* = "not without cause". The English order ("could not … be doubted") invites the reverse
  reading, that it could NOT be doubted, and that breaks Sirmond's *etsi … satius tamen* concession.
- **Confidence:** probable. **In the cruces?** No.

### F11. 0003 col 0481B and 0004 col 0482C: class 2 (a qualifier added to a number)
- **Latin:** *Colbertinus codex 408, de Institutione clericorum ante annos 700 exaratus* / *Germanensis codex, not. 424, canonum collectionem ante annos 800 concinnatam complectens*
- **English as it stands:** `written more than 700 years ago` / `put together more than 800 years ago`
- **Proposed:** `written 700 years ago` / `put together 800 years ago`
- **Why:** *ante annos N* is "N years ago". "More than" is added. Contrast *a 600 saltem annis* at 0482C, which
  the English correctly gives as "at least 600". The plate confirms both numbers (pp. 246, 247).
- **Confidence:** probable. **In the cruces?** No.
