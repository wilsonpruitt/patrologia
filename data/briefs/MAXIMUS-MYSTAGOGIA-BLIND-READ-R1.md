### F1. my-002, col. 673 (band C, near the foot of the left column of pdf p. 371): ἀλλὰ τὴν **μὴ** ἀλήθειαν — KEEP
- **Class:** 1 (polarity), judged.
- **Greek (plate-read, p-0371, crop of plate lines 38–40):** `τὸ ἀγαθὸν, τὸν Θεὸν ἔλεγε δηλοῦν· ἀλλὰ τὴν μὴ ἀλή-|θειαν, ὅταν ἐκ τῆς οὐσίας τὸ Θεῖον σημαίνεσθαι δοκῇ·` The plate clearly sets **μὴ** (accent and all); our file matches the plate.
- **Latin:** *hoc tamen discrimine, ut tum quidem veritas Deum significet, cum is ex essentia designari videtur … bonum autem, cum ex operatione.* (*quidem*).
- **English as it stands:** "the truth, when the Divine seems to be signified from its essence,"
- **Proposed English:** no change.
- **Why:** the clause is the μέν-limb answered by τὸ δὲ ἀγαθόν, ὅταν ἐκ τῆς ἐνεργείας; «the not-truth» would make the following γάρ-clause (truth is simple, one, unchangeable…) a reason for nothing, and would set the not-truth against the good as the pair that signifies God, contradicting s8's own opening (the truth and the good signify God) and s11 (the truth and the good = the pair significant of the Divine). μὴ is Migne's misprint for μὲν; the English follows the sense, as the convention requires.
- **Confidence:** certain.
- **Already in the cruces?** Yes (checked after): cruces 0673 T-note, "Migne prints μὴ (confirmed on the plate) … The English follows μὲν", and the plate table row p0371.40. ⚑ Small correction to the brief: the plate's column number for this line is **673**, not 672 (the left column of p. 371 is headed 673; the file's `[0673]` anchor precedes it). The cruces has it right.

## Candidates considered and rejected
1. **my-001 col. 664, s12** τῆς δὲ καθ᾿ ὑπεροχὴν αἰτίας τοῦ εἶναι πάσης τῶν ὄντων ἀφαιρετικῆς → "the other, by the pre-eminence of the cause, removes from him all the being of the things that are". The genitive πάσης is ambiguous (with αἰτίας or with the beings' being); the English's construal gives the θέσις/ἀφαίρεσις polarity the right way round (being posits God as cause; not-being removes beings' being from him). Not a reversal; rejected.
2. **my-001 col. 661, s6** ἀλλ᾿ ὅσα … παρελείφθη → "but only such things as were left by him". "only" is supplied, but it states what the contrast with ἐγχειρεῖν τοῖς ἐκείνου already implies; no sense change. Rejected.
3. **my-001 col. 668, s9** τὰς ἀρχὰς τῶν ὄντων τοῖς πέρασιν οὐκ ἐῶν συναφίστασθαι → "not allowing the beginnings of the things that are to stand apart together with their limits". Awkward but literal; the negative is kept and the sense (Christ as centre holding beginnings and ends together) survives. Rejected.
4. **my-002 col. 676, s12** ἑαυτόν / ἑαυτῷ in the decad sentence: masculine ἑαυτόν cannot refer to the feminine δεκάς, so "brings back to himself Jesus" (= Jesus to himself) is the right who-to-whom; ἑαυτῷ with ἐμέ rendered "to myself, or rather to God" fits μᾶλλον δὲ Θεῷ. Checked, rejected.
5. **my-002 col. 676, s12** ἡ τὸν λόγον ὑπηχοῦντα τῷ πνεύματι … ἔχουσα → "which has the word sounding beneath it to the Spirit": "it" is supplied; ὑπηχέω here = sound in answer. Not a sense change. Rejected.
6. **my-002 col. 672, ch. 5 s5** Ἐπειδὴ γὰρ … ἔφασκε (an unanswered ἐπειδή, anacoluthon) → "For he used to say". The English drops the dangling subordinator; no clause lost. Rejected.
