### F1. R1 case, p0433.23–25 (col. 860 B–C): the number/tetrad passage. **KEEP** (no change)
- **Class:** 5 (numbers), judged at the plate.
- **Greek (plate-read, p-0433 right column):** `Πέντε δὲ τοῖς ἑπτὰ συνθεὶς, τὸν δώδεκα πληρώσαις. ἂν σα-|φῶς ἀριθμόν.` then a paragraph break; then
  `… συμπλακεῖσαν τῇ τετριάδι [τετράδι] τῶν αἰσθητῶν τριάδα τῶν δυνάμεων τῆς ψοχῆς κατὰ τὴν σχέσιν, …`
- Plate vs our file: the plate sets a POINT (not a comma) after πληρώσαις; our file has a comma. σαφῶς is whole across the line break
  (σα-|φῶς), one word. τετριάδι is Migne's misprint with his own bracketed correction [τετράδι]; ψοχῆς (for ψυχῆς) is also printed so.
  Plate paragraph break after ἀριθμόν is matched by the English paragraph break.
- **Latin:** *Si autem quinque septemque componendo junxeris, duodenarium plane numerum impleveris* · *facultatum animi ternionem … rerum in sensum cadentium quaternioni … implexum*.
- **English as it stands:** "And having added five to seven, thou wouldst plainly fill up the number twelve." / "the triad of the powers of the soul, wickedly entwined through deceit, contrary to nature, with the tetrad of sensible things by relation"
- **Proposed:** none. The misplaced point inside πληρώσαις ἂν (a potential optative) is a mark that cannot be carried without breaking the clause; the English reads the sense (5 + 7 = 12; triad of soul-powers entwined with the TETRAD of sensibles), the correspondence is the right way round (triad = soul, tetrad = sensibles), and "tetrad" follows Migne's own [τετράδι]. Number words all correct (five, seven, twelve, three, four).
- **Confidence:** certain. **In cruces?** see the end.

### F2. col. 857 A / 860 A: Migne's scripture footnote attached to the wrong sentence, a second one dropped, and one figure doubtful
- **Class:** 4/5 (a reference mis-bound; minor, does not touch the sense of Maximus's text).
- **Plate (p-0432, foot of page, plate-read):** `¹ II Reg. viii, 1[?]; x, 7; I Paral. xviii, 1. ² I Reg. x, 19.` Note ¹ is keyed in the LATIN column
  to the title lemma (*et percussit vallem Salinarum duodecim millia ¹*, col. 858 A); note ² is keyed to the Adraazar comparison
  (*ambas Syrias adversus Davidem ²*, col. 858 C). Neither is keyed in the Greek column.
- **English as it stands:** "as Adraazar did the two Syrias against David. [n: II Reg. viii, 4; x, 7; I Paral. xviii, 1.]"
- **Proposed:** move `[n: II Reg. viii, …; x, 7; I Paral. xviii, 1.]` to the end of the p3 lemma ("…twelve thousand.*"), and at the Adraazar
  sentence set note ²: "as Adraazar did the two Syrias against David. [n: I Reg. x, 19.]" (as printed; the plate's "I" may be a dropped "II", since the story is II Reg. x, 19; carry as printed).
- **Why:** 7a″'s mis-bound-reference class: the title note belongs to the title (the burning of Syria Soba and the valley of salt), not to the Adraazar comparison; note ² is lost entirely.
  The verse figure: the plate's digit after "viii," is the SAME glyph as the one after "xviii," which the English reads "1"; reading one "4" and the other "1" is inconsistent. 1 is the likelier (both are chapter openings, II Sam 8:1 / 1 Chr 18:1), but at 600 dpi the glyph is ambiguous: re-read it before changing.
- **Confidence:** probable (placement and missing ² certain from the plate; the 4/1 figure probable only).

## Candidates considered and rejected
- **col. 857, γνωμικήν τε καὶ προαιρετικὴν**: could agree with γνῶσιν; the English attaches them to μεταβολήν ("change of inclination and of choice"). Right: the parallel clause opposes καθολικὴν … φυσικὴν … ἀλλοίωσιν, voluntary vs natural change. γνωμικός/προαιρετικός kept apart.
- **col. 857, ἐπὶ τέλει**: "Unto the end" in the lemma carried through every time; τοῖς ἀλλοιωθησομένοις = "for those who shall be changed" per ruling; ἀλλοίωσις change / μεταβολή transformation held.
- **col. 857 B, Soba = "seven"**: right, and "this plainly shows the property of time" right. Adraazar = "loosing strength" or "razing power" ✓. Sikima = "shouldering" (Ὡμίασις): exact for the noun, and the next sentence's ὦμον = "shoulder" ties it. Not a defect.
- **col. 860 A, "enslaved man to time and to nature"**: Greek order τῷ χρόνῳ καὶ τῇ φύσει: same order; fine.
- **col. 860 C, ὃν καταφονεύσει**: future, "he will slay"; subject the minister of the intelligible David ✓.
- **col. 860 D, Τέσσαρα γὰρ ἐπὶ τρία, καὶ τρία ἐπὶ τέσσαρα τοῖς πέντε συντιθέμενα**: English "four upon three, and three upon four put together with the five". Maximus's two routes to twelve (4×3; 3+4+5) are left as ambiguous as the Greek leaves them; the Latin reads both as addition. Literal, not a finding.
- **col. 861 A, s1+s2 merged**: Greek has a full stop after ὁμιλίας; English runs it on with a semicolon into the four-member series. Punctuation only (Pattern 8 note for the editor), no sense change.
- **col. 861, lemma/exposition word identity**: cast off / brought down / angry / pity; shaken–shook / troubled / heal / fractures / tottered; hard things / wine of compunction / signal / face of the bow / beloved / delivered; save / right hand / hearken; Holy One; exult / divide Sikima / measure out the valley of tents: all carried with the same English word in lemma and exposition. ἀκούει "hears" vs ἐπακούει "hearkens" kept distinct as Maximus distinguishes them ✓.
- **col. 861 C, χρίσεως → "judgement"**: known, by ruling.
- **col. 861 D, Σημείωσις γὰρ ἀληθὴς … καθέστηκε δύναμις**: subject/predicate could be inverted ("the power … is a true signal"); the English equation reads the same either way.
- **col. 864 A–B, ⟦s2⟧ numbered twice in the English (p12)**: the second "⟦s2⟧" (before "And he gives to those who fear him") has no counterpart marker in the Greek; a marker irregularity, not a sense defect. Flag for the mechanical check only.
- **col. 864 B, τῷ βίῳ → "to the world"**: Byzantine βίος = the world of men; Latin *mundoque* agrees. Fine.
- **col. 864, the full stop after οἰκονομίαν**: known, by ruling.
- **col. 864 A, ῥύονται**: the modes of the virtues (practice) deliver the thoughts (contemplation); English keeps the direction ✓.
- **col. 859/860 foot, Combefis note (c) on λύων ἰσχύν** (Hebrew etymology of Adraazar): Migne's lettered apparatus, not in the English; T-note matter, outside this read.
- **col. 860 C plate point after πληρώσαις**: see F1.
