## Cases the brief asked me to judge (R1)
⚠ The brief's question numbers for R1 do not match the chunk headings, which follow Migne. I judged the case each item describes:
- **Q.1, the lists of virtues: KEEP.** The counts match (18 and 6) and the order is preserved.
- **«Q.4 τῷ Ἠλιοῦ»** (this is Migne's **Q.7**, 0001 p23): **KEEP «said of Elijah».** The dative is ambiguous, and the Latin *Elie dictum* is equally ambiguous. The answer's three glosses all point to Elijah: «my Father», because Elisha called Elijah «father», and «the hidden one», because Elijah was taken up. So «of Elijah» is the reading the answer supports.
- **Q.17 «blood» (Migne οἶμαι): KEEP.** «Flesh is desire; and blood, anger» is the only sense the answer can bear under 1 Cor 15:50. It is listed as known in the brief.
- **«Q.19 μωρός / ῥακά»** (this is Migne's **Q.14**): **KEEP.** «Fool» is liable to gehenna and «Raca» is subject to the council, in the question and in the answer alike. This matches the Greek and Matt 5:22. «Raca» receives the lighter penalty (κουφοτέρῳ), which the English keeps.
- **p0400.29 «[against]»** (Q.12): **KEEP.** Without it the accusative ἀντικειμένην … ὑπόνοιαν is ungoverned. The supplied word follows the editor's Ἴσ. πρός and does not reverse anything.
- **«Q.~20» Gregory of Nyssa's three restorations** (this is Migne's **Q.13**): **KEEP.** There are three restorations: the individual one, by virtue; the restoration of the whole nature to incorruption at the resurrection; and the restoration of the soul's powers. The contrast «by full knowledge, not by participation in the good things» (τῇ ἐπιγνώσει, οὐ τῇ μεθέξει) keeps its polarity.
- **Q.32 God's «regret»: KEEP.** μεταμέλεια is rendered «regret», as the fixed term requires. The definition is intact: regret is the passage from one mode of providence to another.
- **«Q.~33» p0409.19 γενεᾶς** (this is Migne's **Q.31**): **KEEP «the habit of the generation of evil».** It is a literal construal of an odd genitive. The Latin reads the same way (*tertiam, generationis mali habitum (nempe consensum)*), and the sense «the third = consent» is preserved.
- **Q.34 οὐ δύναται … οὐδέ: KEEP.** «Why can "Father of the Spirit" not be said» and «so neither can we say that the Son is of the Spirit» both carry their negatives. «said variously» follows the printed διαφόρως; the bracketed [ἀδιαφόρως] is a variant.

## Candidates considered and rejected
1. **Q.7 (0001 p24) «the God of my Father», capital F.** On first reading this looks like it points to God the Father, when the sense is Elijah as Elisha's «father». Rejected: Migne prints Πατρός with a capital, and the Latin has *Patris mei*. The English follows the plate (Pattern 8), and nothing is reversed.
2. **Q.13 (0001 p42 s7) μὴ εὑρίσκουσαν στάσιν «finding no rest».** «halt / standstill» would be closer to Gregory's idea of the soul's unending progress. Rejected: this is a nuance of word choice, not a reversal.
3. **Q.10 (0001 p33) [n: Joan. v, 24] stands in the English with no counterpart in the Greek file.** Rejected: the Greek files hold no [n:] at all, so the notes are Migne's marginal citations carried in the English. The note fits «do not come into judgement» (John 5:24).
4. **Q.26 (0002 p22 s7) «forgiveness comes about for those to which the scale shall have its tilt» (ἐφ᾿ ἃ τὴν ῥοπὴν ἕξει).** The sentence is awkward. Rejected: the sense (the good deeds outweigh, so forgiveness follows) is not reversed.
5. **Q.33 (0003 p4) «of it» for ταύτης [R. ταύτας].** Rejected: it is a literal rendering of the printed word, with the bracket as apparatus.
6. **Q.41 (0003 p27–28) ἀνίσους / ἄνισα «uneven».** Rejected: here it means an odd number, against an even (composite) one, and «uneven» bears that sense.
7. **Q.40 (0003 p25): the question asks about «priests», and the answer says «bishops» (ἐπίσκοποι).** Rejected: the English keeps the Greek shift faithfully.
