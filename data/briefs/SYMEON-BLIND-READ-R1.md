## Findings

### F1. 0000, col. 181 A: class 5 (wrong term: ἁγιάζειν rendered "hallow")
- **Greek (plate-read, p. 96 a):** καὶ τὰ σώματα ἀφθαρτίσῃ ἅμα καὶ ἁγιάσῃ.
- **English as it stands:** "and make our bodies incorruptible and at the same time hallow them."
- **Proposed:** "and make our bodies incorruptible and at the same time sanctify them."
- **Why:** the Symeon register fixes ἁγιάζειν = sanctify, καθαγιάζειν = hallow. The plate prints
  plain ἁγιάσῃ here, against καθαγιάσῃ eight lines earlier in ch. 40 ("hallow our bodies also"),
  which the English renders correctly. Symeon pairs the two verbs on purpose; this one collapses them.
- **Confidence:** certain (term rule); low doctrinal weight.
- **Already in the cruces?** No (checked after writing). cruces.md restates the ἁγιάζειν/καθαγιάζειν
  rule (l. 138–139) and lists the plate reading ἀφθαρτώσῃ for this line among silent corrections (l. 89),
  but does not log the verb's rendering. The ἁγιάσῃ at l. 365 is a different site (0003, Cana:
  "that he might sanctify our birth", rendered correctly).

No other finding in 0000–0004. Every negative in the Greek of the range was checked against the
English (list under "Negatives checked"); none is dropped, added, or moved.

## Candidates considered and rejected
1. **0000 ch. 37, the Calfa junk at the 177/180 break** (ΚΟΝΡΟΤ Οε…): suspected dropped line.
   Plate p. 94 b ends καταστησάμε-, and the English runs straight on; nothing lost.
2. **0000 ch. 37, "having taken up this power of his from among us"** (ἀναλαμβανόμενος ἀφ᾽ ἡμῶν):
   odd sense, but the plate prints ἀφ᾽ ἡμῶν and the English is literal. Not a defect.
3. **0001 opening, "not able to extend to all"**: "extend" has no verb in the file. Plate p. 96 b
   prints καὶ μὴ δυνάμενος εἰς πάντας with no verb; the supplied verb is a fair rendering of the
   ellipsis.
4. **0001 ch. 42, "akin toward it"** (πρὸς αὐτήν, where "toward him" would read better): plate
   p. 97 a prints αὐτὴν. The English follows the plate.
5. **0001 `[d: …]` doubling** ("intelligible and sensible, and the union of the two is man"):
   checked in case it was a Calfa doubling attributed to Migne. Plate p. 97 b sets the clause twice
   on consecutive lines. The marker stands.
6. **0001 "it would not have been saved; for it is saved"**: reads like a contradiction, but it is
   οὐκ ἐσώζετο αὕτη· σεσωσμένη γάρ ἐστιν (the angelic nature needs no saving, being already saved).
   Polarity correct.
7. **0001 "Hast thou considered the mystery… ?"**: the file has no ";". The Cleric answers
   "I have considered it", so the question is right.
8. **0002 "but he does not yet also have the pledges"** (οὐκ ἔτι / οὐκέτι): "no longer" would be
   wrong in sense; "not yet" is the right reading of the argument (baptism without myron). Kept.
9. **0002 Demetrius / Mamas / Sittē passage**: our file drops "σώματος τοῦ θείου καὶ μυροβλύτου
   Δημητρίου, ἢ". Plate p. 99 b prints it, and the English has it. A restoration, not a finding.
10. **0002 `[sic: χειρονοτονηθείς]`**: checked in case the misprint was ours. Plate p. 100 b prints
    χειρονοτονηθείς. The marker stands.
11. **0003 ch. 48 "does not turn away from the begetting of children"**: the file has αὐν
    ἀποστοέσεται. Plate p. 102 a prints οὐκ ἀποστρέφεται. Correct.
12. **0003 ch. 49 "he did not refuse to eat and drink"**: the file has αλ παρητεῖτο. Plate
    p. 102 b prints οὐ παρῃτεῖτο. Correct.
13. **0003 ch. 49 column-end garble** (τεῆ-ιμ·ν… ἱᾳωυιαμ): suspected dropped text. Plate
    p. 102 b prints διὰ πάντων τούτων καὶ ὧν εἴρηκε παραβολῶν, τοῦ ἀσώτου καὶ, which the English
    renders in full.
14. **0003 "him who denied a third time"** (τὸν ἀρνησάμενον τρίτον): "thrice" would also do, and
    both fit the plate. A matter of style, not count; the "thrice confessed" that follows is τρίς
    and is rendered "thrice".
15. **0004 ch. 52 "For this only the monks owe, in a special way"** (Ταύτην… ὀφείλουσι μόνον
    ἐξαιρέτως οἱ μοναχοί· ἀλλὰ καὶ οἱ ἐν κόσμῳ μήπω… συζευχθέντες): μόνον sits as ambiguously in
    the Greek as in the English. The literal rendering keeps the ambiguity. Not a reversal.
16. **0004 ch. 54 "led him up to incorruption"**: our file lacks αὐτὸν εἰς τὴν ἀφθαρσίαν. Plate
    p. 106 a prints it. A restoration, not a finding.
17. **0002 ch. 44 "such a high priest truly became us"** (ἔπρεπεν): archaic "became" = "befitted",
    and correct. The register is the translator's call.
