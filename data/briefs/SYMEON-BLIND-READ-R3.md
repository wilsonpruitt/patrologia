## Findings


### F1 — 0010, col 225 band A — class 2 (how many)
- **Greek (plate-read, p. 118):** Εἶτα μετ᾽ ὀλίγον πάλιν ἐμφυσᾷ τῷ ὕδατι τρίτον, τὴν θείαν χάριν αὐτῷ δαψιλέστερον ἐντιθείς
- **Latin (col 226):** *rursus in aquam ter insufflat*
- **English as it stands:** `Then after a little he again breathes upon the water a third time, putting the divine grace`
- **Proposed:** `Then after a little he again breathes upon the water three times, putting the divine grace`
- **Why:** rubrical τρίτον = "thrice"; no earlier breathing on the water is narrated, so "a third time" asserts two prior acts that do not exist (πάλιν goes with the renewed action after the sealing). The identical rubric in 0007 (ἐμφυσᾷ τῷ κατηχουμένῳ σταυροειδῶς τρίτον) is rendered "three times"; Latin *ter*.
- **Confidence:** probable (near certain). **In cruces?** LOGGED but adjudicated the other way (cruces.md, 0010 col 225 τρίτον: rendered "a third time", "the adverb's literal sense … Not material"). I dispute "not material": it is a how-many claim, and it contradicts 0007's rendering of the same rubric.

### F2 — 0010, col 225 band C (full-width line) — class 2 (how many)
- **Greek (plate-read, p. 118, full-width):** καὶ ἐμφυσᾷ τούτῳ. Καὶ σφραγίζει τρίτον τύπῳ σταυροῦ τῇ χειρί
- **Latin (col 226):** *et in illud insufflat, et ter illud manu signo crucis [signat]*
- **English as it stands:** `And he seals it a third time with the figure of a cross with his hand`
- **Proposed:** `And he seals it three times with the figure of a cross with his hand`
- **Why:** same rubrical τρίτον, and here it is the FIRST sealing of the oil: "a third time" cannot be right. Latin *ter*. Same class as F1; fix together.
- **Confidence:** probable (near certain). **In cruces?** LOGGED in the same entry as F1 ("Not material"); same dispute.

### F3 — 0010, col 225 band C (CHAPTER 63) — class 3 (mis-parsed)
- **Greek (plate-read, p. 118):** Ὃ δὴ ἐπιδημίαν Θεοῦ σημαίνει, καὶ τό, «Ἔρχεται ὁ Θεός, καὶ τοῦτον αἰνεῖτε,» προφητικῶς τεθὲν καὶ ᾀδόμενον, διὰ τὸ μέλλειν τὸν Κύριον ἔρχεσθαι
- **Latin (col 226):** *Quod adventum Dei significat, et duplex sonat et istud: Venit Deus, et illud, Ipsum laudate, prophetice quidem cantatus fuit, quia venturus erat Dominus*
- **English as it stands:** `Which indeed signifies a sojourning of God, as does the «God comes, and praise ye him,» which was set down and is sung prophetically, because the Lord was to come`
- **Proposed:** `Which indeed signifies a sojourning of God, and «God comes, and praise ye him,» having been set down and being sung prophetically, because the Lord was to come`
- **Why:** σημαίνει governs both objects: «God comes, and praise ye him» is the Alleluia's own meaning (the standard Αλ-ηλ-ουια gloss; the Latin says the word *duplex sonat*), not a second chant that "also" signifies; τεθὲν καὶ ᾀδόμενον agree with Ὅ, the Alleluia. "as does" makes a comparison the Greek does not make.
- **Confidence:** probable. **In cruces?** NOT logged; cruces lists ch. 63 among paragraphs where the pass found nothing.

## Candidates considered and rejected

   columns print Isaiah / *Isaias*; rendered as printed. Rejected (and logged in cruces).

4. **0010 col 225** τὸ ἁγιασθὲν ἅγιον τρίς → "the holy thing that was sanctified": plate prints
   ἅγιον, no ἔλαιον; English correctly does not supply the noun. Rejected.

   Καὶ ἀναστάς, δεικνὺς ὅτι πεσόντας ἡμᾶς Χριστὸς ἀνήγειρεν): English restores it correctly. Rejected.
7. **0010 col 228** Ὁ δὲ ἀρχιερεὺς μετὰ τὸ ἁγιάσαι τὸ ἔλαιον τὸν βαπτισθέντα τελειοῖ → "after

8. **0010 col 228** "bears from the flesh the marks of the shame" for τὰ τῆς αἰσχύνης: "marks" is
   a gloss of τά, Latin *signa*. Not a meaning change. Rejected.

   ἐμοῦ, as the English has it (Latin *opus tum agentis, tum unius [in] Trinitate Dei*). Rejected.
10. **0011 col 229** ἀποκαλύπτεται ‖ *abscondit*: verified, see above. Marker stands.
11. **0011 col 232** εἰ καὶ σημειοφόρος … χωρὶς τῆς χειροτονίας and τῆς χειροτονίας χωρὶς πολλοί:

12. **0011 col 232** τοῖς ἐχθροῖς οὐχ ἁλώσιμος → "not to be captured by the enemies" with the

13. **0012 col 233** ἐμφώτιον (file ἐκφώτιον) → "emphotion": plate prints ἐμφώτιον. Rejected.
14. **0012 col 233** "having dismissed them" for ἀπολύσας (no object): a harmless supplied
