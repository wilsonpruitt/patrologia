# Usuardus: plate verdicts on the garbled forms in Bouillart's notes (2026-09-22)

This answers `USUARDUS-NOTE-GARBLE-CHECK.md`. Every site was read at the plate with the printed
corner numbers checked first. Renders were made at 300 dpi and re-read at 600 dpi (3x crop for
single letters). For PL 124, every site was also read on the second printing (Gallica
`bpt6k54939860`, f = (col + 1)/2). The Gallica corners agreed with the archive corners on every
leaf read.

- **PL 123 (9198):** `patrologiaecurs29goog.pdf`, PDF page = leaf + 1.
- **PL 124 (9215):** `patrologiaecurs76unkngoog.pdf`, PDF page = leaf + 1.

Other slips found on screen are listed separately at the end.

**Pattern.** For PL 124, all twelve forms that the second printing corrects are *press variants*.
The archive copy prints the garble and Gallica prints it sound. None of them was our slip. All six
PLATE DIFFERS are transcription slips of ours. Five are in PL 123, which has one witness: `notum bis` for *nobis*, a misread
abbreviation, a doubled word, and a vowel intruded twice. The sixth is in PL 124 (0487), where
the two printings agree and our transcription skipped about four lines.

## Other slips seen while the page was on screen (corrected in the TSV to the plate)

**9198**
- 0649: plate *Spatiolum, ub-\|prima manu legitur* ***episcopi***, with the *i* of *ubi* lost at the line break (TSV had *ubi*), and *Item se-\|cuudi* (TSV had *secundi*). Both are Migne's own misprints. The TSV now reads *ub prima … \*episcopi\** and *secuudi*, with the line-end hyphen dropped. EN is unaffected.
- 0703: *versibus ? si res* is lowercase on the plate (TSV *Si*). *\*presbiteri viri Dei\* eidem* has no comma on the plate (TSV had one).
- 0739: the plate italicizes *Solus Pratensis* and *omisit vocem magnifice* (TSV italicized only *magnifice*).
- 0887: *omnium urbium, compitis* has a comma on the plate. *S. Re-\|gulum. Silvanectensem* has a stop on the plate (TSV had none).
- 0897: *tertio loco. quarto autem* has a stop on the plate (TSV had a comma). The plate reads ***item sancti Eustasti***, italic and with that spelling (TSV had *item sancti Eustasi*, roman). It also italicizes *commendatur* and *abbatis monasterii Luxoviensis*. EN ("'item sancti Eustasi'") renders the sense, so it was left.
- 0925: the plate reads *vel morosis jndicibus* (TSV *judicibus*). The closing quotation *suspicari ausim … censuerit.* is italic on the plate.
- 0941: *Passio S. Nicefori martyris quæ colitur* XV k. *Maii* is italic on the plate.
- 0947: the plate reads *pro Aninsulæ monasterio* (TSV *Ainsulae*). **EN "Ainsula" → "Aninsula".**

**9215**
- 0487: the plate italicizes the Caroline quotation *Item temporibus … actus leguntur* and Sollerius's quotation *Mihi certe … potius quam* XI *Martii collocaret*. ⚑ The restored text reads *hoc an. II Martii* in **both** printings (II in small caps). Taken as *hoc an* (= "or") *II Martii*, while Sollerius, quoted in the same note, gives XI Martii. The printed II is kept in the Latin, and the EN renders it as "this one or II March". It is flagged for the reader: it may be Migne's II for XI, but no second witness decides it.
- 0593: the plate italicizes *quia*, inquit, *quæ sunt ad marginem apposita recentius, textus integritati nihil officiunt*.
- 0665: the plate italicizes *utra manus autographa sit*.

**Seen and NOT changed**
- 9198 0959: *perlustravit autem prope* is read correctly in the TSV. On the plate, the *e* of *autem* looks *o*-shaped at 600 dpi (*autom*?). The copy is single-witness and a broken *e* cannot be ruled out, so the TSV was left.
- 9215 0195: archive *Ne vero litteræ illæ excederent*, Gallica *exciderent*. Both are sound Latin, so this is a press variant with no fault in it. The TSV (= archive) was left.
