## Corrections made (column 4 of the TSV only)

**0609 a** — italics.
- plate: `omittit vocem *papae*, inquit Sollerius, et legit *cimiterio Calixti*.`
- ours was: `omittit vocem papae, inquit Sollerius, et legit cimiterio Calixti.`

**0631 a** — two italic runs (Sollerius quoted) and a sentence stop our text had flattened to a comma.
- plate (600 dpi, confirmed): `*Observat Castellanus codicis ipsius inspector*, verba sunt
  Sollerii, *ex praetenso illo autographo … in quo ea mutatio facta sit*. Cum caeteri Usuardini
  codices habeant: *Apud Smirnam, sanctorum Vitalis, Revocati et Fortunati*: aliud est folium…`
- ours was: `… in quo ea mutatio facta sit, cum caeteri Usuardini codices habeant: Apud Smirnam …`
  (no italics anywhere, and a comma + lower-case *cum* where Migne sets a full stop + *Cum*).

**0641 a** — punctuation before the quoted title.

- Checked at 600 dpi: the closing `in civitate Caesareae in Mauritania.` is as we had it (the
  plate's ligature is `Cæsareæ`), and Migne's `eror` at mid-note stands.

## Migne's own defects left standing (deliberately NOT "corrected")

- **0649 a** `Spatiolum, ub prima manu legitur *episcopi*` — the plate breaks the line as `ub-` /
  `prima`, i.e. it prints a hyphen where *ubi* wants an **i**. Our `ub prima` is the honest reading
  of what is set; I did not repair it to *ubi*. Same note: `secuudi` for *secundi* is the plate's.
- **0641 a** `eror`, `pag 590` — the plate's.
- **0655 a** `qnod` for *quod* — the plate's.
- **0725 a** the note ends `quo sanctum Trifonem colit Romana Ecclesia- BOUILLART.` — the mark after
  *Ecclesia* prints as a **hyphen-height dash**, almost certainly a raised or damaged full point.
  Our text reads it as a full stop and I left it there rather than import a dash into the apparatus;
  flagged here so the reading is on record.
- **0747 a** the plate prints `una omnium **séntentia** est` with what looks like an acute over the
  first e — a damaged or foul sort, not an accent Migne wanted. Our `sententia` stands.

## English twins

**No English `[nt:]` was changed.** Every correction above is an italic, a point, a signature letter
or a spelling of Migne's own — none alters the sense, and each affected twin already reads correctly
against the corrected Latin:

- 0609: twin renders *papae* / *cimiterio Calixti* as quoted forms already.
- 0695: the twin carries the phrase as a quotation of the codex's words; *Lizinio* vs *Licinio* is a
  spelling of the same emperor's name and the English is unaffected.
- 0735: the twin reads "in the autograph", which is what *autogragho* means; the misprint is a
  fact about Migne's type, not about the sense.
- 0655: the twin signs **Bouillart.** while this one note's plate signs **BOUILLARD.** I left the
  English as it is — it is the same man, and "Bouillard" in English would read as a different
  person. Raised here in case the orchestrator wants a house rule on printed signature variants.
