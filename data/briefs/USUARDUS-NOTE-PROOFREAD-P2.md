## Corrections

### 0769 (leaf 399→400) — three
1. **`nunctis` → `inunctis`.** Plate (n400 left col., last line of the note on that page):
   *Nam oculis inunctis codicem inspexisse Castellanum jam pluribus exemplis noveram.* Confirmed at
   600 dpi; the `i` is set with a hair space, which is what swallowed it. The sense is Bouillart's
   point — salved (bleary) eyes, picked up by *lippum* in the next clause.
2. **`manio` → `minio`.** Plate (n401 left col.): *quod pro mi-|nio, quo in reliquo codice Nonae,
   Idus, Kalendae pinguntur*. A hyphenated line break; the minium (red lead) is the contrast to the
   *violaceus color* two words later.
3. **Italic boundary.** Plate: *edita eum* ***in episcopatus honore sublimatum*** *testificantur*.
   Ours opened the italic after *in*; moved to before it.

### 0833 (leaf 431) — one
`omisit vocem nobiliores` → `omisit vocem *nobiliores*`. The quoted word is set in bold italic.

### 0851 (leaf 440) — two
1. **`tractos` → `traductos`.** Plate: *sed aut male connexos aut perperam huc traductos fuisse*.
   Confirmed at 2.5× on the 300 dpi render. Sense unchanged.
2. **Italics on the Hieronymian list.** Plate: *Sic enim illi, teste Henschenio:* ***Nicomediae,
   Castori, Dionysii, Nonni, Cyriacae, Milisae, Eugeniae, Juliani, Asclepiodoti.*** Ours was plain.

### 0853 (leaf 441) — three
1. **`cum` → `com`.** Plate: *Narrat vitae scriptor B. Gertrudem, com dies mortis immineret*. This
   is **Migne's own misprint and it stays** (brief §"Illegible"/no silent improvement). Read at 600
   dpi: the bowl is a closed round `o`, plainly unlike the open `u` of *Gertrudem* on the same line.
   Recorded in column 6.
2. **Italics on Ultan's reply:** *Ultanum respondisse:* ***Hodie decimus sextus Kalendas Aprilis est
   dies. Crastino autem die inter missarum solemnia illa Dei ancilla et Christi virgo de corpore est
   migratura***.
3. **Italics on the Acta quotation:** *defunctam fuisse* ***die Dominica sub die sexto decimo
   Kalendas Aprilis?***
