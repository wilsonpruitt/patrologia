## 3. 0382B — `[var:]` Jo 4:13–14 (the collapsed clause) ⭐

- **Leaf:** n196, right column, **col. 382, band B** (the clause sits in the last four lines of
  band B; the printed **C** stands two lines below it, beside *mihi hanc aquam,*).
- **Migne prints, exactly** (italics as marked, line breaks marked `|`):

  > `VERS. 34. — Domine , semper, etc. Sicut mulier |`
  > `Samaritana audiens : `**`Qui biberit de hac aqua , non |`**
  > **`sitiet in æternum,`**` secundum corpus accepit , et ta- |`
  > `men labore et indigentia carere volens, dixit : `**`Da |`**
  > **`mihi hanc aquam,`**`sic isti quærunt panem qui eos re- |`
  > `ficiat, et nunquam deficiat,…`

  The whole quoted clause is **`Qui biberit de hac aqua , non sitiet in æternum,`** — in italic,
  with a spaced comma before *non*, and the ligature **æ** in *æternum*.
- **Verdict: CONFIRMED as Migne's.** The collapse is Migne's own: the printed clause makes
  drinking of *this* (Jacob's-well) water quench thirst for ever, reversing John 4:13–14's
  contrast (*qui biberit ex aqua hac sitiet iterum* / *qui autem biberit ex aqua quam ego dabo ei
  non sitiet in aeternum*), and the gloss then builds on the reversed sense. Nothing here is our
  transcription's doing. `[var:]` stands — and it is the strongest of the three.

## SECONDARY — the four declined oddities

## 6. `audit` (Jo 7:7) — **col. 385, band D**

- **Leaf:** n198, left column. The printed **D** stands beside the line
  `Judæi habitabant in tabernaculis : in memoriam il-`, eight lines above this passage; the
  passage is the last paragraph of col. 385.
- **Migne prints:** `VERS. 6. — Tempus. Ideo non est nunc tempus | meum, sed vestrum, quia non
  sum amator mundi | ut vos, sed accusator, et ideo non audit vos mun- | dus suos falsos testes,
  qui dicitis bona mala et mala | bona.`
- **Verdict: CONFIRMED as Migne's.** At 2.3× the `au` of `audit` is unmistakable — an *a*
  followed by a *u* with the same two-stroke form as the *u* in *accusator* on the line above;
  there is no *o*. So the reading that the sense resists (John 7:7 has *odit*, "the world
  hateth you") is Migne's own, and our English (*"the world does not hear you"*) renders what is
  printed. Nothing to patch; the stint was right to decline a `[sic:]`, and had it fired one it
  would have been a false accusation against the type.

## 7. `actionem personarum` — **col. 386, band D** (NOT 0387A)

- **Leaf:** n198, **right** column, bottom. ⚠ **Correction to the brief:** this phrase is printed
  in the last five lines of **column 386**, before the turn to 387; the TEI's `<pb n="0387A"/>`
  falls mid-sentence at *secundum veritatem | **rerum***, i.e. after the phrase. The printed **D**
  for this band is the one beside the left column's *Judæi habitabant* line.
- **Migne prints:** `non est judicandum secundum faciem, id est actio- | nem personarum, ut quod
  licuit Moysi, mihi non | liceat, sed justum judicium, id est, secundum veri-`
- **Verdict: CONFIRMED as Migne's.** Set with the line break inside the word (`actio-|nem`),
  and the first element is plainly `actio`, not `accep`. The expected *acceptionem personarum*
  (Rom 2:11 / Acts 10:34, and the sense the next sentence needs — *ne in judicandis causis
  personas accipiamus*) is **not** what Migne printed. No patch.

## 8. `assensionem` — **col. 387, band D**

- **Leaf:** n199, left column. The printed **D** stands beside the `VERS. 33.` line that opens
  this paragraph.
- **Migne prints:** `cientem, quæretis me post resurrectionem et assen- | sionem cum tanta
  miracula fient per apostolos mea | virtute,…`
- **Verdict: CONFIRMED as Migne's.** Set with the line break inside the word
  (`assen-|sionem`); the doubled *ss* and the *e* are clear at 2.1×. The word wanted is
  *ascensionem* (the English rightly reads "the ascension"), but *assensionem* is the plate's.
  No patch.

## 9. `Ecclesiam` (third allegory) — **col. 389, band D**

- **Leaf:** n200, left column. The printed **D** stands **on this very line**, beside
  `Ecclesiam, quæ relinquens Deum, fornicata erat`.
- **Migne prints:** `Vel signat | Ecclesiam, quæ relinquens Deum, fornicata erat | cum dæmonibus,
  quam Judæus accusat, quod ad | Christum accedat, et quasi novum idololatriæ genus | reputata
  est.`
- **Verdict: CONFIRMED as Migne's.** Capital *E*, `Ecclesiam` in full, no abbreviation and no
  competing word. (The third allegory is coherent as printed: the Church *ex gentibus*, which had
  fornicated with demons before Christ and whom the Jew now accuses of a new idolatry.) No patch.
