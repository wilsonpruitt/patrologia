## CALIBRATION — done before any marker was read

**Calibrated on leaf n214.** Cropped the top 7%: printed corner numbers read **417** (left) and
**418** (right), running head `GLOSSA ORDINARIA. — EVANG. JOAN.` The map `n = floor((col+11)/2)`
therefore holds, left column = lower number.

⚑ **Anomaly worth recording, and it is Migne's:** on **leaf n215 the printed right-hand corner
number reads `421`, not `420`.** Magnified at 6× to be sure — the digits are unmistakably 4-2-1.
The next leaf, **n216, reads `421` / `422`**, so `421` is printed twice in the volume and the
corner number **`420` is never printed at all**. The content settles which is which: n215's right
column runs from `VERS. 37. — Tu dicis` through `Sicut agnus coram tondente…`, and n216's left
column opens `tur. Non enim ut conscius convincitur, sed ut mansuetus pro aliis immolatur`, which
is the continuation of n215-right's last sentence and is our `[0421A]`. **So n215-right IS column
420, mis-numbered by Migne's compositor.** Anyone re-reading this range from the corner numbers
alone will mis-file every finding on that column by one; use the running text.

⚑ Band letters were located from the gutter strip on each leaf rather than assumed. On col 420
they fall at A = top, B ≈ line 16, C ≈ line 31, D ≈ line 47. Where the plate's band letter and our
TEI's `<pb>` anchor disagree by a line or two I give **both**, and I flag it in the item.

**Verdicts: 8 items read, 8 CONFIRMED as Migne's, 0 withdrawn.**

## 1. 0417A — `[var:]` Jo 17:19 *sanctifico* — **CONFIRMED as Migne's**

**Leaf n214, column 417, band A** (the lemma line sits ~2 lines above the gutter `B`).

Migne prints, exactly (roman/italic as marked):

> Vᴇʀs. 19. -— *Et pro eis ego sanctifico*. Id est, ut pro eis prosit, ego me hominem
> sanctificabo in me Verbo quod est ab initio Incarnationis suæ quando Verbum factum est caro.
> Ita autem illis prodest, quia et ipsos, qui sunt ego quia caput et corpus sunt unus Christus,
> sanctifico in meipso, unde addit: *ut sint et ipsi* sicut ego, *sanctificati iu veritate* et in me.

The stint's whole argument is on the plate. **The lemma is the present *sanctifico*** (italic),
and **the very next clause expounds it with the future *sanctificabo*** (roman) — the two forms
stand nine words apart in Migne's own type. `[var:]` **CONFIRMED**; no patch.

⚑ **Incidental, not part of my brief and needing no marker:** the closing italic run prints
***sanctificati iu veritate*** — `iu` for `in`, a wrong sort, verified at 4×. Our TEI reads
`sanctificati in veritate`. **This is a Migne typo that Corpus Corporum has silently normalised**,
i.e. the opposite direction from the usual finding. No `[sic:]` is owed (our text is correct); it
is logged only because it is evidence about how the digitisation behaves.

## 2. 0420C — `[var:]` Jo 1:29 *peccata* (plural) — **CONFIRMED as Migne's**

**Leaf n215, column 420** (printed corner `421` — see calibration), **band C.**

Migne prints:

> Vᴇʀs. 2. — *Coronam de spinis imposuerunt*. In spinea corona nostrorum notatur susceptio
> peccatorum, pro qua mortalis fierit voluit. Unde præcursor : *Ecce Agnus Dei, ecce qui tollit
> peccata mundi*. Spinæ namque in peccatorum significatione ponuntur.

***peccata*** is unambiguous at 3× — the plural, against the Vulgate's *peccatum*. And the
stint's corroboration is on the page in Migne's type: *nostrorum … susceptio **peccatorum***
before it, *in **peccatorum** significatione* after it. `[var:]` **CONFIRMED**; no patch.

⚑ **Incidental, no marker owed:** Migne prints ***fierit*** (*pro qua mortalis fierit voluit*) —
a non-word. Our TEI reads `mortalis fieri voluit`. **Again Migne's defect, silently corrected by
Corpus Corporum.** Two such in this stint's range.

## 3. 0420C — `[var:]` Gen 3:18 *Terra tua* / no *tibi* — **CONFIRMED as Migne's**

**Leaf n215, column 420, band C**, two lines below item 2.

Migne prints:

> Unde Dominus : *Terra tua spinas et tribulos germinabit (Gen.* ɪɪɪ*)*. Id est, conscientia tua

***Terra tua*** is printed, in italic, at the head of the quotation; ***tibi* is absent**; and

attribution `(Gen. ɪɪɪ)` **inside** the italic run, which our English carries as `[n:]`.
`[var:]` **CONFIRMED**; no patch.

## 4. 0420D — `[var:]` Isa 53:7 in the Acts 8:32 form — **CONFIRMED as Migne's**

**Leaf n215, column 420, band D** (the last four lines of the column).

Migne prints the whole clause, with his own attribution:

> Vᴇʀs. 9. — *Jesus autem.* … Legitur igitur siluisse Christus ut prophetia impleatur : *Sicut
> agnus coram tondente sine voce, sic non aperuit os suum (Isa.* ʟɪɪɪ*)*. Quæ similitudo de agno
> data est, ut in suo silentio non reus sed innocens habeatur.

not *obmutescet*), **the future→perfect shift** (*aperuit*, not *aperiet*), and **Migne's own

patch. The clause runs on to `[0421A]` *Non enim ut conscius convincitur…* at the head of n216's
left column, which is also what fixes n215-right as column 420.

## 5. 0423B `undet` — **CONFIRMED as Migne's**

**Leaf n217, column 423, band B** (the gutter `B` stands two lines above `VERS. 16`; our TEI
anchors this passage at `0423B`, and plate and anchor agree).

Migne prints:

> Vᴇʀs. 18. — *Venit Maria Magdalenæ annuntians discipulis, quia vidit Dominum*, etc. Ibi culpa
> abscinditur undet processit. Mulier mortem viro propinavit, modo mulier vitam nuntiat viris…

Magnified at 5×: ***undet*** is set as **one word, mid-line, with normal word spacing on both
sides** — no line break, no ligature, no broken sort. This is the 9004 @0897C *prava indirecta*
case again: **a fused word that is Migne's, not ours.** The conjecture *unde* + *et* may well be
right about what the exemplar had, but the fusion is on the printed page.

**CONFIRMED as Migne's.** The Pattern 7/10 carry in the English is correct as it stands, and a
`[sic:]` against Migne would now be defensible here — but the stint declined to fire one and I do
not fire markers, so this stands as a read column with the fact recorded.

## 6. 0422A `Separatur` (the lemma) — **CONFIRMED as Migne's**

**Leaf n216, column 422.** ⚑ **Band correction:** our TEI anchors this at `0422A`, but on the
plate `CAPUT XX.` and the gutter `B` fall *above* it — `VERS. 4. — Currebant autem` is the first

Migne prints:

> cognitione remota sunt. In involuto, nec finis nec initium capitur.

***Separatur*** — the passive verb, in italic, as the lemma — is clean and unambiguous at 2×.
**CONFIRMED as Migne's.** The stint was right to render "*It is separated*"; the divergence from
the Vulgate's *separatim* is the printed text's, not the digitisation's.

## 7. 0423A `Magdalenae` and `vidit` — **BOTH CONFIRMED as Migne's**

**Leaf n217, column 423, band B** (same two lines as item 5; see the band note there — our
anchor says `0423A`, the plate's `B` letter stands above this lemma).

Migne prints, magnified at 4×:

> Vᴇʀs. 18. — *Venit Maria Magdalenæ annuntians discipulis, quia vidit Dominum*, etc.

- ***Magdalenæ*** — set with the **æ ligature**, i.e. *Magdalenae*, the genitive, against the
  received indeclinable *Magdalene*. **CONFIRMED as Migne's.**
- ***vidit*** — the third `i` is a clean dotted `i`, not an `e`, and there is no final `-i`
  reading available: the word is `v-i-d-i-t`. **CONFIRMED as Migne's.**

Two separate letters in one verse, and **both are on the plate.** The stint's caution was
correct in method and, as it turns out, both declines were declines of Migne's own text; the
"correct form prints eleven words later" argument (*Mariae Magdalenae* at 0423C) would have
pointed the wrong way had it been used.

## 8. 0420B `liberatum` — **CONFIRMED as Migne's**

**Leaf n215, column 420.** ⚑ **Band correction:** our TEI's `[0420B]` anchor falls two lines
*after* this word; on the plate the gutter `B` is level with `dicis, quia rex sum ego`, so
***liberatum* is the last line of band A**, immediately above the `B`. Recorded so the site's
citation is not silently wrong by a band.

Migne prints:

> Vᴇʀs. 37. — *Tu dicis*. Non se regem timet confiteri, sed ita liberatum est, ut neque se
> regem neget, nec de mundo se fateatur esse, ut ille sentiebat qui dixit : *Ergo rex es tu?*
> Cui respondit : [B] *Tu dicis, quia rex sum ego*. Quasi dicat, carnalis carnaliter dicis.

Magnified at 5×: ***liberatum*** — `l-i-b-e-r-a-t-u-m`, with the `e` between `b` and `r`
fully formed and separately inked. It is **not** *libratum*.

**CONFIRMED as Migne's.** The *Ita temperat responsionem, ut nec … nec …* parallel at 0419A is a
real parallel and the reading is still odd, but the oddity is the printed page's. The stint's
decision to render as printed and mark nothing stands, and **the plausible emendation would have
been an emendation of Migne, not a repair of our file.**

not read them as new defects.
