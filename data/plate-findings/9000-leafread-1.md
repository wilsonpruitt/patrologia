⚑ **But the citation attached to it does not.** Migne prints **`(II Cor)` with no chapter number**;

premise that "Migne cites it to the verse" (so worded in `cruces.md` line 299) is not what the plate
shows. **Recommend: keep the `[var:]`, patch the note to `[n: (II Cor)]`, and drop the "cited by Migne
to the verse" reasoning from the crux.**

# Declined oddities settled (from `src/english/9000/cruces.md`)

## ⭐ D1. 0258D — *ejunus* (cruces §non-word table, row 2) — **OUR FILE'S ERROR**

Our Latin: *Qui hoc medicamento utitur, non remanet **ejunus**.*

**Plate prints** (witness n134, right col., ~2× crop):
> *ditur. Qui hoc medicamento utitur, non remanet*
> ***jejunus.***

**Verdict: WITHDRAWN — Corpus Corporum's error.** Migne sets **`jejunus`** in full, at the head of
its own line, initial *j* clean and unbroken, no clip and no ink loss anywhere near it. The unique
non-word *ejunus* is our digitization's, not Migne's.

**Patch the Latin:** `non remanet ejunus.` → **`non remanet jejunus.`**
(and the English, "does not remain hungry/fasting", is unaffected — it was already rendered from the
right sense). No `[sic:]` should ever be raised here.
