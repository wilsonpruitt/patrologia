## leaf n156 — columns 301 / 302

### 0302B — `[var:]` Is 9:2 / Mt 4:16 conflation — **CONFIRMED**

Claim: our Latin reads *Qui sedebant sub umbra mortis lumen ortum est eis*, against Vulg. Is 9:2
*habitantibus in regione umbrae mortis, lux orta est eis*.

Plate (450 dpi), col. 302, VERS. 6, three lines below the *Arborem fici* lemma:

> tanquam sub umbra mortis, de quibus dictum est .
> *Qui sedebant sub umbra mortis lumen ortum est eis*
> (*Isa.* xi).

Migne prints the conflated form word for word, including *sub umbra* and *lumen*. **Marker stands.**

⚠ **Incidental, same line — our Latin diverges from the plate in the citation numeral.** The plate
prints **(Isa. XI)**; our file has `[n: (Isa. IX)]`. Read at 450 dpi and again at 5× enlargement:
small-cap **X then I**, unmistakable. Migne's own miscitation of Isaiah 9 as XI; Corpus Corporum has
silently transposed it. Not part of any marker — flagged for the Latin patch queue.

## leaf n159 — columns 307 / 308

### 0307A — `[var:]` Rom 3:23 *gratia* for *gloria* — **CONFIRMED**

Plate (450 dpi), col. 307, end of the *Bos in puteum* gloss:

> sos extrahit : *Omnes enim peccaverunt, et eg-*
> *gratia Dei* (*Rom.* xxxv).

Letter by letter on the italic: **g – r – a – t – i – a**. No *l*, no *o* in the second position;
the ascender count and the *t* crossbar are decisive. Migne prints **gratia Dei**. **Marker stands.**

(The plate's *(Rom. XXXV)* — Romans has sixteen chapters — is Migne's, and our file already carries it.)

### 0307B — `[var:]` Ps 87:16, our *confusus* vs Vulg. *conturbatus* — **WITHDRAWN**

Plate (450 dpi, then the word enlarged 4×), col. 307, VERS. 8, closing the *Ne forte honoratior* gloss:

> serat, humiliat, dicens cum Propheta : *Exaltatus au-*
> *tem humiliatus sum et **confessus*** (*Psal.* lxxxvii).

The plate prints **confessus** — c‑o‑n‑f‑**e**‑**ss**‑u‑s: a clear *e* after the *f* (compare the true
*u* in *sum* and in the terminal *-us* on the same word), and **two** *s* before the ending.
Our Latin's *confusus* is **Corpus Corporum's error, not Migne's.**

**Patch:** `et confusus` → **`et confessus`** at 0307B.

⚑ Note for consolidation: the *witness* the single-word rule requires is absent — the divergence our
file records is not the one the plate makes. After the patch, Migne still diverges from Vulg. Ps 87:16
*conturbatus*, but by the word **confessus**, so whatever marker stands here must be rewritten to the
plate's word. The marker as currently phrased (attaching to *confusus*) must go.

## leaf n168 — columns 325 / 326

### 0326B — `[var:]` 2 Cor 13:4 *mortuus* for *crucifixus*, and 2 Cor 5:16 *sed jam* for *sed nunc jam* — **CONFIRMED**

Plate (450 dpi), col. 326, VERS. 5, after *hoc est quod Apostolus ait :*

> *Etsi cognovimus secundum carnem Christum, sed jam*
> *non novimus. Si enim **mortuus** est ex infirmitate, sed*
> *jam vivit ex virtute Dei* (*II Cor.* v, xiii).

Both claim-bearing readings are Migne's: **mortuus est ex infirmitate** (not *crucifixus*), and
**sed jam non novimus** (no *nunc*). **Marker stands.**

⚠ **Incidental on this same gloss — our Latin has a dittography the plate does not.** Four words
later the plate reads *Manebat aliquando Dominus in domo principis Pharisæorum, **id est** in
Judæorum Synagoga docebat*; our file reads *…Pharisaeorum, **id est id est** in Judaeorum Synagoga
docebat*. Single *id est* on the plate, read at 450 dpi. Corpus Corporum's doubling. Unmarked in our
apparatus; flagged for the Latin patch queue.

### 0326B — `[var:]` Acts 11:3 *ad gentes praeputium habentes* for *ad viros praeputium habentes* — **CONFIRMED**

Plate (450 dpi), col. 326, VERS. 7:

> …Unde et adversus Petrum dicitur : *Quare in-*
> *troisti **ad gentes** præputium habentes* (*Act.* ix).

Migne prints **ad gentes**, and cites *Act.* IX (his own error for Acts 11); our file carries both.
**Marker stands.**

(Minor, not a marker: the plate closes the sentence with a full stop; our file has `?`. Left alone.)
