## Expect the ours-side profile of a COMMENTARY, per 7a″
7914 gave 7 ours-side defects, ALL lemma-conformations or mis-bound references, ZERO
dropped negations. 11065 (homilies) gave the opposite. This work is commentary on the
most familiar texts in the liturgy AND prints two Latin editions side by side, so the
conformation risk is doubled: an English may be conformed to the Vulgate *or* levelled
between Rabanus's two columns.
