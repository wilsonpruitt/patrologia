Quaestiones et dubia

Maximus the Confessor — read the work →

These are the edition's working notes, published as they were written. They record, work by work, where Migne's plate is defective or the reading uncertain: the printed form, what the English does with it, and why. They are not a groomed apparatus. Where a note argues with itself, withdraws an earlier decision, or leaves a question open, that is the record of how the reading was actually reached, and it is left standing — a conjecture that is visible can be checked, and one that has been tidied away cannot.

The English never silently repairs the page. A construable but wrong reading is rendered literally and the conjecture is recorded here, never applied to the text. How the English is produced and checked →

Chunk 0001 (qd-001)

  • 0785 · …compassion, being without arrogance, being without pride, compunction. — T. The adjectival virtues (τὸ ἄθυμον, τὸ ἄφθονον, τὸ ἄκριτον, τὸ ἀκενόδοξον, ἄτυφον, ἀνυπερήφανον, κατανυκτικόν) are given as states; ἀοργησία «freedom from wrath», τὸ ἄθυμον «being without anger» (the facing Latin ab ira, furore … immunem esse); σωφροσύνη «temperance» (Latin castitas animique sobrietas).
  • 0788 · …craving, as though swelling with desire for pleasure. — T. κισσάω is the craving of a pregnant woman; the LXX uses it for conceiving and the answer reads it as craving, hence «conceive with craving» throughout. ὀργῶσα τὴν ἡδονήν «swelling with desire for pleasure»; the facing Latin catuliens, libidine.
  • 0789 · …himself over, and became a curse for us. — S. Gal 3:13.
  • 0789 · …the assarion is a piece of ten nummi. — T. δεκάνουμον, a coin of ten nummi (the facing Latin decem nummos). Iota is the Greek numeral ten and the first letter of Ἰησοῦς.
  • 0789 · …said of Elijah: «Where is the God Aphphu?»? — S. 4 Kgdms (2 Kgs) 2:14 LXX, Elisha striking the water with Elijah's mantle: Ποῦ ὁ θεὸς Ηλιου αφφω; T.   • 0789 · …Answer. It is interpreted according to three approaches: — T. ἀφφώ is the LXX's transliteration of the Hebrew «even he»; the three readings take it as a word. ἐπιβολή «approach».
  • 0792 · …For tropology is so called from being turned. — T. ἀντὶ τοῦ τρέπεσθαι, from τρέπεσθαι, «to be turned»; the facing Latin adds seu moribus mutari.
  • 0792 · …thy mother's son thou didst set a stumbling-block*»? — S. Ps 49:20 LXX (50:20 Heb.). T. Migne's heading for this question (ΕΡΩΤΗΣΙΣ Θʹ) is missing from the machine text; numbered by the sequence.
  • 0792 · …son. QUESTION 10 Since in the holy Diadochus, — P. Diadochus, bishop of Photice in Epirus (5th c.), author of a Hundred Chapters on spiritual perfection. S. cf. Diadochus, Capita centum, ch. 100, as cited here.

  • 0793 · …human, they say, is the equity and gratitude — T. ἰσονομία «equity»; εὐγνωμοσύνη «gratitude», as ruled (the facing Latin probitatem).
  • 0793 · …since the Lord's privations became habits for us: — T. στερήσεις / ἕξεις, the logicians' privation and habit (possession): what the Lord lacked became ours to have.
  • 0793 · …So then, as this opposing supposition of his, — T. Migne prints Ὡς οὖν [Ἴσ. πρός] ἀντικειμένην: [Ἴσ. πρός], the editor's conjecture («perhaps πρός»); the English supplies it, since without a preposition the accusative ἀντικειμένην … τὴν ὑπόνοιαν has nothing to govern it. The facing Latin construes without it (velut … adversantem opinionem insectatus).
  • 0796 · …my purpose». QUESTION 13 Since Gregory of Nyssa — P. Gregory, bishop of Nyssa (d. c. 395), brother of Basil; his teaching on the final restoration (ἀποκατάστασις) was read as Origenist.
  • 0796 · …depth of his lofty contemplation, to hint everywhere — T. Migne prints [R. πολλαχοῦ] after πανταχοῦ: a variant, «in many places», from the witness marked R. The English follows the printed πανταχοῦ, with the facing Latin ubique.
  • 0796 · …Nyssa has made most use in his discourses, — T. κατακέχρηται, «has used to the full», which can also mean «has misused»; the facing Latin abusus est keeps the same ambiguity.
  • 0797 · …said in his heart, There is no God*; — S. Ps 13:1 LXX (14:1 Heb.); also Ps 52:1 LXX (53:1 Heb.). The Psalm has ἄφρων, the word the answer has just used for μωρός.

  • 0797 · …first hoped in Christ*», writing to the Ephesians? — S. Eph 1:12. T. Migne's heading (ΕΡΩΤΗΣΙΣ ΙϚʹ) is missing from the machine text; numbered by the sequence.
