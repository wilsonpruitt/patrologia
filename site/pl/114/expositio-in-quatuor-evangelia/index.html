The ascription is the edition’s own rather than the manuscripts’, and the editor Migne reprints sets out his grounds for it at the head of the work, printed and translated here.

ADMONITIO MARTIANAEI(Opp. S. Hieronymi, tom. V.)

Commaticam in quatuor Evangelia expositionem reperi in codicibus manuscriptis falso ascriptam Hieronymo, tantisque scatentem barbarismis atque soloecismis, ut omnino videretur indigna quae prodiret in lucem: sed cum hujusmodi opuscula non displiceant omnibus, hic cum aliis id genus eam Expositionem edere volui, maxime quod Strabus Fuldensis auctor esse potuerit hujus Commentarii, quem postea imperitissimus aliquis descripserit cum silva barbarismorum ac soloecismorum. Qualecunque illud est, ex antiquo codice Gemeticensi transcriptum opusculum, typographis nostris edendum tradidi cum suis pene universis vitiis. Neque vero dignum judicavi quod more nostro castigaretur, et in eo emendando tempus, oleum operamque perderem.

MARTIANAY'S ADMONITION(Opp. S. Hieronymi, tom. V.)

I found in manuscript codices a clause-by-clause exposition of the four Gospels falsely ascribed to Jerome, and teeming with so many barbarisms and solecisms that it seemed altogether unworthy to come forth into the light: but since little works of this kind are not displeasing to everyone, I have wished to publish that Exposition here along with others of the same sort, chiefly because Strabo of Fulda may have been the author of this Commentary, which afterwards some most unskilled person copied out with a forest of barbarisms and solecisms. Whatever it may be, I have handed over the little work, transcribed from an ancient codex of Jumièges, to our printers to be published with almost all its faults. Nor indeed did I judge it worthy to be corrected after our manner, and to lose time, oil and labour upon emending it.

INCIPIT EXPOSITIO QUATUOR EVANGELIORUM.

861APrimum quaerendum est omnium librorum tempus; locum; persona; et quare non de duodecim Evangelia recipiantur nisi quatuor: quia totus mundus ex quatuor elementis est, id est coelo, terra, igne, aqua. Per coelum Joannes ostenditur: quia sicut coelum omnia superat, ita et Joannes qui dixit: In principio erat Verbum: Per Matthaeum terra: qui dixit: Liber generationis Jesu Christi; Per Lucam ignis, qui dixit: Nonne cor jam ardens erat in nobis, Per Marcum aqua, qui dixit: Vox clamantis in deserto. Id est, quatuor flumina de uno fonte quatuor evangelistas, significat, id est Christum. Fison, insufflictio, significat Joannem; Geon, velocitas, significat Matthaeum; Tigris, felicitas, significat Marcum. Eufrates, fertilitas, significat Lucam; 861Cirrigant mundum, id est Ecclesiam: et significant quatuor virtutes: Id est prudentiam, temperantiam, fortitudinem, et justitiam: et sicut paradisum irrigant quatuor flumina, sic et cor nostrum has quatuor virtutes et homo ex quatuor elementis consistit; ex aere, igne, et aqua, et terra. Ex aere flatus: Igne sanguis, aqua flamma, terra corpus: per caput coelum, ubi sunt duo luminaria; pectus, aer; venter aquam; pedes terram. Item, Adam a quatuor litteris, et a quatuor stellis nomen accepit, quod est artis, dosis, anatholis, mesimbrio: vel quatuor significant evangelistas, et sicut de Adam omnis 862Bhomo nascitur, ita per quatuor evangelistas omnes ad fidem veniunt. Item Arca Noe quadrata legitur ex lignis facta, longitudo trecentorum cubitorum et latitudo unius, altitudo triginta et uno cubito consummata, significat Ecclesiam; per quatuor evangelistas longitudo spes vitae aeternae, latitudo charitas, altitudo fides Trinitatis unum cubitum unum Dominum colere. Bitumina intus et foris charitatem Dei et proximi significat. Item arca testamenti ubi erant duae tabulae lapideae et virga Aaron, quatuor circulis aureis portabatur, et vectes ex auro cooperti quatuor evangelistas significant; arca Ecclesiam, vectes doctores, sicut illi circumdati erant ab annulis, ita sacerdotes de Veteri et Novo Testamento docti, quatuor evangelistae portant Ecclesiam ab 862Chaeresi defendendo; per virgam crux ostenditur; et sicut quadratum lignum in qua parte versum fuerit firmum stat: Ita homo ex quatuor evangelistis edoctus contra haeresim et tribulationem firmus fiat. Item Matthaeus donatus, Marcus donum excelsum: Lucas consurgens: Joannes gratia Domini. Item rota in rota, Spiritus in rota, praedicatio Evangelii in mundo girans: Spiritus in rota, Spiritus sanctus in Evangelio: Item vas linteum et super ipsum quatuor lineae quatuor evangelistas significant, haec sunt, munda et immunda, Judaei et gentes: Item quatuor animalia habent oculos ante se retro clamantes, 863ASanctus, quatuor evangelistae sunt: Item supplantavit Jacob ter fratrem suum, primum per benedictionem, secundo per lentem, tertio per benedictionem: Esau diabolum significat, Jacob Christum qui ipsum ter vicit et supplantavit, ita et nos debemus supplantare fratrem nostrum diabolum, tres vicibus in cogitatione, in verbo et in opere: Ideo frater noster est, quia Deus ipsum creavit qui et nos; unum patrem habemus Deum, et bonum illum creavit, sed per suum vitium superbiendo se privavit: Quatuor 864Aevangelistas significat Matthaeus faciem hominis, Lucas vituli; Marcus leonis; Joannes aquilae: Dominus Jesus Christus totum implevit, homo nascendo vitulus immolando: Leo surgendo, aquila ascendendo, Rota in rota, lege in legem, vetus in novum: cur quatuor evangelistae, non per quatuor apostolos scribuntur, nisi per duos discipulos et duos apostolos; quia filii Jacob de duabus liberis, et duabus ancillis nati sunt. Matthaeus mel: Marcus lac: Lucas oleum: Joannes vinum.

HERE BEGINS THE EXPOSITION OF THE FOUR GOSPELS.

861AFirst, of all books the time is to be asked; the place; the person; and why out of twelve Gospels none are received but four: because the whole world is of four elements, that is, heaven, earth, fire, water. By heaven John is shown: because as heaven surpasses all things, so also John, who said: In the beginning was the Word: By Matthew, earth: who said: The book of the generation of Jesus Christ; By Luke, fire, who said: Was not the heart already burning within us, [Vulg. cor nostrum ardens erat, "our heart was burning"] By Mark, water, who said: The voice of one crying in the desert. That is, four rivers from one fount signifies the four evangelists, that is, Christ. Phison, insufflation, signifies John; Geon, swiftness, signifies Matthew; Tigris, happiness, signifies Mark. Euphrates, fruitfulness, signifies Luke; 861Cthey water the world, that is, the Church: and they signify the four virtues: That is, prudence, temperance, fortitude, and justice: and as four rivers water paradise, so also our heart these four virtues; and man consists of four elements: of air, fire, and water, and earth. From air, breath: from fire, blood; from water, flame; from earth, the body: by the head, heaven, where the two lights are; the breast, air; the belly, water; the feet, earth. Likewise, Adam took his name from four letters and from four stars, which is artis, dosis, anatholis, mesimbrio: or the four signify the evangelists; and as from Adam every 862Bman is born, so through the four evangelists all come to the faith. Likewise Noah's ark is read to have been made four-square out of timbers, its length of three hundred cubits and its breadth of one, its height finished at thirty and one cubit: it signifies the Church; through the four evangelists: the length, the hope of eternal life; the breadth, charity; the height, faith in the Trinity; the one cubit, to worship one Lord. The pitch within and without signifies the love of God and of neighbour. Likewise the ark of the testament, where were the two stone tables and Aaron's rod, was carried by four golden rings, and the bars covered with gold signify the four evangelists; the ark, the Church; the bars, the teachers; as those were encircled by the rings, so the priests taught out of the Old and the New Testament: the four evangelists carry the Church by defending it from 862Cheresy; by the rod the cross is shown; and as a squared timber, on whichever side it be turned, stands firm: So let the man taught out of the four evangelists become firm against heresy and tribulation. Likewise Matthew, given; Mark, a lofty gift: Luke, rising up: John, the grace of the Lord. Likewise a wheel within a wheel, the Spirit in the wheel, the preaching of the Gospel wheeling in the world: the Spirit in the wheel, the Holy Spirit in the Gospel: Likewise the linen vessel, and upon it four lines, signify the four evangelists; these are, clean and unclean, Jews and gentiles: Likewise the four living creatures have eyes before them and behind, crying, 863AHoly: they are the four evangelists: Likewise Jacob supplanted his brother three times: first by the blessing, secondly by the lentils, thirdly by the blessing: Esau signifies the devil, Jacob Christ, who conquered and supplanted him three times; so we too ought to supplant our brother the devil three times, in thought, in word and in deed: He is our brother for this reason, that God created him who created us also; we have one Father, God, and he created him good, but by his own fault, in growing proud, he deprived himself: The four 864Aevangelists it signifies: Matthew the face of a man, Luke of a calf; Mark of a lion; John of an eagle: the Lord Jesus Christ fulfilled the whole: a man by being born, a calf by being sacrificed: a lion by rising, an eagle by ascending. A wheel within a wheel, law within law, the old within the new: why the four evangelists are not written by four apostles, but by two disciples and two apostles: because the sons of Jacob were born of two free women and of two handmaids. Matthew, honey: Mark, milk: Luke, oil: John, wine.

863BLiber generationis Jesu Christi filii David, filii Abrahae. Ideo duos patres nominat, quia de David dicitur: De fructu ventris tui ponam super sedem meam. De Abraham dicitur: In semine tuo benedicentur omnes gentes. Ideo David ante Abraham scribitur, quia propinquior est Christo de illo stirpe nascendo: Jacob genuit Joseph, secundum creationem dixit: Lucas secundum legem dixit ut filius Heli esset; ideo mulieres in genealogia Christi sunt quia ipse ad vitam aeternam veniunt ut viri: et ideo peccatores in genealogia Christi nominantur; quia ipse dixit: Non veni vocare justos, sed peccatores. Lucas de Christo ab Adam dicens, ostendit quod per Christi genus Adae solvitur a peccato sive quod de Christo exeunt qui revertuntur ad Patrem, Figura 863Cquadraginta duorum virorum in genealogia Christi, hoc est quadraginta duae mansiones filiis Israel ab Aegypto: usque ad terram repromissionis, id est, de mundo ad coelum, quod significat per terna genera venire ad Christum per fidem, spem et charitatem. Quid per quatuordecim nisi per decem verba legis et per quatuor virtutes; prima prudentia, servire Deo non idolis: secunda temperantia, non concupiscere: tertia justitia, non adulterare: quarta fortitudo, dimittere non occidere. Per quatuor fuit perditio mundi per virum, per mulierem, per lignum, per serpentem, et per quatuor restauratur, per Christum, per Mariam, per crucem, per virum Joseph, serpens qui fuit in Israel Christum significavit ipsa de aere facta cum quatuor 863Drotis rotundis qui eam videbant sanabantur, et elevavit illum in caput virgae ut toti viderent eam et fugissent animalia deserti, prae timore rotarum, et sic unusquisque sanatus est, si inspexerit Christum in caput virgae, id est in crucem; quatuor rotae, quatuor evangelistae significant: sic fugit diabolus Evangelium quando sonat in coelum.

Christi autem generatio sic erat. Id est secundum carnem, dixerunt: Ideo Christi dicitur, ut ab ipso derivativo vocabulo Christiani vocentur: per ethimologiam iterum Christus, id est unctus, quia per ipsum post lavacrum crisme unctionem percepimus. Sic erat per aeternitatem, sonat Maria stella maris; quia stella dulcis est, mare (deletum est verbum 864Bunum forte vero,) amarum est; sic Maria, in mare mundi fuit inter peccatores, velut stellae maris, quia mos est: stella viros ad portum adducit: si sequantur illam, sic Maria in mundo ubi natus est Christus, qui omnes ad vitam ducit si sequantur illum, desponsata mater ejus, id est, pro quatuor causis ut non lapidaretur ut adultera, et ut in fugam haberet solatium, et genealogia Christi per Joseph, ut partus caelaretur diabolo, antequam convenirent, id est, antequam fuissent in qua habitatione, inventa est ab Joseph in utero: non in vulva ut mulier, sed ut virgo habens de Spiritu sancto, id est factus ex Spiritu Spirius ( sic ), sicut ipse facit pomum super lignum, et Evam de ossa Adae

Joseph autem vir ejus. Ideo vir ejus dicitur, et 864Cideo justus, ne alii humanitatis conjunctionem suspicaverit: « Noluit eam traducere, » id est in judicio; ne legem transisset: quia sciebat virginem, ut diabolum partus ejus lateret. « Haec autem eo cogitante, » id est, dimittere an tenere cogitabat, hic prophetia impletur. Adjutor in opportunitatibus in tribulatione. Angelus Domini in somnis dixit: Non in somno, quia saepius illi angelus in somnis venit. Non semel, quia mysterium Dei non potest cognosere semel: Joseph, fili David, id est, prophetia impleta in Joseph per David, de fructu ventris tui et reliqua. Noli timere, id est, dilige et gaude conjugem non nuptialem, neque creationem, sed cohabitationem: tuam dixit: ut credidisset non ab alio, sed de Spiritu sancto conceptus, Pariet Filium, id est, ut 864Dmajor gaudium esset illi de filio, sicut inter homines mos est, et vocabis nomen ejus. Item ad laetitiam ejus esset Jesum ac dixit et salvatorem, salvum facit populum suum, non dixit omnes nisi qui credunt in eum a peccatis eorum: Id est, quod non potuit alius medicus facere, hoc autem totum factum est: id est, quod angelus dixit: ut adimpleretur quod dictum est a Domino, id est, per prophetam Isaiam de Christo, hic ad litteram tantum; ecce virgo in utero habebit, et vocavit nomen ejus Emmanuel, ideo hoc nomen interpretatum est, quia in utraque lingua excelsum dicitur nobiscum Deus, id est Deus et homo, in una fide sit, fecit sicut praecepit ei angelus, 865Aostendit quod ipse visionem Domini meruit videre, qui fecit sicut ei praecepit.

Et non cognoscebat eam donec peperit filium suum: donec pronum quam [ Forte, priusquam] dicitur primogenitus in lege dicitur, quia prius aperuit vulvam. Non quem sequuntur filii, sed qui prius nascitur. Christus pro tribus dicitur primogenitus, in resurrectione a mortuis, et a Maria, et a creaturis. Cum ergo natus esset Jesus, tres confirmat causas, locum, tempus, personam: In Bethleem aptum fuit qui domus panis interpretatur, ut ubi panis vivus coelestis nasceretur. Juda est respice Bethleem gentium: In diebus Herodis regis, id est, refert reges et duces Judeae, ut dictum est, non deficiat princeps de Juda; nec dux de femoribus ejus donec veniat 865Bcui repromissum est, ipse erit expectatio gentium: Ideo dixit Herodem regem contra Judaeos, quia ipsi dixerunt ad crucem, Non habemus regem nisi Cae sarem. Ecce magi ab Oriente veniunt, dum Oriens est qui quaeritur; ideo ab Oriente indicatur, ipse ad semetipsum nos ducit, in Jerosolyma venerunt, quia de Sion exibit lex et verbum Domini de Jerusalem.

Dicentes, ubi est qui natus est rex Judaeorum? Id est, rex confessionum, addunt causam inquirentibus, quis dixit vobis? vidimus enim stellam ejus in Oriente, stella muta, sed lucida, lux ducit ad verbum: Item stellam ei, et non coeli et terrae, necnon eorum fuit, sed Filii Dei: Nec fuit postea nec antea, similis Christo in carne, ideo stella illos, ut coelestis 865Ccoelestes ad coelestem duxisset, et ideo non alia creatura sed muta, ut mutus muta, ad mutum duxisset, munerant, qui idolis adorabant, Herodes rex turbatus est, id est, per invidiam ne se suosque filios superasset: sive timuerunt omnes, nec non illis bene esse: Rex justus dum apud regem iniquum fuerit, ut non tam fiunt cari apud alium regem socii regis, vel ideo turbati sunt ut regi placuissent, item ad litteram, turbatio fit malis ostensio bona, et malus oculus solis ortu turbatur.

Et congregans omnes principes sacerdotum et scribas populi: id est, sperans ut eum advenissent, vel consilium recepisset ut Deus interroga patrem tuum et annuntiabit tibi, majores tuos et dicent tibi, patres principes seniorum et scribas ubi Christus nasceretur: 865DNon dixit ubi natus est ubi est, at illi dixerunt in Bethleem Judae, verbis dicunt, quod factis negant, caeci palpant ut Isaac Jacob: dum ire negligunt, quem adorando monstrant, tres magi tres filios Noe, cum tribus muneribus, Trinitate adorare significant: Tunc Herodes clam vocatis, hic defecit historia, dum secreti indicat tempus stellae, propter hoc distat opiniones, quando magi venerunt. Nam post annum unum aut duos, alii dicunt non in Bethleem, sed in Aegypto, inveniunt eum; et si in duodecim dies, quomodo de longa terra Persae vel Evile, Arabiae vel Cedar in duodecim dies venire potuerunt, sed potest id facere Deus cujus verbo saxa volant: montes movebunt, cui natura est fieri quod 866Ajubet, diligenter tamen didicit ab eis tempus stellae, id est, ut cissent in quo tempore forte putavit quod ab ipso natus esset filius, vel de suo genere.

Qui cum audissent regem abierunt, quando ad Christum quaerendum mittit, audiendus est, et quando ad se reverti jubet contemnendus est, cum enim salvatorem in altum duceret, renuit, ipse non renuit, dum vero deorsum dimitti et se adorare jubet, recusavit legaliter, Dominum Deum tuum adorabis et reliqua. Sic illi exeuntes exire sciunt, redire nesciunt, et ecce stella, quam viderant, et reliqua, dubium fit ubi se abscondit, quando isti abierunt, aliquid humani consilii: quia Dominus abscondit a cordibus nostris ut etiam inquiratur stella, fides an lex? compunctio quae nos ducit ad 866BChristum, antecedebat eos, quando venerunt ad Christum stella eos perduxit: nam ad Herodem, stella eos non duxit, Herodes diabolum significabatur, quia qui exiet de diaboli suggestione ad Christum cum fidei stellae vadit, videntes stellam gavisi sunt, et reliqua, quia mos est omnibus, perditio inventa gaudere ejus: Ideo ostendit et excelsiorem puerum, quam suam matrem.

Et intrantes domum, et reliqua, utrum domum an diversorium? Et procidentes adoraverunt eum, ante stellam adorabant, cessavit creatura, dum creator adoratur, et apertis thesauris suis et reliqua, aurum regi, tus Deo, mirra sepulcrum, utrum unus aurum, alius tus, tertius mirra obtulit: Non scitur, an unusquisque tria obtulit dona apte non dicitur, sed 866Cutrumque in figura. Non discordant, quia tres fideles unum sunt et in unum esset tres fructus, tricesimus, quadragesimus, centesimus, tricesimus in fide Trinitatis, quadragesimus in perfectione actualis vitae, centesimus aeternae vitae contemplatione, unusquisque tria offerebant, per aurum conscientiam puram, per tus orationem rectam, per mirram mortificationem voluntatum: Item tria munera, cogitatio sancta, verbum bonum, et opus perfectum, stella sic apparuit tantum in ejus nativitate sicut columba in baptismo. Nam stella quando antecedebat eos, non longe a terra.

Et responso accepto in somnis, et reliqua. Ideo in somnis oculo clauso, ut Deum invisibilem et incomprehensibilem adorarent, et vigiles non aperiantur 866Doculi ad creatura, sed ad creatori: Ne redirent ad Herodem, id est, post poenitentiam ad diabolum: per aliam viam reversi sunt in suam regionem. Regio paradisus est. Ille per aliam viam regreditur, qui totam vitam suam emendat, et de pravitate venit ad sanctitatem: qui cum recessissent.

Ecce Angelus Domini, et reliqua. Gabriel fortitudo Dei dicitur, dignum erat, ut infirmitatem humanitatis, fortitudo Dei confortaret, et aliter: quia tunc angelus nobis appropinquat, cum nos recesserimus ab Herode diabolo. Fugit in Aegyptum, fugae victoriam ostendit, sicut Jesu Navae quando dixit: Dividite vos et ite in fugam donec cives foras exeant, postea convertite, et vincetis eos: Christus primus 867Afugit donec retro diabolum vincit, sic Joseph in Aegypto domina sua fugit in licita, et post modum ipse super Aegyptum rex fuit: usque dum dicam tibi, hic ostendit quod non semper fugiendum est diabolum, ut Paulus apostolus ait: Resistite diabolo et fugiet a vobis; Noctem hic, ostendit quod secessit litterae tenebrae legis. Apud Judaeos post Christum usque hodie, usque ad mortis Herodem expectat, id est, tres annos, gentiles significat trinitatem credendum, et non pervenit usque ad mortem, Herodes indicat Judaeos, qui ad Christum veniunt post mortem litterae legis, ex Aegypto vocavi filium meum, primis Moysen et Aaron provocant Christum.

Tunc Herodes videns, quod inclusus sit a magis, hic prophetia impletur, draco iste quem formasti ad inludendum 867Beis, et mittit et occidit omnes pueros, ut regem natum inveniret, hic plus inluditur, quos extinctos putavit vivos in paradiso misit. A bimatu et infra, id est, ab anno praeterito et sequenti, quia infantiae nomen amorum duorum. Vox in rama et reliqua: Rachel interpretatur ovis videns Deum: Rama excelsus, Rachel, Ecclesia qui plorat in vallae lacrymarum, quos perdidit qui ante peccaverunt de quibus non vult consolari, quia non sunt Christiani, Rachel mater fuit Benjamin, sive quia ibi mortua est et sepulta, ubi parvuli occisi sunt, defuncti sunt enim et reliqua. Ideo synedoche, parte pro toto.

Venit in civitatem Nazareth, id est flos munditiae, aptum fuit, ibi habitare qui dixit, ego sum flos campi et lilium convallium: Archelaus, leo investigans, 867CNazaraeus sanctus. Surge, accipe puerum et matrem ejus, primo surge a terra qui terrena relinquis, et puerum postea matrem jubet accipere, potius Deum amare, postea proximum: Archelaus, haeresis, qui se putant esse quod non sunt, et caeci sunt.

In diebus illis, non ad regem Archelaus dicendum, sed a quinto decimo anno Tiberii Caesaris; quia in ipso anno factum est verbum Domini super Joannem, ante Christum, vox ante verbum, lucerna ante solem, flos ante fructum, poenitentiam agite et non operamini, quia opera foras corpora dicitur, agite interiore, interiori homini de corde agatur, poenitentia vera est, amissa deflere et poenitendo non committere.

867DAppropinquabit enim regnum coelorum, id est, baptismum vel Evangelium, Christi martyrium, vel Ecclesia, vox clamantis in deserto, Joannis Christi trinum modum fit clamor, si longe est, longe a peccatoribus salus, secunda a surdum si cum aspis surde et reliqua, tertia indignatur, id est, indignatus est, furor tuus: In deserto, id est sine lege, sine rege, sine sacerdote, vel Prophetia, erant Judaei, et qui amore amico conjugem conjungere sponsam, sic Joannes Ecclesiam Christo conjunxit: Ideo amicus sponsi dicitur, Joannes habebat vestem de pilis camelorum, ipse in jejunio natus est, et habebat vestitum et cibum jejunii, aptum fuit dum, poenitentiam docuit ut prius in se impleret, ut omnis qui verbum praedicat, 868Aexemplo confirmet. Vestitum Joannis de camelolis, significat Ecclesiam gentium, vel divitias eorum quod Christus collegit et fit Ecclesia. Zona, id est mortificatio vitiorum, circa lumbos: Id est, quia ibi est fornicatio viri et mulierum in umbilico, escam ejus locustae miserrimae aves sunt, ostendunt Judaeos qui litteram legis uti locustae flos; mel Sylvestrae, significat dulces sermones Dei, vel Judaeos qui susceperunt dulcem praedicationem et venerunt ad Christum.

Tunc exiebant ad eum Jerosolyma et reliqua. Id est Jude terrae, pro habitatoribus dicitur, Jerosolyma visio pacis, indicat pacificis qui ad Christum veniunt per confessionem: Jordanis, id est, discensio, significat discendentes in humilitate ad Christum.

868BEt baptizabant in Jordanem, ostendit quia per discensionem humiles venerunt ad baptismum: confitentes peccata sua, ostendit quod oportet confiteri peccata antequam ad communionem veniat: Pharisaei, id est divisi, vel sarabate vocantur, Saducaei justificati apud semetipsos.

Et dixit eis progenies viperarum: idem venenum in lingua tantum est, nam patricida et matricidae viperam: Pharisaei, Saducaei simulant qui matrem suam synagogem, et Patres et Prophetas occiderunt. Jesus walking upon the sea, that is, treading down the vices of the world: the crowds going up into the ship, seeking Jesus, shows that all seek Christ with their own labour: it is called "manu" among the Hebrews, whence it took the name manna among the Latins, that is, the living bread or the body of Christ transfigured in mystery; in three ways is a man raised up, that is, from unbelief unto faith, from sin unto penitence, on the last day bodily, in the resurrection: I live because of the Father, they show it according to the divitatem: Scenophegia, that is, the fashioning of tabernacles: ye go up 907Cto the feast day, I go not up: ye in whom the world delighteth, I will not go up, because I am not of the world, the feast day being half over.

Jesus went up to Jerusalem, the zeal of the Jews burning most of all: my doctrine is not mine; and if it is mine, how is it not mine? it is mine which proceedeth to them out of my mouth: it is not mine, because what I have heard from the Father: these things I speak unto you; otherwise, according to the flesh it is mine, but according to the divinity it is not mine; I have done one work and ye all marvel, I have made a whole man whole on the sabbath, that is, the whole human race, in the New Testament: judge not according to the face, that is, accept not the person of a man: ye know not whence I am, that is, according to the flesh.

Yet a little while I am with you, he shows, unto 907Dthe passion: ye shall seek me and shall not find me, that is, bodily; and where I am ye cannot come, that is, through unbelief, save through faith: and in the last day of the feast, that is, the feast of the Jews being finished: he that thirsteth, let him come and drink, that is, he that thirsteth for the word of God, let him come and drink the doctrine of the Gospel: rivers out of the belly, that is, doctrine out of the heart: waters of life shall flow, that is, the preaching of the Gospel which giveth perpetual life: because Jesus was not yet honoured [Vulg. Jn 7:39 glorificatus, "glorified"], he shows the honouring of the passion and of the resurrection: they returned every man unto his own house, that is, unto his own malice.

The woman taken in adultery, shows the synagogue which adulterated the law of Moses, and followed the traditions 908Aof the fathers: or the Church out of the gentiles, which, forsaking God, committed fornication with devils: because they bring her into the midst, he shows the Jews, because they are accusers of the Church of Christ: Moses commanded to stone her, what sayest thou? tempting him for two causes; if he had said to stone her, they would have said, he doth not what he teacheth, send her back to us; if he had said she was not to be stoned, they would have said, he is a gainsayer of the law: Jesus stooping down, that is, from the seats of the Father into the world, to redeem the sins of the human race; he wrote with his finger on the ground, he shows their sins; he raised te himself up, that he might raise up those who walked in sins: again stooping down, he wrote on the ground, he shows the sins of the woman forgiven: Jesus remained alone with the woman in the house, that is, with the Church in the world: he that 908Bfolloweth me walketh not in darkness, he shows not only the steps of the feet but the affection of the heart: not in the darkness, that is, of ignorance, neither in the darkness of sins: but he shall have the light of life, that is, the light of knowledge and the perpetual light; I have many things to say of you and to judge, he shows that, if ye shall have believed, I will teach you many things: and if ye shall not have believed, to speak many things for your excuse.

He was a murderer from the beginning: because he slew his own self by being proud: otherwise, he made mortal the first man, whom God founded immortal, and by his suggestion Abel was slain: which of you shall convict me of sin? he that is of God heareth the words of God, that is, he that understandeth and doeth them: thou art a Samaritan and hast a devil; here what he was not, neither to have, 908Cand what he was, by keeping silence he consented: I seek not mine own glory, there is one that seeketh and judgeth: God the Father; I seek not as dissemblers do, who judge unjustly: Abraham your father rejoiced that he might see my day: he saw it and was glad, that is, he saw the faith in the spirit, and he saw him in the person of the angels: three he saw, as one he saluted, where he said, turn aside, my lords, unto the house of your servant, if I have found grace in thine eyes: amen, amen, I say unto you; the doubled amen is understood as truly or faithfully: announcing the New and the Old Testament: before Abraham I am: the passage is of the divinity: they took up stones to cast at him, that is, having stony hearts: Jesus hid, he shows himself hidden from their hearts, himself he went out of the temple, that is, out of the people of the Jews; Jesus passed 908Dthrough the midst of them, that is, he went away unto the gentiles.

He saw a man blind from his birth: by the blind man the human race is signified: Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents, that thou mayest understand this which he said, neither hath he sinned: Adam, first created for seeing, had his eyes closed to evil, when he transgressed the commandment of God: he held his eyes open to evil and closed them to good? what then are the parents, unless the human race, and the instigators of vices, or the vices themselves, are signified? I must work, that is, preach: while it is day, that is, while I am in the world: the night cometh, that is, the persecution of the apostles or the persecution of Antichrist: the night when no man can work, that is, after 909Athe departure of each one: he spat on the ground, that is, the incarnation of Christ.

He made clay, he anointed mine eyes: he shows that as he formed the first man out of the slime of the earth, so through the same kind of clay he reformed the human race: Go and wash in the pool of Siloe, showing baptism: by the parents is understood the devil or the vices: they who said, he is of age, let him speak for himself: who would not confess the man to be Christ: this man we know not whence he is, he shows that they knew not his divinity: for judgment I came into this world, he shows that he who heareth the word and hath received judgment hath received it unto condemnation: that they which see not may see, he shows the gentiles, which received not the law: and that they which see may be made blind, that is, the Jews, who saw through the law, were made blind, 909Bbecause they believed not in Christ.

They who enter not by the door: [Vulg. Jn 10:1 Qui non intrat per ostium in ovile ovium, "he that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold"] he shows the bishops who enter by rewards, not by the calling of God or of the people; they are shepherds of the sheep who are for the gain of souls, not for the ambition of the world: to him the doorkeeper openeth: the first doorkeeper is John, the forerunner of Christ; of the Scriptures, by divine Scripture called, named, that is, the Lord knoweth them that are his: he leadeth them forth into doctrine or into the kingdom; he goeth before them, that is, what he taught by example he first did: he goeth before them into the kingdoms of heaven, or also all good teachers go before by examples.

They hear my voice, that is, the doctrine: a stranger they follow [Vulg. Jn 10:5 alienum autem non sequuntur, "but a stranger they follow not"], that is, the faith, the doctrine of heretics: as many as came were thieves and robbers, that is, the false prophets 909Cwho have been and shall be in the time of Antichrist: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved: that is, he who shall be saved through the doctrine of Christ: let him enter through penitence into the Church, and they shall go out from the present world unto the kingdom of God: otherwise, go out against the heretics, to be fought down: the thief cometh not but to kill and to destroy [Vulg. Jn 10:10 nisi ut furetur, et mactet, et perdat, "but that he may steal, and kill, and destroy"], these are the orders of priests who enter for the sake of filthy lucre, not for the gain of souls; I am come that ye may have life, that is, my doctrine, and that ye may have it more abundantly, that is, life eternal: he is a hireling who teacheth for earthly gain: he seeth the wolf, he fleeth, that is, the devil or the heretic: other sheep I have which are not of this fold, he shows the gentiles; let there be one flock and one shepherd [Vulg. Jn 10:16 fiet unum ovile et unus pastor, "there shall be one fold and one shepherd"], that is, the Church and Christ: here the corner stone, the middle 909Dwall, which maketh both one.

I have power to lay down my soul and to take it again: he shows it for the part of the flesh; the Father, what he hath given me is greater than all, he shows that Christ, or the Spirit of God, is greater than all the works of the world: I said, ye are gods; it is said in three ways, that is, essentially, he who always is, that is, God: by appellation, ye are gods: falsely are called gods the devils or the idols.

He went away beyond Jordan, that is, unto the gentiles, where John was first baptizing, he abode there, he shows the Church: Lazarus figures the Jews, because the Jews were weak in the faith of Christ: Martha and Mary show the doctrine of the good among the Jews: this sickness is not unto death, he shows that they who are dead to men shall live 910Ato God: he abode there two days, that is, the two testaments. Let us go into Judea again, the faith returned unto the Jews through Elias and Enoch at the end of the world: there are twelve hours of the day, the day is Christ: the twelve hours, the twelve apostles; if any man walk in the day, he stumbleth not; in the day, that is, in the doctrine of Christ or of the apostles, he stumbleth not in sin, because he seeth the light of this world, that is, he seeth Christ through knowledge.

If any man walk in the night, he stumbleth: by the night is understood the darkness of sins, or the ignorance of the heart: he was not there, he shows the presence of the flesh; he knew that he was dead, that is, because he knoweth all things: one in the house of the ruler of the synagogue, two, the son of the widow in the gate of the city, the third, Lazarus out of the tomb: in the house, that is, in the heart: in the gate, that is, in the mouth; in the tomb, 910Bthat is, in the deed; by the same a man dieth, and by the same he shall be raised again.

Martha, when she heard that Jesus was come, went to meet him: Here Martha signifies the Church, which went to meet Christ through faith: Mary sat at home, she shows the life of contemplation; he that believeth in me, and shall have died, liveth, whether bodily, or in sins through penitence: every one that liveth and believeth in me, who here liveth in Christ in good works, shall not die for ever.

Jesus wept: he shows that, as true God and true man, he is an example of compassion; with hands bound, and feet, he shows the bonds of sins: his face bound with a napkin, that is, the veil upon the hearts of the Jews: loose him, that is, through the preaching 910Cof faith. The greater the signs the Jews saw, the more were they blinded: they took away the place and the nation; the place, Jerusalem, the Romans possess, and the gentiles who are scattered through the regions; the prince spoke the causes, it is expedient that one man die rather than the whole nation perish, because he had the ministry of God in hand and the Spirit of God; he spoke this, that the Son of God should gather into one, in the whole world, those who had been divided [Vulg. Jn 11:52 ut filios Dei, qui erant dispersi, congregaret in unum, "that he might gather into one the children of God that were dispersed"]: Martha served, she shows the active life.

Lazarus sitting at meat with Jesus, signifies the Church: Mary took a pound of ointment, so also the whole world, which is understood as the hearts of the faithful: by the ointment the odour of confession; Mary signifies the Church; she anointed the feet of Jesus, that is, through the confession of penitence: the house was filled with the odour of the ointment, 910Dso also the whole world is filled with the passion and the resurrection of Christ.

When the crowd heard that Jesus was come, they took branches of palms, and went forth to meet Jesus. He shows that three, according to the coming after the resurrection, are caught up into the air with the Lord; he found an ass, by the ass he shows the people of the gentiles: he sat upon him, that is, through the confession of faith: fear not thy destruction, for thy Saviour cometh, daughter of Sion [Vulg. Jn 12:15 Noli timere, filia Sion: ecce rex tuus venit, "Fear not, daughter of Sion: behold, thy king cometh"], he shows the gathering of the faithful out of the Jews and out of the gentiles: the whole world went after him, because through his passion and his resurrection he redeemed the whole world from sin, and it followed him: they came to Philip and asked that they might see Jesus, he shows 911Athe gentiles converted to the faith through the preaching of the apostles: the grain of wheat holdeth the figure of Christ; if it shall have died, it bringeth forth many grains: so also Christ . . . multiplied his Church.

I will glorify, and again I will glorify, [Vulg. Jn 12:28 Et clarificavi, et iterum clarificabo, "I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again"] that is, glorify him in the resurrection of Lazarus, again glorify him in his own resurrection: the prince of the world is sent forth, that is, the devil being cast out from the power of the world through the passion of the cross: walk while ye have the light, that is, while ye have Christ bodily and spiritually with you: lest the darkness overtake you, that is, the darkness of ignorance and the persecutions of sins: he hid himself from them, that is, bodily or spiritually; Lord, who hath believed our report? "who" is put for rarity, or for difficulty; the hearing is faith, 911Bwhich we have heard through the Holy Spirit: the arm of the Lord is the Son of God, which was revealed, that is, some part out of the Jews, revealed out of the gentiles, that is, he who believeth in me not only according to the humanity, but according to the divinity; otherwise, not only in me, but also in the Father, because I am in the Father and the Father in me: he that seeth me seeth him that sent me; as if he had said openly, he that hath seen me to be true man and believeth me to be true God, seeth also the Father according to the divinity.

I came not to judge, but to save the world: he shows that through the shedding of his blood he should redeem the world: I have loved you, because I was in the world, unto the end he loved: he gave an example that all should remain in the truth of God unto the end: supper being ended, 911Cit shows the passion of Christ; as after supper no dinner is made, so Christ, after he once suffered, showed not that he should suffer again: rising from supper, he shows our passion, the resurrection or the ascension: he layeth aside his garments, he shows that his garments were to be laid aside for the cross.

INCIPIT CAPITULATIO.

Accipiens linteum et praecinxit se linteo, id est mortificatio ipsius: mittit aquam in pelvim, ostendit gratiam baptismi: coepit lavare pedes discipulorum suorum et extergere linteo, per lavationem pedum baptismum apostolorum intelligitur, per extersionem lintei ostendit per passionem Christi extersionem peccatorum: non lavabis pedes mihi in aeternum, 911Dpavore perterritus dicebat hoc: si non lavero te, non habebis partem mecum, ostendit qui non est lavatus per baptismum et per confessionem poenitentiae non habebit partem apud Dominum: accepit vestimenta sua, iterum ostenditur quod revertetur ad Judaeos in finem mundi: non est servo major domino suo neque Apostolus eo major qui misit illum, id est si ego patior et vos patimini. Aliter si ego me humilio usque ad vestigia vestra, ita et vos humiliate vos omnibus: levabit contra me calcaneum, id est calcaneum extrema pars membri est.

Et per bucellam introivit in eum Satanas, id est, sicut Apostolus ait, qui indigne accipit corpus Domini et sanguinem ejus, judicium manducat et bibit: 912Apraeceptum novum do vobis ut et diligatis invicem: in vetere dicitur diliges amicum et odio habebis inimicum, Dominus dicit diligite inimicos vestros: hoc est praeceptum novum, non conturbetur cor vestrum, hic praedicat Apostolis de passione sua, credite in Deum, et in me credite quia Filius Dei sum.

Credite in domo patris mei, id est in Ecclesia mansiones multae sunt id est sicut in uno homine sensus divisus: est ita in Ecclesia quae est corpus Christi gratiae multae sunt: vado parare vobis loc, id est parabo vobis vitam, sicut Apostolus advocatum habemus apud Patrem, qui interpellat pro nobis: ego sum via et veritas et vita, via sine errore Christus est, veritas sine falsitate, vita sine morte ipse est.

912BAmodo nostis eum et vidistis eum, id est, quem cognoverunt oculis corporis, et cognoverunt oculis cordis, secundum divinitatem per inlustrationem Spiritus sancti: majora horum facit, quia Christus per fimbriam mulierem sanavit: Apostoli vero per umbram infirmos curabunt.

Explicit Passio.

Rogabo Patrem meum et alium Paraclitum dabit vobis: per bis Spiritum acceptum dilectio Dei et proximi intelligitur: spiritum veritatis quem mundus accipere non potest, id est, qui mundum diligit non accipit illum, non relinquam vos orfanos, id est non relinquam vos secundum divinitatem: in illa die cognoscetis, id est in futuro, sive in resurrectione 912CChristi. Ego in Patre, id est secundum divinitatem, vos in me, quia membra mea estis: et ego in vobis, id est, in dilectione cordis vestri: manifestabo ei meipsum, id est, credentes in divinitatem, mansionem apud eum faciemus sicut ipse ait, ubi duo vel tres congregati fuerint in nomine meo, ubi sum et ego et reliqua: pacem meam do vobis, id est, sicut ego patienter suffero omnia, et vos patientiam habetis: pacem meam relinquo vobis, id est, sicut ego passus sum, ita et vos patiemini: non sicut mundus dat pacem ego do vobis, mundus interruptam pacem dat, Deus aeternam dat: venit princeps mundi ad me, et nihil in me invenit, id est, diabolus venit ad crucem, et in Domino non invenit peccatum, quaesivit capere captus est ab eo: 912Dsicut praecepit mihi pater sic facio, misit illum pater ut sanguinem suum funderet pro genere humano, et ita fecit: ego sum vitis vera unde perfluit sanguis, et aqua: et pater meus agricola est, id est, cultor Ecclesiae: vos palmites, id est, apostoli sive fideles Ecclesiae: sicut palmes non potest a se fructum ferre nisi manserit in vite, sic et unusquisque non fert fructum nisi manserit in Christo.

In hoc clarificatus est Pater meus, ut fructum primum afferatis, id est sicut praedicationem, ego et passionem fructificavi, ita et vos facite: sicut dilexit me pater, et ego dilexi vos, id est, sicut misit me pater praedicare et pati, et ego mitto vos: majorem caritatem nemo habet et reliqua: dilectio 913Afortior est morte, ego pro dilectionem patior pro vobis, et vos patimini pro Ecclesia: omnia quae audivi a patre meo nota feci vobis, id est praecepta Evangelii: elegi vos de mundo, id est de mundanis actibus: non estis de mundo, id est, quia non diligitis mundum: ut ait, Salomon, talis est mulier adultera, quae cum comederit tetigit os suum, dicit, non sum operata malum, figurat Ecclesiam ex gentibus, quae per confessionem abstersit se de immunditia peccatorum: ab initio mecum istis, id est ab initio praedicationis.

Si ego non abiero paraclitus non veniet ad vos: cum venerit paraclitus arguet mundum de peccato, id est, quia non credit in me, credat per praedicationem Evangelii: de judicio autem, quia princeps 913Bmundi judicatus est: Arguit de peccato, id est, ut per praedicationem vestram convertantur ad poenitentiam: de justitia, ut sicut diligit proximum, diligat et Deum ex toto corde: de judicio, ut quidquid vultis ut faciant vobis homines, et vos facite illis: haec enim est lex et prophetae.

Modicum non videbitis me, id est, dum jacui in sepulcro: modicum iterum et videbitis me, id est, per resurrectionem: venit hora quando non in proverbiis loquar vobiscum, sed palam de Patre manifestabo vobis, id est, per resurrectionem: in illa die in nomine meo petetis, id est, quia ascendo ad Patrem resurgendo per potestatem. Pater diligit vos, quia diligitis me relinquo mundum, et vado ad Patrem, id est, per praesentiam corporis, nam secundum 913Cdivinitatem non relinquit mundum: pressuram habetis, id est, a mundanis hominibus tribulationem: ego vici mundum, id est alligavi fortem in mundo, qui est diabolus.

Venit hora, Pater, ad clarificare Filium tuum: id est per resurrectionem: ut Filius clarificet te, id est per Filium convertantur et credant: ego te clarificavi super terram, id est notum feci omnibus, apud te manifestavi nomen tuum hominibus, ostendit quod secundum divinitatem, cum Patre erat, et corporaliter in terra: quos mihi dedisti de mundo tui erant, in praescientia ab initio tui erant: mihi eos dedisti, id est, ad docendum: et sermonem tuum servaverunt, id est, in fide permanserunt, ego pro eis rogo non pro mundo, quia scit Deus qui sunt 913Dejus: omnia tua mea sunt, et mea tua sunt: et clarificatus sum in eis, id est, in iisdem gentibus manifestatus sum, id est, quia crediderunt in me: in mundo sum corporaliter, et in mundo non sum corporaliter: et pro eis sanctifico meipsum, id est, per martyrium et resurrectionem: ego claritatem, quam dedisti mihi dedi eis, id est, praecepta divina: exivit cum discipulis suis trans torrentem Cedron, ostendit impetum persecutionis.

Iterum transiens Jesus cum discipulis suis, id est, de mundo ad patrem, et de Judaeis ad gentes, venerunt cum laternis et fagulis, id est, ipsi sibi accenderunt lumen poenale, adduxerunt eum ad Annam, Anna gratia Dei interpretatur, qui gratia Dei acceperat 914Aprincipatum sacerdotis: sed illa visa est ostiaria, quae introduxit Petrum in atrium significat primitivae Ecclesiae, exprobravit eum quia Dei Filium se esse dixisse: certe non rapinam arbitratus est se esse aequalem Deo, sed semetipsum exinanivit, formam servi accepit.

Jesus bajulans crucem suam: Exemplum dedit sicut ipse dixit: qui vult post me venire abneget semetipsum, et reliqua: duo latrones crucifixi erant cum Jesu, qui permansit unus in perfidia sua, ostendit Judaeos, qui confessus est significat gentes: scriptum erat in titulo rex Judaeorum, id est rex confessorum: fecerunt quatuor partes de vestimentis Jesu: quatuor milites, id est quatuor Evangelistae: vestimenta divisa, id est praedicationem, 914Bincarnationem, passionem et resurrectionem, quae praedicaverunt in quatuor partibus mundi, tunica autem inconsutilis desuper contexta, ostendit quia divinitas in Patre et Filio inseparabilis est: non scindamus eam, sed sortiamus de illa cujus erat, ostendit quia viri Catholici non scindunt unitatem divinitatis.

Mulier, ecce filius tuus: discipulo, ecce mater tua: hic virginem commendavit, ut incorruptam servaret: non fregerunt crura Jesu, id est, quod figuratum ante fuerat, per agnum in Aegypto: non crudum nec coctum aqua, sed assum tantum igni, ostendit passionen Christi, baculum in manu, ostendit crucem Christi, de mundo ad Patrem: non comminuetis ex eo, ostendit ut non fieret infracta crux 914CChristi, lancea latus ejus punxerunt, ut impleretur scriptura: viderunt in quem pupunxerunt.

Exivit sanguis et aqua: sicut ex latere Adae sumpta est Eva, ita ex latere Christi exivit redemptio Ecclesiae: per sanguinem remissio, per aquam baptismum: duo discipuli currentes? Petrus et Joannes, ostendit duos populos currentes, Judaeos per legis litteram, gentes per legem naturae.

Joannes praecucurrit citius; vidit linteamina posita et non introivit. Judaei per legem et Prophetas viderunt Christum venturum, et adventum ipsius non receperunt. Venit Petrus sequens eum, vidit linteamina et introivit, ostendit gentes quae in adventum Christi, sive doctrinam illius: per linteamina incarnatio Christi intelligitur: per sudarium mysterium 914Ddivinitatis intelligitur: sudarium non cum linteaminibus, sed separatim in unum locum involutum, ostendit quia divinitas longe est remota a sensibus humanis, in unum locum qui sunt universalis: tunc introivit et alius discipulus qui prius venerat ad monumentum, ostendit Judaeos reversos ad fidem in fine mundi: angeli sedentes ad caput et ad pedes, ostendit initium et finem.

Dixit Maria, nescio ubi posuerunt eum, ostendit secundum carnem diligebat mundum, et per fidem credebat quod dixit Maria ostendit cogitationem vocis: quia per dilectionem proximi cognoscit homo Deum: dixit Jesus, noli me tangere, id est in corde tuo, tibi non ascendi per divinitatem, discipuli piscantes 915Atotam noctem nihil ceperunt: piscatores doctores veteres, in nocte ostendit ante adventum Christi nullus ad perfectionem per praedicationem legis veteris venisset.

Mane autem facto: ostendit factum adventum Christi: mittite in dexteram partem, id est, mittite praedicationem in gentibus. Erat enim Petrus nudus, id est nudi erant a fide Christi: tunica succinxit se, id est fidem Ecclesiae: misit se in mare, id est in fluctuationibus, et amaritudines mundi per dilectionem Christi: alii discipuli navigio venerunt, id est, in Ecclesia fluctuationem mundi calcantes viderunt prunas, et piscem et panem positum, per piscem ostendit fidem, per prunas calorem caritatis, per panem verbum divinum: trahentes rete in 915Bterram, per littora ostendit finem mundi: plenum magnis piscibus, id est, plenitudo et magnitudo Ecclesiae, per remissionem peccatorum: per tres ostendit trinitatem: indivisum rete et non scissum, id est, Ecclesiam in futuro incorruptam: manducavit Jesus post resurrectionem, ostendit veram incarnationem corporis ut credentes crederent.

Dixit Jesus, Simon Joannis amas me? Ostendit sicut ter negavit, ut ter confiteretur fidem Trinitatis: pasce oves meas id est in Ecclesia: ingressus Jesus januis clausis ostendit quia clausis ingressus est januis uterum, sic et omnes sancti post resurrectionem 916Ainduunt spiritale corpus, ubique sine metu ingredi possunt, unde integer et incorruptus matrimonio, tres testificant hanc sententiam.

Discipulus quem amabat Jesus. Secunda virtus est quod fecit Jesus in Cana Galilaeae, vinum de aqua expressit opus paratum virginitatem Joannis, ostendit quia illi sponsa conjuncta est: testimonium quod commendavit Jesus matrem suam Joanni: Maria Ecclesia figuratur. Johannes principatum, sicut fuit Johannes virgo matrimonio, sic fuit incorruptus et morte: Joannes genealogiam de divinitate narrat, nam alii de homine narrant: de divinitate Johannes dicit, in principio erat verbum, iste jacebat super pectus Jesu, et scientia divinitatis quae fuerat in pectore Jesu ascendit Johannes de conjunctione 916Bduorum pectorum; insita arbor, in alter facit fructum arboris, alterius in qua plantatur. Si secundum ordinem canonis iste liber Mathaeum praemittendus esset, et quod in novissimam positus partem, est quia terra primis sanctificata est, deinde aqua, et postea novissime ignis quia clarior est et subtilior: item in similitudinem duarum legum, id est, vetus et novum; vetus prius tradita erat, nova tamen clarior est et major, quia de coelestibus discernit et abundantior mea, quod ad Evangelium Joannis pertinet ex supra dicta.

HERE BEGINS THE CAPITULATIO.

Taking a towel, he girded himself with the towel, that is, his own mortification: he putteth water into a basin, he shows the grace of baptism: he began to wash the feet of his disciples and to wipe them with the towel; by the washing of the feet the baptism of the apostles is understood, by the wiping with the towel he shows the wiping away of sins through the passion of Christ: thou shalt not wash my feet for ever, 911Dstricken with dread he said this: if I wash thee not, thou shalt have no part with me, he shows that he who is not washed through baptism and through the confession of penitence shall have no part with the Lord: he took his garments again; again it is shown that he shall return to the Jews at the end of the world: the servant is not greater than his lord, neither the Apostle greater than he that sent him, that is, if I suffer, ye also suffer. Otherwise, if I humble myself even to your footsteps, so do ye also humble yourselves to all men: he shall lift up his heel against me, that is, the heel is the outermost part of the limb.

And by the morsel Satan entered into him, [Vulg. Jn 13:27 post bucellam, "after the morsel"] that is, as the Apostle saith, he that receiveth unworthily the body of the Lord and his blood, eateth and drinketh judgment: 912Aa new commandment I give unto you, that ye love one another: in the old it is said, thou shalt love thy friend [Vulg. Matt 5:43 proximum tuum, "thy neighbour"] and hate thine enemy; the Lord saith, love your enemies: this is the new commandment; let not your heart be troubled, here he preacheth to the Apostles concerning his passion; believe in God, and believe in me, because I am the Son of God.

Believe, in the house of my Father, that is, in the Church there are many mansions, that is, as in one man the sense is divided, so in the Church, which is the body of Christ, the graces are many: I go to prepare for you a loc, that is, I will prepare life for you, as the Apostle saith, we have an advocate with the Father, who maketh intercession for us: I am the way and the truth and the life; the way without error is Christ, the truth without falsehood, the life without death is he himself.

912BHenceforth ye know him and have seen him, [Vulg. Jn 14:7 amodo cognoscetis eum, "from henceforth ye shall know him"] that is, whom they knew with the eyes of the body, and knew with the eyes of the heart, according to the divinity through the illumination of the Holy Spirit: he doeth greater things than these, because Christ healed the woman by the hem of his garment: but the Apostles shall cure the sick by their shadow.

Here endeth the Passion.

I will ask my Father, and he shall give you another Paraclete: by the Spirit received twice, the love of God and of the neighbour is understood: the Spirit of truth whom the world cannot receive, that is, he that loveth the world receiveth him not; I will not leave you orphans, that is, I will not leave you according to the divinity: in that day ye shall know, that is, in the time to come, or in the resurrection 912Cof Christ. I in the Father, that is, according to the divinity; ye in me, because ye are my members: and I in you, that is, in the love of your heart: I will manifest myself to him, that is, to them that believe in the divinity; we will make our abode with him, as he himself saith, where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I also, and the rest: my peace I give unto you, that is, as I patiently endure all things, so also ye have patience: my peace I leave with you, that is, as I have suffered, so also shall ye suffer: not as the world giveth peace do I give unto you; the world giveth a peace that is broken off, God giveth an eternal one: the prince of the world cometh to me, and findeth nothing in me, that is, the devil came to the cross, and found no sin in the Lord; he sought to take, and was taken by him: 912Das the Father hath commanded me, so I do; the Father sent him to pour out his blood for the human race, and so he did: I am the true vine, whence flow forth blood and water: and my Father is the husbandman, that is, the tiller of the Church: ye are the branches, that is, the apostles or the faithful of the Church: as the branch cannot bear fruit of itself unless it abide in the vine, so also each one beareth not fruit unless he abide in Christ.

In this is my Father glorified, that ye bring forth the first fruit, [Vulg. Jn 15:8 plurimum, "very much"] that is, as I have borne fruit by preaching and by the passion, so do ye also: as the Father hath loved me, I also have loved you, that is, as the Father sent me to preach and to suffer, so I send you: greater charity hath no man, and the rest: love 913Ais stronger than death; I suffer for you out of love, and ye suffer for the Church: all things which I have heard of my Father I have made known unto you, that is, the precepts of the Gospel: I have chosen you out of the world, that is, out of worldly deeds: ye are not of the world, that is, because ye love not the world: as Solomon saith, such is the adulterous woman, who, when she hath eaten, wipeth her mouth and saith, I have done no evil [Vulg. Prov 30:20 Talis est et via mulieris adulterae, quae comedit, et tergens os suum dicit: Non sum operata malum, "Such is also the way of an adulterous woman, who eateth, and wiping her mouth saith: I have done no evil"], it figures the Church out of the gentiles, which through confession wiped herself clean of the uncleanness of sins: from the beginning ye have been istis with me, that is, from the beginning of the preaching.

If I go not away, the Paraclete will not come unto you: when the Paraclete is come, he shall reprove the world of sin, that is, because it believeth not in me, let it believe through the preaching of the Gospel: and of judgment, because the prince 913Bof the world is judged: He reproveth of sin, that is, that through your preaching they may be converted to penitence: of justice, that as a man loveth his neighbour, he may love God also with his whole heart: of judgment, that whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye also to them: for this is the law and the prophets.

A little while, and ye shall not see me, that is, while I lay in the sepulchre: a little while again, and ye shall see me, that is, through the resurrection: the hour cometh when I shall not speak with you in proverbs, but shall manifest to you plainly of the Father, that is, through the resurrection: in that day ye shall ask in my name, that is, because I ascend to the Father by rising again through power. The Father loveth you, because ye love me; I leave the world and go to the Father, that is, through the presence of the body, for according to 913Cthe divinity he leaveth not the world: ye have pressure, that is, tribulation from worldly men: I have overcome the world, that is, I have bound the strong man in the world, who is the devil.

The hour is come, Father, to glorify thy Son: that is, through the resurrection: that the Son may glorify thee, that is, that through the Son they may be converted and believe: I have glorified thee upon the earth, that is, I have made thee known to all, with thee I have manifested thy name to men, he shows that according to the divinity he was with the Father, and bodily on the earth: whom thou gavest me out of the world, they were thine, in foreknowledge from the beginning they were thine: thou gavest them to me, that is, to be taught: and they have kept thy word, that is, they have remained in the faith; I pray for them, not for the world, because God knoweth who are 913Dhis: all thine are mine, and mine are thine: and I am glorified in them, that is, in those same gentiles I am made manifest, that is, because they believed in me: I am in the world bodily, and I am not in the world bodily: and for them I sanctify myself, that is, through martyrdom and resurrection: I have given them the glory which thou gavest me, that is, the divine precepts: he went forth with his disciples over the brook Cedron, he shows the onset of persecution.

Jesus again passing over with his disciples, that is, from the world to the Father, and from the Jews to the gentiles; they came with lanterns and fagulis, that is, they themselves kindled for themselves a light of punishment; they led him to Annas, Annas is interpreted the grace of God, who by the grace of God had received 914Athe chief place of the priesthood: but she was seen to be a doorkeeper, who brought Peter into the hall; it signifies the primitive Church; he reproached him because he had said that he was the Son of God: assuredly he thought it not robbery to be equal with God, but emptied himself, taking the form of a servant.

Jesus bearing his own cross: He gave an example, as he himself said: he that will come after me, let him deny himself, and the rest: two thieves were crucified with Jesus; he who remained in his perfidy shows the Jews, he who confessed signifies the gentiles: it was written in the title, the king of the Jews, that is, the king of confessors: they made four parts of the garments of Jesus: four soldiers, that is, the four Evangelists: the garments divided, that is, the preaching, 914Bthe incarnation, the passion and the resurrection, which they preached in the four parts of the world; but the coat without seam, woven from above, shows that the divinity in the Father and the Son is inseparable: let us not rend it, but let us cast lots for it whose it was, he shows that Catholic men rend not the unity of the divinity.

Woman, behold thy son: to the disciple, behold thy mother: here he commended the virgin, that he might keep her uncorrupt: they did not break the legs of Jesus, that is, what had been figured before by the lamb in Egypt: neither raw nor boiled with water, but roasted only with fire, shows the passion of Christ; the staff in the hand shows the cross of Christ, from the world to the Father: ye shall not break a bone of him, shows that the cross of Christ 914Cshould not be broken; with a lance they pierced his side, that the scripture might be fulfilled: they saw on whom they had pierced.

There went out blood and water: as out of the side of Adam Eve was taken, so out of the side of Christ went forth the redemption of the Church: through the blood remission, through the water baptism: two disciples running? Peter and John, he shows two peoples running, the Jews by the letter of the law, the gentiles by the law of nature.

John outran him more swiftly; he saw the linen cloths laid, and he went not in. The Jews through the law and the Prophets saw that Christ should come, and received not his coming. Peter came following him, he saw the linen cloths and went in, he shows the gentiles who believed in the coming of Christ, or in his doctrine: by the linen cloths the incarnation of Christ is understood: by the napkin the mystery 914Dof the divinity is understood: the napkin not with the linen cloths, but folded up separately into one place, shows that the divinity is far removed from human senses; into one place, who are universal: then went in also that other disciple who had come first to the sepulchre, he shows the Jews returning to the faith at the end of the world: the angels sitting at the head and at the feet show the beginning and the end.

Mary said, I know not where they have laid him, he shows that according to the flesh she loved the world, and through faith she believed what Mary said; it shows the thought of the voice: because through the love of the neighbour a man knoweth God: Jesus said, touch me not, that is, in thy heart, I have not ascended for thee through the divinity; the disciples fishing 915Athe whole night took nothing: the fishermen, the old teachers; by the night he shows that before the coming of Christ no man had come unto perfection through the preaching of the old law.

But when the morning was come: he shows the coming of Christ accomplished: cast on the right side, that is, cast the preaching among the gentiles. For Peter was naked, that is, they were naked of the faith of Christ: he girt himself with his coat, that is, the faith of the Church: he cast himself into the sea, that is, into the tossings and the bitternesses of the world through the love of Christ: the other disciples came in a ship, that is, treading down the tossing of the world in the Church; they saw coals, and a fish and bread laid thereon; by the fish he shows faith, by the coals the warmth of charity, by the bread the divine word: drawing the net to 915Bthe land, by the shores he shows the end of the world: full of great fishes, that is, the fulness and the greatness of the Church, through the remission of sins: by the three he shows the Trinity: the net undivided and not broken, that is, the Church uncorrupted in the time to come: Jesus ate after the resurrection, he shows the true incarnation of the body, that they which believed might believe.

Jesus said, Simon son of John, lovest thou me? He shows that as he thrice denied, so he should thrice confess the faith of the Trinity: feed my sheep, that is, in the Church: Jesus entering in when the doors were shut shows that he entered the womb when the doors were shut, so also all the saints after the resurrection 916Aput on a spiritual body, and can enter everywhere without fear, whence whole and uncorrupt in marriage; three testify to this sentence.

The disciple whom Jesus loved. The second miracle is that which Jesus did in Cana of Galilee, he pressed out wine from water, a work made ready, the virginity of John, he shows that a spouse was joined to him: the testimony that Jesus commended his mother to John: by Mary the Church is figured. John the chief place; as John was a virgin in marriage, so was he uncorrupt also in death: John relateth the genealogy from the divinity, for the others relate it from the man: of the divinity John saith, in the beginning was the Word; he lay upon the breast of Jesus, and John ascended to the knowledge of the divinity which was in the breast of Jesus, from the joining 916Bof the two breasts; a grafted tree, in another it maketh the fruit of the tree, of the other in which it is planted. If according to the order of the canon this book were to be set before Matthew, and that it is placed in the last part, it is because the earth was sanctified first, then the water, and afterwards last of all the fire, because it is brighter and subtler: likewise after the likeness of the two laws, that is, the old and the new; the old was delivered first, yet the new is brighter and greater, because it discerneth of heavenly things, and more abundant mine, which pertaineth to the Gospel of John out of what is said above.
