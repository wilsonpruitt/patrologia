Atto of Vercelli

Atto Vercellensis

c. 885 – 961

Lombard bishop of Vercelli from 924 until his death, and one of the more independent-minded canonists of tenth-century Italy. He served as grand chancellor to Hugh of Provence and to Lothar II as kings of Italy. His writings include the Polypticum, a bleak survey of the morals of his age; De pressuris ecclesiasticis, on the pressures brought to bear on ecclesiastical authority by lay power; and a Capitulare gathering the canon law of his province, drawing on the False Decretals and enlarged with provisions of his own.

893CIn nomine sanctae et individuae Trinitatis Patris et Filii et Spiritus sancti, cui sit gloria in sempiterna saecula saeculorum, amen. Regnantibus domno Hugone et domno Hlotario piissimis regibus, regni domni Hugonis vigesimo, et domni Hlotarii decimo quinto, pontificante venerabile patre et domino domino Marino sanctissimo papa universalis Ecclesiae Dei anno tertio, indictione 3, Idibus Maii, constituta synodo a venerabili patre domno Olderico archiepiscopo sanctae Mediolanensis Ecclesiae in basilica gloriosissimi pontificis et confessoris sancti Ambrosii ante ejus sanctum corpus; astantibus domno Hugone legato misso dominorum qui supra regum, et venerabili patre domno Rogerio episcopo Perusino legato sanctissimi domini papae. In ipsa synodo erat venerabilis 893Dpater dominus archiepiscopus sanctae Mediolanensis Ecclesiae, et venerabiles episcopi Vercellensis, Papiensis, Novariensis, Brixiensis, Laudensis, Bergomensis, Terdonensis, Cremonensis, Astensis, Curiensis, Taurinensis, Eporediensis, Augustensis, Albensis, Aquensis, Savonensis, Januensis, Albingaunensis, 894CVigintimiliensis, Placentinus, Parmensis, Mutinensis, et quamplures alii episcopi, abbates, sacerdotes, et clerici, una cum Vidone comite et quamplures illustres regni proceres.

Completa liturgia, antequam dissolveretur synodus, surrexit domnus Atto sanctae Vercellensis Ecclesiae episcopus, qui petiit reverenter facultatem loquendi, qua ei largita, dominus archiepiscopus eum sedere fecit, quo facto dixit: Venerabiles Patres et illustres viri, cum dixisset Deus in sancto Evangelio ubi duo vel tres in nomine meo congregati sunt, ibi et ego in medio eorum sum; ideo dubitare non debemus, Deum in medio nostrorum esse. Quam ob rem cum sim in bona senectute constitutus, et Deus nos admonuisset dicens: Estote parati, quia 894Dnescitis diem neque horam quando Dominus veniet, propterea si vultis mihi complacere, vellem coram Deo et vobis bona mea ordinare ad salutem animae meae, parentumque meorum et omnium fidelium defunctorum, ut post mortem meam habeam qui pro anima mea Deum exorent, ut a peccatis solvatur. 895AQuibus auditis, omnes dixerunt: Fiat per venerabilem domnum Attonem quidquid vult. Et ipse sic dicere coepit:

In Jesu Christi nomine, ego Atto, sanctae Vercellensis Ecclesiae humilis episcopus, cogitans infallibilia esse verba Domini, videlicet thesaurizate vobis thesauros in coelo, et date pauperibus, quoniam centuplum accipietis et vitam aeternam possidebitis; ideo ego qui supra Atto episcopus volo, judico, seu per hoc meum judicatum statuo una cum Hugone marchione, ac Brucardo comite advocatis meis, quod omnia quae statuero perpetuis temporibus inviolata permaneant: et in primis judico et lego statim post mortem meam palatium meum Stazone cum ejus pomerio et silva et annexis in integrum 895Bvobis venerabili patri, et domine domine archiepiscope Mediolani, et iis qui post vos Ecclesiam Mediolanensem episcopabunt, ita ut perpetuis temporibus permaneat sine imminutione in potestate archiepiscoporum Mediolani: pro quo rogo eos semel in anno sacra facere pro requie animae meae. Item lego et judico Ecclesiae sancti Eusebii seu episcopatui ejus, unde sum indigne episcopus, casales quatuor juris mei in valle Augustae, et alios duos in Verdezzo, omnia cum suis aedificiis et terris et juribus mihi, qui supra Attoni episcopo spectantibus, cum servis et aldiis utriusque sexus in integrum; ita ut qui post me praeerunt dictae Ecclesiae, usufructuario nomine tantum, cunctis diebus suis faciant ex fructibus eorum quidquid voluerint, absque tamen alienatione 895Cfundi. Quod si aliquid alienaverint, praedicta omnia perveniant in potestatem sanctae Eporediensis Ecclesiae: qui omnes mei successores teneantur semel in mense sacrum facere, ut Deus misereatur animae meae pro ignorantiis in regimine Ecclesiae mihi commissae.

Item judico et lego canonicis praedictae sanctae Ecclesiae curtem regiam cum curticella, quae sunt juris mei, qui supra Attonis episcopi, cum eorum aedificiis, terris et juribus, vivis et mortuis in integrum, ut de eorum proventibus in Dominica olivarum unitim sumant refectionem, et residuum dividatur in octava Pentecostis aequis portionibus inter eos, quo die anniversarium faciunt pro omnibus episcopis ejus sanctae Vercellensis Ecclesiae, et prohibeo 895Deis omnem alienationem; et si alienaverint, omnia proveniant in potestatem Ecclesiae Taurinensis. Item volo quod statim post meum decessum, in honorem duodecim apostolorum liberentur duodecim servi mei, qui supra Attonis episcopi, ita ut cum uxoribus suis et filiis, cum omnibus eorum substantiis sint liberi, prout nunc, pro tunc, manumitto et libero.

Item judico et lego domno Aldemano consanguineo 896Ameo archipresbytero sanctae Mediolanensis Ecclesiae, et cardinalibus et presbyteris decumanis, et clero dictae Ecclesiae, qui modo sunt et in futuro erunt, illas quatuor valles juris mei, qui supra Attonis episcopi, quae vocantur Bellaniam, Leventinam, Biascam, et Intrascam, cum omnibus capellis, casis et terris, cujuscunque speciei sint, cum famulis et aldiis utriusque sexus in integrum, ita ut perpetuo sint in potestate cleri dictae sanctae Mediolanensis Ecclesiae, cujus fui indignus archidiaconus. Quorum proventus sic dividatur: dominus archipresbyter tantum habeat, quam duo cardinales presbyteri; et cardinales presbyteri cum primicerio decumanorum tantum habeant per unumquemque, quam duo presbyteri decumani; et diaconi cardinales et subdiaconi 896Bquantum unus presbyter decumanus. Eam vero partem, quae cardinalibus presbyteris spectat, dividat archipresbyter; ea quae cardinalibus diaconis et subdiaconis, dividatur per archidiaconum; et decumanis dividatur per eorum primicerium ad ejus mansionem. Et haec omnia cum conditione ut in perpetuum omni primo die lunae Quadragesimae majoris praedicti archipresbyter et cardinales primicerius et decumani, qui pro tempore erunt, veniant cum litaniis, et crucibus ad hanc basilicam, ubi ante corpora sanctorum Ambrosii, Protasii, et Gervasii, sacrum celebrent pro animabus, mea, et bonae memoriae domni Desiderii regis, et ducis Ermenulfi ejus patris, et Asprandi et Eberardi ejus fratris abavi mei, qui supra Attonis episcopi, ejus filii et aliorum 896Cfiliorum ejus. Et item in die lunae sequenti idem fiat ante corpus sancti Satyri in requiem animae meae et bonae memoriae Vuidonis comitis qui fuit proavus meus, et Attonis qui et Anscarius, qui fuit marchio, ejus filii, qui sunt tumulati in eadem ecclesia. Iterum idem fiat tertio die lunae sequenti, ut supra, et ibi ante corpus sanctae Marcellinae virginis sacrum fiat pro animabus, mea, et bonae memoriae Aldigerii, alias Ermenulfi patris mei, et fratrum suorum in eadem ecclesia quiescentium. Et prima die recedendo ab ipsa ecclesia intrent ecclesiam sancti Naboris; secunda vero vadant ad basilicam trium regum; et tertia pergant ad ecclesiam sancti Georgii, ibi ubi dicitur ad palatium, et ibi faciant kyrie et rogationes pro salute hujus civitatis 896Det omnium habitantium in ea, et in fine dicant requiem pro omnibus defunctis. Qui vero ad istas tres litanias non erit, nihil penitus habeat, salvo si aegrotaret, et tamen teneatur idem facere in alio die.

Item judico et lego venerabili domno Rupaldo abbati monasterii prope eamdem basilicam, et ejus monasterio, curtem meam de Deliciano, cum capella, et casis, et terris, et juribus ejus, et famulis mihi qui supra Attoni episcopo spectantibus, in integrum; 897Acum hac tamen conditione, ut ipse abbas cum monachis suis, dum cardinales et decumani praefati erunt in ecclesia sancti Ambrosii, vadant cum litaniis suis ad ecclesiam sanctae Mariae Majoris sitam intra civitatem Mediolani, ubi dicitur intus vineam, et ibi sacra faciant pro anima mea, et bonae memoriae domnae Mariae matris, ac domnae Imildae sororis, mearum, ibi humatarum: altera die idem faciant pro animabus bonae memoriae comitissae Felindae aviae meae, et filiarum ejus; et die tertio etiam ita fiat pro animabus Rodelindae proaviae meae, et omnium defunctorum meorum: quod si non fiet, omnia praedicta eis legata sint domnae Bertradae abbatissae dicti monasterii sanctae Mariae Majoris. Item judico et lego dictae venerabili domnae abbatissae Bertradae 897Bfratruae meae cum ejus monialibus et monasterio suo mansa quinque terrae juris mei, qui supra Attonis episcopi, in curte Cerdati, cum aedificiis, servis, aldiis et eorum familiis utriusque sexus spectantibus, in integrum, ut commodius possint Deo servire, et semel in mense recordentur orare ut anima mea a peccatis solvatur.

Et haec omnia ego qui supra Atto episcopus judico et facio cum conditione, quod praedicti omnes qui supra cardinales, decumani, abbas, monachi, abbatissa, et moniales, ac eorum successores, vel succetrices, unitim vel separatim, in totum vel in partem, dictas valles, curtes, casales, terras, servos, famulos, aldios utriusque sexus, non possint quovis modo alienare aut invasere, quia mea firma voluntas 897Cest, quod omnia praedicta perpetuis temporibus permaneant ad cultum divinum in requiem omnium defunctorum meorum. Ideo reverenter rogo vos, venerabiles, pater domine archiepiscope, et omnes residentes in hac sancta synodo, ut ponatis excommunicationem et anathema super omnes qui hoc meum judicatum in toto vel in parte violaverint.

Hoc audito, dominus archiepiscopus et omnis synodus ex parte Dei et omnium sanctorum anathematizavit quicunque per qualecunque ingenium hoc judicatum venerabilis patris domini Attonis sanctae Vercellensis Ecclesiae episcopi violaverit; et qui fecerit, partem habeat cum Juda traditore ac Simone mago in gehennam aeternam, una cum Satana et diabolis ejus. Et dixerunt omnes: Fiat, fiat, fiat. 897DQuo facto, ipse Atto episcopus dixit: Audite, omnes venerabiles Patres; hic disposui, quae judicare et disponere potui pro conscientia mea; reliquas vero meas substantias, quae mihi modo pertinere videntur, judico et lego propinquioribus meis, ut de eis faciant quidquid voluerint, juxta dispositionem majorum eorum, et lex Longobarda disponit. Item rogo sanctum patrem venerabilem papam, et clementissimos dominos reges suis litteris hoc meum judicatum confirmare dignentur.

Quibus auditis, dominus Hugo comes et dominus Rogerius episcopus missi praedicti, auctoritatem dominorum 898Aregum et domini papae interponentes, haec omnia convalidaverunt et confirmare facere promiserunt. His peractis, idem dominus Atto episcopus una cum Hugone marchione et Brucardo comite advocatis suis dixit: Haec est mea libera voluntas et ultimum meum judicatum. Sic me Deus adjuvet, amen. Testament

893CIn the name of the holy and undivided Trinity, of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, to whom be glory unto everlasting ages of ages, amen. In the reign of the lord Hugh and the lord Lothair, most pious kings — the twentieth year of the reign of the lord Hugh, and the fifteenth of the lord Lothair — in the third year of the pontificate of the venerable father and lord, the lord Marinus, most holy pope of the universal Church of God, in the third indiction, on the Ides of May, a synod having been convened by the venerable father the lord Olderic, archbishop of the holy Church of Milan, in the basilica of the most glorious pontiff and confessor Saint Ambrose, before his holy body; there being present the lord Hugh, legate sent by the lords the aforesaid kings, and the venerable father the lord Roger, bishop of Perugia, legate of the most holy lord pope. At that same synod was present the venerable 893Dfather the lord archbishop of the holy Church of Milan, and the venerable bishops of Vercelli, Pavia, Novara, Brescia, Lodi, Bergamo, Tortona, Cremona, Asti, Chur, Turin, Ivrea, Aosta, Alba, Acqui, Savona, Genoa, Albenga, 894CVentimiglia, Piacenza, Parma, Modena, and a great many other bishops, abbots, priests, and clerics, together with Count Guido and a great many illustrious magnates of the kingdom.

The liturgy completed, before the synod was dissolved, the lord Atto, bishop of the holy Church of Vercelli, rose, and reverently asked leave to speak; and this being granted him, the lord archbishop had him take his seat. This done, he said: Venerable Fathers and illustrious men, since God has said in the holy Gospel, where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I also in the midst of them, we therefore ought not to doubt that God is in the midst of us. Wherefore, since I am set in a ripe old age, and God has admonished us, saying, Be ye ready, for 894Dye know neither the day nor the hour when the Lord shall come, therefore, if it please you to gratify me, I would set my goods in order before God and before you, for the salvation of my soul, and of my kinsfolk, and of all the faithful departed, so that after my death I may have those who will entreat God for my soul, that it may be loosed from its sins. 895AHaving heard these things, all said: Let whatever the venerable lord Atto wishes be done through him. And he began to speak thus:

In the name of Jesus Christ, I, Atto, humble bishop of the holy Church of Vercelli, considering that the words of the Lord are infallible — namely, lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, and give to the poor, for ye shall receive a hundredfold and shall possess life eternal — therefore I, the aforesaid Bishop Atto, will, adjudge, and by this my adjudication establish, together with Hugh the marquis and Burchard the count, my advocates, that all things which I shall establish remain inviolate for perpetual time. And in the first place I adjudge and bequeath, immediately after my death, my palace of Stazzona with its orchard and wood and appurtenances entire 895Bto you, venerable father, and lord, lord archbishop of Milan, and to those who after you shall hold the bishopric of the Church of Milan, so that it remain for perpetual time without diminution in the power of the archbishops of Milan; for which I ask them once a year to perform the sacred rites for the repose of my soul. Likewise I bequeath and adjudge to the Church of Saint Eusebius, or to its bishopric, whereof I am unworthily bishop, four farmsteads of my right in the valley of Aosta, and two others in Verdezzo, all with their buildings and lands and rights pertaining to me, the aforesaid Bishop Atto, with the serfs and aldii of either sex, entire; so that those who after me shall preside over the said Church may, by title of usufruct only, do with their fruits, all the days of their life, whatever they will, yet without alienation 895Cof the estate. But if they should alienate anything, let all the aforesaid come into the power of the holy Church of Ivrea; and let all my successors be bound to perform the sacred rites once a month, that God may have mercy on my soul for the things done in ignorance in the governance of the Church committed to me.

Likewise I adjudge and bequeath to the canons of the aforesaid holy Church the royal court with its lesser court, which are of my right, the aforesaid Bishop Atto's, with their buildings, lands and rights, living and dead, entire, so that from their revenues they may on the Sunday of Olives take a meal together, and the remainder be divided on the octave of Pentecost in equal portions among them, on which day they keep the anniversary for all the bishops of that holy Church of Vercelli; and I forbid 895Dthem all alienation; and if they shall have alienated it, let all come into the power of the Church of Turin. Likewise I will that immediately after my decease, in honor of the twelve apostles, twelve serfs of mine, the aforesaid Bishop Atto's, be set free, so that with their wives and children, with all their substance, they be free — even as now, so for then, I manumit and free them.

Likewise I adjudge and bequeath to the lord Aldeman, my kinsman, 896Aarchpriest of the holy Church of Milan, and to the cardinals and decuman priests, and to the clergy of the said Church, both those who now are and those who shall be in future, those four valleys of my right, the aforesaid Bishop Atto's, which are called Bellagio, Leventina, Biasca, and Intragna, with all their chapels, houses and lands, of whatever kind they be, with the servants and aldii of either sex, entire, so that they be perpetually in the power of the clergy of the said holy Church of Milan, whereof I was unworthy archdeacon. Whose revenues shall be divided thus: let the lord archpriest have as much as two cardinal priests; and let the cardinal priests, along with the primicerius of the decumans, each have as much as two decuman priests; and the cardinal deacons and subdeacons 896Bas much as one decuman priest. But that portion which pertains to the cardinal priests, let the archpriest divide; that which pertains to the cardinal deacons and subdeacons, let it be divided by the archdeacon; and for the decumans let it be divided by their primicerius at his lodging. And all these things on this condition: that in perpetuity, on every first Monday of the Great Lent, the aforesaid archpriest and cardinals, primicerius and decumans, who shall be in office at the time, come with litanies and crosses to this basilica, where, before the bodies of the saints Ambrose, Protasius, and Gervasius, they shall celebrate the sacred rites for the souls of myself, and of the lord King Desiderius of good memory, and of Duke Ermenulf his father, and of Asprand and of Eberard his brother, my great-great-grandfather, the aforesaid Bishop Atto's, his son and of his other 896Csons. And likewise on the following Monday let the same be done before the body of Saint Satyrus, for the repose of my soul and of Count Guido of good memory, who was my great-grandfather, and of Atto also called Anscar, who was marquis, his son, who are entombed in the same church. Again let the same be done on the third following Monday, as above, and there, before the body of Saint Marcellina the virgin, let the sacred rites be performed for the souls of myself, and of Aldiger of good memory, otherwise Ermenulf, my father, and of his brothers resting in the same church. And on the first day, withdrawing from that same church, let them enter the church of Saint Nabor; on the second let them go to the basilica of the three kings; and on the third let them proceed to the church of Saint George, there where it is called "at the palace," and there let them make the kyrie and the rogations for the welfare of this city 896Dand of all dwelling in it, and at the end let them say the requiem for all the departed. But whoever shall not be present at these three litanies, let him have nothing whatever, save if he be sick; and yet let him be bound to do the same on another day.

Likewise I adjudge and bequeath to the venerable lord Rupald, abbot of the monastery near that same basilica, and to his monastery, my court of Deliciano, with its chapel, and houses, and lands, and its rights, and the servants pertaining to me, the aforesaid Bishop Atto, entire; 897Ayet on this condition, that the abbot himself with his monks, while the aforesaid cardinals and decumans are in the church of Saint Ambrose, go with their litanies to the church of Saint Mary Major situated within the city of Milan, where it is called "within the vineyard," and there perform the sacred rites for my soul, and for those of the lady Mary my mother of good memory, and of the lady Imilda my sister, buried there. On the second day let them do the same for the souls of the countess Felinda of good memory, my grandmother, and of her daughters; and on the third day let it likewise be done for the souls of Rodelinda my great-grandmother, and of all my departed. But if this shall not be done, let all the aforesaid things bequeathed to them belong to the lady Bertrada, abbess of the said monastery of Saint Mary Major. Likewise I adjudge and bequeath to the said venerable lady abbess Bertrada, 897Bmy brother's daughter, with her nuns and her monastery, five mansi of land of my right, the aforesaid Bishop Atto's, in the court of Cerdato, with the buildings, serfs, aldii and their households of either sex pertaining thereto, entire, that they may the more conveniently serve God, and once a month remember to pray that my soul may be loosed from its sins.

And all these things I, the aforesaid Bishop Atto, adjudge and do on this condition: that all the aforesaid cardinals, decumans, abbot, monks, abbess, and nuns, and their successors, male or female, jointly or severally, in whole or in part, may not in any manner alienate or seize upon the said valleys, courts, farmsteads, lands, serfs, servants, aldii of either sex; because it is my firm will 897Cthat all the aforesaid remain for perpetual time devoted to divine worship, for the repose of all my departed. Therefore I reverently ask you, venerable father, lord archbishop, and all who sit in this holy synod, that you lay excommunication and anathema upon all who shall violate this my adjudication in whole or in part.

This heard, the lord archbishop and the whole synod, on the part of God and of all the saints, anathematized whosoever by any contrivance whatever shall violate this adjudication of the venerable father the lord Atto, bishop of the holy Church of Vercelli; and let him who shall do so have his portion with Judas the traitor and Simon Magus in eternal gehenna, together with Satan and his devils. And all said: So be it, so be it, so be it. 897DThis done, Bishop Atto himself said: Hear, all venerable Fathers; here I have disposed of what I was able to adjudge and dispose of according to my conscience; but the remainder of my substance, which is seen now to belong to me, I adjudge and bequeath to my nearer kinsmen, that they may do with it whatever they will, according to the disposition of their elders, and as the Lombard law disposes. Likewise I ask the holy father, the venerable pope, and the most clement lords the kings, that they deign by their letters to confirm this my adjudication.

Having heard these things, the lord Hugh the count and the lord Roger the bishop, the aforesaid envoys, interposing the authority of the lords 898Athe kings and of the lord pope, ratified all these things and promised to have them confirmed. These matters accomplished, the same lord Bishop Atto, together with Hugh the marquis and Burchard the count, his advocates, said: This is my free will and my final adjudication. So help me God, amen. Done in the basilica of Saint Ambrose, pontiff and confessor, where his holy body rests.

On this text

The Latin is Migne's printing of Testamentum (PL 134, coll. 893C–900B), by Atto of Vercelli and Various Authors, from the Corpus Corporum transcription. Parenthetical references in small type stand on Migne's page — editorial identifications inherited from the edition he reprinted or supplied by his shop, not necessarily the author's own; the author's own citations run in the prose itself. Every gilt column mark is an address: migne.app/pl/134/893c resolves to the first.

Du bon, à bon marché — carried to its limit: the good, free.
