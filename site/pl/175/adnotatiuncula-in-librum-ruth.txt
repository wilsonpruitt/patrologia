## A Little Annotation on the Book of Ruth

*[n: (RUTH. IV.)] A man was loosing his sandal.* That man, who was yielding his right to his neighbor, was loosing his sandal *— his own, and was giving it to his neighbor —* to whom he was yielding. [0096C] *This Boaz, then, who was the near kinsman, said to the other who was yielding to him: Take the sandal,* etc.
