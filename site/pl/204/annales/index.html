96.

987BThe passion of Saint Dionysius. Saint Sanctinus, making his way to Rome with Antoninus after the martyrdom of blessed Dionysius, by that same man's command, was instructed by the visitation of an angel and turned aside to the city of the Clavi, which is called 987CVerdun; preaching the word of God to the citizens, he converted many, and, building and consecrating a church in honor of blessed Peter, he was chosen first bishop by those very Christians whom he had converted.
