AETAS PRIMA.

  • 1173D

Adam cum esset CXXX annorum genuit Seth. Seth autem habens annos CV genuit Enos vivente Adam, et habente annos CCXXXV. Enos quippe habens annos XC genuit Cainam vivente Adam, et habente annos CCCXXV. Cainam vero habens annos LXX genuit Malaleel vivente Adam, et habente annos CCCXCV. Malaleel habens annos LXV genuit Jared vivente Adam, et 1174Dhabente annos CCCCLX. Jared autem habens annos CLXII genuit Enoch vivente Adam, et habente annos DCXXII. Enoch vero habens annos LXV genuit Matusala vivente Adam et habente annos DCLXXXVII. Et Mathusalem habens annos CLXXXVII genuit Lamech vivente Adam et habente annos DCCCLXXIV. Lamech enim habens annos CLXXII genuit Noe. Noe habens annos D genuit Sem. Sem autem habens annos C diluvium 1175Afactum est. Et hic perficitur numerus annorum millesimus DCLXVI.

THE FIRST AGE.

  • 1173D

Adam, when he was CXXX years old, begot Seth. Seth, being CV years old, begot Enos while Adam was still living and was CCXXXV years old. Enos indeed, being XC years old, begot Cainan while Adam was still living and was CCCXXV years old. Cainan, being LXX years old, begot Malaleel while Adam was still living and was CCCXCV years old. Malaleel, being LXV years old, begot Jared while Adam was still living and 1174Dwas CCCCLX years old. Jared, being CLXII years old, begot Enoch while Adam was still living and was DCXXII years old. Enoch indeed, being LXV years old, begot Mathusala while Adam was still living and was DCLXXXVII years old. And Mathusalem, being CLXXXVII years old, begot Lamech while Adam was still living and was DCCCLXXIV years old. Lamech in fact, being CLXXII years old, begot Noah. Noah, being D years old, begot Shem. And when Shem was C years old the flood came to pass. 1175AAnd here is completed the number of years, a thousand DCLXVI.

AETAS SECUNDA.

Anno secundo post diluvium Sem annorum CII genuit Arfaxat. Arfaxat habens annos XXXV genuit Sale. Sale autem habens annos XXX genuit Eber. Eber habens annos XXXIV genuit Falech. Falech habens annos XXX genuit Reu. Reu habens annos XXXII genuit Seruch. Seruch habens annos XXX genuit Nachor. Nachor habens annos XXIX genuit Thara. Thara habens annos LXX genuit Abraham. Hic finitur numerus secundae aetatis habens annos CCXCII, et fiunt simul anni duarum aetatum mille DCCCCXLVIII.

THE SECOND AGE.

In the second year after the flood, Shem, at CII years, begot Arphaxad. Arphaxad, being XXXV years old, begot Sale. Sale, being XXX years old, begot Eber. Eber, being XXXIV years old, begot Phalech. Phalech, being XXX years old, begot Reu. Reu, being XXXII years old, begot Seruch. Seruch, being XXX years old, begot Nachor. Nachor, being XXIX years old, begot Thara. Thara, being LXX years old, begot Abraham. Here ends the number of the second age, containing CCXCII years; and the years of the two ages together come to a thousand DCCCCXLVIII.

AETAS TERTIA.

Abraham cum esset C annorum genuit Isaac. Isaac habens annos LX genuit Jacob. Jacob habens annos XC genuit Joseph qui vixit annos CX. Fuerunt ab eo usque ad egressionem filiorum Israel ex Aegypto per 1175BMosem ducem anni CLXV. Et ab egressione Israel usque ad Josue anni XL. A Josue ergo usque ad Othonielem fiunt anni XXVI. Et ab Othoniele usque ad Aoth anni XL. Ab Aoth vero usque ad Debbora fiunt anni LXXX. Et a Debbora usque ad Gedeonem fiunt anni XL. Et a Gedeone usque ad Abimelech anni XL. Ab Abimelech autem usque ad Tota fiunt anni III. A Tola quippe usque Jair sunt anni XXIII. A Jair vero usque ad Jephte sunt anni XXII. Et de Jephte usque ad Ebesan fiunt anni VI. De Ebesa autem usque ad Achialonem sunt anni VII. Ab Achialone usque ad Labdonem fiunt anni X. A Labdone vero usque ad Samsonem fiunt anni VIII. Et a Samsone usque ad Heli anni XX. Ab Heli enim usque ad Samuelem sunt anni XL. Et a Samuele usque ad Saulem sunt anni XII. A Saule ergo usque ad David sunt anni XXX. Hic perficitur numerus tertiae aetatis annorum DCCCCXLII, et sunt simul anni trium aetatum duo millia DCCCXC.

THE THIRD AGE.

Abraham, when he was C years old, begot Isaac. Isaac, being LX years old, begot Jacob. Jacob, being XC years old, begot Joseph, who lived CX years. From him down to the going out of the children of Israel from Egypt under 1175BMoses their leader there were CLXV years. And from the going out of Israel down to Joshua, XL years. From Joshua, then, down to Othniel there are XXVI years. And from Othniel down to Aoth, XL years. From Aoth indeed down to Deborah there are LXXX years. And from Deborah down to Gideon there are XL years. And from Gideon down to Abimelech, XL years. From Abimelech, moreover, down to Tota there are III years. From Tola indeed down to Jair there are XXIII years. From Jair down to Jephthah there are XXII years. And from Jephthah down to Ebesan there are VI years. From Ebesa, moreover, down to Achialon there are VII years. From Achialon down to Labdon there are X years. From Labdon indeed down to Samson there are VIII years. And from Samson down to Eli, XX years. From Eli in fact down to Samuel there are XL years. And from Samuel down to Saul there are XII years. From Saul, then, down to David there are XXX years. Here is completed the number of the third age, DCCCCXLII years; and the years of the three ages together are two thousand DCCCXC.

From Justinian down to Pippin the Elder there are II years. From Pippin the Elder down to Charles, XXVII years. From Charles down to Pippin and to Carloman, XXVII years.
