# Cruces — 10517, *De primordiis et inventione sacrae religionis Hierosolymorum*

Garbled or corrupt printed readings, rendered literally in the English and logged here.

## Chunk 0

- **1098C** — *ad a. p. et libertatem*. Migne prints an unexpanded abbreviation `a. p.`; most likely *ad avitam pietatem* / *ad antiquam patriam* vel sim. Rendered "for the ancestral faith and for liberty" as the least-committal literal reading. Needs a check against another witness.
- **1098C** — *mortuo Alexandrio Philippo Macedonum rege*. Confused: Philip II and Alexander are conflated into one name, and the Maccabean setting is centuries later. Rendered literally ("Alexander Philip, king of the Macedonians").
- **1098C** — *xenoduna* for *xenodochium*. Kept as the technical Latin institution-word in italic (*xenodochium*) per the register rule; the printed spelling is a corruption.
- **1099B** — *De hoc loco acenti ( *sic* )*. Migne himself flags this with *sic*. Probably *adjacenti* ("adjoining") or a truncated *accedenti*. Left untranslated in the English with the *sic* marker preserved.
- **1099C** — *fideique simbolum ad duodecim factum*: "the symbol of the faith was made in twelve parts" — i.e. the legend of the twelve apostles each contributing an article. The *ad duodecim* construction is loose.
- **1100C** — *retrahere de Latino in grammaticam*: "draw it back from the Latin into grammar." Sense inverted from what one expects (vernacular → Latin); rendered literally.
- **1101C** — *Zachariae prophetae* is addressed as Melchiar's brother and given a wife and son. The Zacharias of Luke 1 and the prophet Zechariah are conflated. Rendered as printed.

## Chunk 1

- **1101D** — *Accamanus imperator Romanus*: no such Roman emperor. Rendered as printed.
- **1101D** — *unam insulam Crethii, quae vocatur Rhochas*: "a certain island of Crete which is called Rhochas" — geographically incoherent; *Rhochas* is likely Rhodes. Rendered as printed.
- **1103A** — *Philippus prothomartyr*: Stephen has just been named first martyr in the same clause, so *prothomartyr* attached to Philip is an error (Philip is one of the seven, Acts 6:5). Rendered as printed.
- **1103A** — *Nichauome, Thymocenn Permanan*: badly corrupt forms of the remaining names of the seven (Acts 6:5), i.e. Nicanor, Timon, Parmenas. Left as printed, untranslated.
- **1103B** — *Caryseni*: unidentified ethnonym, possibly a corruption of *Chorasmini* / *Chorosani* (Khwarazmians). Transliterated as printed.
- **1103C–1104B** — The Hospital's first master is named *Conradus* throughout but glossed *vel Gerardus* three times; the historical figure is Gerard. Both names kept in the English exactly where Migne prints them.
- **1104A** — *praecepit sibi, ut sine timore Christianos, ut consueverat, debellaret*: the Sultan orders him to "fight the Christians" — but the whole point of the story is that he was feeding them. Almost certainly a corruption of something like *ut ... Christianos pasceret* / *non debellaret*. Rendered literally; the very next sentence contradicts it.
