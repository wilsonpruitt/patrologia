# Cruces — 10725 (Anselm of Laon, Enarrationes in Cantica canticorum)

Printed-Latin problems rendered literally (not emended). Chunk / column / Latin as printed / problem.

- **Chunk 0, col 1188B** — "Dicitur Salomon secundum hos tres libros quos fecit XXX habuisse vocabula" — Says Solomon had *thirty* (XXX) names, but the passage lists and expounds only *three* names (Idida, Ecclesiastes, Solomon), tied to the three books just enumerated. "XXX" is almost certainly a corruption of "tria" (three). Rendered literally as "thirty."

- **Chunk 1, col 1193A–B (scripture block)** — "Aequitatui meo in curribus Pharaonis assimilavi te" — "Aequitatui" is not a valid Latin form. Evidently a misprint for "Equitatui" (dative of *equitatus*, "cavalry") — confirmed by the commentary lemma at col 1194A/1193D reading "*Equitatui meo.*" and by the running gloss "assimilavi te equitatui." Rendered as "cavalry."

- **Chunk 2, col 1196C** — commentary lemma "*domorum vestrarum*" ("of *your* houses") comments on a scripture block (col 1195B) that reads "tigna domorum *nostrarum* cedrina" ("of *our* houses"). Internal nostrarum/vestrarum discrepancy. Lemma rendered as printed ("your houses").

- **Chunk 3, col 1198B (scripture block)** — "Surge, prospera amica mea" — "prospera" where the Vulgate and this commentary's own lemma (col 1200A) read "propera" ("make haste"). Likely a misprint (prospera / propera). Rendered as printed ("prosper").

- **Chunk 3, col 1199C** — commentary lemma "Similis est *hinnulo servorum.*" ("young hart of the *servants*") where the scripture reads "hinnulo *cervorum*" ("young hart of the *harts*") and the immediately following gloss reads "filius cervorum." "servorum" is a misprint for "cervorum." Rendered as printed ("of the servants").

Note (NOT logged as a crux — deliberate variant, rendered as printed): Chunk 3/4, "umbra abiit et recessit" (Song 2:11) prints *umbra* ("shadow") where the Vulgate reads *imber* ("rain"); the commentary at col 1200A glosses *Umbra* explicitly, so the variant is genuine and load-bearing, not a typo. Rendered "the shadow has gone and departed."
