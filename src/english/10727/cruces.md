# Cruces — 10727, Anselm of Laon, *Epistola*

Garbled or corrupt printed readings, rendered literally in the English and logged here.

## Chunk 0

- **1587D–1588A** — *quem vul indurat*. *vul* is a broken *vult*, split across the column break. Rendered with the intended reading ("whom he wills he hardens").
- **1588C** — *ei ideo Deus juste ibi cum deserit*. Two corruptions in one clause: *ei* for *et*, and *cum* for *eum*. Rendered with the intended reading ("and therefore God justly forsakes him there").
- **1588C** — *Deum peccator flagellatur*. *Deum* is unconstruable here; almost certainly a misprint for *Dum* ("While the sinner is scourged…"), which the following clauses require. Left as printed in italic in the English and flagged, per the render-literally rule.
- **1590A** — *diligit se, diligitamicum*: *diligit amicum* printed run together. Rendered as two words.
- **1590A** — *hoc ostenditur in canticograduum*: *cantico graduum* ("in the Song of Degrees / the gradual psalms") printed run together. Left as the printed single word in the English.
- **1590C** — *Continentia praecedit luxuriam, humilitas superbiam*. The surrounding argument is that the virtues *cut down* (*praecidunt*) their contraries — the previous sentence uses exactly that verb — so *praecedit* is likely a misprint for *praecidit*. Rendered as printed ("goes before"), but the sense wanted is "cuts down."

## Chunk 1

- **1591B** — *impedit menstruis*. Corrupt: an ablative with no governing construction, ending a list of drunkenness's bodily effects. Possibly *impedit menstrua* or a truncation. Left untranslated in italic.
- **1591B** — *turbo captus*. *captus* is unconstruable; almost certainly *turbo capitis* ("a whirlwind of the head"), matching the string of genitives around it. Left untranslated in italic.
- **1592A** — *Exiet Virga de radice Jesse … [n: (Isa. II, 1)]*. The verse is Isaiah 11:1; the printed citation *Isa. II, 1* is wrong. Citation passes through verbatim per the marker rule. Note also that the argument about the rod bearing nuts imports Numbers 17 (Aaron's rod) into the Isaiah lemma — that is the author's move, not a corruption.
- **1592B** — *In testa lignum inserens crucis qui non discrevit, id quod foris et intus, sed quae terrena…*. Badly tangled: *qui* has no antecedent (expect *quod* or *quae*), and *id quod* is left dangling. Rendered as literally as the syntax allows.
- **1592B** — The Colossians quotation is printed **without** an `[n: …]` wrapper and with an unclosed parenthesis and a stray asterisk: `(Colos. I, 20*.` Reproduced exactly as printed; this is a typesetting failure, not an authorial variant, and does not count toward `noteCount` (2).
- **1592B** — *Jugum enim meum suave est… [n: (Matth. XI, 20)]*. The verse is Matthew 11:30; the printed citation is wrong. Passes through verbatim.
- **1592C** — *colabilis sit* for *colabilis fit*. Rendered with the intended indicative ("becomes able to be strained down").
