## CHAPTER VIII (cont.)


⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Christ to the Synagogue concerning the Church: « Our sister is little, and she hath no breasts. » This sister is understood as the Church of the Gentiles. ⟦s2⟧ The apostles say, therefore — the messengers of the Lord say — we are legates; we seek a wife for our Lord. ⟦s3⟧ Whom then shall we give him? ⟦s4⟧ Our sister is little, lately born, unwise and untaught. ⟦s5⟧ She has no breasts: she does not see the Testaments; she cannot yet bear, nor yet nourish [1286B] children; she knows not a husband. ⟦s6⟧ She must therefore be spoken to and admonished concerning the marriage. ⟦s7⟧ But « what shall we do to our sister in the day when she is to be spoken to? » With what words, or with what promises, shall we temper her mind. ⟦s8⟧ It follows: « If she be a wall, let us build upon it bulwarks of silver. » If, he says, we shall find in her a wall — constancy, faith, and fortitude — let us build upon it bulwarks of silver and the divine eloquences. ⟦s9⟧ For the sayings of the prophets and the discourses of the doctors are bulwarks to the wall and to our faith, since by these we are defended, by these we overcome vices, by these we withstand heretics. ⟦s10⟧ And these *633* are called silver, since nothing of lead or of brass, and nothing of falsehood, is found in them, « If she be a door, let us join it together with boards of cedar, » [1286C] etc. ⟦s11⟧ Let us paint the door, he says, let us mark the gates, and let us vary them, [d: let us vary them,] that the army of God and the multitude of the virtues may find an entrance; that they may recognize the sign, and may delight in comely adornments. ⟦s12⟧ Or also let us join it together and shut it, so that, the gates being fastened on every side, vices may be able to enter from no quarter. ⟦s13⟧ And let these be fortified with boards of cedar — with prophetic examples, unfading, and with other sayings. ⟦s14⟧ The voice of the Church: « And my breasts are as a tower. » I, she says, am a wall; I am strong, I am constant; I resist vices, I storm my enemies. ⟦s15⟧ « And my breasts are as a tower. » My breasts, she says — my doctrine and my wisdom — are a tower. ⟦s16⟧ The doctrine is lofty, the wisdom is great, well fortified, well armed, fearing neither the sophisms of heretics [1286D] nor those of philosophers. ⟦s17⟧ For these breasts nourish and defend her children. ⟦s18⟧ Whence they are called both a tower and breasts. ⟦s19⟧ But from what time is such fortitude hers? ⟦s20⟧ It follows: « Since I am become in his presence as one finding peace; » that is, since Christ loved me and reconciled me to the Father; since the angelic trumpet sounded; « Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace to men of good will [n: (Luc. II, 14)]. » The Synagogue says to the Church: « The peaceable one had a vineyard, which hath peoples. » By the vineyard understand the Church; and for the peaceable one, Christ. ⟦s21⟧ « For the vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel [n: (Isa. V, 7)]. » -- « And he [1287A] is our peace, which hath made both one [n: (Ephes. II, 14)]. » And this vineyard has peoples, because it gathers into one Jews and Gentiles, Greeks and barbarians, and all nations. ⟦s22⟧ « He delivered it to keepers, » that is, to the apostles and doctors, that they might cultivate it, and prune it, and guard it from thieves. ⟦s23⟧ « A man brings for its fruit a thousand pieces of silver. » For the number of a thousand is put for the highest perfection and fullness. ⟦s24⟧ And it is just as if he were to say: The holy and catholic man, that he may enjoy the merit and blessedness of this vineyard, gives away all things whatsoever he possesses. ⟦s25⟧ Christ says: « My vineyard is before me. » My vineyard, he says — my inheritance, or my Church — is before me and serves me; and although I had delivered it to keepers, yet I am always present [1287B] to fortify, prepare, and guard it. ⟦s26⟧ « A thousand of thine, O peaceable one, » — understand: are kept for thee. ⟦s27⟧ And those whom above he called pieces of silver he here calls peaceable ones. ⟦s28⟧ But why peaceable? ⟦s29⟧ Because they reconcile us to God, they soften his enmity, wrath, and indignation, and prepare peace for us, and lead us into peace. ⟦s30⟧ And these are reserved for the Church, and shall be rendered back to her multiplied. ⟦s31⟧ « And two hundred to them that keep its fruits. » Two hundred, he says, of the thousand peaceable ones are kept for the apostles and doctors. ⟦s32⟧ For to these, as to kings and masters, a double honour is to be had, since they guard the rest, and for the rest [1288A] they watch and die. ⟦s33⟧ Christ says to the Church: « Thou that dwellest in the gardens, the friends hearken. » Thou, he says, O Church, dwellest in the gardens, thou delightest in the lilies, thou liest among roses and violets; on every side are flowers, on every side virtues, on every side the sayings of the doctors. ⟦s34⟧ « Stay me up with flowers, » thou saidst; thy petition is fulfilled. ⟦s35⟧ My friends — namely, the angels and the apostles — hearken to thee; they desire to hear what thou believest, what hope thou hast, to what end thou tendest. ⟦s36⟧ « Make me hear thy voice; » namely, praise, jubilation, a canticle of praise and of confession. ⟦s37⟧ The voice of the Church to Christ: « Flee away, my beloved, and be like the roe and the fawn of harts upon the mountains of spices. » Flee, she says, leave the lands, fly away to the Father, that they who think thee only a man may believe thee to be true God. [1288B] For my sake thou camest, thou enteredst my house; already thou hast kissed me; to thee I am joined, to thee betrothed. ⟦s38⟧ Return therefore, draw me after thee, render me a like return, bring me into thy house. ⟦s39⟧ « Be like the roe, » seek the mountains, seek the heavens, seek the things above; « and the fawn of harts, » that thou mayest dwell in the shadow, in Selmon, and in refreshment; that thou mayest abide forever upon the mountains of spices, where the odour is most sweet, the place delightful, the splendour ineffable, the abundance unutterable; there the saints exult, there the angels rejoice; there with the Father and the Holy Spirit praise, honour, and glory are sung to thee forever. ⟦s40⟧ Amen.
