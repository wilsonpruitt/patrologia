## Notes on the Books of Kings

[0095D]The Book of Kings is among us closed within four divisions, of which the two former are among the Hebrews called the book of Samuel; the two following, Malachim Ramatham, as in the Hebrew, or as some Arimathaim, whence Arimathea in the Gospel.
