# Cruces — 11059 (Hugh of St Victor, *Adnotatiunculae in librum Judicum*)

Garbled or corrupt printed readings rendered literally per the translation register, logged here with column, printed reading, and note. The Hebrew dropped by the Corpus Corporum transcription (Migne sets Hebrew type followed by a Latin transliteration; the Hebrew fell out, leaving a stray space or doubled comma) was **RECOVERED from the plates (Gallica f126–f127, 2026-07-17) and re-inserted** into both twins; provenance in `data/hebrew-patches/11059.json`, method in `hebrew-recovery.md`.

- **col 0091A** — *In Hebraeo sic habetur: Biflagoth ruben gotholib helchiche lem [Hebrew] ; id est...* Hebrew RECOVERED (f126): בפלגות ראובן גדלים חקקי־לב = Judg. 5:15b, verified against MT + Migne's own transliteration. Unpointed; scan leaves ל/ר and ה/ח indistinct, read per MT.
- **col 0091D** — *In Hebraeo habetur: Romae Zachae quod interpretatur...* **NOT a lacuna:** the plate (f126) carries no Hebrew type here — the transliteration is complete. Earlier "dropped Hebrew" note withdrawn. (Hugh's own point: *Romae* transliterates a Hebrew word for "height," not the place-name Ramah — "in which the translation erred.")
- **col 0092D** — printed *quando Israel habitavit in Hesebon, et vinculis ejus* (lemma of Judg. 11:26). Migne prints *vinculis* ("bonds") where the Vulgate has *viculis* ("its villages / hamlets"); rendered literally as "in its bonds."
- **col 0094B** — *In Hebraeo sic habetur: Sogal ghereth, [Hebrew], id est tibiam super femur.* Hebrew RECOVERED (f127): עַל־יָרֵךְ = Judg. 15:8 (*ʿal-yārēk*, "upon the thigh"). Pointed and legible; matches MT. (Was the doubled-comma lacuna.)
- **col 0094C** — *In Hebraeo sic habetur: In maxilla asini homor hemor, [Hebrew] in maxilla asini percussi mille viros.* Hebrew RECOVERED (f127): הַחֲמֹר חֲמוֹר = Judg. 15:16, Migne's doublet on the root ḥ-m-r (ass/heap), glossed *cumulum de cumulis*, "a heap of heaps." Pointed and legible.
- **col 0096B** — printed *quod facore voluerunt* (JUD. XX comment). Misprint for *facere*; rendered "what they wished to do."

Variant readings in square brackets are editorial, not corruption, and are kept verbatim/untranslated per the register: **[Merome ]** (col 0091C, for *Romae*), **[Meros seu Merotz]** (col 0091D, for *Meroth*), **[Ozri ]** (col 0092B, for *Ezri*), **[ardorem]** (col 0094C, for *odorem*).

Column anchors reproduced exactly as printed; the print skips col 0095D (text runs 0095C → 0096A).
