# Cruces — 11059 (Hugh of St Victor, *Adnotatiunculae in librum Judicum*)

Garbled or corrupt printed readings rendered literally per the translation register, logged here with column, printed reading, and note. Several entries are dropped Hebrew glyphs: Migne sets a Hebrew word followed by its Latin transliteration, but the Hebrew type has fallen out of the digitized text, leaving a stray space or a doubled comma where the glyphs stood. Only the surviving transliteration is rendered.

- **col 0091A** — printed *In Hebraeo sic habetur: Biflagoth ruben gotholib helchiche lem ; id est...* The space before the semicolon marks the lacuna where the Hebrew glyphs of Judg. 5:15–16 dropped out; only the transliteration *Biflagoth ruben gotholib helchiche lem* survives and is reproduced.
- **col 0091D** — printed *In Hebraeo habetur: Romae Zachae quod interpretatur...* Transliteration only; the Hebrew glyphs dropped. (Hugh's own point: *Romae* here transliterates a Hebrew word for "height," not the place-name Ramah — "in which the translation erred.")
- **col 0092D** — printed *quando Israel habitavit in Hesebon, et vinculis ejus* (lemma of Judg. 11:26). Migne prints *vinculis* ("bonds") where the Vulgate has *viculis* ("its villages / hamlets"); rendered literally as "in its bonds."
- **col 0094B** — printed *In Hebraeo sic habetur: Sogal ghereth, , id est tibiam super femur.* The doubled comma marks the lacuna where the Hebrew glyphs dropped out; transliteration *Sogal ghereth* reproduced as printed, doubled comma retained.
- **col 0094C** — printed *In Hebraeo sic habetur: In maxilla asini homor hemor, in maxilla asini percussi mille viros.* Hebrew glyphs dropped; only the transliteration *homor hemor* survives (interpreted *cumulum de cumulis*, "a heap of heaps").
- **col 0096B** — printed *quod facore voluerunt* (JUD. XX comment). Misprint for *facere*; rendered "what they wished to do."

Variant readings in square brackets are editorial, not corruption, and are kept verbatim/untranslated per the register: **[Merome ]** (col 0091C, for *Romae*), **[Meros seu Merotz]** (col 0091D, for *Meroth*), **[Ozri ]** (col 0092B, for *Ezri*), **[ardorem]** (col 0094C, for *odorem*).

Column anchors reproduced exactly as printed; the print skips col 0095D (text runs 0095C → 0096A).
