## PREFACE.

[0371C]Obadiah, fourth in the order of the prophets, simple in speech and manifold in sense; sparing in words, but copious in judgments. According to that saying of Solomon: *A wise man is made known by few words [n: (Eccle. XX)]*, he directs his prophecy literally against Idumaea; allegorically against the world, tropologically against the flesh he sharpens his pen; bearing the type of the Savior, he subtly introduces his coming, through whom the world is destroyed, through whom the flesh is worn away, through whom liberty [0372C] is restored. Hence that word: *If the Son shall make you free, you shall be free indeed [n: (Joan. VIII)]*. This is the Obadiah who under king Ahab fed a hundred prophets hidden in caves; and while he ministered bodily nourishment, he received spiritual nourishment from God. Now the aforesaid man prophesied at the same time as the other prophets also, namely Amos, Joel and Hosea. And he lies buried in Samaria with Elisha and John the Baptist.
