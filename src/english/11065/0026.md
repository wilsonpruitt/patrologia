## HOMILY X. *On the remaining vanities, as far as that place: « The fool walketh in darkness. »* (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The eyes of a wise man are in his head; the fool walketh in darkness.* ⟦s2⟧ Just now it has been proved that wisdom enlightens, folly blinds. ⟦s3⟧ In what has it been proved? ⟦s4⟧ Because the wise man has enlightened eyes, the fool sees nothing. ⟦s5⟧ For if the wise man has enlightened eyes, then wisdom enlightens. ⟦s6⟧ And if [sic: *sit*] the fool sees nothing, then folly blinds. ⟦s7⟧ For wisdom is light, and enlightens. ⟦s8⟧ If you have eyes where she reaches; but if you have eyes where the light is not, you have eyes, but see nothing. ⟦s9⟧ And what does this profit? ⟦s10⟧ Let your eyes therefore be where the light is, lest you be made as one not having eyes, if you have not the light. ⟦s11⟧ But where is the light? ⟦s12⟧ Above is [0182B] the light, where wisdom is. ⟦s13⟧ For wisdom is above, and light is above, and all light comes from above. ⟦s14⟧ And as below there is darkness without light, so above there is light without darkness. ⟦s15⟧ But in the middle, after light there is darkness, and after darkness light. ⟦s16⟧ Therefore there are days of heaven which darkness does not divide, nor does obscurity break in upon them. ⟦s17⟧ And there are days of earth upon which darkness follows. ⟦s18⟧ Because where wisdom always is, there is always light; and where wisdom is not always, there is as much light as there is wisdom. ⟦s19⟧ And where wisdom never is, there is never light. ⟦s20⟧ Therefore there is light where wisdom reaches, and where wisdom does not reach, there is no light. ⟦s21⟧ And where does wisdom not reach, if she reaches from end to end — what is left outside, where she cannot [0182C] reach, she who penetrates the whole and embraces the whole? ⟦s22⟧ *I alone have compassed the circuit of heaven, and have penetrated the depth of the abyss, and have walked in the waves of the sea [n: (Eccli. XXIV)]*: *reaching from end to end mightily, and ordering all things sweetly [n: (Sap. VIII)]*. ⟦s23⟧ It is a great question, therefore, what that may be where wisdom does not reach. ⟦s24⟧ Where the eyes of fools are, who walk in darkness and do not see. ⟦s25⟧ Let us therefore question another Scripture, where the eyes of fools are, who walk in darkness and do not see. ⟦s26⟧ For it says: The eyes of a wise man are in his head; but the eyes of fools are in the ends of the earth. ⟦s27⟧ Therefore in the ends of the earth there is darkness, and for that reason the fools who walk in darkness do not see, because they have their eyes in the ends of the earth. ⟦s28⟧ For if the light is there where he has his eyes, fools do not walk in darkness, but [0182D] have enlightened eyes and see. ⟦s29⟧ But if the fool walketh in darkness, and the eyes of fools are in the ends of the earth, then beyond doubt there is darkness there, where the eyes cannot see. ⟦s30⟧ And where is this? ⟦s31⟧ In the ends of the earth. ⟦s32⟧ Therefore the darkness is far off, and the light is near. ⟦s33⟧ Why then do we find darkness more easily than light? ⟦s34⟧ Perhaps because we are not far from the ends of the earth, and dwell beside the people of darkness, nor have we yet gone from the ends of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon [n: (III Reg. X)], where the light is: therefore we strike against the darkness and are dimmed away from the light. ⟦s35⟧ Yet the light itself is near and presses itself upon us; but bleary eyes love the dark, and have turned themselves into darkness. ⟦s36⟧ Therefore the light is near, but we are far off; and the darkness is far off, and we draw near to it.
