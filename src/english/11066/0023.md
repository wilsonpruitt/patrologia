## I. ON THE EPISTLE TO THE ROMANS. (cont.)

[0511D]QUESTION CCCXXVII. But was not a promise made to the gentiles, since the Lord says in Hosea: *I will call my people that which is not my people [n: (Osee II)]*. Likewise Isaiah: *Rejoice, ye gentiles, with his people [n: (Rom. XV)]*. Likewise: *Rejoice, thou barren, that bearest not [n: (Gal. IV)]*, etc. Solution. A promise was not made to the gentiles, in such a way that the promise was directed to them; for no writings were given to them concerning the promise itself. Or a promise was not made to them, that Christ would come to them, and preach in his own person: for he came only to the Jews, and preached to them. Whence the Apostle says that he was a minister of the circumcision.

QUESTION CCCXXVIII. *There shall be a root of Jesse, and he that shall rise up to rule the gentiles,* etc. It is asked why he is called a root of Jesse. Solution. Because from him proceeded David, [0512A] who was constituted king, and by his power protected many, as a tree is wont to protect by its roots, or by its branches and leaves.

QUESTION CCCXXIX. It is asked also what sort of prophecy this was, since Isaiah began to be after the death of Jesse? For does prophecy use the future for the past, as conversely it often does? Solution. This prophecy is of the future, and the sense is: There shall be a root of Jesse, etc., that is, Jesse shall be called a root, and from that root there shall be one who shall rise up, namely Christ.

QUESTION CCCXXX. *I will set out through you into Spain,* etc. It is asked whether the Apostle was lying, when he said that through Rome he would go into Spain, and it is agreed that then, when he said this, he did not do it. For neither did he then come to Rome. Into Spain perhaps, [0512B] as Jerome thinks, he was carried over by ships: whence it is said, The sea shall be despoiled: which Jerome records was said of Paul alone: for he despoiled the devil of the Spaniards surrounded by the sea, by converting them to Christ. Solution. To lie is, as Gregory says, to speak what is false with the intention of deceiving: which, because Paul did not do, neither do we say that he lied. For he said this because he hoped that he could set out: yet the words which he spoke signified what was false; yet according to intention he did not lie: for he had so disposed to do, because he believed it would be so in fact.

QUESTION CCCXXXI. *According to the revelation of the mystery kept silent from eternal times,* etc. [n: (Rom. XVI.)] [0512C] It is asked how he calls them eternal times, saying, from eternal times. Solution. He calls eternal times all that which preceded the creation of the world from the creation of the angels, as some hold. In which times there was succession, yet not decession. Therefore in a wondrous manner there was there a certain immutability, and thus a certain eternity; and a certain mutability, and thus a certain temporality. Yet almost all say that the angels were not created before the creation of the world. Therefore perhaps he calls them eternal times, as if he said that the mystery was kept silent, and hidden from the origin of the world.

QUESTION CCCXXXII. *To the only wise God,* etc. It is asked whether by this name God is here understood the Trinity, or the person of the Father. Solution. Augustine says [0512D] that this is a name of the Trinity; according to some it is a name of the Father: but according to this it seems that the Father alone is wise, which is against faith; and so it must be known that "alone" is sometimes exclusive of a person, sometimes of nature only.

QUESTION CCCXXXIII. But when it is said, God the Trinity, wisdom, wise, it is asked by what wisdom, whether by the unbegotten, which is the Father; or by the begotten, which is the Son; or by the wisdom proceeding from the Father and the Son, which is the Holy Spirit; or by a wisdom which is neither unbegotten nor begotten, nor proceeding. Solution. This name Trinity signifies as much as the three persons, or the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit: and the Father indeed is wise by the unbegotten wisdom; the Son by the begotten [0513A] wisdom; the Holy Spirit by the wisdom proceeding from the Father, and the Son. And the unbegotten wisdom, and the begotten wisdom, [0514A] and the proceeding, are not three wisdoms, but one wisdom naturally.
