## V. ON THE EPISTLE TO THE EPHESIANS. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ QUESTION XXXVIII. ⟦s2⟧ *Our wrestling is not against flesh.* ⟦s3⟧ Do not the saints fight against flesh and blood, against evil men, or against the vices which arise from flesh and blood? how then does he say: *Our wrestling is not,* etc.? ⟦s4⟧ Solution. ⟦s5⟧ Our wrestling is not only against flesh and blood, but also against demons.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ QUESTION XXXIX. ⟦s2⟧ *Against the rulers of the world.* ⟦s3⟧ Does not God, who founded the world, govern and rule the world, so that the same is founder and ruler? how then does [0576B] the Apostle call the demons rulers of the world? ⟦s4⟧ Solution. ⟦s5⟧ Here by the name of the world he signifies the lovers of the world. ⟦s6⟧ This is the world which is wholly set in wickedness: this is the world in which is found nothing but the concupiscence of the flesh, the concupiscence of the eyes, and the pride of life: this world the Apostle calls darkness, adding *of this darkness,* and elsewhere saying: *You were heretofore darkness; but now light in the Lord [n: (Ephes. V)]*.
