# Cruces — Quaestiones in Epistolas Pauli (PL 175, textIdno 11066)

Garbled type, editorial anomalies, and printer errors logged per translation policy.
**Governed by `translation-style.md` Pattern 7:** the English renders what Migne's
plate PRINTS, never the conjectured reading, and type that is not a word at all is
carried into the English untranslated (in italics in running prose; left standing as
Latin inside an already-italic scripture lemma, where nested italics are impossible).
Conjecture lives here and never in the running text.

*Precedent withdrawn (2026-07-28).* Many entries below previously claimed to have
rendered "the intended reading" — the precedent set in `src/english/10103/cruces.md`
and now superseded. 33 passages have been restored to the printed readings; each
entry records the conjecture and the reading the English carried before.

Structural labels are outside this rule: `QUAESTIP`, `QUESTIO`, `QAEESTIO`,
`QUAESTIO.` etc. are normalized to `QUESTION` by the house label convention, and the
printed form is logged in the entry.
