# Cruces — 11075 (Hugo de S. Victore, *De fructibus carnis et spiritus*)

Garbled or corrupt printed readings in Migne (PL 176), rendered literally per the register rule rather than emended. Column locations follow Migne's plates.

| Column | Printed reading | Note |
|---|---|---|
| capitula (chunk 0000, before col 0997B) | *De fiac, et ejus comitibus* (CAP. XVI title) | *fiac* is corrupt for *fide* ("On faith"); cf. the chapter itself at 1004A ("CAP. XVI. *De fide*"). Rendered as the untranslated nonce word *fiac* in italic, following the "render literally, do not emend" rule. |
| ~0998 (chunk 0000, CAP. II head) | *…virtutum cum virtutibus, viliorum cum vitiis…* | *viliorum* is corrupt for *vitiorum* ("of vices"); cf. the parallel capitula line (0000, before 0997B) which reads *vitiorum* correctly. *viliorum* is itself a real word (gen. pl. of *vilior*, "baser"), so rendered literally as "of baser things with vices." Also present verbatim in the chunk frontmatter `heads` field. |
| 0998 (chunk 0000, CAP. I) | *Fortitudo retinet et reborat* | *reborat* is a non-word, corrupt for *roborat* ("strengthens"); rendered by evident sense ("strengthens"). |
| 1002B (chunk 0001, Blandishments def.) | *cum quadam dulcedine vitioserum delectaminum* | *vitioserum* is corrupt for *vitiosorum* ("of vicious"); rendered by evident sense ("of vicious delights"). |
| 1002D (chunk 0001, Memory def.) | *imaginaria quaedam repraesentatio praeteritorun* | *praeteritorun* is corrupt for *praeteritorum* ("of past things"); rendered by evident sense ("of past things"). |
