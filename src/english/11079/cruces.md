# Cruces — 11079 (Hugh of St Victor, *De potestate et voluntate Dei*)

- **Col 0839C** — Migne prints "Quia dicitis Deum plus quam velle" ("Why? Because you say that God ... more than he wills"). The infinitive **posse** appears to be dropped: the sense the argument requires is *Deum plus posse quam velle* ("that God can do more than he wills"), matching the opening thesis. Translated per that reading ("Because you say that God can do more than he wills").
