# Cruces — 11080 (Hugh of St Victor, De quatuor voluntatibus in Christo)

## Col 0843C (chunk 0000) — likely misprint (sive for sine)
- **Reading (as printed):** `et tamen sive compassionis dolore in certitudine felicitatis persistens [n: (Luc. XVI)], eum per justitiam repulit`
- **Note:** `sive` ("or") gives no clean sense here; the argument (Abraham, in blessedness, feels no compassion-grief for the rich man and repulses him by justice) requires *sine compassionis dolore* ("without the grief of compassion"). A plain misprint (sive/sine). Rendered per sense ("persisting without any grief of compassion in the certitude of happiness"); the Latin itself is not emended.

## Col 0844B (chunk 0000) — stray question mark in a declarative list
- **Reading (as printed):** `Compassio ex vitio, culpabilis est? compassio ex natura, irreprehensibilis; compassio ex virtute laudabilis`
- **Note:** The `?` after `culpabilis est` is anomalous within a three-member declarative list (vice = culpable; nature = irreprehensible; virtue = praiseworthy) — evidently a punctuation slip for a semicolon. Rendered as a declarative clause ("Compassion from vice is culpable;"). Punctuation artifact only; no word affected. Noted for completeness.
