## Flowers, or Sentences, from St. Bernard

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ [1197B] *1569* Chastity is in peril amid delights, humility amid riches, piety amid business, truth amid much speaking, charity in this wicked world. [f: *De convers. ad cler., num.* 37]
