# Cruces — 11299, Auctor incertus, *In parabolam de decem virginibus*

- **cols 1049A → 1050A** — the column sequence jumps from 1049A straight to 1050A within a single unbroken sentence (*quae Petrum post trinam negationem non solum non [1050A] repulit*). Columns 1049B–D are absent from the printed run. Anchors reproduced exactly as found; the gap is Migne's, not a chunking error.
- **col 1046D** — the Sibylline verses at *713* are a paraphrase of the acrostic *Judicii signo* (Augustine, *De civ. Dei* XVIII.23). Rendered as three verse lines matching Migne's lineation; the italics and the inline page number *713* are preserved.
- **col 1043C** — *Sponsae vel sponsi nomen ab eo quod est spondere grammatici derivant.* The etymology is untranslatable without the Latin; the Latin forms are carried in parentheses in the English so the derivation still reads.
- **col 1044B** — *« Palleat omnis amans; »* attributed only to *quidam* (in fact Ovid, *Ars am.* I.729). Left unattributed as printed.
- **col 1045D / 1046A** — *Haec est gloria, sed ab intus, sed invisa, filiae regis et amicae* echoes Psal. XLIV, 14 but Migne gives no `[n:]` here. No citation supplied.
- **col 1044D** — Lot's *Numquid non modicum est, et vivet anima mea?* falls outside the italics in Migne although it continues the Genesis quotation. Italics reproduced exactly as printed.
