## col 0418D (chunk 0004, §13)
- **Printed:** "Haec est effusio in fide vel spe, sed in charitatis tantum plenitudine."

- **Note:** The clause is elliptical; the "sed ... tantum" (but ... only) contrast implies an unexpressed negation ("[not] in faith or hope, but only in the fullness of charity"). Rendered literally, preserving every word without supplying the negative.
