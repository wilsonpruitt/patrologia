## 13.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ There follows: *He who believes in him shall not be confounded.* ⟦s2⟧ To believe God is to give credence to his words. ⟦s3⟧ To believe that God is, is to confess that he is everywhere. ⟦s4⟧ To believe in [1056A] God is to direct all one's hope toward him. ⟦s5⟧ The demons believe that God is, and believe God: but they do not believe in him, in whom whoever believes shall not be confounded; because they do not place their hope in him. ⟦s6⟧ Behold, as much as I have been able, though not as much as I wished, I have discoursed concerning the Sacrament of confession. ⟦s7⟧ But we have drawn out the sermon at length; let us likewise hear the end of speaking. ⟦s8⟧ May he grant us to believe unto justice, to confess with the mouth unto salvation, and to believe in himself — the Bridegroom of the Church, the Son of Mary, who is God blessed forever. ⟦s9⟧ Amen.
