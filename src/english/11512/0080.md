## *282* IN PLACE OF AN EPILOGUE, THE AUTHOR'S PARAENESIS To the sacred virgins for whom he wrote and to whom he inscribed these commentaries. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ This *seed* of his *shall be in many waters,* that is, this [1276C] seed pours forth many waters. ⟦s2⟧ By drinking we have experienced that his *seed* truly is *in many waters.* ⟦s3⟧ *He that believeth in me,* he says, *out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water [n: (Joan. VII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ Again: *He that shall drink of the water that I will give him shall not thirst for ever, but the water that I will give him shall become in him a fountain of water springing up into life everlasting [n: (Joan. IV)]*. ⟦s5⟧ This, I say, we have experienced by drinking of his grace. ⟦s6⟧ For behold, from this bucket, which is Christ himself, we have received many gifts of the Holy Spirit in the one Church, we possess in mind many and most abundant things of the Scriptures.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ In these tabernacles of the Lord, therefore, that new canticle ever resounds which the dwellers of these tabernacles sing with the seven differences of voices, [1276D] the Holy Spirit ruling the song with his sevenfold grace, namely wisdom and understanding, counsel and fortitude, knowledge and piety and the fear of the Lord. ⟦s2⟧ Further, from these seven differences of voices diverse beatitudes come about, as it were eight species of modes; namely humility, meekness, mourning, or three [cj: *tria*; read *tristitia*, "sorrow"] according to the Lord, peace, mercy, cleanness, the desire of justice, patience.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ And this song, or praise of God, is not save in the Church of the saints, because that they are saints, and that they know and are able to do that whereby they are saints, is the gift of the grace of God alone. ⟦s2⟧ And such, going out daily from the Egypt of this world, by contempt of their own will holding the harps of God, that is, the mortification of the flesh, sing not only *a new canticle,* but also [1277A] *the canticle of canticles,* because in this manner they advance to that ineffable charity of God, which is the virtue of virtues, and from which proceeds, and in which is contained, the fullness of all the virtues.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ You therefore, beloved sisters, since you are the tabernacle of God, or dwell in the tabernacles of God, sing together always this new canticle with humility, chastity, patience, obedience, mortification of the flesh, love of God and of neighbour. ⟦s2⟧ Let the justifications [1278A] of the Lord be songs to you in the place of your pilgrimage, [n: (Psal. CXVIII)], that at some time, going out from the Egypt of this age and coming to the land of promise, and eternally [sic: *aeterna liter*] enjoying the good things of that land, which *eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither hath it entered into the heart of man [n: (I Cor. II, Isa. LXIV)]*, you may sing with all sweetness and delightfulness, without intermission, not only the new canticle, but also the canticle of canticles, for ever and ever. ⟦s3⟧ Amen.
