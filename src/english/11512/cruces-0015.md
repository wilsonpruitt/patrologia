## Column notes

- **1053B** — Ps 38:4 printed *exardescit ignis* (present); Vulg. *exardescet* (future). Rendered "blazes out". Tense only, and Wolbero's gloss runs in the present (*tota … ardet*); logged, no `[var:]`.
- **1053C** — *nec quidquam praecedentes partes conferre poterunt. Si haec ultima defuerit, qui enim perseveraverit …*: plate p. 533 sets the full stop after *poterunt*, so the conditional hangs on the quotation. English carries the stop (Pattern 8). Mt 24:13 *enim* for *autem*: trivial.
- **1053C** — *In caudam quippe finis est corporis*: accusative after *in* with *est*, as printed. Rendered "in the tail"; case has no English exponent here, no marker.
- **1054C** — Isa. XXXVI printed *Vivit, inquit, Dominus, cujus ignis est in Sion …*: Vulg. Is 31:9 *dixit Dominus : cujus ignis …*. The oath formula "The Lord liveth" replaces "saith the Lord". `[var:]` fired. Locator *Isa. XXXVI* (for XXXI) as printed.
- **1054D** — Jo 14:2 printed *In domo … Patris mansiones multae sunt*: Vulg. *Patris mei*. Abridgment, no marker. The printed italic is split (*In domo* enim *Patris …*); the English keeps one span round the verse.
- **1055B** — *qui non simultane sed veraciter illum diligant*: plate p. 534 prints **simultane** (our Latin agrees). No such adverb is attested in the sense the antithesis needs (probably *simulate*, "feignedly"). `[sic: *simultane*]`, sense "feignedly" beside it. (If *simultane* is ever shown to be an attested medieval spelling, Pattern 9 would withdraw this marker.)
- **1055B** — Jo 14:23 printed *et Pater meus diligit eum* (present); Vulg. *diliget*. Rendered "loves". Tense only; logged.
- **1055B** — lemma *Ne vagari incipiam*: the *g* of *vagari* is worn on p. 534 (reads like *vaaari*); our *vagari* stands, no marker.
- **1055C** — Lc 2:46 abridged (*sedentem in medio doctorum* omitted, *eum* for *illum*, *audientem* without *illos*): abridgment, no marker.
- **1055C–D** — *Subjecti autem inter se veluti fratres sint ficta charitate junguntur*: *veluti … sint* construes as "as though they were brothers"; rendered so.
- **1055D** — *possit agnoscere*: plate p. 534 sets *po sit* with a gap (uninked *s*); our *possit* taken, no marker.
- **1055D** — ⚑ *… a quibus recte vivendi formam assumere, *43* ut sit ei necesse vagabundam circuire et ignoranter fortasse abire in errores sed alium simulatorum*: plate p. 534 confirms **ut**. The purpose is the one the lemma states (*Ne vagari incipiam*): she asks for discretion so that she need **not** wander. Printed *ut* makes the English assert the reverse. A real word whose faithful English asserts what the author argues against, and the missing force is a negation: `[cj: *ut*; read *ne*, "lest"]` (Pattern 18, negation class).
- **1055D** — same clause: **sed alium** (plate p. 534, our Latin agrees), a broken run with no construction; almost certainly *sodalium*, the word of the lemma just expounded (*post greges sodalium tuorum*). Carried whole, `[sic: *sed alium*]`, sense "companions" beside it.
- **1056A** — the first lemma prints *Si ignoras te, o pulcherrima, inter mulieres?* with a comma after *pulcherrima* and a question mark; the VERS. 7 lemma has neither. Both carried as printed.
- **1056C** — *divina se deferente gratia*: plate confirms *deferente*. Rendered literally, "divine grace betaking itself away" (*se deferre*); *deserente* ("forsaking") may be meant. Crux only.
- **1056D** — *quod tantum ejus naturam Deus diluerit*: plate p. 534 confirms **diluerit** ("washed"), where the sense wants *dilexerit* ("loved"). A real word; rendered "so washed his nature". Crux only (no negation or correlative at stake).
- **1057A** — *sicque ex hoc sui cognitione quasi et cujusdam dici luce dicitur ad desiderandam … justitiam*: plate p. 535 confirms **dici** (for *diei*) and **dicitur** (for *ducitur*, the counterpart of *trahitur* in the next sentence). Neither has a grammatical slot. `[sic: *dici*]` "day", `[sic: *dicitur*]` "is led". *hoc … cognitione*: gender, Pattern 9.
- **1057B** — *quae conquesta fuerat, quae non tantum ab extraneis …*: second *quae* where *quod* is expected; rendered "who had complained, who suffered…", as printed.
- **1057C** — 1 Cor 14:38 / 2 Cor 6:14 conflated and recast (*omnis ignorans ignorabitur*; *nulla participatio luci ad tenebras* for Vulg. *quae societas luci ad tenebras?*): adaptation of two verses into a statement, no marker.
- **1057C** — *nutri et fove notos tuos carnales et luxuriosos*: plate confirms **notos** ("acquaintances"); the parallel at 1058D (*ubi carnalis nutriat motus*) suggests *motus* ("motions"). Real word, rendered "acquaintances". Crux only.
- **1057D** — 1 Jo 2:15 recast (*quia omnis qui diligit mundum non est charitas Patris in illo*): adaptation, no marker. Mt 6:24 and Eph 4:5 agree.
- **1058B** — *omnia enim quae fecit Deus erant valde bona (Gen. II)*: paraphrase of Gn 1:31 (*Viditque Deus cuncta quae fecerat, et erant valde bona*), located *Gen. II*. No marker.
- **1058C** — *quoddam immediatum ostendens*: the dialecticians' "immediate" contraries (no middle term); rendered "showing a certain thing with no mean between".
- **1058C** — our Latin *privatoria actione injustitiae enim malum*; plate p. 535 prints **actione. injustitiae** (full stop, lower-case *i*). English carries the stop and the lower case. TEI patch.
