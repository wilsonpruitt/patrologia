## Column notes

- **1103A** — Ps 136:3–4 and (1103C) Ps 118:54 agree with the Clementine. *in aliena terra* (1103A) is Wolbero's inversion of the verse's *in terra aliena*, in his own sentence: no marker.

- **1103D** — *constituitur tota modulatio, cantici *96* Domini*: the comma after *modulatio* is on the plate; carried.
- **1104A** — *Aliena est terra. Homo terrena semper diligendo, et ob hoc alienus a Deo, atque ideo impotens cantare …*: the second sentence has no finite verb on the plate or in our Latin; carried as a verbless sentence. 1 Cor 2:14 abridged (*autem*, *ea* omitted): no marker.
- **1104B** — *Canticum Domini canticorum*: Wolbero's own play (the "Canticle of the Lord of canticles"), not a quotation; rendered literally. The orator's *officium … finis* formula is Augustine's (*De doctr. christ.* IV), unmarked in the text.
- **1104C** — *Sed ut quidam fuit, liceat sperare timenti*: plate p. 558 confirms **fuit**; *ait* ("as a certain one said") is the obvious sense, introducing a tag. A real word; rendered "as a certain one was". Crux only.
- **1104D** — the lemma re-quoted *laeva ejus sub capite meo et dextera illius amplexabitur me?* with a question mark (it closes a question, *Nonne illa est …*): carried.
- **1105A** — *exoccupata*, *constrata*: plate as our Latin; rendered "disengaged", "laid down". Ps 45:11 agrees.
- **1105C** — *sed adhuc praepudiente mentis infirmitate*: plate p. 559 **praepudiente**, a non-word (for *praepediente*, "hindering"). `[sic: *praepudiente*]`, sense "hindering" beside it. Is 18:4 agrees.
- **1105D** — *Quid autem dilecti est adjuvare, nisi praeceptorum suorum decretali conscripto obligare?*: plate confirms **adjuvare** ("to help"), where the lemma's verb is *adjurare* ("to adjure"), which *obligare* glosses. A real word; rendered "to help". Crux only.
- **1105D** — *Quatuor enim naturae notabilia dicitur habere capreas*: accusative *capreas* as printed; the four marks then run in the singular (*cum vulnerata fuerit*). Rendered "it is said that roes have … that when she has been wounded".
- **1106C** — *Alter alterius onera portate et sic …*: plate p. 559 (and B p. 557) shows a raised spacing quad after *portate*, not a point; no punctuation carried. Gal 6:2 agrees.
- **1106C** — *donec ipsa vetit*: plate p. 559 prints **velit**, clean (B agrees). TEI patch below; English follows the plate ("till she herself please").
- **1106C** — *necessariis utilitatibus*: both copies set *necessa iis* with a gap and no *r* (a dropped sort); our *necessariis* taken, no marker (same class as T04's *po sit*).
- **1106D, 1106D–1107A** — *hujus admirationis talis est vis* and *hujus admirationis verbum*: plate confirms **admirationis** both times, where the passage is about the *adjuratio* of the lemma. A real word; rendered "admiration". Crux only. The same slip recurs at 1107B (*praedictae admirationis*).
- **1106D** — *Inde est quod non audent eos suscitare quasi vicarios Christi officio, sponsus vero sanctae conversationis studio*: plate confirms **sponsus**, a nominative singular in a slot that needs an accusative plural parallel to *vicarios* (*sponsos*). No grammatical slot: `[sic: *sponsus*]`, sense "as spouses" beside it. Hbr 13:17 printed with *reddituri* moved before *pro animabus vestris* and the rest of the verse omitted: no marker.
- **1107A** — our Latin *dilecta semper flagrans, confirmari et defendi tali adjuramento*; plate p. 560 prints **dilecta semper flagrans, gaudeat se confirmari et defendi** (two words our Latin lacks, without which *confirmari* has no governing verb). English follows the plate ("may rejoice that she is confirmed"). The sentence then ends *securior facta proclamat*, so it has two finite verbs; rendered as printed. TEI patch.
- **1107A–B** — *juridicialis* (1107B) is the attested rhetorical term (Cicero); rendered "juridical", no marker. Mt 6:33 printed *Primum quaerite* (Vulg. *Quaerite ergo primum*): no marker.
- **1107B–C** — our Latin *hujus mei, comparem studii*; plate p. 560 **hujus mei comparem studii** (no comma). The comma is ours; English "the partner of this my pursuit". TEI patch. Lc 10:42 agrees.
- **1107D** — *incirconscriptus*: medieval spelling, Pattern 9. *motabilitatem* rendered "movableness" (a late form of *mutabilitas*/*mobilitas*; not marked). *per inspirationem suam venire afferetur*: *afferetur* rendered "alleged" (*affero*, "to allege"), perhaps for *asseretur*. The question *quomodo venire diceretur …* closes with a full stop on the plate; carried.
- **1108A** — Phil 2:8 printed *factus est enim obediens Deo Patri usque ad mortem, mortem autem crucis* (Vulg. *Humiliavit semetipsum factus obediens usque ad mortem*): *Deo Patri* added; adaptation, no marker.
