## Standing renderings (for merge-time reconciliation)

- Following the peers: *dilectus / dilecta* = the beloved; *filiae Jerusalem* = daughters of Jerusalem ("O" only where Migne prints *o*); *sorores dilectissimae / dilectae* = most beloved / beloved sisters, addressed as "you" (plural); the bride and the soul are "thou" by number (Pattern 17); *Scriptura sacra / sancta* = Holy Scripture (Pattern 3), but *sana Scriptura* = "sound Scripture"; *sponsus* lower-case = bridegroom (1244A, 1249D, 1250D, 1255B *sponsa* = bride); VERSE n.--; Cologne numbers *247*–*267* verbatim.
- Introduced here: *vinum conditum* = spiced wine; *mustum malorum granatorum* = new wine of pomegranates; *laeva / dextera* = left hand / right hand; *canticum novum* = a new canticle; *Canticum canticorum* = the Canticle of Canticles; *cythara* = harp; *tetrachordum* = tetrachord; *deliciis affluens* = flowing with delights; *innixa super dilectum* = leaning upon the beloved; *signaculum* = seal; *aemulatio* = emulation; *propugnacula argentea* = bulwarks of silver; *tabulae cedrinae* = boards of cedar; *pacificus* = the peaceable one; *custodes* = keepers (Ct 8:11) and *speculatores* = watchmen (Ezekiel).
- **Heads:** *\*247\* CAPUT VIII.* = `*247* CHAPTER VIII.`; *LIBER QUARTUS.* = `BOOK FOUR.` (after the peers' BOOK ONE / TWO / THREE).
