# Cruces — 11534, Richard of St Victor, *De differentia sacrificii Abrahae a sacrificio B. Mariae Virginis* (PL 196)

Garbled, doubtful, or impossible readings in Migne's plate, logged here. Where
the printed reading is intelligible as it stands it has been rendered literally;
where it is a pure orthographic slip that yields no Latin at all, the intended
reading has been rendered and the printed form recorded here (the convention
followed in `src/english/10103/cruces.md`). Note contents (`[n: …]`) always pass
through verbatim and untranslated, so citation errors are flagged here only.

- **col. 1046B (chunk 0000)** — `quid aliud in Dei auribus quam mors pullorum
  de nido turturum clamitat?` The parallel sentence immediately following reads
  `quid aliud quam pullorum **more** voce columbina clamat?`, which makes
  *mors* almost certainly a misprint for *mos* (or for *more*, "after the
  manner of"). Rendered **literally** ("the death of nestlings from the nest of
  turtledoves"), since the printed reading is grammatical; the sense the
  parallel demands is "the manner of nestlings."

- **col. 1047A (chunk 0001)** — `non patietur nos tentari supra id quod possumus
  **ferro**`. *ferro* ("with iron/the sword") is impossible here; the reading is
  *ferre* ("to bear"), as I Cor. 10:13 requires. Rendered per the intended
  reading, "above that which we are able to bear."

- **col. 1046D (chunk 0001)** — `saepe **non novelli** eorum qui ejusmodi sunt`.
  Almost certainly *nonnulli* ("some") wrongly divided into *non novelli*.
  Rendered per the intended reading, "some of those who are of this sort."

- **col. 1047B (chunk 0001)** — `illud pertinet ad par turturum, istud ad pullos
  **corum**` for *eorum* (dropped **e**); the very next sentence prints *eorum*
  correctly. Rendered per the intended reading, "their young."

- **col. 1046D–1047A (chunk 0001)** — `…ultra modum trepidare Certum namque…`
  Migne prints no full stop after *trepidare*; sentence division supplied in the
  English, none in the Latin.

- **col. 1048B (chunk 0001), note** — `[n: (Sap. VIII)]` on *Corpus enim quod
  corrumpitur aggravat animam…* = Wisdom **9**:15, not 8. Note passes through
  verbatim; flagged so the index does not treat it as a real locus. The
  paragraph also ends with no terminal punctuation in Migne; reproduced as
  printed.

- **col. 1050C (chunk 0002)** — `illud **Eliphat** Themanites` for *Eliphaz*
  (Job 4:13). Rendered as printed, "Eliphat the Themanite."

- **col. 1050D (chunk 0002)** — `*Si dixero. Consolabitur me lectulus meus…*`
  The full stop after *Si dixero* is Migne's and breaks the conditional
  (Job 7:13). Reproduced as printed.

- **col. 1050D (chunk 0002), note** — `[n: (Job XVII)]` on *Si dixero:
  consolabitur me lectulus meus…* = Job **7**:13–14, not 17. Verbatim; flagged.

- **col. 1051B (chunk 0002)** — `qui egrediens de terra et de cognatione sua,
  diu **juxta** Abrahae exemplar, **juxta** tandem aliquando in modum David in
  aliquod flagitium cadit`. The second *juxta* is dittography, and the relative
  clause has lost its main verb (something like *vixit* after *exemplar*).
  Rendered literally, stumble and all; a human read-through may want to record
  the intended sense ("who … long lived after the exemplar of Abraham, but at
  length falls, after the manner of David, into some outrage") in an apparatus.

- **col. 1051C (chunk 0002), note** — `[n: (Luc. XI)]` on the Pharisee's *Non
  sum sicut caeteri hominum* = Luke **18**:11. Verbatim; flagged.

- **col. 1051D (chunk 0002)** — `quilibet praedestinati ad **viam** aeternam`.
  *vitam* is the expected reading ("predestined to eternal life"), but *via
  aeterna* is defensible Victorine idiom; rendered as printed, "predestined to
  the eternal way."

- **col. 1052A (chunk 0002)** — the four degrees are numbered `primo … secundo`
  in the consideration of evil, but the *primo* is missing from the parallel
  consideration of good (`ut cognoscamus … secundo ut cognoscamus`). Reproduced
  as printed.

- **col. 1053C (chunk 0003), note** — `[n: (Rom. VI)]` on *Scimus quoniam
  diligentibus Deum omnia cooperantur in bonum* = Rom. **8**:28. Verbatim;
  flagged.

- **col. 1054A (chunk 0003)** — `Ecce, **homines** formam defectus humani`.
  The parallel a few lines later reads `**Habes** hic item formam defectus
  humani`, so *homines* is very likely a misprint for *habes*. Rendered as
  printed with *homines* taken as a vocative ("Behold, O men, the form of human
  defect"); the intended reading is "Behold, you have the form…".

- **col. 1053D (chunk 0003)** — `illam humilitatis **revelatam** quam superius
  per quatuor gradus distinximus`. The governing noun has dropped out (the four
  degrees were of *cognitio humanae infirmitatis* at 1052A), leaving a bare
  participle. Rendered as printed, "that revealed humility."

- **col. 1054A (chunk 0003)** — `et semel dejectum ad status rectitudinem propria
  virtute roboratur`. As printed the clause asserts the opposite of the argument
  (which is that fallen man cannot rise by his own strength); a negative
  (*nec*/*nunquam*) has evidently dropped out, and *dejectum* is an accusative
  where a nominative is required. Rendered literally as printed.

- **col. 1055A (chunk 0003)** — `et velut per speculum et in **aenigmat
  evidere**` — the word division has slipped: read *in aenigmate videre*
  (I Cor. 13:12). Rendered per the intended reading.

- **col. 1055A (chunk 0003)** — `a mundanis **insuper mundana** transiens`;
  read probably *ad supermundana* ("passing from worldly things to things above
  the world"). Rendered close to the printed text ("passing from worldly things
  and above worldly things").

- **col. 1055C (chunk 0003)** — `sed paulatim **invalescere** prava consuetudine
  ad hoc usque perducitur` — an infinitive where an ablative absolute
  (*invalescente prava consuetudine*) is required. Rendered per the intended
  construction.

- **col. 1057B–C (chunk 0004)** — `et charitas … quantumcunque profecerit bene
  **examinatus** diligenter **inspectus**` — masculine participles agreeing with
  the feminine *charitas*. Rendered per the sense ("when well examined and
  carefully inspected").

- **col. 1056A (chunk 0004)** — `Jucunditas [securitas] securae conscientiae`.
  Migne prints the variant in square brackets inside the text. Passed through
  **verbatim and untranslated**, on the same footing as `[ *al.,* …]` variants;
  the lemma *Jucunditas* is what is translated.

- **col. 1059A → 1060A (chunk 0004)** — the column sequence jumps from 1059A
  straight to 1060A; there are no 1059B–D anchors in the TEI, and the work's
  last sentence straddles the jump. Reproduced as printed; not an error on our
  side.
