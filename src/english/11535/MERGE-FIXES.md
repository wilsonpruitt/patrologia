### ⚑ A third plate habit, alongside *regnum coelorum* and *de monte*

**Migne's `[n:]` citations name the wrong book, repeatedly and reproducibly.** `[n: (Ephes. I)]`
heads non-Ephesians words **twice within three chunks** (Phil. 2:13 at 1362C, then 1 Cor. 12);
round 1 logged three more (Dan. I for Dan. 2:41, Job XXII for Job 30:7, 1 Cor. XII for 2 Cor.
12:7). All are reproduced verbatim per the sacred-marker rule. ⚠ **This has an INDEX consequence**
— `index-work.mjs` keys scripture citations off these notes, so the work will file real citations
under wrong books. Check `unparsed` and spot-check the resulting index before shipping.

### ⚑ The plate is reliable when it QUOTES and unreliable when it RE-QUOTES

Measured on the Daniel 4 tree lemma: the lemma at 1311D agrees with the Vulgate **word for word,
all nine clauses** — while its re-quotations diverge at 1311B (*solutiones*), 1312D (*arbor magna*
pulled forward from the next verse, **and the gloss built on it**), 1319B (*vigil sanctus*, the
*et* dropped, one subject where two are needed) and 1319D (*Praecidite* for *Succidite*, where
cap. XXII's scheme depends on the two verbs staying apart). Book I showed the same shape.
**Give the polarity read the re-quotations first.**
