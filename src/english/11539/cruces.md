# Cruces — 11539 (Richard of St Victor, *De judiciaria potestate in finali et universali judicio*)

Garbled or corrupt printed readings rendered literally per the translation register, logged here with column, printed reading, and note.

- **col 1179C** — printed *altereatione* ("Non erit itaque illud judicium in altereatione sermonum"). Misprint for *altercatione*; rendered as "altercation" (of words).
- **col 1182A** — printed *canorum* ("quasi canorum decreta"). Misprint for *canonum*, as confirmed by the parallel *quasi canonum decretis* at col 1182B; rendered "decrees of canons."
- **col 1183A** — printed *Verbum itaque cordis sententia intelligentias.* The clause does not construe: *intelligentias* (acc. pl.) has no governing verb, and the sense requires something like *sententia intelligentiae [est]*. Rendered "The word of the heart, therefore, is the judgment of the understanding."
- **col 1183D** — printed *siggillatim* ("siggillatim et succedenter respondere"). Doubled-consonant misprint for *sigillatim*; rendered "one by one."

Note: the Latin as printed skips col 1178A–B (text runs 1177D → 1178C) and col 1181... continuous; column anchors reproduced exactly as printed.
