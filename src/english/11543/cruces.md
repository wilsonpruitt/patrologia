# Cruces — 11543 (Richard of St Victor, *De spiritu blasphemiae*)

Garbled/irregular printed readings in Migne PL 196, logged during translation.

## Chunk 0001

- **Col 1190A** — reading: `Nam de districtiss ma pro eadem blasphemia aliquid remittitur`.
  Migne prints `districtiss ma` with a space fracturing the word; the intended word is
  *districtissima* (fem., agreeing with an understood *expiatione* — cf. the phrase
  *de plenitudine districtissimae expiationis* a few lines below at 1190B, and
  *districtissimae damnationis* following). Rendered per sense as "the most strict
  expiation"; the fracture is a typographic break, not a genuine variant.

## Note anomaly (not garbled type, recorded for reference)

- **Col 1190C** — the `[n: (I Cor. XXIII)]` marker cites 1 Corinthians ch. XXIII for
  *Charitas nunquam excidit* (in fact 1 Cor. 13:8). Migne's printed chapter number is
  erroneous, but `[n: ...]` markers pass through verbatim per the marker rule, so it is
  reproduced unchanged in the English chunk and merely noted here.
