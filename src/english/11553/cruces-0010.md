## Carried type (Patterns 7 / 10 / 12 — `[sic: …]` in the English)

### 0559D–0560A (chunk 0010) — *habenta* for *habentia*

*Et mensus est fenestras ejus, et vestibulum ejus in circuitu **habenta** L cubitos
longitudinis…* Not a word. **Decisive internal evidence:** Migne prints the correct
*habentia* two lines earlier in the very sentence that sets up this re-quotation
(*in sequentibus, **habentia**, vel aliquid ejusmodi subaudiendum est*), so the plate
supplies its own conjecture. Carried untranslated in italics, `[sic: *habenta*]`.
Conjecture: *habentia*, certain.

### 0563A (chunk 0011) — *demidii* for *dimidii*

*…longitudine cubiti unius et **demidii**, et latitudine cubiti unius et **dimidii**…*
The same word is printed both ways inside one lemma, four words apart. Carried
untranslated in italics, `[sic: *demidii*]`.

**Pattern 9 was considered and rejected here.** *demidius* could be argued as an
e/i orthographic variant and so translated normally; but the plate's own correct
*dimidii* in the same breath makes this a compositor's slip on this plate, not a
period convention — which is precisely the boundary Pattern 9 draws
("reserve carry-through for forms unique to this plate"). Carried.

### 0564C (chunk 0011) — *in fallor* for *ni fallor*

*…ipsumque, **in fallor**, in his verbis describitur…* The parenthesis is Richard's
standing *ni fallor*, "unless I am mistaken"; *in fallor* will not construe at all
(*in* + a first-person passive verb). Per Pattern 10 the **whole run is carried**,
including the real word *in*, since the defect is the pair and not either token:
`[sic: *in fallor*]`. Conjecture: *ni fallor*, certain.

### 0565A (chunk 0011) — *contracuntem* for *contra euntem*

*…sicut australem viam illa quae ab Occidente erant **contracuntem** habebant.*
Run-together type of the kind Pattern 10 names, with a letter lost at the join.
Carried untranslated in italics, `[sic: *contracuntem*]`. Conjecture: *contra euntem*
("running over against them"), which is what the immediately preceding clause has been
arguing with *contra* three times (*non contra viam Aquilonis … et non contra ea*).

### 0566A (chunk 0012) — *totitem* for *totidem*

*…ejusdemque longitudinis atrium includebant, **totitem**que gradibus assurgebant…*
Not a word. Carried untranslated in italics, `[sic: *totitem*]`. Conjecture: *totidem*,
certain — Richard's point is that the eastern porticoes rose by *as many* steps as the
others, the eight steps of 0560C.

### 0566B (chunk 0012) — *Occiente* for *Occidente*

*…quid tamen vel quantum de illo medio centenario ab **Occiente** … tumor montis
detraxerit…* A dropped *d*; not a word. Carried untranslated in italics,
`[sic: *Occiente*]`. Conjecture: *Occidente*, certain — the plate prints *Occidente*
correctly twice within twenty lines (0566B *versus Occidentem*, 0568C *ab Occidente in
Orientem*).

### 0566D (chunk 0013) — *Scriturae* for *Scripturae*

*Libet hic, sicut et supra, … **Scriturae** testimonia in unum colligere…* A dropped *p*;
not a word. Carried untranslated in italics, `[sic: *Scriturae*]`. Conjecture:
*Scripturae*, certain — the word is printed correctly some forty times in these four chunks.

### 0569D (chunk 0013) — *darietemque* for *parietemque*

*Post quem parietem si porticus aquilonaris domus incoepit, **darietemque** post parietem

`[sic: *darietemque*]`. Conjecture: *parietemque*, certain on the sentence's own evidence —
*parietem* stands correctly twice in the same clause, before and after it.

## The numeral that propagates — 0560B (chunk 0010)

**This is the finding the range was opened for.** `cruces-0005.md` records that at 0559A —
three lines before this chunk begins — Migne prints *et fecit frontes per **quadraginta**
cubitos* where the same lemma is printed *sexaginta* at 0554C and *LX* at 0555C, and where
Richard's own arithmetic (XXV + XXV + X = LX, 0554A–B) requires sixty.

**The corrupt figure recurs at 0560B, inside Richard's own prose:**

> *Per istud vestibulum viginti quinque cubitorum longitudinis facile probari poterit, quod
> mensura illa frontium **quadraginta** cubitorum intra parietes sumpta fuerit.*

The inference is that the twenty-five-cubit porch proves the fronts-measure to be an
**interior** measure. It works only with sixty: the front is composed of two porches of
twenty-five and a middle span of ten, so 25 + 25 + 10 = 60, and the twenty-five being an
inside figure makes the sixty an inside figure. With forty the composition is impossible —
two porches of twenty-five will not fit inside forty at all, and the sentence destroys the
argument it is offered as proving.

So the defect is **not isolated to 0559A**: it appears twice within two columns, once in a
lemma and once in the commentary that reasons from it. That is worth recording, because a
single occurrence looks like a compositor's slip and two consecutive ones raise the
possibility of a corrupt exemplar behind this stint of the plate.

**Rendered as printed** — "that measure of the fronts of forty cubits was taken within the
walls" — so that the figure and the arithmetic disagree on the page. No marker: *quadraginta*
is a real word doing the wrong work (Pattern 12's boundary). Conjecture: *sexaginta*,
effectively certain.

## Readings rendered literally though they will not construe

### 0565C (chunk 0012) — *utrum utrobique*

*…et idcirco duobus porticibus interioris atrii hinc inde **utrum utrobique** simile spatium
in coquinarum officium relinquimus.* Two adjacent forms of *uter* with nothing to govern
*utrum*, in a sentence that already carries *hinc inde*. The sense wanted is "to each of the
two, on both sides." Rendered as closely as the printed words allow — "on this side and on
that, to either on both sides" — which is redundant English because the Latin is redundant
Latin. Both words are real, so no `[sic: …]`. No confident conjecture is offered
(*utrique utrobique* is the likeliest, but the 9604 boundary applies: the run is corrupt
past secure restoration).

### 0566D (chunk 0013) — the chapter head, *De quantitate vel aedificiorum*

`CAPUT XIII. *De quantitate vel aedificiorum quae templo adjacebant.*` The *vel* has no
second member: a noun has dropped out after it (*vel qualitate* is the obvious supplement,
and Richard's chapter does treat both size and disposition). **Rendered as printed** —
"On the size or of the buildings which lay adjacent to the temple" — which is broken English
because the head is broken Latin. No marker; the words are real. This is a **head**, so it is
also a sacred marker: the `## ` line stands 1:1 with the Latin twin and was not repaired.

### 0563C–0564B (chunk 0011) — *extra portam interiorem* re-quoted as *contra portam interiorem*

Migne prints the lemma (Ezek. 40:44) at 0563C as *Et **extra** portam interiorem gazophylacia
cantorum in atrio interiori*, and Richard re-quotes his own lemma at 0564B as *et **contra**
portam interiorem gazophylacia cantorum in atrio interiori*. The plate contradicts itself
between a lemma and its own re-quotation half a column apart — the same shape of defect as
0584A–B (*satius* / *latius*) in `cruces-0014.md`, though here nothing in the argument turns
on it, since Richard's whole discussion is about *in atrio interiori* and not about the
preposition. **Both are rendered as printed** ("outside the inner gate" / "over against the
inner gate"). No `[var: …]`: the divergence is internal to Migne, not a divergence from the
received text, and Pattern 14 exists for the latter.

### 0567D (chunk 0013) — *a terrae cubitis quinquaginta* — the existing `[var: …]` endorsed

The stint left a `[var: Vulg. *a terra cubitis quinquaginta*, "from the ground, fifty cubits"]`
against Migne's *eminebant de inferioribus et de mediis aedificii **a terrae** cubitis
quinquaginta* (Ezek. 42:6), rendering the printed genitive literally as "by fifty cubits of
the earth." **Checked and kept.** The rendering is faithful to what is printed, the note names
its witness as Pattern 14 requires, and its content is not a verbatim substring of the Latin
twin (which prints *terrae*, not *terra*), so the verifier's mirror test is satisfied. It is
the only `[var: …]` in the four chunks.

## Numerals — every sum verified, and stated as results

This work has a confirmed corrupt measurement at 0559A and a second occurrence of it at
0560B (above), so every figure in these four chunks was checked against Richard's own
reckoning rather than spot-checked. **Apart from the 0560B *quadraginta*, all of them close,
and several close exactly.**

- **0559D–0561B (chunk 0010)** — the three inner gates: each *fifty* long by *twenty-five*
  broad, printed three times over (south, east, north) with no variance. The porch is 25 long
  × 5 broad; three little chambers a side leave **two** five-cubit spaces a side, which is
  what the plate says (*inter tres thalamos duo in latere uno, et duo in latere alio*).
  Eight steps to the inner gates against the outer gates' seven. **Consistent.**
- **0560D (chunk 0010)** — one portico divides into three porches, of which the two greater
  subdivide into fourteen. No sum to close; no conflict with the divisions of Capp. V–IX.
- **0562C–0563B (chunk 0011)** — the tables. Four within the porch (two and two) and four
  without (two and two) = **eight along the sides of the gate**, which is the figure the lemma
  prints and the figure chunk 0010 annexed to all three inner gates at 0561C. Richard's own
  breakdown — *four at the one side, two within and two outside; likewise two within and two
  without at the other* — gives 4 + 4 = 8. **Exact.** Table dimensions 1½ × 1½ × 1 cubit,
  lips of one palm. **Consistent.**
- **0565D–0566A (chunk 0012)** — **the cleanest computation in the batch.** From the fronts of
  the inner gates to the place of the common people is *a hundred and forty* cubits; five go to
  the wall; the rest is divided equally among *fifteen* chambers; Richard concludes **nine**
  cubits each. (140 − 5) ÷ 15 = **9 exactly.** Fifteen a side gives the *thirty round about the
  pavement* of the lemma. **Exact throughout.**
- **0565D (chunk 0012)** — chambers of the singers ranged at fifty by fifty, matching the
  scriptural *longitudo … quinquaginta cubitorum* which Richard quotes at 0567C. **Consistent.**
- **0566A (chunk 0012)** — inner court 100 × 100 foursquare, sitting as the middle hundred of
  the five hundred cubits enclosed north-to-south. **Consistent** with the 500-cubit wall of
  0596D and the 100-cubit court of 0573C in the peer chunks.
- **0568A (chunk 0013)** — **215 = 145 + 70, exactly.** From the inner court's portico plus
  five cubits of wall thickness to the place of the common people is *two hundred and fifteen*;
  the house with its portico runs *a hundred and forty-five* west; *seventy* remain over.
  And the 145 is itself derived correctly: house 100 + portico 50 = 150, less 5 for the common
  wall = **145**, which is why Richard says "not a hundred and fifty, but only a hundred and
  forty-five." **Both stages exact.**
- **0568B–C (chunk 0013)** — the separate building: 100 × 80 overall, 90 × 70 within the walls,
  wall five cubits round about. 90 + 5 + 5 = **100**; 70 + 5 + 5 = **80**. **Exact both ways**,
  and both totals are the figures the lemma prints.
- **0568D (chunk 0013)** — **140 = 20 + 100 + 20, exactly.** The space enclosed between the two
  adjacent buildings is a hundred and forty; the separate building's length of a hundred lies
  between them and the twenty-cubit interval of the lemma is added at either end.
- **0570B (chunk 0013)** — 30 + 20 = **50.** Fifty cubits from the chamber's eastern side to
  the northern building; the chamber can hold no more than thirty in breadth, because twenty
  must remain over to satisfy *inter gazophylacia latitudinem viginti cubitorum*. **Exact.**
- **0570B–C (chunk 0013)** — **220 = 30 + 100 + 80 + 5 + 5, exactly.** From the chamber's
  eastern side to the boundary of the five-hundred-cubit sanctified space is *two hundred and
  twenty*: chamber 30, court 100, separate building 80, then ten remaining of which five is
  wall and five a way. Richard says the numbers "rise to two hundred and twenty," and they do.
- **0570C–D (chunk 0013)** — 130 − 100 = **30.** Chamber (30) plus the court set before it
  (100) reach a hundred and thirty to the separate building; the temple with its porch holds a
  hundred; the interval is thirty. **Exact.**

### The one arithmetical tension worth recording

- **0569A–B (chunk 0013)** — the derived length of the singers' chamber. The printed chain is:
  from the northern side of the portico to the inner court, **100**; of which the portico's
  breadth holds **50**; a walk of **10** interposed; then the chamber begins and extends to the
  **twentieth** cubit of the inner court; *"whence it is agreed that its length was of **fifty**
  cubits."* Worked through, 100 − 50 − 10 = 40 to the court, plus 20 into it = **60**, not 50.
  The stated fifty follows if the chamber reaches the **tenth** cubit rather than the twentieth
  (50 + 10 + 50 = 110 = 100 + 10) — and 0569C prints *si a **decimo** cubito post illius aedis
  porticum incipiebat*, which is the same ten-cubit walk counted from the other end. Against
  that, the twenty is a scriptural constant (*contra viginti cubitos atrii interioris*) and
  Richard repeats it at 0569C.

  **Every figure is rendered as printed and none is altered.** Recorded because it is the only
  place in these four chunks where Richard's own figures do not close, and because it is not
  reducible to the 0560B defect.

- **Considered and rejected as a defect:** the singers' chambers are ranged at fifty **broad**
  at 0565D (chunk 0012) but the northern one can be at most thirty broad at 0570B (chunk 0013).
  These are different chambers in different quarters — 0565D ranges the fifteen-a-side ranks of
  the eastern, northern and southern ways, 0570B measures the single chamber beside the
  northern house's portico — and Richard derives the thirty from the fifty-cubit interval, not
  from the ranked chambers. No conflict; no crux.

## Negation fidelity (Pattern 7a) — read clause by clause

Every printed `non` / `nec` / `nisi` / `ne` / `nihil` / `nullus` / `nunquam` / `nusquam` /
`nondum` / `necdum` / `neminem` / `nemo` in cols 0559C–0570D is carried into the English, and
none is added. The negation-dense passages, which is where the PL 196 defect class lives:

- **0560A (chunk 0010)** — *__Nec__ illud debet praeteriri* … *Si autem vestibuli, __non__
  vestibulorum legitur* … *ab Hieronymo __non__ vestibuli, sed vestibulorum ponitur*. All three
  carried; the second and third are load-bearing, since the whole paragraph turns on which of
  two readings is printed.
- **0560B (chunk 0010)** — *quod in praecedentibus __non__ dixerat* … *superius __nusquam__
  descripserat* … *__nisi__ inter thalamos fuit? … __Nec__ unum tantummodo*. All carried.
- **0561A (chunk 0010)** — *cujus utique omnino dici __non__ debuisset, si … jacuisset*.
  Carried; dropping it would reverse the concession the sentence exists to make.
- **0561C (chunk 0010)** — *in frontibus portarum __non__ erit* … *__nullam__ de eis mentionem
  facit* … *__nisi__ ut per hoc idem de omnibus intelligi daretur*. All carried.
- **0561C–D (chunk 0010)** — *quamvis Scriptura hoc __non__ videatur aperte determinare* …
  *quae ratio … latere __non__ sinit*. Both carried.
- **0561D–0562A (chunk 0010)** — the four rhetorical questions: *utrum ratio patiatur
  orientalem portam __non__ habere … An aquilonarem portam __non__ habere … __Nunquid__ …
  __Nunquidnam__ horum __neutrum__ … quia … mentio __nulla__ facta fuit?* Six negations in one
  run, all carried, and all four question marks stand.
- **0562A (chunk 0010)** — *quae juxta litteram stare __non__ possent* … *__Neminem__ ergo
  scandalizet* … *__Nemo__ dedignetur … __nemo__ miretur*. All carried.
- **0562B (chunk 0010)** — *sed __nunquam__ verius honoramus … Attende ergo __non__ utrum dicam
  aliquid novum, sed verum … dum tamen __nihil__ dicam perversum*. All three carried; the last
  is the epigram the paragraph closes on and it survives intact.
- **0562D–0563A (chunk 0011)** — *in diebus __non__ festis* … *haec porta (__nec__ mirum)*.
  Both carried; *nec mirum* is rendered "and no wonder," which keeps a negation on the page.
- **0563B (chunk 0011)** — *expositione __non__ indigent*. Carried.
- **0563C–D (chunk 0011)** — *Atrium namque interius __non__ erat in latere hujus portae* …
  *__non__ uniformiter hoc semper accipere debemus* … *__nihil__ aliud erat __nisi__ unum
  altare*. All carried. The first is the load-bearing one: it is the objection the whole
  chapter answers, and without it the chapter has no occasion.
- **0563D–0564A (chunk 0011)** — *quas … efferre __non__ licebat* … *ut __non__ efferant in
  atrium exterius* … *quae in exterius atrium efferri __prohibebantur__*. All carried.
- **0564A (chunk 0011)** — *__non__ quod intra ipsam sit, sed quod secretiorem locum teneat* …
  *__nullus__ locus secretior et ab aditu portarum __nullus__ remotior*. All three carried.
- **0564B (chunk 0011)** — *__Nihil__ itaque prohibet illum locum intelligere*. Carried.
- **0564D (chunk 0011)** — *si dictum fuisset, quorumdam facies, __non__ quaedam facies* …
  *__non solum__ ibi una … ponitur, sed etiam*. Both carried.
- **0565A (chunk 0011)** — *__non__ contra viam Aquilonis faciem habebant … et __non__ contra
  ea*. Both carried, and they are the pair the sentence's whole distinction rests on.
- **0566B (chunk 0012)** — *__nondum__ ex praedictis puto posse probare* … *__necdum__ patet*
  … *in eumdem cathetum __non__ concurrunt, __nec__ … aequale spatium includunt*. All four
  carried. The *nondum*/*necdum* pair matters: it is Richard deferring the proof, and either
  one dropped would turn a postponement into an assertion.
- **0566C (chunk 0012)** — *De hujus mensura __nihil__ hic dicitur*. Carried.
- **0567A (chunk 0013)** — *ex eo quod __nulli__ caeterorum cohaesit*. Carried — it is the
  etymology of *separatum* and the reason for the building's name.
- **0567D (chunk 0013)** — *Nam frustra ejus porticus quinquaginta cubitis a terra emineret,
  __nisi__ et coenaculum post se haberet*. Carried; the argument is a reductio and collapses
  without it.
- **0568B (chunk 0013)** — *sed __nondum__ constat quantum intercapedinis includebant*.
  Carried.
- **0569A (chunk 0013)** — *__Non__ igitur contra hujus atrii latitudinem quin potius contra
  domum dicta gazophylacia introitum haberent __nisi__ porticus … ex uno loco inciperet.*
  **Both carried, and this is the one to watch in the batch**: the sentence is a contrary-to-fact
  conditional whose entire force is the *non … nisi* pair, and it is exactly the shape that
  invites a translator to smooth one of them away. The English keeps both ("would have their
  entry **not** over against the breadth of this court … **unless** the portico … began from
  one place").
- **0569C (chunk 0013)** — *__ne__ aliud quam aquilonare pavimentum … intelligere possimus* …
  *__nusquam__ invenimus porticum junctam porticui*. Both carried.
- **0570A (chunk 0013)** — *__Nec__ nos movere debet quod idem aedificium, modo gazophylacia,
  modo gazophylacium dicitur*. Carried.
- **0570B (chunk 0013)** — *plus quam triginta cubitos latitudinis habere __non__ poterat*.
  Carried, including the comparative — this is the *non plus quam* class flagged at 11088
  @0896C, and the figure it governs is load-bearing (see the 30 + 20 = 50 above).

**No dropped negative, no intrusive negative, and no comparative reversal was found in chunks
0010–0013.** The 0553A–B site recorded in `cruces-0005.md` has no counterpart in this range;
the two places where the plate defeats its own argument here (0560B *quadraginta*, 0566D
*Scriturae*) are a numeral and broken type, not polarity.

**Also checked for the other three mechanisms of Pattern 7a:**
- *No opposite-word substitution.* Every word in the four chunks that could have been quietly
  flipped to rescue a sentence was read against the Latin; the two places where a translator
  would be tempted (0560B *quadraginta*, which breaks the arithmetic it proves, and 0566D's
  broken chapter head) both stand as printed.
- *No smoothed connective.* The adversative chains at 0564A–B (*Alia … ratione … Alia autem
  ratione*), 0566B (*Nam, licet … tamen*) and 0569C–D (*Sed … Nam … Sed*) were read for the
  8777 @0191B failure — a dropped negative papered over by re-weighting a *sed*. Each *sed*,
  *tamen*, *licet*, *quin potius* and *nihilominus* is rendered with its printed force.
- *No mis-bound reference or agency.* The relative-heavy passages at 0561C (*quidquid post
  illam*), 0564D (*una facies … quae facies*), 0567B (*qui ejus gazophylacia quae ipsam
  supportabant cingebat*) and 0569D (*porticus, quae huic porticui juncta fuerat, ei … ex parte
  eam tangens, et ex majore sui relinquens*) were each traced antecedent by antecedent. The
  0569D sentence — three pronouns for two porticoes — is the hardest in the range and binds
  correctly in the English.
