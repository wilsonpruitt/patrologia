# Cruces — 11555 (Richard of St Victor, *Quomodo Spiritus Sanctus est amor Patris et Filii*)

- **Col 1012B** — Migne prints "sed ab **pso** amor procedit" ("Animus humanus amor non est, sed ab pso amor procedit"). "pso" is a broken/dropped-letter typesetting of "ipso" (*ab ipso* = "from it"). Translated per the evident reading ("but love proceeds from it").
