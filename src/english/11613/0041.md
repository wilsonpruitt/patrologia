## 4.

*My beloved put his hand through the hole, and my belly trembled at his touch.*

Her excuse having been reverently made beforehand within herself, yet no answer having been made to the Bridegroom, she next shows what her beloved had done for her, saying: « My beloved put his hand through the hole, and my belly trembled at his touch. » Whence blessed Gregory thus says, « Bountiful is the mercy of God, which alike opens the gate of life to all who are converted after their sins, that they may know the mysteries of the heavenly country, and by knowing may thirst, by thirsting may run, by running may arrive. » These secrets of the heavenly visitation he had felt [1272A] who says, « My soul hath thirsted after the living God [n: (Psal. XLI, 3)]: » and blessed Paul, saying, « I desire to be dissolved, and to be with Christ [n: (Phil. I, 23)]. » Blessed Ambrose also says hereupon, our hole, that is, that whereby we see the works of Christ, namely, the eye of the mind, through which Christ enters, whereby we truly know him to be God, whereby we ourselves most joyfully sing with the Prophet, « Wonderful are thy testimonies, O Lord, therefore hath my soul sought them [n: (Psal. CXVIII, 119)]. » And likewise that, « Thy testimonies are become exceedingly credible [n: (Psal. XCII, 5)]. » Which testimonies indeed are his wonderful works, which, while all marvelled, he wrought in the flesh. Hence restoring paralytics, cleansing lepers, raising the dead, and finally healing all sicknesses, [1272B] by his word alone, whereby all things were created and abide for ever. Which things the world seeing, and marvelling at the testimonies, declared them to be true; and if they were false, they would receive from falsehood no effect of power. Whence she says, « My belly, » that is, I, made wholly belly, trembled and was afraid. For the belly lacks bone, it has no share in firmness, what is let into it, this it receives, and renders it digested: so also our humanity, not withstanding the divine works, trembles wholly, and is affrighted that God has been made man; yet the things which have been given from above, these it acknowledges with the mouth, loves with the heart, and, being touched by the gifts of the Holy Spirit, trembling, it says, « My belly trembled at his touch.
