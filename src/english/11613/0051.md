## CHAPTER VIII. (cont.)

[1300C] *His lamps are lamps of fire and of flames. 7. Many waters could not quench charity, neither shall the floods overwhelm it.*

This is the commendation of charity, which is the true love of God; for its lamps of fire are the hearts, namely, of the saints, formed after the manner of lamps, which are narrow here below, but widened above, and prepared for a dwelling-place of the spiritual fire. Which fire indeed is always fire, always warming, and always kindling the heart in the love of God; sometimes fire with flame, when it not only burns and kindles, but rather shines in work, and manifests in deed what it does within; in such wise indeed, that many waters cannot quench this charity: for no tribulations, no devices [1300D] of vexations can quench this love; because, the more evils are multiplied, the more it is certain that it grows up hence into God, because « the floods shall not overwhelm it, » that is, evils heaped upon evils shall in nothing prevail against the grace of God. There follows:

*If a man shall give all the substance of his house for love, he shall despise it as nothing.*

This is the consummate praise of it: for if a man shall give the whole of that which is temporally acquired, that he may acquire this charity for himself, he will despise that whole substance as nothing, and will seem to himself to have given nothing, because that which is acquired is dear and lovely. ## 8.

*Our sister is little, and hath no breasts. What shall we do to our sister in the day when she is to be spoken to.*

The Bride has been commended by the Bridegroom, and he has admonished her that she was redeemed by him from death, and she has been taught that she should have this memorial of the heart; taught also that charity conquers all things; she indeed holds all things pleasing and receives them; and now, admonished concerning charity, she adds a question, saying, « Our sister is little, and hath no breasts. » There comes to her mind what the Apostle says, « When the fulness of the Gentiles shall have come in, then all Israel shall be saved [n: (Rom. XI, 25, 26)]. She calls to mind therefore this synagogue, which for the present walks in darkness, denies Christ, and blasphemes the things which are said and believed concerning him. [1301B] Foreknowing therefore that she is to be converted in the last times [by] the holy apostle, and that Elias and Henoch have been reserved by Christ for the converting of these, and naming her sister on account of the conversion to come, she thus says out of charity, « Our sister is little, and hath no breasts: » little indeed in faith, least in hope, and now none at all in charity. She hath no breasts, she contains in herself no bands of doctrine; the things which were done for her sake, these she denies and keeps silent about. Foreknowing therefore that she shall come to the faith at the last, she thus asks of the Bridegroom, « What shall we do to our sister, in the day when she is to be spoken to? » What shall we say? How shall we admonish her? What words of salvation shall we give her? To these questions the Bridegroom thus answers:

## 9.

*If she be a wall, let us build upon it bulwarks of silver: if she be a door, let us join it together with boards of cedar.*

For some of those who are to be converted are as a wall, having in themselves great knowledge, and also acquaintance with letters, and on this account also the hardness of a wall against those that assail them; to whom the Bridegroom says that bulwarks of silver be built upon them, that is, that the testimonies of the divine Scriptures be given them, and of the authorities wherewith we are fortified; that the ensigns be granted to these, whereby these, now fortified, are the defenders and the safeguard of the true Church. « But if she be a door, let us join it together with boards of cedar. » But the door are those through whom some enter, and some go out: who indeed are lesser in knowledge, [1301D] less instructed in the Scriptures, to whom the highest things are not to be set forth, nor things difficult of understanding, but boards of cedar are to be joined together, that is, light Scriptures are to be set forth; as is that board which the Lord painted, saying, « Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven. Blessed are the meek: for they shall possess the land. Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted. Blessed are they that hunger and thirst after justice; for they shall have their fill [n: (Matth. V, 3 et seq.)]. » Which board indeed is of cedar, that is, incorruptible, and bringing forth sentences undefiled, true and unbreakable. And other boards like to these, which, set before them, will be able to help and to confirm them in the truth received. There follows
