## CHAPTER XIX.

*And the lily of the valleys.* Valleys, nay deep valleys, which on this side and on that are overshadowed by neighbouring mountains, and, lest they wither with thirst, are watered by waters flowing down on either hand, seem to me to signify the humble and the lowly, whom the love of discipline keeps unwearied under the laws of their elders. Who, since they bring back nothing lofty or swollen from an indolent Maecenas, judge themselves unprofitable servants with a humble holiness, and bow their ear the more diligently to the admonitions of the Scriptures and to either Testament, desiring to abound rather in plenty of merits than of corn. Christ confesses himself the lily of deep valleys of this kind, [0282C] because, though he is the more exalted, yet he is declared to look upon lowly things [n: (Psal. CXII)]; nor does his Spirit rest save upon the humble and the meek, whose conscience shines again with a clean humility and a humble cleanness [n: (Isa. LXVI)]. And worthily enough did he will to be named a lily, signifying thereby what he came to work among the valleys and for the valleys' sake; that he might not darken the cause of his coming to them that rightly attend, whom, when he has rendered them taught, he may the more effectually conform to himself. The lily is girt about in a round with whiter leaves, after the likeness of a valley, having within it something red in the depth: in its leaves indeed showing itself seemly and bright, but in the red representing the appearance of blood. The whiteness of the lily figured the cleanness of the flesh assumed, which neither [0282D] the conception before nor the converse after blackened; but, though begotten of flesh, it knew not the flesh's own poison, and by the merit of virtues, as with a snowy splendour, it shone. But that red thing bears before it the pleasing memory of the passion, whereby, his blood being shed, he washed away the handwriting of the covenant formerly entered into, which our first parents wretchedly made with the author of sin, when at his will they touched the forbidden tree. Christ therefore is a lily, when he glows with the merit of an innocent life, when in the flesh he is not held by the stain or the glue of a cleaving vice; when the malice of men assails and accuses him as man, and he as man refuses not to suffer hard and bitter things for men. He is the lily of the valleys, when to the abject and the humble he gives an example and a grace of living, commends innocence, [0283A] implants a love of continence; when, having first suffered grievous things, he calls them forth to the virtue of suffering, teaching great things, doing greater, and proclaiming the time of doing. But if it be said that what is red within the lily holds the appearance of fire, « and God is a fire, » as it is certain that Scripture declares [n: (Deut. IV)]; that redness, as I see, hedged about with a snowy splendour, is God in man, [sic: *ooopertus*] with the robe of clean flesh. In whose wrapping he hid himself from the wise and the prudent, but revealed himself to the little ones and the humble that behold with faith [n: (Luc. X)]; who so look upon the covering or whiteness of the assumed flesh without, that they look within upon the fiery redness also of the hidden Godhead. Of whom Peter: « Thou art, he says, Christ the Son of the living God [n: (Matth. XVI)]. » And Nathanael: « Rabbi, thou art the Son of God, thou art the king [0283B] of Israel [n: (Joan. I)]. » And the centurion: « Indeed this was the Son of God [n: (Marc. XV)]. » Christ so made himself the lily of these valleys, when he disclosed himself to his humble ones not only as man, but as God within man; to whom his innocent humanity was so shown with a clean whiteness, that they discerned also the hidden Godhead with an unoffended faith. Whom, after he had taught them what they must faithfully think concerning himself, repeating the Virgin's praises, he invites the more diligently *161* to see how she imitates the Bridegroom amid the troubles of this life, while rest is not yet full, nor unfailing peace obtained.
