## *292* HERE BEGINS THE SECOND MORALITY, INTERMINGLED WITH FLOWERS OF ALLEGORY. (cont.)

He himself, I say, the Holy Spirit, is the fountain of gardens, [0503A] the well of living waters which flow with a rush from Libanus [n: (Cant. IV)] — that is, the holiness, the refreshment, the purification, the cleansing, the joy and the jubilation of the faithful souls which desire to be with Christ. Let our soul labour, therefore, that it may possess this well within itself, that is, let it be busy by good works to become the dwelling-place of the Holy Spirit. Nevertheless the waters of this well, which are said to flow from Libanus, betoken this: that the Holy Spirit is not possessed save with a chaste body and a purified heart. Whence also the same mount Libanus is interpreted *a whitening*. And that the well is reported to have been closed with a great stone declares in elegant speech that *in a body subject to sins* the Holy Spirit in no wise *dwelleth [n: (Sap. I)]*. For the stone is the huge mass of our crimes, which without [0503B] doubt hides the Holy Spirit from us, that he be not seen. Whence also the prophet says: The hand of the Lord is not powerless [0504A] to save, but our sins have made a wall between you and God [n: (Isa. LIX)]. [var: Vulg. Isa. LIX, 1–2 *Ecce non est abbreviata manus Domini ut salvare nequeat... sed iniquitates vestrae diviserunt inter vos et Deum vestrum*, "your iniquities have divided between you and your God"] But neither is this to be passed over through negligence, which Wisdom says: *For the Holy Spirit of discipline shall flee what is feigned [n: (Sap. I)]*. Let the wall of our crimes, therefore, or the millstone, be removed, and let spiritual discipline be sought and held fast by us; that is, let us confess our sins *294*, and let us put ourselves under spiritual chastisement, that we may be able to be the most holy dwelling of the Holy Spirit. Let our spirit be, I say, a field blooming with every kind of flower of the virtues; let there be found in it by Jacob a well pouring forth healthful streams, that is, let the Holy Spirit be found by Christ dwelling there. Whose presence will be clearly made manifest thus, if in all things we shall be meek and humble and peaceable [0504B], and shall abound in the fruits of good working with chastity and sobriety.

[0503] *Here ends the first Tome, that is, the first volume upon the Song of Songs.*
