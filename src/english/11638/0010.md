## RECAPITULATION FROM THE FOREGOING, ON THE IVORY THRONE WHICH SOLOMON MADE, HOW IT OUGHT TO BE REFERRED TO THE PERSON OF CHRIST. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Let us see finally what that is which the text of the history rehearsed to us above: *Beside,* he says, *each several hand [0514D] two lions stood [n: (III Reg. X)]*. ⟦s2⟧ Indeed by manifest reason we understand from the preceding discourse that there were no more hands there than two, and if two lions were assigned to each several hand, we hold for certain that there were not more than four lions only attending there. ⟦s3⟧ What therefore do the four lions signify, which are set beside each several hand, but the four gospels which everywhere preach the clemency and mercy of God? ⟦s4⟧ For thus thou hast it written in the Gospel: *He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved [n: (Marc. XVI)]*. ⟦s5⟧ And in another place: *Do penance, the kingdom of heaven is at hand [n: (Matth. IV)]*. ⟦s6⟧ The twelve little lions which the sacred history relates to have been upon the six steps signify the fulness and perfection [0515A] of the spiritual gifts, which no one doubts to have rested and remained upon Christ. ⟦s7⟧ In Christ, as the Apostle says, *dwelleth all the fulness of the divinity corporeally [n: (Coloss. II)]*. ⟦s8⟧ And *of his fulness,* says John, *we have all received [n: (Joan. I)]*. ⟦s9⟧ Meanwhile, if by the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit which Isaias relates to have rested upon Christ, and by the virtues which holy Church chiefly commends, we wish to understand these twelve little lions, our understanding, even among the enemies of the truth, will be judged worthy of worthy praise. ⟦s10⟧ For thus says Isaias: *There shall come forth a rod out of the root of Jesse, and a flower shall rise up out of his root, and the spirit of wisdom and of understanding shall rest upon him, the spirit of counsel and of fortitude, the spirit of knowledge and of godliness, and the spirit of the fear of the Lord shall fill him* [0515B] [n: (Isa. XI)] [var: Vulg. Isa. 11:2 has *spiritus Domini* before *spiritus sapientiae et intellectus*]. ⟦s11⟧ If therefore to these seven gifts of the Holy Spirit thou shalt add five virtues, namely faith, hope, charity, good work, and chastity, thou wilt beyond doubt fill up the number of twelve. ⟦s12⟧ For seven and five, when they come together into one, undoubtedly make up the number of twelve. ⟦s13⟧ These spiritual little lions therefore, as I judge, are of a better and worthier price than those which Solomon caused to stand upon the six steps in the midst of the golden and ivory throne.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ After these things, the history relates, as it were under a certain wonder, that *there was no such work made in any kingdoms [n: (III Reg. X)]*. ⟦s2⟧ And well indeed and justly enough did the history relate this. ⟦s3⟧ For there was no such work made in any kingdoms as is that which [0515C] *the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us [n: (Joan. I)]*. ⟦s4⟧ There was not, I say, such a work made in any kingdoms, that God should be made man, and should redeem with his blood the human race which had perished. ⟦s5⟧ Truly, I say, there was not so glorious a work made in any kingdoms, that our nature, already utterly despaired of as to salvation, should be drawn away from the lower regions and set in heaven at the right hand of God the Father.
