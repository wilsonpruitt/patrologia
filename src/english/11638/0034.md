## RECAPITULATION FROM THE FOREGOING, IN WHAT MANNER THIS SENTENCE OUGHT TO BE REFERRED TO THE PERSON OF THE FATHER, NAMELY, « SHOW ME, » ETC. (cont.)

Thou representest the image of God in thyself so long as thou sinnest not; but if at any time thou consentest to sin, the image of God withdrawing from thee, thou art made like to the devil himself, who by sinning and by being proud withdrew from God, and his own self, who was the seal of the likeness of the glory of God, as Ezekiel the prophet says [n: (cap. XXVIII)], he irrecoverably damned and destroyed. Above we said [0584B] that, as once Moses first ascended Mount Sinai, and so at length had a soliloquy with the Lord concerning secret and mystical things, so holy Church had done. For she ascends as it were a great and lofty mountain when she came to the soliloquy of her Bridegroom, who is « beautiful in form above the sons of men [n: (Psal. XLIV)], » and sought of him, saying: *Show me, thou whom my soul loveth, where thou feedest, where thou liest down,* etc. Let her come therefore to the soliloquy of the almighty Father, and, the filth of all iniquity being removed from her, let her seek of him in like manner, with the affection of charity, where he himself lies down, and where he himself feeds. Therefore, before God the almighty Father speaks with the Bride of his Son, or before that same Bride deserves to hear from the same Father of her Bridegroom: Understand, O daughter, and see, and incline [0584C] thine ear, and forget thy people and the house of thy father, and then where I feed or lie down I will show thee [var: Vulg. Psal. XLIV, 11, *Audi, filia, et vide, et inclina aurem tuam*, "Hearken, O daughter"] — before that come to pass, it is needful that our mind seek out with ever-watchful attention where in the divine oracles the Son of God is called a mountain, lest perchance our speech be convicted of falsehood by someone less learned, chiefly for this, that we have called Christ a mountain, and have made Mount Sinai equal to him by way of likeness. Many authorities therefore being passed over [sic: *praeter missis*], which testify to this which we say, let one sufficient and fitting authority alone be brought forth into the midst, as is worthy, to which the sons of spiritual grace and the friends of the divine law may gladly lend their spiritual ears, and the enemies of truth, or the less wise, may not have [0584D] anything to contradict. Daniel the prophet, privy to the secrets of God, is filled with the memory of the abundance of the sweetness of the Holy Spirit; and, expounding the wondrous dream of that great and terrible vision which the king of Babylon had seen, among the other things which he speaks forth to him he interprets Christ as the mountain, saying: *Thou hadst seen,* etc.
