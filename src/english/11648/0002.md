## 1463.

The lectern was renewed, and the whole burial place [0996D] of eight bishops of Verdun resting in the cemetery of this monastery of Saint Vitonus, formerly and of old before the high altar.

## 1475.

Anthony, abbot of this place, died.

## 1476.

The death of Charles of Burgundy at Nancy.

## 1481.

Abbot Matthew died; Gerard succeeds.
