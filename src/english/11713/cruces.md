# Cruces — 11713, Peter of Blois, *De divisione et scriptoribus sacrorum librorum*

- **col 1052B** — printed *tamentationes Jeremiae*. Intended *Lamentationes Jeremiae*. Rendered "the Lamentations of Jeremiah." Broken initial capital in the Migne plate.
- **col 1052C** — printed *longitudinm*. Intended *longitudinem*. Rendered "the length."
- **col 1053B** — printed *Malach enim Hebraice, Latine* regnum *interpretatum est*. Hebrew *melekh* is "king," not "kingdom"; but Migne prints *regnum* and the source tradition (Isidore, *Etym.* VI.ii.10) does too. Rendered "kingdom" as printed, per the translate-what-Migne-prints rule.
- **col 1053D** — printed *Nablat* for the Hebrew name of the Psalter (Isidore has *Nabla*). Left as printed.
- **col 1053D** — printed *Ysopocalim, quod interpretatur* volumen hymnorum. Garbled transliteration of *Sepher Tehillim*. Left as printed, with the gloss translated.
- **col 1053D** — printed *Ezechielites* in the list of psalm-authors. The tradition reads *Eman Ezrahites* (Ethan/Heman the Ezrahite). Rendered literally "the Ezechielite" — a human read-through should decide whether the site apparatus flags this.
- **col 1053A** — *Deuteronomium ... praefiguratio est quae sic ea habet quae priora sunt, ut tum ea nova sint omnia quae in eo replicantur.* The clause is syntactically strained (*tum* for *tamen*?). Rendered literally.
- **col 1054C** — printed *erectum coma concludit* (three little verses "an upright *coma* closes"). Isidore reads *erecto comate concluduntur*. Rendered literally following the printed nominative.
