# Cruces — Alcuin, *In Cantica canticorum* (PL 100), chunks 0000–0004

Uncertain/garbled printed readings rendered literally in the English, logged here per translation protocol. Column anchors and `[n:]` notes were reproduced verbatim and are NOT cruces; only genuine printing anomalies are recorded.

## Chunk 0000 (Monitum praevium)

- **col 0640A** — Source prints `Pap. 1361` where the parallel citations all read `Pag.` (page). Treated as a printer's error for "Pag."; rendered as "P. 1361" in line with the surrounding page-references. Reading: `Pap.` → *pag.*

## Chunk 0001 (Caput I)

- **col 0644C (VERS. 7)** — Source prints `mini esse sponsatam`; `mini` is a printer's error for `mihi` ("betrothed to me"). Rendered "betrothed to me." Reading: `mini` → *mihi*.

## Chunk 0003 (Caput III)

- **col 0649A (VERS. 3)** — Malformed apparatus bracket in the source: `( Cod. Vat., sollicitantem]` — opens with a parenthesis but closes with a square bracket. Reproduced verbatim (with the mismatched delimiters) in the English, since the variant content is untranslated apparatus. Reading: mismatched `(` / `]`.

## Chunk 0004 (Caput IV)

- **col 0652A (VERS. 6)** — Dittography in the source: `Duo ubera duo sunt sunt populi` — the word `sunt` is printed twice. Rendered once ("The two breasts are the two peoples...") to avoid nonsensical English. Reading: `sunt sunt` → *sunt* (single).
- **col ~0653A (VERS. 12)** — Source prints `spiritualium eperum germina`; `eperum` is a printer's error for `operum` ("of works"). Rendered "of spiritual works." Reading: `eperum` → *operum*.

# Cruces — Alcuin, *In Cantica canticorum* (21413), chunks 0005–0009

Garbled / uncertain printed readings encountered while translating chunks 0005–0009.
Rendered by the evident intended word; printed form recorded here.

## Chunk 0005

- **col 0656D** — Lemma "Species ejus, ut Libani; electus, ut cedri" is repeated in the commentary as **"Electus ut edri."** — "edri" is a misprint for *cedri* (matching the lemma just above). Rendered as "Chosen as the cedars."

## Chunk 0007

- **col 0661B** — "**oaorem**" in the lemma "Mandragorae dederunt oaorem in portis nostris" is a misprint for *odorem* (confirmed by the commentary "mandragorae dant **odorem**" immediately following). Rendered "The mandrakes have given an **odor** in our gates."
- **col 0659D** — "**pauperihus**" ("in cratere potus, qui pauperihus datur") is a misprint for *pauperibus*. Rendered "given to the poor."

## Chunk 0009

- **col 0666B** — "Hi sua in **perfectione** [ F., imperfectione] octogenario numero comparantur": the printed running text reads *perfectione*, which contradicts the sense (the eighty/concubines signify imperfection). Migne's own apparatus flags the correction (*F., imperfectione*). Printed reading retained in translation ("in their perfection") with the [ F., imperfectione] apparatus preserved verbatim, per the render-as-printed rule.
- **col 0666D** — "ut tibi sint **mercis** non periturae divitiae" [ Ms., sit merces]: *mercis* is an ungrammatical form (for *merces*, "reward"); Migne notes the Ms. reading *sit merces*. Rendered loosely "that there may be to thee, as reward, riches that shall not perish," with the [ Ms., sit merces] apparatus preserved.
