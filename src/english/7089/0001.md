## VI.

[0628B] Without the bishop's command let a presbyter hold masses, in the city when the bishop is present.

## VII.

Without the bishop's command let others also lay a hand on one possessed.

## VIII.

Let all penitents be reconciled on the fifth feria of Easter.

## IX.

Let it be lawful for a presbyter to visit the sick, and to anoint chrism; a sick penitent let it not be lawful to anoint with chrism.

## VI. OF INNOCENT TO AURELIUS THE BISHOP.

[0628C]

Let a hand not be laid easily on a Bishop; let him not be ordained from a layman.

## OBSERVATION OF PHILIPPE LABBE, S. J.

The following canons, sent long ago by some Roman synod to the bishops of Gaul, the Reverend Father Sirmond first brought to light from several manuscript codices in the appendix to volume I of the councils of Gaul; and the Royal collector threw the same, out of their place, into the end of the XXXVII volume, after the indices, with the treatise on the ancient promotions of bishops. We, however, following the not improbable conjecture of the same most illustrious editor, have placed them under the letters of Innocent the first, since neither the form of the decrees nor that of the writing is foreign to them. But in what year they were issued is plainly not established, although, as everyone [0628D] sees, they bear on their face the greatest antiquity.
