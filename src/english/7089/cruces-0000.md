# 7089 cruces — stint T7 (chunks 0000–0001, cols 0623C–0628D, PDF pp. 317–319)

Every page in range was opened at the plate once (`raw/scans/pl020/pl020.pdf`, 300 dpi full width, corners
read first: 317 = 623/624, 318 = 625/626, 319 = 627/628). Text collated in the same visit. **No foot notes on
any of the three pages** for this work (confirming the sweep's zero at the plate); nothing to place.

## Standing decisions

- Heads: *MONITUM* → NOTICE; *DISSSERTATIO* (so printed, three s's, Migne's) → DISSERTATION (Pattern 9,
  section labels normalize; the Latin column keeps the misprint); *OBSERVATIO PHILLIPPI LABBEI* → OBSERVATION
  OF PHILIPPE LABBE.
- Migne's own `( *sic* )` after canons XIX, XXI, XXII and the head IV is reproduced verbatim; no `[sic:]` of
  ours stacked on it (runbook false positive 5). The words he flags are carried: *renovo* (XIX),
  *Paulianeste* (XXI, i.e. the Paulianists, fused with the next word in the codex). XXII's accusative
  *Diaconessas* as subject has no English exponent; rendered "Deaconesses".
- The Nicene epitome and the synopses are telegraphic by design (Coustant calls the Decentius synopsis *plane
  barbara*); the English keeps the jussive shape and does not complete the canons.

## Plate readings that differ from our TEI (patch filed in `data/briefs/7089-7091-PENDING-TEI-PATCHES.md`)

- **0623C** TEI `epitolam` — plate `epistolam`. Rendered "letter".

## Cruces

- **0623 (Nicene canon II)** `non tacito promoveatur in clero`: *tacito* is printed (plate read); rendered
  "tacitly". The Nicene canon (2) forbids promoting a neophyte *quickly*; *tacito* may be a slip for
  *cito*. Not marked (a real form, and the English asserts nothing the canon denies outright).
- **0624C (canon V)** `propter diversorum querimonia`: *querimonia* as printed (nominative/neuter-plural
  form after *propter*); rendered "the complaint of divers persons".
- **0625A (canon XIV)** asyndeton `acceperit communionem, supervixerit` kept: "has received communion, has
  survived".
- **0627A** `Hispanos detrahet de militia saeculari celeri ad sacerdotium non ordinantes`: does not construe
  (*celeri* has nothing to agree with; *ordinantes* has no subject). Rendered word for word; the sense of
  Innocent's letter (Ep. III to the Toledo bishops) is that men should not be ordained swiftly from
  secular service.
- **0628B (canon VI)** `presbyter missae teneat`: *missae* is printed (plate read), plural beside the singular
  verb; rendered "masses". The companion canon V has *missam*.
- **0628B (canon VII)** `vel alii` rendered "others also" (not "or others": there is no first term).
- **0628C** Labbe's *Observatio* announces "the following canons", but none follow in this work: the TEI ends
  here, and so does 7089 on the plate (p. 319 ends with the rule and the next work's title). Not a loss.
