# 7090 Innocent I, *Epistolae et decreta*: cruces, chunks 0018–0023 (stint T4)

Cols 0544C–0576A, PL 20, PDF pp. 277–293 (`raw/scans/pl020/pl020.pdf`, 300 dpi, corners read first
on every page, with spot zooms). Every page in the range was opened once: the text was collated and
the foot notes were read on the same visit. Page reads: `data/briefs/7090-PLATE-READS-0018.json`.
Note edits: `data/briefs/7090-NOTE-EDITS-0018.md`. TEI patches: `data/briefs/7090-PENDING-TEI-PATCHES-0018.md`.

## Standing decisions

- **Coustant's lettered notes.** Latin `[cn: <letter> | …]` at the printed key. The English carries a
  translated `[nt: …]` at the same point and **no `[cn:]`** (Pattern 19, P0, verifier check 9a). This
  follows the launch correction and T3.
- Bare variant words inside a note stay Latin italic, in the case printed. Editors are expanded
  (Merlin, Crabbe, Quesnel, Garnier, Labbe, the Roman edition, the Isidorian collection, the edition of
  Augustine). Manuscript sigla stay as printed (*Colb.*, *Corb.*, *Germ.*, *Pith.*, *Vatic.*, *Reg.*,
  *Navar.*), per P0.
- **Pattern 17.** Innocent's singular addressees (Maximianus, Boniface, Alexander, Decentius) take
  *thou*. So do the African councils and the five bishops writing to Innocent (*sanctitas tua*, *te*).
  Plurals take *you* (*ad vestram synodum*, 0544C; *litteris vel commonitorio vestro*, 0547A).
- **Heads.** `CAP. I.` etc. are left as printed, and the Monitum head is "MONITUM ON THE FOLLOWING
  EPISTLES", both after T3. In the bishops' lists the names keep their Latin form, except
  AUGUSTINUS → AUGUSTINE and the addressee INNOCENTIUS → INNOCENT.
- **Nested italics.** Where a note sits inside an italic run (inscriptions, titles, scripture), the
  italics are closed before the `[nt:]` and reopened after it, as T3 did.
- **Brackets inside a note.** A marker cannot contain `]`. In 0023 *d* the Roman editor's square
  brackets are written as fullwidth ［ ］ in both columns.

## Text: plate against twin

- **0545A** `impleras` (twin) against the plate's `imple·as` (read as *impletas*; the glyph is thin and
  has no *r* shoulder). English: "have been fulfilled". A patch is filed, flagged as a worn glyph.
- **0546B** `(Vide epist. 19, n. 1), etiam`: the plate shows a point with no tail. It could be a period
  or a worn comma. The English keeps the comma, and no patch is filed.
- **0558A** `anctoritatem` (twin) against the plate's clean `auctoritatem`. English: "authority". Patch filed.
- **0560A** `orent super cum` (twin) against the plate's `orent super eum`. English: "pray over him". Patch filed.
- **0571A** `auctoritate sanctitatis tuae` (twin) against the plate's `auctoritati` (clean). English:
  "yield to the authority of thy holiness". Patch filed.
- **0575B** `misimur` (twin): the last sort on the plate is broken, but it looks more like *s* than *r*
  (there is no stem). English: "We have sent both". Patch filed, flagged as worn. A second witness is
  recommended before the patch is applied.

## Readings rendered as printed

- **0548B–0549A** *non ʰ esse vere visum est … Dei Ecclesiam commutari*: the double infinitive is
  rendered "it has truly seemed that the Church of God is not to be changed". The negation is carried.
  Coustant's note *h* gives the variants (*non vere visum*, *non ergo visum*, and the Royal exemplar's
  *non esse visum*).
- **0548A** *nisi quod illa in transitu meruit, ista susceptum apud se consummatumque gauderet* →
  "except that the former merited him in passing, while the latter rejoiced that he was received and
  brought to his consummation in her". The "him" comes from *susceptum*. Nothing is added.
- **0553A–0556B (Ep. XXV)**: the rendering is literal throughout. "leaven" is kept for *fermentum*, as
  Coustant's note *f* requires.
- **0555A** *Verba vero dicere non possum*: rendered "But to say the words I cannot". The note sits
  after "say" to match *dicere ᵃ non possum*.
- **0566A** Rom. 7:23 is printed *captivantem me sub lege peccati* (Vulg. *in lege*). It is rendered
  "under the law of sin". No `[var:]` is fired, because Coustant's own note *e* already gives *in lege*
  (Pattern 18 ❌: the edition already glosses it).
- **0569C, 0574A** *Ne nos inferas in tentationem* → "Do not bring us into temptation", **not**
  conformed to "lead us not" (Vulg. *inducas*). 7a″.
- **0570B** *haec interim duo … quae fidelia corda sustineant* has no question mark on the plate, so it
  is rendered as a statement ("which faithful hearts must endure"). No mark is supplied (Pattern 8).
  Garnier's *non sustinent* is only in note *j*.
- **0574C** *Da mihi intellectum, ut discam mandata tua (Psal. CXVIII, 125)*: this is the wording of
  Ps. 118:73 under the locator of 118:125. It is rendered as printed. Not a `[var:]`: the locator is
  Migne's, and the wording construes.
- **0574C–0575A** *Jubetur ut habeamus continentiam, ubi dicitur, Cum scirem …*: the command half is
  missing, and Coustant's note *d* (placed at the plate) says so and gives the restoration from Aug.
  ep. 147. It is rendered as printed.
- **0575A** Col. 1:10 is printed *in omni opere et sermone bono* (Vulg. *in omni opere bono
  fructificantes*). It is rendered "in every good work and word". This is a `[var:]` candidate, not
  fired: the words are added, but the sense is not reversed.
- **0572B** Ps. 3:3 *Non est salus illi* (Vulg. *ipsi*): same sense, no marker.

## Coustant's notes: readings on the plate

- **0548A note c**: the Jerome quotation reads *regulus quaeris* (Jerome has *regulas*). The italic
  vowel is worn and could be *a* or *u*. The Latin keeps the sweep's *regulus*, and the English renders
  "rules".
- **0558A note e**: Coustant prints *quando aberat, ac nec necessitas urgebat* ("and not even
  necessity pressed"), which sits oddly with the argument (the bishop permitted this in necessity).
  It is rendered literally, with the negation carried (7a). *nec* may be a slip for *ne* or for nothing.
- **0545A note b**: *ut·ue* was read as *utque*. The text's own *utque illi* at 0545A confirms it.
- **0545B note d**: Merlin's corrupt reading is printed *eamdem quum/quam praest·loris*; it is written
  *quam praestaturis*. Where letters are worn, see the note-edits file.
- **0544 note g**: *moram, pati nesciit, quin …*. The commas are on the plate and have no English
  exponent.
- **p. 287 note h** is **not truncated**, although the brief lists it among the truncated notes. It
  runs from the foot of the left column to the head of the right foot and ends *Sufinianus.*
- **p. 283 note b** has a second sentence at the head of the right foot, which the sweep missed. It is
  now added.
- **p. 284 note d** (0558A) goes on for two long paragraphs on the right foot, which the sweep missed.
  It is now added.
- **p. 283 note d** (0554B) prints *Meldense ann. 843; c. 44*, while the same council is *ann. 845*
  in note *a* of p. 281. Both are rendered as printed. The discrepancy is Coustant's (or the
  compositor's).
