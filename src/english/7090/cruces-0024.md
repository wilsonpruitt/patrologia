# 7090 Innocent I, *Epistolae et decreta*: cruces, chunks 0024–0027 (stint T5)

Cols 0576B–0594A, PL 20 PDF pp. 293–302 (`raw/scans/pl020/pl020.pdf`, 300 dpi, corners read first
on every page; zooms at 300/400 dpi where noted). Every page in the range was opened once, the
text collated and the foot notes read in the same visit. Content: the close of the five bishops'
letter to Innocent (Ep. XXVIII, §§8–17 = Augustine Ep. 177), Ep. XXIX to the council of Carthage,
Ep. XXX to the council of Milevis, and the head and inscription of Ep. XXXI.

## Standing decisions

- **Coustant's lettered notes.** Latin `[cn: <letter> | …]` at the key; the English carries a
  translated `[nt: …]` at the same point and **no `[cn:]`** (Pattern 19, P0, verifier 9a), as T3 did.
- Sigla as T3: Merlin, Crabbe, Quesnel, Garnier, Labbe, "the Roman edition", "the Councils" / "the
  edition of the Councils", "the Isidorian (collection/manuscripts)", "the Augustine edition"
  (*Aug.*, *August.*, the Benedictine Augustine), manuscripts as "the Colbert / Corbie / Saint-Germain
  (*Germ.*) / Saint-Remi (*Remig.*) / Pithou / Navarre / Vatican manuscript". Bare variant words stay
  Latin italic in the printed case.
- **Pattern 17.** The five bishops write to Innocent in the singular (thou); Innocent's rhetorical
  opponent in Ep. XXIX §3 (*te negare… consequeris*) is singular (thou); the councils of Carthage and
  Milevis are plural (you).
- *liberum arbitrium* = "free choice" throughout; *possibilitas (naturae)* = "possibility (of nature)";
  *gratis mortuus est* = "died in vain"; *adjutorium* = "help", *auxilium* = "aid".
- Migne's inline *[Forte leg. praeciperent]* (0583B) is his apparatus and is carried verbatim, untranslated.

## Cruces in the text

- **0576D `evacuatum`** (*Ergo evacuatum est scandalum crucis*) — the plate glyph reads like
  *evacuutum*, a worn *a* on the 1-bit scan. Single letter, one witness: no patch, no marker; rendered
  "made void".
- **0579C `beattiudini`** (*tuae beattiudini potius credidimus dirigendam*) — **Migne's type**: the
  plate prints the transposed non-word, and our TEI carries the same. `[sic: *beattiudini*]` beside
  the sense "thy blessedness". (Whole-word transposition, legible on the plate; not a single worn letter.)
- **0587 margin** — the gutter letter prints **C twice** on p. 299 (at *Quis, rogo* / *praecipitiis*
  and again at *sentire* / *sit proficere*), no D. Our doubled `[0587C]` and `[0588C]` are the
  plate's. Anchors carried verbatim.
- **0589B–0590A `in quam`** (*honoris, in quam, illius*) — the plate prints *inquam* split. Pattern 10:
  `I say [sic: *in quam*]`.
- **0583B `quid praecipere … quos abluere`** — the infinitives have no governing verb; Migne's own
  bracket *[Forte leg. praeciperent]* and Coustant's note b (supply *deberent*) both mark it.
  Rendered literally: "what to command … whom to wash".
- **0586A `Si ergo hoc ille ita magnus scit ut assidue dicere, ita necessarium confessus est, ut
  doceret`** — does not construe (Coustant's note h: "we should prefer *ita magnus scivit assidue
  dicere, et ita*"). Rendered as closely as the words allow: "If then he, so great, knows so to say
  this continually, so confessed it necessary as to teach it".
- **0586B `Liberum enim arbitrium olim ille perpessus`** — Coustant (note i) says the sense wants
  *permissus*; the printed *perpessus* is rendered "having undergone free choice".
- **0586B `hujus ruinae jacuisset oppressu`** — *oppressu* (abl. noun), rendered "would have lain under
  the crushing of this ruin"; note j discusses the variants.
- **0592A `celeri`** — plate prints *celeri veritatis … ratione*; our TEI has the non-word *ceferi*.
  English follows the plate ("by the swift reason of truth"); TEI patch filed.
- **0583B `regulis.`** — the plate has no stop after *regulis*; the English has none; patch filed.
- **0582B `laudat`, `Praetextato`** — plate forms (TEI *laudet*, *PRAETENTATO*); English follows the
  plate; patches filed.
- **0593A `nolle se mortem morientis`** — "the death of the dying": a paraphrase of Ezek 33:11 (Vulg.
  *mortem impii*), not set as a quotation; no marker.

## Cruces in the notes (rendered in the `[nt:]`)

- **0582 e (head of Ep. XXIX)** `Quae autem 29 orat, nunc 7` — the plate prints *orat* (for *erat*);
  carried in italics: "And what *orat* 29 is now 7".
- **0587 a** `unde matilus judicetur` — the plate prints *matilus* (for *mutilus*, Garnier's
  *mutilum* just above); carried in italics.
- **0581 b** `corriguntur ex Quesn. Auguts.` — printed *Auguts.*; the `[nt:]` renders "the Augustine
  edition" (abbreviation, no sense at stake).
- **0582 d** `verum etiam no ter licet exiguus` — broken type for *noster*, kept inside the italic Latin.
- **0583C c** `parete` — the plate glyph is ambiguous between *parete* and *parere*; left as the
  sweep read it.
- **0593 f (head of Ep. XXXI)** `Quae autem erat 59, nunc 5` — numeral worn (59 or 39); left 59.
- **0592 o** `tuari` (for *tutari*, quoting the letter's opening) — rendered "protect".
- **0592 n** `digni sunt poena. haud scio` — the plate's stop before lower-case *haud* is kept.

## Scripture as printed (7a″ deliverable)

- **0578A Jn 8:56** *Abraham concupivit videre diem meum* — Vulg. *exsultavit ut videret*: "desired to
  see" vs "rejoiced that he might see". `[var:]`.

  qui non posuit*. `[var:]`.
- **0592B Lk 18:16** *Sinite infantes … talium est enim regnum coelorum* — Vulg. *pueros … regnum
  Dei*. `[var:]`.

Logged, no marker (same sense, or paraphrase not set as quotation):
