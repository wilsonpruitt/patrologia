# Cruces — 7427, *De unitate sanctae Trinitatis* (Auctor incertus)

- **col 1209** — printed *in relativis naturaei vocabulis*. *naturaei* is not a Latin
  form; intended reading is the genitive *naturae* ("in the relative terms of
  nature"), rendered so. Possible misprint for *naturalibus*, which would give "in
  the relative natural terms" — but *naturae* is the lighter emendation and fits
  the parallel *non naturae, quia una est* just above.

- **col 1210** — printed *Abstulisti grandem macheriam*. *macheria* = *maceria*,
  "a wall, a dry-stone enclosure" (the *-ch-* spelling is common in late Latin
  under the influence of Greek); rendered "you have taken away a great wall."
  Not to be confused with *machaera* ("sword"), which would give "you have taken
  away a great sword" — context (an obstacle removed from the argument) favors
  the wall.

- **col 1210** — printed *ac non sputis omnes inlinient*. *inlinient* for
  *illinent* (fut. of *illino*), and the subject shifts from *aures* to *omnes*;
  rendered "and not all of them smear it over with spittle."

- **col 1212** — printed *sed diversae sunt appellationes et personae, licet
  incorporalis Patris et Filii et Spiritus sancti*. The genitive *incorporalis*
  hangs without a governing noun (one expects *licet incorporalium personarum*
  or *licet incorporalis sit natura*). Rendered literally as "albeit of an
  incorporeal Father and Son and Holy Spirit." Worth a human eye.

- **col 1208** — *Sacrificans diis eradicabitur, nisi Domino soli* (Exod. XXII, 20)
  is quoted with no `[n:]` marker in Migne; left unmarked, per the verbatim rule.
