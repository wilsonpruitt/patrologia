# Cruces — 7561 (Vincent of Lérins, *Commonitoria*)

Garbled/broken printed Latin rendered literally per policy, with editorial notes. Column-cited.

## Chunk 0001 (cols 0644–0647)

- **col 0645 (ch. V)** — `aut vero pro alicujus provincio lae temeraria quadam conspiratione certarent`. Printed *provincio lae* is a line-break split/garble of *provinciae* ("of some province"), which the genitive construction (*pro alicujus provinciae*) requires. Rendered per the intended reading, "for the sake of some province."

## Chunk 0002 (cols 0648–0650)

- **col 0648 (ch. VII)** — `a quo nos solum constitutionis Ecclesiasticae disciplina, sed etiam censura Apostolicae deterret auctoritatis`. Printed *solum* lacks the correlative *non*; the following *sed etiam* requires *non solum* ("not only … but also"). Dropped *non* treated as a printer's omission and rendered "not only … but also."
- **col 0650 (ch. IX)** — `ut mihi non modo afirmari ... posse videantur`. Printed *afirmari* (single f) is a misprint of *affirmari* ("to be affirmed"). Rendered "to be affirmed."

## Chunk 0004 (cols 0655–0657)

- **col 0655 (ch. XIII)** — `Haec ergo Nestorius, Apollmaris, Photinus ... latrant`. Printed *Apollmaris* is a misprint of *Apollinaris* (the name is set correctly elsewhere in the same and surrounding chapters). Rendered "Apollinaris."

## Chunk 0006 (cols 0661–0665)

- **col 0664** — `*Si surrexerit in modio tuî propheta, Deut* 13.` Migne prints *modio* ("in your measure/bushel"), almost certainly a misprint for *medio* ("in the midst of you"), which is the wording Deut. 13:1 requires and which Vincent himself prints correctly in the parallel quotation in ch. XVIII (`*Si surrexerit in medio tuî propheta*`, col 0665). Rendered literally as "within your measure" here; the parallel at 0665 rendered "in the midst of you."
- **col 0663** — `...quas ad Philippum Imperatorem... Christiani magisterii auctoritate conscripsi.` Printed *conscripsi* (1st person, "I wrote"); sense requires 3rd person *conscripsit* ("he [Origen] wrote"), the subject throughout being Origen and his Epistles. Rendered as "he wrote."

## Chunk 0007 (cols 0666–0667)

- **col ~0666 (ch. XXI)** — `*Scirdentem sepem mordebit eum serpens, Eccles.* 10` (Eccl. 10:8). Printed *Scirdentem* is not a Latin word; evidently a garble of *Scindentem* ("one who breaks/cuts through"), which fits the sense and the verse (cf. Vulg. *qui dissipat sepem*). Rendered "He who breaks through a hedge, a serpent shall bite him."

## Chunk 0012 (cols 0681–0686B)

- **col 0684 (ch. XXXII)** — `Ait itaque sanctus Papa Xis tus in Epistola...`. The pope's name is printed with a stray internal space, *Xis tus*, i.e. a broken setting of *Xystus* (= Pope Sixtus III), whom Vincent also names *Sixti* at col 0683 (ch. XXXII opening) and *Xysti* at col 0685 (ch. XXXIII). Normalized to "Sixtus" throughout for readability; the Sixtus/Xystus variation is Vincent's/Migne's own and is harmless.
