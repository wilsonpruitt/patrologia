## CHAP. II. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ No. 49. ⟦s2⟧ *Show me thy face, Sion.* ⟦s3⟧ Sion is interpreted contemplation, which is rightly understood of those who say with the Apostle: *But we, beholding the glory of God, are transformed into the same image from brightness to brightness, as by the Spirit of the Lord.* ⟦s4⟧ And again: *while we contemplate not the things which are seen, but the things which are not seen [n: (II Cor. III)]*. ⟦s5⟧ For the things which are seen are temporal, but the things which are not seen are eternal. ⟦s6⟧ She therefore is invited to stand always before God, like Elias, who was saying: *The Lord lives, in whose sight I stand this day* [0972D] [n: (IV Reg. III)]; and let her not be of those whom the prophet marks, saying: *They have turned to me the back and not the face [n: (Jerem. II)]*; and, *thy voice,* he says, *in my ears [n: (III Reg. XVII)]*; and, *the just cried, and the Lord heard them and delivered them out of all their tribulations [n: (Psal. XXXIII)]*.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ No. 50. ⟦s2⟧ *For thy voice is sweet.* ⟦s3⟧ The prayers of the saints, ever acceptable to the Lord, and present by his grace, because they received from the light of Christ, that they might remain in the beauty of justice.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ No. 51. ⟦s2⟧ *Catch us the little foxes.* ⟦s3⟧ The Lord says by a parable in the Gospel [n: (Matth. XIII)] that, when the blade had grown, and had brought forth fruit, then appeared the cockle: that is, as the Church advanced, numerous [0973A] heresies too arose, or, while she abode in that faith, those things which they wrongly held were searched out. ⟦s4⟧ So too that Samson, who is interpreted the sun, having caught three hundred foxes, applied fire to their tails, and kindled the crops of the enemies [n: (Judic. XV)]. ⟦s5⟧ For heretics in their beginnings are always conspicuous, as with a feigned religion, but in their last days they draw fire, assigned to the burning of Gehenna. ⟦s6⟧ And therefore *Catch us the foxes:* convict the heretics, and shut up their cunning with the testimonies of the holy Scriptures. ⟦s7⟧ He calls them little foxes, because they contain nothing great by understanding according to the right faith; for they display all that they know; being petty, they demolish the vineyards, subvert the peoples, corrupting them with depraved traditions. ⟦s8⟧ *Our vineyard has flourished.* ⟦s9⟧ The Catholic Church [0973B] advances with unfailing increase.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ No. 52. ⟦s2⟧ *My beloved is mine, and I am his.* ⟦s3⟧ The Apostle says: *They shall be two in one flesh. ⟦s4⟧ This is a great sacrament; but I speak in Christ and in the Church [n: (Ephes. V)]*. ⟦s5⟧ For concerning him the same Church says: *The Lord is the portion of my inheritance [n: (Psalm. XV)]*. ⟦s6⟧ And again it is said by the Father to the Son: *Ask of me, and I will give thee the gentiles for thy inheritance, and for thy possession the ends of the earth [n: (Psalm. II)]*. ⟦s7⟧ Christ therefore has the Church in his body, and the Church has Christ in the head: which connection, because it cannot be sundered, she has rightly said: *My beloved is mine.*

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ No. 53. ⟦s2⟧ *Who feeds among the lilies.* ⟦s3⟧ Christ feeds among the lilies when his body, which is the Church, is adorned by the companies of the saints and of virgins.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0973C]No. 54. ⟦s2⟧ *Until the day breathe out, and the shadows decline.* ⟦s3⟧ Until the evangelical tradition advance in clearer light, and the figures of the Old Testament, which as shadows preceded in the rite of sacrifices, in typical observances, be driven away.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ No. 55. ⟦s2⟧ *Return, be like, my beloved to me.* ⟦s3⟧ While he intimates the beloved to return, and calls him like to a roe and a fawn, he demonstrates the ascension of Christ into the heavens, to be compared to clean and swift animals. ⟦s4⟧ For these by a swift bound bound over the higher places of the mountains; but Christ surpassed all things that seem lofty, because concerning him the Apostle says: *Wherefore God exalted him, and gave him a name which is above every name, that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, [0973D] of things in heaven, on earth, and under the earth, and every tongue confess that the Lord Jesus is in the glory of God the Father (Philip II)*. ⟦s5⟧ Now Bethel is interpreted the house of God: therefore upon the mountains of Bethel, because to all the saints who are in heaven and on earth, he preaches Christ more powerful.
