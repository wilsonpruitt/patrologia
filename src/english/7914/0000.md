## NOTICE.

[1055C]

[1055D] *505* *Both manuscript codices and printed editions set the name of Cassiodorus at the head of this little work — at least those which it has been possible to see; all the librarians too, if you except one, and very many other authors of better note ascribe it to our author without any controversy. To me, however, it seems most certain that it is not Cassiodorus's; but by what arguments chiefly I am led to that opinion, our preface, which we have placed before the head of this work, will sufficiently show. Consult it therefore, if you please, kind reader.*

## PROLOGUE.

[1056C]

[1056D] Solomon, inspired by the divine Spirit, composed this little book concerning the marriage of Christ and the Church, and in a manner made an epithalamium of Christ and the Church, that is, a song upon the bridal chambers. Whence also he called this work the Song of Songs: because it surpasses all songs. For as it is said, *King of kings, and Lord of lords [n: (Apoc. XVII, 14)]*, and solemnity of solemnities: so it is called the Song of Songs on account of its excellence and dignity. But in this respect that little book is the more obscure, [1057A] because no persons are there named, and it is composed as it were in the comic style.
