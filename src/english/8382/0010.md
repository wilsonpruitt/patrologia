## XXXII.

If anyone is roused, and is saddened, and is angry against him who roused him to prayer, [nt: Another reading: *let him be excommunicated from his own food*, as above, chap. 25.] ought to be excommunicated, and not to eat until, pierced with compunction, he acknowledges his folly, and so, converted, receives pardon.

## XXXIII.

If any Christian is found a liar, which ought not to be, because Christ our Lord, [0434C] from whom he received his name, is truth, let him do penance according to the quality and quantity of the lie. For it is written: *Thou shalt destroy all who speak a lie.* And, *every lie is from the devil [n: (Psalm. V)]*.

## XXXIV.

If any Christian has given himself to idle tales, to foolish talk, to jesting words and those that move laughter, which according to the definition of the Lord, and of his holy apostle Paul, is known to be sin; let him disclose it to his priest, and do penance according to his judgment and the measure of the offence. Let no Christian think little of or neglect even his least sins, which are wont to be done in vain and windy thoughts, in superfluous and idle speeches, in works done without necessity, such as is the appetite [0434D] for foods, immoderate laughter, superfluous sleep, and innumerable such things: but rather let him greatly fear for these, as one who is to stand before the tribunal of the strict judge to render an account. For it is written: *Of every idle word that men have spoken, they shall render an account to God in the day of judgment [n: (Matth. I)]* [var: Vulg. Matt 12:36 *omne verbum otiosum … reddent rationem de eo*, "they shall render an account of it", with no "to God"]. And again Scripture: *God will leave nothing unpunished.* And therefore for these and other sins let him do penance.

## XXXV.

If anyone is contentious or a gainsayer of holy commands, or a liar or disobedient, or clamorous in speech, or wrathful, or a scoffer, or given to jesting more than is seemly, or answering [0435A] harshly, and altogether whatever is against the authority of the Holy Scriptures; according to the measure [0436A] and the work of the sin, let him do penance at the discretion of the priest.
