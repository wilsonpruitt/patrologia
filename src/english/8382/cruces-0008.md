Every column in the range was read at the plate (archive PDF pp. 220–224; see
`data/briefs/8382-PLATE-READS-0008.json`). Single witness: nothing below fires a `[sic:]`.

**Apparatus fired: 3 `[var:]` · 0 `[sic:]` · 0 `[cj:]` · 0 `[d:]` · 0 `[ed:]` · 4 `[nt:]`** (the
four recovered foot notes: head of the Oxford excerpt, @0433A, @0433D, @0434B).

## Standing decisions for this range

- **The *Pater noster* and *Miserere mei Deus* cues (0428D).** Rendered as cues, in the printed
  italics: *Have mercy on me, O God* and the *Our Father*. "Twelve *Pater noster*" is carried as "the
  *Our Father* twelve times" so no plural has to be forced onto an italic prayer title.
- **LIBER QUARTUS head (cont.) in 0008 and 0009** is word-for-word the same as stint B's head in
  0007 (checked by diff), so assembly merges cleanly.
- ***delicatus* (0009 @0430D–0431A) = "effeminate (man)"**: the Latin word's own sense. Not
  reconstructed from the Old English.
- ***hiemes* at 0431A = "winters"** (work vocabulary), beside *annos* = "years" in the same sentence.
- ***crapula* (0429D, 0430C) = "surfeiting"**, kept apart from *ebrietas*/*ebrius* ("drunkenness",
  "drunk") which the Oxford canons also use.
- ***poeniteat* = "let him do penance"**, the same English as *poenitentiam agat*; the twin keeps the
  difference.
- ***andronas* (0433A) is kept in italic Latin** because Migne's own note glosses the WORD
  (*Id est, populi confluentias*); an English word in its place would leave the note explaining
  nothing (Song-of-Songs rule, case 2).

## Cruces, by column

- **0428B *reliquos dies jejunet contra*.** Rendered "the remaining days let him fast in turn".
  *contra* as an adverb ("on the other side, in return"); the sense is presumably the alternate
  days not on bread and water. Plate confirms the word.
- **0429B *per supplicium*.** "through punishment": the eighth remission is dying *per supplicium*
  (by penalty/execution). *cum in vivis esset* then says the friends redeem him "while he was among
  the living", which sits oddly with a remission after death; rendered as printed.
- **0429B *ut homo interior, hoc est anima pro pia habeatur*.** No comma after *anima* on the plate;
  the English carries none ("that is the soul may be held for godly").
- **0430B *ut tibi per Deum peccata tua remittat*.** No expressed subject; "that he may remit thee
  thy sins through God" as printed.
- **0430C *a perjuris*** (plate-confirmed; earlier in the same chunk *perjuria*). Read as *perjuriis*
  with the medieval *-iis* > *-is* contraction (Pattern 9) and rendered "perjuries"; the other
  construal, *perjuris* "the perjured", is possible and would give "of perjurers".
- **0431A *unum annum omnino uti mulier*.** "one year altogether as a woman": elliptical as
  printed; the sense of the Basil tariff is not recoverable from this Latin. Rendered literally.
- **0431A *vel suspendatur*.** "let him either be suspended": *suspendere* is "hang" as well as
  "suspend"; the English keeps the same ambiguity rather than choosing.
- **0432A ⭐ *quoniam mater illorum contigit ea, quae haec opponit*.** Does not construe
  (singular *opponit* with no subject that fits; *contigit* "touched" or "befell"). Rendered as
  closely as the words allow: "because their mother touched those things, which opposes these."
  **The plate answers it and our Latin does not carry the answer**: p. 222 prints a foot note keyed
  `ª` here, *Mallem legere : quoniam illorum matri convenit huic se opponere. WILKINS.* ("I would
  rather read: because it befits their mother to set herself against this"). It is NOT one of the six
  notes recovered at prep, so it was not injected; reported to the caller for
  `data/plate-notes/8382.tsv`. No `[cj:]` fired: Migne's editor has already conjectured it, and
  Pattern 19 forbids relabelling his conjecture as ours.
- **0432B *si hoc frustra fecerit*.** "if he did this to no purpose": after "through anger" (three
  years) a one-year tariff for *frustra*; perhaps "without intent". Rendered literally.
- **0433B *in aliquo gradu in ecclesiastico praefixus*.** Our twin's second *in* leaves
  *ecclesiastico* without a noun; rendered "set over in some grade in the ecclesiastical". At the
  plate the line start is blotted in this copy's clipped margin, so whether Migne sets the second
  *in* is indeterminate. No patch, no marker.
- **0433D cap. XXII.** *Si quis non acquiescit ut satisfaciat qui intulit injuriam*: construed
  with *qui intulit injuriam* as the subject ("If anyone who inflicted an injury does not consent to
  make satisfaction"), which is what the following Matt 18 quotation (*si … non audierit*) needs.
  The alternative (the injured party refusing satisfaction) is the theme of XXIII.
- **0433D cap. XXV / 0434B cap. XXXII, Migne's cross-notes (b) and (c).** (b) at XXV *saepius
  proprio* says "see below, chap. 32"; (c) at XXXII says another reading has *proprio excommunicetur
  cibo* there, "as above, chap. 25", in place of *excommunicari debet*. Both translated as `[nt:]`;
  the printed text is what the English renders.
- **0434B *excitavit,*** — the plate prints a comma where our twin has a full stop (pending patch).
  The English follows the plate, so XXXII is a single sentence: "If anyone is roused … against him
  who roused him to prayer, … ought to be excommunicated". The subjectless *excommunicari debet* is
  Migne's syntax, not a lost word.
- **0434D *De omni verbo otioso*** — plate *otioso*, twin *otiosa* (pending patch). No English
  exponent either way.
- **Single-witness worn sorts, no marker (see the plate reads):** *Domiuo* 0431C, *stiοendio* 0431D,
  *subjaccbit* 0432C, *com lacrymis* 0433B. The words are *Domino, stipendio, subjacebit, cum*; our
  twin already has them.

## Candidates rejected

- **0431A–0432B tariff contradictions.** *homicida decem vel septem* after *per septem annos vel
  decem*; *Qui cum sorore … septem annos; in nonnullis canonibus … duodecim*: these are alternative
  canons the text itself says it is reporting, not defects. Rendered as printed.
- **0431A *jejunet quindecim hiemes* next to *septem annos***: both kept, winters and years.
- **Negation audit (7a), all carried:** *nequit*, *si non adeo*, *si nec adeo*, *ut nec decem*,
  *nesciat/nesciret* (0428), *in non consecrata* (0430D), *et non possit*, *nunquam … nisi*,
  *et non obtineat* (0431–0432), *non statim* (VII), *non ambulet … nisi* (XIV), *non fecerit … nisi*
  (XVI), *non placent* (XVII), *non praebuerit … ne … audiat … non recepistis* (XVIII), *aut non
  petit, aut non ex animo* (XXI), *non acquiescit … non audierit* (XXII), *noluerit* (XXIII),
  *non sunt … non praesumunt* (XXIV), *quod esse non debet … si non emendaverit* (XXVI), *non vult*
  (XXXI), *et non manducare* (XXXII), *quod esse non debet* (XXXIII), *Nemo … Nihil* (XXXIV). None
  added.
- **0434B `suam,.`** on the plate: a speck, not a mark.
- **(Matth. I) as a transcription error**: rejected, the plate prints it.
