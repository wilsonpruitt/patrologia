## THE FIRST AGE.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1173D]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Adam, when he was CXXX years old, begot Seth. ⟦s2⟧ Seth, being CV years old, begot Enos while Adam was still living and was CCXXXV years old. ⟦s3⟧ Enos indeed, being XC years old, begot Cainan while Adam was still living and was CCCXXV years old. ⟦s4⟧ Cainan, being LXX years old, begot Malaleel while Adam was still living and was CCCXCV years old. ⟦s5⟧ Malaleel, being LXV years old, begot Jared while Adam was still living and [1174D] was CCCCLX years old. ⟦s6⟧ Jared, being CLXII years old, begot Enoch while Adam was still living and was DCXXII years old. ⟦s7⟧ Enoch indeed, being LXV years old, begot Mathusala while Adam was still living and was DCLXXXVII years old. ⟦s8⟧ And Mathusalem, being CLXXXVII years old, begot Lamech while Adam was still living and was DCCCLXXIV years old. ⟦s9⟧ Lamech in fact, being CLXXII years old, begot Noah. ⟦s10⟧ Noah, being D years old, begot Shem. ⟦s11+12⟧ And when Shem was C years old the flood came to pass. [1175A] And here is completed the number of years, a thousand DCLXVI.

## THE SECOND AGE.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ In the second year after the flood, Shem, at CII years, begot Arphaxad. ⟦s2⟧ Arphaxad, being XXXV years old, begot Sale. ⟦s3⟧ Sale, being XXX years old, begot Eber. ⟦s4⟧ Eber, being XXXIV years old, begot Phalech. ⟦s5⟧ Phalech, being XXX years old, begot Reu. ⟦s6⟧ Reu, being XXXII years old, begot Seruch. ⟦s7⟧ Seruch, being XXX years old, begot Nachor. ⟦s8⟧ Nachor, being XXIX years old, begot Thara. ⟦s9⟧ Thara, being LXX years old, begot Abraham. ⟦s10⟧ Here ends the number of the second age, containing CCXCII years; and the years of the two ages together come to a thousand DCCCCXLVIII.

## THE THIRD AGE.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Abraham, when he was C years old, begot Isaac. ⟦s2⟧ Isaac, being LX years old, begot Jacob. ⟦s3⟧ Jacob, being XC years old, begot Joseph, who lived CX years. ⟦s4⟧ From him down to the going out of the children of Israel from Egypt under [1175B] Moses their leader there were CLXV years. ⟦s5⟧ And from the going out of Israel down to Joshua, XL years. ⟦s6⟧ From Joshua, then, down to Othniel there are XXVI years. ⟦s7⟧ And from Othniel down to Aoth, XL years. ⟦s8⟧ From Aoth indeed down to Deborah there are LXXX years. ⟦s9⟧ And from Deborah down to Gideon there are XL years. ⟦s10⟧ And from Gideon down to Abimelech, XL years. ⟦s11⟧ From Abimelech, moreover, down to Tota there are III years. ⟦s12⟧ From Tola indeed down to Jair there are XXIII years. ⟦s13⟧ From Jair down to Jephthah there are XXII years. ⟦s14⟧ And from Jephthah down to Ebesan there are VI years. ⟦s15⟧ From Ebesa, moreover, down to Achialon there are VII years. ⟦s16⟧ From Achialon down to Labdon there are X years. ⟦s16⟧ From Labdon indeed down to Samson there are VIII years. ⟦s17⟧ And from Samson down to Eli, XX years. ⟦s18⟧ From Eli in fact down to Samuel there are XL years. ⟦s19⟧ And from Samuel down to Saul there are XII years. ⟦s20⟧ From Saul, then, down to David there are XXX years. ⟦s21⟧ Here is completed the number of the third age, DCCCCXLII years; and the years of the three ages together are two thousand DCCCXC.

## THE FOURTH AGE.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1175C]

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ From David down to Solomon there are XL years. ⟦s2⟧ From Solomon, moreover, down to Rehoboam XL years are reckoned. ⟦s3⟧ And from Rehoboam down to Abia there are XXII years. ⟦s4⟧ From Abia down to Asa there are III years. ⟦s5⟧ And from Asa down to Jehoshaphat, XLI years. ⟦s6⟧ From Jehoshaphat, moreover, down to Joram there are XXV years. ⟦s7⟧ From Joram down to Azariah there are VIII years. ⟦s8⟧ And from Azariah down to Athaliah there is I year. ⟦s8⟧ From Athaliah in fact down to Joam there are VI years. ⟦s9⟧ From Joa indeed down to Amasiah there are XL years. ⟦s10⟧ And from Amasiah down to Ozias there are XXIX years. ⟦s11⟧ From Ozias in fact down to Joatham there are LII years. ⟦s12⟧ From Joatha, moreover, down to Achat there are XVI years. ⟦s13⟧ And from Achat down to Hezekiah there are XVI years. ⟦s14⟧ From Hezekiah, then, down to Manasseh there are XXIX years. ⟦s15⟧ From Manasseh indeed down to Amon there are LV years. ⟦s16⟧ And from Amon down to Josiah there are II years. ⟦s17⟧ And from Josiah down to Joachim there are XXXI years. ⟦s18⟧ From Joachim down to Zesechiah there are XI years. ⟦s19⟧ From Zesechiah down to Nebuchadnezzar, XI years. ⟦s20⟧ Here ends the number [1175D] of the fourth age, containing CCCCLXXXIII years; and the years of the four ages come to III M CCCLXIII.

## THE FIFTH AGE.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The years of the fifth age down to Cyrus are XXX. ⟦s2⟧ From Cyrus down to Cambyses there are XXX years. ⟦s3⟧ From Cambyses down to Darius, IX years. ⟦s4⟧ From Darius down to Xerxes, XXXV years. ⟦s5⟧ From Xerxes down to Artaxerxes there are XXI years. ⟦s6⟧ And from Artaxerxes down to Darius there are XL years. ⟦s7⟧ From Darius down to Artaxerxes again there are XVIII years. ⟦s8⟧ From Artaxerxes, moreover, down to Artaxerxes again there are XL years. ⟦s9⟧ And from Artaxerxes down to Arses, XXVI years. ⟦s10⟧ From Arses indeed to Darius there are IV years. ⟦s11⟧ From Darius, moreover, down to Alexander there are VI years. ⟦s12⟧ From Alexander in fact down to Ptolemy there are V years. ⟦s12⟧ And from Ptolemy down to Philadelphus there are XL years. ⟦s13⟧ From Philadelphus down to Euergetes there are XXXVIII years. ⟦s14⟧ From Euergetes, moreover, down to Philopator there are XXVI years. ⟦s15⟧ From Philopator in fact down to Epiphanes there are [1176A] XVII years. ⟦s16⟧ And from Epiphanes down to Philometor, XXIV years. ⟦s17⟧ From Philometor in fact down to Euergetes again, XXXV years. ⟦s18⟧ And from Euergetes down to Soter there are XXIX years. ⟦s19⟧ From Soter down to Alexander, XVII years. ⟦s20⟧ From Alexander indeed down to Ptolemy there are X years. ⟦s20⟧ From Ptolemy down to Dionysius, XIII years. ⟦s21⟧ From Dionysius indeed down to Cleopatra there are XXX years. ⟦s22⟧ From Cleopatra down to Julius Caesar, II years. ⟦s23⟧ From Julius down to Octavian Augustus there are V years. ⟦s23⟧ From Octavian down to Christ there are XLII years. ⟦s24⟧ Here ends the fifth age, having DLXXX years; and the years of the five ages together come to III M DCCCCLII.
