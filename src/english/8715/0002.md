## IX. ALBINUS ON THE SEVEN SEALS.

[1169A]

The first seal is the nativity. The second, the baptism. The third, the cross. The fourth, the sepulchre. The fifth, the resurrection. The sixth, the ascension. The seventh, the judgment. These are the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit. The first was wisdom, that Christ should be born of the Virgin without the seed of a man. The second was the spirit of understanding, that in baptism, through those three immersions, sins should be forgiven, as it is said: *Stripping off the old man, putting on the new [n: (Col. III, 9)]*. The third was the spirit of counsel, as Caiaphas says: *It is better that one man should die than that the whole nation perish [n: (Joan. XI, 50)]*. The fourth was the spirit of fortitude, because the body was in the sepulchre, and [1169B] Christ bound the devil in hell, and those souls which were unjustly held there he brought back to the kingdom, and the Lord bit into hell. The fifth was the spirit of knowledge, because when he had risen from the dead, he made us believe and rise again — for we shall all rise again, as it is said: *Arise, O Lord, thou and the ark of thy sanctification [n: (Psal. CXXXI, 8)]*. The sixth was the spirit of piety, because when Christ ascended to heaven, the souls of the just also ascend to him, as Paul says: *I desired [1170A] to be dissolved and to be with Christ [n: (Phil. I, 23)]*. The seventh was the spirit of fear, because when Christ shall come to the judgment, to reward the just, there will be great fear for sinners, as it is said: *Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire [n: (Matth. XXV, 41)]*. And the rest.

These seven gifts of the Holy Spirit were in the seven patriarchs. Wisdom in Adam, because he gave names to all the wild animals, or beasts, and the birds of heaven, and the fishes of the sea, and he was the first prophet, when he said: *This now is bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh [n: (Gen. II, 23)]*. Understanding in Noah, who built the ark in the flood and steered it. Counsel in Abraham, as it is said: *Go forth out of thy land, and from thy kindred, and out of the house of thy Father [n: (Gen. XII, 1)]*. Fortitude in Isaac, [1170B] because he loved his enemies. Knowledge in Jacob, because the angel of the Lord blessed him and said to him: *Thou shalt no more be called Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name [n: (Gen. XXXII, 28)]*. Piety in Moses, because he said: *Blot me out of the book of the living [n: (Exod. XXXII, 32)]*, wherein thou hast written me. Fear in David, because he feared God, when Saul went into the cave to relieve himself, and he said: *Far be it from me, that I should put my hand upon the anointed of the Lord [n: (I Reg. XXVI, 11)]*.
