## XIV.

These words, not unskilled, yet unskilfully arranged by an unskilled man, are necessary for all, from [0198B] him who sits upon the throne down to him who labors at the mill. I write not so much to those to whom I owe nothing but charity, as to those who have been committed to us; yet in such a way that under the name of shepherds and rulers there be understood abbots, and provosts, and priests: whom I earnestly beseech to attend with their whole heart to the exhortation and entreaty of the blessed apostle Peter, in which he says: *Therefore the elders that are [0199A] among you I beseech, who am a fellow elder and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a partaker of that glory which is to be revealed in time to come: feed the flock of God that is among you, taking care of it not by constraint, but willingly, according to God; nor for the sake of filthy lucre, but freely; [0200A] neither as lording it over the clergy, but being made a pattern to the flock from the heart; and when the prince of shepherds shall appear, you shall receive the crown of unfading glory. Amen.*
