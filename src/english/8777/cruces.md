# Cruces — 8777, Agobard of Lyon, *De modo regiminis ecclesiastici*

Garbled or irregular printed readings in Migne (PL 104), rendered literally in
the English per the register rules, and logged here.

## 0191B (chunk 0, ch. VI)

- **Printed:** *Scritura exsequitur*
- **Note:** Misprint for *Scriptura*. Rendered "Scripture." No sense is affected.

## 0193A (chunk 1, ch. IX)

- **Printed:** *sicut Paulas ait*
- **Note:** Misprint for *Paulus*. Rendered "as Paul says."

## 0198A (chunk 1, ch. XIII)

- **Printed:** *sicut et beato Petro summus pastor praecibit dicens*
- **Note:** *praecibit* is a misprint for *praecepit* ("charged, commanded");
  the form as printed is not Latin. Rendered "charged." Alternatively *praeivit*
  ("went before") could be conjectured, but *praecepit* fits the direct command
  that follows (*Pasce agnos meos*).

## 0192B (chunk 1, ch. VIII) — not an error, flagged for the read-through

- **Printed:** *vir cum quo est Deus in visione qua erudivit eum mater sua*
- **Note:** Agobard's compressed allusion to Prov. 31 (Lemuel and his mother).
  Rendered literally; the referent is left implicit as Migne prints it.

## Column-sequence irregularities (chunk 1) — verified against the Latin, not errors

Chunk 1's anchor run is 0192B, 0193A, 0194A, 0195A, 0195B, 0195C, 0196A, 0196B,
0196C, 0197A, 0197B, 0198A. The single-anchor jumps at 0193A → 0194A → 0195A
(no B/C/D for cols 193–194) reflect Migne's plates, where those columns are
largely occupied by the editor's notes. Reproduced verbatim in order; no
anchors invented to fill the gaps. Same for chunk 2, where 0199A is followed
directly by 0200A.
