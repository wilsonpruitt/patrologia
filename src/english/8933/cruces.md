# Cruces — 8933, Rabanus Maurus, *De passione Domini*

- **col 1427C** — printed *tot orpus ejus litteris nigris et rubeis plenum*. Intended *corpus*. Rendered "his whole body full of black and red letters." Dropped initial *c* in the plate.
- **col 1427D** — printed *cujus palchritudinem et fulgorem sol et luna mirantur*. Intended *pulchritudinem*. Rendered "beauty."
- **col 1427B** — Migne prints *[asperis] verbis et verberibus affectum* with the editor's own bracketed supplement. Rendered "with [harsh] words and with lashes," bracket preserved as an editorial mark, not a column anchor.
- **col 1429A** — Migne prints *pro illis [oraret]*, again an editorial bracketed supplement. Rendered "[was praying] for them," bracket preserved.
- **col 1429D** — the quotation *Vos me crucifigitis, gens, tota die* is attributed to Malachi; no such verse stands in Malachi (cf. Isa. LII, 5 / Mal. III, 8-9 for the sense). Translated as printed, attribution left as Migne has it.
- **col 1428A** — *seniores cum clericis legis* where the parallel later at 1429D also reads *clerici legis*. Likely *scribis*; rendered literally "the clerks of the law" in both places for consistency.
