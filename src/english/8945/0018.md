## CHAPTER XXXII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Hear, O ye heavens,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Some think that heaven and earth are summoned to hear as though they were living things, according to that saying: *Who looketh upon the earth and maketh it tremble [n: (Psal. CIII)]*, whereas this belongs to the power of God, [0488B] not to their understanding.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Let it grow.* ⟦s2⟧ He compares the doctrine of the law to rain and to dew, because it demands from the hearts of the faithful the buds of spiritual fruit. ⟦s3⟧ For these are the field and the vineyard of God. ⟦s4⟧ Such is that saying of Isaiah: *As the rain and the snow come down from heaven, and return no more thither, but soak the earth, water the face thereof, and make it to spring [n: (Isa. LV)]*.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The name.* ⟦s2⟧ He shews what the rain is. ⟦s3⟧ For to invoke the name of the Lord is to preach the Gospel: and he who hears it and does it shall possess the fruit of life.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of God.* ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *The heavens and the earth were finished, and all the furniture of them: and God completed on the seventh day his work [n: (Gen. II)]*, which is the redemption of the human race, [0488C] which he shewed to us when, completing the spiritual sabbath, he rested in the sepulchre.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *They have sinned against him,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 55.)] LXX: *The blameworthy sons have sinned not against him.* ⟦s2⟧ For he who sins, etc., *as far as* according to this, that they did not harm him by their sin, but themselves.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And not.* ⟦s2⟧ Because not out of faith. ⟦s3⟧ For *he gave them power to be made the sons of God, to them that believe in his name.* ⟦s4⟧ But to the unbelieving [sic: *In fidelibus*] it is said: *You are of your father the devil.*

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A depraved generation.* ⟦s2⟧ Of which it is said in the Gospel: *A depraved and perverse generation seeketh a sign [n: (Matth. XII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ And elsewhere: *Ye offspring of vipers, who hath shewed you to flee from the wrath to come [n: (Luc. III)]*?

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Is he not,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Who is the one God, creator and founder of all, from whom himself, and through him, and [0488D] in him are all things. ⟦s3⟧ A preposterous order. ⟦s4⟧ For first God created by disposing that it should be, secondly he made by forming, thirdly he possessed by ruling.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He created.* ⟦s2⟧ He created when he founded the matter out of which it might be formed. ⟦s3⟧ Foolish therefore and unwise is he, because he does not worship his Creator and Maker with worthy veneration: forgetful of that benefit whereby God snatched him out of the hand of his enemies and took possession of him, led him through the Red Sea, gave him manna from heaven, he himself, ungrateful, worshipped idols, saying: *These are thy gods, O Israel, that brought thee out of the land of Egypt [n: (Exod. XXXII)]*.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Thy father.* ⟦s2⟧ The teacher of the law and the preacher of the Gospel, by whose doctrine and example, namely, thou art to be instructed.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0489A]VERS. 8.-- *When he divided,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if to say, although the multitude of men is innumerable, the Lord chose the sons of Israel alone as his inheritance, to whom he manifested his power and his secrets by the tokens of the law and of the prophets.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He appointed. [n: (GREG., hom. 4 in Evang.)]* Another edition, etc., *as far as* and they ascend into their lot through a likeness of manner of life.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *But the Lord's portion is his people.* ⟦s2⟧ When God created all the nations, he chose the Israelites alone, whom he glorified with the knowledge of himself by signs and miracles.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *He found.* ⟦s2⟧ Because on Sinai he entered into a covenant with him, gave manna, and brought forth water from the rock, furnished quails; and in the desert, leading them about for forty years, [0489B] he taught them his precepts.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He led about.* ⟦s2⟧ By spreading the Church throughout the world, by directing it into the way of his commandments.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He kept.* ⟦s2⟧ Many were laid low in the desert, but after they had transgressed the commandments of God.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *As the eagle.* ⟦s2⟧ The eagle, according to the natural philosophers, is named from the keenness of its eyes, and sees so sharply that, when it is borne over the seas on unmoving wing and is not visible to human sight, it sees the little fishes swimming in the sea, and, descending like a hurled weapon, drags the prey it has seized with its wings to the shore. ⟦s3⟧ Its young too, as they grow their feathers, it beats with its wings and provokes to fly. ⟦s4⟧ So Christ, exhorting us by words and by examples, carries us forward to the heights, that we may follow whither he has gone before; whence: *Wheresoever the body shall be, there shall the eagles also be gathered together (Matth. [0489C] XXIV)*. ⟦s5⟧ Exalted also above the heavens and withdrawn from human sight, he sees with the eyes of mercy those swimming in the sea of this world, and with the wings of love he draws them to the shore of eternal security. ⟦s6⟧ Whence: *If I be lifted up from the earth, I will draw all things to myself [n: (Joan. XII)]*.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As the eagle,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The eagle, as they say, when it sees its young growing their feathers, turns them toward the sun, and nourishes the one that gazes upon the rays of the sun with unflinching eye; and if it turns its eyes aside, it neglects it as degenerate; and it provokes its young to fly, and holds up the weary on its wings: so God invites us to the true Sun, has compassion on our infirmities, and holds us up on the wings of grace.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Provoking.* --To the possessing of the land of Canaan the Lord provoked his people, helped them, and protected them from [0489D] their enemies.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *The Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ Father and Son and Holy Spirit are one God. ⟦s3⟧ He does not say the Father alone, lest he exclude the Son or the Holy Spirit, but the Lord alone, that is, God.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ The heretics have as many lords as they cultivate errors.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *High.* ⟦s2⟧ Palestine, which is mountainous; Egypt is flat and low.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The Church, which is lofty in virtue and in knowledge. ⟦s2⟧ In her the fruit of the fields is eaten, because there the good seed springs up, that is, the word of God. ⟦s3⟧ There is the thirtyfold, the sixtyfold and the hundredfold fruit, that each may eat sufficiently according to his capacity.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0490A] *And that he might suck. [n: (GREG., hom. 26 in Evang.)] That he might suck honey out of the rock, and oil out of the hardest stone.* ⟦s2⟧ That this was done according to the history is nowhere read, etc., *as far as* the gift of the devil through the unction of the Holy Spirit.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Honey indeed, to the letter, signifies an abundance of all things.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Of the children.* ⟦s2⟧ Basan is a region which abounds in cattle on account of its pastures: whence it was handed over to the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half tribe of Manasses, because they had many cattle.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And goats.* ⟦s2⟧ Which signify penitents, because they were offered for sin. ⟦s3⟧ It is not enough for the faithful to receive the gifts of virtues, unless they be eager to do penance for what they have committed. ⟦s4⟧ Whence: *Wash me yet more from my iniquity [n: (Psal. L)]*.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0490B]He calls those goats who by their own example minister to others the works of penance, although they have not committed grave things: for they bewail the sins of their thoughts as though they were deeds.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With the marrow of wheat.* ⟦s2⟧ Of the body of Christ; whence: *Unless the grain of wheat falling into the ground,* etc. [n: (Joan. XII)].

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *The beloved grew fat,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Just as the Jews, grown fat with benefits, became idolaters: so certain Christians turn the received mystery of the body and blood of the Lord to their own perdition.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *They provoked.* ⟦s2⟧ Some become heretics, others idolaters, who out of their heart fashion for themselves idols and abominations.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *New and lately come.* ⟦s2⟧ Withdrawing from the worship of God, they found for themselves gods whom their fathers had [0490C] worshipped.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *And he said: I will hide,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Nothing is more grievous than to be deprived of the vision of God; whence: *Cast me not away from thy face [n: (Psal. L)]*. ⟦s3⟧ Likewise: *Turn not away thy face,* etc. [n: (Psal. LXXXVIII.)]

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They have provoked me,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if to say: just as they have provoked me by worshipping a multitude of false gods and by rejecting me, so I will provoke them by taking to myself a multitude of nations and by rejecting them.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And I will provoke,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ By handing them over to the Gentiles, who once were not a people, but now are the people of God. ⟦s3⟧ In like manner Christians who, forsaking the truth, turn aside to errors, are handed over to the malignant spirits, who lost their angelic dignity. ⟦s4⟧ Who are well called a foolish nation, hating justice, loving iniquity.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0490D]VERS. 22.-- *A fire is kindled,* etc. [n: (GREG. lib. XVIII Moral., cap. 12.)] Of those whom the scourges condemn and do not set free, it is written: *Thou hast struck them and they have not grieved,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ For these the scourges begin in this life, and shall remain unto eternity; whence Moses: *A fire is kindled in his wrath, and shall burn even to the lowest hell and downward.* ⟦s3⟧ For of the present striking it is said: *A fire is kindled in my wrath [n: (Jer. XV)]*; of the eternal there follows: *And shall burn even to the lowest hell and downward [n: (Psal. LXXXVIII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ Yet God does not judge twice for the same thing: for it is one scourge which begins in time, and is consummated in eternal torments.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And it shall devour the earth.* ⟦s2⟧ Others: *they shall eat the earth and the things springing from it,* that is, the flesh, on account of the works of lust, a threefold flame [cj: *terna*; read *aeterna*, "eternal"] [0491A] shall burn; for the things springing from the flesh are the works of carnal concupiscence, which are fornication, uncleanness and the like.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *Birds with their biting,* Which ate the seed along the way.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The teeth of beasts,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Beasts and serpents drag the reprobate along the ground with raging teeth, when what they first persuaded carnal men by flattery in order to seduce them, they now furiously demand of sinners in order to torment them.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Without.* ⟦s2⟧ The burning of eternal fire shall torture the body, and the greatness of anguish the mind; whence: *Their worm shall not die, and their fire shall not be quenched [n: (Isa. LVI)]*.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The young man.* ⟦s2⟧ No age, namely, shall be spared, because idolatry has defiled [0491B] all.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *I will make them cease from among men.* ⟦s2⟧ They were cast out of their land and dispersed through the whole world. ⟦s3⟧ But generally sinners shall be taken away from the land of the saints, and their memory; whence: *Therefore thy God shall destroy thee in the end, he shall pluck thee out, and shall lead thee out of the land of the living,* etc. [n: (Psal. LI.)]

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *I have deferred.* ⟦s2⟧ Lest they should blaspheme my name; nor yet have I taken away vengeance.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *How.* ⟦s2⟧ He shews so great a fear of the Jewish nation, after they had sinned against the Lord, that one of their enemies put a thousand of them to flight, and two ten thousand — that is, a few put very many to flight.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Judges.* ⟦s2⟧ Approvers; even they who worship idols understand that there is one God, who made [0491C] heaven and earth.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *Of the vineyard,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He signifies the wickedness of heretics, who received pure spiritual doctrine from the holy teachers, but corrupted it with their own venom, and perverted it into the bitterness of gall and wormwood. ⟦s3⟧ Often is Jerusalem compared to Sodom and Gomorrah; whence: *Hear the word of the Lord, ye princes of Sodom of Gomorrah [n: (Isa. I)]* [var: Vulg. *principes Sodomorum … populus Gomorrhae*, "ye princes of Sodom … ye people of Gomorrah"].

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Most bitter grapes.* ⟦s2⟧ Their fruits, namely their works, are most bitter, for they offered wine mingled with gall

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *The gall of dragons.* ⟦s2⟧ As if they retained within themselves the venom of malice, lest they should receive the medicine of salvation and of correction. ⟦s3⟧ Heretics, like serpents, hide their bitterness and their venom, that they may wound the unwary.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0491D]VERS. 35.-- *Revenge is mine,* etc, Paul took from this place: *Revenge to me, and I will repay [n: (Rom. XII)]* according to merits.

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In time.* ⟦s2⟧ As if to say, *when I shall take a time, I will judge justices [n: (Psal. LXXIV)]*. ⟦s3⟧ Hence he said to Moses praying for the people: *In the day of revenge I will visit this sin also of theirs [n: (Exod. XXXII)]*

⟦p51⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The day of destruction is at hand.* ⟦s2⟧ For each one. ⟦s3⟧ For whatever is ended is very short, if it be compared to eternity. ⟦s4⟧ According to the history the vengeance upon the Jews makes haste, because in a short time they lost their place and their nation and all their prosperity, because they slew the prophets and the Saviour.

⟦p52⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0492A]VERS. 36.-- *He shall judge.* ⟦s2⟧ By separating them from the wicked, according to that saying: *Judge me, O God, and distinguish my cause [n: (Psal. XLII)]*.

⟦p53⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He shall see that the hand is weakened. ⟦s2⟧ Whatsoever thy hand is able to do, do it earnestly, because neither work, nor reason, nor wisdom is among the dead [n: (Eccle. IX)]*.

⟦p54⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *Where are their gods.* ⟦s2⟧ As if to say, the idols in which they trusted profit nothing, nor riches nor honours nor the pleasures of the world; for they carry nothing with them of all their labour.

⟦p55⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 39.-- *See ye that I alone am,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ A general admonition, as if to say: idolatry or heresy being left behind and all uncleannesses despised, be ye converted to the true God.

⟦p56⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I will kill. [n: (GREG., lib. VII Moral., cap. 18.)]* [0492B] He kills that he may make alive, he strikes that he may heal, etc., *as far as* and makes them sensible through the ardour of charity.
