## CHAPTER II.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Son, coming,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He has shown what is to be avoided; now he shows what is to be done, saying: *Son, when thou comest to the service of God, stand in justice and in fear.* ⟦s3⟧ For he who wishes to withdraw himself from the enticements of the world and to live godly in Christ suffers persecution. ⟦s4⟧ For while he makes ready to be girded for the divine service, he stirs up the war of the devil against himself; and while he subdues some vices, he struggles against others, and by the divine dispensation it is so ordered that he both overcomes and is overcome, lest he be lifted up. ⟦s5⟧ But [1186C] because humility and patience are necessary to him, there follows, *Humble thy heart, and endure,* etc.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Have patience.* ⟦s2⟧ As if to say: *In your patience you shall possess your souls [n: (Luc. XXI)]*, because, namely, patience is the root and the keeper of all the virtues. ⟦s3⟧ And it is to be known that patience is exercised in three ways. ⟦s4⟧ For by God we are scourged, but care must be taken that we do not murmur; from our neighbour we bear persecutions and losses and insults, and care must be taken that we do not render evil for evil; by the adversary we are tempted, but we must look ahead lest we be bent to the delight of sin or to consent to it, or seek present things in return for these, but rather await things to come.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Believe God, and he will recover thee.* [n: (RAB.)] [1186D] The afflicted man must believe, and direct the steps of his works in the sight of God, and hope in him; whence: *Reveal thy way to the Lord, and hope in him, and he will do it. [n: (Psal. XXXVI)]*. ⟦s2⟧ Hence Jeremiah says: *Blessed is he that trusteth in the Lord, and God shall be his confidence [n: (Jer. XVII)]*, etc. [var: Vulg. *Benedictus vir qui confidit in Domino, et erit Dominus fiducia ejus*]

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Ye that fear the Lord,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *The Lord is well pleased with them that fear him, and in them that hope upon his mercy [n: (Psal. CXLVI)]*. ⟦s3⟧ For he who truly fears patiently awaits his mercy: *But they that forsake God shall be confounded, and shall be written in the earth [n: (Jer. XVII)]*. [var: Vulg. *omnes qui te derelinquunt confundentur; recedentes a te in terra scribentur*]

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Ye that fear the Lord, believe him.* ⟦s2⟧ He exhorts them that fear the Lord to believe him, that they may come to a certain reward; whence: *For the just man liveth [1187A] by faith [n: (Rom. I)]*; whence: *Be of good heart, son, thy sins are forgiven thee [n: (Matth. IX)]*. ⟦s3⟧ And elsewhere: *Thy faith hath made thee whole [n: (Ibid., X)]*.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Ye that fear the Lord, hope in him.* ⟦s2⟧ He commends three things to them that fear God: to believe, to hope, to love; because by faith, hope and charity God is truly worshipped, and one comes to contemplation, which is eternal blessedness.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Look ye,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] Here too he says three things, etc., *as far as* because *a sacrifice to God is an afflicted spirit [n: (Psal. L)]*, etc. ⟦s2⟧ And, *God is nigh unto all that call upon him in truth [n: (Psal. CXLIV)]*. [var: Vulg. *Prope est Dominus omnibus invocantibus eum … in veritate*]

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Woe to them that are of a double heart,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He warned that no one should come to God with a double heart; but now he says, *Woe to them that are of a double heart and of wicked lips.* ⟦s3⟧ For he who [1187B] thinks and speaks crooked things, and persists in crooked works, unless he repent, shall perish for ever. ⟦s4⟧ *Going into the land by two ways* is he who does evil and hopes for good; or, he who follows the works of the flesh and judges that he is bringing to perfection the sanctification of the spirit in fear; or, he who displays in work what is God's, and seeks in thought what is the world's. ⟦s5⟧ Such men indeed are inconstant in all their ways, because they are easily terrified in adversity and entangled in prosperity.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *To them that are of a dissolute heart,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] He still teaches that error is to be avoided, etc., *as far as* to these, who do not acquiesce in the truth but believe iniquity, wrath and indignation, etc.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *To look upon,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Although God looks upon all things at every time, yet he is then said to look upon [1187C] when he is discerned to approve or to disapprove something.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *They that fear the Lord will not be incredulous,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He treats alternately of the love and the affection of God, *because the fear of the Lord is holy, enduring for ever and ever [n: (Psal. XVIII)]*, *and every one that loveth is born of God, and knoweth God [n: (I Joan. IV)]*. ⟦s3⟧ But he who fears God, and loves, and believes the Scriptures, keeps the precepts of God by working them, and always by meditating seeks the things that are well pleasing to him: he shall therefore be filled with the wisdom and the charity of God. ⟦s4⟧ *For the end of the commandment is charity, and he that loveth his neighbour hath fulfilled the law.*

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *They that fear the Lord will prepare,* etc. ⟦s2+3⟧ Happy the soul which daily cleanses its heart that it may receive God as its indweller; the possessor of him lacks no good thing, because he has within himself the author of all good things. [1187D] And because for this the keeping of the commandments is necessary, and the patience of good work, by which one comes to the knowledge of the truth, there follows: *They that fear the Lord keep his commandments,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ The just man is first of all his own accuser. ⟦s5⟧ For as a reward remains for humility, so a punishment remains for pride; whence John: *If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves [n: (II Joan. I)]*. ⟦s6⟧ He who is truly wise is a true keeper of justice, because wisdom dictates the rule of justice.

## CHAPTER III.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The judgment of the father.* ⟦s2⟧ Of God, *of whom all fatherhood in heaven and on earth.* ⟦s3⟧ Because from him are all things. ⟦s4⟧ He loves the things which he has made, and loves that they should abide in him in whom they were made; and whoever hears his judgment, and does his precepts, [1188A] shall be saved: whose commandment it is that reverence be shown also to a temporal father and mother; whence: *Honour thy father [n: (Deut. V)]*, etc.; whence there follows: *He that honoureth his father.* ⟦s5⟧ He for whom he appointed God as his honour ought not to be dishonoured by his sons: because God will by his judgment search out how his precept is kept.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *He that loveth.* ⟦s2⟧ He, namely, who keeps the commandments and abstains from sins, and yet does not cease to pray for transgressors, because he knows that a merciful judge hears prayers at every time.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *In the children.* [n: (RAB.)] In offspring or in disciples, etc., *as far as* to honour parents is the first commandment with a promise.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1188B]VERS. 9.-- *In work and word,* not in the womb, namely, whence it is shown that the spiritual father also is most worthy of honour; whence Paul: *Through the Gospel I have begotten you [n: (I Cor. IV)]*. ⟦s2⟧ And elsewhere: *Let the priests that rule well be esteemed worthy of double honour, especially they who labour in doctrine and word [n: (I Tim. V)]*. [var: Vulg. *in verbo et doctrina*, in the reverse order]

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *The blessing of the father,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] The patriarchs blessed their sons, etc., *as far as* they most savagely tore the mockers.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Alms.* ⟦s2⟧ Namely, that which blots out sin. ⟦s3⟧ For the victim which is offered for sin is called sin. ⟦s4⟧ And he who has mercy on his parents, or on the rest of the poor, shall receive the reward of justice, absolved from his sins. ⟦s5⟧ *Blessed are the merciful, for [n: (Matth. V)]*, etc. ⟦s6⟧ And elsewhere: *He lendeth to the Lord that hath mercy on the poor.* [1188C] [n: (Prov. XIX)]. ⟦s7⟧ Hence Daniel to Nabuchodonosor: *Let my counsel, O king, be pleasing to thee: redeem thou thy sins with alms, and thy iniquities with works of mercy to the poor [n: (Dan. IV)]*, if perhaps God may forgive thy offences. ⟦s8⟧ Hence the Lord says: *Give alms, and all things are clean unto you [n: (Luc. XI)]*.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Son, in meekness.* ⟦s2⟧ Meekness and humility bring forth grace; pride and rage bring forth ruin; whence: *Learn of me, because I am meek and humble of heart [n: (Matth. XI)]*, etc. ⟦s3⟧ And elsewhere: *He that exalteth himself shall be humbled [n: (Luc. XIV)]*, etc.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *The greater thou art,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] Above he taught meekness, etc., *as far as* it is enough for a man to think devoutly upon, and to fulfil in work, those things which God has commanded.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1188D]VERS. 24.-- *In superfluous things,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] That is, that thou search not superfluously, etc., *as far as* disputing they fall into errors, and depart from the truth.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *A hard heart.* ⟦s2⟧ Because *according to thy hardness thou treasurest up to thyself wrath in the day of judgment [n: (Rom. II)]*. [var: Vulg. *in die irae*, "in the day of wrath"]

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *A heart going in.* ⟦s2⟧ Which takes no care, nor is at pains to avoid, and because it loves those things which lead to destruction, such as heresies and profane novelties; whence there follows: *A heart that goeth two ways shall not have success,* etc.
