## CHAPTER IV.

VERS. 1.-- *Son, the alms of the poor,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] How much alms avails, etc., *as far as* he who from the heart rebukes in words or in deeds.

[1189A]VERS. 3.-- *The heart of the needy,* etc. Alms is not to be put off, when the means of giving is at hand; whence: *Say not to thy friend: Go, and come again, when thou canst give at once [n: (Prov. III)]*. [var: Vulg. *cras dabo tibi: cum statim possis dare*] And elsewhere: *He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: he that hath mercy on the poor shall be blessed [n: (Ibid. XIV)]*. And alms is to be done not out of necessity, *for God loveth a cheerful giver [n: (I Cor. IX)]*. And elsewhere: The poor widow cast more than all into the treasury, because God does not weigh the quantity of the gift, but the devotion of the heart.

VERS. 5.-- *Turn not away from the needy,* etc. The poor man is not to be exasperated by anger, but to be appeased by gentle speech, *for the anger of man worketh not the justice of God [n: (Jac. I)]*. And, *He that stoppeth his ear at the cry of the poor shall cry, and the Lord shall not hear him [n: (Prov. XXI)]*. [var: Vulg. *aurem suam … et ipse clamabit, et non exaudietur*] [1189B] For he who is able to give ought to give: because a hidden gift quenches anger, and a present in the bosom quenches the greatest indignation. But he who is not able, a good word is better for him than the best gift. *But he that hath the substance of the world, and shall see his brother in need, and shall shut up his bowels from him, how doth the charity of God abide in him? [n: (I Joan. III.)]* [var: Vulg. *substantiam hujus mundi … necessitatem habere*] For faith, if it have not works, is dead. Let us not therefore love in tongue and in word, but in deed and in truth.

VERS. 7.-- *To the congregation of the poor.* In these things meekness and humility are commended, because meekness despises no one, humility subjects itself to all. And it becomes a Christian to be affable and gentle toward his equals and inferiors, and humble toward his elders; whence: *Lord, my heart is not exalted, neither [1189C] are my eyes lofty [n: (Psal. CXXX)]*.

VERS. 9.-- *Deliver,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] As he said that bounty toward the poor is alms, so he shows that the deliverance of one unjustly oppressed is mercy; whence, *Deliver them that are led to death.*

VERS. 10.-- *In judging.* [n: (RAB.)] No kind of alms, etc., *as far as* so also the solace of a husband to a widow.

VERS. 13.-- *And they that watch,* etc. *Blessed is the man that heareth me, and that watcheth before my doors daily, and waiteth at the posts of my door [n: (Prov. VIII)]*. And by the doors thou canst, he understands the Scriptures and their teachers.

VERS. 15.-- *They that serve,* etc. He who loves him that [1189D] begot will love him that is born of God, and whom the Father loves, the Son loves; whence: He that loveth me shall be loved by my Father, and I will love him. But because *the proof of love is the showing of the work,* there follows: *He that heareth her shall judge the nations.* Whence: *When the Son of man shall sit on the seat of his majesty, you also shall sit on twelve seats, judging the twelve tribes of Israel [n: (Matth. XIX)]*.

VERS. 19.-- *Fear and dread,* etc. Wisdom dwelling within directs works; in various temptations it prepares a man, by exercising and proving him, for the joys of the life to come; which is confirmed by this sentence: *Fear and dread and proving,* etc. Because tribulation works patience, and patience trial, and trial hope, [1190A] which confoundeth not, because the charity of God is poured forth in the saints through the Holy Spirit.

VERS. 23.-- *Son, keep the time,* etc. Above he commended the gift of wisdom; now he shows the deed of wisdom herself. For whoever keeps the precepts of wisdom is wary in the change of times, and does not forsake the way of justice in the time of persecution and of distress. Therefore wisdom exhorts her sons to keep the time, because there is a time for everything under heaven: and there is a time of war, and a time of peace.

VERS. 28.-- *Hide not thy wisdom,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] Wisdom is not to be hidden from those who are willing to hear. For he that hideth up corn shall be cursed among the people, but a blessing is upon the head of them that sell; whence: *Do business, till I come [n: (Luc. XIX)]*. [var: Vulg. *Negotiamini*, "trade ye"]

[1190B]VERS. 30.-- *Contradict not.* Obedience is due to superiors, if they agree with truth and justice, and not otherwise; but for right faith and true religion, in which is the soul's salvation, one must contend; whence: *If thy eye scandalize thee, pluck it out and cast it from thee [n: (Matth. V)]*. And, *If any man will come after me, let him deny [n: (Luc. IX)]*, etc.

VERS. 34.-- *Be not hasty in thy tongue.* [n: (RAB.)] Death and life, etc., *as far as* it is written: *The kingdom of God is not in speech, but in power [n: (I Cor. IV)]*.

VERS. 35.-- *Be not as a lion.* In the Church there are heretics like lions, who by the glibness of the tongue lead astray the hearts of the innocent, and by the insolence of their manners trouble the meekness of the simple.

## CHAPTER V.

[1190C]VERS. 11.-- *Winnow not thyself,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] He reproves levity, etc., *as far as* he shows that the doubleness of their teaching, in which they now put forward true things and now false, proves them to be sinners.

VERS. 13.-- *Be meek.* It is good to hear meekly, and to receive the word of truth, and to utter it modestly and prudently; whence: *Let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak and slow to anger. [n: (Jac. I)]*. And a little after: *With meekness receive the ingrafted word, which is able to save your souls [n: (Ibid.)]* In the Proverbs also it is said: *Hast thou seen a man swift to speak [sic: loquendeum]? folly is rather to be looked for than his correction [n: (Prov. XXIX)]*. [var: Vulg. *illius correptio*, "his reproof"]
