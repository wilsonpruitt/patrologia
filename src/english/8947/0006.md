## CHAPTER XV.

VERS. 3.-- *The water of wisdom.* [n: (RAB.)] The grace of the Holy Spirit: whence, *He that shall drink the water that I will give you, [1199B] it shall become in him a fountain of water springing up into life everlasting [n: (Joan. XIV)]*, that is, he shall not yield to persecution, or to heretical depravity. [var: Vulg. *qui autem biberit ex aqua quam ego dabo ei, non sitiet in aeternum: sed aqua quam ego dabo ei, fiet in eo fons aquae salientis in vitam aeternam*]

VERS. 5.-- *And in the midst of the Church.* He signifies the apostles, the preachers of the Gospel, whom the wisdom of God, that is, Christ, set over the Church.

VERS. 7.-- *Foolish men,* etc. The pagans, and the idolaters and the Jews, who crucify the Son of God, following the shadow of the law and spurning the truth of the Gospel: or the heretics, who pervert the Scriptures.

VERS. 8.-- *And truthful men.* Everyone that is of the truth heareth my voice.

VERS. 9.-- *In the mouth of a sinner.* Of the heretics, who despise the teaching of the Fathers and deceive the simple. God envies the gift of his bounty to no one, who [1199C] willeth all men to be saved.

VERS. 12.-- *Say not.* [n: (ID.)] Our evils are not to be imputed to God, to whom they are not necessary: *God is no tempter of evils [n: (Jacob. I)]*.

VERS. 14.-- *He made man* to his own image and likeness, and gave him free will.

VERS. 16.-- *If thou wilt.* That is, if thou wilt fulfil the works and the commandments of God, thou shalt have consolation: if thou shalt despise them, the burning of Gehenna.

VERS. 19.-- *For the wisdom of God is great.* [n: (ID.)] No one can, etc., *as far as* let a man do nothing but what is worthy of the divine sight.

VERS. 21.-- *He hath commanded no man.* God is not the author of sinning, nor does he grant a space in which a man [1199D] might sin unpunished; he always enjoins good things, and forbids evil ones. That alone pleases him which is good, but unfaithfulness and unprofitableness displease him, *for God is just, and hath loved justice. [n: (Psal. X.)]* [var: Vulg. *justus Dominus*, "the Lord is just"]

## CHAPTER XVI.

VERS. 5.-- *By one man of sense a country shall be inhabited.* [n: (RAB.)] He who according to the sound rule of Scripture continues in the path of justice by faith and by work.

*By three ungodly men it shall be deserted [sic: descretur].* Namely, they who by thought, by speech, by work sequester themselves from the fellowship of the good. [var: Vulg. *tribus impiorum deseretur*, "the tribe of the ungodly shall be made desolate"]

VERS. 6.-- *Many such things hath the eye seen.* The more ardently one learns the law of God, so much the more is he enlightened by the light of knowledge: whence, *He that hath my commandments and [1200A] keepeth them, he it is that loveth me. And he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father: and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him [n: (Joan. XIV)]*.

VERS. 8.-- *They prayed not for their sins.* [n: (RAB.)] The ancient giants, entangled in earthly desires and weighed down by the burden of sins; or the heretics, who trust in their own strength, and glory in the riches of dialectic and of rhetoric.

VERS. 12.-- *For mercy and wrath are with him.* [n: (RAB.)] The divine censure keeps an equality, etc., *as far as: He that followeth justice and mercy shall find life and justice and glory [n: (Prov. XXI)]*

VERS. 16.-- *Say not: I shall be hidden from God.* He reproves those who believe that God has no care for mortal things: whence Eliphaz, *For what doth God know? And he judgeth as it were through [1200B] a mist. The clouds are his covert, and he doth not consider our things [n: (Job. XXII)]*. Hence Paul saith, *The word of God is living and effectual, and more piercing than any two-edged sword, and reaching unto the division of the soul and the spirit, of the joints also and the marrow, and a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart [n: (Hebr. IV)]*.

VERS. 18.-- *Behold the heaven, and the heavens of heavens.* As if: If he dispenses and comprehends the greater things according to his will, the lesser cannot lie hidden from him, at whose beck all things quake and tremble; and there is none who can resist his will.

VERS. 22.-- *And the works of justice.* No one can declare the height of his justice, nor bear the strictness of his justice. *From certain men; and the questioning [sic: interrogatia] of men,* etc. From the idolaters, the heretics, or the false catholics: whence, [1200C] *For not the hearers of the law are justified, but the doers [n: (Rom. II)]*. [var: Vulg. *interrogatio omnium*, "the questioning of all"]

VERS. 25.-- *And I will speak in equity.* [n: (RAB.)] He who with intent heart desires, etc., *as far as* but openly manifests the state of the holy Church.

VERS. 26.-- *He distinguished their parts.* He separated the origin of the several nations through the diverse parts of the world, that each nation, named by the word of its own parent, might possess its proper seats. But mystically the divine wisdom, in founding the Church, spread it abroad through the parts of the whole globe, and placed it among the several nations: so that in diverse places it might claim diverse names for itself, whence John in the Apocalypse: *To the angel of the Church of Smyrna write [n: (Apoc. II)]*, and of Pergamus and of Thyatira and the rest.

VERS. 29.-- *Be not incredulous to his word.* He had said [1200D] above how from the beginning God ordered his works in knowledge and in judgment: now, a sentence being interposed, he exhorts the hearer, lest, being incredulous to his word, he be bent away from the state of rectitude; and he returns to the narration, showing how the clemency of God repaired the human race, thus: *After these things God looked upon the earth,* etc.

VERS. 31.-- *He hath declared,* the secrets of thought: whence, *Thou hast understood my thoughts afar off [n: (Psal. CXXXVIII)]*. And, *The Lord knoweth the thoughts of men [n: (Psal. XCIII)]*.

## CHAPTER XVII.

VERS. 1.-- *God created.* [n: (RAB.)] That he may so live, be wise, etc., *as far as* so let a man have charity, that he may be good and just; etc.

[1201A]VERS. 2.-- *And he clothed him according to himself,* etc. Whence: *Put ye on the new man, who according to God is created in justice and holiness of truth [n: (Ephes. IX)]*.

VERS. 3.-- *And he gave him power.* Because in this also man was made to the image of God, in that he excels the irrational creatures by reason, and rules them and has dominion over them, and acknowledges the creator of all. But because he understood not his own honor, *he is compared to senseless beasts [n: (Psal. XLVIII)]*.

VERS. 5.-- *He created out of him.* As from the side of the sleeping Adam Eve was formed, so from the side of Christ dying on the cross there went out blood and water, out of which the Church is formed.

VERS. 9.-- *And the law of life.* The natural law, which he gave to every man, that he should be subject to his creator, and should keep in himself the honorableness of good [1201B] works. Or, the law of the letter, which he willed to be written for the correction of morals and for the repairing of the natural law; for this is what the natural and the literal law intimates, that we should love God with the whole heart, the whole soul, the whole strength: and should keep his commandments in the love of God and of our neighbor.

VERS. 13-- *Before him.* [n: (RAB.)] Whence: *The Lord knoweth the way of the just, and the way of the ungodly shall perish [n: (Psal. I)]*.

VERS. 14.-- *A ruler.* The angels, to whom God committed the keeping of the several nations! Whence, *He appointed the bounds of the nations according to the number of the angels of God. [n: (Deut. XXXII.)]* [var: Vulg. *constituit terminos populorum juxta numerum filiorum Israel*, "he appointed the bounds of peoples according to the number of the children of Israel"] Or, the teachers whom he sent to preach.

VERS. 21.-- *Be converted.* The condition of man being shown, namely how he was created to the image of God, [1201C] and set before the rest of the creatures: he exhorts that after the fall of sin he return to his maker, leaving the error by which he deceived himself; and that he entreat God for his offenses, and with his whole mind flee back to the divine mercy, spurning idolatry and the deceit of diabolical servitude. Let him learn also the rule of God's commandments, that he may remain in the lot of holiness to which he was chosen, and may persevere in the praises of the Most High.

VERS. 27.-- *Thou shalt confess while living.* [n: (RAB.)] In the present life, after which there is no time for working, but for receiving the reward: Whence, *Run while you have the light, lest the darkness overtake you [n: (Joan. XII)]*. [var: Vulg. *Ambulate dum lucem habetis, ut non vos tenebrae comprehendant*, "Walk whilst you have the light, that the darkness overtake you not"]

VERS. 30.-- *What is brighter than the sun?* [n: (ID.)] He shows that the fabric of the world is transitory: nor does the bodily light remain the same perpetually; how much less the vain [1201D] thought of human frailty.
