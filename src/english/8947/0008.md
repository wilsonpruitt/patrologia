## CHAPTER XX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *How good it is.* [n: (RAB.)] He has forbidden anger in judgment, etc., *as far as* and all teaching is discerned through patience.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Lust.* ⟦s2⟧ He proves by an example that it is good not to be angry: because, as the lust of a eunuch defiles a virgin, and begets no offspring: so the violence of the unjust man counterfeits judgment, but keeps not the measure of equity.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5, 6.-- *There is one that keepeth silence.* ⟦s2⟧ Here the modest man is praised, and [1204B] the rash man is blamed. ⟦s3⟧ *But there is one that keepeth silence,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ He sets forth what kind of silence he praises. ⟦s5⟧ For some are silent because they know not how to speak: but others because they know that there is a time of keeping silence, and a time of speaking.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *A wise man,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The prudent moderate their words, and observe the time: but the fool utters his whole spirit.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *There is that redeemeth many things,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] He, namely, who by the confession of humility is loosed from all guilt. ⟦s2⟧ *And restoring,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ When in the place of vices he substitutes a manifold number of virtues: That *where sin abounded, grace may more abound [n: (Rom. V)]*.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *For his eyes,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That is, the intention, namely, stained with divers wickednesses, always ready to do harm. ⟦s3⟧ For the septenary signifies universality.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1204C]VERS. 16.-- *To-day one lendeth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He shows the instability of the heretics: because they violently wrest the meaning of their own error from their hearers, and therefore they are hateful to God and to men. ⟦s3⟧ Or, he who from an alms seeks a repayment in the present, makes himself hateful: whence, *Call the poor and the weak, the blind, the lame, and thou shalt be blessed: because they have not wherewith to make thee recompense. ⟦s4⟧ But recompense shall be made thee at the resurrection of the just [n: (Luc. XIV)]*. [var: Vulg. *voca pauperes, debiles, claudos, et caecos: et beatus eris*, "call the poor, the weak, the lame, and the blind: and thou shalt be blessed"]

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *The senseless man shall have no friend,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because he seeks the reward of his labor in this life, or the heretic who covets human glory.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *How often and how many,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Detestable is the assembly of the heretics, because they interpret the Scripture of God in a depraved sense, and preach their own exposition wickedly [1204D].

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *The slip of a false tongue,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As on the levelness of a pavement, where there are no stumbling-blocks, one falls without cause: so the understanding of the heretics, blinded by its own wickedness, finds a fall in the path of truth, which lies open.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *A man acharis,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Without the grace of the Holy Spirit; or bitter, because he instils into his hearers a bitter and harmful sense.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *The parable of the senseless man shall be rejected,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] The Jews, who still think, etc., *as far as* the heretics, who by sophistical arguments strive to overturn the truth of the Gospel.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *There is that is hindered.* ⟦s2⟧ The falsity [1205A] of the heretics being reproved, he blames the intention of a depraved will. ⟦s3⟧ For there are those who, when they cannot sin in act, are unwilling to rest in the will.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *There is that shall lose his own soul.* ⟦s2⟧ Because, fearing the power of this world, he dares not profess the truth of the Gospel: whence, *He that shall be ashamed of me and of my words, of him shall the Son of man be ashamed [n: (Luc. IX)]*.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *There is that for shame,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ There are those who promise many things, and render few, and make enemies for themselves of those to whom they promise by lying.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *A thief is better.* ⟦s2⟧ He does not excuse the thief, but by the comparison of him he aggravates the assiduity of lying. ⟦s3⟧ But mystically the thieves are the Jews, to whom it is said, *Thou that preachest that men should not steal, stealest [n: (Rom. II)]*. ⟦s4⟧ But the lying heretics are worse, because after they received [1205B] the grace of baptism they have returned to the vomit, and have done despite to the Spirit of grace.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *A wise man in words,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because he shows the wisdom of the mind in things well said: whence, *A good man out of the good treasure of his heart bringeth forth good [n: (Matth. XII)]*. [var: Vulg. *de bono thesauro profert bona*, "out of the good treasure bringeth forth good things"]

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *Wisdom that is hid.* ⟦s2⟧ There is no profit of a treasure which is hidden and is not known, nor of wisdom hidden without discretion.

## CHAPTER XXI.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Son, hast thou sinned,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] He gives counsel, when we sin: *For there is no man that doth good, and sinneth not [n: (Eccle. VII)]*, that we sin not again; but that we do penance for the things past, entreating that they may be forgiven us.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1205C]VERS. 2.-- *Of a serpent.* [n: (ID.)] Of the devil, who always haunts the shadows of sin, and reigns in the children of darkness.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The teeth of a lion,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Our adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, goeth about seeking whom he may devour [n: (I Petr. V)]*. [var: Vulg. *adversarius vester*, "your adversary"]

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Chiding and injuries,* etc. [n: (ID.)] By concord the smallest things increase, etc., *as far as* because he abominates the truth of the faith and the unity of peace.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The prayer of the poor,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because God will judge the ungodly, who afflict the saints.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Reproof.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, the judgment of his own sin, because he has hardened his mind, that it should not obey the truth.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Known afar off.* ⟦s2⟧ Heretical presumption is made manifest in a rash tongue, which speaketh iniquity against [1205D] God.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *He that buildeth a house,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He signifies the heretic, who gathers testimonies of Scripture, estranged by a depraved understanding, for the confirmation of his own error. ⟦s3⟧ But by the vengeance of just retribution the hardness of that abominable action shall be dissolved; or tropologically: the greedy plunderer, who from the goods of others increases his riches, and, empty of all good things, shall be punished in the day of judgment for his iniquity.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *The way of them that sin.* ⟦s2⟧ Therefore is the destruction of the wicked swift, because the way, that is, their life, is full of sorrows: and of the hard works of sins.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *He shall not be taught,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] By these sentences he confounds, etc., *as far as* and he himself has mingled himself with endless questions.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1206A]VERS. 18.-- *It displeaseth him,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *The children of this world are wiser in their generation than the children of light [n: (Luc. XVI)]*.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Fetters on the feet,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Because to their steps, wherewith they walk, the ruin of iniquity is contrary, it reproves their actions by condemning them.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *The senseless man in laughter,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The assembly of the heretics, teaching with plausible workmanship through the art of rhetoric, seeks the favor of its hearers. ⟦s3⟧ But the holy teachers so mingle pleasantness of speech with preaching, that they depart not from the track of truth.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *The foot of the senseless man,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because the heretics study to imitate the sect of their own authors, lurking in the dwelling-place of their writings.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *The fool from the window.* [n: (ID.)] He marks the craftiness of the heretics, [1206B] and the simplicity of the catholics.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *While he curseth.* ⟦s2⟧ Not because he calls the devil accursed, but because he ceases not to imitate him whom he knows to be accursed. ⟦s3⟧ For while he had rather imitate the devil than Christ, he is justly condemned.
