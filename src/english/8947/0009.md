## CHAPTER XXII.

VERS. 1.-- *With a stone,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] When the foulness of sinners, by the hard sentences of the Scriptures, etc., *as far as* is upbraided by the preachers.

VERS. 6.-- *Music.* He marks those who in the Church seek the applause and the favor of the people, and do not rather stir up the compunction of tears.

VERS. 7.-- *He that teacheth a senseless man.* A potsherd fashioned with much labor escapes not its own frailty. The senseless man too, however carefully he be instructed, is scarcely brought [1206C] to perfection.

VERS. 10.-- *Over the dead.* [n: (ID.)] One is the death of the body, etc., *as far as* for the death of the body is the end of worldly toil

VERS. 14.-- *With a fool.* [n: (ID.)] He teaches that speech is not to be drawn out with fools, etc., *as far as: For he that saith to him, God speed you, communicateth with his wicked works [n: (II Joan. I)]*.

VERS. 17.-- *Above lead.* Hence Solomon saith: *A stone is heavy and sand weighty, but the anger of a fool is heavier than both [n: (Prov. XXVII)]*. The heretic sins worse than the catholic, in the multitude of many evils; or the philosopher who leans upon human wisdom; or the gentile, who has not come to the faith, and sins in ignorance: whence, *The servant* indeed *who knoweth the will [1206D] of his lord, and doth it not, shall be beaten with many stripes [n: (Luc. XII)]*. But he that knoweth not, with few.

VERS. 19.-- *A binding.* The timbers signify the just, who give fruit in their season, whose leaves fall not: and all things whatsoever they do shall prosper; but the foundation of the building signifies the apostles or the prophets, whose preaching upholds the Church. *A wooden binding therefore, bound to the foundation of the building, is not dissolved:* because the faith of Christ's passion, joined in the hearts of men to the teaching of the apostles, makes the building of the Church indissoluble.

VERS. 21.-- *As chaff,* etc. Whence: He that heareth my words, and doth them not, is like to a foolish man, who buildeth his house upon the sand. [var: Vulg. *Sicut pali in excelsis, et caementa sine impensa posita*, "As stakes on high places, and mortar laid without cost"]

[1207A]VERS. 23.-- *As the heart.* [n: (RAB.)] As a clear wall, etc., *as far as* so the heart of the senseless man despises the remembrance of its crimes, nor fears the eternal punishments.

VERS. 25.-- *He that casteth a stone.* He shows what preserves a good friend, or rather estranges him. *He that casteth a stone.* As by the stroke of a stone the flight of birds is brought down, so by a word of reviling the force of friendship is dissolved.

VERS. 26.-- *Despair not.* Concerning his friendship, because, the profit of correction being acknowledged, he returns to the right of the former friendship.

VERS. 27.-- *A friend shall flee away.* Not to return, nor after reconciliation; whence: To a friend [sic: *reconciliatione*], once reconciled, trust thou not for ever.

VERS. 29.-- *In the time of tribulation.* In adversity a friend is the more proved. For there are many friends of the table, [1207B] and they remain not in the day of necessity.

VERS. 30.-- *Before the fire,* etc. As smoke goes before the burning of the fire, so hard words before murder; whence, *A hard word stirreth up strifes [n: (Prov. XV)]*. And elsewhere: *He that is angry with his brother shall be in danger of the judgment [n: (Matth. V)]*.

VERS. 31.-- *To salute a friend.* Many good things there are in true friendship: for a true friend rejoices with thee in thy good things, and is saddened in thy evils.

VERS. 32.-- *Every one that shall hear.* That is, he who knows how great good things are in true friendship, will beware lest he hurt his friend by injury or by reviling.

## CHAPTER XXIII.

VERS. 1.-- *O Lord, father.* [n: (RAB.)] As if: By thy gift [1207C] may I be defended from the gulf of vices.

VERS. 2.-- *Who will set.* He desires in his thought the scourges of discipline, and in his heart the teaching of wisdom: lest perchance through ignorance he should not acknowledge the perils of his offenses, and his crimes be multiplied upon him, for which he may fall into the hands of his adversaries.

VERS. 8.-- *In his vanity.* [n: (ID.)] Because he sets it before the love of God and the desire of eternal blessedness.

VERS. 9, 10.-- *To swearing,* etc. He forbids an oath, lest perjury be committed; whence: *Swear not, neither by heaven nor by earth [n: (Jacob. V)]*.

VERS. 13.-- *Doubly.* [n: (ID.)] Because by dissimulation he conceals the truth, and sends others into error by falsehood.

VERS. 15.-- *There is another manner of speech.* Blasphemy, which [1207D] is worse than the things said above: whence it behoves that it be not found *in the inheritance of Jacob,* that is, in the people of the Church, which supplants vices, and in virtues serves God.

VERS. 17.-- *Undisciplined.* As if: Forsake not discipline, and utter not thy words with an undisciplined mouth.

VERS. 22.-- *A crafty soul.* [n: (ID.)] The heretics and the schismatics have a burning soul, etc., *as far as* but forsaking their former husband, they have committed fornication with idols. [var: Vulg. *Anima calida quasi ignis ardens*, "A hot soul is as a burning fire"]

VERS. 26.-- *Darkness compasseth about.* [n: (ID.)] He that loses the virtue of chastity, etc., *as far as* whereas the countenance of the Lord is upon them that do evil.

VERS. 28.-- *The eyes of the Lord.* Because to him nothing is past, [1208A] and nothing is to come: all things are present.

VERS. 30.-- *This man in the streets.* The lustful adulterer, of whom it was said above, who like a horse and a mule runs to and fro in wantonness through the broad and spacious way, which leadeth to death.

VERS. 32.-- *So also the woman.* Equally in the man and in the woman is adultery condemned, and the seed of this coupling is abominable to God. But mystically the Synagogue of the heretics, or the heretical soul deceived by depravity, is abominable to God, because it forsakes its former husband, who joined it to himself in baptism, and adorned it with the ornament of virtues.

VERS. 34.-- *This woman in the Church.* Because her perversity is declared in the assembly of the faithful through the diligence of the teachers. [var: Vulg. *Haec in ecclesiam adducetur*, "She shall be brought into the church"]

[1208B]VERS. 35.-- *And the branches.* Because her sect has not the foundation of truth, it cannot beget the fruit of good works.

VERS. 38.-- *Length,* etc. Because in eternal blessedness they shall remain for ever who follow God.
