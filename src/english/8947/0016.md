## CHAPTER XLIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Of the height,* etc. [n: (ID.)] the beauty of the firmament, etc., *as far as* none can bear with bodily eyes the splendour of the Godhead.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Keeping the furnace.* ⟦s2⟧ Because the eternal flame torments the proud for their evil thought, speech, and working.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *And in his words,* etc. [n: (ID.)] [1225B] With mystic words he mocks the understanding of the proud. ⟦s2⟧ *He hath hastened,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ When in the preaching of the Gospel the way of salvation and the entrance of the heavenly kingdom was made ready.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And the moon,* etc. [n: (ID.)] After he had discoursed of the sun, etc., *as far as* but it is more rightly assigned to April, because in it it either begins, or ends, or is wholly contained.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *The lamp which is diminished,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The moon has not her own inborn splendour, but receives it from the sun: so also the Church from Christ, which in persecution seems to be diminished, but in part grows bright; for it increases wondrously at the consummation.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The vessel of the camps,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because in her consists the multitude of the faithful, which in the firmament of truth and of divine preaching shines gloriously [1225C] throughout the whole world.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *The beauty of heaven,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The adornment of the Church, the fulness of the saints, whom the most high God ordained, that they might give light by the preaching of the Gospel. ⟦s3⟧ These, abiding *in holy words* and in the doctrine of the true faith, look for the judgment of God.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *In words.* ⟦s2⟧ Because in their patience they possess their souls, having their loins girded; and holding lamps in their hands, they await their Lord, when he shall return from the wedding.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *See the rainbow,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The writing of the Old or of the New Testament. ⟦s3⟧ Here a sweet yoke is shown to the faithful, terrible arms to the stubborn.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *He hath encompassed the heaven,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ When through the power and the wisdom of God, that is, through Christ, the Old and [1225D] the New Testament was founded.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And the stones of hail were broken.* ⟦s2⟧ The teachers, who water the earth of the human heart, and bear within themselves the tempest of rebuke, and pour it forth in due time, and break the stones of hail; that is, by rebuking they stone the hardness of a depraved heart, the living grass.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *The south wind shall blow.* [n: (RAB.)] Which is also Auster, etc., *as far as* mighty for the persecution of the Christian name, and sometimes for repentance.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *A tempest.* ⟦s2⟧ God lays upon us divers trials by the malignant spirits, that they may make the fervour of faith and love grow cold.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Frost as salt,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That is, barrenness; [1226A] for where salt is scattered, barrenness follows: but the devil, through the cold of unbelief, makes earthly hearts barren of good works.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *A medicine,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ All eloquence fails in comparison with him, nor does it suffice to declare his power.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *They that sail the sea,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Many, to the letter, are the perils of the sea, and there are the wonderful works of God in divers beasts, as the writers of natural things have handed down. ⟦s3⟧ In the sea also of this world there are many monsters of demons, and many snares of theirs.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *Labour not,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] To comprehend that which is incomprehensible. ⟦s2⟧ Whence seek not riches to which thou canst not attain.

## CHAPTER XLIV.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1226B]

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Let us praise men,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Disputing morally, and touching in very many places allegory and anagogy, he has published the mysteries of the divine sacrament; and now he begins to tell of the holy fathers. ⟦s3⟧ *Our parents.* ⟦s4⟧ The patriarchs, who enjoyed divine speech and vision; and the prophets, who, filled with the Holy Spirit, foresang many mysteries of Christ and of the Church.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *In the testaments he stood,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] These things, according to the history, concerning the patriarchs, etc., *as far as* when the seed of good works shall be recompensed with a heavenly reward.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Henoch pleased God.* ⟦s2⟧ Not from Adam, etc., *as far as* that he may give men counsel of repentance.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Noe was found,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ When, the world perishing [1226C] by the flood, he was preserved, that he might preach repentance to the nations, and show by his example that they who devoutly serve God are delivered from everlasting destruction. ⟦s3⟧ With him God established a covenant, that he would no more destroy all flesh with the waters of a flood [n: (Gen. XVII)]. ⟦s4⟧ And he signifies the rulers, who amid the waves of the world govern the ark, that is, the Church, and preach the baptism of repentance, appeasing God by prayers and by sacrifices for the human race.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *In his flesh he maketh the covenant to stand,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] That it might be signified that our nature is renewed through the baptism of Christ, after the despoiling of the old man.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Therefore by an oath,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The promise to Abraham is twofold. ⟦s3⟧ In the sand of the sea are expressed the barren [1226D] Jews; in the likeness of the stars, the faithful Christians, who shall shine with the light of the resurrection as the stars of heaven.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *And he gave him an inheritance,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Of the Gentile people, which came later to the grace of faith and to the regeneration of baptism, to whom the fulness of the fatherly blessing came.

## CHAPTER XLV.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Commandments.* ⟦s2⟧ On mount Sinai, namely, he gave him the law and the precepts of life, speaking with him in the cloud.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *And he girded him about,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He describes the rite of the ancient priesthood and the adornment of the high priest, all which things were a figure of the New Testament, and of the true priest Jesus Christ. ⟦s3⟧ *And he clothed him.* ⟦s4⟧ The vestments of the high priest are the works of the virtues: wisdom, doctrine.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *A golden crown,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ In which was the name of almighty God, which signifies the honour of the divine majesty, since we ought to set above all things that in all our acts and words God be honoured *through Jesus Christ, who is our head [n: (I Petr. IV)]*.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Sacrifices.* [n: (RAB.)] How Moses, Aaron, etc., *as far as* for the priesthood of Christ and the rite of the sacrifices shall abide for ever.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *Phinees the son of Eleazar.* [n: (ID.)] Phinees, as it is written in the book of Numbers [n: (Cap. XXV)], etc., *as far as* he came happily to the mountain of strength, that is, to Christ. [1227B]

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *And the testament of David.* [n: (ID.)] He signifies that David, in the distribution of the ministries of God, etc., *as far as* and in the tabernacle he ordained the courses of the ministers.

## CHAPTER XLVI.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Valiant in war was Jesus the son of Nave.* [n: (RAB.)] Jesus both in deed and in word signifies Christ, etc., *as far as* triumphing over principalities and powers in himself.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Who before him,* etc. (ID). How at the onset of Joshua the sun was hindered, etc., *as far as* they shall be shortened in the time of perdition.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *From sins.* ⟦s2⟧ Of despair, when, the rumours of the spies being heard, they despaired of entering [1227C] the land.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *And the judges every one,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] After Joshua there succeeded judges who defended the people of God from their enemies; and after the passion of Christ, the apostles, rulers of the Church of God.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *That their memory may be,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because they persevered in the right faith, that their memory might be established with perpetual blessing. ⟦s3⟧ *The bones.* ⟦s4⟧ The strength of good works is given for a reward, that the memory of their name may be handed on to posterity with glory.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Beloved of the Lord,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] The history relates that the Lord, to the child Samuel, etc., *as far as* he pronounces to evil rulers a punishment to come and everlasting death.

## CHAPTER XLVII.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1227D]

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *After this rose up Nathan.* ⟦s2⟧ To the letter, he commemorates the deeds of David, of which the book of Kings and of Paralipomenon most fully relate.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *As the fat.* ⟦s2⟧ He signifies him to be filled with the grace of the Holy Spirit, separated in life and in manners from the fellowship of carnal men.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *He played with lions.* [n: (RAB.)] David signifies Christ, etc., *as far as* and he gave him a name which is above every name.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *After him rose up a wise son.* [n: (ID.)] Mystically, Solomon, etc., *as far as* as in one threshing-floor the grains signify the good, but the chaff the evil.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *That thou mightest make an empire,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Because [1228A] ten tribes followed Jeroboam, and two Roboam; of Ephraim was Jeroboam, who first reigned cruelly over the ten tribes.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *And he left behind him,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Mystically, Roboam, who is interpreted *the breadth of the people,* etc., *as far as* he remained in the rule of the catholic faith.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *He turned away the people by his counsel.* ⟦s2⟧ Believing, namely, the counsel of the young men.
