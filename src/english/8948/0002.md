## CHAPTER VI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0744D]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- « The king passed that night without sleep, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ Remaining movable [cj: *mobilis*; read *immobilis*, "unmoved"] in himself, he [God] contemplates the course of times and the acts of all men, and no thought lies hid from him to whom all things are present; whence the Apostle: « There is not in him, is and was, but in him there always is is [n: (II Cor. I)]. » [var: Vulg. II Cor. 1:19 *non fuit Est et Non, sed Est in illo fuit*, "there was not Yes and No, but in him there was Yes"]

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- « They came. » The deeds of Mardochaeus are called to mind before the king; because the good works of holy teachers shall never be delivered over to oblivion, but the just shall be in everlasting remembrance.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- « Make haste. » The masters of the Church, made illustrious by the practice of all the virtues and by the comeliness of wisdom, are honoured with the diadem of royal dignity, as [0745A] members of the highest king. ⟦s2⟧ They mount upon the royal horse, that is, upon the people of the faithful in whose hearts the king of angels resides; whence Habacuc the prophet: « Mounting upon thy horses, and thy cavalry is health. » [var: Vulg. Hab. 3:8 *qui ascendes super equos tuos, et quadrigae tuae salvatio*, "and thy chariots are salvation"] To these Aman, the special enemy of the people of God, though even unwilling, renders obedience, when the persecutors of the Church are compelled to render testimony to that same Church, not being able to hide what is manifest.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « He took therefore. » This is the change of the right hand of the Most High, he who, if he seemed the more glorious, appears beneath others baser and lower, according to that: « He hath put down the mighty from their seat, and hath exalted the humble. » Likewise Isaias says: « Libanus shall be turned into Chermel, and Chermel shall be esteemed as a forest. » So was the pride of the Synagogue crushed, [0745B] the humility of the Church exalted. ⟦s2⟧ So were the persecutors of the faith brought to nothing: the confessors of Christ exalted in the whole world. ⟦s3⟧ The head was turned into the tail, and the tail into the head: « For every one that exalteth himself shall be humbled [n: (Luc. XXIV)]. »

## CHAPTER VII.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « On the second day, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ But when Esther opened her petition, he [Aman], being condemned, withdrew to his punishment. ⟦s3⟧ For in the Gospel a dinner and a supper are mentioned. ⟦s4⟧ The dinner designates the present time of the Church, but the supper the eternal and last banquet. ⟦s5⟧ Whence, the wicked being separated, the good rejoice for ever. ⟦s6⟧ For Aman, because he had not a garment worthy of the banquet, deserved to be cast forth.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0745C]VERS. 7.-- « He went into the garden. » The king hastening into the garden, that is, inviting the elect with himself to the delights of paradise, he [Aman] strove to beseech the queen for his own safety, but had not a fitting time. ⟦s2⟧ For too late are the remedies of salvation sought, when the perils of death are at hand; whence Solomon: « Then shall they call, and I will not hear them: in the morning they shall rise early, and shall not find [n: (Prov. I)]. »

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- « And immediately. » The entreaty of Aman is called an oppression, because in the day of judgment the prayer of the wicked is a provocation: whence: *When he is judged, may he go forth condemned [n: (Psal. CVIII)]*, etc. ⟦s2⟧ Then the oppression wherewith they oppressed the humble shall be cast in their own teeth.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- « Harbona. » It signifies the doctors of the law, who foretold the crimes and the deceits of the Jews, whence [0745D] Moses: « I know that after my death you will do wickedly, and will decline from the way which I have shown you [n: (Deut. XXXI)]. » [var: Vulg. Deut. 31:29 *declinabitis cito de via quam praecepi vobis*, "will quickly decline from the way which I have commanded you"] And in the Gospel: « There is one that accuseth you, Moses, in whom you trust [n: (Joan. V)]. »

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « In the house of Aman. » Aman signifies the wicked who raise tumult against Christ, and who persecute his Church. ⟦s2⟧ But Christ had been promised to them in the law; the law therefore, which was given for the keeping of life, was turned to death for them, by which they contrived to blot out Christ and to slay his confessors: and they who wished by the law to oppress the guiltless; that same law accusing them, received the sentence of a just judgment. ⟦s3⟧ They who have sinned in the law shall be punished by the law.

## CHAPTER VIII.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0746A]

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- « On that day. » Esther holds the house of Aman, the enemy of the Jews, when the Church possesses the world, which the devil, the enemy of Christians, had possessed; whence: « When a strong man armed keepeth his court [n: (Luc. XI)], » etc. ⟦s2⟧ Mardochaeus goes in before the face of the king, when daily the souls of the saints go in from the sojourning of the present life to the contemplation of the divine majesty.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « The house of Aman. » The dignity and the honour which the Jews had by the knowledge of the law and of the prophets, and by the practice of religion; whence in the Proverbs: « The substance of the sinner is kept for the just [n: (Prov. XIII)]. » And in the Gospel: « The kingdom shall be taken away from you, and shall be given to a nation yielding the fruits thereof [n: (Matth. XXI)]. »

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0746B]VERS. 2.-- « And the king took. » [The king] praising and proclaiming the earnestness of the masters, they receive from the Lord grace for grace in the sight of the creator of all; whence: « He hath nourished the hearts of the faithful. »

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « He delivered it to Mardochaeus. » To the preachers of the Gentiles, that they might cleave to him, and might minister to the Gentiles.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- « She fell down at his feet, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because holy Church, for the deliverance of her sons, daily entreats almighty God through faith and the mysteries of the incarnation, that the boldness of the enemies may be crushed, and the innocence of the faithful set free.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- « I beseech that. » Because the evangelical doctrine, which is preached in the whole world in the name of Christ, is declared to be confirmed by the seal of the Holy Spirit, filled with whose gift the preachers themselves remained [0746C] unconquerable, and glorious triumphers over their enemies.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- « And the scribes being called, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because the doctrine of the Gospel was so composed by the writers of the New Testament, the Lord mediating, that the faith of the holy Trinity might be most fully contained in it, and the highest perfection of the whole decalogue be shown forth in the two precepts of charity.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « Who over a hundred and twenty. » In which the whole world is signified. ⟦s2⟧ For the number ten multiplied by twelve makes a hundred and twenty, to which the number seven being joined concludes the fulness of the whole sum: so, the keeping of the decalogue being spread through the whole world by apostolic tradition, the grace of the sevenfold Spirit is poured into [0746D] the hearts of the faithful, or is consummated.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- « They should kill, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ Lest the revived plantings of an evil seed should sprout again. ⟦s3⟧ Likewise the Lord commanded the seven nations which dwelt in the land of promise to be slain, and afterwards the Amalecites to be utterly destroyed, that he might take away from them every occasion of scandal; whence David says: *In the morning I put to death [n: (Psal. C)]*, etc.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- « In Susan, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ In the pride of the world that is to be overcome. ⟦s3⟧ For Susis is interpreted riding, or overturning; whence the Saviour says: « Have confidence, I have overcome the world [n: (Joan. XVI)]. »
