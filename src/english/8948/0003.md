## CHAPTER IX.

VERS. 4.-- « And they had learned that he could do very much. » [0747A] Because the praiseworthy action of the teachers, and the power of their virtues, bestows honour and reverence upon the multitude of the faithful. Whence in the Acts of the Apostles: « Many wonders and signs were wrought [n: (Act. V)], » whence it is read elsewhere: « For fear came upon every soul [n: (Ibid. II)]. Likewise: « No one durst join himself to them, and the people magnified them [n: (Ibid. V)]. »

VERS. 5.-- « So the Jews smote. » The divine sentence of the Holy Spirit judges not only the other workers of iniquity, who persevere in sins, and care not to receive the remission of sins, to be condemned, but also the carnal Jews, transgressors of the decalogue, that they shall feel in the cities of hell the guilt of the cross of Christ, which they contracted out of perfidy.

VERS. 6.-- « Whose names are these. » The interpretations of these [0747B] names we do not search out with much labour, nor do we force them to serve allegory. For the type is in a part, not in the whole. For if the type is in the whole, it is not history. The historian ought to set forth the true order of the thing done, and neither to stop short of it nor to go beyond it: the allegorical interpreter, to choose out the excellent parts that agree in a spiritual sense.

VERS. 16.-- « So far. » All who receive the law carnally by the five bodily senses, and, though through the sevenfold grace of the Holy Spirit it is especially full and perfect, refuse to understand it spiritually, are as it were overcome and laid low in two days.

VERS. 17.-- « Now the day. » It is to be noted that certain of the Jews slay on the thirteenth day, and [0747C] cease on the fourteenth, and keep the solemnity, certain slay through two days, that is, on the thirteenth and the fourteenth day of the same month, and on the fifteenth solemnly rest: because certain [0748A] of the saints, after the labours are completed in which they serve God by believing well and living well, now, before the time of judgment, have in their souls the sabbath of eternal rest: others, enduring in the flesh until the last day of the resurrection, all their enemies being overcome, suddenly changed, receive the rest of souls and of bodies; whence the Apostle: « The dead who are in Christ shall rise first. Then we who are alive, who remain, shall rise again with them to meet Christ in the air [n: (I Thes. IV)], » etc. [var: Vulg. I Thess. 4:16 *qui relinquimur, simul rapiemur cum illis in nubibus obviam Christo in aera*, "who are left shall be taken up together with them in the clouds"] For the fourteenth, which contains twice seven, signifies the perfect rest of souls, but the fifteenth, embracing the number seven and the number eight, signifies the rest of souls and of bodies.

VERS. 24.-- « And he cast Phur, » etc. The lot cast into the urn [0748B] signifies the disposing of any matter whatsoever in the mind of man, the outcome of which not human but divine judgment governs, whence Solomon: « Lots are cast into the lap: but they are disposed of by the Lord [n: (Prov. XVI)]. »

VERS. 28.-- « These are the days. » Because the rest of souls and the resurrection of bodies is deservedly and firmly kept and celebrated by the faithful: or, because for the sake of the rest of souls and of the resurrection of bodies a festival is kept and celebrated by the faithful.

## CHAPTER X.

VERS. 1.-- « But king Assuerus made all the earth tributary. » Christ, who, cut out of the mountain without hands, grew into a great mountain and filled the whole world: historically, for he [Assuerus] made not all the earth tributary, [0748C] because neither did he possess it.
