## CHAPTER IV. (cont.)

VERS. 24.-- *The Lord met him,* etc. [n: (AUG., Quaest. 11 in Exod.)] Another reading: *And it came to pass on the way at the resting-place, an angel met him, and sought to kill him: and Sephora, taking a pebble, circumcised the foreskin of her son, and fell down at his feet and said: The blood of the circumcision of my child hath stood, and he departed from him because of this which she said: The blood of the [0197B] circumcision hath stood.* First it is asked whom the angel wished to slay, etc., *as far as* by a great mystery, if I am not mistaken.

VERS. 25, 26.-- *Thou art to me a spouse of blood,* etc. According to history, the Gentile mother, wondering at the newness of the thing, could say to Moses: *Thou art to me a spouse of blood;* as if to say, the rite of thy nation compels me to shed the blood of my son, which among other nations is not the custom. But mystically Sephora signifies the Church from among the Gentiles, which strips the foreskin of her son, that is, of the Gentile people, with a most sharp stone, that is, with the teaching of the Holy Spirit, or with that rock of which it is said in II Corinthians X: *Now the rock was Christ,* that it may be cleansed from all defilement of flesh and spirit: *And that he may put off the old man with his deeds [n: (Col. III)]*. For the blood of circumcision stands [0197C] when this corruptible shall put on incorruption.

VERS. 27-31.-- *And the Lord said,* etc. [n: (ORIG., hom. 32.)] Aaron met Moses, and he went out of Egypt. But it matters where he meets Moses, whose mouth is to be opened by the Lord: and he meets him in the mount of God. Deservedly is his mouth opened who meets him in the mount of God. Peter, James and John in the mount deserved both to see the Lord Jesus transfigured, and Moses and Elias with him in glory. Thou also, unless thou shalt have ascended the mount of God, and there met Moses, that is, unless thou shalt have ascended the lofty ascent of the law, unless thou shalt have seized the summit of spiritual understanding, there is no door opened to thee by the Lord. If thou shalt have stood in the lowly place of the letter, thou hast not met Moses in the mount of God; God has not [0197D] opened thy mouth, nor instructed thee what it behoves thee to speak; unless Aaron had met Moses in the mount, unless he had seen his understanding to be lofty, he would not speak to him the words of God, nor would he have delivered to him the power of the signs, nor have made him a sharer of so great a mystery.
