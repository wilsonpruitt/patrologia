## CHAPTER VI.

VERS. 3-6.--. . . *And my name Adonai I did not show them.* As to thee. For through the successions of the ages the increase of divine knowledge grew. The Lord appeared to Abraham; he spoke face to face with Moses; but David says in Psalm CXVIII: *I have understood above the ancients,* etc. The Apostles beheld the Son of God present, and asked whatever they wished, to whom [0201B] it is also said in Luke II: *Many kings and prophets,* etc.

VERS. 7, 8.-- *Out of the workhouse of the Egyptians.* Ergastulum and ergasterium, that is, a workshop: namely where the accused were bound fast for the doing of works.

VERS. 9-11.-- *They did not acquiesce in him,* etc. Note the clemency of God: he is not believed by the children of Israel, and yet he ceases not to admonish. He is contemned by Pharao, and long-sufferingly he waits.

VERS. 12, 13.-- *Behold the children,* etc. [n: (AUG. Quaest. 16 in Exod.)] The LXX. *Behold I am of a slender voice, and how shall Pharao hear me.* Yet he does not seem to excuse himself for the slenderness of his voice on account of the multitude of the people, but on account of one man also: it is a marvel if he was of so slender a voice that he could not be heard even by one. Or [0201C] perhaps the royal disdain did not permit them to speak from near at hand.

VERS. 14-19.-- *These are the princes,* etc. [n: (AUG. Quaest. 15.)] Scripture, wishing to show the origin of Moses, which his action required, began from the firstborn of Jacob, that is, from Ruben; thence to Simeon, thence to Levi: it did not go on further, because from Levi is Moses. And these are commemorated who had already been mentioned among those seventy-five with whom Israel had entered into Egypt. For God willed not the first, nor the second, but the third tribe, that is, the Levitical, to be the priestly one.

VERS 20.-- *And Amram took to wife Jochabed,* etc. [n: (STRAB.)] Amram is interpreted *lofty father,* who signifies Christ; Jochabed, *the grace of God,* [0201D] who signifies the Church: from Christ and the Church is born Moses, that is, the spiritual law, and Aaron, namely the true priesthood.

*And he took,* etc. She was of the tribe of Juda, sister of Naason, who was prince of the tribe of Juda in the desert: for it had not yet been commanded that each man, should take a wife from his own tribe. And these tribes, that is, the Levitical and the royal, were even then joined together, that it might be understood that Christ was to be king and priest from them.

VERS. 21-26.-- *The sons also of Isaar,* etc. [n: (STRAB.)] It is asked why Core, whom the earth swallowed alive for his iniquity, is written in the genealogy of the just? But it signifies that heretics will be with catholics in the Church

[0202A]VERS. 27-30.-- *This is Moses.* [n: (STRAB.)] This Esdras adds of his own, who restored the whole library after the Babylonian captivity, all the books having been burnt by Nabuchodonosor; or Moses himself speaks of himself as of another.
