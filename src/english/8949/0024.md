## CHAPTER XIX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1, 2.-- *In the third month,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] Another reading: *And the going out of the children of Israel from the land [0247B] of Egypt was of the third month: on this day they came into the desert of Sina, and they set forward from Raphidim, and came into the desert of Sina, and Israel pitched there over against the mountain. ⟦s2⟧ And Moses went up into the mount of God, and the Lord called him from the mountain, saying: These things shalt thou say to the house of Jacob, and thou shalt announce to the children of Israel,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ And a little after: *Go down and charge the people, and thou shalt purify them today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments, and let them be ready against the third day. ⟦s4⟧ For on the third day the Lord will come down upon mount Sina in the sight of all the people.* ⟦s5⟧ On this day the law is found to be given, which was written on tables of stone by the finger of God, as the things that follow teach. ⟦s6⟧ And this third day appears to be of the third month from the going out of the children of Israel from Egypt; from the day on which they kept the pasch, that is, immolated and ate the lamb, which was the fourteenth of the first month, as far as [0247C] that on which the law is given, fifty days are numbered, seventeen namely of the first month, the rest from the fourteenth itself. ⟦s7⟧ Then all thirty of the second month, which make forty-seven, and the third of the third month, which from the solemnity of the slain lamb is the fiftieth; and by this, in that shadow of things to come, according to the feast day of the immolated lamb; because on the fiftieth day the law was given, written by the finger of God. ⟦s8⟧ So in the very truth of the New Testament, from the festival of the immolated Lamb Jesus Christ fifty days are numbered, wherein the Holy Spirit was given from the highest places, who is the finger of God.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *On this day,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ On the forty-seventh day, namely, after they went out of Egypt, on the first day, namely, of June, they came to mount Sina, and there, when the two days were passed in which they were sanctified, on the third, [0247D] that is, the fiftieth, they received the law: and this is Pentecost.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (RAB.)] In Sina there was one station, but the greatest of all. ⟦s2⟧ For there the law was received, the tabernacle set up. ⟦s3⟧ There Exodus and the greatest part of Leviticus and all the books of the law were almost wholly written.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *But Moses,* etc. [n: (GREG., lib. VI Moral., cap. 27.)] Another reading: *But Moses went up into the mountain, and the Lord came down to him.* ⟦s2⟧ The mountain is the height of contemplation, into which we go up, that by gazing we may be lifted to those things which are beyond our weakness. ⟦s3⟧ But the Lord comes down, because, to saints who make much progress, he opens some small thing of himself.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4, 5.-- *The wings of eagles.* ⟦s2⟧ He calls the teachings [0248A] of the two Testaments, which bear us from the lowest things to the heavenly.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *You shall be to me a priestly kingdom,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Members, namely, of the high priest who is to come into the kingdom of heaven.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7-9.-- *He set forth all the words,* etc. [n: (GREG.)] It is to be noted that the law is not thrust upon the unwary or the ignorant: but when the rewards which are due to those who keep it have been set forth, and the past benefits which they have already experienced, and the wonders, when of their own accord they devote themselves to the keeping of the law, the law is given to those who ask for it: so that according to their own promise, as men bound by a vow, those who violate it may be judged, and may not complain of violence done to them.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1+s2+s3+s4+s5+s6+s7+s8+s9+s10+s11+s12⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Go to the people,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] *Go down [0248B] and testify to the people, and purify it today and tomorrow, and let them wash their garments, and let them be ready against the third day,* etc. He who wishes to come to the word of God, let him hear what the Lord commands: he ought to come sanctified and with washed garments. For if thou bring soiled garments, it will be said to thee, Matthew XXII: *Friend, how camest thou in hither not having on a wedding garment?* No one therefore can hear the word of God, unless first sanctified, that is, holy in body and in spirit and with garments washed: for he is about a little after to enter to the marriage supper, to eat of the flesh of the lamb, and to drink the cup of salvation. No one enters to this supper in soiled garments; whence Ecclesiastes IX: *At all times let thy garments be clean.* [var: Vulg. Ecl 9:8 has *Omni tempore sint vestimenta tua candida*, "at all times let thy garments be white"] For thy garments were washed once by the grace of baptism. II Corinthians VII: *Thou art purified in body, cleansed [0248C] from all defilement of the flesh and of the spirit.* [var: Vulg. 2Cor 7:1 has the first-person exhortation *mundemus nos ab omni inquinamento carnis et spiritus*, "let us cleanse ourselves from all defilement of the flesh and of the spirit"] Acts XI: *What therefore God hath cleansed, do not thou make unclean.* [var: Vulg. Act 11:9 has *tu ne commune dixeris*, "do not thou call common"; Act 10:15 *Quod Deus purificavit*] Hear the manner of sanctification: go not near, he says, to a woman today and tomorrow, that on the third day thou mayest hear the word of God; this is what the Apostle says, I Corinthians VII: *It is good for a man not to touch a woman,* saving nevertheless to the weak the remedy of marriage. I Corinthians VII: *But because the time is short, it remaineth that they who have wives be as if they had none.*

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Sanctify them,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] He who desires to come to the sight of God, let him sanctify himself by faith and by conversation, and wash his garments, that is, his works, today and tomorrow, that is, in the present and in the world to come, that he may come to the glory of the resurrection, which the third day intimates.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *For on the third day,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ On this day was given [0248D] the law written on tables of stone by the finger of God, this is the third day of the third month from the going out of the children of Israel from Egypt. ⟦s3⟧ From the day therefore on which they kept the pasch, that is, the fourteenth of the first month, until the law was given, fifty days are numbered, seventeen namely of the first month, the rest from the fourteenth, and all thirty of the second, which are forty-seven, and the third of the third month, which from the slaying of the lamb is the fiftieth: as from the passion of Christ, until the Holy Spirit is given, fifty days are numbered. for the finger of God signifies the Holy Spirit.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISID., Quaest. in Exod., cap. 28, tom. V.)] On the fiftieth day after the pasch the law of Moses was given etc., *as far as* or because he spoke the truth of the law to the unbelieving through a mist, [0249A] *that seeing they might not see, and hearing they might understand us [sic: *nos*] [n: (Isa. VI)]*. [var: Vulg. Lc 8:10 has *et audientes non intelligant*, "and hearing they may not understand"; the Clementine Is 6:9 reads *Audite audientes, et nolite intelligere*]

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Take heed,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] First he forbids the people to go up into the mountain, and threatens death: now he commands that at the sounding of the trumpet they go up into the mountain, restraining, namely, human boldness, and inviting to obedience: because at the voice of preaching we ought to raise ourselves to things above, and not of our own accord to presume upon the searching out of the mysteries, but to follow the calling of God.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Trumpet.* ⟦s2⟧ As it were *vocina*: from *vox*, the voice, or from *vocare*, to call, or from *bucca*, the cheek

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14-17.-- *And when they had washed their garments,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ It does not suffice, for the virtue of contemplation, to cleanse one's works with the tears of penance, unless there follow chastity [0249B] of mind and of body. ⟦s3⟧ Whence Matthew V: *Blessed are the clean of heart,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ And Wisdom I: *Into a malevolent soul wisdom shall not enter.*

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18, 19.-- *And all mount Sinai was smoking,* etc. [n: (GREG. lib. VI Moral., cap. 27.)] The Lord, being about to give the law, came down in fire and smoke, etc., *as far as* they overcome what they are.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID., lib. II Moral., cap. 2.)] The sacred words in the opening of their narratives express the qualities and the bounds of their subjects, sometimes from the position of a place, sometimes from the position of the body, sometimes from the quality of the air, etc., *as far as* but they are recorded to have come in the evening when they were to punish Sodom.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20-25.-- *And the Lord came down,* etc. [n: (ID., lib. V Moral., cap. 26.)] Moses went up, etc., *as far [0249C] as* we in some measure echo him by stammering.
