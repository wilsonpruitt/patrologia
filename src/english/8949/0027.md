## CHAPTER XX. (cont.)

VERS. 25, 26.-- *Of hewn stones.* [n: (ISID.)] Hewn [0256C] stones are they who cut the unity asunder, and divide themselves from the fellowship of the brethren: these Christ does not receive into his body, of which body the building of the altar signifies the figure. The stones not hewn, of which the altar is commanded to be built, are they who through faith are made firm in unity; of whom it is said, I Peter II: *You are living stones, built together into spiritual houses.* [var: Vulg. 1 Pt 2:5 *et ipsi tamquam lapides vivi superaedificamini, domus spiritualis*, "be you also as living stones built up, a spiritual house"] Upon these no iron has been laid, because they are uncorrupted, and have not received the fiery darts of the evil one: all make one altar in the unity of faith and the concord of charity.
