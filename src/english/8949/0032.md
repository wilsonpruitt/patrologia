## CHAPTER XXV.

[0266C]

VERS. 2.-- *Speak to the children of Israel, that they bring me firstfruits,* etc. [n: (BED., de Tabernac., lib. I, cap. III, tom. II.)] We offer to God the firstfruits of our goods when, ascribing wholly to divine grace the good that we do, we profess with our whole heart that we cannot have even the beginning of a good action or thought save from the Lord; but that evils are always both begun and consummated by us at the instigation of the devil, and are not to be loosed save by the Lord's gift.

VERS. 3-6.-- *And these are the things you must take,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] All the things which the Lord commanded to be offered materially for the making of the sanctuary, we also, who desire to be spiritual children of Israel, that is, imitators [0266D] of the people that sees God, ought to offer spiritually: that by these voluntary oblations we may ourselves make a sanctuary within us, so that he may vouchsafe to dwell in our heart and to consecrate a habitation to himself.

VERS. 7.-- *Onyx stones,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] These we offer, etc., *as far as* are signified the merits of martyrdom added to good works.

VERS. 8.-- *And they shall make me a sanctuary. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The Lord showed Moses the tabernacle on the mountain, etc., *as far as* it received the glorious multitude of holy souls.

VERS. 9-11.-- *According to all the likeness,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] We ought to imitate the angels by loving God [0267A] and our neighbour, etc., *as far as* and after this life may bring thee into that tabernacle which thou imitatest.

*Of all the vessels.* [n: (BED.)] The vessels of the tabernacle are the several persons of the angels, of whom Jerusalem, the mother of us all, consists in true peace and unity.

*And thus you shall make it. An ark,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 4.)] The ark is commanded to be made, etc., *as far as* but he came in the likeness of sinful flesh.

*The length whereof* [n: (ID.)] It is asked of what size this cubit is, etc., *as far as* whence it appears that it designates that cubit which two palms make.

*The breadth. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The breadth of one cubit for the dispensation of the Lord's charity, etc., *as far as* where we may love him with our whole heart, let us enter.

[0267B] [n: (ID.)] *Mystically.* The length of the ark the long-suffering of Christ, etc., *as far as* because he bore with long-suffering the slowness of the disciples.

*The height, a cubit,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] The ark has a cubit in height, etc., and *as far as* this little we have not save from him.

VERS. 12-15.-- *And four rings,* etc. [n: (GREG. Past., cap. 11, tom. III.)] The ark, the Church, etc., *as far as which is in you,* etc.

*Two rings.* [n: (BEDA, ut supra. )] Because in the figure of the four living creatures, etc., *as far as* they expressed the things which pertain to the glory of the life to come.

VERS. 16.-- *The testimony which I will give. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Of which the Apostle, etc., *as far as* he taught the judge of all ages.

[0267C]VERS. 17.-- *A propitiatory.* [n: (ID.)] The propitiatory was a golden board of the same measure as the ark; so called because there God, appearing to Moses and speaking with him, was made propitious to the people; and it signifies mercy, whence it was fittingly above the ark, because his mercy is above all his works.

VERS. 18-21.-- *Two cherubim also,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Because of the fellowship of angelic peace, which cannot be had among fewer than two. Now the cherubim signify the angelic host; and the angels contemplate Christ in the highest concord and quiet; whence, with faces turned toward one another, they look upon the propitiatory, because they understand that they have that beatitude by the grace of God.

[n: (GREG., hom. 6 in Ezech.)] The two cherubim, which are called the *fulness [0267D] of knowledge,* etc., *as far as* if he promised one thing while he denied another.

[n: (BEDA, ibid.)] In the singular number it is called *cherub,* כרוב but in the plural *cherubim,* כרובים etc., *as far as* they are well designated by wings spread out and fitted for flying.

VERS. 22.-- *Thence will I give orders,* etc., *over the propitiatory. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Over the propitiatory, etc., *as far as* whence Habacuc says: *In the midst of two living creatures thou shalt be made known [n: (Habac. III)]*. [var: Vulg. Hab 3:2 *in medio annorum notum facies*, "in the midst of the years thou shalt make it known"; the whole gloss rests on the two living creatures]

[n: (BED., ibid.)] In the ark the Church can be figured, etc., *as far as* wherein they themselves stand established upon the foundation of the highest truth.

[n: (BED., ibid.)] The table of setim wood, etc., *as far as* [0268A] whence Cant. II: *The king brought me into the wine cellar, and he set in order charity in me.* [var: Vulg. Ct 2:4 *Introduxit me in cellam vinariam, ordinavit in me caritatem*, without *rex*, "he brought me in"]

VERS. 23.-- *In height a cubit,* etc. [n: (BED., ibid.)] That the height has a cubit and a half, etc., *as far as* it is not figured by a full cubit.

VERS. 23.-- *And thou shalt make for it a lip.,* etc. [n: (BED., ibid.)] A golden lip is made on the table round about, etc., *as far as* according to the diversity of merits, the glory of each. [var: Vulg. these words stand at Ex 25:24; Migne's address repeats VERS. 23]

VERS. 24.-- *A crown,* etc., and *over it another crown. [n: (BED., ibid.)]* A golden crown is added, since through the Gospel to those who keep the commandments, etc., *as far as* that a loftier glory is kept for it in the receiving back of their bodies.

VERS. 26.-- *For every one,* etc. [n: (BED., ibid.)] The table [0268B] of the tabernacle has four feet, etc., *as far as* they may enter into the kingdom of heaven.

VERS. 27.-- *That the bars may be put through them. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Through the rings the bars are put for carrying the table, etc., *as far as* they carry the table of the Lord with the loaves and the vessels in four rings.

VERS. 29.-- *Thou shalt prepare also dishes and bowls. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The various vessels which are made for offering libations, etc., *as far as* whence: *Who, thinkest thou, is the faithful and prudent steward [n: (Luc. XII)]*?

VERS. 29.-- *Thou shalt prepare dishes,* etc. [n: (GREG., hom. 6 in Evang., tom. II.)] What is understood by the bowls and the cups but the measure of preaching? etc., *as far as* but also small and subtle things, which afford knowledge as it were by a taste.

[0268C] [n: (IDEM.)] One man, full of the doctrine of truth, inebriates his hearers, and as it were holds out a bowl; another is not strong enough to fill out what he perceives, yet, because he does declare it after some fashion, affords by a cup a taste. Those therefore who cannot minister bowls through the doctrine of wisdom, let them, so far as they suffice by the divine bounty, pour out to their neighbours cups of the word.

VERS. 30.-- *And thou shalt set upon the table the loaves of proposition in my sight always.* Josephus says that twelve loaves were made of fine flour, each of two tenths, which on the Sabbath were set upon the table, six on this side and six on that: upon each one a golden dish with a handful of incense, and thus they were kept until another Sabbath, on which the incense was burned; but the priests ate the loaves warm, putting back the incense.

[0268D] *And thou shalt set upon the table,* etc. [n: (BED. ubi supra. )] They are rightly called the loaves of proposition, because the word of salvation ought always to lie open to all the faithful, nor ought the word of comfort ever to be wanting in the Church: which word the Lord, proposing it to the world through the heralds of truth, willed to appear in his sight and to abound unceasingly, even unto the end, for those who hunger and thirst after justice.

VERS. 31.-- *Thou shalt make also a candlestick,* etc. [n: (GREG., hom. 6 in Ezech.)] The candlestick is Christ, in whom the nature of humanity shone with the light of the Godhead, etc., *as far as* the goblets therefore and the little spheres pertain to labour, the lilies to reward.

*Thou shalt make also a candlestick of beaten work of the purest gold.* [0269A] [n: (BEDA, ibid.)] The candlestick of the tabernacle, etc., *as far as* and by preaching and by doing he showed the brightness of the word to all.

*And the branches, the goblets and the little spheres. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Namely, preachers proceeding from the shaft, etc., *as far as* which blooms for holy souls, that is, for eternal flowers.

VERS. 32.-- *Six branches,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] The number six, in which the world was made, etc., *as far as* whence Paul: *The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, and the charity of God, and the communication of the Holy Spirit, be always with you all.* [var: Vulg. 2Cor 13:13 *sit cum omnibus vobis*, without *semper*, "be with you all"]

*Three out of one. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Three branches out of one side, etc., *as far as* he shall enjoy the vision of him.

[n: (ID., ubi supra )]. The goblets are made on the candlestick after the manner of a nut, etc., *as far as* because the continent of that [0269B] time had three orders of saints by the same distinction of times.

VERS. 33.-- *In the manner of a nut.* [n: (ID.)] The nut signifies the Church, whence: *He went down into the garden of nuts.* [var: Vulg. Ct 6:10 *Descendi in hortum nucum*, "I went down into the garden of nuts"; Migne's third person moves the words out of the Bride's mouth] For the nut has a sweet fruit within, which is not shown without unless the shell be broken: so the life of the saints keeps the sweetness of grace in the heart, which is not known outwardly, save when, the dwelling of the flesh being loosed, they behold themselves in the heavenly light.

[n: (ID.)] The third branch likewise has three goblets and little spheres, and three lilies: because many married persons were before the law, under the law and under the prophets, who rejoiced to hear the word of the Lord, and to run his way, and to look for the rewards of good works.

[n: (ID.)] Likewise from the other side the first branch, the second [0269C] and the third, three goblets and little spheres; three lilies also it had: because in both Testaments, among the doctors, among the continent, and among the married that are devoted to God, there were three orders by a distance of times. Namely in the primitive Church out of Israel, in our election out of the Gentiles, in the last gathering of the remnants of Israel: all of whom, inebriated in their own time with the word of life, desire to hasten in the way of peace, and to see the whiteness of perpetual light.

VERS. 34.-- *And in the candlestick itself,* etc. [n: (ID., ubi supra. )] We have said that the candlestick itself, etc., *as far as* but hearts renewed through the Spirit receive them.

VERS. 35.-- *And little spheres under every one, and lilies. Little spheres under two branches,* etc.

[n: (ID., ibid.)] There are under the several goblets little spheres [0269D] together with lilies, etc., *as far as* whence: *His left hand under my head, and his right hand shall embrace me.*

[n: (ID., ibid.)] The goblets, the little spheres and the lilies are under two branches, etc., *as far as all the fulness of the Godhead corporeally.*

VERS. 36.-- *The little spheres therefore and the branches. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The whole work of the candlestick, etc., *as far as* whence: *In tribulation thou hast enlarged me,* etc.

VERS. 37.-- *Thou shalt make.* [n: (ID.)] The seven lamps are the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit, which always remained in Christ, and are distributed to the faithful according to his will: these are set upon the candlestick, because the Spirit of wisdom and understanding rested upon him [n: (Isai. XI)].

[0270A] *That they may give light,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] The lamps of the candlestick lighted up the tabernacle over against them, when Christ, full of grace and truth, bestowed on all out of his fulness; committing the word of the Gospel to the meek and the poor, conferring on the penitent the remedy of pardon, affirming that now is the time of sparing, in the time to come the time of the universal judgment.

VERS. 38.-- *Snuffers also, and where the things. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The divine words, by which it is manifestly signified that in very many places not the letter of the law but the spiritual sense is to be kept: whence: *If you be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing.*

[n: (ID., ibid.)] The snuffers, and the vessels where the things that are snuffed off are extinguished, etc., *as far as* saying: *Whether prophecies shall be made void, or tongues shall cease,* etc.

[0270B] *Snuffers also,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] There are certain precepts in the Scriptures, etc., *as far as* and elsewhere: *Behold, I make all things new.*

VERS. 39.-- *The whole. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* This is the body of Christ together with the head itself, that is, all the elect, etc., *as far as* because by the desert of their crimes they fall down into the abyss of oppressing punishment.

VERS. 40.-- *Look. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* There was shown to Moses on the mountain the pattern of the candlestick which he should make, etc., *as far as* he showed the pattern to his hearers by the performance of the work and by the word of doctrine.
