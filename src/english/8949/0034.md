## CHAPTER XXVI. (cont.)

VERS. 22.-- *Toward the west,* etc. [n: (BEDA, ibid.)] The western quarter, etc., *as far as* they are to remain in the Church unto the end of the world.

[0274C] *But toward the western quarter,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] Rightly is the tabernacle completed in the western quarter, etc., *as far as* but also for the evils from which he delivered them.

[n: (ID.)] Rightly does the western quarter, which is said to look toward the sea, consist of six boards: because in the number six the consummation of a good work is marked; because in it the Lord consummated the adorning of the world; in it he created man in the beginning; in it he restored the human race by his passion. Or because there are six ages of the world, in which we must be perfected by good works, that we may come to the eternal glory and to the glory of the resurrection.

VERS. 23.-- *And again two others.* [n: (ID.)] That again two other boards, besides the first six, are commanded to be raised up at the corner behind the back of the tabernacle, [0274D] which receive the wall coming from the eastern quarter and are joined to the wall of the western quarter, pertains to the reward of the life to come, which after the labours and the times of one life is secure, or made secure, being divided into a sabbath-rest, that is, the rest of souls after they are released from bodies, and the glory of the resurrection common to either people, to be ended by no end.

VERS. 24.-- *And they shall be joined to one another. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* [var: Vulg. Ex 26:24 *Eruntque conjunctae a deorsum usque sursum*, "and they shall be joined together from beneath unto the top"; Migne's *sibi* makes the joining mutual] Because the whole life of the elect tends by one faith and charity toward the things of heaven, and comes to the one end of the divine vision, and in one voice of right doctrine all the discourse of the holy preachers agrees. For the framing of the boards would fall apart from one another, if what one prophet or Apostle had said, another denied

[0275A] *And to the two boards also,* etc. [n: (BEDA, ibid.)] The corner boards are joined in all things to the boards of the walls, etc., *as far as* the prophets foretold with a consonant voice that these things should be.

[n: (ID., ibid.)] Here the measure of the bases is not stated, etc., *as far as* but that it might be kept unmoved in secure quiet.

*Thou shalt make also bars of wood,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 7.)] The five bars which hold together the boards of the tabernacle, etc., *as far as* nor did he lay upon them other things than the commandments of the law.

VERS. 29.-- *The boards themselves also thou shalt overlay with gold [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The gilded boards of the tabernacle shine forth, etc., *as far as* but they gather also the light of the supernal recompense.

*Which thou shalt cover with plates. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The bars are covered with plates of gold, etc., *as far as* when the spiritual [0275B] understanding is shown full of mysteries.

VERS. 30.-- *Thou shalt set up the tabernacle. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The pattern of the tabernacle, etc., *as far as* they compose both their work and their mind.

VERS. 31.-- *Thou shalt make also a veil. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 8)]*. This agrees, even according to the letter, with the beauty of the heavenly vision. For if thou consider the comeliness and the flames of the stars, the manifold appearance of the clouds and the rainbow itself, drawing out a thousand and diverse colours when the sun is turned away, thou shalt mark in heaven many more and fairer paintings than in the veil of the tabernacle.

*Thou shalt make also a veil,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] This veil, *as far as* that he may appear before the face of God for us.

*Thou shalt make also a veil of hyacinth and purple and scarlet twice dyed. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Thus far concerning the southern wall, [0275C] etc., *as far as* colours when the sun is turned away.

VERS. 32.-- *Which thou shalt hang before four,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] Now the four pillars, etc., *as far as* this shall not be there where there is nothing at all of evil.

*Which indeed shall be gilded. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The pillars before which the veil is hung are gilded, because the powers of the angels, placed within the veil of heaven, are clothed with the grace of the highest brightness. They have heads of gold, because they have the mind by which they are ruled made bright by the presence of divine knowledge and vision.

[n: (ID., ibid.)] The pillars have bases of silver, etc., *as far as* the brightness of words is shown by the silver.

[n: (GREG., Moral. lib. XXVIII, cap. 6)]. He commands the bases of the four pillars to be of silver, etc., *as far as* they carry the sayings of the evangelists by mouth and by work.

[0275D]VERS. 33.-- *And the veil shall be inserted,* etc. [n: (BED., ubi supra. )] Because it was in the eternal counsel of the Godhead, etc., *as far as: in the day of judgment.*

*Within which thou shalt put the ark of the testimony,* etc. Within this veil the ark of the testament was placed, because Christ, conscious of the Father's secrets, after his passion and resurrection, ascending the heaven of heavens, sits at the right hand of the Father.

*They shall be divided.* Because the Church, which consists of angels and of men, partly sojourns in the lowest places, partly reigns in the highest, has her citizens still separated one from another, the veil of heaven parting them.

VERS. 35.-- *And the table without the veil,* etc. [n: (ID. ibid.)] The table and the candlestick of the tabernacle signify the temporal [0276A] benefits of God, etc., *as far as* bearing witness to him.

VERS. 36.-- *Thou shalt make also a hanging,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 9)]. The narrative concerning the veil being set forth, etc., *as far as* because it rejoiced in the continence of flesh and of mind.

[n: (ID.)] According to the letter the tabernacle was a house consecrated to God, etc., *as far as* of breadth fifty.

VERS. 37.-- *And five pillars. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The pillars by which the hanging is suspended, etc., *as far as* the evangelical Scripture not yet laid open.

*Of setim wood. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The pillars of wood, etc., *as far as: but Jesus Christ.*

*The heads whereof shall be of gold,* etc., [n: (ID., ibid.)] The golden heads signify Christ, etc., *as far as* it is built up of the souls of the elect [0276B] that lack corruption.
