## CHAPTER XXVII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS 1.-- *Thou shalt make also an altar of wood. [n: (BED., de Tabernac., lib. II, cap. 11, tom. II.)]* Namely because clean and uncorrupted ought those hearts to be in which the Holy Spirit dwells.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And three cubits. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Such, well nigh, is the stature of a man, etc., *as far as* it does not pertain to the altar.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *And the horns,* etc. [n: (ID., Ibid.)] The altar of holocaust has four corners, etc., *as far as* it abides inviolable, nay rather the stronger.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *And thou shalt make for the uses thereof cauldrons. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The divers vessels of the altar, etc., *as far as* charity, never to be quenched, shall burn.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0276C] *And tongs. [n: (ID., Ibid.)]* The tongs are the preachers, etc., *as far as* they bid also to shine.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And fleshhooks, and firepans,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] The fleshhooks, which in Greek are called κρεάγραι, etc., *as far as* they should go beyond the measure of our capacity.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Receptacles of fire.* ⟦s2⟧ For this were they appointed, that by them the holy fire might be carried from the altar of holocaust to the altar of incense, evening and morning, for the setting on of the incense; because the teachers carry fire as it were from the altar of holocausts to the altar of incense, when by teaching they advance their hearers from virtue to virtue, and, as their merits grow, to higher things and to the more inward secrets of the divine vision. ⟦s3⟧ And they who imitate the hearts of their neighbours fervent with piety become receptacles of fires, because the flame of the heavenly sacrifice, [0276D] which they behold in their brethren's minds, they are busy to kindle in their own.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And a gridiron after the manner,* etc. [n: (BED., ubi supra, cap. 18.)] The whole altar was commanded to be made hollow, etc., *as far as* we think they learned it from the teachers of the Jews.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Beneath the hearth,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] The altar has a hearth, etc., *as far as* they may be brought to the end of perfection.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] If it moves anyone according to the letter, etc., *as far as* it may be found the cleaner.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Thou shalt make also bars.* ⟦s2⟧ The teachers, namely, who may call the Jewry and the Gentile world into one grace of faith with harmonious mouth and work. ⟦s3⟧ Or, on either side of the altar [0277A] there shall be bars for carrying it, when the preachers shall teach the minds of the faithful to hold the constancy of the virtues in prosperity and in adversity.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *As to thee [sic: Sicuttibi] in the mount. [n: (BEDA, ibid.)]* All the things which for us mystically, etc., *as far as* we are bidden always to do.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Thou shalt make also a court. [n: (ID., ubi supra, cap. 13.)]* As the Holy of holies, etc., *as far as* but the memory of it is rooted out from the inmost heart.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A hundred cubits. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* It is agreed that the number of a hundred, etc., *as far as* and they may take up the continence of things temporal.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *And twenty pillars. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The pillars on which the hangings are hung, etc., *as far as* because they sound forth nothing other than the word of God.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0277B] *With bases,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] By the bases of the pillars, etc., *as far as* they shall never lack the fruit of a reward.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of silver.* [n: (ID.)] The heads not silvered over, but wholly of silver: because the teachers wholly subject themselves to the divine Scripture, and by meditating and preaching the divine words make over to them soul and body. ⟦s2⟧ For as the members cannot live without the head, so they, accounting the divine oracles their own life, never cease to bear these by obeying, and to exalt themselves by humbling themselves.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *In like manner also on the side. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The same things are repeated concerning the side of the North, etc., *as far as* they are commanded to be made on one side of the tabernacle.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *But in the breadth,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] The breadth of the court which looks toward the setting of the sun, *as far [0277C] as* ten pillars lift up and bear it.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *In that breadth also of the court. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The breadth of the court looking toward the east, etc., *as far as: and the life of the world to come.*

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Fifty cubits shall be.* [n: (ID.)] By the fifty cubits of the eastern side are signified in general the beginnings of them that believe, because they are kept in the remission of sins and in the hope of future blessedness; and the particular distinction of these is subjoined, which, being well considered, is referred to the same end. ⟦s2⟧ For there follows.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *In which there shall be hangings of fifteen cubits allotted to one side, and three pillars,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] For fifteen, etc., *as far as* and to keep the body without complaint unto the day of the Lord.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And it shall have pillars,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] [0277D] In the entrance of the court there are four pillars, etc., *as far as* they spend all the senses of the body in the obedience of charity.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of hyacinth,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] Rightly in this entrance of the court of the hangings, etc., *as far as* they adorn the hanging woven with a fair variety of colours.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *All the pillars,* etc. [n: (ID.)] What he had said he inculcates the more diligently, that he may earnestly admonish the reader that none can be reckoned among the teachers of truth, that is, the pillars of the heavenly court, who shall not have learned to have, after the likeness of brass, an unconquered patience amid adversities; unless he shall have adorned himself by fencing himself about on every side, as with plates of silver, with the observance and the announcing of heavenly words; unless he shall have put himself under the divine [0278A] precepts by every subjection, as under a head of silver, and shall have made himself steadfast in the fear of God as upon a base of brass.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *In length,* etc. [n: (ID.)] A hundred cubits of length, etc., *as far as* and it shall be made of twisted fine linen, etc.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *All the vessels,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] These vessels are men in the Church, etc., *as far as the courts of the Lord.*

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Command the children of Israel. [n: (ID., lib. III de Tabern., cap. 1, tom. II.)]* All the adornment of the tabernacle being disposed, etc., *as far as* it may show the light of good action.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the trees of the olives. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* It is to be noted that the children of Israel are bidden to offer oil, etc., *as far as* for [0278B] the burning of the incense of prayers.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 21.-- *And they shall set. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* For it belongs not to all to preach the mysteries of the faith to the people, etc., *as far as* to such as James says: *Be ye not many masters, my brethren,* etc. [n: (Jac. III)].

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *It shall be perpetual,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] This clause, like those which to the tabernacle, etc., *as far as* since God is all in all.
