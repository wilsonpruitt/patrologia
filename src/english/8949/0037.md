## CHAPTER XXVIII. (cont.)

VERS. 41.-- *And thou shalt vest with these,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 9.)] With all these were Aaron and his sons to be vested, but distinctly, so that he indeed should use all of them; but the sons the last three, which are properly ascribed to them, when it is said: *But for the sons of Aaron thou shalt prepare linen tunics,* etc.

[n: (ID.)] The breeches, which are made for the covering of the shame of the body or of the flesh, etc., *as far as* with the breeches, [0282C] namely, the linen drawn tight, the girdle, and the tiara.

## CHAPTER XXIX.

VERS. 1.-- *But this also thou shalt do,* etc. [n: (BEDA, ubi supra. )] Thus far of what sort the habit of Aaron and of his sons ought to be, etc., *as far as* they show figuratively.

*Take a calf.* [n: (STRAB.)] Whosoever is high priest or priest ought to offer a bullock, that is, firmly to believe and to imitate the passion of Christ; and two rams, that he may believe that the same Christ consists of body and soul; and unleavened loaves, that is, cleanness of life; and sprinkled with oil, that among all the virtues he may not neglect the works of mercy, or, not of himself, but of the gift of grace, may believe that he has them.

VERS. 2.-- *And a wafer.* [n: (BEDA, ubi supra. )] A wafer [0282D] sprinkled with oil we offer to the Lord for our consecration, when all the things which we do grow fat through the inward grace of the Spirit in the devotion of the heart. Cakes anointed with oil, when we show without all doubt that the things we do are spiritual, for an example of living; by which oblations our consecration is perfected, while through good works and pure thoughts we acquire for ourselves, by God's gift, the merit of holiness.

*And when thou hast washed the father,* etc. [n: (ID.)] They are bidden first to be washed, then to be adorned with vestments, because whosoever undertakes the office of the priesthood ought to wash himself with the wave of tears, that he may approach worthy to the divine ministry.

[0283A] [n: (BEDA.)] There might be understood in this washing the baptism of the sacred font, if it followed that one chosen to the priesthood should then first be washed with the water of baptism unto the remission of sins, and if the Apostle did not contradict, saying: *Not a neophyte, lifted up into pride, he fall into the judgment of the devil [n: (I Tim. III)]*. [var: Vulg. 1Tim 3:6 *Non neophytum: ne in superbiam elatus, in judicium incidat diaboli*, "Not a neophyte: lest, being lifted up with pride, he fall into the judgment of the devil"; Migne prints no *ne*, so the purpose clause has lost its conjunction]

VERS. 5.-- *Thou shalt clothe Aaron,* etc. [n: (ID.)] It is to be noted that whereas in Exodus Aaron is asserted to be clothed with eight vestments, etc., *as far as* because he came in the name of the Father, saying: *I am in the Father and the Father is in me: and he that sees me, sees the Father also [n: (Joan. IV)]*. [var: Vulg. Jn 14:10-11 *ego in Patre, et Pater in me est* joined to Jn 14:9 *qui videt me, videt et Patrem*; Migne's own citation names Joan. IV]

VERS. 9.-- *After that,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 25 in Exod.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* to be sanctified by Moses.

VERS. 10.-- *Thou shalt bring near,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 126.)] [0283B] Another reading, etc., *as far as* upon the calf to be immolated.

VERS. 18.-- *It is an oblation to the Lord.* [n: (AUG.)] An odour of sweetness to the Lord, etc., *as far as* draws in the odour with bodily nostrils.

VERS. 22.-- *And the right shoulder. [n: (GREG., Past., part. II, c. 3.)]* The priest by the divine law, etc., *as far as* he may invite to lofty things by the shoulder of work.

VERS. 30.-- *And he that shall enter in. [n: (AUG., quaest. 129.)]* How then does he say that it is proper to this one alone, etc., *as far as* and on both sides that would be kept which is written: *Mercy exalteth itself above judgment [n: (Jac. II)]*.
