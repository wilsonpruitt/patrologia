## CHAPTER VII.

VERS. 1-3.-- « And the Lord said, » etc. « Of all clean living creatures thou shalt take seven and seven, » etc. [n: (RAB.)] Not a doubled seven, that is fourteen, but only seven ought we to [0107A] understand. Likewise two and two, not four, but only two, the male and the female. And it is to be noted that the clean animals are brought in by an odd number, that is by the number seven, but the unclean by an even one; because an odd number portends the appearance of virtue, but an even one weakness. The clean are the more numerous, that Noah, on going out of the ark, might have what he could offer in sacrifice and eat.

[n: (ISID. in Gen.)] « Of all living creatures, » etc. Not that the good are more numerous than the bad, etc., *as far as* which day is, reckoned from the passion, the third among the days that roll round through all time, and the first, and the eighth.

[n: (AUG. in Gen.)] It is asked: could so great an ark be built in a hundred years by four men, that is, by Noah and his three sons? But if it could not, it was no great matter to employ other workmen, although, [0107B] once the wage of their work had been got, they cared not whether Noah was acting wisely or to no purpose, and did not enter into it, because they did not believe what he believed.

VERS. 4.-- « I will rain, » etc. [n: (ISID. Quaest. in Gen., c. 7.)] Because every guilt against the decalogue is committed throughout the whole world, which is divided into four parts: and four times ten make forty; whether the guilt be contracted out of prosperity, which pertains to the days; or out of adversity, which pertains to the nights, it is washed away in baptism.

VERS. 5-6.-- « Noah therefore did all things, » etc. « And he was six hundred years old, » etc. [n: (ISID., Ibid)]. Noah was five hundred years old when the Lord commanded him to make the ark, etc., *as far as* which is the square of three.

[0107C]VERS. 7-9.-- « Noah went in, and his sons, his wife, » etc. [n: (RAB.)] It is to be noted that Noah goes into the ark apart with his sons, and their wives apart. Therefore it is said distinctly: « Noah went in, and his sons, » and it is distinctly added, « his wife and the wives of his sons. » But they went out mingled, the men with the women, for so it is written: « Noah went out, and his wife.[var: Vulg. Gn 8:18 reads *Egressus est ergo Noe, et filii ejus : uxor illius, et uxores filiorum ejus cum eo*, "Noah therefore went out, and his sons: his wife, and the wives of his sons with him."] For there is a time of embracing and a time of abstaining[var: Vulg. Ecl 3:5 reads *tempus amplexandi, et tempus longe fieri ab amplexibus*, "a time to embrace, and a time to be far from embraces."] [n: (Eccli. III)]. »

VERS. 10.-- « And when they had passed, » etc. [n: (ISID.)] Because we are baptized in the hope of the rest to come, which the seventh day signifies, all flesh outside the ark was consumed by the flood; because outside the Church the water of baptism, though it be the same water, avails not unto salvation, but unto ruin.

[0107D]VERS. 11-14.-- « In the six hundredth year, » etc. *Allegorically.* It is to be noted that in the six hundredth year the waters of the flood overflowed, because in the sixth age of the world the sacrament of baptism was first entrusted to us, and this on the seventeenth day of the second month, because the number ten signifies the decalogue. The number seven, on account of the Sabbath, signifies the seventh rest, and because in the decalogue the renunciation of vices is seen, whosoever is initiated in baptism professes, for the sake of eternal rest, to renounce all vices.

« The cataracts. » Windows, that is to say clouds, which were opened, that from them unwonted and greater rains might be poured out. But cataracts are properly the mouths of the Nile, though by misuse they are set for all windows.

[0108A] [n: (RAB.)] Concerning the ark it is commonly asked: with so great a capacity as is described, could it carry all the animals that are said to have entered, and their food? Origen resolves this by the geometrical cubit, asserting that it was not said in vain that Moses was learned in all the wisdom of the Egyptians, who loved geometry, and that the geometrical cubit contains as much as six of ours.

VERS. 15, 16.-- « Two and two, » etc. [n: (AUG. ubi supra.)] He commanded above that seven and seven of the clean, and two and two of the unclean, be taken. How then does he now say that two and two entered, whether of the clean or of the unclean? But this is referred not to the number of the clean and the unclean, but to the male and the female. For in all there are two, male and female.

[0108B]But it is to be noted that what is said, « in which was the spirit of life, » is said not of men only, but of cattle also: because some would have that which is written, « he breathed into his face the spirit of life, »[var: Vulg. Gn 2:7 reads *inspiravit in faciem ejus spiraculum vitae*, "breathed into his face the breath of life."] understood of the Holy Spirit; but certain codices have better, « the breath of life. »

VERS. 17-19.-- « Forty days, » etc. [n: (ALC.)] The rain overflowed by the number forty, which shows the tribulation of penance. After forty days Noah opened the window, signifying that heaven is opened to them that fast. Whence Moses, Elias, and the Saviour himself consecrated fasts by the number forty, as being necessary in three times: before the law, under the law, under grace.

VERS. 20-22-- « Higher by fifteen cubits, » etc. [0108C] [n: (AUG. in Gen.)] If the earth of Mount Olympus (as history relates) could reach into the space of the tranquil air, etc., *as far as* to the holding fast of rest and of faith in the resurrection.

« Fifteen. » There are those who think that neither the quality of the earth nor the height of the mountains was, before the flood, such and so great as it is today.

VERS. 23, 24.-- « They were destroyed from the earth. » [n: (ALC.)] Therefore the animals of the land perished rather than those of the water, because they live off the accursed earth.
