## CHAPTER X.

[0113B]VERS. 1.-- « These are the generations, » etc. Two sons of Noah being blessed and the middle one cursed, their generations are woven out, from whom seventy-two generations arose: fifteen from Japheth, thirty from Cham, twenty-seven from Sem. To which the Lord sent seventy-two disciples.

« Sem » [n: (ALC.)] Sem, as they reckon, obtained Asia by lot; Cham Africa, Japheth Europe.

« Japheth. » Of the younger son, of whom were born seven sons, who held the northern region from Taurus and Amanus, mountains of Cilicia and Syria, as far as the river Tanais, but in Europe as far as Gadira. Leaving their names to the places and to the nations, of which very many have been changed, while the rest remain.

VERS. 2-6.-- « Gomer. » These are the Galatians, according to [0113C] Josephus, who in Latin are called Gallograeci.

« Thubal. » The Iberians, who are also the Spaniards, from whom Celtiberia, though some suspect them to be Italians.

« Chanaan. » He obtained the land which the Jews afterwards possessed, the Chananites being cast out.

VERS. 8.-- « Regma and Saba, » etc. [n: (HIERON.)] Little by little they lost their ancient names, and what names they now have in place of the old ones is unknown.

« Saba. » Another than the one above, and they differ in the writing: for that one is written with *samech,* this with *schin*; for where we say: The kings of the Arabians and of Saba shall bring gifts, » in the Hebrew there stands both *scheba* and *seba.*

VERS. 8.-- « Nemrod. » Who wished to pierce heaven beyond nature. He signifies the devil, who says: [0113D] « I will ascend above the stars of heaven. » Nemrod, according to Josephus, seized upon tyranny in a new lust of rule, and was the author of the building of the tower which should touch heaven. « He himself began to be mighty, » or « a giant, » according to others. But Scripture records that giants were born before this. But perhaps the newness of the human race to be repaired after the flood is being recorded, « because he himself began to be a giant upon the earth. »

VERS. 9.-- « A stout hunter. » [n: (ALC.)] That is, an oppressor and destroyer of men, whom he enticed to build a tower against God.

VERS. 10, 12.-- « Babylon. » It is confusion, because there the division of tongues was made.

VERS. 11.-- « Assur. » The empire of the Assyrians, [0114A] which was first under Ninus, who built Nineveh, which on account of its penitence is called *beautiful*.

VERS. 13-20.-- « But Mesraim indeed, » etc. [n: (HIERON.)] Except the Laabin, from whom the Libyans were afterwards named, who before were called Phutaeans, and the Chasluim, who were afterwards called Philistiim, whom we corruptly call *Palestinians*, the other six nations are unknown to us, because they were overthrown in the Ethiopian war. But they possessed the land from Gaza as far as the farthest borders of Egypt.

« Ludim and Anamim. Laabim, Nephetuim, » etc. All these are unknown, either because the nations were destroyed in the Ethiopian war, or because it is not known what names they now have.

VERS. 21.-- « Of Sem. » Of him he speaks last, [0114B] because he pursues his generation, and the line of that generation.

« Heber. » Who nevertheless is the fifth from Sem the son of Noah. Whether because the Hebrews are said to be named from him, or because through him the generation passes over to Abraham? What therefore is the more probable, that the Hebrews are called as it were Heberaeans, or as it were Abrahaeans, is rightly asked.

VERS. 22-24.-- « Assur. » Who built Nineveh.

VERS. 25-30.-- « And there were born sons to Heber, » etc. Heber, *a passer-over*, from whom the Hebrews, that is, *passers-over*. They are true Hebrews who pass over from things present to things to come, from things that fall away to things that abide. These nations, from the river Conus, possessed all the region which is called Geria.
