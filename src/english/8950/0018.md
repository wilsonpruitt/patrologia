## CHAPTER XIV.

VERS. 1.-- « Amraphel. » He is interpreted *he said that it should fall,* and he is king of Sennaar, which is interpreted *the shaking out of teeth* [0118D] or *their stench.* He signifies the heretics, who tend toward a fall, and with deceitful words vomit out a stinking meaning.

« Arioch. » He is interpreted *Drunken* or *drunkenness*: he signifies the folly of the Gentiles, who, drunk with iniquity, worship the creature for the Creator, and are rolled about in the enticements of the world. « Chodorlahomor. » He is interpreted *A comely sheaf*, who is king of Elam, that is, *of this world:* he signifies hypocrisy, which, although it be vain and pernicious, yet seems comely on the surface, but in the time to come what it was shall appear.

« Thadal. » *Knowing the yoke* or *a scout,* who is king of the nations; he signifies avarice, which, shrewd and crafty, subjugates many to itself.

[0119A] « Amraphel, » etc. These four kings figure the four principal vices, contrary to the four principal virtues; which strive to subjugate the five senses signified by the five kings, through the concupiscence of the flesh, through the concupiscence of the eyes, through the ambition of the world.

VERS. 2-13.-- « Balae. » [n: (HIERON.)] In Hebrew it is called *devouring*. The Hebrews hand down that this is elsewhere called *Salissam*, and *a heifer that dismays,* because it was swallowed up by a third earthquake. And that from that time in which Sodom and Gomorrha, Adama and Seboim were overthrown by divine fire, it is named *the little one*. For Segor is translated as little, which in the Syriac tongue is called *Zoara*. But the valley of the Salt-pits, in which were wells of bitumen, after the wrath of God and the rain of brimstone, was turned into the Dead [0119B] Sea, which by the Greeks is called the lake of Asphalt, that is, of bitumen.

*Mystically* Four kings overcome five, while the contrary powers, abusing the five senses of the body, ensnare them with vices and lead them captive.

*Allegorically* Such kings overcome first Bara. who is interpreted *malice* or *a creature,* and is king of the Sodomites, that is, *of blindness,* or *of a silent beast,* when they persuade those who persevere in the old malice to follow the blindness of lust after the manner of beasts. They conquer also Bersa, that is, *set in straitness,* king of Gomorrha, that is, of a swelling and seditious people, when they overwhelm and cast down those who are straitened by the cares of the world and fear not to work the wickedness of rapine. They conquer likewise Sennaab king of Adama, that [0119C] is, *of the earthly,* when they exhort those who savour earthly things only to remain in luxury, so that they say: « Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we shall die [n: (I Cor. XV)]. » They conquer Semeber, who is interpreted *there destruction,* and is king of Seboim, that is, *of the girdle of grief,* when through anger and envy they deceive the incautious; whence Job V: « Anger killeth the foolish, and envy slayeth the little one. » They conquer also king Bale, when they cast the faint-hearted into despair; for Bale is interpreted *a headlong fall* or *a swallowing up:* to this above all the devil strives, that after the fall of sin he may cast into despair.

VERS. 4-6.-- « Twelve, » etc. The number twelve is a superfluous number. In which superfluity our five senses serve the vain superstition which [0119D] Chodorlahomor signifies, and the four vices lying next to it; and when they would withdraw, they suffer a grievous persecution, and are often overcome.

VERS. 7-12.-- « And they smote. » [n: (HIERON.)] Another edition has, etc., *as far as* because there God judged the people.

« Raphaim in Astaroch, »[var: Vulg. Gn 14:5 reads *Raphaim in Astarothcarnaim*.] etc. The names of the places and of the nations signify those who live carnally, whom vices easily overcome.

VERS. 13.-- « Hebrew. » The Seventy, *one from beyond the river;* because, namely, coming from Mesopotamia, the river Euphrates being crossed, he set his seat in the land of Chanaan. Whence Josue the son of Nave: « Why will you serve the gods of your fathers who are beyond the river? »[var: Vulg. Jos 24:14–15 has no such sentence; it reads *auferte deos quibus servierunt patres vestri in Mesopotamia* and *utrum diis, quibus servierunt patres vestri in Mesopotamia*.]

[0120A]VERS. 14-17.-- « And when Abram had heard this, » etc. [n: (ISID.)] *Mystically.* In like manner our faith, when it has been strengthened by the principal Spirit, subjects the five senses of the body as five kings, triumphing by the word of God over the outward man. He overcame his adversaries in three hundred and eighteen, because we, in the sacrament of the cross, which the letter *tau* signifies, which signifies three hundred, overcome the five carnal senses that before held us captive in divers vices. Eighteen, multiplied out of three and six, signifies the faith of the Trinity and the perfection of work, by which the full victory of the virtues against the vices is accomplished.

[n: (STRAB.)] *Allegorically.* The war of Abraham with the four kings signifies the war of the virtues and of the vices. The father therefore of faith, and the first way of believing, fought for his brother [0120B] Lot and conquered, not in a multitude of an army, but in three hundred and eighteen trained servants of his household. So let each man wage war for his own soul against spiritual wickednesses with three hundred and eighteen servants of his household, that is, with the help of the holy cross: for the letter *tau*, which has the figure of a cross, expresses three hundred.

Now Dan is one of the springs of the Jordan; for the other is called Jor, which is interpreted *a stream;* the two springs therefore, which are not far distant from one another, being joined into one streamlet, it is called Jordan.

VERS. 18, 19.-- « But Melchisedech indeed, » etc. [n: (HIER.)] Now the Hebrews say that he is Sem the son of Noah, and by the reckoning of the years they show that he lived until Isaac: and that all the firstborn from Noah to [0120C] Aaron were high priests. But Salem was afterwards called Jerusalem; the Apostle, recording Melchisedech « without father and mother, » refers it to Christ, and through him to the Church of the Gentiles, because the glory of the head is referred to the members. Since he blessed Abraham circumcised, and in him Levi, and so Aaron, from whom afterwards the priesthood. From which he would gather that the priesthood of the Church, which has the uncircumcision, blessed the circumcised priesthood of the Synagogue. But because he says: « Thou art a priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedech, » our ministry of the true order is signified, to be consecrated not through Aaron by the immolating of irrational victims, but by the offering of bread and wine, that is, of the body and blood of the Lord.

[n: (STRAB.)] Melchisedech, who is interpreted *king of justice,* [0120D] and is called *king of peace,* signifies Christ, through whom we have been reconciled, and who at the end of the world is to judge justly.

VERS. 20-23.-- « He gave him tithes. » [n: (ISID.)] The patriarch therefore, knowing, etc., *as far as* partly not denying the stipends due to those who fight lawfully.

VERS. 24.-- « Who came, » etc. For faith sometimes uses the testimonies of unbelievers; whence the Apostle to Titus I: « Evil beasts, slothful bellies; » but to the profit of the faithful.
