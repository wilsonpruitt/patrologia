## CHAPTER XVII.

VERS. 1-4.-- . . . . . . « And I will set my covenant, » etc. In the Hebrew *berith* ( ), which signifies not a testament, but a covenant, or a compact.

VERS. 5-7.-- « Neither shall thy name any more be called Abram. » [n: (HIERON.)] The Hebrews say that God added out of his own name, which among them is the tetragrammaton, the letter *h* to Abraham and to Sara. Now he was called [0123A] Abram, which is *an exalted father;* afterward he was called Abraham, which is *a father of many,* for *of nations* is not had in the name, but is understood. Nor is it to be wondered at that, whereas among the Greeks and among us the letter *a* seems to have been added, the letter *h* is said to have been added: for it is the idiom of the Hebrew tongue to write *h* and to read *a*: as, on the contrary, to pronounce *h* and to write *a*.

VERS. 8, 9.-- « And I will give to thee and to thy seed. . . .and all the land of Chanaan. » Chanaan is interpreted *their motion:* whose land is received into possession by the faithful, when the mind, first driven about by divers motions, being changed for the better, is afterward stretched out toward the things before with eyes that turn not back

« Into an everlasting possession. » [n: (AUG.)] Scripture calls that eternal whose end is not appointed, or that which [0123B] is not so done that it is not thenceforth to be done, so far as pertains to the care or the power of the doer; whence: He shall serve for ever who shall not know how to use a little.


VERS. 10.-- « The male among you shall be circumcised, » etc. [n: (STRAB.)] For three causes is this enjoined upon Abraham. First, that he might please by the obedience of the commandment, by the transgression whereof Adam had displeased; secondly, that by this sign he might be distinguished from the other nations; thirdly, that being circumcised in that member wherein lust is wont to have dominion, he might know that chastity was to be kept by him and unchastity to be cut away.

VERS. 11-13.-- « The flesh of the foreskin. » [n: (RAB. STRAR.)] Circumcision made in one member signifies the spiritual circumcision, which is to be shown forth in the heart and in all [0123C] the senses. The heart is to be circumcised, that we think not hurtful things; the eyes, that we see not vanity; the ears are to be circumcised, that is, to be stopped, that they hear not blood: and so in all the senses circumcision is to be celebrated.

[n: (ALCUIN. in Gen., tom. I.)] And circumcision was for this cause commended to Abraham, etc., *as far as* whence of old transgressors begot unto death.

*Allegorically.* Even then, because of the pilgrimage to come, lest his seed should be mingled with the nations, the compact of circumcision is given for a sign: because it signifies nature renewed through baptism, after the stripping off of the old man through Christ, who rose again after baptism. But he commanded the home-born servants, and the bought ones also, to be circumcised, that he might teach that the grace of redemption pertains to all [0123D]. There too the names of the parents are changed, that all things may sound of newness. Abram, *an exalted father;* Abraham, *a father of many nations.*

VERS. 14.-- « The male whose flesh of his foreskin, » etc. [n: (ISID.)] It is asked: how ought circumcised [cj: *circumcisi*; read *incircumcisi*, "uncircumcised"] boys (who did not make void the compact of God, but they who neglected to circumcise them) to perish? But they made void the compact of God not according to their deed, but according to their origin in the first man, in whom all have sinned. For all are born sinners, not properly but originally. Therefore all perish who are not set free through regeneration.

VERS. 15, 16.-- « Sarai thy wife, » etc. [0124A] [n: (ISID.)] The promise concerning the calling of the Gentiles is declared in Isaac, the son of promise, that is, of grace, not of nature, since he is of an aged father and a barren mother: and because this was to be not by generation, as Ismael was, but by regeneration, circumcision was commanded, when the son of Sara is promised in the type of the Church, not when Ismael is, who is the type of the Jews.

[n: (HIERON.-ISID.)] They err who think that Sara was at first written with one *r*, and that afterward another *r.* was added. And because *r* among the Greeks signifies *a hundred*, they surmise many follies about this name, whereas, however they may wish the word to have been changed, it ought to have not a Greek but a Hebrew reckoning. For it is Hebrew. For no one, calling anyone whatsoever in one language, takes the etymology of the word from [0124B] another. Sarai therefore was at first called ( ) by *scin, res, iod.* When therefore the *iod* was taken away, the letter *he* was added, which is read for *a*, and she was called *Sara* ( ). For before she was called *my princess,* because she was as it were the mother of one family: afterward, absolutely, *a princess,* being namely about to be princess of all nations. Some most wretchedly surmise that she was first called *leprosy,* and afterward *a princess,* since leprosy is called *sarai* ( ), which in our tongue seems to have some likeness, but in the Hebrew is utterly different. For it is written by *zadich* and *ain* and *res* and *tau;* which manifestly differs from the three above, that is, *schin, res* and *he,* by which *Sara* is written.

VERS. 17, 18.-- « Abraham fell upon his face, and laughed in his heart. » [n: (HIERON.)] A little after [0124C] there follows: « And thou shalt call his name Isaac: » which laughter is interpreted of the laughter of Abraham, not of the laughter of Sara. For after his son was called Isaac from the laughter of Abraham, Sara is read to have laughed.

« His name Isaac. » It is to be known that four in the Old Testament were called openly by their own names before they were born: Ismael, Isaac, Samson and Josias. Read the Scriptures.

VERS. 19.-- « I will establish my compact, » etc. But the compact of the Lord is with Isaac, that is, with the faithful people, whom mother Church has begotten through faith and baptism. « And an everlasting covenant, » because the possession of eternal life is promised to him alone who with his head, Christ, shall possess the kingdom of heaven for ever.

VERS. 20.-- « Upon Ismael, » etc. *Historically.* [0124D] Ismael signifies the people of the Jews, which was multiplied exceedingly; he begot twelve princes, since the multitude of carnal Jews, begotten of the twelve patriarchs, was divided into twelve tribes.

VERS. 21.-- « In the other year. » The other year, wherein Sara bore Isaac, is the times of regeneration, in which the blood of Christ was poured out and the human race restored to life.

VERS. 22.-- « God went up to Abraham, » [var: Vulg. Gn 17:22 *ascendit Deus ab Abraham*, "God went up from Abraham"] etc. [n: ( Glossa Graeca. )] Therefore, so to speak, he had come down, and had shown himself humble, in appointing the law of circumcision.

VERS. 23-27.-- « And Abraham took, » etc. God did not intend that only the foreskin of the flesh of Abraham [0125A] and of his offspring should be circumcised, but upon him whom he had chosen out of all he was conferring the gifts of the eternal Testament; whence Paul, Phil. III: « For we are the circumcision, who serve God in the spirit. » And elsewhere, Rom. II: « For it is not he that is in the open who is a Jew, neither is that circumcision which is in the open, in the flesh. » Let us therefore circumcise by the word of God our ears, our lips, our heart, our foreskin and all the members of the flesh. Of the circumcision of the ears it is said, Luc. VIII: « He that hath ears to hear, let him hear; » with uncircumcised ears we cannot hear the words of wisdom and of truth. Let us circumcise our lips, that we speak nothing base, or evil, or idle. He circumcises the flesh of the foreskin who uses marriage for the sole cause of posterity, at fixed and lawful seasons: he who does otherwise [0125B] does not circumcise. But the Church, strengthened by the grace of Christ crucified for her, refrains herself not only from things unlawful, but even from things allowed, and as a virgin spouse of Christ flourishes with chaste virgins, in whom the true circumcision of the foreskin is kept in the flesh. But he who does not commit adultery by obscene desires, or by heretical opinions and blasphemous assertions, but keeps the purity of faith in his conscience, is circumcised in heart: and of him it can be said: « Blessed are the clean of heart [n: (Matth. V)], » etc. Let the hands be circumcised, that they do no evil, the feet, that they run not into evil: the sight, that it covet not unlawful things; the taste, that it eat and drink to the glory of God; the smell, that it may take the odour of Christ, and by works of mercy scatter abroad the odour of sweetness. Each several member also, if it serve God in its offices, [0125C] is circumcised. If they run riot beyond the law prescribed, uncircumcised.

[n: (HIERON.-STRAB.)] It is wont to be asked: how were women saved, who were not circumcised? By faith, namely, and by the offering of victims, as before circumcision holy men were cleansed whence: « Purifying their hearts by faith. »
