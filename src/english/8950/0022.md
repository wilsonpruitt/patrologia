## CHAPTER XVIII. (cont.)

*Morally.* Some it lifts up not through the swelling of understanding, but through works of mercy; certain men, while they see themselves [0129B] to abound in earthly things, do not seek the true riches, and love not the eternal kingdom, because they think temporal things sufficient. The fault therefore is not in the understanding, but in the affection. For all things which God founded are good. But he who uses good things ill, acts so that through the gluttony of voracity he dies by that bread whereby he ought to have lived. Lazarus the poor man had come to rest, torments were racking the proud rich man [n: (Luc. XVI)]: yet Abraham had been rich, who held Lazarus in his bosom. Yet he says: « I will speak to my Lord, whereas I am dust and ashes, » etc. How did he value his riches, who accounted himself dust and ashes? Openly we discern in what place he had set himself, who accounted himself dust and ashes even while he spoke with God. With what punishment therefore are they to be struck, who [0129C] make no progress toward the highest things and exalt themselves over the least?

VERS. 28-33.-- « There be five. » Five cities were burned with showers of fire. For all who have handled the five senses of the body lustfully are to be burned in the fire to come.

« What if there be found, » etc. [n: (ALC.)] It is not necessary to take this of every place. But of Sodom it could be said, because God knew that there were not even ten there, and therefore it was thus answered to Abraham, to the aggravating of the iniquity of the Sodomites

« Ten. » The number ten designates the cross of Christ, because the letter X, by which ten is expressed, is formed in the figure of a cross.

« He said. » For he knew that there was not even one there besides [0129D] Lot; this is said to the aggravating of their iniquity, as in Jeremias: « See if you find a man doing justice [n: (Jer. V)]; [var: Vulg. Jr 5:1 *an inveniatis virum facientem judicium*, "whether you can find a man doing judgment"] as if to say: Find but one, and I spare them.
