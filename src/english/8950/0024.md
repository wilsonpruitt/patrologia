## CHAPTER XX.

VERS. 1.-- « Setting forward thence, » etc. Cades, *the fountain of judgment;* Sur, *narrowness;* by which patience is figured.

VERS. 2-6.-- « And he said of Sara, » etc. [n: (AUG.)] It is commonly asked how at that age Abraham could fear peril on account of Sara's beauty: but the force of her form is to be wondered at, which could still be loved.

« And he said of Sara, » etc. [n: (ISID., in Gen., tom. VI.)] He was silent that she was his wife, and said she was his sister, lest he himself should be killed and she be possessed as a captive by strangers, being sure that she would be kept inviolate by God, as at Pharaoh's court, [0133D] etc., *as far as* « Call not [any man] father upon earth, for one is your Father who is in heaven. »

« And he said, » etc. [n: (AUG.)] As to what is said to Abimelech on Sara's behalf, « And I spared thee, that thou shouldst not sin against me, » [var: Vulg. *custodivi te*, "I kept thee"] it is to be observed that God did this when he warned him that she whom he thought a sister was Abraham's wife. And it is to be noted that sin is committed against God when such things are done as men think either not to be sins, or to be reckoned light, as being sins in the flesh.

VERS. 7-11.-- « He shall pray for thee, » etc. [n: (CHRYSOST., hom. 45 in Gen.)] But know thou that his prayers shall grant thee life.

VERS. 12.-- « Otherwise also, » etc. [n: (HIERON.)] Since [0134A] in the Hebrew there stands *vegam* ( ) that is, also truly « she is my sister, the daughter of my father, but not the daughter of my mother, » and it sounds rather that she was Abraham's sister. In excuse we say that such marriages were not yet forbidden by law.

« The daughter of my father. » Another translation, more plainly, « from a father, and not from a mother; » for she was the daughter of Aran, Abraham's brother, and Lot's sister.

VERS. 13.-- « But after God brought me out of my father's house, I said to her, » etc. After the word of God was rejected from Judaea, all the faithful are Christ's brothers and sisters; whence Matth. XII: « Whosoever shall do the will of my Father, who is in heaven, he is my brother, and sister, and mother. »

[0134B]VERS. 14.-- « He took therefore. » Allegorically Abraham signifies Christ, Sara the Church. Abraham is honored on account of Sara's beauty, and Christ on account of the Church; whence psal. LXVII: « God is wonderful in his saints. » Sara had a husband, but he lay hidden; the Church has Christ, who is not seen in the world, being received into heaven.

VERS. 15.-- « Wheresoever it shall please thee, » etc. They who had once put off believing through fear of princes, at the bidding of those same princes betook themselves to the Church, which is Christ's sister and wife, and spread abroad the glory of Christ and of the Church through all the parts of the world.

VERS. 16.-- « A thousand pieces of silver, » etc. He speaks as though in jest: because she called him her brother who [0134C] was her husband, and he exhorts her to remember this hereafter, lest she afterwards, being caught, incur a like reproach. A thousand pieces of silver are given to Christ when eloquent teachers of the world are taken up out of Gentile converse to hold and to preach the faith of Christ. For the number of a thousand, squared from the number ten, designates the stable and solid doctrine of evangelical preaching.

VERS. 17.-- « And when he prayed. » When the Son prays the Father, the barrenness of worldly men, and of princes, and of subject peoples is healed.
