## CHAPTER XXIII.

[0140A]

VERS. 1-6.-- « And Sara lived, » etc. « And she died in the city of Arbee, » etc. [n: (HIER. in Quaest. Hebr.)] Which is in the valley: this is not had in the authentic books, etc., *as far as* as is openly shown in the book of Josue.

VERS. 7-15.-- « Abraham rose up, » etc. [n: (AUG., Quaest. in Gen.)] It is asked how Abraham adored the people of the land, etc., *as far as* and therefore the adorer was to be corrected.

VERS. 16.-- « And when he had heard, » etc. [n: (HIER., de locis Hebr.)] In the Hebrew, as we have set it down, etc., *as far as* since he also is reproved who has received unwillingly.

VERS. 17-20.-- « And the field was confirmed, » etc. [0140B] [n: (GREG., lib. VI Moral., c. 25.)] He hints at a double sepulchre, etc., *as far as* or on account of the urgency of action puts off the things to be contemplated.

« The cave. » And the wooded field round about it is bought for the burial of Sara by the patriarch from the lords of the land for four hundred sicles of silver: because, for being free for wisdom and giving study to action, under the leaves of mystical words, as under fruitful trees, a quiet place is not otherwise acquired for preachers, unless by the evangelical doctrine, shining with the brightness of silver, eternal blessedness be urged upon earthly powers by them. Which is well figured by a hundred taken four times, because it is carried over from the left hand to the right.
