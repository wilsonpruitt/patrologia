## CHAPTER XXIV. (cont.)

« And he loved her so much, » etc. [n: (GREG. ut supra. )] Because from the gain of the Church Christ wiped away the sorrow that came of the loss of the Synagogue, because while the Church, coming out of the Gentile world, is brought to the couch of contemplation, Judaea is held for nothing. Isaac too is called *laughter,* and Rebecca *patience*: and laughter comes of gladness, patience of tribulation. And although the Church be now suspended in the contemplation of heavenly joy, yet she has in herself somewhat whereat she may grieve [0144B] for the weight of the flesh. But Isaac and Rebecca are joined together, that is, laughter and patience, according to that of Rom. XII: « Rejoicing in hope, patient in tribulation, » that prosperous things may gladden out of contemplation, adverse things disturb out of tribulation.
