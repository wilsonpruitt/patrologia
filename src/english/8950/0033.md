## CHAPTER XXVII. (cont.)

« And the time shall come, when thou shalt shake off and loose his yoke. » , etc. This was fulfilled when the [0153A] Idumeans rebelled that they might not be under Juda. « And the time shall come, » etc. As if to say: The Jewish people shall at the end be set free from the servitude of sin, so that when « the fulness of the Gentiles shall have come in, all Israel shall be saved [n: (Rom. XI)]. »

VERS. 41.-- « And he said in his heart. » Since Scripture says that Esau treated in his heart of his brother's death, how or by whom were these things told to Rebecca? But it is given to be understood that all things were divinely revealed to her. Because of the incurable hatred of the Jews, by the spirit of counsel it was commanded to the apostles that, Judea being left, they should pass over to the Gentiles.

« The days of mourning shall come, » etc. For he would not do this while his father lived, lest he should incur his hatred, and be cursed by him.

[n: (DIOD.)] But that which is written, « Esau said in [0153B] his heart, » signifies that Esau longed for his brother's death not with a sudden anger, but with a perfected consent of the mind.

VERS. 42.-- « These things were told to Rebecca, » etc. Here consequently is set down the mother's meeting of it, when it is said, « These things were told. » From which it is plain that Esau did not so hide the aforesaid contrivance in his heart, but that it appeared outwardly in signs and in words.

« Who sending and calling Jacob, » etc. That she might induce him to give place to wrath and to withdraw for a time. There follows:

« Why shall I be deprived of both my sons? » etc. As if she should say: If he assail thee, it may be that you shall both be slain; or if one remain, it will behove that he be a fugitive from me.

[0153C] « Rebecca also said, » etc. Because Jacob could not fittingly withdraw without his father's knowledge, therefore Rebecca turned herself to inducing Isaac to send Jacob his son to Laban, that he might there take a wife, saying.

« I am weary, » etc. That is, because of the wives of Esau: but she did not tell Isaac himself another cause, namely of Esau's contrivance against the life of Jacob, lest in his old age he should be too much afflicted.

« If Jacob take a wife, » etc. As if she should say: If thou lovest my life, send him to the house of my brother, that he may there take a wife.

## CHAPTER XXVIII.

VERS. 1-9.-- « Isaac therefore called, » etc. *(AUG., [0153D] Q. in Gen.)* What the Latin codices have: « Go into Mesopotamia, into the house of Bathuel, » etc., the Greeks have: « Flee into Mesopotamia, » etc., so that it may be understood that Isaac also understood that Esau had thought upon his brother's death.

« And setting out. » [n: (ISID.)] Jacob, fleeing the wiles of his brother, leaving his father's house and his parents, goes into a far country, that he may take a wife. In like manner Christ, leaving his parents according to the flesh, that is, the Jews, and his country, that is, Jerusalem, and all the regions of Judea, went away to the Gentiles, that he might take to himself the Church, according to that of Osee II: « I will call that which is not my people, » etc.

VERS. 10.-- « Jacob therefore going forth, » etc. *(GREG., [0154A] lib. V Moral., c. 11, tom. I.)* To sleep in the way, etc., *as far as* « for the Lord is sweet. »

VERS. 11.-- « He took of the stones, » etc. [n: (ISID.)] The sleeping of Jacob in the way, the death of Christ on the cross. The stone set under his head is Christ according to his humanity, whom Jacob anointed, that Christ might be signified, for χρίστος in Greek is *anointed* in Latin. But the head is Christ God. The stone therefore set under the head of Jacob signifies the humanity of Christ joined to the God who assumed it. « For the head of the man is Christ, the head of Christ is God [n: (I Cor. XI.)]

VERS. 12.-- « And he saw in his sleep, » etc. [n: (GREG., Regist. l. I, ep. 24.)] Jacob signifies preachers, who not only, by contemplating, [0154B] seek after the head of the Church, that is, Christ, but descend by compassion to his members: which the angels ascending and descending show forth. Hence Moses frequently enters the tabernacle, and goes out; and he who within is caught up to contemplation, without is pressed by the business of the weak; within he considers hidden things, without he bears the burdens of the carnal. He who in doubtful matters ever runs back to the tabernacle, consults the Lord before the ark of the testament, giving an example to rulers, that when they are in doubt without as to what they should ordain, they may return to the mind as to a tabernacle, and, as it were before the ark of the testament, may consult the Lord concerning the things they doubt of, seeking within themselves the pages of the sacred eloquence.

*Morally.* To sleep in the way is, in the way of this world, to rest from the hindrance of worldly actions. [0154C] Jacob sleeping saw angels: because they behold divine things who close their eyes from the desire of temporal things: which, being well closed, the devil opened, saying: « In whatsoever day you shall eat, your eyes shall be opened [n: (Gen. III)]. »

« He saw in his sleep, » etc. [n: (ISID.)] The angels ascending and descending by the ladder are the evangelists and the preachers of Christ, ascending to the understanding of the Divinity; passing beyond every creature, that they may find in the beginning the Word with God, by whom all things were made. Descending, that they may find him « made of a woman, made under the law, » etc. In that ladder also from earth to heaven, from flesh to spirit: because in it the carnal, by advancing, as it were by ascending, become spiritual. To whom, to be nourished with milk, [0154D] the spiritual descend, because they cannot speak to them as to spiritual men, but as to carnal. He himself is above in the head, he himself below in his body, that is, the Church; he himself the ladder, who says in John XIV: « I am the way, » etc. To him men ascend, that he may be understood in the heights: and they descend, that he may be nourished in his little members; through him they raise themselves up, that they may look for him on high: and they humble themselves, that they may announce him loftily and with measure.

VERS. 13-15.-- « I am the Lord God of Abraham, » etc. Mystically it signifies the command of the Divinity directed to the humanity of Christ, to whom the land, that is, the Church, is promised; and to his seed, that is, to the Christian people. Whence psalm II: « Ask of me, [0155A] and I will give thee the nations for thy inheritance, and the utmost parts of the earth for thy possession. »

VERS. 16, 17.-- « And when he had awaked, » etc. Whosoever shall have awaked after the torpor of sloth, and shall have truly risen up through the exercise of good work, shall understand in the Church the surpassing grace of God and the entrance of the heavenly kingdom.

VERS. 18.-- « Rising therefore, » etc. [n: (AUG., Q. in Gen., tom. III.)] That Jacob « set up » « the stone which he had put under his head » for a title, and poured oil upon it, he did nothing like to idolatry: because neither then nor afterwards did he frequent the stone by adoring it or by sacrificing to it; but it was a sign and a prophecy pertaining to the anointing, whence Christ is named.

VERS. 19-21.-- « And he called the name of the city [0155B] Bethel., » etc. [n: (HIER., de locis Hebraicis.)] From that which was said, « how terrible is this place, » etc., he imposes on the place the name Bethel, that is, *the house of God,* which before was called Luza, that is, *a nut* or *an almond.* Whence certain men ridiculously think that the Hebrew word *ulam,* is the name of the city: whereas *ulam* is interpreted *before.* This therefore is the order of the reading: He called the name of that place Bethel, and before, Luza was the name of the city. But the ancient writings are full of the word *ulam* or *olam,* which signifies nothing but *before* or *before the porch,* or *a lintel,* or *doorposts,* etc.

VERS. 22.-- « And this stone shall be called the house of God, » etc. It is a prophecy of the house of God that was to be there, and there he himself, returning, sacrificed to God.
