## CHAPTER XLII.

[0171D]

VERS. 1-15.-- « And Jacob hearing that food was sold in Egypt, » etc. [n: (ISID. in Gen., tom. V.)] There is corn in Egypt: God the Father says also, Osee XI: « Out of Egypt I have called my Son. » Ten of the elder brethren go down, that is, the Jews, set under the decalogue; whom he himself knowing, was not acknowledged by them; the Hebrews are known by Christ, and they themselves do not acknowledge him; they gave him money; but Joseph, that is, Christ, gave wheat, and gave back the silver, because it is not with money that Christ is bought, but with grace.

VERS. 16-22.-- « Send one of you . . . . . otherwise, by the health of Pharaoh, you are spies. » [0172A] [n: (AUG. Quaest. in Gen. )] What is it that Joseph, a wise man, etc., *as far as* he shall be God, for he shall not be, but shall be accounted

VERS. 22-26.-- « And they knew not, » etc. [n: (Aug., ibid. )] What is it that, when they were speaking among themselves in penitence of Joseph, etc., *as far as* as they had sold him into foreign provinces.

VERS. 27-34. « And one having opened, » etc. The brethren of Joseph give silver, because the Jews when converted let go the letter that killeth, and receive the spirit that quickeneth: yet the silver is found in the mouth of the sacks, because for the sake of the fruit of spiritual understanding the beauty of eloquence is not forsaken.

VERS. 35, 36.-- « Each of them found, » etc. The brethren of Joseph gave money in the buying of corn, [0172B] but they received it back when they had received the corn, because our Joseph seeks not our goods, but us. For he gives his gifts freely, and in the buying of what is ours makes us richer.

VERS. 37.-- « To whom Ruben answered, » etc. [n: (STRAB.)] Ruben says: « Kill my two sons, if I bring him not back to thee, » etc., and yet he did not prevail. But Judas, saying: « I shall be guilty of sin against my father for ever, » [var: Vulg. Gen. 43:9 *ero peccati reus in te omni tempore*, "guilty of sin against thee"] obtained it, because Benjamin was committed to him. It is more, therefore, to be guilty of sin than to be slain in the body.

VERS. 38.-- « You shall bring down my grey hairs, » etc. [n: (AUG.)] It is asked: does he speak thus of going down to hell because it is with sorrow, or, even if sorrow were absent, as one about to go down to hell by dying? For concerning hell [0172C] there is a great question, and what Scripture holds of it, wherever this is mentioned, must be observed.

## CHAPTER XLIII.

VERS. 11-22.-- « A little rosin and honey and storax, » etc. [n: (HIER. Quaest. Hebr., tom. III.)] Somewhat of rosin, and of honey, etc., *as far as* because in him are laid up divers spices.

VERS. 23-28.-- « Your God and the God of your father hath given you treasures, » etc. [n: (AUG.)] It seems a lie, but it must be believed to signify something. For the silver, which is given and is not diminished, which also has been called tried, is perhaps that of which it is said in psalm XI: « The words of the Lord are chaste words, silver tried by the fire, proved of the earth, purified [0172D] seven times, » that is, tried perfectly.

VERS. 29-31.-- « And Joseph lifting up his eyes saw Benjamin, » etc. [n: (ISID.)] In Acts IX a light as it were shone round about Paul, who is the little one, because he did not yet bear the ripe age of faith. Whence he is also read of as a young man, while he kept the garments of those who stoned Stephen. Joseph wept; the blindness of Paul is the weeping of Christ; Christ washed his face, when Paul is baptized, through whom Christ is to be seen by very many.

[n: (AUG.)] The affection of a good mind is always prone to piety. Whence in Joseph we may consider an abundance of virtues. He was chaste, when he spurned the shamelessness of his mistress; wise, when with diligent search he explored the minds of his brethren, how they felt [0173A] toward their brother by the same mother; he was just in applying discipline to offenders; loving, in the turning back of the penitent.

VERS. 32, 33.-- « And when these were set on, » etc. The dishes were set apart for Joseph as for a stranger, and apart for his brethren, as for men foreign by nation: and for the Egyptians also apart, as for natives, who thought a banquet with foreigners to be profane.

VERS. 34.-- « And they drank and were made drunk, » etc. [n: (AUG.)] Drunkards are wont to bring forward this testimony in their own defence, because of Joseph, who is commended as very wise. But this word is put in the Scriptures for fulness, whence in psalm LXIV: « Thou hast visited the earth and hast made it drunk, » because this is set down in praise of blessing, and the gift of God is recorded; it appears [0173B] that this drunkenness signifies fulness: for to be made drunk as drunkards are made drunk is not useful even to the earth itself; since by more moisture than suffices it is corrupted; like the life of a drunkard, who fills himself not with satiety, but drowns himself in a flood.

## CHAP. XLIV.

VERS. 5-14.-- « The cup which you have stolen, » etc. [n: (ISID.)] What does it mean that Joseph's cup was found in Benjamin's sack, but that in Paul's heart the eloquence of heavenly doctrine already shone forth, while he was learned in the law? But because he was not subject to God, the cup was within the sack, doctrine within the law, the lamp within the bushel. Yet Ananias, being sent, laid his hand upon him, loosed the purse, and the silver [0173C] shone out, and, the scales falling away as it were fetters, the sack being loosed, that is, the veil of the law laid aside, he obtained the liberty of grace, and with unveiled face of speech preaches the Gospel.

VERS. 15.-- « Know you not that there is none like me in the science of divining? » [n: (AUG. Quaest. in Gen., tom. III.)] What this may mean is wont to be asked. Whether because it was said not in earnest but in jest, etc., *as far as* the brother being recognized, whom they thought lost to them.

VERS. 16-34.-- « To whom Judas, » etc. [n: (AUG., ubi supra. )] Many things in the narration of Judas are said otherwise than as Joseph had dealt with them, etc., *as far as* but he who knew that he knew it was inserting into the narration, that he might bend him to mercy.
