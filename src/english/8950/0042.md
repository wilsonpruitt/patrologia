## CHAPTER XLV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0173D]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7-9.-- « God sent me, » etc. [n: (AUG. Quaest. in Gen., tom. III.)] What is it that according to other copies is read thus: « For God sent me before you, that your remnant should remain upon the earth, and that he might nourish up a great seed of your remnant? » For this does not agree in every point, etc., *as far as* « and so all Israel should be saved. »

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10-15.-- « Thou shalt dwell in the land of Gessen, » etc. [n: (HIERON.)] In the Hebrew there stands *Gosen* ( )[ed: Migne prints a Hebrew word within these parentheses; our digitized source has lost it and left them empty.]. ⟦s2⟧ Whence the error grew up that the land of Gessen is in Arabia. ⟦s3⟧ Further, as it stands in our codices, it is written with a final *m*, Gessem, which by no means pleases me, as signifying a land rained upon. ⟦s4⟧ For Gessem is turned into *rain* [0174A], where also in very many copies Gessem is found.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16-21.-- « And Pharao was glad, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ These words of Pharao, which he spoke to Joseph concerning Jacob and his sons, signify the joys of the Gentile people over the conversion of the Jews. ⟦s3⟧ For great is the devotion of the Church of the Gentiles that the people of Israel should come to the faith, to whom, if they be converted and become partakers of the faith, he promises the spiritual riches of the virtues, and the grace of the Holy Spirit, which he that receives shall not labour under want of good.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22-28.-- « But to Benjamin he gave, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ Three hundred pieces of silver are given to Benjamin, which he received from Christ who preaches the Trinity, or the cross of Christ. ⟦s3+4⟧ Whence Paul says in the first to the Corinthians, II: « I judged not myself [0174B] to know any thing among you, but Christ Jesus, and him crucified. » He received five robes, that is, the wisdom of all the senses. ⟦s5⟧ Paul therefore excels in the abundance of his merits: but his brethren also, that is, the other preachers, have their own grace, that is, two robes apiece, that they may confess Christ to be God and man. ⟦s6⟧ Whence Prov. XXXI: « All her household are clothed with double garments, » that is, with mystical and moral understanding. ⟦s7⟧ Gifts too are sent to the father, and Christ invites his people with gifts promised. ⟦s8⟧ Asses bear the gifts, that is, the Gentiles, useless and toilsome: but now, useful, they bear gifts in the type of Christ, in the Gospel the bestower of gifts.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISID.)] Jacob was afraid, that is, the unbelieving people: but after he came to know the deeds of Christ, his spirit [0174C] revived, and he who seemed dead is quickened by faith in the resurrection of Christ. ⟦s2⟧ The people of the Jews therefore is called by his sons, that is, by Peter, Paul and John, unto grace. ⟦s3⟧ Judas meets him, who is interpreted *confession,* because confession goes before those whom faithlessness possessed before: and so Joseph, that is, Christ, meets him, to take up at the last time the people of the Jews, now aged in years, not according to merits, but according to the election of grace, and to lay his hand upon his eyes, and take away his blindness; which he deferred, that he should believe last who before did not think that it was to be believed. ⟦s4⟧ Whence to the Romans in the eleventh chapter: « Because blindness in part is made in Israel, until the fulness of the Gentiles should come in, and so all Israel should be saved. »[var: Vulg. Rom 11:25 *quia caecitas ex parte contigit in Israel*, "because blindness in part hath befallen Israel."]

## CHAPTER XLVI.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0174D]

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- « All the souls that went in with Jacob into Egypt, » etc. [n: (AUG.)] It must be considered what answer is to be made to those who by this testimony maintain that souls are propagated from parents together with bodies: for that "souls" is said for "men," by a figurative manner of speech naming the whole from a part, no one doubts. ⟦s2⟧ But how we are to alienate that very part, that is, the soul by whose name the whole man is signified, from that which is said, « they came forth out of his thighs, »[var: Vulg. Gn 46:26 *egressae sunt de femore illius*, "went out of his thigh."] so as to take it that flesh only was born of him (although souls alone are named), the modes of speech in the Scriptures must be sought out.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- « And the sons of Joseph, » etc. ⟦s2⟧ *(HIERON. [0175A] Quaest. Hebr., tom. III.)* ⟦s3⟧ All the souls of the house of Jacob, etc., *as far as* although very many hand down that Luke, as a proselyte, was ignorant of Hebrew letters.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28-31.-- « And he sent Judas, » etc. [n: (HIER., ibid. )] But Judas he sent before him to Joseph, etc., *as far as* they suppose that the Arsenoite district was so called of old.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32-34.-- « And they are men that are shepherds, » etc. [n: (AUG. Q. in Gen., tom. III.)] It is commended in the patriarchs that they were feeders of cattle from their childhood, etc., *as far as* at the end they shall have a most ordered and everlasting happiness.
