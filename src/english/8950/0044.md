## CHAPTER XLVIII.

VERS. 1.-- « These things being thus done . . . . taking his two sons Manasses and Ephraim. » [n: (HIERON.)] And he took his two sons with him, Manasses and Ephraim. From this appears what we said above, that Joseph up to that time had only two sons, Ephraim and Manasses. But if, after many years, when Jacob was dying, he brought only two to the blessing, then assuredly at that time, when his sons could not yet beget, being little ones and at the breast, at the coming in of his father and his brethren, he could not have grandsons of them.

VERS. 2-4.-- « Who being strengthened, » etc. [n: (HIERON.)] [0177B] And Israel being strengthened sat upon the bed. Why the Seventy have given out the same word now one way and now another I do not know, but I say boldly that the same word *mittha*, which here they translated *a bed*, above, where he said that Jacob adored, they named *a rod*.

VERS. 5, 6.-- « Two sons therefore who were born, » etc. [n: (HIERON., Quaest in Gen.)] It is plain that seventy souls went into Egypt, etc., *as far as* but they shall be mingled into the tribes of Ephraim and Manasses as appendages.

VERS. 7-11.-- « For when I came from Mesopotamia, Rachel died and I buried her, » etc. He points out to Joseph, as to one who did not know, where and when he had buried his mother, perhaps prophetically recalling that his mother was buried in the place where Christ was to be born.

[0177C]VERS. 12-15.-- « And when Joseph had taken them from his father's lap, » etc. [n: (Isid. in Gen., tom. V.)] As was done in Esau and Jacob, the Lord saying: « The elder shall serve the younger, » so also in the sons of Joseph, etc., *as far as* and the younger from the left hand to the right.

VERS. 16-21.-- « Let my name be called upon them. » [n: (AUG.)] It is to be noted from this place that not only a being heard, but an invoking also, is spoken of, which are not God's but men's.

VERS. 22.-- « I give thee one portion, » etc. [n: (HIERON. in Gen., tom. III.)] « I have given thee Sichem as a chief portion above thy brethren, which I received out of the hands of the Amorrhites with my sword and my arrow. »[var: Vulg. Gn 48:22 reads *Do tibi partem unam extra fratres tuos, quam tuli de manu Amorrhaei in gladio et arcu meo* — "I give thee one portion beyond thy brethren, which I took out of the hand of the Amorrhite with my sword and bow."] Sichima is declined [0177D] according to Greek and Latin usage, etc., *as far as* and his tomb is beheld there to this day.

[n: (AUG.)] As to Jacob's saying that he gives Sichem as a chief portion to his son Joseph, and his adding that he had possessed it with sword and bow, it is asked how this may agree to the letter with Gen. XXXIV. For he bought that possession with a hundred lambs, he did not take it by right of war. Or was it because his sons had stormed Salem, the city of the Sichemites, and it could become his by right of war, so that the war might seem just against those who first did the injury by defiling his daughter? Why then did he not give it to his elder sons, who did this? Then, if he now gives it to Joseph as glorying of the victory, why did the sons who committed it displease him at that time? Why, lastly, when he was blessing them, did he mention it by way of reproach? [0178A] There lies hidden here therefore a prophetic sacrament, because Joseph by a certain special signification signified Christ: and to him is given that land where Jacob buried the strange gods [n: (Gen. XXXV)], that Christ may be understood as about to possess the Gentiles who renounce the gods of their fathers and believe in Christ.
