## CHAPTER X.

VERS. 1.-- *So Esdras praying, and imploring God and weeping . . . . . . . The people wept.* [n: (BEDA in Esdram, tom. II.)] [var: Vulg. Esr 10:1 *et implorante eo et flente*, "and he imploring and weeping"] They, that is, who had sinned, doing penance, or who had remained in chastity, grieving for the fall of their brethren, etc., *as far as* But now those also who had sinned came together with their wives and their children, about to do penance.

*Of the children of Aelam.* Josephus says that this man was the first of the men of Jerusalem, who, as befits a prince, presently with the greatest authority helped the intention of Esdras, [0711D] by confessing that the people had sinned together with him, and by persuading to penance, the foreign wives being cast off, with their children.

*And now if there be repentance in Israel.* Because if the people perfectly repents of this sin, first, being converted to the Lord, let us promise amendment and ask pardon; then, returning to ourselves, let us root out every root and shoot of sin, casting out, that is, the wives with all their offspring: for this is truly [0712A] to do penance with the whole heart, and inwardly to be converted to the Lord, and outwardly to have every matter of sinning cut away from its very origin.

*Arise.* Fittingly he teaches how counsel is to be taken among elders, namely, that each should say what he has understood to be best, or seems to himself to have understood: and yet leave to him who presides the place of discerning, being ready to obey in all things that he shall have decreed to be done according to the will and the law of God.

VERS. 6.-- *The children of Eliasib.* Who was then high priest, after Jesus the son of Josedec, Joacim his son, and after him Eliasib his son, held the office of high priest.

*He ate no bread.* [n: (BEDA ubi supra. )] Hence priests have an example, [0712B] etc., *as far as: If indeed, fleeing the defilements of the world,* etc. [n: (II Petr. II)].

VERS. 9.-- *This is.* [n: (BEDA ubi supra. )] Which by the Hebrews is called Casleu, by the Romans December, etc., *as far as* by which art they may either avoid or put away the adversities which rage without because of sins.

*And all the people sat. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* That is, about the court of the priests, with which the house itself was girt about on every side, having round it on every part, foursquare, most spacious buildings of courts, in which the people also, when there was need because of rain, could stand and see the things which were done in the doors of the temple, or about the temple: for they had the inner walls made upon pillars near the ground, the outer solid.

VERS. 10.-- *And Esdras rose up. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* This [0712C] place answers, etc., *as far as* and the deeds might deservedly be held in memory, and handed down to be known by those who come after.

VERS. 16.-- *And they sat down on the day. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Note that the number three is mystical, etc., *as far as* which we too ought to imitate in every year in the Lent of the pasch, the Lord's day of the resurrection being at hand, *let us cleanse ourselves from all defilement of flesh and of spirit, that we may be able to be partakers of the resurrection [n: (II Cor. VII)]*.

*And there were found. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The Hebrews fit to this place, etc., *as far as* But it is to be observed that Esdras does not write that Jesus married a foreign wife, but certain of his sons and of his brethren, etc.

VERS. 18.-- *And his brethren. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* His kinsmen, [0712D] that is, for his brethren german in the flesh could not thus far be living and giving heed to pleasure, since a hundred years and more had passed since Cyrus, beginning to reign, sent back Jesus and Zorobabel with the transmigration of Juda and Benjamin to Jerusalem, to build the house of the Lord.

VERS. 19.-- *And they gave,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] First they cast off the unlawful wives, etc., *as far as* may make him worthy of a perpetual reward in heaven.
