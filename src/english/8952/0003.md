## CHAPTER VI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Five golden emerods,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] Five golden emerods, etc., *as far as* is true sadness and perpetual barrenness.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *A cart.* ⟦s2⟧ The cross of Christ, the ark Holy Scripture.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0548B] *Two kine.* [n: (ISID.)] The kine bearing the ark signify the saints, who renounce the world, who have drawn no yoke of transgression. ⟦s2⟧ For as the kine, in their affection for their calves, turn not aside from the straight way, so the holy fathers with such affection stray not from their good purpose.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Which leadeth,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] By the king's way, namely, going to Bethsames, that is, into the house of the sun, namely the heavenly Jerusalem, where the sun of justice ever shines.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Bethsames.* [n: (ID.)] It is a priestly city in the tribe of Benjamin, which even to this day is shown to those going from Eleutheropolis to Nicopolis, at the tenth milestone toward the East: another Bethsames is in the tribe of Nephthali, in which the ancient inhabitants have remained.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0548C]VERS. 13.-- *And they turned not aside* [n: (ID.)]. ⟦s2⟧ That is, neither were they lifted up by prosperous things, etc., *as far as* let it be relaxed in weeping.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Bethsames.* ⟦s2⟧ The house of the sun. ⟦s3⟧ This is the Church, enlightened by the sun of justice. ⟦s4⟧ For the Bethsamites to reap the corn in the valley is for the doctors of the Church to handle the provision of the word of God in humility. ⟦s5⟧ The stone in the field of Josue, Christ in his Church. ⟦s6⟧ The wood of the cart, the cross of Christ. ⟦s7⟧ To sacrifice the kine upon the stone is to mortify those converted from the world in the imitation of Christ.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Azotus one. [n: (HIERON., ubi supra.)]* Five cities gave five emerods and five mice. ⟦s2⟧ But the remaining cities of the provinces, and the villages which were without a wall, gave mice only, whence: *From the walled city unto the village which was without a wall,* etc.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0548D]VERS. 18.-- *Abel.* ⟦s2⟧ It is a city in the border of Israel, which was formerly called Bethsames, and is now called Abel: either because of the mourning made there over the men of Bethsames, or for the distinguishing of the city from Bethmaca, of which the wise woman cried out to Joab: *They that ask, let them ask in Abela [n: (II Reg. XX)]*.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *He smote,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Who, since they were not Levites, dared to look upon the ark. ⟦s3⟧ That the people might not suffer this, in Exodus it stood afar off, and Moses alone went up to the Lord. ⟦s4⟧ *He smote,* etc. [n: (HIERON.)] In the Hebrew it stands: *And he smote of the people seventy men: and fifty thousand men,* seventy men, judges.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (SEPTUAG.)] The Hebrews say that it was lawful for none to look upon the ark uncovered save for the high priest: they were smitten therefore, for [0549A] that they were unworthy, or had approached unworthily, or had not kept the ark.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *And they sent.* [n: (RAB.)] There is signified the carrying over of the testament of the Lord from the Jews to the nations, because, when they were defiling the law by carnal observance and by the superstitions of the Pharisees, they refused the teaching of Christ and of his disciples; whence: *To you it behoved first to speak the word of God, but because you have judged yourselves unworthy, behold we turn to the Gentiles [n: (Act. XV)]*.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Cariathiarim.* [n: (ID.)] It is interpreted the village of the woods, of which it is said: *Until I find out a place for the Lord, a tabernacle for the God of Jacob. ⟦s2⟧ Behold we have heard of it in Ephrata, we have found it in the fields of the wood [n: (Psal. CXXXI)]*. ⟦s3⟧ For the place of the Lord is the tabernacle of the breast of the Christian people. ⟦s4⟧ For the city of the woods, that [0549B] is, the assembly of the nations, is a place fit for receiving the ark. ⟦s5⟧ In the house of Aminadab, who is interpreted: my father spontaneous, and he is able to say, namely voluntarily, to thee.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Cariathiarim is a city of the forests, one of the cities of the Gabaonites belonging to the tribe of Juda, at the ninth milestone as one goes from Elia to Diospolis: of this was Urias the prophet whom Joachim slew in Jerusalem, as Jeremias writes.

## CHAPTER VII.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Gabaa.* [n: (RAB.)] The hill of the soul of the humble, whence: *Let the mountains receive peace for the people,* etc., that is, the mountains the greater souls, the hills the lesser. ⟦s2⟧ Or else Gabaa is interpreted loftiness, where Eleazar [0549C] the priest is appointed.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Eleazar.* [n: (ID.)] The help of God. ⟦s2⟧ And rightly is he who is called by this name chosen to keep the ark of the Lord, because holy men attribute whatsoever good they have to God, not to themselves. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *Our sufficiency is from God, who hath made us fit ministers of the New Testament [n: (II Cor. III)]*.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The twentieth year* it was, when in the seventh year of the reign of Saul it was brought into the camp from Cariathiarim.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The twentieth year.* [n: (RAB.)] It is not so to be understood, etc., *as far as* because there is no fellowship of light with darkness.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Masphath.* ⟦s2⟧ It is a city in the tribe of Juda beside Cariathiarim.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (RAB.)] *Masphath.* ⟦s2⟧ Watching, or contemplation, [0549D] etc., *as far as* and shall have perceived them to be intent upon the love of the heavenly country.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And they drew water,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That as water poured out returns not, so neither should they themselves return to idolatry.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *A sucking lamb.* ⟦s2⟧ The lamb offered for the people of God affords victory, and, the demons being overcome through the passion of Christ, peace was given to the faithful.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (RAB.)] An innocent life kindled with the fire of charity.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (RAB.)] When the Lord thunders through the preachers of the Gospel, the spiritual enemies are turned to flight before Israel, that is, the people of God, and they pursue them even into the house of acknowledgment: that is, until every error be purged away and God be truly known.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0550A]VERS. 11.-- *Betchar.* [n: (ID.)] The house of acknowledgment, or the house of the lamb.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And he set it.* ⟦s2⟧ Another edition: *between Masphath the new and the old, and he called the name thereof Abenezer,* or *Abongener, that is, the stone of help.* ⟦s3⟧ This is the middle place of the Saviour, through which one must pass over from the old Masphath to the new, that is, from the intention by which a false blessedness is looked for in a carnal kingdom to the intention by which through the New Testament a true blessedness is looked for in the kingdom of heaven, than which, since there is nothing better, God helps hitherto. ⟦s4⟧ Masphath, the intention of them that sit. ⟦s5⟧ The stone between these, Christ set between the intention of the Old Testament and the preaching of the Gospel: he himself the stone because of the firmness of his help, because he helps his own [0550B] as they fight against the demons.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Hitherto he hath helped.* ⟦s2⟧ Because they who overpass these bounds, as the heretics do, do not deserve the help of God.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *And there was.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ is our peace, who hath made both one, through whom they were made nigh who were afar off.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Galgala.* ⟦s2⟧ It is the city where Josue circumcised the people the second time, and celebrated the Pasch, and, the manna failing, Israel used wheaten bread, where also the stones were carried over from the channel of the Jordan, where the tabernacle of the testimony was for a long time. ⟦s3⟧ This fell into the lot of Juda: and even to this day the place is shown at the second milestone from Jericho, to be venerated by the men of that region. ⟦s4⟧ But some suspect another Galgala beside Bethel.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0550C]Galgala is interpreted a rolling, or a revelation. ⟦s2⟧ Rightly therefore did the prophet judge Israel in Bethel and in Galgala and in Masphath, and returning to Ramatha to his own house he judged Israel there; and he built there an altar to the Lord, giving an example that judgment is to be made in the house of God, not indiscreetly or with a malevolent mind, but according to what the Scripture shall have revealed, and with good intention and with brotherly love, and that so one must return to Ramatha to our own house, that is, to the inward parts of the breast, that we may be free for contemplation, and may offer upon the altar of faith the acceptable victims of work and of truth.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *He built also there,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The law commanded that an altar be not built save where the Lord should have chosen; but lest they should sacrifice to gods he built an altar, in which he is not reprehended.

## CHAPTER VIII.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0550D]

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *It came to pass,* etc., *he set his sons. [n: (RAB., in lib. Reg., tom. II.)]* Because according to Josephus he set the one in Bethlehem, the other in Bersabee.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Abia.* [n: (ID.)] The father the lord, or the father was. ⟦s2⟧ Rightly therefore they who remained not in the justice they had begun, and neglected the fatherhood of the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ God was, or the Father was, they were named, that they might be signified unworthy of the fatherhood of God.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *And they walked not.* ⟦s2⟧ Because for a bribe they oppressed justice, turning aside to feasts and costly banquets.
