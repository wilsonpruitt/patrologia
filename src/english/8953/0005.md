## CHAPTER XXI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *There was.* [n: (RAB.)] A famine came in Israel because of the Gabaonites, etc., *as far as* lest by wickedly suggesting they corrupt their life any further.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The king therefore took two.* ⟦s2⟧ Who with Doeg the Idumaean, being still boys, killed the priests and the Gabaonites, that is, Armoni and Miphiboseth

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The sons of Michol the daughter,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Whom, that is, Michol, the wife of David, who is also called Egla, adopted to herself among her sons, whom Merob her sister bore of the son of Berzellai.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Gob.* ⟦s2⟧ Which is interpreted *a pit,* because as one is cast into a pit of lions, so David cast himself [0576D] against Goliath.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19, 20.-- *The third.* [n: (RAB.)] Josephus speaks thus of the third battle, etc., *as far as* whether he called him Adeodatus or any other man whatsoever, is uncertain.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The fourth,* Goliath is repeated, which David did first in the time of Saul. ⟦s2⟧ For in Paralipomenon he is not read to have fought principally against the Philisthaeans except a third time.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (RAB.)] The four wars of David and of his servants, etc., *as far as* whence: *Vain is the salvation of men. ⟦s2⟧ Through God we shall do mightily, and he bringeth to nothing them that afflict us [n: (Psal. CVII)]*.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Bethlehemites,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because Noemi and Ruth, of whom he was sprung, returned to Bethlehem in the time of plenty, [0577A] and because Ruth became known by reason of bread, that place was called the House of bread. ⟦s3⟧ Or because Noemi, returning thither with her daughter-in-law, obtained abundance of bread in the house of Booz.

## CHAPTER XXII.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *He spoke,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] The seventeenth psalm alone is found in the books of Kings, etc., *as far as* and with great exultation the gifts granted by the Godhead are praised.
