## CHAPTER XXI.

VERS. 1.-- *There was.* [n: (RAB.)] A famine came in Israel because of the Gabaonites, etc., *as far as* lest by wickedly suggesting they corrupt their life any further.

VERS. 8.-- *The king therefore took two.* Who with Doeg the Idumaean, being still boys, killed the priests and the Gabaonites, that is, Armoni and Miphiboseth

*The sons of Michol the daughter,* etc. Whom, that is, Michol, the wife of David, who is also called Egla, adopted to herself among her sons, whom Merob her sister bore of the son of Berzellai.

VERS. 18.-- *Gob.* Which is interpreted *a pit,* because as one is cast into a pit of lions, so David cast himself [0576D] against Goliath.

VERS. 19, 20.-- *The third.* [n: (RAB.)] Josephus speaks thus of the third battle, etc., *as far as* whether he called him Adeodatus or any other man whatsoever, is uncertain.

*The fourth,* Goliath is repeated, which David did first in the time of Saul. For in Paralipomenon he is not read to have fought principally against the Philisthaeans except a third time.

[n: (RAB.)] The four wars of David and of his servants, etc., *as far as* whence: *Vain is the salvation of men. Through God we shall do mightily, and he bringeth to nothing them that afflict us [n: (Psal. CVII)]*.

*Bethlehemites,* etc. Because Noemi and Ruth, of whom he was sprung, returned to Bethlehem in the time of plenty, [0577A] and because Ruth became known by reason of bread, that place was called the House of bread. Or because Noemi, returning thither with her daughter-in-law, obtained abundance of bread in the house of Booz.

## CHAPTER XXII.

VERS. 1.-- *He spoke,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] The seventeenth psalm alone is found in the books of Kings, etc., *as far as* and with great exultation the gifts granted by the Godhead are praised.
