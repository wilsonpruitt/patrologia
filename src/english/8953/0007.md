## CHAPTER XXIV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (GREG., lib. XXV Moral., cap. 14.)] According to the qualities [0580A] of the subjects, etc., *as far as* that, as the deeds of the masters displease, so the mind of the subjects may not withdraw from reverence for the mastership.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIERON.)] It is to be noted that in Paralipomenon it is read: A thousand thousand a hundred thousand of Israel, and of Juda forty seventy thousand, [var: Vulg. 1 Par. 21:5 *quadringenta septuaginta millia*, "four hundred and seventy thousand"] whom it is to be understood that Joab numbered, but that he was unwilling to show any but as many as are written in the book of Samuel.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *But his heart smote him,* etc. [n: (AMBR., lib. de Poen., cap. 9.)] He betook himself to the greater punishment that he might be able to call forth clemency. ⟦s2⟧ For the rigour of justice is softened, etc., *as far as* and the shaking poured forth from heaven upon the people might rest.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *The word of the Lord.* [n: (RAB.)] In Exodus it is written, etc., *as far as* there were consumed seventy [0580B] thousand.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Either.* ⟦s2⟧ And it seems that they who are not fed with the bread of the word of God through the grace of the sevenfold Spirit endure famine for seven years, and they who have not been regenerated in the name of the Trinity are not strong enough to withstand the enemies, that is, the demons; but the unbelieving die of the pestilence of impiety.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And there was.* [n: (RAB.)] In Paralipomenon also it is written: *Now the angel of the Lord stood by the threshing floor of Ornan the Jebusite. ⟦s2⟧ And David, lifting up his eyes, saw the angel of the Lord between heaven and earth, with a drawn sword in his hand, and turned against Jerusalem. ⟦s3⟧ And they fell, both he and the elders, clothed in haircloth, prostrate on the ground, beside the threshing floor.* ⟦s4⟧ The threshing floor of Areuna is the Church of the Gentiles, in which, after [0580C] the true David raised up the altar of faith, the death of impiety at once gave way. ⟦s5⟧ This threshing floor David bought for fifty sicles: because Christ, through the grace of the sevenfold Spirit, all offences being let go, sanctified the Gentile world into an everlasting house for himself.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Areuna.* [n: (ID.)] It is interpreted a threshing floor: but Ornan, *a light to us.* ⟦s2⟧ Rightly therefore David, wishing to appease the Lord, is bidden to set up an altar in the threshing floor of Areuna, or of Orna, because God is not otherwise appeased by man, unless in the threshing floor of the heart, through the light of right faith and of true love, he set up the altar of humility, in which he may offer the sacrifice of oblation and of praise, whence: *The sacrifice of praise shall glorify me,* etc. [n: (Psal. XLIX)]. ⟦s3⟧ *A sacrifice to God is an afflicted spirit,* etc. [n: (Ibid. L)]. ⟦s4⟧ The threshing floor of Areuna, or of Orna, is Jerusalem itself.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0580D]VERS. 25.-- *And he offered holocausts.* ⟦s2⟧ Hence in Paralipomenon the tabernacle which Moses had made in the desert and the altar of holocausts were at that season in the high place of Gabaon, and David was not strong enough to go to the altar, to beseech the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ For he had been terrified with exceeding fear, seeing the sword of the angel of the Lord. ⟦s4⟧ And David said: *This is the house of God,* etc. ⟦s5⟧ In this place, according to the Hebrews, Abraham wished to offer his son, when a ram suddenly appeared: which he took and offered instead of his son.
