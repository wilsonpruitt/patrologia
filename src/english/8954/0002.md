## CHAPTER VI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *It came to pass therefore. [n: (RAB. ex Bed., lib. de Templo Salom., cap. 5.)]* Mention is made of the going forth out of Egypt, etc., *as far as* nor a Scripture which should intimate in the Old Testament the grace of the New.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the fourth year.* ⟦s2⟧ In the fourth year of king Solomon the house began to be built: because after the dispensation of Christ's incarnation, written in the four Gospels, was fulfilled, the Holy Spirit being sent, the building of the Church began.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the month Zio (that is the month). [n: (BEDA, ibidem.)]* That in the second month, etc., *as far as* whence it is plain that soon [0586A] after the pasch was over he began to build the house of the Lord, and the people, consecrated by the mystic solemnity, put their hands to the mystic work.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He began to build. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Where the temple was built is made manifest in Paralipomenon, etc., *as far as* but she was made possessed of that inward peace which she should have with her Creator.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Which David had prepared,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ For David had prepared by his psalmody, and the other prophets prepared by their prophesying, a place for the Lord: that is, for the true Solomon, wherein he might build a house, because they instructed the hearts of their hearers in faith, admonishing them to receive the incarnate Son of God with faithful devotion. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *Lift up your eyes and see the countries, for they are white already to harvest: and he that reapeth receiveth wages: and gathereth fruit unto life [0586B] everlasting, that both he that soweth may rejoice together, and he that reapeth [n: (Joan. IV)]*. ⟦s4⟧ Which is to say: Lift up your eyes and see the place that is prepared for the building of the house, and he that buildeth by teaching receiveth wages, and gathereth precious stones unto life everlasting: that both he that prepareth the place for the building may rejoice together, and he that buildeth, that is, that the prophet foretelling him that should come: and the apostle preaching the Lord already come, may obtain one and the same reward together.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In length,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The length of the house signifies the longsuffering of the Church, whereby she patiently bears adversities until she come to her country. ⟦s3⟧ This is of sixty cubits, because the senary number signifies the perfection of good works; because we ought so to bear adversities by longsuffering, that by good works we may deserve to enter the promised [0586C] country.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Twenty cubits.* ⟦s2⟧ Because of the twofold distance of charity, whereby we love God and our neighbour.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In breadth.* ⟦s2⟧ The breadth signifies charity, which with the bosom of the mind enlarged loves friends in God, and enemies for God's sake, until, when they are converted to peace or utterly destroyed, she may rejoice in the Lord with friends alone.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BEDA, ibid.)] It is to be noted that the thirty cubits of height reached not as far as the roof of the temple, but as far as the lower upper chamber. ⟦s2⟧ For it is plainly written in Paralipomenon that the height was of a hundred and twenty cubits, of the mysteries of which matter it shall be more fitly treated in what follows, when the order of the reading shall have come to the middle upper chamber and the third.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0586D] *Thirty cubits.* ⟦s2⟧ Because of the faith of the Trinity, in the vision of which all the desires of our hope are suspended. ⟦s3⟧ The several numbers are multiplied by ten, because through faith and the keeping of the law patience is wholesomely exercised: charity burns manfully, and hope is caught up on high to things eternal.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In height.* ⟦s2⟧ The height signifies the hope of the recompense to come, for which she despises things prosperous or adverse, until she see the good things of the Lord in the land of the living.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *There was a porch,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Of this it is thus written in Paralipomenon: *And the porch before the front, which was extended in length according to the measure of the breadth, of twenty cubits [n: (II Paral. III)]*. ⟦s3⟧ It is plain therefore that this porch was made toward the eastern part of the temple. [0587A] For the temple was turned to the east, as was the tabernacle also: and it had a door on the east over against the door of the temple, according to Josephus, so that the sun rising at the equinox might, by the straight lines of its rays, pour through three doors, namely of the porch, of the temple and of the oracle, upon the ark of the testament. ⟦s4⟧ The temple figures the Church, the porch, which before the temple first received the light of the sun, that part of the Church which went before the incarnation of the Lord: in which the patriarchs and prophets first received the rising sun of justice, and by living, preaching, being born and dying gave testimony to the Lord being born in the flesh.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of twenty cubits in length, according to the measure of the breadth of the temple. [n: (BEDA, lib. de temp. Salom., c. 6.)]* Because the just men of old in patience and longsuffering [0587B] waited, etc., *as far as* yet they could not hear his doctrine nor receive his sacraments.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Slanting windows. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 7.)]* The holy teachers, to whom, going out of their mind unto God, it is granted to see the secret things of heaven more especially. ⟦s2⟧ Who, while they publicly disclose what they see in secret, fill all the inner places of the temple as windows that have taken in the light of the sun. ⟦s3⟧ Whence they are reported to have been slanting, that is, wider within: because he who receives the brightness of the contemplation above, if but for a moment, straightway by chastening enlarges the bosom of his heart the more, and prepares it by diligent exercise for the taking in of greater things.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *And he built,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] These boardings are called in the Gospel, where the Lord is tempted by the devil, the pinnacles of the temple, etc., *as far as* the greatest [0587C] part of whom the history of St. ⟦s1⟧ Stephen testifies more fully to have left their wives, where it calls the women that flourished in the same religion not their spouses, but their widows.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Sides. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* That is, little parapets, lest any should easily fall thence to the lower parts, etc., *as far as Sides,* that is, walls or lattices, or little parapets, the teachers sitting upon the boardings, who harangued the people standing about below.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And he put beams in the house round about on the outside. [n: (BEDA, ibid.)]* The houses which bear the boardings, etc., *as far as* and yet out of those things which they show forth without by speaking, doing or suffering, we find the help of salvation.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Beams,* etc., *on the outside.* ⟦s2⟧ It was of such length that their heads projected on the outside. ⟦s3⟧ In the lowest [0587D] course of seven cubits, in the middle of six, in the highest of five, so that upon their heads the boardings might be set together, not fastened into the walls of the temple, but laid beside the walls upon the beams which had come forth out of the walls.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *And the house,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ These things pertain properly to that part of the Church which after the labours and contests of this world has deserved to be brought in to the eternal rewards. ⟦s3⟧ For nothing defiled shall enter into that city.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And the hammer and the axe.* ⟦s2⟧ Because here we are beaten with adversities and are exercised with the discipline of truth, that there we may be disposed according to our merit in fitting places, and, chastisement ceasing, may be filled with one spirit and steeped in the sole glue of love whereby we are coupled to one another. ⟦s3⟧ But although [0588A] it be said: *Noe a perfect man in his generations,* and: *Blessed are the undefiled in the way,* and the like, no man can truly walk the way of this life perfect and without stain. ⟦s4⟧ For there is none that doth good and sinneth not. ⟦s5⟧ But according to the measure of this present time they are called perfect and undefiled, who are then truly made perfect, when, loosed from the bonds of the body, they shall have come to the immortal comeliness of the house of the Lord.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The door of the side. [n: (BEDA, ibid, cap. 8.)]* Certain men, ill understanding this place, etc., *as far as* and well one door, because of the faith and the love of truth that is harmonious in all the saints.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They went up into the middle upper chamber, and from the middle into the third. ⟦s2⟧ And he built. [n: (BEDA ibid.)]* It is to be noted that the thirty cubits of height, of which we read above, etc., [0588B] *as far as* because with harmonious mind and voice all shall praise together the glory of the divine majesty.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *With panelled ceilings. [n: (BEDA, ibid., cap. 9.)]* Panelled ceilings are boardings which, composed and adorned with great beauty, are fastened to the beams from the under part, and because the house of the Lord had been made of a threefold height, it had threefold panelled ceilings. ⟦s2⟧ That is, the more sublime saints, whose work and doctrine, set before all in the temple, stand out as it were farther on high; whosoever by interceding and exhorting protect the souls of the weak, that they fail not.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of cedar. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The cedar, a tree that cannot rot, etc., *as far as* save to love God with the whole heart, the whole soul, the whole strength, and our neighbour as ourselves.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0588C]VERS. 10.-- *A boarding over the whole house of five cubits in height. [n: (BEDA, ibid.)]* Here the topmost roof of the house itself is spoken of, etc., *as far as in the spirit and power of Elias.*

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *And he built the walls. [n: (BED., ibid.)]* Within indeed the house was clothed with cedar, etc., *as far as* but they have not any whom they may carry.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The walls of the house.* ⟦s2⟧ They are covered when the hearts of the faithful overflow with the love of the virtues. ⟦s3⟧ For as the cedar signifies the perfect, so, in fitting places, the loftiness of the virtues, whereby that same perfection is attained.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *From the pavement.* ⟦s2⟧ All things are covered with timber, from the pavement of the house even to the top of the walls and even to the panelled ceilings, when the elect from the first rudiments of faith even [0588D] to the perfection of good action, and even to the entering in of the heavenly country, sweat at good works, when from the first just men even to the last at the consummation of the world all are zealous for the virtues, by whose merit they may dare to say: *We are the good odour of Christ unto God [n: (II Cor. II)]*.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And he covered the pavement. [n: (BED. ibid.)]* This is more fully written in Paralipomenon, etc., *as far as* he subjoined: *But above all things having charity,* etc. [n: (I Tim. I; Col. III)].

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *He built twenty. [n: (BED., ibid., cap. 10.)]* That the boardings, etc., *as far as* and this throughout the whole breadth of the Church spread abroad through the world.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Moreover. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Ten multiplied four times [0589A] make forty, etc., *as far as* but the four-square world, in which we strive to obtain the same. ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *He hath gathered them out of the countries. ⟦s3⟧ From the rising and the setting of the sun, from the north and from the sea [n: (Psal. CVI)]*.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *And all with cedar.* ⟦s2⟧ The cedar marks the unconquerable comeliness of the virtues, with which timber the whole house is clothed within, when the hearts of the just shine with the love of good works alone.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Having turned work. [n: (BEDA, ibid., cap. 11.)]* The house has in its cedar boards its turned work and its joinings skilfully wrought, when the elect are knit to one another by the most beautiful bond of charity, so that they have one heart and one soul. ⟦s2⟧ For the turned work which is set against the joinings of the boards, that one boarding may be made of them all, is the offices of charity by which the holy brotherhood is [0589B] coupled, and is composed into one house of Christ throughout the whole world.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Carvings.* ⟦s2⟧ The works of the saints set forth by open expression what manner of men they are, for an example to others. ⟦s3⟧ Whence Paul both by deeds and by words sets himself forth for an example.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *All things with cedar,* etc. [n: (ID. ibid.)] The stones of the wall or the pavement, and the boards, and the gold, etc., *as far as* and *to* those who have perfectly received the grace of the Gospel alike enjoy eternal life.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *The oracle. [n: (BED., ibid.)]* The oracle, where the ark was, etc., *as far as* who after the resurrection, ascending into heaven, placed at the Father's right hand the flesh he had taken of the Virgin.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Moreover the oracle.* ⟦s2⟧ And well is the inner [0589C] house twenty cubits long, for the mystery of the twofold love, of which it has already been spoken, which in this life for the greatest part enlightens the minds of the elect, but in the country above, the works of the other virtues ceasing, reigns alone.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Yea and the altar. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 12.)]* Of incense, that is, etc., *as far as* but what the stone, the cedar and the gold signify has been said above.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *The house also before the oracle. [n: (BED., ibid.)]* The house before the oracle is covered with gold, etc., *as far as* whence: *I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.* ⟦s2⟧ And again: *The hour cometh when now I will no longer speak to you in proverbs,* etc. [n: (Joan. XIV; XVI)].

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With golden nails. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Now the nails are the precepts of charity, etc., *as far as* who again, to perfection [0589D] coming, says of the nails of love: *But for me to adhere to God,* etc. [n: (Psal. CXVIII, 72)].
