## CHAPTER VII. (cont.)

*Little shoulder-pieces. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Which withstood the wheels set before them, lest they might slip away from the axle, are the heraldings of the prophets, by which the evangelical and apostolic Scripture is confirmed, lest it come into doubt for any of them that read; whence: [0597B] *We have the more firm prophetical word, whereunto you do well to attend,* etc. [n: (II Pet. I)]. Whence Mark also saith: *The beginning of the Gospel of the Son of God, as it is written in Isaias the prophet;* and Matthew: *Now all this was done, that the Scripture of the prophets might be fulfilled [n: (Mar. I; Matth. XXVI)]*.

*Over against. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Because all prophetic Scripture is in agreement with itself, as being composed by one Spirit. And there were four little shoulder-pieces to each base, that is, according to the number of the wheels, not that the books of the prophets are only four, but because all the things which the prophets spoke gave testimony to the four Gospels, so that out of the consent of both Testaments one faith and love of Christ might make firm the hearts of us all.

[0597C]VERS. 31.-- *One cubit. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Because of perfection, etc., *as far as* whence: *For we know in part, and we prophesy in part,* etc. [n: (I Cor. XIII)].

*The middle.* He means the upper board, which like the others was four-square and likewise carved, and had at its top a roundness of one cubit and a half, in which the laver was set.

VERS. 37.-- *In this manner he made ten bases, of one casting, and measure, and carving,* etc. [n: (BED., ibid.)] Why ten bases were made and as many lavers set beneath has been said above; but that there was one measure and a like carving of all the bases and of the lavers was not done with this signification, that the merits of all teachers can be equal; but [0597D] because they have one faith of the Gospel, by which they are instructed, one sacrament of baptism, by which they are washed, one and the same Spirit, by whom all are consecrated, although they have divers gifts in that same Spirit, who divides to every one according as he will.

VERS. 38.-- *One laver held forty baths,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid)] Forty signifies great perfection, because four times ten make forty, and ten are the precepts by which our whole working is prescribed in the law of God. And there are four books of the Gospels, in which through the dispensation of the Lord's incarnation the entry of the heavenly country has been laid open to us, and because all who belong to the mystery of baptism ought, together with the faith and the sacraments of the Gospel, [0598A] to show the fruit of right working, the several lavers in which the holocausts were washed held forty baths.

*And it was. [n: (ID., ibid)]*. As to what was said above: *and it had likewise one cubit and a half,* he did not add whether it there signified breadth or height: it seems that he willed the bottom of the laver itself to be understood to be of this breadth, which is most easily gathered from the measure of the base on which each laver was set, which is thus described: *And on the top of the base there was a certain roundness of one cubit and a half, so wrought that the laver might be set upon it.* [var: Vulg. *rotunditas dimidii cubiti*, "a roundness of half a cubit" (III Reg. 7:35)] The breadth therefore of the bottom in the lavers was of one cubit and a half. But the capacity itself of the lavers had four cubits, yet whether in height, or in breadth, [0598B] or in both, let him say who knows.

[n: (ID., ibid.)] Because of the four Gospels, in which the form of baptism, or the cardinal virtues wherein the baptized ought to be instructed, or because of the four quarters of the world in which baptism is ministered.

VERS. 39.-- *And he set ten bases, five on the right side of the temple, and five on the left. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* But the right side of the temple and the left, not within in the temple itself, etc., *as far as* and I consider that they are alien from the flame of divine charity.

*And he put the sea on the right side of the temple,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] And in this same court, etc., *as far as* through which the same love is poured forth in our hearts.

VERS. 46.-- *Of Jordan the king cast them in. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* [0598C] In which our Lord was baptized, etc., *as far as* he filled throughout the whole breadth of the world.

*Clayey ground. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 21.)]* Of which were made the moulds for the casting of the vessels of Scripture, etc., *as far as* that by running well we may come to their rewards.

VERS. 48.-- *And Solomon made all the vessels in the house,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] Above he said that Hiram had made all the vessels for Solomon, now the Scripture subjoins that Solomon made the same. For Solomon made them by dictating, Hiram by working.

*The altar of gold. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The hearts of the perfect flash with the light of inward charity and chastity, etc., *as far as* but they offer him only the vows of tears and of prayer for the desire of the heavenly kingdom.

[0598D] [n: (ID., ibid. cap. 22.)] Moses made the altar of incense, etc., *as far as* whence they who labour in the vineyard of the great householder are reckoned with a denary.

*The table. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The Scripture made clear by spiritual understanding, etc., *as far as* which was once thought to be understood according to the letter.

[n: (ID., ibid.)] As the tables are set for a figure of Scripture, etc., *as far as* so the elect are contained in Scripture, and with their mind lifted up to the things above they seek and receive heavenly goods from the Lord.

VERS. 49.-- *As it were of a lily. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* This he seems to say, because the topmost part of the candlesticks was formed after the manner of a lily bent back

*Golden lamps above.* By that which is contained is designated that [0599A] which contains, that is, the golden little vessels in which the oil shone.

*Tongs. [n: (BEDA., ibid.)]* That is, snuffers with which the lamps were snuffed, that being trimmed they might shine the better.

VERS. 50.-- *Waterpots.* The waterpots mark the inward parts of the saints, filled with the water of wisdom and the wine of compunction.

*Fleshhooks.* The fleshhooks with which the meats are prepared are the preachers, who administer to their hearers the food of understanding: whose office it is to distribute the body and blood of Christ to believers, and to deny them to unbelievers.

*Of the inner house. [n: (ID., ibid., cap. 22.)]* The doors of the inner house, angelic, etc., *as far as* or of the charity which they have toward God.

[0599B]VERS. 51.-- *He finished all the work. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* While the state of this world runs its course, he does indeed the work of the house of the Lord, but does not yet finish it, because the Lord inspires and helps the hearts of the elect that they may work good things; yet he grants that none abiding in this life is without sin. For this he reserves to the blessedness to come. But he finishes all the work of the temple of Solomon and renders it fit for dedication, when our peaceable one on the last day glorifies the elect with the immortality of the resurrection, and leads them through to the eternal kingdom. Whence the temple was built in seven years, but in the eighth it was finished and dedicated. To which in time agrees that which follows:

*And he brought in the things which he had sanctified. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* David the father of Solomon had sanctified the silver, when God [0599C] the Father strengthens the eloquent by the grace of his Spirit to speak the word of God. He sanctifies the gold, when he enlightens those endowed with natural talent for the understanding of the law. He sanctifies the vessels also, when he bestows the grace of the Holy Spirit upon all the sons of the Church. These sanctified things Solomon brings into the temple, when the Lord, the judgment being ended, brings the company of the teachers and of the rest of the faithful into the joy of the heavenly kingdom.

*And he laid them up in the treasures of the house of the Lord. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Because he hides his gods in the hidden place of his countenance from the disturbance of men. Many are the treasures in the one house of the Lord, because in the one Church the saints differ in merits. *And the heavenly country is one, although in it star differs from star in glory [n: (II Cor. XV)]*. Which both things the distributor of rewards showed, when he said: [0599D] *In my Father's house there are many mansions [n: (Joan. XV)]*.
