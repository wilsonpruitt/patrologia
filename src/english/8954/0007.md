## CHAPTER X.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Allegorically. [n: (ISID. in lib. Reg., tom. V.)] This queen, the Church that was to come from the Gentiles, etc., *as far as* desiring to become a fellow-citizen in the secret place of the saints.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And seeing. [n: (RAB. ubi supra.)]* The queen, having seen Solomon and his glory, astonished at his prudence, said: *True is the word,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ So holy Church, having heard the miracles of Christ, was provoked [0602A] to seek him: and when he was found by faith, the testimonies of Holy Scripture being considered, acknowledging the power of his divinity, she counts all that was told her of him beforehand to be little.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Blessed are the men.* [n: (RAB.)] The queen in her wonder broke forth into the praise of Solomon, saying: *Blessed are the men.* ⟦s2⟧ Truly blessed are they whose king is Christ, and who deserve to enjoy the eternal vision of him, and who rejoice to behold the glory which he hath with the Father and the Holy Ghost, and to perceive perpetually the wisdom which shows itself to the clean of heart.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Wood.* [n: (ID.)] Our Creator, precious woods, that is, etc., *as far as* and all the instruments of musicians.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *For the singers.* [n: (ID.)] Whatsoever these do [0602B] in word or in work, they do all unto the praise of God: they cease not to utter praise amid adversity and prosperity, in heart, in mouth and in work, whence: *Upon thy walls, Hierusalem, I have set watchmen, all the day and the night they shall not cease to praise the name of the Lord [n: (Isa. LXII)]*. [var: Vulg. Isa. 62:6 *tota die et tota nocte in perpetuum non tacebunt*, "all the day and all the night for ever they shall not hold their peace"]

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *And the king.* [n: (ID.)] And our peaceable one will give to his Church all things that she asks, whence: *Whatsoever you shall ask the Father in my name, he will give it you.* ⟦s2⟧ Not only what we ask, but even those things which human frailty either knows not, or presumes not to ask, he bestows by a free gift.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Two hundred shields.* [n: (Id.)] In Paralipomenon golden spears are added to the shields, because the captains keeping watch before the door of the king's house used lances and shields. ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *seventy valiant ones surround the bed of Solomon.* [var: Vulg. Cant. 3:7 *En lectulum Salomonis sexaginta fortes ambiunt*, "Behold, sixty valiant ones surround the bed of Solomon"]

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0602C] [n: (ID.)] Keeping watch at the doors of the royal house, etc., *as far as* and repelling with the shield of faith the fiery darts of the most wicked one, they quench them.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *The forest of Libanus.* ⟦s2⟧ The wood of Libanus signifies the Church of the Gentiles, which, cut off from the haughtiness of pride, is fitted into the fabric of the house of God. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *We have found it in the fields of the wood.*

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *A throne of ivory.* [n: (RAB.)] A throne or seat is an imperial chair, which in the Canticles is called a carriage, for that it carries those that sit thereon, or carries them about from place to place. ⟦s2⟧ To which men ascended by six steps of ascent. ⟦s3⟧ Under the seat was a golden footstool, and the top of the throne was round, held by two [0602D] hands or arms on the outside, and beside it stood two lions helping to sustain the seat.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *And twelve little lions.* ⟦s2⟧ Because six steps of ascent were placed on either side for a support of the step of him that ascended.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] The seat of Solomon is understood to be the Church, etc., *as far as* they strive to fortify.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *There was not made,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ This aptly agrees with the Church, to which it is said: *Many daughters have gathered together riches, thou hast surpassed them all [n: (Prov. XXXI)]*.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *But also all the vessels,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ All the vessels which discharge the ministry of our peaceable one are golden, because all the souls of the saints, which are the vessels of God, that they may serve the divine will, shining with the splendour of wisdom and of love, contain the draught of life, and there is made in [0603A] them *a fountain of water springing up into life everlasting [n: (Joan. IV)]*.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ *Nor of any,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] Because, according to the Apostle, *The word of God is not in speech, but in power and in the preaching of the Gospel [n: (I Cor. I)]*. [var: Vulg. 1 Cor. 4:20 *Non enim in sermone est regnum Dei, sed in virtute*, "For the kingdom of God is not in speech, but in power"] It consists not in the persuasible words of human wisdom, but in the showing of the spirit and of power, that our faith may be not in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Carrying from thence gold,* etc. [n: (ID.)] What gold or silver signifies, etc., *as far as* he pursues warlike arms.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *And Solomon gathered together,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Of these Josephus discourses. ⟦s2⟧ Their riders were adorned, first flourishing in comely youth, having great stature, spreading out their hair as they sat upon the horses, clad about with purple, who, being armed [0603B] about the king and bearing bows, rode before him whenever he had to go forth to any place.

## CHAPTER XI.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And king Solomon.* [n: (RAB.)] The Scripture vehemently reproves Solomon, etc., *as far as* his evils signify the evils of the Church.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *The gulf.* ⟦s2⟧ The wall of the city fell, for that on one side it hung over a precipice. ⟦s3⟧ And when the king wished to make this gulf level with the walls, he appointed it to the captains that they should fulfil this work; but Jeroboam, who was prince of the tribe of Joseph, would not obey the king, who had before appointed him over the buildings of the bulwarks which he was making in Jerusalem, where he laboured so that the king [0603C] conferred upon him the command of the soldiery over the tribe of Joseph. ⟦s4⟧ But being made a rebel he goes forth out of Jerusalem: whom the prophet met, turning him aside into a field, that no man might hear what he said to him. ⟦s5⟧ And being lifted up by the words of the prophet, he persuaded the people to make him king: Which Solomon hearing, sought to kill him.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Now Jeroboam was a valiant man.* ⟦s2⟧ He refused this command of the restoring of the walls. ⟦s3⟧ But whence so great boldness? ⟦s3⟧ Because the king had appointed him governor.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Take thee ten pieces.* [n: (RAB.)] Ten tribes were separated from the house of David, etc., *as far as* because *there is one God, one faith, one baptism.* [var: Vulg. Eph. 4:5 *Unus Dominus, una fides, unum baptisma*, "One Lord, one faith, one baptism"]

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 42.-- *Now the days.* ⟦s2⟧ Josephus said that he reigned eighty years, and lived ninety-four. ⟦s3⟧ But divine Scripture expresses only those years in which [0603D] he reigned before he transgressed.

## CHAPTER XII.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1-4.-- *And Roboam came. ⟦s2⟧ A most hard yoke.* ⟦s3⟧ How is this true, since it was said above that Solomon appointed none of the children of Israel to serve? ⟦s4⟧ Assuredly he did not make them serve in husbandry or in any other servile work. ⟦s5⟧ But he appointed princes out of them over the several tribes, who rendered to the king by the provision month by month, etc., which things are related above concerning the king's expense, which they now call a most hard yoke.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The young men.* ⟦s2⟧ Of whom Aduram was one, who was afterwards stoned. ⟦s3⟧ Scripture does not always name young men according to age, but according to the instability [0604A] of mind. ⟦s4⟧ For Roboam began to reign in the fortieth year of his age.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *With scorpions.* ⟦s2⟧ It is a kind of scourge, even as knotted rods are wont to be made of thorns or of orchard trees. ⟦s3⟧ Or also the scourge of the Saracens with two or three rods, which has at the top leaden balls, otherwise hailstones.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *To Roboam.* [n: (RAB.)] Roboam was of the house of David, etc., *as far as and whose princes eat in due season.*

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *He made two calves of gold.* ⟦s2⟧ Why Jeroboam made a calf, or how, Josephus sufficiently sets forth.
