## CHAPTER XI.

VERS. 1.-- *But Athalia.* [n: (RAB.)] Athalia, who strove to extinguish the seed of David, expresses the impiety of the Synagogue, which against the seed of David, that is, of Christ, [0616D] was a lier in wait. Which once seemed to reign, when it kept the ceremonies of the law after a temporal manner. For Athalia is interpreted "the temporal one of the Lord." But by the vigour of Josabeth, who is interpreted "the fullness of the Lord," that is, of the Church, in which are true delights, Joas is preserved, that is, "the memory of the Lord." Christ, namely, in whom is the memory of the name of the Lord, lest he be slain through the cruelty of the raging enemy in the hearts of the elect. Rather is he nourished in the house of Joiada, which sounds "beloved of the Lord," of whom the Father says: *This is my beloved Son [n: (Matth. III)]*, whose house is holy Church, where, abiding in the holy faith of the elect, he daily makes increase of his body, until in the time of judgment he shall lift up the sceptre of the kingdom and his power against those [0617A] who thought to press him down, and shall have consigned the murderess of the saints to eternal punishments.

VERS. 4.-- *And in the seventh year Joiada the high priest sent,* etc. There were four and twenty courses of priests, of levites, and of doorkeepers, and they had as many high priests, who severally succeeded one another in order through as many weeks, each having under him four and twenty priests and as many levites, and doorkeepers of the same number. So when a new high priest entered upon the office on the sabbath with a new band of priests, of levites, and of doorkeepers, that high priest with his own band, who had ministered through the week now past, returned home after the sabbath to his wife, for as long as they ministered they ceased from conjugal work, abiding in the porches about the house of the Lord until the sabbath. [0617B] But then the high priest, because of the need of increasing the force, both kept back those who had completed their week, and received those who were about to fulfil theirs. He also gathered other levites out of the cities, and the chiefs of families, whom, being about to bring forth the king's son, he divided upon this plan, that those who had fulfilled the sabbath should stand armed about the king in the inner places of the court. Those who had come for the sabbath should watch the king's house, that is, the palace, and the gate of the shield-bearers, through which the descent was made from the temple to the palace. The rest of the multitude should keep the outer doors of the courts against the fury of the queen, if she should attempt anything: afterwards he brought forth the king out of the house of the Lord where he had been hidden, and they came by the way of the gate of the shield-bearers into the palace, and he sat upon the throne of the kings.

[0617C]VERS. 6.-- *At the gate.* [n: (RAB.)] The gate Sur and the house Messa (which are named together with the gate of the shield-bearers) seem to be in the palace.

*Of the shield-bearers, and you shall keep the watches of the house.* [n: (ID.)] The shield-bearers were the guardians of the king, keeping the vestibule of the palace.

VERS. 12.-- *And he put upon him the diadem and the testimony.* [n: (ID.)] In the book of Days more openly: *And they put upon him the diadem, and gave into his hand the law to be held.* And it was assuredly of saving prudence that, after the slaying of the tyrannical queen, to the king's son succeeding to the kingdom, together with the very habit of royalty, the discipline of the law of God should be committed to be kept: and that he who saw himself set over the people to rule it should remember that he himself ought to be made subject, to be ruled by the divine laws.

[0617D]VERS. 17.-- *Joiada therefore made a covenant between the Lord,* etc. In the Paralipomenon, *between himself:* because he set himself in the place of the king and of God, whose minister he was; whence Joiada: he is interpreted "acknowledging the Lord" or "loving him": because he taught men to know and to love the Lord. He made a covenant between the Lord and the king and the people, that the king and the people should serve the Lord: he made one between the king and the people, that the people should deal faithfully toward the king, and the king should rule the people with justice.

## CHAPTER XII.

[n: (RAB.)] Our king commands that the teachers receive all the money of spiritual knowledge or of good examples which is brought in by the just who pass by into the treasury [0618A] of the Lord, or that the offices of the preachers be applied to the repairing of the spiritual temple, that there they may restore whatsoever they find torn through error or through vices, lest perchance through the negligence of the masters the multitude of hearers perish.

VERS. 5.-- *The repairs.* [n: (ID.)] He speaks of the ruins of the house of the Lord, that what had begun to fall might be propped up. For it is read in the Paralipomenon that Athalia and her sons, that is, the priests of the idols, since it is read that she had no son, destroyed the house of the Lord, and of the hallowed things made Baal.

VERS. 6.-- *Therefore until.* [n: (ID.)] Before the coming of Christ, although the doctrine of the law was in the Jewish nation, yet through the negligence of the teachers it was corrupted in many things, until he himself should come who gave the law, and through [0618B] faith in the holy Trinity should teach that the decalogue in the writings of Moses and of the prophets was to be kept spiritually.

VERS. 7.-- *Restore it for the repairing of the temple,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The Lord says to the teachers of the Gospel: *Preach the Gospel to every creature, heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse the lepers; freely have you received, freely give [n: (Marc. XVI)]*.

VERS. 9.-- *And he took.* [n: (ID.)] Our high priest, who offered himself a victim to God, set one treasury, that is, made one catholic Church, in which he opened a hole above, because he prepared thence a passage to the heavens.

*And he set it.* [n: (ID.)] The altar is the body of the Lord, beside which the treasury stands, when the Church is joined to Christ, at the right hand of those entering the house. Because they who enter the Church through faith purpose to attain to [0618C] the eternal blessedness which the right hand signifies. Thither therefore the priests who keep the doors of the temple send all the money which is brought to the temple, when the preachers who guard the entrance of faith offer the vows of the faithful in words and works: and in the Church duly commend them to God with prayers.

VERS. 10.-- *The king's scribe,* etc. [n: (ID.)] They are the highest teachers, who gave out the money collected according to number and measure to those who were set over the workmen and the masons, that is, to the dispensers of the word of God, who gave the measure of the evangelical wheat to their fellow-servants, to those who labour in the house of the Lord and set the works of the virtues in order in the divine building.

[n: (ID.)] The apostles, whom the divine election appointed princes in the Church, scattered through their disciples the seeds of the word through [0618D] the whole world, that they might set over their hearers workmen fit for the will of God, of whom some wrought the timbers, when they were zealous to prepare themselves and those obedient to them as fruit-bearing timbers in the house of the Lord. Others made the repairs of the temple, when they rebuilt those things which had been broken through heresy and schisms; others hewed the stones, when they strongly rebuked the hard of heart and the unbelieving, so that the repairing was fulfilled in all those things which needed outlay for the fortifying of the house of the Lord, according to that word: *And to every one of you is given grace according to the measure of the giving of Christ [n: (Ephes. IV)]*. [var: Vulg. Eph. 4:7 *Unicuique autem nostrum*, "to every one of us"]

VERS. 13.-- *But nevertheless they were not made.* [n: (ID.)] One method there ought to be of forming vessels for the ministry [0619A] of the Lord, another of preparing timbers and stones for the building of the house, because one doctrine there ought to be by which the untaught are steeped in faith, and another by which those already perfect are instructed unto the fullness of knowledge. The vessels of the Lord are they who are now fit to receive the divine gifts, and are apt for the ministry of God.

*They were not made.* In the Paralipomenon. *When they had completed all the works, they brought before the king and Joiada the remaining part of the money, whereof the vessels of the temple were made.* It is not contrary, because the vessels for the ministry of the temple were not made of the same money which was offered by the people for the repairing of the house of the Lord, but of the remaining part of the money, which had been gathered also in other donations.

*And the fleshhooks,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] The teachers, whose office [0619B] it is to distribute the body and blood of the Lord to believers, to deny it to unbelievers, and moreover to deal out to each, as is fitting, the spiritual nourishment of the word: because there are among the words of God things which he has vouchsafed to reveal to our lowliness, and there are others which pass beyond the measure of our capacity.

*The censers.* [n: (ID.)] They are those who offer to God a clean prayer out of a pure conscience and unfeigned faith, and say: *Let my prayer be directed as incense in thy sight [n: (Psal. CXL)]*.

*The trumpets, every vessel,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The teachers, who send forth through the whole world the voice of evangelical preaching, whence: *Lift up thy voice like a trumpet [n: (Isa. LVIII)]*.

VERS. 15.-- *And there was no reckoning made with all these,* etc. [var: Vulg. 4 Kings 12:15 *iis hominibus*, "with those men"] [n: (ID.)] They were so religious that no man doubted that they [0619C] handled the money of God without fraud. Whence it is plain with how great discretion they have need who dispense to their fellow-servants the money of the word of God, of which an account is to be rendered, that these may be found no less faithful in spiritual things than those in bodily.

VERS. 16.-- *But the money for a trespass,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Here it is shown that the confession of sins and penance is to be carried through not by each man's own judgment, but by that of the priests, whose office it is to discern between clean and unclean, and to judge the stain of leprosy.
