## CHAPTER XXV

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And it was broken up,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] The guard of the peoples was indeed broken up by the divers temptations of demons, etc., *as far as* who is weighed down by depraved habit and by the multitude of his own iniquity.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *In the fifth month.* [n: (RAB.)] The order of the time agrees with the reason of the vengeance. ⟦s2⟧ In the fifth month the city was laid waste, which scorned to keep the Pentateuch of the law, and on the seventh day of the month, because it did not keep the rest of the sabbath, and did things contrary to the precepts of God, [0629B] flattering itself in vain concerning its security because of its dwelling in holy places.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] *Nabuzardan came,* who is interpreted a winnowing-fan, or the prophecy of another's judgment, etc., *as far as* but rather the thorns and thistles of vices grow up.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *But the brazen pillars which,* etc. [n: (ID.)] And by the bases we understand the teachers, who by the sonority of their preaching and by the firmness of their faith ought to have upheld others. ⟦s2⟧ The brazen sea is the laver of baptism, or the compunction of tears. ⟦s3⟧ All these things indeed the spiritual Chaldeans strive, through the sloth of the masters, both to carry away from the house of the Lord, and to break in pieces.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *The pots also.* [n: (ID.)] Because, taking away the divers offices in the ecclesiastical orders from the service of God, they turned them to their own most wicked use.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0629C]VERS. 15.-- *The censers and the vials.* ⟦s2⟧ When by hindering they overthrow the prayer by which the Godhead could be appeased, and the cup of the word by which the weary ought to have been refreshed.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Because the wicked enemy strives to carry away out of the house of the Lord both precious understanding and the comeliness of speech, and to lead them with himself into confusion.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *He took also.* [n: (ID.)] The devil busies himself to deceive not only the common people, but also the strong and the princes in the Church, and to lead sinners captive, whence in Job [0629D] of him: *His food is choice [n: (Job XXXIII)]*.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He took [sic: ulit] Saraias,* who is interpreted bound, *the first [0630A] priest,* when by the love of earthly pleasure he binds fast with the chain of vices the first order of the Church, which is in the bishops. ⟦s2⟧ Likewise *Sophonias,* who is interpreted hidden, *the second priest,* when he subdues under his own dominion the men of the second order, that is, the presbyters (who, digging in the earth, had hidden the talent of the word). ⟦s3⟧ *And three empty,* [cj: *inani*; read *janitores*, "doorkeepers"] who ought to have opened the door of faith, and, holding the key of knowledge, neither entered in themselves, nor suffered others to enter in. ⟦s4⟧ The eunuch signifies the foolish virgins, who held in their hands vessels empty of oil, restraining themselves from bodily intercourse, and yet would not have the oil of grace and mercy in their breast. ⟦s5⟧ Who are in vain set over warriors, since they guard not against arrogance. ⟦s6⟧ *Five men of them that had stood before the king* [0630B] [n: (Matth. V)], that is, those who, serving pleasure with the five senses of the body, always stood by the king of vices.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Sopher the prince.* [n: (ID.)] It is interpreted scattering or dividing: signifying those who, scattering by evil examples the untaught in the Church, whom they ought to have nourished for the warfare of Christ, sever them through error from the assembly of the faithful. ⟦s2⟧ *And sixty men of the common people,* that is, foolish workers, who are rightly of the common people, because they had neglected to learn the divine counsels and to fulfil them in deeds.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And sixty men.* [n: (ID.)] All these Nabuzardan led to the king of Babylon, and they were slain, because the devil goes about to bring the prey which he tears away from the Church to the slaughter of everlasting death: whom the king of confusion strikes in Reblatha, that is, in the multitude [0630C] of wickednesses, and in the love of pleasures.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] When they who seemed to have the confession of the name of God in the Church, being carried away through manifold wickednesses out of the land of the living, are led off into the kingdom of confusion and of error, etc., *as far as* and to contemplate the face of our Creator through the ages.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *And Saraia.* ⟦s2⟧ The son of Saraias the priest was kept back, and the father slain. ⟦s3⟧ Of whom afterwards in the captivity was born Jesus the great priest, who [0630D] brought back the people with Zorobabel.
