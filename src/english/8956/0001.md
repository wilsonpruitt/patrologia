## CHAPTER ONE.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1231D]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The vision of Isaiah,* etc. ⟦s2+3⟧ There are three kinds of vision: the first bodily, by which we see heaven and earth and things of this sort; the second spiritual, by which we see, as in dreams, the images of men and things of this sort; the third intellectual, which is in the reason, by which we know the Creator, and, the Holy Spirit [1232C] revealing it, understand anything at all. "To see" is also often put for "to understand": whence we often ask, saying: Seest thou, that is, understandest thou? ⟦s4⟧ Prophets too are hence called seers, that is, understanders. ⟦s5⟧ This vision Isaiah saw by the Holy Spirit, that is, at his intimation, he understood the things that were to come upon them. ⟦s6⟧ Whence at the beginning he does not set down visions as Daniel does, nor does he say what he saw; but at once he says: Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER. in Isa., tom. IV)]. ⟦s2⟧ Not only Isaiah, etc., *as far as* they were not therefore ignorant, according to Montanus, who saw in this way.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID., Ibid. )] "Isaiah" is interpreted, etc., *as far as* and [1232D] when he shall have done all things, he says: *I am an unprofitable servant; what I ought to have done, I have done.* [var: Vulg. Lc 17:10 has the plural, "We are unprofitable servants: what we ought to have done, we have done."]

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The son of Amos.* [n: (ID., ibid. )] Amos, the father of Isaiah, is not the third of the twelve Prophets, etc., *as far as* they contend that they were Prophets.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Upon Judah and Jerusalem.* [n: (ID., ibid. )] Isaiah principally [1233A] concerning the two tribes, etc., *as far as* that these things were foretold for the correction of the two tribes.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The vision of Isaiah,* etc. [n: (HIER., ibid.)] For Judah, in whom the two tribes are signified, etc., *as far as* of which: *Arise, be enlightened, O Jerusalem, for thy light is come.*

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the days of Ozias. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Ozias is himself the man who is also Azarias, etc., *as far as* so that our histories may appear more ancient than those of the Gentiles.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Hear, O heavens,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] Above, who the Prophet is, etc., *as far as* since this belongs to the power of God, and not to earthly understanding.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The Lord hath spoken. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Not to you, but to me, that what I have heard in the spirit I may report to you, who do not deserve to hear him yourselves.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I have brought up sons. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* In that he says: I [1233B] adopted them as sons, etc., *as far as* whence: *He gave them power to be made the sons of God.*

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The ox hath known,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] Whence: *Thou shalt not plow with a calf and an ass together [n: (Deut. XXII)]* [var: Vulg. Dt 22:10 reads *in bove*, "with an ox"]. ⟦s2⟧ Like Ebion, etc., *as far as* to whom it is said: *Come to me, you that labor and are burdened.*

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The ass.* ⟦s2⟧ The Gentile, or the wanton man converted, unclean before, burdened with sins, who has believed the incarnation of Christ, who as soon as he was born was laid in a manger, that by the bridle of his own flesh he might feed all men.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Israel.* [n: (HIER., uti supra. )] The Scribes and Pharisees, having the key of knowledge and of the law. ⟦s2⟧ Whence they were called Israel, "those who see God."

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He hath not known me.* ⟦s2⟧ Whose day, that is, Abraham saw and was glad [n: (Joan. VIII)]; whose coming [1233C] the choir of the Prophets, in awaiting it, foretold.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Woe to the sinful nation,* etc. [n: (HIER., ibid.)] In this title he records the person, the cause, and the time; secondly, he makes them attentive; thirdly, he tells what the Lord has spoken; fourthly, as though in transports, he rebukes the sinful nation. ⟦s2⟧ *Woe to the nation.* etc. ⟦s3⟧ He threatens everlasting destruction, because they reject the Son, who brought salvation.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With iniquity. [n: (ID. ibid.)]* Whence: *Iniquity sitteth upon a talent of lead [n: (ZACH. V)]*; whence elsewhere: *As a heavy burden they are become heavy upon me [n: (Psal. XXXVII)]*.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Wicked.* ⟦s2⟧ Others, "of the most wicked," so that the fault should not be of the seed, but of the malice of those who by their own will have forsaken God. ⟦s3⟧ For the nature of the good and of the evil is not to be thought different.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1233D] *They have blasphemed.* ⟦s2⟧ Saying: *Thou hast a devil,* and *Thou art a Samaritan [n: (Joan. VIII)]*; and *We have no king but Caesar;* and *Is not this the son of the carpenter?*

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They are gone away estranged. [n: (HIER., ibid.)]* They who were called the portion and the sons of God [n: (Joan. XIX; Matth. XIII)]. ⟦s2⟧ It is said of them: *The strange children have lied to me [n: (Psal. XVII)]*, whence: *No man putting his hand to the plow,* etc. [n: (Luc IX)], whence the Apostle: *Stretching himself out to the things that are before, forgetful of the things behind [n: (Philip. III)]*.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *For what shall I strike you,* etc. [n: (ID. ibid.)]. ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say, etc., *as far as* but that there is now no place fit for healing he shows thus: *Every head is sick* etc.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Every head is sick. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Just as gladness [1234A] of mind sometimes softens the pains of the body, etc., *as far as* whence: *From the sole of the foot,* etc.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *A wound and a bruise. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* As if he should say, etc., *as far as* and they slew the merciful physician who had been sent to them.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Nor dressed with medicine. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* Whence: *We have healed Babylon, and she is not healed,* the city, that is, of confusion and of vices, etc., *as far as* and he expressed the irremediable captivity of the Jews.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Your land is desolate,* etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] These things were fulfilled in part under the Babylonians, etc., *as far as* whence: *Unless the Lord keep the city, he watcheth in vain,* etc.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The daughter of Sion,* etc., [n: (ID., ibid.)] With the affection of a most loving father. ⟦s2⟧ And: *For we are all by nature the sons of God* [1234B] [n: (Joan. XIV)], but by our own fault we are made strangers. ⟦s3⟧ Whence Babylon is often called *daughter*.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Unless the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] This from the person of the Prophet, etc, *as far as* so the Almighty, [he] is the Son of the Almighty.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Hear the word.* [n: (ID.)] The remnant being saved, etc., *as far as* and now he is discerned by the mind, when he is believed to be invisible.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *I am full.* [n: (ID.)] As if he should say: Do you think that I have need of these things, or take delight in them? ⟦s2⟧ But I am full, that is, I have need of nothing. ⟦s3⟧ *For mine is the earth and the fulness thereof.* ⟦s4⟧ Or, I am full, that is, affected with weariness, because you do not offer rightly.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The holocausts of rams,* etc., as far as: *To judge for the fatherless,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He rejects the sacrifices of the Jews, he commends the obedience [1234C] of the Gospel: *The holocausts of rams,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ He points to the last destruction of the temple, which endures for ever. ⟦s4⟧ For after the Babylonian captivity the temple was built by Zorobabel, and sacrifice was offered in it for a long time.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] *The holocausts of rams.* ⟦s2⟧ It can also be taken of those who, while they despise the precepts of God, wish to appease him by a multitude of sacrifices, and to buy themselves licence to sin. ⟦s3⟧ Or of those who offer robbery and avarice upon the altar, and bestow them on the poor.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I would not.* ⟦s2⟧ Using the past tense, he shows that he never loved the sacrifices of the Jews, but bore with them for a time on account of what they signified; and that, the true sacrifice being fulfilled, he reckoned them as nothing.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *When you came.* [n: (ID.)] God did not [1234D] seek these sacrifices as though he were in need; but that they might not sacrifice to demons, and that through carnal and typical things they might come more easily to the spiritual and true sacrifice.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Who required?* ⟦s2⟧ Let the Ebionites hear this, who after the passion of Christ think that the law is to be kept: let their fellows hear this, who decree that it is to be kept only by the Israelite race.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *The new moon,* etc, The feast of the new moon, which they used to celebrate at the beginning of the months: *Neos,* new, *mene,* moon; whence: *Blow up the trumpet to the Lord on the new moon [n: (Psal. LXXX)]*.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Your assemblies are wicked,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Because they do not offer spiritual victims; whence: *A sacrifice to God is an afflicted spirit [n: (Psalm. L)]*.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1235A]Vers. 14.-- *My soul hath hated.* [n: (ID.)] Ανθρωποσπαθῶς. ⟦s2⟧ Not that he has a soul, but he speaks after our manner; as if to say, I have utterly hated.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I have labored,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Symmachus. ⟦s2⟧ *I have failed in pitying,* etc., *as far as* but thou art saved by my help alone.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *For your hands are full of blood,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Of the Prophets, and at last of their Lord, etc., *as far as* they keep watch in prayer by day and by night.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Be washed,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: This I have hated: the truth of the Gospel pleases me: *Be washed,* in the blood of Christ, in the water of baptism. ⟦s3⟧ *Be clean,* abiding in innocence of life. ⟦s4⟧ For baptism alone avails unto the remission of sin, whence: *Unless a man be born again [n: (Joan. III)]*, etc. ⟦s5⟧ And [1235B] elsewhere: *Go, teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father,* etc.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER., ubi supra )]. ⟦s2⟧ The chief order, etc., *as far as* but also in the sight of God, from whom nothing can be hidden.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Cease to do perversely.* [n: (ID.)] It sounds that saying of the Gospel, etc., *as far as* whom they follow in the Apocalypse who shine with the whiteness of virginity.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Learn to do well.* [n: (ID.)] Not trusting in nature, etc., *as far as* the thresholds of masters must be worn away.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Seek judgment,* etc. [n: (ID.)] To the victims of the Jews the commandments of the Gospel succeed. ⟦s2⟧ Provision is made for orphans and widows, so that the martyrs, secure concerning their wives and children, may go forth to their battles.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *As scarlet,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The same thing is the scarlet grain [1235C] and the scarlet, and the little worm, namely a great sin, and murder and adultery, etc. ⟦s2⟧ But what is signified by snow, this is signified by wool: the purging, that is, and the washing away of sins.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As snow.* ⟦s2⟧ Because they shall be cold against the incitements of vices: *And as wool,* because they are clothed against the cold of faithlessness.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *White wool,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Of which the garment of Christ is made.

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *If you be willing and will hear,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Free will is preserved, etc., *as far as* religion might pass over to the praise of God.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Morally; the faithful soul, and full of judgment, while it obeys God, God dwells in it, who is the justifying justice; but if it withdraws from God, not by place but by desert, it is inhabited by malignant spirits, who are the murderers [1235D] of souls.

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Justice.* [n: (ID.)] Justice, in the Hebrew *sedek* צדק, which rather signifies "the just," so that we may understand that the Lord tarried in her, of whom: *But the just man, what hath he done?* which in Ezechiel is signified by Ooliba, which is interpreted *tabernacle,* or *my tabernacle in her.*

⟦p51⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Silver,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The bright, that is, and the shining have turned back to vices.

⟦p52⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Into dross.* [n: (ID.)] Dross, in Hebrew *siggim* (סגים) : which is the rust of metals, or the refuse and filth that are burned off by fire.

⟦p53⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thy wine,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Others: *Thy vintners mingle wine with water.* ⟦s3⟧ Symmachus: *Thy wine is mingled [1236A] with water.* ⟦s4⟧ Morally: *Thy wine is mingled with water:* because the precepts of Holy Scripture, by which they ought to correct their hearers, he softens to the will of those hearers, and, his own meaning being mixed in, he corrupts the wine. ⟦s5⟧ *Thy wine,* etc. ⟦s6⟧ Heretics too corrupt the Gospel, worst of vintners, making water out of wine, whereas the Lord turned water into wine, at which the steward of the feast marvels: and the queen of Saba, at Solomon's banquet, praising his cupbearers and the servers of the wine.

⟦p54⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *Companions of thieves.* [n: (ID.)] Receiving from the Scribes and Pharisees the gifts which they were taking away from the poor. ⟦s2⟧ In the passion of the Lord also they were unbelieving, unwilling to believe in Christ; and companions of thieves, that is, of the Scribes and Pharisees and of Judas the traitor, *Companions of thieves,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ We too ought to beware that we do not receive gifts from [1236B] those who gather riches out of the tears of the poor, lest we be companions of thieves, and lest it be said of us: *If thou didst see a thief, thou didst run with him,* etc.

⟦p55⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They follow after rewards.* [n: (ID.)] Because they do not count them friends, etc., *as far as* that is, bear it patiently.

⟦p56⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *The Strong One of Israel.* [n: (ID.)] LXX: *Woe, ye strong ones of Israel,* etc., *as far as* or in character.

⟦p57⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Ah, I.* [n: (ID.)] With the affection of a loving father he laments and strikes them (though they be enemies), because they perish and will not repent.

⟦p58⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I will comfort myself,* etc. [n: (ID.)] He comforts himself by vengeance, etc., *as far as* wherein he signifies that they shall suffer torments.
