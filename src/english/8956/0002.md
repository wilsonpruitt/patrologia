## CHAPTER ONE. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Thy tin.* [n: (ID.)] The false doctrine of the Scribes and Pharisees, which is like [1236C] the true, as tin is like silver. ⟦s2⟧ And I will give the pure and perfect knowledge of the law and of the Gospel.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *And I will restore thy judges,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Not only after the captivity, as Zorobabel, Esdras, and the others who ruled over the people down to Hircanus; but also after the coming of the Lord, the Apostles and the other doctors of the Church.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ *The city of the just, the faithful city Sion, shall be redeemed in judgment.* [var: Vulg. divides otherwise, closing Is 1:26 with "the faithful city" and opening Is 1:27 with "Sion shall be redeemed in judgment."] The Church, in which they are called back to living justly. ⟦s3⟧ For a city is a gathering of men for living justly.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *As an oak with the leaves falling.* [n: (ID.)] As from an oak shaken by drought, etc., *as far as* it does not bring forth even herbs, which a sick man might eat.

## CHAPTER II.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1236D]

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *In the last days.* [n: (HIER.)] Of which Jacob, etc., *as far as: it grew into a great mountain.*

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A mountain. ⟦s2⟧ Mountains,* the angels, the prophets, the patriarchs, the apostles, the martyrs, and the rest who shone forth with miracles. ⟦s3⟧ *Hills,* the lesser, receiving their light from the greater.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And it shall be exalted.* [n: (ID.)] Above all the saints, because of his fulness all receive (of whom: *The mountains round about it* ), who are called mountains from Christ the mountain.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The house.* [n: (ID.)] Which is said for this reason, that we may receive the Old Testament, and may not, after Manichaeus, seek another house than the house of the God of Jacob.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1237A] *His ways,* which others have trodden. ⟦s2⟧ The Lord too, going up into the mountain, taught his disciples. ⟦s3⟧ First, then, the ways must be learned; afterward one must walk in the paths.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Out of Sion.* [n: (HIER.)] Not from the rock Sina, but from mount Sion, in which is Jerusalem, in which is the temple and the religion of God. ⟦s2⟧ The Lord also taught in the temple, because it was not fitting that a Prophet should die outside Jerusalem.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The law and the word.* ⟦s2⟧ As above, he set the word and the law together, so that the princes might hear the word, and the people take in the law with the ear. ⟦s3⟧ For he who shall have kept the law will come through to the word of God; the law will go forth from the word as from a fountain, that it may water others.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And they shall stand fast their swords.* [var: Vulg. Is 2:4 *conflabunt*, "they shall beat (their swords into ploughshares)"] [n: (ID.)] All zeal for warring shall be turned into peace, etc., *as far as* let us eat at the time of harvest, that is, [1237B] of death.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *O house of Jacob,* etc. [n: (ID.)] After the calling of the nations and the showing of the mountain of God, he invites his own people, who are of Jacob, that, going out from the darkness of unbelief, they may receive the light of Christ.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the light.* [n: (ID.)] *Whilst you have the light, believe in the light. ⟦s2⟧ Come ye to him and be enlightened,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ As if he shall say: *He that doth evil hateth the light: but come with me, let us walk in the light of the Lord, who saith: I am the light of the world,* etc.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Vers. 6.-- *For thou hast cast off.* ⟦s2⟧ After he had exhorted them, he saw in spirit their hardness, and suddenly turning the eyes of his mind to the Lord, he says: *For thou hast cast off,* etc., as if he should say, and therefore they are to be exhorted, because *thou hast cast off,* etc.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1237C] *Because they are filled up, as of old,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The Romans, that is, casting out, the Jews: foreigners dwelt in Judaea, brought in from every quarter by Titus and Vespasian, or by the other princes. ⟦s2⟧ Or, before they were laid waste by the Babylonians, they committed these things under impious kings.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As the Philistines.* [n: (ID.)] The Philisthiim, who are today the Palestinians, because the Hebrew tongue has not the letter *p.*, but in place of it uses the Greek φ.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With boys,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He signifies the lust of the conquerors against the Jewish nation. ⟦s3⟧ For the Greeks and the Latins labored long under this vice: but under the emperor Constantine, and with the Gospel flashing forth, this baseness was carried away together with the rest.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *The land is filled.* [n: (ID.)] Both the avarice of the Romans and that of the Jews is censured; for the Greek [1237D] and Latin histories bear witness that nothing is more avaricious than either nation: among the idols, avarice is condemned.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *With horses, and innumerable.* [n: (ID.)] And this against the Lord, who forbade that chariots and horses should be multiplied: for these animals were given for helping, not for growing proud; whence they are also called beasts of burden.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *He hath bowed himself down.* ⟦s2⟧ Where once was the temple and the religion of God, there is the statue of Hadrian and the idol of Jupiter.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Therefore not.* ⟦s2⟧ Isaiah does not curse, but blesses, etc., *as far as* and they are filled with idols, etc.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *From the face of the fear of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ From the wrath of judgment.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And from the glory of his majesty.* ⟦s2⟧ Because *he shall come with great power and majesty.*

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1238A]VERS 11.-- *The lofty eyes,* etc. [n: (Hier.)] Neither riches, nor power, nor nobility of birth shall save any man; one captivity shall come upon them all. ⟦s2⟧ These things can also be taken of the day of judgment, when every creature shall be humbled in the sight of God, and shall perceive that it is nothing in comparison with him.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.*

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Of the Lord of hosts.* ⟦s2⟧ Wherever he speaks of the victory or of the vengeance of the Lord, he records him as the Lord of hosts, that we may not doubt of the victory of him whose power is so great.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *The high mountains.* [n: (HIER.)] According to the variety of virtues, some are called the mountains of the good, others hills. ⟦s2⟧ According to the diversity of vices, some are the mountains of the impious, others [sic: *allii*] hills, to whom it is said: *I will bring upon [1238B] you the sword,* etc.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And upon every tower.* [n: (ID.)] To the letter: when the Romans came, both the towers and the walls were overthrown from their foundations, etc., *as far as* lest a perverse teaching prevail over the truth.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Ships.* [n: (ID.)] Josaphat had ships which he used to send to Tharsis, etc., *as far as* there are also good ships, of which it is said: *They that go down to the sea in a ship,* etc.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Tharsis.* [n: (ID.)] Of any sea whatever, etc., *as far as* if it is said otherwise, it is Syriac.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Every one shall be bowed down.* [n: (ID.)] Allegorically: human speech seems reasonable, etc., *as far as* by that also, by which they had been framed, they shall be utterly crushed.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1238C]VERS. 18.-- *And the idols utterly.* ⟦s2⟧ This in the captivity of the Chaldeans, etc., *as far as* this is to be referred especially to the former sense.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BED.)] Solomon built a thing in Jerusalem, Astaroth the idol of the Sidonians, etc., where (if I am not mistaken) it is shown that Solomon never perfectly repented, because he did not destroy the idols which he had built.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *To adore moles.* [n: (HIER.)] Not in fact, but like moles, etc., *as far as* by which one comes to the truth.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Cease ye therefore.* ⟦s2⟧ Because these things shall come to pass unless you cease. ⟦s3⟧ Cease therefore, that you may not rage against Christ, who seems a mere man: because he breathes through his nostrils and lives like other men.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *High. ⟦s2⟧ The Lord is high above all nations. [1238D] And the Most High himself hath founded her [n: (Psal. CXII, LXXXIX)]*. ⟦s3⟧ Yet the Hebrew signifies *excelsum,* not *excelsus.* ⟦s4⟧ For he is height itself, not merely high.
