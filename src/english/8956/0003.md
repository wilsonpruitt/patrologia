## CHAPTER III.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *For behold.* ⟦s2⟧ Thus far they think it is spoken of the judgment, etc., *as far as* after all the gifts of grace were taken away from the Jews.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The valiant man.* [n: (HIER.)] There is not among the Jews one who wars for the law, one who judges according to it: for they are all empty and foolish.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And the diviner.* ⟦s2⟧ Because sometimes they divine true things, as Balaam, and the soothsayers of the five cities of the Palestinians, by whose counsel the ark of the Lord was sent back; as though he should say: [1239A] Both the true prophets and the false shall be taken away from the Jews.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The ancient.* ⟦s2⟧ Whom the LXX have rendered "presbyter," etc., *as far as* because he would not hear the presbyters.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The prudent man.* ⟦s2⟧ A teacher or a hearer, etc., *as far as* who might discern by what spirit the prophets were speaking.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Concerning architects.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ himself is called the Son of the carpenter: and in Paralipomenon we read of the valley of craftsmen. ⟦s3⟧ For there were craftsmen there whom knowledge did not puff up, but they followed the humble Christ.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And I will give. children.* [n: (HIER.)] Whereas the rest in the person, etc., *as far as* the Apostle forbids that a neophyte be chosen bishop, lest he grow proud like the devil.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The effeminate.* ⟦s2⟧ The LXX, "mockers," etc., *as far as* the holy state of the Church is thrown into confusion.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1239B]VERS. 6.-- *He shall take hold.* [n: (ID.)] To the letter, etc., *as far as* and let our ruin be propped up by thy help.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *He shall answer.* [n: (ID.)] This is not in the Hebrew, but is added from the LXX. ⟦s2⟧ For according to the Hebrew it is joined thus: *In that day he shall say:*

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I am no healer.* ⟦s2⟧ Let us not at once acquiesce in the judgment of the multitude, etc., *as far as: Seek not to be made a judge, lest perchance thou be not able to offer iniquities [n: (Eccl. VII)]*. [var: Vulg. Sir 7:6 *nisi valeas virtute irrumpere iniquitates*, "unless thou art strong enough to break through iniquities"]

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *For Jerusalem is ruined.* ⟦s2⟧ From the person of the Prophet. ⟦s3⟧ As if he should say: Therefore they shall not find one who is willing to come to their help, because they blasphemed the Lord, saying: *Thou hast a devil [n: (Joan. VIII)]*, and the like.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Because the tongue.* ⟦s2⟧ Although the Romans, so far as concerned themselves, came to the siege on account of the tribute that was being refused, yet the death of the Lord was chiefly the cause.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1239C]VERS. 9.-- *The acknowledging.* ⟦s2⟧ They shall acknowledge that this has befallen them because of their sins, or they shall always have their sins before the eyes of their mind.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Woe to their soul.* [n: (ID.)] The LXX: *Woe to their soul, for they have devised a most wicked counsel against themselves, saying: Let us bind the just man, for he is unprofitable to us [n: (Sap. I)]*.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Let us beware, then, lest we be exactors among the people; lest, after the impious Porphyry, matrons and women be our senate, who should bear rule in the Churches, and the favor of women pass judgment upon the priestly rank.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And the way of thy steps,* etc. [n: (ID.)] He, then, is the ecclesiastical teacher, etc., *as far as* of which: *I am the way, the truth, and the life [n: (Joan. XIV)]*.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *He standeth up to judge.* [n: (ID.)] Now as it were [1239D] angry, etc., *as far as* he comes against the princes and the presbyters that he may judge them, giving them room for defense.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *For you have eaten up.* [n: (ID.)] I judge that there was between the elders and the princes of the old people what there is between the presbyters and the bishops of our own time.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *My vineyard.* [n: (ID.)] Thou hast brought a vineyard out of Egypt. ⟦s2⟧ *The vineyard of the Lord Sabaoth is the house of Israel [n: (Psal. LXXIX)]*.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Why do you grind my people?* ⟦s2⟧ Our own princes too, etc., *as far as* they make them to be the riches of themselves or of their own.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Because they are lifted up.* ⟦s2⟧ This can be referred chiefly to the wives of the Scribes and Pharisees, and to others as well: for whose delicacies and luxury their husbands used to despoil the poor. ⟦s3⟧ Lest, then, they should think themselves [1240A] free from sin, the Lord says: *Because,* etc.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *He will make bald.* ⟦s2⟧ What is hidden shall lie open, and the foul baldness shall appear. ⟦s3⟧ For nothing is covered that shall not be revealed.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *In that day,* that is, of the Babylonian captivity, or more truly when they were taken captive by the Romans, and lost all their ornaments.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the shoes.* ⟦s2⟧ Which in the wilderness were not worn away, etc., *as far as* so that in place of matronly cleanness there may be a turning to the filth of the earth.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Rings.* ⟦s2⟧ By which we are sealed unto the malice [cj: *malitiam*; read *militiam*, "the warfare"] of Christ, whom the Father hath sealed. ⟦s3⟧ The prodigal Son too received a ring together with the robe, and shoes.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1240B] *Jewels on the forehead.* ⟦s2⟧ By which our faces and our head are adorned.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Linens.* [n: (HIER.)] That we may fully enjoy broad delights in the Lord.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *Fillets.* ⟦s2⟧ Which bind fast the mind that flows hither and thither.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Theristra.* ⟦s2⟧ A woman's garment, most fine, and transparent to the sight, suited to summer, and from that it is named: for theria is what summer is called.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *For a stomacher, haircloth.* ⟦s2⟧ By which the breasts are chastened, lest they grow rank.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *And they shall mourn and.* ⟦s2⟧ Whosoever shall have given his place to the devil, and shall not have kept his heart with all watchfulness, his gates shall mourn, and, the bridegroom being absent, he shall be always in grief, and falling down he shall sit [1240C] in the dust.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Her gates shall mourn, and she shall sit desolate on the ground.* ⟦s2⟧ Metonymically: the judges, the kings, the princes who used to sit in the gate.

## CHAPTER IV.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And seven shall take hold.* [n: (HIER.)] When Nabuchodonosor [took] Sedecias the king, etc., *as far as* in this place we can understand more of them.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Seven women.* [n: (ID.)] The seven gifts of the Holy Spirit: *The spirit of wisdom and of understanding, the spirit of counsel and of fortitude, the spirit of knowledge and of godliness, and the Spirit of the fear of the Lord shall fill him.* ⟦s2⟧ Which are called women, because no one is begotten unto God save through the Spirit: and in the plural, because the gifts are many, though the Spirit is one.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1240D] *Our bread.* ⟦s2⟧ Our bread is Christ, the rod from the root of Jesse, and the flower; the bread of the women is the Father, as he is also of Christ, who says: *My food is to do the will of my Father [n: (Joan. IV)]* [var: Vulg. Jo 4:34 *voluntatem ejus qui misit me*, "the will of him that sent me"]. ⟦s3⟧ *We will eat our own bread,* the body of Christ.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *We will be covered with our own garments.* ⟦s2⟧ With the ornaments of the virtues.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Let thy name be called upon.* ⟦s2⟧ Let us be called Christians from Christ.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Take away our reproach.* ⟦s2⟧ Which we suffer from the Jews as though we were without God, etc., *as far as* we are often compelled to change our lodging.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Take away.* ⟦s2⟧ Because false doctrines shall rise up against wisdom, and against the rest of the women, that is, the virtues: to each one its own contrary rises up, when false virtues steal away their name.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1241A]VERS. 5.-- *A cloud,* that is, the overshadowing of the Spirit, that in prosperity they may not be lifted up, just as for the children of Israel there was a cloud by day, a fire by night.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And smoke,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Whence: *The house was filled with smoke [n: (Exod. XIII)]*; and elsewhere: *He toucheth the mountains, and they smoke [n: (Psal. CIII)]*.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For over all.* ⟦s2⟧ Very many of the Jews, etc., *as far as* but we to the first coming of Christ.
