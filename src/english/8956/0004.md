## CHAPTER V.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *I will sing to my beloved,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] A canticle is sometimes in gladness, sometimes in sorrow, as this one is, namely the one which he composed who, seeing the city, wept, saying: *For if thou also hadst known,* etc. [n: (Luc. XIX)].

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *A chosen vineyard.* [n: (ID.)] Another edition, the vineyard [1241B] Sorec, which is a kind of the best vine, most fruitful with unfailing fruits; all the things that are said of the vineyard are referred to the state of the soul, which, planted by God, brought forth not grapes but wild grapes: and it was handed over to the beasts to be trodden down, nor did it receive the rain of doctrine, because it despised the gifts that had gone before.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Now therefore, O inhabitants,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ In like manner Nathan questions David, that he may pronounce sentence upon himself as though upon another.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Is it that I looked for,* etc.? ⟦s2⟧ This, according to some, he said above within himself; but here he asks others what he had been thinking.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *And now I will show you.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: Because you will not answer, therefore I will answer for you.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1241C] *I will take away,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That is, that they who have not felt God through benefits may feel him through punishments; and therefore he threatens a penalty, that they may be turned to repentance.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And the clouds.* [n: (ID.)] Which under Elias, etc., *as far as* when they had not the prophets, and drove the apostles away.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *The house of Israel,* etc. [n: (ID.)] From the time the kingdom was divided, etc., *as far as* because Christ was born of them.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Behold a cry.* ⟦s2⟧ Wherewith it cried out against the Lord and shouted, saying: Away with him, away with him, crucify him; or, they made a cry in place of justice, because they slew Christ, whose blood cries to the Lord: *The voice of the blood [sic: sanguis] of thy brother Abel crieth to me from the earth [n: (Gen. IV)]*.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1241D]VERS. 8.-- *Woe to you that join.* ⟦s2⟧ This especially to the Jews, who were most avaricious, etc., *as far as* until the earth fail, that is, human sense and speech.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *In the ears.* [n: (ID.)] As if the prophet should say: These words I have heard with my inward ears, which are of the Lord of hosts.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Unless the houses,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The pomp, that is, and the structure of heretics, *as far as* which is bound together by unity in comparison with a manifold number.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *For ten acres.* [n: (ID.)] The barrenness of the land, which is to follow upon the captivity, is shown.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Woe to you that rise up.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: Captivity is at hand, etc., *as far as* they do not understand the works of the Lord in themselves.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1242A]VERS. 17.-- *And the lambs shall feed.* [n: (ID.)] According to the history it is the same as above: strangers devour your country before your face.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Strangers: The kingdom of God shall be taken away from you,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ For gathered out of the whole world, etc., *as far as* so that the lambs should take the places of the bulls.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Woe to you that draw.* ⟦s2⟧ These things to the princes of the Jews, etc., *as far as* they anathematize the name of Christians.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With cords of vanity.* [n: (ID.)] In which they were, etc., *as far as* we are held as though by the strongest chains.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Woe to you that call.* [n: (ID.)] This especially against the Jews who chose Barabbas and condemned Christ; generally, to all who justify the unjust and condemn the just. ⟦s2⟧ *Evil, darkness, [1242B] bitter,* are set down in a like signification and figure. ⟦s3⟧ In Barabbas we understand the devil, of whom: *Deliver us from evil.*

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Woe to you that are wise.* ⟦s2⟧ The wisdom of God being despised, etc., *as far as* they neither enter in unto Christ, nor permit [others] to enter.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Drunkenness.* ⟦s2⟧ Wine contains every vice. ⟦s3⟧ Drunkenness (which in Hebrew is *sicera* ) signifies all the things that overturn the state of the mind. ⟦s4⟧ Out of the abundance of bread and wine the sin of the men of Sodom grew.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *You that justify the wicked for gifts.* ⟦s2⟧ It was said above that instead of grapes they brought forth thorns, and hay, and wood, and stubble, and briars that were to be burned, because they did not only these things, but also cast away the law [1242C] and blasphemed the word of God. ⟦s3⟧ Of which: *Out of Sion shall go forth the law, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.*

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *Their root.* [n: (ID.)] A crooked thought, out of which the work proceeds like a bud.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Therefore was he angry.* [n: (ID.)] It is to be noted that he does not reproach them with idolatry and the other sins, but with this, that they cast away the law of the Gospel and blasphemed the holy word of the Lord: who had been angry against the princes and the mighty, who were wise in their own eyes and followers after gifts; whom the tongue of fire devoured, that the mighty might mightily suffer torments.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He was angry.* [n: (ID.)] Not that he lies subject to human passions; but we who transgress, unless we have heard the Lord being angry, do not fear.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1242D] *And he struck,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He speaks things to come as though past, with a prophetic certainty.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *And he shall whistle to him from the ends of the earth,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The Hebrews understand this place of the Babylonians, etc., *as far as* he joins himself to the people in affection, and says:

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *We shall look.* ⟦s2⟧ Not daring to look back upon heaven, whose inhabitant, God, we have offended; and our light, which we always had in God, is darkened with the gloom of tribulations.
