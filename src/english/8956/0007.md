## CHAPTER IX.

VERS. 1.-- *In the first time,* [n: (HIER.)] These two tribes were led into captivity by the Assyrians, etc., *as far as* it first saw the light of Christ preaching.

*The land of Zabulon.* [n: (ID.)] In which Christ first preached and wrought miracles: for there he turned water into wine, and chose the apostles.

*The way of the sea.* The sea in this place is the lake of Genezareth, which is made by the inflowing of the Jordan. Upon whose shore is Capharnaum, [1247D] Tiberias, Bethsaida and Corozain, in which the Lord chiefly abode; and so: *The people that walked in darkness saw a great light.*

*Of the Galilee of the Gentiles.* There are two Galilees: one of the Gentiles, near to the Tyrians in the tribe of Nephtali; the other about Tiberias and the pool of Genesareth, in the tribe of Zabulon.

VERS. 2.-- *In the region of the shadow of death.* [n: (ID.)] They are in the shadow of death who still live in sins: *in the region of the shadow of death,* they who have already gone down to the lower regions with dead works.

VERS. 3.-- *Thou hast not magnified the joy.* To us, concerning the salvation of the Jews; for although they rejoice over the conversion of the Gentiles, yet they grieve over the rejection of the Jews.

[1248A] *They shall rejoice before thee,* etc. The prophet to Christ, concerning the disciples.

VERS. 4.-- *For the yoke.* [n: (ID.)] Christ has a yoke, but a sweet one; the devil also, but one unbearable by its burden. And Christ has the princedom upon his shoulder, upon which he bore the cross; and the devil upon his shoulder a rod, wherewith he is proud.

*Of the exactor.* Of him, namely, that exacts. For daily he exacts from posterity, with usury, the coin of sin which he committed to the first man.

VERS. 5.-- *For every violent plundering.* [n: (ID.)] As if he should say: The human race which the devil has plundered, the Lord shall take away from him violently, that is, powerfully, and shall thrust him down with tumult into hell. Whence both of him and of his members it is said: [1248B] *Their memory hath perished with a noise [n: (Psal. IX)]*.

*The garment mingled.* [n: (ID.)] The devil, who is full of the blood of all, who is a murderer from the beginning; or men, with whom the devil is clothed about as with a garment, whose hands are full of blood.

VERS. 6.-- *A child is born to us.* Not to the Jews, to us, etc., *as far as* but to them that see not, he seems not to rise. [var: Vulg. Is 9:6 *Parvulus enim natus est nobis*, "For a little one is born to us"]

*The princedom upon his shoulder.* [n: (ID.)] While he was being led to the gibbet, he bore the cross, upon which he merited the princedom.

[n: (ID.)] The Seventy: *The angel of great counsel.* Which is not had in the Hebrew; but, terrified by the majesty of the names, they did not dare to say of the child that he was openly to be called God. Therefore for these six names [1248C] they put this, *the angel of great counsel.*

VERS. 7.-- *The zeal of the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] The prophecy began from Emmanuel, where it is said: *Ask thee a sign from God.* Here it is ended.

VERS. 8.-- *The Lord sent a word.* [n: (ID.)] The Hebrew word *Dabar,* etc., *as far as* they despise the Church, and think her simplicity to be unskilfulness.

VERS. 10.-- *The bricks are fallen.* [n: (ID.)] They said above, etc., *as far as* we will fortify the people out of strong and warlike princes.

*The cedars.* That is, we will set over them unconquerable princes out of ourselves.

## CHAPTER X.

[1248D]VERS. 1.-- *Woe to them that make.* [n: (HIER.)] Very many think that these things are still said against the ten tribes, etc., *as far as* when the day of the captivity, or of the judgment, shall have come.

VERS. 5.-- *Woe to Assur.* [n: (ID.)] Thus far against the ten and the two tribes, etc., *as far as* and he not only led captive, but in part destroyed.

VERS. 7.-- *But he.* [n: (ID.)] So the philosophers, so the heretics, and so the persecutors of the Christian name: when God permits some to be scourged by them, they rage against all, and desire not to scourge but to kill; and when a few are overcome they take boldness against all.

VERS. 9.-- *Are not my princes together.* The order is: My princes are kings, that is, serving me: [1249A] for he brought many kings with him, whom he had subjugated.

*Damascus.* Damascus is the metropolis in Syria: Eliezer, the servant of Abraham, founded it; in it Rasin reigned.

VERS. 10.-- *As my hand hath found.* The voice of Sennacherib marvelling at his own power. He fulfilled fully the things he had said. For he plundered all the cities of the ten tribes, and of Juda and Benjamin, except Jerusalem, and the lower part of it, which Sobna the scribe delivered to him.

VERS. 12.-- *When the Lord shall have accomplished.* The overthrows, namely of the city, and also the siege, when Rabsaces came and did the things which are recounted in what follows.

[1249B]VERS. 14.-- *That should yelp.* To yelp belongs to foxes, or, as others say, to birds.

VERS. 16.-- *And under his glory.* Jerome says, according to the Hebrews, that the bodies of the slain Assyrians were burnt up by heavenly fire and reduced to dust, their garments unhurt; and that the Jews, coming and seizing the top of the garments, shook out the dust.

[n: (HIER.)] Some will have it that this king signifies the contrary fortitude, etc., *as far as* for the Hebrews hand down that only ten of his army remained.

VERS. 17.-- *And there shall be a light.* The Hebrews say that it was Michael the archangel, to whom the custody of that nation was committed, as Daniel says.

[1249C]VERS. 18.-- *And the glory of his forest.* Carmel is a great mountain, fruitful in trees. As it were an innumerable army (like the timber of the woods), it shall perish both in body and in soul.

*Unto the flesh.* [n: (ID.)] The heretics also, when the ecclesiastical doctrine has shone forth and their snare has been laid open, shall come to so great a solitude that, out of a forest and unfruitful trees and an innumerable multitude, scarcely a few shall be left who follow their error.

VERS. 20.-- *And it shall be in that day.* [n: (HIER.)] When Phacee and Rasin were minded to come, etc., *as far as* but let them put their hope in God.

VERS. 21.-- *The remnant shall be converted.* According to Josephus, an innumerable multitude of the Jews being slain, [1249D] we understand that a few were saved in the apostles and in apostolic men.

VERS. 22.-- *The consumption abridged.* The perfecting of the things which had been foretold of Christ so long a time before, completed in three years and a half.

*Justice,* because, being unjustly slain, he justly overcame death and the devil, and redeemed the world by his death.

VERS. 23.-- *For the consummation.* [n: (ID.)] To the letter. He did it, because so great a multitude, except ten, he slew in the short space of one night.

VERS. 24.-- *Upon thee in the way of Egypt.* Of the captivity of the ten tribes, etc., *as far as* he took and overthrew.

VERS. 25.-- *A very little and a moment.* This was fulfilled a long time after; but all ages [1250A] compared to eternity (in which all things are present) are momentary.

VERS. 26.-- *His rod into the sea.* [n: (ID.)] As if: Before he come upon thee, he shall set upon Tharaca, king of Ethiopia, and shall cross the Red Sea, and shall lift up his rod over Egypt, and so with a great army shall return against Jerusalem. [var: Vulg. Is 10:26 *virgam suam super mare*, "his rod over the sea"]

VERS. 29.-- *They have passed over in haste. Gaba the seat,* etc. The past for the future, after the prophetic manner.

VERS. 31, 32.-- *Be strengthened; it is yet day.* [n: (ID.)] After Sennacherib had pitched his tents in Nobe, wishing to storm Jerusalem in the morning, he ceased not until the middle of the night to threaten the desolation of Jerusalem, saying: Behold, shall this city alone be able to be delivered out of my hand?

[1250B]VERS. 33.-- *Behold the sovereign Lord of hosts,* etc. He said the name of the child that was to be born of a virgin, etc., *as far as* and let Libanus with its cedars be cut down.
