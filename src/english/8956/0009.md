## CHAPTER XIV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Jacob.* ⟦s2⟧ Note the propriety of the words. ⟦s3⟧ Jacob, who is still in the wrestling, he has mercy on: Israel, who received the name after the victory, he chose.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *How,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Marvelling how the waster of lands, etc., *as far as* but the last enemy, death, shall be destroyed.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The exactor.* [n: (HIER.)] Who exacts to the last [1253B] farthing from his debtors, who say that word: *Forgive us our debts [n: (Matth. V)]*. ⟦s2⟧ To him shall the debtors be delivered; to him Paul delivered the fornicator of Corinth.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *The staff.* ⟦s2⟧ He calls Nabuchodonosor, or the devil, because as through the one the impious are scourged by God, so through the other in the punishments of hell, or daily by suggesting sin, by lying in wait.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The fir trees also.* ⟦s2⟧ The princes of divers nations afflicted by him; or the sons whose fathers he slew.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Since thou hast slept.* ⟦s2+3⟧ Because, the strong man being overcome and his vessels plundered, his satellites fell down; whence: *Go into everlasting fire, which was prepared for the devil and his angels (Matth. [1253C] XXV)*. [var: Vulg. Mt 25:41 *Discedite a me maledicti in ignem aeternum*, "Depart from me, you cursed, into everlasting fire"] Note that the death of death is called a sleeping.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Who should cut down.* ⟦s2⟧ Whence the psalm: *The shadow of it covered the mountains, and its branches the cedars of God [n: (Psal. LXXIX.)]* These, sinning at some time, the Lord delivers over to punishment to be bruised: whence it is said: *The Lord shall crush the cedars of Libanus [n: (Psal. XXVIII)]*. [var: Vulg. Ps 28:5 *confringet Dominus cedros Libani*, "the Lord shall break in pieces the cedars of Libanus"]

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Hell.* ⟦s2⟧ The inhabitants or the princes of that hell beneath thee. ⟦s3⟧ For hell is under the earth, in which that rich man was seen by Lazarus; whence the Lord drew out his own bound ones, and left the devil bound.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *All the princes.* ⟦s2⟧ Even though these things were not done, yet they could have been done. ⟦s3⟧ It is a solace of the wicked to have their enemies as companions of their punishments.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Thou art wounded.* ⟦s2⟧ Thou who didst seem immortal and God. ⟦s3⟧ We, by reason of the weakness of our senses, thought [1253D] that it was not possible to resist the power of God, and that thou alone didst remain in thy height.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Beneath.* [n: (ID.)] Rottenness, the moth and the covering of worms, etc., *as far as* who said to Christ: *All these will I give thee, if falling down thou wilt adore me [n: (Matth. IV)]*.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Who didst rise in the morning.* [n: (HIER.)] These are the false apostles, etc., *as far as* and in the sides of the north, in the cold places.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *I will ascend into heaven.* [n: (ID.)] He was in the firmament; he desired to ascend into heaven, where is the throne of the Lord. ⟦s2⟧ All these things are to be referred to the heretics, who, though they be below with their prince, boast themselves to be on high.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1254A] *In the sides of the north.* ⟦s2⟧ Whence: From the north evils break forth, and the pot of Jeremias is kindled. ⟦s3⟧ Of which: *I will say to the north: Give up:* that is, restore the captives.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the clouds.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, of the prophets, of whom: *I will command my clouds that they rain no rain upon it [n: (Psal. XXXV)]*. ⟦s3⟧ *And thy truth even unto the clouds.* ⟦s4⟧ Judas was a cloud with the other apostles, but by his own fault he received the devil for his rider.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *But yet thou shalt be drawn down to hell.* ⟦s2⟧ Thou who couldst have ascended by virtues shalt fall by vices. ⟦s3⟧ The saints have the wings of a dove and of an eagle, that they may fly and rest: the wicked are sunk as lead *in the mighty waters, and go down into the deep as [sic: quas] a stone [n: (Zach. V)]*. ⟦s4⟧ Iniquity sits upon a talent of lead.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1254B] *Thou shalt be drawn down.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ unwilling; he came down willingly.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The deep.* ⟦s2⟧ To the lowest and worst punishments. ⟦s3⟧ For the higher the step, the heavier the fall.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the pit.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, of hell, etc., *as far as* but through lukewarmness and the pretence of faith they are rejected by the Lord.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Is this the man.* [n: (ID.)] Not God; whence: *An enemy man hath oversown cockle [n: (Matth. XIII)]*.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *The cities.* [n: (ID.)] The churches, that he might make them synagogues of the devil, and might pollute the cleanness of the faith with heretical perversity.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To his prisoners not.* ⟦s2⟧ Whom the apostles loose, imitators of their master, etc., *as far as* but he shut them up in darkness, so that he did not open even to Evilmerodach his son.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *All the kings.* [n: (ID.)] The kings of the nations, [1254C] etc., *as far as* nor does he rest in death from torments.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *But thou.* ⟦s2⟧ He seems to agree with the fable, etc., *as far as* thy father shall live again.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As a carcass.* [n: (ID.)] By the multitude of sins *my sores are putrefied and corrupted [n: (Psal. XXXVII)]*. ⟦s2⟧ Virtue is of a sweet odour.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Fellowship.* ⟦s2⟧ Of burial, namely. ⟦s3⟧ For one is the punishment of the author, another of him who was compelled by the authority: This is the sword wherewith very many were wounded and slain.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For thou the land.* ⟦s2⟧ As if: In thy pride the kingdom of the Chaldeans has been destroyed, which would still have remained, unless thy pride had not been.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1254D] *The people.* ⟦s2⟧ Which God had committed to thee to be corrected and ruled.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thou hast slain.* ⟦s2⟧ Not reserving the living for God, but preparing companions for thy own punishment.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He shall not be called.* ⟦s2⟧ Therefore he shall not be called, for ever, etc., *as far as* all the offspring is destroyed, for the pride and impiety of the father.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 21.-- *For the iniquity:* [n: (ID.)] The Seventy: For the iniquity of thy father. ⟦s3⟧ Which some not understanding, the Hebrews will have the dragon, who reigns in the sea, whom they call Leviathan, to be the father of the devil; according to that: *Because he is a liar and his father [n: (Joan. VIII)]*, which by understanding ill they deprave.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *And I will make.* ⟦s2⟧ There is nothing in it but the royal [1255A] hunting, and it is shut in with brick walls repaired after very many years.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For a possession for the hedgehog.* ⟦s2⟧ The world also is possessed now by the hedgehog and by marshes, that is, by unclean spirits; but at the end of the world the Lord shall sweep it, that is, shall purge it with the fire of purgatory.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *The Lord hath sworn.* ⟦s2⟧ Thus far of Babylon and of the kings, etc., *as far as* and this in the land of Juda and in his mountains, as the Lord had foretold.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *In the mountains.* ⟦s2⟧ Note the propriety, etc., *as far as* but also those who have advanced in virtues.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *This counsel,* that is, let his yoke be taken away, etc., *as far as* to pertain generally to the whole world.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1255B]VERS. 28.-- *In the year in which he died.* ⟦s2⟧ From the beginning of the book up to this place Isaias prophesied under Ozias, Joatham, Achaz; up to the end, under Ezechias, the things foretold and in part fulfilled.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Achaz.* ⟦s2⟧ While he was living, reigning among sinners: neither the burden against the aliens, nor the word of God (according to the Septuagint) could come to the Prophet.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *Rejoice not.* ⟦s2⟧ The king being dead, his enemies rejoice, as though they were to make some gain out of the new state of things: hence the Philistines, whom Achaz afflicted, rejoice at his death and insult the Jews, because, the ripened king being lost, they are subject to his son, in whose reign they think that they can invade their borders.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The rod of the striker.* [n: (HIER.)] The yoke of God being cast off, etc., *as far as* and out of them flying serpents, or such as should swallow up [1255C] birds.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Howl.* [n: (ID.)] As if: O Philistines, bewail your misery, which comes through Sennacherib.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Howl, O gate; cry, O city.* ⟦s2⟧ The Philistines falling by a draught, etc., *as far as* a soul filled with evil thoughts.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For from the north a smoke.* ⟦s2⟧ The arrows of the devil blazing up, who is hurtful to the eyes and contrary to the light. ⟦s3⟧ From the north the pot of Jeremias is kindled.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *There is none that escapeth his troop.* ⟦s2⟧ None but Jerusalem, etc., *as far as* and that Jerusalem alone is to be delivered.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *To the messengers of the nation.* ⟦s2⟧ To the angels who are set over cities and nations: each one marvelling why Sion alone has avoided, or escaped, the bitterness of the smoke.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1255D] *He hath founded Sion.* ⟦s2⟧ Upon the foundation of prudence, justice, fortitude, temperance, which are Christ.
