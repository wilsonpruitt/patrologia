## CHAPTER XVII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The burden of Damascus. ⟦s2⟧ Behold Damascus.* [n: (ID.)] Damascus, the metropolis of Syria, etc., *as far as* but because they did not do penance they were led captive.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Aroer for the flocks.* ⟦s2⟧ The tamarisk, which is a fruitless tree, springing up in deserts and in rocky places, as if he should say: All the Syrians shall be fruitless and desolate. ⟦s3⟧ Others say that from this tree hatreds are stirred up by evil arts.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *And the kingdom.* ⟦s2⟧ That sin may not reign in her who before loved blood.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The remnant of Syria.* ⟦s2⟧ Ironically, for a disgrace, etc., [1258D] *as far as* both therefore were led captive in disgrace.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And it shall be in that day.* [n: (ID.)] When Syria shall have been laid waste by the Assyrians, etc., *as far as* others were carried over into Cyrene, which is a region beyond Egypt.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *It shall wither away.* [n: (ID.)] They shall not have prophets, nor signs, nor powers, nor kingdom, nor priesthood; but the whole body of the nation shall wither.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *In the harvest.* ⟦s2⟧ Of the nations it is said: *The harvest indeed is great, the labourers, however, few [n: (Matth. IX)]*. ⟦s3⟧ But those poor men gather the leavings of the harvest, which have been saved by the apostles, and the very rare ears. ⟦s4⟧ Nor on the mountains, but in the valley of Raphaim, that is, in humility, in the truth of the letter. ⟦s5⟧ For Raphaim sounds giants.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *As the shaking.* [n: (ID.)] When the people [1259A] of the Jews had been struck down, the olive tree, which under Moses had had six hundred thousand armed men, and under David, when Joab numbered them, a people beyond counting, offered scarcely a few fruits to the Saviour.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of two or three.* ⟦s2⟧ Of Paul and Barnabas. ⟦s3⟧ And of three, Peter, and James and John, who beheld the Lord transfigured on the mountain, and saw the daughter of the ruler of the synagogue raised up.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Or four.* [n: (HIER.)] These are the remaining nine, among whom Matthias obtained the place of Judas the traitor. ⟦s2⟧ These, unknown to us by reason of the diversity of graces, were parted into four or into five, that they might show forth in themselves the number of the Gospels and the volumes of the law, as preachers of either Testament.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *In that day he shall bow down.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: [1259B] The Lord hungering, scarcely a few, etc., *as far as* as is read in Paralipomen., and they cast away the idols.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *In that day they shall be.* ⟦s2⟧ When the Assyrians rushed in, and laid waste not only Damascus, but also the land of the ten tribes.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Ploughs.* [n: (ID.)] Of the Amorrhites. ⟦s2⟧ This against the ten tribes, as if he should say: As of old, when the children of Israel came, the Amorrhites fled and left their ploughs, so now the ten tribes, when the Assyrians come.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *A faithful planting.* ⟦s2⟧ A planting is faithful when one plants and tills, and gathers the fruit. ⟦s3⟧ The planting is unfaithful, and the seed a stranger's, when a stranger gathers: *Every planting which my Father hath not planted shall be rooted up [n: (Matth. XV)]*. [var: Vulg. Mt 15:13 *Pater meus caelestis*, "my heavenly Father"]

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A stranger's seed.* ⟦s2⟧ Not receiving the Father, because thou dost not receive [1259C] the Son; for he who believes in the Father, believes in the Son. ⟦s3⟧ A stranger's seed, of blasphemy against Christ.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *In the morning thy seed.* [n: (ID.)] When, namely, the shoot shall have been seen, etc., *as far as* that all the saints are saved with the branches of the wild olive, the root.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Woe to the multitude.* ⟦s2⟧ Sennacherib and his army, etc., *as far as* desiring to crush and to seize upon all things.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *He shall be caught away.* ⟦s2⟧ He was caught away from Jerusalem, when he fled with a few into Ninive. ⟦s3⟧ From the world he was caught away, when he was quickly slain by his own sons, as is plain.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *This is the portion of them that have laid waste.* ⟦s2⟧ The prophet in the person of Israel, as if he should say: Thus is [1259D] God wont to bless our enemies.

## CHAPTER XVIII.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Woe to the land.* [n: (ID.)] Because he had spoken of the rest of the persecutors of the Church, etc., *as far as* as though by straying he had done useless things.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ *Woe to the land of the cymbal of wings.* [var: Vulg. Is 18:1 *cymbalo alarum*, ablative] He turns again to Egypt, and calls it, etc., *as far as* they took away from them the fear of the Lord.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *In vessels of papyrus,* that is, in ships made of papyrus, etc., *as far as* it passes by and slips away.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *A nation waiting and trodden underfoot.* ⟦s2⟧ For all heretics promise themselves great things, etc., *as far as* that is, they hear their doctrine.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1260A]VERS. 4.-- *A cloud of dew.* ⟦s2⟧ Welcome to the wearied reapers, to whom it affords refreshment, and by moistening makes the dry stubble fit for cutting: so my word against Egypt shall be welcome to all that believe in me, and to the Assyrians the victory given them by me.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Little branches.* [n: (ID.)] Useless: whence: *I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman: every branch in me not bearing fruit he will take it away, and every one that beareth fruit he will purge it.*

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And all the beasts of the earth.* ⟦s2⟧ Hyperbolically. ⟦s3⟧ For not all the beasts of the earth shall winter upon him. ⟦s4⟧ But he signifies both a slaughter beyond measure, and a very great multitude of beasts about to come upon them, for whom the corpses shall suffice for food in summer and in winter.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1260B]VERS. 7.-- *In the time.* ⟦s2⟧ The Prophet returns to the people of Israel, and promises them good things, saying: *In the time,* as if he should say: When Egypt has been laid waste, Juda shall hope no more in its shadow; but he would offer to the Lord the gift of faith and of good works. ⟦s3⟧ Which was fulfilled in the seventieth year after the captivity, under Zorobabel and the other leaders.
