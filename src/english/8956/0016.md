## CHAPTER XXVI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *In that day,* etc. [n: (ID.)] This may be referred both to the present Church and to the day of judgment. ⟦s2⟧ Now the Church sings a new song: the nativity of Christ, his passion, his resurrection, his ascension. ⟦s3⟧ For it is old that the old man should go down to the lower regions; new, that he should ascend to heaven.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The city of strength.* ⟦s2⟧ The voice of the just, both now and in the [1269C] time to come.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Open ye the gates.* ⟦s2⟧ This song suddenly changes the persons.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The gates.* ⟦s2⟧ Of justice; whence: *Open to me the gates of justice.* ⟦s3⟧ The gates of life and of justice, etc., *as far as* and through many gates to him who says: *No man cometh to the Father but by me [n: (Joan. XIV)]*.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Peace.* [n: (ID.)] The repetition is a confirmation. ⟦s2⟧ He has this of whom it is said: *Man, man of the children of Israel [n: (Ezech. XIV)]*; [var: Vulg. Ezech. 14:4 *Homo, homo de domo Israel*, "man, man of the house of Israel"] and elsewhere: *A man, a man whose wife shall have defiled the bed [n: (Num. V)]*, [var: Vulg. Num. 5:12 *Vir cujus uxor erraverit*, "a man whose wife shall have gone astray," without the doubled *Vir*] that a twofold man and a twofold husband may obtain a twofold peace.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The foot shall tread it down,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because *Every one that exalteth himself shall be humbled [n: (Luc. I)]*. ⟦s3⟧ We may refer these things also to the contrary powers.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1269D] *Of the needy.* [n: (HIER.)] Of the apostles, whom the Jews drove out of their borders, who, imitating the poverty of the Lord, obtained the privilege of his virtue.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *My soul hath desired,* etc. ⟦s2+3⟧ Not the flesh; whence: *My soul hath thirsted after God, the living fountain [n: (Psal. XLI)]*. [var: Vulg. Ps 41:3 *ad Deum fortem, vivum*, "after God, the strong, the living"] *The flesh lusteth against the spirit [n: (Gal. V)]*.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Let us have mercy.* [n: (ID.)] The Lord to the prophet: Shall we have mercy on the wicked man, so that he may learn, namely my clemency, while he himself is saved? as if to say, this is better.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And the just man shall not learn,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: How shall he be able to know thy justice, having experienced thy clemency?

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And he shall not see.* ⟦s2⟧ The Lord to the prophet, as though it were better [1270A] that he should see, and blush for his own evils. ⟦s3⟧ *And he shall not see,* as if he should say: it suffices for his punishment, that he shall not see me reigning in heaven with my saints. ⟦s4⟧ The Septuagint: *The wicked man shall be taken away, that he may not see the brightness of God.*

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Let them be confounded that are envious.* ⟦s2⟧ The Septuagint: Zeal shall lay hold on the untaught people.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Lord, thou wilt give.* [n: (ID.)] It is to be noted that after he has rendered our works to us, he is also about to give peace, and that they therefore ask for peace because he has wrought in them. ⟦s2⟧ They say that they, namely, have borne torments, that afterwards they may obtain mercy.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Lord, they have possessed.* [n: (ID.)] The demons, whom we worshipped in idols. ⟦s2⟧ The Septuagint: *Lord, apart from thee we know no other,* not absolutely *another,* but *apart from thee:* for the Father is in the Son, and the Son in the Father.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1270B] *Only in thee let us remember.* [n: (ID.)] We ask nothing else, save that after our errors we may be worthy to remember thy name.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thou hast destroyed all.* [n: (ID.)] The Septuagint: Their male, etc., *as far as* he interpreted the memory to be the male, against that word: *Be not deceived, God is not mocked.*

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Hast thou.* ⟦s2⟧ He did not do penance, and this is: *Hast thou,* etc., as if he should say: Security begets negligence, negligence contempt.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *And we have brought forth.* ⟦s2⟧ Others punctuate thus: *And we have brought forth the spirit, we have not wrought salvation in the earth.* [var: Vulg. Is 26:18 *Salutes non fecimus*, plural, "we have not wrought deliverances"]

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Spirit: salvation,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That we may believe with the whole mind, and that we may learn through torments through him by whom we have not felt benefits.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *The earth shall disclose.* [n: (ID.)] Then earthly men [1270C] shall not be able to hide those whom they killed, because then all things are open; or, the earth which received the blood of the martyrs and covered it, shall then be disclosed when they rise again.

## CHAPTER XXVII.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *In that day,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The Hebrews hand down that the devil, etc., *as far as* that the sword may be brought upon him.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The bar.* [n: (HIER.)] The Septuagint: The fugitive. ⟦s2⟧ For then he shall flee, etc., *as far as* but the serpent, because he pours in his snares softly.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The whale.* ⟦s2⟧ The Hebrews, *Tanniu;* whence the Hebrews used to tell in fables that Leviathan dwelt under the earth and in the air, but Tanniu in the sea.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The vineyard of pure wine shall sing.* ⟦s2⟧ To which Jeremias offers a cup of the purest wine. ⟦s3⟧ Merum in Greek, purum in Latin.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1270D]VERS. 5.-- *Or rather?* ⟦s2⟧ The voice of the Father deliberating, etc., *as far as* who are not saved by the authority of the law.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Israel,* that is the people of the Jews converted, seeing that the world has flourished by the teaching of their sons.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Therefore.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: *Therefore shall the iniquity be forgiven to the house of Jacob:* and the rest, on account, namely, of the merits of the apostles, who throughout the world broke to pieces the altars of the idols.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *They shall be broken.* [n: (ID.)] As Delbora went to war and judged the people to the shame of the Jews; and when the captivity of the Chaldeans was at hand, Olda prophesied while the men were silent: so, when the captivity of the Romans was at hand, women perhaps came and taught them.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1271A] *Women.* ⟦s2⟧ The diverse Synagogues; or plainly women, who with bared breasts strike their bleeding arms.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Teaching her.* [n: (HIER.)] It is a great unhappiness for a people, and a reproach, to learn songs of lamentation from women.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *From the channel.* ⟦s2⟧ The Septuagint: from the Euphrates, etc., *as far as* because it has turbid waters, and not perpetual.

## CHAPTER XXVIII.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Woe to the crown of pride.* [n: (ID.)] Up to this point unto the consummation of the world, etc., *as far as* nor had he the fruit of penance unto salvation.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the most fat valley.* ⟦s2⟧ Gethsemani, by its fruitfulness of produce; through it lay their ascent into the city, and there some pitched tents, for sport's sake, while they tarried in the city.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1271B]VERS. 5.-- *In that day.* [n: (ID.)] According to the prophecy, when the Lord, etc., *as far as* for the Scribes and Pharisees were drunk with wine and strong drink.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Strength to them that return.* [n: (ID.)] The wars of Ezechias are not read of, but the Lord waged war for him, a hundred and eighty-five thousand of the Assyrians being slain, whose spoils the Jews gathered: and they returned with joy to the gates of Jerusalem.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *For all.* ⟦s2⟧ While they do not digest the food of the Scriptures, nor make it life-giving to the body, but cast it out unripe and stinking, so that the Lord finds no place in them.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Them that are weaned from the milk, that are drawn away.* ⟦s2⟧ Not others, because they cannot bear it. ⟦s3⟧ For these are they who are now able to use solid food; to others the Apostle says: *I gave you [1271C] milk to drink, not meat [n: (I Cor. III)]*.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 10.-- *For command, command again, command, command again; expect, expect again, expect, expect again, a little there, a little there.* [n: (ID.)] As if he should say: You cannot be taught, because, mocking the prophets (through whom the Lord foretells to you the evils that are to come upon you, that you may do penance and be delivered), you object, *command, command again;* as if to say: O Prophet, enjoin what thou wilt, threaten what thou wilt; yet these things which thou threatenest do not come to pass.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *For in the speech.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: You say these things to your own hurt, because the Lord shall no longer foretell evils to you, but shall bring them at once. ⟦s3⟧ And this is: *For in the speech of the lip and with another tongue they shall speak to this people,* [var: Vulg. Is 28:11 *loquetur*, singular, "shall he speak"] etc.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1271D]VERS. 16.-- *A stone. ⟦s2⟧ The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner.*

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Vexation alone shall give understanding to the hearing.*

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *For as.* ⟦s2⟧ David, coming upon the Philistines gathered together by the help of the Lord, divided the living from the dead and scattered the perverse places; whence that place received the name Baalphrasim.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A strange work.* [n: (ID.)] The proper work of God is to have mercy, to spare, to pardon. ⟦s2⟧ Not his own, but strange, is it to be angry, to punish.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (GREG.)] He shall come into the world, that he may do his own work, namely that he may redeem the human race; but that work of his is alien to him: for it does not befit the Godhead to be spat upon, to be scourged, to be crucified.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *Shall all the day.* [n: (HIER.)] God, kind and merciful, [1272A] ploughs the human race like a husbandman with the ploughshare of preaching, that it may bring forth the fruit of good work; he breaks it up with the lash of tribulation, and corrects it now with pestilence, now with captivity; and this is: *Shall all the day,* etc.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Gith and cummin.* ⟦s2⟧ Gith and cummin, which are beaten out with a rod, etc., *as far as* having the key of knowledge.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *And this hath come forth from the Lord God.* [var: Vulg. Is 28:29 *a Domino Deo exercituum exivit*, "from the Lord God of hosts"]
