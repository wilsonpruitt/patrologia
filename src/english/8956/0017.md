## CHAPTER XXIX.

VERS. 1.-- *Woe to Ariel.* [n: (ID.)] To Jerusalem, with the affection of one that suffers with her: *If thou also hadst known [n: (Luc. XIX)]*; Ariel, the lion of God; for Jerusalem, the lion of God, was most powerful and most strong.

*He took by storm.* [n: (ID.)] Since she was most powerful, the blind and the lame resisting, when Joab first climbed the heights [1272B] of the roofs.

*A year is added.* When they were in captivity under Nabuchodonosor, they had neither temple nor sacrifice, and this for many years: and this is, a year added to a year.

VERS. 3.-- *As a ball round about thee.* Retaining the ancient likeness of the temple, not its magnificence.

VERS. 4.-- *Of the python,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The pythoness who was at Endor raised up Samuel by necromancy, nay rather a demon: and she foretold things to come of this kind among that people. Mania, madness, whence the Manichees; manthos, divination.

VERS. 6.-- *In thunder.* The Romans were seen to rush in with thunder, and the earth to be moved at their coming, and [1272C] with the flame of fire, because they burned both the city and the temple.

VERS. 9.-- *Be astonished and wonder.* [n: (HIER.)] After the overthrow of Jerusalem and of the temple, that is of Ariel, the prophecy is directed against the Scribes and Pharisees.

VERS. 11.-- *For it is sealed.* These things were fulfilled when they lost both their dignity and their priesthood, and the city was destroyed and the temple, by the princes of the Romans.

VERS. 13.-- *Forasmuch as this people draw near.* [n: (ID.)] The Lord uses it in the Gospel of Matthew, etc., *as far as* having used his own words.

*With their lips.* [n: (ID.)] The Septuagint. With their lips they honour me, but their heart is far from me: and in vain do they worship me, teaching the precepts of men and doctrines.

[1272D]VERS. 14.-- *For shall perish.* [n: (ID.)] From this place the Apostle takes a testimony: *I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and the understanding of the prudent I will reject:* [var: Vulg. 1 Cor. 1:19 *prudentiam prudentium reprobabo*, "the prudence of the prudent I will reject"] the same sense in other words: Truly God wrought a great miracle among the people, that at one and the same time the city should burn, and the temple fall down, and all the knowledge of the masters perish.

VERS. 15.-- *That you may hide from the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] As though God did not see. By this error Adam and Eve hid themselves: Cain also said: From thy face I shall be hidden.

VERS. 16.-- *Perverse is,* etc. [n: (ID.)] That the Creator should be ignorant of the creature; whence the Apostle: *O man, who art thou that repliest to God? doth the thing formed say to its maker: Why hast thou made me? [n: (Rom. IX.)]* [var: Vulg. Rom. 9:20 *figmentum ei qui se finxit: Quid me fecisti sic?*, "to him that formed it: Why hast thou made me thus?"]

[1273A]VERS. 17.-- *In a little and a short while.* [n: (HIER.)] A little and short is the time from when this was foretold up to the ascension of the Lord: for all times, compared with eternity, are short and of a moment.

*Libanus into Carmel.* Libanus, a mountain in Phoenicia, etc., *as far as* if she were with an adulterous husband.

VERS. 21.-- *And they have turned aside in vain from the just man.* The demons attempted, etc., *as far as* the Scribes and Pharisees turned aside in vain from the just man, saying: *We have no king but Caesar [n: (Joan. XIX)]*.

VERS. 22.-- *He redeemed Abraham.* From the fire of the Chaldeans, etc., *as far as* for in their tongue fire is called *ur*.

## CHAPTER XXX.

VERS. 1.-- *Woe, ye apostate children, saith the Lord.* [1273B] [n: (ID.)] After the prophecy against Ariel and the rest, etc., *as far as* they fled into Egypt: and this is: *woe, ye apostate children.*

VERS. 2.-- *Who walk.* As Jerome says: Jeremias went with them, etc., *as far as: that you might dwell in your own land.*

*For they were in Thaphnis.* As if he should say: Have you therefore withdrawn from me, that you might flee as far as Thaphnis and Anes? Thaphnis is a city in which the magicians wrought signs against Moses; Anes is situated in the borders of Egypt: perhaps, troubled with fear, they did not dare to remain in Thaphnis, and fled as far as Anes.

VERS. 6.-- *The burden of the beasts of burden.* [n: (ID.)] After the ten burdens of the various nations, he sets down an eleventh burden, of the two remaining tribes, which were left [1273C] when Sedecias and the other princes had been led into Babylon.

*In the land.* [n: (ID.)] The desert through which the pontiff Joanna the son of Chareas and the other leaders led the remnant into Egypt against the precept of the Lord; or, Egypt itself, in which they endured many tribulations and straits.

VERS. 8.-- *Now therefore.* [n: (ID.)] The Lord to Jeremias, or to Isaias according to the Hebrews, etc., *as far as* because I did them good, I taught them well, and they despised it.

VERS. 12.-- *Because you have rejected the word.* [n: (ID.)] Heretics also and every teaching contrary to the truth, etc., *as far as* and at least to offer muddy and scanty water to the thirsty.

VERS. 13.-- *As a breach falling.* As if to say: As a breach in a very high wall which has drawn a long [1273D] ruin can hardly be repaired and receive its former beauty, so shall a sudden destruction come upon you.

VERS. 19.-- *Weeping he shall in no wise weep.* [var: Vulg. Is 30:19 *plorabis*, second person, "weeping thou shalt not weep"] [n: (ID.)] The Jews refer this to that time when under Zorobabel, etc., *as far as* in the watchtower, namely, and the vision of peace, that is the Church.

VERS. 21.-- *Behind the back.* In the first condition, as it were in the face, Adam heard from the Lord, etc., *as far as* and this is: *Thy ears shall hear the word of one admonishing behind the back.*

*Nor to the right hand.* On either side, whatever is beyond measure is in fault. *Nor to the right hand,* etc., is one to turn aside, but one must walk by the public road, that is the royal one, which does not deceive.

VERS. 22.-- *And thou shalt defile the plates.* [n: (ID.)] The idols, [1274A] namely, which the craftsman's tongue shall have fashioned after the likeness of truth, thou shalt break to pieces in the splendour of eloquence (which silver signifies) and in the reason of wisdom (which gold signifies), and shalt scatter them, and shalt judge them unclean.

VERS. 23.-- *And rain shall be given.* [n: (ID.)] In the volume of Kings it is written, etc., *as far as* for unless they have cast off vices, the virtues do not enter in.

*He shall feed.* Some contend that these things were fulfilled hyperbolically in Judaea in the times of Cyrus, who released the captivity of the people.

VERS. 24.-- *Mingled provender.* Isaac sowed barley, etc., *as far* and *as* through the chaff and the barley they advance little by little, and pass over to the wheat.

VERS. 25.-- *And there shall be.* In the day of judgment upon the mountains [1274B] and the hills there shall be streams, etc., *as far as* for he that shall drink of its waters shall not thirst for ever.

*Of the slaying of many.* [n: (ID.)] For many are called, etc., *as far as* whose tongues were confounded in Babylon, and upon whom the tower in Siloe fell.

VERS. 26.-- *And there shall be light.* When they shall receive the reward of their labour, because the creature shall be delivered from the servitude of corruption. It is no wonder if the moon receive the brightness of the sun: *Because the just shall shine as the sun [n: (Matth. XIII)]*.

VERS. 27.-- *Behold the name of the Lord.* The Son in the name of the Father, for the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Ghost is one: whence: *Baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:* not in the names, but in the name which is God.

VERS. 28.-- *His spirit as a torrent overflowing.* [1274C] [n: (HIER.)] As an overflowing torrent, when it has come up to the neck, chokes a living creature, so the spirit of the wrath of God shall choke the wicked in the judgment. Signifying that the end of all things has come, as a torrent chokes him to whose neck it has come; so the judgment of God shall pass over no man unpunished.

*Which was in the jaws.* That they might be carried about at the will of the devil, these things the Lord shall destroy in the day of judgment, or in the preaching of the apostles when the nations shall have come to the faith, and shall leave their idols.

VERS. 29.-- *You shall have a song.* After the punishment of the wicked he treats of the glory of the saints. As the children of Israel, when the Red Sea had been crossed, when they had seen Pharao and the Egyptians drowned, said with gladness: *Let us sing to the Lord, for gloriously,* etc.; so in the day of judgment, [1274D] when the world has been passed over and the devil condemned with his members, the saints shall exult with gladness in the praises of God; in the same manner also in the conversion of the nations.

*As one that goeth.* It was commanded the children of Israel that they should offer to the Lord the tenth of their goods, glorifying the Giver of good things: so the saints in the judgment shall offer their sheaves, exulting and praising God, and the converted nations likewise.

VERS. 33.-- *Of brimstone.* In hell there is an intolerable stench and an inextinguishable fire.
