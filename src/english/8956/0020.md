## CHAPTER XL.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1281A]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Be comforted.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: Because your king has no compassion upon you, be comforted in Christ. ⟦s3⟧ It can be referred to the Gentiles, of whom the Lord says through Zechariah: *I will gather together the people of the east, and they shall be to me for a people, and I will be to them for a God [n: (Zach. VIII)]* [var: Vulg. Zach. 8:7 has *salvabo populum meum de terra orientis*, "I will save my people out of the land of the east"].

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Speak ye to the heart.* [n: (HIER.)] He speaks to the heart who comforts one that is sorrowful, as it is said of Sichem: *Sichem spoke to her heart, and comforted her [n: (Gen. XXIV)]* [var: Vulg. Gen. 34:3 *tristemque delinivit blanditiis*, "and soothed her sadness with flatteries"; the quoted words stand nowhere in the Vulgate].

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Jerusalem.* ⟦s2⟧ Jerusalem by the Chaldeans and by the Romans, in body and in soul; the Church tormented in body by persecutors, straitened in mind.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Her malice.* ⟦s2⟧ Malice is affliction, as in that place: *If there is evil in a city which the Lord does not do [n: (Amos. III)]*.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *It is forgiven.* ⟦s2⟧ The cause of consolation, the remission of sins, [1281B] etc., *as far as* because they are purged and loosed by punishments.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The voice of one crying.* [n: (ID.)] Mindful of this voice, the Jews sent to John to ask him: Who art thou?

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Every valley shall be exalted. ⟦s2⟧ He that exalts himself shall be humbled, and he that humbles himself,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ This the masters cry even to this day in the desert of the Churches of the Gentiles.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *And all flesh shall see.* ⟦s2⟧ From the first coming, caught up suddenly to the second, he says: *And all flesh shall see.*

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The mouth of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ The prophets who preached the Christ to come, namely the salvation of our God.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *What shall I cry?* [n: (ID.)] Not without cause does he ask: for when he had offered himself of his own accord, saying: *Behold [1281C] I, send me:* he announced hard things to his own people, and now he fears the same.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *All flesh.* ⟦s2⟧ He who bears the image of the earthly, etc., *as far as* so man, through the successions of his ages, loses his comeliness and falls into death.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *But the word of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ The Word is Christ with his body, which is the Church.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Sion.* [n: (ID.)] By Sion and Jerusalem he understands the apostles and the other teachers, whom he admonishes to preach the things they have received from the Lord, and, ascending the summits of the virtues, to teach by word and by example.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *His flock,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Now by contemplation, in the time to come by vision itself: now by teaching, afterward by saving or by satisfying.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Them that are with young.* ⟦s2⟧ Teachers daily bringing forth sons to God.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1281D]VERS. 12.-- *Who has measured?* [n: (ID.)] Lest there be any doubt about the calling of the Gentiles, the greatness of him who calls is described.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With his fist.* ⟦s2⟧ A *pugillus* is the fist with the fingers drawn in; a *palmus* is the hand stretched out from the thumb to the least finger, so that by the palm and by the fist we may know the heavens stretched out and the globe of the earth.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *The spirit of the Lord upon me, in that he has anointed me: to preach to the meek he has sent me: And the spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him.* [var: Vulg. Is. 61:1 names the agent, *eo quod unxerit Dominus me*, "because the Lord hath anointed me"; the closing clause is Is. 11:2]

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *As a little dust.* [n: (ID.)] The Hebrews think that by this word the finest dust is meant, etc., *as far as* and the poverty both of the Greek and of the Latin tongue.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Libanus shall not suffice.* ⟦s2⟧ Libanus is a very great mountain, [1282A] most rich in pastures and in trees; but although all its trees be cut down for the burning, and all its beasts be sacrificed for a holocaust, yet for filling or for appeasing the power of his majesty they do not suffice.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Surely not.* ⟦s2⟧ He refutes the folly of the Gentiles, that they may withdraw from idolatry.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Strong wood.* [n: (ID.)] In Hebrew *mesuchan* (מסכן), because it is a kind of wood that cannot rot, of which idols are chiefly made: and he says this so that, the idols being rejected, the teaching of the Gospel may be received and the glory of the Lord revealed, that all flesh may see the salvation of God.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *From the beginning.* ⟦s2⟧ Of the law of Moses, or from the beginning of the world in the very creatures, which all in their own manner proclaim their maker.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1282B]VERS. 22.-- *Upon the circle.* ⟦s2⟧ Hence some contend that the earth is as it were a point and a globe.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As locusts.* ⟦s2⟧ In comparison with him, or considering the greatness of the earth.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A tabernacle.* ⟦s2⟧ Hence also they contend that heaven is after the likeness of a sphere, and that its hemisphere overhangs the lands.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *Who brings forth the searchers of secrets. ⟦s2⟧ God hath made foolish the wisdom of this world,* and this is: *Who brings forth the searchers of secrets, as though they were not [n: (I Cor. I)]*.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *To whom have you likened me, made me equal, says the Holy One.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: Why do you compare God to a likeness, and do not from the greatness of creatures understand God the creator? ⟦s2⟧ If you do not believe words, believe your own eyes, and from the [1282C] servitude of all the elements consider the omnipotence of the Lord.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *In number.* [n: (ID.)] To him are numbered the things which Abraham could not number: and they serve in an assigned office, as the sun fulfils the course of heaven in one year, and the rest in their own manner.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Their host,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The angels, namely; whence: *Thousands of thousands ministered to him [n: (Dan. VII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ Hence too he is called Lord Sabaoth, that is, of the host, or of the armies, or of the powers.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *Why sayest thou,* etc. [n: (ID.)] When the prophet said these things, etc., *as far as* we are without a judge, and are afflicted undeservedly.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *My way.* ⟦s2⟧ Good works: earthly things do not pertain to God, he does not consider what each man does. ⟦s3⟧ Hence a man is unjustly oppressed, like beasts of burden and fishes, without one to rule them.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1282D]VERS. 28.-- *He created.* ⟦s2⟧ He knows all things, contains all things, dispenses all things by his majesty.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of his wisdom.* [n: (ID.)] From this place another saying of the Apostle seems to be taken: *Incomprehensible are his judgments, and his ways unsearchable:* as if he should say: God alone is eternal and impassible; all other things shall fail.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *The children shall faint.* ⟦s2⟧ Those, namely, who rejoice in the health of the body, and think that childhood and youth are perpetual.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *They shall change.* ⟦s2⟧ That they may be strong in God, they shall become weak to the world, and after the death of the flesh they shall become immortal.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As eagles.* ⟦s2⟧ As the eagle, according to the natural philosophers, when its beak, hooked by excessive age, has been dashed against a flint, [1283A] is made new, so they who mortify the oldness of sin by the rock, which is Christ, being dead to the world are made new to God; whence: *Thy youth shall be renewed like the eagle's [n: (Psal. CII)]*.
