## CHAPTER XLIII.

[1285C]VERS. 1.-- *And now these things.* Above he spoke of the captivity of the Jews who would not believe; now he addresses the primitive Church, which is of the Jews, that they may understand the Creator of their soul and of their body.

*Creating thee, Jacob.* Jacob is the supplanter, Israel the man seeing God. These are the apostles and apostolic men, who both supplanted vices and deserved to see God.

VERS. 2.-- *Through the waters,* etc. [n: (ID.)] According to the Hebrews: Through the waters the Egyptians, etc., *as far as* these kingdoms too the apostles passed through by preaching, and the Lord everywhere with them.

VERS. 3.-- *I have given a propitiation.* That from thence thou mayest gain for thyself mercy and a crown, by preaching, namely, to the Egyptians, and to the Ethiopians (who are almost the last) and to the Sabaeans: and Saba is a city situated on the borders of Ethiopia: *Their sound has gone forth into [1285D] all the earth, and unto the ends,* etc.

VERS. 5.-- *From the East. Many shall come from the East and from the West, and shall sit down with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, from all the parts of the world, that is.* [var: Vulg. Matt. 8:11 *multi ab oriente et occidente venient … in regno caelorum*, "in the kingdom of heaven"]

VERS. 8.-- *Bring forth.* The voice of the Father to the Son concerning the Jews who are blind in mind. And note that the calling of the Jews was made first, then that of the Gentiles.

VERS. 9.-- *All the nations.* The prophet speaks to the Gentiles, rebuking them for idolatry and marvelling that none among the Gentiles could foreknow this; but only the people of God, who received the law of God, had prophets.

VERS. 10.-- *There was not formed.* Certain manuscripts have *plasmatus*; but it does not fit well, though it can be expounded thus: Before me no [1286A] idol was formed, because I made man just, by whom an idol was unjustly formed.

VERS. 12.-- *I have declared and have saved.* Through the prophets the nativity of the Son, the calling of the Gentiles, the casting out of demons.

VERS. 14.-- *Thus says the Lord the Savior,* etc. [var: Vulg. Is. 43:14 *redemptor vester*, "your redeemer"] These things, namely, which follow: the Father to the apostles. For your sakes, that is, you who are my witnesses, you who preach the Gospel. To whom he said: Fear not. *The Chaldeans in the ships.* According to the history, the Chaldeans abound in rivers, and are vigorous with ships, and thence take glory.

VERS. 15.-- *I am the Lord your Holy One.* Formerly to the sons of Israel, now to you, apostles, the demons having been cast out in the world and all the princes conquered.

[1286B]VERS. 19.-- *I do new things.* Many things that refresh souls, not bodies: of old I brought forth from the rock one fountain only, to refresh bodies.

VERS. 20.-- *Dragons and ostriches.* The malignant, the venomous, who abode in the solitude of the Gentiles, brutish through the blood of idolatry and the savagery of their manners.

The ostrich has the appearance of wings, but lacks flight. So the hypocrite has the appearance of holiness, but does not have the thing itself.

VERS. 22.-- *Thou hast not called upon me.* [n: (HIER.)] He speaks to the Jews, who before the coming of the Lord worshipped idols; or to those who after his coming would not believe.

*Neither hast thou labored.* I have not made thee labor after the coming of the Son, that thou shouldst offer costly things, because: *A sacrifice to God is an afflicted spirit,* etc. [n: (Psal. L.)] Or: Thou hast not labored in me only, thou who didst offer to idols.

[1286C]VERS. 23.-- *In incense.* By the incense and the cane, which is a shoot of an aromatic tree, understand all spices.

VERS. 25.-- *I am he.* [n: (ID.)] As if he should say: Thou hast made me labor under the burden of thy sin; therefore I no longer call thee my servant, nor my child, but simply name thee Jacob and Israel, that I may show and convict sinners.

VERS. 26.-- *Bring me back to remembrance.* The good things I have done for thee. *What more ought I to have done for thee, and did not do?* [var: Vulg. Is. 5:4 *Quid est quod debui ultra facere vineae meae, et non feci ei*, "for my vineyard … for it"] Or: If thou hast anything of justice, I will gladly receive it.

VERS. 27.-- *Thy father.* Abraham, namely, who, when the Lord promised the land of promise, answered: *Whereby shall I know that I shall possess it?* [var: Vulg. Gen. 15:8 *unde scire possum quod possessurus sim eam*] Or Abraham himself, who is said to have sinned by doubting at the promise of a son; or the people in the desert by murmuring against God, as if he should say: he shall recognize one begotten of sinners.

[1286D] *Interpreters.* As if he should say: *inter partes*, that is, those who bear peace between the parties.

## CHAPTER XLIV.

VERS. 1.-- *And now hear, Jacob.* He addresses the first believers from among the Jews, the others being hardened, with a certain privilege of name and dignity of honor; the choir of the apostles, namely. *Hear, Jacob my servant.* The first is servitude, the second election.

VERS. 2.-- *From the womb thy helper.* In which thou didst seize thy brother's heel; or, in the beginning of the Church thou didst escape the hands of the persecutors.

*And most upright, whom I have chosen.* Israel, according to the Hebrew verity, is *upright* or *most upright*. As for what is said before, *a man seeing God*: [1287A] that is held not from the proper meaning, but from the sound of the word.

VERS. 3.-- *I will pour out my Spirit.* Namely in baptism, through the imposition of hands.

VERS. 4.-- *As willows beside the running waters.* The willow is an unfruitful tree, etc., *as far as* which quickly believed when the apostles preached.

VERS. 5.-- *This one shall say.* He describes the variety of them that believe.

*And in the name of Israel he shall be likened.* That they may receive the law, and the prophets, and the graces of the Holy Spirit which were promised to Israel.

VERS. 6.-- *I am the first and I am the last.* After the calling of the Gentiles, who, having cast away idols, believed, he acts against those who would not be converted.

[1287B] *Besides me there is no God.* He does not say that he alone exists, but that beside his power and his wisdom there is no external God, condemning the opinion of many gods and their images.

VERS. 12.-- *He shall hunger and shall faint.* [n: (HIER.)] With the hunger and thirst of the craftsman, etc., *as far as* whose God is their belly, whose glory is in their confusion.

VERS. 23.-- *Shout, ye ends of the earth.* [n: (ID.)] The angels preside as it were over the inhabitants of the earth; or the apostles, or the prophets, over whom others also are agreed to be; whence it is said to the Ephesians: *[sic: Super aedificati] Built up upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets,* etc.

VERS. 24.-- *Thus says the Lord the Redeemer,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Above he spoke of the overthrow of idols, and of the redemption of a repentant Israel: but now of the greatness of his own [1287C] power, lest anyone doubt of the salvation he promises.

VERS. 26.-- *I who say.* He speaks of the restoration which was made by Zorobabel and the other leaders, Cyrus permitting it: though the captivity was still to be fulfilled.

VERS. 27.-- *Thy rivers.* Under Darius, namely, and Cyrus his grandson. For on the night in which Balthasar was slain by them, the river Euphrates, which flowed through the middle of the city, was divided into streams by their army, so that they might enter the city through its channel.
