## CHAPTER XLV.

VERS. 1.-- *Thus says the Lord to his Christ.* Among the Romans the diadem, among the Jews the anointing, makes both kings and priests, from which they are also called christs. Cyrus is a christ, [1287D] not by anointing, but by dignity.

*And the backs of kings.* [n: (ID.)] What city did not lie open to him? Which of the kings did not turn his back? What walls, before impregnable, were not overthrown by his siege? But you will find these things more fully fulfilled in the history of Xenophon.

VERS. 3.-- *Hidden treasures.* Of all the cities, so that, having cast away idols, and invited by benefits, thou mightest perceive one God, who foretold thy name beforehand, before thou wert.

VERS. 4.-- *And I have called thee.* [n: (ID.)] As I called Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, etc., *as far as* but the Son, Christ, is anointed with the oil of gladness above his fellows.

VERS. 5.-- *Beside me.* Because the Son and the Holy Spirit are in the Father; for beside my word and my reason [1288A] and my power and the wisdom that is in me, there is no other God.

VERS. 6.-- *That they may know.* Esdras says that Cyrus wrote to all the nations that there is no God but the God of Israel. Or, from the freeing of the captives the clemency and power of God became better known to all. Josephus, moreover, in the book of the *Antiquities* says that the people was released for this reason, that Cyrus read in the book of Isaiah the things which the Lord had said of him before through the prophet.

VERS. 7.-- *Darkness.* The night, or the captivity. As if: taking pity on the penitent, I bring them back from captivity; being angry, I take sinners captive.

*Evil.* [n: (ID.)] War, that is, affliction, according to what is said: Sufficient for the day is its own evil. Let them therefore be confounded who judge God to be the author of evils. [1288B] For here evil is set down not as the contrary of good, but for affliction and for war.

VERS. 8.-- *Drop down dew, ye heavens.* [n: (ID.)] Of the coming of Christ, signified through Cyrus, because he will free the human race from the captivity of the devil, he fitly adds these things. Or these words may cling to what goes before, so that, as Cyrus loosens the captivity, heaven and earth rejoice by metonymy.

*And the clouds.* The prophets, watering our hearts with a saving rain, and announcing the nativity and the resurrection and the ascension of Christ; whence: *I will command my clouds that they rain no rain upon it;* [var: Vulg. Is. 5:6 *et nubibus mandabo*, with no *meis*] and elsewhere, *Thy truth unto the clouds.*

*I have created him who:* was, namely, a worm, and is read to have sprung from the earth according to his humanity.

VERS. 9.-- *Woe to him that gainsays:* as if he should say: [1288C] Believe the things I foretell, namely that Christ is to come, because, *Woe to him that gainsays his maker [n: (Psal. XXXV)]* [var: Vulg. Is. 45:9 *fictori suo*, "his fashioner"]; or Cyrus, to whom, through the deliverance of my people, I shall lay all kingdoms under.

VERS. 10.-- *To the woman, what dost thou bring forth?* The man has power over the woman, the woman power over the man.

VERS. 11.-- *His fashioner,* that is, of the Son according to his humanity in the virginal womb; whence: *The Holy Spirit shall come upon thee, and the power of the Most High shall overshadow thee,* etc.

VERS. 12.-- *And to all their host.* On this occasion some think that the stars are rational; for they say that he would not command anything to senseless things, which would not understand the commands.

VERS. 13.-- *I have raised him up.* [n: (ID.)] As if he should say: I who did the things said above; what wonder if I both send the Son [1288D] into the world to redeem the human race, and raise him from the dead?

*I will direct his ways. Who did no sin, neither was guile found in his mouth [n: (I Petr. II)]*.

*He himself shall build.* [n: (ID.)] Of whom it is said: *Thou hast not known me.* Nor did he himself build the city or the temple, but permitted it to be built: nor was this completed in his time, but in Ahasuerus's.

VERS. 14.-- *The labor of Egypt.* The worship of idols. For it labored much in performing the sacrifices of idols. But when the apostles preached they were converted, and above all kingdoms the religious orders of monks abound there, *so that where sin abounded, grace also might more abound [n: (Rom. V)]* [var: Vulg. Rom. 5:20 *Ubi autem abundavit delictum, superabundavit gratia*, indicative and without *et*].

[1289A]VERS. 19.-- *Not in secret,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Of old on Mount Sinai he said to one people only: *Thou shalt not have strange gods [n: (Exod. XX)]* [var: Vulg. Exod. 20:3 *Non habebis deos alienos coram me*]. But the Gospel he preached through the apostles to all nations, whence: *Their sound has gone forth into all the earth [n: (Psal. XVIII)]*.

*I have not said.* [n: (HIER.)] First indeed I came to the Jews: and I admonished them that, leaving the shadow of the law, they should follow the truth.

*Declaring right things.* That the creature may adore the Creator, that, the figures being left behind, it may follow the truth of the Gospel.

VERS. 20.-- *They have not known.* Certain men, remaining in error, were unwilling to know, weighed down, that is, by the burden of images, and hoping in those in whom there is no salvation.

VERS. 21.-- *Take counsel together.* That you all, going through the world, may announce the same thing. For from this they together composed [1289B] the creed concerning the Trinity and the unity, concerning the communion of the Church, concerning the general resurrection.

*I have foretold it.* That the Gentiles were to be saved, but that the idol is nothing: when I spoke to Abraham, or when I gave the law. [var: Vulg. Is. 45:21 *praedixit illud*, third person, "he foretold it"]

VERS. 23.-- *Shall go forth:* into the world, visibly, in the flesh.

*The word of justice,* the son until he shall have fulfilled obedience, etc., *as far as* which the Jews, showing the pride of their mind, do not do at all.

VERS. 25.-- *In the Lord, he shall say.* All the nations, even the barbarous, confessing not in synagogues but in churches.

## CHAPTER XLVI.

VERS. 1.-- *Bel is broken.* [n: (HIER.)] After the calling [1289C] of the Gentiles is subjoined the destruction of the idols.

*Nabo.* Nabo is gold, etc., *as far as* and the prophecy of the demons and the divination of the magi came to nothing.

*To beasts and to cattle.* [n: (ID.)] For they worshipped beasts and cattle, and their region gave to very many towns the names of the beasts and cattle which they worshipped, as, namely, Leonata from the lion, and others of this kind.

VERS. 2.-- *And their soul.* Not that the images have a soul, etc., *as far as* which he subjugated to the devil and to the demons.

VERS. 3.-- *House of Jacob.* He does not say, Jacob, my servants, my elect, with an addition of honor, but simply: and he addresses them as carnal and as departing from his commandments.

[1289D] *You that are carried.* You who are borne like an offspring conceived, through the desert as far as the land of promise. For he waited for them long and patiently, both in the land of promise and, after the passion, for two and forty years.

VERS. 5.-- *To whom have you likened?* He inveighs chiefly against those who in the time of Achaz and Ezechiel worshipped idols in the corners of the streets, in gardens, in groves upon the housetops.

VERS. 8.-- *Remember this.* Because I have begotten you and have carried you even to old age, forsake the idols you have made, and return to the worship of the one God.

*To the heart.* To understanding, you who have lived as it were like beasts, venerating images, dashing yourselves like madmen against wood and stones.

[1290A]VERS. 10.-- *Declaring.* Through the prophets, that when I shall have fulfilled the things foretold through divination, that is, through a prophet foretelling things to come, I may prove my divinity.

*The last. We are they upon whom the ends of the ages are come [n: (I Cor. X)]*. And elsewhere: *But when the fulness of time came, God sent his Son [n: (Gal. IV)]*. *And John: Little children,* he says, *it is the last hour [n: (I Joan. II)]*.

*Counsel:* of redeeming the world through the Son; as if: I now declare my counsel established, so that when you shall have seen the effect, you may know that there is no God save him who knew the things to come and commanded them to come to pass.

VERS. 11.-- *Calling a bird from the east:* [n: (ID.)] According to the Hebrews, Cyrus king of the Persians, etc., *as far as* and him whom the Magi, coming from the east, adored.

VERS. 12.-- *Hard of heart.* You who always resist the truth, [1290B] first the prophets, at last the Son also.
