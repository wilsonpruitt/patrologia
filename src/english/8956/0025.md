## CHAPTER XLIX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Hear, ye islands.* [n: (HIER.)] After the calling of the remnant of Israel and the objection of the unbelieving (concerning whom, *There is no peace to the wicked, saith the Lord* ), under the name of the [1292B] islands he addresses, in the person of the Son, the Churches from among the Gentiles.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *A sharp sword. ⟦s2⟧ I came not to send peace, but the sword [n: (Matth. X)]*, separating, that is, the evil from the good.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *I will be glorified.* ⟦s2⟧ Which he was not in the other servants: *Father, I have glorified thy name [n: (Joan. XVII.)]* And elsewhere: *Arise, my glory [n: (Psal. LVI)]*, etc.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And I said.* [n: (ID.)] As though he said: How have I glorified thee, I who could not call back to thee a great part of the Jews? ⟦s2⟧ But here man's free choice is shown: for it is God's to call, ours to believe, yet with him helping, that the just may obtain the reward of their will.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Therefore my judgment,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The Father shall judge whether [1292C] I have done for them all the things I owed: *I have manifested thy name to the men whom thou gavest me.* ⟦s2⟧ And elsewhere: *I have glorified thee upon the earth [n: (Joan. XVII)]*.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *My work.* ⟦s2⟧ Others: My labour and my sorrow: because he wept over the city, and said in the Psalm: *What profit is there in my blood? [n: (Psal. XXIX.)]* According to the Hebrew, hanging on the cross far from my salvation are the words of my lamentations.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Glorified.* ⟦s2⟧ As though he said: I have lost the Jews, but I am glorified among the Gentiles, whence, *All power is given to me in heaven and in earth. [n: (Matth. XXVIII)]*.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Abhorred.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ and his Nazarenes, whom the Jews daily curse in their synagogues, because on their account they lost their kingdom, their place and their nation.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Kings.* ⟦s2⟧ The prophet to the Son. ⟦s3⟧ *Kings shall see* thee coming to judgment: *Kings,* namely the apostles and the others [1292D] who shall judge with the Lord.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *In an acceptable time.* ⟦s2⟧ Of the Passion, of the Resurrection, in which it pleased God to redeem the human race.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Behold, in my hands,* etc. [n: (ID.)] From this we learn that Jerusalem is not to be sought in the region of Palestine: which is the worst of the whole province, roughened with rocky mountains, and suffers a want of springs.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Thy builders.* ⟦s2⟧ The apostles and others: By the fault of the Jews, that is, of the Scribes and Pharisees, thou wast destroyed; by the preaching of certain Jews, that is, of the apostles, thou wast gathered together both from the Jews and from the Gentiles.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *All these are gathered together.* ⟦s2⟧ The Jews out of every nation, etc., *as far as* daily they are gathered to the faith of the Church.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1293A] *With an ornament.* ⟦s2⟧ With the various graces of the Holy Spirit, with which the bride's array is adorned, of which it is said: *The queen stood on thy right hand in gilded clothing, surrounded with variety [n: (Psal. XLIV)]*.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *I am barren.* ⟦s2⟧ Because after Aggeus, and Zacharias, and Malachias, who is also Esdras, they had no prophets to nourish them in the law, until John the Baptist.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *Queens.* ⟦s2⟧ The same, but more affectionately. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *The queen stood,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ She, namely, who daily nourishes the little ones of Christ and leads them to the perfect age.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They shall adore.* ⟦s2⟧ They shall venerate, according to that: *They shall adore the footstool of his feet [var: Vulg. Ps 98:5 *adorate*, "adore ye" — an imperative, here made a future, matching the lemma *Adorabunt*] [n: (Psal. XCVIII)]*, in which foot majesty is signified.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The dust of thy feet.* [n: (HIER.)] They shall cleanse away, that is, all [1293B] earthliness by word and by example, according to what is said, *Whatsoever thou shalt loose upon earth, it shall be loosed [n: (Matth. XVI)]*, etc., whence: *Shake off the dust of your feet [var: Vulg. Matt 10:14 *excutite pulverem de pedibus vestris*, "shake off the dust from your feet" — the proof-text conformed to the lemma *the dust of thy feet*] [n: (Matth. X)]*.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *For thus saith the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] He answers the question, namely that these things are fulfilled at his coming.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *With their own flesh.* ⟦s2⟧ Not by another's death, but by his own, etc., *as far as* lying open to the bites of savage beasts.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The mighty one of Jacob.* ⟦s2⟧ I who gave Jacob strength to wrestle, etc., *as far as* thou shalt prevail, as it were by prayer also, against Esau whom thou fearest.

## CHAPTER L.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Thus saith the Lord: Who is he?* [n: (HIER.)] After the calling of the Gentiles, after the taking of the strong one, [1293C] whose spoil has been divided to the apostles, he speaks to the people of the Jews concerning Sion, which said above: *The Lord hath forsaken me,* etc.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I have sold. ⟦s2⟧ But I am carnal, sold under sin [n: (Rom. VII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ *He who commits sin is the servant of sin [n: (Joan. VIII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ Whence: *Let not sin reign in your body [var: Vulg. Rom 6:12 *in vestro mortali corpore*, "in your mortal body"] [n: (Rom. VI)]*.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Because I came.* [n: (ID.)] That is, that you may know that I have not repudiated your mother, but that she withdrew of her own will: after many benefits I took flesh, and no longer through the prophets, but present myself I spoke.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *There was not a man.* ⟦s2⟧ To Herod because of his malice, etc., *as far [1293D] as* concerning all in common: *The vision of the four-footed beasts in the desert [n: (Isa. XXX.)]*

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I called.* ⟦s2⟧ As though he said: Ye rational ones, *Incline your ear to the words of my mouth [n: (Psal. LXXVII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ *He that thirsteth, let him come and drink [var: Vulg. Jo 7:37 *Si quis sitit, veniat ad me et bibat*, "If any man thirst, let him come to me and drink"] [n: (Joan. VIII)]*, and the like.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Is it that.* [n: (ID.)] He inveighs against those who thought, etc., *as far as that he may give me more than twelve legions of angels? [n: (Matth. XXVI)]*.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *I will clothe the heavens with darkness.* ⟦s2⟧ The philosophers say that the clouds are lifted on high no more than ten stadia from the earth, and that the brightness of the sun is hidden: thus, when the light of heaven is shut out, the lower air is darkened. ⟦s3⟧ *I will clothe.* ⟦s4⟧ The saints, that is, so that they may know themselves sinners.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And I will put sackcloth.* ⟦s2⟧ That they may do penance in fear and in haircloth.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1294A]VERS. 4.-- *The Lord hath given,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Although these things can be referred to Isaias, yet properly they are referred to Christ, as though he said: I who once wrought such things can now also work the like; my power is not diminished.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *As a garment.* [n: (ID.)] A garment is consumed by age and by the moth. ⟦s2⟧ The Jews too were called to penance for forty-two years after the Passion: at last, being negligent, they were crushed when the Romans came, and devoured by the moth of an evil conscience.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Who is there among you.* ⟦s2⟧ He who fears is not perfect, etc., *as far as* he keeps the souls of the saints whole and chaste and pure.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Who hath walked in darkness.* ⟦s2⟧ The Gentile people in darkness, etc., *as far as* I call to penance; *for I will not [1294B] the death of the sinner, but rather that he be converted and live [var: Vulg. Ezech 33:11 *nolo mortem impii, sed ut convertatur impius a via sua, et vivat*, "I will not the death of the wicked, but that the wicked turn from his way and live"; Ezech 18:32 *nolo mortem morientis*] [n: (Ezech. XVIII)]*.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Behold all you,* as though he said: Exhortation profits nothing, nor is there hope of salvation after such crimes: *They have all gone aside, they are become unprofitable together [n: (Psal. XIII)]*.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Kindling a fire.* ⟦s2⟧ They kindled the fire of the city by which it was burned by the Romans, etc., *as far as* so for sinners the diverse matter of their sin is kindled more or less.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *You shall sleep in sorrows.* ⟦s2⟧ Because neither by benefits nor by torments would they receive the Son of God, whence, *Go, ye cursed, into everlasting fire [var: Vulg. Matt 25:41 *Discedite a me maledicti*, "Depart from me, ye cursed"] [n: (Matth. XXV)]*.

## CHAPTER LI.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Hearken to me, you that follow that which.* [n: (HIER.)] *Look unto the rock.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ. ⟦s3⟧ Of which, *And the rock [1294C] was Christ, out of which you are hewn [n: (I Cor. X)]*, from Christ called Christians.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And to the cavern of the pit.* ⟦s2⟧ On account of the digging of Christ's side, etc., *as far as* we are born through the laver of baptism.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Joy and gladness.* ⟦s2⟧ Note that in Sion, which is compared to the paradise of God, there ought to be so much joy and gladness and thanksgiving and the voice of praise, that what they are to do in heaven with the angels they should meditate on continually upon earth.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *My just one.* ⟦s2⟧ Justifying all, or justice elegantly: not that one nation, but that the whole world may be saved.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The heavens like smoke.* [n: (ID.)] It is written of the saints, etc., *as far as* yet some take "to perish" and "to grow old" for abolition and death.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Arise.* ⟦s2⟧ The voice of the prophet and of the faithful people [1294D] beseeching the coming of Christ, in whom help had been promised.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Who art thou that thou shouldst fear man,* etc., *as far as* that they may blush and do penance.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Arise, arise.* [n: (ID.)] He exhorts to penance those who denied him in the Passion; or who persisted in unbelief, crying, *Crucify him, crucify him [n: (Joan. XIX)]*, etc.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The chalice of wrath.* [n: (ID.)] Physicians are wont to give a most bitter antidote, etc., *as far as* whence it is not clear that this is said of the last captivity.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Behold.* [n: (HIER.)] He foretold the captivity to come; now he exhorts to penance, that they may escape.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *I will put.* ⟦s2⟧ According to the letter they humbled them, [1295A] etc., *as far as* and in the judgment they shall be humbled altogether.
