## CHAPTER LII.

VERS. 1.-- *Arise, arise.* The prophet exhorts those who believed after the Ascension, when the apostles were preaching.

*For he shall no more add.* These things to the heavenly Jerusalem, which no unclean person enters.

VERS. 2.-- *Shake thyself.* A soul defiled with filth loses its former brightness, etc., *as far as* not with corruptible silver and gold, but with the blood of the immaculate Lamb.

VERS. 3.-- *For nothing.* You had neither the profit nor did you escape the loss, when you delivered up the Son of God.

VERS. 4.-- *Into Egypt.* [n: (HIER.)] He accuses the Jews, and foretells things to come, etc., *as far as* that even in his own land [1295B] he may oppress him, and calumniate him?

VERS. 5.-- *What have I here?* [n: (ID.)] Nothing here, he says, do I find among them, etc., *as far as* that they fastened their Redeemer to the cross.

VERS. 7.-- *How beautiful.* [n: (ID.)] Of him who had said above: *I myself who spoke, behold I am here,* the prophet testifies that he himself preached the Gospel upon the mountains.

*Of him that announceth,* Christ, by himself, by the apostles, to whom he says: *Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you [n: (Joan. XIV)]*. Sitting upon whom the Lord preached: they are also the feet of Christ, by carrying his preaching.

VERS. 8.-- *The voice of the watchmen:* [n: (ID.)] of the apostles and of the others who are always in the watchtower, that they may foresee all things and advance those subject to them.

VERS. 9.-- [1295C] *Rejoice.* He consoles the primitive Church, etc., *as far as* by the multitude of the Gentiles it has been both repaired and multiplied.

VERS. 10.-- *The arm. In my arm shall the Gentiles hope [n: (Psal. XCVII)]*. And elsewhere: *Thy arm with power [n: (Psal. LXXXVIII)]*.

VERS. 11.-- *Depart.* [n: (ID.)] This the Jews refer to themselves, etc., *as far as* being admonished by the Holy Spirit.

*Go forth,* preaching the Gospel in the whole world, *Go ye, teach all nations [n: (Matth. XVIII)].*

*The vessels of the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] The temple of the Lord is holy, which you are: vessels, that is, of gold and of silver, whence Paul is called a vessel of election; or the vessels of the Lord, the armour of God, the breastplate of justice, the shield of faith, the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the spirit, which is the word of God.

[1295D]VERS. 13.-- *Behold he shall understand:* lest it be doubted who said, *I who spoke, behold I am here:* and that he is the arm of the Lord, the Father plainly teaches.

VERS. 14.-- *As they were astonished.* The order of the history is not kept. For above the prophet was speaking in the person of the Father concerning the Ascension of the Son, and suddenly he returned to the Passion.

## CHAPTER LIII.

[1296D]VERS. 1.-- *Who hath believed?* [n: (HIER.)] The prophet in the person of the prophets complains that few of the Jews believe what they hear from them concerning the coming of the Son.

*And the arm of the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] The Father announced to the world that his Son would come; and before the scandal of the cross he sent ahead the glory of him rising again. *He shall be lifted up and shall be exalted [var: Vulg. Is 52:13 sets the two verbs the other way about, *exaltabitur et elevabitur*],* [1296A] etc. The choir of the prophets therefore answers that they have fulfilled their office and announced the arm of the Lord.

VERS. 2.-- *A shrub.* Symmachus, a branch; signifying the man who came forth from the womb of the Virgin.

*To a thirsty one.* Aquila, *pathless,* where the privilege of virginity is shown, because without seed he was created from earth that was before pathless.

VERS. 5.-- *By his bruises.* [n: (ID.)] It is plain that, just as his body was torn and scourged, so his soul truly grieved for us, lest in Christ part be believed truth and part falsehood.

VERS. 7.-- *He opened not,* etc. To Pilate or to Herod. For he answered few things, lest the salvation of the human race be deferred.

*And as a lamb.* [n: (ID.)] In figure of whom the people of Israel [1296B] ate the lamb in Egypt [n: (Exod. XII)]. This is the lamb *who taketh away the sins of the world [var: Vulg. Jo 1:29 *peccatum mundi*, "the sin of the world," in the singular] [n: (Joan. I)]*, *who was slain from the beginning of the world [n: (Apoc. XIII)]*, who feeds us with his flesh and his blood, and clothes us with the fleece of his protection.

VERS. 8.-- *From judgment.* The judge of all found no truth of judgment, but was condemned without fault. Whence the prophet marvels that the God of all delivered himself over to the Passion.

VERS. 9.-- *He shall give the wicked.* [n: (ID.)] That is, the Gentiles, who are without piety, to God the Father, because dead and buried he acquired them; and the rich, namely the Jews, who glory in the riches of the law and of the prophets.

*The rich.* The Scribes and Pharisees, glorying in the riches of the doctrine of the law.

VERS. 10.-- *A long-lived seed.* The word of the Gospel, [1296C] namely, which shall not grow old, as the Old Testament did, whence: *He that sowed went out to sow his seed [var: Vulg. Lc 8:5 *Exiit qui seminat, seminare semen suum*, "He that soweth went out to sow his seed" — a present participle where Migne prints a perfect] [n: (Luc. VIII.)]*

VERS. 11.-- *He shall be filled.* With the faith of the Gentiles, whence, hungering and thirsting above Jacob's well, he would not use the food that had been bought, because he was already filled with the faith of the Samaritan woman and of those who were coming out to him from the city.

VERS. 12.-- *And with the wicked.* That he might redeem the wicked from sin, being made all things to all: *For he bore our sins in his own body [var: Vulg. 1 Petr 2:24 *peccata nostra ipse pertulit in corpore suo super lignum*, "himself bore our sins in his body upon the tree"] [n: (I Petr. II)]*. Whose imitator Paul says: *I became all things to all, that I might gain all [var: Vulg. 1 Cor 9:22 *ut omnes facerem salvos*, "that I might save all"; *lucrifacerem*, "gain," stands at 9:19 and 9:22a] [n: (I Cor. IX)]*.

*He prayed.* Father, forgive them. At this voice, according to the Gospel of the Nazarenes, many thousands of the Jews standing about the cross believed.

## CHAPTER LIV.

[1296D]VERS. 1.-- *Praise, thou barren.* [n: (HIER.)] Thus far concerning the Nativity, the Passion, the Resurrection, etc., *as far as* gathered together both from the Jews and from the Gentiles.

*Praise, thou barren.* The Apostle, according to the Seventy: *Rejoice, thou barren, that bearest not, break forth and cry, thou that travailest not, for many are the children of the desolate [n: (Gal. IV)]*, etc. But we, brethren, after Isaac, are the children of the promise.

VERS. 3.-- *For to the right hand:* to all the places of the world. Or, neither lifted up by prosperity, nor broken by adversity. Or, to the goods of the future life and of the present.

VERS. 4.-- *Fear not.* [n: (ID.)] When the tower of Babel was being built, etc., *as far as* who a cord.

VERS. 6.-- *For as a woman.* [n: (ID.)] They who follow [1297A] tropology, even in the most difficult places, etc., *as far as* he holds out the robe and the kiss.

VERS. 9.-- *As in the days of Noe,* etc. That the congregation of the saints may believe the mercy of the Lord to be everlasting, and that they are forsaken but for a moment, that they may become the friends of God by an eternal covenant: he sets forth the examples of the fathers.

VERS. 14.-- *Depart far off.* [n: (HIER.)] He has consoled the Church from among the Gentiles, promising her spiritual graces: now he teaches what she is to do, that she fear not her adversaries.

VERS. 15.-- *Behold, an inhabitant shall come.* This the Jews turn to themselves, as though there were to be proselytes from among the Gentiles in the time of their own Christ.

VERS. 16. *Behold I.* Not that I am the cause of their perdition, but the adversary who fights is the cause of perdition to the conquered, of reward to the conquerors.

[1297B]VERS. 17.-- *This is the inheritance.* Lest the just man, being afflicted, fail, he adds the rewards.
