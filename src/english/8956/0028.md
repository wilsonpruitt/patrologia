## CHAPTER LVIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Cry.* [n: (HIER.)] The Lord to the prophets or to the apostles: the former chapter being ended, in which peace is announced to the just, and no rest to the wicked.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As a trumpet.* [n: (ID.)] Where to the apostles, etc., *as far as* so let Israel, dead by sin, hear his own crimes.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To my people.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he said: Let them see what a Father they have lost, who calls even sinners his people.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *As.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, why should I not hear them as I hear those who do justice in the Church, [1300C] living justly and piously, confessing Christ true God and true man.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They ask.* [n: (ID.)] Another rashness of the Jews, as though, trusting in a good conscience, they demand a just judgment, according to that: *Judge me, O Lord, for I have walked in my innocence.*

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Why have we fasted.* ⟦s2⟧ They have forgotten the ancient history, that the Lord had no regard to the gifts of Cain, because he did not divide rightly with his brother, to whom he grudged charity towards God.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Behold, in the day.* ⟦s2⟧ A fast acceptable to God is to abstain not only from foods, but from all unlawful things.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *You exact.* ⟦s2⟧ He who exacts from one who has not, does violence to God.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Do not fast as until this [1300D] day.* ⟦s2⟧ Lest he seem to reprove fasting, he teaches how one ought to fast.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Is this such a fast.*

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The bundles.* [n: (ID.)] We understand sins, etc., *as far as* despise not the household of thy own seed.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Break thy bread to the hungry.* [n: (ID.)] We break bread to the hungry, etc., *as far as* but the fullness of the soul.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And thy own flesh.* [n: (ID.)] Recognize thy neighbour, in whatever need, as like to thyself, considering that thou art mortal and passible.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Thy light.* ⟦s2⟧ As the morning light dissolves the darkness, so the light of truth puts errors to flight.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1301A] *Justice.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ, who justifies all, shall go before unto glory.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And the glory of the Lord shall gather thee up,* that is, shall receive thee to himself, who said: *Where I am, there also shall my minister be [n: (Joan. XII)]*.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *To stretch out the finger.* ⟦s2⟧ To mark thy neighbours, to disparage them. ⟦s3⟧ That is, not only art thou not to think these things and not to do them, but thou art not even to disparage.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *A watered garden,* the paradise of delight, which the Lord God had planted from the beginning, namely the Church. ⟦s2⟧ The fountain in the midst is Christ, whose waters of spiritual doctrine do not fail, which water the Church. ⟦s3⟧ And this is, *And he shall be as a watered garden* [var: Vulg. Is 58:11 *et eris quasi hortus irriguus*, "and thou shalt be" — the second person, which the gloss's own address keeps], O faithful one, or O Church.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As a fountain.* ⟦s2⟧ Thou shalt be made like to Christ, thy head.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1301B]VERS. 12.-- *They shall be built* [n: (HIER.)] The Jews refer this to the restoration which was made under Zorobabel and Jesus the high priest; or to that which they rave will come at the last. ⟦s2⟧ Better may these things be referred to the Church.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The foundations* [n: (ID.)] The apostles, the prophets and the like: Whence, *The foundations thereof are in the holy mountains [n: (Psal. LXXXVI)]*. ⟦s2⟧ But the foundation in the singular number is Christ, whence, *Other foundation no man can lay, but that which is laid, which is Christ Jesus [n: (I Cor. III)]*.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of iniquities,* otherwise *into rest,* namely the paths of error into rest or into true peace.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Of the earth.* ⟦s2⟧ Of the living, which is above, not below, whence it is said to Lot: *Save thyself in the mountain.*

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1301C] *With the inheritance.* ⟦s2⟧ The heavenly one, which I gave to Jacob, whose son thou art by faith and by working, by supplanting vices and sins. ⟦s3⟧ For whosoever has been the imitator of a man is called his son.

## CHAPTER LIX.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *But your iniquities.* ⟦s2⟧ He shows why they were not heard and were led into captivity.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *His hands.* [n: (HIER.)] He sets forth the iniquities part by part, etc., *as far as* and at last crying out against the Son of God: *His blood be upon us and upon our children.*

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *They have conceived,* [n: (ID.)] They who await Antichrist. ⟦s2⟧ For him whom they seek out with much labour they shall receive in iniquity.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Eggs.* [n: (ID.)] From the egg the offspring, from the heart [1301D] the counsel, from which the act. ⟦s2⟧ They have broken the eggs therefore, when they showed forth in words their wicked hearts and their venomous counsels, saying: *He is guilty of death [n: (Matth. XXVI)]*.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of asps.* ⟦s2⟧ The asp, at the first voice of the charmer, fastens one ear into the earth and stops the other with its tail, that it may not be charmed: so the Jews, cleaving to earthly things, fearing lest they lose their place and their nation, repelled the preaching of Christ, venomous sons of venomous fathers.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *It shall break out into a basilisk.* ⟦s2⟧ From the eggs of the asp the cockatrice, which is also the basilisk; from the Jews, Antichrist. ⟦s3⟧ For from them he shall be born, killing by his breath, that is, by his venomous speech, many who are flying to the heavenly country. ⟦s4⟧ For the basilisk kills with its breath the birds flying through the air.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *For this* [n: (ID.)] The prophet, in the person [1302A] of the penitent people, answers, after the accusation, what the penitent ought to say.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *We shall roar all of us like bears.* ⟦s2⟧ The meditation and teaching of the Jews in Holy Scripture is compared to the roaring of a bear and the moaning of a dove, as useless and vain, like those. ⟦s3⟧ Against their subjects too they rage as bears; against their superiors or their equals, they moan as doves.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Truth in.* [n: (ID.)] Christ, who is the way, etc., *as far as* that the person of Christ may be shown, whom, forsaking, they followed a lie.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And he saw.* [n: (ID.)] Having shown that they repented, he shows that their penance did not please the Lord, because many repented not with a pure heart.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *That there is not a man.* ⟦s2⟧ All are effeminate in mind, [1302B] as also that, *I called and there was none that heard.*

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And he saved for himself.* ⟦s2⟧ The Father, namely, through the Son, the Gentiles, *And his justice,* or his mercy according to the Seventy, shall confirm him who has willed to be converted: not by merit, but by mercy and justice. ⟦s3⟧ For it is just that the Creator spare the penitent creature, which could not be saved by its own merits.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *And they shall fear who.* ⟦s2⟧ The vengeance of God upon the Jews through the Romans having been shown beforehand, he describes the faith of the Gentiles coming from the four parts of the world, when the preaching of the apostles has been heard

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As a river.* ⟦s2⟧ Because most swiftly he filled the world with his preaching and with faith.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Whom the Spirit of the Lord.* [n: (ID.)]. ⟦s2⟧ To redeem the human race, the Holy Spirit, namely. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *Who [1302C] was conceived of the Holy Spirit.* ⟦s4⟧ And, *The Spirit of the Lord resteth upon him [var: Vulg. Is 11:2 *Et requiescet super eum spiritus Domini*, "shall rest upon him," a future] [n: (Isa. XI)]*, etc.
