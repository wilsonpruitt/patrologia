## CHAPTER LX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Arise, be enlightened. (HIER)*. These things the Jews refer to the time of their own Christ, etc., *as far as* that at least on account of these things they may receive the Son of God.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Arise.* [n: (ID.)] In the Churches, thou who didst go down in the synagogues: arise among the Gentiles, thou who didst fall among the Jews. ⟦s2⟧ Arise by confession, by the virtues, thou who didst fall by trafficking, by vices; and when thou hast arisen, be enlightened, that is, receive the faith of Christ.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Light.* ⟦s2⟧ Promised to thee long before as about to come for thy redemption, [1302D] which *enlighteneth every man that cometh into this world,* namely he who says: *I am the light of the world [n: (Joan. I, VIII)]*, by whom thou shalt be enlightened if thou hast received the faith of Christ.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The glory of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ For enlightening. ⟦s3⟧ The Son, namely, just as the risen sun enlightens the world; in figure of which the glory of the Lord was shown to Moses and to Solomon, the one building the tabernacle, the other the temple. ⟦s4⟧ The glory of the Lord also was the raising of the dead man, the cleansing of the leper, and things of this kind.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The earth.* [n: (ID.)] The Jews, who love earthly things, to whom it is said: *You shall eat the good things of the land,* which they take carnally.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Of thy rising.* [n: (ID.)] Because, that is, thou wast born of [1303A] water and of the Holy Spirit, we all walk in the brightness of the rising of the primitive Church, because we have the same faith, and are born again by the same baptism.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Lift up thine eyes round about.* ⟦s2⟧ To which is like: *Lift up your eyes and see the countries, for they are already white unto the harvest [n: (Joan. IV)]*.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thy sons.* [n: (HIER.)] Whom thou hast begotten in the faith: Whence Paul: *Through the Gospel I have begotten you [n: (I Cor. IV)]*. ⟦s2⟧ The first believers from among the Jews, when the Holy Spirit was given, *there were in Jerusalem religious men out of every nation which is under heaven [n: (Act. II)]*, or the Gentiles from all the parts of the world.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They shall come from afar.* ⟦s2⟧ Because they were far from God through idolatry, whence: *You who were far off, are made nigh [n: (Ephes. II)]*.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1303B] *From the side.* [n: (ID.)] Certain codices have, etc., *as far as,* nor does Jerome's sense incline to this.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Of camels.* ⟦s2⟧ The camel is a humped and burden-bearing animal. ⟦s3⟧ These are the rich, carrying the burden of riches and the hump of sins.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Dromedaries.* ⟦s2⟧ Dromedary or dromas, for it is called both ways: for it is an animal smaller than the camel, but swifter, in which are figured the Gentiles running swiftly to the faith.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Madian and Epha.* ⟦s2⟧ Regions across Arabia abounding in camels and dromedaries, etc., *as far as* which is figured by the frankincense, they offered to the Lord.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *All the flocks of Cedar shall be gathered together.* [n: (ID.)] The simple, who before were darkness and mourning, etc., *as far as* where the Saracens dwelt, begotten of his stock.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1303C] *The rams.* ⟦s2⟧ Preachers, leaders of the flock, sons of the prophets, who taught that the things the prophets foretold of Christ have been fulfilled.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Nabaioth,* the son of Ismael, and it is interpreted *prophecy.*

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They shall minister to thee.* ⟦s2⟧ Holy bishops, priests, ministers of the Church, by administering the body and blood of Christ to the faithful.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Who are these.* ⟦s2⟧ The prophet marvels at the Gentiles running so swiftly to the faith, or the primitive Church:

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To the windows.* ⟦s2⟧ At their windows stand the saints, guarding the five senses of the body, lest through them, as through windows, death enter into their souls.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Their silver.* ⟦s2⟧ That is: *With the heart we believe unto justice, but with the mouth confession is made unto salvation [n: (Rom. X)]*.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1303D]VERS. 10. ⟦s1⟧ *The sons of strangers.* ⟦s2⟧ The princes of the Gentiles who throughout the world built churches, as Constantine, who at Rome made twelve churches in honour of the twelve apostles, and many others who raised up many churches, and furnished what was needful.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *The glory of Libanus.* ⟦s2⟧ Libanus is a mountain bordering on the land of promise, etc., *as far as* the kings and princes who came to the Church.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The fir tree.* [n: (ID.)] The proud, namely the rich, the philosophers. ⟦s2⟧ For pride follows riches and knowledge. ⟦s3⟧ Cyprian and Hilary, lofty trees in the world, built up the Church of God. ⟦s4⟧ The diversity of the trees signifies the diverse orders in the Church. ⟦s5⟧ Of all which the Church is constructed, marked out with a wondrous beauty.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1304A] *The place of my feet.* ⟦s2⟧ The Church, in which the apostles, etc., *as far as* they sustain the Church by their institutions, their miracles and their examples.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *The city of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ For a city is so called from fellowship, etc., *as far as* who would go about carefully and afford help.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Hated.* ⟦s2⟧ By the Jews, because she preached the faith of Christ and destroyed the observances of the law; by the Gentiles, because she brought in one God and maintained that idols are nothing.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *There was none that passed through thee.* ⟦s2⟧ Would pass through and bless thee, etc., *as far as* moreover pride is sometimes evil, sometimes good:

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *For wood, brass.* [n: (ID.)] By the wood and the stones, etc., *as far as* others strong in faith, as Cyprian, and other martyrs, like iron.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1304B] *Thy visitation.* [n: (ID.)] Christ, or the peaceable apostles, and just overseers, that they may rightly teach and judge.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *There shall not be.* ⟦s2⟧ This of the day of judgment. ⟦s3⟧ By this chapter we are compelled to refer whatever things have been said or are to be said to the last time, since the service of the heavens and of the earth, of the sun and of the moon shall cease for us, and the Lord himself shall be our light for ever.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *The bud.* [n: (ID.)] The angels, all the elect, who fulfil in works the faith they hold. ⟦s2⟧ They are the bud of the Lord's planting and the work of his hands, because by him they were created, and planted in the Church by faith to glorify their Creator.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *In the time:* of the coming of Christ. ⟦s2⟧ Or, in a fitting or a predetermined time. ⟦s3⟧ When [1304C] the Son of man shall have sent his angels, and they shall gather out of his kingdom all scandals.

## CHAPTER LXI.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The Spirit of the Lord* [n: (HIER.)] Christ according to the humanity, who had said above, *I the Lord, in the time will gather them,* etc.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Because he hath anointed me:* with the oil of gladness, with the abundance of grace. [n: (ID.)] This anointing was completed in the baptism, because the Spirit of the Lord descended in the likeness of a dove, and remained upon him.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To announce.* ⟦s2⟧ The Seventy: To preach the Gospel to the poor.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *An opening,* namely a loosing; for thus Symmachus [1304D] more openly rendered it.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The day of vengeance:* [n: (ID.)] of damnation, which came through the Romans, according to that: *The day shall come,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ And, *when you shall see Jerusalem compassed about with an army,* etc.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *A crown.* ⟦s2⟧ When, that is, they shall have seen that very many of the Jews have believed, and that they receive a most clean robe for a mourning garment.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For ashes.* ⟦s2⟧ For the grief and sorrow of their own fruits, when, that is, they shall have seen the Gentiles flowing together to the faith, they shall have the oil of joy for mourning, the mantle of praise for the spirit of grief.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Vinedressers.* [n: (ID.)] The evil tillers of the vineyard, the Scribes and the Pharisees, of whom it is said: *He will bring those evil men to an evil end, and will let out his vineyard to other husbandmen.*

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *But you.* ⟦s2⟧ This to the preachers from among [1305A] the Gentiles, etc., *as far as* who were priests in the house of the Lord.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Their portion.* ⟦s2⟧ The Lord's. ⟦s3⟧ Whence the Psalmist: *The Lord is the portion of my inheritance,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ Which no man truly says, who seeks another portion.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *For I am the Lord:* As though he said: This shall the Lord do, who loves rather the justice of the poor and true judgment than the gifts of the rich, which are often from rapine; nor do they please him more than the hire of a harlot, or the price of flesh in sacrifice, or if a man should immolate his son to the Lord.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In truth.* ⟦s2⟧ That they may eat the lamb in truth, that they may make the circumcision of the heart, and do in truth all the things that were given in figure.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *An everlasting covenant.* ⟦s2⟧ The eternal Gospel, not as [1305B] the Old Testament, which the Gospel succeeds.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Their seed.* ⟦s2⟧ The faithful, to whom the apostles committed the seed of the word which they received from the Lord.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Rejoicing I will rejoice* in the Lord, not in the world.

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With the garments of salvation:* with the virtues, with Christ himself, etc., *as far as* yet the Church is one in the head and in the members.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And as a bride.* ⟦s2⟧ He sets forth the examples of another comparison, of which the former is referred to the bridegroom, the latter to the bride.

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *And praise:* his own, etc., *as far as* he shall render to the Lord the fruit of praise and of good working.
