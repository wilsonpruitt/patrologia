## CHAPTER LXVI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1312B]VERS. 1.-- *Heaven.* ⟦s2⟧ All the saints whose manner of life is in the heavens; whence: *The soul of the just man is the seat of God. ⟦s3⟧ Heaven is my seat.* ⟦s4⟧ As if he should say: Do not think that these things are to be fulfilled to the letter. ⟦s5⟧ *What is this.* ⟦s6⟧ As if he should say: You think me confined to a place, and abiding in the temple only; but *what is this house,* etc.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *To whom,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The altar and the earthly temple which a human hand built being taken away, the victims of the Jews shall rightly be taken away, lest perchance they should say: We are not so foolish as to think that God can be shut up in this place, but we immolate victims in a place set apart for the glorifying of God, as is commanded in the law.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *He that sacrificeth an ox.* [n: (HIER.)] As if: Your sacrifice pleases me not after the immolation [1312C] of the true sacrifice: in the shadow of which I granted you those sacrifices, and that you might not immolate to idols. ⟦s2⟧ *A dog.* ⟦s3⟧ Of which through Moses: *Thou shalt not offer in the house of thy God the hire of a harlot, nor the price of a dog [n: (Deut. XXIII)]* [var: Vulg. Dt 23:18 *Non offeres mercedem prostibuli, nec pretium canis in domo Domini Dei tui*; *prostibulum* for *meretrix*, the word the gloss goes on to use]. ⟦s4⟧ And well are the dog and the harlot coupled, because they lust alike. ⟦s5⟧ *They have chosen in their own ways.* ⟦s6⟧ Not by my will, from the time that the true lamb was immolated, Whence: *Christ our pasch is sacrificed [n: (I Cor. V)]*.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And the things they feared.* ⟦s2⟧ The Romans; whence: *The Romans will come, and take away our place and Nation [n: (Joan. XI)]*. ⟦s3⟧ Or famine, pestilence, captivity.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *That hate you.* ⟦s2⟧ Because you are not of the world, because you believe in me, because you bear my name, being called Christians from Christ: whence, *For the Jews had already conspired, that if any man should confess Christ, he should be put out of the Synagogue [1312D] [n: (Joan. IX)]*. ⟦s3⟧ *Let him be glorified.* [n: (HIER.)] as if: Let him appear glorious, etc., *as far as* with great power and majesty and might, or even in the present.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *A voice of the people from the city.* ⟦s2⟧ When the Roman army was coming on, according to Josephus, the Jews were divided into three parts, contending about the sovereignty. ⟦s3⟧ Others setting another prince over themselves Now a part of the people and of the priests held the temple, but a part of the people and of the chief men held the citadel and the royal house, and a third part, and chiefly the common sort, the rest of the city. ⟦s4⟧ They fought therefore both without against the enemy, and within against their fellow citizens. ⟦s5⟧ When therefore the Romans burst into the city, there was heard the voice of the people wailing from the city, the voice of the priests, and of those who were with them, from the temple. ⟦s6⟧ The voice also [1313A] of the Lord was heard in the exulting Romans, who came at God's nod, [d: rendering] rendering the recompense to his enemies, namely to the Jews, through the Romans.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Before she was in labour.* [n: (HIER.)] Not little by little, nor yet in a long time, etc., *as far as* and David, he says, *The hope of all the ends of the earth, and in the sea afar off [n: (Psal. LXXIV)]*.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A man child,* a strong people, of which were Stephen and Paul, who says: *Who shall separate us from the charity of Christ [n: (Rom. VIII)]*.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *In one day.* ⟦s2⟧ The Apostle begot his sons together as it were in one day, because in Christ, who is light and day; and in the unity of faith.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Shall I.* As if: I who created all [1313B] men out of nothing will create for myself out of all nations one Church which is built of living stones:

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Rejoice ye,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] O angels, O apostles, sorrowful for the perdition of the unbelieving Jews, rejoice, comforted by the faith and salvation of the Gentiles, of whom the Church is built.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *That you may suck.* ⟦s2⟧ The faith of the simple, and their good works, being delighted thereby. ⟦s3⟧ The faith of believers is the milk and the food of preachers. ⟦s4⟧ Whence Christ also: *I have meat to eat which you know not [n: (Joan. IV)]*. ⟦s5⟧ *And be filled.* ⟦s6⟧ The woman in childbed who bore the male child does not lack an abundance of milk in the rearing of the Nation, and of the little ones who were born together, so that she might offer them the two breasts of the Old and of the New Testament. ⟦s7⟧ And it is to be noted that the little ones need the milk of consolation, as still living in the flesh: but they who after the milk of consolation [1313C] have gone forward to solid food shall abound in the delights of truth and of the knowledge of glory of every kind. ⟦s8⟧ *That you may milk out.* ⟦s9⟧ Holy Church, one in all, in some has milk, that is, simple faith and works. ⟦s10⟧ In others there abounds glory of every kind, namely an abundance of charisms, and the knowledge of the divinity and of the humanity of Christ. ⟦s11⟧ Holy preachers therefore are delighted in all, as gathering from some this, from others that. ⟦s12⟧ *From the breast.* ⟦s13⟧ From the apostles and preachers to the gifts of the Holy Spirit, or to the doctrine of the Old and of the New Testament. ⟦s14⟧ For what they preach they show by their works, so that thus they may afford their hearers a leading.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *They shall caress you.* [n: (HIER.)] Sustaining you, giving milk, like him who says, *I beseech you, [1313D] brethren, by the mercy of God, that you present your hearts [n: (Rom. XII)]* [var: Vulg. Rm 12:1 *ut exhibeatis corpora vestra hostiam viventem*, "your bodies"], etc. ⟦s2⟧ And: *I beseech you as strangers, [n: (I Petr. II)]*. etc.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *As.* ⟦s2⟧ Almighty God, as merciful and pitying, compares himself now to an eagle, now to a hen, now to a caressing sea, that at least by caresses he may allure you. ⟦s3⟧ As also the eagle prepares her nest in inaccessible rocks, that access may lie open to none, and in the midst places the amethyst stone, which puts all poisons to flight (according to the natural philosophers, for the keeping off of serpents), and certain other secrets of nature: so he himself places in our hearts a stone, that is, his own faith, namely himself, who is the stone cut without hands from the mountain, by which all the poisons of the devil are put to flight. [1314A] For the eagle loves her young more than all beasts, and than the beasts of burden, the cattle and the birds, as they say who have written of the natures of living things

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *You shall see,* O apostles, the multitude of those living unto the faith. ⟦s2⟧ *And your heart shall rejoice* for their salvation, *and your bones,* the virtues, shall spring up in the hearts of others, the fruit of good will and of good working. ⟦s3⟧ For as the body is sustained by the bones, so is the soul by the virtues. ⟦s4⟧ *And your bones.* ⟦s5⟧ In the day of judgment your bodies shall live again in immortality, so that as in the present life you served God with body and soul, so with body and soul you may receive the rewards. ⟦s6⟧ *It shall be known.* ⟦s7⟧ In the judgment, namely, the Son shall be known as sweet and mild to the good, an avenger and strong to the wicked, he who is the hand of the Father, through whom he works all things

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1314B]VERS. 16.-- *In fire.* ⟦s2⟧ Which shall be eternal to the wicked whom it shall enwrap, and shall lead them to eternal punishment; by which shall be purged whatever in the good shall need purging. ⟦s3⟧ *His chariots.* ⟦s4⟧ His car, because he is then about to triumph, and to conquer all his adversaries: not that he has need of the ministry of angels.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The Lord in fire.* ⟦s2⟧ In the judgment. ⟦s3⟧ Or, his discernment and his justice is shown, while by a just judgment, not by power, he divides the good from the evil. ⟦s4⟧ *With the sword.* [n: (HIER.)] The sentence: whence Amos, *By the sword of God shall all sinners perish,* etc. [n: (Amos. IX)] [var: Vulg. Am 9:10 *In gladio morientur omnes peccatores populi mei*, "shall die by the sword," and without *Dei*] For when he shall render to every man what he deserves, all shall know him for a just judge, and shall praise his judgment.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *They that were sanctified* [n: (ID.)] In their own opinion, in fountains and baptisms, etc., *as far as* but [1314C] it is nevertheless said to the confusion of those who are not even ashamed to do it. ⟦s2⟧ *They that were sanctified.* ⟦s3⟧ We can say, according to tropology, that all who are lovers of pleasure rather than of God say that they are sanctified in gardens and on thresholds, according to the Septuagint, because they cannot enter into the mysteries of truth, and eat the foods of piety: because they are not holy in body and spirit, nor do they eat the flesh of Christ, that they may have eternal life. ⟦s4⟧ *For Christ our pasch is sacrificed [n: (I Cor. V)]*, who is eaten not without, but in one house and within. ⟦s5⟧ *In gardens.* ⟦s6⟧ Not in the sacrifice of the red heifer, which was commanded in the law.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 18.-- *But I.* As if he should say: Therefore I come to judgment with all nations and tongues, that I may judge together all their works and thoughts, and [1314D] render to them all that they deserve. ⟦s3⟧ *Tongues.* [n: (HIER.)] Which according to the apostles', not only of men, etc., *as far as* According to that: *My sword is inebriated in heaven, and it hath come down even to the earth [n: (Isa. XXXIV)]* [var: Vulg. Is 34:5 *Quoniam inebriatus est in caelo gladius meus; ecce super Idumaeam descendet*, "behold it shall come down upon Idumea"].

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *And I will set a sign in them.* ⟦s2⟧ Of the cross, of Christianity, of salvation, marked with which men are not slain in Ezechiel; with which in Egypt the doorposts are marked and the Israelites are saved. ⟦s3⟧ This sign Achaz would not ask, which Isaias subjoins: *Behold a virgin shall conceive,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ *And I will send of them.* ⟦s5⟧ It seems to be contrary, since judgment has been spoken of before, to return to the first coming, in which the apostles are directed to the Gentiles and hear: *Go ye, teach all nations [n: (Matth. XXVIII)]*. ⟦s6⟧ But the day of judgment [1315A] is foretold, that, terrified by fear of the punishment to come, they may receive the coming of the Saviour, and the preaching of the apostles who are to be sent before. ⟦s7⟧ *Into the sea.* ⟦s8⟧ The world, namely, whose parts he subjoins: Africa, etc., the southern, namely, quarter in which Africa is.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *On horses.* (HIER ] They are brought who believe swiftly and run quickly to the faith. ⟦s2⟧ *In chariots and in litters.* ⟦s3⟧ The philosophers, namely, shining both in doctrine and in uprightness of manners: whence they came the more quickly to the doctrine of the four Evangelists, and, raised up by the four cardinal virtues, attained heavenly things with the mind. ⟦s4⟧ *On mules.* ⟦s5⟧ Upon which David and Solomon are read to have sat. ⟦s6⟧ For those who are barren of the world's offspring serve the Lord, who is figured through them, as it were tame. ⟦s7⟧ *In coaches.* ⟦s8⟧ The Septuagint, in covered vehicles: [1315B] others, simply. ⟦s9⟧ These are they to whom it is said: *Bear ye one another's burdens [n: (Gal. VI)]* [var: Vulg. Gal 6:2 *Alter alterius onera portate*, "one another's," not *invicem*]. ⟦s10⟧ *As.* ⟦s11⟧ As if: With how great gladness do the apostles bring your brethren, whose gifts are faith, hope, charity, pure prayer, good works etc., clean vessels, hearts and bodies undefiled. ⟦s12⟧ *Let them offer.* ⟦s13⟧ Which according to the law they did three times in the year, namely at the Pasch, at Pentecost, at the feast of Tabernacles; whence, *Thou shalt not appear empty in my sight [n: (Exod. XXIII)]*. ⟦s14⟧ *The house of the Lord. ⟦s15⟧ The temple of God is holy, which you are [n: (I Cor. III)]*. ⟦s16⟧ And elsewhere: *You are the temple of God, and the Holy Spirit dwelleth in you [n: (II Cor. VI)]* [var: Vulg. 1Cor 3:16 *templum Dei estis, et Spiritus Dei habitat in vobis*, "the Spirit of God"; 2Cor 6:16, as cited, reads *vos enim estis templum Dei vivi*].

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *For as the heavens.* ⟦s2⟧ As if: As these things made new shall remain unmoved for ever, so your seed, O apostles, made new, shall reign with me for ever, free from all corruption [1315C] of the flesh. ⟦s3⟧ *For as,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] These things to the end of the book pertain to the day of judgment.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1316A]VERS. 23.-- *Month out of month.* ⟦s2⟧ *Mene* in Greek, in Latin *luna*, whence *neomenia*, as it were the new moon. ⟦s3⟧ From *mene* comes *mensis* among those who both end and begin the beginning of the kalends, not according to the course of the sun and the differing spaces of the months, but according to the circuit of the moon, which the Hebrews do. ⟦s4⟧ By the month, therefore, the moon: by the moon (which now waxes, and, as the natural philosophers and the philosophers say, receives from the sun the light which it does not have) the Church is understood, of which, *She shall continue with the sun and before the moon [n: (Psal. LXXI)]*. ⟦s5⟧ And elsewhere: *Fair as the moon, chosen as the sun [n: (Cant. VI)]*, and this is it: there shall be month out of month.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *And they shall go out.* ⟦s2⟧ As it were to see, not by place, but by understanding, or by a manifest vision, that they may be the more kindled to the praise of God. ⟦s3⟧ *And they shall see.* [1316B] [n: (HIER.)] It is no wonder if the saints, now immortal, should see the reprobate, since the prophets, still mortal, deserved to see all these things.
