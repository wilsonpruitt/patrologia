## CHAPTER IX (cont.)

VERS. 32.-- « For neither, he being seen. » [var: Vulg. *viro*, "to a man"] Among men we both hear the charges brought against us, and answer them, and are heard; but in the judgment of God, he himself hears us, because he sees all that we do, but we do not hear him, because we do not fully understand what displeases him. He cannot be heard on equal terms.

VERS. 33.-- « Who may avail for both: » that is, may be able for God [0784D] and for me:

« To reprove. » He reproves who from the authority of reason, etc., *as far as* that he corrected the hearts of offenders by gentleness rather than by terror, whence he adds:

VERS. 34.-- « Let him take away the rod from me » of the law, by which he punishes; but Christ took it away, and through gentleness showed the ways of life. He willed to be feared as God, but to be loved as a father.

VERS. 35.-- « I will speak and I will not fear him. » [n: ( Ibid. )] He has fear no longer, but rather affection as toward a father. Behold, the staff, that is, the fear of the law, when sent does not raise up the boy; but Eliseus himself, brought down to the boy's measure, and breathing seven times, that is, through the love of the spirit.

[0785A] [n: (GREG.)] « To answer » is to render in recompense deeds that are worthy; but does no one who fears in servile fashion answer to the benefits of God? Only he who through the confidence of love does not fear that worthy service be rendered to him. He is not pure who would do evil things if it were allowed. But to one who loves there is a weariness of this life, which before was sweet, even mourning he endures it.
