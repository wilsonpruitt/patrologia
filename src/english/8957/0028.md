## CHAPTER XXII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1, 2.-- « Answering, etc. ⟦s2⟧ Is it to God. » [n: (GREG., in Job., l. XVI.)] They who fail against the words of truth repeat even what is known, lest by keeping silence they should seem overcome; whence Eliphaz, pressed by blessed Job's words, [0817B] says the things of which no one is ignorant; for he says:

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « Is it to be compared to God? » For in comparison with God, knowledge, etc., *as far as* he subjoins as though coming down.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- « What doth it profit? » For in everything that we do well, etc., *as far as* Eliphaz still adds what no one is ignorant of.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- « Shall he, fearing, reprove thee? » Who, even doting, would think this, that God reproves us out of fear, and out of dread sets forth his judgment against us? ⟦s2⟧ « Shall he reprove to thee? » They who know not how to measure their words slip down into idle sayings.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- « And not for thy very great malice. » From idle words, etc., *as far as* but let him always come down to worse things. ⟦s2⟧ For there follows.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0817C]VERS. 6.-- « For thou hast taken away the pledge. » These things are plain to the letter, etc., *as far as* or they lose spiritual gifts, who come to thee.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- « Widows. » Heretics call the peoples, etc., *as far as* they say that it is repaid to him for sins? ⟦s2⟧ And this is, « Therefore art thou surrounded with snares. »

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- « And thou didst think that thou. » As if he should say, etc., *as far as* as wave upon wave.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- « Dost thou not think that God. » As if: is it therefore, etc., *as far as* because by a foolish judgment they think themselves alone wise.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- « Is it the path? » As the path of the Redeemer is humility, so the path of the ages is pride. ⟦s2⟧ And so wicked men tread the path of the ages, who walk about in elation through the desires of this life.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0817D]VERS. 16.-- « Who were taken away before their time; unless that all. » [var: Vulg. Job 22:16 has no clause answering to *nisi quod omnes*] They who love the present life, etc., *as far as* and the sentence is changed, but not the eternal counsel.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « And a river hath overthrown their foundation. » While the wicked do not look upon present things as fleeting, etc., *as far as* because the course of mutability undermines in them the standing of a perverse structure.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- « When he had filled. » The Lord fills the houses of the wicked, because he does not deny his own gifts even to the ungrateful.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « Whose counsel is far. » This also Job, etc., *as far as* he desires to be unlike the wicked.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0818A]VERS. 19.-- « The just shall see, » the destruction of the wicked.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « And shall rejoice. » When they shall have cleaved to the strict judge in a security now made perfect. ⟦s2⟧ For now they look upon the reprobate and groan.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- « Is it not cut down. » Here the wicked are set upright, etc., *as far as* or they are dragged from the sight of the judge to Gehenna.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « And devoured their remains. » Because even if here they leave the flesh behind, at the resurrection they shall receive it back, that they may burn in the flesh in which they sinned.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- « Be at peace therefore. » It is a fault of pride to teach one who is better, which heretics often do to Catholics.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- « If he shall have returned. » [var: Vulg. *reversus fueris*, in the second person, "if thou shalt have returned"] Because thou hast been pulled down from the [0818B] building of justice.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « And thou shalt put far away. » The body is taken as the tabernacle of the soul, and the mind as the tabernacle of thoughts. ⟦s2⟧ And the sense is: If after thy errors thou return to God, thou shalt be cleansed in thought and in work.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- « He shall give for earth. » By earth is signified the weakness of action, by the flint strength, by the golden torrents the doctrine of inward brightness. ⟦s2⟧ To those therefore who are turned to him he gives for earth the « flint » of sturdy work, and « for the flint golden torrents. »

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « For. » Because for sturdy work he multiplies the doctrine of clear preaching.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BEDA.)] He says this, that if, the Lord being made favourable to him, etc., *as far as* they are silver tried by fire.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- « And the Almighty shall be against. » [0818C] [n: (Ibid.)] Often, when we give heed to the sacred words, we grievously endure the snares of malignant spirits. ⟦s2⟧ And the sense is: when he shall have driven the malignant spirits from thee, the talent of the divine word shall grow bright in thee.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- « Then upon. » To abound in delights upon the Almighty is to be filled, in his love, with the banquets of Holy Scripture.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « And thou shalt lift up to God. » To lift up the face to God is to raise the heart to the searching out of things sublime.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- « And thou shalt pay thy vows. » He who has vowed vows, but through weakness is not strong enough to pay them, this is wrought in him out of the punishment of sin, that the power should be withdrawn from him who wills the good: but when the fault which stands in the way is wiped off, it comes to pass at once that possibility follows the vow.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0818D]VERS. 28.-- « Thou shalt decree. » A thing is decreed and comes to pass, when the virtue which is sought after out of desire, the Lord bestowing it, prospers also in effect.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « And it shall come. » Heretics promise the same things to the afflicted, if they will follow them.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « And in the ways. » For light to shine in the ways of the just is to sprinkle abroad the signs of its own brightness through the wondrous works of the virtues.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- « For he that hath been humbled. » Because he who humbles himself shall be exalted.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « And he who. » So far as it can be discerned through the ministry of the members, the first showing of pride is wont to be in the eyes.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- « The innocent shall be saved. » Because when [0819A] grace has here come to him so that he becomes innocent, when he is led to judgment he is rewarded out of merit.
