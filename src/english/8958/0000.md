## PREFACE.

[0505D]

[n: (HIERON., Div. Biblioth., tom. VIII, col. 461.)] At length, the Pentateuch of Moses being finished, as men released from a great debt of interest, we set our hand to Jesus the son of Nave, etc.

[n: (ADAMANTIUS, hom. 1 in Josue.)] God gave to Jesus Christ the name *which is above every name*: and this is *Jesus.* Therefore *in the name of Jesus every knee is bowed, of things in heaven, of things on earth, and of things under the earth.* [var: Vulg. Phlp 2:10 *ut in nomine Jesu omne genu flectatur*, "that in the name of Jesus every knee should bow"] And because this is the name above every name, therefore in no generations before was it named. In Genesis we read of many just men, and not one of them was named Jesus. [0506D] In Exodus I find this for the first time [n: (Exod. XVII)], etc., *as far as* while we sin, and the vices of the passions reign in us, although, our idols left behind, we seem to have gone out from Egypt, yet the reproach of Egypt has not been taken away from us. But if thou shalt have been circumcised a second time, and shalt have cut away from thee every vice, of anger, of pride, of envy, of lust and the like, the reproaches of Egypt shall be wiped away, and, carried over into the land of promise, thou shalt receive the heavenly inheritance through Jesus Christ our Lord.

## CHAPTER ONE.

VERS. 2.-- *Moses the servant (ADAMANT., hom. 2 in [0507A] Josue.) Moses my servant is dead.* [var: Vulg. Jos 1:2 *Moyses servus meus mortuus est*; Migne's quotation gives *famulus* where the Vulgate has *servus*, though his own catchword above is *servus*] Mark how Jesus reigns, etc., *as far as* he is a spiritual Moses who lives in the spirit.

*Arise and pass over,* etc. Now therefore, rising up, pass over this Jordan, etc. See how Jesus the Son of God was Moses' minister. *When the fulness of time was come, God sent his Son, made of a woman, made under the law [n: (Gal. IV)]*, for because he was made under the law of Moses, he was made the minister of Moses. And he was made Christ's [cj: *Christi*; read *Christus*, "Christ was made a minister"] minister of the circumcision, to confirm the promises of the Fathers.

VERS. 3.-- *Every place,* etc. [n: (Orig., hom. 2 in Jos.)] The letter of the law is set upon the ground, and lies below, etc., *as far as* that thou mayest be made not only an heir of God, but a co-heir of Christ.

VERS. 4.-- *From the desert and Libanus,* etc. [n: (ORIG., ibid.)] [0507B] The wilderness and Antilibanus. He did not say *Libanus,* but *Antilibanus,* etc., *as far as* the Church gathered out of the Gentiles through Jesus Christ.

[n: (AUG., quaest. 1 in Josue, tom. III.)] From this testimony and from others it is proved that Moses died as a servant of God and pleasing to God, although in him that vengeance was fulfilled, that he should not enter the land of promise. From which it is given us to understand that God avenges some things bodily or temporally even upon his good servants, and yet holds them among the honourable and useful vessels.

*And Josue commanded. [n: (AUG., quaest. 2.)]* It is asked how he commanded this, after the Lord had exhorted him, etc., *as far as* his arrangement is changed at the divinely prompted suggestion of Jethro.

*And to the Rubenites also. [n: (ADAMAN., hom. 3 in Josue.)]* Let us consider [0507C] what figure the two tribes and a half hold, etc., *as far as* they who hold back the land flowing with milk and honey in the bitterness of malice.

## CHAPTER II.

VERS. 1.-- *He sent therefore,* etc. [n: (BEDA in Josue. )] The city of Jericho is the world, to which the Lord Christ sent the two Testaments to search out the manners of men. For in it, that he might more fully prove the faith of them that believe or the stubbornness of the rebellious, before the coming of his judgment he appointed, as it were two spies, the Law and the Gospel.

[n: (ADAM., hom. 3.)] Spies are sent by Jesus into Jericho, etc., *as far as* if in the mercy of his Lord he shall have understood his goodness.

VERS. 16.-- *Get you up to the mountains,* etc. [n: (ADAM., hom. 3.)] The wise harlot gives mystical and heavenly counsel, etc., *as far as* she who was once a harlot has been cleansed in water and in the Holy Ghost and in the blood of Christ.

## CHAPTER III.

VERS. 3.-- *When you shall see the ark of the testimony of our God, and the priests, and the Levites carrying it,* [var: Vulg. Jos 3:3 *Quando videritis arcam foederis Domini Dei vestri, et sacerdotes stirpis Leviticae portantes eam*, "the ark of the covenant of the Lord your God, and the priests of the Levitical stock carrying it"] etc. [n: (AUG., quaest, 3 in Josue.)] It was commanded that the ark go before at a distance, etc., *as far as* the cloud withdrawn, as it were the veil taken away.

*Go before the people. [n: (ORIG., hom. IV.)]* The people is led forth by the priests, and makes its way to the land of promise under the teaching of the priests. *And below:* For it is they [0508A] who teach the people to go out of Egypt, that is, out of the errors of the world, and to pass through the vast wilderness, that is, to run through the diverse kinds of temptations.

VERS. 7.-- *This day will I begin to exalt thee,* etc. [n: (ID., ubi supra. )] Many wonders were wrought in the wilderness, and nowhere is Jesus said to be exalted. But where the Jordan is passed, it is said to him: *This day will I begin to exalt thee,* etc. For the exaltation of Jesus in the sight of the people takes its beginning from baptism. For they that are baptized are baptized in his death, whose death is accomplished in the exaltation of the cross. Rightly therefore is Jesus then first exalted for each of the faithful, when he comes to the mystery of baptism. Whence: *God exalted him, and gave him the name which is above every name,* etc.

[0508B]VERS. 9.-- *Of the Lord your God,* etc. [n: (ADAM., hom. 4 in Jos.)] To the sinner every creature is an enemy, etc., *as far as: who turned the rock into pools of waters, and the stony hill into fountains,* etc.

VERS. 10.-- *The Chanaanite,* possessing, or a possession, or traders, or movable. *The Hethite,* dreading or amazed. *The Hevite,* gathering stones. *The Pherezite,* separating, or scattering abroad. *The Gergesite,* bring near the husbandman, or casting out the husbandman, or a stranger drawing near. *The Amorrhite,* bitter or speaking. *The Jebusite,* treading down, or their manger.

VERS. 15.-- *[The Jordan] had filled the banks at the time of harvest.* The LXX: *As in the days of the wheat harvest.* This seems incredible for our regions, but there (as they relate who have known it) in the [0508C] beginning of spring is the wheat harvest: and then that river overflows and is filled more than through the winter.

VERS. 16.-- *The waters that came down stood,* etc. [n: (ADAM., hom. 4.)] I judge that it was not written without a reason of mystery, etc., *as far as* and one Spirit by whom all are purified in baptism, and one God the Father of all.

## CHAPTER IV.

VERS. 7.-- *For ever.* [n: (AUG.)] How for ever, since heaven and earth pass away? Or is it because it signifies something eternal, since they themselves cannot be eternal? Although what is in the Greek can be said in Latin unto the age.

VERS. 10.-- *And the people made haste and passed over,* etc. [n: (ADAM., hom. 2.)] They who passed the Red Sea [0508D] were baptized in Moses, etc., *as far as* when there is now none to terrify the children of Israel.

VERS. 14.-- *In that day,* etc. Jesus has always been exalted with the Father; but there is need that God exalt him in our sight, that is, that we may know the height of his divinity. And this comes to pass if we shall have passed the Jordan, and be armed with the diverse defences of the sacraments for the wars to come.

VERS. 16.-- *Command the priests.* [n: (AUG., in Josue. )] Charge the priests that carry the ark of the testament, etc., *as far as* because the Old Testament was given in testimony of the thing to come.

*And the people. [n: (ADAMANT., hom. 2.)]* Not without cause is it marked when they come to the Jordan, etc., [0509A] *as far as* that they may signify that many perfections hang upon one day

*Twelve also,* that is, the mystery of the lamb was commanded in Egypt, because on the same day on which we flee the errors of the world, we enter the land of promise. For the whole present life is one day: to-day therefore, that is, while we live, let us make haste to perfection, and not put it off until the morrow.

*Twelve also.* [n: (ISID.)] The children of Israel, passing over the Jordan, took up out of the midst of the river twelve stones, which they set for a testimony in the place of the second circumcision, to signify to us that, when we rise up from the laver, we ought to carry with us the most firm examples of the apostolic life, whose testimony we should always look upon for the imitation of the virtues.
