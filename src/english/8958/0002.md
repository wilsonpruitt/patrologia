## CHAPTER VIII.

[0512A]

VERS. 1.-- *And the Lord said,* etc. [n: (ADAM., hom. 8.)] At the first we were overcome because of sins, etc. *as far as* who follow Jesus, from the burden of the law.

[n: (AUG., quaest. 10 in Jos.)] When any man has undertaken a just war, etc., *as far as* he is to be judged not so much the author of the war as its minister.

[n: (ID., quaest. 11.)] It must be asked whether every will to deceive is to be reckoned for a lie, and if it is so, whether there can be a just lie, by which he is deceived who is worthy to be deceived. And if this indeed is not found to be just, it remains that, according to some signification, this which was done by way of ambush be referred to the truth.

VERS. 15.-- *And fleeing.* [n: (ADAM., ibid. )] What, [0512B] thinkest thou, does Jesus signify by fleeing? etc., *as far as* who flee fornication, guile, lying and the like.

VERS. 24.-- *Therefore all being slain.* [n: (ADAM., ibid. )]. They struck them with the edge of the sword, etc., *as far as* to be crowned for the palm of victory and by the merit of virtue?

VERS. 29.-- *The king. [n: (ID., homil. 8.)]* The king of Hai is hanged upon a double tree, etc., *as far as* but of the devil he says: *The last enemy death shall be destroyed,* etc. [n: (I Cor. XV.)] [var: Vulg. 1Cor 15:26 *Novissima autem inimica destruetur mors* — feminine, agreeing with *mors*; Migne's masculine makes the last enemy the devil, which is what the gloss requires]

[n: (ID.)] The LXX: *In the double tree,* that is, the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, etc., *as far as* He is the last Adam in a quickening spirit.

VERS. 30.-- *Then he built,* etc. [n: (ADAM., hom. 9.)] Jesus built an altar to the Lord God of Israel, etc., [0512C] *as far as* who are worthy for the building of the altar.

VERS. 33.-- *And all the people. [n: (ORIG., hom. 9.)]* And all Israel and the elders, etc., *as far as* by fulfilling, that is, in work the things that are written in the law.

*As the stranger.* [n: (ID., ibid. )] But both the proselyte and the native together, etc., *as far as* and let them beware of running into the penalties appointed for sinners.

*After this he read.* [n: (ADAM., ibid. )] It is related, etc., *as far as: that we may know the things that are given us from God [n: (II Cor. II)]*.

VERS. 35.-- *Nothing of these things.* [n: (ID.)] There was not a word of those things which Moses commanded, etc., *as far as* or who now strive to be joined to the faithful.

## CHAPTER IX.

[0512D] *Being assembled,* etc. [n: (ADAM., hom. 9 in Jos.)] From these things which were done, etc., *as far as* Satan shall be crushed under the feet of his servants [n: (Rom. XVI)]. [var: Vulg. Rom 16:20 *Deus autem pacis conterat Satanam sub pedibus vestris velociter*, "may the God of peace crush Satan under your feet speedily"]

VERS. 3.-- *But they who,* etc. [n: (ID., hom. 10.)] In my Father's house there are many mansions [n: (Joan. XIV)], etc. *as far as* thou hear not the words of him that calls thee back to thy former liberty, thou become the servant of sin for ever.

VERS. 19.-- *We have sworn. [n: (AUG., quaest. 13 in Jos.)]* It is asked how the Hebrews believed that this oath was to be kept to the Gabaonites, etc., *as far as* if he had not done what he had sworn when troubled by anger, than if he had performed it.

[n: (ISID. in Jos.)] The Gabaonites signify those who believe, and who seem to be devout toward the servants of God or to hold the worship of the Church, [0513A] yet have nothing of amendment or of devotion or of renewal in their manners, such men only put forward for a time within the Church a certain sign of salvation. But among the spiritual, that is, among the saints of God, they do not obtain the eternal kingdom or liberty.

## CHAPTER X.

VERS. 4.-- *Come up to me. [n: (ORIG., hom. 11 in Josue.)]* When the soul joins itself to the word of God, etc., *as far as* and it shakes off the coming of night.

*Being assembled therefore,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 15 in Jos.)] It is asked how the king of Jerusalem, etc., *as far as* and yet that whole land is originally called the land of Chanaan.

VERS. 7.-- *And Josue went up,* etc. *(AUG. quaest. [0513B] 14.)* When the Gabaonites, besieged by the kings of the Amorrhites, had sent to Jesus, etc., *as far as* and he in some sort rewarded this faith of theirs.

*For five kings had fled,* etc. [n: (ADAM., hom 11 in Josue.)] What is it that he says, etc., *as far as* for this is that Jerusalem was at one time under king Adonisedec, or other cities under others.

[n: (ADAM., ibid.)] Jesus slays the enemies, not teaching credulity, etc., *as far as* Jesus Christ alone may reign in thee.

VERS. 19.-- *But stay you not. [n: (ADAM., hom. 12.)]* The wars of Jesus and the slaughters of kings and of enemies, etc., *as far as: because the Lord God hath delivered all the enemies into your hands.*

[n: (ADAM., hom. 13.)] All these things happened in figure [0513C] to them. And they were written for our sakes, etc., *as far as* into the inheritance of the holy land in the Israelite portion, our enemies being destroyed [n: (Ephes. II)].
