## CHAPTER XI.

VERS. 1.-- *And when Jabin had heard,* etc. [n: (ADAMANT., hom. 24 in Jos.)] When Israel according to the flesh read these Scriptures before the coming of Christ etc., *as far as* that they may assault us who follow Jesus our Saviour, but the Lord says: *Fear not before their face,* etc. [var: Vulg. Jos 11:6 *Ne timeas eos*, "Fear them not"]

VERS. 11.-- *For to-morrow at this same hour. [n: (ADAMAN., hom. 14 in Jos.)]* I see that to-day we cannot crush them all, nor slay them every one: on the morrow they shall be destroyed, that is, after the consummation [0513D] of the world, when it shall be said to them: *Go into everlasting fire which God hath prepared for the devil and his angels [n: (Matth. XXV)]*. [var: Vulg. Mt 25:41 *Discedite a me maledicti in ignem aeternum, qui paratus est diabolo, et angelis ejus*, "Depart from me, you cursed, into everlasting fire which was prepared for the devil and his angels"] For then, if we shall have conquered, following Jesus our leader, we shall receive the kingdom which God hath prepared for his saints through Jesus Christ.

[n: (ID., hom. 15.)] Unless these wars bore the figure of spiritual things, etc., *as far as* Jesus therefore did as the Lord had commanded him.

[n: (ORIG.)] This therefore Jesus fulfilled according to the commandment of God, etc., *as far as* which is Christ, and, at his own bidding, to be slaughtered, that nothing may remain in us that breathes.

VERS. 12.-- *And all,* etc. [n: (ADAM.)] In us all sin has reigned and has been in all, etc., [0514A] *as far as* nor dost thou fulfil the precept which the Lord Jesus gave.

*As Moses had commanded him,* etc. This is the word of the law, according to that: *They have Moses and the prophets, let them hear them [n: (Luc. XVI)]*. The law therefore has commanded us to slay all the kings of sin, who provoke us to sin. So did Jesus, nor did he transgress in anything. For it is he who destroys the vices in us and the kingdoms of sin. Christ also did all things that Moses commanded in the law, whence: *God sent his Son, made of a woman [n: (Gal. IV)]*, etc. For he fulfilled all things that the law commanded, that he might redeem us from the curse of the law, who says: *I came not to destroy the law, but to fulfil [n: (Matth. V)]*.

VERS. 15.-- *As the Lord had commanded Moses his servant, [0514B] so did Moses command. [n: (AUG., quaest. 16 in Jos.)]* It is not to be thought cruelty that Jesus left none alive in the cities delivered to him, because God had commanded these things. But they who here judge that God himself was cruel, and therefore are unwilling to believe that the true God was the author of the Old Testament, judge as perversely of the works of God as of the sins of men, not knowing of what each man is worthy, and thinking it a great evil when men doomed to fall are cast down and mortals die.

VERS. 19.-- *There was not. [n: (AUG., quaest. 17.)]* It is asked: how is this true, since neither afterwards from the time of the judges nor of the kings could the Hebrews take absolutely all the cities of those seven nations? But either it is to be understood thus, that Jesus came against no city in war [0514C] which he did not take, or certainly that none of them was taken, but of those which were in the regions mentioned above. For the regions have been enumerated in which were the cities concerning which that conclusion was made, and he took them all in war.

[n: (ADAM., hom. 15.)] And Jesus took in war all who had gone out to war, etc., *as far as* when we do not their works.

[n: (AUG., quaest. 18 in Jos.)] Because it was brought about by the Lord that their heart was strengthened, etc., *as far as* they should take partly by fighting.

VERS. 23.-- *And the land rested. [n: (ORIG., homil. 15.)]* Not under Jesus the son of Nave, under whom it almost never ceased from wars, but under Jesus Christ. For thou hast come to Jesus, and in baptism hast obtained the remission of sins [0514D]; now in thee the flesh does not fight against the spirit, and the spirit against the flesh [n: (Galat. V)]. Thy land therefore has ceased from wars, if thou so carry about the death of Jesus in thy body that, all battles in thee ceasing, thou be made a true peacemaker, and be called a son of God. But this shall be when thou hast finished the wars, and hast subdued thine adversaries: then shalt thou rest under thy vine, which is Christ, and under thy fig tree, which is the Holy Ghost.

## CHAPTER XII.

VERS. 1.-- [n: (HIER., de Loc. Hebr.)] Astaroth Carnaim, the land of the giants, formerly, upon the brow of the Sodomites whom Chodorlaomor slew. And there are to this day two forts in Batanea of this name, [0515A] separated from one another by nine miles. Edrai, where Og king of Basan, a giant, was slain. But now it is Adara, a notable city of Arabia, at the twenty-fourth milestone from Osdra. Esebon, the city of Sehon king of the Amorrhites, in the land of Galaad: which, though it had before belonged to the Moabites, was possessed by the Amorrhites by the right of war. Salecha, a city of king Og, in the region of Basanitis. Machati, a city of the Amorrhites upon the Jordan, near mount Ermon. Jarimuth, a city which Jesus overthrew, in the tribe of Juda, distant at the fourth milestone from Eleutheropolis, near the village of Eschaol. Lachis, in the tribe of Juda. Isaias and Jeremias make mention of this, and now it is a village at the seventh mile from Eleutheropolis. Erma was in the lot of Simeon, or of Juda, etc.

## CHAPTER XIII.

[0515B]

[n: (ADAMAN., hom. 16 in Jos.)] Priests and elders are not so called for length of life, etc., *as far as* as he is first in all things, because he is the head of all.

VERS. 1.-- *And the land.* [n: (ID.)] Another reading: *And there was left very much land.* It was said above, etc., *as far as* but he who shall be subjected of necessity, when the last enemy death shall be destroyed, shall not be in grace.

VERS. 6.-- *And all the Sidonians. [n: (ADAM., hom. 16.)]* When Jesus had enumerated very many nations, of the Sidonians alone he said: *And all the Sidonians will I destroy from before the face of the children of Israel.* But the Sidonians are interpreted hunters. These are the demons of whom the Prophet says: *They have prepared snares for my feet [n: (Psal. LVI)]*. These [0515C] the Lord destroys, that there be none to stretch out snares and nets for the deceiving of souls: and that every man may rest under his own vine and under his own fig tree.

VERS. 7.-- *And now.* But after this it is read that Moses gave an inheritance to certain men, and afterwards Jesus, according to the commandment of the Lord, distributed the inheritance. By Moses understand the law: they who lived according to the law received the inheritance first, but outside the land of promise. And when they had received it, they do not possess it, nor divide it among themselves; but they wait until it be distributed to them by lot through Jesus. Hence the Apostle says: *All these, having testimony through faith, have not yet obtained the promises, God providing something better for us, that they should not be consummated without us [n: (Hebr. XI)]*. [var: Vulg. Hbr 11:39–40 *Et hi omnes testimonio fidei probati, non acceperunt repromissionem, Deo pro nobis melius aliquid providente, ut non sine nobis consummarentur*, "approved by the testimony of faith, received not the promise"] They have therefore [0515D] now their promise and have rested, judged worthy through Moses to receive their inheritance, but still they fight for those who war under Jesus. For all the Fathers who slept before us fight with us and help us with their prayers, whence: *That synagogue shall lick up this synagogue, as the calf licks up the green herb of the field.* [var: Vulg. Nm 22:4 *Ita delebit hic populus omnes, qui in nostris finibus commorantur, quomodo solet bos herbas usque ad radices carpere*, "as the ox is wont to crop the herbs to the very roots"] For the synagogue of the Lord which went before us shall consume with mouth and tongue every contrary synagogue, that is, with prayers it shall consume our adversaries. With prayers therefore, and with meditation of the word of God, and with work and right understanding, must we fight.

[n: (ADAM., hom. 17.)] As shadows serve for a pattern of heavenly things, etc., *as far as* they receive the inheritance through Jesus.

[0516A] *To the tribe.* But to the Levites neither Moses gave an inheritance, nor Jesus. *Because God himself is their inheritance.* These are perhaps they who in the Church go before others by strength of mind and by the grace of merits. These are perhaps they who among the people of them that are to be saved bestow labour upon wisdom and knowledge, and keep their mind pure, and adorn [it] with all excellent virtues, and teach the simpler how they may come to salvation: whose inheritance is the Lord himself, who is wisdom, which they have loved before all others.

VERS. 15.-- *To the tribe of the children of Ruben,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Yet I find some difference in those things which Moses distributed, etc., *as far as: The blind man offering guidance to the blind shall fall into the pit with him.* [var: Vulg. Mt 15:14 *caecus autem si caeco ducatum praestet, ambo in foveam cadunt*, "if the blind lead the blind, both fall into the pit"]

VERS. 32.-- *This possession. [n: (ADAM., hom. 18 in Jos.)]* [0516B] Let us learn from the law, etc., *as far as: Jesus and Eleazar together for the dividing of the land.*
