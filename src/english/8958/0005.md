## CHAPTER XVIII.

VERS. 6.-- *I will cast lots for you. [n: (ADAM., hom. 23 in Jos.)]* In the custom of men, etc., *as far as* that the earthly should not be given to another, which is a figure of the heavenly

[n: (ID., hom. 23.)] Bethlehem also is not without a sure [0519B] reason set apart to the fountain of Juda, etc., *as far as* Whence he says to Timothy: *Be mindful of the words which I commit to faithful men, and to those who are fit to teach others [n: (II Tim. II)]*. [var: Vulg. 2Tim 2:2 *et quae audisti a me per multos testes, haec commenda fidelibus hominibus, qui idonei erant et alios docere*, "the things thou hast heard of me by many witnesses, the same commend to faithful men"]

## CHAPTER XIX.

VERS. 1.-- *Of the children of Simeon. [n: (AUG., quaest. 20 in Jos.)]* And the Amorrhite remained to dwell in Elon, etc., *as far as* which he would not do.

[n: (ADAM., hom. 24 in Jos.)] According to the LXX the Amorrhite remained to dwell in Elon and in Sannanim, etc., *as far as* And the children of Ephrem went away to travel through the land according to their borders.

VERS. 49.-- *And when. [n: (ADAM., hom. 24.)]* See the meekness and the humility of Jesus, etc., *as far as* that [0519C] the Lord Jesus may gladly enter into the lodging of our heart.

## CHAPTER XX.

VERS. 2.-- *Separate the cities of the fugitives,* etc. [n: (ORIG., hom. 34 in Num.)] Not all murderers flee to the cities of refuge, but they who have committed murder unknowingly. For there are some sins which, if we commit them of set purpose and will, make us murderers, and there are others which, if we admit them unknowingly, there is decreed for us, I believe, and prepared by the commandment of God, some place where we ought to dwell for a certain time, if we have committed sins not voluntary, if yet we be found clean from those sins which have been committed with the will. And for this cause certain cities of refuge are set apart.

## CHAPTER XXI.

[0519D]

VERS. 1.-- *They came near. [n: (ADAM., hom. 25 in Jos.)]* All the children of Israel received their lots in the land, etc., *as far as* and he shall sit down with Abraham, with Isaac and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven.

[n: (ADAMANT. hom. 26.)] It was said above, etc., *as far as* he filled the world, and bestowed spiritual gifts upon them that believe.

VERS. 39.-- *And so.* [n: (ISID.)] In that the Levites receive forty-two cities, the preaching of the saints is signified. For they themselves possess the doctrine which consists [0520A] of the decalogue of the law and of the fourfold number of the Gospel, as it were having four times ten cities: to which two also are added; because all the things that they preach, they announce in a moral and in a mystical sense.

VERS. 41.-- *And the Lord gave. [n: (AUG., quaest. 21.)]* It is asked, since Israel not only unto the day of the death of Jesus, etc., *as far as* it had already been given for a certain profit of exercise.

VERS. 42.-- *And none of their enemies durst resist them. [n: (ID., quaest. 22.)]* In that he says, etc., *as far as* before their own places to be defended were divided to the several tribes.

## CHAPTER XXII.

VERS. 1.-- *At the same time. [n: (ADAMAN., hom. 26 in Job.)]* Jesus gathers together the children of Ruben and Gad, etc., *as far as* all assault ceasing.

[0520B]VERS. 10.-- *And when. [n: (ADAMANT., hom. 26.)]* The children of Ruben and Gad and the half tribe of Manasse, etc., *as far as* there is made therefore one flock and one shepherd: they just, and these just Christians.

VERS. 17.-- *Many.* [n: (ADAM.)] The people therefore sometimes fell in the desert and died, etc., *as far as* and where the true Israel is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

VERS. 29.-- *An altar being built. [n: (AUG., quaest. 32 in Jos.)] And in the sacrifices of our peace offerings.* Because the sacrifices are spoken of in the plural, etc., *as far as* For he himself alone is the Saviour

## CHAPTER XXIII.

VERS. 14.-- *Behold I shall this day enter in. (AUG., quaest. [0520C] 24.)* [var: Vulg. Jos 23:14 *En ego hodie ingredior viam universae terrae*, "Behold, this day I go into the way of all the earth" — Migne's future is the reading Augustine's question turns on] But in that Jesus says, etc., *as far as* therefore here also this word has been so interpreted.

## CHAPTER XXIV.

VERS. 3.-- *I took therefore your father.* [n: (AUG.)] In that the LXX have, etc., *as far as* which is the true seed of Abraham.

VERS. 11.-- *They fought. [n: (AUG., quaest. 26 in Jos.)]* With the gates shut they defended themselves by the circuit of the walls, etc., *as far as* an armed defence.

VERS. 12.-- *Hornets. [n: (AUG., quaest 27.)]* The LXX: *Wasps.* That is perhaps, by transference, the sharpest stings of fear, by which fears, being in some sort pricked, they should flee, or the hidden spirits of the air, which is said in the Psalms, *by evil angels,* unless someone say that not all the things which were done are written, and be willing that true [0520D] wasps be understood.

VERS. 19.-- *You shall not be able. [n: (AUG., quaest. 28 in Jos.)]* You shall not be able to serve the Lord, etc., *as far as* and afterwards grace might more abound through Jesus Christ, who is the end of the law.

VERS. 23.-- *Take away. [n: (AUG., quaest. 29 in Jos.)]* What is it that he says, etc., *as far as: face to face we may know him [n: (I Cor. XIII)]*.

*[n: (AUG., quaest. 30 in Jos.)] And Jesus ordained a testament,* etc. [var: Vulg. Jos 24:25 *Percussit ergo Josue in die illo foedus*, "Josue therefore made a covenant that day"] They who search these words more deeply, etc., *as far as: but all forsook me, which let it not be laid to their charge [n: (II Tim.
