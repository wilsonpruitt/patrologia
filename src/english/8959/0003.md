## CHAPTER VIII.

VERS. 27.-- *And Gedeon made an ephod thereof,* etc., [n: (AUG., quaest. 41 in Jud.)] It is asked what the Ephod or Ephot is, etc., *as far as* so that the numerous offspring of his sons should thus perish, as Scripture afterwards testifies.

[n: (AUG.)] It is to be understood that it was said by way of prolepsis that Gedeon made the Ephod against the law of God out of the gold that had been carried off when the enemies were vanquished, so that it might be told at one time both whence the gold was and what was made of it. For this sin was committed at the end of the days of Gedeon: when there followed the evils which Scripture appends, after it had said for how many years in the days of Gedeon the land was at rest.

[0528B] *After that Gedeon was dead. [n: (ID., quaest. 43.)] And it came to pass, when Gedeon was dead, that the children of Israel turned away, and committed fornication after the Baalim, and set up for themselves Baalberith as a covenant, that he might be to them for a God,* etc. [var: Vulg. Jdc. 8:33 *Postquam autem mortuus est Gedeon, aversi sunt filii Israel, et fornicati sunt cum Baalim. Percusseruntque cum Baal foedus, ut esset eis in deum*, "And after Gedeon was dead, the children of Israel turned away, and committed fornication with Baalim. And they made a covenant with Baal, that he should be their god"] Both Baalim and Baalberith are to be understood as idols. Greater therefore is the transgression and the fornication on the part of the people after the death of Gedeon, than while he was alive, on account of the Ephod. And although this had been unlawfully done, yet it was of the sacred things of the tabernacle: but this idolatry has not the false defence of the religion of the fathers. Whence, even if the Ephod was made not at the end of the time of Gedeon, but before, God bore it so patiently that peace continued in the land, because although that had been done which he had forbidden, yet there had not been a far departing from him who had commanded that some such thing should be made in his own tabernacle to his own honour. [0528C] But now he would not have the graver things committed, and the open fornication of the people after idols, go unpunished.

## CHAPTER IX.

VERS. 5.-- *And Joatham remained.* [n: (ISID.)] The younger son, who by fleeing escapes the sword of the enemy, signifies the remnant of Israel that shall believe in the last time and shall escape the sword of Antichrist, he went up to the top of mount Garizim: because one can neither prophesy nor escape the persecution of Antichrist, unless he has first gone up into the mount of blessing, that is, into the loftiness of faith and of the virtues: for this is mount Garizim, which by Moses is appointed for blessing.

[n: (ISID., in Jud. tom. V.)] If this deed were not mystical, etc., *as far as* he will bring in a persecution, that he may slay [0528D] all the saints.

VERS. 10.-- *And they said. [n: (ID., ibid.)]* The trees of the wood are unfruitful men, etc., *as far as* and brought through the most sweet fatness of Christ with glory and honour.

[n: (ID., ibid.)] But the vine is Christ, who said: *I am the true vine [n: (Joan. XV)]*. Because therefore the people that serves Antichrist is given over to a reprobate sense, that it may believe a lie, that is, Antichrist — because it would not believe the truth, that is, Christ — it is rejected from the kingdom of the vine, that is, of Christ; and from the olive, that is, the grace of the Holy Spirit, and from the fig, that is, the sweetness of the divine law.

*And they said.* [n: (ID.)] The bramble is a kind of briar, which they commonly call the bear's thorn, exceedingly rough [0529A] and prickly, by which Antichrist is signified, who shall lay waste the human race with all roughness and savagery: *and fire shall come forth from the bramble,* [var: Vulg. Jdc. 9:15 *egrediatur ignis de rhamno*, "let fire come out from the bramble"] that is, iniquity from Antichrist, and it shall devour together with him all who trust in him.

VERS. 23.-- *And he sent. [n: (AUG., quaest. 45 in Jud.)] And God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and the men of the Sichimites.* [var: Vulg. Jdc. 9:23 *Misitque Dominus spiritum pessimum inter Abimelech et habitatores Sichem*, "And the Lord sent a very evil spirit between Abimelech and the inhabitants of Sichem"] This word, etc., *as far as* they have rendered it also *immisit.*

VERS. 32.-- *Arise. [n: (ID., quaest. 46.)] And now arise by night, thou and thy people with thee, and lie in wait.* [var: Vulg. Jdc. 9:32 *Surge itaque nocte cum populo qui tecum est, et latita in agro*, "Arise therefore in the night with the people that is with thee, and lie hid in the field"] Certain Latin copies have *maturabis,* etc., *as far as* God created the sun on the fourth day.

## CHAPTER X.

VERS. 1.-- *After Abimelech. (AUG., quaest. 47 in [0529B] Jud.)* How the uncle of Abimelech was a man of the tribe of Issachar, etc., *as far as* that the flesh of the Lord should be propagated not only from the royal, but also from the priestly stock.
