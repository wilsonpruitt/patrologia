## CHAPTER XVI.

[0532B]VERS. 1.-- *He went also,* etc. [n: (GREG., Homil. 21 in Evang.)] Whom in this deed Samson, etc., *as far as* and by ascending he pierced through the kingdoms of the heavens.

VERS. 19.-- *And she called a barber. [n: (ISID., in Jud., tom. V, cap. 6.)]* That the crafty woman shaved the head of Samson, etc., *as far as* and, the contest being made ready, he shall in the end most mightily triumph over the demons.

[n: (GREG., lib. XXVII Moral., cap. 13.)] Samson, his eyes lost, is assigned by the Allophyli to the mill, etc., *as far as* they send [him] outside into the round of toil.

VERS. 30.-- *And dying he slew many more. [n: (GREG., lib. XXIX Moral., cap. 7.)]* In this place Christ is signified by Samson, etc., *as far as* by dying he laid them all low together.

## CHAPTER XVII.

VERS. 5.-- *And he made an ephod and theraphim,* etc. [0532C] [n: (HIERON., epist. 130, tom. I.)] In the volume of Judges, etc., *as far as* he is shown to have done it by the teraphim.

## CHAPTER XVIII

VERS. 7.-- *They came to Lais,* etc. [n: (HIERON., lib. de loc. Hebr.)] Laisan the children of Dan, etc., *as far as* near Esthaol.

## CHAPTER XIX.

VERS. 25.-- *He brought out to them his concubine,* etc. [n: (AUG. de Civ., cap. 34.)] 'Concubine' is sometimes used for 'wife'. For both Agar and Cethura are called both wives and concubines of Abraham.

## CHAPTER XX

VERS. 18.-- *They consulted God. [n: (GREG., lib. 24 Moral., cap. 13.)]* He who does not judge himself, etc., *as far as* but let it be rigid against itself, and submissive indeed to all good men.

## CHAPTER XXI.

*The children of Israel also swore in Maspha,* etc.
