## CHAPTER V.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *If a soul shall have sinned,* etc. [n: (AUG. quaest. 1 in Levit.)] He seems to say that a man sins, etc., [0309A] *as far as* but he straightway subjoined: *The soul that shall have touched anything unclean,* etc.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISICH.)] *If he shall have sinned,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He does not repeat what he said above, etc., *as far as* because we ought not to consent to the sins or the error of another, but to reprove them.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The soul who shall have touched.* [n: (ISICH.)] Not of the body, etc., *as far as* but he can say with David: *Our soul hath been delivered as a sparrow out of the snare of the fowlers [n: (Psal. CXXIII)]*.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (GREG. lib. XXXII Moral., cap. 4 tom. II.)] To utter an oath is to bind ourselves by vow to the divine servitude, etc., *as far as* we burn with the fire of grief.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 4.-- *The soul that shall have sworn,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 2 in Levit.)] What is it that for a perjury kept silent, or for a carcass touched, or for anything unclean, no sacrifice [0309B] is enjoined? but that for the fact that anyone swears a falsehood unknowingly, he decrees a she-lamb or a she-goat to be offered? or is this sacrifice to be understood for all the things aforesaid? ⟦s3⟧ For when all had been set forth, he brought in the sacrifice of expiation.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He shall have understood his offence.* [n: (ISICH.)] It is to be noted that an offence, etc., *as far as* as the following words show.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *But if he shall not have been able,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Let us add this, that if a prince shall have sinned in any of these things, he should offer a he-goat of the goats, namely a penance worthy of himself, who ought neither to have sinned, nor to have shared it with others, nor to have provoked his subjects to sinning. ⟦s2⟧ What therefore ought not to have happened, he left undiscussed.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But if,* etc. [n: (ID.)] He who cannot afflict himself continually, etc., *as far as* because he sacrifices himself most fully [0309C] to God.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *He shall turn back the head* [n: (GREG., hom. in Ezech. )] The death of Christ unto the joining of his own body, etc., *as far as* he joined us all to himself more truly in that very death of his.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (GREG.)] *He shall turn back,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ In the sacrifice of God we ought to be a turtledove, that our head may be turned back to the little wings, that is, the senses to the virtues. ⟦s3⟧ By the head we understand the mind, because as the head rules the body, so the mind rules the actions. ⟦s4⟧ But the head cleaves to the neck, and is not cut off utterly, because our mind is to be separated from the delight of the flesh, not from the necessary care of the flesh, and let us not make provision for the flesh in its desires [n: (Rom. XIII)]. ⟦s5⟧ Let the head of the turtledove therefore be partly cut off, and partly cleave to the neck.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0309D] *Let it be broken off,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He wills that we should not serve the body, but be broken off or turned back, lest the law of sin reign in our members. ⟦s3⟧ But because we ought not to hold our body in hatred, nor to reckon it, after the heretics, a making of the devil, but to set it under the mind, he does not command the body to be separated from the neck, for both are the creature of one and the same.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The foundation,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ LXX, the base, which is the Gospel, because, according to the intention of the sacrifice of Christ and of the Gospel, the sacrifice for sins ought to be offered. ⟦s3⟧ Whence he adds: *Because it is for sin,* for nothing so snatches a man from sin as the imitation of Christ and obedience to the Gospel preaching.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0310A] *But if he shall not have been able.* ⟦s2⟧ Because not every time is easy for penance, when namely we abuse penance itself, and destroy its fruit by returning to sin, the lawgiver hints at this, saying: *But if his hand shall not have been able,* etc.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG. quaest. 8 in Levit., tom. III.)] It is asked whether what has been said is to be taken everywhere, etc., *as far as* that all the rest should pertain from these to that generality.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *An ephi.* [n: (ISICH.)] It is a measure of three measures, etc., *as far as* that they should not perish who shall have been under his hand.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He shall not put upon it,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if to say: this is not perfect penance, and therefore it has no oil, that is, the spiritual gladness of cheerfulness, nor frankincense, because neither [0310B] ought he to have confidence in his prayers: but let him who offers the oblation hand it over to the priest, that is, let him commit the hope of salvation to the divine mercy.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Which is for sin.* ⟦s2⟧ Because he knows not whether the prayer of him who often transgresses is of a good odour. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *Who knoweth if God will turn and forgive, and leave behind him a blessing [n: (Joan. III)]*? ⟦s4⟧ He does not therefore suffer them to despair, nor yet to presume, lest they be negligent again.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *A handful.* ⟦s2⟧ In the handful Scripture is signified, wherewith it is written. ⟦s3⟧ And it is written: *I say not to thee seven times, but unto seventy times seven times [n: (Matth. XXVIII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ By the handful therefore he hints that God is to be entreated, that he may remember his written mercy, that he may grant it to us: as he bids it be granted to our brethren; for this [0310C] shall the great priest cleanse the one who prays.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *If a soul transgressing,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] From every sin abstinence must be had, but most of all from that which is committed against holy things: and how horrible it is, the oblation testifies. ⟦s2⟧ For nowhere from the beginning of the history, save in this place, is a ram commanded to be offered, in which the necessity and the usefulness of the sacrifice is set forth: here no he-goat is offered, no she-goat, no sheep, no pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The ceremonies, through,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] There are many kinds of sanctification. ⟦s2⟧ There is the oil, wherewith the tabernacle alone is anointed; and the incense, of which it is said: *Whosoever shall make such a compound, shall perish from among his people:* the loaves also of proposition only the males eat, who are of the stock of Aaron. ⟦s3⟧ But mystically he sins in [0310D] those things which have been sanctified, who usurps anything irreverently in a holy place, or handles divine things unworthily.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *With two sicles.* ⟦s2⟧ Two sicles are the two precepts of charity, wherewith the sinner buys for himself the confidence of remission. ⟦s3⟧ For penance is worth nothing without the grace of remission; whence, according to the LXX, the ram is bought with fifty sicles, because through penance and the observance of the twofold charity perfect rest is acquired, when the remission of sins is given. ⟦s4⟧ LXX, *with fifty sicles of silver, by the holy sicle.* ⟦s5⟧ For no one obtains the sacrifice of the Lord save through the Holy Spirit, because *no man can say the Lord Jesus, but by the Holy Ghost [n: (I Cor. XII)]*. ⟦s6⟧ But fifty [0311A] signifies the coming of the Holy Spirit, because on the day of Pentecost, that is, the fiftieth, he came to the apostles. ⟦s7⟧ LXX: *by the holy sicle.* ⟦s8⟧ In the holy sicle he signified the power and the working of the Holy Spirit. ⟦s9⟧ *A siculus is of 20 obols,* a man of twenty is not liable for sin: whence they who went out of Egypt, when they were of twenty years, did not undergo the punishment of the people's sin [n: (Num. XIV)]; for the spiritual man is indeed clean from every sin.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And the fifth part,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] He who with a holy vessel or food, etc., *as far as* for so shall he make entreaty for our sin.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *If a soul shall have sinned,* etc. [n: (ID.)]. ⟦s2⟧ He seems to have forgotten what he had commanded: for the sins for which he commanded a she-goat or a sheep to be offered, now he seems to command a ram to be offered; but he has neither forgotten [0311B] the former things, nor commanded contrary things: for there by the soul he signified the Gentile: but here him who through ignorance transgresses the commandments of the law.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG. quaest. 7 in Levit.)] It is asked how this offence is distinguished from those which he has generally embraced, etc., *as far as* or some things in sacred services.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *He shall offer a ram,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Above he commanded that the penitent should offer a she-goat, here he bids a ram be immolated, because it profits nothing to immolate the harshness of penance, unless thou shalt have determined to have full confidence in the immolation of Christ.
