## CHAPTER VIII. (cont.)

*He poured out upon the altar.* For when he drank, and gave the apostles to drink, he poured out the intelligible blood upon the altar, that is, upon his own body. But the Church is the body of Christ; whence Mark: *And taking it, giving thanks he gave [0322C] to them, and they all drank of it, and he said to them: This is my blood of the new testament, which shall be shed for many [n: (Marc. XIV)]*.

*But the fat.* What these things signify has been said in the peace offering, or in that of the pacific victims. But the LXX gave, for the tail, the loin; for the entrails, the little belly; and for the caul of the liver, the fin of the liver: and not in vain are they even now commemorated in the ram.

VERS. 26.-- *The basket.* LXX, the basket: of the consummation, which is the tongue of the Lord, which brought in the chief and perfect doctrine. For he came not to destroy the law, but to fulfil it [n: (Matth. XIII)]. This is rightly called a basket because of the weave of his doctrine, since he had said many things in parables. And it was [0322D] both the dish and the table of the whole doctrine. Rightly therefore he sets forth the mystical bread out of the basket of the consummation.

*The bread.* The apostolic doctrine, and the legal, and the prophetic. For the law, and the prophets, and the apostles make his memorial. For the law says: *This is the bread which the Lord hath given you [n: (Exod. XXVI)]*. And the prophet: *His bread shall be given,* etc. [n: (Isa. XXXIII.)] [var: Vulg. Is 33:16 has *panis ei datus est*, "bread is given him"] But the prophetic and the legal doctrine nourish less than the apostolic. Whence Paul: *God hath set in the church first apostles, then prophets [n: (I Cor. XII)]*. But the apostles draw the testimonies of the law and of the prophets, by which the little cakes and the wafers are set beneath them.

[0323A] *And the shoulder [n: (GREG. lib. I Regist., ep. 24.)]* The priest by the precept of the law the right shoulder, etc., *as far as* and Moses says in what follows.

VERS 39.-- *He took the little breast. [n: (AUG., quaest. 23 in Levit.)]* Above, because he was commanding generally, etc., *as far as* for from the beginning it is said: *Let him be to thee for the things which pertain to the people, thou to him for the things which pertain to God.*

VERS. 30.-- *And thou shalt take of the ointment. [n: (ID., quaest. 24.)]* It is asked who anointed the priest after the death of Moses, etc., *as far as* by serving, not by excelling.

*He sprinkled,* etc. Whatsoever good things are in us are to be led back to him as to our head; whence: *For them do I sanctify myself, that they also may be sanctified in truth.* Openly therefore he called his passion *sanctification,* [0323B] which sanctification is given to us.

*And upon the sons* [n: (AUG.)] It did not appear which of the sons should succeed the high priest. For Scripture does not appoint the firstborn or the elder, unless it was done in some divine judgment, although it seems to have come of contention, so that afterwards there were made several high priests, because, when the more excellent were contending, for the sake of ending the strife the honour itself was bestowed on several.

VERS. 31.-- *The loaves also.* He commanded the flesh to be eaten with the loaves, that we may understand the body of Christ, who is flesh, and the bread which came down from heaven [n: (Joan. VI)], and that this ought to be celebrated only in the Church.

VERS. 32.-- *But whatsoever.* We see this to be done in the Church, and the things which shall have remained unconsumed to be given to the fire. But he does not say, for however many days they shall have remained unconsumed, [0323C] so that always, if they shall have remained, they be given to the fire: hinting that, if we fall short of the eating of the sacrifice, and are not able to search out whether the things which we see are the very body of the Lord, we should nevertheless not leave them, but give them to the fire, that the Holy Spirit may eat what we cannot. And he eats, when we consider that the things which we cannot do are possible to him.

VERS. 33.-- *Of the door.* It signifies that during the whole time of the present life one must not depart from the tabernacle, but must persevere in the service of God.

[n: (AUG., quaest. 24 in Levit.)] At the door of the tabernacle of the testimony, etc., *as far as* for the dwelling received this name.

*For seven,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)]. The fiftieth, the number seven [0323D] being multiplied into itself, etc., *as far as* keeping the watches of the Lord, etc.

VERS. 36.-- *And they did,* etc. Aaron is joined with his sons, and does not go out from the door of the tabernacle. For he himself was to be preached, and was to preach in them, as he himself consecrates and is consecrated in his members.
