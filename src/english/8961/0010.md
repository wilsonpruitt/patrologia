## CHAPTER IX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *On the eighth day,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The eighth day after the dedication signifies the day of Pentecost, on which the Holy Spirit came [n: (Act. II)]. ⟦s3⟧ But the seven days from the dedication intimate seven times seven days, which were from the resurrection until Pentecost, namely 49, the very day of Pentecost being added as an eighth.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Pentecost, which fell on the day of the resurrection, [0324A] bears the figure of what is to come, and of the consummation of the present age, because it is rolled round through seven days. ⟦s2⟧ For it behoved that the Spirit should come on the same day on which Christ rose. ⟦s3⟧ Then he bids Aaron and his sons and the elders be called: for then was the assembly of the Church convoked, in which is Aaron, that is, Christ, and his sons who are of a lofty manner of life, and therefore ministers of the priesthood, and the elders, that is, the presbyters: for of these the body of the Church consists.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Take of,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Aaron takes a calf for a holocaust, and a ram, and offers them to the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ For then Christ made his sacrifice known, when the Spirit, coming, gathered Parthians, Medes, Elamites, and the firstfruits of all nations to the upper room of the apostles [n: (Act. II)].

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0324B]Aaron offers a calf and a ram when the doctors of the Church announce the mysteries of the passion, and make known that they were celebrated for the salvation of the human race.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Take ye.* ⟦s2⟧ The apostles offered the he-goat on the day of Pentecost, when Peter, announcing repentance, said: *Be ye converted and do penance.* [var: Vulg. Act 2:38 has *Poenitentiam agite*; these are the words of Ez 18:30]

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG., quaest. 26 in Levit.)] Take ye one he-goat of the goats, etc., *as far as* but where all are, there also are the individuals, though it is not convertible.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *This word,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The word, that is, the thing commanded. ⟦s3⟧ *Do this in commemoration of me [n: (Luc. XXII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ But he says the word, because more is looked to in the word, or because in the word stands the dispensation of this mystery, and chiefly by the Lord's word transferring the things which [0324C] appear into something else, greater and intelligible. ⟦s5+6⟧ Whence of the manna also he says: *This is the bread which the Lord hath given you to eat, this is the word which the Lord hath appointed [n: (Exod. XVI)]*. [var: Vulg. Ex 16:15-16 *Iste est panis quem Dominus dedit vobis ad vescendum* … *hic est sermo quem praecepit Dominus*] In these things, therefore, he signifies that the Holy Spirit came upon them as they were performing the holy mystery and celebrating the Lord's supper. ⟦s7⟧ For it was the Lord's day, on which the apostles were bound to celebrate the sacred mysteries: whose traditions we follow in appointing the Lord's day for the divine assemblies. ⟦s8⟧ In like manner we find Christ made manifest after the resurrection through the mystical supper and the breaking of bread.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *And offer for sin,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 27 in Levit.)]. ⟦s2⟧ Another reading, etc., *as far as* it is not necessary that it be laid upon the holocaust.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0324D] *Offer the holocaust,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He does not say the holocaust of consummation, as above, when it was offered that he might be consummated by sanctification, that he might exercise the priesthood which he was exercising. ⟦s3⟧ It is indeed to be noted that when sacrifices are offered for the people, sacrifices were commanded to be offered both for sin, and a holocaust, and a sacrifice of peace offerings. ⟦s4⟧ But for the priest there was offered a sacrifice for sin, and a holocaust of consummation, not of peace offerings. ⟦s5⟧ But that of consummation was offered when the priests were sanctified. ⟦s6⟧ And these things Moses offered for Aaron and his sons; but afterwards Aaron, now exercising the priesthood, is commanded to offer for himself a calf for sin, and a ram for a holocaust not of consummation; for then it was offered that he might be consummated by sanctification, and might be able to exercise the priesthood, [0325A] which, when he was exercising it, there was no need to consummate again.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *And forthwith,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] For what cause these sacrifices also are performed here we have said above, etc. ⟦s2⟧ *as far as* whence in the person of the priest we discern both to have been offered.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG., ubi supra )]. ⟦s2+3⟧ It moves one also that, when Aaron was performing the gifts of the people which are recalled above, etc., *as far as* both from each living creature, as though he had said little breasts.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *And for sin,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] Of what these bear the figure, etc., *as far as* and they ceased not to meditate the things which they were teaching the Church.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Stretching forth his hand,* etc. [n: (AUG., Quaest. 28 in Levit.)] Standing at the altar he was serving, etc., *as far as* who there [0325B] forbade there to be steps.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *And so, these being completed, and the rest.* [n: (ISICH.)] Plainly he wishes to set forth the whole figure of the Church, etc., *as far as* the things that follow show.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *And Moses and Aaron entering into the tabernacle of the testimony,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Because concerning the intelligible tabernacle, etc., *as far as* he is fit to bestow the blessing.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And entering in.* ⟦s2⟧ He set *entering in* first, and subjoins *going out,* because the priest enters the tabernacle when he prays for himself or for the people, or gives himself to divine contemplation; he goes out when he teaches, or blesses the people, or when he returns to his conscience by searching himself, lest perchance he give offence in his preaching.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And the glory of the Lord appeared.* ⟦s2⟧ In the form of fire, as [0325C] on the day of Pentecost, whence he subjoins: *And there went out a fire from the Lord.* ⟦s3⟧ Whence Luke also: *There appeared to them parted tongues, as it were of fire.*

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To all the multitude,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ To which Luke says the like: *And this voice being made, the multitude came together, and was confounded in mind [n: (Act. II)]*; not only came together, but was terrified. ⟦s3⟧ Whence here there follows: And they were astonished and wondered, etc.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Which were upon the altar.* ⟦s2⟧ He finds that it is necessary to set the aforesaid sacrifices, and the expositions of them, upon the intelligible altar of the body of Christ, that our intention may sigh thither, and then the nourishments of the intelligible fire, that is, of the Holy Spirit, will be at hand, of which it is said: *The Lord our God is a consuming fire [n: (Deut. IV)]*. [var: Vulg. Dt 4:24 *Dominus Deus tuus*, "thy God"; the form with *noster* is Heb 12:29]
