## CHAPTER XII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *If a woman having received seed. [n: (ISID., quaest. in Levit., tom. V.)]* The week is the present life, etc., *as far as* nor shall she be purged in the world to come.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0331D] *She shall be unclean.* [n: (ISICH.)] Because, that is, the sower is unclean, as the heir of Adam, who through his transgression was unclean, and so was mingled with his wife. ⟦s2⟧ For after he went out of paradise he knew his wife, and so is the human race propagated.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Seven days.* [n: (ID.)] Because Adam, etc., *as far as* when Adam, separated from paradise, fell down into this misery.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *On the eighth day,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The eighth day, etc., *as far as* But disobedience contracted for us uncleanness and filth.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Three and thirty days,* etc. [n: (ID.)] seven being added, etc., *as far as* for it is uncertain whether they will bear a female or a male.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0332A] [n: (AUG., quaest. 40 in Levit.)] What difference there is between the seven days, etc., *as far as* to eighty in the case of a female.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *She shall touch no holy thing.* ⟦s2⟧ What does he wish to be understood as the sanctuary, since none but the priests ought to enter the tabernacle, and that as far as the veil of the second: but beyond the veil, where the ark was, the high priest alone entered? ⟦s3⟧ Perhaps that before the tabernacle, where the altar of sacrifices is, could be called the sanctuary. ⟦s4⟧ For often the court also is called a holy place, when it is said: *In the holy place they shall eat them.* ⟦s5⟧ Thither perhaps the women entered, offering their gifts, which they were to lay upon the altar.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And when they shall be expired.* ⟦s2⟧ He opens his intention, namely, that natural motions do not make pollution, but sin, of which David says: *For behold I was conceived in iniquities [n: (Psal. L)]*, etc., [0332B] calling nature the mother, and signifying that the uncleanness of the sin of Adam and Eve has come down into the whole human race. ⟦s3⟧ Therefore Moses, the days of purification being completed, commanded a holocaust, and a sacrifice for sin, to be immolated; plainly showing that the image of God bestows perfection upon our race. ⟦s4⟧ For the days of cleansing show the formation, the holocaust the perfection.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG., ubi supra. )] Is it a sin to have borne a child? etc., *as far as* not that they should say over a son or over a daughter, but for a son or a daughter.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ But it is to be noted how poor the Lord willed to be born, so that there was not offered for him a lamb and a young pigeon or a turtle, but a pair of turtles or two young pigeons according to the Gospel, which the scripture of Leviticus then commanded to be offered, if the hand of the offerer had not [0332C] a lamb, which is great enough.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BEDA, in Lev., tom. II.)] Our Lord not only man, etc., *as far as* and afterwards it shall come into the heavenly Jerusalem, commended to the Lord by the victims of good works.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *From the issue,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As though someone should ask, from what shall she be cleansed who has brought forth lawfully? he says, from *the issue of her blood,* that is, from disobedience, whence uncleanness and punishment flowed forth, by the meditation and contemplation of the law, the neglect of which brought uncleanness upon men.
