## CHAPTER XV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISICH.)] The LXX thus, etc., *as far as* unto a perfect man, etc.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0339C]VERS. 2.-- *[n: (GREG., lib. XXXIII Moral., cap. 15, tom. II.)] The man that suffers,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ What is speech but seed? etc., *as far as* than the fruit of an everlasting reward.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISICH.)] Because there are many kinds of depraved teaching, etc., *as far as* if he shall have been a partaker of his teaching, following his disciples.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The saddle upon which,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] The LXX ἐπίσαγμα ὄνον, that is, the coverlet of an ass. ⟦s2⟧ By which is understood the more negligent disciple, and one to be changed in his soft heart. ⟦s3⟧ For saddle-cloths are soft.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *He shall number seven days.* [n: (ID.)] Because through the sevenfold Spirit remission of sins is given, of whose seven gifts they have need who are to be perfectly cleansed [0339D].

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Two turtledoves.* [n: (ID.)] A sacrifice worthy of doctors. ⟦s2⟧ For the turtledove signifies meditation, and the dove, frequently bearing young and simple, signifies the Holy Spirit. ⟦s3⟧ For a doctor must beget spiritually: worthily therefore did he dedicate this sacrifice to priests and to masters.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Before the Lord, that he may be cleansed.* ⟦s2⟧ According to the will and the law of God, against which he has sinned, he is cleansed by repenting, by offering the sacrifices of prayers, and thereafter by living rightly and by being perfectly wise.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *The man from whom there goeth out,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] *Unless a man,* etc., *as far as* he cleansed the human race.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Because many, etc., *as far as* by the traditions of their teachers.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0340A]VERS. 17.-- *The garment and the skin.* ⟦s2⟧ He expresses the misery of original sin, by which the human race, being defiled, defiles even things without sense. ⟦s3⟧ He expresses the women who for lust's sake are mingled with men for the temporary use which they afford; and the garment and the worn-out skins are cast away, and the custom of women is spat out when the friendship grows old.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *The woman,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ By the monthly courses depraved thoughts are signified. ⟦s3⟧ For she who suffers the monthly courses does not touch another's flesh, and defiles her own. ⟦s4⟧ So iniquitous thoughts and wills, although they are not fulfilled in deed, are accounted before God as deeds and are punished.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The woman who at the return.* [n: (ISICH.)] He aims at women as well, etc., *as far as* which is the corruption of the human race [0340B].

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Seven days,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ It signifies the whole of times, up to the completion in which the Gospel was declared: for men were separated from God until the evangelical preaching should join them.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Whosoever shall touch her garment, shall wash,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ So must a man abstain from idolatry: that we touch neither idolaters nor their disciples or supporters, whom they use as beds or as vessels, nor have communion with them.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *The woman who,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because we have said that the man suffering an issue of seed is he who teaches obscene and corrupting things, and that he who uses Judaic teaching is he from whom the seed of coition goes out; there remain those who scatter the teaching of the Gentiles, [0340C] which is fitly called an issue of blood, being as it were the seduction of idolatry: for the demons rejoice in blood.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *He shall offer.* ⟦s2⟧ Above he commanded that the man suffering an issue of blood take two turtledoves or doves for a sacrifice, because he had occupied the place of a master; and here likewise him who suffers an issue of blood, because he was a master of idolatry too.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Ye shall teach.* ⟦s2⟧ This he ordained neither of lepers nor of those who touch carcasses.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Uncleannesses.* ⟦s2⟧ Every sin is an uncleanness of the soul, but idolatry most of all.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The tabernacle.* ⟦s2⟧ The inward man, in whom is the image of God. ⟦s3⟧ *The Word* also *was made flesh, and shall dwell in [0340D] us.* [var: Vulg. Jn 1:14 *et habitavit in nobis*, past tense, "and dwelt among us"]

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *These.* ⟦s2⟧ Things which cohere with one another and have a common purpose he is wont to bind together, that he may show how they ought to be understood, and which things are joined with which by kinship of understanding.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And he who,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He who teaches the Judaic precepts, and teaches good things badly, is unclean, while he brings forth out of season the things that pertain to the letter, just as he uses clean things as unclean, whence: *All things are clean to the clean,* etc.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *And she who,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ These are they who teach impiety, since the human race is held in its monthly courses by the error of idolatry, that is, in the separation of malice.
