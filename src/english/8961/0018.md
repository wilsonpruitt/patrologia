## CHAPTER XVII.

VERS. 1.-- *And he spoke,* etc. By the present law it is shown that the Lord speaks not to Moses only, but to all in common. He desires all to know what is expedient for all: namely, that the law is temporal, and that, its time being fulfilled, it is observed to no purpose. [0344C] But the perfect fulfilment of the time is the destruction of the place in which he commanded the sacrifices to be offered, by intimating that he who shall have sacrificed outside the camps, or without, only outside the tabernacle of the testimony, shall perish from among his people: namely, that when the tabernacle and the place are destroyed, the sacrifices may have an end. But holy actions, and those which build up the human mind, such as to honor father and mother, and to love one's neighbours, and the like, he did not circumscribe by place, because they always profit, and agree with the Gospel. Whosoever therefore now enact the figures of the destroyed place, shed blood, and stain the common converse, and sin in what is common; and just as they shed blood, they shed also the blood of those [0344D] whom by their example they send into error.

VERS. 3.-- *Any man whatsoever,* etc. Inasmuch as the figures of the law are temporal, and are in a fixed place, which being destroyed they cannot remain.

*If he shall have killed a sheep. [n: (AUG., quaest. 56 in Levit.)]* He does not say this of those, etc., *as far as* whatever he did in this matter could not be against the law, because he is the giver of the law.

VERS. 7.-- *And they shall no more immolate their victims to demons,* etc. Whereas it had been said by the Lord [n: (Exod. XX)]: *Thou shalt not make to thyself an idol, nor any likeness of those things which are in heaven above, and in the earth beneath, and whatsoever is under the earth;* [var: Vulg. Ex 20:4 *Non facies tibi sculptile, neque omnem similitudinem quae est in caelo desuper, et quae in terra deorsum, nec eorum quae sunt in aquis sub terra*] they made a molten calf, and offered sacrifice to it, desiring to lick up the sacrifice. The law therefore, as their schoolmaster, commanded them to sacrifice to God [n: (Exod. XXXII)], [0345A] that being occupied in this they might abstain from the sacrifice of idolatry. Yet he ordained such sacrifices as signify future mysteries.

*With whom they have committed fornication.* He calls idolatry fornication, because God has betrothed our souls to himself. Departure from him is therefore called fornication. He terrifies also those who commit fornication in the flesh, when he compares the sin of fornication to the worship of idols and to the oblation offered to demons.

VERS. 8.-- *A man of the house of Israel,* etc. [n: (AUG., ubi supra. )] If the same commandment is repeated, etc., *as far as* in vain do they boast themselves Israelites or Jews.

VERS. 10.-- *Of the strangers.* The Gentiles, who are not joined to the Jews, but are among us. For among them we were, as having their law and the prophets and the promised [0345B] inheritance, yet cleaving not to them but to God: therefore, according to the LXX, *man, man, he said,* that he may show that both those who are in circumcision and those who in uncircumcision cleave to God ought to keep this commandment.

*If he shall have eaten blood,* etc. By blood we understand murder; hatred of a brother is murder: *For he that hateth his brother is a murderer [n: (I Joan. III)]*. We must therefore abstain from all hatred of the brethren, because against such a soul as eats such blood (according to that word: *They who devour my people as the food of bread [n: (Psal. XIII)]* God sets his face firmly, that is, Christ, who is the image of the Father. *For he that seeth the Son, seeth the Father also [n: (Joan. XIV)]*. [var: Vulg. Jn 14:9 *qui videt me, videt et Patrem*, "he that seeth me, seeth the Father also"] For he himself is the judge, of whom it is said: *But the countenance [0345C] of the Lord is upon them that do evil things, to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth [n: (Psal. XXXIII)]*. And he destroys the eater of blood from his people, that is, from the common race of men: because as one without mercy and impious he sends him with the malignant spirits into the everlasting fire, *which is prepared for the devil and his angels [n: (Matth. XXV)]*. And that he might show how merciful we ought to be to one another, how loving, he shows that for our expiation he poured out his own blood, saying: *I have given it to you,* the blood, namely, of Christ.

VERS. 11.-- *The soul of the flesh. [n: (AUG., quaest. 57 in Lev.)]* There is something vital in the blood, etc., *as far as* for whom it was acquiring so great a merit.

[0345D]VERS. 13.-- *Any man whatsoever.* [n: (ISICH.)] God has much care of us, that we harm not one another: therefore he bids a man give the other cheek to him that strikes it; and to leave his coat to him that takes away his cloak; and not to contend in judgment, lest out of contention we should wish even to do harm [n: (Matth. V)], but by suffering to correct the man himself. Therefore the lawgiver recapitulates the commandment concerning blood, that he may take away the various frauds and cunning devices of doing harm, saying: *Whether by hunting or by fowling,* etc.

*By hunting.* By fraud, which touches blood and life. Whence: *They hunted after the soul of the just, and condemned innocent blood [n: (Psal. XLIII)]*. [var: Vulg. Ps 93:21 future *Captabunt … condemnabunt*, "they will hunt … will condemn"] And again: *Our soul, as a sparrow, hath been delivered out of the snare of the fowlers [n: (Psal. CXXIII)]*. That is, if he shall have seen sinning [0346A] the man who through weakness or simplicity lies open to hunting and to guile.

*Or a sheep,* etc. Him, namely, who withdraws himself from earthly things, on which it is lawful to feed: for such men the violent and the greedy devour. Whence: *If ye devour one another, take heed lest ye be consumed one of another [n: (Gal. V)]*. Or, because they were formerly just, they are rightly counted among clean things.

*Let the earth cover it,* etc. The earth covers the sins of those who confess, as things remaining beneath and mortified by penance: whose cry is forbidden to ascend to the heavens, whither the cry of the Sodomites ascended, who would not repent [n: (Gen. XVIII)].

VERS. 15.-- *The soul that shall have eaten,* etc. Above it was said: Whosoever shall touch the carcasses of beasts taken by wild beasts, [0346B] that is, whoever shall have had part in such things; but here it is said: *The soul that shall have eaten;* that is, who shall have made the whole sin his own, and shall have fulfilled his own will out of it.
