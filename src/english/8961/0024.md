## CHAPTER XXII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Which they themselves offer,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Which they offered, namely, to the priests, to be offered by them to the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ Note the kind of sanctification which comes about by the vow and devotion of the offerer; but whether men are said in the same way to sanctify themselves, when in some matter they vow themselves, is to be observed.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Every man that approacheth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if to say, let him not only abstain from the ministry and the mystical office, [0360B] but let him not even touch the things which have been offered, for the clean must approach clean things.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Who shall be a leper,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He does not abominate the leper who is perceived by the senses, who suffers unwillingly, but the spiritual leper: which he there sets forth: *He that toucheth what is unclean by reason of the dead,* or, according to the LXX: *Every uncleanness of the soul,* that is, sin.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *From whom,* etc. [n: (HIERON., epist. ad Fabiolam. )] If any of the priests have an issue of seed, *as far as* let him be held by the manner of no other.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Let them keep,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ To those who keep the precepts of the Lord they are a cause of justification and of salvation; but to those who transgress, of sin and of death, and chiefly in the receiving of the holy things. ⟦s3⟧ *For he that eateth and drinketh unworthily, eateth and drinketh judgment to himself,* etc. [0360C] [n: (I Cor. XI.)]

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *The sojourner of the priest,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] He who is not purely nor diligently occupied in the Church, as one about to migrate, namely for the sake of glory or of gain, is spoken of as a hireling: who is not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not [n: (Joan. X)].

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Married to any one of the people.* [n: (ID.)] It is not enough to be nourished in the faith of Christ: we ought to abide and to grow in it; nor to be mingled with any of the aliens, that is, with a doctrine or a law or an action alien from God: nor, because we are of faithful fathers, are we therefore to presume upon the holy things, but let us persist where they too were faithful.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *He that eateth of the sanctified things,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] The holy of holies, etc., *as far as* Whence Solomon: [0360D] *When thou sittest to eat with a prince, consider diligently what is set before thee [n: (Prov. XXIII)]*.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He shall add the fifth part,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *Upon it.* ⟦s3⟧ The fifth part therefore is commanded to be added not from elsewhere, but from it and with it, or upon it: this is the word brought forth by Christ himself upon the Lord's mystery. ⟦s4⟧ He himself removes us from ignorance, and let no man think it carnal or earthly. ⟦s5⟧ Here it is rightly called a fifth part, because the spirit and the word which he delivers composes our five senses; nor does it bring forth the taste only to the mystery, but the hearing and the sight, and the touch and the smell, that we may suspect nothing earthly of the things which are above.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And he shall give it to the priest in the sanctuary.* [n: (ISICH.)] The sanctification of the mystical sacrifice, and the passage from things sensible to things intelligible, [0361A] must indeed be given to the priest: because by his power and word the things which are seen are sanctified, so that they surpass all the senses.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Of the children of Israel.* ⟦s2⟧ He signifies the faithful people, who through this mystery see God: and whosoever consecrates to God for the Lord's sacrifice, they are the holy of holies, because of them the body of Christ is made. ⟦s3⟧ For he himself is the firstfruits of our lump: therefore he rightly subsists of our firstfruits.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *The Lord spoke,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] Not only the priests, etc., *as far as* for otherwise it will not be received.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *A man of the house of Israel,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ To the letter also, if thou offer an ox or a sheep or a she-goat, or anything of this kind, by giving firstfruits to the priests, [0361B] or by feeding the poor, or by setting up divine buildings, or by doing anything that pertains to God himself (for therefore is it called a holocaust), let this oblation be undefiled; yet he does not name the defilement, that we may understand the spiritual defilement which is from rapine, injustice, avarice, and the like.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *The man that offereth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He has commanded that the oblation of the people be clean: he adds what kind the ministers ought to be, and to offer.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of peace offerings,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Such as avail unto salvation and peace: even if a man cannot perfectly philosophize, like him who has left all things, who is zealous for virginity and the like. ⟦s3⟧ *For star differeth from star in brightness [n: (I Cor. XV)]*.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *If it be blind,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He who does not know God and his ministry, nor can set it forth to others; [0361C] whence about himself and about others, and about the ministry, he acts negligently. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *See that they have all been without knowledge, all dumb dogs, not able to bark [n: (Isa. LVI)]*. [var: Vulg. Is 56:10 *Speculatores ejus caeci omnes; nescierunt universi: canes muti non valentes latrare*, "His watchmen are all blind, they are all ignorant"]

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *If broken.* ⟦s2⟧ In foot or in hand: whose action, namely, or will is weak or twisted, he is not worthy of salvation and of the holy altars.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *If it have a scar.* ⟦s2⟧ He marks the detractor: for this man is the worst in his manners, and a most bitter wound is not easily assuaged.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *If it have pimples.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *a cut tongue:* he, namely, who because of a sordid life has lost his confidence in teaching; whence David: *God said to the sinner: Why dost thou declare my justices?* etc. [n: (Psal. XLIX.)]

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Scab or ringworm.* ⟦s2⟧ The fornicator is signified, [0361D] who stains his own body. ⟦s3⟧ *For he that committeth fornication sinneth against his own body [n: (I Cor. VI)]*.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *You shall not offer them to the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *They shall not offer these to the Lord:* for such are not to be offered to the ministry of the altar. ⟦s3⟧ He does not add the sacrifice of peace offerings as above, that we may understand this to be said of the ministers of the altar, who ought to be a holocaust, and to lead a perfect and consummated life. ⟦s4⟧ *The ear and the tail being cut off,* etc. ⟦s5⟧ He is cut off in the ear who is disobedient, who delights in base words, turns away from good things.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Voluntarily,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ For such a man either works the commandments, and is called an ox; or is simple, and is called a sheep. ⟦s3⟧ Therefore we do not altogether oppose them, or reckon them on the enemy's side, but we teach and exhort them.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0362A] *Or bruised,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He who yields to the temptations brought upon him, and loses the strength of his soul. ⟦s3⟧ For it behoves us not to suffer these things, nor to yield to the enemy that afflicts us. ⟦s4⟧ Whence: *Wherefore do I go sorrowful, whilst the enemy afflicteth me [n: (Psal. XLI)]*? [var: Vulg. Ps 41:10 *quare contristatus incedo, dum affligit me inimicus*, "why do I go about mourning"]

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And taken away.* ⟦s2⟧ He who by one depraved action cuts himself off, as with a knife, from union with God, deprived of the offspring of virtue and of the signs of manhood: for it is another who is worn away little by little.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *The ox, the sheep and the goat.* [n: (ISICH.)] Nature is the mother of all, etc., *as far as* whence Solomon: *Praise not a man before his death [n: (Eccli. XI)]*.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *They shall not be sacrificed.* [n: (ISICH.)] It signifies the legal pasch, because Moses delivered these things to be sacrificed by the Jews on the day of the pasch, saying: *Thou shalt sacrifice the pasch to the Lord thy God; sheep,* etc. [n: (Deut. XVI)]: [var: Vulg. Dt 16:2 *Immolabisque Phase Domino Deo tuo de ovibus, et de bobus*, "And thou shalt sacrifice the Phase ... of sheep and of oxen"] this [0362B] pasch they cannot celebrate who have received the true one.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In one day,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Because the old pasch had its own time, etc., *as far as:* On the morrow they crucified Christ.
