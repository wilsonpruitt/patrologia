## CHAPTER XXIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *These are the feasts of the Lord.* [n: (ISICH.)] If thou attend subtly to things present, etc., *as far as* he rests in the intelligible sabbath.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *These therefore are.* ⟦s2⟧ Not to the Lord, as all the others are, but the Lord's properly, in which, the enemy being overcome, he saved the human race, and brought it back from captivity [n: (Exod. II)].

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *On the fourteenth day,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] Let us ask the Jews, etc., *as far as* on which Christ our passover was sacrificed, etc.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0362C] *It is the Phase of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ Because the Lord suffered, he did not straightway end the times, but left the remnants of the last and evening hour, that their sound might go out into all the earth [n: (Psal. XVIII)], that is, the sound of the apostles, and their words unto the ends of the world. ⟦s3⟧ At evening also, or at mid-evening, forestalling his cross he kept his own passover, because upon the mystical passover he delivered the true one to the disciples. ⟦s4⟧ It was night therefore already when Judas, having received the morsel, went out to betray him: on the following day he fulfilled the passover. ⟦s5⟧ Whence on the fifteenth day the lawgiver appointed this festival, and proclaimed the solemnity of the unleavened bread.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Seven days,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ If we would truly keep the passover, for seven days, that is, always, we ought to be unleavened of all malice, and most of all of [0362D] the Jewish error. ⟦s3⟧ Whence the Apostle says: *Not with the old leaven, nor with the leaven of malice and wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth [n: (I Cor. V)]*.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *All work,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Whosoever committeth sin is the servant of sin [n: (Joan. VIII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ We ought not to serve sin, when we keep the passover, in which Christ set us free from sin.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Servile,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He does not permit servile things to be done on the other days on which he forbade us, whence he adds: but you shall offer a sacrifice in fire to the Lord.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *A sacrifice,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *holocaustomata,* where it is hinted that he commands them to cease from all servile things; moreover he named not only the first, but the seventh also, that he might take in the whole of [0363A] human life, because that is the beginning, this the end, in which all things can be comprehended.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *On the next day.* ⟦s2⟧ He could have said, the second day of the unleavened bread: but lest he should draw our attention to the following day, that is, to the morrow of the first, he said, on the next day, signifying the resurrection which came after the passion, as day comes after day.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And on the same day.* ⟦s2⟧ Of the resurrection, that is, of the lifting up of the sheaf, that is, when we keep the resurrection, we ought not to forget the Lord's sacrifice, from which the sheaf was offered.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Two tenth parts.* ⟦s2⟧ For it behoves the perfect humanity and the perfect divinity to come together into one: in oil, that is, through the mercy which he had toward us; for so the sacrifice is found an odour of sweetness [0363B] to the Lord, if we understand of him the things that are worthy.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The cakes also.* ⟦s2⟧ Behold the intelligible oblation of Melchisedech, which is accomplished in bread and wine [n: (Gen. XIV)]; thence a fourth part is offered here, that by the fourth part the evangelical doctrine may be signified; but by the drink offering the Lord's word, in which he says: *This is my blood, which shall be shed for many unto the remission of sins [n: (Matth. XXVI)]*.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Bread.* ⟦s2⟧ The oblation of present gifts, which is the mystery of Christ, has reconciled us to God, and has prepared new food.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Parched corn.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: χίδρα νέα, that is, you shall not eat new grain roasted, or rubbed with the hands and cleansed: for so others interpret χίδρα, chidra, because [0363C] the precepts of Christ require action and correction [n: (Matth. XI)].

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Fifty days, and so,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ This is the Lord's day, on which we keep the festival of Pentecost, which the Jews keep neither according to the letter nor according to the spirit, who do not even number the days according to the law.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *You shall offer a new sacrifice,* new, not old, not worldly. ⟦s2⟧ For the Spirit at his coming renewed the whole of human life, because he confirmed the institution of Christ, and perfected in them the confirmation of life: whence, *He shall receive of mine, and shall show it to you [n: (Joan. XVI)]*. ⟦s3⟧ Therefore he came not on another day, but on that on which the resurrection was, and the intelligible sheaf was offered, that he might show that the new manner of life was perfected by the resurrection of Christ and the coming [0363D] of the Holy Spirit.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Your dwellings,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because there is a chief dwelling, of which it is said: *Blessed are they that dwell in thy house, O Lord [n: (Psal. LXXXII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ And elsewhere: *One thing I have asked of the Lord, this will I seek after, that I may dwell,* etc. [n: (Psal. XXVI)].

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The loaves of the firstfruits.* ⟦s2⟧ For the Lord first perfected and taught the Gospel together with the law. ⟦s3⟧ *You have heard that it was said to them of old: Thou shalt not kill: But I say to you: Whosoever is angry with his brother shall be in danger of the judgment [n: (Matth. V)]*: for hereby he joined the law to the Gospel. ⟦s4⟧ Hence Luke says: *Which Jesus began to do and to teach,* etc. [n: (Act. I)].

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of two tenth parts,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That is, of the doctrine which [0364A] teaches the perfect divinity and the perfect humanity, and they cannot be baked otherwise than by such doctrine.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Seven lambs.* ⟦s2⟧ For the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit, which he calls lambs, because the Holy Spirit is one with Christ, of whom it is written: *Behold the Lamb of God, behold him who taketh away the sins of the world, therefore no man can say, the Lord Jesus, but in the Holy Spirit [n: (Joan. I; I Cor. XII)]*.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And two rams.* ⟦s2⟧ By which the enemy is winnowed away. ⟦s3⟧ These are the higher commandments, and the virtues, which others follow as sheep, for example: *Go and sell all that thou hast, and give to the poor [n: (Matth. IX)]*. [var: Vulg. Mt 19:21 *Vade, vende quae habes, et da pauperibus*; the *omnia* is Lc 18:22, and Matthew has the saying at ch. XIX, not IX]

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With the drink offerings,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Which are knowledge and correction; whence: *But thanks be to God, who manifesteth the odour of his knowledge [0364B] by us, because we are the good odour of Christ,* etc. [n: (II Cor. II)]. [var: Vulg. 2 Cor 2:14 *odorem notitiae suae*; Migne's *scientiae* is the very word the gloss expounds]

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *And two lambs,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The commandments of peace and of holiness, whence: *Follow peace with all men, and holiness,* etc. [n: (Heb. XII)]. ⟦s3⟧ Because these were spoken in the apostolic word, he commands them to be offered with the loaves of the firstfruits, which signify the doctrine of Christ. ⟦s4⟧ Whence in what follows: *And when the priest shall have lifted them up with the loaves of the firstfruits [n: (Joan. X)]*.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *They shall fall to the use,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ To the use of that priest, that is, of Christ, who is our priest and our Lord: his are the loaves of the firstfruits and the lambs of the commandments, he spoke in Paul, he offered all the things that have been said; he first announced peace to the disciples, and kept holiness, as the Holy [0364C] of holies.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Most solemn.* ⟦s2⟧ In which the calling of the nations was not only foretold, but fulfilled: when Parthians and Medes and Elamites, and the rest of the nations, heard the apostles speaking in divers tongues the wonderful works of God [n: (Act. II)].

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *After that,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He seems to have said the same things above, but there: *You shall not complete your reaping;* and here he says: *You shall not cut it down to the ground,* and the very change of words hints at a difference.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The corn of the land,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Of the manner of life, whose reaping is after the resurrection of Christ and the coming of the Holy Spirit; yet the word of preaching does not deliver all the knowledge which we are to have in the time to come. ⟦s3⟧ For there are things [0364D] which cannot now be revealed to us [n: (Matth. VIII)], whence among the aforesaid festivals he set this law between.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To the poor and the strangers.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *to the poor man and the proselyte,* that is, to Christ, who had not where to lay his head, and *came unto his own, and his own received him not.* ⟦s3⟧ Here he understands only those who remain; whence: *O the depth of the wisdom and of the knowledge of God! how incomprehensible are his judgments, and how unsearchable his ways! [n: (Rom. XI.)]* [var: Vulg. Rom 11:33 *O altitudo divitiarum sapientiae et scientiae Dei*, "the depth of the riches of the wisdom"]

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *In the seventh month, on the first day of the month.* ⟦s2⟧ The number seven signifies a consummation, either particular or general. ⟦s3+4⟧ Hence God rested on the seventh day [n: (Gen. II)]; and by his rest he signified ours; whence: *He that is entered into that rest, hath rested from his own works, [0365A] as God did from his [n: (Heb. IV)]*. [var: Vulg. Heb 4:10 *Qui enim ingressus est in requiem eius, etiam ipse requievit ab operibus suis, sicut a suis Deus*, "into his rest"] But the beginning of the consummation of the times is the coming of the Lord, whence: *Little children, it is the last hour [n: (I Joan. II)]*. ⟦s5⟧ This time he calls the first day of the seventh month, since the beginning of the consummation is a rest to us also, whom he has made to cease from evil works, and has freed from the heavy yoke; whence: *Come to me, all you that labour and are burdened,* etc., *and learn of me, because I am meek and humble of heart, and you shall find rest to your souls [n: (Matth. XI)]*.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With trumpets sounding.* ⟦s2⟧ Because it proclaims the resurrection of the Saviour, which is a memorial of the general resurrection, which according to the Apostle shall be in the voice of an archangel and in the trumpet of God, etc.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *You shall do no servile work therein.* ⟦s2⟧ As if [sicut] there were no need of doing servile things: before, you did them, *but [0365B] as you have yielded your members to iniquity unto iniquity, so now yield them to serve justice unto sanctification [n: (Rom. VI)]*: for this is the true holocaust.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *On the tenth day,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] From these things we understand that propitiation and deliverance from sins are gained in humility and affliction of soul. ⟦s2⟧ This we ought to keep from the evening, that is, to begin from penance, which Christ made the beginning of his preaching [n: (Matth. IV)]: upon penance a good life, consummated in virtues, is to be built. ⟦s3⟧ Whence he added: *And you shall offer a holocaust to the Lord.*

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *A day of propitiation.* [n: (ISICH.)] It signifies two things, that in this time we shall receive propitiation, [0365C] and therefore in it we ought to abstain from all evil.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Nothing therefore.* ⟦s2⟧ He signifies that there must always be a ceasing from evil and superfluous things. ⟦s3⟧ Whence the Lord neither allows a cause to be pleaded, nor allows us to think what shall be eaten, or what drunk, or what put on; for he proclaimed a true sabbath too, and a sabbath of sabbaths.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *You shall afflict.* ⟦s2⟧ It is a recapitulation of humility, which is the foundation and base of the virtues. ⟦s3⟧ Whence the Lord says: *Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child, he is the greater in the kingdom of heaven [n: (Matth. XVIII)]*.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *From the fifteenth day.* [n: (ISICH.)] With this number the festival is begun, etc., *as far as a building not made with hands is had in the heavens [n: (II Cor. V)]*.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *The day also.* [n: (ID.)] Note that the same things are said of [0365D] the first and of the eighth, namely because the day of the resurrection is the second, and the beginning of the age to come. ⟦s2⟧ The first is, in Christ, the beginning of the manner of life and the opening of things to come; but the eighth succeeds the present age, which he signifies in the number seven. ⟦s3⟧ Moreover the saints have rest and the life to come in this life. ⟦s4⟧ Whence it was said to the apostles: *The kingdom of God is within you,* etc.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *An assembly.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: a going out, because hence is the departure, there the dwelling, whence: *I desire to be dissolved and to be with Christ [n: (Philipp. I)]*.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *These are the feasts of the Lord,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Paul accords with the lawgiver, etc., *as far as* whether in dangers, or of our own will, we promise.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 39.-- *From the fifteenth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The fifteenth, composed of seven and eight, signifies the evangelical preaching, [0366A] which consists of the present life and the life to come; the one it composes, the other it prepares.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *On the first day and on the eighth day.* [n: (ID.)] Which is the eighth, etc., *as far as* because for the faithful who keep the law a rest is reserved in the time to come.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 40.-- *On the first day, the fruits.* [n: (ID.)] The fruit is Christ, etc., *as far as* in his own time.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And the branches of palm trees,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The youthful shoots of justice, because *the just shall flourish like the palm tree;* and because nothing of the palm is rejected, nor anything of those who live justly, he adds, *and the boughs of thick trees.*
