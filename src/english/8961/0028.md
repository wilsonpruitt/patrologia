## CHAPTER XXVI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *You shall not make.* ⟦s2⟧ He forbids the worship of idols, both visible and invisible.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A graven thing.* [n: (ISICH.)] Desiring to carve some memorial of yourselves in the buildings of your houses and the holdings of your fields.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Nor a remarkable stone.* [n: (ID.)] A heap of gold and silver or of precious stones, in which the covetous are led astray, even as the Gentiles are in idols.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Keep.* [n: (ISICH.)] By abstaining from all evil, [0372A] and being at leisure for every good: for this is the true sabbath.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *If in my precepts* He has foretold what ought to be done, and what avoided. ⟦s2⟧ At the end he enumerates what rewards are due to those who keep them, and what punishments to the transgressors.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *If in my precepts.* [n: (ID.)] These things cannot stand according to the letter, etc., *as far as* the things that are signified in this chapter.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ORIG., hom. 16 in Lev.)] If the law is carnal, etc., *as far as, John came in the spirit and power of Elias.*

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *My commandments.* ⟦s2⟧ Such as this: *He that keepeth not the sabbath, let him be stoned by the whole synagogue,* and the like.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I will give you rain,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ And I will give: the *and* is superfluous by the custom of our speech, but is added after the manner of the Scriptures.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0372B] *In their seasons.* [n: (ORIG.)] At a fitting time, not when a man is drunken, not when he is busied about other things: let the teacher therefore judge prudently to whom he should minister the rain and the measure of wheat, and to whom he ought to give milk, let him not give meat.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *The ground shall bring forth.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *The trees of the field shall give their fruit.* [n: (ID.)] The motions of the soul, which are higher than the motions of the earth, that is, of our flesh, whose fruits are charity, justice, meekness and the like.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The trees with fruit.* [n: (ORIG.)] *A tree cannot,* etc., *as far as* of which it is said: *She is a tree of life to them that have laid hold on her.*

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *The threshing of the harvest shall reach.* [n: (ORIG.)] The soul that buds forth from the word of God, etc., *as far as* there remains what is to be dispensed to the fellow servants in due time.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0372C] *Shall reach.* ⟦s2⟧ For *bread strengtheneth man's heart, wine maketh it glad [n: (Psal. CIII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ The things that are said of continence, of observances and of the keeping of the commandments seem to be wheat, out of which is made the bread that strengthens hearts. ⟦s4⟧ But the things that pertain to knowledge, and gladden the mind by the searching out of hidden things, are likened to wine and to the vintage. ⟦s5⟧ For the heart is made glad when dark [occulta] things are explained.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The vintage.* ⟦s2⟧ The fruit of the soul. ⟦s3⟧ For the vintage is a tree. ⟦s4⟧ But to have these together, that is, to bear fruit in body and in soul, is a matter of great blessing.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISICH.)] For the soul, gathering her virtues, soon desires others. ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *Forgetting the things that are behind, I stretch forth myself to those that are before [n: (Philip. III)]*.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The vintage.* [n: (ORIG.)] As if we should say, etc., *as far as* [0372D] that of the spirit we may reap life everlasting.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Your bread.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, which came down from heaven, and gives life to the world; not that which abounds more to the evil than to the good.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Without fear.* ⟦s2⟧ If the body shall have been steadfast, and the soul shall have borne fruit, we shall be secure in our own land, *because the flesh shall not lust against the spirit [n: (Galat. IV)]*. [var: Vulg. Gal 5:17 *caro enim concupiscit adversus spiritum*, "for the flesh lusteth against the spirit"; Migne prints a negative the Apostle does not, and cites Galatians IV for V]

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And the sword shall not.* [n: (ISICH.)] Because there shall be peace in your borders; now from concupiscences come wars and strifes.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Your enemies.* [n: (ID.)] Slain by fasting, by prayer and the like; whence: *This kind is not cast out but with prayer and fasting [n: (Matth. XVII)]*.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They shall fall.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *In your sight by your death,* [0373A] that is, your members being mortified which are upon the earth, that is, fornication, uncleanness and the like [n: (Col. III)].

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *They shall pursue.* [n: (ORIG.)] As the number five points to the wise and the foolish, etc., *as far as* lest they deceive believers.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISICH.)] LXX, etc., *as far as* he can pursue and conquer.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And a hundred.* ⟦s2⟧ They who have not only subtle knowledge but a perfect life also, who are spiritually cleared of blame, and are therefore called a hundred, namely those who bring forth a hundredfold fruit.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *I will look upon you.* ⟦s2⟧ Whose look is salvation. ⟦s3⟧ The Lord looked upon Peter, and he wept bitterly. ⟦s4⟧ If the sun look not upon the corn, it remains without fruit. ⟦s5⟧ God looks upon the corn [0373B] of our heart, and enlightens us with the rays of his word, increases and multiplies us that we may be made great; as Isaac was made great, and Moses great, and John great.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To increase.* ⟦s2⟧ In good works, that small things may become great. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *What you have done to one of the least of mine,* etc. [n: (Matth. XXV.)] [var: Vulg. Mt 25:40 *Quamdiu fecistis uni ex his fratribus meis minimis, mihi fecistis*, "as long as you did it to one of these my least brethren, you did it to me"]

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *You shall be multiplied.* ⟦s2⟧ That few things may become great or many. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *If there be any other commandment, it is restored in this word: Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself [n: (Rom. XIII)]*.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *You shall eat.* [n: (ORIG.)] The old law and the prophets, etc., *as far as* and we have put on the new.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *I will set.* ⟦s2⟧ If we have the things aforesaid, the old man being cast off our heart has been made new: *God comes to us and dwells in us [n: (Col. III)]*. [var: Vulg. has no such verse; compare Jo 14:23 *ad eum veniemus, et mansionem apud eum faciemus* and 2 Cor 6:16 *inhabitabo in illis*; Colossians III has nothing of the kind]

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0373C] *He shall not cast off,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] I dare to say that the soul of God is Christ, as also the word of God is the wisdom of God and the power of God [n: (I Cor. I)]. ⟦s2⟧ It is therefore as though he said: my Son shall not cast you off.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Soul. [n: (AUG., quaest. 93 in Lev.)]* God calls it his own soul, etc., *as far as* who say that Jesus had no soul.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *I will walk.* [n: (ISICH.)] Being made man. ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *This is our God, and no other shall be accounted of beside him; after this he was seen upon the earth, and conversed with men [n: (Baruc. III)]*. [var: Vulg. Bar 3:36 *et non aestimabitur alius adversus eum*, "shall not be esteemed against him", and 3:38 *in terris visus est*, "upon the earth" in the plural]

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *But if.* [n: (ISICH.)] In the preceding chapter he set forth the good things of the obedient: but what are the evils of transgressors he shows in the present chapter, of which the Jews suffered some according to the letter: but all [0373D] according to the spirit.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *In want.* [n: (ID.)] Of the word of God. ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *This saying is hard, who can hear it? [n: (Joan. VI.)]* Because they were transgressing the understanding of the law, that they might make void the evangelical testament, he spoke to them darkly, lest they should understand.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *You shall flee, no man.* [n: (ID.)] Because the gentiles will not pursue them to drive them out, but hastening after their salvation, according to that: *But I follow after, if I may by any means lay hold [n: (Philip. III)]*.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But if.* [n: (ID.)] He frequently uses distinctions of this kind, etc., *as far as* it signifies a full and entire vengeance.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *I will add correction.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *And I will proceed [0374A] to chastise you seven times,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ Here too it is superfluous. ⟦s4⟧ But seven times is to be taken for every number.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *And I will break the pride of your hardness.* ⟦s2⟧ Because *you say: We are the seed of Abraham, and we have never served any man [n: (Joan. III)]*: therefore you shall serve the nations.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *A sword,* of the spirit, which is the word of God, which by preachers convicts the Jews, lest they gainsay the having of the Old Testament together with the Gospel.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I will send into your cities.* ⟦s2⟧ The prophets, in whom the faithful find fortifications and salvation, while the enemies fleeing are taken, and there die, not being able to resist the prophets who speak openly of Christ.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0374B]VERS. 26.-- *So that ten,* etc. [n: (ISICH.)] They have not the bread of doctrine, etc., *as far as* therefore they are not filled who are fed with these loaves.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *But if,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Moses is not only a lawgiver, etc., *as far as* and they blasphemed the author of the law.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *The flesh of your sons.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, the disciples whom you take to yourselves for sons, that you may lead them into the same doctrine by words that are strong and that corrupt the soul.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *You shall fall among the ruins.* ⟦s2⟧ For the people falls together with the Pharisees who fall spiritually, whom the people, intent upon their traditions, admired as idols.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Your cities.* ⟦s2⟧ According to the letter, or else the books of the prophets, in which they find no fruit of holiness.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0374C]VERS. 34.-- *Then shall the sabbaths of the land please,* that is, its rest, namely while you keep holiday and are removed from the law, since in the law and the prophets you find not your own will.

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *It shall terrify them.* ⟦s2⟧ As the leaf hides the fruit, so the letter hides the spirit: take away the leaf, receive the fruit.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The sound of a flying leaf.* ⟦s2⟧ A hyperbole: because they shall dread even the very lightest things.

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They shall fall when none pursueth.* ⟦s2⟧ For we do not pursue them, but follow after them that we may call them back.

⟦p51⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *They shall fall.* ⟦s2⟧ When we dispute with them, they bring forward divers testimonies by turns: but they shall be confounded by one another's words, and the one confounds the other [0374D] while he seems to be helping him.

⟦p52⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 41.-- *Their uncircumcised mind.* ⟦s2⟧ Because they are uncircumcised in heart, defiled with the foreskin of vices, *for neither is that circumcision which is in the flesh [n: (Rom. II)]*.

⟦p53⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Then shall they pray for their impieties.* ⟦s2⟧ LXX: *They shall hold their sins acceptable,* that is, the punishments which they shall suffer for their sins.

⟦p54⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 42.-- *Which I made with Jacob.* [n: (ISICH.)] Note that he has changed the order of time and of birth, etc., *as far as* but they are joined together as kindred and fitting.

⟦p55⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I will remember the land also.* [n: (ISICH.)] That is, the Scripture, which I will entrust to the Jews when they are cast out, who have transgressed [0375A] it, or were transgressing it, and interpreted it perversely.

⟦p56⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 44.-- *And yet even when they were in the enemies' land.* ⟦s2⟧ Well does he speak kindly at the end of the chapter, and console with promises those whom he had frightened with threats.

⟦p57⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Not utterly. ⟦s2⟧ Blindness in part befalleth in Israel, until the fulness of the nations should come in; and the rest [n: (I Tim. II)]*. [var: Vulg. Rom 11:25 *caecitas ex parte contigit in Israel, donec plenitudo gentium intraret*, past *contigit* and simple *intraret*; the passage is Romans XI, not I Timothy II]

⟦p58⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Neither have I so despised them.* ⟦s2⟧ I who will the salvation of all: therefore after so great iniquities I save even the remnant of the Jews.

⟦p59⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 45.-- *These are.* [n: (ISICH.)] Until a certain time, etc., *as far as* not by destroying, but by fulfilling.

⟦p60⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In mount Sinai.* ⟦s2⟧ Not in Sion. ⟦s3⟧ Whence: *The God of gods shall be seen in Sion [n: (Psal. LXXXIII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ And elsewhere: *Out of [0375B] Sion is the loveliness of his beauty. ⟦s5⟧ God shall come manifestly [n: (Psal. XLIX)]*. ⟦s6⟧ And in Malachias: *Suddenly shall come to his temple the ruler whom you seek [n: (Malac. III)]*. [var: Vulg. Mal 3:1 *et statim veniet ad templum suum Dominator quem vos quaeritis*, "and presently he shall come"]
