## CHAPTER ONE.

[0382D]

VERS. 3.-- *Of all the men. [n: (ORIG., hom. 1 in Num.)]* Otherwise the LXX: *Every one who goeth forth in the strength of Israel,* etc. He therefore who goeth forth, etc., *as far as* it is handed down by the evangelical and apostolic faith.

VERS. 4.-- *The princes of the tribes,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 1 in Num.)] What is it that he commands single princes to be chosen out of the single tribes, etc., *as far as* that they be called χιλίαρχοι.

VERS. 5.-- *Of the tribe of Ruben. [n: (RAB. in Num., tom. II.)]* Morally, Ruben the firstborn of Israel, etc., *as far as* one heart and one soul.

[n: (RAB., ibid.)] The prince of the tribe of Ruben signifies them generally, etc., *as far as* bearing the care of the continent, of virgins, and of either sex.

[0383A]VERS. 6.-- *Of the tribe of Simeon.* [n: (RAB.)] Simeon generally [signifies] all the obedient, etc., *as far as* saying: *Unto thee have I lifted up mine eyes, who dwellest in the heavens [n: (Psal. CXXII)]*.

VERS. 7.-- *Of the tribe of Juda.* [n: (ID.)] There can be signified in Juda all who rightly call upon the name of the Lord, dissenting neither in sense nor in word from the rule of faith: of whom Naasson is with reason the prince, who, namely, not only instructs others by preaching, but also slays in himself the venomous darts of the vices by chastising his own body, according to that: *I chastise my body and bring it into subjection,* etc. [n: (I Cor. IX)].

Allegorically, Juda, from whom the kings come, who is interpreted *confession* or *praise,* here signifies the people of the Church, whose head is the lion of the tribe of Juda, that is, Christ. [0383B] For truly the praise or confession of faith, that is, right preaching, is only in the Church. Naasson therefore, that is *serpentine,* or *augury,* the son of Aminadab, namely *of a willing people,* is understood as the Saviour, begotten of the patriarchs, servants devoted to God, who by his own death destroyed the devil. For in the serpent, whence first the cause of death, that is sin, proceeded, death is figured. Whence: *As Moses lifted up the serpent in the desert, so must the Son of man be lifted up,* etc. [n: (Joan. III)].

VERS. 8.-- *Of the tribe of Issachar.* [n: (ID.)] Issachar not unfittingly expresses the monks, who, having despised all the business of the world, by the gift of God, that is, with the Holy Spirit protecting them, seek the eternal reward by a strict life, pleasure being mortified, and the pomp of the world [0383C] trampling under foot, embracing lowly things, desire to please God alone.

*Nathanael the son of Suar.* Of those who piously exercise themselves that they may receive a reward in the heavens, the prince is Nathanael, that is *my God* or *the gift of God, the son of Suar,* that is, of the little Christ, namely who appeared little among men: for he himself is both leader and reward.

VERS. 9.-- *Of the tribe of Zabulon.* [n: (ID.)] Zabulon figures the patient, etc., *as far as* namely against heretics and schismatics.

VERS. 10.-- *Joseph.* The same Joseph, the father of two tribes, signifies Christ, the Father of two peoples and the corner stone. Ephraim the younger, preferred to Manasse the firstborn, [signifies] the Gentile people [0383D] preferred to the Jewish. Whence: *Amen I say to you, I have not found so great faith in Israel [n: (Matth. VIII)]*.

*Elisama.* Elisama signifies Christ, of whom it is said: *He was made obedient to the Father even unto death,* etc. [n: (Philip. II)] [var: Vulg. *factus obediens usque ad mortem*; the received text has no "to the Father"]. Who says of himself: *The things which I have heard from him, these I speak in the world [n: (Joan. VIII)]*. This is the son of Ammiud, that is *of a renowned people,* namely of the stock of kings, of patriarchs and of prophets.

[n: (RAB.)] Elisama, the prince in the tribe of Ephraim, signifies those who attentively hear the word of God with a good and best heart, and bring forth fruit in patience.

*Manasses,* etc. *Forgetful,* namely those *which are behind forgetting, and stretching himself forth unto the things before: that he may receive the crown of life, which God will repay to them that love him [n: (Philip. III; Jac. I)]*. [var: Vulg. *quam repromisit Deus diligentibus se*, "which God hath promised"]

[0384A] *Gamaliel.* Gamaliel, *God hath rendered to me,* the son of Phadassur, that is *of a strong redemption,* is the prince in the tribe of Manasse, which is interpreted *forgotten,* or *forgetful:* since Christ (whom the Father sent into the world to call the Jewish people) found in his kingdom a forgetful nation. For Israel has forgotten his creator. Yet not all have perished, but when *the fulness of the Gentiles shall have come in, all Israel shall be saved [n: (Rom. XI)]*.

VERS. 11.-- *Of the tribe of Benjamin.* Benjamin signifies Jerusalem, which is in his own tribe: whose people, according to the prophecy of Rachel, was Bennoni, that is *the son of sorrow,* because it afflicted its mother with a heavy sorrow. For it shed the blood of the prophets, and conspiring in the slaying of Christ said: *His blood be upon us and [0384B] upon our children [n: (Matth. XXVII)]*. But in those that believe, according to the prophecy of his father, *the son of the right hand.* Benjamin also can signify the Gentile people, which was a son of sorrow by persecuting Christians. It can also be called the son of the right hand, because in the judgment it will be at the right hand of God, or because it does right-hand things, that is, good works. Of this people Abidan, that is *my father,* a judge, the son of Gedeon, who is interpreted *the temptation of iniquity* or *of humility;* is the prince: because Christ (whom the Jew despised as lowly and the devil wickedly tempted) is the head of believers, and the judge to come of the living and the dead. Morally, Benjamin, *the son of the right hand,* signifies those who fight bravely against their enemies, and bravely press down the goads of temptation, and permit nothing womanish or soft to have dominion in themselves.

[0384C]VERS. 12.-- *Of the tribe of Dan.* Dan, *judgment,* or *judging.* These are they who do all things with discretion, who appoint for themselves as prince Abiezer, that is *a helping brother:* because they do all things according to the rule of the Fathers, or ascribe all their good things to God.

[n: (RAB., ubi supra.)] Dan, etc., *as far as* they are exhorted to the fellowship of spiritual grace.

VERS. 13.-- *Of the tribe of Aser.* [n: (ID.)] Aser, etc., *as far as* for it shall be repaid to them in the resurrection of the just.

VERS. 14.-- *Of the tribe of Gad.* [n: (ID.)] Allegorically, etc., *as far as* whence: *A cock girded about the loins, and a ram, and there is no king that resisteth him [n: (Prov. XXX)]*.

VERS. 15.-- *Of the tribe of Nephthali.* Nephthali, etc., *as far as* whence: *Now I will not call you servants, but friends,* [0384D] etc. [n: (Joan. XV)].

VERS. 16.-- *By their kindreds. [n: (AUG., quaest. 2 in Num.)]* With reason it is asked why, etc., *as far as* and the new by the four Gospels.

VERS. 18.-- *From the twentieth.* [n: (ISID.)] From the twentieth year are they numbered who are chosen for war, because from this age the wars of the vices are born against every man. And therefore they are chosen for the battle, that they may fight against lust, lest they be overcome by luxury.

VERS. 37.-- *Thirty-five thousand.* Good married folk, because to signify the number of thirty the forefinger and the thumb are touched together in a gentle kiss. Whence one is thirtyfold, another sixtyfold, and another a hundredfold.

VERS. 39.-- *Seven hundred.* Those advanced by spiritual gifts, and to be placed at the right hand of God. For the centenary [0385A] number is the first reckoned upon the right hand, the thumb and the forefinger being bent into a crown; whence: *Now the right hand counts the years.*

[n: (RAB. in Num., tom. II.)] In the tribe of Levi, etc., *as far as* they carry the Church as it were upon the shoulders of labour and of patience.

VERS. 51.-- *And when they must set forward,* etc. That is, by word and by example they shall dispose how each one, humbled, may walk in the advance of the virtues.

*Whosoever of strangers,* etc. *[n: (AUG., quaest. 3.)] Let the stranger who shall approach die.* Here the stranger is to be understood also of those sons of Israel, etc., *as far as* that they be called allophyli, as it were men of other tribes.

[0385B] *Allegorically.* He who approaches the sacred ministry unworthily does not escape the penalty of presumption: *for no man ought to take the honour to himself, unless he be called by God, as Aaron was [n: (Hebr. V)]*. [var: Vulg. *Nec quisquam sumit sibi honorem, sed qui vocatur a Deo, tamquam Aaron*, "nor doth any man take the honour to himself, but he that is called"] Whence Ozias, who usurped the priesthood, was struck with leprosy.

VERS. 52.-- *Every man by his troops,* etc. [n: (ORIG., hom. 2 in Num.)] Every man according to his own standards, etc., *as far as* if nothing restless, nothing disordered, nothing dishonourable be found in us.
