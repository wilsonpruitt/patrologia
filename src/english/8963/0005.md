## CHAPTER VI.

VERS. 3.-- *From wine and from everything that can intoxicate, [n: (RAB. in Num.)]* At the twelfth station, etc., *as far as* and they take delight in the corruption of the old wickedness.

VERS. 5.-- *The razor shall not pass.* [n: (ID.)] That is, he shall keep neither guile nor malice in his mind, lest, namely, the hair of the head, that is, the thought of the mind, be defiled. But let him hold innocence unto the end of life. For otherwise the vow will not be acceptable. *But he that shall have persevered shall be saved [n: (Matth. X)]* [var: Vulg. *qui autem perseveraverit usque in finem, hic salvus erit*, "he that shall persevere unto the end, he shall be saved"].

[0391C]VERS. 6.-- *He shall not go in over the dead.* [n: (ID.)] That is, he shall not imitate one dead in sins, nor shall he enter upon the life of his iniquity, lest he lose his labour. *For not he that beginneth, but he that finisheth, shall be crowned [n: (II Tim. II)]* [var: Vulg. at 2 Tim 2:5 *Nam et qui certat in agone, non coronatur nisi legitime certaverit*, "he that striveth is not crowned unless he strive lawfully"].

VERS. 7.-- *Nor over his father* [n: (ID.)] That is, let no carnal affection draw him back, every person, every dignity, every kinship is to be set after the service of Christ.

*The consecration of God.* [n: (ID.)] That is, the grace of the Holy Spirit has been poured out in his heart, whence: *Grieve not the Holy Spirit, in whom you are all sealed [n: (Ephes. IV)]* [var: Vulg. *Spiritum Sanctum Dei … in quo signati estis in diem redemptionis*, "the Holy Spirit of God … wherein you are sealed unto the day of redemption"].

VERS. 8.-- *All the days of separation.* That is, let him persevere unto the end without the contamination of sin and of offence.

[0391D]VERS. 9.-- *But if one shall have died.* [n: (ID.)] That is, if he shall have wished to imitate one polluted by mortal sin, and shall have gone down as far as consent, let him know that of necessity his continence has been polluted, and his head must be shaved, that is, his mind must be purged of depraved thought and hurtful delight.

*He shall shave forthwith.* [n: (ID.)] At the time, namely, of compunction, when he knows that he has offended, let him study to amend himself at once, and let him always fear a like ruin.

VERS. 10.-- *Two turtledoves.* [n: (ID.)] Turtledoves signify chastity, young pigeons simplicity. He therefore who wishes to come to the resurrection of the good, let him offer to the true priest, that is, to Christ, chastity of mind and of body, and simplicity in the catholic faith, [0392A] through which he enters into the Church, that by the help of so great a pontiff he may be expiated.

VERS. 11.-- *And he shall pray.* [n: (ID.)] Whence: *Our advocate with the Father [n: (Joan. I.)]* Or because he will make us pray, according to that: *The Spirit asketh for us with unspeakable groanings [n: (Rom. VIII)]*.

VERS. 12.-- *That the former days.* Let him presume nothing of his former manner of life, but let him trust in the grace of God alone, by whose gift he receives pardon and everlasting rest.

*The sanctification is defiled,* whence: *When the just man shall have turned away from his justice and shall have done iniquity, all his justices shall be delivered to oblivion [n: (Ezech. XVIII)]* [var: Vulg. *Si autem averterit se justus a justitia sua … Omnes justitiae ejus quas fecerat, non recordabuntur*, "all his justices which he had done shall not be remembered"].

*This is the law of consecration.* After the lawgiver has instructed the Nazarite how he is to be cleansed, if his consecration be defiled over a dead body, he adds how, [0392B] the vow being completed, he is to present himself to the Lord, about to render thanks with an oblation, intimating mystically that the life of the continent man, with the oblation of work and cleanness of heart, will please the Lord in a good end.

VERS. 14.-- *A lamb of a year old.* He offers this who places all his hope in his immolation, of whom it is said: *Behold the Lamb of God, behold him who taketh away the sins of the world [n: (Joan. I.)]* [var: Vulg. *peccatum mundi*, "the sin of the world"] He offers him in holocaust when, for love of him, mortifying the flesh, he consecrates to the Lord his whole life kindled with the ardour of charity.

[n: (AUG., quaest. 12 in Num.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* and this sacrifice of the peace offering, which he had said before, into a peace offering.

[n: (RAB.)] He offers a lamb in holocaust who shows forth to God simplicity of heart with the devotion of charity; [0392C] a sheep, which displays an example of innocence; but a ram as a peace offering, when he rules himself well in the way of God, and subjects the rebellious flesh to the command of the soul.

*And a sheep.* He offers a sheep for sin who faithfully believes that Christ the innocent was offered for the sin of the human race.

*And a ram.* This is done when he trusts that he himself is the one who through the dispensation of the flesh made peace with things heavenly and earthly, emptying out every principality and power; whom God exalted, and gave him the name which is above every name, etc.

VERS. 15.-- *And wafers:* the old law, dictated and tempered by the Holy Spirit for human profit.

*Without leaven:* because spiritual grace lies open, in the law and in the Gospel, to those who understand rightly.

[0392D]VERS. 16.-- *Which he shall offer.* Presenting in the sight of God, for reward, the merit and the labour, the meditation of each man, and the observance of the law, he brings him, namely, to the entrance of the heavenly life, the contest of good work being accomplished.

VERS. 17.-- *But the ram:* showing him worthy, namely, of the reward of perpetual quiet, as a prince of himself or of others; and a most excellent leader, who has ruled himself well and led himself in the path of the commandments of God, according to the doctrine of the Scriptures and the grace of spiritual charisms.

[n: (GREG., lib. II, Moral., cap. 39.)] What is it that the Nazarites nourish their hair? etc., *as far as* he may consume it in the perfection of devotion.

[0393A]VERS. 19.-- *And the boiled shoulder of the ram, and a cake.* The strength, namely, of work, boiled and proved by the fire of tribulation, the high priest causes his holy one to offer to God the Father.

*And a wafer,* namely, the knowledge of the law, helping by the gift of the Holy Spirit to complete it in work.

VERS. 20.-- *He shall lift up:* he will present to the Father the good things, namely, which the continent man works, and will cause them to be repaid with perpetual reward.

*Of the priest,* because whatever the Nazarite has well willed and has completed in work pertains to his glory: *For it is he that worketh in us both to will and to accomplish [n: (Philip. II)]* [var: Vulg. *Deus est enim, qui operatur in vobis*, "God … who worketh in you"].

*After these things,* that is, after right intention, discreet promise, perfection of work, and [0393B] the repayment of reward.

*To drink:* to draw fully and freely the spiritual gift, whence: *Thou shalt make them drink of the torrent of thy pleasure [n: (Psal. XXXV)]*.

VERS. 22.-- *And he spoke.* Moses signifies the law, Aaron the priesthood, and his sons the order of the Church. The Lord therefore teaches through the law the order of the priests, how they may build up the people by preaching, and confirm them by frequent prayer and blessing.

[n: (RAB., ubi supra. )] Moses has a manifold signification, etc., *as far as* while he completes, raises and anoints the tabernacle.

VERS. 24.-- *May he bless.* [n: (ID.)] It is to be noted that in this blessing the name of the Lord is brought in three times, that the holy Trinity may be understood, from whom and through whom and in whom all good things are to be sought and obtained, because [0393C] of him and through him and in him are all things.

*And may he keep.* [n: (ID.)] May he preserve by his grace the blessing which thou hast received: *Unless the Lord keep the city, he watcheth in vain that keepeth it [n: (Psal. CXXVI)]*.

VERS. 25.-- *May he show.* The showing of the face of God is propitiation, and the showing of true salvation; whence: *Show thy face, and we shall be saved [n: (Psal. LXXVII)]*.

VERS. 26.-- *May he turn,* etc. The turning of the face signifies mercy, the turning away, wrath; whence: *Look upon me and have mercy on me [n: (Psal. CXVIII)]*. And elsewhere: *Why turnest thou away thy face, and forgettest our want? [n: (Psal. XLIII.)]* We must pray therefore that he whose help we turn away from us by sinning may through his own goodness vouchsafe to make his face shine upon us, so that, rescuing us from [0393D] our enemies, he may grant perpetual peace.

VERS. 27.-- *They shall invoke.* [n: (ID.)] It belongs to the priests to invoke the name of the Lord: but to the Lord, to accomplish what is asked. Let no one therefore ascribe to himself what the Trinity alone bestows on whom it wills.
