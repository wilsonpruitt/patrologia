## CHAPTER X.

[0399C]VERS. 2.-- *Make thee two trumpets of silver,* etc. [n: (ISID., in Num.)] By the two trumpets the army is led, etc., *as far as* the minds of the hearers are more ardently stirred up to the contests of temptations.

VERS. 4.-- *If thou shalt sound but once. [n: (RAB., in Num.)]* To sound the trumpets once, etc., *as far as* and that all should say the same thing in the same sense and in the same knowledge.

VERS. 5.-- *The first.* Because, namely, the light of faith first shone forth. When therefore the preachers of the Gospel had been ordained, it was first said to them: *Go ye not into the way of the Gentiles, but go to the sheep that are lost of the house of Israel [n: (Matth. X)]* [var: Vulg. *In viam gentium ne abieritis, et in civitates Samaritanorum ne intraveritis: sed potius ite ad oves quae perierunt domus Israel*, "and into the cities of the Samaritans enter ye not: but go rather to the sheep"]: to whom Christ himself, present in person, preached the Gospel. But after the resurrection, appearing to the disciples, he said: *Going, teach ye all nations,* etc. [n: (Matth. XVIII)]. The tribe of Juda therefore, with the others which [0399D] dwelt with it toward the east, first moved the camp: then the rest according to their own order. Because it behoved that the primitive Church should first be built in Judaea, and afterwards that the Gentiles from all the nations of the world be called to the faith.

VERS. 7.-- *But when they are to be gathered together.* [n: (RAB.)] In the calling together of the people, etc., *as far as* for we seek nothing but that what is closed may lie open.

VERS. 8.-- *And the sons.* The preachers, namely, preach the word of God, and show the way of justice to their hearers, that they may set the step of good work in the path of the commandments of God, and flee the way of the wicked.

*They shall sound the trumpets.* Rousing their subjects, namely, to penance and to the compunction of tears, and [0400A] provoking the hearts of the hearers to implore the mercy of God.

VERS. 10.-- *If at any time you shall have a banquet.* [n: (RAB.)] Note that in adversities, etc., *as far as* and elsewhere: *Preach the word, be instant in season, out of season,* etc. [n: (I Tim. IV)].

VERS. 11.-- *In the second year. [n: (HIERON., epist. ad Fabiolam, tom. I.)]* At the twelfth station the children of Israel came into the desert of Sinai, etc., *as far as* but within they are full of dead men's bones, and of all filthiness.

[n: (GREG., Pastoralis parte III, admon. 18.)] For the most part we might better persuade the elated of profitable things, etc., *as far as* he would thence be bent by the words of one exhorting him.

VERS. 23.-- *And the ark of the covenant.* [n: (RAB.)] The ark of the covenant [0400B] of the Lord, etc., *as far as* it shows the path of truth and of justice to those who follow.

VERS. 34.-- *The cloud also.* The divine protection, which by the grace of the Holy Spirit has defended the elect from the beginning of the world, and keeps them for the quiet that is to come.

VERS. 35.-- *And when it was lifted up.* By the example of Moses the rulers of the people are stirred up, that, always solicitous, they may commend to the Lord the flock committed to them, at the beginning of the work and at its completion, so that he may protect his own sheep and drive off their enemies.

## CHAPTER XI.

VERS. 1.-- *In the meantime there arose a murmuring. [n: (RAB., in Num., tom. II.)]* The kindled fire of the Lord devours the murmuring people, etc., *as far as* he shall be burned with perpetual punishment as much in soul [0400C] as in body.

VERS. 5.-- *The melons and the leeks. [n: (GREG., lib. XX Moral., cap. 19.)]* The minds of carnal men, for the love of the world, etc., *as far as* where they may groan carnally.

VERS. 16.-- *Gather unto me seventy. [n: (GREG., lib. XXIX Moral., cap. 13.)]* As for the most part young men are called, etc., *as far as* it is plain that the old age of the mind, not of the body, is to be chosen.

VERS. 17.-- *That I may come down.* [n: (ISID.)] At the thirteenth station, etc., *as far as* what once had been in Moses and in the prophets.

*And I will take of thy spirit, and will deliver to them, that they may sustain,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 18 in Num.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* not that Moses should therefore have the less.

[0400D]VERS. 21.-- *There are six hundred thousand footmen of this people. [n: (AUG., quaest. 19.)]* It is asked: did Moses say this out of distrust or out of inquiry? etc., *as far as* when the sentence of God which followed was not one to punish, but rather one to teach.

VERS. 24.-- *Moses therefore came, and told the people the words of the Lord, gathering them. [n: (ORIG., Hom. 6 in Num.)]* Another reading: Moses went out, he says, etc., *as far as* and the like, etc.

VERS. 25.-- *Taking away of the spirit.* [n: (ID.)] It is not to be understood, etc., *as far as* and its origin received no loss from the derivation.

*Had rested.* [n: (ID.)] *And the spirit rested upon them, and they all prophesied,* etc. It is not read that the spirit rested, etc., *as far as* [0401A] he set forth the praise of their life and their virtues.

[n: (ORIG.)] *And the spirit rested in them, and they prophesied,* etc. The spirit works continually, etc., *as far as* nor is it fitting that the presence of the Holy Spirit be there.

VERS. 26.-- *But there remained in the camp.* Not by negligence did the Lord command, but by the devotion of humility, judging themselves unworthy of the receiving of so great a gift: therefore God, the judge of hearts, judged them worthy, and showed it.

VERS. 29.-- *Why, saith he, art thou jealous. [n: (GREG., lib. XXIII Moral., cap. 24.)]* The devout mind of pastors, etc., *as far as* who did not envy the good that he had.

*A multitude of quails, who a little,* etc. [n: (RAB. in Num. )] The carnal people of the Jews, etc., *as far as [0401B] But our conversation is in heaven [n: (II Cor. X)]*.

VERS. 34.-- *The graves of lust. [n: (ISID., in Num., tom. V.)]* The thirteenth station is at the graves of lust, etc., *as far as* within they are full of dead men's bones and of all filthiness.

*They came,* etc. [n: (HIERON., epist. ad Fabiolam.)] The fourteenth station, etc., *as far as* who says by the prophet: *Shall he that falleth not add that he should rise again? [n: (Jer. VIII.)]* [var: Vulg. *Numquid qui cadit non resurget?*, "Shall not he that falleth rise again?"]

[n: (ISID.)] The fourteenth station, etc., *as far as* he brought it back to pardon.
