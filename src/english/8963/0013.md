## CHAPTER XIX. (cont.)

*For a keeping.* Because the more subtle sacraments delivered to the clean hearts of the Gentiles are turned into a fortifying of the mind Because while we attend by imitation to the secret and subtle things of that working of his, by this we provide against uncleanness [0413D] for ourselves, and, on account of the working, let the ashes be for our keeping, but on account of baptism, for the water of sprinkling. Then do we providently keep the ashes for our keeping, when we consider carefully our Redeemer, who suffered for our iniquities. For we weigh what he will render to the good, who willed to die for the evil, what we owe to his grace, being brought to the justice of faith, we who, set in iniquity, received the intercession of his death.

VERS. 10.-- *They shall have this. [n: (AUG., quaest. 33.)]* Another reading, etc., *as far as* which the evening signifies.

VERS. 11.-- *He that toucheth these things.* [n: (AUG.)] *He that toucheth a dead body,* etc. He begins to say how men are made unclean, etc., *as far as* according to this saying [0414A] I judge it to be, through the prophet: *In three and four iniquities I will not be adverse.* [var: Vulg. Nm 19:11 *Qui tetigerit cadaver hominis*, "he that toucheth the corpse of a man"]

VERS. 12.-- *If on the third day,* etc. [n: (ID.)] But if he shall not have been purified on the third day, etc., *as far as* still, that is, even after death.

VERS. 13.-- *He shall defile the tabernacle,* as much as in him lies, according to that: *Extinguish not the Spirit [n: (I Thess. V)]*, though he cannot be extinguished. For if he wished the tabernacle to be understood as made unclean by this, he would command it to be cleansed.

VERS. 15.-- *The vessel that,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] The covering of a lid, or the binding, is the censure of discipline, by which whosoever is not restrained is reproved as an unclean and polluted vessel. For he is polluted as a vessel without a lid or a binding, who, lying open through the zeal of ostentation, is covered by no [0414B] veil of silence.

VERS. 16.-- *[n: (AUG., quaest. 34.)] If any man in the field.* Another reading, etc., *as far as* that is, slain by a wound or dead without a wound.

VERS. 19.-- *He shall wash both himself,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* she shall be unclean until the evening.

## CHAPTER XX.

VERS. 20.-- *And the children came,* etc. [n: (ISID., in Num., tom. V.)] The thirty-third encampment is in the desert of Sin: this is Cades. But Sin is interpreted holy, by antiphrasis: as a grove is called lucus when it shines not at all; or war is called bellum, when it is horrid. In this encampment Mary dies and is buried, who signifies prophecy dead, as Moses and Aaron signify the end of the law [0414C] and of the priesthood, who avail not to come to the land of promise, nor to lead the people out of the wilderness of this world, which Jesus alone does, that is, the Saviour, the Son of God.

*Striking with the rod. [n: (AUG., quaest. 35.)]* Because water was brought out of the rock, etc., *as far as* for two pieces of wood are the cross.

VERS. 12.-- *Because you have not believed.* [n: (ISID., in Num. )] Here Moses offends the Lord, etc., *as far as Christ is the power of God and the wisdom of God.*

VERS. 13.-- *This is the water of contradiction, where the children strove,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] What was said of that water, etc., *as far as* for of him it is said: *For a sign which shall be contradicted,* etc. [n: (Luc. II)].

VERS. 22.-- *Into mount Hor, which is in the borders.* [0414D] [n: (RAB.)] It is the thirty-fourth encampment, etc., *as far as* the ascent to paradise.

VERS. 30.-- *But all the multitude.* [n: (BED.)] Aaron is bewailed, Jesus is not bewailed, because in the law there is a descent to hell, in the Gospel a passing over to paradise. The people wept thirty days, because before the law and under the law and under grace the synagogue receives not spiritual joy, but lies mourning over the letter that killeth.
