## CHAPTER XXI. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In Jasa.* ⟦s2⟧ In Jasa, in Hebrew *iahtsah*, which is interpreted the fulfilling of the commandment. ⟦s3⟧ For if we come to the [0418C] fulfilling of the commandments, we overcome the proud and lifted-up one: for to fulfil a commandment is to overcome the devil and his army. ⟦s4⟧ For nothing will be able to hurt us, if we come into *Jasa,* that is, if we keep the precepts of our Lord Jesus Christ.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *In the edge of the sword. ⟦s2⟧ The word of God is living, effectual, and more piercing than any sword, etc. [n: (Heb. IV)]*. ⟦s3⟧ Of which the Apostle says: *And the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God [n: (Eph. V)]*. ⟦s4⟧ In the edge of this Sehon falls, that is, the devil.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *His land.* ⟦s2⟧ Every earthly region is called the land of Sehon; but Christ and his Church bear rule in every land of Sehon.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (RAB.)] Jeboc, a city and the boundary of Sehon's kingdom, etc., *as far as* his name was called Israel.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0418D]VERS. 25.-- *He took therefore. [n: (AUG., quaest. 44 in Num.)]* Here Israel took possession of the cities of the Amorrhites, etc., *as far as* but they turned aside from them.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Israel. [n: (ORIG., hom. 13 in Num.)]* He who is Israel in Christ, etc., *as far as* and he wills that it be kindled.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISID.)] In the thirty-ninth station, etc., *as far as* and once it is understood it is bravely overcome.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *Of Sehon the king.* ⟦s2⟧ Sehon, that is, of one speaking: for those who act under the devil know only how to speak, but they speak empty things, as for instance certain astrologers and philosophers; but the kingdom of the faithful, which is from God, is not in speech, but in the power of God [n: (I Cor. IV)].

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Moab.* [n: (RAB.)] Moab, who is interpreted, etc., *as far as* they fall down and are consumed.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0419A]VERS. 27.-- *Therefore it is said.* [n: (ORIG., ubi supra. )] Another reading, etc., *as far as* not of the Amorrhites, but of the children of Israel.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *Woe to thee, Moab.* [n: (RAB.)] Chamos is interpreted, etc., *as far as* against whom it is said: *Deliver not up to beasts the souls that confess to thee [n: (Psal. LXXIII)]*.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *Jazer,* that is, their strength. ⟦s2⟧ Namely the strongest dogmas of the heretics, framed by the art of dialectic. ⟦s3⟧ *And they possessed it.* ⟦s4⟧ By turning them from error and by teaching the precepts of the Lord, that from their. correction and progress they might gain for themselves the heavenly fatherland.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *Og the king of Basan.* [n: (ORIG.)] To Og the king of Basan, etc., *as far as* for nothing can be honorable which is base.
