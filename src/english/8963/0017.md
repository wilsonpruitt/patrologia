## CHAPTER XXIII.

VERS. 7.-- *Come,* he says, *and curse Jacob for me. [n: (ORIG. hom. 15.)]* In the Latin speech, etc., *as far as* and therefore he says that the word of God was set *in his mouth.*

[0421C]VERS. 8.-- *How shall I curse.* [n: (ID.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* and the authority of pronouncing be taken away.

*Him whom God hath not cursed.* He who marks so great a merit in the man who is cursed, and utters sentence, whom neither the quality of the sin nor the disposition of the sinner deceives: but man knows neither of these, and often, provoked by reproaches or injuries, curses. Therefore the Apostle says: *Bless, and curse not [n: (Rom. XII)]*, that the vice of reviling may be cut away.

VERS. 9.-- *From the highest,* etc. As if to say, therefore he ought not to be cursed, because his life is lofty and heavenly. No one understands it unless he has ascended to eminent knowledge.

[n: (ORIG.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* they shall have practised upon earth, rising [0421D] together with Christ.

*See,* etc. The acts of Jacob, that is, of active men, are seen: the contemplation of Israel, that is, of contemplative men, is only understood. Or, in the resurrection to come Jacob shall be seen, that is, the body; Israel is understood, that is, the soul and spirit of those who rise again.

*The people alone.* Because it is not mingled with the rest of men, nor reckoned among the other nations, being ennobled by fixed privileges, by its observances and its own ordinances, just as the tribe of Levi is not mingled with the rest.

*The people shall dwell alone,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] The spiritual Jacob and Israel, etc., *as far as* it shall be reckoned no more among the nations.

*Who can number.* [n: (ID.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* and [0422A] he suffers those things which are written in the second book of Kings.

VERS. 10.-- *Let my soul die.* [n: (ID.)] He seems to prophesy of himself: but according to the letter it was fulfilled neither in Balaam nor in that Israel, nor could it be, for he died not among them but by them.

[n: (ID.)] Let my soul die the death of the just, etc., *as far as: but faith which worketh by charity [n: (Gal. V)]*.

*[n: (GREG., lib. XXXIII Moral., cap. 27.)] Let my soul die the death of the just, and let my last end be like theirs,* etc. Many within the Church, etc., *as far as* whence, *Their countenances were no more changed to divers looks [n: (I Reg. I)]*.

*And let my last end be like theirs,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] This can be understood of the [0422B] Magi, who came first from the East to adore Christ [n: (Matth. II)], who were of the stock of Balaam by succession of race and by the handing down of his discipline. For it is certain that they recognized the star which Balaam had foretold would rise in Israel

VERS. 11.-- *What is this?* Balac, hearing blessings instead of curses against his hope, and bearing it no longer, broke in upon the words of the prophesying man.

[n: (ID.)] The bitter king does not want a blessing, but seeks curses: for he is of the kindred of him to whom it is said: *Cursed art thou among all the beasts of the earth [n: (Gen. III)]*.

*Balac said therefore.* He thinks him terrified by the multitude of Israel, and that therefore he did not dare to curse, and that a change of place would profit him.

[0422C]VERS. 13.-- *Thou canst not see.* The madman thought that the grace of Israel could be hidden by the meanness of the place, not knowing that *a city set upon a mountain cannot be hid.*

VERS. 14.-- *He built.* The business was carried on with profane sacrifices, and divination was sought by the magic art. Yet God, willing that grace should abound where sin abounded [n: (Rom. V)], deigned to be present, not for the sacrifices, but as coming to meet him. And there he gives his word and his mysteries, and foretells things to come, where the faith and the wonder of the Gentiles chiefly hangs: so that they who will not believe our prophets may believe their own diviners.

VERS. 15.-- *Stand here beside.* He stood at his own holocaust, set fast in his own idolatry, and therefore he was falling the more: he therefore seems to rise who had fallen the more, when by idolatry [0422D] he seemed to stand.

*God is not as a man, that he should lie. [n: (ORIG., hom. 16.)]* Another reading, etc., *as far as* lest the vengeance of his own evils come upon him.

VERS. 19.-- *He said therefore,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* but are these things to be observed more secretly by the more perfect?

VERS. 21.-- *There is no idol in Jacob.* Another translation: *There shall be no labor in Jacob, neither shall sorrow be seen in Israel,* namely in the age to come.

[n: (ORIG.)] *There shall be no labor in Jacob, neither shall sorrow be seen in Israel,* etc. [n: (II Cor. XI)]. The state of the life to come is proclaimed, etc., *as far as* and let them hear those prophets.

[0423A] *The Lord God,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* he (who wrestled and prevailed) shall receive.

*And the sound of the king's victory is in him.* The admonition of preaching, whereby in us the king, that is, Christ, triumphs over the devil.

VERS. 22.-- *God brought him out.* [n: (ID.)] Another reading, *as far as* he will transform the body of our lowliness, made conformable to the body of his glory.

*Whose strength.* Whose? Christ's. Whatsoever is his, is one horn, that is, one kingdom, whose glory is the spiritual Israel. For he himself says: *Father, grant to them that, as I and thou are one, so they may be one in us [n: (Joan. XVII)]*.

VERS. 23.-- *There is no augury in Jacob. [n: (ORIG., hom. 16 in Num.)]* Another reading, etc., *as far as* neither [0423B] divination in Israel. There follows:

*In time it shall be told to Jacob, and to Israel, what God will accomplish:* [var: Vulg. *temporibus suis dicetur Jacob et Israeli quid operatus sit Deus*, "in their own times it shall be told to Jacob and Israel what God hath wrought"] that is, when it is fitting, when it is expedient, etc., *as far as* which Moses (for these causes, I believe) called unclean.

*In Israel.* He who through purity of faith and cleanness of mind sees God would not receive Balaam himself, yet does not dare to reject the word which set God in his mouth. For unless the word were from God, he would not have revealed it to his servant Moses, far away, at the time when Balaam was saying these things.

VERS. 24.-- *Behold the people,* etc. Others: *Behold, he shall rise up as a lion's whelp, and shall leap up as a lion.* For he is a whelp when newborn infants, reasonable, [0423C] take milk without guile: but a leaping lion when the perfect man puts away the things that were of a little child.

[n: (ORIG.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* he gave to those who believe in him the name of a lion, and of a lion's whelp.

*He shall be raised up.* He says that the people is to be raised up hereafter, for of that which he was seeing he would have said, he is raised up.

*He shall not lie down.* [n: (ORIG.)] Another reading, etc., *as far as* and as a leaping lion.

*And when he had brought him.* [n: (ORIG.)] And Balac took him, etc., *as far as* and the affairs of God are forsaken.

*Build me here.* [n: (ID.)] What things the Gentiles sacrifice, etc., *as far as* who do not receive the faith of Christ.
