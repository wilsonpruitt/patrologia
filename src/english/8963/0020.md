## CHAPTER XXVII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Give to them. [n: (ORIG., hom. 22 in Num.)] Thou shalt give him a possession,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ These things according to the history etc., *as far as* rightly therefore have the daughters of Saphaad spoken.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *A man when he shall die without a son,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The law of succession is subjoined, etc., *as far as* he shall not lose his reward.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0430A]VERS. 12.-- *Go up into the mountain,* etc. [n: (ID., hom. 22.)] See how he that is perfect and blessed, etc., *as far as* He alone therefore must be sought, who did no sin.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] The death of Moses is the end of the law, etc., *as far as* that is, under the veil of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit our soul shall rest.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Because you offended,* etc. [n: (AUG., quaest. 53 in Num.)] The Lord names that cause of the death of Moses which he names also of his brother's, namely because they did not sanctify him before the people at the water of contradiction, that is, because they doubted that by his gift water could flow forth from the rock.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ But mystically it is signified that neither the old priesthood, whose person Aaron bore, nor the law itself, whose person Moses bore, brings in [0430B] the people of God into the land of the eternal inheritance: but Jesus, in whom Christ is signified, that is, grace through faith. ⟦s2⟧ And Aaron indeed died before Israel entered into any part of the land of promise: but while Moses was yet living the land of the Amorrhites was taken and possessed, though he did not cross the Jordan with them. ⟦s3⟧ For in some part the law is observed in the Christian faith. ⟦s4⟧ For there are also precepts there which Christians are bidden to observe even to this day. ⟦s5⟧ But that priesthood and that sacrifice hold no part today of the Christian faith, save that they were done and finished in the shadows of things to come. ⟦s6⟧ But when it is said of both brothers that they should be gathered to their own people, it is manifest that there was not in them the wrath of God, which separates from the peace [0430C] of the eternal fellowship. ⟦s7⟧ Whence it appears that not only their offices, but their deaths also, were signs of things to come, not punishments of God's indignation.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Let him provide.* ⟦s2⟧ Note, he did not choose sons, he did not choose grandsons, nor does he ask that they be appointed leaders: he commits the choice to the judgment of God.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *May the Lord God provide.* [n: (ORIG.)] Being about to depart from the world, etc., *as far as* that the children of Israel may be able to hear him.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A spirit.* ⟦s2⟧ The Holy Spirit, namely. ⟦s3⟧ For he would not say this of the spirit of man, which there was none who had not. ⟦s4⟧ Yet he is bidden to lay his hand upon him, lest anyone, however endowed with grace, dare to refuse the sacraments of consecration.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *And thou shalt give.* [n: (ISID. in Num. )] Jesus succeeds [0430D] Moses, etc., *as far as* and the remission of sins, etc.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And part of thy glory.* ⟦s2⟧ As if to say, thou shalt make him a partner of thy glory: for things of this kind are not diminished as though divided out in parts, but are whole to all, whole to each, who have fellowship in them.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *That he may hear,* etc. [n: (ORIG., hom. 22)] If the sacrifices and the legal institutions had remained until the present, they would have shut out the faith of the Gospel. ⟦s2⟧ For there was in them a greatness and a religion full of reverence, which at the first sight would astound the beholder. ⟦s3⟧ For who, seeing the sanctuary, the altar, the priests completing the sacrifice, and the whole order of that matter, would not think this to be the fullest rite whereby God the creator of all things was worshipped? [0431A] But thanks be to the coming of Christ, who destroys those things which seemed great on earth, and in the worship of God has carried men over from things visible to things invisible, and from things temporal to things eternal. ⟦s4⟧ But Christ himself requires ears which may hear these things, and eyes which may see these things.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *For this,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Morally we are instructed that whosoever receives power over a people according to the mastership of the divine law (which is chiefly commended in the office of the priest) should order and rule his own life and the life of those subject to him.

## CHAPTER XXVIII.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The Lord also said. [n: (ORIG., hom. 23 in Num.)]* Another reading, etc., *as far as* who thinks that he bestows something upon God as upon one in need.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0431B] [n: (ID.)] *In my festival days.* ⟦s2⟧ God has his own festival days, etc., *as far as* where as yet there are no sins, he calls them my festival days.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *These are the sacrifices.* ⟦s2⟧ Let us therefore seek diligently, etc., *as far as* but there are other festival days for those who cannot unceasingly offer the sacrifice of chastity.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *But on the sabbath day.* [n: (RAB. in Num. )] The second festival therefore is, etc., *as far as* who is a priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedech [n: (Hebr. V)].

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And two tenths of flour tempered with oil.* [n: (RAB.)] He offers these who, filled with spiritual grace, teaches his hearers by words and by examples the knowledge of the two testaments, the Holy Spirit teaching, not for temporal [0431C] gain, but for the reward of eternal life.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *On the calends.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, in the new moons, when the Church or any soul so draws near to the sun of justice that it becomes one spirit with him, and, renewed through him, casts off the old man.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] The third festival is the day of the new moon, etc., *as far as* being renewed she slays.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Calves of the herd.* ⟦s2⟧ In the two calves can be signified the gladness of good work in the soul or in the body: which is offered to the Lord according to the two Testaments in the two precepts of charity.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *One ram.* ⟦s2⟧ In the ram, the governance of discipline, strong and unmoved according to the unity of the catholic faith.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Seven lambs of a year old.* ⟦s2⟧ Simplicity and gentleness of manners for heavenly rest through the grace of the sevenfold [0431D] Spirit.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And three tenths of flour.* ⟦s2⟧ He offers these who, enlightened with the oil of gladness, sets forth spiritual knowledge in the faith of the holy Trinity together with good work.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And two tenths.* ⟦s2⟧ This he does who studies to turn the decalogue of the law into the two precepts of charity for his own profit and his neighbours', and so to dedicate his whole life to the Lord for a sweet odour.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *And the tenth of a tenth,* etc., *for every lamb.* ⟦s2⟧ This is done when simplicity of manners is established and adorned according to the rule of the Scriptures, the grace of the Holy Spirit working with it. ⟦s3⟧ And rightly are all these things commanded to be offered to the Lord in holocaust, because we ought to do these things not that we may seek human praise or a present [0432A] recompense, but that we may in all things please God the giver of all good things, and may merit the recompense of eternal life.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *The month. [n: (ORIG., hom. 23 in Num.)]* It is set in the fourth place among the festivals, etc., *as far as* and he keeps a festival day with God and with his angels.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *On the fifteenth day.* [n: (ID.)] There follows upon this a continuous festival, etc., *as far as* he is made more wicked and worse.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] All who are to be imbued with higher disciplines, etc., *as far as* with spiritual sacrifices offered in the purification of the mind.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *The day also of the firstfruits, when you shall offer new fruits,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] The sixth festival is called that of the new things, etc., *as far as* goodness, gentleness [0432B] and the like.

## CHAPTER XXIX.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The seventh month also. [n: (ORIG., hom. 23 in Num.)]* There follows the festival of the sevenths, etc., *as far as* whom God hath set forth as a propitiator in his own blood, through faith.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ORIG.)] The last festival day, etc., *as far as, But when that which is perfect is come, that which is in part shall be done away [n: (I Cor. XIII)]*.
