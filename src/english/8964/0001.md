## CHAPTER ONE. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 51.-- *Dukes,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ It is to be noted that he first spoke of kings in Edom, afterwards of dukes, though they were before: of whom he names certain mothers, as Oolibama and Thamna.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ *Duke Aliha.* [var: Vulg. 1 Par. 1:51 *dux Alva*] Alva is read in Genesis, whose interpretation is a lifting up; in Paralipomenon Aliha, that is, upon her.

## CHAPTER II.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0635A]

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Achar who troubled Israel. [n: (HIERON., Quaest. in Paral., tom. III.)]* Achar, who is also called Achan in Josue, that is, a lurking serpent, Achar, troubled, etc., *as far as* that is, they were destroyed by the multitude of their sins.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Calubi,* that is, my dog, who is also Caleb, that is, dog, one of the spies. ⟦s2⟧ Hesron also and Jephone are the same, whence Caleb is read as the son of Hesron, and sometimes as the son of Jephone.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *And Isai begot.* ⟦s2⟧ In the book of Kings Isai is said to have eight sons, in Paralipomenon not more than seven. ⟦s3⟧ But the Hebrews say that Nathan the prophet was the son of Sama (or Sammaa, or Simmaa) the son of Isai, whom he brought up in the place of a son and nourished, numbered among his sons. ⟦s4⟧ Among the sons of Isai also Nathan [0635B] was brought to Samuel, who is also Jonatham, who slew the lion. ⟦s5⟧ And it is to be known that where he is called a prophet, Nathan is written, not Jonathan

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Jether.* ⟦s2⟧ In Kings, Jethra the Israelite; but the quality of merits makes the diversity of the names; for Jethra is interpreted a remnant, but Jether a small remnant, and the remnant is joined with the Israelites, the small remnant with the Ismaelites.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Ephrata.* [n: (HIERON.)] This is Maria the sister of Moses, the mother of Hur, the father of Huri, the grandfather of Beseleel. ⟦s2⟧ And Hur is he who with Aaron held up the hands of Moses while Josue fought.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Jair.* ⟦s2⟧ He was judge after Thola and judged Israel two and twenty years, having thirty sons, princes of thirty cities, which from him [0635C] were named, as is read in the book of Judges.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *They were born.* ⟦s2⟧ Thus far the writer of the history has woven the generation of the two sons of Hesron, of the two later ones, that is, of Aram and Calubi, of whom he narrated before; because he wished in some manner to make known the royal and the priestly dignity. ⟦s3⟧ But now he returns to the genealogy of the firstborn, that is, of Jerameel.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *Onam.* ⟦s2⟧ He took his name from Onam the son of Juda, but they differ, because the son of Juda is written through Num, the son of Jerameel through Mem

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *Sesan.* ⟦s2⟧ The Hebrews say that he is Elimelech, the husband of Noemi, the father of Mahalom and Cheliom, and therefore the writer in what follows intimates that Sesan had not sons, but daughters, because his two sons [0635D] died without children.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 43.-- *Hebron.* ⟦s2⟧ A city in which David reigned seven years before he reigned in Jerusalem. ⟦s3⟧ It signifies that these places took their names from the posterity of Caleb, who received Hebron and its suburbs for his lot.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 50.-- *These were the sons.* ⟦s2⟧ He recapitulates the generation of Caleb by Ephrata, which he left aside before, when he related that Hur was begotten of Ephrata, that is, of Maria the sister of Moses.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 52.-- *Cariathiarim,* which is also Cariathabal, that is, the city of woods, one of the cities of the Gabaonites, at the ninth milestone for those going from Helia to Diospolis, in the tribe of Juda.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0636A]VERS. 54.-- *Bethlehem;* the city of David, in the tribe of Juda, in which the Lord was born, at the seventh milestone from Elia toward the southern quarter, beside the way that leads to Hebron, where the sepulchre of Jesse and of David is shown, and about a thousand paces off the tower of Ader, that is, the tower of the flock, in a certain prophecy signifying beforehand the shepherds made privy to the Lord's nativity.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Who divided the half of the resting places* [var: Vulg. 1 Par. 2:52 *videbat*, "who saw"] [n: (HIERON., ubi supra )], that is, he received the lots of Madian beside the sepulchres of the patriarchs which are in Cariatharbe, and his sons were the Jethrites, etc. ⟦s2⟧ The Seraites and the Esthaolites drew their names from places, which are Sarrai and Esthaol, where Samson is buried. ⟦s3⟧ For a resting place is called a sepulchre. ⟦s4⟧ Others understand the half of the resting place to be the half of the time [0636B] in which the children of Israel began to possess the land of promise, namely from that time in which Moses gave the land of Galaad beyond the Jordan to Ruben and Gad and to the half tribe of Manasse, until the rest of the tribes received their lots. ⟦s5⟧ In the middle of this time they say that Sobal died, and thus saw the half of the resting places, that is, came to the middle of that time.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ But mystically Sobal, that is, vain oldness, or a bar for carrying. ⟦s2⟧ He signifies the Jews, who of old were called the people of God and first received the law of the letter, who bore the books of the law and of the prophets, but did not understand the mystery of the incarnation of Christ. ⟦s3⟧ Whence they saw the half of the resting places, that is, the history. ⟦s4⟧ But he possesses the fulness of the resting places, [0636C] who after good works rejoices greatly in eternal rest. ⟦s5⟧ Or he sees the half of rest, who after the labour of the present life has rest of soul. ⟦s6⟧ But then he shall see the perfection of the resting places, when both in soul and in body he shall fully enjoy the perfection of rest

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 55.-- *Of them that dwelt.* [n: (ID.)] The Hebrews hand down that Jabes was a most skilled teacher of the law, in whose sight sat the sons of Cineus, who are also Jobab, the kinsmen of Moses. ⟦s2⟧ The scribes therefore are called singing and resounding, because they were assiduously engaged in the speaking of the law of God: and the history of the kings relates that they dwelt also in tents. ⟦s3⟧ Jabes signifies Christ, the true master of the saints who sing praises to God. ⟦s4⟧ For Jabes is interpreted *drying up,* and Christ has dried up the flow of carnal desires in his faithful. [0636D] The Cinaeans, that is, possession or possessors, are all the elect of whom it is said: My inheritance is Israel, and whose possession is the Lord. ⟦s5⟧ Rechab is interpreted *charioteer* or *ascending.* ⟦s6⟧ And these came of the heat of the father of the house of Rechab, because, kindled by the Father of lights with the grace of the Spirit, they ascend into the heavens by the chariot of the virtues, of whom it is said: *Whosoever are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God [n: (Rom. VIII)]*.
