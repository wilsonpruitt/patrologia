## CHAPTER THREE.

VERS. 1.-- *But David.* He returns to unfold the progeny of David, which he left aside before, when, enumerating the offspring of Amram the son of Hesron, he came to Isai and to his sons, of whom David was the last.

[0637A] *The Jezraelitess.* [n: (RAB.)] Jezrael is a city in which Naboth dwelt, whom Jezabel slew.

*Daniel.* [n: (ID.)] Daniel, who in the book of Kings is Cheleab, that is, avenging his father, because according to the Hebrews, disputing with Miphiboseth about questions of the law, he appeared the victor and was seen to avenge his father in him, lest a warlike man should be mocked in disputing by a weak man.

VERS. 3.-- *Of Egla his wife.* [n: (HIERON.)] It is asked why this Egla alone is called the wife of David; but this is Michol, who among many is alone called wife, because he took her first to wife in his youth, who is said to have died in childbirth. But Egla is interpreted a heifer, which signifies the Synagogue, which took up the yoke of the law, but by wantoning corrupted it, whence, *Ephraim is a heifer taught to love treading out the corn.* Her our [0637B] David received, when he joined her to himself in the testament that was given. She died in childbirth, because when the Word of God appeared in the flesh and preached the Gospel, whereas by faith she ought to have begotten an offspring of good works, being scandalized at his passion she drew upon herself eternal death

VERS. 4.-- *In Hebron where he reigned,* etc. What is to be taken by the reign of David in Hebron or in Jerusalem, we touched upon in expounding the book of Kings. It is written in what follows: All the time in which David reigned, forty years; he reigned in Hebron seven years, in Jerusalem three and thirty, whence it is asked why in the sum forty years and six months are not numbered; but the Hebrews say that [0637C] David fled from Absalom his son for six months, and that these were deservedly shut out from the total of his reign. They say also that for six months in Hebron he laboured under an infirmity of body, and that therefore they are not rightly reckoned in the reign. For they say that he reigned in Hebron from that time when, the Amalecites being conquered, he sent of their spoils to the elders of Juda, namely to those who were at Hebron and to others.

VERS 9.-- *All these the sons of David,* etc. Because all the sons of Christ look for the eternal palm, but the sons of the concubines are excepted, who by name did not deserve to be numbered among the rest. For Jews, heretics or schismatics cannot have fellowship of this society: for the glory of the heavenly kingdom looks for sons of David of a noble marriage only. *For the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with the son of the free woman [n: (Gen. XXI)]*.

[0637D]VERS. 15.-- *And the sons of Josias.* [n: (RAB.)] Four sons of Josias are read here, etc., *as far as* who likewise was carried over to the Chaldeans.

VERS. 17.-- *Asir, Salathiel, Melchiram, Phadaia, Senneser,* etc. But these are the names of one man, as the Hebrews will have it, he is Phadaia the father of Zorobabel and of Semei. Whence in Esdras and in Matthew Zorobabel is written Salathiel. And Phadaia is interpreted *redeemed of the Lord,* Asir *imprisoned,* Salathiel *the shrub of the Lord;* all which agree with the holy teachers, by whose office the people of the Church is begotten, who, chastised by divers afflictions and redeemed through the grace of the Lord, bring forth the buds of the virtues both in themselves and in their subjects.

[0638A]VERS. 18.-- *Senneser.* He is Selbasar, to whom Cyrus counted out the vessels of the house of the Lord, as is said in Esdras. Now Senneser is one formed, Sesbasar one afflicted, which things befit the keepers of the vessels of God, that is, of the holy souls to whom Christ, whom Cyrus signified in his name and in the release, to whom it is said: *Ask of me, and I will give thee the Gentiles for thy inheritance [n: (Psal. II)]*, and who loosed the captivity of the human race, counted out the holy souls to be kept, which are the numbered vessels of the Lord, because *the Lord knoweth who are his [n: (II Tim. II)]*.
