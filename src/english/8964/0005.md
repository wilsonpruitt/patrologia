## CHAPTER IX.

VERS. 2.-- *Nathinaei. [n: (HIERON., Quaest. in Paral., tom. III.)]* A gift, or those serving in humility. These are the Gabaonites whom Josue granted their lives, and appointed to the service of the temple, that they might carry water and wood [n: (Josue IX)].

VERS. 11.-- *Of Helcias the son of Mosoliam,* etc. [var: Vulg. *Mosollam*] This was [0644A] the great priest who found Deuteronomy in the temple of the Lord in the time of Josias, and he is said to have been the father of Jeremias the prophet.

VERS. 16.-- *Idithun.* [n: (HIERON.)] The singer of David, namely. For at the king's gate toward the east there kept watch, of the sons of Levi, the singers by their courses, and they came together by turns to sing psalms with David, because, before the temple was built, the ark of the covenant was in the palace, and there the keepers of the house served.

VERS. 19.-- *And Sellum the son of Core.* The first Cora is written with Aleph, and sounds Cora, the second with He, and sounds Core. Cora is interpreted *crying out,* Core *bald.* This is he who in Numbers stirred up sedition against Moses [n: (Num. XVI)].

*The keepers of the porches.* [n: (HIERON.)] Because they kept [0644B] the outer door of the tabernacle. But their fathers, who are written below and who have titles in the Psalter, kept the inner door and were singers.

VERS. 20.-- *And Phinees.* The grandson of Aaron, who was still living, as the Hebrews hand down, and appointed the courses of the ministers. And there are twenty-four courses which David appointed for the ministry of the tabernacle. Whence in the Gospel: Zacharias of the course of Abia [n: (Luc. I)].

VERS. 26.-- *To these four Levites,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] Who these four Levites were, to whom the whole number of the porters had been entrusted, is shown when it is said: *And of Hosa, that is, of the sons of Merari, Semri the chief,* etc., as it is had in chapter XXVI. The four chiefs of the porters are the holy teachers, who by the four Gospels, keeping the door of faith, open it to believers and [0644C] shut it against blasphemers, to whom with Peter the keys have been committed [n: (Matth. XVI)]. These keep the doors by the four winds, because through the four quarters of the world they call the nations to the faith, and watchfully guard the Church.

VERS. 28.-- *Of their kindred there were also a minister over the vessels.* [n: (ID.)] [var: Vulg. *ministerii*, "over the vessels of the ministry"] Mystically they are ordained from the company of the holy preachers, who keep an ever-watchful guard over the vessels of the Lord, that is, the souls of the faithful (in whom the will of God is daily ministered). Under their keeping are the fine flour, the wine and the oil, whereby the sacraments of the body and blood of the Lord are made, and the grace of the Holy Spirit is delivered by a spiritual anointing. They themselves in the Church [0644D] compound the incense of prayers and the spices of the virtues, and render acceptable wine in the sight of the Lord.

VERS. 31.-- *And Mathathias, a Levite, the firstborn of Sellum the Corite, was set over the things that were fried in the frying-pan.* Because Christ is the author of the heavenly sacraments, where daily bread and wine are offered in memory of his passion, and the head of all who crucify their own flesh after his example, and deliver it to suffering for his name.

VERS. 32.-- *There were loaves of proposition.* Two and two were baked apart before the sabbath, according to Josephus, and on the sabbath morning were set upon the table, of which we have spoken more fully in Leviticus.

VERS. 34.-- *The heads of the Levites by their families.* It is fitting that the more a man is set over the rest, [0645A] the more devout he should be in the divine offices. He keeps well the headship of the ministry who is zealous to abide in Jerusalem, that is, in the vision of peace, that he may flee the tumults of the world and the pleasures of the flesh, and have the quiet of mind in the contemplation of God, saying with the Psalmist: *I rejoiced in the things that were said to me.*

VERS. 35.-- *Jahiel,* who in Kings is Ahiel, father of Cis the father of Saul, and is interpreted *my father is God.* [var: Vulg. *Jehiel*] Jahiel, eloquent of God, perhaps according to the difference of merits. And it is to be noted that among the sons of Jahiel, Cis and Ner are called brothers and are so, and in Kings it is thus had; but in what follows it is said that Ner begot Cis, not that he begot him, but that he brought him up.

VERS. 39.-- *Abinadab.* He, as in Kings, is related to have been slain with his father, he is called Abinadab and is interpreted [0645B] Abinadab, *my father is a vow,* Abinadab, *my people is a vow.*

*Esbaal.* The husband of fire, this is Isboseth, that is, a man of confusion.

VERS. 40.-- *Meribbaal.* Striving against a husband, this is Miphiboseth, who is interpreted *from the mouth, ignominy.*
