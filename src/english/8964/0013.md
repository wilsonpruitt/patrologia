## CHAPTER XXVIII.

VERS. 1.-- *With the eunuchs and all the mighty and most valiant men in the army at Jerusalem.* [n: (RAB.)] The law forbids the Israelites to be cut, and foreigners are not wont to be admitted to the secrets of counsel. But they hand down that in this place [0663C] those are called eunuchs who, being strangers to the actions of the world, were free for prayer and for reading: and had wives not for the cause of lust, but for the receiving of offspring: whom Josephus calls Essenes.

VERS. 11.-- *And David gave.* David the Psalmist, and the other Fathers of the Old Testament, designated by figures and by riddles all the things which the true peaceable one was to do in the Church: how all nations had through the faith of baptism an entrance into the Church, what the state of the Church should be in time to come, what the perfection of the saints and the reward in the heavenly kingdom to be rendered to each according to his merits, through the diverse cells and chambers, because in the kingdom of God there are many mansions.

VERS. 17.-- *And little lions.* [n: (RAB.)] Neither in Exodus in the construction of the tabernacle, etc., *as far as* we have touched upon.

[0663D] [n: (ID.)] The Hebrews hand down that the pattern of the temple and of the vessels [0664A] shown to Moses on the mount came to Jesus, then to the judges and to Heli and to Samuel and to David, at last to Solomon. Or, the hand of the Lord is the Son, by whose gift David and the other saints received whatsoever they brought forth concerning the structure of the holy Church.

## CHAPTER XXIX.

VERS. 6.-- *They promised therefore,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] He who shall have learned to rule well himself and others, etc., *as far as* he builds upon it wood, hay, stubble, he shall suffer loss.

VERS. 10.-- *And he blessed the Lord,* etc. Because the congregation of the faithful, admonished by the writings of the prophets, and stirred up by the sayings and examples of the holy Fathers, should sing praises and hymns to God, offering up the victims of pious confession and the [0664B] fruitful works of holy conversation.

VERS. 22.-- *And they ate and drank before,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] This is the joy of the saints and their intent, that by the works of virtues they may guard themselves from the hunger to come, and may feast, and exult in the sight of God.

*They anointed Solomon the son a second time,* etc. Because Christ is celebrated by all the faithful, with sound faith and pious confession, in the royal and at once the pontifical dignity. Solomon is interpreted peaceable, Sadoc just. But Christ is our peace, who made both one, he is the just Lord and loveth justice: *the Judge of the living and of the dead [n: (Ephes. II)]*, him every faithful man anoints first as king and priest, when in the profession of baptism he confesses the true king and priest. And he anoints a second time when by teaching and by working [0664C] he preaches the same to others, whence: *Thy name is as oil poured out [n: (Cant. I)]*.

VERS. 28.-- *And he died in a good old age, and full of days and riches and glory,* etc. It signifies that he ended the present life with abundance of virtues and with works of light, worthy of the rest to come and of the glory of the heavenly kingdom. Abraham likewise died in a good old age and of a perfect age and full of days. Isaac was gathered to his people full of days. Joseph died in a good old age, the hundred and ten years of his life being completed, and was embalmed with spices. Moses died at a hundred and twenty years, whose eye was not dim, nor were his teeth moved. *But bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half of [0664D] their days [n: (Psal. LIV)]*. *Who seem to themselves to lead their days in good things, and in a moment go down to hell [n: (Job XXI)]*. [var: Vulg. Job 21:13 *Ducunt in bonis dies suos, et in puncto ad inferna descendunt*, "They spend their days in wealth, and in a moment they go down to hell" — without *qui sibi videntur*]
