## CHAPTER ONE.

[0663D]

VERS. 1.-- *Solomon therefore was strengthened,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] When in death he triumphed over him who had the empire of death, and won for himself the kingdom of all. Whence: *All power is given to me in heaven and in earth [n: (Matth. XXVIII)]*.

VERS. 3.-- *Gabaon,* etc., Which was the mother city [0664D] of the Hevites, and it fell to the lot of Benjamin, and was set apart for the Levites. It is a wonder therefore that Josephus says that Solomon came to Hebron to sacrifice upon the brazen altar which Moses had made: unless perhaps he meant Hebron itself to be understood as the high place of Gabaon, on the ground that he reigned there first. For Gabaon is interpreted loftiness, or a cousin's hill.

[0665A]VERS. 10.-- *Give me wisdom,* etc. Not gold, not riches, not earthly glory does he ask of God, but that he may know how to rule and to judge the people of God. If the ruler of bodies chose this, much more ought the rulers of souls to choose this, that they may show to the people of God the entrance unto faith and the going forth unto hope.

VERS. 13.-- *From the high place,* etc. It signifies the ark, in which were the tables of the Testament. For above it is written: *But the tabernacle of the Lord which Moses had made in the desert, and the altar of holocausts, were at that same season in the high places of Gabaon,* [var: Vulg. 1 Par. 21:29 *ea tempestate erat in excelso Gabaon*, "at that season was in the high place of Gabaon" — singular verb, singular high place] etc. [n: (I Paral. XXI)]. And the ark of the Lord, after David brought it into his own house, remained in that same place until the temple should be built.

*And twelve thousand horsemen.* This number signifies the time [0665B] of grace, in which the apostolic teaching forms the people of the New Testament. And rightly are the chariots and horsemen of Solomon numbered by the number of forty or of twelve: because through either testament holy men, learned in the sacred letters, are fitted for the sitting of God, whom he himself, the highest charioteer, governs at his own beck.

VERS. 17.-- *For six hundred pieces of silver.* Because by preaching, the testament of the teachers, men are brought through preachers into the use of the true Solomon, unto the knowing of the truth of the faith. Six hundred therefore, that is, sixty times ten, signify the perfection of evangelical doctrine, in which the perfection of the law wholly consists: especially since our Redeemer, having suffered on the sixth day, fulfilled all the things that were written of him in the law and the prophets; [0665C] whence he says. *It is consummated. And bowing his head, he sent forth his spirit [n: (Joan. XIX)]* [var: Vulg. Joan. 19:30 *tradidit spiritum*, "gave up his spirit"; *emisit spiritum* is the wording of Matth. 27:50].

*A hundred and fifty.* Which are thrice fifty, and, on account of Pentecost and the jubilee, manifestly signify the distinction of the Old Testament and of the threefold Scripture, because it is said: *All things must needs be fulfilled which are written in the law of Moses and in the prophets and in the psalms concerning me [n: (Luc. XXIV)]*.

## CHAPTER TWO.

VERS. 1.-- *Solomon determined to build a house,* etc. [n: (BED., quaest. in lib. Reg., tom. II.)] The house which Solomon built, etc., *as far as* who make themselves ready, by believing, by working, by teaching others, for the building of God.

*Solomon.* Both by his name and by his most peaceable reign he [0665D] signifies him, etc., *as far as* and of the illumination of the Holy Spirit.

VERS. 8.-- *And they shall be servants.* [n: (RAB.)] My apostles and first teachers, etc., *as far as* and therefore they stormed it the more subtly.

VERS. 14.-- *The son of a woman.* He shows him to be of the offspring of Salomith; of the tribe of Dan, of whom it is read in the book of Numbers. The Hebrews hand down that his father was a Hebrew, of the stock of Oliab, of the tribe of Dan, who wrought with Beseleel in the wilderness. But as to his calling him a Tyrian, the Hebrews say that this came about by the translating. For Zocri in their tongue is interpreted a singing of psalms. For Chyros, which they call Zor, sounds sometimes narrowness, sometimes a forming.

[0666A] *Who knoweth how to work in gold and in silver.* In all these things the spiritual Hiram knows how to work, that the adornment of the house of God may shine with worthy comeliness before the eyes of men, and by the odour of good repute may become acceptable to God.

VERS. 16.-- *But we,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] It signifies that the Gentiles, the report of the Gospel having been heard, sends diverse persons through faith unto baptism. Yet it belongs to the true peaceable one alone, by absolving sins through the sacraments of baptism, to unite them to the unity of the Church, of whom it is said: *Upon whom you shall see the Spirit descending and remaining upon him, he it is that baptizeth [n: (Matth. III)]* [var: Vulg. Joan. 1:33 *videris*, singular, "thou shalt see"].

VERS. 17.-- *He numbered therefore,* etc. There were workmen of the house of the Lord of the children of Israel, etc. [n: (RAB.)] *as far as*, by living rightly and by teaching, they [0666B] build up the Church of Christ.

*Three thousand and six hundred.* In Paralipomenon, in place of the three thousand three hundred overseers who are numbered in Kings, three thousand six hundred are written, for the perfection of exalted men. For in the number six God completed the adornment of the world: and because Holy Scripture teaches the faith of the Trinity and the works of justice, rightly are three thousand six hundred reported to have been.
