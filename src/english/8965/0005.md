## CHAPTER VIII.

VERS. 1.-- *And when twenty were ended,* etc., *Hiram had given,* etc. [n: (RAB. in Paral. ex Hieron.)] He had given them back, namely, because when Solomon had given them they did not please. him. Therefore Solomon appointed them to his own uses.

VERS. 5.-- *Bethoron.* [n: (RAB.)] It is a city, as far as which Jesus pursued the enemy kings in the tribe [0677B] of Ephraim, etc., *as far as* and the other Bethoron the lower, given to the Levites for a possession.

VERS.--6.-- *And all.* Ezer, namely, and Mageddo and Gazer and Mello, all which show forth the comeliness of the Church: because the true Solomon in the pleasantness of his kingdom prepared them for himself unto praise and gladness. And Mello is interpreted a fulfilling; Ezer separated and sanctified; Mageddo his upper chamber; Gazer a cutting off or a division; Bethoron the house of mountains; Balaath ascending. And holy Church is the city of the living God, where the will of God is daily fulfilled, and, separated from every error of the unbelieving, she is sanctified by faith and by good work. There is the refreshment of the virtues. She herself discerns the clean and the unclean, and vice from virtue: and she is the house of mountains, that is, the habitation [0677C] of the saints, and by the fruit of justice *as a flourishing palm she goeth up from virtue to virtue, that she may see the God of gods in Sion [n: (Psal. LXXXIII)]* [var: Vulg. Psal. 83:8 *ibunt de virtute in virtutem: videbitur Deus deorum in Sion*, "they shall go … the God of gods shall be seen"]. Solomon fortified all his villages, because Christ so fortifies the assemblies of his faithful with the protection of his grace, that they may be able to withstand their spiritual enemies.

*In Libanus.* [n: (HIERON.)] In the temple, namely, which was clothed with the comeliness of cedars, whence Zacharias: *Open thy gates, O Libanus [n: (Zach. XI)]*; or in the royal buildings which had been made of the woods of Libanus.

VERS. 10.-- *Two hundred.* The hundred, which in the reckoning of the fingers passes over from the left hand into the right, signifies eternal life. But the fifty signifies the remission of sins, and eternal rest, and the grace of the Holy Spirit: because in the psalm of the same [0677D] number penance, confession, and the remission of sins is described; and through the jubilee the rest to come. But through Pentecost, in the coming of the Holy Spirit, the sevenfold grace is expressed.

*Who taught.* Because out of either people he chose rulers of the Church, who, enlightened by spiritual grace and fully taught, might teach by their own example the remission of sins, and the life of eternal salvation, and the perpetual blessedness that is to be sought after.

VERS. 12.-- *Holocausts.* [n: (RAB.)] A holocaust is called a thing wholly burnt, this is consumed upon the altar, when in the heart of each of the elect who has vowed his whole self, namely in flesh and in heart, to God, a good work is kindled by the burning fire of charity.

[0678A]VERS. 14.--According to the fitness of each season he disposes all things in the Church, that each order, having discharged its own office, may worthily minister

VERS. 17.-- *In Asiongaber.* It is said to be an island not far from Ahila in the Red Sea, where the fleet of Josaphat perished.

[n: (RAB.)] The fleet which Solomon made, etc., *as far as* they find through the grace of the Holy Spirit, and, ascribing divine things to wisdom and to counsel, they attribute him

VERS. 18.-- *In Ophir.* The name of the province Ophir, called from one of the later descendants of Heber, from whose stock those that came, Josephus relates, dwelt from the river Cophene as far as the region of India which is called Geria.
