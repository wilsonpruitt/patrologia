## CHAPTER II.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *My son.* ⟦s2⟧ He notes two things here, that wisdom is of heavenly matters, prudence of secular.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ He hides the commandments of God with himself, etc., *as far as* as though the birds should take away the seed cast by the wayside.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *That thy ear may hear wisdom.* ⟦s2⟧ He hears not with the ear, etc., *as far as* for this is that which follows:

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Incline thy heart.* ⟦s1⟧ Incline, humble, cleanse, etc., *as far as* and thou hast revealed them to the humble.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For if thou shalt call upon wisdom.* ⟦s2⟧ To call upon, etc., *as far as* nor ought free will to be absent.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And as treasures.* ⟦s2⟧ He who digs out treasures, [1083B] etc., *as far as* nor let him rest until he find.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Then shalt thou understand.* ⟦s2⟧ A friendly fear, etc., *as far as* but thou shalt find also the knowledge of the Godhead itself (which makes blessed for ever).

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Because the Lord giveth.* ⟦s2⟧ This little verse looks back from that which he had said above: *For if thou shalt call upon wisdom,* etc.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ For therefore wisdom, etc., *as far as* but is given by the Lord.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Out of his mouth.* ⟦s2⟧ By the eternal nativity of the Son, of which elsewhere: *I came out of the mouth of the Most High, the firstborn before all creatures [n: (Isai. LVIII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ Or, the mouth of the Lord is in the saints, whence: *The mouth of the Lord hath spoken.* ⟦s4⟧ And out of his mouth knowledge is born and prudence, while the Lord sanctifies the human mouth, and makes it his own, that it may speak knowingly and prudently.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1083C]VERS. 7.-- *He will keep.* ⟦s2⟧ That in whatsoever degree of the virtues he may help the upright and the simple. ⟦s3⟧ Job [was] *simple and upright [n: (Job. I)]*; *simple,* through the innocence of meekness, *and upright,* through the caution of discretion: *simple,* injuring none, nay rather profiting all, *upright,* corrupted by none.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *From the evil way.* ⟦s2⟧ To which they call, who say: *Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood, he speaketh perverse things,* who is the author of this persuasion.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Who leave the way.* ⟦s2⟧ Beautifully, etc., *as far as* his end is in the exterior darkness.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Who rejoice.* ⟦s2⟧ Because they walk through dark ways, etc., *as far as* and they should chastise their hurtful gladness with wholesome weeping.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *That thou mayest be delivered from the woman.* ⟦s2⟧ This verse hangs, [1083D] etc., *as far as: and they themselves are darts.*

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *And she leaveth the guide.* ⟦s2⟧ It is plain also of the heretical mind, etc., *as far as* which she had promised on the day of baptism that she would keep.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Of the covenant of her God,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Which she covenanted with her husband at the time of the nuptials, the Lord being witness, that they should keep faith, namely of chastity, to one another, or in baptism.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For she is inclined.* ⟦s2⟧ All the converse of the adulteress leads to destruction, etc., *as far as* that is, it tends to the torments of the malignant spirits.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *They shall not return to her. ⟦s2⟧ Death,* namely, not the woman, etc., *as far as* let him not attribute it to himself, but to the grace of God.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1084A]VERS. 21.-- *For they that.* ⟦s2⟧ Here he joins the simple to the upright, etc., *as far as* they come to that which reigns with Christ in the heavens.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *The wicked.* ⟦s2⟧ It is said specially of those, etc., *as far as* concerning the end of the good, *but he that shall hear me shall rest without terror.*
