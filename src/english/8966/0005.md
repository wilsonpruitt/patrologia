## CHAPTER IX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Wisdom hath built herself a house.* ⟦s2⟧ Because he had spoken sufficiently of the eternity [1092A] of the divinity of Christ, etc., *as far as* because he raised up men severed from the love of the world to bear the fabric of the Church.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Seven pillars.* ⟦s2⟧ Namely the Churches throughout the world, etc., *as far as* as though by supporting they held it together.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Come, eat my bread.* ⟦s2⟧ The divinity and the humanity joined into one person of Christ, or, in the *bread* his body, in the *wine* his blood, wherewith we are filled at the altar.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Forsake childishness.* ⟦s2⟧ After the banquet set forth he adds also the counsels of life, that those whom he refreshes with the mysteries of his incarnation received, he may equally instruct with the words of doctrine.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *He that instructeth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As though thou shouldst ask, etc., *as far as* and believing him the greater.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Rebuke not a scorner.* ⟦s2⟧ It is not to be feared, etc., [1092B] *as far as* but for the sake of love toward him it must be given over.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *If thou be wise.* ⟦s2⟧ I admonish thee unto wisdom, which nevertheless profits thee, not me. ⟦s3⟧ For if *thou be wise,* etc.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *A foolish woman.* ⟦s2⟧ Above he commended wisdom by the divinity and the humanity, now by the contrary he shows that same wisdom more commendable, where *a foolish woman,* etc., who is contrary to her, *sitteth,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ *A woman,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ The woman here is heresy, etc., *as far as* she is lifted up in the arrogance of the mind.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *He that is a little one.* ⟦s2⟧ Wisdom the little ones and the unwise, etc., *as far as* and to turn aside to her.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Stolen waters.* ⟦s2⟧ Wisdom set forth her table openly to all, etc., *as far as* she affirms that forbidden and unlawful wedlock is the sweeter.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1092C]VERS. 18.-- *And she knew not that there are giants there.* ⟦s2⟧ The adulterous woman knows not, etc., *as far as* and in the heights of the heavens the guests of the faithful.

## CHAPTER X.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The parables of Solomon.* ⟦s2⟧ He sets a new title, because he begins a new kind of speech, namely, that he should not, as before, dispute at greater length concerning the several parts of good men or of evil men; but should describe the acts of both in alternating verses.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A wise son.* ⟦s2⟧ He who keeps well the mysteries of the faith received gladdens God the Father; but he who defiles these by evil action or by heresy saddens his mother the Church.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The treasures of wickedness shall profit nothing.* ⟦s2⟧ Both from a transitory death, etc., *as far as* because they were given for life, they profit.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1092D]VERS. 3.-- *The Lord will not afflict with famine.* ⟦s2⟧ And if at any time, etc., *as far as* he turns it back by justly avenging.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *The slack hand hath wrought want.* ⟦s2⟧ He who lives negligently in this world shall be in want of good things in the world to come; but they who valiantly war for the Lord are repaid with the riches of everlasting blessedness.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *He that gathereth.* ⟦s2⟧ He who gathers to Christ the souls of the faithful is a wise son. ⟦s3⟧ And indeed this *harvest is great, but the labourers few [n: (Matth. IX)]*. ⟦s4⟧ But he who in this acceptable time grows sluggish about the care of his own salvation shall be confounded in the day of tribulation.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *But the mouth of the wicked.* ⟦s2⟧ That which served rioting, drunkenness and evil converse shall be condemned for the iniquity which it heaped together. ⟦s3⟧ In fine, [1093A] the *mouth* of that man *iniquity hath covered* who, set in torments, sought refreshment for himself in his tongue, wherein he was the more punished for his many crimes.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *The memory of the just.* ⟦s2⟧ In this life the good the good, etc., *as far as* and their fire shall not be quenched.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The fool is beaten with lips,* either with his own, whereby he deserved to be condemned, because *death and life are in the hands of the tongue [n: (Isa. LXVI)]* [var: Vulg. Prov. 18:21 *Mors et vita in manu linguae*, "in the hand of the tongue," singular; and the words are Proverbs, not Isaias], or with those of the men from whom, because he could not be corrected, he received the sentence of condemnation; whence: *Lord, deliver my soul from wicked lips [n: (Psal. CXIX)]*.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *He that walketh,* who knows how to live in simplicity, etc., *as far as* and receives things worthy of his deeds, *for there is nothing hidden that shall not be revealed [n: (Matth. X)]* [var: Vulg. Matth. 10:26 *Nihil enim est opertum, quod non revelabitur*, "for nothing is covered that shall not be revealed"].

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *A rod on his back.* ⟦s2⟧ Vengeance in the following [1093B] life, etc., *as far as* it is rightly subjoined: *The wise hide,* etc.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *The substance of a rich man.* ⟦s2⟧ Spiritually: he who is rich toward God through good works trusts in him, as one who is in a city that cannot be stormed, which can be overcome by no enemy; but they who are straitened by poverty of the virtues therefore lack the heavenly riches, because with hurtful dread they fear to bear hard labours for the Lord.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 18.-- *They hide knowledge,* etc. [var: Vulg. Prov. 10:18 *Abscondunt odium labia mendacia*, "lying lips hide hatred"; *abscondunt scientiam* is the wording of Prov. 10:14] If therefore thou desirest to be truthful and wise, thou shalt neither cover hatred in the hiding-place of the heart, nor bring it forth through the reviling of the mouth; but let thy heart be filled with love and thy mouth with truth.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *As it were in sport a fool worketh wickedness.* ⟦s2⟧ Prudence has taken its name from providing. ⟦s3⟧ A fool [1093C] therefore is he who rejoices in wickedness. ⟦s4⟧ But it belongs to a wise man, and to him who is worthy of the name of man, to foresee that laughter shall be mingled with grief, and that the punishment of vengeance shall follow the joys of sinning.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *That which the wicked feareth shall come upon him.* ⟦s2⟧ He speaks of that wicked man, etc., *as far as* that which they hold in their heart shall come.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *As a tempest.* ⟦s2⟧ He speaks of the persecutors of the Church, who seek to overthrow the house of faith. ⟦s3⟧ But that house being set upon the true foundation, that is, upon Christ, they themselves perish forthwith.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *As vinegar to the teeth.* ⟦s2⟧ As the treachery of heretics, etc., *as far as* and they assail them even with words, whence: *They went out from us, but they were not of us [n: (I Joan. II)]* [var: Vulg. I Joan. 2:19 *Ex nobis prodierunt, sed non erant ex nobis*, "they went forth from us"].

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1093D]VERS. 28.-- *But the hope of the wicked.* ⟦s2⟧ Not against that which is said above, etc., *as far as* Of those who knowingly transgress, he subjoins.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 29.-- *The strength of the simple is his way, and terror.* [var: Vulg. Prov. 10:29 *Fortitudo simplicis via Domini, et pavor his qui operantur malum*, "the way of the Lord, and terror to them that work evil"] If the obscure things of the Scriptures, etc., *as far as* they disdain to work.

## CHAPTER XI.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A deceitful balance.* ⟦s2⟧ Either of money or of judgment, etc., *as far as* and it agrees with the action.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Where pride shall be.* ⟦s2⟧ The proud either bear themselves insolently through contempt or through ignorance of discipline, or they heap reviling upon their neighbours, or he that exalteth himself shall be humbled.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *When the wicked man is dead.* ⟦s2⟧ This Origen [1094A] knew not, etc., *as far as* and to them perhaps belongs that which follows.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The expectation of the solicitous.* ⟦s2⟧ The solicitude of good men is to fulfil the precepts of God; but that of evil men who knowingly sin is that they shall be hurried away to everlasting punishment. ⟦s3⟧ And therefore the expectation of the former shall be crowned; that of the latter shall be condemned, even before the judgment, their own conscience condemning them.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The dissembler.* ⟦s2⟧ The heretic, feigning catholic doctrine, deceives his hearer. ⟦s3⟧ They who follow the truth of the Gospel shall be set free by catholic wisdom, lest they be taken by the heretical snare.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *He that despiseth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He is not to be despised who loves us in simplicity, even if he does something unfittingly: for he who does this lacks wisdom. ⟦s3⟧ But the prudent man [1094B] keeps silence in public, chastises in secret, which is better opened in what follows.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Where there is no governor, the people shall fall, but safety where there are many counsels.* ⟦s2⟧ For lest thou shouldst think, etc., *as far as* that he may be corrected by many.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *He shall be afflicted with evil.* ⟦s2⟧ It is plain according to the letter, etc., *as far as* he shall render himself worthy of vengeance before the eyes of the strict judge.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But he that is aware of snares shall be secure.* ⟦s2⟧ He shall be secure in the world to come who now, being rightly fearful, both heals his own sins through penance and keeps himself untouched by the fellowship of the impenitent.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Hand in hand.* ⟦s2⟧ He that joins hand in hand, etc., *as far as* whence it was set down before: *A perverse heart is abominable to the Lord.*

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1094C]VERS. 22.-- *A golden ring in the nostrils,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ If thou shouldst fix a ring of gold in a swine's nostrils, etc., *as far as* because he is given over to carnal things.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *He that seeketh good things riseth well at daybreak,* etc., that is, he rises to life in the time of the resurrection, because he now does good things in the time of working. ⟦s2⟧ But he who searches out the evil things which he may speak or do is held back from higher things by their weight.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *But the just as,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As it were, they who by hope of future rewards do good things in the present rightly receive what they hope for. ⟦s3⟧ For the green leaf on the tree signifies that the fruits which it does not yet show will come: and the just spring up as a green leaf, who, being saved by hope, cease not to advance in the grace of faith and of the virtues.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1094D]VERS. 29.-- *He that is a fool.* ⟦s2⟧ The wicked man lives for the godly; the former is condemned, the latter receives the prize for which he strove.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *He that receiveth souls.* ⟦s2⟧ He who takes up the care of souls for the Lord, etc., *as far as* he provides that he may reign more loftily with the Lord.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *If the just man receive on earth.* ⟦s2⟧ If the martyrs suffer so much unjustly, etc., *as far as* for even if we are not wicked, certainly if we deny that we are sinners, *we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.*
