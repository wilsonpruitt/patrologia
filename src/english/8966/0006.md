## CHAPTER XII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *He that is good,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The connexion of these little verses is this; he who is good does not trust in his own thoughts; but seeks the grace of the Lord, [1095A] and through it lives godly. ⟦s3⟧ But he who trusts in his own thoughts cannot be good: for he who cares not to seek the grace of God persists in depraved action.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *A woman.* ⟦s2⟧ According to the letter, a good and chaste woman, etc., *as far as* the rottenness of depraved teaching corrupts.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Thoughts.* ⟦s2⟧ The just their own deeds, etc., *as far as* having forgotten the fear of the Lord.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Turn the wicked.* ⟦s2⟧ Much impiety among the gods of the nations, etc., *as far as* but whatsoever members pass from hence to that place find the joys which they were hoping for.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *He knoweth,* that is, he has compassion on the dulness and the frailty of the neighbours committed to him.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But the bowels of the wicked are cruel,* who do not have compassion on those subject to them; but as the Lord says: *They strike [1095B] the boys and the maidservants,* saying: *My Lord delayeth his coming.* [var: Vulg. Luc. 12:45 *Moram facit dominus meus venire: et coeperit percutere servos, et ancillas*, "shall begin to strike the menservants and the maidservants" — and in Luke the delay is spoken first]

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *The desire.* ⟦s2⟧ The wicked man desires, etc., *as far as* on the contrary he speaks of the good.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But the root of the just shall prosper,* faith, hope, charity, whereby they are rooted in Christ, do not deceive them; but they shall receive in the world to come more than they hope for.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *The fool,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He who desires to be avenged in the present is a fool; but he who for the Lord's sake despises it is wise.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But he that dissembleth:* he does not so wish it to be hidden that it should last, but that it should be utterly blotted out; for it is the nature of anger that, being uttered, it burns the more; being hidden, it languishes.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *But the tongue of the wise is health,* etc., because both, by themselves fulfilling the good things which they promise, [1095C] they come to life; and they show to others the things which they are to follow

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *The lip of truth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The catholic faith shall abide for ever; but he who suddenly brings in new teachings is a minister of falsehood.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 23.-- *A crafty man.* [var: Vulg. Prov. 12:23 *Homo versatus celat scientiam*, "a practised man hideth knowledge"] Wise and prudent: who sometimes hides knowledge, either not being able to speak to them as to spiritual men, but as to carnal; or not willing to give what is holy to dogs, or to cast pearls before swine, or to send them.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The heart of the unwise provoketh.* ⟦s2⟧ Either when they desire to be wise more than it behoves to be wise, and, vanishing away in their thoughts, they have and hold folly instead of wisdom; or, forcing upon others the things which they cannot grasp, as it were the light of the sun upon weak eyes, that is, of wisdom, [1095D] they take away that which they seemed to have.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *The hand of the valiant.* ⟦s2⟧ The perfect not only overcome the wars of the vices, but rule also over their chosen brethren with a greater grace of the virtues.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But that which is slack.* ⟦s2⟧ They who are loose in mind, etc., *as far as* for such men are not to be despaired of, when he adds, *the sorrow of the just,* etc.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Sorrow in the heart,* etc., because it is necessary that first the sorrow of penance should healthfully humble the sinner, and afterwards that the word of the reconciliation granted through the judgment of the priest should gladden or refresh him.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He that neglecteth a loss,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ If this is taken according to the letter, etc., *as far as* or delivers his very flesh to death?

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1096A]VERS. 27.-- *He shall not find,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ For money got by fraud, etc., *as far as* wherewith the brightness of eternal life, more precious than gold, is bought.

## CHAPTER XIII.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A wise son.* ⟦s2⟧ So great is the distance between the wise man and the fool, that the one sometimes by the advance of learning comes to the teaching of him who had taught him: the other, that he may not hear when he is reproved.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *The sluggard willeth and willeth not.* ⟦s2⟧ He wills to reign with the Lord, etc., *as far as* and Jesus the son of Sirach: *Woe to the sinner that goeth upon the earth two ways.*

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But the soul of them that work* the Lord's commands *shall be made fat,* because it shall be refreshed with the perfect sweetness of the things above; whence: *As with fat [n: (Psal. XXVI)]*, etc.; or, they who [1096B] sweat at godly labours for the Lord shall be endowed with an unfading crown.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *There is as it were a rich man,* not truly so, as he who was clothed in purple and fine linen [n: (Luc. XVI)]; but because he had not God, etc., *as far as* what true poverty does is subjoined.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Substance,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ They who wish to become rich fall into temptation, etc., *as far as* he shall receive the manifold gifts of heavenly blessedness, of whom:

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *The hope that is deferred,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because doubtless, etc., *as far as* for he himself is *the tree of life to them that embrace him.* [var: Vulg. Prov. 3:18 *Lignum vitae est his qui apprehenderint eam*, "a tree of life to them that lay hold on her"; Prov. 13:12 reads *lignum vitae desiderium veniens*]

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *He that detracteth.* ⟦s2⟧ Otherwise, etc., *as far as* whence: *If a man be overtaken in any fault,* etc.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1096C]VERS. 14.-- *The law of the wise man.* ⟦s2⟧ From the time that a man begins to be wise, he uses this law, that he may shun death and gain life.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *With counsel,* chiefly with divine counsel, which is in the Scriptures. ⟦s2⟧ For he is a fool who lives outside this counsel, however naturally prudent he may seem.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *He that walketh with the wise.* ⟦s2⟧ Any simple or rustic man who cannot comprehend the hidden things of wisdom, if nevertheless he follows the examples of the wise in his living, shall rightly be numbered among the wise.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The friend of fools.* ⟦s2⟧ He who loves fools, not because they are men, but for their folly's sake, because they are mimes, or players, or the like, not that he may correct them by instructing them, but that by favouring them he may make them worse: [1096D] though he seem wise, he shall be held in the condemnation of fools.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *A good man,* etc., often good men die without children, etc., *as far as* the kingdom of God is given to a nation yielding the fruits thereof.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *Much food.* ⟦s2⟧ Another translator, etc., *as far as* but that which he subjoins agrees with either sense.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *He that spareth the rod hateth his son,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ For both a good father his son, and a catholic master his disciple, carefully instructs, lest he turn aside to iniquity.

## CHAPTER XIV.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A wise woman,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *She shall destroy* by living ill, [1097A] and sometimes by reigning openly, etc., *as far as* concerning whom he openly subjoins:

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *In the mouth of a fool.* ⟦s2⟧ Because fools by vain speech afflict the humble whom they despise; but these fortify themselves by the doctrine of the wise, lest they be deceived.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Where there are no oxen,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ These things cleave to the things above, etc., *as far as* who feeds the widow of Sarepta by whom he himself also is fed.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The scorner seeketh wisdom,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As Caiphas, etc., *as far as* because they seek perversely, they never find at all.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Go against,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Therefore the scorner does not find the wisdom which he seeks, etc., *as far as* and they busy themselves to give heed to the meditation of it.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1097B]VERS. 10.-- *A stranger.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, a reprobate, etc., *as far as* whence it is subjoined: *The Lord of the wicked,* etc.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The wise man feareth and turneth aside.* ⟦s2⟧ As it belongs to a fool to trust in his own briskness, etc., *as far as* or that after death he may bravely bear the punishments.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *The impatient man,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The impatient man is indeed blameworthy, etc., *as far as* he is reckoned among the reprobate.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *The evil shall lie before the good.* ⟦s2⟧ Seeing their torments, etc., *as far as* for the beholding of their punishments.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *The folly of fools.* ⟦s2⟧ Imprudence is so called, as it were improvidence.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *A faithful witness delivereth souls.* ⟦s2⟧ He is declared a catholic who faithfully [1097C] preaches and brings forth the testimonies of the Scriptures; the heretic, lies.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He bringeth forth lies.* ⟦s2⟧ Another translation: But the deceitful deceiver sets souls on fire with a lie.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The turncoat,* that is, the devil, who lies that the evils which he suggests are good: and while he promises eternal goods, he leads to torments.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *In the fear of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ The fear of the Lord affords the confidence of strength, etc., *as far as* they have hoped in the Lord.

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *The fear of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ This is their hope, etc., *as far as* whence, *The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom [n: (Psal. IX)]*.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *The life of the flesh.* ⟦s2⟧ By the flesh, weak things, by the bones, firm things. ⟦s3⟧ If the innocence of the mind is kept, even if [1097D] some things without are weak, they are at some time strengthened; whence he subjoins:

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Envy is the rottenness of the bones,* because through the vice of spite those things perish before the eyes of God which seem strong to human eyes. ⟦s2⟧ For that the bones should rot through envy is that whatsoever is robust should perish.
