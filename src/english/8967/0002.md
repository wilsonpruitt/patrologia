## PSALM ONE.

VERS. 1.-- « Blessed is the man who hath not gone away, etc. » [n: (AUG., Enarr. in Psal., tom. III.)] The order, Adam went away when he withdrew from God; he stood, when he was delighted with sin; he sat, when, confirmed in pride, he could not return, save as set free by this man, who had none of these things. [0844D] Adam went away when he consented to the persuasion of the devil; he stood, while he ate the apple; he sat, while he excused himself: « The woman whom thou gavest me seduced me. » [n: (Gen. III)] [var: Vulg. Gn 3:12 *Mulier, quam dedisti mihi sociam, dedit mihi de ligno, et comedi*, "The woman, whom thou gavest me as a companion, gave me of the tree, and I did eat"; the printed *me seduxit* imports the seducing that the Vulgate assigns to the serpent at Gn 3:13.]. After the likeness of exiles, who, while they are on the road, are more easily called back, while they now stand, with more difficulty: at length with the greatest difficulty, when they have there been made masters and lords. [n: (REMI.)] There is sinning by thought, by act, and by words in teaching. [n: (CASS.)] The impious man is so against God, the sinner against himself, the pestilent man against his neighbour. « In the chair, giving an example of sinning. » [n: (CASS.)] The throne is of kings, the tribunal of judges, the chair properly of teachers. [n: (Ibid.)] It is as though he said: You who have fallen through the first Adam, be ye conformed to this second one: because the blessed man is the contrary of the first

[0845A]VERS. 2.-- « But in the law. » AUG. [n: (CASS.)] Here he is shown full of every good in himself. [n: (AUG.)] It is not perfect praise to flee the things to be blamed, unless the things to be praised follow. In the order in which they grew in the first man, they are taken away in the second.

« In the law. » [n: (Ibid.)] He who is in the law acts according to the law, he who is under the law is acted upon according to the law: the one is free, the other a slave. [n: (Ibid. AMBR.)] One thing is the law which is written for him that serves, another that which is beheld with the mind by him who has no need of letters. « The will. » [n: (CASS.)] That there be no weariness of labour, nor idleness, but he shall meditate always. The will of sense is under the law; of reason, in the law; of divinity, above the law.

VERS. 3.-- « And he shall be. » [n: (Ibid.)] Here he is shown useful to us, giving the fruit of life, and leaves, and overshadowing us, [0845B] « Blessed is the man. But in the law. And he shall be. » The full definition of the blessed man. All these things belong not to every blessed man, but to Christ alone. « As a tree. » [n: (Ibid.)] After the likeness of the tree of life which is in paradise, whereof obedient man was to have eaten. « A tree. » [n: (Ibid.)] In paradise there is the tree of life, a tree unto life, and the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. « Of waters. » [n: (AUG.)] The waters are the Holy Spirit, according to that word: « He that shall have believed in me, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water [n: (Joan. VII)], » and it is against the heats of the vices. [n: (CASS.)] Or, by water, the wisdom of God, by which the inner man is restored, as thirst by water. [n: (AUG.)] Or the mortal race, as Job: « Many waters, many peoples, » because they flow down into death, as waters into the sea. « He shall give. » [n: (Ibid.)] To give pertains to the rational sense and to the will of the one offering. [0845C] « And the leaf. » [n: (Ibid.)] As the leaves cover the fruit, so the words of the Lord guard his promises. « And all things. » [n: (Ibid.)] Why go through them one by one.

VERS. 4.-- « Not so the impious. » Here by the vengeance upon the wicked he terrifies the adverse, the dread of these makes what has gone before more welcome. « From the face of the earth. » [n: (AUG.)] Of eternal stability, because as this earth nourishes and holds up a man, so that other earth holds up the inner man.

VERS. 5.-- « Therefore they shall not rise again. » Because the wind casteth them away, that is, therefore, because they have been given over to a reprobate sense: therefore they do not rise again in the first resurrection, that they may judge themselves in counsel, that they may set the wills of God before their own. Or in the council, that is, in the harmonious multitude of the just, the impious idolaters, or apostates. Sinners, false Christians: or else therefore they do not rise again in the time to come, [0845D] that they may judge, or that, being judged, they may rest in the council, that is, in the college of the just. « In judgment, etc. » In the judgment there will be four orders: some shall judge and shall not be judged, as the most perfect; some shall neither judge nor be judged, as the damned unbelievers; some shall be judged and shall be saved, as the moderately good; some shall be judged and shall perish, as the evil faithful. « In the council. » [n: (AUG.)] In the eternal good, to follow which was counsel to the saints. « Or in the council, » in the judgment, that they may judge together with them; for there is judgment when different sins are punished with different penalties; and it is said from this by likeness. « They rise again. » What resurrections these are: of the soul, from sins in the present; of the body, from death in the time to come, whereby it shall be made impassible and immortal.

[0846A]VERS. 6.-- « For the Lord knoweth, etc., » That is, these do not rise again in the soul, because God does not approve their works, but those of the just only.
