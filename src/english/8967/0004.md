## PSALM IV.

[0848D]

VERS. 1.-- « Unto the end. » [n: (AUG.)] The end is said in two ways, when something fails, or when it is completed. [n: (Ibid.)] A canticle indicates gladness. Psalms are those things which are sung to the psaltery, which the Prophet used in a great mystery. « A psalm, » concerning morals, for the doing of them, that the false gods and the vanities of the world may be forsaken; « Of a canticle, » because mention is made of the eternal delight.

VERS. 2.-- « When I called upon, etc. » [n: (CASS.)] By his own example he rebukes the unbelieving concerning false things. « Thou hast enlarged for me. » [n: (Ibid.)] From the straits of sadness thou hast led me into the breadth of joy. V. « We rejoice in tribulations, knowing that tribulation, etc. » [n: (Rom. V.)] [var: Vulg. Rm 5:3 *gloriamur in tribulationibus*, "we glory in tribulations."] He first showed that he had been heard: now, as though having God in his heart, [0849A] he speaks to him. « Have mercy on me. » He had mercy concerning righteousness and concerning the enlarging. Again, have mercy against the miseries of this life. « My prayer. » Concerning the good things that are to be asked for. And when it is thus concerning me, « Ye sons of men, how long will ye be heavy of heart? »

VERS. 3.-- « Ye love vanity, etc. » [n: (CASS.)] With your desire you embrace, with labour you seek vain idols, and lies, which are not God: or earthly things, which do not do what they promise.

VERS. 4.-- « And know ye, etc. » The new man being set forth, he bids them put off the old. [n: (AUG.)] Note that « And » is placed at beginnings, so that it may join the things said with the vision that is in the heart, so here, as: « Love not vain things. And know ye. » But the diapsalma forbids their being so joined. Diapsalma, according to Jerome, is always, the Hebrews, [0849B] Sela, that is, always, in confirmation of the preceding part. Sympsalma, that is, the joining of a psalm. « The Lord will hear. » Concerning rest it is thus, and so with you: and therefore cry out, being thus worthy to be heard.

VERS. 5.-- « Be ye angry. » If a motion of the soul arises which is not in our power, let not reason consent to it. [n: (CASS.)] A motion of the soul is not in our power, but by the grace of God we are able to temper it: what therefore belongs to custom, he permits; what belongs to fault, he forbids.

[n: (HIER.)] This the Apostle teaches: « Let not the sun go down upon your anger [n: (Ephes. IV)], » so that it is altogether a sin to be angry even lightly: but it is righteousness to soften anger by a swift repentance. « In your hearts. » [n: (AUG.)] Concerning those things which you handle in the heart, which God knows: that is, concerning consent; and concerning those things which you turn over [0849C] in the bed, that is, in the delight of the flesh, in which reason dwells captive, as a wild beast in its lair, « Be ye pricked, » through penance; or the beds are the thoughts of the fierce. [n: (CASS.)] Venial is the anger which is not carried through to effect. Diapsalma. « Sacrifice ye. » [n: (CASS.)] The old man being laid aside, we are bidden to be offered in sacrifice, because we are sure of good things. [n: (AUG.)] « The sacrifice of righteousness, » a contrite spirit, that, punishing itself, it may be slain to God: or, just works after penance, which the diapsalma marks, that from the old life there may be a passing over to the new, and so hope ye for good things. But because there is scarcely one who loves the inner goods, he adds:

VERS. 6.-- « Many say: Who showeth us good things? » Things to come, namely, of which they doubt and despair. Answering to which he shows briefly what [0849D] good things are to be sought.

VERS. 7.-- « The light is signed upon us, etc. » [n: (AUG.)] This light is the whole and the true good of man, wherewith he is signed, as a penny with the image of the king. « Light. » [n: (AUG.)] Light, that is, a shining countenance, and one that enlightens us, an image whereby thou art known. [n: (CASS.)] Or: the cross has been imprinted upon us, for a sign of our king, which is the light of the countenance: because in such men God shines out. The image of creation is reason; of re-creation, grace; of likeness, the whole Trinity. « In the heart. [n: (AUG.)] Not without in vain things, but within, where God dwells, is gladness to be sought.

VERS. 8.-- « From the fruit. » [n: (CAS.)] Because they think that in these things the good is consummated. These three, as being more suited to human [0850A] uses, he sets down for all riches.

VERS. 9.-- « In peace in the selfsame. » [n: (CASS.)] But I will sleep in the peace of the mind, here forgetful of the world, and I will rest in the time to come: because I am already in one hope, not in the manifoldness of the world. « In peace I will sleep and I will rest. » [n: (AUG.)] From all the things of the world, when this mortal shall put on immortality: and this will truly be, because it is already hope: and this is: « For thou, O Lord, » and assuredly that will be which is hoped for.
