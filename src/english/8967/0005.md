## PSALM V.

VERS. 1.-- « For her that obtaineth the inheritance. » Because he is honoured here to whom the inheritance is affirmed to belong, from which the rival is shut out. It is the Church, [0850B] which receives the inheritance, that is, eternal life, God himself, which here speaks of the inheritance which it asks for itself and asserts, saying that the rival is shut out. [n: (BEDA.)] That is, for the Church, which, at Christ's rising again, received the gifts of spiritual goods, which is itself also sometimes called the Lord's inheritance, purchased with his precious blood: whence it is said: « Ask of me, and I will give thee the Gentiles, thy inheritance. » The whole psalm therefore is uttered in the person of the catholic Church, which in the first section asks that her prayer be heard, proclaiming that heretics and schismatics are to be shut out from the Lord's gifts. In the second section she prays that by the understanding of the divine Scriptures she may be directed by a straight path to that happy country, asserting that the faithless are thereby made utterly [0850C] strangers to it. At the last, calling to mind the rewards of the blessed, that by one preaching the punishment foretold might convert the wicked also, and the promised rewards rouse the just.

VERS. 2.-- « My words. » [n: (CASS.)] Here the prophet hints that a threefold prayer is to be made. For there are three principal vices in prosperity, three in adversity: against which we ought to make a threefold prayer. In prosperity, against the concupiscence of the flesh, against the concupiscence of the eyes, against the pride of life. In adversity, against the fear of death, of peril, of profit. [n: (Ibid.)] A threefold prayer: words, the psalmody of the mouth, a cry, the affection of the heart, therefore: « Thou shalt hear. » Behold, three times of the same thing. « With thine ears. » The ears of God are power, mercy. Power, whereby he can: mercy, [0850D] whereby he wills to hear; for unless there were mercy, he would hear no one's prayers.

VERS. 3.-- « To the voice. » That prayer has a voice which is worthy to be heard. « King. » [n: (AUG.)] Properly the Son, rightly first king, because through him one goes to the Father, whence he adds: And « my God: » and yet he does not say attend ye, but attend, because God is one.

VERS. 4.-- « For, etc. » [n: (AUG.)] Thus far the prayer, that it may be heard, etc., *as far as* and this in the morning, as soon as it forsakes the darkness.

VERS. 5.-- « In the morning I will stand. » [n: (CASS.)] Or, in the morning when eternity shall begin, and then I shall fully see that iniquity pleases not: because then it shall not dwell, « but the wicked shall be taken away, that he may not see the glory of God *(Isa. [0851A] XXVI)*: » [var: Vulg. Is 26:10 *et non videbit gloriam Domini*, "and he shall not see the glory of the Lord"; the Vulgate has nothing answering to *tolletur impius*] and then they shall not remain, even though they have had his mercy.

VERS. 6.-- « Neither shall the unjust remain. » Who do evil things. For thou hast hated those who work them, not those who have worked them.

VERS. 7.-- « Thou wilt destroy all that speak a lie. » [n: (AUG.)] Because this is contrary to truth. Truth is of that which is. A lie is not a substance or a nature, but is of that which is not, and deservedly is that destroyed which turns aside from that which is to that which is not. [n: (Ibid.)] To lie is to speak against that which is felt in the mind. [n: (Ibid.)] There are certain lies told for safety's sake, etc., *as far as* I did not consider that one raving could demand it back.

VERS. 8.-- « But I. » [n: (AUG.)] Thirdly, he says that he [0851B] will be the house of God, and that now he draws near in fear, before that consummation which casts out fear.

« I will go in. » [n: (CASS.)] By this I shall go in, because I shall adore. [n: (Ibid.)] He asks against those things which the rival brings in: whence she herself shall perish.

VERS. 9.-- « Lord, lead me. » [n: (AUG.)] Fourthly, making progress amid hindrances, he prays to be helped within, lest he be turned aside by deceitful tongues. « In thy justice. » [n: (CASS.)] Because thou receivest the penitent and the suppliant. « In thy sight. » [n: (AUG.)] Where man does not see, who is not to be believed whether he praise or blame: because he does not see into the conscience, in which the way to God is directed. Therefore he adds: « For there is no truth in their mouth. »

VERS. 10.-- « For there is not, etc. » Not only is there no truth in the mouth, but a sepulchre: because they bring forth stinking things [0851C] to corrupt, and if they do not prevail with them, they act deceitfully, that is, they do not only contrive, but accomplish.

VERS. 11, 12.-- « Judge them, O God. Let them fall from their thoughts. » He does not wish it, but foretells what shall be. He falls away who is disappointed of a good hope.

« And let them be glad. » [n: (CASS.)] After the retributions of the wicked he adds the rewards of the good, that as the punishment terrifies, so the rewards may incite.

VERS. 13.-- « As with a shield. [n: (CASS.)] In one word he indicates the free benefits of God. The will which calls is a defence against all things, and a crown, and a shield. Fitted to the head it is a crown, to the heart, a defence.
