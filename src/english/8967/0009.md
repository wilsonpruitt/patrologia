## PSALM IX.

VERS. 1.-- « Unto the end, for the hidden sons. » [var: Vulg. Ps 9:1 *pro occultis filii*, "for the hidden things of the son", a genitive singular, which is what the gloss's *cujus* presupposes] Nor is it added whose, by excellence he is the only-begotten of God. « Hidden. » Because other things of his are manifest, etc., *as far as* and of the punishment by which he judges, in purging, in converting, in blinding.

VERS. 2.-- « I will confess to thee, O Lord, with my whole « heart, etc. » The prophet sings joyfully, because the worship [0857B] of the devil is destroyed by the coming of Christ: and Christ reigns, judges, helps.

VERS. 4.-- « When my enemy is turned back, etc., » « they shall perish, etc. » [n: (AUG.)] Because when thou hast turned the devil backward, his members are weakened, so that they can do no hurt, and they shall not be, or, they shall be utterly destroyed by thy assault, when thou shalt say, « Go, ye cursed [n: (Matth. XXV)], » [var: Vulg. Mt 25:41 *Discedite a me maledicti*, "Depart from me, ye cursed"] or, they shall perish when God takes away iniquity from them.

VERS. 5.-- « Thou hast made my judgment and my « cause. » [n: (Ibid.)] Because they have served me to my profit, because « thou hast sat. » « Thou judgest. » [n: (HIER.)] The Father, that Christ might die for the race of men. [n: (AUG.)] Or, Christ shall judge at the end, to whom the Father hath given all judgment.

[0857C]VERS. 6.-- « For ever and for ever. » [n: (Ibid.)] The age is this present one which rolls on: the age of the age is eternal, of which that one is the image. Or, it is a determination, lest thou shouldst take this present one.

VERS. 7.-- « The javelins. [n: (CASS.)] The power of the devil shall be destroyed at the end of the world. « Cities. » His peoples. « With a noise, » that is, with the greatest outcry, as happens when prosperous things are shut up with a heavy « end. » But on the contrary the Lord remaineth. « The end, [n: (AUG.)] » is the sword which he shall brandish at the second coming, judging openly, and which now judges secretly, up to which the javelins of the enemy avail somewhat, but then they shall wholly fail. « Of them. » [n: (CASS.)] In the plural, although above he said the enemy in the singular, because although the demons are many, yet because in [0857D] malice they are alike, they are as it were one.

« With a noise. » [n: (AUG.)] That is, both the din and the tumult of impiety hath perished: or, when impiety is overthrown, a din is made, because none comes to the highest peace but he who has fought with the vices with a great din. « His throne. » [n: (CASS.)] The judiciary power, he who was judged in humility shall judge in majesty.

VERS. 10.-- « And he is become. » How great a thing it is, that he who is judge should be a refuge to the poor man, who desires nothing of the world. « In opportunities. » [n: (AUG.)] Man is not more opportunely turned away from the world, unless to these pleasures labours and sorrows be mingled.

VERS. 11.-- « Let them hope. » [n: (AUG.)] When they cease to hope in the things of the world. Those turned away from the world, seeking where to fix their hope, the knowledge of the name receives. The name [0858A] is known, when he whose the name is, is known. His name is Lord, which he knows who willingly serves him, or, who is, and therefore let no one hope in transitory things, which have nothing besides "it was" and "it will be." For what in them is to come, when it has come, straightway becomes past, it is awaited with desire, it is lost with sorrow, it is not held with certainty. « For thou hast not forsaken. » The prophet provokes the faithful to praise, looking upon the benefits of the present time and of the time to come.

VERS. 12.-- « Sion. » [n: (Ibid.)] Contemplation, which is practised now, that we may come to the vision of peace, that is, from Sion into Jerusalem: but any zealous contemplation falls into error, unless the Lord dwell in it. « Pursuits. » [n: (HIER.)] That is, his commandments, upon which zeal is to be spent.

[0858B]VERS. 13.-- « For requiring. » [n: (Ibid.)] But as if they who are sent to evangelize should say, No one believes us, and we are slain, he answered: not without fruit, because « requiring in vengeance their blood, » [var: Vulg. Ps 9:13 *quoniam requirens sanguinem eorum*, without *ultione*] in the judgment, where there shall be glory to the slain, punishment to the slayer: « he hath remembered, etc » [n: (Ibid.)] not after forgetfulness, but after a long time, according to the feeling of the weak, who think him forgetful, because it is not done so quickly as they wish: to whom also add, nay, he is not forgetful, as you think: and if I have said « he hath remembered, » this is against the complainers, who because of the delay think him forgetful.

VERS. 14.-- « Have mercy. » [n: (CASS.)] In his own person he says what each one asks. « See. » [n: (Ibid.)] The look of God is help, and when it comes, the light also puts the darkness to flight: from the pride of the enemy is measured the humility of the sufferer, [0858C] because these are humbled just so much as those are exalted.

VERS. 15.-- « Who liftest me up from the gates of death, etc. » [n: (AUG.)] That is, from all covetous desires, by which one goes to death: death now is that very gladness in the enjoying of things desired; whence the Apostle: « A widow that liveth « in delights is dead [n: (I Tim. V)]. » [var: Vulg. 1Tim 5:6 *Nam quae in deliciis est, vivens mortua est*, "for she that is in delights is dead while she liveth"] « In the gates of the daughter « of Sion. » They are all the best pursuits, etc., *as far as* which neither eye hath seen, nor ear heard. [n: (CASS.)] Gates against gates, because these are of death, those of life. « Sion. » [n: (Ibid.)] She begat all the Churches of the world, because there Christ was born, whence faith began.

VERS. 16.-- « I will exult. » [n: (AUG.)] Thus far the cry, thereafter the Church which is afflicted says, I will exult in Jesus. [n: (CASS.)] For by hope she is saved. « In the snare. » [n: (Ibid.)] [0858D] A hidden snare is a deceitful thought: the foot is the soul's love, which, if it be crooked, is called covetousness, if right, charity. For by it the soul is moved as it were to a place, whither it rejoices to have come by love. « In the snare » therefore, that is, in a fraudulent counsel: the foot, that is, the love which through fraud comes to a vain gladness, is caught, because that delight binds them, so that they dare not break off their love from thence and turn it to useful things. For if they should try, it is grief to leave the things which delight, and that grief does not suffer them to depart.

VERS. 17.-- *He shall be known.* [n: (CASS.)] Or, of the judgment to come, etc., *as far as* as certain men falsely think.

VERS. 18.-- *Let sinners be turned.* [n: (AUG.)] Here is the canticle of the diapsalma, as it were the hidden gladness of the separation: [0859A] which is made here not by place, but by affection, between the good and the evil. [n: (CASS.)] To sinners with Antichrist he foretells the end of evils; as it were this meanwhile, but at last *they shall be turned,* lest they rejoice in their own things. *Into hell,* lest they think themselves to be sent elsewhere.

VERS. 19.-- *Patience.* [n: (AUG.)] Patiently, for this chiefly will God be mindful of the poor, namely because of their patience.

VERS. 20.-- *Arise.* [n: (AUG.)] He prays for the judgment to come. [n: (CASS.)] The prophet, speaking of the end of the world, foresaw the coming of Antichrist; whence as if terrified he cries: *arise.*

VERS. 21.-- *Let them know.* That is, being tormented let them find by experience that they were foolish men.
