## PSALM X.-- *According to the Hebrews.*

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0859B]VERS. 1.-- *Why.* [n: (AUG.)] Diapsalma, because it will be permitted to him (to Antichrist) to do such great things that he may be thought God: he adds, as it were, the voice of those who groan and who ask concerning the deferring of judgment.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS., AUG.)] *Why.* ⟦s2⟧ He treats at length of the evils of that time, etc., *as far as* but opportunely, namely thus, *While the wicked man is proud.*

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The poor man is set on fire, they are caught in the counsels which they devise.* [n: (AUG.)] Wondrously does the providence of God work out of evils, etc., *as far as* thus the evil are damned, the just are saved.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *For the sinner is praised.* ⟦s2⟧ Therefore he is proud, or is caught, because he is praised by his own, by the fulfilling of the desires of his sensuality.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] The tongues of flatterers bind souls fast in sins: [0859C] for it is a delight to do those things in which not only is no reprover feared, but a praiser is even heard.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Of his wrath.* [n: (CASS.)] Of Antichrist's, because he will do all things turbulently and cruelly; or, of God's: he will not seek God by repenting; or, God will not seek him out, that he should take vengeance here, but is about to punish him more heavily in the time to come.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Defiled.* [n: (CASS.)] The cause of his wickedness is that he does not hold God before his eyes, and therefore his ways, that is, his thoughts and his works, are foul.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *At all times.* [n: (AUG.)] His own, namely of his life, etc., *as far as* and because *he shall have dominion over all his enemies.*

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *He hath said,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ I will take possession of all nations, but not *without evil,* that is, without sin, which is proper [0859D] to him. ⟦s3⟧ Or: I shall always be without evil; this he promises himself, *whose mouth is full of cursing,* etc.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I shall not be moved.* [n: (AUG.)] Or, he will think that he will come by evil arts, and not otherwise, from a mortal generation into an eternal one, which Simon Magus also thought.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Under the tongue.* [n: (CASS.)] That is, his thoughts are of the labour of the saints and of bodily sorrow, which is plain in the martyrs. ⟦s2⟧ Or, the labour of the saints and the sorrow which lies in the punishments of the body is *under his tongue,* that is, it is the lighter; his tongue, which harms within: that other, namely, is the punishment without.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Or, *under the tongue,* because words are more feared and will do more harm than stripes, by which the soul is cast headlong and will perish.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0860A]VERS. 8.-- *In secret places,* etc. [n: (AUG.)], that is, in ambiguous ones, where it is not easily seen what is to be sought and what is not.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *His eyes are upon the poor man,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] He sets out the ambushes: *the eyes,* as though with mercy and affection; or, he so deceives others that his eyes look cruelly upon the poor in spirit.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A lion in his den,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because in him is violence and guile, etc., *as far as* through heretics and false brethren.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *He shall bow himself down.* [n: (CASS.)] When through excessive prosperity he has let his mind slacken into idleness, he shall fall by a sudden destruction: and the Lord shall slay him with the spirit of his mouth.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Unto the end.* ⟦s2⟧ They deny that God sees, etc., *as far as* he rouses the Lord, *Arise, O Lord.*

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Arise, O Lord God, and let [0860B] thy hand be exalted, that thou mayest forget,* etc. [var: Vulg. Ps 9:33 *exaltetur manus tua ; ne obliviscaris pauperum*, "let thy hand be exalted: forget not the poor"] [n: (Ibid.)] He prays for judgment, that these things may be quickly ended here, things so great that no one would wish the time in which they happen to be prolonged.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Arise.* ⟦s2⟧ As it were unlooked for, that they may be crushed, while thine own, exalted with thee, are glorified.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Wherefore hath the wicked provoked God.* [var: Vulg. Ps 9:34 *Propter quid*, the interrogative "for what reason"] [n: (AUG.)] And so now, both understanding and exulting concerning that judgment, he says, wherefore? that is, what does it profit to do such great evils?

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For God hath said in his heart,* etc. [var: Vulg. Ps 9:34 *dixit enim in corde suo*, with no *Deus*: the speaker there is the wicked man] [n: (Ibid.)] This sense calls for a delivery, etc., *as far as* lest they toil, or grow angry.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] Christ bore with Judas and sent him to preach, and gave him the eucharist, that he might show that the gifts reach those who receive the faith, such as [0860C] Judas was.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *For thou seest that thou considerest labour and sorrow.* ⟦s2⟧ This thou hast already foreordained, where thou deliverest them into thy hand.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To the fatherless,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] To him for whom the world dies as a father, by which he was carnally begotten, so that he may say: *The world is crucified to me, and I to the world.*

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Break thou the arm.* ⟦s2⟧ By punishments he terrifies, by good things he soothes.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *It shall be sought.* [n: (AUG.)] While it is still needful for our purging, as above, while the wicked man is proud, etc.; but because it shall not be found, that is, because there is now no one in whom there is room for his sin, smooth iron has no need of a file.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0860D]VERS. 16.-- *The Lord shall reign for ever, and unto ages of ages.* ⟦s2⟧ This kingdom will be the more welcome for the evils that run alongside it, in that it will come when Antichrist has been destroyed; from this kingdom ye shall perish, O nations, but the poor shall have it, because *the desire of the poor,* etc.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *The preparation of the heart.* [n: (CASS.)] Or, concupiscence, which is where a thing is sought more ardently with all the powers of the soul. ⟦s2⟧ Or, this, which God himself has prepared in their hearts. ⟦s3⟧ Or, that God may hear as soon as they wish to ask.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The ear,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] When members are named which in us are visible and bodily, in God powers of operation must be understood.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To judge for the fatherless and the humble,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The second coming [0861A] will be manifest, etc., *as far as* the author of evils will be damned with his own people.

## PSALM X.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* [n: (AUG.)] A psalm of David the prophet, teaching men to resist heretics.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *In the Lord I put my trust.* [n: (AUG.)] Of the heretics who wish to turn Catholics to their own depravity.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *[n: (Ibid.)] In the Lord I put my trust.* ⟦s2⟧ As to the letter it can be read of the passion; and Christ says, according to his manhood, as he is wont, against the terrors of the Jews, *In the Lord I put my trust.*

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the Lord I put my trust.* ⟦s2⟧ When sinners wish to shoot at the upright.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0861B] *For behold. [n: (Ibid.)]* These are the terrors of those who threaten us with sinners, that we may pass over to them as to just men. ⟦s2⟧ For they say: *The sinners have bent their bow,* the Scriptures, whence they may send poisoned arrows, which they prepare in the secret place of the heart.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *In the dark.* [n: (CASS.)] Those who live in the simple sense, or in their own ambiguous opinions; not that Scripture avails for this, but because:

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *For the things which thou hast perfected.* ⟦s2⟧ He turns his speech away from these to God, as it were: Why do they say, Remove? ⟦s3⟧ Why is he terrified by sinners, who bend the bow to shoot at the upright? ⟦s4⟧ *because the things which thou hast perfected they have destroyed* in their assemblies, where they slay with poisons the little ones who were to be nourished with milk, out of whose mouth praise is perfected.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0861C] *But the just, what hath he done?* [n: (AUG.)] According to either part, etc., *as far as* he harms his own soul, not the just man, who believes God.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The Lord questioneth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *But the just.* ⟦s3⟧ He threatens judgment, by fear of which they may forsake their falsehoods.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He questioneth. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, he proves; because the good are not wearied by obscure things, but are exercised in knowledge: they are not puffed up, but confirmed.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The things which thou hast perfected.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, thou hast published them perfectly, or by fulfilling what was foretold. ⟦s3⟧ They have destroyed them by expounding them ill.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *[n: (Ibid.)] In the dark.* ⟦s2⟧ Or *when the moon is dark.* ⟦s3⟧ There are two opinions about the moon, etc., *as far as* he then calls the sacraments holy, when they are given through holy men.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *He shall rain upon.* ⟦s2⟧ He shows what he renders to the wicked. --[n: (AUG.)] He rains from the clouds, that is, from false prophets. [0861D] Or from good things, whence snares flow out when they are ill understood: but not save upon those who are sinners. ⟦s3⟧ *Fire.* ⟦s4⟧ First they are laid waste by the fire of covetous desires: thence by the stench of evil works they are cast out from the saints, thence they are plunged in eternal punishments.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Justices. [n: (Ibid.)]* In the plural, that thou mayest take it of the just, in whom there are many justices, though God's is one.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He hath beheld equity.* ⟦s2⟧ Equity was seen in his face, that is, in the knowledge of him: for his face is the knowledge of him, that is, the power by which he will become known to the worthy. ⟦s3⟧ Or *he hath seen,* because he does not give himself to be known to the evil, but to the good, which is equity.
