## PSALM XI.

VERS. 1.-- *For the octave.* [n: (CASS.)] *For the octave,* because [0862A] here the prophet asks that the iniquity of the world be destroyed, so that he may come through to the truth of the promise to come.

[n: (AUG.)] The octave stands for the day of judgment, as was said in the sixth psalm.

VERS. 2.-- *Save me.* And he calls men back from the falsehoods of the world to the words of God, where salvation is; he asks to be saved from the perversity of the world.

*For there hath failed.* [n: (CASS.)] He sets out the many things he feared, gathering them into one, that the force may be the greater.

*The holy one. [n: (Ibid.)]* God, who is everywhere present, has failed so far as concerns men, who do not believe in him.

VERS. 3.-- *His neighbour.* [n: (AUG.)] Every man is a neighbour, since with none is evil to be done: by the condition of the first birth every man is a neighbour; by kinship of the flesh, friends and kinsmen; by the reckoning of mercy, [0862B] any man who does good.

*With a heart,* etc. Germination [cj: *Germinatio*; read *Geminatio*, "the doubling"] signifies a double heart, in which the deceitful man is described; whence: *A man double in mind is inconstant in all his ways.* The good have one heart and one soul.

VERS. 4.-- *May the Lord destroy all lips,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] That is, let them perish on every side who come together in one depravity.

*The tongue that speaketh great things. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, a proud one: these are hypocrites, having a show in speech to deceive men, and not subject to God. [n: (REMIG.)] Or, philosophers.

VERS. 5.-- *Who have said: our tongue.* [n: (CASS.)] They boast of the power of their lips, as though they had not received it from God.

[0862C]VERS. 6.-- *Of the needy and the groaning of the poor.* AUG., [n: (CASS.)] *The needy,* the Gentiles, who had neither the law nor the prophets; *the poor,* the Jews. *Now.* Which was not so in the former people. *I will arise.* I shall appear in the Son. *I will set.* The consolation of the poor. *With confidence.* Powerfully, in that nothing resists him.

VERS. 7.-- *The words of the Lord. [n: (Ibid.)]* Against the evils of the world Christ was sent by the Father, whose words are true, those of the wicked false. *Chaste.* Against that which is above, *they have spoken vain things.--Tried by fire.* Against, *with deceitful lips, in a heart and a heart have they spoken.*

*Sevenfold.* [n: (AUG.)] Perfectly, etc. *as far as* and it shows what is perfect.

VERS. 8.-- *Thou, O Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] Turning himself to the Lord, he [0862D] shows the usefulness of the divine words.

VERS. 9. *Round about.* A conclusion concerning either part. [n: (AUG.)] *Round about,* that is, in the desire of temporal things, which are rolled round in seven days and do not come to the eighth, that is, to the eternal: whence *a wise king is the winnower of the wicked, and brings in upon them the wheel of evils [n: (Prov. II)]*, [var: Vulg. Prov 20:26 *Dissipat impios rex sapiens, et incurvat super eos fornicem*, "a wise king scattereth the wicked, and bendeth an arch over them"] that is, the circuit of things that roll.

*Thou hast multiplied the children of men. [n: (Ibid.)]* There is also a multiplication in temporal things which turns a man away from the unity of God: but the just are multiplied according to the height, when they go from virtue to virtue.

## PSALM XII.

VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* [n: (CASS.)] A psalm concerning the charity [0863A] of Christ, etc., *as far as* according to that word: *I desire to be dissolved and to be with Christ. [n: (Phil. I.)]*

VERS. 2.-- *How long.* [n: (CAS.)] Great is the ardour of him who endures, etc., *as far as* because hope is drawn out.

*Counsels.* [n: (AUG.)] There is no need of counsel, etc., *as far as* grieving and praying for eternal things.

VERS. 3.-- *How long shall he be exalted,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] He says that before the coming of Christ the devil held men captive. Or *over me,* that is, over my cruelty, because he is worshipped in the whole world; and because the world so perishes, he weeps.

VERS. 4.-- *Look thou upon me. [n: (Ibid.)]* After the complaint, a prayer, that he may be enlightened, lest he yield to the frauds of the enemy, he who trusts in mercy. *Look thou upon me.* [n: (AUG.)] Send him whom thou art to send. *Hear me.* [n: (CASS.)] For all, for whom he asks in general. *Enlighten.* If not in body, [0863B] let us see at least in mind. *That I sleep.* When, the light of faith being buried, they are shut up in carnal delight. *In death.* That is, in the sin which is committed against the Holy Spirit, of which John says: *There is a sin unto death, I do not say for it that any man should pray.*

VERS. 6.-- *They shall rejoice.* [n: (AUG.)] Not only for enlightening me, but lest the enemy rejoice. *I have hoped* [n: (CASS.)] And though he makes haste, yet he is patient: because even if it is put off, he is firm in hope, and already seeing inwardly he exults over him whom he longs to see in the flesh: whence he who before complained says that good things have already been given him. *He hath given.* Not, he will give: the great power of faith already has what is to come. *My heart shall rejoice.* If he desires so much, if he so serves him who is to come, what must be done when he has been received?

## PSALM XIII.

[0863C]VERS. 1.-- *The fool hath said.* [n: (CASS.)] A rebuke of the Jews, their malice being described. The first psalm concerning the rebuke of the Jews, etc., *as far as* this man is not God.

VERS. 2.-- *The Lord from heaven. [n: (Ibid.)]* But from thence they are without excuse, because the Lord looked down, that is, he stood forth out of human nature. [n: (AUG.)] From heaven, that is, he looks down through holy souls: for by himself nothing lies hidden from him. Or, from heaven, through holy souls, which are the heaven and the seat of God. Or, the Lord the Father from heaven, that is, from the secret of the divine mind. [n: (CAS.)] Or, from the equality of God the Father. *If there be one that understandeth.* Or, he saw whether there be one that understandeth, and he found that not one understands: because he does not seek, but turns aside, for: *All have gone aside,* etc.

VERS. 3.-- *A sepulchre. [n: (Ibid.)]* Thus made useless to others, [0863D] because their throat is a sepulchre, etc. *A sepulchre.* These three verses, which run as far as the second division, at *Shall they not know,* etc., are said not to be from the Hebrew, etc., *as far as* it does not have them.

*Destruction* [n: (AUG.)] Thus they have been made useless to themselves, etc., *as far as* and unhappiness in the time to come.

(CASS) The way of the wicked, etc., *as far as: The fear of God before their eyes,* etc.

VERS. 4.-- *Shall they not know.* He threatens judgment to the wicked.

VERS. 5.-- *The Lord they have not called upon,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] For they do not truly call upon him who love the things that displease God.--[n: (CASS.)] Thence destruction is increased, because they must needs do evil, since the proud will not call upon God. As [0864A] it is caution to fear at the fitting time, so it is cowardice to tremble unfittingly, as for nation and place, the fear of God being cast away.

VERS. 6.-- *The counsel of the needy.* [n: (CASS.)] An invective against the Jews, etc., *as far as,* that is, proceeding from the Jews, etc.

VERS. 7.-- *Who shall give out of Sion,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Last of all the conversion of the Jews is foretold, which will come to pass when the fulness of the Gentiles has come in. *When he shall have turned away.* For the Jews too the Apostle sets down this testimony of Isaiah: *There shall come out of Sion he that turneth away the captivity, or the impiety, from Jacob.*
