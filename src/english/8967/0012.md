## PSALM XIV.

VERS. 1.-- *Lord, who?* [n: (CAS.)] This psalm is properly an instructor in morals, a repressing of presumption: where [0864B] it treats of the unity of the present Church and of the quiet to come. First the prophet asks, as a priest standing before the face of the Lord, and seeks answers; wishing to know whom God judges worthy of his Church.

VERS. 2.-- *He that. [n: (Ibid.)]* The answer of the Lord, etc. *as far as* as Christ the lamb, without breaking of members, who properly entered the temple, not that he might be expiated like the rest, but without stain. *He worketh justice.* As when he cast out of the temple those that sold and bought.

VERS. 3.-- *He that speaketh.* Here he follows it out more fully. Truth is when the thing is so as it is said: this Christ did properly. *In his heart,* who at times kept silence toward the unworthy who questioned him fraudulently. *He hath not wrought deceit.* Deceit is in the tongue, when one thing is said and another is in the breast. Or, *he hath not wrought deceit,* because all that he heard from the Father, [0864C] without any addition or suppression, he made known to his neighbours, that is, to the disciples; to the Jews, his neighbours according to the flesh. *Nor hath he done evil to his neighbour.* But he prayed for them; he did not take up reproach against his neighbours, as is plain from this, that he did not tear Judas with a biting rebuke.

VERS. 4.-- *To nothing,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] This is perfection, that the malignant one avail nothing in a man, and that this be in his sight, that is, that he know for certain that no one is malignant save he who turns from the Creator to created things. *The malignant one,* that is, the devil, is despised; as when he said: *Go back, Satan,* etc. On the contrary, *he glorifieth them that fear the Lord.* He swore, when he promised the apostles with most certain truth: *Now I will not call you servants, but friends.*

[0864D]VERS. 5.-- *He that sweareth to his neighbour,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] These three things are not great, etc., *as far as* since in the conclusion above he set down a past tense, *he is brought to nothing.*

*Money.* Either it is earthly, which is utterly forbidden to be given out at usury, and which the Lord handed over to Judas to be paid out to the poor, not for usury. Or it is spiritual, which is commanded to be given out at usury, that is, preaching for the instructing of men. *And gifts against the innocent.* Christ does indeed receive gifts, as from the Magi, and daily devout offerings; but not against the innocent; rather he gave his soul for them. *He that doeth these things shall not be moved.* Behold the complete answer to the questioner: he does them (he says), that he may invite us to the act, not only to [0865A] the singing of them. *He that doeth these things shall not be moved.* [n: (CASS.)] The conclusion. In like manner he that doeth these things shall rest on the mountain.

## PSALM XV.

VERS. 1.-- *The inscription of a title.* [n: (CASS.)] This title is taken from the Gospel, not from the Old Testament. What the title noted, that is, victory and kingdom, belongs to Christ, and that is what is treated of here.

VERS. 2.-- *Preserve me,* etc. The second psalm, which touches briefly on the passion and the resurrection; first, Christ, showing the one nature of man, asks to be preserved, saying that all that he endured has been brought through to the glory of his inheritance. [n: (AUG.)] Christ speaks here according to his manhood, of whom the royal title stood out in the passion: *Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews.--Of my [0865B] goods,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] If God has no need of the goods of Christ the man, but Christ the man has all things from him, the arrogance of men is overthrown, and the heresy of the Pelagians, who think that anything is done by their own good powers.

VERS. 3.-- *To the saints who are in the land. [n: (Ibid.)]* Christ has now set out how he prayed; he now tells what good things God did for him. [n: (AUG.)] *To the saints,* etc., *as far as* and power that I might rise again.

*He hath made wonderful.* [n: (CASS.)] As if to say, these things I said to the Lord: but the Lord made them wonderful, that is, he made my wills wonderful; and they are wonderful in that those who obey them become heavenly out of earthly.

VERS. 4.-- *They are multiplied. [n: (Ibid.)]* Whereby he made them wonderful, [0865C] etc., *as far as* which sound together in one note, by which his wills are shown.

VERS. 5.-- *The portion of my inheritance.* [n: (AUG.)] Let others choose other things, etc., *as far as* but those who choose God as their portion. [n: (CASS.)] Or, *the Lord* is the *portion,* that is, the share and the reward, *of my inheritance,* that is, of the saints. And *the portion of my cup,* that is, the wage of the passion. For the inheritance belongs to the Gentiles, the cup to the passion, which when drunk gives resurrection. But let no one think himself an heir save he that is illustrious.

VERS. 6.-- *The lines,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] As it were by lot, in which is the divine election, as the lot also fell upon Matthias, and each man takes according to his own measure. *In goodly places.* That is, among the saints.

VERS. 7.-- *I will bless the Lord. [n: (Ibid.)]* He gives [0865D] thanks to the Father for understanding, and for the passion, in which by his favour he conquers, and for the resurrection, and for confession; and these are the things through which the inheritance is restored, so that he is king of the Jews, as the title says. *Understanding,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Whereby this inheritance can be seen and possessed. *Moreover,* etc. That is, above understanding. *Even till night,* etc. That is, till death, he hath instructed me, that is, my lower part, the assumption of flesh, that he might drive out the darknesses of mortality which that understanding does not have, because for them it could neither be troubled nor fail. *My reins.* From whom according to the flesh I came forth, namely my kindred: they have chided me with injuries, with temptation and with passion.

VERS. 8.-- *I set the Lord before me. [n: (Ibid.)]* From this draw an example, whereby sins may be shunned: for he who [0866A] always beholds God with the keen edge of the mind is not bowed down by sins.
