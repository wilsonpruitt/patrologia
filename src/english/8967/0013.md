## PSALM XVI.

VERS. 1.-- *The prayer of David.* [n: (CASS.)] The prayer belongs to the person of the Lord, the Church being joined to him, which is his body. *Hear, O Lord. [n: (Ibid.)]* This psalm makes us attend to the true nature of his humanity. The prayer of Christ is an instruction of the faithful: as by weeping over Lazarus he taught us to have charity toward our neighbour; he fled his persecutors, that he might take from us the boldness of ill-considered rashness. First, he asks that it be rendered to him according to justice. *Not in deceitful lips.* As one who is unjustly condemned; or, as one who says: Lord, Lord, and does not do the works. [n: (AUG.)] Or, let my judgment come forth from thy countenance not in deceitful lips, so that in judging I may not put forth [0866B] anything other than I understand.

VERS. 2.-- *From thy countenance.* [n: (CASS.)] That is, according to those things which thou knowest in me, who have no sin. *Judgment.* Whereby he who was judged may be seen to have come forth as his own. *From thy countenance,* not from the disposition of the Jews, which deed is plain in the resurrection. Or, *judgment,* whereby he discerns all things.

VERS. 3.-- *Thou hast proved.* [n: (CASS.)] From the likeness of a furnace, etc., *as far as* thou knowest me to be pure.

VERS. 4.-- *Hard ways. [n: (Ibid.)]* Namely the works of men: which, namely, the works of men deserve. Or, which men ought to imitate, for whom the road of the vices is easy, that of the virtues hard. Or, it was not found, so that he should not speak, that is, that I may not say these things boastfully, or, that I may not sin with my tongue, which is the work of men. [0866C] [n: (AUG.)] Or, the hard ways of the thief, etc., *as far as* after I had gone along the hard ways and completed my steps.

VERS. 5.-- *Perfect thou.* [n: (CASS.)] Even to the end: before that there is nothing but a beginning. Steps, that is, human actions, so that they who wish to imitate may not be moved away from thee, nor their footsteps from them.

VERS. 6.-- *I have cried. [n: (Ibid.)]* Secondly, he prays that his purity be delivered from the ambushes of the Jews. I too have cried, as though thou shouldst say, this belongs to none but the just man, who prays with confidence, because he knows he is heard.

VERS. 7.-- *Make wonderful.* [n: (AUG.)] Wonderful are the things that are new: these are the things which he yielded in the flesh, the Jews marvelling.

VERS. 8.-- *The apple of the eye.* It is very small, and yet through it the keen edge of sight is directed, whereby light and darkness are distinguished, [0866D] just as through the humanity of Christ the godhead is what discerns between the good and the evil.

VERS. 10.-- *Their fat.* [n: (CASS.)] From the likeness of animals, which are puffed up from fat, that is, they have kept pride, not lean, that is, not humble; or the fat, that is, they have kept the carnal understanding; or, fattened with crimes they have lost true understanding, whence it remains for them to speak proud things. *Their mouth hath spoken pride.* Not their heart, because often the wicked condemn in their heart what they defend with their mouth.

VERS. 12.-- *They have taken me,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] Whence had they this opportunity? I answer: they took it from God, whose ordering it is; or, from the betrayer; or, they received it greedily from the judge. *As a lion prepared,* etc. [n: (AUG., CASS.)] [0867A] The princes are to be compared to the lion, the devil, by whose authorship they act: the people is *the young of a lion,* that is, the son of the devil, *dwelling,* remaining in ambushes, because it is the way of evil men to cover their vows, that they may accomplish them secretly.

VERS. 13.-- *Arise, O Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] A prayer concerning retributions. *Prevent,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] From the likeness of one running: there is no one who does not sin against himself before he sins against another. *Supplant him:* from the likeness of one wrestling, that is, make them ineffectual and cast them down. *Deliver my soul from the wicked one.* [n: (AUG.)] By raising it again from the death brought upon it by the wicked. *The sword.* My soul is the sword, which thy hand, that is, thy power, took up, that through it he might war down the kingdoms of iniquity and divide the just from the wicked. *Divide them,* etc., [n: (CASS.)] that is, the Jews divided, etc., *as far as* misery, as though deservedly: because, of the hidden things. *Of the hidden things* [0867B] *that are thine.* That is, of uncleannesses, etc., *as far as* that is, they shall be sharers of evils even to satiety. *Of the hidden things.* [n: (AUG.)] Not only is this punishment visible to them, but from hidden things, that is, from sins (which are hidden away from the light of thy truth), their memory is full, so that they forget God.
