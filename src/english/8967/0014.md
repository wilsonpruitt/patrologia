## PSALM XVII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *I will love thee, O Lord, my strength,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Christ says this, and so the Church, that is, the whole Christ.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (Ibid.)] First, the prophet, gathering up the sum, gives thanks for the deliverance.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *My refuge. [n: (Ibid.)]* When there was need of counsel, David fled to him and found it. ⟦s2⟧ *My deliverer,* as one who, being powerful, sets free a man taken captive by his enemies. ⟦s3⟧ *My protector,* [0867C] [n: (AUG.)] because I have not presumed upon myself, as though raising a horn of pride against thee; but I have found thee the height of my salvation, and that I might find it thou hast taken me up.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Praising I will call upon.* [n: (CASS.)] After all things he does not boast of himself, puffed up, but ascribes all to God: and therefore he is safe; for he who does otherwise, left to himself, perishes, even though he seemed to have conquered.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *They have compassed me about. [n: (Ibid.)]* In the person of the just before the coming of Christ, etc., *as far as* who have been troubled by rushing iniquities.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The sorrows of hell.* ⟦s2⟧ The Gentiles, to be tormented in hell with the sorrow they have deserved. ⟦s3⟧ *The snares of death have prevented me,* there follows:

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *It was moved.* ⟦s2⟧ Behold how, etc., *as far as* and this, which mortality and possibility forestalled.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0867D] *Out of his temple.* [n: (CASS.)] Either of heaven, or of the body of Christ that was to come.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *It was moved.* [n: (AUG.)] Thus, when Christ was glorified, sinners were moved and trembled. ⟦s2⟧ *The foundations,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] The presumptions of the proud, that is, riches and the like on which they leaned: for the hope of the world, which God hates, has passed away.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *The heavens. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, he bowed the preachers down from the contemplative life to ministry, and to the bearing of troubles. ⟦s2⟧ *He came down,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ He himself suffered in them, and came into the knowledge of certain men. ⟦s4⟧ *Darkness:* the dark, who even persecute, are under his power; or, those who believe simply are subject to him.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0868A]VERS. 11.-- *And he ascended.* ⟦s2⟧ For he makes certain men full of knowledge, in whom he stands out above, whose swiftness of understanding he transcends: but the dark he uses secretly: whence, *Assur is the rod of my wrath, yet he himself hath not known it,* but his own know. ⟦s3+4⟧ *He flew upon the wings of the wind.* [var: Vulg. Ps 17:11 *volavit super pennas ventorum*, "the wings of the winds", plural — the plural the gloss's own *Ventos* takes up] The winds, than which nothing is swifter, his fame outstrips, as when, newly born, he was rather announced by a star.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Darkness.* [n: (AUG.)] The obscurities of the sacraments; or, in these darknesses, where seeing we walk by faith, and not by sight [n: (Matth. II)].

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Not through the heavens. [n: (CASS.)] He came down and ascended [n: (II Cor. V)], but through the ethereal clouds (which is in the Hebrew), that is, through the prophets in whom the teaching is obscure; but through the clouds which (not of the ether, that is, the Apostles) [0868B] are exceedingly bright in the knowledge of him, that is, contemplating more nearly.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Hail.* [n: (AUG.)] Rebukes, which strike hard hearts; but if the fire of charity dissolves them, in a gentle soul it becomes water that satisfies [ *alias* saliens]. ⟦s2⟧ *And coals,* because by that fire dark hearts are kindled and come to life again.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *He thundered.* [n: (CASS.)] To the letter, as when the thunder was seen, God saying: *I have glorified it, and I will glorify it again. ⟦s2⟧ He gave his voice:* in that he did not say: This is my beloved Son in whom I am well pleased.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *And he sent. [n: (Ibid.)]* The Lord thundered, and the Lord sent: for the passing over to the Gentiles was not always held to be certain nor reasonable.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And the fountains appeared.* ⟦s2⟧ When Christ came, [0868C] there lay open what the obscurity of the divine word had covered. ⟦s3⟧ *At thy rebuke.* ⟦s4⟧ A turning to God: as it were with thanksgiving, because through preaching he rebuked sinners, and breathed into them that they should fear the wrath to come.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He sent. [n: (Ibid.)]* Thus far concerning the coming of the Lord; from here on, concerning Christian times. ⟦s2⟧ *He sent,* the Father, Christ. ⟦s3⟧ *He took me.* ⟦s4⟧ The Church his bride. ⟦s5⟧ *Out of the waters. [n: (Ibid.)]* Of baptism. ⟦s6⟧ Or *he sent the Spirit,* who is the gift. ⟦s7⟧ *From on high,* because he is equal to the Father and to the Son. ⟦s8⟧ *He took,* as it were a runaway. ⟦s9⟧ *He assumed,* into the height of virtue and of good work.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *From my enemies.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, from vices, which above all enemies do the more harm. ⟦s2⟧ *And from them who,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ That is, from outward enemies, men, and also demons, as must needs be, because they have been strengthened [0868D] in a certain respect. ⟦s4⟧ *Behold, they have prevented,* that is, they have prevailed. ⟦s5⟧ By this he delivers the more, because they have been strengthened; for when the Church is afflicted, it grows the more.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Into a large place. [n: (Ibid.)]* Through the savagery of the wicked, the Church grows in number and in merit. ⟦s2⟧ *He hath saved me,* in that it came to the faith. ⟦s3⟧ *Because he would have me.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, he chose freely.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *And he will reward according to the justice* of a good will. ⟦s2⟧ *And according to the cleanness of the hands [n: (Ibid.)]* the works which come after the first benefits of God, namely faith and charity, which God bestows freely, without preceding merit.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Because I have kept. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or, joining it thus [0869A] with the preceding verse, etc., *as far as* and as I have begun, *I shall be unspotted,* whence in time to come he will reward me.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *With him,* cleaving to him. ⟦s2⟧ *And I will keep myself,* lest I fall again. ⟦s3⟧ From iniquity, which is the death of the blessed man.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *And he will reward.* [n: (CASS.)] God rewards in two ways: here in the present, that the just may be justified, and he that is in filth may be filthy still, whence here he says: *With the holy thou wilt be holy,* etc.; and in time to come a reward for the good and a punishment for the evil, whence there: *For thou wilt save the humble people,* etc., as if to say, so here: because in time to come *thou wilt save the humble people.*

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] And he will give: now not only for the breadth of faith which works through love, but for long-suffering he will reward me. ⟦s2⟧ *In the sight:* [0869B] this belongs to the just, that as they always behold God, so God may continually look upon them.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *With the holy.* [n: (CASS.)] He had said that it was rendered according to justice, etc., *as far as* because *thou, O Lord, wilt save the humble people. ⟦s2⟧ And thou wilt bring down the eyes of the proud.*

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] There is also a hidden depth, by which thou art understood to be holy with the holy, because it is thou that sanctifiest.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For thou dost light.* ⟦s2⟧ What he declared concerning the elect and the sanctified, he now prays concerning others.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *My lamp. [n: (Ibid.)]* There were preachers, in whom was light, that is, the knowledge of truth, by which they enlightened others; whence: *We have this treasure in earthen vessels. ⟦s2⟧ Darkness.* [n: (AUG.)] those who are still in darkness. ⟦s3⟧ *Mine.* ⟦s4⟧ By predestination.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0869C]VERS. 30.-- *In my God.* [n: (CASS.)] For my God is the way, undefiled, by which one goes to heaven. ⟦s2⟧ Or, the way by which the holy and immaculate Virgin came into the world. ⟦s3⟧ And whatever he said has been tried by the Holy Spirit, that is, it is useful and honourable: as a wise man first judges within himself whatever he says. ⟦s4⟧ *I shall go over. [n: (Ibid.)]* A man cannot utterly pull it down, but by the help of God he passes over the wall, the obstacle of sins.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *My God. [n: (Ibid.)]* And, joining them, there follows: *Thou wilt deliver me from the contradictions of the people.* ⟦s2⟧ The rest he interposes by way of commendation. ⟦s3⟧ *The words.* ⟦s4⟧ They are proved by the fire of trial. ⟦s5⟧ *Of all. [n: (Ibid.)]* No one is excepted, save he who will not hope.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *For who. [n: (Ibid.)]* I say God: for who is another God, save he who also [0869D] is Lord over things? ⟦s2⟧ And because he is known in Judea, not the gods of the nations; these things are against the pagans, who fashion many for themselves.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *God who. [n: (Ibid.)]* Still further, beyond what is common, he commends him by what is special, for thirdly Christ is commended for his virtue and his power. ⟦s2⟧ *He hath girded. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, he has armed him thoroughly. ⟦s3⟧ Or, from the likeness of one running. ⟦s4⟧ Or, it marks dignity and strength, which belongs to him who by his own power will judge the people, or the world. ⟦s5⟧ *Unspotted,* without sin. ⟦s6⟧ The stag leaps over thorns and gaping places, so Christ over the thorns and pits of sins: and as the stag climbs the heights, so Christ ascends above all creatures.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 35.-- *Who teacheth:* I make him wary of the ambushes that threaten. ⟦s2⟧ *Hands,* works or wills. ⟦s3⟧ *To battle,* [0870A] against the devil. ⟦s4⟧ *As a bow,* etc., by the likeness of a thing which does not exist, that is, unrelenting strength. ⟦s5⟧ *Arms.* ⟦s6⟧ The prophets of Christ, and the apostles, through whom he works: who are a brazen bow, because they do not grow soft by preaching, but, always strong, hurl forth the words of salvation.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *The protection of salvation.* ⟦s2⟧ Not that I should suffer nothing, which nevertheless is for my profit. ⟦s3⟧ Tribulation is correction, and teaching, and enlargement, and confirmation.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *Thou hast enlarged* [n: (AUG.)] Lest fleshly straits hinder, because thou hast made wide the charity that works cheerfully, even out of those things which are under me, namely out of mortal things and members.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 38.-- *I will pursue.* [n: (CASS.)] By reproving, by wearying them with divers afflictions.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 43.-- *And I will break them small.* [n: (AUG.)] Behold the same [0870B] operation of the Father and of the Son. ⟦s2⟧ Above he says: *Thou hast destroyed, O Father,* and now he says, I will break them small, that is, I will take away their powers, so that afterwards they may yield to every wind of doctrine.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 44.-- *Thou wilt deliver.* [n: (CASS.)] Upon them, wrath: *But thou wilt deliver me,* the casting down of the Jews, and the exalting of the Gentiles.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 46.-- *The children.* ⟦s2⟧ The Jews whom I nourished, made strangers: *They have lied to me,* as to my honour. ⟦s3⟧ *They have grown old and have halted from their paths,* following their own traditions rather than God's.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 47.-- *He liveth. [n: (Ibid.)]* Fourthly, the Church exulting praises in sum the gifts of God. ⟦s2⟧ Or *he liveth,* because he gives life. ⟦s3⟧ *Blessed,* because he blesses all things. ⟦s4⟧ *And let the God of salvation be exalted.* [n: (AUG.)] I will not think of the God of my salvation after an earthly manner, nor of that salvation itself as earthly, [0870C] but on high I will hope in him.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 48.-- *Revenges.* [n: (CASS.)] The vengeance is dutiful, when the rebel is subdued. ⟦s2⟧ *From the wrathful.* ⟦s3⟧ It is a greater glory to deliver from raging evils.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 51.-- *Magnifying, the salvations.* ⟦s2⟧ God who magnifies: Doing, God who does.
