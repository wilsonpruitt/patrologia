## PSALM XVIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* [n: (CASS.)] A psalm of the first coming, whereby the tyrant falls and man is set free; here the prophet looks unto the end, that is, unto Christ, or unto the last times.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The heavens shew forth. [n: (Ibid.)]* First, speaking of the incarnation, he commends the preaching of the new law. [n: (AUG.)] The heavens, the apostles, concerning the glory of Christ in which [0870D] he is equal to the Father. [n: (CASS.)] Or the heavens, they who sent the star. ⟦s2⟧ *The glory. [n: (Ibid.)]*: That God freely forgives sins. ⟦s3⟧ *The works.* [n: (AUG.)] Those which are according to man, etc., *as far as* and these same are made night to him, giving to the darkened the knowledge which is of things human. [n: (CASS.)] And this so wonderfully, that it is in all tongues; and so widely, that it is in every part of the compass of the earth.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Day unto day.* [n: (CASS.)] He treats of it by demonstration. [n: (AUG.)] *Day unto day.* ⟦s2⟧ The Spirit brings forth to spiritual men the fullness of wisdom, etc., *as far as* this teaching is continued by days and by nights down to those who come after.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Speeches.* [n: (CASS.)] Public persuadings. ⟦s2⟧ *Words.* ⟦s3⟧ Common narrations. ⟦s4⟧ *Whose voices are not heard.* ⟦s5⟧ He would say more plainly, in whom their voices are not heard, [0871A] or, the voices of all their words are heard: he does the same thing by means of the relative.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] The Spirit made the first men speak with all tongues, he who was to gather all nations into one; then one man was for all, so now one Christ, head and body.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *In the sun* [n: (CASS.)] What he praises others for having done, he himself does: he preaches the incarnation. ⟦s2⟧ Or in the sun, in the open sight of the world, or in labour. ⟦s3⟧ The sun burns, and shines, and varies the seasons. ⟦s4⟧ *His tabernacle.* [n: (AUG.)] As it were a soldier's dwelling, that is, the dispensation of his incarnation, in which he came to storm the errors of the world. ⟦s5⟧ Or the tabernacle, the Church in the open, not in secret, so as to lie hid. ⟦s6⟧ *A bridegroom.* [n: (CASS.)] So often promised by the prophets, a great likeness of the sacrament: therefore born of [0871B] a virgin, that he might join to himself the Church a virgin. ⟦s7⟧ *Out of his bride-chamber.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, the virginal womb, where God was joined to human nature as a bridegroom to a bride. ⟦s8⟧ Behold, humble in mercy, but strong in majesty. ⟦s9⟧ *As a giant to run.* [n: (CASS.)] Unshaken, not to be called back, overcoming human nature by his power. ⟦s10⟧ *From the highest.* ⟦s11⟧ Here the Trinity is shewn, etc., *as far as* it marks the unity of the Trinity. ⟦s12⟧ *Neither is there any that can hide himself,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] The Word made flesh permitted no man to excuse himself out of the shadow of death: because the heat of the Word pierced even that. ⟦s13⟧ Or, the heat is the Spirit, whom he sent when he had returned, who is law, testimony, justice, commandment, fear and judgments, because he does these things.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The law of the Lord.* [n: (CAS.)] Secondly, he praises the precepts of the New and the Old Testament. [n: (Ibid.)] The law given through [0871C] Moses, correcting all things by the distinction of its words, and sending them on to the grace of Christ. ⟦s2⟧ *Converting.* [n: (AUG.)] By liberty, unto the imitating of himself, not pressing down with the yoke of servitude. ⟦s3⟧ *Souls.* ⟦s4⟧ Wills, not merely hands, as the Old Testament did. ⟦s5⟧ *The testimony.* ⟦s6⟧ Whatsoever [sic: *Quaecunquae*] testimonies he gave to Israel are full of truth, and have given wisdom to the humble. ⟦s7⟧ *Wisdom:* concerning things divine.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The justices of the Lord. [n: (Ibid.)]* All are right in him, who taught not what he did not do, so that they who imitate them might rejoice in them, seeing they would do them freely with charity, not slavishly with fear. [n: (CASS.)] *Justices,* the law which justifies; whatsoever it commands is just.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *The fear of the Lord. [n: (Ibid.)]* The fear of the world is not holy, because it is not profitable, and it changes with the times. ⟦s2⟧ *The fear of the Lord* is without any perturbation, [0871D] because it is a love mingled with dread, because if it fears the judge, it knows him merciful. [n: (AUG.)] Fear, not that penal fear for earthly things, for love of which the soul is afraid, but the chaste fear whereby, the more it loves, the more it is wary of offending the bridegroom, and therefore it is not cast out, but abides. ⟦s3⟧ *Judgments.* ⟦s4⟧ The judgments of God need not another's authority.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Desirable things. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or, much to be desired, or much gold and stone; or much that is precious. ⟦s2⟧ Desirable above gold, etc., *as far as,* whose will he sets before his own, and they are sweeter than he himself is to himself. ⟦s3⟧ *Above gold,* that is, everything that is sought after, whether for riches, or for power, or for pleasure. ⟦s4⟧ *Honey.* ⟦s5⟧ The food of God is honey; that is, one now loosed from the world; and the honeycomb, that is, because he is still wrapped about, for whom there is need [0872A] of the pressure of God's hand squeezing him out, that he may go into life.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1+s2+s3+s4+s5⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Who understandeth sins. [n: (Ibid.)]* Thirdly he asks to be purged of vices, that he may be a worthy psalmist, to report these sacraments. To a mind that sees, truth is sweet, and judgments sweet; to one that does not see, what sweetness is there? But why is it not seen? Because of sins: nor is it any wonder: *For who understandeth sins?* [n: (CASS.)] *Sins:* all of them, for certain of them are known. *Mine* defile, *others'* afflict, and now spare, drive back the persuader. *From my secret ones.* He includes all sins under two heads, etc., *as far as* whereby man was seduced; and by consenting he made it his own. [n: (AUG.)] There is no greater sin than to apostatize from God, which is the beginning of pride: but pride is the beginning and the cause of all sin.

## PSALM XIX.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0872B]

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Unto the end.* [n: (CASS.)] A figure of the working; the Prophet sings the things that are to come.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (Ibid.)] The Prophet out of exceeding charity wishes a prosperous issue for the Church, which he saw would come to pass through Christ.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *May he hear thee:* the things which he wills to be done for her in the time of tribulation. ⟦s2⟧ *In the day of tribulation,* wherein we pray to God with the greater desire. ⟦s3⟧ *Of the God of Jacob,* that as to him, so to thee he may give the grace of blessing, and set thee before the Jews.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *May he send:* the Father. ⟦s2⟧ *Help,* by sending the Son.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Mindful.* ⟦s2⟧ He treats as it were of a priest about to offer sacrifice and of a king about to fight. ⟦s3⟧ *Of thy sacrifice. [n: (Ibid.)]* He foresaw the sacrifice [0872C] of the Church, not of cattle, but of the blood and the body of Christ.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *May he give.* ⟦s2⟧ Diapsalma, the things which he wishes and affirms for the time of glory. ⟦s3⟧ *All thy counsel.* [n: (AUG.)] Not only in that thou hast laid down thy soul for the life of very many; but also in that *blindness in part hath happened in Israel, and so all Israel should be saved.*

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *We shall rejoice. [n: (Ibid.)]* Therefore this prayer, because we shall rejoice and shall be magnified; he shews himself to be one of the Church so long a time before.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *In powers.* [n: (AUG.)] Our powers are the salvation of his favour, since he gives help even out of tribulation, because vain is the salvation of men, which is of the left hand, whence also pride is born. ⟦s2⟧ *The salvation of his right hand.* [0872D] [n: (Ibid.)], that is, spiritual grace, etc., *as far as* they who presume upon earthly dignity.

## PSALM XX.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* ⟦s2⟧ The third psalm, of the two natures in Christ; it describes the heavenly king, that the world might believe by hearing him whom the Jew, having seen him, despises. ⟦s3⟧ He makes a demonstrative oration in praise of God.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Lord, in thy strength shall he rejoice.* [n: (CASS.)] He sets forth the sum, namely, the merit and the reward.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *For thou hast prevented him.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalma; he describes his virtues and his glory, etc., *as far as* as it were for a crown to the head. ⟦s2⟧ *A crown. [n: (Ibid.)]* Because the circuit [0873A] of the Apostles hemmed him about as he taught, or the whole circle of the world, that is, the general Church.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *In thy salvation: glory and great beauty,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] By the Word of the Father, whence comes that glory. ⟦s2⟧ *Glory* as to the judgment, because he shall judge in his Godhead. ⟦s3⟧ *Beauty* in majesty, which shall then appear. ⟦s4⟧ *Glory and great beauty,* etc. ⟦s5⟧ Glorious, as Peter, by whose shadow the sick were healed; and beauty is *upon him,* beside whom there is no other foundation. ⟦s6⟧ Therefore *upon him,* because thou shalt give . . . . .

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *In joy with thy countenance. ⟦s2⟧ For the king hopeth in the Lord, and in the mercy,* etc. [n: (ID.)]. ⟦s3⟧ That is, of this, that his own shall rejoice with thy presence, when thou shalt be all in all.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Let thy hand be found,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Of [0873B] the punishments of his enemies, as above of the praises of Christ.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seed.* ⟦s2⟧ There shall not be for them the fruit of blessedness in the land of the living.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *For thou shalt make.* [n: (CASS.)] Things said of what is to come, etc., *as far as* that when they thought to kill, salvation might come to be.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Be thou exalted.* [n: (ID.)] The conclusion.
