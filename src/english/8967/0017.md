## PSALM XXII.

VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of David.* [n: (CASS.)] The Prophet foretold the return of the people from Babylon. *The Lord ruleth me.* A psalm of mercy, enumerating ten graces, that all may hope in the mercy of God. [n: (AUG.)] The Church speaks of Christ.

VERS. 2.-- *In a place of pasture.* [n: (CASS.)] The first grace, a place of pasture, that is, the divine word, which fattens those who meditate upon it. [0876A] *Upon the water.* The second grace, the water of refreshment.

VERS. 3.-- *My soul.* [n: (ID.)] The third grace, etc., *as far as* but first he brought me up upon the water of refreshment, first he converted my soul. *He hath led.* [n: (ID.)] The fourth, in that he led me upon the precepts of charity. *Upon the paths.* [n: (AUG.)] The narrower commandments and the more perfect counsels; as it were upon paths few just men walk, even as paths are narrower than roads.

VERS. 4.-- *For though I shall walk.* [n: (CASS.)] The fifth, that in the shadow of death he fears no evils. *Thou art with me.* [n: (AUG.)] In the heart by faith, that after the shadow of death I may be with thee. *Thy rod.* [n: (CASS.)] The sixth, correction; or the rod is strictness, which converts vices. The staff is governance, which upholds the faithful.

[0876B]VERS. 5.-- *Thou hast prepared in my sight.* [n: (CASS.)] The seventh, the table of the Lord, the blessed banqueting, the fullness of faith, the heavenly food, of which the Apostle: *He that eateth and drinketh unworthily, eateth and drinketh judgment to himself. In my sight a table:* even if not that of the wicked. Of this it is said: *But these at the table of the rich man,* that is, of Christ, *know that thou too must prepare the like,* that is, to suffer as he did. *Thou hast anointed:* the eighth, the anointing of the head, namely of Christ, which was dried up by no barrenness of sin, whence the rest of the members rejoice, because from the head the ointment ran down upon the beard, the beard of Aaron, and unto the skirt of his garment. *And my chalice.* The ninth, the chalice, the blood of Christ, which so inebriates that it makes men sober. *Mine,* because I receive it. Or, thine, because thou givest it. The table and the chalice, flesh and blood, although they are one sacrament, yet under diverse [0876C] species, because both are taken, are here set down separately.

VERS. 6.-- *And mercy.* [n: (CASS.)] The tenth, mercy following after unto keeping, which goes before unto the conferring of grace.

VERS. 7.-- *And that I may dwell.* [n: (HIER.)] This is joined to all the things above. As though: thou hast placed, thou hast brought up, thou hast led, etc., and this to the end that I may dwell.

## PSALM XXIII.

VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of David.* [n: (AUG.)] On the first day of the week the world was founded, on the same day man was restored through the resurrection of Christ: [n: (CASS.)] whence he admonishes us to receive the conqueror with triumph. *The earth is the Lord's.* First he says that the whole world is God's, etc., *as far as* [0876D] but who shall be in his mountain, save the innocent?

VERS. 2.-- *And upon the rivers.* [n: (AUG.)] As rivers glide into the sea, so the covetous glide into the world; and these too the Church overcomes, which, its covetous desires being conquered, is made ready for immortality.

VERS. 3.-- *Who shall ascend?* He determines with what virtues they are endowed in the Church.

VERS. 4.-- *Who shall stand?* [n: (HIER.)] For many ascend who do not abide. [n: (ID.)] *The innocent:* toward his neighbour, in hand and in heart, and he who is innocent toward himself, etc., *as far as* they should desire his presence and his knowledge. *Who hath not taken.* [n: (AUG., HIER.)] That is, who has not reckoned his soul among things that do not abide, but perceiving it immortal shall desire things eternal, and therefore [0877A] has not sworn deceitfully. *In vain.* [n: (HIER.)] That is, who has stained it with no dregs of sins after baptism.

VERS. 7.-- *Lift up.* Diapsalmus. Christian religion having been declared, etc., *as far as* which were set there by the prince the devil. *And be ye lifted up.* Against the gates of death. *Ye everlasting gates:* all the grace which, at Christ's coming, was given.

VERS. 8.-- *Who is?* he asks. The Prophet, to convict the perfidy of the Jews, and that by commending him the more he may invite others.

VERS. 10.-- *The Lord strong* is described by his powers as though by his proper name. *Strong,* [n: (AUG.)] even if ye took him with swords.

*Mighty,* even if ye delivered him to Pilate.

[0877B] *Mighty in battle,* lest ye think ye have overcome him in the fight. *The powers.* [n: (Hier., AUG.)] The ministering powers going before Christ in the ascension cry out to the other heavenly ones, that they may open the way to the Lord as he comes: and these, astonished at the new mystery of one clothed in flesh, ask, who is he?

*Lift up.* [n: (CASS.)] A doubling, etc., *as far as* who gives all good things to all, or of the heavenly powers, that is, of the angels.
