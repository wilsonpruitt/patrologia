## PSALM XXIV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* [n: (Hier.)] Here the penitent who has been scourged is set before the rest, lest they murmur in adversities: he who, having had the trial in himself, coaxes through mercy and terrifies through justice.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0877C] *To thee, O Lord, have I lifted up.* [n: (AUG.)] A psalm against the adversities of the world, etc., *as far as* who not through all, the less perfect.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS.)] First he asks to be taught the ways of the Lord, because he is raised up toward God.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Toward the contemplating of thee; he easily despises earthly things who beholds things divine.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *I trust.* [n: (ID.)] Having had trial of my own weakness in evils, I shall not blush because of my enemies.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Neither let my enemies laugh at me:* and I am sure that he deceives none that endures patiently.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In vain.* [n: (ID.)] Because all things are vain and unfruitful which are alien from God.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Shew me thy ways, O Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] The ways are of the order of life, etc., *as far as: Direct,* belongs to life, [0877D] *teach* to knowledge.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Teach.* [n: (AUG.)] Truth itself, that I may understand what I believe, because I have endured; and if this suffices not: *Remember.*

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *God my saviour.* [n: (CASS.)] There are two things which make men good, that we believe God our saviour, and that we patiently await his retribution.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Remember.* [n: (ID.)] Of former sins and of those which are daily done, he prays through the mercy shewn to the ancients, and because he fears a true judge.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of thy tender mercies.* ⟦s2⟧ Compassion is properly in the shewing forth of good things.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Which are from of old.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, they are from the beginning of the world: therefore let him not fail in me.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0878A]VERS. 7.-- *Sins.* [n: (CASS.)] They are when the way of equity is forsaken.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of my youth.* ⟦s2⟧ Not for age, but for the rashness of headlong haste, which is then, when the heat of the mind exceeds moderation.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Ignorances.* ⟦s2⟧ We do those things which we do not know to be evil. ⟦s3⟧ Or the sins of ignorance in little ones, who are held at any rate by original sin.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] Of my youth. ⟦s2⟧ Before I went out of the house of my wicked father.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Remember me, thou.* [n: (AUG.)] Whose work it is to have mercy and to forgive sins.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *According to thy mercy.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, thy mercy is ample. ⟦s3⟧ Mercy is toward the wretched, goodness toward any whatsoever. ⟦s4⟧ But God does good both when [0878B] implored by the wretched, and when led by his goodness: none is good but God.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Sweet.* [n: (ID.)] Because he forgives all former things, where grace is without merits.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Right.* ⟦s2⟧ Because after mercy, etc., *as far as Sweet,* because he gives sweet things; *right,* because he corrects.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Therefore.* [n: (CASS.)] Because he is sweet and right; not out of cruelty, but out of kindness.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the way.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, faith, or the precepts of God. ⟦s3⟧ Or in the way of this life, where one must live rightly, he will give a law.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *In judgment.* ⟦s2⟧ While he punishes, he will direct to better things. ⟦s3⟧ But let them be meek who are corrected: for from wild beasts they are tamed, that they may bear it. ⟦s4⟧ For if they are mild, that is, of a gentle mind, as though having this by nature: he will teach them the precepts for the neglect of which came [0878C] the scourges.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Mercy and truth.* [n: (AUG.)] But what ways he will teach, etc., *as far as* and then he guards against the severity of the judge.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Testament.* [n: (CASS.)] The New, which is excellently called a Testament, where there is a clear and certain promise of an inheritance, which is confirmed by death also.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And the Testament.* ⟦s2⟧ Of the old law, where the Prophets were witnesses of the promises.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *For thy name's sake, O Lord.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, thy glory, etc., *as far as* and they are interpreted *the clean,* who assert themselves clean of all sin.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Who is the man?* [n: (CASS.)] Here he treats of things eternal; how they are had, labouring and praying, that both [0878D] he himself, and all Israel, may be freed from these evils.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Soul.* [n: (ID.)] and if not at once, etc., *as far as* whatever others may do, etc.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Seed.* [n: (Hier.)] It is good, whence fruit is gathered.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Is a firmament.* [n: (AUG.)] Fear seems to belong to the weak, but the Lord is a firmament to them that fear him, and he brings it about that his Testament be made manifest to them.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Eyes.* ⟦s2⟧ Sal. ⟦s3⟧ *The eyes of a wise man are in his head, that is, in his heart.*

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Only.* [n: (ID.)] An only one is the more loved, a poor man the more pitied.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *From necessities.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, from adversities, [0879A] which are necessary for all who wish to live godly in Christ.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Behold.* [n: (AUG.)] The perdition of so many men cannot be without grief, and therefore he prays for them.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *The innocent and the upright have adhered.* [n: (ID.)] The evil were borne with; they did not adhere.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Israel.* [n: (AUG.)] The people, whom thou hast prepared for the vision of thee.

## PSALM XXV.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm.* [n: (CASS.)] This psalm admonishes that the fellowships of the wicked be shunned, and that we be joined to the just, because from living together manners are formed.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Judge.* [n: (AUG.)] Because after mercy, etc., *as far as* they are carried off by the wind of pride.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0879B] [n: (CASS.)] *Judge:* it is not the presumption of a just man, but the certainty of God's mercy, which he shews there in hoping in the Lord; then, I shall not be weakened.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In innocence.* [n: (AUG.)] Not that of angels, but such as is of men.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Prove me.* [n: (CASS.)] That is, first look upon my strength, etc., *as far as* I regard not merits, but the mercy which has made me such.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS.)] *Prove me.* ⟦s2⟧ But do thou prove me in temptations, that no sin remain, etc., *as far as* lest any man murmur about them.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Burn my reins.* [n: (ID.)] With the fire of thy word, the *heart* with the heat of the spirit. ⟦s2⟧ *Heart* for thoughts, *reins* for delights.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Mercy.* [n: (CASS.)] Which suffers not [0879C] that we be tempted above our strength; or that very burning is mercy, not harshness

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I have been well pleased in truth.* [n: (AUG.)] He to whom his own lie is displeasing, and who himself is pleasing along with her and in her.

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *I have not sat.* [n: (CASS.)] *I have been well pleased* in truth, because I have not sat. ⟦s2⟧ *I have hated,* which is to shun evils. ⟦s3⟧ *And I will wash,* which is to do right things.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The vain.* [n: (CASS.)] *The vain* are they who labour about perishable things; the wicked are heretics, who pervert the Scriptures: those seek superfluous things, these poisonous.

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Of the malignant.* [n: (ID.)] Who do evils to their neighbours.

⟦p51⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *I will wash.* [n: (AUG.)] By doing penance. ⟦s2⟧ *And the altar,* I will adorn my mind with virtues, or faith, because there is no place of true sacrifice outside the catholic Church.

⟦p52⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0879D] *Among the innocent.* [n: (AUG.)] Conversing; *that I may hear* from them, that is, that I may learn with what voice thou art to be praised; or, *that I may hear:* Come, ye blessed of my Father.

⟦p53⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *The voice of praise* of the Holy Ghost, who teaches to praise thee.

⟦p54⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] He asks that he be not mingled with the wicked in the judgment, because it is not with them, but with the house of God, that he agrees.

⟦p55⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I have loved the beauty.* [n: (CASS.)] These things therefore will I do, because I have loved to make for thee a comely house, and a place wherein thou mayest dwell in glory, that I may take in something of the glory and the essence of God.

⟦p56⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Destroy not.* [n: (CASS.)] And if for the present thou sufferest us to be together, destroy us not together.

⟦p57⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0880A] [n: (CASS.)] Destroy me not in time to come *with the wicked,* with whom here I have no fellowship.

⟦p58⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Their right hand,* [n: (AUG.)], that is, if they do any good, etc., *as far as* let mercy accompany in all things, which thou owest, because:

⟦p59⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Foot,* that is, affection, which is wont easily to slip, *hath stood* unmoved against all scandals, stretching beyond, and persisting *in the right way.* ⟦s2⟧ And because thou so redeemest in the churches, I will bless.

⟦p60⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *It hath stood.* [n: (CASS.)] Even though shaken on every side, or confounded by scandals.
