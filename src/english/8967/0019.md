## PSALM XXVI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* [var: Vulg. Ps 26:1 heads this psalm *Psalmus David, priusquam liniretur*, "before he was anointed"] [n: (AUG., CASS.)] First, David chosen, etc., *as far as* whence groaning and labouring in the sacrament; that he may rejoice in the reality.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0880B] *The Lord is my light,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Of the first anointing, through which the second is made sure, he speaks here.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The Lord is the protector of my life.* [n: (AUG.)] He gives the life of the virtues, which he also protects, without which all his gifts are lost.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of whom shall I be afraid?* [n: (ID.)] Whom should the flesh fear, when it shall have put on corruption [cj: *corruptionem*; read *incorruptionem*, "incorruption"].

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The wicked upon me.* [n: (ID.)] That, insulting, they may prefer themselves to me: which God turns to the better, so that with their cursing tooth they consume not me, but my carnal desires. ⟦s2⟧ Or *let them eat,* that is, let the cruelty of my enemies take its delight in my death, *the flesh;* the soul they cannot.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They were weakened.* [n: (ID.)] While Christ took flesh, they were made to waver, and afterwards submitted themselves wholly to faith.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0880C] [n: (CASS.)] Or, being weakened little by little they came to the ruin of the soul.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Camps.* [n: (CASS.)] Efforts, battle, conflicts.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In this will I hope.* [n: (AUG.)] I have hope of victory in the Lord of the eternal reward.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *One thing I have asked.* [n: (AUG.)] This one house is eternal, in which one may abide always, where the days are eternal; why there? ⟦s1⟧ That I may contemplate the delight of God, which is the true good.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *All the days of my life.* [n: (AUG.)] That as long as I live, etc., *as far as* I will make manifest the work.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *That I may see.* [n: (ID.)] Another reading: *That I may contemplate the delight of the Lord,* that is, to this end, that at last he may appear, [0880D] etc., *as far as* and therefore not rashly do I ask *one thing,* though a sinner.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *In secret*: [n: (AUG.)] as it were there are many parts, etc., *as far as* him whom presently he calls a rock.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *I have gone round.* [n: (AUG.)] Because he offered himself to me as a leader, etc., *as far as* that I might fulfil all his wills.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] Or I have looked round upon all created things, or the examples of the saints.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] I have gone round in mind all created things, etc., *as far as* the more ardently it desires.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Hear, O Lord, my voice,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] What labour, what groaning? ⟦s2⟧ The prayer before the second [0881A] anointing, that he may be able to have that which he also promises himself by the spirit of prophecy.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *To thee hath said.* [n: (CASS.)] *The heart,* the reason taught by thee, converses with thee of the one thing, that it may handle nothing other than what thou teachest.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Turn not away.* [n: (AUG.)] Another asks here for temporal goods from God, and rejoices in what is given; this man asks nothing but *one thing.* ⟦s2⟧ Another, having them, fears him angered, lest he take them away; this man thinks there is no anger unless he *turn away his countenance,* even if he punish something in him; nothing more divine could he have said.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Turn not aside.* [n: (AUG.)] He does not turn aside in anger, when he turns his face from sins; or, *turn not aside,* so as to let me slip in anger, that is, in what thou mayest punish; but that our weakness may be made more wary.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0881B] *My saviour,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] As though: in vain hast thou created and re-created, etc., *as far as* he was chosen by the Lord through Samuel.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *For my father,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He made himself a little one to God, and made him his own *father:* because he commands, calls, founded and rules. ⟦s3⟧ *A mother,* because he cherishes, contains, suckles and nourishes; but the world is a father, or the devil, the mother Babylon, that is, the fellowship of the wicked.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Set me a law,* etc.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Of them that trouble me.* [n: (CASS.)] As of Saul and his helpers, etc., *as far as* because they are false.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *I believe to see.* [n: (AUG.)] But among all perils, God directing, I believe to see.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Good things.* [n: (ID.)] Good things which are true, eternal. ⟦s2⟧ Those men [0881C] persuade to a blessedness in this earth, which is the earth of the dying.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Expect.* [n: (CASS.)] As I am sure of it, etc., *as far as* endure what thou sufferest.

## PSALM XXVII.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ *A psalm to this one.* [var: Vulg. Ps 27:1 heads it *Psalmus ipsi David*] By David is sometimes understood the head, that is, Christ; sometimes the body, that is, the Church; sometimes both together, that is, the whole Christ is understood, as here.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS.)] The third psalm, which treats briefly of the passion and the resurrection.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Here Christ is set forth (and though he is so worthy) as not trusting in himself, but asking the whole from God; a useful example to the just.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *To thee, O Lord.* [n: (ID.)] A prayer had in the time [0881D] of the passion.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Be not silent.* ⟦s2⟧ Less is in the Hebrew, etc., *as far as* and the raising up from death.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Be not silent,* etc., *and I shall be likened,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] From this, that the Word is always united to me, I am not such a man as the rest, who are born into the deep misery of the world.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] Where, as it were, if thou be silent, thy word is not known.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *To the temple.* [n: (CASS.)] According to the custom of the Jews, to whom it was commanded that wheresoever they were they should pray toward that quarter where they knew Jerusalem to be: which Jesus also did, fulfilling the law. ⟦s2⟧ Or toward heaven, whence we hope for help.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0882A]VERS. 4.-- *Give to them.* [n: ( Ibid. )] It is to foretell, not to wish, according to what they intended, not according to what came of it: or he wishes the justice of God to be preserved in this.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Because not,* etc. [n: (ID.)] And if works were wanting, this would suffice for damnation, that they understood not through the law and the Prophets the things which God invisibly works through me, nor even the visible things.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thou shalt destroy.* [n: (AUG.)] That nothing hurt me, nor afterwards thy Church, or mine.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Blessed.* [n: (CASS.)] Having been heard, in the resurrection he gives thanks, adding of the salvation of them that believe.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The Lord.* [n: (ID.)] The same who is my helper, and my protector, is the strength of the people which trusts in him, and [0882B] a protector that they be not hurt, and thou, O Lord:

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Save.* ⟦s2⟧ Behold how great is the hope of them that believe, when he prays for them: he who suffered for them is judge and advocate.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And rule them,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Lest they turn aside to crooked things.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Lift up,* etc., from earthly things to the seeking of things eternal, and by the understanding of the Scriptures, and by good work, through this whole world unto the end.

## PSALM XXVIII.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of David in the consummation.* [n: (ID.)] The ark brought back from the Philistines, etc., *as far as* that they may offer themselves to such a consummator.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Bring to the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] First he admonishes that sacrifices be offered.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0882C] *The sons of rams.* [n: (AUG.)] Because assault is not wanting, etc., *as far as* and the seventy-two disciples sent to preach are signified.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The voice of the Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] The preaching of Christ terrified minds within, which is, that it gave the spirit of fear to the Jews and to the Gentiles.

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (GREG.)] This man ascends from the lowest, that is, from the spirit of fear to the spirit of wisdom, etc., *as far as* that is, spiritual understanding: these the Lord shakes.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] As a husbandman a barren land, etc., *as far as* and he will give strength, and will bless the same in eternal peace.

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The voice of the Lord upon the waters,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Of the establishing of the Church, where he enumerates the powers of the Holy Ghost [0882D] with great proclamation.

⟦p51⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of Libanus.* ⟦s2⟧ Libanus is interpreted a whitening. ⟦s3⟧ *The cedars of Libanus,* that is, high men lifted up in earthly things, whom God by breaking will crush small, that is, will bring them back from pride to himself through his beloved Son: and this he will crush by the spirit of fortitude, as a unicorn taken is crushed.

⟦p52⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *The desert.* [n: (CASS., AUG.)] The Jewish people, etc., *as far as* whence flows the living water.

⟦p53⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *The Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ He shews what is done for the Church now established.

⟦p54⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ *I have given,* he says, *you power to tread down upon serpents.* [var: Vulg. Lc 10:19 has *calcandi supra serpentes*, "to tread upon serpents"] But as it is unheard of that stags should strike every kind of wild beast with the horn; so that the Apostles, unlettered, should refute all philosophy.

⟦p55⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0883A] *A flood.* [n: (CASS.)] The waters of baptism, which purges the crimes of the world.
