## PSALM XXIX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of a canticle, at the dedic.* [n: (AUG.)] Above, concerning the finishing of the tabernacle in a time of war: but this is a psalm of gladness, because the house is dedicated, that is, made firm, which shall abide in eternal peace: the body being made new, not only Christ's but also the whole Church's. ⟦s2⟧ The whole Christ speaks in this psalm, because some things belong to the head, some to the body, some to both.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] The psalm is briefly of the passion and the resurrection: that the passion may not terrify those whom the hope of the resurrection gladdens.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The house of David.* [n: (CASS.)] The whole Christ is the bridegroom, etc., [0883B] *as far as* the second state, the state to come, when there is rest in it.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS.)] First he treats of his own dedication: and bids the saints who hope for the same to utter praises.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *O Lord my God, I have cried to thee,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Christ, etc., *as far as* but not in the dedication.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Into the pit.* [n: (HIER.)] That is, out of the pit of hell, where the souls of sinners are, lest, like them, I should fall headlong into death.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Confess ye,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Because out of his own goodness and holiness, not ours, he was mindful of us.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *For wrath.* [n: (ID.)] That is, vengeance, by which he avenged upon us the first sin, which by death you have [0883C] loosed.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *At evening.* [n: (ID.)] Because wrath, etc., *as far as* that is, in the time of grace after the time before the law and under the law.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] *At evening.* ⟦s2⟧ Because, when the Lord had suffered and been buried, the Apostles and the women continued in weeping and groaning. ⟦s3⟧ *And in the morning gladness.* ⟦s4⟧ Because coming to the sepulchre in the morning they received from the Angels the glory of the resurrection. ⟦s5⟧ *Weeping.* [n: (CASS.)] It shall abide for men who look toward the evening, at which Adam turned aside and went out from paradise: but there shall be gladness for those who look toward the morning, at which Christ rose again.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 7.-- *But I.* How he acted before the dedication and deserved it. [n: (AUG., CASS.)] He acts at evening. ⟦s3⟧ But I, while abundance was mine in paradise, [0883D] said: I shall not be moved from this good, believing the devil who said: *Thou shalt not die the death [n: (Gen. III)]* [var: Vulg. Gn 3:4 has the plural and a stronger negative, "ye shall by no means die the death"]. ⟦s4⟧ But, O Lord, in *thy will thou hast given strength to my beauty;* which is, I was good and strong not of myself, but of thee. ⟦s5⟧ And that thou mightest show it: *Thou hast turned away thy face,* that is, thou hast sent me forth out of paradise: but now, set here, *I will cry to thee,* etc.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Thou hast turned away.* [n: (CASS.)] God turned away his face from Christ, by exposing him to the passion and to death; and thus Christ was troubled: *My soul is sorrowful even unto death. [n: (Matth. XVI.)] Troubled.* [n: (AUG.)] Calling to mind that time of trouble and of misery, and set in it, I hear the voice of the head who is to die for me, saying: *To thee, O Lord, will I cry.*

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0884A]VERS. 10.-- *What profit is there in my blood.* [n: (ID.)] He shows the necessity by which he does not suffer him to go down into corruption. ⟦s2⟧ *Whilst I go down into corruption.* ⟦s3⟧ For if I do not rise again at once, and the body is corrupted, I shall announce to no one, I shall gain no one. ⟦s4⟧ Confession is twofold, let us never be without it: when it is ill with us in tribulations, let us confess our sins; when it is well, let us confess praise to God in the exultation of justice.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *The Lord hath heard.* [n: (CASS.)] He treats of how he received it, namely the dedication. ⟦s2⟧ He said: I will cry, I will beseech, but the Lord heard me beseeching.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Thou hast cut my sackcloth.* [n: (ID.)] Sackcloth is a rough garment, granted for the use of labour or in a time of [0884B] sadness, as to the Ninivites; that is, human flesh, which was rent by dying: and there came forth the corn of the glorious resurrection, or of the godhead. ⟦s2⟧ Or else, the penalty of sin, which being destroyed in the resurrection, the godhead before hidden was brought forth: just as what the sack conceals whole, it opens when rent. [n: (AUG.)] Sackcloth of goats or of kids is mortality, which Christ drew or took from sinners; whence: *Whilst they were troublesome to me, I was clothed with haircloth [n: (Psal. XXXIV)]*, that they might not see what was within.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *I shall not be pricked.* [n: (ID.)] I shall not now die unto the end. ⟦s2⟧ *O Lord God, I will confess to thee for ever.* ⟦s3⟧ What is: *I will confess to thee for ever?* ⟦s3⟧ For ever I will praise thee.
