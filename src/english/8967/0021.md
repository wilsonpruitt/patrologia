## PSALM XXX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0884C]VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, a psalm.* [n: (AUG.)] Ecstasy is here taken as the dread of a people troubled by the persecution of all the nations and by the failing of faith throughout the world.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS.)] Against dread, hope and prayer are set first, etc., *as far as: They shall be two in one flesh [n: (Gen. II)]*, therefore in one voice also. [n: (AUG.)] The Redeemer speaks first, then the redeemed, giving thanks. ⟦s2⟧ Then, at length concerning the disturbance, the voice of the Prophet is put in near the end, and at the end. ⟦s3⟧ Here is described the humbling of Christ and of the martyrs: whence follows the exaltation.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *In thee, O Lord, have I hoped,* etc. [n: (ID.)] There is an eternal confusion, from which he asks to be delivered: and there is a temporal one, for an hour, namely the kind that profits.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Make haste.* [n: (ID.)] In the things to be done, in the [0884D] souls to be loosed and the like, that thou mayest rescue me on the third day and not defer it to the common resurrection; and that this may be, be thou to me a protector as great as it befits God to be. ⟦s2⟧ *Be thou to me.* ⟦s3⟧ He said: Give justice, etc., *as far as* he affirms the deliverance.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Thou wilt bring out.* [n: (CASS.)] From death to the things above, so that the counsels of the Jews may be in vain. ⟦s2⟧ *Out of the snare which they have hidden.* [n: (AUG.)] The devil tempts by two gates, etc., *as far as* whence: *Thou wilt bring me out of the snare.*

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Into thy hands.* [n: (CASS.)] Note that he speaks here who uttered these same words in the passion. [n: (AUG.)] *Into thy hands.* ⟦s2⟧ From these words which he said on the cross, he gives notice that he has spoken in this psalm.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *But I, in the Lord,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Of [0885A] joy, because he hopes in the Lord, who hates the vain. ⟦s2⟧ *I will exult and be glad.* ⟦s3⟧ To exult is to rejoice with great eagerness of soul: to be glad, with a tempered affection. ⟦s4⟧ *My humility.* ⟦s5⟧ He takes it for weakness, and for the very virtue of humility. ⟦s6⟧ *From necessities.* [n: (AUG.)] *Necessities* are unbounded, etc., *as far as* and fully in the world to come.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9, 10.-- *In a spacious place.* [n: (CASS.)] The spacious place of going freely to the fatherland of the virtues is charity: but because the flesh is weak and evils press: *Have mercy on me, O Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ Now the dread is recounted, that is, how many and how great are the things that disturb, against which is the prayer, *I am troubled.* [n: (AUG.)] Since he said before: *Thou hast set my feet in a spacious place,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ But for some the place is spacious, out of the thanksgiving of deliverance; for others, out of tribulation, it is a narrow strait. ⟦s4⟧ *Troubled.* ⟦s5⟧ He is angry [0885B] for the sins of the chaff, etc., *as far as* for the life of the perfect is the advancement of others. ⟦s6⟧ *In anger the eye.* ⟦s7⟧ In the anger of the persecutors, etc., *as far as* that God may be sought from every degree. [n: (CASS.)] Or the understanding is disturbed and the mind confounded, etc., *as far as* neither in mind nor in body can there be any presumption of man. [n: (AUG.)] Anger through vice blinds the eye of the mind; anger through zeal troubles it: because this too, the mind being shaken, scatters contemplation, which is not except with an unquiet mind; but thereafter, after a little, it profits the more toward seeing.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *In poverty.* [n: (ID.)] Or *in want.* ⟦s2⟧ I lack health of body, and the torments are not spared. ⟦s3⟧ I lack release, and death is spared; and in this my confidence has been weakened.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0885C]VERS. 12.-- *Above all my enemies.* [n: (ID.)] They live worse than the pagans and the Jews, etc., *as far as* for whom even their acquaintance are afraid, lest all be such. [n: (CASS.)] Or, among all my enemies, or, held in greater reproach than those who are worthy of it; and *to my neighbours,* to kinsfolk, or to those about to believe; and *to my acquaintance,* to those of the household.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *They that saw me without.* [n: (HIER.)] *Without,* Christ, that is, those who thought him a mere man. ⟦s2⟧ *I am forgotten as one dead from the heart.* [n: (CASS.)] Or, I have fallen out of their heart, like a dead man whose memory is buried with his body, of whose restoration there is despair. ⟦s3⟧ *A vessel that is destroyed,* Jesus dead is thought fit to be cast away.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *The blame of many.* [n: (AUG.)] [0885D] They are blamed, etc., *as far as* not any man. ⟦s2⟧ *Round about. ⟦s3⟧ In that while they assembled together.* [n: (CASS.)] Of errors, etc., *as far as* that is, by the appetite of temporal things.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *My lots.* [n: (ID.)] Or, the times of life. [n: (AUG.)] Because we live or die when thou wilt: therefore *deliver.* ⟦s2⟧ Although the order of election is with thee, just and hidden, yet I, etc., *as far as* which, without merits, chooses whom it will.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Illumine.* [n: (CASS.)] Make thy good will manifest upon me, that as they despair of the dying, so they may have confidence in the rising. [n: (AUG.)] *Illumine.* ⟦s2⟧ Let it appear who belongs to thee, since now there is confusion; whence elsewhere: *Distinguish my cause [n: (Psal. XLI)]*; yet [0886A] lest he seem to justify himself, he adds: *Save me in mercy,* not in my own justice.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *I have called upon.* [n: (ID.)] Thou callest upon God when thou callest him into thyself, and when thou invitest him into the house of thy heart.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Let them be made dumb.* [n: (CASS.)] Which here are talkative, while they do not fear the judgment. ⟦s2⟧ Dumb in punishments, in hell.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Which speak.* ⟦s2⟧ He fitly sets iniquity over against the just man.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In pride and in abuse.* [n: (CASS.)] Because the creature is against the Creator, and by a perverse use of the benefits of God, who, besides all else, came for their sake.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *How great.* [n: (CASS.)] Which are the goods of God against the evils aforesaid, and they lighten those very evils. [n: (AUG.)] Seeing these things and marvelling, the Prophet cries out, etc., *as far as* but in the world to come it will lie open.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0886B]--VERS. 21. ⟦s2⟧ *From the disturbance.* [n: (AUG.)] That he may now suffer no human disturbance, even those who are now in tribulation, entering with the mind to the face of God, are not troubled. ⟦s3⟧ *Thou shalt protect.* [n: (CASS.)] Lest the promise only of what is to come should trouble them, he adds this: *thou shalt protect,* that they be not harmed.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Blessed.* [n: (ID.)] As if to say, what more? ⟦s2⟧ He concludes the fullness of the benefit with praise, comparing it to his own feebleness. ⟦s3⟧ And it is the Prophet that speaks, or the faithful people. ⟦s4⟧ *He hath made wonderful.* [n: (AUG.)] The Spirit being sent, he filled the whole world, etc., *as far as* whence: *Thy name is ointment poured out.* [var: Vulg. Ct 1:2 has oil, not ointment, and no verb: "oil poured out is thy name"] [n: (CASS.)] Or else, God the Father made his mercy wonderful, etc., *as far as* according to that saying: *He rested in the heart of the earth.*

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *I said in my excess.* [n: (AUG.)] A figure of our fear, etc., *as far as: Therefore thou hast heard the voice of my [0886C] prayer.* [n: (AUG.)] *In the excess of my mind.* ⟦s2⟧ Either, his mind being caught up, he saw a height from which he feels himself cast very far off: or, he went out from reason by fearing. ⟦s3⟧ *From the face of thy eyes.* ⟦s4⟧ If I were in thy face, etc., *as far as* but I accused my own heart.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *O love the Lord, all ye,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] An oratorical or hortatory conclusion, for the obtaining of the things aforesaid. [n: (AUG.)] Having experienced the things aforesaid, he now exhorts us; as if to say: I was in tribulation, I feared, I cried out, I was not confounded: so you also. ⟦s2⟧ *His saints,* whose part it is to love God, because they do not love the world, nor the things that are in the world; the friend of this world is set down as the enemy of God. ⟦s3⟧ Learn therefore not to love the world, that thou mayest learn to love God: turn away, that thou mayest be converted; pour out, that thou mayest be filled. ⟦s4⟧ *The Lord will require,* etc. ⟦s5⟧ Even here sometimes, [0886D] if he did this to none, the divine providence would not seem to be watchful, if to all, the divine patience would not be preserved.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Abundantly to them that do.* ⟦s2⟧ Those who do not quickly repent.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Pride.* ⟦s2⟧ For every vice, because it is the root of all.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Do ye manfully,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The force of the whole psalm, that the frailty of the flesh may not fear so many evils of the world.
