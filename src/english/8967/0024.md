## PSALM XXXIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of David.* [var: Vulg. Ps 33:1 heads the psalm with the dative alone, "to David," and names no psalm] [n: (AUG.)] Christ speaks, and in him the Christian: he teaches humility, while he commends the body and the blood. ⟦s2⟧ *When he changed the countenance* — Christ, that is, the sacrifice — *before* the Jews, who are the kingdom of his own father David, according to that saying: *And the Lord shall give unto him the seat of David,* the Church is the kingdom of the Father.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0890D]VERS. 2.-- *I will bless.* [n: (CASS.)] The second psalm through an imperfect alphabet, etc., *as far as* he is glorified together with him.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *In the Lord shall my soul be praised.* [n: (ID.)] For his own praise is the preaching of his Lord, as a good servant glories in the good repute of his lord. ⟦s2⟧ *The meek.* ⟦s3⟧ Not the learned, not those who fast, because they are proud: but the meek, because they have temperance in all things. ⟦s4⟧ The meek, not being proud of their own understanding; not bearing the yoke of God after the manner of the untamed.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4-6.-- *O magnify the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ Spiritual grace does not wish to do alone what profits many; whence it calls others: *Magnify,* that it might draw those invited, [0891A] it tells what goods came to it thereby: *I sought the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ Out of all.* ⟦s4⟧ It cannot be told piecemeal how great things he obtained who sought nothing other than God. ⟦s5⟧ *Come ye.* [n: (CASS.)] Now piecemeal he admonishes the saints concerning the new countenance. ⟦s6⟧ As if to say, *Magnify:* and if among the first, come ye into what is pleasant. ⟦s7⟧ Or, *Magnify,* and in what order? behold: *Come ye* by faith. [n: (AUG.)] Come ye by cleaving with faith, and by running with the two feet of a twin charity. [n: (CASS.)] The praises being set first, he exhorts to communion. ⟦s8⟧ *Let them not be confounded.* [n: (AUG.)] Whatever happens here to the just man, he does not blush, because he is always the higher with God: but the proud man blushes in adversities, because he has not drawn near to God.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *The poor man.* [n: (CASS.)] *This one* is here the spiritual man, because he draws near and is enlightened, and shall not blush; because, [0891B] when he cries out, he is freed from all the straits of the world at his death, when he now passes over to God.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *He shall send in an Angel. ⟦s2⟧ Come ye,* [var: Vulg. Ps 33:8 has the nominative and no such object: "the angel of the Lord shall encamp round about them that fear him"] and as this poor man cried out and was heard, in the same way *he shall send in an Angel,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] *He shall send in an Angel.* ⟦s3⟧ Other interpreters have rendered it thus: *The Angel of the Lord surroundeth in a ring them that fear him.* ⟦s4⟧ An Angel from heaven, or a man, who is a minister of the will of God. ⟦s5⟧ Faulty codices have it. ⟦s6⟧ *The Lord shall send in an Angel.*

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Taste and see.* ⟦s2⟧ Here openly of the sacrament which he bore in his hands. ⟦s3⟧ *Taste* the flesh and blood of Christ. ⟦s4⟧ *The Lord is sweet.* [n: (ID.)] And delightful: because he is life to him that takes him, driving away all troubles in the world to come, that is: Hope through this for eternal life. [n: (CASS.)] And because God is life, the flesh which he [0891C] united to himself is life-giving.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *All ye his saints.* [n: (CASS.)] No one, however well deserving, shall leave off from this, let him not presume upon security. ⟦s2⟧ *For.* [n: (AUG.)] The Lord was feeding him that despised him, and he will forsake him that fears him. ⟦s3⟧ But he promises not superfluous things, but necessary ones. ⟦s4⟧ *There is no want.* ⟦s5⟧ Because even in the present they have as much as nature requires.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *The rich have wanted and have suffered hunger.* [n: (CASS.)] Because they have not faith. ⟦s2⟧ *They have suffered hunger,* because they are not filled with the body of the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ For what have they who have not God? ⟦s4⟧ Or, *the rich have wanted,* that is, they shall lack the highest good, although they hunger, that is, desire. ⟦s5⟧ But those who seek with their whole zeal shall have without diminution the good which is every good.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0891D]VERS. 12.-- *Come, children, hear me.* ⟦s2⟧ Diapsalmus. ⟦s3⟧ The sacraments of the Church having been treated, he admonishes those who are outside, who take the first rudiments of faith, lest the name of fear be shuddered at, he adds: *I will teach you,* the sons, the fear of the Lord, as a useful thing.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS.)] Now he warns beginners also from what things they should abstain.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *To see good days.* ⟦s2⟧ Because all confess that they wish it.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Keep.* [n: (ID.)] Note that he says the tongue is to be restrained first, since, when we wish to speak, we first move the lips, which the motions of the tongue soon follow. ⟦s2⟧ *Keep* thou who desirest life. ⟦s3⟧ Behold how they ought to fear.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0892A]VERS. 15.-- *Turn away from evil.* [n: (CASS.)] This avoids fault, etc., *as far as* otherwise he will not be received in the world to come.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *The eyes of the Lord,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Of the retributions of the good and of the evil, lest in dangers the just man grow soft with any doubt.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The eyes of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ Nor shouldst thou distrust out of thy weakness, because *the eyes.*

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Unto the prayers.* [n: (ID.)] The swiftness of hearing, since the hearer dwells in them.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *The memory.* [n: (AUG.)] That not even the mention, etc., *as far as* which they have committed.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *The Lord is nigh.* ⟦s2⟧ He delivered: for he is inclined to this, because *he is nigh;* or, there is an allusion to the crying out.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Many are the tribulations of the just.* ⟦s2⟧ As [0892B] also in the New Testament, *he is nigh, and he will save,* which is necessary, because *many are the tribulations.* [n: (CASS.)] *Many;* from the devil, from envious men, and from oneself. ⟦s3⟧ *He will deliver.* [n: (AUG.)] Not bodily, but by giving them patience: or, he will deliver spiritually.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *The bones.* [n: (ID.)] The virtues. ⟦s2⟧ *And one,* etc., that not even patience may perish.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Most evil.* [n: (ID.)] Not merely evil, although it end amid riches and perfumes and the lamentations of men, yet it is most evil, because in sins it drags down to hell.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *That hate.* ⟦s2⟧ Whose? ⟦s3⟧ Not of all, because they are sinners; but of those who hate the just one, who justifies, that is, Christ, through whom he that is not reconciled perishes: but *he will redeem the souls,* in respect of which the death is [0892C] most evil.
