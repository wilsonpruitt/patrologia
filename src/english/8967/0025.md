## PSALM XXXIV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Judge, O Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] He begins from a prayer for both sides of the battle, with a recalling of the fault, and there was for each one what he deserves, showing how much he bore from his adversaries, and the punishments that are repaid to them for their iniquity.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Arms and shield* [n: (HIER.)] With arms he strikes the enemies, with the shield he protects us. ⟦s2⟧ The shield he calls elsewhere: *of good will;* the arms are we ourselves, because as we are armed by him, so he himself is armed from us. [n: (AUG.)] Or, the soul of Christ, which vanquished the enemies, which does nothing unless God fight on its behalf.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Take hold of arms.* ⟦s2⟧ Make me to have arms, teaching, miracles. ⟦s3⟧ *Shield.* ⟦s4⟧ Steadfastness.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0892D] *Arise.* ⟦s2⟧ Make me to use the things received.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Pour out.* ⟦s2⟧ Me, who am now in obscurity, make clear so evidently that thou mayest shut them in and convict them, that they may not have what to bark at: and speak to my soul in such wise that all may know that salvation is hers; against this, which they say: *There is no salvation for him in his God [n: (Psal.III.)] The sword.* [n: (AUG.)] The sword is the soul of the just man. ⟦s3⟧ When the just abound, the sword is poured out, while life flashes forth: whence the enemy is shut in, having nothing to speak ill of us. [n: (ID.)] It is a prophecy, not a wish, or this concerns those who are to be converted.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS 5.-- *Let them be.* [n: (ID.)] A prophecy: or in that manner in which God does it, by just judgment, not with a bitter mind. ⟦s2⟧ *As dust before the face of the wind.* [n: (CASS.)] Loosed [0893A] by God, so that they do not even cleave to one another, they whom divine grace does not moisten.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Let the snare which he knoweth not come upon him.* [n: (CASS.)] Or in a good sense, the *snare* of truth, which the sinner knows not. ⟦s2⟧ *And the net which he hath hidden.* ⟦s3⟧ That is, the hidden death, namely of Christ. ⟦s4+5⟧ *Let it lay hold on them.* [var: Vulg. *apprehendat eum*, "lay hold on him," singular where Migne prints the plural (Ps 34:8)] Namely, that they be not left to their own crimes. ⟦s6⟧ *And let him fall into the snare.* ⟦s7⟧ A good one, by which they may be restrained from evils. ⟦s8⟧ *Into that very one.* ⟦s9⟧ That is, out of that very death. ⟦s10⟧ *Into that very one.* [n: (AUG.)] Not into another, but into the one which they stretched out. ⟦s11⟧ For there is no evil man who does not first hurt himself.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *But my soul shall rejoice.* [n: (CAS.)] Having set forth the hope of good and the joy, he relates how malignantly it acts toward the kind one. ⟦s2⟧ *In the Lord.* [n: (AUG.)] Than whom nothing is sweeter: nor has he failed, who made all things: seek him therefore, [0893B] and in him thou shalt have all things.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *All my bones shall say.* ⟦s2⟧ Carnal men have not the word of praise and confession, because things weak before thee are utterly silent; but strong things praise thee as great and tremble. [n: (ID.)] *All the bones.* ⟦s3⟧ All the strength of the mind, because another cannot say this, namely: O Lord, who? etc., as if none, because he alone delivered: *The helpless out of the hand.* [n: (CASS., AUG.)] The human race is *helpless,* because mortal; *needy,* because it seeks bread in sweat; *poor,* because, cast down from pure wisdom, it has a thin and shadowy reason.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Unjust witnesses rising up.* [n: (CASS.)] The calamities of man having been told, lest Christ himself should seem a stranger to them, he sets forth his passion. ⟦s2⟧ *Which I knew not.* [n: (AUG.)] They are sins, etc., *as far as* but not [0893C] by committing them.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *They repaid.* [n: (ID.)] When I had brought fruitfulness, they repaid barrenness: I life, they repaid death; I honors, they insults; I medicine, they wounds. ⟦s2⟧ *Barrenness.* ⟦s3⟧ In the fig tree the Lord cursed, where, when he sought fruit, he found none, but leaves: so in the Synagogue, words and not deeds; whence: *Thou that preachest that men should not steal, thou stealest.*

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *With haircloth.* [n: (ID.)] The flesh, a haircloth, on account of the likeness of the flesh of sin. ⟦s2⟧ *My prayer.* [n: (CASS.)] As he said: *Your peace shall return to you [n: (Matth. X)]*; so his prayer, not accepted by the Jews, returned to him. ⟦s3⟧ *In my bosom.* [n: (AUG.)] The bosom is a secret place, in which we are commanded to pray. [0893D] But the secret place of Christ is his divinity, to which his humanity prays.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *As a neighbor and as a brother,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Case for case. ⟦s3⟧ As it were: as concerning our neighbor and brother, so I was well pleased, that is, I rejoiced in the kinship of those who were persecuting me. ⟦s4⟧ Behold grace for hatred, a benefit for injury. [n: (AUG.)] *As a neighbor.* ⟦s5⟧ It befits one drawing near to God to say this, and one rejoicing, who pleases God, as to a brother and a neighbor.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As one mourning.* [n: (CASS.)] Other translators, *as a mother mourning her son, I was bowed down over them. ⟦s2⟧ As one mourning.* ⟦s3⟧ Though I did all these things, yet they would not be converted; and yet, though they would not be converted, so was I humbled, that is, I grieved, as a father mourning a son.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0894A]VERS. 15.-- *And against,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ When I am piously saddened, the Jews impiously rejoice.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *They were scattered.* [n: (CASS.)] Loosed, when the miracles had been seen at the passion, and yet *not,* for their hardness, *pricked to the heart.*

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Or, *scattered* through the whole world: *nor* yet were they *pricked to the heart,* that is, did not repent. ⟦s2⟧ Or, *scattered,* that is, divided: some say that he is good, others not.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They tempted me.* [n: (AUG.)] The things done to the head on the cross are done everywhere to the body. ⟦s2⟧ *They gnashed.* ⟦s3⟧ It is the way of savagery, when reason fails, to gnash with the teeth: and what it wills, it shows in silence. ⟦s4⟧ In prayer he ends these things:

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Lord, when wilt thou look.* [n: (ID.)] When shall we see vengeance upon those who insult us. ⟦s2⟧ *My only one.* [n: (CASS.)] The only one is the soul of Christ, [0894B] which alone is free from sin.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *I will confess to thee.* [n: (AUG.)] Now from the fruit he sends before the confession of the whole Church: thence he prays, with a recalling of the fault, for the confounding of the persecutors and the exultation of the faithful.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Let them not rejoice over me.* [n: (ID.)] He repeats concerning the passion, that he may add concerning their punishment. ⟦s2⟧ *They wink.* [n: (CASS.)] Because by turns they warn one another with silent nods, to deceive and to tempt.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Speaking.* [n: (CASS.)] That is, while they were speaking peaceably. ⟦s2⟧ *In wrath.* ⟦s3⟧ That is, destructively. ⟦s4⟧ For a man plots guile who does not wish to kill: but these were plotting thus, because they wished to slay.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *And they have opened wide.* [n: (ID.)] First winking, then plotting guiles, they opened wide their mouth, crying out death with free [0894C] and open voice: Crucify, crucify.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Thou hast seen, O Lord.* [n: (ID.)] This is said when we wish to call something back to memory: thou hast seen that they are cruel, be not silent, delay not to give sentence.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *Attend.* [n: (ID.)] By restoring the name above every name, that I, undeservedly stretched out. ⟦s2⟧ *In my cause. [n: (Ibid.)]* The punishment of a like crime, but not the cause: for it is not the punishment that makes a martyr, but the cause.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *According to thy justice, O Lord God.* [n: (ID.)] Which is in himself, or which he gave to him. ⟦s2⟧ *Let them not rejoice over me.* [sic: *super gaudeant*] [n: (CASS.)] Let them rather weep than rejoice at having fulfilled evils.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0894D]VERS. 25.-- *We devour.* [n: (AUG.)] Those who are incorporated are devoured.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *Let them blush and stand in awe together.* [n: (CASS.)] Let them rather blush at the glorification of the resurrection, and stand in awe, that is, fear the punishments. ⟦s2⟧ *Let them blush.* ⟦s3⟧ He who blushes condemns his own acts by his own estimation: but he can blush who has not reverence; therefore he adds: *reverence,* that he may mark conversion. ⟦s4⟧ Reverence is the fear of the Lord with love. ⟦s5⟧ *Let them be clothed with confusion and reverence.* ⟦s6⟧ With shame, as with the haircloth of penitence and a mourning garment. ⟦s7⟧ *Shame,* against the boldness which they had in their raging; *reverence,* against the shamelessness of falsehood, that by these they may cure those.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *Let them rejoice and be glad.* [n: (ID.)] In great hope is the end of the faithful, as others are concerning the passion. ⟦s2⟧ *Let them rejoice and be glad.* ⟦s3⟧ Those say, Well done, to their own soul, from which [0895A] gladness flees, these not to their own soul, but they turn their vows to God, and place their gladness in his praises, not in themselves, which gladness is perpetual.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *And my tongue shall meditate thy justice.* [n: (AUG.)] Let this be done, and my preachers out of zeal will announce thee just, who hast fulfilled thy promises, and all things universally in which thou art to be praised. [n: (CASS.)] *My tongue.* ⟦s2⟧ His tongue meditated justice, when it preached the New Testament to the peoples.
