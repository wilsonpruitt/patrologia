## PSALM XXXVI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS.)] The third psalm, an imperfection of the alphabet: wholly concerning the correction of morals.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of David.* [var: Vulg. Ps 36:1 *Psalmus ipsi David.*, "a psalm for David himself"] This psalm is as it were the garment of Christ, which covers his whole body with precepts, and protects it from adversities, and cherishes it as in the bosom of counsel. ⟦s3⟧ It is a draught against those who murmur at the flowering of the wicked and the toil of the good, as though all things were done by chance, so that, spurning God, they cleave to the world. ⟦s4⟧ The Church therefore admonishes that no one imitate the malignant, but that whatever good is hoped for be sought from the Lord, who gives things profitable and [0896C] eternal.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The herbs of the grasses.* [n: (AUG.)] It is now winter, etc., *as far as* because it hath not yet appeared what we shall be.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG., CASS.)] Herbs not of gardens, but of grasses, that is, the meanest, which spring up of themselves through the fields. ⟦s2⟧ *Hay,* the noble, who prosper easily; herbs, the middling: but both those quickly wither and these fall away.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Cease.* [n: (AUG.)] Anger is sudden, fury lasting; but both are to be forsaken, through which men sin. [n: (CASS.)] This is said to him who is still weak, etc., *as far as* by doing it he flourishes for a time.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *And yet a little while,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] After this short life, etc., *as far as* nor did he feel the worm of a gnawing conscience.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0896D]VERS. 12.-- *He shall watch,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] It having been said that the good ought not to emulate the wicked, etc., *as far as* he strives to slay.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *But the Lord,* etc. [n: (ID.)] So let us also, seeing that they are to perish, laugh even at those who are present, it is a great consolation.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *The sword,* etc. [n: (ID.)] If he prevail neither by flatteries nor by threats, he proceeds to punishment. ⟦s2⟧ If he does not prosper so either, he lies in wait with guiles, when by speaking well he intends evil.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Better is,* etc. [n: (ID.)] A little before, concerning malice and endurance: here, concerning riches and poverty.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *But the Lord confirmeth,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Chiefly by the example of his cross. ⟦s2⟧ For the cross of Christ was ended [0897A] in punishment, and abides in glory: for from the places of tortures it has passed to the brows of emperors.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *In the days of famine.* [n: (CASS.)] That is, in this life, etc., *as far as* by how much the more they are lifted up, by so much the more quickly shall they fail like smoke.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *He shall borrow.* [n: (ID.)] He hears the word of God, he does not repay it in works. ⟦s2⟧ *And shall not pay.* ⟦s3⟧ That is, he does not repay in works what he received by hearing. ⟦s4⟧ *He sheweth mercy and shall repay,* etc. ⟦s5⟧ Concerning those things which he receives, etc., *as far as* bounty follows.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *The steps.* [n: (ID.)] Which of themselves are crooked, because we are born in sins. ⟦s2⟧ *His way.* [n: (AUG.)] The way of the earth is not hard, but is full of robbers: or, God shall will the way of man.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *I have been young,* etc. [n: (ID.)] He treats here [0897B] that the just man is never forsaken, mingling the rewards of the good and the punishments of the wicked with exhortations set between. [n: (AUG.)] The Church speaks as one man, which in the first times was younger, at the end of the world grew old, *in a plentiful old age,* because she was spread through all nations. [n: (ID.)] *I have been young.* ⟦s2⟧ A boy in Abel; in the prophets and patriarchs a young man; in the time of the martyrs as it were an old man; at the end of the world as it were in decrepit age. [n: (CASS.)] *Young,* when I received the beginnings of the law in Adam; *I grew old,* when in an honorable age I received Christ at the last.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *All the day he sheweth mercy.* ⟦s2⟧ Or, he speaks of almsgiving, which begins from mercy, and afterward opens the hand: or, of spiritual things.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *He loveth judgment and will not forsake [0897C] his saints.* [n: (CASS.)] Therefore: do thou also love, that thou mayest rejoice in thy good things.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *They shall be supplanted.* [n: (ID.)] To supplant is to spread pits of ambush before the soles of the feet.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *But the Lord* [n: (ID.)] The consolation of the just man. ⟦s2⟧ *In his hands.* [n: (AUG.)] Job's earth was delivered into the hands of the impious, that is, his flesh: but God brings forth his soul unconquered, that it may not yield.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *When he shall be judged.* [n: (CASS.)] Or, he does not permit the just man to be condemned when he is judged by the impious; or, for that impious man, namely to his own harm.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *And he shall exalt.* [n: (ID.)] Because none but one exalted can dwell in heaven. ⟦s2⟧ *Thou shalt see* [n: (ID.)] What thou now believest; in two ways he marks the joys of the good; [0897D] first, because then they shall feel in how great delights they are. ⟦s3⟧ Thence, because they shall see the punishments of the wicked, whence the beatitude of those set free from such evil is the more welcome.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 35.-- *I have seen the wicked highly exalted.* [n: (ID.)] Against this, etc., *as far as* from whom, being bound, the place of harming has been taken away.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *I passed by.* ⟦s2⟧ The world, striving toward God by a better manner of life: and I saw that it could do nothing, since all those things are nothing in which man glories. ⟦s3⟧ *I sought.* ⟦s4⟧ I called back to memory: because the good then grieve the more over the impious, that they are separated from them, when they are changed for the better. ⟦s5⟧ *The place.* ⟦s6⟧ Their world is what shall also be dissolved. ⟦s7⟧ *I passed by.* [n: (AUG.)] He passes by who attends not to the present, [0898A] but to the last things, etc., *as far as* he who has this virtue is set in the place of the blessed.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *The remnants.* ⟦s2⟧ The good things which shall be left after this life, or a good memory after death.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 39.-- *But the salvation of the just is from the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ All these things are for the just, yet the whole is from God, and he gathers it up summarily.
