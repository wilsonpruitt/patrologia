## PSALM XXXIX.

VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, a psalm.* [n: (CASS.)] First he brings in the Church as having awaited the Lord, and as having received [0902A] him, and from him the New Testament, which many also are to follow: whence he strikes at all superstitions and invites to the wonders of God.

[n: (ID.)] Here Christ, out of his members.

VERS. 2.-- *Waiting I have waited.* [n: (ID.)] He waits, and is ungrateful: but waiting he waits. [n: (AUG.)] Authority from the ancients, in Abraham and the rest, etc., *as far as* who is ours by worship, not of others.

*The Lord.* [n: (ID.)] Not a man, who can deceive and be deceived, and who is in need together with me. *He gave heed.* [n: (ID.)] To me, that he might profit me: not me, as the wicked also.

VERS. 3.-- *The pit.* [n: (ID.)] The darkness of iniquities, that is, of unbelief and of ignorance, etc., *as far as: to a rock* against mire. [n: (HIER.)] But in *the pit* and in *the mire,* the Gentiles worshipping idols: or, all who love earthly things.

[0902B]VERS. 4.-- *A song.* [n: (HIER.)] Not a shameful one, not a theatrical one, but through the New Testament. *They shall see.* [n: (CASS.)] The wonders wrought in the time of the incarnation. [n: (AUG.)] They shall see: this which he first said of those before, many later peoples shall see.

VERS. 5.-- *A name.* [n: (ID.)] Which is Saviour. As if: *Blessed,* etc., who hopes not for temporal things from him, but for salvation, which his name indicates. In the Hebrew genitives are often doubled in this manner: or, *blessed* is he to whom the name belongs, that is, by whom is the praise of God, that from him alone he may believe salvation to be, whence also is his hope. *Madnesses.* [n: (CASS.)] Idols, which deceive away from the truth: or, madnesses, in deceitful spectacles. Therefore in the plural, that it may be taken of them all. These things are false, but thy wonderful works, heaven, earth, dashes superstitions to pieces.

[0902C]VERS. 6.-- *Thy wonders.* [n: (AUG.)] Peter walking upon the waters in a sign, not in the world, that is, in the fruits of the world. [n: (AUG.)] Salvation is truthful, madness lying; it is not what it is thought to be. [n: (HIER.)] Earthly things are vain, madnesses, angers and brawls, etc.

*There is none like unto thee.* [n: (AUG.)] No authors of the arts. *I have declared.* Here Christ in the head and in the members. These are the wonders of God, these the thoughts to which no one is like. [n: (CASS.)] Behold the wonders: the incarnation, the justice of preaching, the deliverance of men from iniquities, the confounding of enemies, the gladness of those who hope. First, the rude and unformed were prepared, that they might willingly receive the words of Christ. [n: (AUG.)] See the wonders of God. I declared beforehand through the Prophets, and afterward I myself spoke. *They are multiplied above number.* [n: (HIER., AUG.)] Above the number [0902D] of those to be saved: or, above what anyone could count: or, while I speak, more have believed than of old.

VERS. 7.-- *Sacrifice and oblation thou didst not desire.* [n: (AUG.)] When the thing came, he did not want, as it were, the words that promise, etc., *as far as* but he who poured it out, being deaf, hears not. *But ears thou hast perfected for me.* That is, thou hast made me perfectly understand what thou wouldst. *Or, a body thou hast fitted to me,* as the Apostle says to the Hebrews [n: (Heb. X)], that is, thou hast given me a body fit for offering.

VERS. 8.-- *A holocaust.* Holocausts also thou didst not require for sin. *For sin.* Which stands above *oblation,* as concerning the goat ἀποπομπάιῳ, that is, the emissary, which was sent into the desert with the sins of the people: or, for sin [0903A] may be two words. *Behold I come* [n: (CASS.)] Since the sacrifices cease, it is established that Christ comes.

VERS. 9.-- *In the head of the book it is written of me.* Behold, I come at once to do thy will, because in the head of this book. Or, it is thus predetermined with God the Father, who is my head, I who am the book, that is, the form of justice for men.

VERS. 10.-- *A great church.* [n: (AUG.)] In the whole round of the lands, not a small one as before: and if indeed fear shall rise up, it will not forbid speaking. *Lord, thou hast known.* [n: (ID.)] Lips sound to men, etc., *as far as* that I be not slothful unto this.

VERS. 11.-- *I have not hidden.* [n: (CASS.)] (When I could profit) the truth, the promise, and the salvation.

[0903B]VERS. 12. *Thy mercy.* [n: (AUG.)] Love and fear lead me through unto thee.

VERS. 13.-- *Hairs.* [n: (AUG.)] Rightly are hairs brought in by way of comparison with offences. For therefore in the Old Testament the priests were shaven, that so they might be seen to have laid aside the vices of the flesh.

VERS. 14.-- *May it be pleasing.* [n: (CASS.)] *May it be pleasing,* marks the Trinity of persons; *to thee,* in the singular, marks the unity of essence. [n: (AUG.)] *May it be pleasing,* he says, not distrusting the power of God, if the will be present. As if: If thou wilt, thou canst, Lord. *Look.* [n: (CASS.)] His regard is our help.

VERS. 16.-- *Who say to me: Well done, well done.* [n: (AUG.)] Both he who speaks good things with an evil mind, and he who evil things by praising them deceitfully, each is evil and to be guarded against. Of the first sort [0903C] he said: *Who wish me evils;* of the second: *Who say to me: Well done, well done.*

VERS. 17.-- *And let them say.* [n: (CASS.)] This is their exultation: for this profession makes Christians rejoicing unto eternity.

VERS. 18.-- *But I.* [n: (CASS.)] This Christ, out of the form of a servant: lest any should arrogate to himself the glory of the aforesaid gladness. A beggar, who does not suffice for himself. *Delay not.* [n: (AUG.)] *Unless the days had been shortened,* etc., *as far as* who now nourish the things they have sought.
