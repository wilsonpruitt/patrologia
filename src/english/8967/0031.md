## PSALM XL

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* [n: (HIER.)] *An understanding for the sons of Core,* that although poor and suffering, etc., *as far as* the Apostle saying: *But to me,* etc.--[n: (AUG.)] It is as it were [0903D] the voice of one man, of the head and of the body.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Blessed.* ⟦s2⟧ He is mocked who reverences a dead poor man; but *blessed.* [n: (AUG.)] The Prophet celebrates him that understands concerning the needy and the poor. [n: (HIER.)] First the Prophet from himself, afterward he will bring in Christ. ⟦s3⟧ *Who understandeth.* [n: (AUG.)] Note *who understandeth,* that it may be given even to those who do not ask: he who gives to one asking does well; he who understands one that is silent is without doubt blessed.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *May the Lord preserve.* [n: (ID.)] Well does the Prophet wish it for him that understands concerning the needy and the poor, which is a sure promise. ⟦s2⟧ *And may he quicken.* [n: (ID.)] In the life to come, or, here through justification.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *The Lord bring help.* [n: (CASS.)] Do not think that the blessed man has rest here: he mitigates his sorrows, and prays [0904A] that the infirmity of man be not overcome by hard things; as it were, these temporal goods are a bed of rest: as it were, he makes them full of sorrows, that hope be not had in them. ⟦s2⟧ *The bed.* [n: (AUG.)] For *the whole* indeed *couch,* that is, worldly happiness, God providently turns into straits. [n: (CASS.)] Here he himself, trembling, asks for mercy: because he who knew that he had sinned feared to be handed over to these things by right: but lest the servants of God, afflicted by these things, should seem deserted by God, since God himself bore such things; whence he says: *My enemies have spoken.* ⟦s3⟧ Or, *a bed of sorrow.* [n: (AUG.)] The flesh, in which is sin, etc., *as far as* when Adam was weakened by transgressing. ⟦s4⟧ *Help* [n: (AUG., HIER.)] In the infirmity of the flesh, which weighs down the soul. ⟦s5⟧ *The couch.* [n: (AUG.)] There is some earthly thing which the sick man, etc., *as far as* for the house of eternal habitation.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0904B]VERS. 6.-- *My enemies.* [n: (CASS.)] The Lord concerning his passion, which was after the manner of the needy one. [n: (AUG.)] *My enemies.* ⟦s2⟧ These things are said specially of Christ, but they can be said also of his members: and today tribulation is made, that the name of Christ may be extinguished: but through his own death and that of his own the Church has grown the more.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *And if he came in.* [n: (CASS.)] Note that the wicked are to be borne with.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Not only open enemies, but also those who feigned themselves friends.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *He went forth abroad.* ⟦s2⟧ From the bosom of truth; an evil entrance, an evil going out. ⟦s3⟧ *Unto the selfsame.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, with one counsel they all whispered against me. ⟦s4⟧ *They whispered.* [n: (CASS.)] They do not dare to uncover their counsel, because in the crime all are *partners:* whereas few [0904C] are wont to be the *authors* of crimes.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Shall he.* [n: (AUG.)] He who has the power of laying down his soul, has he not also the power of taking it up again?

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *I have hoped.* [n: (AUG.)] In the members, because other brethren hoped somewhat in him, because he seemed such as the rest. ⟦s2⟧ So too he says that he hungers in the poor.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *But thou, O Lord.* [n: (AUG.)] Of the resurrection, which is the glory of the needy one, for the confirming of the hope of the faithful. ⟦s2⟧ *And I will requite them.* [n: (AUG.)]. ⟦s3⟧ For because they are scattered through the world, it is for admonition, not yet for damnation.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *But me.* [n: (ID.)] He affirms what he prayed for.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Blessed.* [n: (CASS.)] A concluding praise, the passion and resurrection having been set forth. ⟦s2⟧ *The God of Israel.* [0904D] [n: (AUG.)] The Jews are our book-carriers, who carry the codices for us: we are Israel. ⟦s3⟧ Otherwise the pagans would have thought the things said of Christ and the Church to be feigned, but they are overcome by the testimony of enemies. ⟦s4⟧ *From the age,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] From which all things began to be administered, unto the age to come, where all things are eternal.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Let it be done.* [n: (HIER.)] The doubling shows that this is to be done by all. ⟦s2⟧ In place of *let it be done,* in the Hebrew is written *amen, amen,* which Aquila translated *truly, truly,* or *faithfully.* ⟦s3⟧ And here ends the first book of the Psalter according to Jerome.
