## PSALM XLI.

VERS. 1.-- *At the end, an understanding.* [var: Vulg. Ps 41:1 reads "In finem" in the accusative and adds "filiis Core": "unto the end; an understanding for the sons of Core"] [n: (CASS.)] *Core* is interpreted *Calvary.* Therefore the sons, etc., *as far as,* whom [0905A] the insult of the wicked meets. [n: (HIER.)] *Sons of Core,* that is, *sons of the bald man,* etc., *as far as* the word of the boys mocking Elisha.

VERS. 2.-- *As the hart desireth,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] This voice suits catechumens, and also all who are still journeying abroad.

*The hart.* The hart, burdened with age, its hairs and horns growing out, draws in a serpent by its nostrils: whence, burning with the venom, it most ardently desires a fountain from which to drink; and when it has drunk, it lays aside its horns and its hairs.

VERS. 3.-- *It hath thirsted.* [n: (AUG.)] He determines his desire, because the hart could be understood to desire a fountain for the sake of drinking, or of washing; here all desire is shown to be toward God. *When shall I come.* What is quicker to God is slow to the desire of man. [0905B] Such a hart, while it runs, suffers those who insult; whence: *My tears have been my bread.* In prosperity also, which he despises, as in adversity. *Bread.* [n: (CASS.)] Not bitterness, or destruction, but *bread.*

VERS. 5.-- *These things I remembered.* [n: (AUG.)] While they ask, Where is thy God? I also have meditated through all these visible things, and through my own soul, that through those outward things I may find the invisible things of God. Behold the understanding of the title. *I poured out. [n: (Ibid.)]* For understanding and attaining I have spread it out above itself. *I poured out.* [n: (CASS.)] Or, I have rejoiced. My soul is melted within me; not outside, but pricked with compunction it returns into itself. Although I do not see God visibly, yet I consider that through the Church, in which the saints are wonderful, I shall come to the heavenly Jerusalem, [0905C] taken captive by the sweetness of the angelic praise, which is received by the mind. *Into the place of the tabernacle.* [n: (AUG.)] The tabernacle of God on earth, etc., *as far as,* that is, of those rejoicing in the eternal refreshment. *In the voice of exultation. [n: (Ibid.)]* Of the eternal festival there sounds something sweet to the heart: whence the hart is snatched away to the fountains of waters, and is soothed. But because the world makes a din, and the flesh weighs down the soul, comparing this sorrow with those joyful things, he says:

VERS. 6.-- *Why art thou sad?* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Of the disturbances against which reason strengthens the sensual soul with hope of God. *Why dost thou trouble?* [n: (AUG.)] Who, if thou searchest, there is nothing in the world for which thou shouldst owe this: hope rather for the saving remedy still, and if not now, fully in time to come. *Hope.* [n: (CASS.)] Or he can hope, because *still* in the present *I will confess,* that is, I will do penance [0905D] for my sins, that I may not withstand thee.

VERS. 7.-- *To myself.* Turned to God he tells the troublings from himself, the end of them from God: whence in him is his hope. And this is *to myself,* or from myself. [n: (CASS.)] Behold, he who above was correcting the soul to the condition of man, having turned back says here that the soul is troubled by itself, which, were there not the vices of the flesh, would remain tranquil. Behold out of what diversity man is composed. As if: therefore, that is, on account of the troublings of the soul, *I will be mindful.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, I hasten to thee, because thou shuttest out all evils. Behold the remedy by which he may escape the troublings: whence he subjoins for what things he is mindful: *Of the land of Jordan,* where the Lord consecrated baptism, in which the humbled are washed, and [0906A] become fruitful earth. [n: (CASS.)] He who is mindful of these things is not troubled by evils. *Therefore mindful.* [n: (AUG.)] Whereby the soul is troubled toward me, therefore *mindful,* etc. No one runs to remission, save he who is displeasing to himself, save he who confesses himself a sinner: which *Hermoniim* denotes; no one confesses unless he humbles himself, which is denoted in *the little one. Jordan. [n: (Ibid.)]* A going down. Descend therefore that thou mayest be lifted up. [n: (CASS.)] In the Jordan the Lord consecrated baptism, in which the humbled are washed, and become fruitful earth, which yields fruit thirtyfold, sixtyfold and a hundredfold [n: (Matth. XIII)]. *Hermoniim.* [n: (AUG.)] A small mountain beside the Jordan, etc., *as far as* because God is mindful not among the proud, but among the humble.

VERS. 8.-- *Deep unto deep. [n: (Ibid.)]* It is fitting that [0906B] I be mindful, etc., *as far as* and on account of all these things I pray in adversities, and this is: *With me a prayer. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or *deep calleth unto deep,* that is, the judgment which God brought upon the sinner, etc., *as far as* because he fears the judgments. *Of the cataracts.* Cataracts, according to the letter, are the mouths of rivers.

VERS. 9.-- *In the day. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, in prosperity, etc., *as far as* whence we may live in adversity. *With me a prayer. [n: (Ibid.)]* Prayer in troublings is mine, and this is with me.

VERS. 10.-- *Why hast thou forgotten.* [n: (CASS.)] Why dost thou now suffer me to be vexed with these miseries? He saw what sweetness of the fatherland there was, and shuddered at the perils of the world.

VERS. 11.-- *While my bones are broken.* [n: (AUG.)] As it were bones are broken, while the strength of patience is afflicted, [0906C] at which the wicked laugh. This is that reproach: *While it is said to me: Where is he?* either there is none, or he cares not for thee.

## PSALM XLII.

*Unto the end, a psalm of David.* [n: (AUG.)] Of the body of Christ, which groans here among the tares, that is, among the wicked, having many things in common with them: to be born, to die, prosperity and adversity: with whom (because it has not a common cause of merits) it cries out: *Judge me,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] This psalm is against pressures, lest they too draw us back from God. [n: (CASS.)] He complains of the mingling of the wicked, and prays for separation.

VERS. 1.-- *Judge me. [n: (Ibid.)]* He does not ask that his own sins be examined, which is dangerous, but that his cause be made ready apart from the impious, that he may be with the elect: whence he adds: *And [0906D] distinguish.* The nation that is not holy is the unjust and deceitful man, from whom he is separated in the judgment: but here too lest he be corrupted by them.

VERS. 2.-- *Why do I go sorrowful?* [n: (AUG.)] He seeks the cause of sadness, which is sin, as justice is the cause of gladness. Isaias on account of sin: *For a little while indeed I made him sorrowful,* for so God punishes.

VERS. 3.-- *Send forth thy light.* [n: (CASS.)] Because, when he comes, all the sadness of confusion will be taken away. [n: (AUG.)] Because we now have a sure pledge, since those very things, that is, light and truth, have led the present Church away from her sins, which is the tabernacle.

VERS. 4.-- *And I will go in.* As the high priest to the Holy of holies. [n: (CASS.)] *And I will go in.* Of the final gladness, [0907A] for which he gives praise in tribulation, he who above was sad, whence also he strengthens himself.

VERS. 5.-- *I will confess to thee on the harp.* [n: (AUG.)] He who does the precepts of God, and does not suffer, confesses on the psaltery: but he who does and suffers, which is from the lower nature, *on the harp,* which sounds from below, as the psaltery from above. *On the harp.* [n: (CASS.)] Behold, he who above was sad about the evils of the world, makes joy for himself out of good understanding. *Why dost thou trouble me? [n: (Ibid.)]* Frequently and vehemently he urges that the sadness of the world must be fled, which extinguishes patience, charity and hope, and confounds the whole life. *The salvation of my countenance.* [n: (AUG.)] And elsewhere: *Have mercy on me,* etc., *as far as* almsgiving, and prayer which flies to God on those two wings.
