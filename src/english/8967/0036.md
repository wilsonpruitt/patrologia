## PSALM XLVII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0913C]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm, or the praise of a canticle, for the sons of Core, the second,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] On the first day, God made light, etc., *as far as* of which it is here sung to the sons of Core. [n: (CASS.)] Concerning the founding of the city, which he describes commendingly. [n: (AUG.)] He commends the city, etc., *as far as* from its eternal stability.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *In the holy mount,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] This is that mountain, etc., *as far as* upon which the Church is founded.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *It is founded with the joy.* [n: (HIER.)] As if he should say, she who before was subject to curses, etc., *as far as* he himself is Christ, who through the universal Church spreads joys abroad. ⟦s2⟧ *Mount Sion, the sides.* [n: (AUG.)] Whosoever [0913D] cleave to anyone are called his sides, etc., *as far as* likewise *from the north shall every evil be spread abroad,* etc.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *In the houses.* ⟦s2⟧ Or, in the degrees, that is, according to the degrees of merits, because in each one severally he will give the knowledge of himself. ⟦s3⟧ *When he shall receive her.* ⟦s4⟧ To be guarded, and what need is there? ⟦s5⟧ *For the kings against.* ⟦s6⟧ But seeing by faith, namely thus, that this city is so worthy, they wondered, etc.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *The kings.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] The princes of the Jews. ⟦s2⟧ *Being gathered together.* ⟦s3⟧ By Herod inquiring the place of the nativity. ⟦s4⟧ *They came together.* ⟦s5⟧ Saying with one accord: *In Bethlehem of Juda according to the Scriptures.*

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Seeing.* ⟦s2⟧ The things which they had read in the prophets. ⟦s3⟧ *They wondered.* ⟦s4⟧ At so great glory, not lightly or idly. [0914A] *But they were troubled.* ⟦s5⟧ For sin, and *They were moved.* ⟦s6⟧ To faith, and so great was the dread, that the body trembled.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *There pains as of a woman in labour,* Of penance, seeing thus; but:

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *With a vehement spirit,* Of Herod. ⟦s2⟧ *Thou shalt break the ships of Tharsis.* ⟦s3⟧ Because, in Tharsus, the ships of Cilicia which were believed to have carried the Magi over into their own country, were broken by Herod. ⟦s4⟧ *Tharsis.* [n: (AUG.)] Cilicia is a region named from Tharsus, etc., *as far as* having run through all he concludes. ⟦s5⟧ Therefore:

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *As we have heard. ⟦s2⟧ In the city of the Lord of hosts,* because there he appoints angels as guards. ⟦s3⟧ He repeats. ⟦s4⟧ *In the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God.* ⟦s5⟧ That is, the Catholic Church, lest the assembly of heretics usurp this name to itself. ⟦s6⟧ But lest it be thought temporal, [0914B] he adds: *God hath founded it for ever.* ⟦s7⟧ Not for an hour, as before the tabernacle of Moses

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *We have received, O God, thy mercy.* [n: (CASS.)] Giving thanks for the coming of Christ, he admonishes concerning the degrees of the Church that are to be distributed, through which the Saviour may be known. ⟦s2⟧ *In the midst of the temple.* ⟦s3⟧ Jerusalem: they are the more grievously accused, who despise him who was beheld in the midst of them. ⟦s4⟧ By this the incarnation of Christ is noted, who according as he is man was presented in the temple.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *The right hand.* [n: (CASS.)] On this side the just are placed.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] *We have received, O God.* ⟦s2⟧ In what order she was founded, or what we have heard and seen. ⟦s3⟧ *Of thy temple. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, of a people not receiving [sic: *sucipientis*] mercy, who to many: [0914C] so that no grains seem to be there, but nevertheless some, because according to the name. ⟦s4⟧ *According to the name.* [n: (CASS.)] Which is everywhere spread abroad, because there is none, even if he know not the worship, who does not submit himself to this name. ⟦s5⟧ *Or according to the name.* ⟦s6⟧ This is, as the reverence of thy name, etc., *as far as* thy propitiation hath justified many, who all praise thee.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Let it be glad.* [n: (AUG.)] Although the just labour among thorns, etc., *as far as* it is theirs now to gather, God's to separate. ⟦s2⟧ *Mount Sion, and let them rejoice.* [n: (CASS.)] The Church, which is in the beholding of the people. ⟦s3⟧ *The daughters of Juda, because of thy judgments, O Lord.* ⟦s4⟧ Holy women. ⟦s5⟧ By Juda their nation is shown, which is on account of Christ, who is of Juda, and let these rejoice, that in both sexes the Church may be. [n: (AUG.)] The cause of gladness is the judgments [0914D] to come, wherein they know that they shall come to blessedness. ⟦s6⟧ Let those who are within be glad; you who are without, encompass.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Encompass.* ⟦s2⟧ By showing honours. ⟦s3⟧ *Embrace her.* ⟦s4⟧ With charity. ⟦s5⟧ *Tell in her towers. ⟦s6⟧ Set your hearts.* [n: (CASS.)] Preach to others in the concord of the Apostles, etc., *as far as* lest, being remiss through gladness, they grow slack.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Her strength.* [n: (AUG.)] The strength of the city is charity, which nothing overcomes; whence Solomon: *Love is strong as death.* [n: (CASS.)] He admonishes to set the hearts on the strength of the Church. ⟦s2⟧ *Distribute her houses, that you may relate it in another generation.* ⟦s3⟧ That is, the offices distinguished, etc., *as far as* whom the Lord first chose.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *For this is,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ This is what [0915A] must be related, a brief sentence, but one which concludes all things. ⟦s3⟧ *For ever,* against those who made temporal men gods.
