## PSALM XLVIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Unto the end, a psalm,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] The Prophet treats of the lovers of the world, and of Christ the redeemer, not of those, but of the despisers of the world, that he who is cast down in the world may not set a price upon the exalted. [n: (AUG.)] They who worship God, etc., *as far as* he makes them attentive to new things and to mighty things.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Hate these things, all ye nations.* [var: Vulg. Ps 48:2 *Audite haec, omnes gentes*, "Hear these things, all ye nations"] [n: (CASS.)] I except none, etc., *as far as* when, namely, the cockle shall be separated from the wheat, the sheep from the goats.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *And ye that are earthborn.* [n: ((AUG.)] I withhold my speech from none, let the heir of the earth hear, because of the judgment: [0915B] the just man, because of the kingdom.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *My mouth shall speak.* [n: (CASS.)] He commends the things to be said. ⟦s2⟧ *Wisdom* for divine things, *Prudence* for instructing morals; in these two the whole divine discourse is signified.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *I will incline.* [n: (HIER.)] I will humble myself to the truth speaking within, which teaches to speak in parables, lest it lie open to all.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Why shall I fear.* [n: (CASS.)] He begins to narrate the things he promised, saying that Christ will not redeem the ungodly; here the prophet, in his own person pleading a general cause, seeks the cause of the damnation and the fear of the wicked, which is iniquity. [n: (AUG.)] Why shall I fear? as though there is not what I should fear, since to avoid iniquity is in my own power; for that alone shall there be fear in the [0915C] last day, which shall be evil to the damned, not to the just. ⟦s2⟧ *Of the heel. [n: (Ibid.)]* He alludes to the words, etc., *as far as* that is, it shall oppress and shall make to fear.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *They that trust.* ⟦s2⟧ These, namely, who trust, etc., *as far as* in which they are so foolish.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *A brother doth not redeem.* ⟦s2⟧ If a brother, that is Christ, does not redeem, etc., *as far as* because such a one gives not to God a propitiation.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *And shall live still. [n: (Ibid.)]* After the end of life, he shall live according to the soul, because the soul shall not perish with the body, but shall be saved unto grief, until, the body being received, he be tormented on both sides.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *He shall not see. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, he does not understand, etc., *as far as* when the wise man passes through life. ⟦s2⟧ Or, *the unwise. [n: (Ibid.)]* Who attends not. ⟦s3⟧ *The fool.* ⟦s4⟧ Who, if he attends, does not [0915D] beware. ⟦s5⟧ *Riches* [n: (CASS.)] For the sake of which they did evil. ⟦s6⟧ *His own:* which are not according to God.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *They have called their names.* [n: (AUG.)] The heirs, who at the sepulchres of the dead make themselves drunk in memory of them, but neither does a drop reach the tongue of him that burns in hell.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *And man when.* ⟦s2⟧ And both in this, and in the rest, man does not understand; or, therefore God is not to be accused, he understood not his own dignity, he acted not understandingly, but was compared, reason being lost, to beasts, not in another thing, but in wisdom.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *This way.* [n: (CASS.)] Here now he gathers into one all that has been said. ⟦s2⟧ *Way,* that is, life, because it leads to death. ⟦s3⟧ *A stumbling block.* ⟦s4⟧ A goad and a grief, etc., *as far [0916A] as: This people honoureth me with their lips, but their heart is far from me. ⟦s5⟧ In their own mouth they shall be well pleased.* [n: (AUG.)] Since they are such, etc., *as far as* and they are worse than open evils.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Like sheep.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalmus. ⟦s2⟧ What straitness is to be rendered to the ungodly. ⟦s3⟧ They boast themselves, but to me it is already clear that they are placed in hell. ⟦s4⟧ Sheep, their wool being lost, remain, so in these, without the consuming of their substance, punishment finds what it may torment. ⟦s5⟧ *Death.* [n: (AUG.)] As Christ is life, so the devil is death; who is a shepherd to them, as to sheep, whom he draws to the pastures of death. ⟦s6⟧ *He shall feed upon.* [n: (CASS.)] From the likeness of beasts, etc., *as far as* he shall lead them from punishment to punishment. ⟦s7⟧ *In the morning.* [n: (AUG.)] Because now it is night, while the merits of the just do not appear, and the felicity of the ungodly is as it were named. ⟦s8⟧ *He shall grow old in [0916B] hell from their glory,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] How great glory he seemed to have, etc., *as far as: From his own glory* they were cast out.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Fear not.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalmus. ⟦s2⟧ An admonition, that the powerful be not feared, who leave all things behind with the light. ⟦s3⟧ For why these things are permitted to sinners, is a common complaint. ⟦s4⟧ *He is made rich.* ⟦s5⟧ For these things the abject also have. ⟦s6⟧ *Glory.* ⟦s7⟧ In the honours of the world. ⟦s8⟧ *House.* ⟦s9⟧ Because not he only, but all who pertain to him.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *He shall be blessed. [n: (Ibid.)]* Not for a good act, but for delights shall he be blessed, by the favour of the luxurious, who amid banquets wish well to those who feed them. ⟦s2⟧ *He will praise thee when thou shalt do well to him.* [n: (AUG.)] Despised is the confession which prosperity makes; but that is of [0916C] great merit, which the force of grief takes not away.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Into the generations of his fathers. [n: (Ibid.)]* They who follow the worst fathers are to be damned by their fellowship.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And man.* [n: (CASS.)] He repeats the deformity of man, that the evil man may rest from evil.
