## PSALM XLIX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of Asaph.* [n: (HIER.)] *Asaph,* in Latin a congregation; in Greek, a synagogue, which here speaks as faithful, which awaits Christ, through which the unbelieving Jews are the more confuted. [n: (CASS.)] *Asaph,* about to bring in the voice of God concerning the change of the sacrifices, first treats of both comings, by the first inviting, by the second terrifying. ⟦s2⟧ *God of gods.* [n: (CASS.)] Lest anyone think meanly [0916D] of the incarnation, etc., *as far as* by way of appellation, as the false gods, Jupiter, Venus. ⟦s3⟧ *He hath spoken, and hath called the earth.* [n: (AUG.)] By himself, by heralds. ⟦s4⟧ *From the rising of the sun unto the going down.* [n: (CASS.)] As if he should say, etc., *as far as: the law shall come forth from Sion, and the Word of the Lord from Jerusalem.*

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Out of Sion, the loveliness of his beauty,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] The comeliness of the Gospel began, etc., *as far as* that they might be witnesses in Jerusalem and in the whole earth.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3, 4.-- *Fire shall blaze in his sight and round about him. [n: (Ibid.)]* It shall go before him; let punishment therefore terrify those whom rewards do not invite, etc., *as far as* it shall accompany the wicked to hell. ⟦s2⟧ *A mighty tempest. ⟦s3⟧ He shall call heaven from above, and the earth.* [sic: *Tempesta*] [n: (CASS.)] Not a wind, etc., *as far as* first he called all in common, [0917A] then he shall call that he may separate. ⟦s4⟧ *To judge his people.* [n: (AUG.)] First he called the mingled from the rising of the sun unto the going down, but then that he may separate.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Gather ye together his saints unto him. [n: (Ibid.)]* The Prophet seeing things to come, etc., *as far as* it is said of the wicked, *of whom he hath not filled his hand. ⟦s2⟧ Who set his covenant above sacrifices. [n: (Ibid.)]*. ⟦s3⟧ The New Testament above the Old, etc., *as far as* who hold the works of charity of more worth than the sacrifices of cattle.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And the heavens shall declare his justice,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] Already the heavens have declared to us this justice of God, etc., *as far as* and if now things are mingled, because *God is a just judge.*

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Hear, O my people, etc.* [n: (CASS.)] The voice of Christ, that, the sacrifices of cattle being left, there may be made a sacrifice of praise. [n: (AUG.)] Or, he repeats under another word, Hear [0917B] as Israel, that is, as one seeing God, and *I will testify,* these things, namely, *I am thy God.*

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thy God.* [n: (AUG.)] First he says simply: *I am God.* ⟦s2⟧ And afterwards he says: *I am thy God.* ⟦s3⟧ As if he should say: Even if I am not thy God, yet I am God. ⟦s4⟧ For to my own good I am God, but to thy evil I am not thy God. ⟦s5⟧ *I am.* [n: (CASS.)] To be is proper to the Deity. ⟦s6⟧ *Hear, O Israel. [n: (Ibid.)]* As if he should say: If thou art Israel, that is, if thou seest me, thou dost not neglect to hear. ⟦s7⟧ That God is general, thy God properly.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *But the holocausts.* [n: (AUG.)] If the soul is inflamed, and seizes the members into its own use, that they may not serve concupiscence: whence it is commanded in Leviticus, that they offer the fat with the little kidneys, by which the inward [0917C] charity is signified.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *I will not take,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Even if thou shalt so do: *I will not take calves out of thy house,* as though I took the things signified, not those out of the house. ⟦s2⟧ *For mine are:* as if he should say, because they are not thine, but mine. ⟦s3⟧ *For mine are,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Mine are the things which thou dost not possess, the untamed, namely: *The beasts of the woods, the cattle, the oxen,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] By these likenesses he figures the Church, etc., *as far as: The flesh of bullocks* he wills not in sacrifices, but in the poor.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS, 11.-- *I know all the fowls.* [n: (AUG.)] With him are all things, by a certain knowledge of ineffable wisdom.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *If I should be hungry, I would not. [n: (Ibid.)]* If you give these things, perhaps you think that I hunger, which is madness?

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0917D]VERS. 13.-- *Shall I eat the flesh of bullocks?* ⟦s2⟧ Of old these things were sacrificed, not for this reason that God was delighted therein, but lest they should be offered to demons, and they were sacrificed to God only, in which there was some signification.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Sacrifice to God.* [n: (CASS.)] He says not "to me," but of himself, as of another, as also afterwards: *But to the sinner God hath said, not I.*

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Of tribulation.* [n: (AUG.)] Our tribulation is properly by the fear of salvation, not of carnal things.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *But to the sinner. [n: (Ibid.)]* From whom he would have sacrifice, where to the sinner his own wickednesses are imputed

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0918A] [n: (CASS.)] These things he said to his own people, etc., *as far as* For reading is not denied to those who are to be corrected.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *But thou hast hated. [n: (Ibid.)]* But thou sayest with the mouth, and deniest discipline in deed: if I correct, thou murmurest.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The thief, thou didst run. [n: (Ibid.)]* In these he thunders against all vices; the whole from a part, or the thief of the sound understanding of Scripture. ⟦s2⟧ *With adulterers.* ⟦s3⟧ Who set down the false, by taking away the true.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Thy mouth hath abounded,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] He accuses the conscience, and the tongue, etc., *as far as* he detracts from another not by chance, but of set purpose.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Sitting. [n: (Ibid.)]* What he does passing by or standing, he does not do with the will. ⟦s2⟧ *Brother. [n: (Ibid.)]* Every man. ⟦s3⟧ *And against thy mother's son,* of the Church, to whom we are joined by charity. ⟦s4⟧ *Thou didst lay a scandal,* heresies or other things by which the innocent may be caught.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0918B] *Thou didst speak against.* [n: (AUG.)] While he detracts who seems learned, etc., *as far as* with the food of stronger doctrine.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Like unto thee.* [n: (CASS.)] By consenting to the wicked.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I will set against thy face.* [n: (AUG.)] What thou hast put behind thy back, I will set before thy face.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Let him snatch away.* [n: (CASS.)] God unto punishment, and *there be none to deliver.* ⟦s2⟧ When the devil snatches, God delivers; but when God snatches, none avails to set free.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *The sacrifice of praise.* [n: (CASS.)] Not that which the wicked sing, *the sacrifice of praise,* not of animals. ⟦s2⟧ And this is the way to God, the ladder of Jacob, by which *the angels,* that is, the faithful, *ascend* to the praise of the divinity, and *descend* to the praise of the humanity. ⟦s3⟧ *There is the way by which,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] In the sacrifice of praise. ⟦s4⟧ *The salvation of God.* [n: (AUG.)] Christ, who came with grace, whom he knows, who attributes anything [0918C] to another than to the grace of God.
