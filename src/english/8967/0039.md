## PSALM L.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, a psalm.* [n: (CASS.)] When Nathan rebuked him, the king did not blush to confess his sins publicly, etc., *as far as* what avails with God for the penitent. ⟦s2⟧ *Of David.* [n: (AUG.)] He is set forth as an example, not of falling, but of rising again if thou hast fallen, that thou despair not. [n: (CASS.)] The first thing is entreaty, etc., *as far as* full remission of sins is granted.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Have mercy on me, O God. [n: (Ibid.)]* This is a penitential psalm, etc., *as far as* because he foresaw that through this psalm many would perish. ⟦s2⟧ *According to thy great mercy.* ⟦s3⟧ Of a grave sin one would have to despair, [0918D] were there not a great mercy [sic: *misericodia*]. ⟦s4⟧ A great one cannot be defined, how great it is. ⟦s5⟧ *Great mercy. [n: (Ibid.)]* A great mercy brought God down from heaven, etc., *as far as* when God is asked to pardon according to himself? ⟦s6⟧ *Of thy tender mercies,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] He asks through mercy and through tender mercy. ⟦s7⟧ And mercy is in the nature, tender mercy in the effect.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Wash me yet more.* [n: (CASS.)] What manner of blotting out he would have, he adds: *Cleanse me,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] So wash that thou make clean; for certain things are washed which nevertheless are not pure.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *For iniquity.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] Whose mercy he asks that he may pardon, because he knows him just, that he punishes sins, he adds: Punish thou not, because [0919A] I punish. ⟦s2⟧ *For my iniquity,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ *Against me.* [n: (AUG.)] He had his sin behind his back, when the king gave sentence against the rich man who had seized the poor man's ewe, forgetful of himself.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *To thee only have I sinned.* [n: (HIER.)] For he alone punishes justly who has not in himself anything that may be reproved; and among men this is Christ alone. ⟦s2⟧ *To thee only.* [n: (CASS.)] Because a king is superior to all, he is to be punished by God alone. ⟦s3⟧ *Before thee.* ⟦s4⟧ Because he knows that he is everywhere; whence he accuses himself, because in his presence he did not blush to sin. ⟦s5⟧ *That thou mayest be justified,* etc. ⟦s6⟧ Whose words do not pass away, but are fulfilled. ⟦s7⟧ *And mayest overcome when thou art judged.* ⟦s8⟧ God is of such great justice [sic: *t antae*], etc., *as far as* that he may overcome as between himself and him, if they be judged.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To thee only.* [n: (AUG.)] To Christ, who had no sin, [0919B] etc., *as far as* in that he had so grievously offended God. ⟦s2⟧ *And mayest overcome.* [n: (AUG.)] Or, thou art so just that thou overcomest the devil, and death, and the Jews, when thou art judged: and in the judgment wherein thou wast judged.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *In iniquities.* [n: (CASS.)] The odium of the sin is lessened, etc., *as far as* he is accused of one and confesses all.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *For behold,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] He so gives mercy that he keeps truth, so that not even the sins of him whom he pardons are unpunished. ⟦s2⟧ For he pardons the man who punishes himself. ⟦s3⟧ Mercy therefore it is that a man is set free; truth, that the sin is punished. ⟦s4⟧ *Truth,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Because in confessing he spoke the truth, which God seeks above sacrifices, he entreats that help be brought him. ⟦s5⟧ *The uncertain and hidden things.* [n: (AUG.)] There are hidden things also, in that [0919C] to such men, etc., *as far as* whence he had the more to beware of sin. ⟦s6⟧ *The hidden things.* [n: (CASS.)] From the authority of him who makes them manifest, the confidence of washing and of being made white.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Thou shalt sprinkle me.* [n: (CASS.)] The second thing is confidence in the mercy of God, which is very profitable: lest despair creep in, which is graver than all sins. ⟦s2⟧ *With hyssop. [n: (Ibid.)]* Dipped in the blood of the victim, it was sprinkled over the body of the leper, signifying that the stains of sins are to be washed away by the blood of Christ. [n: (AUG.)] Hyssop is a lowly herb, etc., *as far as* in which there is a swelling, so humility, pride. ⟦s3⟧ *Above snow. [n: (Ibid.)]* Incomparably: whence also on the mountain the garment of Christ shone as snow.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *To my hearing.* [n: (AUG.)] Mark the humble man. ⟦s2⟧ Let me hear thee, etc., *as far as* and because he is now humble, now [0919D] *the bones that were humbled rejoice.* [var: Vulg. Ps 50:10 *exsultabunt*, "shall rejoice"]

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Turn away.* [n: (CASS.)] Made secure by that confidence, he asks the blotting out of all sins and a full restoration.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *In my bowels. [n: (Ibid.)]* Whence the adultery went forth. ⟦s2⟧ In both parts, in soul and in body, he asks the remedy of healing, because in them he sinned. ⟦s3⟧ He so desires to be cleansed that he may be seen to wish to commit no such thing further.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Cast me not away from thy face.* [n: (CASS.)] Turn thy face from my sins, but not me from thy face, whence light and health may come.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *With a principal spirit.* [n: (AUG.)] Some here note the Trinity in God, who is spirit, since [0920A] not body: by *a right spirit,* the Son; *the Holy Spirit,* by his own name; by *a principal spirit,* the Father. [n: (CASS.)] The spirit of prophecy, which is more precious to him than all things, he asks that its strength alone be not taken away, saying nothing of the rest.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *I will teach. [n: (Ibid.)]* What he pays when restored: before, weeping; now he becomes a teacher.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *From blood. [n: (Ibid.)]* Because carnal sins are many, etc., *as far as* which come from the corruption of flesh and blood. ⟦s2⟧ *Thy justice. [n: (Ibid.)]* He praises justice, when he ought to give thanks to mercy; but it belonged to justice also to receive one confessing; and these two things are always joined together in the judgment of the Lord.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Thy praise.* [n: (AUG.)] That thou didst create me; [0920B] that thou didst not forsake me when I sinned; that thou didst admonish me to confess; that thou didst cleanse me.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *A sacrifice to God.* [n: (CASS.)] Which at that time was offered for expiation. ⟦s2⟧ *Afflicted [n: (Ibid.)]* A glad and wanton spirit binds a man to sin. ⟦s3⟧ There follows the promise of this sacrifice: *A contrite heart.*

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Deal favourably. [n: (Ibid.)]* Now rejoicing, he prophesies of the building of Jerusalem, where is the sacrifice of justice. ⟦s2⟧ *The walls.* [n: (Au.)] Let the defences of our immortality be built up in faith, hope, and charity.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Then shalt thou accept a sacrifice. [n: (Ibid.)]* Now the sacrifice is for iniquity, etc., *as far as* then justice shall be the sacrifice, *oblations* the lesser saints, *holocausts,* the perfect.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0920C] *Then shall they lay.* [n: (CASS.)] The priests; *calves,* the innocent, etc., *as far as* the faithful who suffer for thy name shall be acceptable to thee. ⟦s2⟧ *The altar.* [n: (AUG.)] The heavenly Jerusalem.

## PSALM LI

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Unto the end, for,* etc. [n: (HIER., AUG.)] Doeg betrayed David, etc., *as far as* This is not here a prayer for the wicked, but a prophecy of what shall come upon them.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Doeg.* [n: (CASS.)] The adversary of David signifies Antichrist, who shall stir up the whole world; who shall come by signs and wonders before the coming of the judgment, and seeking help from Saul, that is, from the devil, shall announce that David, that is, Christ, has come into the house of Achimelech, that is, to the Church [sic: *Eccesiam*], which is the kingdom [0920D] of the Father.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Abimelech.* [n: (HIER.)] He says, in the Hebrew Achimelech, etc., *as far as* therefore Abimelech is the kingdom of my father, and it signifies the Jews. [var: Vulg. Ps 51:2 *Achimelech*, the form the gloss itself contrasts]

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Why dost thou glory in malice,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] The first is an invective against Doeg, that he may strengthen the faithful against Antichrist and his accomplices.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *It hath devised. [n: (Ibid.)]* A light mind, a headlong utterance: it does not think before it speaks, whence: *In the mouth of fools is their heart.* [sic: *stulorsorum*]

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As a razor,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] Which, while it promises renewal, cuts: so does that deceitful one, namely Antichrist, cut blessedness, and slay.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Thou hast loved malice,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] He does not forsake [0921A] evil who loves it above kindness; and if kindness touches his mind, he flees.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *A deceitful tongue. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, deceitful words, the ministers of falsehood, of men who bear one thing in the heart and bring forth another in the mouth.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Therefore God.* [n: (CASS.)] Of the swift end of the wicked man. ⟦s2⟧ *And thy root out of the land of the living.* [n: (AUG.)] Our root is charity, etc., *as far as* for here is the dust which the wind drives from the face of the earth. [n: (Ibid.)] The root of evil is covetousness, which does not bring forth fruit in the land of the living. ⟦s3⟧ Or, he speaks of Antichrist, whose root is his ministers, who with him shall have nothing with the saints.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The just shall see. [n: (Ibid.)]* Derision for his casting down. ⟦s2⟧ *[n: (Ibid.)] The just shall see.* ⟦s3⟧ What shall befall the wicked, etc., *as far as* because more than by all other evils he shall deceive the faithful [0921B].

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *But I,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] How the good man bears himself against him. [n: (CASS.)] Thus far the censure of that man; here the praise of the contrary part: behold a perfect demonstration. ⟦s2⟧ *A fruitful olive tree.* [n: (AUG.)] The people of God is an olive tree, from which the proud branches were broken off, that is, the Jews, and the wild olive of the Gentiles was grafted in. [n: (CASS.)] A fruitful olive tree, from whose shoot the Lord Christ came into flower, who was anointed with spiritual oil above his fellows.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For ever and for ever and ever.* [n: (AUG.)] What he said *for ever,* he repeats *for ever and ever:* that by repeating he may confirm how firmly he is founded in the love of the kingdom of heaven.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0921C]VERS. 11.-- *Thou hast done, and I will wait on thy name. [n: (Ibid.)]* The things foretold, namely, that I am an olive tree, that I have hoped in mercy for ever.
