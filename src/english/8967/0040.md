## PSALM LII.

VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, for,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] The understanding of David the prophet, etc., *as far as* here of the coming of the judgment. [n: (AUG.)] Or, for mahalath, etc., *as far as* consolation at the end of the psalm.

*The fool hath said. [n: (Ibid.)]* A rebuke of the wicked.

*[n: (Ibid.)] The fool,* every one who lives ill, etc., *as far as* he says, there is no God.

VERS. 3.-- *God from.* [n: (CASS.)] Or from heaven, that is, through holy souls he gave warning, that he might see, etc. *If there be,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] Or, if he does not understand, if at least he is one seeking [0921D] to understand God.

VERS. 4.-- *They are all gone aside. [n: (Ibid.)]* A fitting order. First they go aside; then they are become unprofitable; thence there is none that doth good.

VERS. 5.-- *Shall they not know. [n: (Ibid.)]* A threatening of the wicked by the end. [n: (AUG.)] He threatens them, etc., *as far as.* But deservedly this befalls them, because:

VERS. 6.-- *They have not called upon God. [n: (Ibid.)]* He consoles those who groan, lest they imitate the prosperous, because *they have not called upon God,* whence they shall quickly perish: a consolation of the faithful. *The bones of them that please men.* [n: (CASS.)] The good displease themselves while they chastise the flesh: the wicked please.

*They have been confounded. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, they shall be confounded, they shall be separated, [0922A] so that it may be read generally of all the wicked; but Israel shall be saved.

VERS. 7.-- *Who shall give out of,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] But salvation is to the Church in her travail.

## PSALM LIII.

VERS. 2.-- *The Ziphites.* [n: (HIER., AUG.)] They are interpreted the flourishing ones, etc., *as far as* he admonishes us to praise God in adversities.

VERS. 3.-- *O God, in thy name.* [n: (AUG.)] He prays among the Ziphites. [n: (AUG., HIER.)] Here, save me in thy name; afterwards, I fear not to be judged in thy strength. [n: (AUG.)] O God, in thy name: the words of the Church hiding among the Ziphites, whose good is within and whose reward is hidden; others ask to be delivered by other means: I, in thy name.

VERS. 4.-- *O God, hear.* [n: (CASS.)] Again God, a name [0922B] of power; he repeats it that the help may not be slow. *Give ear,* [n: (AUG.)] The Ziphites, even if they hear these things, yet do not understand, who know nothing except things temporal.

VERS. 5.-- *Strangers.* [n: (AUG.)] Ziph was a hamlet which belonged to the tribe of Juda, whence the Ziphites.

VERS. 6.-- *For behold.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalmus. What God does for him and for the Ziphites, whence he pays his praises. *He helpeth.* [n: (AUG.)] Which they themselves know not, among whom I lie hid; but this, that he helps me, they would see, if they set God before their eyes.

VERS. 7.-- *Turn back the evils upon mine enemies. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or, turn back: he prays for such as are to be converted; and this is the victim which he pays for his deliverance. *In thy truth.* [n: (AUG.)] Or according to another reading: *In thy strength,* destroy those whom [0922C] in thy weakness thou hast borne with. *I will freely sacrifice. [n: (Ibid.)]* Because for his own sake, not for anything else that I might wish from him. He who praises for the sake of something else praises of necessity; if he had that thing, he would not praise. *But to me,* and the things he gave are pleasing for the giver's sake.
