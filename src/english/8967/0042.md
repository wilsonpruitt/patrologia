## PSALM LV.

VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] *The people that is made far from the holy ones, in the inscription of a title:* [var: Vulg. Ps 55:1 *pro populo qui a sanctis longe factus est*, the perfect where Migne prints the present] whom the Jew contradicts, and every one who will not have Christ for king; and those also who afflict the Church: against whom this psalm strengthens the Church. *In the inscription of a title, to David himself, when they held him.* The inscription of the title, etc., *as far as* the persecutors who tread the Church as grapes. *The Allophyli.* The same as [0925C] *the people that was made far from the holy ones. Geth,* that is, in the winepress. We read of David, etc., *as far as* but, when it is pressed, it is not barren.

VERS. 2.-- *Have mercy on me, O God.* [n: (CASS.)] The Church prays, touching summarily on her tribulations, in which through hope in God she *ends* by declaring that she fears not, that is, at the end of the first part she says that she fears not. *Fighting against me he hath afflicted me,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Man visibly, the devil invisibly, whence the tribulation is the more dangerous.

VERS. 4.-- *From the height.* [n: (CASS.)] *The height of the day,* the world, where is the swelling of pride: that the tribulation may be the graver, she says that she is trodden down by the proud. [n: (AUG.)] *From the height.* Another reading, etc., *as far as* he does not say: I will not fear, but, *I will hope* in thee. *Of the day. The Day,* is Christ, whose twelve hours are the twelve Apostles: whence he himself says: [0925D] *Are there not twelve hours in the day?* The *height of this day* his enemies cannot reach, whence in the passion of Christ: *There was darkness.*

VERS. 6.-- *All the day,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] She enumerates the evils, by the enduring of which the conversion of her enemies also comes about, whence again through hope in God she says that she fears not, as she had done before.

VERS. 7.-- *They shall dwell.* [n: (AUG.)] Or, some of those who are far from the holy ones, etc., *as far as* for if they say false things of thee, they do not take away heaven, nor do these men hurt thee.

VERS. 8.-- *For nothing. [n: (Ibid.)]* Because thou hast no labour from it, as though it were nothing to thee, because, even though they are past hope, yet thou healest them by a word; or, for no merits of theirs, but freely, out of thy mercy. *In thy wrath thou shalt break the peoples in pieces,* [0926A] while by the scourges of God they come to their senses, or while they foresee the wrath of God. *In thy wrath thou shalt break the peoples in pieces.* Because thou terrifiest by tribulations, etc., *as far as* and the wrath of consuming, whereby the wicked are punished unto destruction.

VERS. 9.-- *O God, I have declared my life to thee.* The life of man is threefold: of nature, whereby we live and breathe; of grace, whereby we live in Christ; of sin, which is passed in vices. Two are from God, because good, namely the life of nature and the life of grace: the third is from man, because evil. All these we ought to declare to God. [sic: *Ha*] [n: (CASS.)] *O God, my life. Thou shalt break them in pieces,* and I, for their instruction, declare my life beforehand, which was always evil. He declares his life to God who confesses his sin: not teaching God, but condemning himself, that he may be loosed. *Thou hast set my tears,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Devout tears, which [0926B] plead for enemies: and therefore acceptable to God, as he himself promised.

VERS. 10.-- *In whatsoever day,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Whether in boyhood, or in middle age, or at the end of life. Wonderful clemency, which waits for whatever time of our conversion, and whensoever it is asked, forgives! *Thou art my God, thou.* [n: (AUG.)] Though thou art the God of all, mine properly, who succourest me, whom I worship and love. Others are strangers.

VERS. 11.-- *In God I will praise the word. [n: (Ibid.)]* The word, that is, teaching and discourse; whatever I speak, I will praise in the Lord, that is, I will attribute it to him: and if anything presses me, I have hoped, and therefore I shall not fear. [n: (AUG., HIER.)] Or, in God the Father the Word, that is the Son, because whatever is said of the Father and of the Son according to substance, is said of the Holy Spirit also. This is said of the power [0926C] of the Word, whereby it is eternal and consubstantial with the Father [sic: *consubtantiale*]; and afterwards, of the humanity, he adds: *In the Lord,* that is, in Christ, who is the Word made flesh. *I will praise the discourse,* etc., that is, the preachings by which he gave to the world a rule of living and salvation. These two things this man preaches, the power of the word and the humanity of Christ. *In God I will praise.* Or thus, that the Trinity of the nature stands personally in the three words which he sets down. *In God* the Father *I will praise the Word,* that is, the Son; *In the Lord,* that is, in the Holy Spirit, *I will praise the discourse,* that is, the Son.

VERS. 12.-- *They are in me.* Within, things which never depart from us; *Vows of praise* [var: Vulg. Ps 55:12 *vota tua, quae reddam, laudationes tibi*, "thy vows, which I will render, praises to thee"] which shall be rendered in eternity. [n: (CASS.)] *They are in me.* The Church, now free from the evils of the world, says that in the beatitude to come, or even here, [0926D] she will sing the praise of God.

VERS. 13.-- *Because thou hast delivered.* [n: (CASS.)] Or it may be taken thus of the future, etc., *as far as* where also she pleases God perfectly, whence he adds: *That I may please,* etc.

## PSALM LVI.

VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* [n: (AUG.)] A psalm of the resurrection and the passion, in brief. This the psalm does, *lest* thou, Pilate, or thou whosoever thou art, *shouldst corrupt David,* [var: Vulg. Ps 56:1 *ne disperdas*, "destroy not"] etc., by denying him king, because, being covered with flesh, he escaped the knowledge of the Jews [n: (Ibid.)] No title was inscribed to David, etc., *as far as* where Christ lay hid, unknown to the Jews.

VERS. 2.-- *Have mercy on me.* [n: (CASS.)] The prophet in the person of the Lord, because in him the Lord, as it were amid [0927A] evils, cries out and foretells deliverance. [n: (CASS.)] Christ prays according to that which is man, so humble concerning the passion that he might be thought to be one of the faithful crying out. *And in the shadow.* The voice of the body is joined to the head: and speaking in the person of the members and in his own, he will say how he can be delivered, *he sent his Word.*

VERS. 4.-- *He sent his Word from heaven.* [n: (AUG., HIER.)] Out of his own hidden essence, etc., *as far as* whom they thought they had put out. [var: Vulg. Ps 56:4 *Misit de caelo, et liberavit me*, without *Verbum*] *He sent from heaven.* [n: (HIER.)] Diapsalmus. Now he declares what he sent and from what he rescued them, and he speaks in the person of the members. *He hath delivered. [n: (Ibid.)]* Because *He hath sent his Word* is now certain, it is as though he already were who *hath delivered,* that is, hath delivered [cj: *liberavit*; read *liberabit*, "will deliver"]: and *he hath given,* that is, will give *for a reproach them that trod upon me. God hath sent.* [n: (AUG.)] His Word, in whom he dealt mercifully, in whom also he fulfilled the promises [0927B]. *Mercy,* because out of mercy he died for us. *Truth,* because, as he had foretold, he rose again, to justify us. *His mercy,* etc. [n: (HIER., AUG.)] For both are contained in him. For if there had been mercy alone, there would have been licence for us to sin; if truth alone, man could not have borne it.

VERS. 5.-- *I slept.* [n: (AUG.)] Easy to be roused out of a deep rest: *Troubled* by the passion, *I slept.* David did not sleep in the cave, but Christ in death. [n: (CASS.)] Troubled, according to the flesh, by the memory and the anguish of the passion, whence; *My soul is sorrowful,* etc. *And Jesus began to be weary and to fear. The sons of men.* The right order is this: the sons of men prepared snares; but he inserts in what manner they troubled him. *The tongue.* Those cries [0927C] Crucify, crucify, and the like, wrought death also, as a sharp sword [n: (Matth. XXVI; Marc. XIV)].

VERS. 6.-- *Be thou exalted. [n: (Ibid.)]* In the person of the prophet, the injuries being seen, and it being seen that in the resurrection the Jews were made empty, the man on the cross cries out rejoicing: *Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens,* that he may judge the Jews on the earth. *Be thou exalted.* The Lord [speaks] the things above; the prophet indeed, but in the person of the Lord, because the Lord speaks in the prophet. Even when the prophet speaks out of his own person, the Lord speaks through him, who dictates [sic: *dicta*] to him. [n: (AUG.)] *Be thou exalted.* The words of Christ. But, O Father, etc., *as far as* because the whole world is made subject to him.

VERS. 7.-- *They prepared a snare.* The exaltation being interposed, he pursues the narration he had begun. *For the feet* [0927D] [n: (CASS.)] of the Lord are the rebukes of the wicked and the promises to the faithful, for which *they prepared deceits,* as in the matter of the adulteress. *They bowed down,* while he, having compassion on them, was made barren by them. *A pit before my face,* etc. When they said to his face: He is guilty of death.

VERS. 8.-- *Prepared. [n: (Ibid.)]* Diapsalmus. After the resurrection he promises praises. Or, prepared to give thanks (which he there shows) *I will sing.*

VERS. 9.-- *Arise, my glory. [n: (Ibid.)]* And therefore, O flesh, that art the cause of my glory, *Arise,* mightily, as it were a rejoicing together at a miracle to be wrought, being certain: whence, affirming the same thing, he repeats it. *Arise,* I say, because I am certain also of the hour, because *I will arise early. Arise, psaltery and harp.* [n: (AUG.)] The one [0928A] flesh of Christ rose again, etc., *as far as* the *psaltery and harp,* therefore, rose again, and give praise to the Lord.

VERS. 11.-- *To the heavens. [n: (Ibid.)]* Which are above the clouds, where is the seat of the angels, etc., *as far as* mercy ascends to the heavens.
