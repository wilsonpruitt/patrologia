## PSALM LVII.

VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, destroy not, of David, in the inscription of a title,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] He warns that no one contradict the kingdom of Christ. The first thing is a reproach of the Jews, that we may not do the like.

VERS. 2.-- *If in very deed you speak justice.* [n: (AUG.)] It is easy for all to speak justice, etc., *as far as* because I am your king, as the title says: *Rightly.* The Jews so judged of Christ that they are proved to have spoken of him feignedly.

VERS. 3.-- *Your hands.* The hands follow the heart: [0928B] whatever thou willest and canst not do, God accounts as done. *They frame together.* Or they knit together, that is, joining one thing to another, etc., *as far as* whence with a scourge of little cords the Lord beat out those buying and selling in the temple.

VERS. 4.-- *Sinners are alienated from the womb.* Or, they are alienated from the womb of the Church, etc., *as far as* they are cast out as untimely births.

VERS. 5.-- *They have fury. [n: (Ibid.)]* The thing itself is not praised by Scripture, from whatever quarter a likeness is taken, as in the case of the unjust judge, who scarcely heard the widow that asked him. *Of the deaf one, and that stoppeth.* [n: (CASS.)] Not only do they not do, but they will not even hear. [n: (AUG.)] *The earth,* present things, in which man delights; *the tail,* things past.

VERS. 6.-- *Of the sorcerer that charmeth. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or according to another reading: *Of the medicine compounded by a [0928C] wise man.* The medicine compounded, etc., *as far as* this therefore is the wise man by whom the medicine is compounded.

VERS. 7.-- *God shall break.* [n: (CASS.)] What their recompense is. [n: (AUG.)] Asps, the Jews, while they were attempting treacherous things; lions, while they clamoured for his death; of these are the *Grinders broken,* when Christ rose again. *Teeth.* Deceitful words. *In the mouth.* By their own words he shall take them. *The Lord shall break the grinders of the lions. They shall come to nothing:* The clamourings for death. *He shall break them,* lest they come to effect; which he expounds, *To nothing,* saying.

VERS. 8.-- *As water. [n: (Ibid.)]* They are compared also to torrents, because, sounding against the rocks of pride, they are sudden and headlong, but quickly pass away. To so many evils are they compared. *He hath bent his bow.* Vengeance, until [0928D] they be weakened within and without, and be taken away from the sight of God, sent into hell, and here in the present, from their own land.

VERS. 10.-- *Before your thorns. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, before they understand their sins. *The bramble,* that is, sharp damnation: at the last the fire of vices swallows them up, that is, devours them. *The bramble.* A kind of herb, soft at first, but which afterwards turns into thorns: so also sins delight for a while, but afterwards prick. *As living, so,* etc. Because the life of the ungodly is not true but false: they seem to live, and they do not live. *So in his wrath he swallows them up,* because God judges in tranquillity, or because it is not wrath in comparison with that which is to come.

VERS. 11.-- *The just shall rejoice when he shall see the revenge* [n: (CASS.)] The correcting of the just by the punishment of the wicked.

[0929A]VERS. 12.-- *And man shall say.* [n: (AUG.)] As it is done by the just judgment of God, etc., *as far as* and because fire swallows up the wicked, therefore there is *a God that judgeth them* on the earth.

## PSALM LVIII.

VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, destroy not.* [n: (CASS.)] In vain does any man attempt to destroy what the divine authority so often forbids. *Destroy not.* [n: (AUG.)] When Pilate said: *What I have written, I have written,* etc., *as far as* and to have renown among all nations. [n: (CASS.)] He prays first to be delivered, ascending from the lowest things to the highest: he prays also for the salvation of the Gentiles. [n: (AUG.)] Here the whole Christ speaks, namely the head and the body.

VERS. 2.-- *Deliver me. [n: (Ibid.)]* While he who is without stain asks this, he shows what we must needs ask. *From my enemies.* [n: (CASS.)] There are four kinds of adversaries, [0929B] etc., *as far as* of these therefore he says: *Deliver me,* etc.

VERS. 4.-- *The mighty.* [n: (AUG.)] There are also those who presume upon their own justice, etc., *as far as* but *hoping in riches they have prevailed in their vanity.*

VERS. 5.-- *Iniquity.* [n: (CASS.)] Ill-will: *sin,* of the deed.

VERS. 6.-- *Arise.* [n: (AUG.)] Because thou seemest to sleep, *To meet me.* As though I run to thee by deserving: and do thou run to meet me, that is, render according to my desert. *See. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, cause it to be seen how I run to thee, etc., *as far as* after thou hast seen me die, thou believest me equal to the Father.

VERS. 7.-- *They shall be converted.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalmus. The salvation of the Gentiles that was prayed for is affirmed, and, for him who is yet to pray, the salvation of the Jews. *At evening,* that is, later, after the ascension and the sending of [0929C] the Spirit, etc., *as far as* I fast twice in the week. *They shall go round about.* [n: (AUG.)] They shall fortify the Churches against the assaults of heretics. And, behold, they shall speak openly.

VERS. 8.-- *And a sword.* That they may slay and eat. *For who hath heard?* As though none had: so shall all wonder, etc., *as far as* thou shalt bring to nothing, or convert. *A sword. [n: (Ibid.)]* This is the sword twice sharpened, cutting with either Testament, wherewith Peter slays the creeping things shown to him in the dish, when it was said to him: *Arise, Peter, slay and eat.* [var: Vulg. Act 10:13 *occide, et manduca*, "kill, and eat"]

VERS. 9.-- *Thou shalt laugh them to scorn unto nothing. [n: (Ibid.)]* Those who say, *Who hath heard?* Why? Because he will count it as nothing to convert all the Gentiles, because all the Gentiles are to be Christian; and you say, who hath heard?

[0929D]VERS. 10.-- *I will keep my strength to thee, for God is my protector, O God. [n: (Ibid.)]* Even though I could destroy the Jews, who as mighty men rushed in upon me, yet I permit them to live, keeping my strength for thee, that is, for thy disposing.

VERS. 12.-- *He hath shown. [n: (Ibid.)]* that is, how great a mercy he has toward me he has shown me in those toward whom he has none; whence the Apostle: *He endured the vessels of wrath, fitted, and unto destruction, with much patience, that he might make known his riches upon the vessels of mercy [n: (Rom. IX)]*. [var: Vulg. Rom 9:22–23 *sustinuit in multa patientia vasa irae, apta in interitum, ut ostenderet divitias gloriae suae*, "the riches of his glory"] *Slay them not.* It may be taken of the Jews, etc., *as far as* it is shown what mercy belongs to *those grafted in.* [n: (CASS.)] To one considering what is to be done concerning them, if they be not witnesses of the law and of the death of Christ. *Lest at any time they forget.* [0930A] [n: (AUG.)] Even though they have not just now forgotten, since they are not witnesses of the law and of the death of Christ. *In thy strength.* Because thou art strong, not they. *Bring them down,* there was a reproach there to all. Or, bring them down in that wherein they are evil, that the good may rise: as in Paul, because Paul would not rise, unless Saul first fell [n: (Act. IX)].

VERS. 13, 14.-- *The sin of their mouth. [n: (Ibid.)]* It is not them, but *the sin,* that thou art to slay, so that they may fear him whom they wished to destroy, now raised up again. [n: (CASS.)] *And let them be taken* when converted; because their deed, etc., *as far as* but pardon blotted out the crime. *In their pride.* [n: (AUG.)] Pride is the companion of envy: nor can it come about that a proud man should not envy: whence the devil also envies man. *And of cursing.* Or of the curse, which you thought a curse, etc., *as far as* because in these things they were caught. [0930B] *They shall be declared in consummation.* Or, consummations shall be declared, that is, it shall be preached by the Apostles: Where the sin of exercise and of lying abounded, the grace of perfection did more abound; and it belonged to the medicine of man's humility and weakness. *In the wrath of consummation.* For there is a wrath, that is, a vengeance of God, *of consummation,* because God sometimes takes vengeance in order to perfect; and there is one *of consuming,* as when he shall say: *Go, ye cursed, into everlasting fire. And they shall know.* [var: Vulg. Mt 25:41 *Discedite a me maledicti in ignem aeternum*, "Depart from me, ye cursed"] For before this the Jews seemed just to themselves: but it is shown to them that this is not so without Christ, who rules over Jacob: and, their pride laid aside, they know themselves equal to the Gentiles, because all have sinned.

VERS. 15.-- *They shall be converted at evening.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalmus. The salvation of the Jews is affirmed, and, both [0930C] walls being gathered in, thanks are given.

VERS. 16.-- *They shall murmur. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or, *they shall murmur,* that is, they shall grieve that even now they are so far scattered; or, *they shall murmur* against the slothful, that is, they shall rebuke others who are unwilling to believe.

VERS. 17.-- *But I. [n: (Ibid.)]* Here this psalm is concluded, where, both walls being joined in the corner stone, the whole Church now rejoices. *And I will exalt.* When the times are past, when the night of the world shall have gone by, as Christ also rose in the morning: whence *In the evening weeping shall have place, and in the morning gladness.* The Apostles wept in the evening for him who was dead; in the morning they rejoiced at him rising again. *Mercy.* Because thou givest all things freely: though he has said many things, yet not all, in this one thing he has comprehended all.
