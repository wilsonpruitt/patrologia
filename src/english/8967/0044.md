## PSALM LIX.

[0930D]VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, for those who shall be changed.* [n: (AUG.)] It is not read in the histories, etc., *as far as* a man unto the faith; earthly men, that they may live as heavenly.

[n: (BASIL. et THEOD.)] The history of the kings describes these things. But he foresees that the people will transgress, that they are to be led away captive, and that, repenting, they are to be brought back. Therefore he inscribed it: *For those who shall be changed. Doctrine.* [var: Vulg. Ps 59:1 *pro his qui immutabuntur … in doctrinam*, "for them that shall be changed … unto doctrine"] [n: (AUG.)] *They are changed* into doctrine, etc., *as far as Edom*, the earthly man, his life into a heavenly one. *And he smote.* When he smote Edom with the sword of the spirit, etc., *as far as* he teaches that blessed is the contrition through which he proves those who are to be changed for things eternal.

[0931A]VERS. 3.-- *O God, thou hast cast us off.* [n: (CASS.)] First are recounted the strokes inflicted for the avoiding of eternal punishments: *Thou hast cast off.* In Adam. *And thou hast destroyed* from immortality. Because *thou art angry* for our sins, and yet *hast had mercy,* through the Son, in such wise that thou hast moved us out of error: *And thou hast troubled,* [var: Vulg. Ps 59:4 *conturbasti*, "hast disturbed"] penitence: as therefore thou hast begun, complete it, heal.

VERS. 5.-- *Thou hast shewn.* [n: (AUG.)] For when a man has been changed for the better, etc., *as far as* to bear adversities even unto the end. *Hard things.* [n: (CASS.)] That is, the miseries of time, which are delightful to us through charity, and grievous through the infirmity of the flesh. *With wine,* that is, with tribulation, by which the sufferer is purged, as a wound is by wine: *with wine,* spiritual, whence not vices, but compunction.

VERS. 6.-- *A bow.* [n: (AUG.)] The further back it is drawn, [0931B] the more violently is the arrow carried forward, that is, the longer judgment is deferred, the more heavily will it come. *And that they may be delivered.* [var: Vulg. Ps 59:6 *ut liberentur*, a purpose clause, "that they may be delivered"] [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalmus. Concerning the deliverance which he prays for and which is affirmed, for the sake of which the strokes are welcome.

VERS. 7.-- *Thy right hand.* [n: (AUG.)] That we may stand at the right hand, etc., *as far as* concerning earthly things not save profitably.

VERS. 8.-- *God hath spoken. [n: (Ibid.)]* With this confidence I pray, which God promised in Christ, etc., *as far as* it is the voice of the Church: *I will rejoice. Sichem.* It is interpreted *shoulders,* that is, the burden of Christ, etc., *as far as* others may bear the yoke of Christ. *The vale of tabernacles:* because of the sheep of Jacob it signifies the Jews, who also are divided; because some believe, others do not, that is, of the Jews [0931C] I shall have not a few.

VERS. 9.-- *Manasses. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or, Manasses is every man who, having forgotten his former life, etc., *as far as: and thou shalt not remember the reproach of thy widowhood. Ephraim.* Fruit-bearing: and this is the *strength of the head,* that is, of Christ: for the dying grain has power in itself, that it might bring forth fruit.

VERS. 10.-- *Moab is the pot of my hope. [n: (Ibid.)]* Daughters ill-using their father, etc., *as far as* when charity burns against the pot of iniquity. [n: (CASS.)] *Moab is the pot of my hope.* Or by *Moab,* etc., *as far as* whence there is hope of life for me. [n: (HIER.)] *A pot,* whence: *I see a boiling pot,* etc., *as far as:* From him, therefore, the pot of tribulations boils up in the Church.

VERS. 11.-- *A fortified city.* [n: (CASS.)] The [0931D] Jerusalem that is to come. *Into Idumaea,* that is, even to earthly men, that, the number being filled, it might come together unto blessedness.

VERS. 12.-- *Thou wilt not go forth.* [n: (AUG.)] But thou wilt work within, and so he will lead thee better, because thus he is seen to fight not for the temporal life, but for the life to come.

## PSALM LX.

VERS. 3.-- *I cried while my heart was in anguish.* [n: (CASS.)] In anguish, etc., *as far as* there follows, of the cry heard, on the rock Christ. *Thou hast exalted me.* [n: (AUG.)] Out of the depth of vices. [n: (CASS.)] And thou hast led me exalted. [n: (AUG.)] *On a rock,* and therefore I am not overcome; *Thou hast exalted:* by the example of this man a great hope of being heard is afforded to others. *Thou hast led [n: (Ibid.)] me,* through thee *the way,* unto thee *the truth and the life.*

[0932A]VERS. 5.-- *I will dwell.* [n: (CASS.)] He trusts in the benefits of God, that against the perils of the world he may persevere safe in the tabernacle of God. *For ever.* Until the ages be accomplished; for although many are against him: *I shall be protected under the covering. I shall be protected under the covering of the wings.* Which neither burden, and turn away every hurt. For ever, not one man for ever, but the Church, which until the end of the world dwells on the earth, and is never rooted up by adversities.

VERS. 6.-- *For thou, my God, hast heard my prayer,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] Diapsalma. He now affirms what has been heard concerning the inheritance possessed or to be possessed, whence he promises praises. *Thou hast given.* Nor will I say, thou wilt give: so certain am I, as though I were already in it.

VERS. 7.-- *Day upon day.* [n: (AUG.)] Whatever thou wilt, [0932B] thou sayest of eternity, etc., *as far as* but let the saints reign with him for ever. Or *thou wilt give an inheritance.* [n: (CASS.)] This one, namely, because it is upon the days of Christ, etc., *as far as* because that king abides for ever, so also do they. *Mercy and truth.* [n: (AUG.)] It is *mercy,* because he forgives sins not by merits but by goodness, and promises life. *Truth,* because he does not deceive, but renders what he promised. *Mercy* we ought to follow toward the weak and toward enemies. *Truth,* by not sinning, that he may be just. [n: (CASS.)] *Mercy and truth.* Here is the conclusion of the psalm, etc., *as far as: who shall seek them for him?* [var: Vulg. Ps 60:8 *misericordiam et veritatem ejus quis requiret*, "who shall seek his mercy and truth"] [n: (AUG.)] That no one may seek to learn these things in books, and afterwards live to himself, not to him.
