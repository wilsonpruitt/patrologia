## PSALM LXIII.

[n: (CASS.)] This psalm treats briefly [cj: *isto*; read *iste*], etc., *as far as* that in sufferings we may hope in the Lord. [n: (AUG.)] As our head did, so let us pray in tribulations.

VERS. 2.-- *Hear, O God, my prayer.* That thou mayest exalt, *and deliver,* because I entreat. *From the fear of the enemy.* [n: (CASS.)] But not from the fear of death.

VERS. 3.-- *Thou hast protected me.* [n: (AUG.)] This was done in the head, [0935B] which the members also experience: by this example the rest are made firm.

VERS. 4.-- *For they have whetted their tongues like a sword.* Or, those namely who are malicious and who work iniquity. *A bow.* Hidden snares, as through a disciple, which is a bitter thing, that a guest at table should be a betrayer. *Bitter.*

VERS. 6.-- *They will shoot suddenly. [n: (Ibid.)]* Treacherously, as at one who does not know, so it seems to them who believe that they deceive God. *They will not fear. [n: (Ibid.)]* O hard heart! to kill a man who raised the dead, and thereafter not to fear. *They have made firm.* [n: (CASS.)] By obstinacy, not by truth, their word. *He is guilty of death:* or, *It is expedient that one should die. For themselves. [n: (Ibid.)]* Not for the Lord, but for themselves, when they say: *His blood be upon us and upon our [0935C] children. That they might hide.* The way of the wicked; he who prepares a deceit thinks that he deceives.

VERS. 7.-- *They have searched out.* [n: (CASS.)] How they might accuse the just man, or how they might destroy him, lest the world should go after him. [n: (AUG.)] Searchings, that is, bitter and hidden counsels; but the more keenly they think, the more do they fall away from truth and equity, and are plunged into darkness: for because they do not see God, they think that they are not seen by God. *A man shall come.* [n: (CASS.)] For he who searches out unjust things, etc., *as far as* but it grows great in the heart of man. [n: (AUG.)] They searched out, but in vain, because of his own accord the man shall come to the passion. *To a deep heart. [n: (Ibid.)]* He had one, because he did this according to a deep counsel.

*The arrows of little ones are made.* [n: (CASS.)] He derides the enumerated [0935D] wickedness of the Jews, who were the more troubled when the resurrection was announced, while the just rejoiced.

VERS. 9.-- *Troubled. [n: (Ibid.)]* They were, who, seeking counsel for their own salvation, said: What *shall we do, men and brethren?*

VERS. 10.-- *And every man feared.* [n: (AUG.)] If a man fears, let not a man terrify him from proclaiming, as the Apostles did, whom the chief priests threatened, saying that they should not preach in the name of Jesus.

*And they understood his doings. [n: (Ibid.)]* Why did he suffer? why did he not come down from the cross? because he had to die and to rise again: which then was not understood.

VERS. 11.-- *The just shall rejoice in the Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] A conclusion from the things aforesaid, and the promise of the just is shown. [0936A] *All. [n: (Ibid.)]* Do not think that it is said of the apostolic men alone.
