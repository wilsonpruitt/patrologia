## PSALM LXV.

*Unto the end.* [n: (AUG.)] The Jews from the justice of the law hoped for themselves temporal goods, and the resurrection, etc., *as far as* that for the faith of the Gentiles he would call them to the same hope of resurrection.

[0938B]VERS. 1.-- *Shout with joy to God, all the earth,* etc. [n: (CAS.)] First he admonishes all to rejoice over the resurrection of Christ, which gives life to all.

VERS. 3.-- *Give ye glory.* [n: (AUG.)] Not as the Jews, who give justice to their own merits, and envy the Gentiles their grace. *How terrible. [n: (Ibid.)]* If thou rejoicest, fear also, lest what has been given to the humble be taken away from the proud: as was done to the Jews. Do not therefore insult over the broken branches, but fear, lest perchance he spare not thee either. *Thy works, O Lord.* [n: (CAS.)] Briefly he calls the mysteries of the incarnation works, that God is man, that he suffered, died, and the like. *In the multitude of thy strength.* [n: (AUG.)] Here he hints at the resurrection, to which, the lying Jews being shut out, the Gentiles are admitted.

[n: (BEDA.)] The Lord rose again; many believed in him, etc., *as far as* they drank through grace. *They shall lie.* [0938C] They are works of the earth, which the enemies belie, against those who lie, *let all the earth adore,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] *They shall lie.* Enemies are wont to lie, etc., *as far as* let all the earth adore.

VERS. 5.-- *Come ye.* [n: (CAS.)] Diapsalma. He invites beginners to consider the works of God, that one faith may join those whom one reward awaits: where, setting forth those works, he plainly says that the resurrection pertains to the Gentiles, the Jews being repressed.

VERS. 6.-- *Who turneth. [n: (Ibid.)]* The works in which he is terrible, and what they are, he subjoins: *Who turneth the sea.* In the river Jordan according to the letter, mystically in the passing world. *In the river. [n: (Ibid.)]* Although sins are forgiven, yet in the course of mortality they are still there, for immortality is not at once.

[0938D] *In the river.* [n: (AUG.)] The river is the mortality of the world, where some come and others depart: but he who believes in Christ passes over on foot, humble in himself, not on horseback and proud. *There we shall rejoice. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or thus: The works are terrible, etc., *as far as* and in these Christians rejoice. *In him. [n: (Ibid.)]* When he shall be all in all, or in him who rules, in his own strength.

VERS. 7.-- *Let them not be exalted. [n: (Ibid.)]* On account of the law, as though more were owed to them. *For all have sinned,* etc. [n: (Rom. III.)]

VERS. 8.-- *Bless ye.* [n: (CAS.)] Diapsalma. He admonishes the Gentiles to bless God, who, although he tries them by tribulations, yet leads them to the kingdom; for these things seem to stand in the way of the promised resurrection. *Make ye heard.* [0939A] [n: (AUG.)] Openly, so that he who loves God loves his neighbour also. *The voice of his praise.* Which the Father speaks of the Son: *This is my Son.*

VERS. 9.-- *Who hath set my soul. [n: (Ibid.)]* Here he enumerates for what causes he ought to be preached.

VERS. -- *For thou hast proved us.* [n: (AUG.)] Thou hast not given us over unto commotion. Because thou hast proved us, thou hast not cast us headlong, thou hast proved us by adversities, by the fire of charity, which does not suffer us to feel torments, or, thou hast tried us by the fire of tribulation, etc.

VERS. 12.-- *A snare.* [n: (AUG.)] Not one that deceives, but one that proves. *We have passed over.* [n: (CAS.; AUG.)] Thou all these things, and we, etc., *as far as* this happens to us in temptations.

VERS. 13.-- *I will go in.* [n: (CAS.)] Behold what good things against [0939B] the evils that went before, that he may strengthen us. *In holocausts.* That is, that I may offer myself to thy sight, etc., *as far as* but he withdrew that which is dearer, himself.

VERS. 14.-- *They have distinguished.* [n: (AUG.)] He distinguishes who says that he himself is nothing, and God all things; and that he himself has need of God, not God of him. *And my mouth hath spoken.* Whom before all men I have declared to prevail above all other vows in my tribulation, not overcome by tribulation.

VERS. 15.-- *Marrowy holocausts.* Myself, with the marrow of inmost love. *With the burnt offering of rams. [n: (Ibid.)]* Or, with *the burnt offering,* which is prayer; and with *rams,* because the leaders of the flock pray most of all for the flock. *With the burnt offering.* [n: (HIER., AUG.)] With the prayer of the Apostles, because that pleases God which is done by their tradition. Or with the burning and destruction of brutishness and of resistance. [0939C] Oxen; stiffness of neck; *with he-goats,* wantonness; these vices being slain in me. *I will offer thee oxen.* [n: (AUG.)] If those who thresh are offered, yet they do not remain sinners: but healed through penance they are offered; and they not by themselves, but rather joined to the oxen, because they have made themselves friends of the mammon of iniquity.

VERS. 16.-- *Come ye, hear.* Diapsalma. All things now being set forth, he again admonishes not so much the untaught, but the devout, that by the example of his own deliverance they may trust the more in the Lord: blessing him for the hearing of his prayer. So do I, and O ye Gentiles, *come ye.*

VERS. 17.-- *To him I have cried with my mouth.* [n: (CAS.)] He sets forth how great things he did for his own soul, subjoining: *To him,* etc. This he did for my soul, that I should cry to him, while the Gentiles cried to idols: and what I speak without [0939D] I have within, and not iniquity, because thus is the purity of prayer, it is heard. *I have cried and exalted in my heart.* [var: Vulg. Ps 65:17 *et exaltavi sub lingua mea*, "and I extolled under my tongue"] [n: (AUG.)] That is, I have preached him publicly, and confessed him in secret. What I have in the mouth, this I have in the heart also: yet *I will offer marrowy holocausts.* By these two the Christian is made perfect.

VERS. 18.-- *Mine.* That is a man's own which is not perverse; he sets it forth with reason, that they may learn to labour first before they seek the reward.

VERS. 20.-- *My prayer,* etc. These two are joined together; if prayer is not suspended, neither is mercy deferred.

## PSALM LXVI.

*Unto the end.* [n: (CAS.)] After the canticle of the resurrection, the Prophet subjoins his own [0940A] entreaty, that the blessed may be led to the knowledge of God. He prophesies the blessing of all through the coming of Christ. [n: (AUG.)] Because in the preceding psalm he had admonished us to bless God, which we cannot do unless he first bless us; therefore he prays for this, to be blessed, saying: *May God have mercy on us and bless us,* etc.

VERS. 2.-- *May God have mercy. [n: (Ibid.)]* The blessing of God is first, which is as it were a rain watering the earth of our mind: thence we bless him, which is as it were the fruit; and in both it is we that grow, not he. Let him therefore who desires the rain of the Lord's blessing say: May God have mercy on us, etc. *May God have mercy on us and bless us.* [n: (CAS.)] But many who wish, etc., *as far as* and because Christ is known among the nations: [0940B] *Let them confess,* etc. *May he bless us. [n: (Ibid.)]* The Creator blesses the creature, by multiplying it, in number and in merit. The creature blesses the Creator, by praising him with heart, voice and work, and by ascribing to him every good thing that it has.

VERS. 4.-- *Let the peoples confess to thee, O God. [n: (Ibid.)]* After knowledge he exhorts them to confess, for direction and for judgment.

VERS. 5.-- *Let them be glad and rejoice.* [n: (AUG.)] Because, having now confessed, they do not fear the judgments to come, to whom Christ is an advocate, not a judge, *let them be glad.*

VERS. 6.-- *Let the peoples confess to thee, O God.* [n: (CAS.)] The same admonition, tending to another thing, because the earth has given her fruit, and although the beginning of the sense is the same, yet it tends to different things.

[0940C]VERS. 7.-- *The earth hath given her fruit.* [n: (HIER.)] To Mary, Christ, etc., *as far as:* But not she alone owes this, nay rather, *let him bless* also *us. God, our God. [n: (Ibid.)]* Three times, the Trinity, *him,* the unity. *And let them fear him.* [n: (CAS.)] Fear is asked for in the midst of benefits, that what is entrusted may be warily kept: the fear of God begins and completes; he who has learned to fear does not cease to love.
