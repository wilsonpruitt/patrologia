## PSALM LXVII. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *In the churches bless ye.* [n: (CAS.)] Diapsalma. ⟦s2⟧ He prays and affirms that this beginning may be enlarged and confirmed, and for its sake that the heretics also may be destroyed. [n: (AUG.)] *In the churches.* ⟦s3⟧ These are the young maidens playing on timbrels, lest anyone should think carnally of the dances of luxury. ⟦s4⟧ For the churches are young girls adorned with new grace. ⟦s5⟧ *In the churches,* not elsewhere, not in other conventicles. ⟦s6⟧ *From the fountains of Israel.* [n: (CAS.)] That you may preach unto their imitation, as is fitting: because they are the princes of faith, of hope and of charity, and through this Benjamin, that is, the son of the right hand.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0944C]VERS. 28.-- *There Benjamin,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Princes,* etc. [n: (Ibid.)] By the names of the tribes he points to Christ and the Apostles. ⟦s3⟧ *There,* namely in the temple of Jerusalem, or in the Church. ⟦s4⟧ The interpretation of the names also suits the Church. [n: (CAS.)] Perhaps the Apostles were of these tribes [sic: *Apo-toli*], etc., *as far as: confession* in faith, *fortitude* in hope, *breadth* in charity. ⟦s5⟧ *In the height of mind,* [var: Vulg. Ps 67:28 *in mentis excessu*, "in ecstasy of mind"] or, as the Greek word properly stands, etc., *as far as* as a faithful and prudent steward, he divided.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *Give command, O God, to thy strength. [n: (Ibid.)]* O God, make the Son known; as it were: what I announce as to come, do thou perfect. ⟦s2⟧ Or, *to thy strength,* that is, enjoin it upon thy strong ones, that they may announce thee. ⟦s3⟧ Or, O Father, commit thy work *to thy strength,* to the Son, or to the Holy Spirit. [n: (AUG.)] *For God commendeth his charity toward us, since, [0944D] when we were sinners, Christ died for us:* which he prays well and confidently. ⟦s4⟧ *Confirm this, O God, which thou hast wrought.* ⟦s5⟧ By making the beginning.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *From thy temple in Jerusalem.* ⟦s2⟧ Which is free, our mother, which is the temple, from which is every best gift; from that temple, therefore. ⟦s3⟧ *The kings of the earth.* ⟦s4⟧ Or, particular ones shall offer thee gifts, that is, sacrifices of praise. ⟦s5⟧ But because the heretics clamour against this praise.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Rebuke the wild beasts of the reeds.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, those who are moved by every wind of doctrine. ⟦s2⟧ *That they may shut out them that are approved.* ⟦s3⟧ This is what the Apostle says: *That they who are approved may be made manifest [n: (I Cor. XI)]*; or, let the approved shut them out from among the cows. ⟦s4⟧ For the stable, the strong, and the grave have not wherewith to seduce: *In the mighty people I will praise [0945A] thee. ⟦s5⟧ Scatter thou.* ⟦s6⟧ Rebuke the wild beasts, and also scatter those that dissent. ⟦s7⟧ *The nations that will wars.* ⟦s8⟧ Heretics and others consenting to them, whom he calls nations because of the kinds of the diverse sects, lest a series of successions there confirm the error.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *Out of Egypt, Ethiopia.* [sic: *Aegppto*] [n: (AUG.)] By the name of Egypt or of Ethiopia he signifies the faith of all nations, the whole from a part. ⟦s2⟧ He prophesies therefore that the heralds to come are not from the Jews alone, but from the Gentiles. [n: (HIER.)] Ethiopia, the Gentile world black with sins, hastens to give her hands to God. ⟦s3⟧ A likeness taken from the conquered, who, their arms laid down, give them to the victors, lest they perish. ⟦s4⟧ *His hand shall prevent. [n: (Ibid.)]* That is, his vengeance, through confession, lest, remaining in their sins, they be punished.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *To God, kingdoms.* ⟦s2⟧ That *to God* may stand at the beginning [0945B] of the following verse. ⟦s3⟧ Or rather it stands at the end of the preceding verse, *to God.* ⟦s4⟧ And faith is commended (as if to say, a man is not justified by preceding works) which begins to work through love. ⟦s5⟧ Ethiopia therefore will believe God, and so *shall prevent* his works, that is, Ethiopia's, which in the Greek is not ambiguous — that is, by believing in God she shall prevent her own works. ⟦s6⟧ Or *his,* Egypt's, to God, that is, unto God. ⟦s7⟧ *Ye kingdoms of the earth, sing to God.* [n: (AUG.)] Having run through all the things which we now behold fulfilled, he exhorts to the praise of Christ, and foretells his coming that is to be.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Sing ye psalms.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalma. ⟦s2⟧ *Sing ye psalms:* he will come who ascended, where he will make the saints perfect.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *He ascendeth. [n: (Ibid.)]* according as he is man. ⟦s2⟧ For according as he is God, equal to the Father, [0945C] above the heaven of heaven, above the loftier creatures, so that he had nothing above himself. [n: (HIER., CAS.)] *The East.* ⟦s3⟧ The Word begotten of the Father. ⟦s4⟧ *And he ascended from the east* [var: Vulg. Ps 67:34 *ad orientem*, "to the east"; the gloss takes up that reading next] by the power of the Word.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To the east.* ⟦s2⟧ He has expressed the place where he rose again and whence he ascended.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] *To the east,* of our illumination. ⟦s2⟧ Or because Jerusalem is in the East. ⟦s3⟧ *To his voice. [n: (Ibid.)]* He who was lowly in the passion will give the voice of power in the common resurrection. ⟦s4⟧ *The voice of power.* [n: (CAS.)] The efficacy of raising up all the dead.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 35.-- *His magnificence and power.* [n: (AUG.)] Because then there will no longer be a mingling of the wicked, thence his magnificence over the true Israel will be evident. ⟦s2⟧ *In the clouds.* ⟦s3⟧ Because he will not come alone to the judgment, but [0945D] with the elders of the people, who are the clouds: and lest thou take them otherwise, he adds: *Wonderful in his saints.*

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (EUTHYM.)] By *clouds.* ⟦s2⟧ Here he understands the Apostles themselves [sic: *Aposlos*], as existing above, and standing high away from earthly affairs, and as it were full of spiritual water, which they drew from the depths of the Holy Spirit, that they might water the fields and plains of souls, that is, the believers themselves.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *He himself will give power and strength to his people; blessed be God.* (CAS). ⟦s2⟧ He has promised great things, lest anyone, because of human infirmity, should despair of coming to such great rewards.
