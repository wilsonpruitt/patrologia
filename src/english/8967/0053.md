## PSALM LXX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end.* [var: Vulg. Ps 70:1 has no *In finem*; the title begins *Psalmus David, filiorum Jonadab, et priorum captivorum*] [n: (AUG.)] Concerning the commendation of grace, etc., *as far as* grace is commended in almost every single syllable.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the sons of Jonadab.* [n: (ID.)] To sons that are willing, etc., *as far as* who of themselves are evil.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CAS.)] Or thus: we are admonished in the title to have the devotion of the sons of Jonadab, etc., *as far as* but they, by their accustomed malice, remained in obstinacy.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *In thee, O Lord, have I hoped.* ⟦s2⟧ The just man in general [0952A] speaks, from the beginning of the world unto its end: placing all his hope in God, he commends his grace, lest we presume upon false things in ourselves.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CAS.)] Or, the former captives are in the world, from which one can escape: the second in hell. ⟦s2⟧ Or, *of the former,* that is, of those who were taken by vices before they became sons of Christ. ⟦s3⟧ This whole psalm commends with the utmost earnestness the grace of God, which is given freely.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Be thou unto me a God, a protector, and a place of strength,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Two things he promised, two others he renders.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Of the sinner.* [n: (AUG.)] There are two kinds of sinners: some received the law, as the Jews and the Christians; others did not, as the pagans.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *From my youth.* [n: (CAS.)] That is, from my conversion, or from the time when I began to fight against the devil. [0952B] [n: (AUG.)] *From my youth,* from the time when I began to hope in thee, etc., *as far as* a young man fights, but falls, unless God be his hope.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *In thee is my song.* [n: (CAS.)] It is a delight to me to be in thee. ⟦s2⟧ *Always.* ⟦s3⟧ That the benefits may be unbroken. ⟦s4⟧ *Always.* [n: (AUG.)] Both in the present, etc., *as far as* against which nothing stands.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Let my mouth be filled.* [n: (CAS.)] He has told the benefits; now he asks that he may be able to give thanks. ⟦s2⟧ For if God does not fill the mouth, desire does not burn. ⟦s3⟧ *All the day thy greatness.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, without intermission, etc., *as far as* under the name of greatness therefore he includes all things.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Cast me not off.* [n: (CAS.)] Concerning the grace to be given in the time of old age and of decrepitude. ⟦s2⟧ *Of old age.* [n: (AUG.)] [0952C] Old age was in Christ, when he was made a wonder, when he was made weak upon the cross and mocked; and it is called old age because he took it on from oldness, whence: *Our old man is crucified with him [n: (Rom. VI)]*.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *For my enemies have said.* [n: (AUG.)] This is said of Christ, who among the hands of his enemies was made weak, and, once seized, as though he could do nothing. ⟦s2⟧ But here this is said of his members, because he had such things also from our person.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *God hath forsaken him.* [n: (AUG.)] Whence elsewhere: *Why hast thou forsaken me?* that is, why do they think me forsaken, to their own hurt. ⟦s2⟧ *For if they had known it, they would never have crucified the Lord of glory.*

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Depart not from me.* [n: (CAS.)] They think him far off while he delays; *depart not,* that he may expose [them] to graver things [0952D].

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Let them be confounded,* etc. [n: (CAS.)] Because the things ordained of God are not changed. ⟦s2⟧ *That detract my soul.* ⟦s3⟧ Otherwise, that set my soul at strife. [n: (AUG.)] That is, provoking us to resist God, which is to wage war against God; as those do to whom God is not pleasing in their adversities, which is to hold him unjust. ⟦s4⟧ The soul is also set at strife if it hates those from whom it suffers. ⟦s5+6⟧ *That seek.* [n: (ID.)] As Job's wife did, who said: *Say somewhat against God, and die [n: (Job. VII)]*. [var: Vulg. Job 2:9 *Benedic Deo, et morere*, "Bless God, and die"] As also Tobias's wife says: *Where are thy justices? [n: (Tob. IV, 2.)]* [var: Vulg. Tob 2:22 *Manifeste vana facta est spes tua*, "Manifestly thy hope is become vain"] She said this that God might be displeasing to him.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *And I will add.* [n: (AUG.)] The addition of praise is, after the creation of all things, the praise concerning the incarnation [0953A] and the resurrection, which the lines below teach. ⟦s2⟧ *All.* [n: (AUG.)] Because, though he be praised in all other things, not yet in this, that he raised up our flesh unto the eternal way [cj: *viam*; read *vitam*, "life"], which is the praise added for the resurrection of Christ. ⟦s3⟧ Or, all praise would be there, and nothing would be lacking to praise, if he condemned sinners, because it would be justice; but because thou deliverest even these: *I will add above all thy praise.*

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *My mouth shall show forth justice.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ; but lest thou take it otherwise, he adds: *Salvation.* ⟦s3⟧ Why? because it is not approved that any man is justified by the works of the law. ⟦s4⟧ And this is: *Because I have not known learning,* that is, the figures of the law, through which I might be made mighty. ⟦s5⟧ *All the day.* [n: (AUG.)] Or thus, etc., *as far as* let him serve justice. ⟦s6⟧ *Because I have not known learning.* [n: (ID.)] I will preach thy salvation all the day, etc., *as far as* because [0953B] in habit he was found as a man [n: (Philip. II)], and the dead man rises.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17, 18.-- *Thou hast taught me.* [n: (CAS.)] Through books, or by inspiring even until now: through the spaces of the times doctrine has grown. ⟦s2⟧ *Wondrous things.* ⟦s3⟧ That out of an unjust man a just man is made. ⟦s4⟧ *Wondrous things.* [n: (AUG.)] What is more wonderful than to raise the dead? etc., *as far as* this is "from my youth" and "now". ⟦s5⟧ *Youth.* [n: (ID.)] Some mark four ages of the Church, others that only three are distinguished, signifying the same by *now* and *old age.* ⟦s6⟧ Whence the youth of the Church was when the courage of the martyrs did battle. [n: (CAS.)] But the time going forward is "now", when faith advances through the churches. [n: (AUG.)] Old age, that is, the heavy time of life, when the charity of many shall begin to grow cold, as the times of the world diminish. ⟦s7⟧ *Decrepitude.* [n: (CAS.)] The declining time, etc., [0953C] *as far as* against whom the Lord says: *I am with you even unto the consummation of the world,* etc. [n: (Matth. XXI)].-- *Until I show forth thy arm.* [n: (CAS.)] While through the incarnation he sets the captives free. ⟦s8⟧ And the justice of judgment, that is, of punishing, whereby by a just judgment he condemns the sinning man.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19, 20.-- *Even unto the highest.* ⟦s2⟧ And lest thou think that man alone stands in need of grace, etc., *as far as* he holds them together in that which they are.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Even unto the highest.* ⟦s2⟧ Because the man who is God sits at the right hand of the Father, reigning with him. ⟦s3⟧ *The great things thou hast done.* ⟦s4⟧ Both are wonderful: that man is redeemed through grace, who through justice had been condemned.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *O God, who is like to thee? ⟦s2⟧ How great troubles hast thou shown me, many and grievous, and turning thou hast brought me to life again, [0953D] and out of the depths of the earth,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ To what person what grace belongs — namely to the captive person: the twofold resurrection of mind and of body; whence he promises thanksgiving.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *In the vessels of psalm.* [n: (CAS.)] In the quickening of the spirit, or in the contemplative life.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *My soul which thou hast redeemed.* [n: (AUG.)] With those inward lips with which it silently cries out to God.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *When they shall be confounded and put to shame.* ⟦s2⟧ Then is there place for true and secure exaltation, when it is not permitted to the ungodly to bear rule.
