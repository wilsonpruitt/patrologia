## PSALM LXXII.

[0956C]VERS. 1.-- *How good.* [n: (CAS.)] He admonishes that we should praise God not for temporal things, but for eternal ones.

VERS. 2.-- *Feet.* [n: (CAS.)] For the judgment of the mind, which was at variance with the truth. *Steps,* headlong acts; *almost,* because he quickly returned.

VERS. 3, 4.-- *The peace of sinners.* [n: (AUG.)] Temporal, fleeting, perishable. *Seeing.* Not understanding. Because this peace is seen, it is not understood, it eludes the eyes, for it is fleeting and perishable. [n: (CAS.)] *The peace of sinners, for there is no turning aside to their death.* [var: Vulg. Ps 72:4 *quia non est respectus morti eorum*, "no regard for their death"] That is, they do not die quickly, but live long; although *a stroke* is sometimes not firm, as the scourge of correction lasts. *And a firmament in their stroke.* God does not have mercy by consolidating their strokes: or, by a likeness, that of an incurable man, [0956D] to whom nothing is forbidden.

VERS. 6.-- *They are covered with their iniquity and impiety.* [n: (CAS.)] Lest they come to their senses; not merely wrapped about, so as at least to have the head free, but wholly covered.

VERS. 7.-- *Their iniquity hath come forth as it were,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] There are some who are evil out of leanness, that is, out of need: others out of abundance, as the poor man steals out of want, the rich out of covetousness. *They have passed over.* [n: (CAS.)] As it were from the straight paths.

VERS. 8.-- *On high.* [n: (AUG.)] Openly, in the open: or, blasphemers against God.

VERS. 9.-- *Into heaven.* [n: (ID.)] As if they were gods; or, against God, whom they blaspheme, saying that God either does not know their own evils, or does not punish them. *The tongue hath passed over the earth,* etc. [var: Vulg. Ps 72:9 *lingua eorum transivit in terra*, "their tongue hath passed on the earth"] [n: (ID.)] Which speaks beyond the measure of humanity, [0957A] etc., *as far as* but their tongue passes beyond human frailty.

VERS. 10.-- *Therefore shall [my people] be converted.* [n: (CAS.)] Expounding Asaph, etc., *as far as* he shall return to it. *Here.* [n: (AUG.)] In this opinion in which I now am. *Full days shall be found in them.* [n: (HIERON.)] They are found perfect, although it does not now appear. [n: (CAS.)] Or, *the days are full,* when Christ came: these therefore are among the Jews, who have known Christ.

VERS. 11.-- *And they said.* [n: (AUG.)] Whence shall they be converted? etc., *as far as* this is why it seemed to them that he did not know this. *How doth he know.* [n: (ID.)] He does not say, He knows not: but he still hesitates and is near to health: and from so grave an infirmity health is the nearer, just as the sick are more feverish when health is at hand.

[0957B]VERS. 13.-- *And I said: Then have I in vain justified my heart,* etc. [n: (CAS.)] These things they said, for the confuting of whom I say these: or still in the person of one doubting. He says that he was deluded by foolish thoughts, so that, when he shall have repudiated them, he may the better provide for us.

VERS. 14.-- *And chastisement.* Otherwise also *my discloser in the morning.* [n: (CAS.)] Behold now the remedy, Christ the discloser, who disclosed the way of truth, and, rising in the morning, transferred our hope from the world to the heavens.

VERS. 15.-- *If I said.* [n: (ID.)] Behold, now he restrains himself by the authority of the saints, and now, with the truth near at hand, he deliberates thus: *If I said. Behold the nation.* [n: (AUG.)] He begins to resolve another incongruity. *I have reproved;* I judged that which is not. *I thought,* by the things aforesaid I wish to prove God not just; but in this manner I think, I do not [0957C] absolve. *Before me,* that is, as regards present things; or, greater than I, surpassing me, unless God open it. Or, behold the nation of thy sons, to which I have appointed, and with which I have sung together, understand it, meets and is over against me [cj: *obvia*; read *obviat*, "meets"], that is, my former preaching meets me, which I made to the Israelites, that they should worship God, who created all things, and who renders to all according to their works: the teaching comes against me, to which I agreed and to which I was fitted. But lest I should reprove the nation of the former fathers, or contradict my former teaching: *I thou[ght],* etc.

*Of thy sons.* For all, Abraham and the rest, teach otherwise, whom I ought to believe: for I am not wiser than all. But lest I should reprove: *I thought,* or, I undertook to know; he is now called back from his fall, who does not presume himself to be knowing against the saints. That I might know [0957D] the truth of this matter, which I did not before, while he was saying that God did not care for these things. I was thinking that I might know this: the first degree of knowledge, when a man knows that he does not know what he seemed before to know: and he repudiates the former sense, while he seeks another.

VERS. 16.-- *Labour is before me.* [n: (AUG.)] As it were a wall stands in my way, etc., *as far as* he sees them condemned in the judgment.

VERS. 17.-- *The sanctuary.* [n: (AUG.)] Christ, who as God discerns the merits of each. Or *the sanctuary,* the Holy of holies, that is, the secret of the divine disposition.

VERS. 17.-- *But yet.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] Behold what this man learned in the sanctuary: that the wicked are deceived here, and afterwards [0958A] at the end are to be forsaken by all; from which evil he himself was freed, who also sought earthly things, but from God; here he says that in this world the wicked are not free and unpunished; because although they are happy, yet they bear guilt and frequently run into ruins. *But yet,* that is, but: and here thou hast set for them this flower and abundance of temporal things, *because of deceits,* etc.

*Because of deceits.* [n: (AUG.)] Or, beside a slippery place. As if: In a deceitful manner thou hast dealt with them, giving evils under the appearance of good. Or, beside a slippery place, such as temporal things are: where they fall who are near by love; for while a deceitful man wishes to cast down another, he sets him beside a slippery place.

VERS. 20.-- *As a dream.* [n: (ID.)] An open comparison. With our eyes closed we see goods which presently, when they are opened, we do not find [sic: *mo*]; in life a dream: in death they awake, [0958B] knowing what it is. *In the city.* In the sight of thy people, or, while thy city is being established. [n: (HIER.)] Or in the heavenly Jerusalem their image, the likenesses of the deformities which they make for themselves: one a lion by ferocity, another a boar by the filth of lust. [n: (CAS.)] Or the glory of time, which is not true, but a likeness of glory: because when truth shall be wanting to them, the image shall perish; nor shall they retain the likeness, who shall have nothing of blessedness, therefore thou wilt destroy them, because through them,

VERS. 21.-- *My heart hath been inflamed.* That is, I envied the happy. *And my reins have been changed.* By which the standing, that is, the constancy of the mind, was dissolved, so that I loved prosperity; it is a grave fault to afford to others an occasion of sinning. *I am brought to nothing.* Clinging to vain things, made injurious to God. *I knew not.* It is a great and deep [0958C] ignorance not to know that thou sinnest. [n: (CAS.)] *In thy city the image.* Or this can be taken of the heretics, etc., *as far as* or in the supercelestial Church. *For it hath been inflamed.* [n: (AUG.)] Behold the conversion of this Asaph, etc., *as far as* they have been changed into spiritual ones

VERS. 24.-- *With,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Because before I was brought to nothing, that is, I went down to those things which are nothing: because I knew not that it was nothing, and afterwards I was made gentle as a beast of burden for the bearing of a load, and I shall always be with thee.

*A beast of burden.* [n: (CAS.)] With God they are in the place of cattle, who think against God: and yet, *erring* in these things, not knowing the judgments of God, I was always with thee in a manner, a stranger to idols, and believing thee, and thou didst hold me.

*Thou hast held my hand.* [n: (AUG.)] Because, although a beast, he did not depart [0958D] from God. *Thou hast held my hand,* that is, the power of the right hand, that is, thou hast covered him, in that he is always with thee, in that he is a son. *He gave,* indeed, *power to be made the sons of God to them that believe in his name. On the left,* a beast, loving these temporal things. Or the *right* hand.

[n: (CAS.)] Not the left. God holds the right hand, while he directs to good works, and delivers us lest we be drowned, as he did Peter. [n: (ID.)] Having seen the evil of the impious, and his own good, he accuses himself of his former state, because he sought earthly things from God, and he invites to the contrary.

[n: (AUG.)] He compares the heavenly reward with his own earthly pleasure, and accuses himself.

*For ever,* etc. From him I shall never be separated any more.

*Who go a-fornicating from thee,* etc. Contrary to this fornication [0959A] is chaste fear, which loves him freely, in whom it has all things.

VERS. 28.-- *In the gates of the daughter of Sion.* [n: (AUG.)] In public, or in the concord of the Apostles, who are the gates of the Church. Because preaching which is outside the unity of the Church is empty. Or, by virtues the Church is the daughter of Sion, that is, of contemplation.
