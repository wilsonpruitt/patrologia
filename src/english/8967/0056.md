## PSALM LXXIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The understanding of Asaph.* [n: (AUG.)] Of the Synagogue, etc., *as far as* that the hardness of the Jews might either be terrified by calamities, or softened. [n: (CAS.)] He admonishes, the figures being left behind, to pass over to the truth.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Why, O God.* [n: (ID.)] Describing the irreparable overthrow of Jerusalem, he asks the cause, wherefore, wondering and complaining; and he prays for the people cast off, and for vengeance [0959B] upon the destroyers, which indeed is useful to them, and has already been done. ⟦s2⟧ *Thou hast cast us off.* [var: Vulg. Ps 73:1 *Ut quid, Deus, repulisti in finem*, without *nos*] [n: (ID.)] As it were strangers to thy protection. ⟦s3⟧ *Unto the end.* ⟦s4⟧ Not even the temple is spared. ⟦s5⟧ He signifies the destruction and the captivity, which came from Titus and Vespasian. ⟦s6⟧ He names sheep, that he may move the mercy of the loving shepherd. ⟦s7⟧ *Thine.* ⟦s8⟧ We take vengeance more temperately upon those whom we remember to have been ours at some time.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Thou hast possessed.* [n: (CASS.)] Or, thou hast created, although he creates and disposes all, yet the Jews especially, to whom he gave the law and the Prophets. ⟦s2⟧ *Thou hast redeemed.* [n: (HIER.)] Thee for a price, that is, through thee a price. ⟦s3⟧ *Mount.* [n: (AUG.)] An eminence; Sion is contemplation, in which, in which thing thou hast dwelt. ⟦s4⟧ Or it is redundant in it. [n: (HIER.)] The mount, or of mount Sion, or in it. ⟦s5⟧ Thus far the deeds of the true Testament; what follows is the truth promised. ⟦s6⟧ By Sion he signifies Jerusalem, [0959C] in which the presence of Christ dwelt, and therefore he entreats that he give it not over to devastation. ⟦s7⟧ *The rod of inheritance.* [n: (AUG.)] That congregation itself is called the rod of inheritance: wishing to possess which, he turned the rod of Moses into a serpent, but when the tail was seized it returned into a rod. [n: (HIER.)] Because death entered into the world by the persuasion of the serpent, the rod is in the serpent, Christ in death. ⟦s8⟧ Whence he also who looked upon the serpent, that is, believes Christ dead, is healed from serpents, that is, from eternal death. ⟦s9⟧ Moses was terrified and fled, so the Apostles when Christ was dying. ⟦s10⟧ The tail being held, that is, the later things, it becomes a rod, because first he was slain, afterwards he rose. ⟦s11⟧ The tail also is the end of the world, in which we return to the hand of God, and are made stable, we who through death, as through a serpent, have flowed away. [n: (AUG.)] But because these things are also of the Gentiles, he expresses of whom he [0959D] speaks, *Mount Sion.* ⟦s12⟧ That is, not of the Gentiles. ⟦s13⟧ Or, this mount Sion, which thou hast inhabited in it: *Lift up thy hand against their pride,* [var: Vulg. Ps 73:3 *Leva manus tuas*, plural] by whom Jerusalem was overthrown, that is, the kings of the Gentiles. ⟦s14⟧ *Unto the end,* because they too have now known Christ.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Lift up.* [n: (CAS.)] Thy power, that thou mayest turn their pride to humility, leading them unto the end, that is, Christ, which also has been fulfilled, because at Rome the Christian religion flourished. ⟦s2⟧ *Lift up,* because from being exceedingly superstitious she has been made most holy. ⟦s3⟧ *How greatly he hath done wickedly,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] The priests and the ministries of the temple were plunder. ⟦s4⟧ The whole nation was either slain or led captive. ⟦s5⟧ *The enemy,* the Roman army; and to this end he set *their* beforehand by prolepsis.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0960A]VERS. 4.-- *They have gloried.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] Of their own victory. ⟦s2⟧ Behold that they did it not with a good zeal. ⟦s3⟧ Having gloried: whereas repentance is wont to follow a crime, these turn it to glory; *who hate thee,* the slaves of demons, who are by no means to be tolerated. ⟦s4⟧ *In the midst of thy solemnity.* ⟦s5⟧ Not in the outermost place, but in the middle court. ⟦s6⟧ Another reading: they committed sacrileges. ⟦s7⟧ *In the holy place.* [n: (CAS.)] In the sanctuary, through contempt of the holy temple, where the Godhead seems to be despised, it stirs up ill-will against the presumptuous. ⟦s8⟧ *Signs,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Eagles, etc., *as far as* that is, through the high places.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *In the going out.* [n: (CAS.)] Or as in a going forth from above, that is, they did not know that this licence went forth from God. ⟦s2⟧ *As in a wood,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Before it was built, he prophesies the overthrow of the temple.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With axe and hatchet they have defiled the tabernacle of thy name,* [0960B] etc. [var: Vulg. Ps 73:6 *in securi et ascia dejecerunt eam*; Ps 73:7 *in terra polluerunt tabernaculum nominis tui*] [n: (AUG.)] *The axe* cuts beams: *the hatchet* follows up more minutely. ⟦s2⟧ By this therefore he says that both great things and the least were cast down, so that the city was utterly laid waste, first cut down, afterwards the fire devoured all.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *On the ground,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Down to the ground they cast the roofs raised for the praise of God. ⟦s3⟧ He magnifies the crime, that it may be more atrociously avenged. [n: (CAS.)] Or the whole can be read in the person of the despairing Jews, etc., *as far as* who amid these things do not repent. [n: (AUG.)] These things they said, but how long dost thou endure the reproaches of the Jews: He did not say this in order that he might wish them to perish, but to be changed. ⟦s4⟧ *Unto the end* of the world, namely the evening, when the Jews shall be converted.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 9.-- *We have not seen our signs.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] [0960C] As if they should say: Deservedly have these evils befallen us, Because we have not seen the signs; as if he should say: We have seen, but we have not understood [sic: *dica*]: and truly we have seen, because this is the sign, that there is no Prophet, nor priest, nor unction; whence: *When the Holy of holies shall have come, there shall cease the unction [n: (Dan. IX)]* of yours. [var: Vulg. Dn 9:24 *et impleatur visio et prophetia, et ungatur Sanctus sanctorum*] *And he shall no more know us,* some Prophet, or God through a Prophet; that is, he shall not announce Christ as about to come, because he has already come. ⟦s3⟧ *Any more.* [n: (ID.)] Or he shall not know as yet, until, namely, the Jew acknowledge him.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *How long?* etc. [n: (CAS.)] And as if he had suddenly come to know. ⟦s2⟧ *How long,* he subjoined, *doth the adversary provoke?* as if he should say: So long shall he reproach, as long as the adversary, that is, the Jew, provokes thy name. ⟦s3⟧ *Why dost thou turn away.* [n: (AUG.)] After the sign of the rod there was added [0960D] the sign of the hand of Moses; this is the people of God: which, sent forth outside from him, is unclean, but being called back returns into its own colour. ⟦s4⟧ He says therefore: How long dost thou not call back thy hand, that it may acknowledge the Saviour. ⟦s5⟧ *Thy hand and thy right hand,* that is, the Jews.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *But God is our king.* [n: (CAS.)] The signs being destroyed, he shows the thing itself, namely, that in the coming of Christ salvation was made everywhere. [n: (AUG.)] *But God.* ⟦s2⟧ Asaph, understanding, says, etc., *as far as* [sic: *uque ad*] if therefore he did not go before, let him at least follow.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the midst of the earth.* [n: (CAS.)] Made man; Jerusalem is said to be in the midst.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Thou hast confirmed.* [n: (AUG.)] He made firm the fallings of the Red Sea, that there might be dry ground between. ⟦s2⟧ Whose [0961A] mystery he subjoins, that in the waters of baptism he annihilated the heads of the unclean spirits.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Meat.* [n: (AUG.)] Christ is meat, etc., *as far as* or they bite him by reproving, by accusing. [n: (CAS.)] Or while he tempts, he is meat, because thereby profit is made. ⟦s2⟧ Or, to the peoples of the Ethiopians. [n: (HIERON.)] To those unwilling to be converted thou hast given him for meat, in whom they may take delight.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Fountains and torrents.* [n: (AUG.)] In others the word is made, etc., *as far as* and he notes the abundance of preaching. [n: (CAS.)] Or the whole of the wicked. ⟦s2⟧ *Fountains,* whose evils flowed in continually: *Torrents,* who sin suddenly and rapidly: these God breaks up and dries. ⟦s3⟧ *The rivers of Ethan.* ⟦s4⟧ The floods and counsels of the devil.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thine is the day and thine is the night.* [n: (AUG.)] He illumines the day [0961B] but of unchangeable wisdom. ⟦s2⟧ The moon consoles the night, that is, the manifestation of the flesh: *The Word was made flesh and dwelt among us [n: (Joan. I)]*. ⟦s3⟧ And likewise: *Night to night sheweth knowledge [n: (Psal. XXVIII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ *The dawn and the sun.* [n: (AUG.)] Or *the moon,* that is, the carnal man, who like the moon is changed, who nevertheless is not to be forsaken. ⟦s5⟧ Or *the dawn and the sun,* that is, the beginning and the advance of wisdom, the unwise and the perfect.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Thou hast made all.* [n: (CAS.)] Or all things historically from this place: *Thine is the day. ⟦s2⟧ The bounds of the earth,* etc. [n: (CAS.)] The Apostles and Prophets: as boundary-marks distinguish the limits, so these guard the laws of the faith: or all things historically.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Be mindful.* [n: (CAS.)] Now what is done everywhere: he prays for the Jews, and by the benefits reckoned up he provokes the merciful judge [0961C]. ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: Be mindful of this thy creature. ⟦s3⟧ *Of this.* [n: (AUG.)] Of thy creature; of what creature of thine? ⟦s4⟧ *The enemy hath reproached the Lord, and a foolish people hath provoked thy name,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Asaph with understanding grieves over the former blindness, and so prays for the Jews, that they may confess their errors. ⟦s5⟧ This he prays, which he knows will come to pass; for there were among the Jews vessels of mercy.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Have regard to thy testament.* [n: (ID.)] Let the remnant be saved, because many who tend toward the earth have been darkened and filled with earth. [n: (CAS.)] *Have regard.* ⟦s2⟧ Fulfil what thou hast promised; that thou mayest establish the New Testament, which is concerning eternal things. ⟦s3⟧ He calls the promise to mind, because merits are wanting. ⟦s4⟧ Likewise for this reason, because they have been filled with iniquities, whence darkness follows, [0961D] because they lose the light of wisdom, being filled with iniquities, because the houses of the earth have been darkened by an earthly habitation, that is, blind through love of earthliness.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Let not the humble be turned away, made confounded.* [n: (CAS.)] The acceptable is set for the hateful, that the love of the devout may temper the hatred of the obstinate. ⟦s2⟧ *Confounded.* [n: (ID.)] To be confounded belongs not to the humble, but to the proud, who attributes not to God but to himself.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Arise, O God.* [n: (CASS.)] Now Asaph (understanding) shakes off the earthly promises of the Old Testament. ⟦s2⟧ *Thy cause.* ⟦s3⟧ For they say: Where is thy God? ⟦s4⟧ He was despised by the Jews as he walked on the earth, and by false Christians as he sits in heaven. ⟦s5⟧ Judge therefore; let not my hope be deceived, who have believed what I have not seen. ⟦s6⟧ *Thy [0962A] cause.* ⟦s7⟧ After all things, he makes it the judge's cause, to whom the character of his own business is made known. ⟦s8⟧ *Thy cause,* against those who murmur, that with the truth made manifest they may preach what they foolishly denied. ⟦s9⟧ *All the day.* [n: (AUG.)] He says this for this reason, that the very continuance might rouse the mildness of the patient judge.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *The pride of them that hate thee.* [n: (CAS.)] At the end he sets the execrable evil, which is pride.
