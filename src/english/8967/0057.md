## PSALM LXXIV.

VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, destroy not,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] This psalm admonishes, etc., *as far as* that the Jews may believe, and not corrupt the faith.

VERS. 2.-- *We will praise thee, O God.* [n: (AUG.)] Certain therefore of what has gone before, he begins: *We will praise.* Repetition [0962B] is affirmation, lest it repent thee of having confessed: whence the Lord too doubles it. *Amen, amen.* So be it, so be it. And Joseph concerning the dream of Pharao, who saw seven ears of corn, and as many barren ones. And seven fat oxen and as many lean. [n: (CAS.)] Repetition is confirmation for the signifying of truth. One would suffice for the signifying of firmness; a second was added. To confess sins even once to an earthly judge brings in death, but frequent confession to God, life? Thence invocation, afterwards narration; whence follows: *We will narrate. We will call upon thy name.* [n: (ID.)] After confession God can be called upon, into a man thus cleansed: and as by confession he is emptied of evils, so by invocation he is filled with good things.

*We will narrate thy wonders,* etc. [n: (CAS.)] The proud man is terrified [0962C] by the judgment, etc., *as far as* in whose person it is subjoined:

VERS. 3.-- *When I shall take a time,* he receives after the manner of man what God himself gives, that is, the power of executing judgment. *Justices.* Therefore let them pray beforehand to be absolved of him who is to judge.

VERS. 4.-- *The earth is melted.* [n: (CAS.)] He shows all to be inexcusable in the judgment, through this, that he sent before the Apostles and Prophets. *The earth and all.* [n: (AUG.)] Firm before, it flows away into divers concupiscences. Or, the earth, bound before by vices, when the Holy Spirit blew, was melted, that it might send forth the buds of virtues.

VERS. 5.-- *To do wickedly.* [n: (AUG.)] It is evil to sin; it is worse to persevere; pernicious, by defending [0962D] oneself, to pour back the fault upon God. *Lift not up your horn.* [n: (CAS.)] The vice of excusing is the greatest; he as it were raises a horn who strives to excuse his own sins through others, as through the persuasions of devils, or through the necessity of the stars.

VERS. 6.-- *To speak.* As those who say that they have sinned not by their own fault, but by the necessity of the stars, that it may be imputed to God, not to themselves. Or, those who know their own sins, but promise themselves impunity.

VERS. 7.-- *For neither from the east.* [n: (CAS.)] Why these things? because the judge is such that he cannot be avoided: because he is everywhere; for he is God. [n: (AUG.)] He judges from no place separately, who is everywhere present as God. [n: (CAS.)] Mystically. *The east,* those for whom the sun of justice [0963A] rises, etc., *as far as* because God is present and is a knower.

VERS. 9.-- *For a cup.* [n: (AUG.)] The cup, the law and all Scripture, etc., *as far as* what went before, *and he exalteth this one.* [n: (CAS.)] *A cup,* the law; *pure wine,* the heavenly mystery, etc., *as far as* not only the Jews.

VERS. 10.-- *But I will declare for ever: I will sing to the God of Jacob.* [n: (CAS.)] All shall drink, but severally: I, that is Christ, with the body, *will declare for ever,* as long as the world shall be.

VERS. 1.-- *The horns of the just,* that is, the gifts of Christ, which are the loftiness of the just.

## PSALM LXXV.

VERS. 1.-- *A canticle.* Because it treats of the good things of Christ, [0963B] namely that he is known, that he *dwelleth,* that he *enlighteneth,* that he *saveth.* It breaks the glory of the Jews. *To the Assyrians.* [n: (CAS.)] Against the Jews, who are not directing themselves, but erring. Or, because they raise themselves up, since without grace they seek salvation and persecute the Church, as of old the Assyrians persecuted the Jews. And these are here confuted, the names of glory being taken away, and are proved to be able to do nothing without grace.

VERS. 2.-- *Known in Judea.* [n: (AUG.)] The Jew glories that God is known to the Jew, and it is true; but to the Jew within, not in the flesh. [n: (AUG.)] The Prophet: if ye are Jews, then God is known among you: but if he is not, then ye are not Jews. *In Judea.* [n: (ID.)] The two sons of Joseph being taken up, with the remaining eleven sons of Jacob there are twelve tribes, await [cj: *exspecta*; read *excepta*, "the tribe of Levi being excepted"] the tribe of Levi, which was priestly, and lived on the tithes. Among twelve tribes the land [0963C] was divided. Among these one is Juda, whence the kings, and from the kingdom all are called Jews. They confessed Caesar, not Christ, and being of Juda they are now not Jews.

VERS. 3.-- *In Sion.* [n: (AUG.)] Mark the fitting order of the words: Judea is confession; after that, Israel, that is, seeing God, and thus there is a place in one at peace with God: whence follows: *In peace,* after *in Sion,* that is in contemplation (when he shall see God face to face), there shall be a habitation.

VERS. 4.-- *There he broke the powers.* [n: (CAS.)] In Judea, in Israel, in Salem, in Sion, where God the author of peace came, all these things (which are also the names of their glory) flee away.

VERS. 5.-- *Thou enlightening wonderfully from the everlasting hills, they were troubled,* etc. Diapsalma. Christ enlightens, [0963D] the Jew sleeps, Christ judges, that man perishes. [n: (AUG.)] Thou *enlightenest,* but through *the mountains,* who first receive the light, namely through the Prophets and the Apostles. But from the enlightening *all the foolish of heart were troubled.* He sets down thence why he is terrible in the time to come, etc. *They have slept.* [n: (CASS.)] Growing cold from eternal goods, they acquiesced in the flesh, not seeing the things that would profit them. *Their sleep,* which differs from the rest of the good: because this one is deceitful; seeing in a dream that they have treasures, when they awake they find nothing, whence he says: *And all the men of riches have found nothing.* The definition of the covetous. *They have found nothing.* Because they placed nothing in the hands of Christ, who says: *I was hungry,* etc. [n: (REM.)] For he hungered in the poor, who in heaven is rich. *Men of riches.* [0964A] Who place their only hope in the uncertainty of riches. Whence he does not say, having riches: for many have who do not love. *In their hands,* etc. [n: (CAS.)] As though they still sought in their hands, when they now know that they have held nothing.

VERS. 7.-- *At the rebuke,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] The noise of a rebuke is wont to arouse, but so great is the weight of the God who rebukes, that they slumber, that is, they have a hardened heart. *Who have mounted.* [n: (ID.)] Whose pride grew, not humbled by the humble Christ. *Horses.* [n: (CAS.)] Any secular exaltations whatsoever: whence it had been commanded in the law to the children of Israel, that they should not multiply horses to themselves. Solomon too had no horses from Judea, but used to buy them from Egypt.

VERS. 9.-- *Hearing from heaven.* [n: (AUG.)] Whence the Apostle: [0964B] *In the revelation of our Lord Jesus from heaven they shall come with angels to execute judgment [n: (I Thes. I)]*. [var: Vulg. 2Thes 1:7 *in revelatione Domini Jesu de caelo cum angelis virtutis ejus*, "with the angels of his power"]

VERS. 10.-- *When he should rise up,* that is, when he shall appear as mighty, who here was humble. Or, *when he should rise up* here from death, afterwards to come to judgment. [n: (AUG.)] When, coming from heaven, thou shalt make a hearing, that is, [make it] known to all. The power of the judgment is set forth, whence earthly men are terrified, and, their power lost, are still; it was still, when he should rise up. *The meek of the earth.* [n: (ID.)] Who have peace of mind, not refusing the yoke of God.

VERS. 11.-- *For the thought.* [n: (CAS.)] Diapsalma. He now admonishes the devout to offer vows. As if: deservedly he saves the meek. *For the thought,* that is, they shall praise thee in thought and in work, that is, *the remainders of the thought,* [0964C] etc. *The thought of man.* [n: (ID.)] The conception of a vow. *The remainders,* the rendering of the vow, the working, the things remaining after the thought. *The remainders of the thought.* [n: (AUG.)] Since the first thought, etc., *as far as* which is to celebrate solemnities. [n: (CAS.)] Because the Lord alone is he to whom festivity is owed: they shall render vows to God. The first thought condemns evils. *A remainder.* Because afterwards a man ought always to remember past evils, that he may mourn: although the clean man here keeps a feast-day, when he feels himself free from sin, as after amendment the Jews are commanded to offer a festal gift to God.

VERS. 12.-- *Vow ye and pay.* [n: (AUG.)] Some vows are common, concerning those things without which there is no salvation; others proper, as to vow chastity, and things of this kind. [n: (CAS.)] *Vow ye.* There are certain things which, not even promising them, [0964D] we ought to fulfil: as, Thou shalt not kill, and things of this kind, etc., *as far as* who are round about, that as servants they shall offer, or let them offer, etc. [n: (AUG.)] There are, as it were, middling ones, etc., *as far as* having the spirit of God, not of princes, that is, a proud spirit. *Gifts.* [n: (ID.)] Which are offered upon the altars. *To him that is terrible and to him that taketh away.* Whom the good fear, but the wicked do not fear. *The spirit of princes.* [n: (CAS.)] He takes away the spirit of pride or of fear, and makes them reins of his own, who govern their own body.
