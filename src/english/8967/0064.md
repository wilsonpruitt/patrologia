## PSALM LXXXI.

VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of Asaph.* [n: (CASS.)] A psalm on the two natures in Christ, in which Christ, or the Prophet speaking, reproaches Asaph himself, that is, the Synagogue, which saw the Lord bodily, and yet did not know him. First he announces that God has stood, and [0981B] convicts the adversaries: man stands, God discerns. *God hath stood.* Whose part it is not to stand, who filleth heaven and earth, who is within all things not enclosed, outside all things not excluded; but briefly he indicates God as man. *He hath stood.* [n: (AUG.)] The Son, who was sent to the sheep of the house of Israel; but the spiritual presence of the Godhead, which, being everywhere, does not properly belong to the Synagogue, nor is varied by time. Why he stood there: *Behold, he judgeth gods,* [var: Vulg. Ps 81:1 *in medio autem deos dijudicat*, "but in the midst he judgeth the gods"] that is, according as he is God, that is, not by merits but by grace he distinguishes the vessels, some unto honour, some unto dishonour. But this he does standing in the midst, whence: *There hath stood one in the midst of you, whom you know not,* whence also here: *They have not known, neither have they understood,* but *the friend of the bridegroom standeth and heareth, and rejoiceth with joy. Of gods.* Whom afterwards he calls *sons of the Most High,* there: *I have said: you are gods, and [0981C] all of you to the sons of the Most High.* Whence also, *I have brought up children and exalted them, but they have despised me.* They are called sons not only by the grace of the New Testament, but by the grace of the Old, which delivered them out of Egypt.

VERS. 2.-- *How long.* [n: (AUG.)] This is the voice of him that discerneth: he sent the law and the Prophets, and you resisted; but how long? Did I send Prophets until the coming, and you afflicted them with injuries, or slew them, or consented to those who did this? But since it is not worthy even to speak to those who do such things, you who kept silence, and wished to imitate them, *how long do you judge iniquity, and accept the persons of sinners?* Is the heir also to be slain, who came for you, who for your sake willed to be without a father, as a fatherless child? who for your sake hungered and thirsted, as a needy man, who for your sake was made poor, when he was rich.

[0981D]VERS. 3.-- *Judge for the needy and the fatherless, justify the poor and the humble.* [var: Vulg. Ps 81:3 *humilem et pauperem justificate*, the two transposed] [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalma, he admonishes that one should understand Christ to be God, even if he was needy and poor, which he says that they did not do.

VERS. 4.-- *Rescue the poor.* [n: (AUG.)] He shows that not even those are guiltless who left Christ to the princes, since they were feared on account of their multitude, and could have freed them from the deed, and themselves from consent. [n: (CASS.)] For he who ceases to withstand when he can, consents.

VERS. 5.-- *They shall be moved.* [n: (CASS.)] Hence it is plain of whom he had spoken: this sign happened at the crucifixion. Or spiritually, earthly men were moved at that time, so great a miracle being seen; as the centurion, who said: *Indeed this was the Son of God.* [n: (AUG.)] *All the foundations shall be moved.* [0982A] As if he should say: through this blindness of Israel, *shall be moved* unto faith *all the foundations of the earth,* that is, the fulness of the Gentiles shall come in. Or, in the death and passion of the Lord the earth was moved. Or *the foundations,* that is, those made happy by abundance of earthly things, shall be moved. Or by wondering that a poor dead man is worshipped. Or by following after him, earthly things being despised. He adds *all,* because there is none who does not wonder, or is not converted. But the kingdom of earthly felicity is pride, against which the humble Christ came, reproaching them whom he wills to make high through humility, in these words: *I have said: you are gods,* you the good: *but you* the evil, *shall die like men.* Or he says to all: *you are gods,* that is, to all I promised heavenly things; *but you as men,* the first men, who sinned by persuasion, and you greater ones as the devil, who by pride [0982B] sinned.

VERS. 6.-- *I have said.* [n: (CASS.)] What was set before them? but they perished. *I have said: They have not known, neither have they understood;* yet *I have said.* All receive these promises, if they keep the commandments, that they may be sons, and of God: this to the just; *but you,* this to the wicked.

VERS. 7.-- *Like men.* [n: (AUG.)] As if: Whereas you are quickly to die, like men, you are not corrected thereby; but like the devil, who dies not in the flesh, you are lifted up, that you may fall: because through diabolical pride they envied the glory of Christ, and the humility of the cross became vile to them. But, that this vice may be healed, there is subjoined in the person of the Prophet: *Arise, O God,* etc., *as one.* The crowd which fell is called one, because it is alike in pride: You shall fall, but:

[0982C]VERS. 8.-- *Arise, O God.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, condemn these, because, these being cast off, thou shalt inherit. Or judge the world, because it is thine, and this is: thou shalt inherit.
