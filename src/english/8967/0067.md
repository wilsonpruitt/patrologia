## PSALM LXXXIV.

VERS. 2.-- *Thou hast blessed, O Lord.* [n: (AUG.)] A psalm on the new blessing, etc., *as far as* of the turning away of iniquity.

VERS. 3.-- *Thou hast forgiven.* [n: (ID.)] He speaks of things to come as of things past.

VERS. 4.-- *Thou hast mitigated* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalma, of the turning away of anger, that is, of punishment.

VERS. 5.-- *Convert us.* He asks what he knew to be still to come. As if: what I have said as past, I pray may be done. [n: (AUG.)] *Convert us.* I said prophetically things to come, as past; but here he prays for them as truly to come, saying, Convert [0987B] and turn away, as if he should say, I have said them as though done. And because I see things to come, and because they are not, I pray that they may be done.

VERS. 6.-- *Is it for ever.* He foresees that this punishment will pass away: *For as in Adam all die, so also in Christ all shall be made alive. Thou art angry.* [n: (AUG.)] The anger of God is every punishment which one suffers. As it was said to Adam when he sinned: *Cursed is the earth in thy work, and in labour and in the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat thy bread.* [var: Vulg. Gn 3:17 *maledicta terra in opere tuo : in laboribus comedes ex ea*; the second clause is Gn 3:19 *In sudore vultus tui vesceris pane*] Of anger therefore we are mortal and passible, but renewed in Christ. *From generation.* The first generation was mortal, of anger; the other shall be immortal, of mercy: but neither do we merit this mercy by being converted to God through ourselves, for he himself converts us. And thence he subjoins: *God converting,* as if [0987C] to say: as the quickening is from thee, so also the conversion.

VERS. 7.-- *O God, thou being turned.* [n: (CASS.)] Not extending, but God converting. First he gives the will of conversion, afterwards he leads to life. Or, O God, thou being turned from anger at the coming of the Son. *Thou wilt quicken us.* So long a time before it is had. And then, *Thy people shall rejoice in thee,* which before shall rejoice wickedly in itself.

VERS. 8.-- *Shew us, O Lord.* [n: (AUG.)] This the Prophet foretold by praying, etc., *as far as Blessed are the clean of heart, for they shall see God [n: (Matth. V)]*. [n: (CASS.)] *Shew.* Or this one asks that Christ be shewn to him spiritually only; he knew indeed that he was to come, but he asks that we may behold him with a pure heart, not with bodily eyes as the unbelievers: for the Jews saw and crucified him. And for the time to come: *Give us thy salvation,* that [0987D] is, Christ, to be embraced and possessed, that he be not taken away from us; and thus it is read partly of the present, and partly of the future. *Mercy.* [n: (AUG.)] Christ is called mercy, because he showed us mercy, teaching that whatever of good a man has is from God, and so man is not proud. But cleaving thus and enjoying God, he rejoices in him.

VERS. 9.-- *I will hear what the Lord speaketh in me.* [n: (CASS.)] Whence he knows, and by whom it is done, and what he himself bestows in coming. *Peace.* [n: (AUG.)] Christ. Peace toward God and toward the neighbour, and through Christ.

VERS. 10.-- *Near them that fear him.* [n: (ID.)] Everywhere demons were worshipped, etc., *as far as* immortal in our body now mortal.

[0988A]VERS. 11.-- *Mercy,* etc. [n: (ID.)] After he had said, etc., *as far as how truth is sprung out of the earth [n: (Luc. VIII)]*.

VERS. 12.-- *Truth is sprung out of the earth.* [n: (ID.)] Confession from man, that he may accuse himself, etc., *as far as* the justification of him that confesses is given.

VERS. 14.-- *Justice shall go before him.* [n: (ID.)] The first justice of man is, etc., *as far as* and visiting he shall set his steps in the way. *Shall walk.* It shall increase, or shall run before, preparing the way for him; and the Lord shall set his steps in the way made ready.

## PSALM LXXXV.

*A prayer of David.* [var: Vulg. Ps 85:1 *Oratio ipsi David*, "a prayer for David himself"] [n: (AUG.)] This psalm is the second of those which are entitled by the name of prayer, in which Christ, the Son of God and of man, one God with the Father, [0988B] one man with men, prays in the form of a servant, who is prayed to by us in the form of God. He prays for us as our priest; he prays in us as our head; he is prayed to by us as our God.

[n: (CASS.)] The prayer of Christ, of the faithful, etc., *as far as* he might restrain the boldness of foolish and inconsiderate rashness.

[n: (AUG.)] These things Christ says in us, and we in him.

[n: (CASS.)] He prays by ascending, whence he concludes: All the nations adoring.

VERS. 2.-- *For I am holy.* [n: (AUG.)] Christ properly holy, sanctifying, etc., *as far as* have mercy on him that cries, gladden him that is lifted up.

VERS. 5.-- *Meek.* [n: (ID.)] Bearing man, until he perfect him, etc., *as far as* when he is not yet perfect.

[0988C]VERS. 6.-- *Give ear.* [n: (CASS.)] See the order of the prayer, etc., *as far as* a hearing to my supplication.

VERS. 7.-- *Of tribulation.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] When one cries more earnestly, etc., *as far as because there is none like to thee among the gods, O Lord.*

VERS. 9.-- *All whom thou hast made shall come and adore before,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] If there is a nation which God did not make, that one shall not adore. He announces the Church spread abroad everywhere, which is now fulfilled: And note that, as the Scripture says: *In a broad nation is the glory of the king, in the diminishing of the people is the ruin of the prince.* [var: Vulg. Pr 14:28 *In multitudine populi dignitas regis, et in paucitate plebis ignominia principis*, "in the multitude of the people is the dignity of the king"] There follows: *And they shall glorify the name.*

VERS. 10.-- *Thou art God alone.* [n: (ID.)] Or thou alone art great against those to come, who call themselves great and gods.

[0988D]VERS. 11.-- *Conduct me, O Lord, in thy way.* [n: (CASS.)] He prays for all who now adore, and speaks in his members. *O Lord, conduct,* etc. It is one thing to be led to the way, and another to be led in the way, lest one fall in it: everywhere men need to be helped, whether they be in the way, or outside it. But the way is Christ, who says: *I am the way, the truth, and the life. Let my heart rejoice.* [n: (AUG.)] In worldly fear the cause goes before, etc., *as far as* but he who fears, and does not hope, is cast down.

VERS. 13.-- *From the lower hell.* [n: (ID.)] By this we understand, etc., *as far as* these are opinions, not assertions.

VERS. 14.-- *O God, the wicked have risen up against me, [0989A] and the Synagogue of the mighty,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Again he prays for himself, mention being made of his passion and of his glory, whence those for whom he prayed have much mercy and are rescued from hell.

VERS. 16.-- *Give thy command.* [n: (AUG.)] As if he should say: Let the time of patience pass, let the time of judgment come, whence not only Christ, but the saints also, shall sit as judges, who are *the sons of the handmaid,* that is, of the Church.
