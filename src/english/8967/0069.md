## PSALM LXXXVIII.

VERS. 2.-- *The mercies.* [n: (CASS.)] This Psalm says many things concerning the [0992A] hope of Christians and the magnificence of the Lord, by which no one is led astray who prays worthily, which the true Israel so understands. First the Prophet promises that he will sing of mercy and truth.

*For ever.* [n: (CASS., REM.)] How does he *sing the mercies for ever,* and give thanks, who is not mindful of misery? And how is there full blessedness if the memory of guilt touches the mind? But we often remember sad things in gladness, and sorrows in health without sorrow, and thence we are the more glad and thankful; and so likewise the recollection of misery takes away nothing of blessedness.

*Truth.* The promise. In the mouth: it does not slip out of the mouth, but is turned over long in the mouth as something sweet.

VERS. 3.-- *For thou hast said.* Here he says: Whence does he know? and what are the mercies of God through Christ? [n: (AUG.)] [0992B] Which mercies, and what truth shall I sing? Behold.

*For thou hast said.* As if he should say: I, a man, say securely what thou, God, hast said, hast made manifest through the Spirit.

*Mercy shall be built up.* [n: (AUG.)] He repeats both, which he foretold. Because all the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth. Truth is not shown forth in the fulfilling of the things promised, unless mercy go before in the remission of sins. Finally, truth pertains to the Jews, to whom the promises were made, mercy to the Gentiles: as the Apostle says: *For I say that Jesus Christ was a minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, which was to be fulfilled: but that the Gentiles honour God for his mercy [n: (Rom. XV)]* [var: Vulg. Rom 15:8 has instead "to confirm the promises of the fathers"; the gloss's argument turns on the fulfilling, which it repeats two lines above].

*In the heavens.* They who declare the glory of God, through whom mercy is built up [0992C].

VERS. 4.-- *I have sworn to David my servant.* This man is secure, for this reason, because God has said it: more secure, because he has sworn. God's swearing is the confirmation of the promise, and out of both is made the man Ethan, that is, the strong one.

VERS. 5.-- *For ever.* [n: (CASS.)] The building up of mercy is not temporal, as the ruin of iniquity is temporal: for temporal iniquity is destroyed in certain men, that in them eternal mercy may be built up, whence the Lord says to Jeremiah: *Behold therefore I have set thee over the nations and over kingdoms, to root up, and to pull down, and to waste, and to destroy, and to build, and to plant. Unto generation.* [var: Vulg. Jer 1:10 reads "Behold I have set thee this day over the nations", with no "therefore"] [n: (AUG.)] For generation cannot, etc., *as far as* he shall sit, and then in the saints.

VERS. 6.-- *The heavens shall confess.* [n: (CAS.)] Diapsalma. [0992D] Secondly he describes the praises and the power of God in divers ways, as if to say, as thou hast said, so shall it be done. *They shall confess,* they shall praise.

*The heavens shall confess thy wonders.* For in every mercy shown to the lost, in the justifying of the ungodly, what do we praise but the wonders of God? Thou praisest because the dead have risen; praise the more because the lost have been redeemed. For thou seest a man who yesterday was a whirlpool of drunkenness, today the ornament of sobriety. For thou seest a man who yesterday was the mire of luxury, today the grace of temperance: yesterday a blasphemer of God, today a praiser of God. Thou seest a man who yesterday was a slave of the creature, today a worshipper of the Creator: these are the wonders which the heavens confess. *For thy truth also.* [n: (CASS.)] Or without *for.* Wonders, [0993A] in that he does and administers all things everywhere. Truth is in the Church where it is believed, *the Word was made flesh,* and that he was Trinity, and before that God.

VERS. 7.-- *For who.* [n: (AUG.)] It is to be noted that the heavens and the clouds are the same, namely the preachers. But heavens, that is, preachers, because of the splendour of truth. *Clouds,* which are cloudy because of the mortality of the flesh, and because they pass away, where also there are hidden things, which afterwards shall be made clear in the judgment. *Among the sons.* Either of those above, or of men. Let him not therefore be thought to be man only: for he is natural, we adoptive; he begotten, we created; he begotten from eternity, we made in time; he the only one, we many.

VERS. 8.-- *Above all.* [n: (ID.)] The angels, or men, who are his unequal attendants, who draw near to him by his own gift.

[0993B]VERS. 9.-- *To thee.* [n: (ID.)] Thou who hast made all things, etc., *as far as* to be tempted above that which a man can bear.

VERS. 11.-- *Wounded.* He was humbled by losing those whom he held; wounded by the smiting of the heart, wherein he was proud. *The proud one.* [n: (AUG.)] Namely the dragon, who was made in the sea to be mocked at, who through the humble Christ, in whom others are humble, are humbled; Christ humbled himself, that he might humble the proud one. For the proud one held the proud: the devil is wounded by the wounded Christ, he is not yet wholly dead. *The enemies.* The powers of the air, or the Jews dispersed through the world, or of men he took up some who are the spoils of the devil, and others thou didst leave, whom thou didst permit to go away after him.

VERS. 12.-- *Thine are the heavens, and thine is the earth, the world,* [0993C] etc. [n: (ID.)] Therefore he triumphed in heaven over the devil, because heaven is his; on earth over Judea, because the earth is his. Why then were they proud in what was another's?

*Thou hast founded.* Why then do they raise themselves up against him, whose is the compass of the world, and all the creatures which are in it?

VERS. 13.-- *The north.* The devil. *The sea thou hast created.* The world. Foolishly therefore do they raise themselves up against him by whom they were created. *Thabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.* [n: (AUG.)] Mountains of Syria, and by them the Gentiles are understood, the whole from a part, by synecdoche.

*They shall rejoice.* At Christ's coming, allegorically. *Thabor,* the Jews, to whom the light first came. *Hermon,* the Gentiles, who were formerly accursed. *In thy name they shall rejoice,* the two walls being joined together [0993D] in the corner stone.

[n: (AUG.)] *Thabor.* The light coming from the highest light, namely, at whose coming the anathema of him, that is, of the devil, who held us in his error, is made. Which is: it is given by thee that we should be enlightened, that we should give the devil to anathema.

VERS. 15.-- *The preparation of thy seat.* [n: (CASS.)] The *seat* of God is eternal, but in the judgment it shall appear *prepared,* when he himself shall be seen to judge justly. Or in this present time men, by discerning and by holding to just things, prepare themselves as a seat for the Lord.

*Mercy and truth shall go before thy face.* From the likeness of a forerunner preparing a lodging for the Lord.

[0994A]VERS. 16.-- *Blessed is the people that knoweth,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] He knows, who places his gladness not in his own strength, but in the Lord's, or, who understands in the heart what he sings with the mouth: whence this reward follows, *in the light,* that is, they shall persevere in the contemplation of the Trinity.

*Jubilation.* Jubilation is a joy which cannot be unfolded in words, nor yet altogether kept silent. *O Lord, they shall walk in the light of thy countenance, and in thy name they shall rejoice.* [n: (CASS.)] He commends the grace, of which the jubilation is.

[n: (AUG.)] Or in thy good pleasure, that is, in Christ, therefore in him, because he took our flesh, in whom is all hope.

VERS. 20.-- *Then he spoke in a vision.* [var: Vulg. Ps 88:20 has the second person, "then thou didst speak" — as the gloss itself has it four lines below] [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalma. Thirdly, the promises of the Father concerning Christ. [0994B] The praise above is not doubtful, which the Father's authority confirms. [n: (CASS.)]

[n: (AUG.)] *Then.* When thou didst appoint the testament, or when thou didst say these things to me, *then thou didst speak to the saints,* to the rest of the prophets *in a vision,* for they did not speak unknowingly, as Montanus and certain other heretics have said.

VERS. 21.-- *David my servant, with my holy oil I have anointed him.* [n: (AUG.)] Strong of hand, an obedient servant, therefore in him I have placed help.

VERS. 23.-- *The enemy in him.* [n: (ID.)] The devil, whence: *The prince of this world cometh, and in me he hath not anything [n: (Joan. XIV)]*.

*The son of iniquity shall not go on to hurt.* Judas shall not go on, shall not add the effect to the will.

[0994C]VERS. 24.-- *From his face his enemies.* [n: (AUG.)] When his face looks upon sinners, they are separated from crimes, for which there is no place under his presence.

VERS. 25.-- *And my truth and my mercy shall be with him, and in my name shall his horn be exalted.* [n: (ID.)] Often mercy and truth are commended to us, etc., *as far as* where these meet one another.

VERS. 31.-- *But if they forsake.* Not only concerning those who persist, but also concerning those who fall, do I promise these things.

VERS. 33.-- *With a rod.* For who is a son? etc., *as far as: And with stripes,* etc.

*I will not scatter.* [n: (AUG.)] He himself does not take away his mercy from him who is indeed a Christian: but if a man is so wicked that he flees when his father draws near, he alienates [0994D] himself, the father does not cast him off.

VERS. 35.-- *Neither will I profane.* The promise would be profane, if it were unfulfilled. Is nothing sent into the vessels out of that net because of the bad fishes? The good fishes shall surely be sent, that is, those whom he has predestinated.

VERS. 36.-- *I have sworn once.* [n: (CASS.)] Man swears often, God *once,* who is not changed.

*In my holy one.* In the man Christ, in whom was to be fulfilled what I have sworn; or David, that is Christ, through holiness. *The seed,* Christians.

VERS. 38.-- *As the sun in my sight.* [n: (AUG.)] According to the soul, etc., *as far as* in heaven, that is, Christ.

*But thou hast rejected.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalma. Fourthly [0995A] he sets adverse things against the promises aforesaid, lest they be thought to be fulfilled in another than in the Christ who was then to come. *But thou hast rejected.* As if to say, these goods are for Christians, but not for the Jews, who have deprived themselves of the promise, and this is it: But thou hast rejected *thy Christ,* that is, David the anointed, from the kingdom for the sin which he committed against Urias concerning Bethsabee; *And thou hast despised,* or *hast spurned,* because he suffered many unworthy things; *thou hast deferred,* long in tribulation, *the testament,* the things promised to the Jews, they have been carried over to the Gentiles. [n: (AUG.)] *But thou.* Or, all these things concerning Christ and the Church: *thou hast rejected* to the gibbet *thy Christ,* and hast given him over to contempt. Or, thou hast deferred him from glory, for a time, or according to his members.

VERS. 40.-- *The testament.* The promise seems overthrown, and the saints are reckoned profane. *The hedges,* the prelates, [0995B] *thou hast destroyed,* while they are slain. *And thou hast made his strength fear.* They who strengthened others by teaching are now for a terror, while they are afflicted, and some are now plundered *by them that pass by the way,* which is Christ.

VERS. 44.-- *Thou hast turned away the help of his sword.* That is, preaching. *Thou hast not helped him in battle,* but hast given him over to sufferings. *Thou hast destroyed him from amendment,* etc., that is, certain men of the world have failed, and have been brought down to the earth. [var: Vulg. Ps 88:45 reads "from cleansing"] *Thou hast lessened,* as it is thought, while they are quickly taken away from life.

[n: (AUG.)] Why did God promise those things, etc., *as far as* that this promise might be looked for in others.

VERS. 45.-- *Thou hast destroyed him.* All these things came upon the Jews, yet Christ was not taken from them, nor the eternal [0995C] kingdom, but deferred. *Thou hast cast his seat down to the ground.* That is, their kingdom has been blotted out everywhere on earth, because there is now no prince from among them.

VERS. 46.-- *Thou hast lessened.* Because their kingdom would stand long, if it were under God. *Thou hast covered him with confusion.* Because he bears the reproach of unbelief. *Thou hast lessened.* [n: (AUG.)] They thought that they would reign for ever, but thou hast lessened the days of their kingdom, that it should not stand, according to their opinion.

VERS. 47.-- *How long, O Lord?* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalma. Fifthly. Behold in whom the promises were fulfilled, namely in *the substance of David,* which lives, and delivered the soul, that is, in Christ. [n: (AUG.)] But, how long dost thou not raise up Christ? What *substance?* which is nothing, save through him. *Who is the man that liveth and shall not see death?* [var: Vulg. Ps 88:49 has the future, "that shall live"] [0995D] no one. In these things he now asks that help be brought to man, or especially to the Jews.
