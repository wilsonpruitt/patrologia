## PSALM XC.

VERS. 1.-- *The praise of a canticle.* Praise is human also: but this praise is of a canticle, because it is nothing if not divine. For this psalm is a hymn against the demons. The Prophet therefore says of any holy man whatsoever: *He that dwelleth in the help,* etc.

[n: (CASS.)] He shows that the temptations of the devil were overcome through humility by Christ, and by his own, who remain in the help of God, that we too through him may learn to overcome temptations, because he is the door through which, if any man shall enter in, [0998C] he shall go in, and shall go out, and pasture shall come forth.

[n: (AUG.)] Christ is the door; to enter through him is to imitate him, not in godhead, and power, and miracles, but in that wherein he was made for us, that is, in the humility to which he calls, saying: *Learn of me, because I am meek and humble of heart.* Out of this psalm the devil tempted Christ. But Christ's temptation is our teaching, and following him we overcome by him.

*He that dwelleth.* [n: (CASS.)] He describes first the man who trusts in God alone, wholly walled about by the protection of God. *He that dwelleth,* that is, who sets all his hope always not in himself, but in God, shall be protected by God in every way. Note the correspondence of the words: for as he always *dwelleth,* so also shall he always *abide.* The one he affirms, namely that he *dwelleth;* the other he promises, [0998D] namely that he *shall be protected.*

*He shall abide in the protection of the God of heaven.* That is to say, that he turn not aside to the right hand or to the left, as in the Old Testament it was commanded to the children of Israel, that they should walk by the king's highway, not turning aside to the right hand or to the left. The king's highway is the way of Christ our king, who so ran the way of the world that he was neither enticed by prosperity, nor broken by adversity.

VERS. 2.-- *My God.* [n: (CASS.)] Lest thou think that after the upholding and the refuge nothing further is to be asked for, he adds: *In him will I trust,* lest at any time he presume of himself; and if Adam had done this, he would have stood well.

VERS. 3.-- *From the snare.* [n: (ID.)] Temptation, either by deeds, which is here called *a snare;* or by words, which is here called *a sharp word;* [0999A] as if to say, even though it seem light, yet it is a grave thing to be tempted by a word. *A snare,* the craftiness of spiritual or of carnal enemies; *a sharp word,* every saying which moves a man from the divine rules. [n: (AUG.)] *From the snare of the hunters,* that is, of the demons, who set their snares not in the way, Christ, but round about it, to the right hand or to the left. Into these some fall even through a sharp word, as they who will not be Christ's because of those who insult the Christian name, and they who fear the detractions of men, so that they cease from the way of God.

VERS. 4.-- *With his shoulders.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] To him that hopes he promises the benefits of God. *The shoulders* are the workings of wonders, by which, as by arms, the power of God is shown. *The feathers* are the counsels of the Prophets, which carry men to the heavens.

[0999B] *He will overshadow.* That neither the sun of the godhead, nor the moon of the humanity, may scorch thee. *Under his feathers* is the protection. *With his shoulders.* Elsewhere, *Between the shoulders.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, he places thee before his breast, etc., *as far as* that we may hope under his wings.

VERS. 5.-- *The truth.* [n: (ID.)] It is God's, who does not regard persons, nor mingle those who hope in themselves with those who hope in God, but discerns the humble or the proud. [n: (CASS.)] Or the truth is the incarnation of Christ, whence: *Out of the earth truth is sprung [n: (Psal. LXXXIV)]*, which both overshadows with the shoulders, and defends with the shield. As he has noted the kinds of defence, so he notes also the kinds of dangers, that the deliverance may be the more welcome.

*From the fear of the night.* From the likeness of a danger, whence only fear is had. *From the fear of the night.* [n: (AUG.)] He who [0999C] sins in ignorance, etc., *as far as: A thousand shall fall at thy side.*

VERS. 7.-- *They shall fall at the side.* [n: (CASS.)] Secondly, he says that the head of him who trusts in God, that is, Christ, lifted up into heaven, can be touched by none, who also protects the offspring on earth: but the presumptuous fall from him. Behold the certainty of the protection of him who trusts in God through Christ. *At the side.* The side of God is the crown of the just, who shall judge with him, whence it is said: *Send ye from your own side,* that is, they who hold counsel with you. The side of Christ, therefore, is the Apostles and their imitators, to whom he promised: *You shall sit upon twelve seats, judging the twelve tribes of Israel [n: (Matth. XIX)]*. The twelve seats are the perfection of the tribunal, in which shall sit all they who have left all things, and have followed Christ.

[0999D]VERS. 8.-- *But yet.* [n: (CASS., AUG.)] Although indeed it is allowed, thou permitting, that the wicked persecute thine own, yet not unpunished: with thine eyes he shall consider the evil which they willed, not the good which is done through those who know it not, it shall be repaid to them; and the holy man, who has the eyes of faith, sees them now exalted and thundering, and at the last struck by lightning: this he said lest the weak be moved. [n: (CASS.)] *But yet with thine eyes.* Even if they that fall should tempt thee with hard things, *yet.* Or, even if the demon does not draw near, which is through the wicked: yet nevertheless not what they do, but what they intend, *thou shalt consider.*

VERS. 9.-- *For thou art.* [n: (AUG.)] One Christ is head and body, of whom he speaks now as of the head, now as of the body, the person not being changed. Therefore [1000A] diligently mark where he treats of Christ according to the head, and where according to the members. *The Most High.* In the height is the refuge of God, deep in secret, that flight may be made from the wrath to come. For many so make for themselves the refuge of God, whereby they flee from temporal evils. *Thou hast made the Most High thy refuge,* that is, by rising again, by ascending, thou hast made the refuge high, that I may now hope to follow whither thou hast gone before.

VERS. 10.--[n: (ID.)] *The tabernacle of God* is the flesh, etc., *as far as* this is rightly taken of Christ.

VERS. 11.-- *For to his angels.* This the tempter said to the Lord, when he tempted him: this he cast up against him, doubting of his Godhead. But Christ was tempted for our sake, that in him also we might learn to overcome the tempter.

[n: (AUG.)] So also he willed to be baptized by a servant, not for his own need, but for man's profit, that we should not disdain [1000B] to be baptized by our fellow servants.

VERS. 12.-- *In their hands they shall bear thee up.* [n: (ID.)] By the hands of angels Christ was lifted up into heaven, etc., *as far as* he who cannot be overcome.

VERS. 13.-- *Upon the asp.* After the human infirmity, the divine power is shown, which commands such creatures. And he treats of Christ according to the members. *And he shall trample.* [n: (AUG.)] The Church, which is wary of his cunning, treads upon the serpent. *The lion.* He rages openly against the martyrs.

The dragon lies in wait through the heretics, that he may corrupt the virgin of Christ, whence the Apostle: *I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you a chaste virgin to Christ [n: (II Cor. XI)]*.

VERS. 14.-- *Because he hoped in me.* [n: (AUG.)] Thirdly, the words of God, by which he consoles the Church, to which he promises both defence here, and rewards in the world to come. [n: (CASS.)] [1000C] He gives charge to the angels concerning thee, saying: *Because in me.* The certainty of the promise, through the promise of God.

VERS. 15.-- *He shall cry to me.* [n: (AUG.)] There is no need to fear in tribulation, etc., *as far as* at that time let us labour the more to resist.
