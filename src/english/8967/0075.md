## PSALM XCVII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1007C] *A psalm of David.* [var: Vulg. Ps 97:1 *Psalmus ipsi David.*, "a psalm for David himself"] [n: (CASS.)] The third psalm of those which treat of both comings: in which there is matter of hope through the mercy of the first coming, and of fear through the judgment of the second: these things, even if they are often repeated, are yet always new by reason of the varied setting forth of the words and of the sentiments.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Sing ye to the Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] First he speaks of the showing forth of the saving right hand.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He hath saved.* [n: (AUG.)] It is a greater thing that he raised up the whole world from eternal death, than that he raised the dead. ⟦s2⟧ *To himself.* ⟦s3⟧ Many are saved bodily, but to themselves, not to him: they who, having received bodily health, grow wanton, but they who by faith are healed within, are healed to him, or save themselves.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Salvation . . . justice.* [n: (AUG.)] He who saves and justifies. ⟦s2⟧ *He hath revealed.* ⟦s3⟧ Things to come were foretold under a veil by the [1007D] prophets: but the veil has been taken away, since *truth is sprung out of the earth.*

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Of mercy and of truth.* [n: (AUG.)] *Mercy* is that whereby he promised, *truth* follows upon this, because he rendered it. ⟦s2⟧ But to whom? ⟦s3⟧ *To Israel,* that is, to him who now by faith sees the truth, afterwards by sight.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *All.* [n: (ID.)] Because Christ was not given to certain men only.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Rejoice ye.* [n: (CASS.)] Secondly he says that in all ways he is to be praised and declared, because the judge is come.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *With ductile trumpets and with the voice of a trumpet of horn.* [n: (AUG.)] Of brass, which are drawn out by beating, etc., *as far as* as the horn cleaves to the flesh, and surpasses it. [1008A] [n: (HIERON.)] With ductile trumpets, of silver, after the manner of the Jews. ⟦s2⟧ Mystically, drawn out under the hammer of the whole earth, that is, the devil. [n: (AUG.)] Tribulation is the beating; advancement, the drawing out: such a trumpet was Job, whence he sounded as a trumpet, saying: *The Lord gave, the Lord hath taken away.*

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Let it be moved.* [n: (ID.)] That is, let the sea of the law be made sweet, the wood of the cross being set therein. ⟦s2⟧ *And the fulness thereof.* ⟦s3⟧ All the sacraments of the law, etc., *as far as* but *the rivers,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ *The earth.* [n: (CASS.)] That is, every sinner.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The world of the earth.* ⟦s2⟧ All earthly men. ⟦s3⟧ *In justice.* [n: (AUG.)] Because they did not merit mercy. ⟦s4⟧ *The peoples.* [n: (CASS.)] The faithful out of the Jews and the Gentiles, who shall receive things equal to their merits.

## PSALM XCVIII.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1008B]

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of David.* [var: Vulg. Ps 98:1 *Psalmus ipsi David.*, "a psalm for David himself"] [n: (AUG.)] The prophet describes Christ the king giving good things, etc., *as far as* but the veil has been taken away, since *truth is sprung out of the earth, and justice hath looked down from heaven [n: (Psal. XLVIII)]*. ⟦s2⟧ *The Lord hath reigned* [n: (CASS.)] Concerning the power and the kingdom of Christ, that he may be adored in the man. ⟦s3⟧ He shows Christ reigning, and (whosoever may resist) high above all, whence he admonishes to yield to him. ⟦s4⟧ *Let the peoples be angry.* [n: (CASS.)] A derision. ⟦s5⟧ It can safely be granted that they be angry, because they are not going to hurt any further: he speaks by permitting, not by commanding. ⟦s6⟧ *He sitteth upon the cherubim.* [n: (ID.)] Against him the earth can do nothing, even though he himself was on the earth where he suffered the wrath of the people. ⟦s7⟧ *Cherubim.* [n: (AUG.)] *The fulness of knowledge* [1008C] is its interpretation, which is charity. ⟦s8⟧ The fulness of the knowledge of the law, which is the seat of God, shall be in thee also, if thou hast charity, against which the angry can do nothing. ⟦s9⟧ For what is the earth against heaven?

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The Lord in Sion.* [n: (ID.)] If thou dost not understand what is *upon the Cherubim,* see this, that he is *great in Sion.* ⟦s2⟧ Sion is the soul which beholds God. ⟦s3⟧ *Great in Sion.* [n: (CASS.)] Who the Lord is, he sets forth: *In Sion* born. ⟦s4⟧ *Great,* lest he grow cheap on account of the passion. ⟦s5⟧ Or, yet the Lord is great in the Sion of the Jews, and above the peoples of the Gentiles.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Great.* [n: (AUG.)] It was little when they were angry, that is, made known among few, but now enlarged in all the earth. ⟦s2⟧ *Terrible.* [n: (ID.)] Because even if he is preached as crucified, he is the judge to come, and he shall not err [1008D] in judgment. ⟦s3⟧ *And the honor of the king.* [n: (CASS.)] He sets forth how they ought to confess. ⟦s4⟧ *The honor of the king,* is the glorious preaching, *judgment* requires discernment; he is to be worshipped not with a light will, but with fixed deliberation, which is from him; whence he subjoins: *Thou hast prepared directions.* ⟦s5⟧ Or *the honor of the king* is the damnation of the guilty, whereby the glory of the king is increased.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *In Jacob.* [n: (AUG.)] Not only the patriarch, but the people whom he signifies. ⟦s2⟧ *Thou hast done.* ⟦s3⟧ These things, which another could not do.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Exalt ye.* [n: (CASS.)] The second part admonishes to adore him, because his fathers *were.* ⟦s2⟧ As it were, because he does these things for us, therefore *Exalt ye.*

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The footstool.* [n: (AUG.)] *The earth is the footstool of his feet,* says [1009A] Scripture, etc., *as far as* in whose honor it adores the footstool. ⟦s2⟧ *Of the feet.* [n: (CASS.)] *The feet,* the unfailing stability of the Godhead. ⟦s3⟧ *Holy.* ⟦s4⟧ That thou mayest distinguish the body from the Godhead, even though it be one person.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Moses.* [n: (CASS.)] A priest, because if not victims, he offered the vows of the people, and made supplication for the people.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *A pillar.* [n: (CASS.)] The strength and the beauty of the house. ⟦s2⟧ In this appearance he spoke, which announced the Church, or the flesh, but then in a cloud, now openly through the Son: *He spoke to them out of the cloud.* [n: (ID.)] To them therefore then in a cloud, who now speaks to us in the footstool, that is, openly in the flesh assumed. ⟦s3⟧ *His testimonies and precepts.* [n: (ID.)] A testimony is a signification set down by certain signs of a preceding thing: whence often in the law it is said: *This shall be to you for a testimony.* ⟦s4⟧ The precepts [1009B] are as the precepts of the law given through Moses. [var: Vulg. Ps 98:7 *praeceptum quod dedit illis*, singular, "the precept which he gave them"]

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Thou wast merciful to them.* [n: (AUG.)] It is not said save of sins, merciful in forgiving, merciful also in avenging, behold, even though these were so great, yet they had somewhat of sin; whence he adds, *upon all their inventions.*

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Avenging.* ⟦s2⟧ Lest human elation should creep upon them; whence: There was given me a sting of my flesh *[n: (II Cor. XII)] Inventions.* ⟦s3⟧ Otherwise, affections. [n: (AUG.)] Which God alone knew in their hearts; whence it was a punishment to them to bear with those among whom they were, for whose sins they grieved as for their own, through charity. ⟦s4⟧ For it was not, as is thought by very many, a punishment to Moses not to enter into the land of promise, into which the wretched also entered; but a figure, that they who were under the law, and were unwilling to be under grace, should not enter into rest.

## PSALM XCIX.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1009C]

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm in confession.* ⟦s2⟧ Confession is twofold; one is of praise, the other of sins. ⟦s3⟧ The confession of praise befits the perfect, the confession of sins those who are beginning [sic: *inicipientibus*]; whence in this psalm the prophet admonishes to confession. [n: (CASS.)] This psalm teaches both to praise God unceasingly, and unceasingly to bewail our sins: because neither in evils is one to despair, nor in good things ought the mind to be lifted up. ⟦s4⟧ First he enjoins upon the perfect the chief kind of confession, that is, praise.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Rejoice ye.* [n: (AUG.)] If the voice rejoices outwardly, etc., *as far as* and in what order and how wonderfully he made it. ⟦s2⟧ *All the earth.* ⟦s3⟧ Admonishing to praise. ⟦s4⟧ Not one corner of the earth does he [1009D] exhort: but as he knows the blessing to have been sown everywhere, so from everywhere he requires jubilation. ⟦s5⟧ *Serve ye in gladness.* [n: (AUG.)] This servitude is not of bitterness, nor of indignation, but of happiness and of liberty. ⟦s6⟧ Let charity therefore make thee a servant, because truth has made thee free. ⟦s7⟧ Whence the Lord: *If you shall abide in my word, you shall be my disciples indeed: and you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free [n: (Joan. VIII)]*. [var: Vulg. Jo 8:31 *Si vos manseritis in sermone meo*, "in my discourse"]

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In gladness.* ⟦s2⟧ Because they are set free; not murmuring, as in the desert: whence he subjoins the reward.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Enter ye into his sight.* ⟦s2⟧ It is a great thing to rejoice in the sight of so great a one. ⟦s3⟧ *Enter ye in.* [n: (CASS.)] Secondly, to those about to enter, the second kind of confession; nor let it be doubted, because mercy is with him.
