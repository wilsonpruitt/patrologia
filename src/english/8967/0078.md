## PSALM CII.

[1014B]

VERS. 2.-- *Bless, O soul.* [n: (AUG.)] After the prayers of the poor man, the proclamations of one rejoicing. All who are in Christ are as it were one man, who rouses and exhorts his own soul to bless the Lord, wishing to define why he is to be blessed, lest any be found slothful thereto. Cry with the voice, if there is a man who [sic: *qut*] hears; without voice, if there is no man to hear, because God hears. [n: (CASS.)] By his own benefits he exhorts men to bless God. [n: (AUG.)] The prophet, speaking in the person of the just man in general, according to the higher power of the soul, that is, the reason, exhorts the soul to bless, saying: *Forget not.* Let not the delight of sin be before thine eyes, but the condemnation and the remission of God: think over all thine evils, [1014C] because as many as they are, so many are the good retributions of God. *Retributions.* Not bestowings. Man bestows evils for goods, and God requites him with goods for evils. He bestowed when he made me, he requites when he justifies.

VERS. 3.-- *Who forgiveth.* [n: (AUG.)] This he does in the sacrament of baptism. *Who healeth all thy diseases.* This he does in the life of a faithful man, while the flesh lusts against the spirit, so that we do such things as we would not; and those languors of the old state are healed from day to day, as newness increases, out of the faith which works through love.

VERS. 4.-- *Who redeemeth thy life from destruction.* This is done in the last resurrection of the dead. *Who crowneth,* etc. This is done at the judgment, where, when the king shall sit upon his throne, about to render to every man according to his works [1014D], who shall boast that he has a heart glorious and clean from sin? Therefore it was necessary to make mention of mercy and of compassions in that place where dues would seem to be so rendered that there should be no room left for mercy.

VERS. 5.-- *Thy youth like the eagle's.* [n: (AUG.)] So great a likeness is set down, etc., *as far as* whose mouth old age has closed, who have forgotten to eat our bread.

VERS. 6.-- *He that doeth.* [n: (AUG.)] The second part, where he admonishes men to bless through [cj: *par*; read *per*, "through"] those things which were done for the men of old, and are done for those that come after. *Mercies.* Which they shall obtain who love their enemies, etc., *as far as* for after *mercy* he adds concerning judgment, saying, *And judgment.*

[1015A]VERS. 7.-- *To Moses.* [n: (AUG.)] By Moses, all the saints, etc., *as far as* through whom he gave the law, that the offence [cj: *dilectum*; read *delictum*, "the offence"] might abound.

VERS. 11.-- *For.* [n: (ID.)] The heaven is on every side about the earth, whence there is even for them that sin light, air, breath, rain, whence fruit: without heaven the earth comes to an end, so is the protection of God over them that fear him.

VERS. 12.-- *How far distant,* etc. [n: (ID.)] When sin is forgiven, sins set, grace rises, sins are in their setting; turn thyself away, therefore, from the setting of sins, and stretch toward the rising of grace, and thou risest.

VERS. 14.-- *He hath remembered,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Or according to [cj: *secundam*; read *secundum*, "according to"] another reading: *Remember that we are dust.* As if he should say: *He knew our frame,* that is, our weakness: which frame, being turned [1015B] to God, says: *Remember that we are dust,* that is, let thy mercy persevere toward us, lest we forget thy grace. Or, *Man is as grass,* etc. For man goes forth as *a flower,* etc., *as far as The mercy of the Lord. He shall flourish forth* [n: (CASS.)] That is, at the most he shall thus flourish or flourish forth, that is, shall shed his flower. And so it is either intensive or privative.

VERS. 16.-- *For the spirit shall pass in him. And he shall not abide.*

VERS. 17.-- *And justice.* [n: (AUG.)] Mercy and justice are a retribution. He says, *upon the children's children;* many just men have no children, much less children's children, but the *children* are works; the *children's children* are the wages of works.

VERS. 19.-- *The Lord hath prepared his seat in heaven.* [n: (CASS.)] He proclaims the judgment to come, lest they that sin grow insolent; [1015C] as if: and thou oughtest to be mindful, so as to act, because the Lord is in heaven.

VERS. 21.-- *Bless the Lord, all ye his angels, ye mighty.* [n: (ID.)] In the third place he admonishes the heavenly beings and all other creatures to bless. *Bless the Lord,* etc. He speaks by way of congratulation, not by way of command or of admonition. *His ministers.* [n: (AUG.)] Bless ye, etc., *as far as* that what he has in his mouth he may show forth in his work.
