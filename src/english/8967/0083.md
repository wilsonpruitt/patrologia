## PSALM CVIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end, a psalm.* [n: (AUG.)] A prophecy concerning Christ, etc., *as far as* and the things which are expressed concerning Judas, as that: *And his bishopric let another [1028D] take.*

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (CASS.)] The fifth of those which treat more at large of the passion.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *O God, praise.* [n: (CASS.)] He admonishes men to turn aside from the malice of Judas, etc., *as far as* Christ therefore, according as he is man, praying to the Father says: *For the mouth of the sinner.* [n: (ID.)] A comely diversity, because the wicked are not silent in their reviling.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Without cause.* [n: (AUG.)] As equity pleases the godly of itself, so does iniquity the ungodly.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *In return for this, that me.* [n: (ID.)] They do more hurt who detract from Christ in his members, because they slay the souls of them that would believe, than they who slew his flesh, soon about to rise again.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1029A]VERS. 5.-- *And they set [sic: *posueruent*] against.* [n: (AUG.)] There are six differences, etc., *as far as* which is good for evil, which is, God the judge does. [n: (CASS.)] In three ways is every sin contracted, etc., *as far as* which Christ did when he prayed for the Jews.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Set thou over.* [n: (AUG.)] The second part, where he begins to prophesy what they shall receive for their impiety. ⟦s2⟧ Above, of many, etc., *as far as* whose person Judas bore. ⟦s3⟧ *Set thou a sinner over him.* ⟦s4⟧ As of Judas, so of them.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *When he is judged, may he go out condemned.* ⟦s2⟧ Because, abiding in wickedness, they treasure up wrath to themselves. ⟦s3⟧ *Prayer unto sin:* because it is not made through the Mediator.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Few days.* ⟦s2⟧ According to the kingdom, which [1029B] did not stand long after. ⟦s3⟧ *The bishopric,* that is, Christ (who was sent to them), the people of the Gentiles received.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Children fatherless:* the kingdom, as a father, being lost; or God the Father. ⟦s2⟧ *The wife,* that is, the people. ⟦s3⟧ *Widows,* the kingdom being lost.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Let it be transferred,* as was done in the captivity. ⟦s2⟧ *Let them beg,* of the kings of the Gentiles.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *All his substance,* that is, his life, punishing each single thing of sin; their debts are not forgiven, because in Christ alone are they forgiven. ⟦s2⟧ *And strangers,* that is, the devil and his angels. ⟦s3⟧ *Let them plunder his labours,* Because they lay up treasure in heaven [cj: *thesaurizant*; read *non thesaurizant*, "lay not up"].

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And let there not be for him,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That is, there is no helper for him to whom Christ is wanting. ⟦s3⟧ *Nor let there be any to have mercy.* ⟦s4⟧ I say not for the having of temporal things, but of eternal.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1029C]VERS. 13.-- *Let his children be given over to destruction* everlasting. ⟦s2⟧ Therefore, because they remained in *one generation,* that is, because they were not regenerated. ⟦s3⟧ *And let the name be blotted out.* ⟦s4⟧ That is, of the earthly Jerusalem, which is in bondage with her children, which slew the prophets.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *And let it perish from the land of the living,* that is, the Church. ⟦s2⟧ *Their memory,* because, though they were natural branches, so they became.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Because he remembered not.* ⟦s2⟧ By doing penance.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To shew mercy.* ⟦s2⟧ Upon the members of Christ.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Cursing, and it shall come to him: and he would not have blessing.* [n: (AUG.)] Sins delight for a season, but they are called a cursing, because they prepare torments.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Wherewith he is covered.* ⟦s2⟧ He seems to say here, [1029D] etc., *as far as* let him be always ready, whence here he says *he is girded always.*

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *With the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] They detract from Christ especially who say that he is not of the Father, or that he is less than the Father. ⟦s2⟧ *Evils against the soul.* ⟦s3⟧ Saying that he could not rise again when he would.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *And thou, O Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] The third part, where he prays, etc., *as far as* help me, according to the man. ⟦s2⟧ *For thy name's sake.* ⟦s3⟧ For by no works, etc., *as far as because thy mercy is sweet.*

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Because needy and poor.* [n: (ID.)] The neediness and poverty of Christ is the infirmity out of which he was crucified.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1030A]VERS. 23.-- *I am taken away as the shadow when it declineth.* [n: (CASS.)] As night comes of the declining shadow, so death comes of mortal flesh.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *It hath been changed.* [n: (AUG.)] The flesh of Christ therefore has been changed, etc., *as far as because Jesus was not yet glorified. ⟦s2⟧ Because of the oil,* that is, spiritual grace, whence Christ is named.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *They moved their heads.* [n: (CASS.)] Madmen threaten by the shaking of the head. [n: (AUG.)] They moved their heads, because they saw him hanging on the cross, but not yet when the flesh was changed.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *Help me, O Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] He asks as man, he grants as God.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *This is thy hand.* [n: (AUG.)] Christ is this hand, and he is the Word with God, and he made it, when the Word was made flesh. ⟦s2⟧ For he was without [1030B] time according to the Godhead, and it was made in time according to the flesh.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *They shall curse, and thou wilt bless.* ⟦s2⟧ One is the cursing of the wicked; God, when he blesses, does what he says.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *As with a diploide,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Others interpret, for diploide, a double cloak, that is, let them be confounded within and without, that is, before God and before men.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *Exceedingly in my mouth.* ⟦s2⟧ *Exceedingly* is wont to be put for more than is due, but also for greatly. ⟦s3⟧ *In the midst.* [n: (AUG.)] That is, in common, or in the heart.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Because he hath stood at the right hand of the poor.* ⟦s2⟧ The devil at the right hand of Judas, etc., *as far as* but that he might save the soul [1030C] from them that persecute it.

## PSALM CIX.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *A psalm of David.* [n: (CASS.)] This psalm is the seventh, etc., *as far as* whatsoever [sic: *quiquid*] is contained in either Testament. [n: (CASS.)] The prophet exhorts men to be made subject to Christ. ⟦s2⟧ First the prophet relates the words of the Father concerning the Son, etc., *as far as* this belongs to the humanity.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The Lord said.* [n: (CASS.)] After our manner, that man may be instructed. ⟦s2⟧ *The Lord to the Lord.* ⟦s3⟧ Distinguish the persons, and receive one nature and one power. ⟦s4⟧ *Sit thou.* ⟦s5⟧ After labour, rest, and reign together, according as thou art man. ⟦s6⟧ *At my right hand.* ⟦s7⟧ Among my better and hidden goods, until thou reign manifest. ⟦s8⟧ And this is, *until I make thy enemies,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] *Sit thou,* not only on high, that thou mayest have dominion over the exalted, but also in secret, etc., [1030D] *as far as* if he sees not what he believes. ⟦s9⟧ *A footstool for thy feet:* from a similitude, wherein also full subjection is marked. ⟦s10⟧ By the feet, eternal stability: this daily in them that are converted.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The rod of power.* ⟦s2⟧ Now the kingdom of Christ begins out of Sion, that is, from the Jews, whence he subjoins, *The rod of power,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ *Out of Sion.* ⟦s4⟧ The prophet speaks not of that kingdom of Christ, etc., *as far as and remission of sins throughout all nations, beginning at Jerusalem.*

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *With thee the beginning.* [n: (CASS.)] Secondly the Father declares the nature of the Godhead, etc., *as far as* when in the resurrection they shall be resplendent, as the angels of God. [n: (AUG.)] [1031A] Already he has dominion, already there is power; but *in the day* of his special power, etc., *as far as* in what manner the Father is with him the beginning. ⟦s2⟧ This then, but now. ⟦s3⟧ *From the womb,* that is, *as* from my secret and hidden substance, etc., *as far as* one who may offer himself, whence there follows, *He hath sworn,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Or *With thee the beginning:* this the Father says; as it were, this I grant to thee more than to another, because thou art the Son by nature. [n: (AUG.)] Or it can be taken as of the person of the prophet speaking, etc., *as far as* with the Holy Ghost, who makes the saints resplendent.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *The Lord hath sworn.* [n: (CASS.)] Thirdly the prophet treats of the humanity of Christ, etc., *as far as* hence it is plain that the victims pass away according to the order of Aaron. ⟦s2⟧ *It shall not repent him: thou art a priest, for ever.* [n: (AUG.)] Upon God no repentance falls: for he knows [1031B] what he does, nor does he change his counsel; but by the word "repentance" is signified the change of things falling out otherwise than one hoped. ⟦s3⟧ For in what manner, when it repents thee to have done anything, thou grievest for the deed which thou hast done.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Kings* [n: (AUG.)] Who wish, or wished, to blot out the name of Christ.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *He shall crush,* that is, he has rendered them weak, etc., *as far as* and now *he shall judge* among the Gentiles, that he may fill *ruins,* not the upright. ⟦s2⟧ *He shall judge among nations.* [n: (CASS.)] As it were, he breaks kings for their pride: he judges peoples for their common sins: both, that they may be humbled unto conversion. ⟦s3⟧ Or,

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Of the torrent.* [n: (CASS.)] Of the passing and troubled passion. ⟦s2⟧ In the way, whereby men go to another dwelling.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1031C] *He shall lift up the head.* ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *For this cause he exalted him, and God gave him a name which is above every name, that in the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of them that are in heaven, on earth, and under the earth, and that every tongue should confess that the Lord Jesus Christ is in the glory of God the Father.*
