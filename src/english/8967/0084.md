## PSALM CX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Alleluia.* [n: (CASS.)] The first psalm through all the letters of the alphabet, etc., *as far as* thirdly he affirms the same men redeemed, and the New Testament abiding, there, *Redemption.*

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *I will give praise.* [n: (AUG.)] Here there is no confession of sins, but of praise: the one mourns, this rejoices: the one shows the wound to the physician, this gives thanks for health. ⟦s2⟧ *In the council.* ⟦s3⟧ Not rashly, but out of reason, namely [1031D] for things eternal, as all the just do. [n: (CASS.)] *In the council of the just.* ⟦s4⟧ Here never among all the just, where the wicked are mingled with them, but they are seen already to sing in the fellowship to come, caught up by hope to the things above. ⟦s5⟧ *In the council,* which is, when they judge with the Lord. ⟦s6⟧ *In the congregation,* when they are gathered together out of the whole.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Great works.* ⟦s2⟧ This is the matter and the cause of the confession. ⟦s3⟧ Great are the works of the Lord, in that he makes a godly man out of an ungodly. ⟦s4⟧ *Sought out.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] To which nothing is like, while he suffers the devil to rage, etc., *as far as* not: *for his justice remaineth for ever and ever. ⟦s5⟧ Unto all the wills.* [n: (AUG.)] Nothing surpasses the will of God, who, even if he wills that thou sin not, yet does not prevent it. ⟦s6⟧ If he wills to spare the penitent, he wills also to punish the impenitent; [1032A] so concerning man God always fulfils his own will, because man does nothing out of which God does not work what he wills.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Confession and magnificence is his work.* [n: (CASS.)] As it were: I will give praise, because great are the works of the Lord. ⟦s2⟧ Any works soever are also great, if in them there be seen the intention and the will, which is the *confession* of sins, and the *magnificence* of good works: and *justice,* that is, the reward which is justly given.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *He hath made a remembrance.* [n: (ID.)] Secondly he says that the faithful are filled with a plentiful gift, promising the coming of the Lord, that the inheritance may be eagerly sought. [n: (AUG.)] *He hath made a remembrance of his wonderful works.* ⟦s2⟧ Humbling this man and exalting that, etc., *as far as* what shall he receive, he is glorified [cj: *glorificatur*; read *glorificatus*, "glorified"], in the things to come?

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1032B]VERS. 5.-- *He hath given food.* [n: (CASS.)] He makes confession and magnificence, etc., *as far as* that all things may be fulfilled. ⟦s2⟧ *He will be mindful,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Nor did he give the whole who gave a pledge, etc., *as far as* they enter through the piercing straits of the passion.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The power of the works.* [n: (CASS.)] The power of the works is that the blind see, etc. ⟦s2⟧ Which things he made known to the Christian people. ⟦s3⟧ *That he may give them the inheritance,* this was the intent of the miracles, that they might believe.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *That he may give them the inheritance of the Gentiles.* [n: (ID.)] As it were: although he gives them the inheritance, yet they shall suffer many things. ⟦s2⟧ *The works of his hands.* [n: (ID.)] That is, of his power. ⟦s3⟧ *Truth,* while he restores to the converted what was promised: *Judgment,* which he threatens to the ungodly. [n: (AUG.)] *Truth,* which the holy martyrs hold, and for which they are also judged [1032C] here; *and judgment,* whereby in time to come they shall judge those by whom they are here judged. [n: (ID.)] *The works of his hands are truth and judgment.* ⟦s4⟧ Let *truth* be held by those who are judged, etc., *as far as* this is the inheritance of the New Testament.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *All the commandments are faithful.* [n: (CASS.)] Because they keep those who keep them, they are not lies. ⟦s2⟧ *Confirmed.* ⟦s3⟧ Not sent forth in vain, as human words are: *Made,* not only spoken, *in truth,* because all are fulfilled.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In equity.* [n: (AUG.)] Because all things are done with the balance of justice.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9, 10.-- *He hath sent redemption.* [n: (CASS.)] Thirdly he affirms the redeemed, and the New Testament consecrated by eternal grace; or. ⟦s2⟧ *His name: the beginning of wisdom.* [n: (ID.)] That is, the Son, who in his humanity is *holy,* [1032D] in the power of the Godhead *terrible.* ⟦s3⟧ Let the advocate therefore be loved, let the judge be feared, lest thou be negligent, lest thou despair.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The fear of the Lord. [n: (ID.)] The dread of judgment is the gate of conversion, if the discernment of love be added to it. ⟦s2⟧ *His praise continueth.* ⟦s3⟧ The intent of the psalm, whence Alleluia is prefixed, began with praise and ends in praise: the faithful people, delivered from the devil and giving thanks, has expressed what is to be done here also by the devout, and to be accomplished in heaven: here because he delivers, there because he crowns.

## PSALM CXI.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Alleluia of the return.* [n: (AUG.)] Aggeus and Zacharias were long after David, etc., *as far as* it may be made firm eternally. [n: (CASS.)] The perfection of this building, etc., *as far [1033A] as* as though men delivered [sic: *liberavi*] from captivity and glad because of the good things which they have.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Blessed is the man.* [n: (CASS.)] Here the Psalmist shows, after the deliverance, etc., *as far as* thirdly he affirms that adverse things befall the ungodly. ⟦s2⟧ *That feareth the Lord.* ⟦s3⟧ The fear of the world makes men wretched, but the fear of God makes a man desire those things whereby he is blessed. ⟦s4⟧ *In the commandments.* ⟦s5⟧ This the fear of God does, that he should will. ⟦s6⟧ And he says not "he shall do," etc., *as far as* and *the earthly habitation presseth down the mind that museth upon many things.*

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Mighty.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] Behold the rewards of that blessed man. ⟦s2⟧ *His seed.* ⟦s3⟧ That is, the reward of a good work; *Shall be mighty upon earth,* not upon this earth, where he is a laughing-stock, but upon the heavenly. ⟦s4⟧ Or even here in this present time, wherein it is less seen. ⟦s5⟧ *Seed,* the works of mercy, whence comes the everlasting harvest. ⟦s6⟧ *Mighty,* because with a very small thing, even as with a great one, the kingdom of heaven [1033B] is bought. ⟦s7⟧ *The generation.* ⟦s8⟧ Not of the flesh, but of imitation. ⟦s9⟧ Or the works of the upright. ⟦s10⟧ *The upright* are they who resist not the Father when he corrects, and who believe him when he promises, and who believe not that their works have perished when no requital is made them in time. ⟦s11⟧ For there are those who do those good things while hoping here for a reward from God, or wishing to please men, which is not the blessed man: and yet,

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Glory and wealth in his house.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, in the secret of the heart, where he dwells with the hope of eternal life, where holy riches and eternal glory are laid up. ⟦s3⟧ *Justice.* ⟦s4⟧ This is his glory, these are his riches, which nevertheless he has from God. ⟦s5⟧ *It remaineth for ever.* ⟦s6⟧ As it were, he is not blessed for a time.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *There hath risen up.* [n: (CASS.)] Secondly he treats of the coming of the Lord, etc., *as far as* that he may fortify it beforehand with firm allegations [1033C] and assertions in the judgment. ⟦s2⟧ Or,

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *He sheweth mercy.* [n: (ID.)] He who gives to the needy; *He lendeth,* he who grants a loan, to receive afterwards as much again.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7, 8.-- *His heart is ready to hope in the Lord, his heart is strengthened, it remaineth,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] It is plain therefore that this man seeks not his own things, but the things which are Christ's; he bears labours patiently, he awaits the promises with confidence. [n: (CASS.)] Or, if any man threatens evils, he fears not, but is ready.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *He hath distributed.* ⟦s2⟧ He hopes not to fear, because he has distributed. ⟦s3⟧ *He hath given to the poor.* [n: (AUG.)] Behold, this man was buying what he did not see, but that other was keeping a treasure for him in heaven, who vouchsafes to hunger and to thirst in the poor upon earth. ⟦s4⟧ *His justice remaineth for ever and ever.* [n: (CASS.)] [1033D] He gave what was his own, and lived justly and godly; or, he who gives what is his own works justice. ⟦s5⟧ *His horn shall be exalted in glory.* ⟦s6⟧ Because he is humble here, there he shall have power.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *The sinner shall see.* [n: (ID.)] Thirdly he affirms that contrary things befall the ungodly. ⟦s2⟧ By the good things of the blessed he exhorts, by the evils of the enemies he terrifies, and this psalm is an instructor of the faithful.
