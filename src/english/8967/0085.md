## PSALM CXII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Alleluia* [n: (CASS.)] Because he is worthily to be praised who by the merit of humility takes up the Gentiles, he expounds the title of the psalm. [1034A] For Alleluia, as we have said, is interpreted "praise ye the Lord," in which manner the psalm also begins.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Praise ye.* [n: (ID.)] First the Prophet admonishes the devout to praise continually, and everywhere to preach. ⟦s2⟧ Secondly he himself does what he admonishes others to do, that is, he praises God there: *Who is as the Lord [n: (I Cor. XIV)]*? ⟦s3⟧ Admonishing therefore unto praise, he shows and says who and whom, and how long, and where, and why they should praise. ⟦s4⟧ *Children.* ⟦s5⟧ Not in sense, but in malice, and chiefly in pride, that is, the humble; whence, *Be not children in sense, but in malice be children, that in mind ye may be perfect.* [n: (AUG., CASS.)] Praise the Lord, ye children, the simple and the pure, who ought to praise.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Let the name be.* [n: (CASS.)] Behold, he has said who and whom they ought to praise; then he subjoins how long we praise [cj: *laudamus*; read *laudandus*, "is to be praised"], namely always. ⟦s2⟧ *Let the name of the Lord be:* [1034B] lest ye think that I command praise for an hour, etc., *as far as* by the Gentiles the carnal, by the heavens the spiritual.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *From the rising of the sun.* [n: (AUG.)] Here he says that he ought to be praised everywhere.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Above the heavens.* [n: (ID.)] The Apostles, through whom he reigns among the Gentiles, and is; therefore I invite to praises, because he is to reign above all.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Who is as.* [n: (CASS.)] Secondly he himself does what he admonishes others to do, that by the deed also he may teach others. ⟦s2⟧ *Who?* ⟦s3⟧ None that can of himself. ⟦s4⟧ *In the high places,* etc. ⟦s5⟧ In the angels and the saints: and although he is high, he despises not the lowly.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The lowly things.* ⟦s2⟧ These are they who by his gift are made high, and in them he sits. ⟦s3⟧ *He beholdeth,* the lowly one by one. ⟦s4⟧ *In the earth,* the saints, whom he so beholds as, on the contrary, he hates the proud.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1034C]VERS. 7.-- *Raising up from the earth:* from the labour of the body. ⟦s2⟧ *Out of the dunghill,* he is lifted up over whom the vices of the flesh have no dominion.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Who maketh to dwell.* [n: (CASS., AUG.)] He expounds where he raises up and lifts up, etc., *as far as* out of the mammon of iniquity by works of mercy. ⟦s2⟧ *The barren woman.* [n: (AUG.)] Namely Sara, that is, the Gentile Church, which was barren before, whereby she is signified by Sara. ⟦s3⟧ Her, I say, he makes to dwell in the house, that is, in the Church of the Jews, among the Jews themselves, that in the corner-stone the two walls may be joined together, and there may be made one fold. ⟦s4⟧ *Of children.* [n: (ID.)] In whose person these things are said, etc., *as far as* since I know myself to be without children, and a widow.

## PSALM CXIII.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1034D] *Alleluia.* [n: (CASS.)] Here again the Alleluia is set down from another occasion, etc., *as far as* as becomes clear in the exposition of the psalm.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *At the going out.* [n: (AUG.)] Here things past are not related, but things to come are foretold, because in those deeds things to come were signified: and therefore not all the things which were done there are spoken of here, but certain of them otherwise, lest he be thought to be recounting things past. [n: (CASS.)] First the Prophet tells what wonders God gave to the Hebrews in figure, and to Christians in spirit. ⟦s2⟧ Secondly he asks why these things were done, there, *What ailed thee, O sea?* ⟦s3⟧ Thirdly he reproves the empty idols of the Gentiles, and commends the religion of God, there, *the idols of the Gentiles.*

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *At the going out.* ⟦s2⟧ As it were, when Israel, that is, the house of Jacob, went out of Egypt, that is, from a barbarous people.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1035A] *Barbarous.* [n: (AUG.)] That tongue is barbarous which praises not God.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *It was made.* [n: (CASS.)] As that people, when it went out of Egypt, etc., *as far as* they are able to resist vices, and this.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The sea saw,* that is, the sinners of the world. ⟦s2⟧ *Jordan,* they who by divers desires snatch men into the great sea, that is, into the embittering malice of this world; but these things cease at the coming of the Lord, being as it were turned backward.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The sea saw.* [n: (AUG.)] This is here said otherwise than it was done. ⟦s2⟧ There it is read to have stood on that side from which the waters were flowing down, nor is anything said there of the mountains skipping; but both the things done there and the things said here are figures.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1035B]VERS. 4.-- *Rams.* [n: (ID.)] Who with the horns of both, etc., *as far as* every knowledge lifting itself up against Christ. ⟦s2⟧ *The hills.* [n: (CASS.)] From tilling, the middling sort: for these things are not according to the letter; whence also by the sea and by Jordan, that is, by the ancient deeds, doubt not that those things are signified which today are done spiritually.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *What ailed thee.* [n: (ID.)] Secondly he asks why these things were, that he may stir up wonder. ⟦s2⟧ And he resolves it: *At the face of the Lord.*

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *At the face.* [n: (ID.)] One answer for two, because one author both made the ages to be astonished, and made his own, that is, the mountains and the hills, to rejoice: for at the appearing of Christ men were moved from their own superstition to the worship of God.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *At the face of the God of Jacob.* ⟦s2⟧ And as though one should ask, who is the Lord? [1035C] The God of Jacob, not a new one; who makes them wrestle.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Who turned the rock.* [n: (ID.)] The hard hearts of the Jews, unto the water of baptism.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The crag.* ⟦s2⟧ The Gentiles, that they also may pour forth the watering fountains of preaching.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AUG.)] That is, himself, hard before, while he was not known, etc., *as far as* he might overflow upon all that believe, saying: *If any man thirst, let him come to me and drink.*

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Not to us.* [n: (CASS.)] When he had said that God gives good things, etc., *as far as* truth, while he renders the rewards, or punishes the ungodly. ⟦s2⟧ Therefore, Give.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Lest at any time:* this the ungodly often lay to the charge of the good when they are afflicted.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Mercy.* [n: (AUG.)], that is, by restoration.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Truth,* that is, by the promise fulfilled.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1035D] *Where is God.* [n: (ID.)] This the Gentiles are wont to say, who have visible gods: because we worship an invisible God.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *In heaven.* [n: (ID.)], that is, in Christ the man, according to that: *God was in Christ, reconciling the world to himself.* ⟦s2⟧ Or, *in heaven,* [n: (CASS.)] that is, mighty above all creatures.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He hath done all things whatsoever he would.* ⟦s2⟧ Whereby he appears almighty.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *The idols of the Gentiles.* [n: (ID.)] Thirdly he proves the idols empty, and shows how profitable the religion of God is. ⟦s2⟧ And here is a perfect demonstration in dispraise and in praise. [n: (AUG.)] The power of the true God having been shown from his works, he passes to the visible gods of the Gentiles. ⟦s3⟧ *Silver.* [n: (ID.)] But because God made this, he says, *The works [1036A] of the hands of men:* they venerate that which they made thereof, for God created the matter; but the folly of men added the form.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *They have mouths.* [n: (ID.)] They were formed by hand, etc., *as far as* and they served the creature rather than the Creator.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *They shall not cry.* [n: (ID.)] After speaking (which is proper to man) he adds this which is common, etc., *as far as* and an idol wanting life and sense.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Like unto them.* [n: (ID.)] See the worship that should shame them, when he says *like,* which indeed they are; or let them understand themselves to be like unto them.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The house of Israel.* [n: (CASS.)] Here he shows the profit of the Christian religion.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *The house of Aaron.* [n: (AUG.)] But lest that should seem to be already a thing possessed by the greater which to the lesser is [1036B] a hope, he adds, *The house of Aaron.*

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Mindful.* [n: (CASS.)] That he might convert them, and he blessed the converted through the same.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Upon you.* [n: (AUG.)] Because the number of teachers increased.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Upon the children.* ⟦s2⟧ Because the number of followers also increased. ⟦s3⟧ Therefore ye both, fathers and sons, are blessed of the Lord, who made you *heaven* in the greater, *and earth* in the lesser; or who made every creature.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Blessed be ye of the Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] He who above wished to be blessed, now with confidence says, *Blessed,* which belongs to God, that ye may serve him. ⟦s2⟧ Truly *of the Lord,* because even *the heaven of heaven,* that is, Christ, is unto the honour of the Lord; or,

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *The heaven of heaven,* that is, the empyrean, he gave [1036C] to the Lord Christ, according as he is man. ⟦s2⟧ *But the earth.* ⟦s3⟧ This lower earth he gave. ⟦s4⟧ *To the children of men.* ⟦s5⟧ That in it they may purify themselves; or the people to the preachers; whence: As the Father hath sent me, and I will send you [n: (Joan. VI)].

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The heaven of heaven.* [n: (AUG.)] He speaks of those whose minds, etc., *as far as* whom the weak cannot yet see without a support.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Nor all they that go down.* [n: (CASS.)] Or, not all they that go down; because the saints also went down, but not into the lower place.
