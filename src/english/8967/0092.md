## PSALM CXXXI.

VERS. 1.-- *A canticle of degrees.* [n: (ID.)] In the thirteenth degree, now humble and meek, etc., *as far as* whence in Isaias: *Upon whom shall my Spirit rest, [1050C] but upon the humble and the quiet, and him that trembleth at my words?* [var: Vulg. Is 66:2 *ad quem autem respiciam, nisi ad pauperculum, et contritum spiritu, et trementem sermones meos*, "but to whom shall I have respect, but to him that is poor and little, and of a contrite spirit"]

*Remember, O Lord, David, and all.* [n: (ID.)] First, showing the vow of building a house, he prays that he may fulfil it. Secondly, he shows the house built, there, *Behold we have heard.* Thirdly, he tells the promises of God made to Sion when built, there, *The Lord hath sworn.* Willing therefore to build a house for the Lord, he says in the person of the whole Christ, the head: *O Lord,* etc. *David.* [n: (AUG.)] According to the letter, meek, in that he rendered not evil for evil to Saul.

*As he swore and vowed a vow.* That is, that he may fulfil what he promised: let no man presume to render by his own strength what he has vowed. And the Church has vowed in meekness that she may be the house of God, because nothing more pleasing can be offered to God. And it is a house if it seeks not its own, but rejoices in [1050D] what is common: from things of one's own come evils, whence he says: *If I shall enter.* He knew that he was hindered by what was his own, lest a place for God should be made.

VERS. 3.-- *If I shall enter* as far as *till I find out a place for the Lord.* The letter is left hanging, and it is an aposiopesis, as if he said: If I shall do this or that, let me never hereafter be believed. As it were, David swore to the Lord: *If I shall enter into the tabernacle of my house,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] Or, in the person of Christ, from that place: *If I shall enter into the tabernacle of my house,* namely of the heavenly habitation. The tabernacle is the heavenly habitation. *If I shall go up,* etc., to the right hand of the Father, where is the bed, that is, rest and the end of labours.

VERS. 4.-- *Sleep* [sic: *Sumnum*], the rest of the three days' death. [1051A] Then in other words he repeats the same. *And to my eyelids slumber. And rest to my temples* until the holy Church be founded by preaching. It is an aposiopesis, as it were, let me be believed no more.

*And to my eyelids.* [n: (AUG.)] There is he who sleeps not, but slumbers: now he is drawn back from earthly things, now he is rolled back into them, often he nods his head.

VERS. 5.-- *And rest.* From the rest of the temples sleep comes to the eyes, because for them that are about to sleep the temples begin. When therefore something temporal begins to give delight unto sin, the temples are weighed down; but give not thyself to that delight, and sleep comes not to the eyes, by this thought, if, as though rubbing thy forehead, thou shakest off sleep.

*I shall find:* [n: (ID.)] he says it, because he sought long.

[1051B] *A place for the Lord:* he sought it in himself, who rests upon the humble and the quiet. And a man is a place of God if he forsakes his own things; or at the least, if he loves not himself, but God.

*A tabernacle.* [n: (ID.)] As yet we groan. In the house we shall praise, because groaning belongs to them that are on pilgrimage; praise to them that already abide in the fatherland. *To the God of Jacob.* He says that he has paid, as above he said that he had promised.

VERS. 6.-- *Behold we have heard.* (CAS.) The second part, where the house is shown built, and where he also prays that they may be exalted, and that the Jews be not utterly strangers. *It in Ephrata,* [n: (AUG.)] or *it,* etc., *as far as* where were the brambles of idolatry.

[n: (CASS.)] Behold, hear, namely that the Lord was to be born in Ephrata Bethlehem, where the Lord was born: it was heard [1051C] that he should be born in Bethlehem, but for the faith of the Gentiles he was found *in the fields of the wood.*

VERS. 7.-- *We will go in.* [n: (AUG.)] We too, by persevering in our warfare, have nevertheless gone in (or shall go in) into his tabernacle, the tabernacle of the God of Jacob: to this house belongs he who is framed together with others in charity as with living stones. He therefore enters who loves, and he who enters is made the house of God. *In the place.* The house of God is where he ought to be adored: apart from which God hears not unto life eternal. He belongs to the house who is framed together with others in charity. Or the place in which the feet of the house of the Lord stand is Christ, in whom they persevere.

VERS. 8.-- *Arise.* [n: (ID.)] And it is said to him who says, *I have slept troubled:* Arise from death, into thy rest, [1051D] that thou be troubled no more, because *death shall no more have dominion.*

VERS. 9.-- *Priests.* [n: (ID.)] He takes the same men for priests and for saints, namely all the faithful. Or, by *priests* he signifies the prelates of the Church; by *saints,* the faithful that are subject. The ark therefore, which he had prayed might arise, he distinguishes by the parts of the Church.

*Priests,* as in the Old Testament the priests are adorned with divers ornaments, so the priests of the Gospel with divers virtues: these are the ornaments which God requires. For what the pontiffs of the law bore upon their garments, the priests of the Gospel ought to have in their hearts.

VERS. 10.-- *For thy servant David's sake.* [n: (CASS.)] *Remember:* [1052A] And well do I say *for David's sake,* because *the Lord hath sworn to David.*

*For the sake of:* then in the person of David himself entreating God the Father.

[n: (AUG.)] For because Christ turned away his face from the Jews unto the Gentiles, the Father is entreated for the remnant, that they may be saved, which was fulfilled in the apostles and in many others. And he says: *for David's sake,* as if he said: Remember me also, that for my sake thou cast not off the Jews utterly.

VERS. 11.-- *He hath sworn.* [n: (CASS.)] The third part, where he tells the promises of God made to Sion when built, that is, to the Church. And David speaks in his own person or in the person of the whole Christ. *And he will not make it void* (otherwise) *he will not repent.* [n: (AUG.)] God is said to repent, etc., *as far as* [1052B] for thou doest it because thou hadst erred: he does it, and delivers. *Of the fruit of thy womb.* He could have said, of the fruit of thy loins, etc., *as far as* because those whom David ruled temporally, Christ rules spiritually.

VERS. 12.-- *If they shall keep.* [n: (ID.)] They seem to earn merit, etc., *as far as* this is not changed. *And their children.* [n: (CASS.)] *If they shall keep the testament. They shall sit upon thy seat,* that is, they shall be partakers of thy blessedness: that as thou art of kingly power, so they also, according to their measure, and this, *Unto the age of the age,* that is, unto eternity.

VERS. 14.-- *This is my rest.* [n: (ID.)] So does God love us, that he says that he himself rests, if we rest in him.

VERS. 15.-- *The widow.* [n: (ID.)] The soul is a widow [1052C] which understands itself forsaken of all help save of God alone. *With bread.* God is the bread, who in the flesh made himself milk.

VERS. 16.-- *Her priests.* Or a distinction is to be made between priests and saints, as above. And it is, *Her priests,* that is, the prelates, *I will clothe with salvation,* that is, with Christ, that is, that they may be honourable for teaching. *And her saints,* that is, those subject. *They shall rejoice with exceeding great joy* over Christ.

*They shall rejoice with exceeding great joy.* [n: (ID.)] Because they are clothed with salvation, etc., *as far as* who dwells in him; therefore he has exalted [sic: *exaltaxit*] securely.

VERS. 17.-- *The horn of David, I have prepared a lamp for Christ.* [n: (ID.)] The spiritual height of the Church, etc., *as far as* before the death and resurrection of Christ.

[1052D]VERS. 18.-- *I will clothe with confusion,* etc. [n: (CASS.)] This the Son will do, to whom all judgment is given. But note that the Father, who here speaks, does what the Son does.

## PSALM CXXXII.

VERS. 1.-- *A canticle of degrees.* [n: (ID.)] The fourteenth degree: charity, which makes brethren to dwell in one, whence blessing and life. *Behold how good.* And this psalm is an atom, etc., *as far as* here therefore the just man, rejoicing over the dwelling together of brethren, says: *Behold how. Behold how.* [n: (AUG.)] That verse, most famous in the whole world, has as the trumpet of the Holy Ghost gathered together them that were divided: it has brought forth very many monasteries. [n: (CASS.)] *Behold.* As though more were set forth by the showing than could be unfolded by the tongue. *To dwell in [1053A] one.* [n: (AUG.)] This concerning all Christians, but it properly befits monasteries, from which nevertheless the blessing comes down to the rest, as also to those brethren of Judaea, of whom it is said: *They had one heart,* and *one soul.*

VERS. 2.-- *As the ointment.* [n: (THEOD.)] The priestly oil, etc., *as far as* it perfects the odour of perfect virtue. *The beard.* [n: (AUG.)] Behold the determination, that is, upon the apostles and the other primitive saints, who first strove in faith, but could not be overcome, because the head that had suffered had already gone before them. *Aaron.* The priest who offered up himself, etc., *as far as* but they could not be overcome. From them. *To the skirt.* [n: (CASS.)] Or by the *skirt,* which is the edge of the garment, etc., *as far as* the profit of concord reaches even to them that are subject.

VERS. 3.-- *As the dew.* [n: (ID.)] *Dew* is thin water, whence the earth brings forth, and it signifies grace, whereby sinners are converted. *Hermon.* [n: (AUG.)] According to the letter a mountain beyond Jordan, etc., *as far as* the rest are taken up, who are *in one* in heart.
