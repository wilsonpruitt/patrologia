## PSALM CXXXIII.

VERS. 1.-- *A canticle of degrees.* [n: (CASS.)] The fifteenth degree: the charity of the world. And this is the third psalm concerning the charity of God, of which he here treats, as above of the charity of the neighbour. There is not one grace whereby God is loved, and another whereby the neighbour. But because grace has divers acts, and by it divers things are loved. He admonishes therefore unto the loving of God. First, he invites many to bless God; secondly, he wishes them to be blessed, there, *May he bless.*

*Behold,* is the word of one pointing out. And to whom he makes this pointing out he determines: you, *ye servants of the Lord.*

*Ye stand.* It belongs to servants not to sit in the house of their lord. Or, it is understood of the same men: and by the *house* the Church is understood, by the *courts,* the breadth of charity. For in charity, which extends even to enemies, there is breadth; in hatred, narrowness. As though he said: do ye *bless, who stand,* that is, who persevere, *In the house of the Lord,* that is, in the churches; and *who stand, In the courts of the house of our God,* that is, in the breadth of charity, where no man is straitened.

VERS. 2.-- *Lift up your hands.* As Moses lifted up his hands while the people of Israel fought against Amalec, *Lift up,* I say, *unto the holy places:* that with such intention ye may work the eternal good, for otherwise it avails not.

[1053D] *May the Lord bless thee.* The second part, where he wishes the faithful to be blessed. As though he said, *bless ye,* and that ye may be able to do this, *May the Lord bless thee,* with the blessing which is *out of Sion.* Who is able, because he it is who made heaven and earth. [n: (CASS.)] *May he bless.* Above he exhorts many to bless God, but he himself blesses one only, because, though they be many, yet they are in one, and to unity blessing belongs. Whence one was healed in the pool, he who went down first. Be thou therefore in one, and the blessing shall come unto thee.

## PSALM CXXXIV.

VERS. 1.-- *Alleluia.* [n: (CASS.)] After the degrees, which lead unto eternity, the alleluia is set, that the Church may enjoy the praises of God, for whom such a gift is prepared. [1054A] *Praise ye.* First, by a demonstrative address he admonishes, etc., *as far as* he subjoins who ought to praise and why: *Praise ye, servants.*

VERS. 3.-- *Praise ye the Lord,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] If all things are praised because they are good, etc., *as far as* in whom the whole seed of Israel is understood, the whole Church is thought of.

*Sing ye to his name,* that is, praise him for this, that he did what the reality of his name requires, when, being incarnate, he gave himself to men to be tasted.

VERS. 4.-- *Jacob.* [n: (ID.)] He is interpreted "wrestler," and signifies those who wrestle against spiritual wickednesses. *Israel for a possession.* According to the letter. The other nations the Lord set under angels to be guarded: Jacob he chose for himself, that he might till him as a field, sow him, possess him, keep him.

[1054B]VERS. 6.-- *All things whatsoever.* [n: (ID.)] As though he said: He is great in essence, great in works, etc., *as far as* but the spirit of man is unknown, whence, behold, it is said.

VERS. 7.-- *Who bringeth forth winds out of his treasures. Who slew the firstborn.* [n: (ID.)] Faith, whence we begin, by which, being alive, a man advances unto better things; Egypt, affliction: whosoever therefore afflicts the Church and makes scandals, though he be called a Christian, loses faith.

*From the end.* Either from the lowest part of the earth, or from the compass of the ends of the earth. *Lightnings and rain.* [var: Vulg. Ps 134:7 *fulgura in pluviam fecit*, "he hath made lightnings for the rain"] The lightning works terror, the rain gladness. He therefore who terrified with the lightning refreshes with the rain.

VERS. 8.-- *Who slew.* [n: (ID.)] This he did among men for his own cup's sake. If he has told what things thou shouldst love, [1054C] hear also what things thou shouldst fear. *From man even to beast.* [n: (CASS.)] This he often repeats, etc., *as far as* which he destroys in the prudent and in the simple.

VERS. 10.-- *Mighty kings.* [n: (ID.)] The spirits of the world: who are slain, while they cannot do in us what they will.

VERS. 11.-- *Sehon king of the Amorrhites,* etc. This was done under Moses: of this it is read in the book of Numbers, that when they forbade the children of Israel to pass through their borders, they were smitten by him, and the people of Israel possessed their land.

*Chanaan.* [n: (ID.)] Humiliation, an evil one, namely, when the holy fall away from honour.

VERS. 12.-- *And he gave their land for an inheritance.* [n: (ID.)] That is, the heavenly blessedness, which [1054D] the demons forsook through pride, he gave to the faithful. This is the true land of promise, which is more truly called an inheritance, which is in peace, than that actual land, which the children of Israel often lost. *The inheritance of Israel.* [n: (AUG.)] The inheritance: they are the nations of Israel, that is, of the faithful people, whom they feed with tribulation, teaching patience; or which Israel cultivates by conforming them to himself.

VERS. 13.-- *Unto generation and generation.* [n: (ID.)] One generation there is, whereby we are born again through baptism, another whereby we shall rise again: God has not forgotten to call in the one, to crown in the other.

VERS. 14.-- *For he will judge.* [n: (CASS.)] The cause of praise, because the Lord saw the people for whom he did so much, to whom he sent prophets, [1055A] and afterward the Son also. But because they were hard, *he will judge* them in the time to come, *and in his servants,* that is, in the faithful, *he will comfort,* [var: Vulg. Ps 134:14 *et in servis suis deprecabitur*, "and he will be entreated in favour of his servants"] when he shall render the eternal rewards. Behold, the gradation of praise is brought through unto judgment. [n: (AUG.)] Or, thus. Therefore concerning the present, etc., *as far as they may see, and they who see may become blind.*

VERS. 15.-- *The idols.* [n: (CASS.)] The second part: after he had filled himself with the praises of God, he refutes the worshippers of idols by mocking them, because the perfect praise of truth is to destroy falsehood; above the same things were said, but repeated they increase shame.

VERS. 16.-- *They have a mouth, and they shall not speak.* [n: (AUG.)] Those outward things the craftsman could make; but he could not give a spirit, nor hearing to the ears, nor light to the eyes, nor voice to the mouth.

[1055B]VERS. 18.-- *Let them become like unto them.* [n: (ID.)] Knowing these things they mock the idols, etc., *as far as* neither do they perceive the good odour of Christ.

VERS. 19.-- *House of Israel, bless ye.* [n: (CASS.)] This is the third part, where, after the idols have been mocked, he admonishes all orders to celebrate the praises of God: as it were: Let the worshippers of idols become like unto them. But you, who are *the house of Israel. House of Aaron, bless ye.* [n: (AUG.)] The prelates are the house of Aaron: the ministers, the house of Levi; the peoples, all that fear; all generally are the house of Israel: let us all with one voice say: *Blessed.*

VERS. 21.-- *Blessed be the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] Or according to the interpretation of the names, etc., *as far as* where we shall see him in eternal peace.
