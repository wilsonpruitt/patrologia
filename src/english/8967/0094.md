## PSALM CXXXV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Alleluia.* [n: (AUG.)] This psalm contains the praise of God, where, although many things are said in the praise of God, yet chiefly here the mercy of God is commended. [n: (CASS.)] The divers beginnings of the verses, etc., *as far as* so is the divine presence at hand if it be sung purely. ⟦s2⟧ *Give praise to the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] First, he sets forth the magnificence of God and the founding of the whole world [cj: *orbi*; read *orbis*, "of the whole world"]. ⟦s3⟧ Secondly he tells what things he did in Egypt and in the nation of the Jews as a figure of us, there, *Who smote.* ⟦s4⟧ Thirdly, he treats of Christians, there, *and he gave their land for an inheritance. ⟦s5⟧ Give praise to the Lord.* [n: (AUG.)] Let us now praise, etc., *as far as* through mercy is eternal blessedness.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Give praise to the God of gods.* ⟦s2⟧ He calls men gods, to whom the word of God came in another psalm, where he says, *I have said, You are gods and sons of the Most High,* [1055D] etc. ⟦s3⟧ And they are not therefore called gods because all are good: for if all were good, he would not say in the same psalm: *but in the midst he judgeth gods,* that is, he distinguishes them; of whom also he subjoins: *Give praise to the Lord of lords.*

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Who doth wonders.* [n: (ID.)] As it is everywhere set at the end, etc., *as far as* therefore *give praise, for his mercy endureth for ever.*

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Great wonders alone.* [n: (ID.)] Because he does certain things through men or angels, and certain great things he himself alone, which also he sets forth first.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Who made the heavens.* [n: (ID.)] The whole creation therefore he made by himself, etc., *as far as* whence, *Thou hast made all things in wisdom, O God.*

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1056A]Or typically, *The heavens in understanding,* that is, the spiritual saints, to whom he granted not only to believe, but to understand divine things. ⟦s2⟧ But the simple, who hold fast only a firm faith, as though beneath the heavens, are called *earth;* who, because in the baptism which they received they stand fast as it were *upon the waters* by a firm faith, as it were *upon the waters* he established the earth.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *The earth above the waters.* [n: (ID.)] But whereby the heavier earth is borne by the lighter waters, etc., *as far as* so the Lord sat above the well, that is, beside it.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Who made the great lights.* [n: (ID.)] that is, he conferred the gifts of graces upon his faithful.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The moon and the stars for the power of the night.* [n: (CASS.)] It can also be said otherwise, mystically, etc., *as far [1056B] as* that through these each of the lesser may give light.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Who smote Egypt.* [n: (ID.)] The second part, where he tells what things he does in Egypt and in the nation of the Jews under a figure of us. ⟦s2⟧ Now he begins upon the things which he did through men or through angels. ⟦s3⟧ *With their firstborn.* ⟦s4⟧ He smote the firstborn of Egypt, etc., *as far as* but God shuts them out from himself.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *With a mighty hand.* [n: (ID.)] The hand of God is his invincible action, which comes from the arm, that is, from his whole singular power.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *He divided the Red Sea into parts.* [n: (ID.)] According to the letter *into parts* twelve, for the number of the tribes, that each tribe might have its own ways to go by; typically, thus by divers ways there is a passing out of the world unto God. ⟦s2⟧ By the *Red Sea* the world is understood.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1056C]VERS. 15.-- *And he shook off Pharao.* [n: (ID.)] He shook off the devil, with his ministers, as dust, which is quickly shaken off. ⟦s2⟧ In this quickness both the power of God is shown, and those earthly defilements are pointed out which we are wont to shake off. ⟦s3⟧ *Pharao.* [n: (ID.)] Fitly by Pharao the devil is understood: Pharao kills the males, keeps the females alive; so the devil stifles the virtues, nourishes concupiscence.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *And he gave their land for an inheritance.* [n: (CASS.)] The third part, where he comes down to Christians, setting forth the benefits given to them, that thus thou mayest know one author of all things.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *To Israel his servant.* ⟦s2⟧ He takes by *Israel the servant* Christ, etc., *as far as* he gave no price [1056D] to the Egyptians.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *He giveth food.* [n: (THEOD.)] From his singular Providence, etc., *as far as* in the Gospels he says: *Behold the birds of the air, which neither sow, nor gather into barns, and your heavenly Father feedeth them. ⟦s2⟧ To all flesh.* [var: Vulg. Mt 6:26 *Respicite volatilia caeli, quoniam non serunt, neque metunt, neque congregant in horrea*, "behold the birds of the air, for they neither sow, nor do they reap, nor gather into barns"] [n: (AUG.)] To every kind of men, of Israel and of the Gentiles, not as the manna to the Jew alone.

## PSALM CXXXVI.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A psalm of David.* [n: (CASS.)] The third psalm concerning the lamentation of Jerusalem, etc, *as far as* but this one concerning the captivity brought about by Nabuchodonosor. [n: (AUG.)] Two cities there are, mingled together in body, etc., *as far as* who in mind, in desire are citizens of Jerusalem.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Upon the rivers.* [n: (CASS.)] First, that they should be led captive [1057A] long afterward, etc., *as far as* he begins with the desire of the fatherland, saying: *Upon the rivers,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *While we remembered,* [n: (AUG.)] In a hostile land sweet is the remembrance of the fatherland, etc., *as far as* both are of Babylon.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *On the willows.* [n: (ID.)] Which are watered by the rivers of Babylon, etc., *as far as* but by deferring we hang them up. [n: (CASS.)] The force of grief is heightened when the delight wherewith they were wont to console themselves is hung up.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For there they asked us.* [n: (BASIL.)] And hence they began no small profit [cj: *coeperunt*; read *ceperunt*, "they took"], etc., *as far as* so that they were even willing to hear our songs.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The words of the songs.* [n: (AUG.)] Or, of the canticles, as, what does Christ profit? or, what is that other life? ⟦s2⟧ But here, among those who are full of evils, we ought not [1057B] to strike the instrument, that it should sound, but to hang it up. [n: (ID.)] The devil craftily seeks occasion to mock, etc., *as far as* and he bound himself with an oath, saying: *If I forget thee,* etc.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *How shall we sing.* [n: (ID.)] Care must be taken lest any man, fearing to displease such men, court their friendships, and begin to take delight in Babylon.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *My right hand.* [n: (ID.)] Whatever thou doest for eternal things, the right hand works; if thou aim also at another thing, the left hand has mingled itself in; and delighting in it as in the right hand thou losest eternal things.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Let it cleave.* [n: (CASS.)] Let me become dumb in the praises of God, which is the consolation of weeping and of miseries. ⟦s2⟧ *My tongue.* [n: (AUG.)] The canticle of Jerusalem is our tongue, the canticle of the world a barbarous tongue. ⟦s3⟧ He is dumb [1057C] to God who has forgotten Jerusalem.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Remember.* [n: (CASS.)] The second part, where the turning to God is made against the enemies. ⟦s2⟧ *Remember, in the day* of the separation, that they may be punished. ⟦s3⟧ *Of the children of Edom.* [n: (AUG.)] The children of Edom, that is, the Idumeans, were a hindrance to them that rebuilt Jerusalem; or, in the destruction they were joined with Babylon, whence he says, *The daughter of Babylon, miserable. ⟦s4⟧ Edom morally* is carnality, which seduces us unto unlawful motions. ⟦s5⟧ *Edom.* [n: (ID.)] Esau, all the carnal; Jacob, the spiritual; the elder, the carnal man, the younger, the spiritual, because first indeed that which is animal, afterward that which is spiritual. ⟦s6⟧ All the carnal are enemies to the spiritual, whence he says: *Remember,* deliver us from them. ⟦s7⟧ *Empty it out.* [n: (CASS.)] From the likeness [1057D] of a cistern, out of which, if the water be drawn, the bottom is reached. ⟦s8⟧ But what does it profit even to take away the life of such men, for whom, in the foundation (which is Christ), there is life eternal? while the wicked think to hurt, they give crowns.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *The daughter.* [n: (AUG.)] By succession the city of the wicked is made the daughter of Babylon, as the Church, that is, Jerusalem, the city of the good, is by succession the daughter of Sion. [n: (CASS.)] *The daughter of Babylon* morally. ⟦s2⟧ It is the flesh, which confusion, etc., *as far as* they dash them against the rock, Christ, that being broken they may perish.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Blessed is he that shall take.* [n: (AUG.)] What retribution? that as Babylon smothers those born in the world, while they are little, with the errors of their parents and of the world: so they who are now grown up, the knowledge of God being given them, dash the little ones of Babylon [1058A] to pieces, that is, the desires as they are being born, before they receive strength; thus they are more easily overcome, but the greater ones also must be slain against the rock.
