## PSALM CXXXVII.

VERS. 1.-- *A psalm to David himself.* [n: (CASS.)] The captive, who had wept, as though Jerusalem and the temple were rebuilt, and as a fellow-citizen with that part of Jerusalem which was not captive, that is, with the angels, rejoices and praises first the benefits given him. In the person therefore of the Church, which is in the things above in hope, awaiting to be there in reality, he says, *I will confess.*

*I will confess to thee, O Lord.* First, he commends the benefits given him: secondly, he tells how these are spread abroad unto all, there, *Let them confess to thee, O Lord,* etc. *In the sight.* [1058B] [n: (AUG.)] In praising I set before myself the angels, to whom I look to be made equal in dignity hereafter, as I am now. But these in the praise of God set before themselves nothing else but himself. *In the sight* therefore *of the angels,* in that purity wherein the angels behold thee, that in thee alone they may rest, I *will sing to thee.*

VERS. 2.-- *For thy mercy.* [n: (ID.)] *All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth:* There are no other ways by which he came to us, there are no others by which we may come to him: let us render these to God, mercy in succouring, truth in judging. *Above every holy name of thine.* [n: (CASS.)] Or above thy holy name, when thy worship became known to us.

VERS. 3.-- *In my soul.* And because in the soul a carnal multiplication can take place, he adds, *strength,* as though: I seek nothing [1058C] else in the soul but strength.

VERS. 4.-- *Let them confess.* [n: (ID.)] The second part, where he tells how these good things are to be spread abroad unto all. As it were, after my example: *Let them confess,* etc. *All the words of thy mouth.* [n: (AUG.)] Which have now been revealed everywhere, and sent unto all nations, etc., *as far as* and thus, the fleece being dried, *they heard all the words,* etc.

VERS. 5.-- *Great is the glory.* [n: (ID.)] If they are humble, they love and sing this; for if they exalt themselves, let them fear what follows. *For the Lord is high. He looketh on the low things.* [n: (CASS.)] Here is not the human condition, where the humble man is despised and the proud man regarded; but the Lord regards the humble.

*The high he knoweth afar off.* [n: (AUG.)] The proud man does not this, etc., *as far as* mourning this sojourning, whence [1058D] it is subjoined.

VERS. 7.-- *If I shall walk in the midst of tribulation.* Otherwise he does not quicken, save *in the midst of tribulation,* whence, *Blessed are they that mourn, woe to them that laugh.*

*Upon the wrath.* [n: (ID.)] This very thing, that they are angry with me, is thy chastisement upon them, that is, thy punishment. For to be angry is to them a punishment, not only a fault. While therefore thou permittest this, thou stretchest forth the hand of vengeance upon them all.

*Thy right hand.* God has not a right hand or a left, but the right hand of God is called eternal happiness, the left, temporal happiness. Sometimes therefore he does not save his saints in the left hand, but he always saves them in the right.

[1059A]VERS. 8.-- *He will repay.* [n: (AUG.)] What I cannot do, etc., *as far as* in whom the princes of the world found no sin, wherefore they should kill him.
