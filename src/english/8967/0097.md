## PSALM CXXXIX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1061D]VERS. 1.-- *Unto the end. ⟦s1⟧ A psalm of David.* [n: (ID.)] A complaint, a groaning, the prayer of the body of Christ set among the wicked, not yet having the good in fact, but in hope, where there is full exultation. ⟦s2⟧ He admonishes us to pray to God against the evils of the world.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Deliver me, O Lord, from the man.* [n: (CASS.)] First he sets forth who the enemies are, what evils they stir up, setting prayers against them. ⟦s2⟧ Secondly, he says what help he has against evils, there: *I said to the Lord:* Thirdly, he discloses what the evils of his enemies are, there: *The head.* ⟦s3⟧ The Church therefore, labouring among the wicked, cries out. ⟦s4⟧ *From the unjust man.* [n: (AUG.)] He is unjust who does harm; whence, when he had said, *From the evil man,* as though expounding it, he added, *From the unjust man.*

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1062A]VERS. 3.-- *Who have devised.* [n: (CASS.)] He pursues in what manner the wicked persecute, for they assail the good in three ways, namely with the tongue, the hand and the heart: and he puts the heart first, saying, *Who have devised,* as if he were to say, Deliver me from the people of the wicked. [n: (AUG.)] Who, although for the most part they put forward good things upon their lips, have nevertheless devised iniquity in the heart.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *They have sharpened.* ⟦s2⟧ He shows in what manner they persecute with the tongue.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Like that of a serpent.* [n: (ID.)] In the serpent there is cunning and the guile of doing harm: therefore also it creeps, having no feet, that it may not be heard when it comes; its motion is smooth, but not straight: so the wicked creep to do harm, having a hidden venom beneath a smooth motion, whence he adds, *The venom of asps is under their lips.*

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1062B]VERS. 5.-- *Keep me, O Lord.* [n: (CASS.)] He adds concerning the persecution which is done by the hand, that is, by deed. ⟦s2⟧ Keep me, because he deceives through many things which he cannot guard against beforehand.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [sic: VERS. 9.]-- *The proud.* [n: (AUG.)] By this name he has briefly set forth the whole body of the devil: hence it is that they both call themselves just and are unwilling to confess; every proud man is also envious, as the devil is, who envied man that which he himself had lost. ⟦s2⟧ *And they have stretched out cords for a snare,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ Whence Isaias: *Woe to them that draw sins as a long garment,* [var: Vulg. Isa. 5:18 *Vae qui trahitis iniquitatem in funiculis vanitatis, et quasi vinculum plaustri peccatum*, "that draw iniquity with cords of vanity"] because they add sins to sins, etc., *as far as* and he be cast into the exterior darkness.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *I said to the Lord.* [n: (ID.)] Diapsalma. ⟦s2⟧ The second part, what help there is against evils, as if he should say, In so many ways do the wicked assail, and what is the remedy amid [1062C] so many and so great evils?

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I said to the Lord: My God.* ⟦s2⟧ Great confidence: though he is the God of all, yet he is properly the God of those who enjoy him.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Hear, O Lord, the voice of my supplication.* [n: (ID.)] The voice of men and their soul, etc., *as far as* not as the men of the Lord, who buy with a purse, thou with blood.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *O strength of my salvation, thou hast overshadowed my head.* [n: (ID.)] Thou who givest the strength of my salvation, etc., *as far as* however much the enemy may rage.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Deliver me not, O Lord, from desire.* [var: Vulg. Ps. 139:9 *Ne tradas*, a prohibitive subjunctive] [n: (ID.)] The devil sets forth gain, etc., *as far as* save out of thy desire.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *The head of the compassing.* [n: (CASS.)] Diapsalma. ⟦s2⟧ The third part, where he says what the evils of his enemies are. [1062D] [n: (AUG.)] As if he should say, Thy shadow covers me, etc., *as far as* or they are overcome out of their own assertion. ⟦s3⟧ *The labour of their lips shall overwhelm them.* [n: (CASS.)] That is, their false and lying assertion shall overwhelm them, because they are overcome out of their own false assertion.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *They shall fall upon them.* [n: (AUG.)] Or coals of fire upon the earth, etc., *as far as* whence the Apostle: *To some we are the odour of life unto life, to others the odour of death unto death [n: (II Cor. II.)]*

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *A man full of tongue.* [n: (ID.)] Whosoever wills to be directed, etc., *as far as* in outward things there is necessity, not will. ⟦s2⟧ *The unjust man,* etc., *unto destruction.* ⟦s3⟧ Which does not befall the needy man, who, if he is taken by evils, is yet not taken unto destruction; God scourges every son whom he receives. ⟦s4⟧ And although [1063A] the good be for the present afflicted, yet *they shall confess* amid grievous things, which is the more praiseworthy: *And they shall dwell,* therefore nothing is feared in the world.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *But yet.* [n: (CASS.)] The conclusion of the psalm, where he says that if the good have been delivered, and avenged of their enemies, they do not nevertheless give it to themselves, but to God, whence, and they shall dwell with him.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They shall dwell with thy countenance.* ⟦s2⟧ Because they shall see God as he is.

## PSALM CXL.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Let prayer be directed.* [n: (CAS.)] Let *prayer* be *incense,* etc., *as far as* he who repents at the end.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The lifting up of my hands,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] Let all understand this of the Head, etc., *as far as* he adds it from the voice of the body.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1063B]VERS. 3.-- *Set, O Lord, a watch.* ⟦s2⟧ Note that he asks, he sets [cj: *apponit*; read *apponi*, "to be set"] a watch upon the heart as well as upon the mouth: there is need of a twofold defence, that neither may an unjust thought have savour, nor may a depraved will break out into foolish words.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A door.* [n: (AUG.)] From the likeness of him who stands about the door, that he may not enter or go out to whom it is not lawful.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Incline not.* [n: (CASS.)] As if: Set a door to this end, that he may not be excused: for this greatest vice has dominion over men, whence they repent the more slowly; whence in the book of Kings: *As the sin of soothsaying is it to rebel, and as the crime of idolatry to be unwilling to acquiesce [n: (I Reg. XV)]*.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To make excuses for excuses.* [n: (ID.)] It is the custom of the shameless that, when caught, they first excuse themselves by certain falsehoods, and then, convicted of these, they seek others, imputing their vices to the time [1063C] or to necessity, lest at any time they should yield to the truth.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I will not communicate.* [n: (ID.)] Or, *I shall be coupled.* ⟦s2⟧ Men are said to be coupled when two are bound together, so that the one hinders the other, that flight may not be free. ⟦s3⟧ I shall not be coupled after the likeness of the flesh. ⟦s4⟧ *With the elect.* [n: (AUG.)] The elect are those who justify themselves, despise the rest, as the Pharisee did [n: (Luc. XVIII)]; and defend their sins by whatever means. ⟦s5⟧ With such men this man does not wish *to be coupled,* that is, associated, lest he be hindered by them.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *He shall correct me.* [n: (CASS.)] The second part, where this faithful man, now on the contrary corrected by the just man, spurns false praisers, being about to profit the wicked by word and by manners, wherein also he asks to be kept.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *But the oil of the sinner,* etc. [n: (ID.)] A feigned love, [1063D] which softens minds away from the rigour of truth toward hurtful things.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Judges.* [n: (AUG.)] The powerful, the learned, who judge concerning manners, etc., *as far as* whom all worship.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *They shall hear.* [n: (ID.)] With the ear of obedience, not like Adam, etc., *as far as* those nearest to a curse.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The fatness of the earth.* [n: (ID.)] There are [sic: *quae dam*] contemptible things from which nevertheless the earth receives its fatness, etc., *as far as* who are the bones and the firmament of the faithful or of the faith.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Near hell.* [n: (ID.)] He says *near hell,* not, in hell: because although they die by the common lot, they do not nevertheless go down into hell as the wicked do, but eternal life is given them. ⟦s2⟧ *For unto thee, O Lord.* [1064A] [n: (CASS.)] The frailty of man having been spoken of, he takes refuge in the divine remedies.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Take not away from thy good things, but let my hope be always in thee: and let my eyes ever be intent upon thee. ⟦s2⟧ For this man does not ask against that which the saints will, namely to be snatched from the world and to be with Christ.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Keep me from the snare.* ⟦s2⟧ By snares and scandals he briefly notes the evils of the whole world. ⟦s3⟧ Snares are the flattering bonds, the desires of the flesh; but while the devil does not prevail through these, he strives with scandals, which he stirs up by means of the restless.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Or otherwise. ⟦s2⟧ And by those setting the snare he means open persecutors: but by those working iniquity he means those consenting to them, who fail through fear of persecution. ⟦s3⟧ And the sense is: Take not away my soul, but [1064B] rather, *Keep me from the snare* of persecution, *which they have set for me.* [n: (AUG.)] This the persecutors do, who set the bait of this life as it were in a trap. ⟦s4⟧ If the bird loves this bait, it falls into the trap: because through fear of death it succumbs to persecution. ⟦s5⟧ *Keep me.*

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *From the scandals of them that work iniquity.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, from the scandal of those who, by consenting to them, fall, and so work iniquity. ⟦s3⟧ As if he should say: Keep me from the persecutors, who terrify, and from those who have fallen.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *They shall fall in the net.* ⟦s2⟧ Words fitted to the comparison, since he does not wish to be coupled with these.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ They shall fall from vice into vice, but

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I am alone until I pass.* ⟦s2⟧ Alone, that is, because he does not walk with the worst. ⟦s3⟧ *Alone.* [n: (AUG.)] The same that he says, etc., *as far as* so also other ancients.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1064C] *I pass.* ⟦s2⟧ As a wayfarer of this world, who is taken by no pleasure.
