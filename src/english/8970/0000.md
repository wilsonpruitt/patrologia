## CHAPTER ONE.

[0725B]

VERS. 1.-- « Tobias. » [n: (BED., in Tob., tom. II.)] The book of Tobit is, on the surface of the letter, wholesome. For it abounds in the greatest examples of the moral life, and in admonitions. But by as much as apples excel leaves, by so much does the allegory excel the history. For it contains the greatest mysteries of the Church. For Tobias himself signifies the people Israel, who, while the other nations were given over to idolatry, served God with right faith [0725C] and with works. Whence: « When they went, etc. » For Jeroboam, who fashioned golden calves for the deceiving of his subjects, expresses the worshippers of idolatry, whose accomplices are signified in the subjects.

« Nephthali. » [n: (ID., ibid.)] Breadth, concerning which: « Thy commandment is exceeding broad [n: (Psal. CXVIII)], » whose tribe, that is, the sons, is the faithful people, its city is the Church, concerning which: « A city cannot be hid, set upon a mountain [n: (Matth. XV)]. »

« Above Naasson. » Naasson, augury, his city augurs the things above, that is, contemplates the heavenly things.

[n: (BEDA ubi supra. )] The captivity of Tobias designates the captivity of the human race under the king of all the depraved, [0725D] that is, the devil, who cast us down from our heavenly fatherland into the valley of this exile.

VERS. 3.-- « So that all things, etc. » [n: (ID., ibid.)] So the people Israel, through the teachers of the divine word, ministered alms not only to the untaught hearers of their own nation, but also to the Gentiles who came over to Judaism. For whatever of good it had by nature, which the capturing enemy did not take away, this it showed to its own for an example of virtue, and it offered a portion of saving knowledge, as it were the tithe of its substance, to strangers, that is, to the Gentiles.

VERS. 4.-- « Yet nothing childish. » Already he bore in his breast that apostolic word: « Be ye not made children, etc. [n: (I Cor. XIV)]. Now this was said with respect to those who walked in the commandments of the Lord without blame, [0726B] according to that rule of Tyconius by which, as though treating of the same [subject], we sometimes praise all on account of the good, and sometimes blame all on account of the bad, which is the second rule.

VERS. 6.-- « All the firstfruits [n: (ID., ibid.)]. » It seems to contradict this, that the first tithing went to the uses of the Levites, and the second to the disbursing of necessaries when the temple was visited. But the tithes of the third year, which [0726C] they were then accustomed to make, they granted to the poor. And therefore we say all that tithe, or all the second tithes, because he could not approach the temple for fear of the king's guards.

VERS. 9.-- « But when he was become a man [n: (ID., ibid.)]. » So the people Israel, etc., *as far as* whence: « My firstborn Israel [n: (Exod. IV)]. » [var: Vulg. Exod. 4:22 *Filius meus primogenitus Israel*, "Israel is my son, my firstborn"]

VERS. 10.-- « Whom from infancy he taught to fear, » etc., namely by believing and confessing that [the boy] would never commit sin, but that [God] would fill him with the spirit of the fear of the Lord.

VERS. 13.-- « In the sight of Salmanasar, » etc. It does not contradict what is elsewhere said, that he is Salmanasar, who is also Sennacherib: for he bore two names, or generally all the kings of the Medes were so called, as the [0726D] Pharaohs of Egypt.

VERS. 16.-- « But when he had come, etc. » [n: (ID., ibid.)] So the people of God, etc., *as far as* and they lay open the secret things of the Scriptures.

VERS. 20.-- « He gave burial, etc., » to sins: so that not even the memory might remain in them. With such a burial did our fathers wish to be honoured, after the burial of Christ. Whence Jacob wished to be buried in Judea, and Joseph gave commandment concerning his bones.

VERS. 21.-- « And at last, when he had returned, etc. » Well did Sennacherib flee from Judea: for the devil greatly dreads true confession: and before its face he does not abide

## CHAPTER II.

VERS. 10.-- « It came to pass, » etc. [n: (BED., in Tob., tom. II.)] [0727A] Let no one wonder if sometimes a good thing typically signifies an evil, and evil things a good. For if this were not lawful, the name of God would never be written with black ink, but always with shining gold: because God is light. But if thou shouldst write the name of the devil with a white pebble, it nonetheless signifies darkness. Tobias therefore, being blinded, signifies the people Israel. « For blindness in part hath happened in Israel [n: (Rom. II)]. » Being wearied from the burial, he was blinded. For he who persists unwearied in good works does not admit [cj: *admittit*; read *amittit*, "does not lose"] the light of faith. So he sleeps spiritually [sic: *spiritualer*] wearied, who neglects to watch, to stand, to act manfully, and to be strengthened. To whom it is said: « Rise, thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead [n: (Ephes. V)]. »

[n: (BED., ibid.)] Swallows, because of their light flight, signify pride and levity: whose uncleanness [0727B] blinds those over whom it has dominion. He sleeps set beneath a swallow's nest who incautiously [sic: *incantus*] subjects himself to the levity of wantonness and to pride. This blindness prevailed most of all in the people Israel when the Lord's coming in the flesh was at hand, when it laboured under the yoke of Roman servitude, and violated the precepts of the divine law by living ill.

VERS. 14.-- « But immovable, » etc. Because judgment begins from the house of God. Whence: « Begin ye at my sanctuary [n: (Ezech. IX)]. » He who is not corrected is not a son: « He that spareth the rod hateth the son [n: (Prov. XIII)]. Paul is buffeted as a boy, is exalted as a young man.

VERS. 15.-- « The kings insulted, » etc. [n: (BED., ibid.)] There were in the people, etc., *as far as* whence: « Charity is patient, is kind [n: (II Cor. XIII)]. »

## CHAPTER III.

[0727C]

VERS. 7.-- « That Sara, etc. » [n: (BED., in Tob. tom. II.)] It signifies the multitude of notions [cj: *notionum*; read *nationum*, "of the nations"], etc., *as far as* Daniel prays in an upper chamber, and Eliseus has upper chambers, and Christ celebrates the pasch in an upper chamber.

## CHAPTER IV.

VERS. 3.-- « Bury my body. » By burying bodies we commend them to the earth as a most sacred deposit, which we believe are to be rendered back and glorified in the resurrection.

## CHAPTER V.

VERS. 7.-- « Of the children of Israel. » For he was of the number of the angels, who are the children of Israel, because they always see [0727D] God face to face, and are often sent in the keeping of the children of Israel, that is, of those who see God. Whence: « I appointed the bounds of the peoples according to the number of the children of Israel [n: (Deut. LII)]. » [var: Vulg. Deut. 32:8 *constituit*, "he appointed"]

VERS. 10.-- « Then having entered, » etc. [n: (BED., ubi supra. )] The angel appeared to Tobias and offered himself as a companion, and the Son of God assumed man, and, conversing visibly with men, saved the human race. Tobias brought the angel in to his father, etc., and the Lord, through the miracles which he did in the flesh, showed to the people of the Jews, from whom he had taken flesh: that he is both son and angel, that is, the messenger of the Father's will, to whom also he preached the joy of everlasting salvation, saying: « Do penance, for [0728A] the kingdom of heaven is at hand. [n: (Matth. III.)] » And to those despairing of the heavenly light, « I am, he says, the light of the world, he that followeth me walketh not in darkness [n: (Joan. VIII)]. »

VERS. 15.-- « And the angel said to him. » [n: (ID., ibid.)] The angel promises Tobias to lead his son into the city of the Medes and to bring him back, etc., *as far as* Tobias asking the angel whence he was.

VERS. 18.-- « I am, he says, Azarias the son of the great Ananias, [n: (ID., ibid.)] Azarias *helper,* Ananias *the glory of God.* Christ also indicates to his faithful that he himself is he of whom it is said: « Thou art my helper and my deliverer, O Lord, delay not [n: (Psal. LXIX)]. » « And we saw his glory, as of the only-begotten of the Father [n: (Joan. I)]. »

VERS. 22.-- « Then, all things being made ready. » [n: (BED., ubi supra. )] [0728B] The Lord appearing in the flesh, etc., *as far as* and thus it came to the salvation of the Gentiles through the apostles.
