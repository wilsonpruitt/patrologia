## CHAPTER XI.

VERS. 3.-- « Let us go before. » [n: (BEDA, ubi supra. )] After the people of the Gentiles has been enlightened, divine grace goes before to enlighten the blindness of the Jews, that in their own books they may know Christ true man and true God, and so at last, as though the angel and their son whom they had long not seen had been seen, they may greatly rejoice; at last, that is to say, [the Jewish people] may join itself to the Church gathered from among the Gentiles by the communion of the mysteries.

[0731A]VERS. 9.-- « By fawning. » [n: (BED., ubi supra. )] The dog rejoiced while it saw again the roofs of its masters, long unseen. The teachers rejoice at the effect of their work, when they understand that Judea is to be gathered again by the Lord. They rejoice over the reward of eternal life, and they foretell to all that their hearts are to be gladdened with the same reward, and they declare that the grace of the Holy Spirit is straightway to come.

VERS. 10.-- « Rising up. » The word of salvation having been heard from the teachers, the people of the Jews rises up out of the long blindness of its perfidy, and runs in love to the Lord; but stumbling with the steps of its works, until, born again, instructed in Christ, it may receive the light of faith and of working.

VERS. 13.-- « Then taking. » [n: (BEDA, ibid.)] The Lord openly reveals to believers how great is the malice of the ancient enemy, who longed to devour him in his passion; [0731B] but, slain by this, he lost his members, that is, those whom he before held.

VERS. 14.-- « The white film. » [n: (BED., ibid.)] The people of the Jews still has a veil before the face of the heart, etc., *as far as* but they who, conscious of frailty and of ignorance, say, Lord God, enlighten my darkness; are enlightened by the Lord.

VERS. 18.-- « She entered in, » etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] It signifies the light of spiritual grace, which is sevenfold. After the seven days of enlightenment the wife enters in: because after Judea shall have been enlightened by faith, after it shall have received the grace of the Holy Spirit; the Church shall enter in to her, that there may be one fold and one shepherd, and that the house of Christ may be one, made firm by one corner-stone.

## CHAPTER XII.

[0731C]

VERS. 14.-- « And now, » etc. [n: (BEDA, in Tobiam. )] The angel, about to return into heaven, sets forth more openly what he is, and why he came, and whither he is to return: and Christ laid open his own condition to that same people as it advanced more widely, showing that he is in the Father, and [0732A] the Father in the Son. The angel returns to God, Tobias remains with the Father, and Christ is understood by his faithful to be equal to the Father in divinity, consubstantial with men in humanity.

## CHAPTER XIII.

VERS. 1.-- « And Tobias opening his mouth. » [n: (BEDA, in Tobiam. )] He confessed the truth and the mercy, etc., *as far as* by preaching the joys of the heavenly fatherland.

## CHAPTER XIV.

VERS. 1.-- « And after Tobias was enlightened, » etc., the number forty is laborious, of fasting and of abstinence. But we all ought to endure the labours of this life, with the [0732B] love of God and of our neighbour, and so we shall bring forth fruit.

VERS. 3.-- « For at fifty and six years of age he lost the light of his eyes, » etc. He had passed the hallowed number of fifty, and had not attained to the sixtieth, which signifies perfection, because of the number six, in which all things were made.

VERS. 5.-- « But in the hour of his death he called Tobias to him. » [n: (BEDA, in Esdram. )] Tobias, that is, the teachers of the Jews, who, being about to depart out of this world, announce to their kinsfolk: that the world draws near to its end, and the good things of the life to come near at hand.

VERS. 14.-- « Tobias departed out of Ninive with his wife, and his sons and his sons' sons, » etc. [n: (ID., ibid.)] This Christ does daily, etc., *as far as* living long, he does not forsake lightness of mind.

[0732C]VERS. 16.-- « They buried. » The burial of Tobias designates the end of the world, in which our Lord with his body, which is the Church, enters into rest, the angels rejoicing together at the fellowship of men, and placing each one through the diverse mansions according to the quality of his merits.
