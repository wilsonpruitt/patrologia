## To the Reader (cont.)

We have also given to each tome an index, and a brief one, of the principal matters only, and of those which are explained at greater length: for to bring into the index all things which are expounded in any way would have been to copy out the commentaries themselves again; and besides, the smaller and briefer matters mostly serve the verbal explanation of the text, and will accordingly be found more easily, once the sacred text to which they are spoken has been found, than through the index; but those which are noted more diligently and at greater length, whether in the gloss or by Lyra, those alone we have gathered into the index.
