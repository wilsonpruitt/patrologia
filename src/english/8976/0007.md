## CHAPTER VII. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 51.-- *Hard of neck. [n: (RAB.)]* Purged of the crime of which the false witnesses accused him, he begins to accuse the Jews, because he who is without crime is worthy of the office of a teacher.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And uncircumcised,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He shows them that circumcision, in which they gloried against the grace of the Gospel, avails not to salvation for those who had thought and hearing unclean.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 52.-- *Your fathers. [n: (RAB.)]* Above he said *our fathers*, speaking by way of persuasion, now by way of rebuke he calls them the fathers of those men only, to whom they are like in malice.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And they slew. [n: (RAB.)]* He sets down three degrees of their malice [0444D]: the first is, that they resisted the spirit; the second, that they persecuted the prophets; the third, that with malice increasing they slew them.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 55.-- *Standing.* ⟦s2⟧ Immovably, because he is not *ite rum* to fall, or the bearing is signified of one helping, and judging wrongs, and fighting for his servant.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I see the heavens. [n: (RAB.)]* About to depart from the earth, he saw the heavens opened, that he who despised the earthly life for Christ might merit the inheritance of heaven, and therefore in his very suffering he sees the reward.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The Son of man.* ⟦s2⟧ Although Christ is the Son of God, yet he chose rather to call him the Son of man, that the unbelief of the Jews might be confounded, who crucified him as a mere man, whom they would not believe to be God.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0445A]VERS. 57.-- *And casting him out. [n: (RAB.)]* In this they gloried that they fulfilled the law. ⟦s2⟧ For Moses said of the blasphemer: *Bring him forth without the camp, and let the people stone him [n: (Lev. XXIV)]*.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 58.-- *Lord Jesus, receive my spirit,* into the heaven which I see opened. ⟦s2⟧ Christ said to the Father: *Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit.* ⟦s3⟧ Stephen, as a servant to the Lord, *Lord,* etc.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Because they know not. [n: (RAB.)]* Many of them, in killing Stephen, thought that they fulfilled the law, because they believed him a blasphemer. ⟦s2⟧ This also Christ had foretold: *There shall come,* he says, *an hour, that everyone who killeth you may think that he rendereth a service to God [n: (Joan. XVI)]*. [var: Vulg. Joan. 16:2 *venit hora*, "the hour cometh"]

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 59.-- *He fell asleep.* ⟦s2⟧ Beautifully is it said, *He fell asleep,* and not, *He is dead,* because he offered the sacrifice [0445B] of love, and fell asleep in the hope of the resurrection. ⟦s3⟧ The saints do not die, because they are changed for the better, and the temporal life is turned into the eternal.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And Saul,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He did not indeed stone him, but consented to them that stoned. ⟦s3⟧ Whence he would have perished, unless the grace of God and penance had come to his aid; because they who do and they who consent are worthy of death.
