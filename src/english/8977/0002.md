## CHAPTER II.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And to the angel.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, he writes to the bishop, at whose hand he requires the sins of those beneath him, and without whose consent [0714B] John himself does not presume to judge those beneath him. ⟦s3⟧ *Of the church of Ephesus.* ⟦s4⟧ Ephesus, according to the part of those who persist well, is interpreted *will,* in whose works God delights, concerning whom he sets it forth, that by the example of these they (who had fallen) may be corrected. ⟦s5⟧ Again Ephesus is interpreted *counsel,* that is, needing counsel, or a deep fall, or my will. ⟦s6⟧ *Seven.* ⟦s7⟧ He begins the several admonitions, which severally can be universal also, according to the diverse members of any church whatever. ⟦s8⟧ *Thou canst not bear.* ⟦s9⟧ By the force of humanity. ⟦s10⟧ Or, *thou canst not bear* but that thou amend or cast out.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Thou hast tried them who say themselves to be apostles.* ⟦s2⟧ They are not tried save those who are within in the Church.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Thou hast endured for my name.* ⟦s2⟧ This thou hast not done for human praise, but for the glorifying of my [0714C] name. ⟦s3⟧ *And thou hast not fainted.* ⟦s4⟧ He notes that there had been cause of human failing, had not help gone before.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *But I have against thee, that thou hast left thy charity.* ⟦s2⟧ Either the bishop in his own person, wearied with loathing of vices, is reproved in those beneath him, or those beneath him are found to offend through love of earthly things. ⟦s3⟧ Heli was a good priest, and yet he was condemned because he would not correct his sons.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *I will move the candlestick.* ⟦s2⟧ I will take away the virtues and the gifts of the Holy Spirit, through which the candlesticks have been established.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 6.-- *But this good thou hast.* [var: Vulg. Apc 2:6 *Sed hoc habes, quia odisti facta Nicolaitarum,* with no *bonum*] Although this is against thee, yet despair not, because thou hast good. ⟦s3⟧ *The deeds of the Nicolaites.* ⟦s4⟧ That is, the common use of women, and the eating of things offered to idols.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0714D]VERS. 7.-- *He that hath an ear.* ⟦s2⟧ Why must one hear? ⟦s2⟧ Because to him that perseveres Christ is the tree of life, because he is a shade and a strength and one giving fruit, that is, body and blood, both here and most of all in time to come.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Of Smyrna.* ⟦s2⟧ Smyrna is interpreted *a song,* or *as it were myrrh:* a song also, in which things he delights, myrrh is so bitter that it even preserves bodies from rottenness. ⟦s3⟧ *These things saith the first.* ⟦s4⟧ He describes Christ such as may avail to the consolation of those upon whom tribulations are heaped. ⟦s5⟧ *These things saith the first.* ⟦s6⟧ As if he should say: Faint not you, because I, so great as I am, suffered death for you, from which I rose again. ⟦s7⟧ This same do you also look for.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Some of you.* ⟦s2⟧ Here it is plain that it is understood not of one alone, but of many. ⟦s3⟧ *That [0715A] you may be tempted.* ⟦s4⟧ That is, that you may be proved, or be held in contempt by the devil and his members. ⟦s5⟧ *Ten days.* ⟦s6⟧ That is, in the time of that war. ⟦s7⟧ For God, sending his own to wars, arms them with the Decalogue. ⟦s8⟧ Or ten days, that is, all this time in which through seven days there is fighting against the three principal vices, avarice, cupidity, vainglory.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *From the second death.* ⟦s2⟧ The first death of the soul is in sins, the second in punishments: the first death of the body, when it is dissolved; the second, when it shall be condemned in the judgment.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *A sword.* ⟦s2⟧ He requires that these have this sword in the choosing of the good and the reprobating of the evil.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Thou hast not denied my faith.* ⟦s2⟧ Tribulation [0715B] is illumination to the just, correction to the contumacious, blinding to the obstinate.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Balaam.* ⟦s2⟧ By Balaam are to be understood the heretics who teach earthly princes how they may overthrow the worshippers of the true God. ⟦s3⟧ Or Balaam, that is, the demons, who teach the flesh to deceive souls that see God.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *The doctrine of the Nicolaites.* ⟦s2⟧ He therefore repeats concerning the Nicolaites, that he may invite to penance, which he has not done.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Manna.* ⟦s2⟧ By manna understand the eternal glory of which all shall say. ⟦s3⟧ What is this? ⟦s4+5⟧ *Because neither hath eye seen, nor ear heard [n: (I Cor. II)]*, etc. [var: Vulg. 1Cor 2:9 *Quod oculus non vidit, nec auris audivit,* "That eye hath not seen, nor ear heard"] *And I will give him.* ⟦s6⟧ In Daniel it is written: *A stone was cut out without hands [n: (Dan. I)]*. ⟦s7+8⟧ In the Proverbs: *The expectation of him that waiteth is a most weighty gem [n: (Prov. XVII)]*. [var: Vulg. Pr 17:8 *Gemma gratissima,* "a most acceptable gem"] The same body solid, through him lucid, [0715C] white with virtues and with the hope of immortality.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And in the stone.* ⟦s2⟧ Or upon baptism add martyrdom, whose force the hypocrite does not understand, who suffers for the praise of men. ⟦s3⟧ *Written.* ⟦s4⟧ With five letters, namely the five wounds with which his body was shown upon the cross, because he was a martyr.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *I know thy works,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The Son has the gifts of the Holy Spirit, that is, he gives to whom he will, or takes away. ⟦s3⟧ Therefore let these who have now received those gifts see to it, lest by consent to the evil they lose them. ⟦s4⟧ Or understand charity and faith, so that the order be backward; for charity is not before faith. ⟦s5⟧ First faith, from faith charity, from charity good works.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *To commit fornication.* ⟦s2+3⟧ Fornication is said to be fourfold, in the mind, *If thou shalt see a woman to lust after [0715D] her [n: (Matth. V)]*, etc. [var: Vulg. Mt 5:28 *omnis qui viderit mulierem ad concupiscendum eam,* "everyone that shall look on a woman to lust after her"] In the act itself, in the love of earthly things, in the worship of idols.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *I will not put.* ⟦s2⟧ Certain false ones were terrifying them by saying that the Lord was yet to send greater tribulations than they had suffered: against this he says, that he will not send.
