## CHAPTER III.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And to the angel of the church of Sardis.* ⟦s2⟧ Sardis is interpreted *the beginning of beauty,* and by this is signified he who at first has nothing or little of virtue, and afterwards becomes base through vices; or the hypocrites are understood, who are outwardly beautiful, inwardly deformed. ⟦s3⟧ *I know thy works.* ⟦s4⟧ And I know that in all thy works thou seekest human praise.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0716A]VERS. 2.-- *Be watchful.* ⟦s2⟧ Therefore watch, because, although thou be unmoved in mind, or praiseworthy in mind, yet thou art not perfect unless thou rouse the rest, and it is not enough to confess Christ unless thou also do works. ⟦s3⟧ *For I find not thy works full.* ⟦s4⟧ While thou art without charity, or for praise, or because thou committest a criminal thing.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Have in mind therefore.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, remember in what manner thou didst receive the faith of Christ in baptism: where thou didst promise that thou wouldst renounce the devil and all his pomps. ⟦s3⟧ Remember also that thou hast heard from the teachers of the Christian faith in what manner it behoves thee to live: and keep what thou hast heard, and do penance, in that thou hast so long been slothful from good works. ⟦s4⟧ *Thou shalt have heard* from the holy Fathers, that, *he that offendeth in one is guilty of all;* [var: Vulg. Jac 2:10 *Quicumque autem totam legem servaverit, offendat autem in uno, factus est omnium reus,* "whosoever shall keep the whole law, but offend in one point, is become guilty of all"] whence, *but if I have not charity, I am nothing* [0716B] [n: (Jac. II)].

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *They have not defiled their garments.* ⟦s2⟧ The garments of immortality and of innocence, which they received in baptism, or, their own good works.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *I will not blot out.* ⟦s2⟧ He blots out the reprobate, according to that: *The Lord hardened Pharao's heart [n: (Exod. X)]*. ⟦s3⟧ *Out of the book of life.* ⟦s4⟧ The book of life is the foreknowledge of God, in which all things are contained. ⟦s5⟧ *I will confess his name,* saying: *Come, ye blessed of my Father [n: (Matth. XXV)]*. ⟦s6⟧ Or here, *You gave me to eat and to drink.*

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *These things saith he.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: Labour for the conversion of the brethren as thou hast begun: because in that which I promised, *whatsoever you shall ask the Father in my name, he will give it you [n: (Joan. XIV)]*, [var: Vulg. Jo 14:13 *hoc faciam,* "that will I do"; the ending *dabit vobis* stands at Jo 16:23] I am truthful in my promises, whence you can know that I will gather many to you [0716C] as you desire. ⟦s3⟧ *He shutteth.* ⟦s4⟧ That we may seek, *he openeth,* that we may find, that the seeker may receive the fruit of his sweat, and the bestower may have the thanks of his goodness.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Behold I have given before thee.* ⟦s2⟧ Deservedly have I given, because openly thou couldst not have it of thyself, because thou hast little strength.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A door.* ⟦s2⟧ By the door, which is so called from withstanding, are understood the hearts of men, hard beforetime. ⟦s3⟧ Or thou canst understand the obscure Scriptures. ⟦s4⟧ Or Christ, who is the true door unto life, whence the disciples, forbidden to speak and beaten, said: *We cannot but speak the things which we have seen [n: (Act. IV)]*. [var: Vulg. Act 4:20 *Non enim possumus quae vidimus et audivimus non loqui,* "which we have seen and heard"]

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Jews.* ⟦s2⟧ They lost this name when they said of Christ: *We know not this man [n: (Joan. IX)]*. ⟦s3⟧ *They lie.* ⟦s4⟧ They strive to prove it by words and by certain [0716D] feignings. ⟦s5⟧ *Behold I will make them to come.* ⟦s6⟧ Ready to imitate thy works through the likeness of thy humility. ⟦s7⟧ *They shall know.* ⟦s8⟧ Those set outside did not believe that God loved the Church which he suffered to be afflicted, but now he will make an assurance of his love, that is, a certainty, that you sink not down, because I come to reward.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Thou hast kept the word of my patience.* ⟦s2⟧ The precept concerning patience which I showed in myself, praying for my persecutors. ⟦s3⟧ *From the hour of temptation,* or of the general persecution against Christians that was to be straightway after Nero, or in the last time of Antichrist. ⟦s4⟧ *To try them that dwell upon the earth.* ⟦s5⟧ As in what follows, often in the sixth place, so here also in the sixth angel the last persecution is designated, in which certain of the Jews are [0717A] to be deceived: certain, being about to fulfil the law spiritually by the warnings of Elias, believe that they will vanquish the enemy.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Behold I come quickly.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: The temptation indeed will come, but sink not down, because I come to reward.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *A pillar in the temple.* ⟦s2⟧ Firm in himself, and sustaining others by word and by example. ⟦s3⟧ *He shall go out no more.* ⟦s4⟧ Since God has a fixed number of his own, if any one falls away, another is brought in by his mercy, as through the pride of the Jews the gentile world entered in. ⟦s5⟧ The younger son, that is, the people of the gentiles, had gone out, but by the death of the fatted calf, that is, by the blood of Christ, being reconciled he will go out no more. ⟦s6⟧ *New.* ⟦s7⟧ Where there is nothing of oldness, but the full vision of peace. ⟦s8⟧ *From heaven.* ⟦s9⟧ From the conformity of the heavenly creatures.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0717B]VERS. 14.-- *The witness,* undaunted, of those things which he heard from the Father. ⟦s2⟧ Or, to whom faith is to be given in all things. ⟦s3⟧ Or, the witness of our works before the Father. ⟦s4⟧ *And true.* ⟦s5⟧ He is truth, because through him the promises of the Father are fulfilled.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Thou art neither cold nor hot,* because neither art thou wholly ignorant of the faith, nor dost thou openly deny it when received. ⟦s2⟧ *Would that thou wert cold.* ⟦s3⟧ He does not wish him cold simply, but such a one of whom greater hope may be had.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *I will begin to vomit thee out.* ⟦s2⟧ About to say a marvellous thing, that the lukewarm were to be excommunicated, because they were believed good by men, he says himself truthful in all things, that in this he may be believed, and, if they be willing to come to themselves again, he promises preservation, because he gave the first [0717C] pledge. ⟦s3⟧ *Out of my mouth.* ⟦s4⟧ He is vomited out of the mouth who is cast headlong into worse things, or who dies impenitent.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *I am rich,* in divine knowledge, or in secular.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *I counsel thee to buy of me gold fire-tried and proved.* ⟦s2⟧ Because such works will not lead through unto salvation. ⟦s3⟧ *That the shame may not appear.* ⟦s4⟧ It is better to blush here before a few, than in time to come before all. ⟦s5⟧ *With eyesalve.* ⟦s6⟧ The eyes, that we may see, are anointed, when, for the knowing of the brightness of the true light, we help the keenness of our understanding with the medicine of good working. ⟦s7+8⟧ *The commandment of the Lord enlightening the eyes [n: (Psal. XVIII)]*. [var: Vulg. Ps 18:9 *praeceptum Domini lucidum, illuminans oculos,* with *lucidum,* "bright"] Or anoint with the gift of the Holy Spirit the mind which earthly things had shut in.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Behold I stand at the door and knock. ⟦s2⟧ If [0717D] any man shall hear,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ I wait, or I inspire, or I call through preachers.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *To sit with me in my throne.* ⟦s2⟧ To judge in those in whom I sit and judge. ⟦s3⟧ These things concerning prelates, and, through prelates, concerning those beneath them.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As I also have overcome, and have sat with my Father in his throne.* ⟦s2⟧ He says this thus, because the victory and the session of Christ is the cause of the victory and the session of Christians.
