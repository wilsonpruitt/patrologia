## CHAPTER V.

VERS. 1.-- *And I saw in the right hand of him that sat.* By the right hand I understand the Son, through whom God the Father made all things, to whom the whole intention of the book is directed. *A book.* That is, the two Testaments, which are one and the same book. *Without.* Signifying the Old, which promises things outward. *Within,* signifying the New, which openly and often promises blessedness.

*Sealed.* The writings of the prophets were made closed and obscure, but afterwards through Christ they were [0720A] opened. *With seals.* That is, with all obscurities, or closed with the sevenfold Spirit, through whom faith is opened. *Seven.* He says so, because (as he will determine in what follows) those are the seven seals, namely that the horse is white and red and black, and so forth: which were opened and known at the beginning of the Church, which was the white horse upon which Christ sat, and which he fortified by doctrine and by example, and which the red horse assailed.

VERS. 2.-- *An angel.* The holy and ancient fathers of the Old Testament understood at one time that the restoration of Jerusalem had been appointed by God: and they longed to see when it should come to pass, but how it should come to pass they knew not, until, that is, he revealed it to certain men before it came to pass. *Preaching.* Inquiring, and announcing to others, and out of exceeding desire complaining that it was so long deferred. [0720B] *To open the book.* The divine dispensation, and to take away all the obscurities of the Scriptures.

VERS. 4.-- *I wept much.* This is said in the person of the former and ancient fathers, in that they did not understand, or grieved that the restoration was deferred, whence: *Many kings and prophets desired to see [n: (Luc. X)]*, etc.

VERS. 5.-- *And one of the elders.* Every prophet, by announcing the coming of Christ, consoles the rest. *The lion hath prevailed.* The lion, when he is put to flight, flees through the mountains, and with the knot that he has in his tail wipes out his tracks, lest he be found: so Christ fled through the mountains, that is, his divinity lay hid from the Jews, having taken flesh he covered his divinity, that it might not be known. The lion sleeps with open eyes, so Christ expired while his divinity lived.

[0720C]VERS. 6.-- *And behold in the midst of the throne.* The Church, which is possessed by Christ as a throne, is fed as the living creatures, is honoured as the elders in the judgment of power, she herself, built upon Christ, is exalted with a kingdom in the seven horns of strength, is enlightened in the seven eyes, in signs and in powers. *As it were slain.* Because although he was dead, yet by the power of God he lives. Or among his own he is as it were slain, in whom he is daily mortified. *Seven horns,* etc. Because he gives a kingdom, which is denoted in the horns. Eyes, because he enlightens. Or the horns are the more eminent saints, founded upon Christ their head, surpassing the flesh.

VERS. 8.-- *They fell down before the lamb.* They thought lowly things of themselves, in that they imitate him, whence there follows. [0720D] *Having every one harps.* The mortifications of vices and of concupiscences. In a harp there is wood and there are strings: by the wood, the cross of Christ, by the strings, the flesh of the saints, which is stretched upon the wood, while by divers torments, rendering the same sound, they imitate the cross of Christ.

VERS. 9.-- *They sang a canticle.* With exultation the Church professes the New Testament in her work, while she baptizes into his death. *Tribe.* It is named from the three orders, in which there are seventy-two tongues in each, of which there are many peoples, and in a people, nations. Three orders of those to be saved, Noe, Daniel, Job. Or three orders of the nations, Greeks, Hebrews, Christians.

VERS. 11.-- *I saw.* Not only the living creatures and the elders bearing the same witness out of their office, but also *I heard,*

[0721A] *The voice of angels.* That is, of the supernal spirits who fortify and guard the Church, who rejoice over the restored fellowship of the saints, who bear witness to Christ, and who both at his nativity, when they appeared to the shepherds, and at his passion, and at his resurrection and ascension, testified that the judge would come. Or the angels are the lesser subjects in the Church, leading an angelic life, who surround and honour their preachers and their judges, consenting to their testimony and conforming themselves to it. *Of many round about the throne and of the living creatures and of the elders,* etc. The more they are, the greater the attestation, and the greater the joy. *Thousands of thousands.* He set down this number, because that is finite with God which to men is infinite.

VERS. 12.-- *Wisdom, and strength,* etc. [0721B] The knowledge of all things, even as the Word united to himself has it.

VERS. 13.-- *Benediction.* That is, the increase which we have in good works, *and honour* which follows therefrom, *and glory* from the virtues which we receive in baptism, *and power* by which we resist concupiscences, let it be attributed not to us, but to thee.

VERS. 14.-- *And the four living creatures.* All the elders and the living creatures give their assent to the testimony of the angels. *They fell upon their faces,* etc. They are not proud because they judge others, but they humble themselves.
