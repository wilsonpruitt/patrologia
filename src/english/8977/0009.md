## CHAPTER IX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And the fifth angel.* ⟦s2⟧ By the order of the narration and by the order of time this angel is said to be the fourth. ⟦s3⟧ For this is the damnation of those whom the devil sends in to prepare the ways before the face of Antichrist. ⟦s4⟧ *And I saw a star.* ⟦s5⟧ That is, the flame of the heretics, of which he had said little, and he sets forth from what fuel it grew. ⟦s6⟧ *A star.* ⟦s7⟧ That is, the devil falling, and labouring that no one may go into his place, that is, into paradise. ⟦s8⟧ *And there was given.* ⟦s9⟧ The abyss, that is, the darkened. ⟦s10⟧ The pit, that is, the deeper heretics, because they plunge others with depraved opinions. ⟦s11⟧ Over these he is said to receive the key, that is, the power, because then he will not be bridled by God as he now is. ⟦s12⟧ Or that key are the very princes through whom the heretics [0726D] work.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *He opened the pit of the abyss.* ⟦s2⟧ He caused the heresies which lay hidden in their hearts to be brought forth. ⟦s3⟧ *The smoke of a furnace.* ⟦s4⟧ The doctrine of Antichrist is rightly called smoke, since Antichrist himself is the furnace purging the good and reducing the evil to ashes. ⟦s5⟧ *The sun and the air were darkened.* ⟦s6⟧ And by this were struck certain who enlighten others, and certain who are enlightened by them.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *There came out locusts.* ⟦s2⟧ The disciples of the heretics are compared to locusts, because they neither fly on high; that is, contemplation through knowledge, nor walk firmly by good working: but they leap by pride, and fall back into worse, and are gnawers of the good.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0727A] *As the scorpions have.* ⟦s2⟧ The scorpion, mild of face, stings secretly with its tail; or he compares them to the scorpion, because as the scorpion does with its tail, so the heretics deceive by temporal things, which ought to come after, as the tail is the hinder part in an animal. ⟦s3⟧ Or he compares them for this reason, that when the scorpion stings it is not felt, and afterwards it spreads its venom, so those deceived by heretics do not feel it, and at last they perish.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *It was commanded them that they should not hurt,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because God bridles the guiles of these men, although they themselves do not understand it.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *That they should not kill.* ⟦s2⟧ When by their guiles the heretics are not able, they wish to overthrow in certain matters, against whom it has been ordained by God that, although they kill bodies, yet they should not deceive souls. ⟦s3⟧ *Five months.* ⟦s4⟧ Another [0727B] reading has *six months,* on account of the six ages by which this life is divided. ⟦s5⟧ *As the torment of a scorpion.* ⟦s6⟧ Although a power of tormenting has been given, they do not torment openly, but accuse before the princes of the world.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And death shall fly from them.* ⟦s2⟧ Because the care of the flock binds them to labour.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *The shapes of the locusts,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ When he had shown in what manner they hurt, both by guiles and by hidden assault, and in both the bridling of God, now he shows what manner of men they are through whom they can work. ⟦s3⟧ *Like unto horses.* ⟦s4⟧ Because they are swift to run about, fierce to assail, and they do not foresee whom they run upon, whether upon neighbours or upon enemies. ⟦s5⟧ *As it were crowns.* ⟦s6⟧ That is, victories won over those cast down and overthrown, not by true wisdom.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0727C]VERS. 8.-- *And they had hair.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, wanton and effeminate manners, which cleave to them, and hang from them for the deceiving of others. ⟦s3⟧ Or the hairs are lesser men, whom, cleaving to them, they deceive. ⟦s4⟧ *The teeth of lions.* ⟦s5⟧ They have a tearing and a stench.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Breastplates.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, obstinate hearts, which the arrow of truth does not pierce. ⟦s3⟧ Or opinions fortified with deceptions, which the truth breaks. ⟦s4⟧ *As the sound of chariots,* etc. ⟦s5⟧ Divers chariots are hurried by divers ways and by divers horses to the same battle, so these with divers heresies assail the Church with one mind.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *And they had tails.* ⟦s2⟧ After their reasoning or their uproar avails not, they seek the help of princes, who are called tails, because, that they may bind fast, [0727D] they terrify and they flatter. ⟦s3⟧ Or the tails are deceitful opinions, with which they flatter to the face and secretly sting.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *And they had over them a king.* ⟦s2⟧ Having shown what they are in themselves, he shows through whom they are able to do these things. ⟦s3⟧ *Whose name in Hebrew is Abaddon.* ⟦s4⟧ As if he should say: Against this beware, you Hebrews, Greeks and Latins, that is, the whole Church. ⟦s5⟧ And he set down three tongues, because the Gospel of Christ was written and received in some one of these three tongues.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *One woe is past.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, the first by the order of the narration and by the order of time. ⟦s3⟧ For this is the declaring and the damnation of the evil which shall be in the time of Antichrist.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0728A]VERS. 13.-- *From the four horns.* ⟦s2⟧ By the four horns understand those four things which are preached concerning Christ, which are the nativity, the passion, the resurrection, and the ascension: about which the rest stand. ⟦s3⟧ *Of the golden altar.* ⟦s4⟧ The altar is Christ, the horns of the altar are the preachers who lift Christ up, and are ready to die for him, as the blood was placed upon the horns of the altar. ⟦s5⟧ Or the altar is the Church, in which are the horns, that is, the defenders of the rest, who all tend to the same end. ⟦s6⟧ Or by the four horns we can understand the evangelical teachings which teach that the frauds of Antichrist are to be uncovered. ⟦s7⟧ *One voice.* ⟦s8⟧ The heralds of past time forewarn those who shall be in the time of Antichrist.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Loose the four.* ⟦s2⟧ For the four parts of the world, or because they assail the four things preached concerning Christ. [0728B] *Who are bound in the great river Euphrates.* ⟦s3⟧ Euphrates is the river encircling Babylon, that is, the worldly princes through whom the devil, while he was in them, worked secretly. ⟦s4⟧ But saying *loose,* he bids these to be excommunicated, that he who was in them as it were bound may work more openly through them, delivered over to him. ⟦s5⟧ Or the four angels are all the unclean spirits reigning in the four parts of the world, who were bridled in the passion of Christ.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *They were loosed,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The devil being loosed, many are cast headlong who labour to blind others, *For an hour and a day.* ⟦s3⟧ By *an hour* take the half of a year; by *a day,* one year; by *a month,* another year; and by *a year,* a third: for the persecution of Antichrist shall last three years and a half.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0728C]VERS. 16.-- *Twenty thousand times.* ⟦s2⟧ The saints made perfect by the ten precepts of the law, against whom a double number of the evil is set down, because the evil are more than the good. ⟦s3⟧ *And I heard,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ As if he should say: Rightly do I set it down under a certain number, because *I heard,* that is, I understood, that the evil were more than the good.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *And thus I saw.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, as I had understood that they rode unto the destruction of others, so he understood that they did this through the devil, whom they bore as horses, or the horses can be understood to be devils, upon whom the evil are founded. ⟦s3⟧ *They had fiery breastplates.* ⟦s4⟧ That is, they fortified them with opinions upon which eternal punishment follows, where there is fire and stench and smoke. ⟦s5⟧ *Or they had fiery breastplates,* that is, eternal punishment, which [0728D] never releases those whom it receives. ⟦s6⟧ *As the heads of lions.* ⟦s7⟧ Above, in the plague of the locusts, he saw the faces of men, here, of lions: because the heretics show something of humanity, but the ministers of Antichrist will compel men by punishments also to confess that which they shall teach by words and by signs. ⟦s8⟧ *Fire went forth.* ⟦s9⟧ The cause of eternal punishment. ⟦s10⟧ *Or fire,* that is, cupidity; *smoke,* that is, pride; *brimstone,* that is, the stench of evil works. ⟦s11⟧ *Smoke and brimstone.* ⟦s12⟧ They are blind and set in darkness, because of the stench of vices and of evil works.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *The third part was slain.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, all the reprobate of the Church, and likewise all the Jews or pagans, who were never baptized, nor separated from the Church.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0729A]VERS. 19.-- *Like unto serpents.* ⟦s2⟧ Who flatter with the face, and secretly send in venom.
