## CHAPTER XIII.

VERS. 1.-- *And I saw,* etc. This beast spiritually is Antichrist, or generally the whole gathering of the wicked. *Seven heads and ten horns.* To this end, that he might be well able to conquer these, he appointed such a household for himself. *Names of blasphemy.* The beast was full of various heresies, or hiding its vices with various pretences.

VERS. 3.-- *As it were slain.* Antichrist will feign himself dead, and lying hid for three days will afterward appear, saying that he has been raised up. *And the wound of his death was healed.* By magic art he will ascend into the air, the demons bearing him, and so *the wound of his death* will be healed, because [0733C] before he was believed dead, afterward he will be reckoned living.

VERS. 6.-- *And he opened his mouth.* He broke out into such boldness that what before was in secret, then he now dares to blaspheme publicly. *And the tabernacle.* That is, his whole institution, through which he appears praiseworthy.

VERS. 8.-- *He was slain from the beginning of the world,* in his own, as in Abel. Or because before all things it was disposed that at the end of times he should be slain. Or he was slain mystically in the lamb which Abel offered, or he was prefigured in Abel himself, slain by his brother.

VERS. 10.-- *He that leadeth into captivity.* Who takes men captive, now from the faith, at last from salvation, namely Antichrist, or the devil himself. *He that shall kill by the sword.* Antichrist and his own will kill by the material sword and by the sword of persuasion, [0733D] and they shall perish by the sword of eternal judgment. *Here is,* etc. That is, the saints will hold patience and faith, because, he being destroyed, they know that they are to be rewarded. Looking to the punishment of the wicked and the reward of the good, they become patient and hold the faith.

VERS. 11.-- *And I saw.* The tribulation having been described which shall be through Antichrist and his partners, he subjoins another which shall come to pass through his apostles, whom he himself will scatter through the whole world. *Horns.* Because they will feign that they have innocence, and a pure life, and true doctrine, and the miracles which Christ had and gave to his own; or, they will usurp the two Testaments to themselves. *And he spoke as a dragon.* That is, the false apostles will so speak as the devil had spoken in Antichrist.

[0734A]VERS. 12.-- *And the power,* etc. Because in all things they set Antichrist, their head, before themselves.

*The earth.* That is, earthly men, and those persevering in the love of earth; or: *the earth* according to the body, *them that dwell therein* according to the soul. *To adore.* Because these also, by a feigned resurrection, make the foundation of their preaching, as the apostles of Christ also did.

VERS. 13.-- *Fire,* that is, he will make the malignant spirit descend upon his own, that they may speak with divers tongues. As the Holy Spirit was given to the apostles in the appearance of fire, so also these will give the malignant spirit in the appearance of fire. *In the sight.* That all may be able to see, whereas the apostles received the Holy Spirit in a closed chamber.

VERS. 15.-- *That the image should speak.* This man by magic art will make a statue speak and foretell things to come.

[0734B]VERS. 16.-- *To have a character,* etc. On the hand and on the forehead they will set signs, that all may confess by word and by work that he is their God.

VERS. 17.-- *The number.* That is, those who look for from him a reward so much the less, or a middling one, or the greatest.

VERS. 18.-- *He that hath.* Since the present book was published in Greek, the number is to be sought according to the Greeks, among whom all the letters signify a number. His name is *Antemos,* which befits him who is called contrary to Christ: *A,* one; *N,* fifty; *T,* CCCC; *E,* five; *M,* forty; *O,* seventy; *S,* CC. Or *Arnoyme,* that is, I deny, who denies Christ to be God, in which is the same number: *A,* one; *R,* CC; *N,* fifty; *O,* LXX; *Y,* CCCC; *M,* XL; *E,* five. Or *Teian,* [0734C] that is, the sun a giant, which belongs to the true Christ, but by usurpation suits Antichrist: *T,* CCC; *E,* five; *I,* X; again *T,* CCC; A, one; *N, L.* Or by Latin letters: *Diclux,* because he himself will say that he is the light: *D,* five hundred; *I,* one; *C,* a hundred; *L,* fifty; *V,* five; *X,* ten; and thus the same sum.

Six is the first perfect number, and signifies those less perfect who are married, and who according to their own measure keep the ten commandments. Sixty signifies the moderately perfect, who, lawful marriages being dismissed, remain in chastity, and again fulfil the ten commandments according to their own measure. A hundred signifies the most perfect, who do not violate the integrity of mind and of body and keep the ten commandments most perfectly. *These render fruit, some thirtyfold, [0734D] some sixtyfold, some a hundredfold.* [var: Vulg. Mt 13:8 *aliud centesimum, aliud sexagesimum, aliud trigesimum*, in the reverse order] And the disciples of Antichrist will then say that no one can be saved unless they have offered to Antichrist some one of those fruits.
