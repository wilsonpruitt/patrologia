## CHAPTER XVI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *A wound.* ⟦s2⟧ Disobedience is the cause why that wound is made.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *And it became blood.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, its damnation was brought in for the blood which they shed. ⟦s3⟧ *Blood,* that is, that damnation shall be *as of a dead man,* that is, irrevocable; for as a dead man does not return to life, so that sentence of damnation shall not be revoked. [0738A] *Every living soul.* ⟦s4⟧ All who consent, who seem to live, shall be damned not because they openly persecute, but on account of their consent.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Upon the rivers,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because these likewise shall be damned for the blood which they shed, either corporally or spiritually.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *I heard.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, I understood those preachers who shall work this vengeance, attributing it not to themselves but to the Lord, and affirming that it is justly done. ⟦s3⟧ *Who art and who wast.* ⟦s4⟧ He does not set down "who is to come," because he understood him to be coming at hand.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And thou hast given them blood to drink.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, eternal punishment for the shedding of blood, in the deserving of which they took delight.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *And I heard.* ⟦s2⟧ What the masters say, the disciples [0738B] confirm. ⟦s3⟧ *Yea, O Lord God almighty,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ Out of great affection they turn their speech to God. ⟦s5⟧ *Just.* ⟦s6⟧ For it is just that he who shed blood should drink the vengeance of blood.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *And they were scorched.* ⟦s2⟧ Or, being afflicted, they will afflict their tormentors with the heat of wrath, while they shall be found inseparable, and so they were scorched with envy and with anger. ⟦s3⟧ *Neither did they do.* ⟦s4⟧ Inwardly repenting they grieved, outwardly they dared not confess.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Upon the seat of the beast.* ⟦s2⟧ He says this because these are only bodies, and not spirits: over whom he will sit, because he will give bodily things and not spiritual, *They gnawed,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ All refreshing themselves by mutual detraction.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *The way might be prepared.* ⟦s2⟧ Because, the world being ended, [0738C] there shall be the resurrection of the faithful, who shall come from the rising of the sun, that is, from Christ, when he shall have begun to shine, that is, to manifest himself.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *I saw.* ⟦s2⟧ The pouring out of the vial upon these, and that they themselves were not corrected, but were cast headlong into a greater blinding. ⟦s3+4⟧ *Spirits to go out.* [var: Vulg. Apc 16:13 has no *exire*, reading only *spiritus tres immundos*] That is, all the impious who by reason of exceeding malice are to be called unclean spirits; or, the demons themselves, whom they beget in the hearts of others. ⟦s5⟧ For as the faithful by their doctrine beget the faith of Christ in the hearts of others, so these by malice beget the devil. ⟦s6⟧ *Of frogs.* ⟦s7⟧ Frogs tarry in mud, and are garrulous, and take away rest from others: so also are these.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *They go forth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The demons go forth, and the devil will gather them together, because all are one.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0738D]VERS. 16.-- *And he shall gather together.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, they will make them have confidence in Antichrist, who is a refuge to all who wish to steal from the saints their faith; or unto whom all the wicked of the diverse sects will glory.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *And the seventh angel.* ⟦s2⟧ It having been shown upon whom the angels pour out their vials, he subjoins what befalls the wicked themselves through that pouring out, namely that here they will resist as much as they shall be able, as if from the very infusion they had received increase, but at last they shall be thrust down into hell.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *And the city was made.* ⟦s2⟧ Because of that damnation and because of the sins which they themselves add, that city was damned, and the punishment was divided to each one [0739A] according to his desert, namely to the Jews and to the Gentiles, and to false Christians. ⟦s3⟧ *The cities of the Gentiles fell,* that is, all the gatherings of the wicked who remain in the first generation shall be damned: the carnal generation, not in the second, spiritual regeneration.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Came in remembrance.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, God remembered their several sins, of which he before seemed forgetful. ⟦s3⟧ *To give her the cup.* ⟦s4⟧ To this end, that God himself might give them a punishment measured for the delights which they had. ⟦s5⟧ God repays the wicked according to their deserts, that is, what they deserved, but not as much as they deserved, that in that very retribution, where justice is manifest, there may also be mercy. ⟦s6⟧ *For all the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth.* ⟦s7⟧ To the good also he gives according to their deserts, and more than they deserved, that there mercy over justice [0739B] may prevail.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *And every island fled away.* ⟦s2⟧ And he saw that all who were afflicted for God in the world and eminent in virtues, in respect of these punishments, in their own life avoided the fellowships of the impious, and therefore were not found in their punishments.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *A talent.* ⟦s2⟧ There are three kinds of talent: the great, of a hundred and twenty pounds; the middle, of seventy-two, the lesser, of fifty. ⟦s3⟧ Here he speaks of the middle, because sinners in the seventy-two tongues shall not escape this vengeance.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And they blasphemed.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, being placed in hell, although they know that they are punished according to their desert, yet they will grieve that God has so great power that he brings plagues upon them, and that is blasphemy.
