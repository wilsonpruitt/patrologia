## CHAPTER XX.

VERS. 1.-- *And I saw an angel.* When the damnation of Babylon and of Antichrist and of the false apostles has been told, he subjoins the damnation of the devil himself, and sets before it the cause for which and the time in which he shall be damned: and who shall be able to overcome him, namely he who in his lowly coming was able to bind him as far as he willed, being glorified shall be able [0744C] utterly to destroy the same. *Of the abyss.* The hearts of the ungodly are called a dark abyss; or the devil himself, whom the Lord permits to rage and bridles when he wills.

VERS. 2.-- *He laid hold.* That is, he showed him to have sinned, in that that devil slew him who was free from sin. *He bound him.* It is to be known that the devil was bound in Abraham and in the other faithful in like manner as in these that are present; but in the former the hope of the Christ to come bound him, in the latter Christ himself coming bound him.

VERS. 3.-- *And he cast him,* etc. Because, cast out from the faithful, he began to lord it more cruelly among the wicked: so it is read in the Gospel, that he entered into the swine. *He sealed him up,* etc. That is, he set a seal, namely the sign of the cross, which so overcomes him that it drives him back from the faithful. *And after these things he must,* etc., that he may receive back the power [0744D] which he had before the coming of Christ.

VERS. 4.-- *And see thrones.* [var: Vulg. Apc 20:4 *Et vidi sedes*, "And I saw thrones"] The devil being bound, I saw the faithful loosed, and the lesser ready to obey their prelates, and the prelates judging their subjects. I saw the Church in the time of that binding so loosed unto good working, and at that same time I saw those who die for Christ enter straightway into glory, never to go down to the lower regions, as Abraham and the rest of the prophets went down, although they were just. *And the souls of them that were beheaded.* And I saw in like manner the souls of those who adored not. *And they lived.* And I say not, they shall live and reign in the time to come, but also in this present time, that is, from the time they were slain they lived and reigned with Christ.

[0745A]VERS. 5.-- *But the rest of the dead lived not,* that is, they shall suffer eternal punishments, and that in this present time also, because they die straightway. *This is the resurrection.* That the devil is thus bound, and that men rise again from sins, and that when they themselves are dead their souls straightway enjoy eternal rest, is the first resurrection and the chief. *The first.* For a difference from that which shall be in soul and body together, this is called the first.

*He that hath part.* If all cannot be perfect, neither shall they be in the same brightness. *The second death,* that is, eternal damnation, which shall be in the body and in the soul of the reprobate, shall not lay hold of the elect, who are never in anything increased [cj: *augentur*; read *anguntur*, "are distressed"]. *But they shall be priests,* that is, offering eternal praises to the Godhead and to the manhood.

[0745B]VERS. 7.-- *Out of prison.* That is, out of the hearts of the reprobate, in which he is now bound, that he may not rage at his will. *Magog.* That is, the uncovered, that is, all persecutors covering the devil within themselves, and at last proceeding to open persecution. Or, according to the letter, he shall first seduce these two nations, and through them proceed to the others.

*And they compassed the camp of the saints,* that is, those same saints fortified in virtues and of one mind in this war.

VERS. 10.-- *Day and night.* This he says in respect of the glory of the saints, which is day, and of their own punishments, which is night.

VERS. 11.-- *And I saw a throne.* When the destruction of the devil and of his members has been told, he subjoins the destruction of the world, unto the greater punishment of the ungodly. For it is fitting [0745C] that, the wicked being destroyed, the world be destroyed, according as it had been corrupted by them. Having often spoken of the glorification of the saints and of the punishments of the ungodly, he had made no mention of bodies, whether they ought to be glorified or punished; therefore here he shows them openly.

VERS. 12.-- *And the books were opened.* Books, that is, the divine precepts, and they who have let them go shall know that they are punished according to desert; or the books are the consciences of each man, which shall be open to all. *Another book.* That is, Christ, who shall then appear to all as mighty, and shall give life to his own. Or, the book is the foreknowledge of God, because then the wicked shall openly know that they are not predestined to the life which, while they were in the world, they promised themselves. *Out of those things which were written.* By comparison with the saints the ungodly shall be damned: for the ungodly themselves shall read in the [0745D] saints themselves the things which they were unwilling to do.

VERS. 13.-- *And the sea gave up the dead.* He wishes to show openly that the bodies of the dead, howsoever they may have been cut asunder, are made alive at that judgment, which certain men do not believe.

VERS. 14.-- *This is the second death.* As if he should say: Beware you of the first death, because from that first this second follows.
