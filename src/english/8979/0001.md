## CHAPTER II.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0695C]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *My little children, these things I write,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Lest you take up an evil security, hearing that you are cleansed by God, but that you may have a profitable fear. ⟦s3⟧ *If any man shall have sinned.* ⟦s4⟧ If out of human frailty you cannot beware of all vices, give diligence that you beware at least of the greater and the more open ones, that temptation may not take hold of you except such as is human. ⟦s5⟧ But if anyone shall have fallen even after my admonition, let him not despair. ⟦s6⟧ *We have an advocate.* ⟦s7⟧ He is not an advocate save to those who call upon him. ⟦s8⟧ Let sins displease thee, cry out: and he both hears and sets free. ⟦s9⟧ *Jesus Christ the just.* ⟦s10⟧ A just advocate does not undertake unjust causes, yet he will defend us as just in the judgment, if we now accuse ourselves as unjust.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0695D]VERS. 3.-- *And in this we know.* ⟦s2⟧ To know or to recognize is not always said on account of acquaintance, but on account of experience and union with another thing. ⟦s3⟧ We know God when we are united to him. ⟦s4⟧ He who so knows, keeps his commandments. ⟦s5⟧ *If we keep his commandments.* ⟦s6⟧ We ought to attend to our frailty, we ought in our sins to seek an advocate, which that we may obtain, let us labour at the keeping of the commandments, through which one comes to the knowledge of him.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *He that saith that he knoweth him,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ It is not a great thing to know one God, since the devils also believe and tremble.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Not a new commandment.* ⟦s2⟧ The same charity is the old commandment, because it was commended from the beginning. [0696A] The same is a new commandment, because, the darkness being cast out, it pours in the desire of a new light.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Which thing is true.* ⟦s2⟧ According to that which he promised, that through the observance of God's commandments one comes to the knowledge and the love of him. ⟦s3⟧ *And in him.* ⟦s4⟧ Fulfilled in Christ, because he obeyed the Father unto death: and therefore he was glorified. ⟦s5⟧ *And in you.* ⟦s6⟧ Of whom certain ones already through this make their boast with God, and it is truly new *because the darkness* is now *passed away.* ⟦s7⟧ Darkness pertains to the old man, but light to the new man.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *He that saith,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He determines what commandment he takes, that is, charity, and in what manner charity itself is to be held, namely, the love of God and of the neighbour.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10, 11.-- *He that loveth his brother,* is in the [0696B] light of knowledge and of working; but *he that hateth is in the darkness* of ignorance, and through that very ignorance he walks from vice to vice, *and knoweth not,* that is, does not foresee, *whither he goeth; and* to what punishment he is to be received. ⟦s2⟧ Or, *he knoweth not whither he goeth,* that is, he is ignorant of the way by which he may be turned to better things, and this not for this reason, as though the way were not open, but *because the darkness hath blinded his eyes,* who, departing from the light of Christ, is so hindered by sins and by carnal pleasure that even if he sees the good, yet he does not follow it out.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *I write unto you,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He puts first a cause which might draw them away from the love of the world, because to those baptized in the name of Christ and calling upon the name of Christ, sins are forgiven, therefore one must cleave to Christ rather than to the world. ⟦s3⟧ *Little children.* ⟦s4⟧ Fathers, youths, [0696C] young men. ⟦s5⟧ Therefore children: because sins are forgiven. ⟦s6⟧ Therefore fathers, because you have known ancient things. ⟦s7⟧ Therefore young men, because you are strong, because you have overcome.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Youths.* ⟦s2⟧ The time of youth is slippery because of the incitements of the flesh; but because of the vigour of that age it is fit for the contest, and these overcame the temptations of pleasures by the love of the word of God, and despised persecutions.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *And you have overcome.* ⟦s2⟧ At that time young men were dragged by custom to the brothel, but these, by resisting manfully, kept overcoming.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Love not the world.* ⟦s2⟧ Use the world for necessity, but love it not for superfluity. ⟦s3⟧ And make not provision for the flesh in its desires. ⟦s4⟧ *Love not the world.* ⟦s5⟧ One heart does not hold two mutually adverse [0696D] loves. ⟦s6⟧ Rooted in charity, build nothing upon this root except what befits charity, because you cannot serve two masters. ⟦s7⟧ As the love of God is the fountain of all virtues, so the love of the world is the fountain of all vices.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *All that is in the world. [n: (BEDA.)]* That is: All lovers of the world have nothing but these three things, by which, etc., *as far as* he willed not to be exalted above the kingdoms of the world.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *But he that doth the will,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ In this way also charity can be had, if indeed by no instigation of heretics [sic: *hae eticorum*] you have been cast down from the faith and from the other things which are God's. ⟦s3⟧ And that you may hold it, let there remain in you the things which you have heard from the beginning.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0697A]VERS. 18.-- *Little children, it is the last hour.* ⟦s2⟧ And therefore you ought to persist, because there are many antichrists who wish to seduce you. ⟦s3⟧ *As you have heard that Antichrist cometh.* ⟦s4⟧ For with how great an onset and terror you have heard that Antichrist would come, with so great a violence do these also come. ⟦s5⟧ We are in the eleventh hour, the Saviour has come in the flesh, and the plague of Antichrist is to follow, who is to assail the proclamations of salvation, who is to root up the vineyard that Christ cultivates, and many members of this most wicked head have already been sent before, of whom we must beware, because the end of the world is now at hand; or *the last hour,* that is, like the last, this persecution is like that which is to come. ⟦s6⟧ *Now there are many antichrists.* ⟦s7⟧ All heretics are antichrists, all who destroy the faith which they confess in words [0697B] by their acts; all who are contrary to Christ, who bear witness to their head that is to come, because the mystery of iniquity is already working in them.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *They went out from us,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Although there are many antichrists, yet let it not terrify you if they have returned to Judaism or to paganism, do not for that reason think that the Church suffers any loss from this; because if they went out from us, yet they were not truly of us. ⟦s3⟧ They could not have gone out unless they were contrary to Christ. ⟦s4⟧ He who is not contrary to Christ remains in the body of Christ. ⟦s5⟧ The feigned are in the Church just as evil humours are in the body; when they are vomited out, the body is relieved; so when the wicked go out, the Church is relieved. ⟦s6⟧ *But that they may be made manifest.* ⟦s7⟧ Many who are not of us receive with us the sacraments of Christ, but [0697C] temptation proves that they are not of us. ⟦s8⟧ When temptation shall have come upon them, they fly outside as if by occasion of a wind, because they were not grains. ⟦s9⟧ All will then fly, when the Lord's threshing-floor shall have begun to be winnowed in the day of judgment. ⟦s10⟧ Therefore, God permitting, certain men go out of the Church before the last dissension, showing that they were not of the body of Christ, that through this it may become manifestly clear that not all are of us who, placed within with us, receive the sacraments of Christ, and therefore their separation ought not to burden you.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *But you have the unction.* ⟦s2⟧ When he was speaking of heretics, turning suddenly to his own, he says that they have the unction from the Holy One, that he may show on the contrary that heretics and all antichrists are deprived of the gift of spiritual grace, nor do they pertain [0697D] to him who is called Holy by the prophets. ⟦s3⟧ *You know all things.* ⟦s4⟧ You know the truth of faith and of life, taught by the unction of the Spirit, nor have you need to be taught, save that you persist in that which you have begun.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *And that every lie. [n: (BEDA.)]* He had said that every lie is not of the truth, etc., *as far as* of all who scorn to obey the commandments of Christ.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *This is Antichrist,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Behold, we are admonished how we may know Antichrist, namely, *whosoever denieth Christ,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ *He that denieth the Father.* ⟦s4⟧ In vain does he confess God the Father who denies the Son, who proceeds from God.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *That which you have heard,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ If anyone shall say: [0698A] Behold, here is Christ, behold, there, believe not, but that which you have heard from the apostles, hold fast. ⟦s3⟧ *And you also in the Son and in the Father.* ⟦s4⟧ So shall you be members of the Father and of the Son, that you fall not away from the memory and the protection of the Father and of the Son, and this you ought to seek, that you may be in the Father and in the Son, because from thence you will obtain the reward.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *This is the promise.* ⟦s2⟧ Let the memory of the promised reward make thee persevering in the work.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *But as his unction.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, the Holy Spirit, whose sacrament is in the visible anointing; or there is understood the unction of charity which is poured forth in our hearts through the Holy Spirit, who is given to us; which inflames to the keeping of God's commandments the heart that it fills.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *And now, little children, abide.* ⟦s2⟧ By frequent repetition [0698B] he drives it home, that he may fix it the more closely in their minds. ⟦s3⟧ *Abide in him.* ⟦s4⟧ And if you have any good, impute it all to him and not to yourselves; impute the evils to yourselves and to the devil.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *Every one,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] Perfect justice scarcely exists in the angels, etc., *as far as* when there will not be a struggle with the flesh, but a triumph over the enemy.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of him.* ⟦s2⟧ Now if we are born of the just one, we must follow the justice of the just Father.
