## CHAPTER IV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Because many false prophets are gone out.* ⟦s2⟧ Many heretics, many schismatics confess that Jesus has come in the flesh, but by their deeds they deny it, by not having charity. ⟦s3⟧ By charity the Word was made flesh; he who has not charity denies that he came in the flesh, and he is convicted of not having the Spirit from God. ⟦s4⟧ The Spirit of God says that Jesus has come in the flesh not by the sound of the tongue, but by loving and by doing. ⟦s5⟧ Jesus came that he might gather, the heretic scatters: he has not the Spirit of God.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Every spirit,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Many heretics, who [0701B] wished by a depraved dogma to divide the unity of the Church, erased this little verse from the epistle, lest through it they should be convicted of error. ⟦s3⟧ *He dissolveth.* ⟦s4⟧ He who separates God from man, who divides the members from God, who interprets the words of God ill, who departs from God by living ill, he dissolves Jesus.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *They are of the world.* ⟦s2⟧ These spirits who are of the world are not to be loved, are not to be heard. ⟦s3⟧ For although they invoke the name of Christ, and mark themselves with the sign of Christ, yet they are of the number of those who desire worldly things, who are ignorant of heavenly things. ⟦s4⟧ *Therefore they speak of the world.* ⟦s5⟧ Proving by the reasoning of worldly wisdom that God cannot raise up a dead man, that a mortal man cannot have a dwelling in the heavens, and other things of this kind.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0701C]VERS. 7.-- *Dearly beloved, let us love one another.* ⟦s2+3⟧ He has commended charity in many ways, now he comes to its singular praise, wherein we ought most of all to hear him, namely: *He that loveth is born of God and knoweth God, because God is charity.* [var: Vulg. 1 Jo 4:7 reads *Et omnis qui diligit, ex Deo natus est, et cognoscit Deum*, and closes *quia caritas ex Deo est*; *Deus caritas est* belongs to v. 8] He had said: *Charity is of God;* here he has added further: *God is charity.*

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Because God is charity.* ⟦s2⟧ Charity does not differ in God, since he is called both just and justice, as also loving and love, because he is not called just by a transferred name from justice, nor loving by love, as though he had a justice or a love distinct from himself, but because he is his own justice and love.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *In this hath appeared.* ⟦s2⟧ By proving that God is charity and that he loves us, he wished to lead us on by his own example, that we should love one another and come to the knowledge [0701D] of him. ⟦s3⟧ As God did not wait for us to love him, so let us not wait for others to love us, but let us love first. ⟦s4⟧ The love of the Father has been proved, because he sent the Son: the love of the Son appeared in this, that he died for us whom he loved beforehand.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Not as though we had loved God.* ⟦s2⟧ We did not love first, so that he should love us as though by the merit of our love, but he himself loved us first, that with his grace going before we should love him.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *And we ought to love one another.* ⟦s2⟧ Love, and do what thou wilt. ⟦s3⟧ Whether thou art silent, be silent in love: whether thou criest out, cry out in love. ⟦s4⟧ From this root nothing can come forth except good.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0702A]VERS. 12.-- *No man hath seen God at any time. [n: (BEDA.)]* As those visible things known to the senses of the body are seen, so God is not seen, etc., *as far as* while we are still pilgrims in the body, let us love one another, etc.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *In this we know.* ⟦s2⟧ Whence shall we know whether we have the Spirit of God? ⟦s3⟧ Question thine own bowels: if they are full of charity, thou hast the Spirit of God. ⟦s4⟧ *Because we abide in him.* ⟦s5⟧ God abides in us, and we in him, and therefore let no one despair of salvation, because although the diseases of crimes weigh us down, the physician who saves is almighty. ⟦s6⟧ Let us rejoice in hope, that we may come to the reality.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And we have known.* ⟦s2⟧ We have seen and bear witness that God sent his Son, and we have known for what cause he did this: not because he had need, not because he owed anything, but out of charity alone. ⟦s3⟧ Though he had [0702B] an only Son, he willed not that he should be one alone, but that he might have brethren, and he adopted for him those who should possess eternal life with him.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Fear is not in charity.* ⟦s2⟧ In that charity, namely, which in imitation of the divine goodness does good even to enemies, there is no fear.
