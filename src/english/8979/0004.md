## CHAPTER V.

VERS. 1.-- *Every one that believeth.* He believes who so lives as Christ commanded, otherwise the devils also believe. But works apart from faith are either nothing, or, even if they seem good, are empty, because they are apart from the way, which is Christ. Truly he who loves God loves his brother. For he who loves God the Father loves God the Son, begotten of the Father, and he who loves God the Son loves also the sons of God, the members of that [0702C] head.

VERS. 3.-- *They are not heavy. [n: (BEDA.)]* They do not draw downward, like a talent of lead, etc., *as far as* lest anyone should trust that by his own strength he can overcome the world, he adds concerning faith:

VERS. 4.-- *And this is the victory,* etc. That faith, namely, which works through love, which craves God's help.

VERS. 5.-- *But who is he?* etc. He determines what faith conquers, namely the faith of Christ. As if to say: Truly through faith he conquers the world, because through anything else it is not conquered. *Our faith.* Behold what faith conquers, that which believes him true man and true God.

VERS. 6.-- *This is he,* etc. Because faith and the confession of the divinity alone does not suffice for salvation and for overcoming [0702D] the world, he adds also concerning the humanity: therefore he expressed the blood.

*The spirit.* That is, the human soul which he gave up in the passion; *water and blood,* which flowed from his side. This could not have been done, if he had not had the true nature of flesh. But even before the passion, the sweat which was made like drops of blood showed the truth of the flesh. And this, that from the side of one already dead, against nature, water, blood flowed vigorously, bore witness that the body of the Lord would live better after death, and that his death would give us life. But that the sweat flowed like blood onto the earth signified that he would wash the Church in the whole world with his own blood.

VERS. 7.-- *For there are three.* Through this it appears, [0703A] that Jesus is the truth, true God, true man. And of both we have sure testimony, of the Godhead indeed through the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit: of the humanity through the soul, the water, and the blood. The Father gave the testimony of the Godhead when he said: *This is my beloved Son.* The Son himself gave it, when he was transfigured on the mountain, and showed both the power of the Godhead and the hope of eternal blessedness. The Holy Spirit gave it, when he rested upon him who had been baptized in the form of a dove, or when at the invocation of the name of Christ he filled the hearts of believers.

VERS. 10.-- *He that believeth in the Son of God.* He understands the Father to be truthful in the testimony of the Son. He who does not believe says that the Father has lied. In vain therefore do the [0703B] Jews say that they believe in the Father, so long as they despise Christ. *In the Son.* Because the Father bears witness of the Son, let us believe in the Son. Because he who so believes in the Son that by good desiring he strives toward him has the testimony of God in himself, as if he should say: Of him also God bears witness, that he who so believes will be in the number of the sons of God, the Son himself so promising to his own: *If any man minister to me, my Father will honour him [n: (Joan. XII)]*. If therefore thou hast God as the witness of thy faith, what does the ill fame or the persecution of men hurt thee? *If God be for us, who is against us? [n: (Rom. VIII.)]*

VERS. 11.-- *And this is the testimony.* He bore witness of the Son, and also of us his adoptive sons, that through his only Son he would give eternal life to us also, already in hope, but one day in reality.

[0703C] *And this is the life. [n: (BEDA.)]* In faith and in the confession of the name, in the receiving of the sacraments, etc., *as far as* who gives eternal life to his own.

VERS. 14.-- *And this is the confidence.* In many ways he drives home the same things which he had promised above, that he may rouse us more keenly to prayer. *According to his will.* By which he wills that we ask what he wills, or that we come to the asking such as he desires us to be. If we will our own salvation and that of our neighbours, we do not disagree from the will of God. If our will through ignorance departs from the will of God, the good will of God corrects our foolish will, as befell the apostle Paul.

VERS. 15.-- *We know that,* etc. Because we know that we have from him the petitions which we ask, let us ask nothing that is contrary to him, let us ask nothing but what he has [0703D] taught us, what he has inspired in us: and if we err in anything, we are corrected at once.

VERS. 16.-- *He that knoweth his brother. [n: (BEDA.)]* He speaks of daily and light sins, etc., *as far as* and by their examination chastise thyself.

[0704A] *There is a sin unto death.* This sin separates from God, as death separates the soul from the body.

VERS. 17.-- *All iniquity is sin.* As if to say: Truly one must pray for those who sin not unto death, because all are taken up with many sins, and no one can be without sin, because all iniquity is sin. But above, the sin unto death is another thing, which is not committed out of human frailty, and therefore is not purged by the prayer of the just: since to whom [cj: *cui*; read *qui*, "who"] do such things, they shall not obtain the kingdom of God.

VERS. 18.-- *Every one that is born,* etc. He who is born of God does not sin unto death: he who is not born, sins; but we are of those who are born of God: therefore we shall not sin, we shall not be touched by the enemy, but the lovers of the world are subject to the malignant enemy. [0704B] *He sinneth not.* David sinned grievously, but because, born of God, he belonged to the fellowship of the sons of God, he did not sin, unto death, but by repenting he earned pardon. *But the generation of God,* etc. They who persevere in the generation of God cannot sin, nor be touched by the malignant one. As day and night cannot be mingled, so justice and iniquity, the malignant one and the generation of God. The malignant one therefore touches some, hurting and afflicting them, but not to their harm.

VERS. 19.-- *And the whole world.* Not only the lovers of the world, but also the newly born, who have not the discretion of good and evil, belong to the kingdom of the devil because of the first transgression, unless by the grace of God they are rescued from the darkness.

[0704C]VERS. 20.-- *And we know,* etc. No one can come to eternal life without the divine knowledge, no one can know without the grace of God, because no one knows the Son but the Father, neither does anyone know the Father but the Son, and he to whom the Son shall have willed to reveal both the Father and the Son. For the Son reveals both, who, appearing visible in the flesh, laid open to the world the secrets of the Godhead through the Gospel.

VERS. 21.-- *Little children.* And because you know true God and true man, and look for eternal life, keep yourselves from the doctrines of heretics, who take to themselves an appearance of sanctity, who by depraved dogmas change the glory of the incorruptible God into the likeness of corruptible things. For covetousness is the service of idols; they therefore who set the world before God [0704D] are idolaters. *Keep yourselves.* Although he had borne them testimony of perfection in many things, yet it could still be feared lest someone newly converted should retain in his mind the remnants of superstition, and therefore he shows that the worship of idols must be fled.
