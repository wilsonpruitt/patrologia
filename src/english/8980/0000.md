## THE ARGUMENT.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0679B]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Simon Peter, the son of Jonas, of the province of Galilee, of the village of Bethsaida, the brother of the apostle Andrew, shows that the disciples of the unconquered Saviour are spread through the whole world and are pilgrims in this age, and persuades them to repent of their past life, and exhorts them, [0679C] with all solicitude, to advance into a new life.

## CHAPTER ONE.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *To the strangers. [n: (Bed.)]* Advenae in Latin, in Greek proselytes, etc., *as far as* having: *Dearly beloved, I beseech you as strangers and pilgrims.*

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the dispersion.* ⟦s2⟧ In the year of the ascension Stephen died, and many were scattered through fear. ⟦s3⟧ *Of Pontus.* ⟦s4⟧ All these provinces of the Greeks are in Asia, but there is also another Bithynia in Europe: from which those Bithynians who are in Asia took their origin. ⟦s5⟧ But that Bithynia which is in Asia is called greater Phrygia, which is bounded off from Galatia by the river Hiera.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0679D]VERS. 2.-- *Grace.* ⟦s2⟧ Without the grace of Christ one never comes to the peace of his reconciliation, we are able to have nothing peaceable save through his grace. ⟦s3⟧ *And peace be multiplied.* ⟦s4⟧ That is, let no adversity draw you back from the praises of God, let every tribulation invite you to praise, because all things are multiplied unto happiness: that what you have well begun, you may perfectly complete.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *He hath regenerated. [n: (Bed.)]* Whereas by our own merits we had been begotten unto death, etc., *as far as* that through cleanness you may be heavens and the seat of God.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Incorruptible.* ⟦s2⟧ The soul is not naturally incorruptible, but by the grace of God, through faith and justice, it is perfected incorruptible in respect of the understanding. ⟦s3⟧ *In you.* ⟦s4⟧ He who gave to you that believe the power to become the sons of God has placed in you that perseverance [0680B] by which you may receive the inheritance in the heavens, because he who has not kept the discipline of the Father does not merit his inheritance.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *In the power.* ⟦s2⟧ No one by the power of his own liberty is able [0680C] to be kept in good things, unless he from whom you have the beginning of a good action bring it to perfection. ⟦s3⟧ Labour out of faith through faith, being about to come unto eternal salvation, which, even if it does not now appear, is nevertheless prepared to be revealed, if we shall have been prepared.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Now, if it be needful.* ⟦s2⟧ If, that is, although it be needful. ⟦s3⟧ Or, *if it be needful to be made sorrowful,* let us not fail.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *That the trial,* etc., *unto praise.* ⟦s2⟧ When the judge, praising your faith, shall say: *And you gave me to eat,* etc [n: (Matth. XXV)].

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *More precious than gold. [n: (Bed.)]* The patience of the saints is compared to gold, etc., *as far as* whence he also subjoins: *May it be found,* etc.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0680D] *Unto praise.* ⟦s2⟧ That you may be praiseworthy and glorious through constancy *in the revelation,* that is, in the day of judgment, when it shall be revealed of how great power God is. ⟦s3⟧ *And honour,* that it may be said to you: *Come, ye blessed of my Father [n: (Matth. XXV)]*.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *The salvation of souls.* ⟦s2⟧ This salvation is much to be loved, because concerning this salvation they sought much, when or in what order eternal salvation should come to the world; whence it is said: *Many prophets and kings have wished to see the things which you see, and have not seen them [n: (Matth. XIII)]*.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *They prophesied.* ⟦s2⟧ By speaking openly to men and by expounding those things which they themselves had known in the secrecy of inward contemplation.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *In what or,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ What time, that [0681A] is, in what year, or under what prince. ⟦s3⟧ Of what kind, warlike or peaceful, or in what order, by the childbearing of a virgin, or in what other manner. ⟦s4⟧ *The Spirit of Christ.* ⟦s5⟧ He calls archangels and those near him spirits, through whom the Lord works, because they are subject to Christ. ⟦s6⟧ *The later glories.* ⟦s7⟧ There are two glorifications of the Lord according to the form of the manhood he took upon him. ⟦s8⟧ One, by which he rose from the dead, and another, by which he ascended into heaven before the eyes of the apostles. ⟦s9⟧ A third remains, and that one shall be in the sight of men, when he shall come in majesty to render to every man according to his works.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *But unto you they ministered.* ⟦s2⟧ He says this for this reason, that he may admonish them to take care of the salvation offered, which the former saints so loved.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *By the Holy Ghost. [n: (BEDA.)]* The Spirit in the prophets, the Spirit [0681B] in the apostles, so it appears, that the prophets and the apostles announce the same salvation. ⟦s2⟧ They announced one to come, these one fulfilled. ⟦s3⟧ And so there is one Church, part of which went before the coming of Christ, and part follows.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Wherefore.* ⟦s2⟧ Because so great a grace has been promised to you, that you may see unveiled that which the angels now see, take care to be so much the more worthy, that you may be able to receive it. ⟦s3⟧ *Hope.* ⟦s4⟧ Await secure in mind, and chaste in body. ⟦s5⟧ For he who knows not that he pleases the Lord, deservedly lacking the hope of good things, fears lest it come the sooner.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Children.* ⟦s2⟧ And that you may be able to await securely, be children obedient to the Father who chastises.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Holy.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, sanctified and sanctifying you, that in all conversation you may be [0681C] holy: whence: *Be ye also perfect,* etc. [var: Vulg. Matt. 5:48 *Estote ergo vos perfecti*, "Be ye therefore perfect"]

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *In fear,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] I, defiled with all the filth of sins, day and night await with trembling to pay the last farthing. ⟦s2⟧ And the time when it shall be said to me, Jerome, come forth.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of your sojourning.* ⟦s2⟧ That you may be able to be holy, have your conversation in fear, in the time of your sojourning, so long as you are in the exile of this world. ⟦s3⟧ *For blessed is he that is always fearful,* [var: Vulg. Prov. 28:14 *Beatus homo qui semper est pavidus*, "Blessed is the man that is always fearful"] this I say if you call upon God, as if he should say: You do not truly call upon God, unless you be solicitous and fearful.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *But with the precious blood.* ⟦s2⟧ The order of the letter is: but with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb uncontaminated and unspotted. ⟦s3⟧ Here he touches upon the Levitical and [0681D] priestly celebrations. ⟦s4⟧ And he signifies the clean soul, through justice, which is offered to God.
