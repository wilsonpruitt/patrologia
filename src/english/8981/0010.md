## CHAPTER XIII.

VERS. 1.-- *If I speak with tongues,* etc. Here he proves that charity is the more excellent: because the others without it avail nothing, and it without them profits very much. *But charity.* Charity is the proper and singular fountain of good things, in which a stranger has no share. Which, like oil, cannot be pressed down to the bottom, but rises above; which, if it be lacking, the rest are had in vain: if it be present, all things are had? *As sounding brass,* etc. As brass resounds by some striking and not of itself, so he who speaks with tongues does so not of himself, who knows not what he speaks, but by the power of the Spirit brings forth things wherewith he may soothe his hearers.

[0543A]VERS. 2.-- *And if I should have prophecy.* As Balaam, and Caiaphas, and Saul. *I should know mysteries.* As Judas also among the apostles, and the devil, who (as Ezechiel says) knew the divine mysteries. *Knowledge.* As the scribes and the Pharisees, whence: *You have the key of knowledge, but neither do you enter in, nor do you suffer others to enter in [n: (Matth. XXIII)]*. [var: Vulg. Matth. XXIII, 13 *vos enim non intratis, nec introeuntes sinitis intrare*; the clause on the key of knowledge is Luc. XI, 52 *tulistis clavem scientiae*, "you have taken away the key of knowledge"] The wicked too assuredly have these things, who will also confess them in the judgment, where they will not dare to lie: to whom it will not be said, You have not had them, but *I have not known you:* because they had them without charity.

VERS. 4.-- *Charity is patient,* etc. These are the arms wherewith the soldier of Christ is armed. Of which armour he has set foremost, as the chief arms, patience and kindness.

[n: (ORIG.)] Patience, with an even mind, [0543B] bears the evils brought upon it by its neighbours, kindness lovingly lays out its own goods upon its neighbours. *It is not envious.* Because another's good fortune does not sadden it. *It is not puffed up.* Because its own good fortune does not lift it up.

VERS. 5.-- *It seeks not,* etc. It is no lover of money.

VERS. 7.-- *It believes all things.* He does not say, it believes all men, because it believes God alone. *It hopes all things.* So it appeared in the fathers, that is, in the people of Israel, who hoped to have what God was promising. *It endures all things.* In the head, that is, in Christ, who patiently awaited the glory of the resurrection and of the ascension.

VERS. 8.-- *Charity.* Again, in this it is the worthier, because it is ended neither in this world nor in the world to come, even though certain of its works cease. *It never falls away.* If by believing and [0543C] hoping we love what is not yet seen, how much more when it shall be seen?

VERS. 10.-- *That which is in part shall be done away.* It is the destruction of imperfection, when that which is imperfect is fulfilled in the whole.

VERS. 12.-- *Now.* The images of the truth are seen through faith. *But then face to face.* That is, the things themselves will be seen manifestly. *Then I shall know.* That is, I shall see the things promised, *as he himself is,* which is to be present with God, where Christ is. Or, as I have been foreknown to know that which now is secret, *A glass.* It is the soul: a glass by whose power we in some manner know God, but darkly. *An enigma.* Now an enigma is not every allegory, but an obscure one. Whence, as by the glass he signified an image, so by the name of enigma he understands a likeness, [0543D] albeit an obscure one and hard to perceive.

VERS. 13.-- *Faith, hope,* etc. Which knowledge and prophecy serve as soldiers, without which the life of no just man is perfect. *But the greater of these is charity.* Charity is that to which here faith and hope cannot be lacking, but faith and hope can be without charity.
