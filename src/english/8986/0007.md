## CHAPTER X.

VERS. 1.-- *But I myself, Paul.* He begins to treat of the uncorrected, who were following the false apostles, and thought the Apostle [0564D] fierce, as though he should say: The corrected I invite to almsgiving: *Paul,* truly *humble,* not proud, I myself who admonish others to almsgiving, the same to you as to them, I beseech, I say, etc.

VERS. 3.-- *We do not war according to the flesh.* For we do not exercise the warfare given us by God according to the flesh, that is, for earthly advantage.

VERS. 4.-- *Unto the destruction of fortifications.* That there may be destroyed the counsels fortified with the divers craftinesses of men or of demons. *Fortifications,* etc. Fortifications are the hearts of unbelievers, which the devil holds, and fortifies with divers errors, lest the word of God be able to enter in by faith, or by the likeness of fortifications. And to overcome these things let the arms of the Apostle's warfare avail, [0565A] and he shows the parts of that fortification, namely, the counsels.

VERS. 6.-- *And having in readiness.* Because nothing bites us back, whereby we should be the less bold. *To avenge,* etc. Then does a man avenge disobedience, when he condemns it by obedience. Then does he destroy it, when he leads those who resist to the faith. And this is a worthy vengeance, that perfidy be condemned by those by whom it was defended.

VERS. 7.-- *The things that are according to the face.* I have said many things by which I have shown you that you ought rather to follow us than the false apostles, as though he should say: If the things which I have said do not suffice you, why should you follow me rather than the false apostles? See these things which are according to the face, that is, they are as evident as those things which are set before the eyes.

[0565B]VERS. 8.-- *I shall not be ashamed.* Because it is both true, and not for my own glory, but this is said for your profit.

VERS. 9.-- *But that I may not be thought.* Here he says that, when he shall be present, he fulfils in deed what when absent he threatens in word. And he says this on the occasion of that sentence: *ready to avenge all disobedience:* that I may not, he says, be thought by you only to terrify with words, and not to fulfil in deed what I threaten with words, know that what I threaten with words I am about to fulfil in deeds.

VERS. 11.-- *Let him think this,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Lest anyone think that I threaten those things, etc., *as far as* and toward sinners I stand forth strong by severity.

*Such.* Because if we were to stroke your vices, then we should not build up, but destroy.

[0565C]VERS. 12.-- *For we dare not,* etc. Such shall we be in deed, because we are not like the false apostles, who usurp authority to themselves, and stroke vices. And this is: We do not insert ourselves among certain ones, that is, the false apostles who commend themselves, that is, who usurp authority. Which is, we do not put ourselves in the number of those men, that we should usurp authority, but we have received it from God. Or we do not compare ourselves to them, that, like them, we should flatter you and stroke your vices: which even one having authority might do.

VERS. 13.-- *But we,* etc. As those who by usurping glory beyond measure. *But according to the measure.* For at the beck of God he was admonished to preach the Gospel to the Corinthians. It had been apportioned to each one to whom he should be directed in his preaching, so that individuals might have [0565D] certain peoples of their own, of whose faith they might glory. But to the Apostle's rule all those belonged whom he had founded in the faith: he who had been sent by God to all.

VERS. 15.-- *Not in other men's labours.* To do this would be to glory beyond measure; but having hope in you: that is, to be magnified among others through you, and to preach the Gospel according to our rule, that is, according to what has been enjoined by God, not having hope to glory in another's rule.

VERS. 17.-- *But he that glorieth.* I will not glory in another's rule. And in like manner let him who glories, that is, who wishes to exercise authority, glory in the Lord, that is, in the measure given by God.

[0566A]VERS. 18.-- *But whom God.* Him God commends, and he is approved, whom he holds worthy, and whom he sends to preach his gift: whom he does not send, he does not commend, because unsent he preaches.
