## CHAPTER XII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *I know a man.* ⟦s2⟧ As if concerning another, both the thing is told, and boasting is avoided. ⟦s3⟧ *Whether in the body, or,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ This the Apostle did not know, whether, when he was caught up, the soul was in the body, or whether it went out of the body altogether, so that the body lay dead, or whether, according to some other manner of a living body wherein the soul was, his mind was taken up to see or to hear the unspeakable things of that vision. ⟦s5⟧ And because this [0568B] was uncertain, therefore perhaps he said, *whether in the body, or without. ⟦s6⟧ Caught up.* ⟦s7⟧ Lifted up against nature.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Even unto the third heaven.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, unto the knowledge of the Godhead. ⟦s3⟧ The first heaven is the airy; the second, the firmament; the third spiritual, where the angels and the holy souls enjoy the contemplation of God.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *He was caught up. [n: (THEOPH.)]* See his utmost lowliness and humility, how he recounts these things as though of another: for of such a one, he says, I will glory. ⟦s2⟧ But for whose sake (if it was another that was caught up) dost thou glory? ⟦s3⟧ And so he says these things of himself. ⟦s4⟧ *Secret words.* ⟦s5⟧ An intimation concerning the hidden essence of God, as it were through words.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Above that which,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ For if I should relate great things [0568C] and many, perhaps some would think that I am not weak, but an immortal man, or an angel, etc.; and therefore I speak sparingly of myself.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *And lest the greatness.* ⟦s2⟧ I forbear to glory much, lest I be thought to be other than I am. ⟦s3⟧ And therefore a sting was given, pricking the flesh. ⟦s4⟧ Who? ⟦s4⟧ A malignant angel sent by Satan, that he might buffet him, that is, might repress every motion of pride by striking in tribulations, or by tempting (as some say) through lust. ⟦s5⟧ But a temptation to which consent is not given is not a sin, but matter for the exercise of virtue.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *He said to me.* ⟦s2⟧ By inspiration, or by an angel. ⟦s3⟧ *For power is made perfect in infirmity.* ⟦s4⟧ That is the perfection of virtues which has a contrary infirmity, with which it may lawfully strive: and therefore I will glory [0568D] in infirmities, not unwillingly, but gladly.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *I am become foolish.* ⟦s2⟧ In all these commendations I seem foolish, but by your fault, who ought to have commended me against the false apostles.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *In all patience.* ⟦s2⟧ He mentions patience first, which pertains to morals: because he long bore with them, as with impatient sick men, that, the power of signs being applied as a medicine, he might heal their wounds. ⟦s3⟧ *And mighty deeds.* ⟦s4⟧ A mighty deed is the genus of signs and wonders, that is, every miracle. ⟦s5⟧ A sign is that which at any time whatsoever signifies something, and is the genus of a wonder. ⟦s6⟧ A wonder, that which is in the future. ⟦s7⟧ Or a sign and a wonder are in those things which are against nature, a mighty deed in those things which are not against nature, [0569A] as to free a man from disease by the imposition of a hand and by prayer.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *For neither ought the children,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Carnal fathers gather for their children, but it is meet that spiritual fathers should receive their charges from their children. ⟦s3⟧ But here he proves that he is unwilling to receive, to such a degree that he transfers the case of the carnal father to the spiritual: and he says that not only does he spend himself for their salvation, but that he is ready even to die.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *And I will be spent over and above,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ This indeed is perfect charity, that one should be ready to die even for the brethren. ⟦s3⟧ But is it so as soon as it is born? ⟦s4⟧ When it has been born, it is nourished: when it has been nourished, it is strengthened: when it has been strengthened, it is perfected: when it has come to perfection, it says: *I desire to be dissolved, and to be with Christ.*

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0569B]VERS. 19.-- *Of old you think.* ⟦s2⟧ As if to say: Perhaps you have long thought: that I say for the sake of excuse, as one guilty, that I did not burden you, but I say (God being witness, who knows) that I did not circumvent you, and not proudly, but in the magnifying of Christ in you.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Lest again when I come,* etc. [n: (CHRYS.)] It is glory to punish, to upbraid, to exact penalties, etc., *as far as* but because of his commandment.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And have not done penance.* ⟦s2⟧ It does not suffice to change one's manners for the better and to depart from evils, unless satisfaction be made to the Lord for the things which have been done, by the sorrow of penance, by the groaning of humility, by the sacrifice of a contrite heart, alms co-operating.

## CHAPTER XIII.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0569C]VERS. 2.-- *I will not spare.* ⟦s2⟧ After so many corrections, if any do not amend themselves, they ought not to be spared.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Or a proof.* ⟦s2⟧ It is the custom of divine Scripture to attribute to the person of God what he does in us, as the Apostle attributed the very speaking to him by whose gift he was speaking, that is, to Christ. ⟦s3⟧ *Who in you is not weakened,* but appeared powerful when he gave gifts. ⟦s4⟧ Which things having experienced, let them not doubt that they can be punished by him.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *For we also are weak.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ lives, who before had been weak. ⟦s3⟧ *For we also,* much less than he, although *weak in him,* that is, although in imitation of him we suffer many things from the faithless, *yet we shall live,* that is, we shall have power [0570A] through him in judging you. ⟦s4⟧ Or, *we shall live with him,* that is, in a like blessedness with him: and this out of *the power of God;* which is such *in you,* that is, in your consciences: *In you.* ⟦s5⟧ Toward you. ⟦s6⟧ The death inflicted by the faithless is life toward believers, because rising again by the power of God they shall live with Christ.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Now we pray,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ This the Apostle prays, that, these men doing well, he may not find in them things to be corrected, and that so the apostles may appear reprobates: for they are seen to be approved while they judge sinners. ⟦s3⟧ So then, if there be none whom he may judge, authority ceasing, they seem as it were reprobate. ⟦s4⟧ *We may be reprobates,* that is, we may appear reprobate, not having anything to avenge or to judge.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *For we rejoice,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] We rejoice if it be not necessary that our power should appear, [0570B] as Peter's appeared in Saphira and Ananias.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *According to the power.* ⟦s2⟧ Again he shows that he does not desire to abuse his power upon them, adding that he received it not unto destruction but unto edification, whence he subjoins: *Not unto destruction.* ⟦s3⟧ But they are not destroyed who are corrected, but being corrected they are built up.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Rejoice, brethren.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, work well, that you may be able to have true joy. ⟦s3+4⟧ *And the God of peace shall be with you.* [var: Vulg. 2Cor 13:11 *et Deus pacis et dilectionis erit vobiscum*, "and the God of peace and of love shall be with you"; Migne's lemma lacks *et dilectionis*, and yet the gloss beneath expounds love by name] Hence there shall be to you peace from his love, hence the taking away of all evils. ⟦s5⟧ This peace has saved the world, has dissolved a long war, has joined earth to heaven, this has made men angels. ⟦s6⟧ Let him therefore imitate this peace. ⟦s7⟧ For love is the mother of innumerable goods, through this we have been saved, through [0570C] this we attain all unspeakable goods.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Grace.* ⟦s2⟧ He desires that the whole Trinity may bless them. ⟦s3⟧ He assigns to each his own fitting things. ⟦s4⟧ Grace, that is, remission, to the Son, charity to the Father, who out of love sent the Son, communication to the Spirit, through whom union is made. ⟦s5⟧ *And the charity of God.* ⟦s6⟧ The love of God sent Christ, whose grace saves. ⟦s7⟧ And that we may possess this grace of salvation, may the communication of the deed of the Holy Spirit, or the Spirit himself, be so with you, that he may communicate and confer upon you both the grace of Christ and the charity of God. ⟦s8⟧ As if: May the whole Trinity be with you, giving grace for the things committed and charity through the Spirit.
