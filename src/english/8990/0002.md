## CHAPTER III.

VERS. 1.-- *The things that are above.* Not by place, but by merit of excellence.

VERS. 2.-- *The things above.* Spiritual things, which were signified through the carnal observances, these are eternal glory and the things which lead to it.

VERS. 3.-- *Your life hidden.* That is, your time has not yet come, because the summer has not yet come, that is, the coming of Christ, where the fruitful trees shall be discerned from the barren; but the time of those who love earthly things is always ready. But when Christ shall have appeared, this is our time.

VERS. 5.-- *Mortify,* etc. After the reasonings concerning the fulness of [0614B] Christ, a general exhortation to all concerning morals is subjoined, lest they return to their former habit. *Your members.* That is, the parts of the old man, or the concupiscences of the members. *Fornication,* etc. He describes the offences which he calls members, that after receiving the reason of truth they might give their labour to a good life. It much harms to know the reason of the true, and not to carry it out in deeds. *For he who knows the will of the Lord, and does not do it, shall be beaten with many stripes [n: (Luc. XII)]*. [var: Vulg. Luke 12:47 *Ille autem servus qui cognovit voluntatem domini sui, et non praeparavit, et non fecit secundum voluntatem ejus, vapulabit multis*; the Vulgate has no *plagis*] *And covetousness.* Under one name he forbids two most evil things, idolatry and covetousness, which is not a dissimilar wickedness, because it is the root of all evils, and they are fulfilled in almost one work: because, as idolatry strives to take glory away from the one God, lest he alone should have the name of deity, so the covetous man extends himself into the things of God, that he alone may usurp [0614C] what God made for all: both are hostile to God, because they deny to God the things which are his.

VERS. 7.-- *In which you also walked.* He mentions [sic: *me morat*] this, that they may rejoice that they have escaped, and may beware for the rest.

VERS. 8.-- *But now.* Above he forbade the graver things, now the lesser, lest these be thought not dangerous. There are those who have not the habit of drunkenness, who sometimes get drunk: as also there are those who have not the habit of lying, and yet sometimes lie, so it happens in other matters also. He forbids therefore to lie sometimes, and especially to lie continually.

VERS. 10.-- *Who is renewed.* The renewal of the mind is said to be according to God, as is read in the epistle to the Ephesians, or *according to the image of God,* [var: Vulg. Col. 3:10 *secundum imaginem ejus qui creavit illum*, "according to the image of him that created him"] as is read here. But it is said according to God for this reason, lest it be thought to come about according to some other [0614D] creature.

VERS. 14.-- *Charity.* For it is itself the more eminent way, which is above knowledge and all precepts. This is the seamless garment of the Lord, woven from above. Seamless, lest at any time it be unsewn, which reaches to one, because charity gathers all into one.

VERS. 15.-- *Peace.* This is necessary to the unity of the body, which it has itself made. *The peace of God.* [var: Vulg. Col. 3:15 *pax Christi*, "the peace of Christ"] There can be called a peace which has not charity; but charity always has peace with itself.

VERS. 16.-- *Teaching and admonishing.* He says this, that there may be teaching for those who know not, and admonition for those who know.

[0615A]VERS. 17.-- *All whatsoever.* Because above he commanded God to be praised in the heart, now he exhorts that the name of God be praised and exalted both in words and in deeds: so that our words and works may thus lack reproof, that the Lord may be magnified, by whose teaching the disciples are unblameable. *All things.* Not only what you do in word or in deed, but all things, even thoughts, and if there be any others. Or, whatever you do, do the whole, that is, the perfect, by attributing it to God, and this is, doing in the name. *Giving thanks.* Then do we truly give thanks to God through Christ, if we keep the measure of his precepts.

VERS. 21.-- *Fathers.* He commands fathers to be moderate toward their sons, lest the sons, hemmed in by them, transgress against them, [0615B] and offend God. For anger is an inconsiderate thing, so that sometimes he who is angry spares not even himself.

VERS. 23.-- *Work from the heart as to the Lord.* God approves that innocence by which a man becomes innocent not through fear of punishment, but through love of justice: for he who, through fear, does not sin, although he does not harm others, yet harms himself very greatly. Feigned equity is not [0616A] equity, but a doubled sin. In which there is iniquity and dissimulation.

## CHAPTER IV.

VERS. 3.-- *A door.* That is, may he make my mouth open: which was given for this, that the word might go forth from it. It is open, when it speaks many and great things.

VERS. 5.-- *Redeeming the time.* That is, preparing, by some means, the opportunity of preaching to them.

VERS. 8.-- *That he may know the things which concern you and may comfort your hearts,* etc. He who sees himself known is more on his guard than he who sees himself ignored.

VERS. 10.-- *You have received,* because he has returned to me, and therefore if he shall come.

[0616B]VERS. 16.-- *Cause that it also in,* etc. From this place we understand that the Apostle's instructions are general, and that the epistles were written for the profit of all men.

VERS. 18.-- *Be mindful,* etc. Before he sets down the salutation, he prefixes an exhortation, saying: *Be mindful,* etc.
