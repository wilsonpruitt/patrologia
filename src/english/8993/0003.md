## CHAPTER III.

VERS. 1.-- *Wherefore, brethren.* He infers it from all that has gone before; as if: Because he is mighty, because he suffered for us, and he is able to help.

VERS. 2.-- *Who is faithful.* He begins to compare Christ with Moses, as above with the prophets and the angels, so that he is faithful above Moses, because he sought not his own, but the Father's glory, [0648D] nor did he hide his commandments. Which Christ is faithful to the Father (as Moses was) in all the house of the Jews and of the Gentiles.

VERS. 3.-- *Of ampler glory.* As if: That he was faithful, the outcome itself declares: because he is worthy of glory, as Moses was: and indeed of an ampler glory above Moses, that is, than Moses, or being above Moses in deserving.

VERS. 4.-- *For every house,* etc. He built it, I say, for it cannot come to be of itself, but by some one. Was it then by Christ? Truly, because it is God who makes the house, and all things. *But he that created all things,* the house also. *Is God.* For of both houses, both of that which Moses ruled and of that which Christ raised up, God is the maker.

[0649A]VERS. 5.-- *And Moses.* He shows Christ worthy of ampler glory than Moses, because Christ is the builder of the house, but not Moses. Here he shows another reason also, namely that Christ is the son, Moses a servant, who also delivered carnal things to carnal men, but Christ spiritual things.

VERS. 7.-- *Wherefore.* When above he had commended Christ in many things above the angels and the prophets, he subjoined that we ought to observe the things we have heard from him; so now also, he himself being commended above Moses, he begins to terrify them, lest they be unbelieving toward Christ, by the likeness of the fathers; and lest they lose the rest, he coaxes them, by the opportunity of the time. *To day.* Now he speaks through himself, who before spoke through heralds: if then you were hard, now be soft.

[0649B]VERS. 8.-- *Harden not your hearts;* that is, be not rebellious and stubborn toward God, but obedient.

VERS. 9.-- *They proved;* that is, they searched out for curiosity's sake.

VERS. 10.-- *Offended.* [var: Vulg. Heb 3:10 *infensus*, "enraged"] Exceedingly and implacably angered. *Enraged.* Or *near at hand,* by applying the scourges of correction.

VERS. 11.-- *In my wrath.* God is said to grow angry by the figure anthropopathos. *If they shall enter into my rest.* It is an aposiopesis, that is, they shall not enter.

VERS. 12.-- *An evil heart of unbelief.* That you should think Christ does not suffice without the law, which is evil, because thus man rushes into many sins. Nor is this a light thing, because this is a heart of departing from the living God.

[0649C]VERS. 13.-- *Whilst to day;* that is, the time of grace, and that of which the prophet spoke above in the psalms, because grace unto salvation is now bestowed on believers no less than when Christ himself was bodily present. *By the deceitfulness of sin,* that is, of the devil, who is the cause of sin.

VERS. 14.-- *For we are made partakers of Christ.* You ought to exhort one another, and not to be hard, because what we were not, we have been made by grace, having part with Christ in the inheritance: yet on this condition, *if the beginning of his substance,* that is, of faith, which is the beginning of good things, through which God exists, in us, and through which we are made gods and partake of the divine substance, *we keep firm unto the end.*

VERS. 16.-- *For some who heard.* He says this for this reason, lest anyone think it enough to hear of the rest, because [0649D] they also all heard, but not all heard, but not all arrived.
