## THE ARGUMENT.

[0601A]

The Philippians are Macedonians. These, having received the word of truth, persevered in the faith, and did not receive false apostles. This the Apostle commends, writing to them from Rome, out of prison, by Epaphroditus.

## CHAPTER ONE.

VERS. 1.-- *Paul.* After his wonted manner he sets the salutation first; [0601B] then he gives thanks to God for them, imploring for them greater goods, that growing also in virtues they may come to perfection. Then he admonishes them to patience in tribulations by the example of himself and of Christ, afterwards, that they beware of the wiles of the false apostles; at last he inserts a moral admonition, and near the end he says that he rejoices over the favour which they had sent him to Rome by Epaphroditus. *Timothy.* He puts him beside himself, because he was about to send him to them: on this account commending him in a certain measure, that they may receive from him the same things which he writes. *Servants.* He said "servants," that they too may bear hard things for the Lord. *Of Christ.* That they may stand against the false ones.

VERS. 3.-- *I give thanks to my God.* As if to say: Do the things which you do, because they are such that from them I praise God.

[0601C]VERS. 5.-- *Communication.* Through which you made your own things common, in the exalting of the Gospel.

VERS. 6.-- *Being confident.* As if: I beseech that you stand fast, and I trust that this very thing which I pray is done by you, and not another; but see that I trust not in vain.

*He will perfect.* He himself, beginning, works that we may will, who, perfecting, cooperates with those who will.

VERS. 7.-- *As it is meet for me.* So do I give thanks, beseech and trust, *as it is just to think,* that is, so to will. And he sets forth the cause, *for that I have you in my heart,* I desire good for you, whether I be in bonds, knowing that you also are bound, or whether I be defending the Gospel, which you also do.

VERS. 8.-- *I long after you all,* etc. That is, with the inmost [0601D] love of Christ, that you may be perfectly loved by him, or that you may love God and your neighbours with that affection wherewith Christ [loved], who laid down his soul. Or this is referred to that which he said above, that he desired them to be partakers of his joy. And he determines in what manner, namely by the love of Christ, not by human love, that he may have them as sharers in God.

VERS. 10.-- *That you may approve the better things.* That is, that your charity may grow in knowledge, so that you may know how to approve and to discern not only what things are evil and good, but even among good things which are the better, that after the proving *you may be sincere* in yourselves, without works of corruption, and, as regards your neighbour, *without offence.* [var: Vulg. *sine offensa*, "without offence", the other noun-form]

VERS. 11.-- *Filled with fruit,* etc. That is, with the reward of righteousness, so far that you may be the glory and praise of God, so that it may be said on your behalf. *God is wonderful in his saints [n: (Psal. LXVII)]*.

[0602A]VERS. 12.-- *But I wish to know,* etc. [var: Vulg. *Scire autem vos volo*, "I wish you to know"] Thus far he has spoken in general, thence he sets down in particular the things which may persuade to endurance, namely, *For you I give thanks: I am confident. But I wish you to know,* etc., as if to say: Since you are thus perfect, persevere and beware, lest the tribulations at hand diminish this your perfection, but look upon my example.

[0602B]VERS. 13.-- *That my bonds,* etc. For because I patiently bear whatsoever evils, it is plain that it is not a crime of falsehood which a just vengeance strikes in me, but a certain power of truth which the fortitude of enduring magnifies.

VERS. 14.-- *They would dare,* etc. For his constancy so animated many that they did not fear to preach the Gospel.

VERS. 17.-- *But some,* etc. Convicted in contention by me, they preach according to that which I convicted them they ought to preach. *Of contention.* Out of private hatred, because at some time they disputed with me and were confuted. And lest they should seem corrected, he adds: *Not with a pure mind.* [var: Vulg. *non sincere*, "not sincerely"]

VERS. 18.-- *For what?* He does not forbid the hirelings, but permits them, because they too are useful for some things. [0602C] *Yea, and I will rejoice.* Therefore I rejoice over my own evils, or over the advancement of the Gospel, because thence is salvation to me.

VERS. 21.-- *But to me to live is Christ,* etc. [var: Vulg. *Mihi enim vivere Christus est*, "For to me to live is Christ"] As if he would say: Deservedly do I magnify Christ, whether by life or by death, because *Christ is to me to live,* that is, he is life to me here through faith and hope, and in the future through sight. For this life he delivered up his mortal body, that he may receive that same body immortal for ever, whence he also subjoins, and to die is gain.

VERS. 22.-- *But if to live,* etc. Christ is to me to live, that I may magnify him. And if that is so, then I know not which I should choose, to die or to live. As if: Unless this fruit were, there would be no doubt but that I would choose to die, that I might have Christ for my reward: and therefore, do not doubt to die for Christ.

[0602D]VERS. 24.-- *But to abide,* etc. Behold, he chooses not what is better for himself, but what is expedient for many, on account of charity, which is to be chosen before all things.

VERS. 25.-- *And the joy of faith,* etc. That is: That we may rejoice over your faith, or that you may rejoice over the faith, advancing in it. Which is not save where there is a clean conscience.

VERS. 28.-- *And this from God,* etc. Even that which you suffer is not from you, but from God: because, for the merit of Christ who obeyed the Father, this among the other graces has been given to you.

VERS. 29.-- *Not only,* etc. Both therefore pertain to the grace of God, both the faith of them that believe and the endurance of them that suffer, because he said that both were given.

VERS. 30.-- *Such as you have both seen in me.* When I was scourged naked [0603A] on account of the pythoness woman out of whom I shut out the malignant spirit, who furnished great gain to her masters.
