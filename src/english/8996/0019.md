## CHAPTER XV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *To be of one mind.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: Unless ye be of the same mind, ye will not be able to honor God.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0516D]VERS. 8.-- *Of the circumcision.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, of the Jews, to whom he came bodily; whence: *I am not sent but to the sheep that are lost of the house of Israel [n: (Matth. XV)]*. ⟦s3⟧ *For the truth.* ⟦s4⟧ He did not cast off the Jews, to whom truth belongs properly, and mercy to the gentiles, to whom no promise had been made. ⟦s5⟧ *The promises.* ⟦s6⟧ For he had promised to Abraham: *In thy seed shall all nations be blessed [n: (Gen. XXII)]*. ⟦s7⟧ And to David: *Of the fruit of thy womb I will set upon thy throne [n: (Psalm. CXXXI)]*. ⟦s8⟧ And likewise Jacob said: *There shall rise a star out of Jacob [n: (Num. XXIV)]*.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *I will confess.* ⟦s2⟧ But confession is not only of sins, but also of praise.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *There shall be a root of Jesse.* ⟦s2⟧ And of this root shall be he that shall rise up, etc. ⟦s3⟧ Jesse is the root, David the tree, [0517A] which by the branch, that is, Mary, brought forth the fruit, that is, Christ.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *And with peace.* ⟦s2⟧ That ye may be able to rejoice, may he give peace and concord toward one another; and this will be, if the faith be sound. ⟦s3⟧ *That ye may abound.* ⟦s4⟧ That is, that by having these things ye may be the more certain of eternal blessedness, and may abound in the virtue of the Holy Ghost, that is, in the fortitude of good working, which is given through the Holy Ghost.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *I am sure.* ⟦s2⟧ Lest the Apostle should seem to understand them all to be discordant and unwise for correcting, he removes this, in that he begins for this end, that he may admonish the more perfect concerning the correction of the lesser; and thence he sets himself before them as an example, who labors for others, and there is need that these should do this, since he himself, hindered by other things, cannot come to them. ⟦s3⟧ *Filled with all wisdom.* ⟦s4⟧ By this praise he provokes them [0517B] to better things, after the manner of one exhorting. ⟦s5⟧ Whence he does not say to teach, but to admonish; as if he should say, that which is known, but escapes the mind. ⟦s6⟧ For that is wont to be brought to mind which, although it is known, sometimes escapes the mind, or is held negligently.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Because of the grace.* ⟦s2⟧ He does not write rashly; but as the apostle of the gentiles he dares to write to all the nations by the authority delivered to him through grace.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *I have therefore.* ⟦s2⟧ So also ye, if ye labor for the brethren. ⟦s3⟧ He says this for this reason, that he may prove that he can do nothing less than the apostles who had been with Christ.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Not where he was named,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because he knew that false apostles and others taught false things under the name of Christ, which afterward it was a labor of the greatest kind to correct; therefore he was careful to go before them, and to deliver the truth whole, where Christ had not yet been announced; which [0517C] he proves by the testimony of the prophet.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *And they that have not heard,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That the understanding of the word of God might be true and inviolable, he hastened to imbue the nations with the truth.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *For they have approved.* ⟦s2⟧ To works of this kind he invites the Romans, because he who lives by the mercy of God ought to be dutiful toward his fellow-brother. ⟦s3⟧ *Toward the poor.* ⟦s4⟧ These gave themselves wholly to the divine services, caring for nothing worldly, they gave to the faithful an example of good conversation.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *That ye help.* ⟦s2⟧ Many least ones, while they are gathered together of one mind, become great, and it is impossible that the prayers of many should not obtain. ⟦s3⟧ If therefore they desire to see him, let them pray that they may receive him delivered, with the joy of charity; whence there follows *that I may be delivered.*

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0517D]VERS. 31.-- *Acceptable.* ⟦s2⟧ That they, understanding his charity toward them, may of one mind with him give thanks to God. ⟦s3⟧ For it is a great advancement to him by whose ministry many, being made glad, praise God.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *Now the God of peace.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ, who says: *My peace I give unto you, and my peace I leave unto you [n: (Joan. XIV)]*; who is with his own even to the consummation of the world, so may he be with you also; for he it is who says: *I am with you all days, even to the consummation of the world [n: (Matth. XXVIII)]*.

## CHAPTER XVI.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And I commend unto you.* ⟦s2⟧ Henceforth the Apostle sets before them many just men, whom they ought to believe [0518A] at Rome, as if he should say: Do ye so as I have said, and imitate these. ⟦s3⟧ *Phoebe.* ⟦s4⟧ Namely, Phoebe was a most wealthy and most noble woman, who sustained by her own substance the Church in the place which is called Cenchris. ⟦s5⟧ And she at that time set out for Rome upon some business, by whom perhaps he sent this Epistle.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *That ye receive her worthily.* ⟦s2⟧ Or, as other codices have it, very becomingly and honorably.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Prisca and Aquila.* ⟦s2⟧ Aquila is the husband of Priscilla: these fellow-helpers of Paul had come to Rome for the confirming of the Romans. ⟦s3⟧ So also all the others whom he salutes are understood to have been at Rome.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *The churches of the gentiles.* ⟦s2⟧ Because these labor for the advancement of the gentiles, exhorting to the faith of Christ, who also did not refuse to suffer perils for the Apostle; [0518B] whence the Romans too ought to obey them.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Apelles.* ⟦s2⟧ Through temptations he was found faithful in Christ: even if not his friend, or a partaker of his work.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Of Aristobulus.* ⟦s2⟧ This Aristobulus was wont to gather the brethren in Christ, whom the Apostle salutes, thus far approving his deed. ⟦s3⟧ *Of the household of Narcissus.* ⟦s4⟧ Narcissus is said to have been a priest, as is read in other codices, who by traveling confirmed the holy brethren; and because he was not then present, he salutes his people; and because he did not know the merits of them all, he distinguishes, subjoining: *who are in the Lord,* that is, those whom ye shall know to be worthy.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *She hath labored.* ⟦s2⟧ This labor, of which the Apostle speaks more frequently, is in exhortation and in the ministry of the saints, [0518C] and in affliction and in want for God's sake.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *And mine.* ⟦s2⟧ For her sanctity he calls her his own mother, whose son he set over an ecclesiastical office.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *And all.* ⟦s2⟧ All, to whom he writes and whom he names, he bids to salute one another in a holy kiss, that is, in the peace of Christ, that the kisses may be religious, not carnal.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Of Christ.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, those that trust in Christ, not in any other thing.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *That ye mark them.* ⟦s2⟧ He touches the false apostles, whom throughout the whole Epistle he warns are to be avoided.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *For your.* ⟦s2⟧ Therefore I admonish you to avoid them, because your faith and obedience is praised everywhere: [0518D] because ye are in the head of the world, and so by your example others might already be corrupted. ⟦s3⟧ Or, I ask you to avoid them, because ye have readily obeyed, which is good, and thereat I rejoice; but I wish you to be wise in discerning the good, and without any share of evil.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *And the God.* ⟦s2⟧ Be ye wise: but may God *crush Satan,* that is, false preachers, or any man whatsoever opposing you, or the devil. ⟦s3⟧ *Under the feet.* ⟦s4⟧ That ye may despise the head, that is, the first motions of suggestions, and if it is the devil, that ye may crush his head, as was said to Eve in the figure of the Church, or of sensuality. ⟦s5⟧ *Grace.* ⟦s6⟧ The grace which he promised at his coming, he now wishes for them.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *There salute you.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: So many and so great men [0519A] rejoice together at what ye have begun, and therefore it becomes you to persevere.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *And the whole.* ⟦s2⟧ Above he had said all the Churches; but here he says the Church of another province, whence Caius was.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Now to him.* ⟦s2⟧ Thus do I admonish you. ⟦s3⟧ But to him who is able to confirm you be glory, from whom alone, being good, are all good things; wherein the sum of the epistle is noted, because here the Apostle principally purposes to admonish them that they attribute all things to the grace of God.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *According to the revelation.* ⟦s2⟧ By *mystery* he means the incarnation of Christ, etc., which things were hidden from eternal times: because although in some part they had been known to the ancient fathers, [0519B] yet by none were they fully [0520A] known, until in their own time they were revealed through Christ himself.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *By the Scriptures of the prophets.* ⟦s2⟧ Unlocked by him, giving testimony to this preaching. ⟦s3⟧ *Made manifest* among all nations unto obedience, that is, that they may obey the faith by working well. ⟦s4⟧ *Kept silent* to others, but known to God alone: because although it be now revealed to men, yet it is known to God alone, because he alone knows why it was so done. ⟦s5⟧ Wherein he restrains the inquirers who ask: why has God deferred so long? why has he permitted so many nations to perish.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *To whom be honor. [n: (AUG.)]* That which is added: To whom be glory, etc., *as far as* because through him God the Trinity became known [0520B] to men, which is the mystery.
