## CHAPTER IX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And passing by.* ⟦s2⟧ Not standing in the way of sinners, but beside the way of our mortality. ⟦s3⟧ *A blind man.* ⟦s4⟧ The blind man signifies the human race, in which blindness is [0394D] natural, because, when the first man sinned, the fault grew ingrained by reason of nature; whence according to the mind every man is born blind.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Neither hath this man sinned.* ⟦s2⟧ They did indeed sin, but it was not brought about by that sin that he should be born blind; rather this is the cause, *that the works of God should be made manifest in him.* ⟦s3⟧ That, by enlightening him and by signifying through him what he is to do in the blindness of the human race, the Son of God may be made manifest; whence he adds: *I must work.*

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *As long as I am in the world.* ⟦s2⟧ Not only then was Christ the light in the world, but he is also always with the elect through faith unto the consummation of the age, and it is the time for working. ⟦s3⟧ That night is the infernal night, [0395A] in which it is permitted to none to work, as neither was it permitted to the burning rich man; but there is only a time of receiving.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And he made clay.* ⟦s2⟧ As he formed the first man out of the slime of the earth, so through the same kind of clay he re-formed the human race. ⟦s3⟧ Of spittle he made clay, because the Word was made flesh. ⟦s4⟧ The spittle is the wisdom which came forth from the mouth of the Most High. ⟦s5⟧ The earth is the flesh of Christ. ⟦s6⟧ *He anointed the eyes.* ⟦s7⟧ That is, he made him a catechumen, who believes the Word made flesh. ⟦s8⟧ The anointed man does not yet see, but is sent to wash, that is, to be baptized in the Sent One, that is, in Christ, on account of which the name of the pool is so interpreted. ⟦s9⟧ Baptized, baptized from his sins, he received the light.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *And he came seeing,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ It is asked: how did he heal this man without faith, when it is read that he healed no one outwardly without an inward healing? ⟦s3⟧ But this is said [0395B] of those who were sick on account of their sins: this man was languishing not on account of sin, but for the glory of God.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *This man is not of God,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He keeps the sabbath spiritually who is without sin; not they, who are servants of sin.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *What sayest thou,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ They seek a pretext for accusation, that he might be driven out of the synagogue, but be found by Christ. ⟦s3⟧ *But he said,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ Still anointed in heart: he confesses, yet he does not lie.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Until they called,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Mystically: the parents, that is the devil or the vices, who say. ⟦s3⟧ He is of age, let him speak for himself: they who are unwilling that a man confess Christ.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Give glory to God: We know,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ To give glory to God is to speak the truth about someone as though God were present, [0395C] that is: to confess that this man is not God, as we do, but a sinner.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *Why again,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] He is vexed at their hardness, having from a blind man been made one that sees, and now not bearing with the blind.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *They reviled him therefore.* ⟦s2⟧ According to their heart it is a curse. ⟦s3⟧ But what is said is to be desired by all, that they should be disciples of Christ.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *But this man we know not.* ⟦s2⟧ Above they had said: *This man we know:* whence here they say that they know not; but we say that we know not the things which we cast away. ⟦s3⟧ He is not known, he lies hid by humility, he is betrayed by the power of his signs: it is not known whence he is, and as God he enlightens the blind.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *Unless this man were of God,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Their anger despised, he freely confesses the truth. ⟦s3⟧ For these things would not be done [0395D] save by the Lord, nor by the disciples, unless God were in them.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *He was born wholly in sins.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, with closed eyes, which they say to be for the sins of the parents; but Christ heals the whole: outside, the eyes; within, he opens the heart. ⟦s3⟧ *And dost thou teach us? ⟦s4⟧ And they cast him out.* ⟦s5⟧ By questioning him so often that they might learn, they had made him a master; but ungrateful, they cast out the one who taught. ⟦s6⟧ Yet because he was expelled, he was the more made a Christian.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *Who is he,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Still anointed, by these words he shows that he had long had a heart ready to believe, but had not known in whom he ought to believe.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *And thou hast seen.* ⟦s2⟧ Now the Lord washes the face [0396A] of the anointed, now he enlightens the eyes of the heart, and he confesses not only the Son of man but also the Son of God.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 39.-- *That they who see not.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, they who think that they see seek to see; and they who see, that is, they who think that they see and do not seek the physician; and this is the judgment, that is, the discernment which distinguishes the cause of believers from the proud. ⟦s3⟧ For he will execute the judgment of damnation not now, but at the end; whence: *I judge not any man.*
