## CHAPTER XIV.

[0407B]

VERS. 1.-- *Let not your heart be troubled,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] But lest they should fear his death as a man's, and on that account be troubled, etc., *as far as* one glory of the moon, another glory of the stars.

VERS. 2.-- *In the house,* etc. The house of God, the temple of God, the kingdom of God. The kingdom of heaven is just men, among whom there are many differences one from another. And these are the mansions of that same house.

[n: (AUG.)] Just men are now the kingdom of God and the kingdom of heaven, etc., *as far as* that what you were in hope, you may be in reality.

*If otherwise,* etc. That is, if I had not predestined a mansion, which is unfitting as regards God, who predestined all good things before all times, then I should only now be preparing, that is, predestining, [0407C] which again is unfitting, as regards God's predestining anything in the present.

[n: (ID.)] He confirms that they are, because if they were not he would have said, I will go and will prepare, etc., *as far as* and this is the multitude of mansions.

VERS. 3.-- *And if I shall go. [n: (ID.)]* He went away by hiding, he came by appearing, but he remains by governing, that we may make progress by living well, and thus a place is prepared.

[n: (ID.)] The faith which does not see believes, for if it saw, etc., *as far as* the desire of love is the preparation of a mansion.

VERS. 4.-- *And whither I go.* This was said with respect to certain ones who knew both the Son and the Father. But Thomas, although he supposed himself not to know, is convicted by this, that he knew him, and knew both the way and the life, [0407D] that is, the Son and the Father. Of these who knew, it is subjoined: From henceforth you shall know him, known through me, and you have seen him in the heart, while you have seen me like to him in all things.

VERS. 5.-- *Thomas saith to him,* etc. He who does not lie said that they knew whither and by what way one goes; but Thomas says that they know neither, not knowing that they know; yet Christ convicts them of knowing, because if they knew him, and he is the way, they knew the way; and if they knew him, and he is the truth and the life, then they knew the truth and the life to which he goes: he therefore goes by himself as the way, to himself as truth and life, and to the Father, because he is one with the Father.

VERS. 7.-- *If you had known me.* Because he had said that none [0408A] comes to the Father except through himself, who is inseparably the same with the Father, lest they should ask who the Father is, he shows that from himself, once known, the Father is known, which certain ones had not yet known, whom he reproves, saying: *If you had known,* etc. *And you have seen him.* As it is said of two things wholly alike: If you have seen this one, you have seen that one; they had therefore seen the Son as most like [him], but they had to be admonished to understand the Father also to be such a one, and not unlike.

VERS. 9.-- *How sayest thou.* And that, when he is wholly like. *How sayest thou,* etc. As if he should say: I see with what mind thou sayest it; thou dost not seek another like me, but thou thinkest [him] better. *Of myself.* Because I who speak am not of myself: he attributes to him what he does, of whom is he himself who does it.

[0408B]VERS. 12.-- *He that believeth in me.* I and the Father work without difference. And whosoever believes in me (lest this should be taken of the apostles alone), the works that I do through myself, he also shall do, that is, I do in him or through him, and even greater ones. *The works that I do.* Not however of himself, but of me. *The servant is not greater than his lord.* For he himself [does] many things without them, they without him nothing, save sin.

*Greater than these. [n: (AUG.)]* Because if he himself [healed] by the hem of his garment, Peter healed by a shadow. Or his work, etc., *as far as* because if both are of equal power, this is of greater mercy.

VERS. 15.-- *If you love me.* How does he admonish them to love and to keep the commandments, that they may receive the Spirit, whom unless they have they cannot love and keep? But [0408C] what they had in less measure, secretly, they were to receive more abundantly, openly, so that what they had might become known to them.

VERS. 16.-- *And I will ask the Father. [n: (THEOPH.)]* I will ask, that is, I will beseech the Father, and he will send you another Paraclete, etc., *as far as* that the Paraclete Spirit and his condescending grace may wholly come upon them, he says to them: *I will ask the Father.*

VERS. 17.-- *Because it seeth him not,* etc. The lovers of the world have not spiritual eyes wherewith they might see the Holy Spirit. Nor indeed can they see him with carnal eyes, because the Holy Spirit did not appear clothed in humanity. *And in you.* He shall be in you; he shall not abide in order to be, because to be somewhere is prior to abiding. Let no one therefore think that he so abides as a guest visibly with another; but in them he can be known, not by others.

[0408D]VERS. 18.-- *I will not leave.* The Spirit shall not be given in my stead in such a way that I too am not with you. Orphani is Greek, pupilli is Latin.

VERS. 19.-- *Yet a little while, and the world.* But the world, which now sees with the eyes of the flesh, after a little while shall not see, because when raised again he did not show himself to them; but the whole time is little, and when it is ended the impious man shall be taken away, that he may not see the glory of God, which the just shall see. *Because I live, and you shall live.* He uses the present, because his resurrection is soon to be. Of those he uses the future, whose resurrection is to be at the end.

VERS. 20.-- *And you in me, and I in you.* This is so now, and is known after a fashion through faith; [0409A] but then it shall be known through sight, when this conjunction shall be perfected which is now begun.

VERS. 21.-- *He that hath,* etc. Not only you apostles shall live and shall know, but also whosoever has [them] in memory and keeps [them] in life. Or, he has by doing, keeps by persevering. Or he who has [them] in his discourses keeps [them] in his manners. Or, he has by hearing, keeps by doing. *And I will love him.* To this end will I love him, that I may manifest [myself], because even now he loves, but not to this end, but rather that we may believe and keep the commandments, that we may deserve what we may afterwards receive. *And I will manifest to him.* He who has my commandment, he knows me, and this is [what stands] there: *I will manifest myself to him.* But he sets first the cause of the commandment, because he loves me; and he who loves me, my Father will love him, and because the Father [loves him], then I too will love [0409B] him, and thence comes the manifestation.

VERS. 22.-- *Judas saith to him,* etc. Having heard that the world will presently see him, but that they themselves shall see, Judas, about to ask on behalf of all, asks the cause of this distinction. But this cause is, according to the Lord's answer, love, through which alone and through no other is the holy nation distinguished from the unholy.

VERS. 23.-- *We will come to him.* God comes to man, while man comes to God. Man comes by believing, obeying, beholding, receiving; God comes by succouring, enlightening, filling. *And a mansion,* etc. Charity makes [a man] dwell in the house in which the Father and the Son make a mansion, whence he will also give the manifestation concerning which that man had asked.

VERS. 25.-- *These things have I spoken to you.* These things concerning the mansion [0409C] in those who love him, in which is signified the future spiritual Church, in which are the blessed, from whom he does not withdraw. *Abiding with you.* Here [is] the present bodily Church, in which he visits by delivering, and returns.

VERS. 26.-- *The Paraclete.* Although to console is the work of the Trinity, yet it pertains most of all to the person of the Holy Spirit, because in Scripture the Holy Spirit is designated by the name of goodness: through goodness therefore God consoles us, through it he bestows on us all his good things.

*He you.* Because even though the Son teaches us and reveals the Father, he does all this through his goodness. *He shall teach you all things.* He shall teach the things that I shall have said, not that the Son says and the Spirit teaches, because what he says and teaches, the Trinity itself also says and teaches. But because it is a Trinity, [0409D] it behoves the single persons to be intimated, whom we ought to hear distinctly, to understand inseparably. When therefore we say, Father and Son and Holy Spirit, we do not say them at the same time, since they cannot be not at the same time.

VERS. 27.-- *Peace I leave with you.* Being about to go to the Father. *Peace,* etc. And repeating concerning the same. *My peace I give.* And this is what the Prophet says: *Peace upon peace [n: (Isa. XXVII)]*. There is a peace in this world, abiding in which we conquer the enemy, love one another, and do not judge one another concerning hidden things. There will be a peace in the world to come when we shall reign without an enemy, where one will not be able to dissent from another, where *all things are open and praise is to each one [n: (I Cor. IV)]*. But in him and from him is each peace. *For he himself is our peace [n: (Ephes. II)]*, and we [0410A] believe him, because he is, and we shall see him as he is. Whence what he says, my, from the latter is to be understood also of the former. Or, the peace in this world is ours, which is some [peace] and not full; and from him in the world to come is his own peace, where nothing is repugnant, in the manner in which he himself has it.

*Let it not be troubled.* Because I leave you in peace, let not your heart be troubled at that which I said, I go, as though at the shepherd's absence the wolf invades; because as man I go, as God I am present.

VERS. 28.-- *If you loved. [n: (AUG.)]* This was to go to him, and to withdraw from us, etc., *as far as* that incorruptible dust might sit at the right hand of the Father.

*You would rejoice,* etc. Because human nature is to be congratulated, which is raised to the right hand of the Father, in whom the rest hope for the same for themselves.

[0410B]VERS. 28.-- *That you may believe.* Not that you should have seen it done, because there is no praise or merit of faith; nay, faith is not even of that which, is seen, but of that which is not seen. But that you may believe me to be the Son of God, not with a new faith, because they had believed before also, but with a faith increased and restored, because even now, when he was saying these things, it was small, and when he was dying, none at all.

VERS. 30, 31.-- *And in me,* etc. The Lord would not have anything that he might lose; he came poor, lest the devil should have anything that he might take away.
