## CHAPTER XV.

VERS. 1.-- *I am the true vine.* And you ought to follow me as I go before to the passion, because I am the vine, that is, your head, of the same nature with you, that in me human nature may be able to bear fruit. [0410C] *And the Father,* etc. I too am the husbandman together with the Father, because I am the cleanser of the branches, which is the office of the husbandman, not of the vine, who also makes the branches workmen, though not out of what is their own, nor through them, but through myself.

VERS. 3.-- *Because of the word,* etc. He does not say: because of the water of baptism, because that also, without the word, is water only; through the word it becomes a sacrament.

VERS. 5.-- *I am,* etc. He who reckons that he bears fruit of himself is not in the vine. Whence, with a repetition and an explanation of what went before, he adds: *I am the vine, you the branches.* Cleaving to me as to a vine. *He that abideth in me.* And therefore whosoever (not you only) abides in me by believing, by obeying, by persevering. *And I in him.* By enlightening, by succouring, by giving perseverance. *He beareth.* And although according to the man Christ [0410D] is the vine. yet he would not give this grace to the branches unless he were also God.

VERS. 6.-- *If any one.* Shoots that have been cut off are of less worth than other *liguis*, because they are appointed to no uses but the fire; they are either in the vine or in the fire.

VERS. 7.-- *And the words.* The words of God are then said to abide in us, when we do the things he commands and love the things he promises. But he who does not do this touches, he does not cleave; and therefore they are not to him for a benefit but for a testimony of damnation. *You shall ask.* If a faithful man asks anything and it is not done, he does not ask that which the mansion in him has, nor that which his words which are in him have, but that which cupidity and the infirmity of the flesh have.

[0411A]VERS. 8.-- *In this is glorified,* etc. He who abides in me bears much fruit, which is from the grace of God, and so it is his glory, not man's; and this is: *In this,* etc. *That you bring forth very much fruit.* Just as he shows not an equality of our nature and his, but the grace by which he is Mediator; for the Father loves us, but in the Son. *That you may become.* How? If you shall have loved me. From what love? From the observance of the precepts. If you shall have loved me as the Father loved me, not as much. For an equality is not noted, but a likeness giving them eternal eternity.

VERS. 11.-- *That joy.* The joy of God concerning us, whom he foreknew and predestined, is always full; in us it begins from faith, it grows, nay and shall be perfected in the resurrection.

VERS. 12.-- *This is the commandment,* etc. And above concerning [0411B] love toward the neighbour; but repetition is confirmation: there, however, he says, I give a commandment, as though it had not been before; but here, this is, as though it were not another. There, New, lest we remain in oldness; here, Mine, lest it be contemned.

VERS. 13.-- *Greater than this.* He defines the fulness of mutual love which he commands them to have.

VERS. 15.-- *Now I will not call.* Love having been commended, he adds what belongs to condescension, that he should call good servants (who know by doing the things he commands) friends, and takes from them the name of servant, which is from fear, by casting it out, [the servant] who does not know what his Lord does. Because when he does anything, he is so exalted as though he himself did it, and not the lord.

[0411C]VERS. 17.-- *These things manao,* etc. Here he shows what is the fruit of which he had spoken, for which the Father will also give what we ask, who gave this also, that we should love.

*That you love one another. [n: (AUG.)]* Deservedly does he often commend love, as though it alone were to be commanded, without which the rest cannot be, and which without the rest cannot be had. And for its sake we ought patiently to endure even all the adversities of the world. Whence he subjoins:

VERS. 18.-- *If the world.* By the name of the world sometimes the good, sometimes the evil are designated, because there is one mass which wholly perished in Adam, of which some are vessels unto honour, others unto contumely.

VERS. 19.-- *The world what was its own.* If an evil man punishes the crime of an evil man, in part the world hates what is its own; while he punishes, in part it loves, because an evil man favours [0411D] the evil.

VERS. 20.-- *Of my word.* This, namely: *It is not.* And of that above: *Know ye, that it hath hated me before you.* And he subjoins the cause: *The servant is not greater,* etc. Because by his own example (than which nothing else is better) he admonished them to bear the hatreds of the world, he subjoins why they ought, to imitate him in this, because he himself [is] Lord, they servants.

VERS. 22.-- *If I had not come.* Here he shows more expressly the Jews, to whom he came, of whom he had spoken the things above under the name of the world. And because he came to them, he here shows them inexcusable concerning all evils. *An excuse,* etc. Those to whom he did not come nor speak have an excuse, not concerning every sin, but concerning [0412A] this, that they have not believed in Christ. But these, to whom he came and spoke in the apostles, are not excused. But the former, *who have sinned without the law, shall perish without the law [n: (Rom. II)]*. *Of sin.* Because they do not believe me but hate me, and will persecute me in myself and in my own; but this sin against me redounds also upon the Father, because he who hates me hates my Father also. And what is most grievous, not only did I come and speak, but I did works, which, seeing, they hated both me and the Father.

VERS. 24.-- *Which no man,* etc. [n: (AUG.)] That he fed so many thousands from a few [loaves], that he walked upon the waters, etc., *as far as* and because he did it through himself, no one without him.

*They hated,* etc. The Jews say that they love God, and [0412B] yet, not knowing, they hate him, because those who wish to be evil do not wish there to be a truth by which they are condemned. And as they hate their own punishment, they hate the truth which inflicts the punishment, not knowing that he is the truth. And if they hate the truth, they hate him of whom the truth was born; and so, not knowing the Father, they hate the truth born of him.

VERS. 25.-- *Because with hatred.* He hates gratis who seeks no advantage from his hatred nor flees any disadvantage. So do the impious hate Christ. So do the just love gratis, not for the sake of anything else but for his own sake.

VERS. 26.-- *But when.* Now, seeing the works, they hate gratis both me and the Father. But when the Paraclete shall have come, he will convict by a more manifest testimony those who saw and hated; but some of them also, pricked [0412C] for so great a crime, he will convert. *Whom I will send.* On the day of Pentecost the Holy Spirit came upon a hundred and twenty men gathered together, with whom were all the apostles in the house where the supper had been. *Who from the Father.* The Spirit proceeds from the Father and the Son, but the Son is said to proceed from the Father, because he is wont to refer to the Father even what is his own, from whom he himself is. From whom therefore he has that he is, from him he has that the Spirit proceeds from him.
