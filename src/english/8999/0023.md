## CHAPTER XVII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0415C]VERS. 1.-- *These things spoke.* ⟦s2⟧ Not only the things [said] at the supper, but also those from the beginning. ⟦s3⟧ But chiefly he says that the things at the supper were said for this cause, that in him they might have peace. ⟦s4⟧ This is the peace for which we are Christians, for which we are steeped in the sacraments, for which we are instructed in every way, for which we have received the Spirit as a pledge, for which we believe in him and hope, and are kindled with love of him. ⟦s5⟧ This is consolation in afflictions and deliverance. ⟦s6⟧ *Father, the hour is come.* ⟦s7⟧ He could have prayed in silence, but in praying he is also a teacher, and makes his prayer known, which is the edification both of those present and of those to come. ⟦s8⟧ *The hour is come.* ⟦s9⟧ Not with fate urging, but with God disposing, who without time ordained all the things that come to pass through times in a fitting order. ⟦s10⟧ *Glorify thy Son.* ⟦s11⟧ Humility began in the passion, glory in the resurrection. ⟦s12⟧ The hour of the passion is come, [0415D] the hour of sowing humility; do not defer the glory of the resurrection, *that thy Son may glorify thee.* ⟦s13⟧ And thus the glory of the Father can neither be increased nor diminished in itself. ⟦s14⟧ Among men it was less while he was known in Judaea alone, but through the resurrection of the Son he became known everywhere. ⟦s15⟧ *That thy Son may glorify thee.* ⟦s16⟧ In the knowledge of men, as thou hast given, as far as *that they may know thee, and him whom thou hast sent.* ⟦s17⟧ For the Father cannot be glorified in the knowledge of man unless he be known through whom he is glorified, that is, unless the people become known; and this is the glorification of the Father, which is not about the apostles alone but also about all believers.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *And this is,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The order [is], *That they may know thee, and him whom thou hast sent,* that is, Christ with the Spirit, [0416A] who is of both, to be the one and only true God. ⟦s3⟧ If therefore the Son glorifies thee in that manner in which thou hast given him power over every creature, and hast so given it, *that all that thou hast given him, he may give them life everlasting:* And *this is life everlasting, that they may know thee.* ⟦s4⟧ So therefore does the Son glorify thee, that he may make thee known to all whom thou hast given him. ⟦s5⟧ *That they may know thee.* ⟦s6⟧ What is here from the Father, in the world to come will be fully. ⟦s7⟧ He therefore who advances in knowledge tends the more to live. ⟦s8⟧ But when knowledge shall be full, there will be praise without end. ⟦s9⟧ And this is that glorification; but first he is glorified here, while he becomes known to the faithful; whence: *I have glorified thee upon earth.*

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *I have finished the work.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, as if he said openly of what is to come, I will glorify [thee], by preaching to the Gentiles. ⟦s3⟧ I will finish the work, because it is already done by predestination; [0416B] and what most certainly is to be is to be held as already done. ⟦s4⟧ This predestination he opens more manifestly in his own glorification, saying: *Which I had with thee before the world was;* that is, as I then had it with thee in predestination before the times, so now in reality make it in the world in time. ⟦s5⟧ *Thou hast given me,* etc. ⟦s6⟧ Wherein grace is noted. ⟦s7⟧ For he has nothing in the man which he has not received.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *To men.* ⟦s2⟧ All these things can be said of all the faithful who are to be. ⟦s3⟧ And this is that glorification by which the Son, being glorified, glorifies the Father upon earth, according to which the past stands for the future.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *They were thine. [n: (AUG.)]* These were the Father's, as all things are, etc., *as far as* because he was not yet man.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *They have known.* ⟦s2⟧ Lest the knowledge [0416C] should be thought already made through sight, he adds: They have believed truly; not in that manner in which it was when he said, Now you believe, the hour cometh that you be scattered. ⟦s3⟧ But not even yet were they such; rather he foretells what they will be when the Spirit has been received, without whom they did not even keep the word which he had spoken.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *I pray for them.* ⟦s2⟧ The first prayer was: *Glorify the Son, that the Son may glorify thee,* and he pursued these things and concluded in the faith of believers. ⟦s3⟧ Now he prays for them, that the Father may keep those whom he himself has left in body.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *And all mine.* ⟦s2⟧ For the Father did not lose because he gave to the Son, since the Son further adds: *All mine thine and thine mine are.* ⟦s3⟧ Of the only-begotten Son are all the things that are the Father's, through this, that he himself is also God, and, being also born of the Father, is equal to the Father.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0416D]VERS. 11.-- *Keep them.* ⟦s2⟧ That they may be one in their own nature, as we also in ours are one; and therefore he does not say, that they may be one with us, or that we and they may be one as we are one, because they are not of the same nature with God, as the Son, according to that which he is God, is one with the Father.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *When I was.* ⟦s2⟧ We ought not to understand that the Father and the Son keep alternately, as it were the one after the other. ⟦s3⟧ For the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, who is one God, keep together. ⟦s4⟧ But Scripture does not lift us up unless it comes down to us. ⟦s5⟧ When it speaks thus, let us understand that we are to distinguish the persons, not to separate the nature. ⟦s6⟧ The joy which he expressed above, that they may be one as we also, this is that peace and blessedness in [0417A] the world to come, for the sake of which all things are done, for the sake of which he says that he has spoken in the world.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Sanctify.* ⟦s2⟧ As from thy grace the sanctification was begun through which they are now not of the world, by the same grace make them holier, that they may persevere in the truth, of which the ancient sanctifications were a shadow; that is, in me who am the truth, which he opens by subjoining: *Thy word is truth,* which is, I am the truth. ⟦s3⟧ In Greek λόγος, in Latin verbum or sermo, who is the only-begotten of the Father.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 19.-- *And for them I sanctify.* [var: Vulg. Jo 17:19 has the future *sanctificabo*, "I will sanctify"; the gloss that follows expounds the future.] That is, that it may profit for them, I will sanctify myself as man in myself the Word, which is from the beginning of his Incarnation, when the Word was made flesh. ⟦s3⟧ And it profits them in this way, because I also sanctify in myself those who are I, since head and body are one Christ, [0417B] whence he adds: *that they also may be,* as I am, *sanctified in truth* and in me.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Not for them.* ⟦s2⟧ He now begins not concerning the apostles alone, or concerning the other members: but he does not pray for those who have already died in his faith, but for those who are in temptations. ⟦s3⟧ *But also for those who.* ⟦s4⟧ Even unto the end of the world, or [those who] already believe wherever they are, yet not so perfectly as they are to believe afterwards after the resurrection. ⟦s5⟧ *Through the word.* ⟦s6⟧ Through the word of the Gospel, which is theirs, not that it was preached through them only, but also through Paul and any others whatever, but because it was committed to them first and principally to be preached. ⟦s7⟧ This is the word of faith, the word of God, through which the thief believed, Paul believed, and whosoever believes.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0417C]VERS. 21.-- *That all may be one.* ⟦s2⟧ He does not say, We all are one, but that they all may be one, as thou, Father, in me and I in thee, understand, we are one: so also the Father [is] in the Son and the Son in the Father, that they may be one, because of one substance. ⟦s3⟧ But we can be in the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. ⟦s4⟧ Or the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit can be in us; yet we cannot be one with God, because we are not of one substance. ⟦s5⟧ But God is in us as in a temple, we in him as a creature in the Creator. ⟦s6⟧ *That they also.* ⟦s7⟧ He says, in us, that what they are one by faith and charity they may ascribe to the grace of God, but that now light in the Lord.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *That the world.* ⟦s2⟧ The world is here taken for all those who are one. ⟦s3⟧ Nor does this, that they are, make a cause that they should believe, because rather by believing they are one; although [0417D] they were one by nature, yet by dissenting from the One they were not one; but by praying he said that the world may believe, as by praying he said that all may be one, as if a third time it should be understood, I pray that the world may believe.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *Whom thou hast given.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, thou hast drawn them to me, so I also have chosen them out of the world: others are not given to him, are not his, although the power over all be his. ⟦s3⟧ *I will.* ⟦s4⟧ What the Almighty wills cannot fail to be done; but that is the will of the man, concerning which he says: *Not as I will [n: (Matth. XXVI)]*. ⟦s5⟧ *That they may see.* ⟦s6⟧ When they shall have seen him, in the form of a servant made glorious, judging the good and the evil, the impious shall be taken away, that he may not see the glory of God whereby God is, which the clean of heart alone shall see, which will be to them life everlasting.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0418A]VERS. 26.-- *That the love.* ⟦s2⟧ The love with which the Father loves the Son is in all the just, because they are his members and are loved in him who is wholly loved; therefore he subjoins: *And I am in them.* ⟦s3⟧ As if he should say: Because I am in them, he is in us in one way, as in a temple, we in him in another way; because we are he, since according to the man we are the body of his head.
