## CHAPTER ONE.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judaea. [n: (AMBR.)]* The calf is the priestly victim. ⟦s2⟧ By the calf, therefore, this Gospel is figured, in which the beginning is made from the priests and the end is made in the calf, that is, in Christ, who is offered up for the life of the world.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A certain priest.* ⟦s2⟧ He has filled out the order: he has named the king, and the region in which he was; he points out the priest and his nation and his wife. ⟦s3⟧ *Zacharias.* ⟦s4⟧ Zacharias, mindful of the Lord: to whom an Angel appears at the right of the altar, because to him who is mindful of the Lord the mystery is shown.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the course of Abia. [n: (BEDA.)]* Abia, the Lord is Father, etc., [0244D] *as far as* just as by the seven the Old Testament is declared.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Elizabeth.* ⟦s2⟧ Elizabeth, the fullness of my God, signifies Mary, who was full of God.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And they were both. [n: (BEDA, AMBR.)]* A full praise, which comprehends birth in his forebears, character in fairness, office in the priesthood, deed in the commandments, judgment in the justifications. ⟦s2⟧ *Just before God. [n: (AMBR.)]* For not everyone who is just before men is just before God; for men see in one way, God in another: men in the face, God in the heart. ⟦s3⟧ And therefore it can come about that someone, by an affected popular goodness, should seem just to me, and yet not be just before God, if his justice is not formed out of simplicity of mind but is feigned by flattery.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0245A]VERS. 7.-- *And there was not unto them. [n: (BEDA.)]* It was divinely provided that John should be born of parents advanced in years and long deprived of the fruit of wedlock, so that by the unlooked-for birth of the child the gift of God might touch them the more gratefully, and the astonishment of the miracle might prepare the rest for the hearing of the prophecy to come. ⟦s2⟧ Whence afterwards it is added: *All they that had heard laid it up in their heart, saying: What manner of child, thinkest thou, shall this be?*

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *And it came to pass.* ⟦s2⟧ Whereas by the precept of Moses, when one priest died, one is bidden to succeed him, in David's time it was appointed that, ministering by turns for eight days at a time from sabbath to sabbath in the time of their course, they should each give themselves to chastity, and should not meanwhile touch their house.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *By lot. [n: (BEDA.)]* He was not then chosen by a new lot, when the incense was to be burned, etc., *as far [0245B] as* he is foretold while that other is promised as one who will go before.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And Zacharias was troubled at the sight, and fear fell upon him,* etc. [n: (AMBR.)] We are wont to be troubled and estranged from our own feeling when we are shaken by the encounter of a higher power. ⟦s2⟧ But as it belongs to human weakness to be terrified, so it belongs to angelic kindness to console with soothing one who is afraid at its appearing; on the contrary, the demons, if they have perceived any terrified by their presence, shake them with a larger horror.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Because thy prayer is heard: and thy wife Elizabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name.* ⟦s2⟧ It was heard for more than thou didst ask. ⟦s3⟧ Thou didst pray for the deliverance of the people, and a forerunner is given thee.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AMBR.)] Many divine benefits are heaped together here. ⟦s2⟧ First, the fruit of the prayer; then the childbearing of a barren woman. [0245C] Then the gladness of very many, and greatness of virtue, and even the prophecy of the Most High is promised. ⟦s3⟧ Moreover, that there might be no doubt, the name of the child to come is appointed. ⟦s4⟧ Since therefore so many things flow beyond what he had prayed for, not undeservedly is his diffidence punished with the penalty of silence.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *And there shall be joy,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] It is a token of singular merit as often as a name is either imposed upon men by God or changed, etc., *as far as* because this one preaches salvation to many, but that one gives it to all. ⟦s2⟧ *[n: (AMBR.)] Joy and exultation.* ⟦s3⟧ The parents are admonished to give thanks no less, etc., *as far as* mothers, because they are honoured with the rewards of wedlock.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Wine and strong drink,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] It is of great virtue before God to preach heavenly joys in the desert, etc., *as far as* and already there was the spirit of grace.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0245D]VERS. 16.-- *And he shall convert many of the children of Israel. [n: (ID.)]* Since John, bearing witness to Christ and baptizing in the faith of Christ, is said to have converted the children of Israel to God, it is plain that Christ is the Lord God of Israel.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *He shall go before. [n: (ID.)]* As Elias is the herald of the judge, etc., *as far as* he is made manifest in glory with him. ⟦s2⟧ *In the spirit and power. [n: (AMBR.)]* Spirit and power are rightly joined. ⟦s3⟧ For there is no spirit without power, nor power without spirit. ⟦s4⟧ Elias had the power of abstinence, and of patience, and of turning the minds of peoples from faithlessness to faith; and the spirit of prophesying he had.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *That he may turn,* etc., [n: (BEDA.)] He turns the hearts of the fathers unto the children, [0246A] etc., *as far as* almost the same words which the angel foretold of John, Malachi foretold of Elias.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Whereby,* etc. [n: (ID.)] If it were a man who was promising, it would be allowable to seek a sign unpunished, etc., *as far as* and it would be the penalty of unbelief which he deserved.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Before God. [n: (ID.)]* When angels come to us, they so fulfil their ministry outwardly that nevertheless they stand within, before God, by contemplation. ⟦s2⟧ For although an angel is a circumscribed spirit, the highest Spirit, who is God, is uncircumscribed, and within him the angel runs wherever he is moved or sent.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *And he was making signs to them, and remained dumb,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Labouring to indicate bodily acts without a voice, and not expressing his will. ⟦s3⟧ Like to him is the people of the Jews, [0246B] unable to render an account of their own acts.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *When they were fulfilled,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because in the time of their course the priests of the temple, given over to its offices alone, abstained not only from the embrace of their wives but even from entering their houses. ⟦s3⟧ But to our priests, from whom not a carnal succession but a spiritual perfection is sought, who ought to be daily at hand at the altar, perpetual chastity is enjoined.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *She hid herself. [n: (AMBR.)]* She hides herself because she blushes at the age of her childbearing, etc., *as far as* for neither would she who would not blush at intercourse in old age blush at the childbearing.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Because thus hath he done. [n: (BEDA.)]* These things were done, says John Chrysostom, etc., *as far as* because through him the affliction of penance was to be preached.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *And in the sixth month. [n: (HIER.)]* As in the sixth [0246C] month the angel Gabriel was sent by God into a city of Galilee, so in the sixth millennium Christ was sent,

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Gabriel. [n: (BEDA.)]* For this reason angels are sometimes marked out by a name, so that from the name itself it may be shown what they come to minister. ⟦s2⟧ Gabriel is the strength of God, because he announces him who was coming to war down the devil.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *To a virgin.* ⟦s2⟧ A woman seduced by the devil brought in death: on the contrary, a woman taught by an angel brought forth salvation.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *The Lord is with thee.* ⟦s2⟧ Whom by a new love of chastity he raised up to heavenly things, and afterwards, human nature mediating, consecrated with all the fullness of the Godhead. ⟦s3⟧ *Blessed art thou among women.* ⟦s4⟧ Who, without example in the condition of women, is both virgin and mother, and brought forth God.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *She was troubled,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because she did not know whether [0246D] what the angel said was past or to come.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *And the angel said to her: Fear not, Mary.* ⟦s2⟧ Her, troubled by the unwonted salutation, he calls by name as one more familiarly known. ⟦s3⟧ He bids her not to fear, and more fully explains why he had called her full of grace.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Jesus.* ⟦s2⟧ Jesus is called saviour, or salutary. ⟦s3⟧ For he himself shall save, not the people of Israel, but his own people, whether from the uncircumcision or from the circumcision, gathered into one fold under one shepherd.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *The throne of David his father. [n: (BEDA.)]* That is, the Israelite people, etc., *as far as* in those who, cut from the wild olive, have been grafted into the good olive.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 35.-- *The Holy One shall be called the Son of God.* ⟦s2⟧ Jesus is born holy, who was to overcome the condition of corruptible nature, [0247A] and he was not conceived by the mingling of carnal union. ⟦s3⟧ We, bound fast by the condition of corruptible nature, can be sanctified through grace. ⟦s4⟧ And it is fitting that she who conceived as a virgin against custom should bring forth the Son of God above the custom of human usage.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *And behold,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] Lest the virgin should despair of the childbearing, etc., *as far as* she might know that the forerunner too was to be born of a barren old woman.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thy cousin.* ⟦s2⟧ Because she was of the tribe of Levi, that is, of the tribe of God.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BEDA.)] We read that Aaron, who was of the tribe of Levi, etc., *as far as* which according to God did not lack the fellowship of a spiritual kinship.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 38.-- *Behold the handmaid of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ She does not exalt herself for the singularity of her merit, but, mindful in all things of her own condition and of the divine kinship, she confesses herself to be the handmaid [0247B] of him whose mother she is chosen to be, and with great devotion desires the angel's promise to be fulfilled.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 39.-- *And Mary rising up in those days, went into the hill country with haste into a city of Juda. [n: (AMBR.)]* Not incredulous of the oracle, etc., *as far as* when it is added: *And it came to pass, that when she heard.*

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 40.-- *And she entered into the house of Zachary.* ⟦s2⟧ From this let holy women learn what attentiveness they ought to show to kinswomen with child.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And saluted Elizabeth. [n: (BEDA.)]* She salutes first, because it is fitting that the chaster she is, the humbler she should be.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 41.-- *He exulted,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because he could not with the tongue, he salutes with an exulting spirit, and begins the office of his forerunning. ⟦s3⟧ Behold, there appears what the angel had said: *He shall be filled with the Holy Ghost even from his mother's womb.*

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0247C]VERS. 42.-- *Blessed art thou. [n: (BEDA, AMBR.)]* The words of the Gospel agree with the words of the psalm in which it is promised to David: *Of the fruit of thy womb I will set upon thy throne [n: (Psal. CXXXI)]*. ⟦s2⟧ And Mary is blessed by Elizabeth with the same voice as by Gabriel, so that she may be shown venerable both to angels and to men, and to be preferred before our women.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 43.-- *The mother of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ I perceive the miracle, I acknowledge the mystery: the mother of the Lord, teeming with the Word, full of God.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 44.-- *As was made.* ⟦s2⟧ In the very moment in which the voice enters the ears of the body, a spiritual power enters the heart of the hearer, and kindles not only the mother but also the offspring with love of her who comes.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He exulted. [n: (BEDA.)]* By the revealing of the Spirit with whom she was filled, etc., *as far as* the virgin believed where the priest denied.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0247D]VERS. 46.-- *My soul doth magnify the Lord. [n: (ID.)]* First she professes the gifts spiritually granted to her, etc., *as far as* I spend in the keeping of his commandments.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 47.-- *And hath exulted. [n: (ID.)] [n: (ID.)]* Because I do not care for earthly things, prosperity does not soften me, adversity does not break me, but the memory of him alone delights me, from whom eternal salvation is hoped for; I rejoice in the Godhead of him by whose conception in time my flesh is made fruitful.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 48.-- *Because he hath regarded. [n: (ID.)]* Behold how lowly are the things she feels concerning herself, etc., *as far as* and out of the small and the weak he makes them great and strong.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Because he hath done great things to me.* ⟦s2⟧ Because in me he took flesh, and cleansed me from sins, and conferred upon me the gifts of the Holy Ghost.

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0248A] *And mercy. [n: (BEDA.)]* From the special gifts she turns herself to the general judgments of God, etc., *as far as* he adds what the despisers deserve.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 53.-- *He hath filled the hungry with good things. [n: (ID.)]* They who desire eternal things with all zeal; shall be filled, when Christ shall have appeared in glory. ⟦s2⟧ But they who rejoice in earthly things are sent away at the last empty of all blessedness. ⟦s3⟧ In the present life also we see the humble filled with divine grace, and the proud deprived of the light of truth.

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 55.-- *As he spoke to our fathers.* ⟦s2⟧ Making mention of the fathers to whom this salvation was revealed, she names Abraham expressly, to whom the incarnation itself was first made manifest. ⟦s3⟧ *And to his seed for ever.* ⟦s4⟧ Not so much to those born of the flesh as to those who have followed the footsteps of faith, to whom the coming of the Saviour was promised for ever, [0248B] because the promise of the inheritance itself shall be closed by no end. ⟦s5⟧ For both unto the end of the world believers shall not be lacking, and the glory of blessedness shall be everlasting.
