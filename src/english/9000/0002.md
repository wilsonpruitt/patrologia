## CHAPTER ONE. (cont.)

VERS. 56.-- *And Mary abode. [n: (BEDA.)]* She abode so long, until she should see the nativity of the forerunner, for the sake of which above all she had come. Nor did she abide so long for the sake of familiarity alone, but that in so long a time the advancement both of Elizabeth and of John might grow. For if at Mary's first coming they were filled with the Holy Ghost, much was added to them over and above by so long a delay of her stay.

VERS. 59.-- *On the eighth day.* By circumcision the resurrection of the Lord is figured, because it too was made on the eighth day, that is, after the sabbath. And as the one was wont to absolve from [0248C] the guilt of death, so the other both exhibited in our author the birth of immortal life, and showed that it is to be hoped for in us.

*And they called him. [n: (BEDA.)]* The celebrated nativity of John is the grace of the New Testament begun, etc., *as far as* it expresses the grace of the Gospel, if it be rightly understood.

VERS. 62.-- *And they made signs to his father,* etc. They who make signs to the father about the child's name signify those who by the testimony of the law aim to build up the grace of faith.

VERS. 67.-- *And he prophesied, saying. [n: (BEDA.)]* Great is the bounty of the divine mercy. Behold, the speech which alone was taken away from him while he doubted is restored, together with the spirit of prophecy, to him now believing.

VERS. 68.-- *Blessed.* Who, while he besought the goodness of God, [0248D] was given a forerunner in place of deliverance.

*He hath visited.* What he had known was shortly to be done, he relates after the manner of the prophets as though it were already done.

VERS. 69.-- *A horn of salvation to us. [n: (BEDA.)]* By the horn, which rises above the flesh, since all the bones are covered with flesh, the kingdom of Christ is signified, by which the world and the joys of the flesh are overcome. In figure of which David and Solomon were consecrated to the glory of the kingdom with a horn of oil, as Saul before them with a vial.

VERS. 71.-- *Salvation from our enemies, and from the hand,* etc. He hath raised up for us salvation from our enemies; and it is an explanation of the earlier verse, in which he had briefly set the same thing before: *And he hath raised up a horn of salvation to us.*

VERS. 72.-- *To perform mercy to [0249A] our fathers, and to remember his testament,* etc. He hath spoken, and hath raised up salvation for us, to perform mercy: that the mercy which he promised to the fathers he may fulfil in us, and that the oath which he made to the fathers concerning our deliverance may be accomplished in us through Christ.

VERS. 76.-- *And thou, child, shalt be called the prophet of the Highest: for thou shalt go before the face of the Lord to prepare,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] It is no wonder if he addresses an infant of eight days, etc., *as far as* which he acknowledged that he had received among his sons.

VERS. 79, 80.-- *To direct our feet into the way of peace. And the child grew,* etc. That in all things the steps of our works may accord with the grace of him who enlightens us, until we enter into the dwelling of perpetual peace.
