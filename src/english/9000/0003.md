## CHAPTER II.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0249B]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *There went out a decree from Caesar. [n: (AUG., BED.)]* Augustus reigned in peace for twelve years about the nativity of Christ, etc., *as far as* here first Judaea was made tributary to the Romans.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *The first. [n: (BEDA.)]* Already at various times very many parts of the world are read to have been enrolled, but this is the first of those which took in the whole world; and it is a profession of minds, because no one is shut out. ⟦s2⟧ Or certainly it then first began, when Cyrinus was sent by Caesar into Syria, to be an assessor of patrimonies.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Nazareth. [n: (ID.)]* Daily the Lord is conceived in Nazareth, etc., *as far as* the insistence of the teacher who speaks avails nothing unless it has received the increase of help from above [0249C] that it may be heard.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And it came to pass.* ⟦s2⟧ The Lord willed to be conceived in one place and born in another, that he might the more easily escape the fury of the plotting Herod. ⟦s3⟧ The humility of Christ is commended, because he willed not only to be made flesh, but even to be born at that time in which, as soon as he was born, he should be made subject to Caesar's census for the sake of our deliverance.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *She wrapped him up in swaddling clothes.* ⟦s2⟧ He is clothed in mean cloths, that we might receive the robe of immortality. ⟦s3⟧ His hands and feet are bound fast, that our hands may be directed to working well, and our feet into the way of peace; he was made a little one, that we might be perfect.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *And there were shepherds. [n: (BEDA.)]* The highest shepherd being born, the shepherds from the snares of the night, etc., *as far as* upon whom the divine grace flashes more abundantly.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0249D]VERS. 9.-- *And behold an angel of the Lord. [n: (ID.)]* The citizens of heaven bear witness to the Lord both as to be conceived, and as conceived, and as born, that they may both sufficiently instruct mortals and pay their own service to their author.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *An infant wrapped in swaddling clothes. [n: (ID.)]* The infancy of the Saviour has been pressed home by frequent testimonies both of the angels and of the evangelists, that it may be fixed the more deeply in our minds what he was made for our sake: in which his humility is commended: *Because, whereas he was rich, he became poor for us, that by his own poverty he might enrich us [n: (II Cor)]*. [var: Vulg. 2 Cor 8:9 reads *propter vos egenus factus est*, "for your sakes he became needy," and *ut illius inopia vos divites essetis*, "that by his poverty you might be rich"; Migne's clause has *pauper* for *egenus* and puts in the first person what the Vulgate puts in the second.]

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *And on earth peace.* ⟦s2⟧ When by sinning we were strangers to God, the angels reckoned us strangers; but because we have known our king, the angels [0250A] have recognized us as their own fellow citizens. ⟦s3⟧ And the angel fears to be adored by a human nature which he beholds in his own king.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To men of good will. [n: (BEDA.)]* Who receive Christ when born; not to those who at the hearing of his nativity were troubled and persecuted him. ⟦s2⟧ *There is no peace to the wicked, saith the Lord [n: (Isa. XLVIII)]*.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And they came with haste. [n: (BEDA.)]* The presence of Christ is not to be sought with sloth, etc., *as far as* they will find him as it were in a manger.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *And seeing.* ⟦s2⟧ It is the vision or knowledge of God, and this alone is the life of men, that they should know the true Father, and that Jesus Christ, whom he sent, is one God.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *They wondered.* ⟦s2⟧ They wonder both at the mystery of the incarnation and at so great a testimony of shepherds, who [0250B] did not know how to feign what they had heard, but with a simple eloquence proclaimed true things. ⟦s3⟧ But the Lord appointed not orators but fishermen to preach the Gospel. ⟦s4⟧ So too in the Old Testament he ordained shepherds as the messengers of his dispensation. ⟦s5⟧ Therefore the testimony of the shepherds is not to be lightly esteemed.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *And returning,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] Thus spiritual shepherds, while others sleep, are borne up by contemplating heavenly things, etc., *as far as* when they say: *Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace to men of good will.*

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *They carried him.* ⟦s2⟧ The little ones who from the whole kingdom of the Jews were carried to the temple, if they were of the tribe of Levi, were kept back perpetually for the ministry of the house. ⟦s3⟧ If of another tribe, the firstborn were redeemed by a price given by their parents, to be carried back to their own homes.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0250C]VERS. 23.-- *As it is written. [n: (BEDA.)]* It is written in the law: *A woman who, having received,* etc., *as far as* she enters upon the joys of heavenly blessedness with the fruits of good works.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *A pair of turtledoves. [n: (ID.)]* This was the offering of the poor, who were not able to offer a lamb, so that in all things the humility of the Lord might be plain.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *And behold there was a man.* ⟦s2⟧ Not only the angels, but every age and sex render testimony to the child that is born. ⟦s3⟧ And as he was foretold by the faithful of all ages, so at his coming he is proclaimed with the praise of all the saints.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *The child.* ⟦s2⟧ Since boyhood begins after the seven years of infancy, Jesus is frequently called a child, [0250D] not so much for his age as for his service; whence the Prophet: *Behold my child,* [var: Vulg. Isai 42:1 reads *Ecce servus meus*, "Behold my servant"; the gloss's whole argument turns on *puer*.] because *the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister [n: (Isa. XLII)].*

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *Now thou dost dismiss thy servant. [n: (AMBR.)]* He who wishes to be dismissed, let him come into Jerusalem, etc., *as far as* then he shall be dismissed so that he may not see death, because he had seen life.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *Behold this child is set.* ⟦s2⟧ For the ruin of those who had stood, and the resurrection of those who had fallen; or for the ruin of vices, and for the resurrection of virtues. ⟦s3⟧ Not in himself alone, but in his preachers also is he set for ruin and resurrection; whence the Apostle: *We are the good odour of Christ unto God, in them that are saved, and in them that perish.* ⟦s4⟧ For by the good odour some are saved, others perish [n: (II Cor. II)].

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0251A]VERS. 35.-- *A sword. [n: (BEDA.)]* The sorrow of the Lord's passion, etc., *as far as* she beholds the cockle springing up.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *That may be revealed.* ⟦s2⟧ Before, it was uncertain which of the Jews would receive the grace of Christ, and which would spit upon it: but at the hearing of the nativity the thoughts are revealed, and Herod and others are troubled, while certain men come to the teaching of Christ.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *And there was Anna,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because through a man and a woman the life of the whole world was lost, in the testimony of life returning through Christ Anna is joined to Simeon. ⟦s3⟧ It agrees with the mysteries of the Church that Anna is interpreted grace, who is the daughter of Phanuel, who is called the face of God; whence above: *The light of thy countenance, O Lord, is signed upon us [n: (Psal. IV)]*. ⟦s4⟧ *Of the tribe of Aser.* ⟦s5⟧ That is, of the blessed, she is descended, from him who among the twelve [0251B] patriarchs is eighth in the order of birth, on account of the mystery of the resurrection. ⟦s6⟧ There are five Annas. ⟦s7⟧ The first the mother of Samuel, the second the wife of Raguel, the third the mother of Tobias, the fourth the daughter of Phanuel, the fifth the mother of Mary.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *She had advanced in days. [n: (BEDA.)]* According to the history, Anna, and of a devout manner of life, etc., *as far as* they are multiplied on account of the apostolic teaching.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 38.-- *To all who,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ To all the faithful who, weighed down by the yoke of the foreigner Herod, were awaiting the deliverance of the city and of the people, she promised through the coming of Christ a redemption close at hand from the tyranny of the stranger.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 40.-- *And the child. [n: (BEDA.)]* In that he was a child, etc., *as far as* he had begun to be made man, he was perfect [0251C] as God.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 41.-- *And his parents went,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Deservedly Luke is compared, among the four living creatures, to the calf, etc., *as far as* at the end of his Gospel he concludes with the disciples of the Lord praising in the temple.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 45.-- *And not finding him. [n: (ID.)]* If it is asked how he could be left behind by his parents through forgetfulness, etc., *as far as* returned with the other parent.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 46.-- *In the midst. [n: (ID.)]* As a fountain, he sits in the midst of the doctors; but as a pattern of humility, he asks and hears before he instructs, lest little ones should blush to be taught by their elders, lest one who is weak should dare to teach.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 49.-- *How is it that you sought me? [n: (ID.)]* He does not find fault with their seeking him as a son, but [0251D] he suggests who is his true Father, and compels them to lift the eyes of the mind to what is rather owed to him whose eternal Son he is. ⟦s2⟧ For because he is God and man, now he sets forth the lofty things of the Godhead, now the weak things of the humanity. ⟦s3⟧ As the Son of God he stays in the temple; as the Son of man he goes back with his parents whither they bid.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 51.-- *And his mother kept all these words in her heart.* ⟦s2⟧ All the things which she knew to have been done concerning the Lord or by the Lord, whether those she understood or those she could not yet understand, she laid up all in her memory, so that, when the time should come for his incarnation to be preached or written, she might be able sufficiently to set forth to those who asked all things just as they had been done. ⟦s3⟧ So let us, by frequent meditation on the deeds and words of the Lord, [0252A] drive off importunate thoughts and labour to instruct others.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 52.-- *He advanced in wisdom. [n: (BEDA.)]* As it belongs to the flesh to advance in age, etc., *as far as* both alike might be saved.
