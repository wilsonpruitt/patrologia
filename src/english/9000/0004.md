## CHAPTER III.

VERS. 2.-- *The word was made,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] About to gather the Church, the Son of God works first in his little servant, etc., *as far as* he therefore said one thing, and declared all.

VERS. 3.-- *Preaching the baptism of penance,* etc. [n: (ID.)] John preached the baptism of penance, and even gave it to some, etc., *as far as* there will be a going before by his own baptism, by which sins cannot be loosed.

VERS. 4.-- *The voice of one crying.* A voice, because he is the herald [0252B] of the Word. *In the desert.* Because he announces the consolation of redemption to a deserted and forsaken Judaea. No word is heard without a voice, nor does a voice avail without the understanding of the word. *Prepare ye the way of the Lord,* etc. He who preaches faith and good works, what else than for the Lord who comes? He prepares a way to the hearts of his hearers, that this force of grace may give light, that it may make straight paths for God, while by the word of preaching it forms clean thoughts in the mind.

VERS. 5.-- *And the crooked shall be. [n: (BEDA.)]* The crooked, that is, the hearts of the wicked, twisted by injustice, shall be directed to the rule of justice.

VERS. 6.-- *And shall see. [n: (ID.)]* In this life not every man could see Christ, but on the day of judgment, on the seat of majesty, both the elect and the reprobate alike shall see him, that [0252C] the just may be rewarded and the wicked groan for ever. Whence there is also added:

VERS. 7.-- *Who hath shewed,* etc.? [n: (AMBR.)] Who hath shewed, in the prudence poured in by the mercy of God, etc.? *as far as* which he prophesies is to be transferred to the Gentiles.

VERS. 8.-- *For I say to you. [n: (ID.)]* They who, having lost the sense of reason, etc., *as far as* they are filled with fruits, they are clothed with leaves.

VERS. 11.-- *He that hath two. (BEDA)*. The tunic is more necessary of itself than the cloak, etc., *as far as* but let him share what he has with the poor man.

VERS. 12.-- *The publicans. [n: (ID.)]* They who exact the public tolls, etc., *as far as* they might at length attain to sharing their own goods with their neighbours.

VERS. 15.-- *And while they were supposing. [n: (ID.)]* Not only [0252D] were they thinking, etc., *as far as* they do not believe that he came through a virgin.

VERS. 16.-- *But there shall come.* Therefore I baptize with water, because he comes who will baptize with the Spirit, and it is fitting that a way be prepared for him.

*Whose I am not worthy. [n: (BEDA.)]* I am not worthy to search out the mystery of the incarnation, etc., *as far as* the evangelical preaching, with which the apostles are shod.

VERS. 19.-- *Herod.* Before Luke relates anything of the acts of Jesus, he says that John was seized by Herod, in order to show that he will describe only those deeds which were done in that year in which John was either seized or punished.

VERS. 21.-- *And Jesus being baptized. [n: (BEDA.)]* The Lord is baptized, [0253A] not needing to be cleansed, etc., *as far as* now taught, he openly preaches the Son of God.

VERS. 22.-- *In thee I am well pleased.* Not what is another's in the Son, but his own, is praised, as though he should say: Whatsoever things thou hast are mine.

VERS. 23.-- *Thirty years of age. [n: (BEDA.)]* That he is baptized in his thirtieth year, etc., *as far as* as in Jeremias and Daniel.

VERS. 26.-- *Who was of Mathathias, who was of Amos, who was of Nahum, who was of Hesli. [n: (BEDA.)]* Mathath, who is descended through Solomon, etc., *as far as* who through carnal generation is shown to have been made the Son of man.

VERS. 29.-- *Who was of Levi.* But these four who are brought in in this place were not the sons of Jacob, but they shared in their names.

[0253B] [n: (BEDA.)] As if alluding by the names to what was figured in them, etc., *as far as* diverse kinds of virtues might go before.

VERS. 31.-- *Nathan. [n: (ID.)]* That Matthew, through Solomon, etc., *as far as* to whom judgment is given by God the king: *He is a priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedech.* [var: Vulg. Psal 109:4 opens the clause *Tu es sacerdos*, "Thou art a priest"; Migne reports in the third person what the psalm addresses in the second.]

VERS. 37.-- *Mathusale. [n: (AMBR.)]* The years of Mathusale are numbered beyond the flood; it signifies Christ, whose life knows not age.

*Who was of Enoch. [n: (BEDA.)]* Ascending from the baptized Son of God as far as God the Father, he sets Enoch at the seventieth step, etc., *as far as* whom he shows to be joined, in the resurrection, through the ages, [0253C] to the contemplating of the unchangeable wisdom of God.

VERS. 38.-- *Seth.* Seth, the later son of Adam, is not passed over in silence, so that, since there are two generations of the people, it may signify that Christ is to be numbered in the later rather than in the earlier generation.

*Who was of God.* The generation begins from the Son of God, and is ended in the Son of God; the created goes before in figure, that the born may follow in truth; he who was made to the image goes first, that for his sake the image may come down.
