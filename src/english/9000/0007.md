## CHAPTER V. (cont.)

VERS. 38.-- *But new wine.* The frailty of the human condition is laid open, when our bodies are compared to the cast-off skins of dead animals. But when the bottles have been renewed, new wines are brought in, when we keep unviolated the sacraments which we have received. These bottles, if they are full, grace preserves; if empty, moth and rust consume: therefore let them always be full.
