## CHAPTER VIII. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 46.-- *For I also know.* ⟦s2⟧ Whoever touches is not hidden: [0276C] God does not need eyes in order to see, nor does he perceive bodily, but has in himself the knowledge of all things. ⟦s3⟧ The power also which is merciful to all our iniquities, and heals all our infirmities, goes forth not from us, but from God.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 47.-- *And the woman seeing.* ⟦s2⟧ This woman, modest, touches the hem; faithful, she drew near; religious, she believed; wise, she knows herself to be healed: so the people of the Gentiles, which believed in God, blushed at sin that it might forsake it, brought faith that it might believe, showed devotion that it might ask, put on wisdom that it too might perceive its own healing, took confidence that it might confess that it was seizing beforehand what belonged to another.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 51.-- *The father of the girl.* ⟦s2⟧ The father of the girl is the assembly of the teachers of the law, namely the Scribes and Pharisees, who, if they [0276D] shall have been willing to believe, the Synagogue that is subject to them will also be saved.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 52.-- *And they wept.* ⟦s2⟧ They weep who see the Synagogue estranged from God, who is life, and who grieve, and think that she can no longer be quickened; and she, who has lost the bridegroom's presence by which she ought to live, lying as it were dead among the mourners, does not understand even this very thing, why she is mourned. ⟦s3⟧ *Weep not.* ⟦s4⟧ Let them bewail their dead who think them dead; where there is faith of the resurrection there is not death, but the hope of rest. ⟦s5⟧ *She is not dead,* etc. ⟦s6⟧ The girl is dead to men who cannot raise her: not to God, to whom the soul lives, and to whom the flesh rests in order to be raised.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 53.-- *They laughed him to scorn,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Rightly were they shut out, [0277A] who preferred to mock rather than to believe, unworthy to see the miracle of the resurrection.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 54.-- *Holding her hand.* ⟦s2⟧ He holds her hand, because unless the hands of the Jews (which are full of blood) shall first have been cleansed, the Synagogue will not rise. ⟦s3⟧ *Maid, arise.* ⟦s4⟧ Whosoever comes to himself from the death of the soul, Christ strengthening his hand for him, ought to rise from the filth of vices, and (as Mark says) to walk in good works, and it is necessary that he be presently filled with heavenly bread, that is, with the bread of the word of God and of the altar.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 56.-- *Whom he charged.* ⟦s2⟧ As a public offence needs a public remedy, so light and secret sins can be blotted out by a lighter penance. ⟦s3⟧ Whence the girl, lying in the house, rises with few witnesses, and these same are forbidden to publish the miracle abroad.
