## CHAPTER IX. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *It is good for us to be here.* ⟦s2⟧ He who had climbed to the mountains does not wish to descend to earthly things, [0281B] but always to persevere on high; and Peter stands in the person of those entering the kingdom of God. ⟦s3⟧ *It is good for us to be here.* ⟦s4⟧ It is good to be in the kingdom of God. ⟦s5⟧ As if he should say: We alone shall enjoy this light, not the envious Jews. ⟦s6⟧ *Three tabernacles.* ⟦s7⟧ That is, words, thoughts, works. ⟦s8⟧ *One for Moses.* ⟦s9⟧ How do they know those whom they had not seen before? ⟦s10⟧ By the majesty coming upon them, as in the resurrection to come each one will know the other, and the one will understand through charity the things desired in the other.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *There came a cloud, and overshadowed them.* ⟦s2⟧ Although Peter is ignorant out of weakness, yet the devotion of his service pleases. ⟦s3⟧ Whence also the cloud overshadows; this overshadowing is of the divine Spirit, which does not darken the affections of men, but reveals hidden things, whose perfect one is [0281C] shown when the voice is heard saying: *This is my Son.* ⟦s4⟧ Because he sought a material tabernacle, he received the shade of a cloud, that he might learn that in the resurrection the saints are to be protected not by the covering of houses, but by the glory of the Holy Spirit. ⟦s5⟧ *And they were afraid.* ⟦s6⟧ They fear, because human frailty does not endure to bear the sight of a greater glory; and by how much the greater the things a man has sought, by so much the more does he sink down to lower things, if he has been ignorant of his own measure.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *And the voice.* ⟦s2⟧ The voice of the Father, the terror being taken away, teaches Peter the truth. ⟦s3⟧ And note that, as when the Lord was baptized, so also when he was transfigured, the mystery of the Trinity is declared, because the glory which we confess in baptism we shall see in the resurrection. ⟦s4⟧ The Spirit in a dove, [0281D] here in a bright cloud, because he who now keeps the faith with a simple heart will then contemplate in the open light of vision what he believed, and by that very grace by which he shall be illuminated he shall be protected. ⟦s5⟧ *Jesus was found alone.* ⟦s6⟧ Not Elias, not Moses, but he whom alone you see: for him the tabernacle must be fixed in the heart. ⟦s7⟧ They therefore withdraw when the Lord began to be pointed out, lest the Father's voice should be thought to have been sent to the servants.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *And it came to pass on the next day, as they came down from the mountain.* ⟦s2⟧ According to the quality of merits, the Lord daily ascends for some, while by lifting them up more loftily he glorifies the perfect, whose conversation is in heaven, and instructs and teaches them of eternal things which cannot be heard by the crowd. ⟦s3⟧ For others he descends, while he comforts and teaches and chastens the earthly and the beginners. ⟦s4⟧ *There met [0282A] them a crowd.* ⟦s5⟧ On the mountain he prays, teaches, shows forth his majesty, opens the Father's voice to the apostles; coming down, he is received by the crowd, is beset by the weeping of the wretched, reproaches the sins of unbelief, drives out the evil spirits.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 39.-- *And behold a spirit seizeth him.* ⟦s2⟧ Matthew describes him as a lunatic, Mark as deaf and dumb. ⟦s3⟧ But it signifies those who are changed like the moon, never remaining in the same state, but who wax and wane through diverse vices, who neither confess the faith, nor are willing to hear the discourse of that faith.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 42.-- *And as he was coming.* ⟦s2⟧ While the boy draws near to the Lord he is dashed down, because those converted to the Lord are for the most part more grievously assailed by the demon, that either they may be brought back to vices, or that the devil may avenge himself for his own expulsion. ⟦s3⟧ As in the beginning of the Church's birth he set many [0282B] and grievous struggles against those whom he saw being withdrawn from his kingdom.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 43.-- *And he rebuked.* ⟦s2⟧ Not the boy suffering violence, but the devil doing violence is rebuked, because he who wishes to heal a sinner ought to reprove and drive out the vice, but to cherish the man by loving him, until he render him healed to the spiritual fathers of the Church.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 44.-- *Lay you up.* ⟦s2⟧ You who cleave to my discipleship, to whom I have more openly disclosed my secrets, lay up in mind the issue of the precious blood by which the world is to be redeemed, while the rest wonder at such great deeds of the divine loftiness. ⟦s3⟧ *It shall come to pass that he be delivered.* ⟦s4⟧ Among the great works of the divine power he the more often repeats the abject things of his human passion, lest, coming suddenly, it should terrify, but that, thought upon beforehand, it may be more lightly borne.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0282C]VERS. 45.-- *But they knew not.* ⟦s2⟧ This ignorance arises not so much from slowness as from love, because, being still carnal and ignorant of the mystery of the cross, they cannot believe that he whom they had known to be God was to die; but as they were accustomed to hear him speaking through figures, so also they thought that what he spoke concerning his own betrayal signified figuratively something else.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 46.-- *And a thought entered.* ⟦s2⟧ Because they had seen Peter and James and John led apart into the mountain, and that some secret had been delivered to them. ⟦s3⟧ And they thought that above the keys of the kingdom of heaven had been promised to Peter, and that the Church was to be built upon him; or that these three were set before the rest, or that Peter was set before all the apostles: or, because they had seen Peter made equal to the Lord himself in the payment of the tribute, [0282D] they judged that he was to be preferred to the rest. ⟦s4⟧ But it must be known that this question was raised both before the tribute and after the tribute was paid.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 48.-- *Whosoever shall receive a child.* ⟦s2⟧ Or simply: he teaches that Christ's poor are to be received for his honour by those who wish to be greater. ⟦s3⟧ Or he teaches that they should be little ones in malice, that they may keep the simplicity of childish innocence, so that the form of virtue which a child observes under nature's leading they themselves may follow of set purpose for the name of Christ. ⟦s4⟧ *Receiveth me.* ⟦s5⟧ He who receives an imitator of Christ receives Christ, and he who receives the image of God receives God.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 49.-- *John said.* ⟦s2⟧ John, loving God and beloved of the Lord, thinks that he is to be shut out from the benefit [0283A] who does not use the service; therefore he is taught that no one is to be kept back from the good which he has in part, but is rather to be called forward to that which he has not yet. ⟦s3⟧ Such men are judged to be admitted even for the salvation of others. ⟦s4⟧ Therefore in heretics or in bad Catholics we ought to detest and forbid, not the sacraments held in common, in which they are with us and are not against us, but the division contrary to peace and to truth, by which they are against us and do not follow the Lord with us.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 50.-- *Forbid not. ⟦s2⟧ For he that.* ⟦s3⟧ John is not reproved, because he acted out of love, but he is taught, that he may know that there is a distance between the weak and the strong, because the Lord, although he rewards the stronger, does not exclude the weak.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0283B]VERS. 54.-- *Lord, wilt thou. [n: (BED.)]* The apostles, still rude and ignorant of the measure of vengeance, etc., *as far as* such a power has been conferred by God upon holy men.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *We say that fire come down from heaven.* ⟦s2⟧ At the word of these men fire would come down from heaven, because they are the sons of thunder. ⟦s3⟧ *And consume them? ⟦s4⟧ And turning he rebuked them and said.* ⟦s5⟧ In the likeness of Elias, who both by his own hand and by fire divinely obtained put the elders of Baal to death. ⟦s6⟧ And in this, that the apostles presume to themselves the power of such a prophet, it is shown that the merit of the prophets was in them.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (AMBR.)] The apostles do not sin, who follow the law, etc., *as far as* he does not seek vengeance who does not fear.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 55.-- *You know not of what spirit you are.* ⟦s2⟧ By whose [0283C] spirit you have been sealed, whose deeds you imitate, now taking counsel mercifully, but in the future judging justly.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 56.-- *The Son of man came not to destroy souls, but to save,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Perfect virtue has no zeal for vengeance, nor is there any anger where there is the fulness of charity.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BED.)] Vengeance is not always to be taken upon those who sin, etc., *as far as* from whom in this place the fire is kept back.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 57.-- *A certain man said. [n: (ID.)]* This man, moved by the greatness of the signs, wishes to follow, etc., *as far as* and he feigned the service of a disciple, which feigning is figured by the fox.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 58.-- *The foxes have holes. [n: (ID.)]* The fox is a deceitful animal, intent upon ambushes, practising the robberies of fraud, [0283D] dwelling in holes even amid the very lodgings of men: so the heretic, not having the house of faith, draws others into his own fraud and seduces them from the faith.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 59.-- *And he said to another.* ⟦s2⟧ Note that the Lord spurned not faults but frauds, because he who rejected the fraudulent man chose the innocent one; but him whose father he knew to be dead, that father of whom it is said: *Forget thy father's house [n: (Psal. XLIV)]*, etc.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 60.-- *Suffer that the dead. [n: (BED.)]* Mystically. ⟦s2⟧ The son is not called back from his duty to his father, etc., *as far as* he ought not to bury the devil's treacheries in himself.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 61.-- *To take leave of those.* ⟦s2⟧ If a disciple about to follow God is rebuked because he wishes to take leave of his house, what [0284A] will become of those who often, with no profit, visits the houses of their own people which they had forsaken.
