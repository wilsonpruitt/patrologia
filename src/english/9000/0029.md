## CHAPTER XVII. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 31.-- *In that day.* [var: Vulg. Lc 17:31 *In illa hora*, "in that hour"] Thus far concerning this, that the kingdom, that is, Christ, comes not with observation but unlooked for: now, by what sort of men that coming ought to be awaited. ⟦s3⟧ He is on the housetop who, going beyond carnal things, lives spiritually as it were in the open air. ⟦s4⟧ His vessels [0321C] in the house are the carnal concupiscences which he had loved before, but, seeking things above, had left behind in the world, and which, with the judgment imminent, he must needs not seek again.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And he that shall be in the field.* ⟦s2⟧ What he had said, that he who is in the field ought not to turn back, lest it should seem to have been said only of those who are openly to return from the field, that is, who are to deny the Lord openly; he shows that there are many who, while they seem to keep their face toward the things before, yet look backward in mind.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *Be mindful.* ⟦s2⟧ Loth's wife signifies those who look backward in tribulation, and turn themselves away from the hope of the divine promise, and therefore she was made a statue of salt, because by admonishing men not to do the like, she seasons their hearts that they be not foolish.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *Whosoever.* ⟦s2⟧ He who shall wish to save the carnal life (by denying [0321D] Christ) in the world, shall lose it in the world to come; or he who seeks the eternal salvation of his soul, let him not hesitate to give it up unto the death of this present time.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *In that night.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, in that tribulation so dark *that they may be led into error, if it be possible, even the elect [n: (Matth. XXIV)]*. ⟦s3⟧ *Two in a bed.* ⟦s4⟧ Those, namely, who choose leisure, occupied neither with secular nor with ecclesiastical business.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 35.-- *The other shall be left.* ⟦s2⟧ He who, whether by human praise or by any corruption whatever, forsakes the state of life in which he was steeped. ⟦s3⟧ Of whom Jeremias says: *The enemies saw her, and mocked at her sabbaths [n: (Thren. I)]*. ⟦s4⟧ *There shall be two,* etc. ⟦s5⟧ Two differences, of those who follow the round of moveable things. ⟦s6⟧ Which are designated by the feminine gender, [0322A] because they are ruled by the counsel of their elders, as women by men; which nevertheless grind together in so far as out of their own business they serve the uses of the Church. ⟦s7⟧ But some only for the sake of things eternal, *who have as though not having,* [var: Vulg. 1 Cor 7:29 *et qui habent uxores, tamquam non habentes sint*, "and they that have wives, as though they had none"] some for the sake of things temporal, *who hope in the uncertainty of riches [n: (I Tim VI)]*.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *Who said to them.* ⟦s2⟧ To those asking two things he answers one, namely that the saints are to be taken up with him; the other he intimates by subjoining, namely that the evil, driven back from him, are to be damned with the devil. ⟦s3⟧ *Wheresoever,* etc. ⟦s4⟧ Wheresoever the Lord shall be bodily, thither shall the elect be gathered, who by humbly imitating his passion are filled as it were from his flesh, whose youth shall be renewed as the eagle's through the resurrection.
