## CHAPTER ONE.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The beginning of the Gospel. [n: (ISID.)]* The Gospel, *good tidings,* which belongs properly to the kingdom of God and to the remission of sins. ⟦s2⟧ Whence: *Repent ye and believe the Gospel: and the kingdom of heaven hath drawn nigh.* [var: Vulg. Mc 1:15 has *regnum Dei*, "the kingdom of God," and sets the two members in the other order, the kingdom's approach before the summons.]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] The four Gospels are one, and the one is four. ⟦s2⟧ And so the book of Mark also is called the Gospel, and likewise that of the others: because all are one, and one is all.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of Jesus Christ. [n: (HIER.)]* Jesus in Hebrew, Soter in Greek, Salvator in Latin, Christus in Greek, Messias in Hebrew, anointed in Latin, that is, king and priest: he is said [0179B] to be of the line of David the king, and of the Levitical line.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Of the Son of God. [n: (BEDA.)]* Matthew says: *of the son of David, the son of Abraham [n: (Matth. I)]*. ⟦s2⟧ Mark says: *of the Son of God,* so that little by little from the lesser to the greater, etc., *as far as* John begins from the eternity of the Word of God, and ends in the resurrection of the Lord.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *As it is written.* ⟦s2⟧ Mark sets the testimonies of the prophets first, by which he asserts that these things are old and not new, but foreknown and foretold by the prophets, and in this he invites also the Jews, who had received the law and the prophets, to the Gospel: and he calls the Gentiles, who held the Gospel, to the veneration of the law and the prophets, that no one, after the heretics, may receive the one without the other.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Behold, I send. [n: (BEDA.)]* John is called an angel not [0179C] by fellowship of nature, as Origen falsely pretends, but by the dignity of his office: as the priests also are, of whom it is said: *The lips of the priest keep knowledge, and they seek the law out of his mouth, because he is the angel of the Lord of hosts [n: (Mal. II)]*.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Note that this testimony: *Behold, I send my angel,* etc., is Malachi's, yet he says that both are Isaiah's, because, etc., *as far as* all the prophets spoke by one spirit as with one mouth.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Of one crying. [n: (ISID.)]* To the Jews, deaf because of their sins, set far off, worthy of a cry of indignation. ⟦s2⟧ *In the desert.* ⟦s3⟧ In the desert the voice and the cry are made: because the house of Judaea is deserted, standing empty and swept, without the Spirit of God, without prophet, king, priest. ⟦s4⟧ Whence Jesus and John seek what was lost [0179D] in the desert. ⟦s5⟧ Where the devil conquered, there he is conquered: where man fell, there he rises again.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Prepare ye. [n: (ISID.)]* Do penance and preach. ⟦s2⟧ Whence the Parasceve before the sabbath, etc., *as far as* that we, being at rest, may possess the desirable land.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The way of the Lord. [n: (HIER.)]* The way of the Lord by which he enters in unto men is penance, through which God comes down to us, and we ascend to him. ⟦s2⟧ Whence the angels are remembered as ascending and descending upon the Son of man.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Make straight the paths. [n: (ISID.)]* He commands that, going by the royal [0180A] road, we love our neighbours as ourselves, and ourselves, etc., *as far as* that the mouth be not sundered from the work, nor the work from the mouth.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The paths come after the way, because the moral commandments are expounded after penance, according to Mark or Matthew. ⟦s2⟧ The way is prepared by faith and baptism and penance.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The paths are made straight by the austere tokens of raiment, of girdle, of food, of drink, of a most humble voice.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *John. [n: (ISID.)]* The grace of God. ⟦s2⟧ He begins from the grace of God, whence there follows: *Baptizing,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ Through baptism sins are forgiven; whence: That which *ye have freely received, freely give [n: (Matth. X)]*. ⟦s4⟧ And the Apostle: *By grace ye are saved [n: (Ephes. II)]*.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Baptizing. [n: (HIER.)]* He gave the baptism of penance, he preached a baptism unto the remission of sins, [0180B] yet he did not give it. ⟦s2⟧ As by preaching, etc., *as far as* now the bride is brought in by the friend of the bridegroom, as Rebecca to Isaac, veiled by the servant with a white cloak.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *In the Jordan [n: (ID.)]* Jordan is the descent of another, where sins are loosed. ⟦s2⟧ For the ark, etc., *as far as* the body of Christ profits not those who are not cleansed, but changes them for the worse.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Confessing,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *[n: (ID.)] Confession and beauty are in his sight [n: (Psal. CXV)]*, that is, in the sight of the bridegroom. ⟦s3⟧ Whence the bride alights from her camel, when the Church humbles herself to Christ, who is figured by Isaac, at whose birth God made laughter for Sara. ⟦s4⟧ As it is said to Mary, who is a princess with God: *Blessed art thou among women [n: (Luc. I)]*.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0180C] [n: (BEDA.)] Confessing their sins, they are baptized. ⟦s2⟧ For they ought to promise the newness of a better life, who baptism, etc., *as far as* and so change those who are cleansed and promise the Christian religion into members of the Church.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *And John was,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] The prophet's raiment and food and drink signify the whole austere life of preachers, and the Gentiles, etc., *as far as* because all things are from the grace of the Gospel, in which there is no distinction of Jew and Greek.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *[n: (BEDA.)] Clothed with camel's hair.* ⟦s2⟧ Because he did not cherish sins, but rebuked them sharply, saying: *Ye brood of vipers,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ The leathern girdle, etc., *as far as* for in the honey is sweetness, in the locust a nimble flight, but one soon falling.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Mightier than I. [n: (HIER.)]* Who is mightier than [0180D] the grace by which sins are washed away? ⟦s2⟧ He who, etc., *as far as* and it reaches even to the hundred and forty-four thousand.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To loose the latchet. [n: (BEDA.)]* There was a custom among the ancients, that if any man were unwilling to take to wife her who fell to him, he, etc., *as far as* I am not able to bare the feet of the Redeemer, because I claim not for myself the name of bridegroom.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] The latchet of the shoes, that is, the mystery of the Incarnation of God. ⟦s2⟧ For the heel, etc., *as far as* the bride among the patriarchs, the friend among the prophets, the neighbour [0181A] in Mary and Joseph, the beloved in John the Baptist, the dove in Christ and the apostles.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *I have baptized you with water. [n: (BEDA.)]* John does not yet say openly that he is God or the Son of God, because his hearers were untaught: but little by little, through the humanity glorified, he leads them to faith in the divinity, saying: *But he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost.* ⟦s2⟧ Yet afterwards, when he saw them now more capable, he preaches the Son of God openly, saying: *He gave testimony, that this is the Son of God [n: (Joan. III)]*.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *[n: (ISID.)] I baptize you with water, he,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ The river of God is always full, always equal, of which: *The stream of the river maketh the city of God joyful [n: (Psal. XLV)]*. ⟦s3⟧ There is a great distance, etc., *as far as* the anointing of Christ comes from the bitterness of the passion to the glory of the resurrection; [0181B] whence the Apostle: *Have this mind in you, which was also in Christ Jesus,* etc., even to: *For which cause God also hath exalted him [n: (Phil. II)]*.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *And he was baptized in the Jordan.* ⟦s2⟧ For three causes: that he might fulfil justice and humility; that he might approve the baptism of John; that, sanctifying the waters of Jordan by the descent of the dove, he might show the Holy Ghost to be present in baptism.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] Mark: As the hart, longing for the fountains of waters, gives leaps, crops the tops of the meadows, bears palms upon his crown, so, like the honey-flowing bee, he tastes but lightly the flowers of the field (to which the odour of the true Isaac is likened). ⟦s2⟧ Whence Jesus coming from Nazareth of Galilee to the baptism, etc., *as far as: To him that overcometh I will give to eat of the tree of life, which is in the paradise of my God [n: (Apoc. II)]*.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0181C]VERS. 10.-- *He saw the heavens opened,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] The mystery of the Trinity is shown in the baptism: the Son is baptized, the Spirit is in the dove, the Father sounds in the voice. ⟦s2⟧ The heavens are opened, etc., *as far as* the Spirit shall abide with us and shall be in us according to the measure of faith. ⟦s3⟧ But of Christ it is said: *Full of grace and truth [n: (Joan. I)]*.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Remaining in him.* ⟦s2⟧ The Spirit remains in him, not from the time he was baptized, but from the time he was conceived. ⟦s3⟧ But that he descended at the baptism signifies that spiritual grace is given to us in baptism, to whom, being regenerated of water and the Spirit unto the remission of sins, a fuller grace of the Spirit is wont to be given from heaven by the imposition of the bishop's hand.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Thou art my beloved Son. [n: (BEDA.)]* [0181D] He is not taught what he knew not, but it is shown to us what we ought to believe, namely that he, etc., *as far as* and rightly did the Spirit descend in the likeness of a dove, which is a simple creature and without gall: because he seeks the simple, nor does he dwell in impure minds.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *The Spirit. [n: (ID.)]* Lest it be doubtful which spirit, Luke deliberately sets before it: *Jesus being full of the Holy Ghost returned from the Jordan;* then he added: *and was led by the Spirit into the desert, lest an unclean* spirit be thought to have had any power against him.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Forty days. [n: (ID.)]* It signifies the whole time in which the devil tempts his members. ⟦s2⟧ For the fourfold world, by the observance of the ten commandments, serves the Lord in striving against the devil. [0182A] And four times ten make forty. ⟦s3⟧ Everywhere and always the adversary is at hand, who ceases not to hinder our journey.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And he was with the beasts.* ⟦s2⟧ We also, if in the desert of holy conversation we bear with undefiled mind the bestial manners of men, deserve the mystery of the angels, by whom, loosed from the body, we may be carried over into heaven

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *But after that. [n: (ISID.)]* We have received grace for grace, things carnal are beheaded, things spiritual are quickened. ⟦s2⟧ The shadow ceasing, the truth is at hand. ⟦s3⟧ John in prison, the law in Judaea, Jesus in Galilee, salvation among the Gentiles.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Preaching,* etc. ⟦s2+3⟧ *[n: (HIER.)] Blessed are the poor in spirit, for they shall possess [n: (Matth. V)]*, etc. [var: Vulg. Mt 5:3 reads *quoniam ipsorum est regnum caelorum*, "for theirs is the kingdom of heaven"; *ipsi possidebunt* belongs to the next beatitude, Mt 5:4, "Blessed are the meek, for they shall possess the land."] To the earthly kingdom [0182B] succeeds poverty, and to the poverty of Christians an everlasting kingdom; whence: *My kingdom is not of this world [n: (Joan. XVIII)]*: for every earthly honour is froth, smoke, a dream.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Repent ye. [n: (HIER.)]* The sweetness of the fruit makes up for the bitterness of the root: the hope of the harbour delights away the perils of the sea, the hope of health softens the pain of the medicine. ⟦s2⟧ He who desires the kernel, let him break the nut: let him do penance who wishes to cleave to the eternal good. ⟦s3⟧ They can tell forth the praises of Christ who have deserved to come to the palm of pardon. ⟦s4⟧ Whence after penance preachers are chosen.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *He saw Simon. [n: (HIER.)]* Simon, *obedient;* Andrew, *manly;* James, *supplanter;* John, *grace.* ⟦s2⟧ By these names, etc., *as far as* by prudence [0182C] we obey, by justice we act manfully, by temperance we tread upon serpents, by fortitude we merit the grace of God.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Whom does worldly eloquence not intoxicate? ⟦s2⟧ With difficulty do men that are powerful, and noble, and rich, and with much more difficulty than these the eloquent, believe God. ⟦s3⟧ Their mind is blinded by riches and wealth and luxury, and, encompassed about with vices, they cannot see the virtues, and they judge the simplicity of Holy Scripture not from the majesty of its meanings, but from the serviceableness of its words.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] Four fishermen are called, Simon, Andrew, James and John: leaving all things they follow. ⟦s2⟧ By this chariot we are borne to the upper air, etc., *as far as* we are covered with the skins of Solomon, in which the bride [0182D] glories that she is beautiful.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BEDA.)] It is asked how he called first Peter and Andrew, and after a little James and John, as he relates, etc., *as far as* for then they followed him, not with the ships drawn up to land as though with a care of returning, but as one calling and commanding.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And they enter in. [n: (HIER.)]* Mark, disposing the sayings of the Gospel within himself, not following the order of the history, keeps the order of the mysteries, whence on the sabbath he relates the first miracle, saying: *And they enter in,* etc.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Capharnaum. [n: (ID.)]* Capharnaum is the village of consolation, the sabbath is rest. ⟦s2⟧ The man in an unclean spirit, [0183A] the human race, in which is uncleanness, etc., *as far as* Pharao, let go by Israel, we pursue him, the devil, despised, rises up into scandals.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BEDA.)] That he heals and teaches chiefly on the sabbath shows that he is not under the law, but above it: and he teaches us the true sabbath, that, being zealous namely for the salvation of souls, we may abstain from every servile work, that is, from all unlawful things.
