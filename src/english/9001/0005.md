## CHAPTER III. (cont.)

[0193A]VERS. 22.-- *And the Scribes. [n: (BEDA.)]* If any through slowness of mind do not understand the word of God, there yet remains a hope of salvation; if perchance they should understand, none. But for those who are unwilling to understand and who pervert what they do understand, there is no hope. Whence, *Scribes,* that is, the wise, etc. *From Jerusalem.* Which signifies that he was to be persecuted by his own fellow-citizens even unto death. We read above that a crowd followed him from Galilee and Jerusalem, and from beyond Jordan and about Tyre and Sidon, which are cities of the Gentiles: and the Scribes from Jerusalem were blaspheming: because the crowd of the people of the Jews conducted him with palms and praises; the Gentiles longed for him; the Scribes and the elders of the people were treating of his murder. *He hath Beelzebub.* Man of flies, or one having flies: by which name they surnamed the prince [0193B] of demons, on account of the filth of the blood of the sacrifices.

VERS. 23.-- *How can Satan,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] Let them choose which they will. If Satan cannot cast out Satan, they say nothing against the Lord. But if he can, let them withdraw from his kingdom, which, being divided, cannot stand.

VERS. 27.-- *And then shall he plunder his house. [n: (ID.)]* He plundered the house, because he distributed all the parts of the world possessed by the devil to the apostles and to their successors, as provinces parted out among them, that they might convert the peoples to the faith.

VERS. 29.-- *But he that shall blaspheme. [n: (HIER.)]* Because he does not deserve to do penance so as to be received, who, understanding Christ, was [0193C] saying that he was the prince of demons, etc., *as far as: For he that shall do and teach, he shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven [n: (Matth. V)]*.

[n: (BEDA.)] Matthew says [n: (Matth. XII)]: *He that shall blaspheme against the Holy Ghost or the word,* etc. Where he shows that certain things are revealed in the world to come. Which is to be understood of the least matters, as of an idle word, of excessive laughter; for which even this life has earned pardon.

*For ever.* This against Origen, who promises pardon to all sinners after the judgment, when countless ages have passed.

*He shall be guilty of an everlasting sin. [n: (BEDA.)]* Not because [it is said] that he is not a spirit: or that he is, but is not God: or that he is God, but less than the Father and the Son: because this comes not of diabolical envy, but of human ignorance, etc.

[0193D]VERS. 31.-- *Brethren. [n: (HIER.)]* Not sons of Mary ever Virgin, as Helvidius lies: nor of Joseph by another wife, as others prate, but kinsmen.

[n: (BEDA.)] The mother and the brethren, the people of the Jews of whom Christ is according to the flesh, stand outside in the literal sense. The crowd within the house, the Gentiles in the spiritual mystery. The Jews wish to see Christ outside, because, not seeking the spiritual understanding, they rather compel or seek him to come out to teach carnal things.

VERS. 33.-- *Who is my mother. [n: (ID.)]* He does not refuse obedience to a mother, who says: *Honour father and mother [n: (Exod. XX)]*: but he shows that he owes more to his Father's mysteries than to a mother's affections, and he showed by example the same thing which he says in word: *He that loveth father or [0194A] mother more than me, is not worthy of me [n: (Matth. X)]*. Nor does he contemn his brethren injuriously, but he sets the spiritual work before kinship, teaching that the bond of hearts is better than the bond of bodies.

VERS. 34.-- *Behold my mother,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] That we may know that we are both his brethren and his mother, if we shall have done the will of his Father, so that we may be co-heirs, because he discerns not by sexes but by deeds. Sister and brother by believing, mother by preaching, while through preaching the love of Christ, as it were Christ himself, is begotten in the heart of the hearer.
