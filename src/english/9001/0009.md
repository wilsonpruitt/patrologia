## CHAPTER VI. (cont.)

VERS. 47.-- *And he alone,* etc. Who sometimes seems to have forsaken the Church altogether in her perils; whence: *Why, O Lord, hast thou retired afar off [n: (Psal. X)]* etc. But because he defers his help, he does not take away his succour.

VERS. 48.-- *And about the fourth. [n: (BEDA.)]* The military stations and watches are divided by a space of hours: it is plain therefore that they, etc., *as far as* for the morning star is [0204C] said to give light for three hours of the night, that is, for the whole morning watch.

*And he would have passed by them. [n: (ID.)]* That for an hour, namely, being troubled: but straightway set free, they might the more marvel at the miracle of their deliverance, and to their deliverer, etc., *as far as* When thou shalt walk in the fire, thou shalt not be burnt, and the flame shall not burn in thee;

VERS. 49.-- *They thought. [n: (BEDA.)]* Even yet heretics think that he is a phantasm, and that he did not take true flesh of the Virgin. For Theodorus, etc., *as far as* and how, with feet not sinking in, having a bodily weight and a material burden, he walked upon the moist and unstable substance.

VERS. 50.-- *Fear ye not.* The first relief is to drive away fear; the second, to quell the tempest by the power of his own [0204D] presence. Nor is it wonderful that, when the Lord goes up into the ship, the wind ceases: because in whatever heart he is present through the grace of his love, straightway all the wars of the vices and of the opposing world and of malignant spirits are stilled.

VERS. 52.-- *For they understood not. [n: (BEDA.)]* By the miracle of the loaves he showed that he was the founder of things. By walking upon the waves, that he had his whole body free from every heaviness of sin. By calming the winds and the waves he showed that he had dominion over the elements. But the disciples, being yet carnal, were astonished indeed at the greatness of the mighty works, but did not know the truth of the divine majesty.

VERS. 54.-- *They knew him.* By report, not by his face nor by [0205A] the greatness of his signs: he was also known by his countenance to very many.

VERS. 56.-- *The hem of his garment. [n: (BEDA.)]* The least commandment, which whosoever shall have transgressed shall be called least in the kingdom of heaven: or the assumption of flesh through which we come to the Word of God.
