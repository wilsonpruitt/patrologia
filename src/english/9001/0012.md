## CHAPTER VIII. (cont.)

*The kingdom of God,* etc. That is, the Church raised up against the glory of this world. Or the blessedness to come in heaven, which they saw upon the mountain. Which was done by a merciful provision, that the eternal joy, though foretasted for a brief moment, might the more strongly hearten them to bear adversities.
