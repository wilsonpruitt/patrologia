## CHAPTER VIII. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The kingdom of God,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That is, the Church raised up against the glory of this world. ⟦s3⟧ Or the blessedness to come in heaven, which they saw upon the mountain. ⟦s4⟧ Which was done by a merciful provision, that the eternal joy, though foretasted for a brief moment, might the more strongly hearten them to bear adversities.
