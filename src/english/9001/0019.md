## CHAPTER XIV.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Now it was the pasch. [n: (HIER.)]* Pascha is interpreted a passing over, but phase an immolation, etc., *as far as* and armed with virtue let us eat, saying: *For Christ our pasch is sacrificed [n: (Matth. XXVI)]*.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (BEDA.)] Pascha, which in Hebrew is phase, from a passing over, [0229A] not from suffering, etc., *as far as* it admonishes us, namely, to undertake the way of the virtues. ⟦s2⟧ *And the chief priests sought. [n: (HIER.)]* Iniquity went forth from the princes, who ought to have made ready the temple, and the vessels, and to have purified themselves according to the law for the eating of the lamb.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Not on the festival day. [n: (ID.)]* They shun the festival day, and that befits them: there is no festival for those who have lost life and mercy.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *And when he was in Bethany. [n: (ID.)]* The young hart of the harts always returns to his own bed, that is, the Son obedient to the Father even unto death: he asks obedience of us. ⟦s2⟧ For Simon is interpreted obedient. ⟦s3⟧ *Of Simon the leper.* ⟦s4⟧ Simon the leper signifies the world unbelieving at first, afterwards believing. ⟦s5⟧ *And as he was at meat, there came,* etc. ⟦s6⟧ He himself reclining, that is, humbling himself, that the faith of the sinful woman [0229B] might touch him: which faith goes up from the feet to the head, and comes down from the head through faith, that is, from Christ to his members.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *A woman. [n: (BEDA.)]* Mary Magdalen, the sister of Lazarus whom the Lord raised up, etc., *as far as* she poured holy oil upon him, as Matthew and Mark relate.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *An alabaster box.* ⟦s2⟧ Alabaster is a white marble marked with divers drops, and it keeps ointments uncorrupted. ⟦s3⟧ *Of spikenard.* ⟦s4⟧ Nard is called pistic, that is, mixed, because it was compounded not of the root only, but of the spikes and of the leaves: which is the more precious.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And breaking. [n: (HIER.)]* The house filled with the odour is heaven and earth. ⟦s2⟧ The broken alabaster is carnal desire, which is broken at the head: from which the whole body is framed together. [n: (BED.)] The devotion of Mary designates the faith and [0229C] the piety of the Church, etc., *as far as* she venerates him with pious preaching and with devout observances.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And there were some. [n: (ID.)]* By synecdoche he put the plural for the singular, etc., *as far as* whose habit of thieving he took care for this cause to make known. ⟦s2⟧ *To what purpose is this waste. [n: (HIER.)]* He that was lost found perdition out of salvation, so that in a fruitful fig tree he might light upon the noose of death.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *For this ointment might have been. [n: (ID.)]* Under the pretext of avarice he speaks the mystery of faith, etc., *as far as* let us divide the spoils at evening.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *She hath wrought a good work. [n: (ID.)]* He who believes in God, it is reputed to him unto a work of justice. ⟦s2⟧ For it is one thing to believe him, another to believe in him, that is, to cast oneself wholly upon him.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0229D]VERS. 7.-- *But me you have not always. [n: (BEDA.)]* Joined to you by bodily presence and familiarity as now. ⟦s2⟧ Whence the Apostle: *Although we have known Christ according to the flesh, yet now we know him [n: (II Cor. V)]* [var: Vulg. 2 Cor. 5:16 *sed nunc jam non novimus*, "but now we know him so no longer"]. ⟦s3⟧ But spiritually he is always with us, whence: *Behold I am with you even to the consummation of the world all days. [n: (HIER.)]* Thus is it to be understood: But me you shall not always have in bodily presence, as before in companionship and familiarity.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *What she had.* ⟦s2⟧ What you think to be a waste of the ointment is the office of burial. ⟦s3⟧ Nor is it a wonder if she has given me the good odour of faith, since I am about to shed my blood for her.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Amen I say to you. [n: (BEDA.)]* Note in Christ [0230A] the knowledge of things to come, those about to suffer after a few days, he foreknows that his Gospel is to be preached in the whole world. ⟦s2⟧ It is to be noted that, as Mary through the whole world in which the Church is spread abroad earned glory for the loving service which she devoutly rendered to the Lord: so also Judas, who disparaged her, is defamed far and wide with the mark of perfidy. ⟦s3⟧ But the Lord rewards the good with loving praise, and is silent about the coming reproaches of the wicked man.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *One of the twelve. [n: (HIER.)]* One in number, not in merit, in name not in divine power, in body not in mind, whence the bridegroom to the bride: *Thou hast wounded my heart, my sister, with one of thy eyes, and with one hair of thy neck [n: (Cant. IV)]*. ⟦s2⟧ The eye and the hair are wisdom and virtue, since it is said to Judas with the rest: *To you it is given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God [n: (Matth. XIII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ And: [0230B] *I have given you power [n: (Luc. X)]*, etc.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *He went to,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He went away to the princes: and after he went out, Satan entered into him. ⟦s3⟧ Here *night to night showeth knowledge. ⟦s4⟧ Every living thing is joined to that which is like itself [n: (Matth. XXVIII)]*. ⟦s5⟧ Mary runs to the apostles, that day to day may utter speech; Judas to the Jews, that night to night may show knowledge.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *And he sought how he might,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] He promises to betray him, as his master had said before: To thee will I give all this power. ⟦s2⟧ *BEDA.* ⟦s3⟧ Many today shudder at the crime of Judas and yet do not beware of it, etc., *as far as* that, the witnesses being removed, they may exchange truth for a lie, and virtue for a crime.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And on the first day of the unleavened bread. [n: (Id.)]* On the fourteenth [0230C] moon, when, the leaven being cast away, the lamb was slain at evening, etc., *as far as* he consecrated the beginning of that very immolation, that is, of his own passion.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ISID.)] With bitterness is the unleavened bread eaten, which is our redemption. ⟦s2⟧ For the bitterness is the passion of the Lord.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Go into the city. [n: (BED.)]* It is a token of the foreknowledge of his godhead, etc., *as far as* and that the baptism of the life-giving font may be consecrated for the taking away of the crimes of the world. [n: (HIER.)] The city is the Church, which is girt with the wall of faith: the man who meets them is the primitive people, the pitcher of water the law of the letter. ⟦s2⟧ *A man carrying a pitcher of water. [n: (BEDA.)]* Of set purpose are the names both of the water-bearer and of the master of the house passed over in silence, that it may be signified that the opportunity is to be given to all who wish to keep the true pasch, that is, to be steeped in the sacraments of Christ, and who seek to receive him [0230D] into the lodging of their own mind.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Follow ye. [n: (HIER.)]* He who leads on high, where the refreshment of Christ is, whence Rahab charges the spies to go not by the low places but by the heights.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *A large upper room. [n: (ID.)]* The great Church, which declares the name of the Lord, strewn with the variety of the virtues and of tongues, whence: *Surrounded with variety [n: (Psal. XLIV)]* of virtues, in which the pasch is prepared for the Lord. ⟦s2⟧ The master of the house is Peter the apostle, to whom the Lord entrusted his house: that there may be one faith under one shepherd. [n: (BED.)] The upper room is the spiritual law, which, going out from the straits of the letter, receives the Saviour in a lofty place, etc., *as far as* because it knows that all the sacraments of the pasch [0231A] and the other decrees of the law are his sacraments.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *And in the evening. [n: (HIER.)]* The evening of the day points to the evening of the world. ⟦s2⟧ For about the eleventh hour come the last: who first receive the penny of eternal life: because before the cross Abraham was in hell, and after the cross the thief was in paradise.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Amen I say,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As he had foretold of his passion: so he foretells of the betrayer, giving place for repentance, that when he had understood that his thoughts were foreknown by God, he might repent of his deed. ⟦s3⟧ Yet he does not point him out by name, lest, being openly refuted, he become more shameless. ⟦s4⟧ He casts the crime into the number: that he who is conscious of it may do penance. ⟦s5⟧ *One of you.* ⟦s6⟧ While falsehood is reproved, truth is proved and fulfilled. ⟦s7⟧ *He that did eat [0231B] my bread, hath greatly supplanted me [n: (Psal. XL)]*. ⟦s8⟧ All are touched, that harmony may be made in the harp. ⟦s9⟧ All the strings, being well strung, answer with a concordant voice: *Is it I, Lord?* ⟦s10⟧ One, slack and moved by the love of money, said: *Is it I, Rabbi?*

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *To be made sorrowful.* ⟦s2⟧ As the eleven, because they had thought nothing evil against the Lord: but they believe their master more than themselves, and fearing their own frailty, sorrowful they ask concerning their own sin, of which they had no consciousness.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *One of the twelve. [n: (HIER.)]* The wolf separates apart the sheep which he desires. ⟦s2⟧ The sheep which goes out of the fold lies open to the bites of the wolf. ⟦s3⟧ *He that dippeth. [n: (BEDA.)]* Wonderful is the patience of the Lord. ⟦s4⟧ First he said: *One of you shall betray me,* the betrayer persists in his evil. ⟦s5⟧ He reproves more openly: [0231C] and yet he is not named outright. ⟦s6⟧ Judas, when the others are made sorrowful and draw back their hand from the food, with the rashness and shamelessness with which he betrays the Lord; puts his hand also with his master into the dish, that by boldness he might feign a good conscience.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *But woe. [n: (ID.)]* For ever woe to that man who comes unworthily to the table of the Lord, etc., *as far as* the punishment is foretold, that he who is not overcome by shame may be corrected by fear.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *As they were eating. [n: (ID.)]* The old pasch being ended, which was kept in commemoration of the deliverance of the people of God from Egypt, etc., *as far as* and therefore that he was to be raised the more swiftly from death. ⟦s2⟧ *He took. [n: (HIER.)]* Figuring his own body in the bread, etc., *as far as* the enemies are extinguished, which are the mysteries of the Church of Christ. [0231D] [n: (BEDA.)] The bread which strengthens the heart of man, etc., *as far as* or that we are saved without his passion.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *Thanks.* ⟦s2⟧ He gave thanks and blessed, being now near to his passion, he who takes upon himself the punishment of another's iniquity: who did nothing worthy of suffering: that he might show with how even a mind every man ought to bear the scourges of his own fault.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They had drunk. [n: (HIER.)]* Happy drunkenness: saving fulness, etc., *as far as* for the blood of the New Testament which is shed for many cleanses not all.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Now I will not drink. [n: (ID.)]* Here he changes the sacrifice, but he does not change the time; so that we should never keep the supper of Jesus before the fourteenth moon. ⟦s2⟧ He who makes the resurrection on the fourteenth, keeps the supper of the Lord on the [0232A] eleventh, which has never been found either in the New or in the Old Testament. ⟦s3⟧ *The vine. [n: (BEDA.)]* The vine or the vineyard of the Lord is the Synagogue, etc., *as far as* I shall be filled with a new joy together with you over the salvation of that same people regenerated by baptism.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26. ⟦s2⟧ *And a hymn being said. [n: (ID.)]* It can be understood as the hymn which, according to John, he sang, giving thanks to the Father, etc., *as far as* and that we ought to ascend by the graces of the Holy Spirit with which we are anointed in the heart. ⟦s3⟧ *Unto the mount of Olives. [n: (HIER.)]* On Mount Olivet Jesus is held fast: and thence he ascends to the heavens: that we may know that we ascend to the heavens from that place where we watch, and pray, and are bound, and do not fight back on the earth.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *You shall all be scandalized. [n: (BEDA.)]* He foretells what they shall suffer: that when they have suffered they may not despair, but [0232B] repent.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *In this night. ⟦s2⟧ They who are drunk, are drunk in the night [n: (I Thess. V)]*, and they who are scandalized, are scandalized in the night, that is, being darkened in mind. [n: (HIER.)] All fall, but not all lie prostrate. ⟦s3⟧ *Because he sleepeth, shall he not rise again? [n: (Zach. XIII.)]* To fall is carnal: to lie prostrate is devilish. ⟦s4⟧ *I will strike the shepherd.* ⟦s5⟧ Strike the shepherd. ⟦s6⟧ The prophet asks for the passion of the Lord: the Father answers: *I will strike the shepherd.* ⟦s7⟧ At the prayers of the saints the Son is sent by the Father: that is, he is made flesh and is struck, and suffers: the sheep are scattered, the shepherd being taken. ⟦s8⟧ The resurrection is promised, that hope be not extinguished.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 29.-- *But Peter. [n: (ID.)]* A bird without feathers strives to fly on high: but the body weighs down the soul, so that, [0232C] by the fear of a human death, the fear of the Lord is overcome.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *The cock shall twice give voice. [n: (BEDA.)]* Others simply: *Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny me thrice,* etc., *as far as* as also that word: *He that shall look on a woman to lust after, hath already committed adultery with her [n: (Matth. V)]*. [n: (HIER.)] The cock crows: Peter denies thrice, etc., *as far as* and he washed away the filth of the threefold denial with tears.
