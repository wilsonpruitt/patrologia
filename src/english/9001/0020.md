## CHAPTER XIV. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *Gethsemani. [n: (ID.)]* That is, the valley of fat ones, where fat bulls beset him and many calves surrounded him. ⟦s2⟧ *Sit ye here. [n: (ISID.)]* They are separated in prayer who are separated in the passion: because he prays, while they sleep, weighed down by the fatness of the heart. ⟦s3⟧ *Till I pray. [n: (BED.)]* When the Lord prays on the mountain, etc., *as far as* whence: *He humbled himself, becoming obedient unto death. ⟦s4⟧ And greater love than this [0232D] no man hath, that a man lay down his life for his friends [n: (Phil. II; Joan. XV)]*.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 33.-- *And he began to fear. [n: (HIER.)]* We are taught to fear and to be sorrowful before the judgment of death, we who cannot say of ourselves, save through him: *The prince of this world cometh, and in me he hath not anything [n: (Joan. XIV)]*.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *My soul is sorrowful,* etc. [n: (BED.)] He is not sorrowful because of death, etc., *as far as* but from the sleep of unbelief and from the torpor of the mind.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 35.-- *And he prayed. [n: (ID.)]* This voice is the sound of our infirmity, etc., *as far as* but because it will not be otherwise, *Not what I will, but what thou wilt.*

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] This is against the Eutychians, who say that there is in Christ only one operation, one will: [0233A] but here he shows the human, which through the infirmity of the flesh refuses the passion, and the divine, which is ready to accomplish the dispensation.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *But not what I will. [n: (HIER.)]* Even to the end he ceases not to teach us to obey our fathers: and to set their will before our own.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *And he says to Peter,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He who had said: *And if it behove me to die with thee, I will not deny thee: even if all shall be scandalized, yet not I [n: (Matth. XXVI)]*; now, through the greatness of his sorrow, cannot overcome sleep.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 38.-- *That ye enter not.* ⟦s2⟧ He does not say, that ye be not tempted, but *that ye enter not into temptation,* that is, that temptation may not overcome you, may not hold you within its nets.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (HIER.)] He enters into temptation who neglects to pray. ⟦s2⟧ Thrice the disciples sleep, thrice the Lord in prayer [0233B] rouses them. ⟦s3⟧ The threefold sleeping signifies the three dead whom the Lord raised up. ⟦s4⟧ The first in the house, the second at the sepulchre, the third out of the sepulchre. ⟦s5⟧ The Lord's threefold watching teaches us that we have three persons in praying, and to ask pardon both for things past and for things present and for things to come. ⟦s6⟧ *But the flesh is weak. [n: (BEDA.)]* For the flesh by its own weight ever draws to the lowest things.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 42.-- *Arise.* ⟦s2⟧ After he had prayed the third time, he taught that the fear of the apostles was to be corrected by penance, and goes untroubled to the passion, saying: *Arise, let us go.* ⟦s3⟧ As if he should say, let them not find us fearing, but let us go of our own accord to meet them, so that, we being about to suffer, they may see joy and confidence.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 44.-- *A sign. [n: (HIER.)]* He gives the sign of a kiss with the venom of the devil: as Cain offered a sacrifice [0233C] guileful and reprobate, whence they set wine with vinegar upon the cross.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 45.-- *Rabbi. [n: (BEDA.)]* With shameless and wicked assurance he calls him master, etc., *as far as* he fulfils that also: *With them that hated peace [n: (Psal. CXIX)]*, etc.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 47.-- *One. [n: (ID.)]* Peter, according to John, with the same ardour of mind as in the rest. ⟦s2⟧ For he knew that Phinees, by punishing the sacrilegious, had received the reward of justice and of a perpetual priesthood. ⟦s3⟧ Luke says: *That the Lord touched the ear and healed it [n: (Luc. XXII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ For he himself never forgets loving-kindness, and suffers not even his own enemies to be wounded. ⟦s5⟧ Mystically: teaching that they who in consenting to his death contracted a wound of the soul can, if they have worthily repented, deserve [0233D] salvation.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 48.-- *As to a robber. [n: (ID.)]* As if he should say: It is foolish to seize with swords and clubs one who delivers himself up to you of his own accord: and by night, as though he were in hiding, to hunt him out by means of a traitor, when daily he teaches in the temple. ⟦s2⟧ But therefore you gather against me in darkness, because your power is in darkness.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 50.-- *Then,* etc. [n: (ID.)] That is fulfilled which he said, that all should be scandalized, etc., *as far as* and they that were to be called back by God learned to give to their adversaries.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 52.-- *But he,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As Joseph, leaving his cloak behind, fled naked out of the hands of the unchaste mistress. ⟦s3⟧ But whoever wishes to escape the hands of the wicked, let him leave in mind the things that are of the world, and flee after Jesus. [0234A] *From them.* ⟦s4⟧ Whose presence he loathed, and whose deeds. ⟦s5⟧ Not from the Lord, whose love, though absent in body, he kept fixed in his mind.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 53.-- *The high priest,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Caiphas, namely, who according to John was the high priest of that year, concerning whom Josephus writes to the same effect, that he had bought the high priesthood for himself from the Roman prince without any merit of worth: what wonder then, if an unjust high priest judges unjustly?

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 54.-- *Afar off. [n: (BEDA.)]* Because he was near to denial, had he been near to Christ he would not have denied: in this, nevertheless, he is to be admired, that he did not leave the Lord even though he fears. ⟦s2⟧ That he fears is of nature, that he follows is of devotion: that he denies is of stealth, that he repents is of faith. ⟦s3⟧ *The court. [n: (HIER.)]* The court is the worldly [0234B] circuit. ⟦s4⟧ The servants are the demons. ⟦s5⟧ The fire is fleshly desire, and he who abides with these is not able to weep for his sins. ⟦s6⟧ *And he warmed himself. [n: (BEDA.)]* There is a fire of charity; whereof it is said, etc., *as far as* and forthwith he set the secret places of his heart aflame with the coals of love.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 56.-- *False testimony. [n: (ID.)]* He is false who does not understand things said in the same sense in which they are said, etc., *as far as* so that he may seem to have spoken properly of the Jewish temple.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 60.-- *And rising up,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Being angry, because he found no place for calumny, he shows by the motion of his body the madness of his mind. ⟦s3+4⟧ *Not answering,* etc. [var: Vulg. Mc 14:60 *Non respondes*, "Dost thou not answer"] He provokes him to an answer, that from any occasion of speech he may find place for accusing. ⟦s5⟧ But Jesus answered not, [0234C] because he foresaw that whatever he should answer would be turned into calumny.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Thou art Christ. [n: (HIER.)]* Whom he was awaiting from afar, him he did not see near at hand: as Isaac, with darkened eyes, did not know Jacob under his hands, but long afterwards sings of things to come concerning him.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 62.-- *And you shall see.* ⟦s2⟧ The priest asks after the Son of God; Jesus answers the Son of man; that we may understand the same to be Son of God and Son of man, lest we make a quaternity in the Trinity, and that man may be in God and God in man. ⟦s3⟧ *On the right hand of power.* ⟦s4⟧ Because *he humbled himself, becoming obedient unto death, even the death of the cross: for which cause God also hath exalted him, and hath given him a name which is above every name* [0234D] [n: (Phil. II)].

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *With the clouds of heaven. [n: (HIER.)]* He ascended in a cloud, with a cloud he will come: that is, he ascended with his own body alone, which he took from the Virgin, and he is to come to judgment with the manifold Church, which is his body and his fulness, as Matthew says: But when the Son of man shall come, and all the angels with him, etc.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 63.-- *But the high priest. [n: (BEDA.)]* The same madness which before had shaken him from his seat, etc., *as far as* they did, when in Lycaonia they were honoured as gods. ⟦s2⟧ *His garments,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] That is, they lost the ephod in which the Jews had their honour, etc., *as far as* yet her chastity remains inviolate [0235A] even to the consummation of the world in those whom the lot of election shall find.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 64.-- *Who all,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] That by his own guilt he might loose our guilt, etc., *as far as* hence the prophet: *What shall I render to the Lord for all the things that he hath rendered to me? [n: (Psal. CXV.)]*

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 65.-- *To cover the face,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Not that he might not see their crimes, but that, as they themselves once did to Moses, they might hide from themselves the grace of the knowledge of him. ⟦s3⟧ This veil remains upon their heart until this day, which is taken away for them that believe in Christ. ⟦s4⟧ Whence, at his dying, it was as though the temple was rent, and the secret things of the holy of holies laid open.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They struck him with the palms of their hands. [n: (BEDA.)]* He who was then struck with blows is now struck with the blasphemies of false Christians. [0235B] He who was consumed with the spittle of unbelievers is now dishonoured by the raving reproaches of the faithful.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 68.-- *But he,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Note that he denies Christ who denies that he is his disciple. ⟦s3⟧ For the Lord did not say: Thou shalt deny thyself to be my disciple, but *thou shalt deny me.* ⟦s4⟧ He denied him therefore when he denied that he was his disciple.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And he went forth without,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Peter, without the Spirit, yielded to the voice of a maidservant, etc., *as far as* and we go forth outside of what we were.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 70.-- *For thou art also a Galilean. [n: (BEDA.)]* The Galileans and the men of Jerusalem have the same tongue, but nevertheless every province and region has its own sound of speaking, which to change, etc., *as far as* because the men of Jerusalem sounded that name in one way, and the Galileans [0235C] in another.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 72.-- *And he remembered. [n: (ID.)]* Peter denies by night, at the cock's crowing he repents. ⟦s2⟧ By day he professed three times that he loved him whom he had three times denied. ⟦s3⟧ For that which, etc., *as far as* he goes forth without (as the other Evangelists relate), that, set apart from the ungodly, he may the more freely wash away the guilt of his denial with tears.
