## CHAPTER XV. (cont.)

VERS. 35.-- *And some,* etc. Not all. Perhaps the Roman soldiers, not understanding the property of the Hebrew tongue, from that which is said Eloi, thought [0239C] that Elias was being called upon by him. Or the Jews, perverting the Lord's words in their accustomed speech, defamed him of weakness, in that he was calling for the help of Elias.

VERS. 36.-- *And running,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] John says more fully: *Afterwards Jesus, knowing,* etc. As if: This you have done the less, give what you are. For they were, etc., *as far as* whereof: *The Lord planted the vine Soreth: and how art thou turned into the bitterness of a strange vine: and I looked that thou shouldst bring forth grapes, and thou hast brought forth thorns [n: (Isa. V)]*.

VERS. 37.-- *But Jesus,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] Luke discloses what he said: *Father, into thy hands I commend my spirit; and saying this he expired [n: (Luc. XXIII)]*. But that which John says: *When he had taken the vinegar, he said: It is consummated: and bowing his head he delivered up [0239D] the spirit [n: (Joan. XIX)]*. Between this which he says: *It is consummated,* and that: *bowing his head he sent forth the spirit,* there was sent forth that great voice which John passes over in silence, and the rest record: *Into thy hands,* etc.

[n: (HIER.)] For, the flesh being made weak, the divine power straightway prevailed, which says: *Open ye to me the gates of justice [n: (Psal. CXVII)]*, etc. But we who are of the earth die with no voice, or with one; for he expired with a lifted-up voice who came down from heaven.

VERS. 38.-- *And the veil of the temple.* That the ark of the testament and all the mysteries of the law which were covered may appear, and may pass over to the Gentiles. Before it was said: *God is known in Judea, in Israel his name is great [n: (Psal. LXXV)]*; but now: *Be thou exalted above the heavens, [0240A] O God [n: (Ibid. LVI)]*, *and in all,* etc.; and in the Gospel before: *Go ye not into the way of the Gentiles [n: (Matth. X)]*.; and after the passion: *Go ye, and teach all nations [n: (Ibid. XXVIII)]*.

VERS. 39.-- *And seeing,* etc. It is shown why the centurion wonders, because he saw that he had so expired, that is, had sent forth his spirit. For no one has the power of sending forth his spirit, save the creator of souls.

*Truly,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Note that the centurion before the cross, in the very scandal of the passion, confesses him truly the Son of God: and Arius, etc., *as far as* and, the Synagogue keeping silence, he affirms him truly the Son of God.

VERS. 40.-- *And there were. [n: (ID.)]* As the female sex is not shut out from salvation through Mary the Virgin: so it is not driven back from the mystery of the cross, of knowledge [0240B] and of the resurrection, through the widow Mary Magdalen and the other mothers.

*Of James the less and of Joseph. [n: (BEDA.)]* By James the less he means James the son of Alpheus, who is also the brother of the Lord, in that he was the son of Mary the Lord's mother's sister. Of whom John: *Now there stood by the cross of Jesus his mother [n: (Joan. XIX)]*, etc. He seems to call her the wife of Cleophas from her father or from her kindred.

VERS. 42.-- *And when it was now evening,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Παρασκευὴ in Greek, preparation in Latin. By this name the Jews dwelling among the Greeks called the sixth day of the week, because on it they prepared the things that were needful for the sabbath, according as, etc., *as far as* until, the eighth age coming, the bodies themselves also, by the resurrection [0240C] glorified, may together with the souls receive the incorruption of the eternal inheritance.

VERS. 43.-- *Joseph came. [n: (HIER.)]* Joseph came in the evening of the parasceve from Arimathia, which is interpreted *laying down,* to lay down the body of Christ, etc., *as far as* the elect, who are the stars of the sea, looking on from afar, when, if it can be done, even the elect shall be scandalized.

VERS. 46.-- *And Joseph,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] From the simple burial of the Lord the ambition of the rich is condemned, who not even in their tombs, etc., *as far as* celebrates with earthly linen, as the body of the Lord was buried in a clean linen cloth: so was it decreed by pope Sylvester.

*Which was hewn out. [n: (BEDA.)]* It is said that the Lord's monument was a round house hewn out of the underlying rock, of such a height that a man standing upright [0240D] can scarcely reach the top with his hand stretched out. And it has an entrance, etc., *as far as* but the colour of the monument and of the tomb is said to be mingled of white and red.

VERS. 47.-- *And Mary,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Luke says: *That all his acquaintance stood afar off, and the women that had followed him [n: (Luc. XXIII)]*. But when the others, his body being laid down, had returned to their own, the women alone, etc., *as far as* and they weigh carefully, with pious curiosity, whether haply they may be able to imitate him, and in what order his passion is to be embraced.
