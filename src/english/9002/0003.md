## CHAPTER ONE. (cont.)

VERS. 24.-- *And Joseph rising up,* etc. [n: (BEDA.)] Morally. Whosoever is moved by God, let him break off delays, let him rise from sleep: let him do what is commanded. *He did as he had commanded.* Perfect obedience. He did not only what the angel commanded, but also as he commanded. Obedient by the showing of service and by the effect of love, he is rightly said [0072C] to have done as he commanded him. *The angel of the Lord.* No place is left for lying, where the office of angels is performed. *And he took his wife,* etc. To avoid the infamy of the virgin, to conceal the coming of the Saviour, for the necessary service of the child at his birth.

VERS. 25.-- *Till she brought forth.* Not that afterwards, which is the more certain: since in many ways he knew that he who was born was God.

Not that he knew her afterwards: but *till* is set for *and,* or for *for ever,* as: *Till I make thy enemies the footstool of thy feet [n: (Psal. CIX)]*. It is said that Joseph could not see Mary face to face, whom the Holy Spirit had wholly filled from the conception. And therefore he did not know face to face her whom he had espoused, until the womb was emptied: [0072D] of which there is no question here. *The firstborn,* etc. Everything is called firstborn which opens the womb, whether another follow or not. Or the firstborn among all the elect by grace. But properly he is called the only-begotten of God the Father, or of Mary.
