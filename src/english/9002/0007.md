## CHAPTER III. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Then he came.* ⟦s2⟧ When John was preaching and baptizing, namely when Jesus was thirty years old, showing that no one ought to be made a priest or a preacher save at a serviceable age. ⟦s3⟧ As Joseph at thirty took up the rule of Egypt. ⟦s4⟧ David began his reign at that age: Ezekiel merited the gift of prophecy. ⟦s5⟧ Jesus came for these causes, that he might approve the baptism of John, and that (because he was man) he might fulfil all righteousness and the humility of the law: and that, sanctifying the waters, he might show through the dove the coming of the Holy Spirit in the laver. ⟦s6⟧ For he came not out of any need [0082D] of washing, but that no one, however holy, should judge the grace of baptism superfluous. ⟦s7⟧ *From Galilee to the Jordan.* ⟦s8⟧ Galilee, a passing over: the Jordan, a going down. ⟦s9⟧ He therefore who wishes to be baptized, let him pass over from vices, and going down, let him be humbled.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Suffer it now.* ⟦s2⟧ Suffer me now to be baptized by thee, that afterwards thou mayest be baptized in the Spirit with that which thou seekest of me. ⟦s3⟧ For as it is fitting to give an example of fulfilling all righteousness, that all may learn that no one is perfect without the water of baptism.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Then he suffered him.* ⟦s2⟧ When he had come to know that righteousness was to be fulfilled in such an order. ⟦s3⟧ He suffered him, because that is true humility which its companion obedience does not desert.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The Spirit taught him in a moment what he had not known before, [0083A] and therefore what he had before humbly feared, he devoutly fulfilled.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And being baptized.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ sanctified the waters of baptism: because it belongs to him whose it is to create, to sanctify also.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ As man consists of two things, that is, of body and of soul, so he is reborn of two: of water, which cleanses filth and buries him together with Christ: and of the fire of the Spirit, by whose blaze we are caught up to heaven. ⟦s2⟧ So the Church after baptism reaches after the summit of the virtues by certain increases, as Christ when baptized came up out of the water. ⟦s3⟧ So after baptism those go up who advance to the virtues: and those who before were carnal and sons of Adam become spiritual and sons of God.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They were opened,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ And this too belongs to the fulfilling of righteousness. [0083B] For not then for the first time did the heavenly things lie open to him, nor was the Holy Spirit then given: but he showed that to us, through the baptism we have received, the entrance of heaven lies open, and that the Holy Spirit is given. ⟦s3⟧ For that the heavens were opened, that the Holy Spirit came, that the voice of the Father sounded, etc., these mystical things are within us.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The entrance into heaven lies open to the reborn through Christ, which through Adam had been closed.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *As a dove.* ⟦s2⟧ He had a true body, formed for the hour, in the appearance of a dove: because otherwise the Spirit could not be seen by men as a dove, not because it was a true dove, but because the Spirit was in a bodily appearance, yet was not itself a body. ⟦s3⟧ So of this figure it is judged as of the others in which God appeared: which, their office fulfilled, were dissolved. ⟦s4⟧ Fitly the Spirit came down in a dove, [0083C] which is simple and gentle, that he might point out both the simplicity of his own nature, and him upon whom he was descending, the meek herald and giver of mercy. ⟦s5⟧ In like manner all who are reborn by baptism ought to be filled with the seven virtues signified in the dove. ⟦s6⟧ The dove is a stranger to the malice of gall, in which we are forbidden anger. ⟦s7⟧ It wounds none with beak or claws, in which innocence is marked. ⟦s8⟧ Nor does it fall upon the smallest little birds, on which other birds feed themselves and their young: in which we are forbidden robbery. ⟦s9⟧ It feeds on clean grain: in which abstinence is marked, it cherishes the young of others as its own, behold charity. ⟦s10⟧ It gives a moan for a song, behold compunction. ⟦s11⟧ It sits above the waters, that by the shadow foreseen in the waters it may turn aside from the hawk: behold watchfulness. ⟦s12+13⟧ In a dove the Spirit appeared above the Lord: because he came to gather us through meekness. [0083D] Above the disciples in fire, whom he was coming to kindle against their own selves for the consuming of the rust of sin. ⟦s14⟧ Adam by sinning lost heaven, Christ appeared glorified by the Holy Spirit through the dove: the quivering flame which barred the way, the water of baptism quenched: because water is contrary to fire.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I am well pleased.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, I have established my good pleasure, that thou, in whom is nothing but good, mayest please, and that through thee others may please, not in themselves, but in thee, that is, united to thee by faith and brightness.
