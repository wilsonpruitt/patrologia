## CHAPTER VII. (cont.)

VERS. 20.-- *Therefore by their fruits.* As if he should say: Beware of false prophets, and know them by their fruits.

VERS. 21.-- *Not every one that saith.* As those are to be guarded against who have the appearance of a good life because of a doctrine of wickedness, so here on the contrary are those who, though they be of entire faith and doctrine, live basely. For both are needful, that the work be approved by the word, and the word by the works.

*That saith: Lord, Lord.* The road of the kingdom of God is obedience, not the calling of a name. This is not that [0111C] saying truly and properly, where the utterance is at variance with the will. Of which the Apostle says: *No man can say: Lord Jesus, but in the Holy Ghost [n: (I Cor. XII)]*.

VERS. 22.-- *Many will say.* But most of all are those to be guarded against who, for the name of Christ, have even miracles, and although the Lord wrought these for the sake of unbelievers, yet he warned us not to be deceived by such men: thinking that invisible wisdom is there where there is a visible miracle; whence he adds: *Many will say.* How clean and single an eye then is needed, that the way of wisdom may be found amid so many deceptions.

VERS. 23.-- *I never knew you.* The light does not know the darkness, that is, it does not look upon it, since if it looked upon it, it would not be darkness. *You that work iniquity.* Not "who have worked", lest he take away repentance, but "who work" at the judgment; [0111D] although you have not the power of sinning, yet you have the desire.

VERS. 24.-- *Every one therefore.* As if he should say: All the foregoing must be well kept, because he who keeps them shall be likened to Christ; he who keeps them not, to the devil. *But he who,* which things were delivered on the mount: this he says in order to show that the sermon delivered on the mount is perfect in all the precepts by which the Christian life is formed. *Shall be likened.* Because, as Christ out of various persons builds up one Church, instructs it, rules it, being about to dedicate it unto eternal life, so this man by various virtues builds for himself a perpetual dwelling, and in the dedication to come will rejoice with Christ. *To a man.* To him who says: As a wise architect, I have laid the foundation in Christ. [0112A] *Who built.* He builds in Christ who does what he hears from him. *Upon a rock.* From which Peter received his name, for the firmness of his faith, that is, he built the Church upon himself.

VERS. 25.-- *And there came down.* In three ways the devil assails: by the concupiscence of the flesh, by the outward delights of the world, or by himself, when he looses upon all the wind of his impulsions. *The rain.* Concupiscence, by which each man is tempted, which softens the sense, the rigour of faith, which if it does not prevail, greater floods come on.

VERS. 26.-- *Who heareth these my words,* etc., outwardly. And therefore he has no excuse, but great is his ruin: because he despised the warnings of salvation. *To a foolish man.* Of whom it is said: *An enemy hath done this, whose* works are all unstable, and lie open to ruin. *Upon the sand.* [0112B] Of whatever sort the things be that are founded upon the concupiscence of the vices, they shall not stand.

VERS. 27.-- *And it fell, and great was the ruin thereof.* Every conscience which does not remain fixed in God by hope has no strength to stand in temptations; and it is driven the further, the more it is severed from the things above in those things which are of the world: and truly all who are evil, or feignedly good, become worse when any temptation presses upon them.

VERS. 28.-- *The crowds.* Either some in the crowd, because unbelievers, or rather all, [wondered] at the excellence of his wisdom.
