## CHAPTER XXVII. (cont.)

VERS. 55.-- *Many women.* Matthew and Luke say that Mary Magdalen stood afar off. But John [0176C] says she was beside the cross, because she was distant by so great an interval that she could be said to be beside it: because she was at hand in his sight, and afar off by comparison with the nearer crowd. We can also say that those women who were with the mother of the Lord, after he had commended her to the disciple, had already begun to go away, that they might draw themselves out of the density of the crowd and behold from farther off the other things that were done. *Ministering unto him.* It was of the Hebrew custom that women should minister of their own substance to their teachers: which the Lord accepted, that he might give an example to the apostles.

VERS. 56.-- *Of James.* James the son of Alphaeus is called the less, to distinguish him from that James who was called among the first apostles and chosen by the Lord.

VERS. 57.-- *A rich man.* Because none but a rich man could approach the governor, not that the evangelist commends [0176D] riches. He was called also a decurion, because he was of the order of the curia and discharged the office of the curia, by this he could have the means, by this, that he was a disciple, he was worthy of this ministry. *He was a disciple of Jesus. He went to Pilate.* Although in secret, avoiding beforehand the enmities of the Jews, in this last office he cares nothing for them.

VERS. 59.-- *In a clean linen cloth.* Mystically. He wraps Jesus in a clean linen cloth who receives him with a pure mind. Hence also the custom of the Church holds that the sacrifice of the altar be celebrated not upon silk, not upon dyed cloth, but upon pure linen.

VERS. 60.-- *In his own new monument.* Because, if he were laid in a monument where another had been laid, another [0177A] perhaps would be feigned to have risen again. In the rock, lest, if it had been built of many stones, he should be said to have been taken away by theft, the foundations of the tomb being undermined.

VERS. 61.-- *And there were there.* While the other acquaintances of Jesus were returning to their own, the women alone, who loved more closely, were watching the service of the burial, awaiting what Jesus had promised: and therefore they are the first to see the resurrection.

VERS. 63.-- *After three.* The whole from a part, of the sixth day, on which he was crucified, with its own night, for one day. The sabbath entire. The third day from its first part, that is, from the night, for the whole with its own daytime, and so there is a space of three days.

VERS. 66.-- *They made the sepulchre sure.* Their diligence profited our faith. For the more it was guarded, the more is the power of the resurrection shown.

## CHAPTER XXVIII.

[0177B]

VERS. 1.-- *In the evening,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] That the different times of these women, etc., *as far as* they could not approach, they approach in the morning. *Mary Magdalen, and the other Mary.* Two women of one name and of one devotion signify two peoples embracing with equal devotion the passion and the resurrection of Christ.

VERS. 2.-- *An earthquake.* As in the passion, so also in the resurrection there is a movement of the earth: and it signifies that the hearts of earthly men are to be moved to penance through faith in the passion and the resurrection. *He rolled back the stone.* He could go out from the sepulchre while it was closed, who was born from the virgin's womb while it was closed. The rolling back of the stone signifies the unlocking of the sacraments of Christ, which were covered by the veil of the letter: [0177C] which being taken away, the resurrection, the abolition of death, life eternal began to be preached throughout the whole world.

VERS. 3.-- *As lightning.* In the whiteness of the garment he signified that he would be gentle to the just, in the terror of the lightning he signifies that he will be terrible to the reprobate.

VERS. 5.-- *Fear not.* You who see your own fellow citizens. Let those fear who do not belong to their fellowship.

VERS. 7.-- *And going quickly.* Nor is it granted to us to keep this joy in the secret of the heart, but to lay it open to those who love in like manner.

VERS. 8.-- *With fear and joy.* The affection in them is twofold, of fear and of joy. The one from the greatness of the miracle, the other from desire of him who was rising again, and each urged on the womanly step.

[0177D]VERS. 9.-- *And behold Jesus,* etc. So Christ meets with his help all who begin the way of the virtues. *And they held.* It was said above that he went out while the monument was closed, that thou mightest understand the body to be immortal: here his feet are held, that thou mightest prove the flesh to be true. And because they trembled at so great a vision, fear is first driven out: *Fear not,* that with a peaceful mind they might be able to hear the things that are said. These women hold his feet, who adore him raised up again: but she who did not yet know that the Son of God [0178A] had risen again deservedly hears: *Touch me not, for I am not yet ascended to my Father [n: (Joan XX)]*.

VERS. 10.-- *Tell.* As a woman told the death-bearing words of the serpent, so now the words of the life-giver, that from her from whom death then came, now life may come. *To my brethren.* Before he called them servants also, now brethren, that he may teach that after the resurrection he has taken up again the same humanity, and that they may hope for the same.

VERS. 12.-- *A great sum of money. [n: (HIER.)]* Those who misuse the goods of the Church upon other things by which they may fulfil their own will are like to the scribes and the Pharisees and the priests who bought a lie and the blood of the Saviour.

VERS. 13.-- *They stole him away while we were asleep. [n: (AUG.)]* If they slept, how did they see? If they did not [0178B] see, how could they be witnesses? The avarice which took captive the disciple, the companion of the Lord, deceived also the soldiers who guarded the sepulchre: and so it seduced the Jews, whence he adds: *And it was spread abroad.*

VERS. 16.-- *They went away.* From Jerusalem into Galilee. *Into a mountain:* because the body taken up from the common earth had been lifted above all earthly things. And he admonishes us that, if we wish to see him, we should be lifted from the lowest things to the things above. Christ went before, the firstfruits of them that sleep, the others follow in their own order, and seeing him they adore him, that is, they praise him without end.

VERS. 17.-- *But some doubted,* as Luke says: thinking that they saw a spirit, which he gave up in dying.

VERS. 18.-- *And coming near.* And therefore the loving master both [0178C] strengthens them in the faith, and calls the doubting to faith, intimating to how great a glory he had come. *It is given.* He says this not of the coeternal Godhead of the Father, but of the humanity he assumed, in which he is less than the angels, in which also he was crowned with glory and honour and set above all things.

VERS. 19.-- *Going.* He who has now come into the light, which until now had for certain causes been doubtful to men. *Teach.* A fitting order. For first the hearer is to be taught. Then he is to be steeped in the sacraments of the faith, then to be instructed in the keeping of the commandments. Because unless the soul first receive the faith, baptism is not to be given, nor is it of avail to be cleansed, if afterwards he does not press on in good works.

VERS. 20.-- *And behold I am with you.* But what is the reward [0178D] of a holy manner of life, and what the pledge of the blessedness to come which the faithful have even in the present, he adds: *Behold I am with you,* etc. Which he promises to the apostles, and through their successions to the universal Church. Note that until the end of the world there will not cease to be men who are worthy of the divine dwelling.

[n: (AUG.)] On the first day of the week at daybreak, etc., *as far as* the angel saying to them: *So shall he come as you have seen him going into heaven [n: (Act. I)]*.
