## ON PSALM XVI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ This is to be assigned to the person of the Lord, the Church being joined to him, which is his body.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Hear, O Lord, my justice,* etc. [0782A] With the Lord it is certain that *justice* has its own voice, which knows silent things by the power of its own understanding. ⟦s2⟧ For his prayer is perfect whose cause cries out as well as his tongue. ⟦s3⟧ *Not in deceitful lips,* that is, not proceeding to thee *in deceitful lips.* ⟦s4⟧ For he prays well in whose lips there is no guile.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Let my judgment come forth from thy countenance,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ From the enlightening of thy knowledge may I judge what is true. ⟦s3⟧ Or certainly he says *from thy countenance* according to those things which thou beholdest and knowest in me. ⟦s4⟧ But he rightly asked this who knew that he had not the pollution of sin.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Thou hast proved my heart and visited it by night,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ My heart has been proved by the visitation of tribulation. ⟦s3⟧ But that very tribulation, by which I was tried and found just, is to be called not *night* only, because it is wont to disturb, [0782B] but also *fire,* because it burns.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *That my mouth may not speak the works of men,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That nothing else may proceed out of my mouth than that which pertains to thy glory. ⟦s3⟧ Not to *the works of men,* who do beside thy will. ⟦s4⟧ *Because of the words* of thy peace and of thy prophets *I have kept the laborious ways* of human mortality and of the passion. [var: Vulg. Ps 16:4 *propter verba labiorum tuorum, ego custodivi vias duras*, "because of the words of thy lips, I have kept hard ways"]

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Perfect thou my goings in thy paths,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Let there be perfected* the charity of the Church in the narrow journeys by which one comes to thy rest. ⟦s3⟧ *That the signs of my journey be not moved,* [var: Vulg. Ps 16:5 *ut non moveantur vestigia mea*, "that my footsteps be not moved"] which as *footprints* have been impressed upon the sacraments and the apostolic Scriptures, which they may behold and observe who wish to follow me. [0782C] Or certainly, that I may now abide steadfastly in eternity, after that I have accomplished the hard ways and have perfected my goings in the straits of thy paths.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *I have cried, for thou hast heard me,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He who cries because he is heard is surely acknowledged pure, innocent, and unspotted: for he prayed confidently, because he presumed out of the purity of his conscience that he could be heard. ⟦s3⟧ Or certainly it is thus to be understood: I directed to thee a free and strong intention of prayer, because, that I might be able to have this, thou didst hear me praying more weakly. ⟦s4⟧ Let not therefore thy hearing forsake my lowliness.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Show forth thy mercies wonderfully, thou who savest them that hope in thee,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Let not thy mercies grow cheap, lest they be less loved.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0782D]VERS. 8.-- *From them that resist thy right hand,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *From them that resist* the favour wherewith thou favourest me, *keep me as the apple of the eye,* which appears small and slight, yet through it is directed the keenness of the light by which light and darkness are distinguished: so through the humanity of Christ the divinity of judgment, discerning between the just and sinners.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Under the shadow of thy wings protect me,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ That is, under the defence of thy charity and thy mercy protect me.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *My enemies have surrounded my soul,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ His *enemies* are the Jews, who, *surrounding* him, sought to snatch away his *soul,* that is, his temporal life.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0783A]VERS. 10.-- *Who have shut up their own fat.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, they were covered with their own fat gladness, after that their desire was sated with wickedness. ⟦s3⟧ And therefore *their mouth hath spoken pride: We have no king but Caesar;* and, *thou that destroyest the temple,* and other such things.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Casting me forth they have now surrounded me,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Casting* me forth outside the city, they have now *surrounded me* on the cross, and the intention of their heart they *have set to decline* to these earthly things, thinking that he who was being slain suffered a great evil.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *They have taken me as a lion prepared for the prey,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *They have taken* me, as that adversary going about seeking whom he may devour, *and as his whelp,* that is, the people to whom it was said: *You are of your father [0783B] the devil,* devising of the snares whereby he might encompass and destroy the just man.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Arise, O Lord, prevent him and supplant him,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Arise,* O God, whom the unbelieving people thinks to sleep and not to care for the iniquities of men, before his malice is put in practice, that vengeance may prevent his deed, and so *supplant him.* ⟦s3⟧ But *deliver my soul,* [var: Vulg. Ps 16:13 *eripe animam meam ab impio*, "snatch my soul from the ungodly one"] by raising me from the death which was inflicted upon me by the ungodly. ⟦s4⟧ But my soul is thy *sword,* which thy hand hath taken up, that is, thy eternal power, that through it it may war down the kingdoms of iniquity and divide the just from the ungodly. ⟦s5⟧ This therefore deliver from mine enemies.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *O Lord, from the few of the earth divide them,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Few* are the good in comparison with the evil; [0783C] but the Jews have been *divided* throughout the world *from the few* who out of them believed, from the Church, which is the *land* of the living, separated *in their life,* that is, in this life which alone they think their own, because they despair of the eternal. ⟦s3⟧ But not only did this visible punishment follow them, but also out of the sins which as darkness *are hidden* from the light of the truth of God their memory *was filled full,* so that they forgot God.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *They are filled with children, and they have left their remnants to their little ones;* that is, they are filled with the fruits of evil works, and they have left the sins of their unbelief even to their posterity.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 15.-- *But I shall appear in thy justice,* etc. [var: Vulg. Ps 16:15 *Ego autem in justitia apparebo conspectui tuo*, "But I shall appear in justice to thy sight"] But I, who appeared not to those who with a foul and darkened heart cannot see the light of wisdom, [0783D] *in thy justice* shall see thy face, and *shall be satisfied when thy glory shall appear,* as do they who understand me. ⟦s3⟧ But it must be diligently noted, and skilfully impressed upon the memory, how clearly the glory of the resurrection of the faithful is declared, according to the Hebrew edition, in these last little verses, in which it is said: *I shall see thy face in justice; I shall be filled with thy likeness when I shall have awaked.* ⟦s4⟧ For since it is agreed that, unless the body of Christ be justified by his grace, it cannot see the face of God, who is the fount of justice, he not only rejoices over so excellent a gift, but moreover trusts with entire certainty that he shall be filled with his likeness when he shall have awaked, that is, risen again. ⟦s5⟧ For what else is, *I shall be filled with thy likeness when I shall have awaked,* [0784A] but what the blessed Apostle John says: *We know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like to him: because we shall see him as he is.*
