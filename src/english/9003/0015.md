## ON PSALM XVII. (cont.)

VERS. 38.-- *I will pursue my enemies, and I will overtake them, and I will not turn back till they fail. I will pursue* my fleshly affections, lest I be overtaken by them, but I will overtake them, that they may be consumed, and from that purpose *I will not turn back* to rest, *till they fail* who make a clamour against me.

VERS. 39.-- *I will break them, nor shall they be able to stand, they shall fall under my feet. I will break them,* nor shall they last against me: I will set before them the upright fears in which I walk unto eternity.

VERS. 40.-- *And thou hast girded me with strength unto battle, thou hast supplanted under me them that rose up against me.* Thou hast bound tight [0788B] the flowing desires of my flesh with strength, lest in such a fight I should be hindered, and thou hast made those who pursued me to be deceived, so that they became beneath me who desired to be above me.

VERS. 41.-- *And thou hast given me the back of my enemies,* etc. Thou hast turned my enemies about, and hast made them to be a back to me, that is, that they should follow me, but others of them, who persisted in hatred, thou hast destroyed.

VERS. 42.-- *They cried out, nor was there any that should save them unto the Lord, nor did he hear them.* For who should save those whom thou wouldst not save? Not to anyone at all, but to the Lord did they cry out, nor did he judge worthy of a hearing those who departed not from their own malice, which is to be understood specially of the Jews, who cry out in their synagogues and are not heard, because they desire an earthly Jerusalem, [0788C] not a heavenly.

VERS. 43.-- *And I will break them small as the dust before the face of the wind,* etc. And they are dry, not receiving the shower of God's mercy, so that, lifted up with pride, they may be snatched away from a firm and unshaken hope as from the solidity and stability of the earth; *as the mire of the streets,* because through the broad ways of perdition, which many walk, they run riot and are slippery.

VERS. 44.-- *Thou wilt deliver me from the contradictions of the people,* etc. *Thou wilt deliver me from the contradictions* of those who said: *If we let him go alive, the world goeth after him.* [var: Vulg. Joan. XI, 48 *si dimittimus eum sic, omnes credent in eum*, "if we let him alone thus, all will believe in him"; the second clause answers to Joan. XII, 19 *ecce mundus totus post eum abiit*, "behold, the whole world is gone after him"]

VERS. 45.-- *A people which I knew not,* etc. *The people* of the Gentiles, whom I visited not with bodily presence, hath served me: neither did it see me with its eyes, but receiving [0788D] my preachers *in the hearing of the ear it hath obeyed me.*

VERS. 46.-- *The children that are strangers have lied to me,* etc. *Children* not to be called mine, but rather *strangers,* to whom it was rightly said: *You are of your father the devil, they have lied to me: children that are strangers,* to whom, that they might be renewed, I brought the New Testament, they remained in the old man, and, as though feeble in one foot (because holding the Old they spat out the New), they were made lame, following even in the old law itself their own traditions rather than God's.

For they brought false charges about unwashed hands, because such were the *paths* which they themselves had made and had worn smooth by custom, wandering away from the road of the commandments of God.

[0789A]VERS. 47.-- *The Lord liveth, and blessed be my God,* etc. Not after an earthly custom do I judge of the God of my salvation; nor that salvation itself as earthly, but on high concerning him may I hope.

VERS. 48.-- *O God, who givest me revenges,* etc. O God, who avengest me by subduing peoples under me, *my deliverer from my enemies.* The Jews, crying out. Crucify him, crucify him!

VERS. 49.-- *And thou wilt exalt me from them that rise up against me,* etc. From those same who rise up against me suffering, thou wilt exalt me rising again, and from their wicked reign thou wilt deliver me.

VERS. 50.-- *Therefore will I confess to thee among the nations, O Lord,* etc. Therefore the Gentiles will confess to thee for me, O Lord, and thou wilt become more widely known by my good [0789B] works.

VERS. 51.-- *Magnifying the salvations of his king,* etc. God magnifies, that he may make lovely the salvations which his Son gives to believers, and *he doeth mercy to his Christ,* to that same deliverer with a strong hand, who overcame this world, and to those whom, believing, he begot in the Gospel unto eternity.

Whatsoever things have been said in this psalm which cannot properly fit the Lord himself, that is, the head of the Church, are to be referred to the Church. For here the whole Jesus speaks, in whom are all his members.
