## ON PSALM XIX.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The title is known, nor does Christ speak, but the Prophet speaks to Christ, singing, under the figure of wishing, the things that are to come:

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *May the Lord hear thee in the day of tribulation, may the name of the God of Jacob protect thee,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *May he hear thee,* [0792B] the Lord, *in the day* in which thou saidst: *Father, save me [sic: *salavifica*] out of this hour:* for to thee belongs the people, born later, because the elder shall serve the younger.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *May he send thee help from the Sanctuary,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Making for thee a sanctified body, the Church, which awaits thee as from a watchtower, for the time when thou shalt come from the wedding.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *May he be mindful of all thy sacrifice,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ May he make us mindful of all thy injuries and of the reproaches which thou didst bear for us, and may he turn the cross, on which thou wast wholly offered to God in soul and body, into the gladness of the resurrection.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *May he give thee according to thy heart,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *May he give thee* not according to the heart of those who thought that by persecuting thee thou couldst be blotted out, but *according to thy own [0792C] heart,* who knewest what profit thy passion held. ⟦s3⟧ *And may he confirm all thy counsel,* not only that by which thou didst lay down thy life for thy friends, that the grain, being mortified, might rise the more plentifully, but also that by which blindness in part is come in Israel, that the fullness of the Gentiles might enter in, and so all Israel might be saved.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *We will rejoice in thy salvation,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *We will rejoice,* because death will do thee no harm, for so wilt thou show that it can do no harm to us either. ⟦s3⟧ And the confession of thy name will not only not destroy us, but will even magnify [sic: *magnifibabit*] us.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *May the Lord fulfil all thy petitions,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *May the Lord fulfil* not only the *petitions* which thou hadst on earth, but also those by which in heaven thou intercedest for us. ⟦s3⟧ For now it has been shown to me by prophecy [0792D] that the Lord will raise up his Christ.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ *May he hear him from his holy heaven,* etc. [var: Vulg. Ps 19:7 *Exaudiet illum*, "he will hear him"] *May he hear* him not from earth only, where he asks to be glorified, but also *from heaven,* where already, interceding at the Father's right hand for us, he has poured out thence the Holy Spirit upon those who believe in him. ⟦s3⟧ But our *powers* are the *salvation* of his favour, since even out of tribulation he gives help, so that when we are weak, then we are strong. ⟦s4⟧ For vain is the salvation of men, which is not his *right hand* but his left: for behold, they are lifted up in great pride, whosoever, sinning, have been made safe in temporal things.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *These in chariots and these in horses, but we [0793A] will call upon the name of the Lord our God.* ⟦s2⟧ These are drawn along by the rolling succession of temporal goods, and these proud ones are carried away by honours, and exult in them. ⟦s3⟧ But we, fixing our hope in things eternal, and not seeking our own glory, *In the name of the Lord our God we will exult.* [var: Vulg. Ps 19:8 *invocabimus*, "we will call upon"]

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *They are bound and have fallen,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *They* by the covetousness of temporal things *are bound:* fearing to spare the Lord, lest they should lose their place at the hands of the Romans, and rushing upon the stone of stumbling and the rock of scandal, they fell away from the heavenly hope, they to whom blindness in part of Israel is come, being ignorant of the justice of God and willing to establish their own: but we, that the fullness of the Gentiles might enter in, sons of Abraham raised up out of the stones, we who did not follow after justice, have laid hold of [0793B] it, and *have risen up,* and, justified not by our own strength but through faith, *we are set upright.*

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *O Lord, save the king,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *O Lord, save the king,* and let him himself, who by his passion showed us the example of doing battle, offer also our sacrifices as a priest raised up from the dead, and, he now offering for us, *hear us in the day in which we shall call upon thee.*

## ON PSALM XX.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The title is known: it is sung of Christ.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *O Lord, in thy strength the king shall rejoice,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *O Lord, in thy strength,* in that the Word was made flesh, the man Christ Jesus shall rejoice, *and over this,* that thou quickenest all things, *he hath exulted exceedingly.*

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Thou hast given him the desire of his heart,* etc. [0793C] He desired to eat the Pasch, and to lay down his life when he would, and to take it again, and thou hast given it to him; *my peace,* he says, *I leave to you,* and it was done. [var: Vulg. Jo 14:27 *pacem relinquo vobis, pacem meam do vobis*, "peace I leave to you, my peace I give to you"]

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *For thou hast prevented him with blessings of sweetness,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because he had first drunk *the blessing of the sweetness* that is thine, the gall of our sins did not hurt him, whom, at the beginning of his discourse, precious stones drawing near encompassed, his own disciples, from whom the beginning of his proclamation should be made.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *He asked life of thee, and thou hast given him length of days,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He asked the resurrection, saying: *Father, glorify thy Son:* and thou hast given him the long times of this world, which the Church should have, and thereafter eternity *for ever and ever.*

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *His glory is great in thy salvation,* [0793D] etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Great is* indeed *his glory* in the salvation by which thou hast raised him up; but thou wilt yet add to him *glory and great beauty* [cj: *gloria*; read *gloriam*, "glory"] [0794A] when thou shalt set him in heaven at thy right hand.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *For thou wilt give him for a blessing,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ This is the *blessing* which thou *wilt give* him *for ever and ever: thou wilt make him joyful in gladness with thy countenance,* according to the man whom he lifted up to thee.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *For the king hopeth in the Lord,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *For the king* is not proud, but, humble in heart, *hopeth in the Lord, and in the mercy of the Most High he shall not be troubled,* [var: Vulg. Ps 20:8 *non commovebitur*, "shall not be moved"] humbling himself and obedient unto the death of the cross.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Let thy hand be found by all thy enemies,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *May there be found,* O king, thy power, when thou shalt come to judge, *by all thy enemies,* who did not understand it in thy lowliness, and may the brightness by which [0794B] thou reignest at the Father's right hand *find,* on the day of judgment, as men to be punished, *those that hate thee,* because now they themselves have not found it.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Thou shalt make them as an oven of fire,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Thou shalt set them burning within by the conscience of their own ungodliness in the time of thy manifestation: then, troubled by the vengeance of the Lord, after the accusation of conscience they shall be given over to the eternal burning to be devoured.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Thou shalt destroy their fruit from the earth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Their fruit,* because it is earthly, *thou shalt destroy from the earth,* and their works, and whomsoever they have seduced, thou wilt not reckon among the sons of men whom thou hast called unto an everlasting inheritance.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1+2⟧ VERS. 12.-- *For they have intended evils against thee,* etc. [0794C] This punishment shall be repaid them, because the evils which they thought hung over them while thou wast reigning they turned back upon thee, to be slain, and they devised counsels, saying: *It is expedient that one die for all.* [var: Vulg. Jo 18:14 *expedit unum hominem mori pro populo*, "it is expedient that one man die for the people"] And: *Come, let us kill him, and we have his inheritance.* ⟦s3⟧ Which therefore *they could not establish,* not knowing what they had said, nor obtaining what they desired.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *For thou wilt place them as a back,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because thou wilt set them in order among those things from which, being set behind and despised, thou wilt turn away, and among those things which thou leavest, that is, the covetous desires of an earthly kingdom, thou wilt prepare for thyself their shamelessness unto the passion.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Be thou exalted, O Lord, in thy strength,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ O *Lord,* whom, being lowly, they did not know, *be thou exalted in thy strength,* which they thought to be weakness. [0794D] But we with heart and with work will celebrate, and will make known thy wondrous works.
