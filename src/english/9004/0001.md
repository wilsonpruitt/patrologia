## ON THE GOSPEL OF MATTHEW.

[0863]

[0863B] *The book of the generation of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham.* He names two fathers for this reason, that of David it is said: *Of the fruit of thy womb I will set upon my throne.* [var: Vulg. *super sedem tuam*, "upon thy throne"] Of Abraham it is said: *In thy seed shall all nations be blessed.* David is written before Abraham for this reason, that he is nearer to Christ by being born of that stock: *Jacob begat Joseph,* he said according to generation: Luke said according to the law, that he was the son of Heli; women are in the genealogy of Christ for this reason, that they themselves come to eternal life as men do: and sinners are named in the genealogy of Christ for this reason, that he himself said: *I came not to call the just, but sinners.* Luke, speaking of Christ from Adam, shows that through Christ the race of Adam is loosed from sin, or that from Christ they go forth who return to the Father. The figure [0863C] of the forty-two men in the genealogy of Christ, this is the forty-two stations of the children of Israel from Egypt: as far as the land of promise, that is, from the world to heaven; which signifies coming to Christ by threefold generations, through faith, hope and charity. What by the fourteen but by the ten words of the law and by the four virtues: the first, prudence, to serve God and not idols: the second, temperance, not to covet: the third, justice, not to commit adultery: the fourth, fortitude, to let go and not to kill. By four things came the destruction of the world: by a man, by a woman, by a tree, by a serpent; and by four it is restored: by Christ, by Mary, by the cross, by the man Joseph. The serpent that was in Israel signified Christ: it was itself made of bronze with four [0863D] round wheels; they that looked upon it were healed; and he raised it upon the head of a rod, that all might see it and that the beasts of the desert might flee for fear of the wheels; and so each one was healed, if he looked upon Christ on the head of the rod, that is, upon the cross; the four wheels signify the four evangelists: so the devil flees the Gospel when it sounds unto heaven.

*Now the generation of Christ was in this wise.* That is, according to the flesh, they said: It is called Christ's for this reason, that from him, by a derived word, they may be called Christians: by etymology again Christ, that is, anointed, because through him, after the laver, we have received the anointing of chrism. *Was in this wise*: through eternity. Mary sounds "star of the sea"; because a star is sweet, the sea (one word [0864B] has been deleted, perhaps *vero*,) is bitter; so Mary was in the sea of the world among sinners, like the star of the sea, since it is the custom: the star brings men to harbour, if they follow it; so Mary in the world, where Christ was born, who leads all to life if they follow him. His mother being espoused, that is, for four reasons: that she might not be stoned as an adulteress, and that she might have solace in the flight, and the genealogy of Christ through Joseph, and that the birth might be hidden from the devil. Before they came together, that is, before they had been in whatever dwelling, she was found by Joseph with child: not in the womb as a woman, but as a virgin having of the Holy Spirit, that is, made of the Spirit, *Spirius* ( *sic* ), as he himself makes an apple upon a tree, and Eve from the bones of Adam

*But Joseph her husband.* He is called her husband for this reason, and [0864C] for this reason just, lest others should suspect a conjunction of human kind: « He was not willing to expose her, » that is, in judgment; lest he should transgress the law: because he knew her a virgin, that her childbearing might lie hidden from the devil. « *But while he thought on these things,* » that is, he was thinking whether to put her away or to keep her; here the prophecy is fulfilled: A helper in due seasons, in tribulation. *The angel of the Lord said in dreams:* Not in one dream, because the angel came to him in dreams more often. Not once, because the mystery of God cannot be known once for all: *Joseph, son of David,* that is, the prophecy fulfilled in Joseph through David, of the fruit of thy womb, and the rest. *Fear not,* that is, love and rejoice in a wife not nuptial, nor a begetting, but a dwelling together: he said *thy*: that he might believe that he was conceived not of another, but of the Holy Spirit. She shall bring forth a Son, that is, that [0864D] there might be greater joy to him over a son, as the custom is among men, and thou shalt call his name. Likewise, for his gladness it should be Jesus, and he said also Saviour: he saves his people; he did not say all, but those who believe in him, from their sins: That is, what no other physician could do. Now all this was done: that is, what the angel said: that there might be fulfilled what was spoken by the Lord, that is, through the prophet Isaiah concerning Christ, here according to the letter only; Behold a virgin shall be with child, and he called his name Emmanuel [var: Vulg. *vocabunt nomen eius*, "they shall call his name"]. This name has been interpreted for this reason, that in either tongue it is called lofty, God with us, that is, God and man, that he may be in one faith. He did as the angel bade him: [0865A] it shows that he deserved to see the vision of the Lord who did as he bade him.

*And he knew her not till she brought forth her son:* Till, *pronum quam* [ *Forte,* priusquam], he is called firstborn; in the law he is so called, because he first opened the womb. Not one whom sons follow, but one who is born first. Christ is called firstborn for three things: in the resurrection from the dead, and of Mary, and of creatures. When therefore Jesus was born, he establishes three heads: the place, the time, the person: In Bethlehem it was fitting, which is interpreted "house of bread," that where the living heavenly bread should be born. It is Juda: look upon Bethlehem of the nations: In the days of Herod the king, that is, he recounts the kings and leaders of Judaea, as it was said: Let not a prince fail from Juda, nor a leader from his thighs, till he come [0865B] to whom it has been promised; he shall be the expectation of the nations [var: Vulg. *Non auferetur sceptrum de Iuda … donec veniat qui mittendus est*, "The sceptre shall not be taken away from Juda … till he come that is to be sent"]: He said "Herod the king" against the Jews for this reason, that they themselves said at the cross, We have no king but Caesar. Behold, wise men come from the East, since it is the East that is sought; therefore it is indicated from the East: he himself leads us to himself. They came to Jerusalem, because out of Sion shall go forth the law, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.

*Saying, where is he that is born king of the Jews?* That is, king of confessions. They add a cause to those inquiring, Who told you? For we have seen his star in the East: the star is dumb, but bright; light leads to the Word: Likewise the star was his, and not of heaven and of earth, nor was it theirs, but the Son of God's: Nor was there after nor before any like to Christ in the flesh; therefore the star led them, that a heavenly thing [0865C] might lead heavenly men to the Heavenly One; and therefore not another creature, but a dumb one, that a dumb thing might lead dumb men to the dumb one. They brought gifts, who had worshipped idols. Herod the king was troubled, that is, through envy, lest he should surpass himself and his sons: or, they all feared, nor was it well with them: A just king, while he is with an unjust king, so that the king's companions do not become so dear in the presence of another king; or they were troubled for this, that they might please the king. Likewise, according to the letter, trouble comes to the wicked at the showing forth of good, and an evil eye is troubled at the rising of the sun.

*And gathering together all the chief priests and the scribes of the people:* that is, hoping that they might come to him, or that he might receive counsel; as God says, Ask thy father and he will declare to thee, thy elders and they will tell thee: the fathers, the chiefs of the elders, and the scribes, where Christ should be born: [0865D] He did not say where he was born, where he is; but they said, In Bethlehem of Juda. They say with words what they deny by deeds; they grope blind, as Isaac Jacob: while they neglect to go to him whom by adoring they point out. The three magi signify the three sons of Noah, with three gifts, adoring the Trinity: Then Herod, having privately called them: here the history fails, since he indicates in secret the time of the star; for this reason opinions differ as to when the magi came. For after one year or two; others say they find him not in Bethlehem, but in Egypt; and if within twelve days, how could Persians from a far land, or Evila, Arabia or Cedar, come in twelve days, but God can do this, at whose word the rocks fly, the mountains shall move, for whom it is nature that what [0866A] he bids should come to pass. Yet he diligently learned from them the time of the star, that is, that they might know at what time; perhaps he thought that a son had been born of him, or of his own race.

*Who, when they had heard the king, went their way,* when he sends them to seek Christ, he is to be heard; and when he bids them return to himself, he is to be despised; for when he led the Saviour on high, he refused; he himself did not refuse; but when he bids him be let down below and adore him, he refused lawfully: Thou shalt adore the Lord thy God, and the rest. So they, going out, know how to go out, they know not how to return; and behold the star which they had seen, and the rest: it becomes doubtful where it hid itself when these went away, something of human counsel: because the Lord hides it from our hearts, that the star may even be sought. Faith or the law? Compunction, which leads us to [0866B] Christ, went before them; when they came to Christ the star led them through: for the star did not lead them to Herod. Herod was signified as the devil, because he who goes out from the devil's suggestion to Christ goes with the star of faith. Seeing the star they rejoiced, and the rest, because it is the custom with all to rejoice over what was lost and is found: Therefore it shows the child also more exalted than his mother.

*And entering into the house,* and the rest: whether a house or an inn? And falling down they adored him; before, they were adoring a star: the creature ceased, when the Creator is adored. *And having opened their treasures* and the rest: gold to a king, frankincense to God, myrrh for a grave; whether one offered gold, another frankincense, a third myrrh: it is not known; whether each one offered three gifts is not fitly said, but [0866C] either way in a figure. They do not disagree, because three faithful are one, and in one there would be three fruits: thirtyfold, fortyfold, hundredfold; the thirtyfold in faith of the Trinity, the fortyfold in the perfection of the active life, the hundredfold in contemplation of eternal life; each one was offering three: by gold a pure conscience, by frankincense right prayer, by myrrh the mortification of the wills: Likewise three gifts: holy thought, good word, and perfect work. The star so appeared only at his nativity, as the dove at the baptism. For the star, when it went before them, was not far from the earth.

*And having received an answer in dreams,* and the rest. Therefore in dreams, with the eye closed, that they might adore God invisible and incomprehensible; and let watchful eyes not be opened [0866D] to the creature, but to the Creator: That they should not return to Herod, that is, after penance to the devil: they returned by another way into their own country. The country is paradise. He returns by another way who amends his whole life, and comes from depravity to holiness: who when they had departed.
