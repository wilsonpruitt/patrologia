## ON THE GOSPEL OF MATTHEW. (cont.)

*Do not think that I am not come to destroy the law:* [var: Vulg. *Nolite putare quoniam veni solvere legem*, "that I am come to destroy the law"] That is, [0873D] whatever work has been done, he himself fulfilled it: when the children of Israel sinned, Moses and Levi took three swords for vengeance upon them, which Christ did when he says, I came not to send peace, but the sword; and to fulfil, that is, what was lacking: because the old law, if anyone had committed adultery, commanded him to be punished; but now it is not destroyed but fulfilled, when it is said, he that shall look upon a woman to lust after her, and the rest: and again: of every idle word to render an account in the day of judgment: behold, I came not to destroy the law, but to increase what the old did not say: Amen I say to you.

*Amen,* that is, truly or faithfully: *One iota,* the name of a letter, which is made by one stroke: the letter of ten, that is, the ten words of the law; among the Greeks the letter κ, which we [0874A] have called one; *one tittle,* by one point, according to the letter; but according to the sense, the least commandments of the law: till all be fulfilled, because the things that were least in the law become full of mysteries; the iota, the old law; the tittle, the new.

*For he that shall break one of these least commandments.* That is, he who teaches much and fulfils least, the Pharisees straining out the gnat and swallowing the camel: that is, he who preaches least to others and takes greater things upon himself, shall be called least in the kingdom of heaven, that is, in the Church: *Unless your justice abound more than that of the Scribes and Pharisees:* because they taught great things and did little; or those who rejected other commandments out of the law: what they did not love, they did not say; and what they loved, they said.

*You have heard that it was said to them of old, thou shalt not kill:* [0874B]That is, not to destroy the law: *he that is angry with his brother.* That is, anger in the heart without a voice, shall be in danger of the judgment: that is, in the day of judgment: *Raca,* that is, empty or void. *Thou fool,* that is, without brain: *shall be in danger of hell fire,* that is, hell is a twofold punishment, in that it punishes the soul and the body.

*If thou offer thy gift at the altar,* and the rest: *go to be reconciled to thy brother,* that is, the gift, prayer, oblation, or thyself, thou shalt offer unto communion: one is never to come to the Church so long as thou art not reconciled to thy brother: *leave there thy gift:* how this will be fulfilled, he rebukes both alike: if he is near at hand, well; and if far off, yet he ought to be reconciled in mind; and now what has been said, to have peace with [0874C] all men.

*Be at agreement with thy adversary.* That is, the Gospel, *whilst thou art in the way with him,* that is, in this life; or with thy adversary the devil, that thou do not what he suggests to thee, lest his punishment be increased for thy sin: if a child asks for a sword, do not hold it out to him, whereby he may wound himself, and so thou art at agreement with him: *lest perhaps the adversary deliver thee to the judge,* that is, lest the law of the Gospel deliver thee to Christ the judge: because you did not agree with the Gospel; to the officer, that is, to the angel, who sends souls into punishment; into prison, that is, into hell.

*Thou shalt not go out from thence,* that is, because no one will be out of hell till he repay the last farthing: *till* is taken for *always*; the farthing is the fourth part of a coin, which is given as a fee for a washing, or is said for the least [0874D] sin: to repay the farthing is to be sent into hell, or because man is made of four elements. You have heard that it was said to them of old, thou shalt not commit adultery, that is, thou shalt not commit adultery: he that shall look upon a woman to lust after her. There are two concupiscences: the first, which, if possibility follow the affection, the deed does not follow: the second, which is consummated if possibility be at hand; adultery is committed in the body and in the heart.

*If thy right eye scandalize thee, pluck it out and cast it from thee.* That is, thy prince or thy teacher; pluck it out, that is, separate him from thee, if he shall have hindered thee from the service of God; one of thy members, that is, it is better for thee that the prince perish than that thou go with him into hell: [0875A] *the eye,* that is, thy desire, because the body is sent into hell for it: *the right hand,* that is, thy servant or thy anger: *scandalize thee,* that is, amend it away from thee: *the hand,* that is, cut off the evil work.

*Not to swear,* that is, not to forswear; *not to swear at all,* that is, never to swear; *neither by heaven, because it is the seat of God,* [var: Vulg. *quia thronus Dei est*, "because it is the throne of God"] otherwise it is, it is; *no, no,* that is, that the word be said in the mouth and in the heart; *that which is over and above, is of evil,* that is, from the unworthiness of him who is not believed.

*An eye for an eye,* that is, not to render evil for evil: *if any one shall strike thee on the right cheek,* that is, if a heretic shall strike thee with the doctrine of evil scripture, offer him another doctrine of divine scripture, unto living: *he that will take away thy coat,* that is, [0875B] to strip thee of the fault which men know in thee concerning thee: *let go thy cloak also,* that is, the fault of which thou alone art conscious: give him confession: *and if any man shall force thee one mile, go with him another mile,* [var: Vulg. *alia duo*, "another two"] that is, that as thou hast gone forth in body, go also in mind: *he that asketh of thee, give to him,* that is, he who inquires, tell him: or to a poor man, alms.

*It was said to them of old, thou lovest thy neighbour, and thou shalt hate thy enemy:* how he said, not to destroy the law: he rightly fulfils it, because in the old he speaks of the devil, to hate the enemy: but here of both. In three ways we love enemies: in heart, him who hates us in heart; in work, to do good to him who persecutes us by work; to pray in word for those who accuse us in word: to them that calumniate you, that is, false accusation: that you may be the children of your Father, [0875C] and the rest, that is, as if someone had brought us gold and silver: and if he had cursed us and struck us, we would have loved him; and when those things had failed, we too would have failed; therefore it is promised with a life that does not fail. Who maketh his sun, and the rest: and raineth upon the just, because in both the whole world is contained: in the sun, the dry and the hot; in the rain, the moist and the cold: of these four the world was made; so we ought to give to our enemies food and drink, clothing and the word, of love.

*For if you love them,* and the rest: he is called a publican because he sins publicly, or because he manages public business: the heathen, a Gentile; be ye perfect, that is, that you fulfil these things perfectly, in alms, in fasts, in prayers: *that you be not seen by men,* that [0875D] is, that good works be not done for human praise.

*When therefore thou dost alms,* that is, to a priest, and to a poor man, and to an enemy, *hypocrite* means feigned; *they have received their reward,* not God's: *let not thy left hand know,* that is, vainglory: *the right hand,* do all things for God's sake: *enter into thy chamber,* that is, the secret places of the heart, *the door being shut,* that is, against vainglory.

*Speak not much,* that is, not to learn many petitions or many words, or to entreat God with loudness of voice: *for your father knoweth what is needful for you; thus therefore pray you, Our Father,* that is, let us so live on earth that we may have a Father in heaven; we show charity and brotherhood when we say *our*: this the emperor says, [0876A] and the beggar; the lord says it, and the slave; together they say *Our Father*: therefore they who have one father are brothers: therefore let a lord not disdain to have his slave as a brother, whose brother the Lord Christ willed to be; and let us so act that we may be able to be heirs to the Father, for human law also makes sons to inherit.

*Hallowed be thy name:* here we ask that it be hallowed in us, that is, in our works; for his name is always holy. *Let thy kingdom come,* that is, as sons wish a kingdom for their father, so we too pray that he may reign in us and not the devil: he, and not sin; or the Church, or the Gospel.

*Thy will be done,* and the rest, etc. As the angels in [0876B] heaven serve without fault, so we also; or as in souls, so also in the body; or as Christians, so heretics; as the Church, so the synagogue; as justice, so sin: let them turn, that thy will be done in them.

*Our daily bread:* that is, every day until the head of the year: *this day,* that is, always: *bread,* that is, Christ; we ought so to act that we may daily be able to receive him for a remedy. *And forgive us,* and the rest: here we make a bond with God: if you are forgiven, it is forgiven you.

*And lead us not into temptation.* As thou didst lead men astray in the flood, or those in Sodom, or Pharaoh in the sea; or in the temptation of the devil, or the ambushes of men.

*But deliver us from evil.* That is, from the devil, from sin, [0876C] from an evil man: of these seven prayers four are common to all, as though the seven graces of the Holy Spirit had prayed: three petitions belong to heavenly things, that is, hallowed be thy name, thy kingdom come, thy will be done; four to this life.

*But when he shall have fasted,* and the rest: *they disfigure their faces.* That is, they transform themselves, that is, that they may be seen by men to fast: *anoint thy head, and wash thy face,* that is, what thou wishest to give to the Lord, who is thy head, give to thy neighbour: *lay not up treasure on earth,* that is, in heaven; lest rust, that is, vainglory or anger; the moth, envy or pride; thieves, demons; gold, the understanding; the world, clothing; for food, divine preaching.

[0876D] *The lamp of thy body:* that is, thy teacher or thy prince; of the body, that is, of the inner man; if it be single, of one thing: *No man can serve two masters; where thy treasure is,* and the rest: either he will hate the one, that is, him who is not good even to himself; he loves the other, who is good to himself, that is, to despise the devil, to love God. He who serves riches draws out devils; mammon means riches, or the name of the devil.

*Is not the soul more than the meat.* That is, he who gives lesser things to God and greater to the devil. *Behold the birds of the air,* and the rest. Because it is the custom of birds to fly on high and to sing a song, and whatever they may wish for themselves on earth they receive from the Lord; so men too ought to think on heavenly things and to praise God; and when with their fellow-citizens [0877A] they are in need, they receive from the Lord on earth the restoration of divinity: how much more your Father, who is good by nature, *shall give good things to them that ask him,* that is, faith through charity. Enter ye in at the narrow gate, where sinners do not find room, unless they lay aside the gate, faith, [sic: *palasticas*] II. That is, the love of God and of neighbour, the two lights, the higher the Gospel, the lower the old law; where anyone gives offence he does not enter; how wide is the gate, and the rest: that is, those serving riches and their own will.
