## ON THE GOSPEL OF MATTHEW. (cont.)

*The woman,* that is, wisdom, hid in the meal, that is, in the hearts of the faithful, three measures, shows the three sons of Noah, until the whole was leavened, that is, while the Gospel was preached into the whole world. The kingdom of heaven is like to a hidden treasure, the treasure, [0880D] that is, Christ or wisdom. In the field, that is, of the Church or in the divine Scriptures, he sold all things, that is, he left cheap things, or earthly matters, he bought the field, that is, the kingdom of heaven: the man trading, who seeks the divine doctrine, he found one pearl, that is, the wisdom of Christ, he sold all things, that is, vices. The kingdom of heaven is like to a net cast into the sea, by the net, the word of preaching is shown, into the sea, that is, into the world, gathering of every kind of fishes, that is, every kind of man; he hears the word of God beside the shore, that is, near the end of the world, the father of the household, is Christ, the Scribes, the teachers, are the apostles, he came into his own country, that is, to the Jews. The tetrarch, that is, an entered part of the kingdom.

[0881A] *He bound John and sent him into prison:* it shows the end of the old law: Herodias signifies the synagogue, the girl danced at the banquet, it shows the gentiles dancing before an idol, the head of John in a dish signifies the body of Christ upon the altar, his head in the heart of the faithful, it was given to the girl, that is, to the Church out of the gentiles, she brought it to her mother, it shows the conversion of the Jews, in time to come, they having followed, he shows them on foot, he shows that not with [sic: *anteguis*] moneys, but with his own labor ought a man to follow God: he healed the sick, that is, he washed them from sins and from idols: the hour is now past, that is, the letter of the law, five loaves, that is, the five books of Moses, two fishes, that is, the two testaments, or two orders, that is, of priests and of Prophets, or the two books of Joshua: the crowd sat down [0881B] upon the hay, that is, upon dry hearts, or it sat down upon vices, and having taken the loaves, looking up to heaven, that is, he drew off the sense, he broke, that is, he pressed out the history of the law into the spiritual sense and the understanding appeared, he blessed, that is, he multiplied: twelve baskets full, that is, of the holy doctrine the twelve apostles, for what cause are the women and the little ones not counted as the men, because little ones are not strong to fight back against their vices, so also women, because it is an imperfect sex: he bade them go up into the little ship, that is, into the Church, to go before over the strait, that is, from the Jews to the gentiles. In four ways is he called: the first by nature. The second by kinship. The third by nation. The fourth by affection: in four ways is a man spoken of, what is said, that is, good, well, do ye penance, and the rest. [0881C] Evil, ill, his blood be upon us, good, ill, where it is said of the blind man, art thou alone his disciple? Evil, well, where the Apostle says, I have delivered such a man to the destruction of Satan, [var: Vulg. *tradere huiusmodi Satanae in interitum carnis*, "to deliver such a one to Satan for the destruction of the flesh"] that the spirit may be saved. But when evening was come he was alone, it shows the end of the world, he alone came to redeem the human race.

*The ship in the midst of the sea:* that is, the Church in the midst of the world, it was tossed with the waves, that is, it suffered persecutions, a contrary wind, that is, the devils, in the fourth watch of the night Jesus came, he shows in the fourth law, that is, of the Gospel, Jesus walking upon the sea, that is, treading down the bitternesses of the age, Peter walked upon the waters, upon the bitternesses or, treading down the vices of sinners, Peter sinking into the sea, that is, the doubting of faith, so everyone who doubts in the faith, sinks [0881D] into sin, Jesus stretched out his hand, he took hold of him, that is, he gave him the help of faith: these are the four laws: the first in nature: the second the law of the letter: the third of the Prophets, the fourth of the Evangelists: when he had crossed over, that is, when he had passed over, they came into the land of Genesar, that is, from the Jews to the gentiles, they offered to him those who were ill, that is, those serving idols.

*The Canaanite woman:* she shows the primitive Church, her daughter vexed by a demon, that is, the Church of the gentiles, corrupted by idols, the girl was healed from that hour, it shows the conversion of the gentiles, from that hour in which they believed in Christ: I am not sent but to the sheep that were lost of the house of Israel, it shows bodily [0882A] to the Jews; otherwise, because all who believe in God are called Israel: to take the bread, that is, the doctrine, of the children, that is, of the Jews, to give it to dogs, that is, to the gentiles, the whelps eat, that is, the gentiles, they receive of the crumbs, that is, of the lesser commandments.

*I have compassion on the multitude:* it shows that God is wholly merciful, they continue with me three days, it shows the faith of the Trinity, they have not what to eat, that is, they have not the word of preaching, I will not send them away fasting lest they faint in the way, that is, lest they perish in the way of error in the present life, because faith without works is dead: by the seven loaves he shows the Heptateuch, the few fishes show the order of prophets or of priests, sitting down upon the ground, he shows the more perfect who have already trodden down their vices, by the seven baskets, [0882B] the seven teachers of the Holy Spirit, the Pharisees, divided, the Sadducees, justified, a clear sky, that is, the resurrection of Christ, a red sky the shedding of blood: a lowering sky shows the passion of Christ, a glowing tempest signifies persecution: beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, that is, of their doctrine, Simon, that is, obedient, that is, son of Jona, that is, a dove which is interpreted the Holy Spirit, flesh and blood has not revealed it to thee that is, father and mother has not shown it to thee, the gates of hell are the sins of men, the keys of the kingdom of heaven is the power of binding and loosing, that is, through penance they shall be opened, to suffer many things from the elders and the scribes, saying these things Jesus shows the passion and the resurrection, he says to Peter go behind me, he shows him crucified, after thee let him deny himself, [0882C] that is, let him despise the pleasure of the flesh, he who loves pleasure loses his own soul: the soul here is put for pleasure, he who loses his soul shall find it, in two ways does a man lose his soul, by martyrdom and by the denial of the body: he shall find it, that is, in the kingdom of God: he gives an exchange for his soul, who torments his flesh and nourishes his soul.

*After six days.* Now in the seventh he shows the ages of the world, the seventh which is understood as rest: Peter, James and John, here he shows the whole Church, he leads them into a mountain, that is, into the kingdom of God, his face shone as the sun, that is, the saints in the glory of the Father, otherwise the Old Testament shone in the New: garments white as snow, that is, the congregation [0882D] of all the faithful. By Moses he shows the letter of the law: By Elias, the order of prophets, Christ with the disciples shows the New Testament: A dark cloud, that is, the letter of the law. A white cloud, that is, the preaching of the Gospel. A rain cloud, that is, the preaching of the apostles, who made the fruit of penance, after the ascension of Christ, when he had come to the crowds, that is, when he had come down into the world.

*A man fallen upon his knees:* he shows the order of the Prophets, who prayed for himself, as it were the Church: his son a lunatic, shows the people of the gentiles, worshipping idols, he falls into the fire, that is, into cupidity, often into the water, that is, into luxury, he passes hence from the Jews to the gentiles, go to the sea, that is, to the gentiles.

[0883A] *Cast the hook:* that is, the word of preaching: the fish which first ascends into another, shows [sic: *cornemium*], in its mouth thou shalt find a stater, that is, the confession of faith, the humanity and the divinity. Give it to them for me and for thee, that is, for me faith, and for thee excuse, a little one enters the kingdom of heaven, that is, not little in age but pure in mind, whence the Apostle says, Be ye little ones in malice.

*He who shall scandalize one of these:* that is, whether a bodily injury, or in the turning away of faith. Let a millstone be hanged, that is, the care or the circuit of this age: About his neck, in the labor of the age: Let him be drowned in the sea, that is, in the age: Thy hand, that is, thy peer: The feet, that is, a minister: The eye, that is, thy bishop or priest or teacher and elder. And despise not [0883B] one of these, lest it be against their innocence: The sheep which strayed shows the human race: The shepherd is Christ: The sheep left in the mountains, shows the orders of angels who abide in the heavens.

*If thy brother shall have sinned, rebuke him between thee and him:* so that the uncovering of the sin be not made, and a reproach be not born to the brother, tell the Church, that is, the congregation of the faithful, a heathen, that is, gentiles, and a publican is he who sins publicly: Let not their image be hateful to thee, but their works. If two shall have agreed, that is, the flesh and the soul: If the flesh shall have agreed with the soul: he who asks, through faith receives.

*The kingdom of heaven is like to a man a king, who would take an account with his servants.* He who owes ten thousand talents, is he who sins against God. He who owes [0883C] a hundred pence, is he who sins against his brother, the fellow-servants who judged, are angels.

*When he had ended* these discourses, Jesus passed to Galilee, that is, at the end of the world, from the gentiles to Judea.

*A man leaves father and mother:* it shows that Christ left the Father in heaven, yet so left him that he did not forsake the mother, that is, the synagogue: he shall cleave to his wife, that is, Christ to the Church: They shall be two in one flesh, it shows that Christ is the head, and the body indeed the Church: The wife to forsake from the true one, that is, when anyone shall have departed from Christ, then he joins himself to heresy in one crime: Four souls perish, that is, he who dismisses his wife outside of reason and he who marries her that is dismissed: with difficulty a rich man entered and [0883D] the rest: he speaks of those who possess under avarice; the eye of a needle, is called a postern of Jerusalem: In another way the eye of a needle, the confession of faith, or of penance, the rich are said to be the Jews, the camel the people of the gentiles: in a third way the eye of a needle, shows the passion of Christ, the camel, that is, he who bears the sins of the human race: In the regeneration, that is, through the resurrection.

*The last shall be first:* and the first last. The last were the people of the gentiles; the Jews were first, they were made last: So of Peter and the thief: So of the infant and the elder: The father of the household hired workmen into his vineyard, the Father is Christ, early in the morning shows the beginning of the world: the workmen the tillers of the commandments of God, the vineyard, that is, the Church, in the old [0884A] vineyard, the law or the synagogue, the daily penny, eternal life: At the first hour came Abel, at the third hour Noah, and his sons: In the marketplace, that is, in the world, at the sixth hour came Abraham. At the ninth hour came Moses and David: At the eleventh hour, came the people of the gentiles: In another way, the first hour, that is, infancy the second; hour adolescence, the sixth youth, the ninth old age, the eleventh decrepit age: at whatsoever hour a man being converted shall have groaned from his whole heart he has received the daily penny: Evening being come, that is, the end of the world: The steward of the vineyard Christ, they who murmured are the Jews, saying we have borne the burden of the day, that is, bearing the law and the Prophets.
