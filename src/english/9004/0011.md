## ON THE GOSPEL OF LUKE.

[0893]

[0893D]Luke himself rising up, a Syrian of Antioch by nation, a physician by art, a disciple of the Apostles: he followed Paul, serving God without blame: never having a wife; at eighty-three years of age he died in Bithynia, full of the Holy Spirit, buried at Constantinople. In Achaia he wrote his Gospel in the Greek tongue: Matthew [0895A] wrote for the believing Jews: Mark for the penitent: John for the perfect: Luke for the believing Greeks.

*There was in the days of Herod,* and the rest. Here he filled out the order: he named the king and the region in which he was; he indicates the priest and his people and his wife: of the course of Abia, that is, in the days of the Lord; Zacharias is interpreted mindful of the Lord: to whom the Angel of the Lord appeared at the right hand of the altar, it shows that to him, because he is mindful of the Lord, the mystery of God is displayed: Abia is interpreted father of the Lord; it was fitting that in the time of the office. Of the Father of the Lord he should have been begun, he who was to preach the Lord as Father: Elizabeth: is interpreted the satiety of my God: she bears the figure of Mary, who was full of God; and both just before the Lord, not before men, because of old the barren woman was accursed in the law: [0895B] Walking in all the commandments, that is, they kept the whole law.

*They had no son,* because Elizabeth was barren, and both persevere in their days: Therefore these two causes; he calls the testimony to mind, the power would have been greater: Zacharias entering into the temple, that is, Christ into Mary; the people pray, likewise the people first entreat Zacharias, that is, for liberty from captivity: the angel appeared to him, he did not say came, because he had been there before: Zacharias was troubled, that is, because the Jews were troubled at the coming of Christ: thy prayer is heard, that is, more than thou didst ask: thou didst ask for the deliverance of the people, and a forerunner is granted thee, and many shall rejoice in his nativity, because after perdition they shall rejoice unto life eternal: because he himself said, behold the Lamb of God, and [0895C] the rest: he goes before Christ, he is baptized before and preached before his face, born before and dead before: six causes, because there were six months between them: I am Gabriel, that is, the strength of God: Elizabeth conceived, he did not say, of Zacharias: he knew his wife: because the will of desire failed from them: and she hid herself for modesty's sake: that she might prove whether what she had conceived were true: five months, that is, the five thousand of the age, or the five books of Moses.

*Take away my reproach,* that is, the reproach of barrenness and of conception in old age among men, because it was not a reproach with God: as in the sixth month Gabriel was sent by the Lord into a city, so in the sixth milestone of the world Christ was sent: Joseph is interpreted savior, who saved Egypt from [0895D] famine: or Mary; he blessed, that is, from the flesh of Christ: but she was troubled because she knew not whether what the angel said were of the past or of the future: The Holy Spirit came into thee; Spirit is the name of all grace when it is breathed in; how did Mary acknowledge what the angel said, or, while she was full of the Spirit she knew what the angel spoke to her: The power of the Most High overshadowed thee, that is, it hid thee in Egypt in thy flight: she is therefore called thy kinswoman, in that Elizabeth was of the tribe of Levi, that is, of the tribe of God: behold the handmaid of the Lord, as he says, the son of thy handmaid.

*She went into the mountain with haste:* all who are to fulfil the will of God: they ought to make haste: the infant leaped in the womb of his mother, that is, the son honored the Son as the woman honored the woman. She cried out [0896A] with a loud voice. It was fitting, because of John it was said, the voice of one crying in the wilderness: John is his name; why did he not say, He shall be John? because he was called by the angel before he was conceived; and they marvelled, that is, at the agreement between the father and the mother: blessed be our Lord, the goodness of God: when Zacharias asked for the deliverance of the people, a forerunner was granted to him: He hath raised up a horn of salvation, that is, Christ, because a horn is neither flesh nor bone: so also Christ is neither flesh [d: nor flesh]: only divinity; the child grew, that is, in body: this first enrolment was made, that is, for the Romans, because Julius reigned first over the Romans, but did not hold the whole world: and after him Augustus reigned, after Augustus Tiberius; in the time of Augustus and Tiberius the time of Christ about to complete his baptism: [0896B] because in the fifteenth year of the reign of Augustus, Augustus and his son Tiberius came in one chariot to their city, after they had become accustomed to the lamb of the world; it shows that in his kingdom he himself should be born who was to reign in the whole world, and the father the unity of his son's will; at that same time there appeared a brazen circle about the sun; it is shown that he is born in the time of him whose power and beauty and light and wisdom had encompassed every might: the first beauty, light and wisdom: at that same time a fountain of oil flowed from the hired lodging all the day, even until evening: it shows that he is born in the time of him from whom the fountain of oil had flowed, that is, the spiritual anointing through the whole Gospel even unto the end of the world: Augustus figures the Father, Tiberius the Son: [0896C] therefore the census is given, that the king might know the number of men and the greatness from Persia to Syria; it shows the place from which Christ was, that each should prophesy in his own; it shows the city, that no one outside the Church should render tribute to God: laid in a manger, that is, the body of Christ upon the altar: the ox and the ass at the manger of Christ shows the Gentiles and the Jews communicating upon the altar: room in the inn, that is, a house between two walls, having two doors: the figure of the Church, between paradise and the world; the army of soldiers shows that Christ the leader is to be followed: the shepherds were speaking, [d: it was fitting that the shepherds were speaking,] it was fitting that the shepherds should bear testimony: of the true lamb: let us pass over unto Bethlehem, it shows us not to look back, neither to the right hand nor to the left, but only unto [0896D] Christ: they found Mary, Joseph and the infant; by these three the world was healed.

*Mary kept all these words,* that is, what the angel said to her, after the eight days were accomplished that he should be circumcised: and in the eighth beatitude it shows the passion of Christ, when it is said: blessed are they who suffer persecution: the circumcision of Christ on the eighth day shows the blood of the martyrs, even unto the day of judgment: or because he fulfilled the law and did not loose it: after the days of her purification were fulfilled, that is, twelve days for a boy, for a girl thirty, it was in the law that a woman should not enter into the Church: a pair of turtledoves, or two young pigeons; by the fierceness of the turtledove he shows that we are to be prudent as serpents, by [0897A] the dove to be simple: a pair, he shows that a man should offer his whole self to God: the revelation of the Gentiles, that is, the glory of faith: behold he is set for a fall, that is, for those who do not believe in the resurrection: for those who believe in him, and for a sign, that is, the Gospel: I came to set a man against his mother, and the rest: As he himself says, who is my mother? blessed is that womb which shall bear thee, and the rest, that is, that many should think good thoughts: since Mary is not the only mother of Christ.

*And let a sword pass through thy own soul,* that is, the word of God, or the passion of Christ: she had lived with her husband seven years, that is, the synagogue with the Heptateuch: and she departed not from the temple, that is, because the Church ought not to depart from heaven: and the child grew, that is, in body, and was strengthened, that is, in Spirit: when he was [0897B] twelve years old, that is, the twelve tribes which came to Christ: after three days they found him: The father, that is, the Church, and the mother, that is, the synagogue, who sought him through the three laws and did not find him, save through the Gospel: *and he was subject unto them,* it shows that the Son of man came not to be ministered unto but to minister.

*From the fifteenth year of Tiberius Caesar,* the first king, the first governor are recorded at the nativity of Christ, that is, while they had not begun to teach: But now a fourth king, a tetrarch, is commemorated, when he comes to the preaching and to the baptism: the king signifies Christ, and the four tetrarchs show the four Gospels: two priests is the two laws: by the number of six the world was made: the first king [0897C] of the Romans shows the unity of the kingdom of the Romans, and the fourth tetrarch over the Jews shows the division of the kingdom of the Jews: the word of the Lord came upon John, go and preach baptism The valley shall be filled, that is, Jews and Gentiles: The mountains and hills shall be brought low, that is, he who humbles himself is exalted: and the crooked shall be indirect [var: Vulg. Lk 3:5 *prava in directa*, "the crooked into straight"], that is, let him who was lustful be chaste: and the rough ways into plain ways, that is, let him who is proud be humble. All flesh shall see the salvation of God, that is, Jews and Gentiles, men and women.

*He came from Nazareth:* in which he was brought up, it shows the Church in which he was brought up, that a man should not forget it; and he rose up to read, here it shows that if it be revealed to a younger man, let the elder hold his peace, that is, the Evangelist and the Pentateuch: Peter also rises and stood in the midst of the disciples, [0897D] it shows that what is not to be rendered back is to be preached: as to he anointed me, that is, not common oil, but spiritual: to preach the Gospel to the poor, that is, that blessed are the poor in spirit: and redemption to the captives, that is, to the Jews from Pharaoh, and the Gentiles from the devil: sight to the blind, that is, to Jews and Gentiles: dismiss the bruised, that is, the Jews: Clay to be wrought, and the Gentiles by supplicating idols: the year of the Lord, that is, because the year has four seasons, it shows the four Evangelists: and twelve months, it shows the twelve Apostles: and the day of retribution, that is, the day of judgment: to the letter, this day is the scripture fulfilled in your ears.

*There were many widows in Israel,* that is, the Church: Elias signifies Christ: Naaman the Syrian signifies the Gentiles: [0898A] the daughter, that is, the synagogue: Giezi, that is, the Church: having a devil, that is, idols to be believed in: What have we to do with thee? that is, to us torments, to thee glory: beside the lake, that is, beside the law: two ships, that is, two Churches: launch out into the deep, that is, Christ ascending into heaven: toiling all the day we have taken nothing [var: Vulg. Lk 5:5 *per totam noctem*, "through the whole night"], that is, through the old law we led no one unto life: They filled both the ships, that is, because he converts through the Church and the synagogue: so that they were sinking, that is, the man who riches: and poverty does not bear: depart from me, Lord, for I am a sinner, it shows, that is, the Lord did not depart, because he does not forsake true humility.

*Fear not,* that is, let not the sinner fear the physician: the report of him went abroad the more, that is, of his healing and [0898B] of his doctrine; when he prayed it shows the contemplative life, when he sounded forth it shows the active life: through the tiles, that is, through the divinity: rise and walk, it shows the resurrection of all: he went out into the mountain to pray: and he departs to the crowds, it shows concerning the contemplative and the active, into one.
