## ON THE GOSPEL OF LUKE. (cont.)

Four beatitudes are read, because the four cardinal virtues are contained in them: [sic: *caudes*], in Latin it is said, of the heart: these four gentle ones, the four parts of the world are shown: Likewise the eight pertains to the eight souls who were in the ark of Noah, through whom the whole human race; he shows your reward is great in glory, that is, for preaching, for persecution and for patience: according to these things did their fathers to the Prophets, that is, they hated them, they reproached them and cast out [0898C] their names, that is, you who have your consolation, and do not give thanks to God. Not to God but to your riches: Woe to you when men shall bless you, that is, your enemies: Despairing of nothing, give what you have, that in glory you may receive your reward from the Lord.

*Can the blind lead the blind,* that is, the fool teach the fool: both shall fall into the ditch, that is, into perdition: He went into a city which is called Naim, it shows that after the sick man was healed he raised the dead man, because infirmity precedes death: the only son of his mother, that is, Adam is carried from the earth by four, that is, by the four elements, whence man is: the place is touched by God, that is, the wood of the cross, because Adam perished by wood: A man is carried by earth, when he thinks only earthly things, or when he is sick and [0898D] cannot walk; he is carried by water, when he thinks to live wantonly, or when for excess of cold his body cannot be touched: he is carried by air, when he does all things for human praise, or when for sickness he cannot breathe: he is carried by fire when he thinks upon avarice, or when for excess of fever he cannot be touched: the Lord raised three dead, that is, the son of one mother, and the daughter of the ruler, and Lazarus; the first in the house, that is, in thought: the second at the gate, that is, in word: the third from the tomb, that is, in deed: he who is less in the kingdom of God is greater than he, that is, Christ, because the angels were not born: as he says, thou hast made him a little less than the angels: nevertheless Christ is greater than John, reigning with the Father wisdom [0899A] is justified by her sons, that is, the peoples and the publicans justified God.

*An alabaster box of ointment,* that is, the body with faith: standing behind, that is, after the ascension of the Lord: at his feet, that is, of the Apostles: she watered them with tears, that is, inward charity in God: the hairs, that is, superfluous food, and to give a garment: she kisses his feet, that is, to have peace with all: One owed five hundred pence, that is, the sins of the gentiles committed through the five senses: The other fifty, that is, the sins of the Jews: This woman was Mary Magdalene, out of whom seven devils were cast: who is he that forgiveth sins? that is, divine might and power: this Mary figures the Church, out of which he cast seven vices, because in seven days man sinned.

[0899B]A certain man, that is, the people of the gentiles: for a long time, that is, from the beginning of the world: He was not clothed with a garment, that is, with faith. Nor did he abide in a house, that is, in the Church: but in the tombs, that is, the custom of sinning: with chains upon his feet, that is, the law of nature and of the letter: sitting, that is, in faith; after baptism: clothed, that is, in immortality: at his feet, that is, at the Apostles: he sent him away and said, return into thy house, it shows concerning the contemplative, not to come to the active.

*A woman,* that is, the Church: an issue of blood, that is, the law of nature: for twelve years, that is, two ages.

*She touches the hem of his garment,* that is, she believed in the least commandment of Christ: who is it that touched me, that is, who has believed me: that virtue has gone out from me, [0899C] that is, unto the proving of him who had faith: Peter and John and James, that is, faith, hope, charity: the father, that is, the bishop: the mother, the Church: the villages and the cities, that is, servants and lords; they told him what they had done: the day began to decline, that is, to become night; he dismisses, the crowd, the Jews.

*Give ye them to eat,* that is, preach to the gentiles; he blessed, that is, the Gospel: he broke, that is, to the letter: the Son of man must suffer many things, that is, to die: sailing out of the commonwealth by the peril of the ship, who after the peril saw the calm, his decease, to suffer many things on the following day, that is, in the second law: a man of the crowd cried out, that is, Abraham: an only son, that is, the whole race: suddenly crying out, that is, growing angry, and it dashes him in the flattery of his neighbor: it teareth him, that is, [0899D] in despair; with foaming, that is, with folly: the hand upon the plough, that is, the work upon the Gospel: back, that is, in the world: The ploughshares, that is, charity: four oxen, that is, the four Evangelists: the Lord appointed other seventy disciples, as Moses chose seventy elders out of the twelve tribes, that is, that the seventy tongues which had been divided through pride should be gathered into one tongue through humility: the animals two by two in the ark, and two disciples for preaching, charity: it shows the New and the Old Testament: body and soul, man and woman, and two disciples.

*I send you as lambs among wolves:* I the shepherd, you the sheep; the wolves, that is, the Scribes and Pharisees. The devils are subject [0900A] unto you, that is, what was not in the law was in the mouth: over serpents, that is, over the heathen, because the serpent does not kill, but pours out the venom of doctrine: the scorpion kills with its tail and its head alike: the same is the devil, who wills to deceive at the beginning and at the end: a man was going down, that is, the world, which is called accursed: among robbers, that is, devils: they stripped him, that is, of good faith: they scourged him, that is, they made him to sin: they left him half alive, that is, the body living, the conscience and the soul dead: the priest, that is, Aaron: the Levite, that is, the tribe of Levi with its law: the Samaritan, that is, Christ: who is of the suburbs of Samaria; he himself is interpreted keeper, because concerning Christ God: the Lord keeping the little ones: he bound up his wounds, that is, through penance he bound fast [0900B] his sins: he pours in oil and wine, by the severity and the sweetness of scripture, by reward and by punishment: upon his own beast, that is, upon his own body; because through the passion he led the human race to the inn, that is, to the Church: two pence, that is, the two laws: he gave to the innkeeper, that is, the order of teachers: whatsoever thou shalt have spent over and above, that is, whatsoever thou shalt have done by thy diligence, concerning the doctrine of men, when I return, that is, at the judgment, I will repay thee: Martha, that is, the active Church.

*Mary,* that is, the contemplative Church, which ever hears the words of Christ: Martha said: that her sister should have helped her, because there is need, of the active life, that the contemplative ought to help it: Friend, that is, the teacher At midnight, that is, in due season: three loaves, it shows the faith of the Trinity: My friend, that is, another doing penance [0900C] out of the way, that is, out of the world. I have not what I may set before him, that is, I know not how to teach him: the door shut, that is, the door of heaven, which nothing opens save charity, my children are with me, that is, the heavenly virtues: what is without and what is within, that is, he who made the body himself made the soul: give alms and all things are clean: and if you shall not give, all things which you have are unclean. Fear not, little flock: these are the Jews, they said that they believed: because he was accursed in the law who became poor.

*Let your loins be girt:* it shows chastity: Lamps burning, that is, good works. That when he shall come, that is, in sorrow: and shall knock, that is, the soul through death: and that they may open immediately, that is, to be always ready: he will pass and minister unto them, that is, after the judgment, life [0900D] eternal: I came to send fire upon the earth, that is, charity: and those eighteen Galileans signify, upon whom the tower fell, that is, the Holy Spirit, because *sylva* is called sent: let it alone this year also, that is, the Gospel: I will dig about it, that is, I will preach; a basket of dung, to confess sins; in time to come, that is, unto the day of judgment: a woman, that is, the human race: a spirit of infirmity, that is, faith and works: eighteen years, that is, the two laws, or they who have not fulfilled the ten words of the law and have not believed in the resurrection, because it comes to pass on the eighth day; by the three sixes it shows she was infirm before the law: under the law, under grace: bowed down to the earth, that is, earthly desires: to look upward, that is, because heavenly things through penance [0901A] she did not seek: he called her to him, that is, through confession, and he laid his hand upon her, that is, good examples: she was healed, the hope of health: the first day of the week, that is, unto the rest to come: tell that fox, that is, Herod, because the devil is an animal, it dwells within, it destroys the vineyard, so Herod the devil destroys the vineyard, as it is said, thou hast brought a vine out of Egypt.

*A man with the dropsy,* that is, the human race: the fornicator, having laid hold of him he healed him, it shows that then the assault of fornication is healed: a man, that is, God the Father: a great supper, that is, the Gospel; he called many, that is, the Jews: he sent his servant, that is, Christ in the flesh: all things are ready, that is, the things which were prophesied of Christ: I have bought a farm, that is, for avarice I have not come to Christ: yokes of oxen, that is, pride: [0901B] otherwise the yokes of oxen are the five senses, or the five books of Moses: I have married a wife, that is, luxury: the poor and the feeble, these are they who know their own sins: go out into the ways, that is, to the gentiles: compel them to come in, that is, through confession: to build a tower, that is, to follow Christ; the charges, that is, to renounce the world: what king, that is, the Apostles; to join battle, that is, the weighing of works: against another king, that is, against Christ with ten thousand, that is, with the works which a man perceives: with twenty thousand, that is, with the works which are in secret, and in the open, he sends an embassy, that is, by showing mercy, by giving alms; he asks the things which are for peace, as it is, enter not into judgment with thy servant, O Lord: on the earth, that is, upon riches: on the dunghill, that is, upon poverty; a certain [0901C] woman, that is, riches; a lamp lighted, that is, the Gospel: the groat found in the house, that is, the sinner converted in the Church: a certain man, that is, God the Father.
