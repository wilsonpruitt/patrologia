## HERE BEGINS THE CAPITULATIO. (cont.)

*But when the morning was come:* he shows the coming of Christ accomplished: cast on the right side, that is, cast the preaching among the gentiles. For Peter was naked, that is, they were naked of the faith of Christ: he girt himself with his coat, that is, the faith of the Church: he cast himself into the sea, that is, into the tossings and the bitternesses of the world through the love of Christ: the other disciples came in a ship, that is, treading down the tossing of the world in the Church; they saw coals, and a fish and bread laid thereon; by the fish he shows faith, by the coals the warmth of charity, by the bread the divine word: drawing the net to [0915B] the land, by the shores he shows the end of the world: full of great fishes, that is, the fulness and the greatness of the Church, through the remission of sins: by the three he shows the Trinity: the net undivided and not broken, that is, the Church uncorrupted in the time to come: Jesus ate after the resurrection, he shows the true incarnation of the body, that they which believed might believe.

*Jesus said, Simon son of John, lovest thou me?* He shows that as he thrice denied, so he should thrice confess the faith of the Trinity: feed my sheep, that is, in the Church: Jesus entering in when the doors were shut shows that he entered the womb when the doors were shut, so also all the saints after the resurrection [0916A] put on a spiritual body, and can enter everywhere without fear, whence whole and uncorrupt in marriage; three testify to this sentence.

*The disciple whom Jesus loved.* The second miracle is that which Jesus did in Cana of Galilee, he pressed out wine from water, a work made ready, the virginity of John, he shows that a spouse was joined to him: the testimony that Jesus commended his mother to John: by Mary the Church is figured. John the chief place; as John was a virgin in marriage, so was he uncorrupt also in death: John relateth the genealogy from the divinity, for the others relate it from the man: of the divinity John saith, in the beginning was the Word; he lay upon the breast of Jesus, and John ascended to the knowledge of the divinity which was in the breast of Jesus, from the joining [0916B] of the two breasts; a grafted tree, in another it maketh the fruit of the tree, of the other in which it is planted. If according to the order of the canon this book were to be set before Matthew, and that it is placed in the last part, it is because the earth was sanctified first, then the water, and afterwards last of all the fire, because it is brighter and subtler: likewise after the likeness of the two laws, that is, the old and the new; the old was delivered first, yet the new is brighter and greater, because it discerneth of heavenly things, and more abundant mine, which pertaineth to the Gospel of John out of what is said above.
