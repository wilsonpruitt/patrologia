## PREFACE.

This book, which is headed with the name of Baruch, is not contained in the Hebrew canon, but only in the Vulgate edition, and likewise the epistle of Jeremiah. But for the [0064] knowledge of readers they have been written here, because they indicate many things concerning Christ and the last times.

[nt: ( The ancient copies of the Glossa ordinaria exhibit only the text of Baruch, with no accompanying exposition. )]
