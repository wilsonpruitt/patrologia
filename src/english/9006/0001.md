## CHAPTER II.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Thy youth. [n: (HIER.)]* This is read more fully in Ezechiel, where the Lord joins Jerusalem to himself [0011A] in wedlock, and under the person of a wife unites her to his own embraces. ⟦s2⟧ Or, that he may show a more ardent affection, he calls her a maiden and betrothed. ⟦s3⟧ For what we have not yet obtained, that we desire the more ardently.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *All they that devour. [n: (HIER.)]* Even as they who devour the firstfruits, etc., *as far as* the firstfruits are owed to the priests, not to enemies.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Israel. [n: (ID.)]* Both names are set down, Jacob and Israel, not according to the two and the ten tribes, but according to the whole people, since Jacob himself was afterwards called Israel.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *What have your fathers found.* ⟦s2⟧ God speaks of an offence committed by the fathers, not because the sins of the fathers are imputed to the sons, but because the sons, having the likeness of their fathers, are punished both for their own crime and for their parents'. ⟦s3⟧ For the sake of [0011B] the holy fathers God has mercy on the sons.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Neither did man dwell. [n: (ID.)]* He who is ever hastening to greater things, etc., *as far as* but a struggle, and an uncertain issue of things to come.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *And I brought you in,* as though he should say: In return for the labour of a most hard journey, etc., *as far as* and we make his inheritance abominable.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Cethim. [n: (ID.)]* Cethim, that is, either of Italy or of the western parts, because near to Judaea is the island of Cyprus, in which there is a city of this name, whence Zeno the prince of the Stoics.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And consider. [n: (ID.)]* We can say this against those who follow vices with greater zeal than virtues, to whom it is said: *I speak a human thing, because of the infirmity of your flesh; for as you have yielded your members [0011C] to serve uncleanness and iniquity unto iniquity [n: (Rom. VI)]*, etc.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *My people.* ⟦s2⟧ Like the anthropophormitae, who, taking occasion from this testimony: *Let us make man to our image and likeness [n: (Gen. I)]*, stubbornly contend that the boundless and simple substance of the Godhead is composed of our lineaments and of a human shaping.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *Be astonished, O ye heavens. [n: (ID.)]* The Seventy: The heaven was astonished, etc., *as far as* and both the heavens and the heaven are called by the same name, as Thebae, Athenae.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *They have forsaken me.* ⟦s2⟧ I who gave the commandment, saying: *I am the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt.* ⟦s3⟧ And according to that which in the same place is [0011D] written: *Let there be no strange gods before me,* [var: Vulg. Exod. 20:3 *Non habebis deos alienos coram me*, "Thou shalt not have strange gods before me"] in whose stead he followed after devils.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The fountain of living water. [n: (ID.)]* A fountain is perpetual and has living waters. ⟦s2⟧ Cisterns and pools are filled from torrents and troubled waters by the rains.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Is he a servant. [n: (ID.)]* From this passage of Juda, lifted up into pride, etc., *as far as Cursed be Chanaam, a servant shall he be to his brethren [sic: *fratibus*] [n: (Gen. IX)]*. [var: Vulg. Gen. 9:25 *Chanaan, servus servorum erit fratribus suis*, "a servant of servants shall he be to his brethren"]

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Lions.* ⟦s2⟧ According to the anagogy, we understand lions, etc., *as far as All that commit adultery, their hearts are as an oven [n: (Osee VII)]*. [var: Vulg. Osee 7:4 *quasi clibanus succensus a coquente*, "as an oven heated by the baker"]

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Unto the crown of the head.* ⟦s2⟧ According to that: *From the sole of the foot unto the crown of the head,* etc., *as far as* who defile the purity of the Church with their own foulness.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0012A]VERS. 19.-- *Thy own wickedness shall reprove thee. [n: (ID.)]* Note that wickedness and prevarication, etc., *as far as* because he hath forsaken the Lord his God.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Of old thou hast broken. [n: (ID.)]* This can be said of him, etc., *as far as* and they make their feet to straddle for every one that passeth by.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *A chosen vineyard.* ⟦s2⟧ In the Hebrew, *soreth,* which is a kind of the choicest vine, from whose hoe [cj: *sarculo*; read *surculo*, "shoot"] he planted Israel, and he marvels how the true seed and the chosen vineyard, turned into bitterness, has been made a stranger.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *How then art thou turned?* ⟦s2⟧ Let no man be secure if the planting, etc., *as far as* if it shall have abided in that whereunto it was planted.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Borith. [n: (ID.)]* The Hebrew word itself. ⟦s2⟧ But the Seventy, the fullers' herb, which in Palestine grows in moist and green places, and for the washing away of filth [0012B] has the same power as nitre.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *See thy ways,* that is, look upon the valley of the sons of Ennon, which is watered by the springs of Siloe, there thou shalt discern the shrine of Baal, whom, having forsaken God, thou hast worshipped.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *Keep back thy foot.* ⟦s2⟧ About to keep the pasch, etc., *as far as* which ought to be trodden and crushed by the evangelical foot.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *They have turned. ⟦s2⟧ Casting my words behind them.* ⟦s3⟧ For when a master gives command, etc., *as far as* but they show the swelling of their mind by the gesture of their body.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 28.-- *According to the number.* ⟦s2⟧ The several cities worshipped gods, whether the same or different, so that not even in impiety might they seem to have agreement; but that superstition, fighting against itself, might follow a divergent error.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0012C]VERS. 29.-- *Why will you?* ⟦s2⟧ Human perversity is prone to excuse itself: and what it deservedly bears, it seems to itself to bear unjustly.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 30.-- *Your children.* ⟦s2⟧ That by the stripes of your children you might learn that you were to be healed by a sterner medicine, and lest perchance you should say, Thou wouldst not rebuke us when we sinned.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *To see the word of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ Moses saw the voice of the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ And John the apostle says that he saw and handled the Word of God.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *Will she forget? [n: (ID.)]* He loses his ornament who withdraws from the Lord, and the understanding of doctrine, which is signified in the breast: whence, *John at the supper leaned upon the breast of the Lord [n: (Joan. XIII)]*, and among other things the little breast [0012D] of the victims is kept for the priests.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *In all.* ⟦s2⟧ In these, or another reading, under every oak; for the Hebrew signifies both the oak and these things. ⟦s3⟧ But in pleasant places, in shade and leaves, they used to sacrifice.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 35.-- *And thou hast said.* ⟦s2⟧ These things are to be used among those, etc., *as far as* not to mourn, but to hold out excuses.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Behold I will contend in judgment. [n: (ID.)]* Let the new heresy sprung from the old hear that it is the very greatest wrath of God, to be unwilling to confess sin humbly, but shamelessly to boast one's righteousness.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 36.-- *And from Egypt.* ⟦s2⟧ That the onset of the Egyptians, etc., *as far as* they are rebuked therefore because, having forsaken God, they place their hope in men.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 37.-- *Upon thy head. [n: (ID.)]* After the manner of mourners, [0013A] as Thamar, corrupted by Amnon, strewed ashes upon her head, and laid her hands above it, and so returned into her own house.
