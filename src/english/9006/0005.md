## CHAPTER VI.

[0018D]VERS. 1.-- *Strengthen yourselves,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Because at any moment now Nabuchodonosor is to come from the north, etc., *as far as* among these there is another village, set upon a mountain, which in the Syriac and Hebrew tongue is named Betacaren.

VERS. 2.-- *Comely.* The beauty of Jerusalem is described, which is Sion itself, so that the one name sounds the whole city, the other the citadel of the city. For Sion is interpreted *citadel,* that is, *watchtower,* and is compared to a comely woman.

VERS. 3.-- *Shepherds. [n: (HIER.)]* The word is here set down elegantly enough, etc., *as far as* of whom some make manifest for the defiling of the harlot, others for the overthrow of the city.

[0019A]VERS. 4.-- *Woe to us,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] While those say: *Rise up and let us go up,* these answer: *Woe to us,* etc. And this is the sense: If by day we suffer this, what shall we suffer by night?

VERS. 5.-- *Rise up.* They who said, rise up together and go up, etc., rouse themselves by those words to war: *Rise up, and let us go up in the night,* that the adversaries may know that the victory is not of the time, but of the virtues.

VERS. 7.-- *As a cistern maketh her water cold.* Without the heat of faith: the Church, fervent in spirit: but the wicked, cold; whence: *The charity of many shall grow cold [n: (Matth. XIV)]*. And that: *I shall be likened to them that go down into the pit [n: (Psal. XXVIII)]*. Let it suffice to have said once that "lacum" sounds not a pool, according to the Greeks, but a cistern, which in the Syriac and Hebrew [0019B] speech is called Gab.

VERS. 8.-- *Be thou instructed. [n: (HIER.)]* By this we learn that *God scourgeth,* etc., *as far as* through every grief and scourge thou shalt be instructed, Jerusalem.

VERS. 9.-- *Even to the cluster. [n: (ID.)]* As if, when Jerusalem shall have been laid waste, etc., *as far as* as it were into a basket or into a winepress.

VERS. 10.-- *Uncircumcised ears,* etc. It is to be noted that circumcision is spoken of in the Scriptures in three ways: in the foreskin, in the heart, in the ears, whence: *He that hath ears to hear, let him hear [n: (Matth. XI)]*.

VERS. 11.-- *Therefore with fury. [n: (HIER.)]* As if he should say: I look upon the coming wrath of God, and I am full of his fury and indignation, and I can bear no more: [0019C] nor do I dare to entreat God for sinners.

VERS. 12.-- *I will stretch out. [n: (HIER.)]* The Seventy: *I will lift up:* but each is the bearing of one striking; whence: *And his hand is stretched out still* or, *raised on high [n: (Isa. V)]*.

VERS. 14.-- *And they healed the breach. [n: (ID.)]* As if he should say: When they were doing such great evils, etc., *as far as* rather making them ready for punishment and for the anger of God.

VERS. 15.-- *They were confounded. [n: (ID.)]* This is to be read more expressly, etc., *as far as* nay rather they increased their sin by contempt.

*They knew not.* They would not; but by excessive contempt and by the vice of violent evil they could not even understand. A great impiety, not only not to beware of sins, but not even to be willing to understand them, and to have no distinction [0019D] between good and evil works.

VERS. 16.-- *Stand ye upon the ways.* Let us read the Gospel parable, in which the good merchant sold all his pearls that he might buy one precious one: that, namely, through the patriarchs and the prophets we may come to him who saith: *I am the way, the truth and the life [n: (Joan. XIV)]*, whence, *Stand ye upon the ways,* etc.

VERS. 17.-- *The voice of the trumpet. [n: (HIER.)]* The voice of the trumpet, the commandments of the Gospel or the teaching of the apostles: whence, *Go up upon a high mountain, thou that bringest good tidings to Sion, lift up thy voice like a trumpet, thou that announcest to Jerusalem.* [var: Vulg. Is 40:9 *Super montem excelsum ascende, tu qui evangelizas Sion ; exalta in fortitudine vocem tuam, qui evangelizas Jerusalem*, "lift up thy voice with strength … thou that bringest good tidings to Jerusalem"; the trumpet is drawn in from Is 58:1]

VERS. 20.-- *To what purpose do ye bring me frankincense. [n: (ID.)]* As if he should say: In vain for me, for the compounding of ointments, etc., *as far [0020A] as* since the Scripture saith, *The redemption of a man's soul is his own riches [n: (Prov. XIII)]*.

VERS. 21.-- *Fathers and sons. [n: (ID.)]* The sons follow the blasphemies of the fathers, daily taking upon themselves that imprecation: *His blood be upon us and upon our children [n: (Matth. XXVII)]*.

VERS. 22.-- *Behold a people,* etc. Properly this is said of the Babylonians, who are to come against Jerusalem. And the whole array of armour is described, and the onset of the fighters, that, moved by terror, they may do penance and appease the most merciful God.

VERS. 23.-- *His voice like,* etc. Allegorically. [n: (HIER.)] We can turn this testimony to another use, etc., *as far as* and like the waves of a most violent sea, so they overwhelm those who resist.

[0020B]VERS. 25.-- *Go not forth, [n: (ID.)]* The Gospel also teacheth, etc., *as far as* but let them guard themselves most firmly with fortifications.

VERS. 27.-- *And thou shalt prove. [n: (ID.)]* As if he should say: That when thou shalt have proved and known the way, etc., *as far as* who like deaf asps stop their ears, that they may not hear the voice of the charmers.

*All these. [n: (GREG., Moral., l. XVIII, c. 14)]*. They are princes departing from the Lord and disobedient, etc., *as far as* he who is at variance with the unity of the Church loveth not his neighbour.

## CHAPTER VII.

VERS. 2.-- *Stand in the gate of the house of the Lord,* etc., [n: (HIER.)] Through which the multitude of the people enters in to entreat the Lord, etc., *as far as* and by the occasion [0020C] and the throng of the place they are compelled to hear the word of the Lord.

VERS. 4.-- *The temple of the Lord. [n: (ID.)]* This can fit those virgins, etc., *as far as* in which is true faith, holy conversation and the choir of all the virtues.

VERS. 8.-- *Behold you trust in yourselves. [n: (ID.)]* These sins show that in vain do they have confidence in the temple, etc., *as far as* and they break out into so great a madness that they boast themselves delivered, because they have departed from the worship of God.

VERS. 11.-- *A den of thieves,* etc. [n: (ID.)] From this place I think is taken what is said in the Gospel, etc., *as far as* my eyes have beheld what you think hidden.

[0020D]VERS. 12.-- *Go ye to my place in Silo,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Where, namely, my first tabernacle was, etc., *as far as When the Son of man shall come, thinkest thou he shall find faith upon the earth [n: (Luc. XVIII)]*.

VERS. 13.-- *Rising early to you,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Not that there is for him any time apart from the daybreak, etc., *as far as* they are called sons of the light and not of the night nor of the darkness, nor sleeping, they, namely, who keep the commandments of God.

VERS. 14.-- *As I did to Silo.* I cast off Silo, being about to cast off the temple also: I cast off the ten tribes, being about to cast off the two tribes also. Whatever he threateneth to that people, let us fear for ourselves also, lest we do the like.

VERS. 16.-- *Do not thou therefore,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Lest [0021A] the prophet, praying, should seem not to obtain what he asks, the Lord commands, etc., *as far as* this impenitence, this blasphemy, or word against the Holy Spirit hath not remission for ever.

VERS. 17.-- *Dost thou not see.* Lest we think God cruel, who suffers not even to be entreated, he gives the reason why he does not hear: *Dost thou not see?*

VERS. 20.-- *Is enkindled. [n: (HIER.)]* As if he should say: What I long would not do, by the multitude of your sins I am compelled to do upon men and upon beasts. When God is angry, both men and the things that are man's feel a like severity.

VERS. 22.-- *Because I have not spoken. [n: (ID.)]* He plainly shows that he first gave the decalogue, etc., *as far as* therefore the evangelical grace was necessary, which [0021B] should preserve them not by their own merit, but by the mercy of God.

VERS. 27.-- *And thou shalt speak to them. [n: (ID.)]* Hesitate not to harden your necks, etc., *as far as* now at least speak to them my words.

VERS. 28.-- *This is the nation.* This was done in the time of the prophets, and went before in shadow. In Christ it was fully accomplished, whose discipline they would not hear, whence it is elegantly inferred: *Faith is perished and is taken away.* Which is properly the Christians'.

VERS. 29.-- *Cut off the hair.* Among the ancients it was the custom of mourners to shear the hair, now, on the contrary, to let the hair grow long is the token of grief.

VERS. 31.-- *The high places of Tophet. [n: (HIER.)]* Tophet in the Hebrew, breadth in the Latin, which is a place, etc., *as far as* [0021C] for neither, if a dog or an ass or any unclean animal whatsoever had first met the master, ought he to have offered it.

VERS. 32.-- *Behold the days shall come. [n: (ID.)]* When he departed from fealty to his lord, then the siege began. But when the king of Egypt came and would deliver Jerusalem, Nabuchodonosor put him to flight. Afterwards Nabuzardan besieged her, and took her, and led Sedecias into Antioch, and there deprived.

VERS. 34.-- *And I will cause to cease.* When the place of idolatry shall have been turned into graves, so that where they had offended God, there their corpses should lie unburied.
