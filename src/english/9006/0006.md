## CHAPTER VIII.

VERS. 1.-- *In that time,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] All things [0021D] which the prophetic discourse describes, etc., *as far as* the gold and silver which were laid in the tombs after the ancient manner.

VERS. 4.-- *And thou shalt say.* After so great evils he calls to penance those who remained. Or, before there come the things which he has threatened, he exhorts to conversion, and points out the place of penance. *He is turned away,* or *he turneth away.* As if he should say: One converted can by prayers withstand and turn away the anger of God.

VERS. 5.-- *Why then,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] The more I called them to penance, the more they went back, not so much out of zeal for sinning as for overcoming me.

VERS. 6.-- *No man speaketh that which is good,* etc. *[n: (ID.)] Every kind of men is prone to evil [n: (Gen. VIII)]*. In [0022A] the time of the Saviour, *all have gone aside, they are become unprofitable together, there is none that doth good [n: (Psal. XIII)]*. Whence he himself: *Save me, O Lord, for the holy man hath failed [n: (Ibid. XI)]*. Where then are they who say that it is placed in our own power to be free of every sin?

VERS. 7.-- *The kite in the air. [n: (ID.)]* As if he should say: Even the birds know their own times, so that they know when, hastening to warm places, they ought to shun the harshness of winter, and at the beginning of spring to return to their accustomed regions.

VERS. 10.-- *Therefore will I give. [n: (ID.)]* They have received their reward, and they who cast away the word of the Lord, they themselves are cast away.

VERS. 11.-- *And they healed the breach. [n: (ID.)]* As if he should say: Good physicians desired to heal others' wounds with words, etc., *as far as* and the shame of those who deceive, [0022B] saying, peace, peace, etc.

VERS. 12.-- *Therefore shall they fall. [n: (ID.)]* That they whose dignity was eminent should be mingled also in the ruin of the people, because, namely, *from the least even to the greatest all study avarice [n: (Jer. VI)]*. [var: Vulg. Jr 6:13 *a minore quippe usque ad majorem omnes avaritiae student*; the verse under gloss, Jr 8:10, reads *a minimo usque ad maximum omnes avaritiam sequuntur*]

VERS. 13.-- *There is no grape,* etc. As if he should say: When the seasons pass, and autumn succeeds to summer, and in winter the leaves of the trees fall: from afar you shall see all things and shall not take food from them.

VERS. 14.-- *Why do we sit.* The voice of the people is brought in, answering, and confessing their vices, and exhorting one another. *Assemble yourselves, and let us enter into the fenced city.* Or the one city Jerusalem: for the others had already been taken.

VERS. 16.-- *From Dan was heard.* Where the river Jordan [0022C] rises: Nabuchodonosor is described as coming from Dan through Phoenicia with his army.

VERS. 17.-- *I will send serpents among you. [n: (ID.)]* That they may strike you incurably, etc., *as far as* they who despise the words of the Lord are delivered over to adverse powers.

VERS. 18.-- *My sorrow. [n: (ID.)]* These things are to be read in the person of the Lord, bewailing the overthrow of Jerusalem, and not bearing her misery.

VERS. 19.-- *And behold the voice.* He describes the weeping and the howling of the city when the enemies have entered in. The enemies coming from a far land cry out against the city, or the citizens against the enemies.

VERS. 20.-- *The harvest is past.* The words of the people shut up in Jerusalem by the long siege, because changed are the [0022D] times, the circuit of the year rolled round. But our hope is void.

VERS. 21.-- *For the breach.* The Lord's answer, who in the affliction of Jerusalem is seen to be afflicted.

## CHAPTER IX.

VERS. 1.-- *Who will give. [n: (HIER.)]* The king of Jerusalem, the city being besieged by the enemies, etc., *as far as* but this can be understood as well in the person of the Lord as in the person of the prophet.

VERS. 2.-- *Who will give me,* etc. As if he should say: It is better to dwell in the wilderness than to abide among such great crimes of men. Whence in the Gospel: *How long shall I suffer you? [n: (Matth. XVII.)]* And elsewhere: *He that [0023A] understandeth shall sit still, for the time is very evil [n: (Thren. III; Mich. II)]*.

VERS. 3.-- *Because from evil,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Sinners pass over from evil to evil, etc., *as far as* and the tongue of them that arm themselves unto blasphemy.

VERS. 4.-- *Every one,* etc. [n: (GREG.)] The crafty enemy, when he sees himself driven out from the hearts of the good, seeks out those who are greatly loved by them, and, flattering, speaks through the words of those who are loved more than the rest, so that, while the force of love pierces the heart, the sword of persuasion may easily break through to the inmost parts of the inward uprightness. *Of his neighbour. [n: (HIER.)]* Because *a man's enemies are they of his own household [n: (Mich. VI)]*. The favour of brethren also is rare. *And the father shall deliver up the son, and the son the father. And two against three, and three against two shall be divided [n: (Luc. XII)]*.

[0023B]VERS. 5.-- *They have laboured to do wickedly. [n: (GREG.)]* As if it were openly said: They who could without labour have been friends of the truth, labour that they may sin: and while they refuse to live simply, they exact by labours that they should die.

VERS. 7.-- *And I will try them. [n: (HIER.)]* In the furnace of tribulation. *The furnace trieth the potter's vessels [n: (Eccli. XXVII)]*: that whatsoever of adulterate matter is in us may be burnt away. For the silver of the Lord is *tried by the fire, purged from the earth, refined seven times [n: (Psal. XI)]*.

VERS. 9.-- *Shall it be for these things [n: (ID.)]* He often repeats this little verse, that, their evils being enumerated, he may infer that he does justly what he does.

VERS. 10.-- *Upon the mountains. [n: (ID.)]* Tropologically. Upon [0023C] the mountains weeping is taken up, etc., *as far as* they who can ascend on high, even to the simple, have gone back from God.

VERS. 11.-- *And I will give Jerusalem. [n: (ID.)]* When the ecclesiastical teachers fail, etc., *as far as* and he who saith, *I will dwell in them and walk among them [n: (Lev. XXVI)]*.

VERS. 14.-- *Of their own heart.* Not in our own heart, but in the Lord must we trust. The heart of man is depraved, out of which come forth depraved thoughts.

VERS. 15.-- *Therefore thus saith the Lord.* It can be prophesied of the near time, in which they were taken captive by the Chaldeans: and properly of this, in which they are dispersed among all the nations and scattered over the whole world.

VERS. 16.-- *The sword. [n: (HIER.)]* Either material or spiritual, by which they are divided, that they may not consent unto evil, [0023D] but that in this, that they are evil, they may perish.

VERS. 17.-- *And call ye.* Call the mourning women because of the captivity to come, etc., *as far as* with modulated voice they may melt the people into tears.

VERS. 18.-- *Our eyes.* Therefore: or God himself joins himself to the affection of one suffering with them, so that what the people endures, he himself is said to endure and to feel.

VERS. 19.-- *How are we wasted.* Let the crowds of believers say this in persecution, which were therefore laid waste and confounded, because they forsook the law of the Lord, and abandoned their tabernacles.

VERS. 20.-- *Hear therefore.* Above he said: *call the mourning women,* etc. Now he addresses them as though present, to the reproach of the priests, and of the teachers, and of [0024A] all men: so that, they ceasing from teaching, the women may hear the word of the Lord.

VERS. 21.-- *For death is come up.* Because so great will be the strength and the swiftness of the enemies, that they will not wait for the door, but will climb up through the windows and the roofs.

VERS. 22.-- *Speak: Thus saith,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] The Hebrew word which is written with three letters, etc., *as far as* so that there is none to bury them that fall.

VERS. 24.-- *Who do mercy. [n: (ID.)]* Although certain things seem unjust, yet all things, etc., *as far as He that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord [n: (II Cor. X)]*.

VERS. 25.-- *Behold the days shall come. [n: (ID.)]* Many of the nations, and chiefly those bordering upon Judaea and Palestine, etc., *as far as* for circumcision profiteth not, unless thou keep the law.
