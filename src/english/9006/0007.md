## CHAPTER X.

[0024B]

VERS. 1.-- *Hear ye the word,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] He speaks properly against those, etc., *as far as* and that from the causes of heavenly things they may govern earthly ones.

VERS. 3.-- *Wood. [n: (ID.)]* What we have said concerning idols, etc., *as far as* it is proper also to them who are ignorant of God.

VERS. 4.-- *With silver and gold. [n: (ID.)]* That by the glitter of the material he may seize the simple and deceive them. This error has reached even to us, that we should reckon religion to lie in riches.

VERS. 5.-- *Fear them not therefore.* Most of the nations are wont to worship demons lest they should do harm, and to entreat others that they may do good: whence Virgil, *Aeneid.* III: A black beast for Winter, a white for the favouring Zephyrs.


VERS. 6.-- *There is none like to thee.* There is none like to thee, [0024C] O Lord, among the gods, namely those which are feigned by the heretics.

VERS. 7.-- *For thine is. [n: (HIER.)]* Although the heretics, according to the wisdom of the world, etc., *as far as I will destroy the wisdom of the wise, and the prudence of the prudent I will reject [n: (I Cor. I)]*.

VERS. 8.-- *Wrapped up. [n: (ID.)]* Otherwise it deceiveth not; falsehood is wrapped up, truth, etc., *as far as* while they display upon themselves the colour of heaven, and promise heavenly things.

VERS. 11, 12.-- *The heavens and the earth.* Which the fellow workers of Christ, who are called gods and lords, in part made through ecclesiastical teaching.

*Who maketh. [n: (ORIG., Homil. in Jer.)] The Lord who maketh the earth in strength,* etc. The prophet taking up, as it were, three powers of God, etc., *as far as* but if heavenly things, they carry their treasurer up to the region near [0024D] to themselves.

VERS. 13.-- *The clouds. [n: (HIER.)]* Which know, namely, from whom they should withhold the rains and upon whom bestow them: Moses spoke as a cloud, who said, *Let the earth look for my words as for rain [n: (Exod. IX)]*. And Isaias: *Hear, O heaven, and give ear, O earth, for the Lord hath spoken [n: (Isa. I)]*. [var: Vulg. Is 1:2 *Audite, caeli, et auribus percipe, terra, quoniam Dominus locutus est*, "Hear, ye heavens" — plural, where Migne prints the singular] *From the ends.* He who will be first among you, let him be the last of all. For otherwise no man becometh a cloud.

*Lightnings into rains. [n: (ORIG.)]* It is reported that lightnings are begotten from the collision of clouds or of showers, etc., *as far as* Jeremiah also and Baruch, conversing together, send forth glittering lightnings.

*And he bringeth forth the wind out of his treasures. [n: (ID.)]* The winds which sweep the lands [0025A] are not in the treasures of God, etc., *as far as* thence it arises that one is wise, another faithful, and the like.

VERS. 14.-- *Foolish. [n: (HIER.)]* Foolish in comparison with God, etc., *as far as* for God took upon him all contrary things, that he might dissolve contraries.

*It is false. [n: (ORIG.)]* That which he fashions in his own order. For that which he hath cast is false, just as every man is foolish through knowledge, and foolish also is that which he makes.

*There is no spirit. [n: (HIER.)]* It is to be noted that in this chapter wind and spirit are called among the Hebrews by one name, *ruah.*

VERS. 15.-- *They are vain.* For either they are rustic, like wood, or ringing with fair speech, like silver; or, feigned out of their own conceit, they lie, like [0025B] gold. *In the time of visitation.* For a time heresy avails, that they *who are approved may be made manifest [n: (I Cor. XI)]*. But when the visitation of God shall have come, and his eye shall have looked upon all things, all shall fall silent.

VERS. 17.-- *Gather up. [n: (HIER.)]* Jerusalem is commanded to gather into the most fortified city whatsoever substance she has without, and long beforehand to prepare food against the siege. For God has threatened not, as before, things to come long after, but things to come at once.

VERS. 19.-- *Woe is me,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Jerusalem complains that she is grievously afflicted, and with an incurable wound.

VERS. 20.-- *The tabernacle. [n: (ID.)]* Jerusalem bewails her own overthrow, made so easy, etc., *as far as* a great part of them was slain and blotted [0025C] out.

VERS. 21.-- *The flock. [n: (ID.)]* The Seventy: *cattle.* Which cannot stand according to the history: for in so long a siege the cattle would not have lasted.

VERS. 22.-- *The voice of a report. [n: (ID.)]* All these things which the past and the present discourse describes, etc., *as far as* who *have done foolishly,* and have not sought the Lord.

*Of dragons.* Or *of ostriches.* Symmachus, *of sirens,* so that in the place of men there dwell, namely, dragons, and all venomous things: or ostriches, which, familiar with solitude, are born and nourished in the deserts. Or Sirens, that is to say, certain monsters and phantoms of demons.

VERS. 23.-- *I know,* etc. Let them blush who say that each man is governed by his own judgment. For not of a man [0025D] is his way, etc.; whence David: *By the Lord shall the steps of a man be directed [n: (Psal. XXXVI)]*.

VERS. 24.-- *In judgment, and not in fury. (HIER)*. As if, the things which we suffer are just, etc., *as far as* but the nations, which have not known thee, deserve not judgment, but indignation.
