## CHAPTER XI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The Word. [n: (ORIG., Hom. in Ezech.)]* Which, namely, was in the beginning with God, etc., *as far as* and he saith to his disciples: *I am with you even to the consummation of the world [n: (Matth. XXVIII)]*.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The word that was made. [n: (HIER.)]* It is not set down in the title at what time, etc., *as far as* the discourse is directed to the men of Juda and Jerusalem.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0026A]VERS. 2.-- *Hear ye,* etc., *The men of Juda,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Allegorically. ⟦s3⟧ The Christians, namely, for Christ arose out of Juda. ⟦s4⟧ Of whom it is truly said: *Juda, thy brethren shall praise thee: thy hands shall be upon the back of thy enemies [n: (Gen. XLIX.)]*, etc. [var: Vulg. Gn 49:8 *manus tua in cervicibus inimicorum tuorum*, "thy hand on the necks of thy enemies"] *Jerusalem.* ⟦s5+6⟧ Of the Church, which is the city of the great king, and the vision of peace. ⟦s7⟧ For peace is multiplied and beheld in her.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *Cursed. [n: (ORIG.)]* For the Jews are cursed, who heard not the testament of God, when he brought them out of the land of Egypt. ⟦s2⟧ But we who believe in Christ obey his testament which is handed down through Moses: and this is said to us, that we be not cursed.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *I brought them out. [n: (HIER., ORIG.)]* Us also God brought out of Egypt and out of the iron furnace, etc., [0026B] *as far as* in the law, the obedience of the commandments, here, the likeness of God.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And you shall be.* ⟦s2⟧ He is not the God of all, but only of those to whom he bestows himself; whence: *I am the Lord thy God, the holy one of Israel [n: (Ezech. XIV)]*. ⟦s3⟧ And elsewhere also he saith, *I will be their God [n: (Exod. III.)]* And, *I am the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob.* ⟦s4⟧ Which the Saviour, expounding it, saith: *But he is not the God of the dead, but of the living [n: (Luc. XXIX)]*. ⟦s5⟧ He is dead, namely, the sinner.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *With milk and honey. [n: (HIER.)]* By honey and milk he signifies the abundance of all things, etc., *as far as* or, so be it, Lord, that is, let what thou hast given always remain.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Because protesting,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As far as *that they should do these [0026C] things,* they are not found in the Septuagint. ⟦s3⟧ What follows: *And they did it not, from them:* is placed at the end of the preceding chapter. ⟦s4⟧ *Hear ye the words of this covenant,* etc.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *A conspiracy is found. [n: (ORIG.)] A binding together is found among the men of Juda,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ If we shall have sinned, etc., *as far as* and thou shalt be made a son of God in Christ Jesus.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *They shall cry.* ⟦s2⟧ In their necessities, and I will not hear, because they would not hear me. ⟦s3⟧ Which Saul also suffered, etc., *as far as* what is now said pertains to Juda and Jerusalem, upon whom the captivity presses.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *According to the number. [n: (ID.)]* In the book of Kings and of Paralipomenon we find Juda and Jeremiah much [sic: *multc*] worse things, etc., *as far as* they had so many altars to their own confusion, on which they might sacrifice to idols.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Do thou therefore. [n: (ID.)]* The prophet is commanded, [0026D] not to pray for those against whom, etc., *as far as* from these things we learn that in vain is entreaty made for anyone who does not deserve to receive.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *What is it,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Let us say this to the princes of the churches and to the rich, who, while they seize what is another's and take not away the malices of their own heart, think that they merit the clemency of God. ⟦s2⟧ *Shall the holy flesh.* ⟦s3⟧ But now the names of those who make offering are publicly recited, and the redemption of sins is turned into praise: nor is the memory of the evangelical widow kept, who cast into the treasury more than all.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *At the voice of speech. [n: (HIER.)]* As if he should say: Exalted through pride, not humbly hast thou understood thy creator and thy ruler, but, having spoken loftily against [0027A] the Lord, kindled by his fire thou shalt be reduced to nothing.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *And the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ As if: Thy lord called thee a plentiful olive tree, fair, fruitful, and planted thee: but because of the voice of the great speech his flame came down upon thee.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *But thou. [n: (HIER.)]* The Jews and our own judaizers refer this to the person of Jeremiah, etc., *as far as* unless perhaps they should say that the people thought it, and did it not? [n: (ORIG.)] But thou, O Lord, etc. ⟦s2⟧ *Or make it known to me, O Lord, and I shall know [n: (Psal. CXLII)]*. ⟦s3⟧ According to the Psalmist, *The Lord teacheth man knowledge [n: (Ibid. XCIII)]*. ⟦s4⟧ Whence: *Be not ye called masters upon the earth, for one is your master who is in heaven [n: (Matth. XXIII)]*, [var: Vulg. Mt 23:8 *Vos autem nolite vocari Rabbi : unus est enim magister vester*, and Mt 23:9 *Et patrem nolite vocare vobis super terram : unus est enim pater vester qui in caelis est*; Migne's one sentence conflates the two verses] who, namely, instructs men, etc., *as far as* [0027B] but when the wood of Jesus Christ comes, and the word of the Saviour descends into it, it is made sweet.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *For to thee. [n: (HIER.)]* Because I am crucified not by my own merit, but by their crime. ⟦s2⟧ *The prince of this world cometh, and in me he hath not anything [n: (Joan. XIV)]*.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Therefore thus saith the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ This seems contrary to the sentence above, in which, etc., *as far as* and what in the present is fulfilled in Jeremiah, this is prophesied for the future concerning the Lord.

## CHAPTER XII.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *Thou indeed art just,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] The disputation is against all who act wickedly, as in that of the Psalmist: *How good is God to Israel, to them that are upright [n: (Psal. LXXII)]*, etc. ⟦s2⟧ But chiefly against the heretics.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0027C]VERS. 3.-- *And thou, O Lord. [n: (ID.)]* As if he should say: It is not a scandal if the ungodly or the heretics flourish for a time, etc., *as far as* when they shall have been, namely, slain with the ecclesiastical sword.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *If with feet.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: If the captivity of the neighbouring nations has wearied thee, that of the Moabites, namely, of the Philistines, of the Ammonites, of the Idumaeans, who because of the difficulty of their places are fitted not so much for battle as for brigandage. ⟦s3⟧ *How.* ⟦s4⟧ That is, what wilt thou do, when thou shalt have come to captivity in Chaldea? ⟦s5⟧ For the Chaldeans and the Persians delight in horses.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *For even thy brethren,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] This can be understood of Christ, against whom his brethren cried out: *Crucify him: We have no king but Caesar [n: (Joan. XIX)]*.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0027D]VERS. 7.-- *I have forsaken my house.* ⟦s2⟧ Whence, *Your house shall be left to you desolate [n: (Luc. XIII)]*: And: *Arise, let us go hence [n: (Joan. XIV)]*.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *My inheritance,* etc. [n: (ORIG.)] It is not to be wondered at if the inheritance was then compared to a savage beast, since even to this day there are lions in the wood anathematizing Christ, and blaspheming, and lying in wait for the faithful.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Is my inheritance a speckled bird?* ⟦s2⟧ The Seventy: *Is my inheritance to me a hyena's den? [n: (HIER.)]* The hyena, a nocturnal beast, devours the carcases of the dead, and digs up bodies out of the graves and feeds upon all filth, to whose uncleanness Israel is compared.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0028A]VERS. 10.-- *Many pastors,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] Let them hear these things who wish to be princes, etc., *as far as* who have scattered the people of God.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *With desolation. [n: (ORIG.)]* Because the Jews have filled up the measure of their fathers and have added to the slaying of the prophets that of the Saviour also, etc, *as far as We sow not in the flesh, but in the spirit, that we may not reap corruption of the flesh, but of the spirit life everlasting [n: (I Cor. XVII)]*.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *There is no peace,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] The flesh cannot receive the peace of God into itself. ⟦s2⟧ *For the wisdom of the flesh is an enemy to God, and they who are in the flesh cannot please God [n: (Rom. VIII)]*.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *They have sown.* ⟦s2⟧ This also is said to churchmen, etc., *as far as* and: *To whom more is committed, [0028B] more is required of him [n: (Sap. VI)]*.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *From the fruits.* ⟦s2⟧ The Seventy. ⟦s3⟧ *From your boasting. [n: (HIER.)]* In precious garments, namely, in adorned houses, in many possessions, in worldly wisdom: *But he that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord [n: (I Cor. I)]*. ⟦s4⟧ And, *in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ [n: (Gal. VI)]*. ⟦s5⟧ And, *in his own infirmities, that the power of Christ may dwell in him [n: (II Cor. XI)]*.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Neighbours. [n: (HIER.)]* To the letter, the Idumaeans, the Philistines, Moab, and Ammon. ⟦s2⟧ Allegorically. ⟦s3⟧ Heretics who are called Christians, and who become borderers upon the holy land rather than dwellers in it.
