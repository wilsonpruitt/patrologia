## CHAPTER XVII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The sin of Juda is written,* etc. [n: (HIER.)] As if to say: The sins of the Gentiles have been blotted out, of which it is said to Moses, *Let me alone,* etc., *as far as Rest ye from the man whose spirit is in his nostrils [n: (Isa. II)]*.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ORIG.)] Perhaps the sin of Juda is our sin, [0034A] we who believe in Christ of the tribe of Juda, etc., *as far as* if thou shouldst wish to understand it of Juda the traitor: *The sin of Juda is written,* what follows, "of them," is repugnant to it, etc.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *The high places. [n: (HIER.)]* Which in Hebrew are *Ramoth.* ⟦s2⟧ And heretics are signified, who set their mouth against heaven, etc., *as far as* and the servitude of the demons, who are enemies and avengers.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Cursed be the man. [n: (ID.)]* Cursed therefore be Paul of Samosata and Photinus, who although a holy, etc., *as far as* that both might become one, and that we should adore not the creature, but the Creator.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Blessed be the man,* etc. [n: (ID.)] As if to say: Cursed are the Jews, and the heretics, who have not hope in the Lord, etc., *as far as* who once died [0034B] for us, and dies no more: and he says, *I am the life [n: (Joan. XIV)]*.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Which sendeth forth its roots toward moisture. [n: (ID.)]* As if he should say: from the Jewish dryness he passes over to the grace of baptism.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *In the midst of his days. [n: (ID.)]* His days are of this world. ⟦s2⟧ Christ has plucked us out of this wicked world, and we forsake [them] through the day. ⟦s3⟧ *His last shall be foolish,* [var: Vulg. Jer. 17:11 *et in novissimo suo erit insipiens*, "and in his latter end he shall be a fool"] when all things shall have been made subject to Christ. ⟦s4⟧ *The last enemy that shall be destroyed is death.*

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Heal me, O Lord. [n: (ID.)]* Many physicians have tended him, etc., *as far as* he desires to be tended and healed by him whose is true praise and true medicine.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Behold they say to me: Where is the word,* etc.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0034C]VERS. 16.-- *I am not troubled, following thee the shepherd. [n: (ID.)]* The more thou followest Christ, etc., *as far as* the day of judgment is evil to those who suffer torments.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Go and stand,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: Because they scorn to hear thy words, etc., *as far as* as though they had not heard, but let them hear whether they will or no.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Keep your souls.* ⟦s2⟧ Lest we tear in pieces the precept of the sabbath renewed through Jeremias, we set the whole down together, that we may know all things together.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *That you bring not in burdens through the gates of this city on the sabbath day. [n: (ID.)]* If our judaizers, etc., *as far as* but he also called God his own Father.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *But if you will not hearken to me, to sanctify [0034D] the sabbath day, and not to carry a burden, and not to bring it in through the gates of Jerusalem,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if to say: These are the rewards of those who sanctify the sabbath, and are weighed down by no burden. ⟦s3⟧ *But if,* etc.

## CHAPTER XVIII.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The word that came to Jeremias from the Lord, saying. [n: (HIER.)]* Through all the senses unto judgment, etc., *as far as* and there to hear the precepts of the Lord.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And it was marred,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Marred by divine providence, that the craftsman, unknowing, might by his own mistake figure forth the parable.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Suddenly I will speak. [n: (ID.)]* Free choice [0035A] it signifies, etc., *as far as* that in all things the grace of the giver may excel.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Behold I frame evil against you. [n: (HIER.)]* He fulfils the parable which he taught both by sight and by speech.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *After the thoughts. [n: (ID.)]* Where it is apart from the grace of God, etc., *as far as* all the nations round about her.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Shall the snow of Libanus fail from the rock:* as if he should say: As from the heights of Libanus, etc., *as far as* my people hath forgotten me.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *That the land might become a desolation. [n: (ID.)]* This seems to have been more fully fulfilled after the coming of the Lord, when no one of the Jews is permitted by *ege* to enter the land and the holy city. ⟦s2⟧ But when they have come to the mourning, they wonder at and bewail the oracles of the prophets fulfilled.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0035B]VERS. 17.-- *As a burning wind. [n: (ID.)]* Unto this day this sentence remains, etc., *as far as then all Israel shall be saved.*

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *He came. [n: (ID.)]* These things then of the Jews against Jeremias or Christ, etc., *as far as* pursuing the prophecy with all confidence of words and of meanings.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Give heed to me, O Lord, and hear the voice of my adversaries. [n: (ID.)]* In a type of the Saviour, etc., *as far as his blood be upon us and upon our children [n: (Matth. XXVII)]*.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 23.-- *Forgive not their iniquity,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ It is not contrary to the sentence above, etc., *as far as* lest sin unavenged should become to them an example.

## CHAPTER XIX.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0035C]VERS. 1.-- *Thus saith the Lord: Go and take a potter's bottle. [n: (HIER.)]* Holy Scripture wills to teach the people not only by the ears, but also by the eyes. ⟦s2⟧ For that which is taken in by sight is retained in the mind more than that which is taken in by hearing.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Lagunculam figuli* for laguncula figuli, etc., *as far as* and for Hierihoh, *Chiricho.*

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *Because that. [n: (ID.)]* Every heretic forsakes God, etc., *as far as* and they burn to idols their own sons whom they have begotten in heresy.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *Which I commanded not.* ⟦s2⟧ I have not commanded or thought, etc., *as far as* these things are to be understood of God, as others are.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Topheth, and the valley of the son of Ennom,* etc. [0035D] [n: (ID.)] What the valley of the son of Ennom is, etc., *as far as* or, historically, when the Babylonian was laying it waste.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *And I will give the carcasses.* ⟦s2⟧ Although these things befell the people in the Babylonian captivity, etc., *as far as* they made their own bellies the sepulchres of their children.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *So will I break this people.* ⟦s2⟧ Under Aelius Hadrian the city lost both its name and its standing, for the breaking of the pride of its inhabitants. ⟦s3⟧ But the names of the holy cross and of the resurrection signify not the city, but the place.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (ID.)] The Jews a golden and jewelled Jerusalem, etc., *as far as* and let all things be kept for the true judge.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And they shall be buried in Topheth,* etc., *as far as* which hangs over this place, let it become as Topheth.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0036A]VERS. 13.-- *And the houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings shall be.* ⟦s2⟧ Every house of the kings of Juda as the place of Topheth. ⟦s3⟧ This is not had in the Hebrew, but is inserted by the LXX.

## CHAPTER XX.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And Phassur heard. [n: (HIER.)]* He was the high priest of the temple, etc., *as far as* but he considers him that commands.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *And when it had dawned.* ⟦s2⟧ The patience and prudence of the prophet are to be noted. ⟦s3⟧ Sent to prison he is silent, and by silence overcomes the wrong, nor yet does he dissemble what he knows is to come, that at least God's high priest may cease from sinning, and may entreat God's clemency.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1+s2⟧ *Not Phassur hath the Lord called thy name.* According to the interpretation above, etc., *as far as* thou mayest look about thee hither and thither and dread the adversaries as they come.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0036B]VERS. 7.-- *Thou hast deceived me, O Lord. [n: (ID.)]* The prophet says that he was deceived by the Lord, etc., *as far as* he may endure persecutions and straits.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *I am become a derision. [n: (ID.)]* The prophet had thought that what the Lord threatened would come at once; and the people reckoned that what had not come at once was not going to come.

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And thou, O Lord of hosts,* etc. [n: (ID.)] God alone is, etc., *as far as* therefore dead in his sins.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Let me see.* ⟦s2⟧ Although the just man knows that he has God for his defender, yet in the patience of human frailty he desires to see even now what he knows is to come.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *For to thee.* ⟦s2⟧ Thou who sayest: *Vengeance to me, and I will repay* [0036C] [n: (Heb. X)], etc. ⟦s3⟧ Happy the conscience whose cause is revealed to the Lord: whence the Apostle: *All that is made manifest is light [n: (Ephes. V)]*.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Because he hath delivered. [n: (ID.)]* The poor in spirit, having obtained vengeance from the Lord, praises him, and glories that he has been snatched from the hand of the worst of men. ⟦s2⟧ But this comes about not by merit, but by the grace of him who set the poor man free, who loves not the riches of a pride that is falling, but the humility of the poor man set free.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Cursed be the day wherein I was born.* ⟦s2⟧ In the fifth month, that is in August, Jeremias was born, etc., *as far as* and should they suppose it a prophecy of the coming overthrow of the temple?

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Cursed be the man,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ They who think that souls were in the heavenly places, etc., *as far as* and, *Redeeming [0036D] the time, because the days are [n: (Ephes. V)],* etc.
