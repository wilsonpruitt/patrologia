## CHAPTER XXV

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The word. [n: (HIER.)]* These former things belong to the vision that is past, etc., *as far as* which a lyric poem does not observe.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the fourth year.* ⟦s2⟧ In the fourth year of king Joachim, etc., *as far as* but Joachim was slain in the eleventh year of his reign.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *From the thirteenth year.* ⟦s2⟧ From the thirteenth [0040A] year of the reign of Josias, etc., *as far as* unless he be called back by the Lord's unceasing admonition.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And the Lord sent to you all his servants.* ⟦s2⟧ Not through one prophet, but through all, did he admonish his people, and, as though set in watches and on guard, he rose at daybreak to warn them, and they did not hear; so that the more frequent the admonition, the greater the contempt.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Neither have you inclined. [n: (ID.)]* So great was the hardness, etc., *as far as* which does not inflict punishment for the crime.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *After gods,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if to say: These things, with the foregoing, I was warning, that you should not serve strange gods, nor provoke me to anger and force me, unwilling, to your punishment.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *And I will take,* etc., *Nabuchodonosor [0040B] my servant.* ⟦s2⟧ Not as the prophets and the other saints who serve God; but because in the overthrow of Jerusalem he serves the will of the Lord.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And I will bring them,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ These are the rewards of despisers, and of those unwilling to hear God. ⟦s3⟧ Whatever evil therefore is brought upon us, our sins deserve it; and as the nations were brought against Jerusalem, so today they serve against a negligent Church.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *And I will destroy,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ This is proved in the assembly of the malignant, in that their masters do not teach the word of God, but hiss after the manner of a snake: among whom the voice of joy and of gladness has perished, so that they do not hear that word of the Apostle: *Rejoice in the Lord always, again I say, rejoice [n: (Phil. IV)]*.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *And they shall serve.* ⟦s2⟧ As Jerusalem, etc., [0040C] *as far as* he describes it in prophetic speech.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *When they are fulfilled.* ⟦s2⟧ Note that Jeremias prophesies not only against Babylon, but against all the nations which were in the army of Babylon and fought against the people of God.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *Take the cup of the wine of fury,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ *(ID)*. The giving of the cup to drink is a token of the Lord's fury, etc., *as far as* the cup is given to drink, but for a punishment.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *To Jerusalem and to the cities of Juda,* etc., As if to say: I thought that I was giving drink to the nations only, and therefore I had offered myself gladly to this ministry, but among the other nations, nay rather before the others, I gave drink to Jerusalem and to the cities of Juda, etc.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *To Pharao king of Egypt,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ After Jerusalem drinks Pharao king of Egypt, and his servants, and the princes, [0040D] and the whole people, and he who is not indeed an Egyptian, but dwells in his regions.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *Philisthiim and Ascalon,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ By a general name he signifies the land of the Palestinians, etc., *as far as* but Isaias writes that the Palestinians were taken and laid waste by the Babylonians.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *Of Tyre and to all the kings,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Tyre and Sidon are on the shore of Phoenicia, etc., *as far as* the Hebrew tongue is for the greater part akin to it.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 25.-- *To the kings of Elam and to all,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Because there follows, *Elam and the kings of the Medes* they judge Zambri to be a region of Persia: unless perhaps, because Arabia went before, they be understood to be the kings of the wilderness.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And king Sesach,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if to say: All round about, [0041A] etc., *as far as* to be destroyed before Antichrist according to Daniel.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Judgment for the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ Because the merits of the nations are diverse, *he that believeth not is already judged,* namely in this, that he does not believe; but they who do not believe shall be visited with divers punishments.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 32.-- *Behold there shall go forth,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Let them blush who, doing violence to Holy Scripture, take the Lord's threatenings in a good sense.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 34.-- *Howl, ye shepherds. [n: (HIER.)]* The howling of the chief men of the flock, etc., *as far as* the good things of peace shall fall silent and shall be taken away in fury. ⟦s2⟧ Of the Lord.

## CHAPTER XXVI.

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *In the beginning of the reign,* etc. [n: (ID.)] This [0041B] prophecy is earlier than the one above, although under the same king. ⟦s2⟧ For that one was made in the fourth year, this in the beginning of the same king's reign. ⟦s3⟧ The order of history, therefore, is not to be woven together in prophecy, since former things are told later, and later things first.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *In the court of the house.* ⟦s2⟧ That thou mayest speak even to the outlying cities. ⟦s3⟧ And beautifully does he stand in the court, and in the porch of the temple, that those who come together to pray may be compelled to hear his words.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 3.-- *If perhaps they hear,* etc. [n: (ID.)] As if to say: Perhaps they will hear, etc., *as far as* Perhaps they will reverence my Son.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *If you will not hear me.* ⟦s2⟧ It is set in our own will, etc., *as far as* nor once and from of old, but always and with anxious care?

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0041C]VERS. 6.-- *I will make this house like Silo.* ⟦s2⟧ As, when the temple had been built upon the threshing-floor, etc., *as far as* made a curse for us.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *Of the gate of the house.* ⟦s2⟧ It is called new, because those who sat in it, and presided at the judgment, resisted the calumny of the priests and of the false prophets.

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *And they spoke.* ⟦s2⟧ The princes of the city sitting in the gate of the temple, etc., *as far as* but thinking of the good things to come.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And he said.* ⟦s2⟧ Jeremias, they accusing, and the charge, etc., *as far as: For in truth the Lord sent me to you.*

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Now therefore make your ways good.* ⟦s2⟧ As if to say: If you are angry, etc., *as far as* that nothing be withdrawn from the truth.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0041D]VERS. 16.-- *And they said.* ⟦s2⟧ The princes of the city, and the people, understood, etc., *as far as* whom by a change of sentence we are able to set free.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *There was also.* ⟦s2⟧ It is asked, since Urias the same things, etc., *as far as* Christ also slipped away out of the hands of them that persecuted him.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 22.-- *And the king sent.* ⟦s2⟧ It is asked how Joachim the king, etc., *as far as* and that this prophecy was made when he had begun to reign.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *Therefore the hand of Ahicam.* ⟦s2⟧ Although Jeremias was set free by the Lord's help, yet it is reckoned unto his reward through whom he set his prophet free.

## CHAPTER XXVII.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *In the beginning of the reign,* etc. [n: (HIERON.)] [0042A] Many think that this is the beginning of what follows; but it is to be joined to what goes before, so that whatever is reported as said or done, we may believe to have been in the beginning of the reign of Joachim.

⟦p36⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 2.-- *Thus saith the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ The former vision was in the beginning of the reign of Joachim: but this one under Sedecias, who reigned last in Jerusalem, under whom the city was taken and overthrown.

⟦p37⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *The earth and men.* ⟦s2⟧ The order is the contrary, in Genesis the living creatures are made first, man afterwards; but here he names man first, afterwards the things which are made subject to him.

⟦p38⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In strength.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ, the power of God and the wisdom of God.

⟦p39⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *And I have given,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ It signifies that all things have been bestowed upon the human race through grace.

⟦p40⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0042B]VERS. 6.-- *Into the hand of Nabuchodonosor. [n: (ID.)]* Of how great unhappiness is Israel, in comparison with whom Nabuchodonosor is called the servant of God?

⟦p41⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Moreover also.* ⟦s2⟧ Every kind of living creature; for with man all things are handed over which are made subject to him; or the wild nations, that they who had not known how to serve may serve him.

⟦p42⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Until there come,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Lest the kingdom of Nabuchodonosor be thought to be everlasting, he signifies that it is to be taken by the Medes and by the Persians.

⟦p43⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *And to Sedecias.* ⟦s2⟧ After all the nations, he passes over to Sedecias and threatens the same things, for neither do the Jews deserve a privilege, who have sinned either like the nations, or more grievously, or more greatly; and they who disdain to hear are consumed by pestilence and by famine.

⟦p44⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0042C] *Hear ye not.* ⟦s2⟧ In this place the allegorical interpreter, etc., *as far as* and that they would not receive back their former seat.

⟦p45⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *You shall not serve.* ⟦s2⟧ It is better to serve willingly, and to have God for a friend, and to till a Gentile land, than to serve as a captive by necessity.

⟦p46⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *The words of the prophet.* ⟦s2⟧ It is to be noted that in the Holy Scriptures false prophets are called prophets.

⟦p47⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *And to the priests.* ⟦s2⟧ After the nations and the kings, he speaks to the priests, and to the people, who had already announced destruction to the prophets, saying: *And I will cast you out,* etc. ⟦s3⟧ And he speaks the same things as above.

⟦p48⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *That you may live.* ⟦s2⟧ Note the clemency of God, etc., *as far as* In which it is shown that God can be held back by the prayers of the saints.

⟦p49⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0042D]VERS. 18.-- *Let them meet the Lord. [n: (ID.)]* Let them meet him, and let them show fulfilled in deed the things which they foretell, and then the prophecy will be proved by truth.

⟦p50⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *To the pillars. [n: (ID.)]* He enumerates the vessels which were carried over into Babylon, when Sedecias had been taken, the city burned, and the temple overthrown.
