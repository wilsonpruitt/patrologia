## CHAPTER XXVIII.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And it came to pass. [n: (HIER.)]* Ezechiel not yet prophesying in Babylon to those who had been carried over with Jechonias.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *In the house of the Lord.* ⟦s2⟧ In which he speaks confidently, because he promises prosperous things to the people; and lies are gladly heard, especially those which promise what is wished for.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0043A]VERS. 5.-- *All the vessels,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Jeremias had said that the remaining vessels also were to be carried away, this man affirms that those carried away are to be brought back.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Amen, so,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ He wishes that to be done which the other has falsely said, etc., *as far as* under the example of others he asserts the truth.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Nevertheless.* ⟦s2⟧ He could have said, thou liest, etc., *as far as* that they who hear may await the outcome.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *And he took. [n: (HIER.)]* The prudence of Jeremias is to be considered, etc., *as far as* the knowledge of which belongs to God alone.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 13.-- *Ananias.* ⟦s2⟧ He is called a prophet in appearance only. ⟦s3⟧ For many things in the Holy Scriptures are said according to the opinion of the time in which the deeds are reported, not according to the truth of the matter.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *And thou shalt make.* ⟦s2⟧ When Jeremias had gone away [0043B] and had swallowed up the wrongs in silence, the word of the Lord came to him, not that he should speak in his own words to Ananias, who gloried in his lie, and should say: *Thus hath the Lord said;* which nevertheless that Ananias had said, but a lie always imitates the truth.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Chains.* ⟦s2⟧ He shows that, keeping the lesser punishment, etc., *as far as* they have been banished into the bodies of brute animals.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 15.-- *And he said. [n: (ID.)]* It is to be noted that Jeremias, having suffered the wrong, is silent, the word of the Lord not yet having come to him, but afterwards, sent by the Lord, he boldly rebukes, announcing to the liar his near death.

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *This year. [n: (ID.)]* If, whenever we die, we are set free from the prisons of our bodies, according to that [0043C] which the heretics wrongly interpret: *Bring my soul out of prison;* [var: Vulg. Ps. 141:8 *Educ de custodia animam meam*, "out of ward"] how is death inflicted as a punishment?

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *In the seventh month.* ⟦s2⟧ Perhaps in the seventh month, which is wont to signify rest, they for that reason falsely say that he died, that he might be set free from the evils of the body, according to that saying: *Death is rest to a man;* but we know that the bodies of them that believe are temples of God, if the Holy Spirit dwell in them.

## CHAPTER XXIX.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *And these. [n: (HIER.)]* He purposes to persuade them not to yield to the false prophets, who were promising them a return to Jerusalem after a short time. ⟦s2⟧ But let them know that they will tarry a long time in Babylon.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0043D] *The words of the book. [n: (ID.)]* This little book of Jeremias, etc., *as far as* he might urge upon the people the things which had been commanded him by the Lord.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *To the residue of the ancients.* ⟦s2⟧ First to the elders, then to the priests, thirdly to the prophets, fourthly to all the people, the word of God is directed, so that according to the order of age the prophet's letter may be sent.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 6.-- *Take ye wives,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ To Jeremias (because the captivity of Jerusalem is near) it is commanded that he take not a wife, nor beget sons. ⟦s3⟧ But if, on account of the straitness of the time, the use of wives is taken away from those who have them, how much more is it commanded to those who have them not, that they take none.

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 8.-- *Let them not deceive you. [n: (ID.)]* The false prophets, [0044A] etc., *as far as* which was the same [year] of the reign of Sedecias.

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *When they shall begin.* ⟦s2⟧ Do not believe the false prophets, who promise you a near return: for the seventy years shall first be fulfilled for you before you return.

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Seventy years.* ⟦s2⟧ So long are we engaged in the confusion of the world, etc., *as far as* and then is the end of our labour and of our patience.

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *And to return.* ⟦s2⟧ That you may know more surely that you ought not to hope for this return as though it were near, but to build houses, to plant orchards, to beget sons, and to await the promised time.

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 17.-- *Like figs.* ⟦s2⟧ Theodotion *suarinas,* that is, the second ones, Symmachus the last ones, which in Hebrew are suarim ( ). [ed: Migne prints a Hebrew word within these parentheses; the digitized source carries nothing there, and recovery requires the plate.]

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0044B]VERS. 19.-- *In the night. [n: (ID.)]* I have not ceased to warn, that they might imitate you who in the transmigration enjoy an untroubled ease, until the promises of God be fulfilled.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *To Achab.* ⟦s2⟧ The Hebrews say that these are the elders, etc., *as far as* that, penance being done, after the true sabbath they might possess their former place.

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 24.-- *To Semeias.* ⟦s2⟧ Semeias of the place Nehelam, etc., *as far as* But the discernment of spirits is a spiritual grace.

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *Why hast thou not rebuked. [n: (ID.)]* By a lie he forestalls the truth, what he himself deserved he lays upon others, because the children of darkness are more prudent than the children of light, and what they fear for themselves they bring upon others.

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 31.-- *Thus saith.* ⟦s2⟧ Semeias truly a Nehelamite, etc., *as far as* against whom it is subjoined, *Behold I will visit.*

## CHAPTER XXX.

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0044C]

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *This word. [n: (HIER.)]* Jeremias was promising a return, etc., *as far as* But when Israel and Juda shall return into their own land.

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *Alas, for it is great. [n: (ID.)]* First the sad things are announced, etc., *as far as* nor will they hold arms in their hands, but their loins, like women in travail.

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *And David.* ⟦s2⟧ Christ, that is to say; for as the first Adam and the second are written of according to the truth of the body, so also David. ⟦s3⟧ For Christ is of David according to the flesh.

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 10.-- *My servant. [n: (ID.)]* He calls him familiarly, as Abraham, Isaac, Jacob are called servants of God. ⟦s2⟧ Moses too and the other prophets, and Paul at the beginning [0044D] of his Epistles, glory in this title.

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *A wound. [n: (ID.)]* In one way a friend strikes, in another an enemy; in one way a father, in another a foe: for the one strikes that he may correct, the other that he may slay.

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 16.-- *Therefore. [n: (ID.)]* We know that these things were done under Zorobabel, etc., *as far as* but they are fulfilled more fully and more perfectly in Christ.

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *Behold I will bring back. [n: (ID.)]* Since the type went before, etc., *as far as* they are fulfilled more fully and more perfectly in Christ and in the apostles.

⟦p35⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 19.-- *Of them that play, and I will multiply them,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Not according to that saying, *The people sat down to eat and to drink, and they rose up to play [n: (Exod. XXXII)]*; but as David played before the ark of the Lord [n: (I Par. XIII)]
