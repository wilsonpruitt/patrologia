## CHAPTER XXXI.

[0045A]

VERS. 1.-- *At that time. [n: (HIER.)]* When the work is finished, the craftsman is known; when health is regained, the physician's diligence. So, Jerusalem being overthrown and the former people cast off, it is understood that the rejection of the Jews is the salvation of the Gentiles.

VERS. 3.-- *The Lord hath appeared afar off.* Because Israel had offended the Lord, etc., *as far as* he was saved not by merit.

VERS. 5.-- *They shall plant,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Who after the captivity of Israel were possessed by strangers, to whom it is said: *Plant ye plantings and gather ye the vintage [n: (Deut. XXVIII)]*.

VERS. 7.-- *The remnants of Israel.* Which are saved according to the election.

VERS. 8.-- *Behold, I will bring them,* etc. Through the apostles and apostolic men who are the watchmen, crying on [0045B] the mountain of Samaria, to whom it is commanded: *Sound ye forth, sing ye [n: (III Reg. XVIII)]*, etc.

*The lame man. [n: (ID.)]* To such it is said through Elias: *How long halt ye upon both feet?* [var: Vulg. III Reg. 18:21 *in duas partes*, "on two sides"] But afterwards they ran in Christ; and, *he that walked in darkness hath seen a great light.*

*They shall come with weeping, and in,* etc. [n: (ID.)] The Jews think this to have been fulfilled, etc., *as far as* they can prove that these things were fulfilled.

VERS. 9.-- *And Ephraim.* Ephraim, who is a type of the Gentile people, etc., *as far as* he first obtained the kingdom in Samaria, etc.

VERS. 11.-- *Out of the hand of one mightier,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Of the adverse power, which is by nature the stronger: but we are the stronger by faith, if indeed we are set free by him who binds the strong man, [0045C] and plunders his house.

VERS. 14.-- *The soul of the priests.* Who have the knowledge of God, of whose mouth they ask the law, who believe in him who is a priest for ever.

*With fatness, and my people shall be filled with my good things, saith the Lord,* etc. Whence the Lord was seized in the valley of Gethsemani, that is, of fatness.

VERS. 15.-- *A voice on high. [n: (ID.)]* Certain of the Jews expound this place thus, etc., *as far as* that the Hebrews expressed in their own words what they read in Hebrew.

*Rachel weeping.* Rachel the mother of Joseph, etc., *as far as* she is said to weep for the children slain beside her.

VERS. 16.-- *And they shall return. [n: (ID.)]* We better understand this of the little ones, etc., *as far as* and the infants shall rise again unto the perfect man.

[0045D]VERS. 18.-- *I was instructed.* As if he should say: Correction profits unto salvation, and that which for the present seems to be of sorrow, afterwards yields the fruit of peace.

VERS. 19.-- *Because I have borne. [n: (ID.)]* He says that he sinned through the ignorance of his age, that he may the more easily obtain pardon, whence: *The sins of my youth and my ignorances do not thou remember.* And in what follows, God called him a little child and one abounding in delights, because, that is, of the greatness of his riches and the fruitfulness of the land [n: (Psal. XXIV)].

VERS. 20.-- *If Ephraim be an honourable son,* etc. Ephraim doing penance and saying: *Thou hast instructed me, O Lord, and I was instructed,* [var: Vulg. Jer. 31:18 *Castigasti me, et eruditus sum*, "Thou hast chastised me, and I was instructed"] etc., the Lord answered, and sustains him, now fully converted, with such an oracle, *If Ephraim be an honourable son,* etc.

[0046A] *Therefore are my bowels troubled.* As if he should say: With eager mind he received my words, and kept them, but nevertheless I will have mercy on him; because the justice of men also has need of the mercy of God.

VERS. 21.-- *Set up for thyself,* etc. He provokes to satisfaction, that he may consider in how many things he has offended, and, doing penance, may return to his former grace, according to that, *Remember whence thou art fallen, and do penance, and do the first works [n: (Apoc. II)]*.

*Direct thy heart. [n: (ID.)]* He directs his heart into the right way, etc., *as far as* nor does he consent to turn aside through the luxuries of the world.

*Return, O virgin, Israel, return to these thy cities. [n: (ID.)]* Although certain men [take] these things according to the history, etc., *as far as* and let her cease to wander through the luxuries of the world.

VERS. 23.-- *They shall yet say. [n: (ID.)]* In Christian, that is, [0046B] times, in which the Lord turned back the captivity of his people.

*May he bless thee.* These things are seen in part under Zorobabel, etc., *as far as* carnally, according to the Jews and to our own judaizers.

VERS. 25.-- *For I have inebriated.* A change of persons, etc., *as far as* the people returned from the captivity answers: *therefore I was awakened,* [var: Vulg. Jer. 31:26 *Ideo quasi de somno suscitatus sum*, "therefore I was as it were raised up out of sleep"] etc.

VERS. 26.-- *Therefore as it were raised up out of sleep.* That I might imitate him who says, etc., *as far as* and awaits the day of resurrection.

VERS. 27.-- *Behold the days come saith the Lord.* All promises of this kind, etc., *as far as* but that God may be all in all.

VERS. 30.-- *The teeth shall be set on edge. [n: (ID.)]* The teeth of those who eat cannot feel sweetness, etc., [0046C] *as far as* not by the harshness of the foods, but by the fault of the teeth.

VERS. 31.-- *Behold the days.* By this testimony Paul, etc., *as far as* and that circumcision and the sabbath might be spiritually fulfilled.

VERS. 34.-- *And he shall not teach.* That they may not seek Jewish masters and the commandments of men, but may be taught by the Holy Spirit, if indeed they shall have been the temple of God, and the Spirit of God shall have dwelt in them, who breathes where he wills, and has diverse graces: Yet the knowledge of the one God is the possession of all the virtues.

*For all shall know. [n: (HIER.)]* Because all shall be etc., *as far as* not in the nature in which he lay hidden in himself even when he was seen.

VERS. 35.-- *Who giveth the sun. [n: (ID.)]* According to Genesis [0046D] the sun was set in heaven for the light of the day, the moon and the stars for the light of the night. In the psalm also it is read: *Day to day uttereth speech, and night to night sheweth knowledge,* because days and nights succeed one another in turn.

VERS. 36.-- *If they shall have failed,* etc. If the heavens shall perish, and shall grow old so, a garment [cj: *sic*; read *sicut*, "as a garment"], how can the seed of Israel be everlasting? But the heavens shall perish.

VERS. 37.-- *If they can be measured.* As if he should say: How of things, etc., *as far as* nor shall it ever cease.

VERS. 38.-- *Behold the days.* Who a kingdom of a thousand years, etc., *as far as,* but there Ananehel through the letter *men*.

VERS. 39.-- *Even to the gate of the corner. [n: (ID.)]* For no [0047A] true justice, etc., *as far as* this building shall remain for ever.
