## CHAPTER XXXVI.

VERS. 3.-- *If so be, when the house of Juda heareth,* etc. Immense clemency, etc., *as far as* that through the mercy of God they may obtain pardon.

VERS. 4.-- *He called therefore.* The prompt obedience of the prophet [0050B] accords, etc., *as far as* who, loosed and free, neglect to lay out the word of God to their neighbours?

VERS. 9.-- *They proclaimed a fast in the sight of the Lord,* etc. That they who sinned through excess, etc., *as far as* the things in which they offended in the greatest matters.

VERS. 16.-- *Therefore when they had heard,* etc. It is necessary also for hearers, etc., *as far as* they were able to hear the words of God with the ear of the body, but not of the heart.

VERS. 18.-- *He spoke out of his mouth,* etc. It signifies that the apostles and prophets suffered no necessity of treating or poverty of speaking; but readily and easily brought forth all things.

VERS. 23.-- *And he cast it into the fire.* The astounding wickedness of a hardened heart, etc., *as far as* lest they should believe and repent.

[0050C]VERS. 26.-- *But he hid,* etc. Against whom human presumption strives in vain. *For the Lord keepeth all them that love him, and all sinners he will destroy.*

VERS. 30.-- *Therefore,* etc. A worthy vengeance, etc., *as far as* he should be burnt by the heat of the sun and by the frost of the night.

## CHAPTER XXXVII.

VERS. 1.-- *And Sedecias the king reigned,* etc. That Sedecias after Joachim, etc., *as far as* but they were all wicked and idolaters, and therefore they perished.

VERS. 3.-- *Pray for.* By no means did he ask to pray for himself, because he would not obey the divine commandments: *For he that turneth away his ear that he may not hear the law, his prayer shall be an abomination.*

VERS. 4.-- *Therefore the army.* How the Chaldeans [0050D] broke off, etc., *as far as* but Jeremias prophesied things contrary and true.

## CHAPTER XXXVIII.

VERS. 5.-- *And the king Sedecias saith,* etc. The father, Sedecias without pure affection, etc., *as far as* But afterwards, having scourged him, he delivered him to the Jews, that he might be crucified.

VERS. 7.-- *And Abdemelech heard.* As those princes, who sent the prophet, etc., *as far as* by how much the more strictly we hold ourselves guilty.

VERS. 17.-- *If thou wilt go forth,* etc. The prophet, foreseeing that the city was to be delivered into the hands of the Chaldeans, gave counsel to the king that he should deliver himself into the hands of the king of Babylon, that he might so mitigate his furious mind, [0051A] and receive a lighter sentence; but the hardened mind could not be corrected.

VERS. 22.-- *Behold.* It signifies that a flowing thought, etc., *as far as* and shall burn up the soul with unquenchable fire.

## CHAPTER XXXIX.

VERS. 17.-- *And I will deliver thee. They that hope in the Lord shall not be confounded [n: (Sap. XIX)]*. *They shall all be confounded that do unjust things [n: (Psal. XXIV)]*. [var: Vulg. Ps. 24:4 *Confundantur omnes iniqua agentes supervacue*, "let all them be confounded that do unjust things without cause"] Behold, the princes who sought the soul of Jeremias are led captive. The eunuch who had delivered him obtained perfect liberty. So also, the holy preachers being delivered, the persecutors shall be punished with eternal vengeance.

## CHAPTER XL.

VERS. 1.-- *The word that came,* etc. It is asked [0051B] how this word of the Lord, etc., *as far as* he was set free according to the word of the Lord.

VERS. 2.-- *Taking him therefore.* The devastation of the city having been described, and the destruction, and the captivity of the people, and the perfidy of the king, there follows what was done concerning Jeremias, and concerning the remnants of the people, whom the prophet admonished out of the word of the Lord to sit in their own land, and to serve the king of Babylon, but they themselves chose rather to go into Egypt, where afterwards Nabuchodonosor slew them, and laid waste Egypt itself.

VERS. 3.-- *And it is come upon you.* From this place also it is manifest that God, etc., *as far as* but they preached the sentence of God to be just.

## CHAPTER XLI.

VERS. 8.-- *Ten men were found among them,* etc., *we have a treasure.* [var: Vulg. Jer. 41:8 *habemus thesauros in agro*, "we have treasures in the field"] For a treasure in the field, etc., *as far as* For by the heel, which is the end of the body, the end of action is signified.

VERS. 16.-- *Johanan therefore the son of Careas took, and all the princes of the warriors that were with him,* etc. This flight of the remnants and passage into Egypt against the word of the Lord, which Jeremias has here recounted in a long narration, Isaias foretold long before, a hundred and fifty years, that is, which were between Isaias and Jeremias, saying: *Woe to you, apostate children, saith the Lord, that you should take counsel, and not of me [n: (Isai. XXX)]*, etc.

## CHAPTER XLIV.

[0051D]VERS. 15.-- *And they answered Jeremias,* etc. A foolish answer. They confess that he speaks in the name of the Lord, and they do not acquiesce, but follow the counsel of their own depravity. Mystically. All heretics in the Egypt of this world. That is, being established in the darkness of error, they offer sacrifices not to the true God, but to demons, when they write fictions and depraved dogmas for deceiving men and for overthrowing the faith. Therefore evils shall come upon them, and they shall be for a desolation and for an astonishment.

VERS. 26.-- *The Lord liveth,* etc. The prophet gainsays, out of the word of the Lord, lest his name be called by the mouth of the Jews in the land of Egypt, since they who had passed over thither against the precept of God ought not to have the protection [0052A] of the Lord in that same place. But mystically the name of the Lord is not called by the mouth of heretics in the land of Egypt: because neither their speech nor their prayer, while they are in error, is accepted of God. *For praise is not beautiful in the mouth of a sinner [n: (Eccl. XV)]*. They therefore who shall have persevered in error shall perish by the sword of strict judgment. But they who, error being left behind, shall have returned repentant out of Egypt into the land of Juda: and thenceforth holding the catholic faith shall be saved.

## CHAPTER XLV.

VERS. 1.-- *The word that Jeremias spoke,* etc. Baruch, as yet unpractised in the endurance of the sufferings which he was about to experience, was terrified at the calamity of the captivity which was threatening in the time of Joachim son of Josias king of Juda, whence he complains of sorrow added to his sorrow, [0052B] to whom it was answered by the Lord that he seeks peace for himself to no purpose, when the whole people labours under the bundle of tribulations. And it signifies those who in the pressure of tribulations or of persecutions, by which the Church is weighed down in the present time, waver through faint-heartedness, seeking the consolation which is promised in the time to come. Whence: *But you shall be made sorrowful, but your sorrow shall be turned into joy [n: (Joan. XVI)]*.
