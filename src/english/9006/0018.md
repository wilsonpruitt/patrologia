## CHAPTER XLVI.

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The word that came,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] Thus far the prophet has treated mixedly of Juda and Israel, of the Chaldeans and the Egyptians, according to the fitness of the narration, etc., *as far as* in the year of Joachim son of Josias when Pharao Nechao king of Egypt fought in Charchamis with [0052C] Nabuchodonosor, and was overcome.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 9.-- *Get ye up on the horses. [n: (ID.)]* Heretics and schismatics, sprung from divers provinces, in vain set wars in array against the city of God, etc., *as far as* when they are delivered into the hands of him who set his throne toward the North, that he may send them into the pool of fire and brimstone.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 11.-- *Go up into Galaad. [n: (HIER.)]* It is a mountain to which Jacob came on the seventh day, fleeing from Charram: and it is at the back of Phoenicia and of Arabia, joined to the hills of Libanus, and it stretches through the desert as far as beyond Jordan where dwelt Seon king of the Amorrhites. ⟦s2⟧ And it fell into the lot of the sons of Ruben and Gad, and of the half tribe of Manasse.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ *Take balm. [n: (RAB.)]* The Greeks call it ῥητίνην; for ῥέω [0052D] is said of that which flows, etc., *as far as* because they shall receive from them not health, but confusion.

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 12.-- *The nations have heard. [n: (ID.)]* Because, the solace of idols being frustrated, etc., *as far as* they receive not the victory of truth, but, captives to error, fall together into the pit of perdition.

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 14.-- *Declare ye to Egypt.* ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: You who have heard my prophecy, announce to Pharao that the time and the tumult of war is near.

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 18.-- *For as Thabor.* ⟦s2⟧ That is, from the comparison of fruitful places, as it were sunk in the sea, you can understand the great ruin of the Egyptians. [n: (HIER.)] Thabor is a boundary of Zabulon, a mountain in the midst of the plain of Galilee, lofty with a wonderful [0053A] roundness: distant ten miles from Diocaesaraea toward the eastern region. ⟦s3⟧ Which was also the border between the tribes of Issachar and Nephthalim, Carmelus, where Nabal the Carmelite was and now there is a village Carmela by name, at the tenth milestone of the town of Chebron, sloping toward the eastern region where the garrison of the Romans is.

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [n: (RAB.)] But mystically, because Thabor, etc., *as far as* sunk in the sea of this world, and in perpetual damnation.

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 20.-- *A heifer.* ⟦s2⟧ Because the Egyptian people, nourished in delights, untamed, wanton, refused the yoke of discipline.

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 21.-- *Hirelings. [n: (RAB.)]* He calls the princes of the Egyptians not shepherds, but hirelings, etc., *as far as A hireling however, and he that is not the shepherd, when he seeth the wolf coming, leaveth the sheep, and flieth* [0053B] [n: (Joan. X)].

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 26.-- *And after this,* etc. [n: (ID.)] Although he seems to intimate that the kingdom of the Egyptians was destroyed by Nabuchodonosor not irreparably, etc., *as far as: The Lord shall strike Egypt with a scourge and shall heal it [n: (Isa. XIX)]*, etc.

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 27.-- *And fear thou not,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ This promise does not well suit the Jews: who, scattered over the whole world, etc., *as far as: For blindness in part hath happened in Israel, and when the fulness of the Gentiles shall have come in, then all Israel shall be saved [n: (Rom. XI)]*.

## CHAPTER XLVII.

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 1.-- *The word that came,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ Jeremias, like Isaias and Ezechiel, foretells to the neighbouring nations the plagues [0053C] to come, because they were harassing the people of God, and rejoicing in his adversity. ⟦s3⟧ To the Palestines therefore, as before to the Egyptians, he foreannounces the captivity to come.

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 4.-- *And it shall be laid waste.* ⟦s2⟧ He shows that Tyre and Sidon pertain to the Palestines, although Tyre fell into the lot of Nephthalim, and Sidon, the border of the Chanaanites, into the lot of Aser: but he did not possess it, because he was not able to drive out the enemies. ⟦s3⟧ *The remnants of the island of Cappadocia.* ⟦s4⟧ In which the Cappadocians dwelt, the former inhabitants having been slain. ⟦s5⟧ This is the border of the Chanaanites next to Egypt, and it fell into the lot of Juda: but he did not obtain it, because the Enachim, that is, the giants of the aliens, most strongly resisted: and to this day it is a notable city of Palestine, although it was prophesied of it *Gaza shall be an everlasting heap:* [0053D] but where it formerly was scarcely any traces remain, for it was built in another place.

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 5.-- *There is come,* etc. [n: (RAB.)] It signifies the plague to come upon heretics and schismatics and all the enemies of the Church, etc., *as far as* For through lust he burns up the minds of carnal men: but at the last he shall be cast down.

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ VERS. 7.-- *How shall it be quiet,* etc. ⟦s2⟧ As if he should say: The sword cannot be quiet, unless the Lord command, whose vengeance it executes, or: As it were in the person of the afflicted he had spoken to the sword of the Lord, but here he answers in his own person.
