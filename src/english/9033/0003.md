## HERE BEGINS THE BOOK (cont.)


That we may now open out the very text of the divine oracle: as maidens, as companions, let us hear the words of the bridegroom, let us hear the words of the bride, and in their discourses let us learn the fervor of love. And so holy Church, long awaiting the coming of the Lord, long thirsting for the fountain of life — how she wishes to see the presence of her bridegroom, how she desires and speaks.
