## CHAPTER III. (cont.)

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « Behold the little bed of Solomon, threescore valiant ones of the most valiant of Israel surround it, all holding swords, and most expert in war. ⟦s2⟧ Every man's sword upon his thigh. » Whither this beloved had gone up is shown [0312A] when it is added: « Behold the little bed of Solomon, » etc. ⟦s3⟧ The bed of Solomon, although the heavenly rest of that celestial blessedness may be understood, in which God rests with his saints, yet is more probably understood as the present rest, in which the saints of God, the tumults of the vices being lulled to sleep, are delighted with the embrace of Solomon, that is, of the true Peaceable One. ⟦s4⟧ Whence too the Psalmist says of Christ: « His place is made in peace, and his abode in Zion. » The little bed of Solomon, therefore, that is, the Church of Christ, threescore valiant ones of the most valiant of Israel surround. ⟦s5⟧ By the threescore valiant ones is understood the order of the elect, who either by preaching fortify the Church, or by contemplating desire to attain to that heavenly blessedness. ⟦s6⟧ Now the number sixty consists of six and ten. ⟦s7⟧ For six times ten, or ten times six, [0312B] make sixty. ⟦s8⟧ And six signifies the perfection of work, because in six days God perfected his works: but ten signifies the recompense and reward, which at the end shall be given to the elect. ⟦s9⟧ Whence too those who came into the vineyard are read to have received a penny. ⟦s10⟧ The threescore valiant ones, therefore, signify (as has been said) the holy teachers, valiant and constant in mind. ⟦s11⟧ And it is added: « Of the most valiant of Israel. » All who believe in Christ, and love Christ, are called Israel (that is, "a man seeing God"). ⟦s12⟧ But the most valiant of Israel are those who know how to protect the Church by preaching, and to defend it from the assaults of demons or the attack of heretics. ⟦s13⟧ These, therefore, surround the little bed of Solomon, because to them the Church is committed, and by them it is to be guarded with watchful care. ⟦s14⟧ « All holding swords, and most expert in war; [0312C] » for they hold the spiritual sword of the word of God, of which the Apostle says: « And take (he says) the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God. » And they are most expert in war, because it is necessary that those be instructed in the art of spiritual battle who wage wars against principalities and powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against the spirits of wickedness in the high places. ⟦s15⟧ For that enemy is most cunning, and of so great subtlety of spiritual nature, being also by the long span of time most expert, against whom the saints take up their wars. ⟦s16⟧ « Every man's sword upon his thigh because of fears in the night. » By the thigh is received the propagation of offspring. ⟦s17⟧ Upon the thigh, therefore, they have their swords, because by the fear of God all are restrained, and they curb the motions of carnal pleasure. ⟦s18⟧ And this « because of [0312D] fears in the night, » that is, because of the hidden snares of the malignant enemy, who in the night of this world especially lies in wait for the saints who are preeminent in the Church, that, they being deceived, he may defile the little bed of Solomon, that is, the rest of the saints. ⟦s19⟧ And it is to be noted that above they are said to hold swords, but here to have the sword upon the thigh. ⟦s20⟧ For they hold swords, that they may resist the adversaries, and pierce through the heretics and all who contradict the sound faith. ⟦s21⟧ Upon the thigh, therefore, they have their swords, that they may take care of themselves, that they may repress the flux of carnal pleasures, lest, preaching to others, they themselves become reprobate.

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « King Solomon made himself a litter of the wood of Lebanon. » What above was designated by the bed of Solomon, this [0313A] now is designated by the litter, namely the holy Church, in which the saints of God rest as in a bed, and as in a bed recline at the banquets of eternal satiety. ⟦s2⟧ Of the wood of Lebanon Solomon made a litter. ⟦s3⟧ Lebanon is a mountain of Phoenicia, whose trees are preeminent above the rest in height and beauty and durability, and therefore they signify the saints shining with the hope of the virtues and hastening to the eternal things. ⟦s4⟧ For Lebanon is interpreted "whitening," or "whitening out." ⟦s5⟧ The wood of Lebanon, therefore, are the saints made white and whitened in baptism, and adorned with all the beauty of the virtues.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « The pillars thereof he made of silver. » By the pillars are signified the teachers of that same Church, who sustain and strengthen it by word and example. ⟦s2⟧ Whence in the Acts of the Apostles: « James, and John, and Cephas, [0313B] who seemed to be pillars. » These pillars are of silver, because they shine with the brightness of heavenly eloquence. ⟦s3⟧ « For the words of the Lord are chaste words, » etc. ⟦s4⟧ « The seat of gold, the going up of purple. » By the seat of gold, the eternal rest is received, which is promised to the saints in the Church, to which nevertheless the going up is of purple. ⟦s5⟧ The going up of purple is the passion of Christ, because purple imitates the color of blood. ⟦s6⟧ By the going up of purple, therefore, one comes to the seat, because they come to the reclining at the eternal banquets and to the rest of the heavenly quiet who in the present strive worthily to venerate and imitate the passion of their Redeemer. ⟦s7⟧ « The midst he paved with charity, for the daughters of Jerusalem. » He paved the midst with charity, because Christ commended his charity to all the faithful, [0313C] by suffering for us, and by preparing rest for us, according to what the Apostle says: But God commendeth his charity toward us, because when as yet we were sinners, he delivered up his Son to death for us. ⟦s8⟧ In another way: By the silver pillars, the apostles and apostolic men are designated; by the seat of gold, the order of teachers, who, when they promise the ineffable things of eternal life, cause us assuredly to rest sweetly. ⟦s9⟧ And this seat is well called golden, because ineffable is that reward which the saints await. ⟦s10⟧ For among perishable things nothing is more precious than gold. ⟦s11⟧ But by the going up of purple, the order of martyrs is marked, who, having imitated the passion of Christ, poured out their blood for him. ⟦s12⟧ But what shall we do, says the blessed Gregory, who are of no merit? ⟦s13⟧ Neither [0313D] among the teachers, nor among the martyrs, do we seem to have a place. ⟦s14⟧ There follows: « The midst he paved with charity. » Let us therefore have charity, with which the midst of this litter is paved, which is also for this reason called the broad commandment, because for all its keepers it prepares eternal salvation. ⟦s15⟧ Let us hold this effectually, and through it we shall be saved. ⟦s16⟧ And this for the daughters of Jerusalem, that is, for the simple souls, conscious to themselves of no virtue, who, the more they are conscious of their own frailty, the more do they strive to love their Savior and Redeemer.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ « Go forth and see, O daughters of Zion, king Solomon in the diadem wherewith his mother crowned him in the day of his espousals, and in the day of the joy of his heart. » [0314A] The voice of the Church inviting the souls of the faithful to behold how wonderful and beautiful is her bridegroom. ⟦s2⟧ For the daughters of Zion are the churches, which are also daughters of Jerusalem, namely holy souls, citizens of that heavenly city, who both enjoy perpetual peace with the angels, and through contemplation behold the glory of the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ Go forth (she says), O daughters of Zion, that is, go out from the turbulent conversation of this world, that with mind unencumbered you may be able to contemplate him whom you love. ⟦s4⟧ And see (she says) king Solomon, that is, the true Peaceable One, Christ, « in the diadem wherewith his mother crowned him. » As if she said: Consider Christ, clothed for you in flesh, which flesh he took on from the flesh of the Virgin his mother. ⟦s5⟧ For he calls the diadem the flesh which Christ took on [0314B] for us, in which by dying he destroyed the empire of death, in which too he conferred on us the hope of rising again. ⟦s6⟧ Of this diadem the Apostle says: We see Jesus, for the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honor. ⟦s7⟧ And his mother is said to have crowned him, because the Virgin Mary furnished him, from her own flesh, the material of his flesh. ⟦s8⟧ « In the day of his espousals, » that is, in the time of the incarnation, when he joined the Church to himself, not having spot or wrinkle, or when God was joined to man. ⟦s9⟧ « And in the day of the joy of his heart. » For the joy and gladness of Christ is the salvation and redemption of the human race, according to what in the Gospel, seeing many flow together to his faith, in that (says the evangelist) hour he rejoiced, and said: « I confess to thee, O Father of heaven and earth, because thou hast hid these things from the wise and [0314C] prudent, and hast revealed them to little ones. » And in the Gospel parable, the sheep being found, he called together his friends, saying: « Rejoice with me. » This too can be referred simply and wholly to the passion of Christ according to the letter. ⟦s10⟧ Solomon also, foreseeing in spirit the passion of Christ, long before forewarned the daughter of Zion, that is, the Israelite people, saying, « Go forth, » « and see king Solomon, » that is, Christ, « in the diadem, » that is, in the crown of thorns, « wherewith his mother crowned him, » the Synagogue, « in the day of his espousals, » namely when he joined the Church to himself; « and in the day of the joy of his heart, » in which he rejoiced that the world was redeemed through his passion from the diabolical power. ⟦s11⟧ Go forth, therefore, that is, go out from the darkness of unbelief, and see (that is, understand in [0314D] your mind) that he who suffers as a man is true God: or also go forth outside the gate of your city, that you may see him crucified on Mount Golgotha.
