## Commentaries on the Gospel according to John

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1243]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1243B]Saint Ambrose enumerates in the following order the prayers that were mentioned: prayers, supplications, requests, thanksgivings. ⟦s2⟧ Under prayer he places, when we request good things for ourselves, under supplication, when we request absolution from evils, under request, the fight against all adversities. ⟦s3⟧ And he prescribes that we ought to know to do this when we come together into one. ⟦s4⟧ Saint Augustine has it thus: precations, prayers, interpellations, or, as your codices have, requests, thanksgivings. ⟦s5⟧ And he says: *all of which, or nearly all, the church frequents in the solemnity of the sacraments.* ⟦s6⟧ Precation is every prayer before that which is done at the Lord's table; prayer is whatever is done afterwards up to communion; [1244B] interpellation or request is when the people is blessed; thanksgiving concludes all. ⟦s7⟧ Where saint Augustine says *precations,* our codices have *supplications.* ⟦s8⟧ Under supplication, as has been said, the request for the absolution of sins is placed by saint Ambrose. ⟦s9⟧ The rest we name in one way, as *pra . . . . . . . st* [nt: One or two words are lacking; where they stood, the parchment has been cut away. Perhaps read *ut praedictum est*, "as was said before".] concerning saint Augustine. ⟦s10⟧ And they hold the same force which saint Ambrose placed under them, both in the celebration of the sacraments and in the other offices. ⟦s11⟧ In all that I write, I hang upon the judgment of men and of pious Fathers. ⟦s12⟧ Meanwhile I say what I think. ⟦s13⟧ The things which are done in the celebration of the mass are done in the sacrament of the Lord's passion.
