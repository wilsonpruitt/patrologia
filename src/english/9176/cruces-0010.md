## Vocabulary chosen for this range (hold at merge)

- ***vir*** is "husband" throughout the Samaritan-woman exposition, including the Pauline quotation
  *Caput mulieris vir, caput viri Christus* (0336A), because Eriugena expounds that verse *de hoc
  viro*, the woman's husband; "man" there would cut the verse off from the gloss.
- The soul's parts (0336A–C): ***animus*** kept in italic Latin, *mens* "mind", *intellectus*
  "intellect", *anima* "soul", *spiritus* "spirit". Eriugena lists *intellectus, mens, animus,
  spiritus* as four names of one thing and then builds the triad *animus / ratio / sensus
  interior*; English "mind" cannot serve both *mens* and *animus* without erasing his list.
- *adorare* "adore" (Douay, John 4:20–24); *colere* (0334B) "worship", kept distinct.
- *coutor* "deal with" (John 4:9, and the gloss's *coutebantur / coutimur / coutuntur*).
- *hauritorium* "drawing-vessel"; *puteus* "well"; *fons* "fountain"; *hydria* "water-pot";
  *praedium* "estate". Names as printed, Douay forms: Noe, Zorobabel, Ornan the Jebusite, Messias,
  Sichar / Sychem (both, as he contrasts them).

## Col 0329C (chunk 0010) — Floss's VL ³ placed: the open item

Leaf n201 prints two identical notes, ² and ³ *Cod. nunquid.* The 0007 stint placed ² after the
first *Nonne*. At 2× on the archive and 3× on Princeton PA329 a second raised key stands after the
**second** *Nonne* (*Nonne ³ et apostoli ab eo missi sunt*); on the archive the digit is a blotch
of 3-shape, on Princeton a clean ³. So the codex read *nunquid* for both *Nonne*. Row placed.
The English follows Floss (*Nonne*, "Were not…?"), E9.

## Col 0329D (chunk 0010) — `denorum`: E12

*ceteraque spiritualium denorum divisio*. Our twin's *denorum* is not a word; the plate's second
letter is a broken sort on both copies (e/o unsettled). The only word the sentence can be is
*donorum* (*plenitudo donorum Dei* six lines above). Rendered "the division of spiritual gifts",
no marker, no patch.

## Col 0329D (chunk 0010) — scripture as printed, not fired

*Non enim ex mensura dat Deus spiritum* (Vulg. *ad mensuram*); 1 Cor 12:8 abridged; 1 Cor 12:11
*Unus autem operatur atque idem Spiritus, dividens singulis propria* (Vulg. *Haec autem omnia
operantur unus atque idem Spiritus, dividens singulis*; Eriugena adds *propria*, drops *haec
omnia*). None changes the argument; logged, not fired. The anchor [0330A] falls inside the
quotation between *operatur* and *atque*; English order puts it one phrase later.

## Col 0330B–C (chunk 0010) — John 3:36 and the Pauline quotations, not fired

Lemma *Qui autem non credit in Filio, non videbit vitam aeternam* (Vulg. *qui autem incredulus est
Filio, non videbit vitam*); rendered as printed, "believeth not in the Son … life everlasting".
Gen 3:17 *in operibus tuis* (Vulg. *in opere tuo*); Eph 2:3 *Fuistis aliquando filii irae sicut et
ceteri* (Vulg. *eramus natura filii irae*): second person and without *natura*. Rendered as printed
("You were once children of wrath"). A possible `[var:]` for Eph 2:3 was declined: the gloss uses
only *ira*, not the person or *natura*.

## Col 0330C–D (chunk 0010) — `[var:]` John 4:1–2 *etsi Jesus ipse non baptizavit*

Printed *audierant … etsi Jesus ipse non baptizavit, sed discipuli ejus*; Clementine Jo 4:1–2
*audierunt … (quamquam Jesus non baptizaret, sed discipuli ejus)*. Fired on the perfect, for the
same reason the 0007 stint fired *baptizavit* at 0322C: the gloss turns on it (*Baptizavit Christus
et non baptizavit*). Column read (n201).

## Col 0331B (chunk 0010) — Floss's bracket *[abiit]*

## Col 0331C (chunk 0010) — `[d:]` *in in Judaeam*

*de qua Dominus nascens in carne venit in / in Judaeam*: the plate sets *in* at the end of one line
and again at the start of the next (checked, n202). A line-break doubling of an identical word, the
compositor's, not our hyphen class: both copies are real *in*. Pattern 11, identical runs, second
copy marked: "came into [d: into] Judaea".

## Col 0331C (chunk 0010) — *neque eos, qui in eum credituri essent*

*Quoniam vero noluit manere sub lege, neque eos, qui in eum credituri essent, reliquit legem*: the
accusative *eos* has no verb of its own (presumably "nor [willed] those … [to remain]"). Rendered
literally, "since he would not remain under the law, nor those who were to believe in him, he left
the law". No supply, no marker.

## Col 0332A (chunk 0010) — *quae soli carnali Israel imposita* and *futurae*

Two elliptical phrases with no verb / no noun: "which [was] imposed on carnal Israel alone" and
"the beatitude of the future [life]". Supplied words bracketed, per corpus convention.

## Col 0332A–C (chunk 0010) — Floss's NOTAE a, b, c → `[nt:]`

Translated at the keys, per E10. (a) Jerome *Galilaea, volutabilis, aut transmigratio perpetrata
vel rota*, with two Hebrew words (the verb "to roll" and Gilgal, both pointed); Floss prints
*interpretio* (so on both copies), rendered "interpretation". (b) *Sic S. Hieronymus: Sichar
conclusio sive ramus*. (c) Jerome on *Sichar* for *Sichem* "shoulders"; Floss suggests Eriugena
read *numeri* for *humeri*, so the English keeps both Latin words beside their glosses, since the
note is about the letters. Hebrew is `(Hebrew)` in all three, not transcribed letter-exact.

## Col 0332C (chunk 0010) — *conclusio … conclusus erat. Conclusit Scriptura*

The etymology drives the next two sentences, so one English word carries all three: "*enclosure*
… was enclosed … Scripture hath enclosed all things under sin" (Gal 3:22 *conclusit*). Rom 11:17
is paraphrased, not quoted; not collated.

## Col 0332C (chunk 0010) — *electos ex gentibus numeratos investigatio manet*

*manet* with an accusative object: "an investigation awaits the chosen numbered from among the
Gentiles". The sense of the clause (as an etymology of *numeri*) is obscure; rendered literally.

## Col 0332D–0333A (chunk 0011) — verbless definitions kept verbless

*Iter ejus descensus divinitatis suae …*; *Sessio Christi … possessio*; *Hauritorium vero studium

as the plate sets them. Lemma *sedit sic super fontem* (Vulg. *sedebat sic supra fontem*),
*Venit mulier ex Samaria* (Vulg. *de*): not fired.

## Col 0333B (chunk 0011) — *in quo Abraham jussus a Deo immolare Isaac*

## Col 0333C (chunk 0011) — *Christo inhumanato*

*quae Christo inhumanato plenissime illuxit mundo*: dative or ablative absolute. Rendered as an
absolute, "which, Christ being inhumanated, shone most fully upon the world", keeping
*inhumanatus* "inhumanated" (E4).

## Col 0334C (chunk 0011) — `[var:]` John 4:10 without *vivam*

Printed *tu petisses eum, et dedisset tibi aquam.* Clementine Jo 4:10 *tu forsitan petisses ab eo,
et dedisset tibi aquam vivam*. Fired because the lemma as printed never mentions living water,
while the woman's reply in the next lemma (*Quomodo ergo habes aquam vivam?*) and Eriugena's own
gloss (*darem tibi … aquam vivam*) both presuppose it. Column read (n203). *petisses eum* with a
bare accusative is rendered "asked him".

## Col 0336A (chunk 0011) — 1 Cor 11:3, not fired

*Caput mulieris vir, caput viri Christus, caput Christi Deus*: the Clementine order is *omnis viri
caput Christus … caput mulieris vir … caput Christi Deus*. Eriugena's order (woman → husband →
Christ → God) is the ladder his gloss climbs, and the words are all there. Logged.

## Col 0336C (chunk 0012) — `[var:]` John 4:17 *quia virum non habes*

Printed *Bene dixisti, quia virum non habes*; Clementine Jo 4:17 *Bene dixisti, quia non habeo
virum*, Jesus quoting the woman's own words. The printed lemma turns it into Jesus's statement
about her, and the gloss follows that (*Novi enim te non habere virum*). Fired: the speaker of the
clause changes. Column read (n204, line on archive and Princeton).

## Col 0336C (chunk 0012) — *quae vere dixisti*: Floss's *forte quia?*

VL ⁵ *Sic cod., forte quia?* So Floss prints the codex's *quae* and queries *quia* ("because thou
hast said truly"). The English follows the printed *quae* (E9), taken as feminine singular
referring to the woman: "thou who hast said truly". No marker.

## Col 0336C (chunk 0012) — *neque ideo novi, quia tu dixisti*

## Col 0337A (chunk 0012) — *veluti naturali viro errori subditur*

As printed (n205): the soul "is subjected, as if to a natural husband, to error". The irony is
presumably intended (error takes the husband's place); no conjecture.

## Col 0337C (chunk 0012) — *gravitatem sacramentorum opprimendam*

*non ad liberandam animam … sed ad redarguendam, et gravitatem sacramentorum opprimendam*: the
gerundive agrees with *gravitatem*, so literally "to press down the weight of the sacraments".
Obscure; rendered literally. Rom 5:20 *peccatum* for Vulg. *delictum*; 1 Cor 15:56 exact.

## Col 0337C (chunk 0012) — the mark after *Samaritanae*

## Col 0339B (chunk 0012) — John 4:25 and *Venit … non « veniet »*

*Scio, quia Messias venit, qui dicitur Christus* matches; *Cum venerit ille, adnuntiabit nobis
omnia* (Vulg. *cum ergo venerit ille, nobis annuntiabit omnia*), not fired. The gloss turns on
present *venit* against future *veniet*, so the lemma's *venit* is "cometh" and « veniet » is
« will come », guillemets 1:1.

## Col 0339C (chunk 0012) — *Ego sum, qui loquor tibi*

Rendered "*I am, who speak to thee*", not "I am he": the gloss supplies the predicate itself
(*Ego sum ipse Christus*), and a supplied "he" in the lemma would pre-empt it. Vulg. *loquor te*,
not fired.

"no one was saying", not fired.
