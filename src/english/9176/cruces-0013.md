## Col 0340B — *Deus tentator malorum est*

Printed *tentator* (both copies), "God is the tempter of the wicked", where Jas 1:13 reads
*Deus enim intentator malorum est* ("God is not a tempter of evils"). Rendered as printed. It
construes with what follows (*Tentat autem electos probandi gratia … altera impiorum reprobat
perfidiam*: God's temptation proves the just and reprobates the wicked), so it does not meet the
Pattern 18 bar. *malorum* taken as "the wicked" from the paired *impiorum*; "of evils" is possible.
No marker.

## Col 0341A — `[var:]` *puerulus … pisciculos*

Printed *Est puerulus unus hic … duos pisciculos, sed haec quid sunt in tantos?*; Vulg. *puer*,
*pisces*, *inter tantos*. Fired because the gloss builds on the diminutive: Moses *veluti quidam

tantos* noted here only. At 0342A Eriugena re-quotes it as *sed hoc quid est in tantos?* (singular
*hoc … est*); rendered as printed, no second marker.

## Col 0347A — `[cj:]` *allegoria facti, non autem dicti* ⚑ FOR A HUMAN

✅ **RULED by Wilson 2026-09-17: the `[cj:]` stands** (style file E13, transpositions settled by the author's own text).

The plate (both copies) prints *In symbolis itaque, hoc est in dictionibus spiritualis doctrinae,
quas sola allegoria facti, non autem dicti, tradit*: symbols are handed down "by the allegory of
deed alone, not of word". Eriugena's own definition, three times in these columns, is the
reverse: *allegoria dicti, non autem facti appellatur* (0345A), *allegoria sola dicti, non autem
facti et dicti* (0345B), *sola spiritualis doctrina allegorice dicti, non autem secundum historiam
facti* (0346D), and the very next sentence says *Solus enim intellectus in eo cogitatur, nullum
autem factum*. The faithful English asserts the opposite of the distinction the whole passage
builds, with the negation bound to the wrong member. Rendered literally, glossed additively:
`[cj: *facti, non autem dicti*; read *dicti, non autem facti*, "of word, but not of deed"]`. This
is a transposition, at the edge of the Pattern 18 class (negation / correlative misbound); a
human should confirm the marker rather than a crux alone.
