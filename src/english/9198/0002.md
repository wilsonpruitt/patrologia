## The seventh day before the Kalends · 26 January

[0693]

At Smyrna, the birthday of Saint Policarpus, who, a disciple of blessed John the apostle, and ordained bishop by him, was the chief of all Asia. Afterwards, under Marcus Antoninus and Lucius Aurelius, while the whole people [0695] of the same City cried out against him in the amphitheatre, he was given over to the fire. With whom also twelve others, coming from Philadelphia, were consummated by martyrdom in the aforesaid city. On the same day, of the holy martyrs Theogenes with thirty-six others, who, despising temporal death, obtained the crown of eternal life. [Bouillart: . . . under the emperor Lizinius obtained the crown of eternal life, *omitting what follows.]* In the territory of Paris, of Baltildis, queen.

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 27 January

[0699]

The birthday of blessed John, bishop of Constantinople, surnamed Chrysostom, who by word and example greatly profited the Christian religion. Also, of Saint Marius, abbot of the monastery *Bobacensis*; whose life full of virtues the illustrious man and patrician Dynamius writes. In Bethlehem of Judah, the falling-asleep of Saint Paula. Saint Jerome, writing her Life, admirable for virtues, testifies that she was crowned with a long martyrdom. In Africa, of Saint Avitus, martyr.

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 28 January

[0703]

At Rome, the birthday of Saint Agnes, the second time. And in the city of Apollonia, of the saints Leucius, Thyrsus and Calinicus, martyrs, who in the time of Decius, tormented with divers kinds of torments, the first and the last by the cutting off of the head, the middle one, called forth by a heavenly voice, giving up his spirit, completed their martyrdom. At Alexandria, of blessed Cyril, bishop, who stood forth as a most illustrious champion of the catholic faith. In the monastery *Beomensis*, the deposition of John, presbyter, a man of God. [Bouillart adds: At Cinomanni, of Saint Julian, the first bishop of that city.]

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 29 January

[0707]

At Rome on the Numentan Way, the birthday of the saints Papias and Maurus, soldiers, whose mouths Laudicius, prefect of the City, ordered at their first confession of Christ to be bruised with stones, and so to thrust them back into prison, and afterwards to be beaten with cudgels, and at the last with leaded scourges, until they expired. On the same day at Trier, the deposition of blessed Valerius, bishop, disciple of Saint Peter the apostle. In the territory of Troyes, of Saint Sabinianus, who at the command of the emperor Aurelian was beheaded for the faith of Christ. [Bouillart adds: And the octaves of Saint Vincent, martyr.]

## The third day before the Kalends · 30 January

[0709]

At Antioch, the passion of blessed Ypolitus, martyr, who, somewhat deceived by the schism of Novatus, corrected by the working of the grace of Christ, returned to the charity of the Church, for which [Bouillart adds: and in which] he completed an illustrious martyrdom. At Jerusalem, of the most holy Matthias, bishop, of whom they relate things wondrous and full of faith, that, having suffered many things for Christ, he at the last ended his life in peace. On this same day, the passion of Saint Flavianus, martyr. At the monastery of Malbodium, of Saint Aldegundis, virgin. [In Bouillart: . . . . . Flavianus, martyr. In the territory of Paris, of Baltildis, queen.]

## The day before the Kalends · 31 January

[0713]

At Alexandria, the birthday of blessed Metranus, martyr, when he would not utter impious words at the command of the pagans, they bruised his whole body with cudgels; and piercing his face and eyes with sharp reeds, they drove him out of the city with torments, and there with stones cast out the spirit which still remained in him. Also at Alexandria, of the holy martyrs Saturninus, Thyrsus and Victor. At the city of Trent, of Saint Vigilius, bishop and martyr. [Bouillart omits: At the city of Trent . . . . . martyr.]

## FEBRUARY HAS 28 DAYS.

[0717]

## On the Kalends · 1 February

[0717]

The birthday of blessed Ignatius, bishop and martyr, who ruled the church of Antioch third after Peter the apostle: and in the persecution of Trajan he is sent bound to Rome, where, in the presence of the same, the senate sitting around, he was first afflicted with most monstrous punishments of torments, then he is cast to the lions, and, choked by their teeth, becomes a victim of Christ. At Smyrna, of Saint Pionius, martyr, who after the filth of prison, where he strengthened many of the brethren by his exhortations to the endurance of martyrdom, harassed with many torments, fixed with nails, and placed upon a burning pyre, obtained a blessed end of life for Christ. There suffered with him also fifteen others. On the same day, of blessed Ephrem, deacon of the church of Edessa. In the town *Tricastrinum*, of Saint Paul, bishop, [0719] whose life both shone with virtues, and his precious death is commended by miracles. In Scotia, of Saint Brigid, virgin, whose life shone with miracles.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 2 February

[0721]

The *Ypapanti* of the Lord. At Caesarea, of the most blessed Cornelius, the Centurion, whom blessed Peter baptized. He, raised at the aforesaid city to the episcopal honour, rested. At Rome on the Salarian Way, the passion of Saint Apronianus, who, hearing together with Sisinnius the deacon, while still a gentile, a voice made from heaven, *Come,* saying, *ye blessed of my Father* and the rest; believing, was baptized, and so afterwards in the confession of the Lord received the end of life by a capital sentence. [0723] Also at Rome, of the saints Fortunatus, Felicianus, Firmus, Candidus. At Orléans, of Saint Fuscolus, bishop.

## The third day before the Nones · 3 February

[0725]

In Africa, the birthday of blessed Celerinus, deacon and confessor, and of the holy martyrs Laurentinus, Ignatius, and Celerina: of the glorious praises of all of whom there is extant an epistle of blessed Cyprian. [Bouillart inserts: in the city of Nicaea, the passion of Saint Triphon in the time of the emperor Ducius. *And in the margin:* of blessed Blasius, bishop and martyr.--At Latiniacum, the birthday of Saint Deodatus, bishop and confessor.] At the town of Vapingum, the deposition of the bishops Tigris and Remedius.

## The day before the Nones · 4 February

[0729]

In a city of Egypt which is called Thmuis, the passion of blessed Fileas, bishop, and of Filoromus, tribune, with whom also an innumerable multitude of the faithful from the same city, following the example of their shepherd, was crowned with martyrdom, as the eighth book of the ecclesiastical History tells. At Rome, at Forum Simpronii, of the holy martyrs Aquilinus, Geminus, Gelasius, Magnus, Donatus. At Troyes, of Saint Aventinus, bishop and confessor.

## On the Nones · 5 February

At Sicily, in the city of the Catanians, the birthday of Saint Agatha, virgin and martyr, who, after blows and prison, after the rack and tortures, after the cutting off of her breasts, after rolling upon potsherds and coals, was at length consummated in prison under the judge Quintianus. At Vienne, of blessed Avitus, bishop and confessor, by whose faith, and industry and admirable doctrine, the Gauls were defended from the assault of the Arian heresy.

## The eighth day before the Ides · 6 February

At Caesarea of Cappadocia, the birthday of Saint Dorothea, virgin, who, first harassed on the rack, then long beaten with palms, at the last was punished by beheading. At whose confession a certain Theophilus, a scholastic, was converted, and soon, himself most sharply tortured on the rack, at the last was slain with the sword. At Arverni, of blessed Antholianus, martyr. On the same day, of the holy bishops Vedastus and Amandus, whose life and death alike were glorious with very many miracles. Of whom the former ruled the church of the *Adartenses*, but the latter that of the *Trajectenses*.

## The seventh day before the Ides · 7 February

[0739]

In the Britains, in the city of Augusta, the birthday of blessed Augulus, bishop, who, completing the course of his time through martyrdom, deserved to receive eternal rewards. Also, of Saint Moyses, bishop, This man, first leading a solitary life in the desert, had become magnificently known by signs and virtues, but afterwards converted in great part to the faith of Christ the nation of the Saracens, to which he had been made bishop, and so, glorious in merits, rested in peace.

## The sixth day before the Ides · 8 February

At Armenia the Lesser, the birthday of the saints Dionisius, Emilianus and Sebastian. On the same day at Alexandria, of Saint Cointa, martyr, her the pagans, having seized her and leading her to the idols, were forcing to adore; which when she, execrating them, refused, binding fetters upon her feet, and dragging her through the streets of the whole city, they tore her apart with a horrible punishment. [Brouillart: In the province of Lusitania, in the city of Cordoba, of Saint Salominis, martyr. *And in cursive type:* At Verdun, of Saint Paul, bishop and confessor.] In the city of Cordoba, of Saint Salomon, martyr.

## The fifth day before the Ides · 9 February

[0745]

At Alexandria, the passion of Saint Apollonia, virgin, from whom the persecutors first knocked out all her teeth. Then, when they threatened that they would burn her alive, unless she uttered impious words with them, she, deliberating herself a little something, suddenly burst forth from the hands of the impious, and leapt into the fire which they had prepared. At Suevo near Cyprus, of the saints Ammon and Alexander. [Bouillart adds, *in cursive type:* At Fontenella, of Saint Ansbertus, bishop and confessor.]

## The fourth day before the Ides · 10 February

[0747]

At Rome, the birthday of the holy martyrs Zoticus, Hireneus, Jacinctus, and Amantius. In the East, of Saint Sotheris, virgin, who, beaten most heavily and very long with blows, when she had also overcome the other kinds of punishments, completed her martyrdom by the sword. Also at Rome on the Lavican Way, of ten soldiers. At the Castrum Cassinum, of Saint Scolastica, virgin, sister of Saint Benedict, abbot. In the district of Rouen, of Saint Austreberta, virgin.

## The third day before the Ides · 11 February

[0751]

At Lyon, the birthday of Saint Desiderius, bishop and confessor. On the same day at the fort *Nantonense*, of Saint Severinus, abbot of the monastery of Agaunum, by whose prayers the worshipper of God, King Clovis, was freed from his long infirmity. At Alexandria, the deposition of Saint Eufrasia, virgin, who in the monastery shone by the virtue of abstinence and also by miracles.

## The day before the Ides · 12 February

[0753]

In the Spains, the birthday of Saint Eulalia, virgin, who, it is agreed, in the time of the emperor Diocletian received the glorious crown of martyrdom, in the city of Barcelona. In Africa. of the holy martyrs Saturninus, presbyter, Dativus, Felix, Apelius and his companions, who suffered for Christ under the proconsul Anolinus. At Alexandria, of Modestus and Ammonius, infants.

## On the Ides · 13 February

[0755]

The birthday of Saint Agabus, the prophet, at Antioch, of whom blessed Luke writes in the apostolic Acts. In the city Militana of Armenia, of Saint Polioctus, martyr, who is interpreted in Latin *much praying.* In the city of Angers, of Saint Lizinius, bishop, a man of venerable holiness.
