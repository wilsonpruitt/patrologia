## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 14 February

[0759]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Valentine, presbyter, who, after many marks of healings and of doctrine, was beaten with cudgels, and so beheaded, under Claudius Caesar. Also at Rome, of the holy martyrs Vitalis, Felicula and Zeno. In the city of Interamna, of blessed Valentine, bishop, who, after a long beating, was consigned to custody. when he could not be overcome, cast out of prison in the silence of midnight, he was beheaded. At Alexandria, of the holy martyrs Bassus, Antonius, Protolicus, who were drowned in the sea. Also, of Cyrion, presbyter, Moyses, Bassianus, Agatho, who were all burned with fire. Also of Dionisius and Ammonius, beheaded.

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 15 February

[0763]

At the city of Sebaste, the passion of Saint Blavius, bishop, who, a worker of many miracles, under the governor Agricolanus, after a long beating and hanging upon the wood, where his flesh was torn apart with iron combs, after a most foul prison, and immersion in a lake, whence he came out safe, at the command of the aforesaid judge is beheaded together with two boys. But at the last seven women, who were gathering the drops of his blood as they flowed down, caught because they were Christians, after dire torments were struck with the sword. At Rome, of Saint Craton, martyr, who, instructed in the teachings of the faith by blessed Valentine, not many days after was consummated by martyrdom with his wife and his whole household. In the city of Brixa, of the holy martyrs Faustinus and Jovia, virgin. In the Gauls, in the city *Vasionensis*, of Saint Quinidus, bishop, whose death, precious in the sight of the Lord, frequent miracles testify. The city of Interamna, of Saint Agapis, virgin.

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 16 February

[0767]

The birthday of blessed Honesimus, of whom Saint Paul writes to Philemon: whom the same apostle, ordaining him bishop, and committing to him the word of preaching, left at the city of the Ephesians. He, brought to Rome, and there stoned for the faith of Christ, thence his body was carried to the places where he had been ordained. In the city of Cumae, of Saint Juliana, virgin, who, after various torments, and the custody of prison, openly fought with the devil. Then, overcoming the flames of fires and a boiling pot, she completed her martyrdom by the beheading of her head. At Egypt, of Saint Julian, martyr, with others to the number of five thousand.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 17 February

[0769]

In Persis, in the city of Babylon, the birthday of blessed Polochronius, bishop and martyr, who, in the presence of Decius the persecutor, struck on the mouth with stones, his hands stretched out, lifting his eyes to heaven, sent forth his spirit. At the city of Concordia, the passion of the saints Donatus, Secundianus, Romulus, with eighty-six others. In Scotia, of Saint Fintanus, presbyter and confessor, a man of great virtue. On this same day, of Saint Silvinus, bishop *Taruanensis*. [In Bouillart: . . . a man of great virtue. On this same day, of Saint Silvinus, bishop of the city of Toulouse.]

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 18 February

[0773]

The birthday of blessed Simeon, bishop and martyr, who is known to have been a kinsman of the Saviour according to the flesh, and the son of Cleophas, who was the brother of Joseph. He, ordained bishop of Jerusalem after James the brother of the Lord, in the persecution of Trajan, afflicted with punishments for a long time, was consummated by martyrdom. All who were present, and the judge himself, marvelling that an old man of a hundred and twenty years had endured the punishment of the cross. In the city of Ostia, of the holy martyrs Maximus, Claudius and his wife Prepedigna with two sons, who, since they were of a most illustrious family, were seized at the command of Diocletian, and deported into exile; then, burned in a fire, they rendered to God the sweet-smelling sacrifice of martyrdom.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 19 February

[0777]

At Rome, the birthday of Saint Gabinius, presbyter and martyr, who, long afflicted with chains in custody by Diocletian, purchased the joys of heaven by a precious death. In Africa, of the saints Publius and Julian.

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 20 February

[0779]

At the city of Tyre, of the blessed martyrs, whose number the knowledge of God alone gathers. Who, at the command of Diocletian, were slain by many punishments of torments succeeding one another. First indeed torn with scourges over the whole body, then given to divers kinds of beasts, but guarded from these by divine power, after the added ferocity of fire and of iron, they completed their martyrdom. This glorious multitude Tyrannio and Silvanus urged on to victory, and also Peleus and Linus, bishops, Zenobius too, presbyter, who by a happy contest obtained, together with them, the palm of martyrdom. In the island of Cyprus, of the saints Potamius, and Nemesius. At Alemannia, of Saint Gall, presbyter and confessor.

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 21 February

[0781]

At Sicily, of the holy martyrs seventy-nine, who under Diocletian, through divers torments, deserved to receive the crown of their confession prepared for them by Christ. In Africa, in the city of Adrumetum, of the saints Verolus, secundinus, Servulus and twenty others. [Bouillart, at the edge of the page: At the monastery of Saint Germanus, the consecration of the altar in honour of the blessed martyrs John and Paul and of Saint Blasius, and also of the holy confessors Benedict, Lupus and Egidius. Also the consecration of the altar in honour of the Holy Innocents and of the holy virgins Columba, Margaret, Catherine.]

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 22 February

[0785]

At Antioch, of the Chair [Bouillart: *Cathedra*] of Saint Peter. On the same day, of blessed Papias, bishop of the city of Hierapolis, who was a hearer of John the apostle, but a fellow disciple and companion of Polycarpus. Also, of Saint Aristion, who was one of the seventy-two disciples of Christ. At Alexandria, of Saint Abilius, bishop, who, second bishop after blessed Mark, conspicuous in virtue, ministered the priesthood thirteen years, [In Bouillart, at the edge of the page: On the same day the consecration of the altar in honour of the blessed martyrs Eustachius and his companions, and of Saint Medard, confessor of Christ.]

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 23 February

[0787]

At Syrmium, of blessed Sinerus, monk and martyr, who, seized at the command of the emperor Maximian, when he confessed that he was a Christian, was beheaded. Also the birthday of sixty-two, who, completing the contest of martyrdom in the aforesaid city, received the abiding kingdoms. On this same day, of Saint Policarpus, presbyter and confessor, who with blessed Sebastian converted very many to the faith of Christ, and by exhorting led them to the glory of martyrdom.

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 24 February

[0791]

The birthday of blessed Matthias the apostle, who after the ascension of the Lord, chosen by lot by the apostles, at Judaea the Gospel of Christ, preached. Also, the finding of the head of the Forerunner of the Lord, in the time of the emperor Marcian, when the same Forerunner first revealed to two monks where that same head of his lay hidden. In Caesarea of Cappadocia, of Saint Sergius, martyr, whose most illustrious acts are extant.

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 25 February

[0793]

At Egypt, the birthday of the saints Victorinus, Victor, Nicoforus, Claudianus, Dioscorus, Serapion and Papias under the emperor Numerian: of whom the first, second and third steadfastly enduring exquisite kinds of punishments for the confession of the Godhead, the remaining four, Claudianus and Dioscorus, burned with flames. Serapion [sic: *Serapionis*] and Papias were slain with the sword. All of them therefore dedicated a celebrated martyrdom.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 26 February

[0797]

In the city of Perge of Pamphylia, the birthday of blessed Nestor, bishop, who in the persecution of Decius, when he was intent upon prayer day and night, asking that the flock of Christ might be guarded, was seized, and, having confessed the name of the Lord with wonderful freedom and eagerness, was most cruelly tortured on the rack. Thence by hanging on the cross he passed away a victor. At Alexandria, of Saint Alexander, bishop, a glorious old man. He, after blessed Peter, cast out of the church Arius, his presbyter, depraved by heretical impiety and convicted by divine truth, and afterwards, among the three hundred and eighteen Fathers, condemned the same also in the council of Nicaea.

## The third day before the Kalends · 27 February

[0799]

At Alexandria, the passion of Saint Julian, martyr. He, since he was so bound by gout that he could neither walk nor stand, together with Eunus his servant, is presented to the judge in a chair; who, set upon camels, are ordered to be led about through the whole city, and to be torn with scourges on this side and that, the people looking on, until they met their end in the very beatings. In the Spains, in the city of Seville, the birthday of Saint Leander, bishop and confessor, by whose preaching and industry the whole nation of the Goths was converted from the Arian impiety through King Recharedus. At Lyon, of Saint Baldomeris, a man of God, whose tomb is made illustrious by frequent miracles.

## The day before the Kalends · 28 February

[0803]

In the territory of Lyon, in the Jura places, the deposition of blessed Romanus, abbot, who first led the hermit's life there, and, renowned for many virtues and miracles, afterwards became the father of very many monks. On the same day, of the saints Macharius and Rufinus.

## MARCH HAS 31 DAYS.

[0805]

## On the Kalends · 1 March

[0805]

At Rome, of the holy martyrs two hundred and sixty, whom Claudius first ordered, condemned for the name of Christ, to dig sand. Then he commanded that they be sent outside the walls of the Salarian gate, and be killed in the amphitheatre of the same city by the arrows of soldiers. On the same day, of Saint Donatus, martyr, who suffered at Carthage under the duke Ursacius and Marcellinus the tribune. At Angers, of Saint Albanus [Bouillart: *Albini*], bishop and confessor, a man of most illustrious virtue and holiness. In the city of Marseille, of the saints Hermes and Adrianus.

## The sixth day before the Nones · 2 March

[0809]

At Rome on the Latin Way, of the holy martyrs Jovinus and Basileus, who suffered under the emperors Gallienus and Valerian. Also at Rome, of very many martyrs, whom, long tortured, the emperor Alexander at the last condemned by capital sentence.
