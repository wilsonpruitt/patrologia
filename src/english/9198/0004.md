## The fifth day before the Nones · 3 March

[0811]

At Caesarea of Palestine, of the holy martyrs Marinus, soldier, and Asterius, senator, under the persecution of Valerian; of whom the former, when he had been accused by his fellow soldiers of being a Christian, and he testified with a most clear voice that he was a Christian, received the crown of martyrdom by the cutting off of his head. And when the latter received upon his shoulders set beneath it, and upon the garment he was wearing spread under it, the martyr's beheaded body, the honour which he paid to the martyr he himself at once received as a martyr. On the same day, the birthday of the saints Emetherius and Celedonius, who, at first soldiers at the city *Legionensis* of Gallicia, when the storm of persecution arose, afflicted with very many torments for the confession of the name of Christ, were led as far as Calagurris, and there were crowned with martyrdom.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 4 March

[0815]

At Rome on the Appian Way, the birthday of blessed Lucius, pope and martyr, who, in the persecution of Valerian and Gallienus, banished into exile for the faith of Christ, and afterwards by the divine will permitted to return to his church, completed his martyrdom by the cutting off of his head. Also at Rome on the Appian Way, of the holy martyrs nine hundred, who are laid in the cemetery at Saint Cecilia. On the same day, of Saint Caius, of the palace, drowned in the sea.

## The third day before the Nones · 5 March

[0817]

At Antioch, the birthday of blessed Focas, martyr, who, after the many injuries which he suffered for the name of the Redeemer, how he triumphed over that ancient serpent is declared to the peoples even today. On this same day, the passion of Saint Eusebius, of the palace, and of nine other martyrs.

## The day before the Nones · 6 March

[0819]

At Nicomedia, the birthday of the saints Victor and Victorinus, who for three years, with Claudianus and Bassa his wife, afflicted with many torments and thrust back into prison, there fulfilled the course of their life. In the city of Toledo, the deposition of Julian, bishop, who is held most famous among the inhabitants of the same place.

## On the Nones · 7 March

[0821]

In Mauritania, in the city of the Tuburbitani, the birthday of the holy martyrs Perpetua and Felicitas, and with them of Revocatus, Saturninus and Secundolus, of whom the last rested in prison, all the rest were given to the beasts under the emperor Severus.

## The eighth day before the Ides · 8 March

[0825]

In the city of Antinoum, the birthday of the saints Philemon and Apollonius, deacon, who, seized and heard by the judge, when they had steadfastly refused to sacrifice to idols, their heels pierced, were dragged horribly through the city, and at the last, slain with the sword, completed their martyrdom. On the same day at the aforesaid city, the passion of Arrianus, Theoticus, and three others; whom the judge killed, sunk in the sea, but by the service of a dolphin their bodies were restored to the shore. At Carthage, of Saint Pontius, deacon of blessed Cyprian, bishop, who, bearing exile with him up to the day of his passion, left an excellent volume of his life and passion, and, renowned in sufferings, merited the crown of life.

## The seventh day before the Ides · 9 March

[0827]

At the city of Nisena the deposition of Saint Gregory, bishop, brother of blessed Basil of Caesarea as most illustrious in life as in eloquence. In the city of Barcelona, of Saint Patianus, bishop who, in the time of the emperor Theodosius, in an excellent old age obtained the end of his life.

## The sixth day before the Ides · 10 March

[0829]

At Apamia, the birthday of the saints Alexander and Gaius, martyrs, who, as Apollinaris, bishop of Hierapolis, writes in the book *Against the Catafrigae*, in the persecution of Antoninus Verus were crowned with a glorious martyrdom In Persis, of the holy martyrs to the number of forty-two. At Paris, the deposition of Saint Droctoveus, abbot disciple of blessed Germanus, bishop. [Bouillart adds: On this same day, of Saint Attalus, abbot, disciple of Saint Columbanus.]

## The fifth day before the Ides · 11 March

[0833]

At Sebaste of Lesser Armenia, the birthday of the forty holy soldiers, who, in the time of King Licinius, after chains and very frequent imprisonments, after their faces were beaten with stones, were sent into a pool, and so at length completed their martyrdom by the breaking of their legs. And the more noble among them were Quirion and Candidus.

## The fourth day before the Ides · 12 March

[0835]

At Rome, of the blessed pontiffs Gregory, doctor, and apostle of the English, and Innocent. At Nicomedia, the passion of Saint Peter, martyr. He, since he was chamberlain of the emperor Diocletian, and complained too freely of the immense punishments of the martyrs, at the command of the same is brought out into the midst, and first, hung up, he is tortured very long with scourges, then, drenched with vinegar and salt, at the last he is roasted on a gridiron, and so he stood forth heir both of the faith and of the name of the true Peter. Also at Nicomedia, of the saints Egdunus, presbyter, and seven others, who were suffocated day by day, that fear might be struck into the rest.

## The third day before the Ides · 13 March

[0839]

[0839]At Nicomedia, the birthday of the holy martyrs Macedonius, presbyter, Patricia and Modesta. In the city of Nicaea, of the holy martyrs Theuseta, Horris, Theodora, Nimpodora, Mark, Arabia, who were all given over to the fire. In the Thebaid, the deposition of Saint Eufrasia, virgin.

## The day before the Ides · 14 March

[0841]

At Rome, the passion of the saints forty-seven. These were baptized by blessed Peter the apostle, when he was held in the custody of the Mamertine, with his fellow apostle Paul, where they were detained nine months; all of whom, under a most devoted confession of the faith, were consumed by the Neronian sword. In Africa, of Saint Peter, martyr. Also of Saint Eufrosius.

## On the Ides · 15 March

[0843]

In Caesarea of Cappadocia, the passion of Saint Longinus, who pierced the side of the Lord with a lance, as is found in his acts. At the city of Thessalonica, the birthday of Saint Matrona, martyr, who, caught and afflicted in many ways, at the last beaten to death with stout cudgels, in the confession of Christ rendered her spirit uncorrupted to God.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 16 March

[0847]

At Aquileia, the birthday of blessed Hilary, bishop, and Tatianus, deacon, who under the governor Beronius, after the rack and other torments, together with Felix, Largus, and Dionysius, ended their martyrdom. At Rome, the passion of Saint Cyriacus, deacon, who after a long wasting in prison, drenched with melted pitch and stretched upon the scaffold, and dragged, beaten also with thongs and cudgels, at the last, with Largus and Smaragdus and twenty others, at the command of Maximinus, was beheaded. Their feast, however, is kept on the sixth day before the Ides of August, on which day their bodies were raised by blessed Marcellus, pope, and reverently entombed. At Arverni, the deposition of Saint Patrick, bishop and confessor.

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 17 March

[0849]

In Scotia, the birthday of Saint Patrick, bishop and confessor, who first preached Christ there. [0851] On the same day, at the monastery of Nivigella, of Saint Gertrude, virgin. [In Bouillart . . . . : preached. In the city of Nicomedia, of the holy martyrs Eugenius, Pamphilianus, Castor and Serenus.]

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 18 March

[0853]

The birthday of blessed Alexander, bishop, who, coming from Cappadocia, from his own city, to Jerusalem with a holy desire, by divine revelation thereupon took up the government of that place, and, after a venerable old age, led to Caesarea, in the persecution of Decius completed his martyrdom for the confession of Christ. [Bouillart adds: At the monastery of Nivigella, of Saint Geretrudis, virgin.]

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 19 March

[0855D]

The birthday of blessed John, a man of great holiness, who, a native of Syria, came to Italy: and there, having built a monastery at the city *Penarensis*, being for forty years the father of many servants of God, renowned for many virtues he rested in peace. On the same day at Surrentum, of the saints Quintus, Quintillus, Quartilla, Mark, with nine others. In the city of Brixa, of Saint Calocerus, martyr.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 20 March

[0859]

In Asia, the birthday of Saint Archippus, fellow soldier of blessed Paul, of whom the same Apostle makes mention in his epistle, writing to the Colossians. At Britain, the deposition of Saint Cuthbert, who from an anchorite became bishop of the church of Lindisfarne, and led his whole life, from infancy to old age, renowned for the signs of miracles. In Syria, of the saints Paul, Cyril, Eugenius with four others. On this same day, of Saint Vulfrannus, confessor.

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 21 March

[0861]

At the fortress of Cassinum, the birthday of Saint Benedict, abbot, the life of this man, glorious in virtues and miracles, blessed Pope Gregory writes. At Alexandria, of blessed Serapion, anchorite, a man of great virtues. In the territory of Lyon, of Saint Lupicinus, abbot, whose life was illustrious for holiness, and for the glory of miracles.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 22 March

[0863]

At Septimania, in the city of Biterris, the deposition of Saint Afrodisius, bishop and confessor. He, ordained bishop for the same city by blessed Paul, [0865] bishop of Narbonne, distinguished in the teachings of the faith and adorned with the merits of virtues, rested in peace.

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 23 March

[0867]

In Africa, of the holy martyrs Victorianus, Frumentius, and another Frumentius, and two brothers, who, in the Vandal persecution, as Victor, an African bishop, writes, under King Huneric, for the constancy of their catholic confession, tortured with most savage punishments, were crowned with an excellent martyrdom. At Antioch, of Saint Theodorus, presbyter. In the city of Caesarea, of Saint Julian.

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 24 March

[0869]

At Rome, the passion of blessed Pigmenius, presbyter. He nourished Julian the apostate from a boy and instructed him in sacred letters, by whom he himself was afterwards put to death in the Tiber for the faith of Christ. In Mauritania, the birthday of the saints Romulus and Secundolus, brothers, who were born for Christ. At Syria, of Saint Seleucus. Also in Phrygia, of Saint Agapitus.

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 25 March

[0871]

At Nazareth, a city of Galilee, the Annunciation of the Lord. At Sirmium, the passion of blessed Hireneus, bishop, in the time of the emperor Maximian, who, first vexed with most bitter torments, then tortured in prison for very many days, at the last was consummated by the cutting off of his head. At Rome, of Saint Cyrinus, martyr. who, after the loss of his property, after the filth of prison, after the affliction of many blows, was slain with the sword by King Claudius. At Nicomedia, the birthday of Dula, the handmaid of a certain soldier, who was slain for her chastity. In the island of Antrum of Saint Hermelandus, abbot, whose glorious manner of life is praised by the proclamation of miracles.
