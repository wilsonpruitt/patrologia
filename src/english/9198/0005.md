## The seventh day before the Kalends · 26 March

[0875]

At Pentapolis of Libya, the birthday of the saints Theodorus, bishop, Hireneus, deacon, Serapion and Ammonius, readers. At Rome on the Lavican Way, of Saint Castolus, martyr, who, as is read in the acts of blessed Sebastian, hung up by the persecutors three times, heard three times, persevering in the confession of the Lord, was sent into a pit, and, a mass of sand being let down upon him, passed to Christ by martyrdom. At Sirmium, of the saints Montanus, presbyter, and Maxima, drowned in the sea.

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 27 March

[0879]

In Egypt, the deposition of blessed John, hermit, who, among the other marks of virtues, full also of the prophetic spirit, foretold to the most Christian emperor Theodosius his victories over the tyrant. In Pannonia, of Saint Alexander, martyr. [Bouillart adds: On this same day, of Saint Eucherius, bishop of Tours.]

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 28 March

[0881]

In Caesarea of Palestine, of the holy martyrs Priscus, Malchus, Alexander, who in the persecution of Valerian, kindled by the divine heat of faith, going of their own accord to the judge, rebuke him for his savagery against the blood of the godly; whom he at once gave to the beasts to be devoured for the name of Christ. At the city of the Cabilonenses, the deposition of Saint Guntramnus, king, who so gave himself over to spiritual works that, leaving behind the pomps of the world, he paid out his treasures to churches and to the poor. At Tarsus of Cilicia, of Castor and Dorotheus.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 29 March

[0883]

At Africa, the birthday of the holy confessors Armogastus, Archinimus and Satyrus, who, in the time of the Vandal persecution, under King Geisericus, having endured many and grievous punishments and reproaches for the confession of the truth, [0885] fulfilled the course of a glorious contest. On this same day, the deposition of Eustasius, abbot of the monastery *Luxoviensis*, who was the Father of almost six hundred monks, and, conspicuous for holiness of life, shone also with miracles. [Bouillart, after the African martyrs: In the city of Nicomedia, the passion of the saints Pastor and Victorinus.]

## The third day before the Kalends · 30 March

[0887]

At Rome on the Appian Way, the passion of blessed Quirinus, tribune and martyr, who under the emperor Aurelian, after the cutting out of his tongue and hanging on the rack, and the cutting off of his hands and feet, consummated the contest of martyrdom by the sword. At Thessalonica, the birthday of the saints Domninus, and Victor. [Bouillart: At Thessalonica, the birthday of the saints Domninus, Filopolus and Achaicus, *omitting what follows.*] At the fortress of the Silvanectenses, the deposition of Saint Regulus, bishop and confessor.

## The day before the Kalends · 31 March

[0891]

Of Amos the prophet, whom Ozias, king of Israel, killed, pierced through the temples with a bar, and afterwards, half alive, he was carried to his homeland and there was buried. At Rome, of Saint Balbina, virgin, daughter of Quirinus the martyr, who, after the course of this world was overcome, is buried on the Appian Way beside her father. In Africa, of the saints Diodolus and Anesius.

## APRIL HAS 30 DAYS.

[0893]

## On the Kalends · 1 April

[0893]

At Rome, the passion of most blessed Theodora, sister of the most illustrious martyr Hermes. She, martyred under the emperor Aurelian, was buried beside her brother on the Salarian Way, not far from the city of Rome. On the same day, of Saint venantius, bishop and martyr. In Egypt, of the saints Victor and Stephen.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 2 April

[0897]

At Caesarea of Cappadocia, the passion of Saint Theodosia, virgin, who, in the time of Diocletian, joining herself of her own accord to the confessors in custody, was seized and nonetheless tortured on the rack, by the power of God afterwards rescued from chains, waters, beasts, at the last completed her martyrdom by the cutting off of her head. On the same day, the birthday of blessed Nicetius, bishop of Lyon, whose life shone with miracles, and whose precious death is nonetheless commended by miracles. At Palestine, of Saint Mary the Egyptian, who is called the sinner. [Bouillart adds: Also of Saint Eustasius, abbot of the monastery *Luxoviensis*.]

## The third day before the Nones · 3 April

[0901]

In Scithia, in the city of Thomis, the birthday of the saints Evagrius and Benignus. At Thessalonica, the passion of the holy virgins Agapes and Chionia under Diocletian, who, first wasted in prison, afterwards sent into the fire, but untouched by the flames, after prayer poured out to the Lord, gave up their souls. At Tauromenium of Sicily, of blessed Pancratius.

## The day before the Nones · 4 April

[0903]

At Milan, the deposition of blessed Ambrose, bishop and confessor, by whose zeal, among the other marks of doctrine and of miracles, in the time of the Arian perfidy, the whole of Italy was converted to the catholic faith. At Seville, the deposition of Saint Isidore, prelate.

## On the Nones · 5 April

[0905]

At Egypt, the birthday of the holy martyrs Martiana, Nicanor, and Apollonius. At Thessalonica, of Saint Hirenis, virgin, who, after enduring prison, was struck with an arrow by Sisinnius the count, under whom her sisters also, Agapes and Chionia, were martyred together. At Caesarea of Licia, of Saint Amphianus.

## The eighth day before the Ides · 6 April

[0909]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Sixtus, pope and martyr, who in the times of Hadrian, that he might gain Christ for himself, willingly endured temporal death. In Macedonia, of the saints Timotheus and Diogenes.

## The seventh day before the Ides · 7 April

[0911]

At Africa, the birthday of the saints Epiphanius, bishop, Donatus and thirteen others. On the same day, of Egesippus, a most holy man, who, near to the times of the Apostles, wove together all the histories of ecclesiastical deeds from the passion of the Lord up to his own age, so that he might express also the manner of speaking of those whose life he followed. At Alexandria, of Saint Pelusius, presbyter.

## The sixth day before the Ides · 8 April

[0915]

At Corinth, of blessed Dionisius, bishop, by whose learning and grace, which he had in the word of God, many profit even to this day. And this chosen one of Christ likewise shone in the times of Marcus Antoninus and Lucius Aurelius. At Tours, of Saint Perpetuus, bishop, a man of admirable holiness.

## The fifth day before the Ides · 9 April

[0917]

The birthday of blessed Procorus, who was one of the first seven deacons; he, renowned in faith and miracles, was consummated by martyrdom at Antioch. At Sirmium, the passion of the seven holy virgins, who, paying together the price of their blood, purchased eternal life.

## The fourth day before the Ides · 10 April

[0919]

Of Ezekiel the prophet, who, slain by the judge of the people of Israel at Babylonia, was buried in the tomb of Sem and Arfaxad. At Rome, of very many blessed martyrs, whom Saint Alexander, pope, baptized when he was held in prison. All these the emperor Aurelian caused to be put on board an old ship, to be led out into the deep sea, and there, stones being tied to their necks, to be sunk in the depth of the sea. At Alexandria, of Saint Appolonius, presbyter, and five others.

## The third day before the Ides · 11 April

[0921]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Leo, pope, in whose times there was the holy synod of Chalcedon. At Crete, in the city of Gortina, of Saint Philip, bishop, who was endowed with great virtues and the best studies. And he flourished [0923] in the times of the emperors Antoninus Verus and Lucius Aurelius. In Dalmatia, in the city of Salona, of Saint Domio, bishop, with eight soldiers. At Nicomedia, of Saint Eustorgius, presbyter.

## The day before the Ides · 12 April

[0925]

At Rome on the Aurelian Way, the birthday of blessed Julius, pope, who, having endured tribulations and exiles for ten months under the emperor Constantius, after his death returned with great glory to his see, and rested in peace. [Bouillart inserts: On the same day, of Saint Zeno, bishop, who amid the storms of persecution wonderfully ruled the city of Verona, and in the time of Gallienus was crowned with martyrdom.] At Vapingum, the deposition of Constantinus, bishop.

## On the Ides · 13 April

[0929]

At Pergamum, a city of Asia, the birthday of the saints Carpus, bishop. Papirius, deacon, and Agathonica, an excellent woman, and of many other women, who for their blessed confessions were crowned with martyrdom under Antoninus Verus and Aurelius Commodus. With whom also the wonderful man Justin the philosopher, labouring very much in writing for the religion of Christ, received the gift of martyrdom as the reward of a faithful tongue. In Spain, of Saint Erminigildus, who, for the confession of the catholic faith, struck on the head with an axe, entered the heavenly kingdom in place of the earthly [sic: *terterreno*], a king and martyr. [Bouillart, at the edge of the page: On the same day at the monastery of Saint Germanus, the consecration of the altar in honour of the holy and undivided Trinity and of the life-giving cross, and also of the most blessed Mother of God, Mary, and of Saint Guandregisilus, and also of all the saints.]

## The eighteenth day before the Kalends · 14 April

[0933]

At Rome on the Appian Way, the birthday of the most blessed martyrs Tiburtius, Valerian and Maximus, Almachius being prefect, of whom the first were beaten with cudgels, struck with the sword, the last was beaten with leaded scourges so long, until he breathed out his spirit. At Alexandria, of Saint Fronto, abbot, whose life stood forth glorious in holiness and miracles. At Interamna, of blessed Proculus, martyr. Also, of Saint Domnina, virgin, crowned with her companion virgins.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 15 April

[0935]

At Persis, in the city of Cordula, the birthday of the saints Olympias, and Maximus, nobles, who at the command of Decius were beaten with cudgels, and then with leaded scourges, at the last their heads were beaten with axes, until they gave up their spirit. In Italy, of the blessed martyrs Maro, Euticetis and Victorinus, who, first leading a long exile at the island Pontia in the confession of Christ, afterwards under the emperor Nerva, when they had converted very many to the faith, were ordered to be slain by various punishments.

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 16 April

[0937]

At Corinth, the birthday of the saints Callistus and Carisius, with seven others, all drowned in the sea. At Caesaraugusta, of the eighteen holy martyrs Quintilianus, Cassianus, Matutinus, Publius, Urbanus, Martialis, [0939] Faustus, Successus, Felix, Januarius, Primitivus, Evotus, Cecilianus, Optatus, Fronto, Lupercus, Apodemus, and Julius. These all together, under Datianus, governor of the Spains, were afflicted with punishments and slain.

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 17 April

[0941]

At Africa, the birthday of Saint Mappalicus, martyr, who, as blessed Cyprian writes in the epistle to the martyrs and confessors, was crowned with very many others. At Antioch, of the holy martyrs Peter, deacon, and Hermogenes. [Bouillart inserts: In the East, of blessed Nicephorus, crowned under Valerian and Gallienus for the martyrdom of Christ.] Also at Cordoba, of the holy martyrs Helias, presbyter, Paul and Isidore, monks.
