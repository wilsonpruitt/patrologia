## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 18 April

[0945]

At Messana, a city of Apulia, the birthday of the holy martyrs Eleutherius, bishop, and Anthia his mother, who, since he was illustrious both for holiness of life and for the power of miracles, under the emperor Hadrian, overcoming a fiery iron bed, a gridiron and a frying pan boiling with oil, pitch, and resin, thrown also to lions, but unharmed by them, at the last has his throat cut together with his mother. At Rome, of blessed Apollonius, senator, who under the emperor Commodus, betrayed by a slave as being a Christian, composed a notable volume, which he read in the senate, and then by sentence of the senate was beheaded for Christ. At Cordoba, of Saint Perfectus, presbyter and martyr.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 19 April

[0947]

[0947]Of blessed Thimo, deacon, of the first seven, who first settled as a teacher at Beroea, and then, sowing the word of the Lord, came to Corinth, and there by Jews and Greeks, as is handed down, was first cast into flames, but was harmed in nothing, then, fastened to a cross, fulfilled his martyrdom. In Armenia, in the city of Militana, the birthday of the holy martyrs Hermogenes, Gaius, expeditus, Aristonicus, Rufus, Galatae, crowned on one day. In the city of Caucoliberi, the passion of Saint Vincent, martyr. At the monastery of Laubiae, of Saint Ursmarus, bishop and confessor. [Bouillart: . . . Vincent, martyr. In the district *Hainoensis*, of Saint Orsmarus, bishop and confessor. *And in cursive type:* At the monastery of Saint Germanus, the dedication of the church in honour of Saint Simphorianus, martyr, and of the most blessed Nicolaus, confessor.]

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 20 April

[0951]

At Rome, the birthday of Saint Victor, pope, who, fifteenth after blessed Peter, ruled the Church ten years, and under the emperor Severus is crowned with martyrdom. Also at Rome, of the holy martyrs Sulpitius and Servilianus, who, converted to the faith of Christ by the preaching and miracles of Domitilla, virgin, when they would not sacrifice to idols, were beheaded by Anianus, prefect of the City. In the Gauls, in the city of Ebredunum, of Saint Marcellus, bishop and confessor of the same city, who, by divine command, coming from Africa with his holy companions Vincent and Domninus, converted to the faith of Christ the greatest part of the Maritime Alps, by the word and by admirable signs, with which he shines even to this day. [Bouillart adds, in cursive type: On the same day, at the monastery of Saint Germanus, the revelation of the body of Saint George, monk of Bethlehem and martyr, and of Saint Venantius, abbot.]

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 21 April

[0955]

At Persis, the birthday of blessed Simeon, bishop and martyr, who, at the command of Sapor, king of the Persians, seized and loaded with iron, when he testified of the Lord with a free and most constant voice, first wasted in a prison dungeon with a hundred others, of whom some were bishops, some presbyters, some clerics of divers orders, at the last was ordered to be beheaded together with them all. There suffered with him also certain most illustrious men, Ustazadis, Abdella, Ananias and Pusitius, with a daughter, who was a holy virgin. At Alexandria, of the saints Arator, presbyter, Fortunus, Felix, Silvius, Vitalis, who rested in prison.

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 22 April

[0957]

At Rome on the Appian Way, the birthday of blessed Gaius, pope, who, when he had ruled the Church eleven years, four months, twelve days, was crowned with martyrdom under the emperor Diocletian. At Persis, of the holy martyrs who had their throats cut with the sword for the name of Christ under King Sapor. In which contest of the faith there suffered Melisius, bishop, Acepsimas also, bishop, with James, presbyter. Mareas and Bicor, no less bishops, with clerics about two hundred and fifty, with monks also and very many consecrated virgins. Among whom also the sister of Simeon the bishop, Tarbua by name, with her maidservant, was cut through with a saw. In the city of Cordula, the birthday [0959] of Parmenius, Helimenae and Chrisotelus, presbyters, Luke and Mucius, deacons, the triumph of whose martyrdom is contained in the passion of blessed Lawrence. At Lyon, of Saint Epipodius, who, in the persecution of Antoninus Verus, after grievous torments inflicted upon him, completed his martyrdom by the cutting off of his head. [Bouillart adds: At Senones, of Saint Leo, bishop. *And in cursive type:* On the same day, the finding of the holy martyrs Dionysius, Rusticus and Eleutherius.]

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 23 April

[0963]

In Persis, in the city of Diospolis, the passion of Saint George, martyr, the acts of whose passion, although they are numbered among the apocryphal writings, yet the Church of God reverently honours his most illustrious martyrdom among the crowns of the martyrs. In the Gauls, in the city of Valentia, the birthday of the saints Felix, presbyter, Fortunatus and Achilleus, deacons, who, when they had converted the greatest part of the aforesaid city to the faith of Christ, were thrust into prison by the duke Cornelius, then beaten very long, and, their legs broken, bound about the whirling of wheels, having endured smoke also in their hanging on the rack, at the last were consummated by the sword. [Bouillart adds: At the fortress of Silvaenectis, of Saint Regulus, bishop and confessor.]

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 24 April

[0967]

At Lyon of Gaul, the birthday of Saint Alexander, martyr, who, in the persecution of Antoninus Verus, after the custody of prison, was first so torn by the cruelty of those beating him that, the framework of his ribs loosened, his bowels laid open, the inward parts of his body were exposed. Then, fastened to the gibbet of the cross, breathless, he sent forth his blessed spirit. There suffered with him also others to the number of thirty-four. On the same day, the deposition of Saint Mellitus, bishop, in Britain. Also in the city of Heliberri, of Saint Gregory, bishop and confessor.

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 25 April

[0969]

At Rome, the Greater Litany at Saint Peter. At Alexandria, the birthday of blessed Mark the evangelist. He, disciple and interpreter of the apostle Peter, being asked at Rome by the brethren, wrote the Gospel, which having taken up, he went to Egypt, and, first at Alexandria proclaiming Christ, founded a Church, and afterwards, seized for the faith of Christ and grievously afflicted, first strengthened by an angelic visitation, then, the Lord himself appearing to him, he was called to the heavenly kingdoms. And he died in the eighth year of Nero. In the city of Syracuse, of the saints Evodius and Hermogenes [Bouillart, at the edge of the page: On this same day, at Paris, the translation of the body of blessed Droctoveus, abbot, disciple of the most blessed Germanus, confessor and pontiff.]

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 26 April

[0973]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Anacletus, pope who, second after the apostle Peter, when he had ruled the Church twelve years, was crowned with martyrdom in the persecution of Domitian. Also at Rome, of Saint Marcellinus, pontiff, who, when he had ruled the Church nine years and four months, was beheaded by Diocletian for the faith of Christ, with Claudius, Cirinus and Antoninus. At which time there was a great persecution, so that within one month seventeen thousand were crowned. At the monastery of Centula [Bouillart: in the district of Pontivus], of Saint Richarius, presbyter and confessor.

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 27 April

[0977]

At Rome, the deposition of blessed Anastasius, pope, of whom Saint Jerome testifies in venerable speech that the world was unworthy to enjoy his life for long. At Nicomedia, the birthday of Saint Anthimus, bishop and martyr, who, in the persecution of Diocletian, for the confession of Christ, as is described in the ecclesiastical history, received the glory of martyrdom by the cutting off of his head. There followed him also almost the whole multitude of his flock, by the same way of martyrdom, of whom the judge caused some to be cut down with the sword, some to be burned up with fires, some to be put in small boats and sunk in the sea. At Tarsus of Cilicia, of Saint Castor.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 28 April

[0979]

At Ravenna, the birthday of Saint Vitalis, martyr, who, when he had buried with honour the body of blessed Ursicinus, taken up, seized by Paulinus the consular, after the torments of the rack, was ordered to be put down into a deep pit, and to be crushed with earth and stones, and by such a martyrdom passed to Christ. At Alexandria, of Saint Theodora, virgin, who, scorning to sacrifice, when she had been handed over into a brothel, suddenly a certain one of the brethren, Didimus by name, by the wonderful favour of God, snatched her away, and afterwards was struck down together with her, and crowned with her. On the same day, of Afrodisius, Carilippus, Agapius and Eusebius, martyrs. In Pannonia, of Saint Pollio, martyr.

## The third day before the Kalends · 29 April

[0983]

At Paphos, of Saint Tychicus, deacon, disciple of blessed Paul. Of him the same Apostle makes mention in his epistles, declaring him to be his dearest brother and fellow servant in the Lord. In Numidia, at the colony *Cirtensis*, the birthday of the holy martyrs Agapius and Secundinus, bishops, who, in the persecution of Valerian, after a long exile, at the aforesaid city, in which the rage of the gentiles was then most eagerly gaping to try the faith of the just, from illustrious priesthood were made glorious martyrs. There suffered in their company Emilianus, a soldier, Tertulla and Antonia, holy virgins, and a certain woman with her twins.

## The day before the Kalends · 30 April

[0987]

At the city *Lambesitana*, the birthday of the holy martyrs Marianus, reader, and James, deacon, of whom the former, when he had long before overcome the assault of the Decian persecution in the confession of Christ, again seized with his dearest colleague. Then, after dire and exquisite punishments, wonderfully strengthened and relieved a second time by divine revelations, at the last they were consummated by the sword with many others. In the city of Sanctonas, of blessed Euprobus, martyr, whom Saint Clement, consecrated with the grace of the pontifical order, sent [sic: *dixerit*] into the Gauls, and, his preaching accomplished, for the testimony of Christ, his head dashed, he fell a victor.
