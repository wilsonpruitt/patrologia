## Text cruces

- **0811 day head** — plate n420 prints the numeral after *Die.* as a flat-topped italic 3 with no stem (stint A's P6 shape
  rule). *Die 3*, as the twin. "The fifth day before the Nones · 3 March".
- **0815 *ad sanctam Gæciliam*** — plate n422 prints a clear G (single letter, single witness;
  not patched). English "Cecilia".
- **0827** — plate n428 *Basilli* (for *Basilii*; single witness, not patched), and three commas the
  twin supplies and the plate lacks (B1–B3). The English drops them at those points: "…brother of
  blessed Basil of Caesarea as most illustrious in life as in eloquence", "…Patianus, bishop who…".
  *tam vita quam eloquentia clarissimi* may belong to Gregory or to Basil; the genitive leaves it
  open and so does the English.
- **0829** — plate n429 *Apaniam* (for *Apamiam*; single witness, not patched), English "Apamia".
  The plate has **no full stop** before *In Perside* (B4): rendered "…were crowned with a glorious
  martyrdom In Persis, of the holy martyrs…", the capital kept. No comma after *abbatis* (B5):
  "abbot disciple of blessed Germanus". *in libro adversum Catafrigas*: rendered as a title, *Against
  the Catafrigae*.
- **0833 *tempore Licinii regis*** — Licinius called *rex*: "King Licinius", as printed.
- **0839 `[0839][0839]`** — a doubled anchor in the twin, the second fused to *Apud*. Carried
  verbatim (`[0839]At Nicomedia`); Migne's layout, reported only here.
- **0847 *reliquata pice*** — printed so (n438) and in the twin. Solier's VARIANTES on the page:
  *Reliquata passim scribunt codices*, so it is the codex tradition's word, not a slip of type.
  Rendered "melted pitch" (*re-liquo*, to melt); Pattern 9, no marker.
- **0855D *constructo constructo*** — the plate (n442) prints the participle ONCE. The doubling is
  the digitization's (B6); English "having built a monastery", no `[d:]`. Plate *virtudibus* for
  *virtutibus* (single witness, not patched), English "virtues".
- **0869 *qui pro Christo nati sunt*** — plate n449 against the twin's *passi*; an OCR candidate
  confirmed. Rendered "who were born for Christ" (B8). It construes and suits the martyrology's
  *dies natalis* idiom, so no `[cj:]`. The Auctaria on n450 quote other codices with *passi*.
- **0871 *Cyrini martyris. qui*** — plate full stop (B9); lower case carried, as A did. *In Antro
  insula* with no comma (B10).
- **0891 Amos** — *quem Ozias … vecte per tempora transfixum necavit, ac postea semivivus patriam
  devectus ibique sepultus est*: "killed", then "half alive… carried to his homeland". The
  contradiction is printed and rendered (7a); the subject also shifts silently from Ozias to Amos.
- **0893 month head and `[0893]` twice** — carried as printed, as A did at 0805.
- **0901 day head** — the plate (n465) prints **III** *Non.* where the twin has *II* (B11), so the
  twin's two consecutive *II Non.* heads become III (3 April) and II (4 April) on the plate.
  English "The third day before the Nones · 3 April".
- **0911 *ecclesiasticorum actum*** — plate n470 (twin *actuum*): a syncopated genitive plural, one
  letter, sound; not patched. The English is the same either way.
- **0921** — plate n475 *magis virtutibus* (twin *magnis*) and *extiti* (twin *extitit*). Both one
  letter, one copy: not patched, English follows the twin ("great virtues", "there was"). *magis* is
  a real word, the class of A's 0649 *edictus*; orchestrator's call.
- **0929** — plate n479 *Carpi episcopi. Papirii* (B13): rendered "Carpus, bishop. Papirius,
  deacon, …". *pro ter-|terreno* (B14): the syllable is set twice across the compositor's own hyphen;
  English "in place of the earthly", deferred `[sic: *terterreno*]`. *remunerationem linguae fidelis
  martyrii munus accepit*: "received the gift of martyrdom as the reward of a faithful tongue".
- **0933 *caesi in gladio percussi*** — plate n481 (twin *et*), B15: "beaten with cudgels, struck
  with the sword", with no "and" because none is printed.
- **0935 *posmodum*** (plate n482; twin *postmodum*): attested spelling, not patched. *capita eorum
  securibus sunt tunsa*: "their heads were beaten with axes" (*tunsa*, printed; Solier's VL notes
  codices with *tonsa*).
- **0937** — *decem et octo martyrum*: eighteen names follow, counted.
- **0947 `[0947][0947]`** — doubled anchor, as 0839. *sed nihil laesus est*: the negation is carried
  ("harmed in nothing"). *expeditus* lower case, kept.
- **0951 *pratem*** (plate n490, twin *partem*; B16, deferred `[sic:]`, flagged). Plate also
  *maritimarum, verbo* with a comma the twin lacks. Two specks on the band (after *jussu,* and after
  *Domitillae*) are stray sorts, not keys.
- **0955 *cum filia*** — "with a daughter": whose daughter the Latin does not say.
- **0969 *Hic discipulis et interpres apostoli Petri*** — printed so (n499) and in the twin. A dative
  or ablative plural where the sentence needs the nominative *discipulus*. It has no slot (Pattern 12,
  test 1), but it is one letter on one copy, so no `[sic:]` (brief §9). English "disciple". Plate
  also *Hermogenis [Bouillart…* with no stop (B17).
- **0977 *quod ejus vita diuturna mundus immeritus sit frui*** — "that the world was unworthy to
  enjoy his life for long": *immeritus* carries the sense, and no negation is added.
- **0983 *Tertulia*** (plate n506, twin *Tertulla*): a broken *l* or an *i*, single witness; English
  "Tertulla".
- **0987** — `[sic: *dixerit*]` (see Markers). *iterum cum carissimo collega tentus.* is a sentence

  *Civitate Sanctonas*: "In the city of Sanctonas", printed form kept.
