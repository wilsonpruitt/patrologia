## On the Ides · 15 May

[0055]

The birthday of the holy confessors Torquatus, Tesifon, Secundus, Indalecius, Cecilius, Esicius, Eufrasius, who were ordained at Rome by the holy apostles, and sent to preach the word of God to the Spains, at that time still entangled in the error of the gentiles. And when they had evangelized in divers cities and had subjected innumerable multitudes to the faith of Christ, Torquatus at Acci, Tesifon at Vergium, Secundus at Abula, Indalecius at Urci, Cecilius at Heliberri, Esicius at Carcesi, Eufrasius at Eliturgi, rested. At the island of Chios, of blessed Isidore, martyr, in whose basilica there stands a well, into which also he is said to have been cast, from whose water the sick, often given to drink, are made whole. At Lamosacum, the passion of Saints Peter and Andrew, Paul and Dionisia.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 16 May

[0059]

In the province of Isauria, the birthday of Saints Aquilinus and Victorianus, whose acts are extant. At Auxerre, of blessed Peregrinus, bishop, the first of that city, who, punished by capital sentence, merited an everlasting crown. In the district *Forojuliensis*, of Saint Maxima, virgin, who, renowned for many virtues, rested in peace.

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 17 May

[0061]

In Tuscia, of Saint Torpes, martyr. He was at first great in the service of Nero Caesar; but afterwards, for the faith of Christ, at the order of the same man he is struck with blows of the hand, is afflicted with scourgings for a very long time, and is handed over to be devoured by beasts, but is not in the least harmed; at length, however, he completed his martyrdom by beheading on the third day before the Kalends of May. But nevertheless his festival is more solemnly kept here. In the city of Nivedunum, of Saints Eraclius, Paul, Aquilinus, with two others.

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 18 May

[0065]

In Egypt, of Saint Dioscorus, reader, upon whom the governor exercised many and various torments, so that he dug out his nails and set his sides aflame with torches; but the attendants, terrified by the brightness of a heavenly light, fell down; last of all, burned with glowing plates, he consummated his martyrdom. On this same day, of Saint Felix, bishop, who at the city *Spellatensis*, under the emperor Maximian, obtained the palm of martyrdom.

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 19 May

[0067]

At Rome, on the Appian Way, the birthday of Saints Calocerus and Parthenius, eunuchs, of whom the former was chamberlain of the emperor Decius, the other primicerius; refusing to sacrifice to idols, they were slain by the same man. Also at Rome, of Saint Potentiana, virgin, who, after innumerable contests, after the burials of many martyrs reverently performed, after all her means were paid out to the poor for Christ, at length passed from earth to the heavens. On the same very day, of blessed Pudens, father of the aforesaid virgin, who, clothed with Christ in baptism by the apostles, kept and preserved the innocent tunic unto the crown of a spotless life.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 20 May

[0071]

At Rome, on the Salarian Way, the birthday of Saint Basilla, virgin and martyr, who, since she was of royal stock and had a most illustrious betrothed, was accused by him of being a Christian, and it was decreed by Gallienus Augustus that she should either receive her betrothed or perish by the sword. Being summoned about this, and refusing to give assent, she was pierced through with the sword. In the Gauls, in the city of Nemausus, of Saint Baudelius, martyr, who, when he was seized and would not sacrifice, and persisted immovable in the faith of Christ amid blows and torments, received the palm of martyrdom by a precious death. At Bourges, of blessed Austregisilus, bishop and confessor.

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 21 May

[0073]

In Mauritania Caesariensis, the birthday of Saints Timotheus, Polius and Eutichius, deacon, who, sowing the word of the Lord in the aforesaid region, merited to be crowned together. Also at Caesarea of Cappadocia, the birthday of Saints Polyeuctus, Victorius and Donatus. At Cordoba, of Secundinus, martyr.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 22 May

[0077]

In Africa, the birthday of Saints Castus and Emilius, who, as blessed Cyprian writes in the book On the Lapsed, consummated their martyrdom through the fire of the passion. At Corsica, of Saint Julia, who was crowned with the punishment of the cross. At Auxerre, of Helena, virgin.

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 23 May

[0079]

At Langres, the passion of Saint Desiderius, bishop. He, when he saw his people harried by the army of the Wandals, going out to make supplication for them, was at once ordered to be slain; and he, who willingly stretched out his neck for the sheep entrusted to him, was struck with the sword and passed to Christ. There suffered with him also many others of the number of his flock, and they were buried in the same city.

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 24 May

[0081]

The birthday of Manahen, foster-brother of Herod the tetrarch, a teacher and prophet under the grace of the New Testament, resting in Christ at Antioch. Also of most blessed Joanna, wife of Chuza, the steward of Herod, whom the Evangelists commemorate. At Portus Romanus, the birthday of Saint Vincent, martyr. In the Gauls, in the city of Namnetis, of the blessed martyrs Donatianus and Rogatianus, brothers, who for the constancy of their faith were put into prison, and [0083] hung up on the scaffold of the rack and torn, then pierced through with a soldier's lance; last of all their heads were cut off. At Histria, of the holy martyrs Zoellus, Servilius, Felix, Silvanus and Diocles.

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 25 May

[0085]

At Rome, on the Numentan Way, the birthday of blessed Urbanus, pope and martyr, by whose teaching, under the persecution of Alexander, many were crowned with martyrdom. At Milan, of Saint Dionisius, bishop and confessor, who, condemned to exile in Cappadocia for the Catholic faith, rested there at first; but afterwards the relics of his body, received through Saint Basil, bishop of the aforesaid city, blessed Ambrose laid to rest with fitting honour. At Mesia, in the city of Dorostorum, of the holy martyrs Passecratis. Valentio, and two others crowned together. In the territory of Troyes, of Saint Leo, confessor.

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 26 May

[0089]

At Athens, the birthday of blessed Quadratus, bishop, disciple of the Apostles, who, in the persecution of Hadrian, gathering together by his faith and diligence the Church scattered by great terror, also published a book in defence of the Christian religion, full of faith and worthy of Apostolic doctrine. At Rome, of the holy martyrs Simmitrius, presbyter, and twenty-two others. On this same day also, of Saint Quadratus, martyr, on whose solemnity sermons of Saint Augustine are found to have been delivered. At Tuder of Tuscia, the birthday of Saints Felicissimus, Eraclius, Paulinus. In the territory of Auxerre, the passion of Saint Priscus, martyr, with a vast multitude. In the Britains, of Saint Augustine, bishop and confessor, who, sent by blessed Pope Gregory, first preached the Gospel of Christ to the nation of the English.

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 27 May

[0093]

At Mesia, in the city *Dorostorensis*, the birthday of blessed Julius, who, in the time of persecution, being a veteran and of completed service, was seized by the officials and brought before the judge; in whose presence, when he cursed the idols and most constantly confessed the name of Christ, he was punished by capital sentence. In the Gauls, in the city *Arausica*, of Saint Eutropius, bishop, whose life, illustrious with virtues and miracles, Verus the bishop describes in a splendid discourse. In the territory *Adartensis*, of Saint Ragnulfus, martyr.

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 28 May

[0095]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed John, pope, whom Saint Gregory relates to have been first held in custody for the orthodox faith, and then brought even to death, with other equally catholic men. In the city of Paris, the passing of Saint Germanus, confessor. Of how great holiness and how great merit this blessed bishop of the same city was, and by what miracles also he shone, Fortunatus the bishop has set forth with a truthful pen. In Sardinia, of Saints Aemilius, Felix, Priamus, Lucianus. On the same day, of Saint Charaunus, who at the city of Carnotum, his head being cut off, took up martyrdom.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 29 May

[0099]

At Iconium, a city of Isauria, under the emperor Aurelian, the passion of Saint Conon, martyr, and of his son, who, first placed upon red-hot iron, then upon a gridiron with live coals set beneath and oil poured over, constantly overcame. But afterwards, having suffered the frying-pan as well as the hanging on the rack, and the want of fire, their hands at the last crushed with a wooden mallet, they gave up the spirit in praise of almighty God. On the same day, of Saints Sisinnius, Martyrius, and Alexander, who in the parts of Anaunia, as Saint Paulinus writes, the gentile men persecuting them, obtained the crown of martyrdom. At Trier, of blessed Maximinus, bishop and confessor, by whom the bishop Athanasius, fleeing the persecution of Constantius, was honourably received. At Rome, on the Aurelian Way, the birthday of Saint Restitutus. On the Tiburtine Way, of seven brothers.

## The third day before the Kalends · 30 May

[0101]

At Rome, on the Aurelian Way, the birthday of blessed Felix, pope, who, when he had ruled the Church for five years, was crowned with martyrdom under the emperor Claudius. At Turres of Sardinia, of the holy martyrs Gabinus and Crispolus. On this same day, of Saint Hucbertus, bishop and confessor.

## The day before the Kalends · 31 May

[0105]

At Rome, of Saint Petronilla, virgin, who, after many miracles of healing, when a certain Count wished to join her to himself in marriage, asking a respite of three days, and giving herself to fastings and prayers, on the third day, as soon as she received the Sacrament of Christ, gave up her spirit. At the city of Aquileia, the birthday of the holy martyrs Cantius, Cantianus and Cantianilla, brothers, who, since they drew their descent from the family of the Anicii, were beheaded for the constancy of the Christian faith, together with their tutor Protus. At Turres of Sardinia, of Crescentianus, martyr.
