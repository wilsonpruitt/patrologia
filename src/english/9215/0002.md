## JUNE HAS 30 DAYS.

[0109]

## On the Kalends · 1 June

[0109]

At Rome, the dedication of Saint Nicomedes, presbyter and martyr. At Caesarea of Palestine, under the persecution of Maximinus, of blessed Pamphilus, presbyter and martyr, whose Life Eusebius, bishop of Caesarea, set down in three books. And Saint Jerome also writes that he found certain volumes written out by his hand, which, he says, I embrace and keep with such joy that I believe I possess the riches of Cresus. At Augustodunum, of Saints Reverianus, bishop, and Paul, presbyter, with ten others, who were crowned with martyrdom under the emperor Aurelian. On this same day, of Saint Caprasius, abbot of the monastery *Lirinensis*. [Bouillart adds: At Paris, in the monastery of blessed Germanus, the dedication of the crypts in honour and veneration of Mary the Mother of God and of blessed Peter the apostle, and of the holy martyrs Georgius, Aurelius and Nathalia. Where the principal altar is consecrated to the name of the same virgin and of blessed Peter and of all the apostles: and at the head of the holy martyrs there is an altar in honour of Saint Michael and of all the blessed heavenly powers; and at their feet, of Saints Vincentius, Sebastian, Christophorus, Andochius, Tirsus and Felix. There is there also an altar in honour of Macharius Dionysius, Rusticus and Eleutherius and of all the holy martyrs. And another of Saint Marcellus, of Genevieve the virgin, of Savinus, Audoenus, Ansbertus and of all the confessors and virgins.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 2 June

[0113]

At Rome, the birthday of the blessed martyrs Marcellinus, presbyter, and Peter, exorcist, who, instructing many in prison unto the faith, after harsh chains and very many torments were beheaded under the judge Serenus. At Lyon, of Saints Fotinus, bishop of the same city, Zacharias, presbyter, Sanctus, deacon, Epagatus, Maturus, Attalus, Albina, Grata, with forty others. All these servants of Christ, as is written in the ecclesiastical history, were crowned together. There suffered also Saint Blandina, of their company, who, beaten with torments on the first, second and third day, when she was not overcome, on the fourth was driven with blows, burned on gridirons, and, having suffered many other things, at the last is slain with the sword.

## The third day before the Nones · 3 June

[0117]

In Campania, the birthday of Saint Herasmus, bishop and martyr; he, under Diocletian Augustus, first grievously bruised with leaded scourges, then with cudgels, afterwards drenched with resin, sulphur, lead, pitch, wax and oil, appeared unharmed. Then under Maximian, tormented with divers and most savage punishments, at length, the Lord calling him, he rested in a holy end. At Aretium, a city of Tuscia, of the holy martyrs Pergentinus and Laurentinus, brothers, who, in the persecution of Decius, when they were boys, after harsh punishments endured and great miracles shown, were cut down with the sword. In the city of Cordoba, of blessed Isaac, monk, who, when he was twenty-seven years old, was slain with the sword for the faith of Christ. In the territory of Orléans, of Saint Lifardus, presbyter.

## The day before the Nones · 4 June

[0119]

At Illiricum, in the city of Siscia, the birthday of blessed Quirinus, bishop, who, for the faith of Christ, as Prudentius writes, a millstone tied to his hand, was cast headlong into a river, and, having long spoken with those standing round, lest they be terrified by his example, scarcely obtained by prayers that he might be drowned. [Bouillart adds: In the city of Nivedunum, of Saints Zoticus, Attalus and Euticus.]

## On the Nones · 5 June

[0123]

At Egypt, the birthday of the holy martyrs Marcianus, Nicander and Apollonius, whose acts are extant. On the same day, the passion of Saint Boniface, martyr, who suffered under Diocletian and Maximian at the city of Tarsus, but was buried at Rome on the road which is called the Latin. He, when he had long been mingled in fornication, at length pricked at heart by the divine will, not only deserted the vice to which he was subject, but also ran ardently through divers torments to the palm of martyrdom. Also of Saint Boniface, bishop, who, coming from the Britains and evangelizing the faith of Christ to the nations, when he had subjected a very great multitude in Frisia to the Christian religion, last of all slain with the sword, consummated his martyrdom with Eobanus the bishop and other servants of God.

## The eighth day before the Ides · 6 June

[0127]

The birthday of blessed Philip, who was one of the seven deacons. He, renowned for signs and wonders, rested at Caesarea, and beside him lie buried his three virgin daughters, prophetesses. At Tarsus of Cilicia, of twenty martyrs, in the times of Diocletian and Maximian, who through divers punishments glorified the Lord in their bodies. At Rome, of Saints Artemius with his wife Candida and his daughter Paulina. Which Artemius was struck with the sword; but his wife and daughter were overwhelmed with stones. In the city of Nivedunum, of Saints Amantius and Alexander.

## The seventh day before the Ides · 7 June

[0129]

At Constantinople, the birthday of blessed Paul, bishop of the same city, who, driven into exile by the emperor Constantius for the catholic faith, to a certain little city of Cappadocia, Cucusa by name, cruelly strangled by the plots of the Arians, passed to the heavenly kingdoms. Also at Cordoba, of Saints Peter, presbyter, Aventius, Hieremias and three others.

## The sixth day before the Ides · 8 June

[0131]

In the Gauls, in the city of Suessionis, the birthday of blessed Medard, bishop *Noviomensis*, of what great merit he was, was shown when at his death the heavens were opened, and divine lights flashed before him. On the same day, of Saint Gildardus, bishop of Rouen. Also at Senones, of Saint Eraclius, bishop and confessor. In Sardinia, of Saint Salustianus. At Cordoba, of Saint Abundius, presbyter and martyr.

## The fifth day before the Ides · 9 June

[0135]

At Rome, on the Caelian hill, the birthday of Saints Primus and Felicianus, under the emperors Diocletian and Maximian. These most glorious martyrs, leading a long life in the Lord, and bearing now the same torments together, now different and exquisite ones severally, at length, put to the sword, consummated the course of their happy fight. In the Gauls, in the city of Agennum, the passion of blessed Vincent, levite and martyr. Also in Scotia, of Saint Columba, presbyter and confessor, a man of great and wonderful virtue. At Nicea, of Saint Diomedes. *Bouillart adds:* [At the monastery of Saint Germanus, the dedication of an oratory in honour of blessed Peter the apostle.]

## The fourth day before the Ides · 10 June

[0139]

At Rome, on the Aurelian Way, the birthday of Saints Basilides, Tripos, and Madalis, under the emperor Aurelian, the governor being Plato, and of twenty other martyrs. On the same day, on the Salarian Way, the passion of blessed Getulus, a most illustrious and most learned man, and of his companions Cerealis, Amantius and Primitivus, who by order of the emperor Hadrian were first beaten, then shut up in prison, lastly given over to burning. At Nicomedia, of Saint Zacharias. At Auxerre, of Saint Censurius, bishop and confessor.

[Bouillart adds: At Paris, of Saint Landericus, bishop and confessor. On the same day, at the monastery of Saint Germanus, the dedication of the crypts in honour of blessed Mary the Mother of God and of blessed Michael the archangel and of all the heavenly powers, and of the holy apostles Peter and Paul, and of all the apostles and evangelists, and also of all holy virgins. There is there also an altar of Macharius, of Dionysius and his companions, and of Saint Urbanus, pope and martyr, and of all the martyrs. And another of Saints Georgius, Aurelius and Praejectus, martyrs, and of the holy confessors Martin, Nicolaus, Marcellus, Ansbertus, and of Saints Caecilia, Genevieve, Nathalia and all the confessors of Christ.]

## The third day before the Ides · 11 June

[0143]

The birthday of Saint Barnabas the apostle, who, being a Cypriot, was ordained apostle of the gentiles with Paul. His body was found in the time of the emperor Zeno, he himself revealing it. At Aquileia, the passion of Saints Felix and Fortunatus, under the persecution of Diocletian and Maximian, who, being two brothers german, were hung upon the rack, and burning torches were set about their sides; which being presently extinguished by the power of God, they were drenched through the belly with boiling oil, and at the last were cut off by the head in the confession of Christ.

## The day before the Ides · 12 June

[0145]

At Milan, the birthday of blessed Nazarius, martyr, whom Anolinus, under the fury of the persecution which was stirred up by Nero, long bruised and afflicted in prison, with whom the most holy boy Celsus, whom the same man had brought up, he ordered to be struck with the sword. Their bodies, as Saint Paulinus writes, when they had lain hidden until the times of the emperors Honorius and Arcadius, blessed Ambrose, the Lord revealing them, found. Also at the aforesaid city, of the holy martyrs Basilides, Cirinus, and Nabor.

## On the Ides · 13 June

[0149]

At Rome, on the Ardeatine Way, the birthday of Saint Felicula, virgin and martyr, who, unwilling to marry, nor to sacrifice to idols, was handed over to a certain vicar, who caused her, persevering in the confession of Christ, after a dark custody and the want of hunger, to be raised on the rack, and so at last to be taken down and cast headlong into a sewer. On the same day, of Saint Fandila, presbyter, who at the city of Cordoba, his head being cut off, took up martyrdom.

## The eighteenth day before the Kalends · 14 June

[0151]

Of Heliseus the prophet, who is laid at Samaria of Palestine (as blessed Jerome writes), where also Abdias the prophet rests. At Caesarea of Cappadocia, the birthday of Saint Basil, bishop, who, in the time of Valens, distinguished in doctrine and wisdom, and grounded in all virtues, shone wonderfully. In the territory of the city of Suessionis, of the holy martyrs Rufinus and Valerius, who, after torments inflicted on them, were ordered by the governor Rictiovarus to be beheaded. On this same day, of Saint Quintianus, presbyter and confessor.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 15 June

[0155]

In Sicily, the birthday of Saints Vitus, Modestus, and Crescentia, who under the emperor Diocletian, after a pot of boiling lead, after beasts and scaffolds overcome by divine power, finished the course of their glorious contest [Bouillart inserts: In the city of Beneventum, of Saint Mercurius, martyr.] At Mesia, in the city of Dorostorum, of Saint Esichius, soldier, who, seized with blessed Julius, was afterwards crowned with martyrdom under the governor Maximus.
