## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 16 June

[0159]

In the Gauls, at the city of Vesuntio, of the holy martyrs Ferreolus, presbyter, and Ferrutio, deacon, who, sent by blessed Hireneus the bishop to preach the word of God, afterwards, under the judge Claudius, tormented with punishments, are struck with the sword. At Antioch, the birthday of the holy martyrs Cyricus and Julitta his mother, who, after cruel blows and grievous torments, fulfilled the course of their martyrdom by the cutting off of the head. At Lyon, the deposition of blessed Aurelianus, bishop of Arles. In the city of Namnetis, of Saint Similianus, bishop and confessor.

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 17 June

[0161]

At Rome, of the two hundred and sixty-two holy martyrs, who were laid on the old Salarian Way at the Clivus Cucumeris. On the same day, of Saint Vulmarus, confessor, a man of admirable holiness and religion. For I shall say in its place that Saint Vulmarus departed this life not on this day but on 20 July; and the mention of Saint Gundulfus could not have been made more fittingly than on this day. ## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 18 June

[0167]

At Rome, on the Ardeatine Way, the birthday of the holy martyrs Mark and Marcellianus, brothers, who, held by the duke Fabianus and bound to a stake, received sharp [nt: In the Saint-Germain manuscript there is an empty space of one word. Bouillart.] [nails] in their feet. And when at last they did not cease to praise Christ, pierced through the sides with a lance, they passed with the glory of martyrdom to the starry kingdoms. In the Spains, in the city of Malaca, of the holy martyrs Siriacus, and Paula, virgin, who, after many torments inflicted on them, overwhelmed with stones, gave back their souls to heaven among the rocks. At Alexandria, the passion of Saint Marina, virgin.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 19 June

[0169]

At Milan, the birthday of the holy martyrs Gervasius and Protasius, brothers, of whom Count Astasius ordered the former to be beaten with leaded scourges so long, until he breathed out his spirit: but the following one, beaten with cudgels, to be cut off by the head. Their sepulchres Saint Ambrose found, the Lord revealing them, and their bodies so incorrupt, as if they had been slain on that same day. At Ravenna, of Saint Ursicinus, martyr, who under the judge Paulinus, after excessive torments, remaining immovable in the confession of the Lord, completed his martyrdom by the cutting off of his head.

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 20 June

[0173]

At Rome, the deposition of Saint Novatus, brother of Timotheus the presbyter, who were instructed in the faith by the apostles. Their sisters were blessed Potentiana and Praxedes, holy virgins of Christ. In the city of Thomis, of Saints Paul and Ciriacus.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 21 June

[0177]

At Sicily, in the city of Syracusa, the birthday of the holy martyrs Rufinus and Martia. In the city of Caesarea of Palestine, of Saint Eusebius, bishop and confessor, a man of most excellent genius and a historiographer. She, when she was being brought in before the impious Julian for the faith of Christ, gave up her spirit in the confession. In the district *Madriacensis*, of Saint Leutfredus, confessor, who, as a monument of his eminent holiness, is said to have brought forth a spring from the earth by his prayers. In Africa, of Saints Quiriacus and Apollinaris.

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 22 June

[0181]

In Britain, of Saint Albanus, martyr, who in the time of Diocletian, in the city of Verolamium, after blows and bitter torments, was beheaded. There suffered with him also one of the soldiers, because, ordered to strike him, he was unwilling. At Nola of Campania, the birthday of blessed Paulinus, bishop and confessor, of whom Saint Gregory makes mention in the book of Dialogues. He was renowned not only for learning and abundant holiness of life, but also for power against the demons. On this same day, the deposition of Saint Niceae, bishop of the city *Romatiana*.

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 23 June

[0183]

The Vigil of Saint John the Baptist. On the same day, of Saint John, presbyter, whom the impious Julian ordered to be beheaded, unheard, on the old Salarian Way, before the image of the sun. In Tuscia, in the city of Utrina, of Saint Felix, presbyter, whose mouth Turtius the prefect ordered to be pounded with a stone so long, until he gave up the Spirit. In Britain, the birthday of Saint Ediltrudis, virgin, whose body, when it had been buried eleven years, was found incorrupt.

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 24 June

[0187]

The Nativity of blessed John the Baptist and forerunner of the Lord. In the territory of Paris, at the village of Christoilum, the passion of Saints Agoadus and Glibertus, with innumerable others of both sexes. At Augustodunum, the deposition of Simplicius, bishop.

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 25 June

[0189]

At Pirriberoea, of Saint Sosipater, disciple of blessed Paul the apostle. At Alexandria, of Saint Gallicanus, martyr: who, when, raised up with triumphal insignia, he was accepted by Constantine Augustus and dear to him, was converted to the faith through his daughter, Constantia by name, and through the martyrs of Christ John and Paul. The fame of his holy repute afterwards grew so much in the whole world, that people coming from the East and the West saw a man, from patrician and consul, washing the feet of the poor, setting the table, pouring water on hands, ministering carefully to the sick, and performing the other offices of holy service. But he himself afterwards, when he was being compelled to sacrifice under Julian, and scorned it, struck with the sword in the [0191] body, made a martyr of Christ. At Rome, of Saint Lucia, virgin, with twenty-two others. [Bouillart adds: [nt: It is true. To this article the Pratensis subjoins the translation of Saint Eligius. But in what did it sin in this? Bouillart.] Also the translation of Saint Eligius, bishop and confessor.]

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 27 June

[0199]

At Galatia, of blessed Crescens, disciple of Saint Paul the apostle, who, making a passage into the Gauls, converted many to the faith of Christ by the word of preaching. But returning to the nation for which he had been specially given as bishop, he strengthened the Galatians in the work of the Lord until the blessed end of his life. At Tiburtina, a city of Italy, the birthday of Saint Symphorosa with her seven sons, Crescens, Julian, Nemesius, Primitivus, Justinus, Stacteus, Eugenius; of whom the mother, under the emperor Hadrian, on account of her insuperable constancy, first beaten with palms, then hung up by the hair, last of all, a stone tied to her, was cast headlong into a river. But the sons, stretched on stakes to pulleys, completed their martyrdom by divers manners of death. At Cordoba, of Saint Zoilus, martyr, whose body, when it had long lain hidden where it had been buried, was made manifest by divine revelation to the venerable bishop of the same place, Agapius by name.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 28 June

[0203]

The Vigil of the apostles Peter and Paul, and the birthday of blessed Leo, pontiff and doctor. At Lyon of Gaul, of Saint Hireneus, bishop and martyr, who, it is agreed, was the disciple of blessed Policarpus, priest and martyr, as Jerome writes, near to the times of the apostles. But afterwards, in the persecution of Severus, with almost all the people of his city, he was crowned with a glorious martyrdom. At Alexandria, of the holy martyrs Plutarchus, Serenus, Heraclides, Heros, Potamiena, Marcella, with three others, among whom Potamiena the virgin shone forth especially, who first sweating through immense and innumerable contests for her virginity, then also, having suffered exquisite and unheard-of torments for martyrdom, at the last was consumed by fire.

## The third day before the Kalends · 29 June

[0207]

At Rome, the birthday of the blessed apostles Peter and Paul, who suffered under Nero Caesar, in the consulship of Bassus and Tuscus. Of whom the former is celebrated in the same city beside the Triumphal Way with the veneration of the whole world; the following one also, buried on the Ostian Way, is held in equal honour. On the same day, of Saint Marcellus, martyr, who at the fort of Argentomacum, for the faith of Christ, together with Anastasius, a soldier, was beheaded [Bouillart: with Anastasius, a military man, was crowned with martyrdom]. In the territory *Senonicus*, of Beata, virgin.

## On the Kalends · 1 July

[0213]

On Mount Or, the deposition of Aaron, the first priest. In the territory of Lyon, the deposition of blessed Domitianus, abbot, who among the first there practised the hermit's life, and, gathering very many to himself in the service of God, and very renowned for great virtues and glorious miracles, was gathered to his fathers in a good old age. In the city of Engolisma, of Saint Eparchius, monk and confessor. In the monastery of Aninsula, of Saint Carilefus, presbyter. In the territory of Reims, of Saint Theodoricus, presbyter. At the port of Valencianae, of Saint Salvi, confessor. [In Bouillart: In the city of Engolisma, of Saint Eparchius, confessor. [nt: Of the last three commemorations the Caroline autograph has thus: *In the monastery of Aninsula, of Saint Carilefus, presbyter. Also in the territory of Reims, of Saint Theodoricus, presbyter. At the port of Valentianae, of Saint Salvius, confessor.* In his own codex Usuardus, for *in the monastery of Aninsula*, wrote *in the district Cinomannicus*. This correction occurs not in this place only but in many others too. It is likely that the cause was this: that to most people certain particular places are much less known than the regions in which they are situated. So that those who were to have the martyrology in use might the more easily perceive with whom they shared a common veneration of some saint, he designated the regions where the saints' cult was celebrated rather than the places. Perhaps too a second cause was added to this one, namely to vary the phrasing. He had already written *in the territory of Lyon* for the particular place where Saint Domicianus had died and was venerated: afterwards he wrote *in the district Cinomannicus*, so as to denote in the same manner, yet with another word, the place where Saint Carilefus was known. And so the word *territorium*, which he had to use in the third commemoration, brings less tedium. I add that corrections of this kind do not come into an interpolator's mind. Such men are indeed wont to add, take away, transpose; but what is gained by deleting the name of a particular place so that the more general name of a region may be shown? Of Saint Theodoricus there is nothing for which I need labour in defending the correction. Whether he called him presbyter in the Caroline autograph and granted him the title of confessor in his own codex is all one, and there is no leisure for divining in trifles of this kind. How learnedly Salvius was erased from here is plain from what we observed at 26 June. Lastly, that Saint Leonorius was fittingly commemorated here is a matter placed beyond controversy. This is the Leonorius who, brought up from a boy and instructed in the first elements of the Christian religion by Saint Hildutus, was afterwards made a bishop without a see, so that he might carry the name of Christ wherever he would the more freely. He was celebrated in the sixth century of the Church, in the reign of Childebert in Gaul, to whom he was most acceptable. More is told of him by Ussher in his Antiquities of the British Churches and by Le Cointe in the Ecclesiastical Annals of the Franks, which it is not the purpose of the present work to relate here.] In the district *Cinomannicus*, of Saint Carilefus, presbyter.
