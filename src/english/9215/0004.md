## The sixth day before the Nones · 2 July

[0219]

At Rome, on the Aurelian Way, the birthday of Saints Processus and Martinianus, who were baptized by blessed Peter the apostle, but under Nero, having endured the bruising of the mouth, the rack, sinews, cudgels, flames and scorpions, at the last cut down with the sword, are crowned with martyrdom. On this same day, of the three holy soldiers who suffered with blessed Paul the apostle. Also, of the holy martyrs Aristo, Crescentianus, Euticianus, Urbanus, Vitalis, Justus, Felicissimus, Felix, Martia and Symphorosa, who were all crowned with glorious martyrdom in Campania. At Tours, the deposition of Saint Monegundis, a religious [Bouillart: [nt: This reading of the Pratensis codex, *of Saint Monegundis, consecrated to God*, Sollerius confesses to be *the best*. But because the Heriniensis and Tornacensis codices read *religiosam* ('a religious'), he feared lest he should seem to have preferred the Pratensis even in things of no account.] *Deo sacratae*].

## The fifth day before the Nones · 3 July

[0221]

At Edessa of Mesopotamia, the translation of the body of Saint Thomas the apostle. On this same day, at Cneocaesarea of Pontus, the birthday of blessed Gregory, bishop and martyr, who, endowed with the great glory of signs and miracles, among other things on a certain occasion, so that there might be room enough for building a church, moved a mountain by his prayers. In the city *Clusina*, of the holy martyrs Hireneus, deacon, and Mustiola, a noble matron, who suffered under the emperor Aurelian. In Laodicea of Syria, of Saint Anatholius, bishop, who left behind writings to be admired not only by religious men but also by philosophers. At Alexandria, of Saint Triphon and ten others. Also at Constantinople, of Saint Eulogius.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 4 July

[0225]

Of Oseas the prophet. At Tours, the translation of blessed Martin, bishop and confessor, and the ordination of his episcopate. In Africa, the birthday of Saint Jocundianus, martyr, plunged into the sea. At Sirmium, of the holy martyrs Innocentius and Sebastia, with thirty others. In the territory of the city of Bourges, of Saint Laurianus, martyr, whose head was carried to Seville, to the Spains.

## The third day before the Nones · 5 July

[0227]

At Syria, the birthday of Saint Domitius, martyr, who by his virtues bestows many benefits on the inhabitants. At Rome, of Saint Zoa, martyr, who, while she was praying at the Confession of Peter the apostle, was seized by the persecutors and thrust into a most dark prison, then hung upon a tree by the neck and by the hair, a horrible smoke being applied beneath, and gave up the spirit in the confession of the Lord.

## The day before the Nones · 6 July

[0229]

At Judaea, of Isaias the prophet, who, cut into two parts, fell, and was buried under the oak Rogel, beside the crossing of the waters. Also the Octave of the apostles, and the first entry of blessed Paul the apostle into the city of Rome, in the second year of the reign of Nero. At Rome, the birthday of Saint Tranquillinus, martyr, who, while he was praying at the Confession of blessed Paul, was seized by the pagans and stoned, and consummated his martyrdom. In the district of Trier, of Saint Goar, presbyter and confessor.

## On the Nones · 7 July

[0233]

At Alexandria, the birthday of Saint Pantenus, an apostolic man and adorned with all wisdom, whose zeal and love towards the word of God was so great that he even set out, with the warmth of faith and devotion, to preach the Gospel of Christ to all the nations which are hidden away in the farthest recesses of the East. At Rome, of the holy martyrs Nicostratus, Claudius, Castorius, Victorinus and Simphronianus, whom Fabianus the judge, dealing with them for ten days by threats and blandishments, when he could not move them in anything at all, ordered to be tortured a third time, and afterwards to be cast headlong into the sea. [Bouillart adds, from the margin of the *Pratensis* codex: On the same day, the Octave of Saint Marcialis, bishop and confessor.]

## The eighth day before the Ides · 8 July

[0237]

At Asia Minor, of Saints Aquila and Priscilla his wife, of whom it is written in the Acts of the Apostles. In Palestine, the birthday of blessed Procopius, martyr, who, brought from Scitopolis to Caesarea, at the first boldness of his answers was beheaded by the judge Fabianus. On the same day, [nt: The Caroline autograph joins companions to Saint Cilianus. Yet there is no erasure here in the Pratensis: because it is another leaf, and I have never failed to observe that there no erasure is seen and yet the codex differs from the Caroline autograph. Bouillart.] of Saint Cilianus, martyr.

## The seventh day before the Ides · 9 July

[0239]

At Rome, at the Gutta that flows continually, the birthday of Saint Zeno, martyr, and of ten thousand two hundred and three others. On the same day, of blessed Cirillus, bishop, who, cast into the flames, when he had come out unharmed and had been released by the judge in amazement at so great a miracle, was again, for the urgent and eager preaching he had made of Christ, beheaded by the same man. In the city of Tyre, the passion of Saints Anatholia and Audax under the emperor Decius; of whom Anatholia, the virgin of Christ, harassed with various kinds of blows, was at the last run through with the sword while at prayer. Audax also was given into custody; and without delay he is crowned by capital sentence. In the city of Martula, of Saint Brictius, bishop and confessor, who, after he had suffered many things for the confession of the Lord, and had been strengthened by a holy angel and by blessed Peter the Apostle, gaining likewise great multitudes of believing peoples for almighty God, rested in peace.

## The sixth day before the Ides · 10 July

[0243]

At Rome, of the seven brothers, sons of Saint Felicitas, that is, Januarius, Felix, Philip, Silvanus, Alexander, Vitalis and Martialis. Of whom Januarius, in the time of Antoninus, after beatings with rods and prison, was killed under the leaded scourges. Felix and Philip were slain with cudgels. Silvanus was killed by being thrown down from a height. Alexander, Vitalis and Martialis were punished by capital sentence. In Africa, the birthday of Saints Januarius, Marinus, Nabor and Felix, beheaded. Also at Rome, of the holy virgins Rufina and Secunda, who, subdued by torments under the persecution of Valerian and Gallienus, at the last departed, the one with her head struck by the sword, the other with her neck cut.

## The fifth day before the Ides · 11 July

[0247]

The translation of Saint Benedict, abbot. At Armenia Minor, in the city of Nicopolis, the birthday of the holy martyrs Januarius and Pelagia, who, tortured for four days with the rack, with hooks and with fragments of potsherds, fulfilled their martyrdom [Bouillart inserts: [nt: Since the love of truth alone drove me to undertake this work, such as it is, I am far from wishing to hide the truth or even to disguise it, even when it is inconvenient to me. Also in the territory of Poitiers, of Saint Sabinus, confessor.

## The fourth day before the Ides · 12 July

[0249]

At Cyprus, of blessed Nason, an old disciple of Christ. In Aquileia, the birthday of Saint Hermagoras, bishop, disciple of blessed Mark the evangelist, who, amid miracles of healing and earnestness of preaching and the conversion of peoples, having experienced very many kinds of punishments, at the last, with Fortunatus his archdeacon, merited by capital punishment an everlasting triumph. In Caesarea, of Saint Dius [nt: Saint Dius was added here by Usuardus after the first copy of his Martyrology had been offered to King Charles. Of that saint there is nothing in the Dervensis codex. The Hieronymian lists and Ado record him. Bouillart.].

## The third day before the Ides · 13 July

[0253]

Of Joel and Esdras the prophets. At Macedonia, the birthday of blessed Silas the apostle; who, being one of the first brethren, and appointed by the apostles to the churches of the Gentiles, earnestly consummated the office of preaching, full of the grace of the Lord, and, glorifying Christ in his sufferings, afterwards rested. In Africa, of the holy confessors Eugenius, bishop of Carthage, glorious in faith and virtues, and of all the clergy of the same Church, who, wasted by slaughter and hunger, about five hundred or more, among whom were very many readers who were little children, rejoicing in the Lord, were driven far off into cruel exile. And there were among them men most noble, an archdeacon named Salutaris, and Muritta, second in the office of the ministers, who, made confessors a third time, were made illustrious by the title of glorious perseverance in Christ. In Lesser Britain, of Saint Turianus, bishop and confessor, a man of wonderful simplicity and innocence.

## The day before the Ides · 14 July

[0257]

At Pontus, the birthday of blessed Focas, bishop of the city of Sinope, who under the emperor Trajan overcame prison, chains, iron, and fire also, for Christ. At Alexandria, of Saint Heracleae, prelate, to whom, on account of his most celebrated repute, Africanus the historiographer records that he hastened.

## On the Ides · 15 July

[0261]

At Nisibis, the birthday of Saint James, bishop, a man of great virtue. He was one of the number of the confessors under the persecution of Maximinus, and of those who in the synod of Nicaea condemned the perversity of Arius by the opposition of the *Omousion*. At Rome, at the Port, the birthday of Saints Eutropius, Zosima and Bonosa, sisters. At Carthage, of the holy martyrs Catulinus, deacon, Januarius, Florentius, Julia and Justa, who are laid in the Basilica of Faustus. At Alexandria, of Saints Philip, Zeno, Narseus and ten children.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 16 July

[0263]

At Antioch of Syria, the birthday of blessed Eustachius, bishop and confessor, under the emperor Constantine, who, for the catholic faith driven into exile to Trajanopolis, a city of Thrace, rested there. In Ostia, the passion of Saint Hilary, monk, who, when he would not sacrifice under the persecution of Julian, beaten with cudgels, took up martyrdom.

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 17 July

[0267]

In Carthage, the birthday of the holy Scillitan martyrs, that is, Speratus, Natalis, Cithinus, Berturius, Felix, Aquilinus, Letatius, Januaria, Generosa, Bessia, Donata and Secundina, under the prefect Saturninus, who, after their first confession sent into prison and fastened in the wood, were then beheaded with the sword.

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 18 July

[0271]

At Carthage, of Saint Guddenes, martyr, who, by order of the proconsul Rufinus, for the confession of Christ, was four times, at different seasons, harassed by stretching on the rack, and tortured by the horrible tearing of the hooks, afflicted also for a very long time by the filth of prison, and at the last was cut down with the sword. In the city of Dorostorum, of Saint Emilianus. [Bouillart adds, in cursive type: In the district *Castrensis*, the deposition of Saint Arnulfus, martyr and bishop.]

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 19 July

[0275]

The birthday of Saint Epafras, who, ordained bishop at Colossae by blessed Paul, renowned for virtues, received there the palm of martyrdom for the sheep entrusted to him, in a manly contest. At the Thebaid, of our holy father Arsenius, of whom it is related in the Words of the Elders that, on account of the overflowing of his tears which had to be wiped, he always had a cloth in his bosom or in his hand. In Spain, in the city of Seville, the passion of the holy Justa and Rufina, who, seized by the governor Diogenianus, were first harassed by stretching on the rack and by the tearing of the hooks, afterwards afflicted with prison, hunger and pains; at length Justa breathed out her spirit in prison, and the neck of Rufina was broken in the confession.
