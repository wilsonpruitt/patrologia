## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 20 July

[0277]

The birthday of blessed Joseph, who was surnamed Justus. He, serving in the office of preaching and holiness, and sustaining much persecution from the Jews for the faith of Christ, rested in Judaea by a most victorious end. Of whom it is related that he drank poison and suffered nothing harmful from it, on account of his faith in the Lord. At Damascus, of Saints Sabinus, Maximus, Julian, Macrobius, Cassius and Paula, with ten others. In the city of Cordoba, of Saint Paul, deacon and martyr. [Bouillart: . . . . . with ten others. In the district *Bononiensis*, of Saint Vulmarus, confessor. *And in the margin:* On the same day, of Saint Margaret, virgin and martyr.]

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 21 July

[0281]

Of Daniel the prophet. At Rome, of Saint Praxedes, virgin. She, instructed in all chastity and in the divine law, giving herself constantly to vigils and prayers and fasts, rested in Christ, and was buried beside her sister Potentiana on the Salarian Way. In the Gauls, in the city of Marseille, the birthday of Saint Victor, who, since he was a soldier and would neither serve as a soldier nor sacrifice to idols, was first thrust into prison, and there visited by angels, then tortured with various punishments, and at the last crushed in a baker's mill, consummated his martyrdom. There suffered with him three soldiers, Alexander, Felicianus and Longinus. At Troyes, of Saint Julia, virgin and martyr.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 22 July

[0285]

At Ancyra of Galatia, the birthday of Saint Plato, martyr, whose acts are extant. On the same day, of Saint Mary Magdalene, and of blessed Sintice, who sleeps buried at Philippi: of whom the apostle Paul writes. In the monastery *Fontinella* [Bouillart: In the district of Rouen], the deposition of Saint Wandregisilus, confessor. In the territory *Arvernensis*, of Saint Meneleus, abbot.

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 23 July

[0289]

The birthday of blessed Apollinaris, bishop, who, ordained at Rome by the apostle Peter and sent to Ravenna, afterwards suffered for the faith of Christ diverse and manifold beatings. And at length under Vespasian Caesar, amid the very torments succeeding one another by turns, he consummated a glorious martyrdom.

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 24 July

[0291]

At Rome, on the Tiburtine Way, the birthday of Saint Vincent. At Emerita, a city of Spain, of Saint Victor, a soldier, who with his two brothers Stercatius and Antinogenus, tried by diverse tortures, consummated his martyrdom. In Italy, in the city of Tyre, of Saint Christina, virgin, who, believing in Christ, first under two judges bearing torments steadfastly, at the last through their successor, by the cutting out of her tongue and the piercing of arrows, completed the course of her martyrdom. On the same day, the birthday of Saints Niceta and Aquilina, who, converted to Christ by the preaching of blessed Christopher the martyr, took up the palm of martyrdom by the cutting off of the head. In the city *Amiternina*, of eighty-three soldiers. [Bouillard adds in the margin: At Senones, of the holy prelates Ursicinus and Pavacius.]

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 25 July

[0295]

The birthday of blessed James the apostle, brother of John the evangelist, who was beheaded by king Herod. His most sacred bones, translated from Jerusalem to the Spains and laid in their farthest borders, are honoured with the most celebrated veneration of those peoples. In Lycia, in the city of Samon, of Saint Christopher, who, worn down with iron rods, and saved by the power of Christ from the flames of a raging fire, at the last pierced through with the strokes of arrows, completed his martyrdom by the cutting off of his head. In the city of Barcelona, the birthday of blessed Cucufas, martyr, who, having experienced under three judges the most atrocious kinds of torments for the name of the Lord, at the last passed away a victor by the sword. In the city of Paris, the translation of Saint Germanus, bishop and confessor.

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 26 July

[0297]

The birthday of Saint Herastus, who, left as bishop at Philippi by blessed Paul, having been crowned there with martyrdom, rested. At Rome, at the Port, of Saint Jacinctus, martyr, who, first thrown into the fire, then cast headlong into a most violent torrent, came out unharmed: after this, struck with the sword by the consular Leontius, he ended his life. On the Latin Way, of Saints Simpronius, Olimpius, Theodolus and Exuperia, who, burned up with fires, as is read in the acts of Saint Stephen the pope, obtained the palm of martyrdom. [Bouillart adds, in cursive type: The translation of Saint Marcellus, bishop and confessor.]

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 27 July

[0301]

At Ephesus, the birthday of Saints Maximianus, Malchus, Martianus and four others, whose acts are extant. At Nicomedia, of the blessed martyrs Hermolaus, presbyter, Hernempus and Hermogratis, brothers, who, after many punishments inflicted on them by the emperor Maximian, were punished by capital sentence for the confession of Christ. On the same day in Sicily, of blessed Simeon, monk. At Auxerre, the deposition of Etherius, bishop.

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 28 July

[0305]

At Nicomedia, the passion of blessed Pantaleon, who, seized for the faith of Christ by Maximian, tortured by the punishment of the rack and by the burning of torches, but refreshed amid these things by the Lord appearing to him, at length consummated his martyrdom by the stroke of the sword. [Bouillart adds in the margin: On the same day, the passion of the holy martyrs Nazarius and Celsus the boy.] In Lesser Britain, in the monastery of Dolum, of Saint Samson, bishop and confessor. On the same day at Lyon, of Saint Peregrinus, presbyter, whose blessedness the glory of miracles attests.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 29 July

[0309]

At Rome, on the Aurelian Way, the birthday of blessed Felix, pope, who, cast down from his see by Constantius Augustus for the defence of the catholic faith and slain with the sword, fell most gloriously. Also on the Portuensian Way, of the holy martyrs Simplicius, Faustinus and Beatrix, in the times of Diocletian, of whom the two, after many and diverse tortures, were ordered to undergo capital sentence. But Beatrix, their sister, was stifled in prison in the confession of Christ. In the city of Troyes, of Saint Lupus, confessor, who, sent into Britain with blessed Germanus, came there. He discharged the priesthood venerably for fifty-two years.

## The third day before the Kalends · 30 July

[0313]

At Rome, the birthday of Saints Abdon and Sennes, under-kings, who, when in Cordula, a city of the Persians, they were suffering chains from Decius, at the last he brought them to Rome bound with chains and wasted by diverse punishments, where they were first beaten with leaded scourges, and then slain with the sword. At Africa, in the city of Lucernaria, of the holy virgins Maxima, Donatilla and Secunda, who, in the persecution of Gallienus, first given vinegar and gall to drink, then beaten with most bitter blows, and tortured by stretching on the rack, afterwards also burned on gridirons, and moreover thrown to the beasts, but untouched by them, at the last had their throats cut with the sword.

## The day before the Kalends · 31 July

[0315]

At Caesarea, the passion of Fabius, martyr, who, since he refused to bear the governor's standards, was first thrust into prison for some days, then, questioned once and again, he persists in the confession of Christ: and forthwith he is condemned by the judge by capital sentence. At Ravenna, the passing of Saint Germanus, bishop of Auxerre, most renowned in birth, faith and doctrine, and in the glory of miracles. In the city of Sinnada, of Saints Democritus, Secundus and Dionisius.

## AUGUST HAS 31 DAYS.

[0319]

## On the Kalends · 1 August

At Antioch, the birthday of the holy Maccabees. At Rome, at Saint Peter at the Chains. At Italy, in the city of Vercelli, of blessed Eusebius, bishop, who, for the confession of the faith banished by Constantius to Scitopolis, and thence to Cappadocia, afterwards returned to his church, and at the last, the Arians persecuting him, suffered martyrdom. Also at Rome, the passion of the holy virgins Spes, Fides and Charitas, and of their mother Sapientia: who under the emperor Hadrian obtained the crown of martyrdom. On the Latin Way, of the holy martyrs Bonus, presbyter, Faustus and Maurus, with seven others, who are described in the Acts of Stephen the pope. In the Spains, in the city of Gerona, the birthday of Saint Felix, whom the judge Dacianus, after inflicting kinds of torments, ordered to be scraped with hooks, and then to be torn to the bones, and wounds to be renewed upon wounds so long, until he gave back to Christ his unconquered spirit. At Arabia, in the city of Philadelphia, of the holy martyrs Cirillus, Aquila, Peter, Domicianus, Rufus and Menander, crowned on one day. In the territory of Paris, of Justinus, martyr. In the district *Lisuinus*, of Saint Nemesius, confessor.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 2 August

At Rome, in the cemetery of Calixtus, the birthday of blessed Stephen, pope, who, in the persecution of Valerian, when he was being pursued for the martyrdom of Christ, and was standing undaunted before the solemnities of the altar and continuing steadily with what he had begun, is beheaded in that same place, as it were in his own see. In the province of Bithynia, in the city of Nicaea, of Saint Theodota with her three sons, of whom the consular of Bithynia had the first-born, named Evodius, confessing Christ confidently, first beaten with cudgels, then herself with all her sons consumed by fire.

## The third day before the Nones · 3 August

At Jerusalem, the finding of the body of the most blessed Stephen the protomartyr, and of Saints Gamaliel, Nicomedi and Abibon. This, revealed in the seventh year of the emperor Honorius, and written up by the blessed presbyter Lucian, is made known, in the person of the whole Church, to almost all nations. At Constantinople, of Hermellus, martyr.

## The day before the Nones · 4 August

[0329]

The birthday of blessed Aristarchus, disciple of Saint Paul the apostle. At Rome, on the Latin Way, the passion of Saint Tertullinus, martyr, who, under the emperor Aurelian, after the impious slaughter of cudgels, and the burning of fires about his sides, and the battering of his mouth, and the stretching of the rack, and the cutting of the sinews, sentence being given, consummated his martyrdom by the cutting off of his head.

## On the Nones · 5 August

[0333A]

At the province of Raetia, in the city *Augustana*, the birthday of Saint Afra, who, being a pagan and a harlot, converted to Christ through the teaching of Saint Narcissus, bishop, and baptized with all her house, was given over to the fire for the confession of the Lord. On the same day, of Saint Oswald, king of the English, whose Acts the Venerable Bede, presbyter, records in the history of that same people. At Augustudunum, of blessed Cassianus, bishop and confessor. At Catalauni, of Saint Memmius, bishop.
