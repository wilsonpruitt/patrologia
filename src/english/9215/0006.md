## The eighth day before the Ides · 6 August

[0335]

[On Mount Tabor, the transfiguration of the Lord Jesus, *Bouillart in the margin.*] At Rome, on the Appian Way, the birthday of blessed Xystus, bishop and martyr. And in the cemetery of Praetextatus, of Saints Felicissimus and Agapitus, his deacons, who were beheaded together with him under Decius. There were beheaded with him also four others, that is, Januarius, Magnus, Vincent and Stephen, subdeacons, as is read in the pontifical acts. There suffered with them blessed Quartus, as Saint Cyprian writes. In the Spains, in the city of Complutum, of Justus and Pastor, brothers, who, while they were still [0337] boys being instructed in letters, threw away their tablets in the school and ran of their own accord to martyrdom. And forthwith ordered by Dacianus to be seized, when they strengthened one another most steadfastly with mutual exhortations, they were led out beyond the city, and had their throats cut by the executioners.

## The seventh day before the Ides · 7 August

[0339]

At Tuscia, in the city of Aretium, the birthday of blessed Donatus, bishop and martyr, under the emperor Julian. Who, as Gregory the pope records in the books of the Dialogues, restored, by lingering at mass, the holy chalice broken by the pagans. At Rome, of the holy martyrs Peter and Julian, with eighteen others. At Milan, of Saint Faustinus, martyred in the time of Aurelius Commodus

## The sixth day before the Ides · 8 August

[0343]

At Rome, on the Ostian Way, the birthday of blessed Ciriacus, deacon, and of the holy martyrs Largus and Smaragdus, with twenty others, in the time of the emperor Diocletian. [nt: The place where the commemoration of Saint Severus is now read was once erased. ## The fifth day before the Ides · 9 August

[0345]

The vigil of Saint Laurence, martyr. At Colonia of Tuscia, the birthday of Saints Secundianus, Marcellianus and Verianus, who, in the time of Decius, under the consular Promotus, were first beaten, then hung upon the rack, and scraped with hooks, and roasted with fire applied to their sides, and, beheaded, merited the triumphal palm of martyrdom. At Rome, of Saint Romanus, a soldier, who, pricked to the heart by the confession of blessed Laurence, asked to be baptized by him, and forthwith, at the command of Decius, was brought forth with cudgels and beheaded.

## The fourth day before the Ides · 10 August

[0349]

At Rome, on the Tiburtine Way, the birthday of blessed Laurence, archdeacon, who, in the presence of the emperor Decius, after very many torments of prison, of diverse scourgings, of burning plates, at the last roasted on an iron gridiron, completed his martyrdom. On the same day at Rome, of one hundred and sixty-five soldiers.

## The third day before the Ides · 11 August

[0351]

At Rome, between the Two Laurels, the birthday of Saint Tiburtius, who, under the judge Fabianus, when he had walked with bare soles over burning coals and was confessing Christ with the greater constancy, is ordered to be led to the third milestone from the city and there to be put to the sword. Also at Rome, of Saint Susanna, virgin, who, since she was sprung of noble lineage and was the niece of blessed Gaius the pontiff, merited the palm of martyrdom in the time of Diocletian, by the cutting off of her head. In the city of Camberacum, of Saint Gaugericus, bishop and confessor. At the fort *Ebroas*, of Saint Taurinus, bishop.

## The day before the Ides · 12 August

[0353]

In Sicily, in the city of Cathina, the birthday of Saint Euplus, deacon, under Diocletian and Maximian Augusti, who, when he had been tortured a very long time for the confession of the Lord, at length received the palm of martyrdom by the sword striking. At the city *Augustana*, of Saint Hilaria, mother of Saint Afra the martyr, who, while she was keeping watch at her tomb, [0355] was given over to the fire there by the persecutors for the faith of Christ, with Digna and Eumenia and Euprepia. There suffered also on the same day in the aforesaid city Quiriacus [Bouillart: [nt: In place of *on the same day there suffered at Rome under the prefect Pertinax*, which Usuardus added in his own codex, the Caroline autograph had thus: *There suffered also on the same day in the aforesaid city.* No one with sound eyes would doubt that the correction was made by Usuardus. But whence he learned that those martyrs suffered on the same day, that Pertinax was then prefect of the City; that Nimmia and Juliana and twenty other companions are to be joined to Quiriacus, Largio and Crescentianus; and lastly that all those martyrs passed to heaven on the 12th day of August, I plainly do not know. I do not remember seeing any of these things in the Acts of blessed Marcellus in Mombritius, nor anywhere else. But all these things are proof that Usuardus took from Ado and other Martyrologies what he might use as his own, when he had nothing better; and that he read many things which they had never read. Would that the Acts of those holy martyrs which he saw might now come forth from the darkness. For in those which are published there are almost as many fables as lines. Bouillart.] on the same day there suffered at Rome under the prefect Pertinax Quiriacus, etc.], Largio, Crescentianus, Nimmia and Juliana, with twenty others. Also in the city of Falari, the passion of Gracilianus and of Felicissima, virgin, whose mouths were first bruised with stones for Christ, and who then, struck with the sword, received the longed-for crown of martyrdom. In Syria, of Saints Macharius and Julian.

## On the Ides · 13 August

[0357]

At Rome, of blessed Ypolitus, martyr, who, for the glory of confession, at the command of the governor Valerian, bound by the feet to the necks of untamed horses, dragged thus through thistles and thorns, gave up the spirit. There suffered also Concordia his nurse, who, beaten with leaded scourges before him, was martyred: and nineteen others of his household, who were beheaded together. On the same day at Forosillae, the birthday of Saint Cassianus, who, when he had refused to worship, the boys were summoned, to whom he had become hateful by teaching, and the power of killing him was given them; and the weaker their hand was, the heavier they made the punishment of his martyrdom, death being delayed. [nt: Saint Radegundis was added to this article by a contemporary hand indeed, but certainly not the same; yet Sollerius admits her as written by Usuardus, making no mention meanwhile of the former erasure, which he keeps quiet whenever nothing occurs in his codices by which he might bring a charge of interpolation against the Pratensis codex. Nor is there any trace of this erasure in the Dervensis codex, although two whole lines were erased. They were erased, therefore, before any other copy. Therefore by the author. Bouillart.] At Poitiers, of Saint Radegundis, queen, whose life shone far and wide with virtues and miracles.

## The nineteenth day before the Kalends · 14 August

[0361]

The vigil of the Assumption of Saint Mary. At Rome, on the Appian Way, the birthday of blessed Eusebius, presbyter and confessor, who, for the defence of the Catholic faith, was shut up by Constantius Augustus in a certain chamber of his own house, and there, persevering steadfastly in prayer, received the falling-asleep.

## The eighteenth day before the Kalends · 15 August

[0365]

The falling-asleep of Saint Mary, the Mother of God, whose most sacred body, although it is not found upon the earth, yet holy mother Church so keeps her venerable memory as a feast, that she does not doubt that she passed away according to the condition of the flesh. But where that venerable temple of the Holy Spirit has been hidden by the divine will and counsel, the sobriety of the Church has chosen rather, with piety, not to know, than to teach anything frivolous and apocryphal by holding it from that source. At Rome, on the Appian Way, of Saint Tharsicius, acolyte and martyr, who, when on a certain day he was carrying the Sacraments of the body of Christ, and would not betray them to the pagans who were searching him, was so long wasted by them with cudgels and stones, until he breathed out his spirit.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 16 August

[0367]

In Nicaea of Bithynia, the birthday of blessed Ursacius, confessor, who, under the persecutor Licinius, having abandoned military service and leading a solitary life, shone with such virtues that he is read to have both expelled demons and slain a huge dragon by praying. At the city of the *Metenses*, of Saint Arnulfus, bishop, who, illustrious for holiness of life and the grace of miracles, loving the hermit's life, rested by a blessed end. At Rome, of Saint Serena, once the wife of Diocletian Augustus.

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 17 August

[0371]

At Africa, of the holy martyrs Liberatus, abbot, Boniface, deacon, Servius and Rusticus, subdeacons, Rogatus and Septimus, monks, and Maximus, a boy; who, in the Vandal persecution, by order of king Hunericus, for the confession of the catholic faith and the defence of the one baptism, finished the splendid course of their contest, the Lord crowning them. In Caesarea of Cappadocia, the birthday of Saint Mammes, martyr, who suffered while Aurelian was emperor, under the governor Alexander. On this same day, the Octave of Saint Laurence, martyr.

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 18 August

[0373]

At the city of Praeneste, at the thirty-third milestone from the City, the birthday of Saint Agapitus, martyr, who, when he was fifteen years old and burned for martyrdom with the love of Christ, was seized by order of the emperor Aurelius, and first beaten a very long time with raw sinews: then from the governor Antiochus he suffers grievous tortures. Thereupon, when by the command of the emperor he was being exposed to lions, and was not harmed at all, he is struck with the sword of the attendants, to be crowned. On the same day at Rome, of the blessed presbyters John and Crispus, who in the persecution of Diocletian buried very many bodies of the saints most dutifully, and who afterwards, themselves also joined to their merits, merited the joys of life. On the Lavican Way, of Saint Helena, mother of the emperor Constantine.

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 19 August

[0377]

The birthday of Saint Magnus, or of Saint Andrew, martyr, with his companions, two thousand five hundred and ninety-seven. In the district *Sigistericus*, of blessed Donatus, presbyter, who, endowed with the wonderful grace of God from the very rudiments of infancy, led the anchoritic life for many years, and, renowned for the glory of miracles, passed to Christ. At Rome, of Saint Julius, senator and martyr, who, at the command of the emperor Commodus, was beaten with cudgels so long, until he gave up the spirit. In the territory of Bourges, of Saint Marianus, confessor.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 20 August

[0379]

Of Samuel the prophet, whose sacred bones, as Jerome writes, Arcadius Augustus translated from Judaea to Thrace. On the same day, of blessed Porphirius, man of God, who instructed the holy martyr Agapitus in the faith and doctrine of Christ. On the island Herio, of Saint Philibertus, abbot, who, after temporal military service, bound over to the novitiate of Christ, became a devoted founder of monasteries. In the city of Cordoba, of the holy monks Leovigildus and Christopher, whose bodies, after the cutting off of the head, were given over to the fire

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 21 August

[0383]

In the territory of the city *Gavalitana*, the passion of Saint Privatus, bishop and martyr, who suffered in the persecution of Valerian and Gallienus. On the same day, the birthday of the holy martyrs Bonosus and Maximianus, whose acts are extant. And in the city of Salona, of Saint Anastasius, martyr, who, for the confession of the name of Christ, was slain at the command of the emperor Aurelius.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 22 August

[0385]

At Rome, on the Ostian Way, the birthday of blessed Timotheus, martyr, who, seized by the prefect of the City, and wasted by a long custody in prison, when he had refused to sacrifice to idols, was beaten a third time and handled with most grievous tortures, and at the last was beheaded. On the same day at Augustudunum, of Saint Simphorianus, in the time of the emperor Aurelius, who, since he would not sacrifice to idols, was first afflicted with blows, then handed over, and at the last, his head cut off, consummated his martyrdom. At the Roman Port, of the holy pilgrim martyrs Martialis, Epictetus, Saturninus, Aprilis and Felix, with their companions. Also at Rome, of Saint Antonius, martyr, who, since he confessed himself a Christian freely, at the command of the judge Vitellius was condemned by capital sentence, and was buried on the Aurelian Way.
