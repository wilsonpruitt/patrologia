## On the Nones · 5 September

⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0439]

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At Rome, of blessed Victorinus, martyr, who, illustrious for holiness and miracles, obtains the priesthood of the city *Amiternina* by the election of the whole people. ⟦s2⟧ Then afterwards, under the emperor Nerva, he was ordered to be hung head downward, in a place where stinking and sulphurous waters flow out; and when he had suffered this for three days for the name of Christ, gloriously crowned, he passed as victor to the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ Also at Rome, in Portus, the birthday of Saint Herculanus. ⟦s4⟧ At Capua, of the holy martyrs Quintus, Arcontius and Donatus. ⟦s5⟧ On the same day [In Bouillart, [nt: In the Caroline autograph: *On the same day, of Saint Bertinus, abbot*: and since this was against the custom of our author, he deleted the whole line and restored it in a smaller form of letters, so that there might be room in which to designate the place where Bertinus was chiefly venerated. And that place was fittingly designated by these words, *in the district Tarvenensis*. For at that time the monastery which Bertinus first ruled, or on whose building he put the finishing hand, stood some leagues distant from Tarvenna. But now it is enclosed within the walls of Saint-Omer. Bouillart.] in the district *Tarvenensis*, instead of *Eodem die*], of Saint Bertinus, abbot.

## The eighth day before the Ides · 6 September

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0441]

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Of Zacharias the prophet, who, returning as an old man from Chaldea into his homeland, lies buried beside Aggeus the prophet. ⟦s2⟧ In Africa, of the blessed confessors and bishops Donatianus, Presidius, Mansuetus, Germanus, Fuscolus, who for the assertion of the catholic truth were most cruelly beaten and driven out into exile. ⟦s3⟧ Among whom also a bishop, Letus by name, an energetic and most learned man, after long-lasting filth of prison, was burned up by fire. ⟦s4⟧ Also in Cappadocia, in the city Reaten, of Saint Cottidi, deacon. [Bouillart adds in the margin: [nt: This commemoration, 'At Senones, of the holy siblings', etc., did not creep from the margin into the text, as Sollerius conjectured from ambiguous indications: but it is read even now in the margin, and indeed the upper one. Bouillart.] At Senones, of the holy siblings Sanctianus and Beata, and of Saint Augustine, bishop.]

## The seventh day before the Ides · 7 September

⟦p5⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0445]

⟦p6⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At Nicomedia, the birthday of the most blessed John, martyr. ⟦s2⟧ He, kindled by an exceeding heat of faith, laid his hand on the book in which the cruel edicts against the Christians were contained, pulled it down, and tore it apart; and when this had been reported to Diocletian and Maximian, the Augusti, who were stationed in the same city, raging with exceeding cruelty they ordered every kind of torment to be spent upon him, which the most noble man bore with such great cheerfulness of face and spirit that he could not even have been seen to be sad on account of them. ⟦s3⟧ In the city of Orléans, the deposition of Saint Evurtius, confessor, who, as his acts testify, was first a subdeacon of the holy Roman Church, then by divine gift was designated through a dove as pontiff of the aforesaid city. ⟦s4⟧ In the territory *Augustudunensis*, of Saint Regina, virgin, who, under the proconsul Olibrius, having suffered the torments of the rack, of prison and of lamps, was at length ordered to be ended by capital sentence. ⟦s5⟧ Also in the territory of Paris, of Saint Chlodoaldus, presbyter, [nt: In the Caroline autograph, 'of Saint Chlodoaldus, presbyter and confessor'. Sollerius, who wished to admit nothing but the original, ought not in this matter to have preferred the Pratensis codex to his own: for that eulogy was added by Usuardus. Bouillart.] conspicuous for nobility as much of birth as of mind.

## The sixth day before the Ides · 8 September

⟦p7⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0449]

⟦p8⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The nativity of the most blessed Mary, Mother of God, ever virgin. ⟦s2⟧ At Nicomedia, the birthday of Saint Adrianus, martyr, with twenty-three others, who all, after many tortures, consummated their martyrdom by the breaking of the legs, under Diocletian Augustus. ⟦s3⟧ At Alexandria, of Saints Ammon, Theophilus, Neotherius and twenty-two others. ⟦s4⟧ Also at Antioch, of Timotheus and Faustus.

## The fifth day before the Ides · 9 September

⟦p9⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0451]

⟦p10⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At Nicomedia, the passion of the blessed martyrs Dorotheus and Gorgonius, who, in the presence of Diocletian, when they detested the persecution which he was bringing upon the Christians, were ordered first to be hung up and torn with scourges over the whole body: thereafter, their entrails laid bare of the skin, to be drenched with vinegar and salt, and so to be roasted on a gridiron, and at the last to be killed by the noose. ⟦s2⟧ But after an interval of time blessed Gorgonius was translated to Rome and laid on the Latin Way. ⟦s3⟧ Among the Sabines, at the thirtieth milestone from the City, of Saints Jacinctus, Alexander and Tiburtius. ⟦s4⟧ In the territory *Taruanensis*, [nt: In all the copies, the Dervensis too, these are the words: *In the territory Tarvennensis*. Yet in the Pratensis they are written on an erasure. The erasure, then, is older than all the copies; made, therefore, by the author. Bouillart.] of Saint Autmarus, bishop and confessor. ⟦s5⟧ In Scotia, of Queranus, abbot.

## The fourth day before the Ides · 10 September

⟦p11⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0455]

⟦p12⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At Rome, of blessed Hilary, pope. ⟦s2⟧ At Africa, the birthday of the holy bishops Nemesianus, Felix, Lucius: also of Felix, Litteus, Polianus, Victor, Jader and Dativus, who under Decius and Valerian, as the rage of persecution rose up, at the first steadfastness of the confession of Christ were grievously beaten with cudgels, then bound with fetters, and assigned to dig the mines, and consummated the contest of a glorious confession. ⟦s3⟧ There is extant a letter of blessed Cyprian written to them and about them. ⟦s4⟧ On the same day at Chalcedon, of Saints Sosthenes and Victor, who for the confession of the faith, under the consul Priscus, overcoming prison, chains and beasts, at length, called from heaven in prayer, gave up the spirit.

## The third day before the Ides · 11 September

⟦p13⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0457]

⟦p14⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At Rome, on the old Salarian Way, the birthday of the holy martyrs Protus and Jacinctus, eunuchs, who, under the emperor Gallienus, having been caught because they were Christians, are compelled to sacrifice; but, not consenting, they are first most cruelly beaten, and then are beheaded together.

## The day before the Ides · 12 September

⟦p15⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0461]

⟦p16⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At the city of Ticinum, of the holy confessors Syrus and Iventius, who, sent by blessed Hermagoras to the aforesaid city, first preaching the Gospel of Christ there, and flashing with great powers and miracles, illuminated the neighbouring cities also with divine works; and so, in pontifical honour, they rested with a glorious end in peace.

## On the Ides · 13 September

⟦p17⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0463]

⟦p18⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At Egypt, in the city of Alexandria, of blessed Philip, bishop, father of Saint Eugenia, virgin. ⟦s2⟧ He, deserting the dignity of the prefecture and obtaining the grace of baptism, afterwards attained the honour of the episcopate; and while he was set in prayer, Perennius the prefect ordered him to have his throat cut with the sword, in the time of the emperors Volusianus and Gallienus. ⟦s3⟧ On the same day, of Saint Amatus, presbyter and abbot of the monastery of Romaricus, who, a follower of wonderful abstinence and of the anchoritic life, led a way of life made illustrious by the grace of virtues and miracles. ⟦s4⟧ At Angers, of Saint Maurilio, bishop and confessor.

## The eighteenth day before the Kalends · 14 September

⟦p19⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0467]

⟦p20⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The exaltation of the holy cross, when the emperor Heraclius brought it back from Persis to Jerusalem. ⟦s2⟧ But also, as the years ran on, Pope Sergius, the Lord revealing it, found a portion of wonderful size of the same wood in the sacristy of blessed Peter, which every year, on the very day of its exaltation, is kissed and adored by all the people. ⟦s3⟧ At Rome, on the Appian Way, of blessed Cornelius, pope, whose mouth, after the banishment of exile, Decius ordered to be beaten with leaded scourges, and so to be beheaded with twenty-one others of mixed sex. ⟦s4⟧ But also Cerealis, a soldier, with his wife Salustia, whom the same Cornelius had instructed in the faith, was beheaded on the same day. ⟦s5⟧ In Africa, of Saint Cyprian, bishop, who under the emperor Valerian, after a long exile, consummated his martyrdom by the cutting off of the head, at the sixth milestone from Carthage, beside the sea. ⟦s6⟧ There are reported as having suffered with him Crescentianus, Victor, Rosula and Generalis.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 15 September

⟦p21⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0471]

⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At Rome, on the Numentan Way, the birthday of blessed Nicomedes, presbyter and martyr, under the emperor Domitian. ⟦s2⟧ When he said to those compelling him to sacrifice: I do not sacrifice except to God almighty, who reigns in the heavens, he was beaten for a very long time with leaded scourges and passed to the Lord. ⟦s3⟧ On the same day, in the territory *Cabilonensis*, of Saint Valerianus, martyr, whom the governor Priscus, having him hung up and tortured with heavy tearing of the claws, when he saw him immovable in the confession of Christ and persisting with joyful mind in His praises, caused to be put to the sword. ⟦s4⟧ At Tullum, of Saint Aper, bishop and confessor. ⟦s5⟧ Also at Carnotum, of Saint Leobinus, bishop.

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 16 September

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0473]

⟦p24⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At the city of Chalcedon, the birthday of Saint Eufemia, virgin and martyr, who, under the proconsul Priscus, overcame for Christ torments and prisons, blows and the contrivances of wheels, fires and the weights of angular stones, beasts and strokes of rods, axes and fiery frying-pans, and again the bite of a beast. ⟦s2⟧ At Rome, of the holy martyrs Lucia, a noble matron, and Geminianus, whom Diocletian, grievously afflicted with punishments and long tortured, at length, after the praiseworthy victory of martyrdom, ordered to be put to the sword.

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 17 September

⟦p25⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0477]

⟦p26⟧ ⟦s1⟧ At Rome, on the Tiburtine Way, the birthday of Saint Justinus, presbyter, who in the persecution of Decius, Gallus and Volusianus was most distinguished in the glory of confession. ⟦s2⟧ Also at Rome, of the holy martyrs Narcissus and Crescentio. ⟦s3⟧ At Leodium, of blessed Lambert, bishop *Tungrensis*, who, slain innocently by the guilty, entered the hall of the heavenly kingdom, to live for ever. ⟦s4⟧ In the Britains, of Saints Socrates and Stephen. ⟦s5⟧ At Nivedunum, of the holy martyrs Valerianus, Macrinus and Gordianus. ⟦s6⟧ Also at Cordoba, of Saint Emilianus, deacon, and of Hieremias, beheaded.

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 18 September

⟦p27⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0481]

⟦p28⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The birthday of blessed Methodius, bishop of Olimpus of Licia and afterwards of Tyre, who under Diocletian, at Chalcis of Greece, as Saint Jerome writes, was crowned with martyrdom. ⟦s2⟧ At Vienne, of Saint Ferreolus, who, since he was of tribunician power, was seized by order of a most impious governor, and first most cruelly beaten, then loaded with the heavy weight of chains, was thrust into a most foul prison, and afterwards received the palm of martyrdom by the cutting off of the head.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 19 September

⟦p29⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0483]

⟦p30⟧ ⟦s1⟧ In Naples of Campania, the birthday of the saints Januarius, bishop of the city *Beneventana*, Festus and Proculus, deacons, Desiderius, Euticus and Acuti, who after chains and prisons were beheaded in the city *Puteolana*, under the emperor Diocletian. ⟦s2⟧ Also in Palestine, of the holy martyrs Peleus and Nilus, bishops, who in the time of persecution were consumed by fire for Christ with very many clergy. ⟦s3⟧ At Nuceria, the birthday of the holy martyrs Felix and Constantia, who suffered under Nero. ⟦s4⟧ In the territory of the city of Langres, of Saint Sequanus, presbyter.

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 20 September

⟦p31⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0487]

⟦p32⟧ ⟦s1⟧ In Cyzicus, the birthday of the holy martyrs Fausta, virgin, and Evilasius, under the emperor Maximian; whom the same Evilasius, since he was first of the palace, ordered first to be dishonoured, hung up, tortured, and to be sawn through the middle like wood; but afterwards, himself also believing in Christ, he underwent the contest together with Fausta. [nt: The Caroline autograph inserts between the first and second announcements these words: 'Also in the times of the emperor Hadrian, the passion of the blessed Eustachius, surnamed Placidus, and of his wife Theospis, whose wonderful acts are read.' Nor has it a word of Eulogius of Cordoba. Yet Sollerius on this day rejected the Heriniensis codex, which was most pure. For what cause, I ask? Four lines had been blotted out in the Pratensis codex; the Heriniensis, his Achilles, was against the Pratensis. Was there anything else, anywhere, as a cause why he should reject the Pratensis? For the rest, I do not wonder that Usuardus struck Eustachius out of his codex: but I wonder greatly that he could ever have admitted him. Concerning Eulogius of Cordoba, on what day he won the palm of martyrdom, this one or II March, there is great controversy. That Usuardus, a contemporary of Eulogius, almost an eyewitness of his martyrdom, was mistaken is not credible. I embrace Sollerius's opinion and declare it in his own words: 'To me certainly there is no doubt that Usuardus, who beheld the contests of Cordoba almost at close quarters, had a reason weighty for that time why he should place Eulogius on this day rather than on 11 March.' Bouillart.] In the city of Cordoba, of blessed Eulogius, presbyter, beheaded for the martyrdom of Christ. ⟦s2⟧ In Phrygia, of Saints Dionysius and Privatus.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 21 September

⟦p33⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [0491]

⟦p34⟧ ⟦s1⟧ The birthday of blessed Matthew, apostle and evangelist, who, preaching at Ethiopia, suffered martyrdom. ⟦s2⟧ His Gospel, written in the Hebrew tongue, was found, himself revealing it, in the time of the emperor Zeno. ⟦s3⟧ On the same day, on the Claudian Way, at the twentieth milestone from the City, the passion of Saint Alexander, bishop, who for the faith of Christ, under the emperor Antoninus, overcoming chains, cudgels, the rack, burning lamps, the tearing of the claws, beasts and the flames of the furnace, at length by the sword obtained a glorious life. ⟦s4⟧ Also at Rome, of Saint Pamphilus, martyr.
