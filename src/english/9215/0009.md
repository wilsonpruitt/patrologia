## The tenth day before the Kalends · 22 September

[0493]

In the Gauls, in the city of Sedunum, at the place Acaunum, the birthday of the holy martyrs the Thebeans, Maurice, Exuperius, Candidus, Victor, Innocentius, Vitalis, with their companions, six thousand five hundred, who, suffering for Christ under Maximian, were most gloriously crowned. At Beuuaria, of Saint Emerammus, bishop and martyr. He persuaded that a crime of debauchery committed by a certain man should be imputed to himself for the sake of Christ, and accordingly bore patiently the peril of death. In the district of Poitiers, of Saint Florentius, presbyter and confessor. Also, of Saint Silvanus, confessor, in the territory of Bourges.

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 23 September

[0497]

In Campania, the birthday of blessed Sosius, deacon of the city *Messenata*. When at a certain time he was reading the Gospel in the church of the aforesaid city, in the presence of Bishop Januarius, the same bishop suddenly saw a flame rising from his head, and declared that he would be a martyr. And after not many days, when he was thirty years old, he received martyrdom with the same bishop by the cutting off of the head. On the same day, of Saint Tecla, virgin, of the city of Iconium, who in the confession of Christ vanquished fire and beasts, and after many contests, coming to Seleucia for the teaching of many, rested in peace. In the territory of the city of Constantia, of Saint Paternus, bishop and confessor.

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 24 September

[0501]

The conception of Saint John the Baptist. And at Augustidunum, the birthday of Saint Andochius, presbyter, Tyrsus, deacon, and Felix, who, sent from the East by Policarpus the bishop to teach Gaul, were most gloriously crowned under the emperor Aurelian. For, beaten with whips, at the last hung up the whole day by their hands turned back, sent into the fire but not burned, at length they are struck on the necks with bars.

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 25 September

[0503]

The birthday of blessed Cleophas, who, they relate, was slain by the Jews at the village of Emaus, in the same house in which he had prepared a table for the Lord, for the confession of Him, and was buried with glorious memory. In the city of Amiens, of blessed Firminus, bishop, who under the governor Rictionarus, after various torments, took up martyrdom by the beheading of his head. At Lyon, the deposition of Saint Lupus, bishop, formerly an anchorite. On this same day, of Saint Herculanus, [0505] soldier, who is read of in the passion of Alexander the bishop. At Auxerre, of Saint Aunarius, bishop and confessor. [Bouillart adds: Also of Saint Sollempnensis, bishop.]

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 26 September

[0507]

The birthday of the holy martyrs Cyprian, bishop, and Justina, virgin; of whom Justina, having suffered many things for Christ, converted to Christ Cyprian himself also, when he was a magician and was trying to drive her mad with his magic arts; with whom afterwards, when he had now been made a bishop and a noble teacher, she took up martyrdom. Their bodies were afterwards carried to Rome by Christians. In the city of Albanum, of Saint Senator.

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 27 September

[0511]

At the city of Egea, the birthday of the holy martyrs Cosmas and Damian, who, in the persecution of Diocletian, after many torments, chains and prisons, after the sea and fires, the cross, stoning and arrows had been overcome by divine power, are beheaded. There are reported also as having suffered in their company three full brothers of theirs, that is, Anthimus, Leontius and Euprepius. On the same day, of the holy brothers Adulfus and John, who were crowned for Christ at the city of Cordoba. At the fortress Pseudunum, of Saint Florentinus, who, together with blessed Hilary, after the cutting out of the tongue, was ordered to be struck with the sword.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 28 September

[0515]

At Toulouse, of Saint Exuperius, bishop and confessor, who, the blessed man, how sparing he was to himself and how generous to others, Saint Jerome has set out in a memorable account. At Rome, the birthday of Saint Stacteus. In the city of Genoa, of Saint Salonis, bishop and confessor.

## The third day before the Kalends · 29 September

[0517]

On Mount Garganus, the venerable memorial of blessed Michael the archangel, where a church consecrated in his name is held, made in a mean fashion, but endowed with heavenly power. In Thrace, the birthday of Saints Euticus, Plautus and Eraclea. At Auxerre, of Saint Fraternus, bishop and confessor.

## The day before the Kalends · 30 September

[0521]

In the Gauls, at the fortress Solodorum, the passion of the holy martyrs Victor and Ursus, of the glorious legion of the Thebeans, who, first tortured with dire punishments, but, a heavenly light flashing over them and the ministers falling to the earth, were delivered; then sent into the fire, but in nothing at all harmed, at the last were consummated by the sword. On the same day, at Bethlehem of Judah, the deposition of blessed Jerome, presbyter, who died in the ninety-eighth year of his age, in the sixth month. In the city of Placentia, of Saint Antoninus, confessor.

## OCTOBER HAS 31 DAYS.

[0525]

## On the Kalends · 1 October

[0525]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Arethas, martyr, and of five hundred and four others. In the city of Thomis, of Saints Priscus, Crescens and Evagrius. At the province of Lusitania, in the city of Olisepona, of the holy martyrs Verissimus, Maxima and Julia, sisters. In the city of Tornacum, the passion of Saint Piato, presbyter, who with blessed Dionysius, bishop, and his companions went from the city of Rome to Gaul for the sake of preaching, and afterwards, his martyrdom consummated, passed to Christ. On the same day, the translation of the holy confessors Germanus and Remigius, of whom the former ruled the church of Auxerre, but the other that of Reims. In the port of Ganda, of Saint Bavo, confessor. [Bouillart adds, in italic type: And the translation of the body of Saint Vedastus, bishop.]

## The sixth day before the Nones · 2 October

[0529]

At Nicomedia, the birthday of Saint Eleutherius, martyr, with innumerable others, of whom, at the command of the emperor Diocletian, some were cut down with swords, some were burned with fires, some were thrown headlong into the sea; but the first of these, Eleutherius, when, long tortured, he was rendered stronger through each torment, completed the martyrdom of his victory, tried like gold by fires. In the territory *Adartensis*, the passion of blessed Leodegarius, bishop *Augustudunensis*, whom, afflicted for the truth with various injuries and diverse tortures, Ebroinus, mayor of the royal house, slew. On this same day, of Saint Serenus, confessor.

## The fifth day before the Nones · 3 October

[0533]

The birthday of blessed Dionisius, bishop and martyr, who, after a most illustrious confession of the faith, after the gravest kinds of torments, was crowned with a glorious martyrdom, as Aristides the Athenian testifies, a man wonderful in faith and wisdom, in that work which he composed on the Christian religion. At the Old Saxons, of the holy martyrs, the two Ewaldi, who, when they were presbyters and had begun to preach Christ there, were seized by the pagans and killed; whose bodies a great light, appearing long by night, declared both where they were and of what merit they were. At Rome, at *Ursus pilleatus*, of Saint Candidus, martyr.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 4 October

[0537]

At Corinth, the birthday of the blessed Crispus and Caius, whom Saint Paul the apostle mentions, writing to the Corinthians. In Egypt, of Saints Mark and Marcianus, brothers, and of innumerable others with them, of no inferior glory, of either sex and age; of whom some, after blows and horrible tortures of diverse kinds, were given over to flames, others thrown headlong into the sea, not a few also beheaded, very many consumed by hunger, others fastened to gibbets, some also, the head pressed downward and the feet raised on high, merited the most blessed crown of the passion. In the city of Paris, of Saint Aurea, virgin.

## The third day before the Nones · 5 October

[0541]

At Sicily, the birthday of the holy martyrs Placidus, Euticius and thirty others. In Eumenia, of blessed Traseus, bishop, consummated by martyrdom at Smyrna. In the Gauls, in the city of Valentia, of Saint Apollinaris, bishop, whose life was both distinguished by virtues, and his death no less is adorned with signs and prodigies. At Auxerre, the deposition of the holy siblings Firmatus, deacon, and Flaviana, virgin.

## The day before the Nones · 6 October

[0543]

At Capua, the birthday of the holy martyrs Marcellus, Castus, Emilius and Saturninus. On the same day, of blessed Sagaris, martyr and bishop of Laodicea, who was one of the ancient disciples of Paul the apostle. In the city of Agennum, the birthday of Saint Fides, virgin and martyr, by whose example blessed Caprasius was animated to the contest of martyrdom.

## On the Nones · 7 October

[0547]

At Rome, on the Appian Way, the deposition of blessed Mark, pope. At the province which is called Augusta Eufratesia, of the holy martyrs Sergius and Bachus, under the emperor Maximian; of whom Bachus was beaten so long with raw sinews, until, wholly torn apart in body, he gave up the spirit in the confession of Christ: but Sergius, shod on his feet with nailed boots, when he remained fixed in the faith, sentence having been given, was ordered to be beheaded. Also at the same province, of Saint Julia, virgin, who under the governor Marcianus consummated her martyrdom. On the same day, of the holy martyrs Marcellus and Apuleius, who, through the Apostle Peter, having left Simon, gave themselves over to the apostolic teaching, and afterwards, under Aurelian the consular, carried off the crown of martyrdom, being buried not far from the city of Rome.

## The eighth day before the Ides · 8 October

[0551]

The birthday of the most blessed old man Simeon, who is read in the Gospel to have received the Lord in his arms. At Thessalonica, the passion of Saint Demetrius, martyr. Also in the city of Seville, of Saint Peter, martyr. In the territory of Laon, of Saint Benedicta, virgin and martyr. On this same day, of Saint Pelagia, who is called the sinner.

## The seventh day before the Ides · 9 October

[0555]

Of Abraham the patriarch. At Paris, the birthday of the holy martyrs Dionisius, bishop, Rusticus, presbyter, and Eleutherius, deacon; which blessed bishop, directed by the Roman pontiff into the Gauls for the sake of preaching, came to the aforesaid city, where for some years ardently pursuing the work committed to him, at length by the prefect Fescenninus, together with his companions, put to the sword, he completed his martyrdom. At Julia, on the Claudian Way, of Saint Domninus, martyr, under Maximian, who, when he wished to decline the rage of persecution, being at once pursued, and nonetheless struck with the sword, fell gloriously.
