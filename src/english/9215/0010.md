## The sixth day before the Ides · 10 October

[0557]

At Crete, of blessed Pinitus, most noble among the bishops, who in his writings left behind, as in a mirror, a living image of himself. In the city of Cologne, of Saint Gereon, martyr, surnamed Mallosus, with three hundred and eighteen others, who in the persecution of the tyrant Maximian submitted their necks patiently to the swords for true piety. [0559] Also in the territory of the same city, of Saint Victor, martyr, and seventeen others. Also of the holy martyrs Cassius and Florentius, with many others. In Britain, of Saint Paulinus, bishop and confessor.

## The fifth day before the Ides · 11 October

[0561]

At Tarsus, the metropolis of Cilicia, the birthday of the most blessed martyrs Tharacus, Probus and Andronicus, who in the persecution of Diocletian, afflicted for a long time with the filth of prison, and a third time tried with diverse torments and tortures, obtained the triumph of glory in the confession of Christ. In the district *Vilcasinus*, the passion of the saints Nigasius, [0563] presbyter, and his companions Quirinus and Pientia. [nt: Not certain words here, not a few lines, but the article from head to foot has been wretchedly defiled and torn with the knife, and no trace of the old article remains. Turn over your codices, Reverend Father: where has the original simplicity of Usuardus gone, if not the author himself but a faithless corrupter brought the corrections into the Pratensis codex? Bouillart.] In Scotia, of Saint Cainichus, abbot. In the city of Uzetia, of Saint Firminus, bishop and confessor.

## The fourth day before the Ides · 12 October

[0565]

At Ravenna, on the Laurentine Way, the birthday of Saint Hedistius, martyr. In Africa, of the holy confessors and martyrs, four thousand nine hundred and seventy-six, under King Hunnericus, who, since they were bishops, presbyters, deacons of the Churches of God, with crowds of the faithful peoples joined to them, for the defence of the catholic truth, [0567] were thrust into the exile of a dreadful desert, and at the last variously tortured, celebrated their martyrdom; among whom were Cyprian and Felix, eminent priests of the Lord. In Syria, of Saint Eustachius, presbyter.

## The third day before the Ides · 13 October

At Troas, the birthday of blessed Carpus, disciple of the apostle Paul. In the Spains, in the city of Cordoba, the passion of Saints Faustus, Januarius and Martialis, who, first tortured with the punishment of the rack, then with their eyebrows shaved, their ears also and noses cut off, their upper teeth also pulled out, at the last consummated their martyrdom by the suffering of fire. At Antioch, of Saint Theophilus, who held the pontificate of the Church sixth from the apostle Peter. At Tours, of Saint Venantius, abbot. [Bouillart adds, in italic type: [nt: « We have excluded the Pratensis codex from the pure, says Sollerius, because at the end it has an addition: *On this same day, of the holy confessors Giraldus and Leobonus.* » I pardon him, if he was not advised that those holy confessors were added by a more recent hand. Bouillart.] On this same day, of the holy confessors Giraldus and Leobonus.]

## The day before the Ides · 14 October

[0573]

At Rome, on the Aurelian Way, the birthday of blessed Calistus, pope and martyr. The emperor Alexander ordered him, under prolonged custody, to be tormented with hunger and to be beaten daily with cudgels; but at length, thrown headlong through the window of the house in which he was being guarded, he merited the triumph of victory. In the city *Tudertina*, of Saint Fortunatus, bishop, who shone with the grace of immense power in putting unclean spirits to flight.

## On the Ides · 15 October

[0575]

At Cologne, the birthday of the fifty holy martyrs who, since they were of the sacred legion of the Thebeans, completed the course of their contest at the same city. At Rome, on the Aurelian Way, of Saint Fortunatus at Lyon, of Saint Antiochus, bishop, who, having energetically administered the height of the pontificate to which he had been taken up, obtained the heavenly kingdom. In the territory of Reims, of Saint Basolus, confessor.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 16 October

[0579]

In Africa of two hundred and seventy holy martyrs, crowned together, and of the holy martyrs Martianus and Satirianus with their two brothers, who, for the constancy of the catholic faith, first beaten with knotted cudgels and torn to the bones, when they suffered such things for a long time, were always restored unharmed on the following day; at the last, in the banishment of exile, their feet bound behind the backs of running four-horse chariots, among thorny places of the woods, they were together ordered to perish. Maxima also, a virgin, suffered with them. In the territory [0581] of Bourges, of Saint Ambrose, confessor. [Bouillart: [nt: For 'in the district of Bourges' Sollerius writes 'in the territory of Bourges'. On what authority? Is it because there is an erasure here in the Pratensis codex? But he admits Saint Ambrose *Caturcensis*, set likewise on an erasure. Then he says coldly: *The Pratensis has besides an addition: at the monastery Dervum, of Saint Bercharius, abbot and martyr.* And for that cause Molanus alone is either pure or next to pure. Yet that cause does not seem just, except that he conceals what he knew well, that that addition is of a more recent hand. The Dervensis codex calls Ambrose *Caturcensis* not bishop but confessor, and adds: *Also in the territory of Toul, of Saint Elifius, martyr.* Bouillart.] of Saint Ambrose, bishop *Caturcensis*; and he adds, in cursive type: At the monastery Dervum, of Saint Bercharius, martyr and abbot.]

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 17 October

[0585]

In Antioch, the birthday of Saint Hero, disciple of blessed Ignatius the martyr, who, made bishop after him, follows the way of his master as a dutiful imitator, and fell, a lover of Christ, for the flock entrusted to him. In the city *Arausica*, of Saint Florentius, bishop, who, famous for many virtues, rested in peace. [Bouillart adds: [nt: Sollerius judges the commemoration of Saint Vincent a mere addition, on the authority of the Heriniensis and Tornacensis, most approved codices, although he himself cites ten codices which imitate the Pratensis, and all ours have the same reading. I have long restrained myself from setting my tooth into those two codices. But I have been patient too long. The reader will perhaps wonder how I can lower the authority of codices which I have neither seen nor been able to see: but Sollerius's censure has this convenience, that the defences in which he most trusts he himself betrays, either in good faith or by negligence. How much those two codices ought to be valued, the use which they have so far rendered Sollerius will show, if anything does. On the days of this month I, XI, XV, XVI; of September I, III, VII, X, XX, XXV, XXVII; of August XIX, XXV; of July I, XI, XVIII; of June I, III, VIII, XXI, XXIII; of May I, IX, XXIX; of April XXX; of March XVI, XXV; of February III, X, XV; of January XXIII; both are excluded from the pure: and the days on which one or the other is excluded are so many through the year that I could not bear the tedium of counting them. Here there is an addition, there an omission, now a cutting-down of a eulogy, elsewhere depraved and corrupt readings. From these codices, forsooth, Sollerius corrects the Pratensis. Bouillart.] In the territory of Nevers, of Saint Vincent, presbyter and confessor.]

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 18 October

[0589]

The birthday of blessed Luke the evangelist, who, when he was seventy-three years old, died in Bithynia full of the Holy Spirit, whose bones in the twentieth year of Constantius were translated to Constantinople. On the same day, of Saint Asclepiades, bishop of Antioch, who was one of the illustrious number of confessors who suffered gloriously under Decius. Also at Rome, of Saint Triphonia, wife of Decius Caesar. In the territory of Beauvais, of Saint Justus, martyr.

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 19 October

[0593]

At Alexandria, the metropolis of Egypt, the birthday of the holy martyrs Ptolomeus and Lucius, under Antoninus surnamed Pius; of whom the former, wasted for a long time with the filth of prison, and at the last, when he bore witness to the teaching of Christ by public confession, was ordered to be led to death. The latter also, when he confessed most freely that he was a Christian, received a like sentence. In Antioch of Syria of the holy martyrs Beronicus, Pelagia and forty-nine others. [Bouillart adds in the margin: [nt: Although a more recent hand wrote Saints Savinianus and Potentianus in the margin, Sollerius nonetheless counted the Pratensis among the pure codices. I beg the reader to notice by what reason he was moved to do so: because, he says, what has been set in the margin more recently does no harm to the integrity of the text. He was of the same fairness on 18 January, 21 February, 13 and 25 April, 15 May, 8 and 28 July, 6 September. I ask you, Sollerius, tell me what difference there is between the things that have been set more recently in the margin and those that have been added more recently in the remaining space of the article? ## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 20 October

[0597]

At the province *Aviensis*, the birthday of blessed Maximus, levite and martyr, who, from desire of suffering, showed himself openly to the persecutors seeking him, and after the constancy of his answers, hung upon the rack and tortured, then beaten with cudgels, and at the last overwhelmed, fell. In the Gauls, in the city of Agennum, of Saint Caprasius, martyr, who, when, declining the rage of persecution, he was hiding in a cave, at length, animated to the endurance of sufferings, hastened to the arena of the contest, and by fighting bravely merited the palm of martyrdom. In the city of Cologne, the passion of the holy virgins Martha and Saula with many others. On this same day, the reception of the holy martyrs Georgius, deacon, and Aurelius [nt: The Dervensis codex adds: « On this same day, the deposition of Saint Sindulfus, confessor, who, contending in the arena of contempt of the world, turned the revolving sun to use, and, perfectly despising the present age, followed Christ with his whole heart. Whose conversion, shining with virtues and miracles, declares that he led a celibate life. And who, fleeing effectually the twofold land and leaping forth from it, sowing in tears with the patriarchs, and leaving the fruit of a holy way of life, sacrificing himself for Christ with the apostles, stretching out both hands with the martyrs from the sea of this pilgrimage to the shore, filling his fingers with most approved myrrh with the confessors, answered the bridegroom of his soul with a new song with the virgins, until he should run to meet him upon the mountains of spices, like a roe and a young hart. » These things are drawn from the Life of Saint Sindulfus, which Almannus, monk of Hautvillers, wrote in the ninth century, and our Mabillon edited, tom. I *Act.*, p. 368. Everyone knows that the monasteries of Hautvillers and of Der were first founded by Saint Nivardus: from which kinship it came about that the feast days of both were common. Bouillart.].

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 21 October

[0601]

At Nicomedia, the birthday of the holy martyrs Dasius, Zoticus, Gaius with twelve soldiers. On the same day, of our holy Father Hilarion, whose Life, full of virtues, Jerome writes. In the city of Ostia, of Saint Asterius, presbyter, who is read of in the passion of blessed Calistus, pope. On this same day, of Saint Viator, minister of blessed Justus, bishop of Lyon [nt: In the Dervensis codex, before the last commemoration, *In the city of Lyon, of Saint Cilinia, mother* of the most blessed Remigius, archbishop of Reims. Bouillart.].

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 22 October

[0605]

At Adrianopolis of Thrace, the birthday of the holy martyrs Philip, bishop, Eusebius and Hermes. Also of blessed Mark, bishop, a most illustrious and most learned man, who first from the Gentiles received the church of Jerusalem to rule, and not long after merited the palm of martyrdom. Also of Saint Salome, who is read in the Gospel as solicitous about the Lord's burial. In the city of Osca, of the holy virgins Nunilo and Elodia, who for the confession of Christ, wasted by long custody and punished by capital sentence, celebrated their martyrdom.

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 23 October

[0609]

At Antioch of Syria, the birthday of Saint Theodoricus, presbyter, who, in the persecution of Julian the impious, after the punishment of the rack and many and most harsh tortures, also set aflame with lamps applied about his sides, when he persisted in the confession of Christ, consummated his martyrdom by the slaughter of the sword. In the Spains, of Saints Servandus and Germanus, who, after blows, the filth of prison, the injury of thirst and hunger, and the toil of a very long journey, at the last fulfilled the course of their martyrdom, their necks cut with the iron. Of whom Germanus at Emerita Servandus however at Seville was buried. In the city of Cologne, of Saint Severinus, bishop and confessor. In the district of Poitiers, of Saint Benedict, confessor.

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 24 October

[0613]

In Venusia, a city of Apulia, the birthday of the saints Felix, bishop, Audactus and Januarius, presbyters, Fortunatus and Septimus, readers, who in the times of Diocletian, long wasted with many chains and prisons in Africa and in Sicily, at length were consummated by the slaughter of the sword; Felix died a virgin in the fifty-sixth of his years. At the monastery Vertavum, of Saint Martin, abbot. [In Bouillart, in the margin: In Britain, of Saint Maglorius, bishop and confessor.]

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 25 October

[0617]

At Rome, the birthday of forty-six soldiers, who, baptized together by Dionisius the pope, soon at the command of the emperor Claudius were beheaded, and buried on the Salarian Way, where also one hundred and twenty-one other martyrs were laid: among whom there were four soldiers of Christ, Theodosius, Lucius, Mark and Peter. On the same day, in the city Petragoricas, of Saint Fronto, who, ordained bishop at Rome by blessed Peter, coming to the aforesaid city with Georgius the presbyter, whom through the apostle's staff he received back from death as companion on the journey, converted a great multitude of that people to Christ, and, famous for many miracles, rested in peace. At the city of Suessio, of Saints Crispinus and Crispinianus, who in the persecution of Diocletian, after monstrous torments inflicted on them, slaughtered with the sword, obtained the crown of martyrdom. In the city of Florence, the passion of blessed Miniatis, under the emperor Decius. Also in the city of Gavali, of Saint Hilary, bishop and confessor [nt: In the Dervensis codex the article closes with: *At the metropolis of Reims, of Saint Celsinus, confessor.* Bouillart.].

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 26 October

[0621]

At Africa, the birthday of the holy martyrs Rogatianus, presbyter, and Felicissimus, who in the persecution of Decius and Valerian were crowned with illustrious martyrdom, of whom also blessed Cyprian writes in the letter to the confessors, In the city of Narbonne, of Saint Rusticus, bishop and confessor.

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 27 October

[0625]

In the Spains, in the city of Abula, the passion of Saints Vincent, Sabina and Cristeta, who first were so stretched on the rack that all the joints of their limbs were loosened; then their heads, laid on stones, were crushed with strong bars even to the dashing out of the brain; and so they completed their martyrdom, the governor Dacianus conducting it. On the same day, at the fortress Tile, of Saint Florentius, martyr. [Bouillart adds in the margin: On the same day, the finding or translation of Saint Vincent, levite and martyr.]
