## The fifth day before the Kalends · 28 October

[0629]

The birthday of the blessed apostles Simon the Cananaean and Thadeus, who is also called Judas; of whom Thadeus is related to have preached at Mesopotamia, but Simon at Egypt. Then, entering Persia together, when they had subjected an innumerable multitude of that people to Christ, they consummated their martyrdom. At Rome, of Saint Cirilla, daughter of Decius Caesar, who under the emperor Claudius had her throat cut for Christ and was slain with the sword. In the city of Meldis, of Saint Faro, bishop and confessor. At Paris, the translation of Saint Genevieve, virgin.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 29 October

[0631]

At Jerusalem, the birthday of blessed Narcissus, bishop, whom ecclesiastical history relates to have been praiseworthy for holiness, patience and faith. On this same day, at the city of Sidon, of blessed Zenobius, presbyter, who under the bitterness of the latest persecution was crowned with martyrdom. In the province of Lucania, of the holy martyrs Jacinctus, Quintus, Felicianus and Lucius.

## The third day before the Kalends · 30 October

[0635]

At Africa, the birthday of the holy martyrs, two hundred and twenty in number. In the city *Tingitina*, the passion of Saint Marcellus, centurion, who consummated his martyrdom by the cutting off of the head under Agricolanus, acting as deputy of the praetorian prefects. At Antioch, of Saint Serapion, bishop, most illustrious in learning. [Bouillart adds in the text: On this same day, of Saint Germanus, bishop of Capua; and in the margin, in cursive type: And of Saint Lucanus, martyr.]

## The day before the Kalends · 31 October

[0637]

The vigil of All Saints. At Rome, the birthday of blessed Nemesius, deacon, and Lucilla his daughter, who, when they could in no way be turned from the faith of Christ, at the command of Valerian and Gallienus were beheaded, on the eighth day before the Kalends of September. Here, however, their solemnity is held more venerable, when by blessed Sixtus their bodies [0639] were more honourably entombed on the Appian Way. In the Gauls, in the town *Virmandensis*, of Saint Quintinus, who suffered martyrdom under the emperor Maximian, whose body, after fifty-five years, an angel revealing it, was found.

## On the Kalends · 1 November

[0641]

The festival of the blessed Mother of God and of all the martyrs, which Pope Boniface instituted to be kept, celebrated and general, every year in the city of Rome. But also Gregory the pontiff afterwards decreed that the same be observed solemnly in honour of All Saints perpetually by the whole Church. At the fortress Divio, of Saint Benignus, presbyter, who, sent by blessed Policarpus to preach in Gaul, after he had been afflicted in manifold ways with most grievous torments by Count Terentius, at length it is ordered that his neck be pounded with an iron bar and his body pierced with a lance. In Campania, in the city of Terracina, of Saints Caesarius, deacon, and Julian, presbyter: which Caesarius, in the time of Claudius, was wasted in custody for many days, and afterwards, with the same Julian, put into a sack and so thrown headlong into the sea. In the city of Paris, the deposition of blessed Marcellus, confessor, by whose merits, that he might be shown a worthy future prelate, water is changed now into wine, now into balsam. Also of Saint Severinus, monk, from the city *Tiburtina*. And the birthday of Saint Mary, virgin, who, afflicted with dire lashes, harassed with the stretching of the rack and the tearing of the claws, consummated her martyrdom. Also in the city of Bajocas, of Saint Vigor, bishop. In the district *Vuastinensis*, of Saint Maturinus, confessor.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 2 November

[0645]

The birthday of blessed Victorinus, bishop *Pitabronensis*, who, after many teachings, as Saint Jerome testifies, was crowned with martyrdom in the persecution of Diocletian. At Rome, in the times of the emperor Hadrian, the passion of the blessed Eustachius, surnamed Placidus, and of his wife Theospis, with their sons Agapius and Theospis, whose wonderful acts are read. Their festival, though it is held on the Kalends, is here however kept more fully. At Laodicea, of Saint Theodotus, bishop, who was adorned not only with words, but also with deeds and virtues. On the same day, of Saint [0647] Ambrose, abbot of the monastery *Agaunensis*, who, famous for great virtues of life and holiness, rested in the Lord with a blessed end.

## The third day before the Nones · 3 November

[0651]

The birthday of Saint Quartus, disciple of the apostles. At Caesarea of Cappadocia, of the holy martyrs Germanus, Theophilus, Caesarius and Vitalis, who under the Decian persecution carried through their martyrdom most excellently. At Caesaraugusta, of innumerable holy martyrs, who under Dacianus, governor of the Spains, fell wonderfully for Christ.

## The day before the Nones · 4 November

[0655]

At Alexandria, of blessed Hierius, presbyter, who, nobly learned in the divine Scriptures, of most pure life, and bare and wholly unencumbered for the Christian philosophy, was an incomparable doctor of the Church; but he rested in the time of the emperor Philip. In the Gauls, in the city of Rotenus, of Saint Amantius, bishop, whose life was glorious in holiness and miracles. In the district *Vilcasinus*, of Saint Clarus, presbyter and martyr. At Augustudunum, of Saint Proculus, bishop.

## On the Nones · 5 November

[0659]

Of Zacharias the prophet, father of John the Baptist. In Terracina, a city of Campania, the birthday of Saints Felix, presbyter, and Eusebius, monk, in the time of the emperor Claudius; which Eusebius, when he had buried the holy martyrs Julian and Caesarius, and was converting many to the faith, whom Felix the presbyter baptized, both were seized and led to the judge's forum, and were not overcome; then shut up in prison, and in the same night, when they would not sacrifice, they were beheaded. In the territory of Orléans, of Saint Laetus, presbyter.

## The eighth day before the Ides · 6 November

[0661]

At Toniza of Africa, the birthday of Saint Felix. On whose solemnity blessed Augustine appears to expound a certain psalm to the people. In the East, in the city of Theopolis, of ten holy martyrs, who are read in the acts of the forty saints to have suffered under the Saracens. In Phrygia, of Saint Atticus. [Bouillart adds, at the edge of the text: In the district *Lemovicensis*, at Nobiliacum, the birthday of Saint Leonard, confessor.]

## The seventh day before the Ides · 7 November

[0665]

In Alexandria, of blessed Achillas, bishop, who was distinguished in learning, faith, way of life and morals. At Perusina, a city of Italy, the birthday of Saint Herculanus, bishop, whose body, after the cutting off of the head, was found on the fortieth day so united and sound, as blessed Gregory the pope writes, as if no incision of iron had touched it. On the same day, of blessed Amarantus, martyr, who, at the city *Albigensis*, having finished the course of the faithful contest, buried, lives in glory. In Frisia, the deposition of Saint Willibrord, bishop.

## The sixth day before the Ides · 8 November

[0669]

At Rome, on the Lavican Way, the passion of Saints Claudius, Nicostratus, Simpronianus, Castorius and Simplicius, who were first thrust into prison, then most grievously beaten with scorpions, but when they could not be changed from the faith of Christ, they were ordered by Diocletian to be thrown headlong into the middle of the sea. Also on the Lavican Way, the birthday of the four crowned ones, Severus, Severianus, Carpoforus and Victorinus, who at the command of Diocletian were beaten to death with blows of leaded scourges. Since their names could by no means be found, which, the Lord revealing them, were afterwards shown when years had intervened, it was established that their anniversary day should be kept under the names of the five martyrs above-mentioned.

## The fifth day before the Ides · 9 November

[0673]

At Amasia, a city of the *Marmaritani*, the birthday of Saint Theodore, martyr, who in the times of the emperors Maximian and Maximinus, for the confession of the Christian faith, was severely beaten and sent into prison; then, the Lord Saviour appearing to him and admonishing him to act constantly and manfully, he was relieved. At the last, after he had been hung upon the rack and gnawed with claws, so that his inward parts appeared bare, he is given over to burning fires. On the same day, in the city of Bourges, of Saint Ursicinus, confessor, who, ordained at Rome by the successors of the apostles, is appointed the first bishop to that same city.

## The fourth day before the Ides · 10 November

[0677]

The birthday of blessed Martin, pope, who, for the catholic faith, was seized from the Church by the heretical emperor Constantine, and brought to Constantinople, and banished to Cersona of the province of Licia, ended his life there, shining in the same place to this day with many signs of virtues. In the territory *Agatensis*, of the holy martyrs Tiberius, Modestus and Florentia, who in the time of Diocletian, tortured with various torments, completed their martyrdom. At Antioch, of Saints Demetrius, bishop, Anianus, deacon, Eustosius and twenty others.

## The third day before the Ides · 11 November

[0681]

In the Gauls, in the city of Tours, the birthday of blessed Martin, bishop and confessor, whose life was so glorious with miracles that he merited to be the raiser of three dead men. In Scithia, the metropolis of Phrygia Salutaria, the passion of Saint Menna, martyr, who, in the persecution of Diocletian, after he had striven to serve as a soldier to the heavenly King by a hidden way of life in the desert, coming forth into public and declaring himself a Christian with free voice, first tried with dire tortures, at the last, his knees fixed in prayer and giving thanks to the Lord Jesus Christ, was slain with the sword. Also at Lyon, of Saint Veranus, bishop, whose life was illustrious for the merits of faith and virtues.

## The day before the Ides · 12 November

[0685A]

At the province of Tarragona, in the city of Tirassona, of blessed Emilianus, presbyter and confessor, whose admirable life Braulio, bishop of Caesaraugusta, described in simple speech. In the city of Cologne, the deposition of Saint Cunibertus, bishop. In the territory *Senonicus*, of Saint Paternus, martyr. At the castle Milidunum, of Saint Leonius, confessor.

## On the Ides · 13 November

[0689]

At Ravenna, the birthday of the holy martyrs Valentinus, Solutor and Victor. In the Province, at the city Aquis, of blessed Mitrius, most illustrious martyr. In Africa, of the holy martyrs Archadius, Paschasius, Probus and Euticianus, who, when they would by no means bear to decline into the Arian perfidy, first proscribed, then driven into exile, then tortured with most atrocious punishments, at the last were slain by diverse deaths [Bouillart, at the edge of the page: At Paris, of Saint Gendulfus, bishop.]. At Tours, the deposition of Saint Briccius, bishop. In the city of Toledo, of Saint Eugenius, bishop and confessor. [Bouillart: of Saint Eugenius, prelate. At the monastery Malbodium, of Saint Aldegundis, virgin.]
