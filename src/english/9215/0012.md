## The eighteenth day before the Kalends · 14 November

[0693]

At Thrace, in the city of Eraclea, the birthday of the holy martyrs Clementinus, Theodotus, and Philominus. At Alexandria, of Saint Serapion, whom the persecutors under the emperor Decius afflicted with most cruel punishments, so that, first loosening all the joints of his limbs, they cast him headlong from the heights, and so he was made a martyr of Christ.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 15 November

[0695]

At Nola, a city of Campania, the birthday of blessed Felix, bishop, who from the fifteenth year of his age was distinguished by the glory of miracles, and under the governor Martianus completed his martyrdom with thirty others. On this same day, of Saint Eugenius, who, at the district of Paris, the course of martyrdom being consummated, received from the Lord the crown of a blessed passion. [*In Bouillart, in the margin:* On the same day, of Saint Macutus, bishop and confessor. At the monastery of Saint Germanus, the dedication of the church in honour of Saint John the Baptist, and of Saint John the apostle and evangelist, and of Saint Martialis; of the holy martyrs Laurence, Sebastian, Christopher, and also of the holy confessors Martin, Gregory, Benedict, and of Saint Mary Magdalene.]

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 16 November

[0699]

At Lyon, the birthday of Saint Eucherius, confessor, who, from the most noble order of senators, converted to the religious life and habit, long shut up of his own will within the enclosure of a cave, served Christ afterwards, at the aforesaid city, an angel revealing it, was solemnly set in the pontifical chair.

## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 17 November

[0703]

At Alexandria, of blessed Dionisius, bishop. This man, often quite illustrious in many confessions, and being magnificent for the diversity of passions and torments, rested in the times of the emperors Valerian and Gallienus. In the city of Cordoba, the passion of the holy martyrs Acisclus and Victoria, where, in commendation of their precious death, on the same day roses sprung up by divine power are gathered. At Orléans, of Saint Anianus, bishop and confessor whose death, precious in the sight of the Lord, frequent miracles attest.

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 18 November

[0707]

At Antioch, the birthday of blessed Romanus, who, in the times of Diocletian, when a certain prefect was trying to break into the church and to overturn it from its foundations, exhorted the other Christians to oppose him; whence, together with a certain little child named Barala, he was crowned with a celebrated martyrdom. Also in the same city, of Saint Esichius, who, being a soldier, and having heard the command that whoever did not sacrifice to idols should lay down the belt of military service, at once loosed his belt. For this cause, a huge stone being bound to his right hand, he was ordered to be cast headlong into the river.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 19 November

[0711]

At Rome, on the Appian Way, the birthday of Saint Maximus, presbyter and martyr, who, having suffered in the persecution of Maximian, was laid at Saint Sixtus. On the same day, of Saint Crispinus, bishop, who in the city *Astiagensis*, his head being cut off, obtained the glory of martyrdom. At Vienne, of the holy martyrs Severus, Exuperius and Felicianus, whose bodies, after many courses of years, found by their own revelation, and raised with honour by the bishop of the city, the clergy and the people, were also laid up with fitting honour. Also of Saint Faustus, deacon, this man, very aged and full of days, persevering as a confessor, his martyrdom perfected, was consummated by the cutting off of the head. [*In Bouillart, in the text, in cursive type:* At Tours, of Saint Oddo, abbot; *and at the edge of the page:* At Paris, the dedication of the church in honour of the holy cross and of the holy Mother of God, Mary, and of the holy martyrs Stephen, Vincent, and of the holy confessor Germanus.]

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 20 November

[0715]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Pontianus, pope, who, when Maximinus stirred up a persecution against the priests of the churches, was deported to Sardinia with Ypolitus the presbyter, and, slain with cudgels everywhere, consummated his martyrdom. His body was brought back by blessed Fabian the pope, and buried in the cemetery of Calixtus. At Cabilone, of Saint Silvester, bishop and confessor. This man, in the forty-second year of his priesthood, full of days and of virtues, passed to the Lord. At Sicily, in the city of Messana, of Saints Ampeli and Gaius. On this same day, in the city of Turin, of Octavius, Solutor and Adventor.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 21 November

[0719]

The birthday of the most blessed Rufus, of whom the apostle Paul writes to the Romans. In Italy, at the monastery Bobium, the deposition of Saint Columbanus, abbot; who was the founder of many monasteries and the father of innumerable monks, and, illustrious with many virtues, rested in a good old age. Also in Histria, the passion of Saint Maurus, martyr. [*Bouillart:* The birthday of the most blessed Rufus, of whom the apostle Paul writes to the Romans. At the city *Neocorensis*, of Saint Mary, virgin, who suffered under the emperors Hadrian and Antoninus. In the province of Histria, the passion of Saint Maurus, martyr. In the city of Ostia, of the holy martyrs Demetrius and Honorius.]

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 22 November

[0723]

At Rome, the birthday of Saint Cecilia, virgin, who thoroughly taught her betrothed Valerianus and his brother Tiburtius to believe in Christ and to martyrdom, and afterwards was herself martyred, overcoming fire indeed, but slain by the sword. She suffered, moreover, in the times of the emperors Marcus Aurelius and Commodus. Also at Rome, of Saint Maurus, martyr, who contended under the prefect Celerinus. At Augustudunum, of Saint Pragmatius, bishop.

## The ninth day before the Kalends · 23 November

[0725]

The birthday of Saint Clement, bishop, who, fourth from blessed Peter the apostle, held the pontificate of the Roman Church, and, under the persecution of Trajan cast headlong into the sea, is crowned with martyrdom. On the same day, of Saint Felicitas, mother of seven martyred sons, who at the command of Antoninus was beheaded for Christ. [*Bouillart inserts:* In Italy, of blessed Columbanus, abbot, founder of many monasteries]. In the district *Hasbanius*, of Saint Trudo, presbyter and confessor. At Paris, the deposition of blessed Severinus, monk and solitary. In the city of Emerita, of Saint Lucretia, virgin.

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 24 November

[0729]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Chrisogonus, martyr, who, after long chains and prisons, endured for the most constant confession of Christ, at the command of Diocletian was beheaded and thrown into the sea, and consummated his martyrdom. Also at Rome. of Saint Crescentianus, martyr, who is read of in the passion of blessed Marcellus the pope. At the fortress Blavia, of Saint Romanus, presbyter and confessor, the proclamation of whose holiness the glory of miracles declares. In the city of Cordoba, of the holy virgins Flora and Mary, who after long imprisonments were slain with the sword for Christ. At Perusia, a city of Tuscia, of Saint Felicissimus.

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 25 November

[0733]

The birthday of blessed Peter, bishop of Alexandria, who, being adorned with all virtues, in the divine Scriptures also inferior to none of those before him, both priest and victim of God, is suddenly seized, and by the order of Maximinus is cut off by the head. With whom at the same time many other bishops from Egypt are also slaughtered, about, with clerics and laymen, six hundred and sixty. Also at Antioch, of Saint Erasmus, martyr. [*Bouillart, at the edge of the page:* At Alexandria, the passion of Saint Catherine, virgin and martyr.]

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 26 November

[0737]

The birthday of blessed Linus, pope, who, first after Peter, held the Roman Church for twelve years, and, crowned with martyrdom, was buried in the Vatican. At Alexandria, of the holy martyrs Faustus, presbyter, Dius and Ammonius, who, with blessed Peter, bishop of the same city, at the command of Maximinus were slaughtered. At Augustudunum, of Saint Amator, bishop.

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 27 November

[0741]

At the city *Bononiensis*, the birthday of the holy martyrs Agricola and Vitalis, of whom the latter was before the former's slave, now his partner and colleague in martyrdom. Then, when the same was being forced by the persecutors to deny Christ, and he professed the Lord Jesus Christ the more, they exercised every kind of torment upon him. Last of all they slew blessed Agricola, fastening him to a cross. In the Gauls, in the city *Regensis*, of Saint Maximus, confessor, who, endowed from his earliest age with the grace of all virtues, first Father of the monastery *Lirinensis*, then bishop of the Church *Regensis*, stood out renowned for signs and prodigies.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 28 November

[0743]

At Corinth, the birthday of blessed Sostenes, disciple of Saint Paul, whom the same Apostle mentions, writing to the Corinthians. On the same day, at Africa, of the holy martyrs Papinius and Mansuetus, bishops, who, [0745] in the time of the Vandal persecution, for the defence of the Catholic faith, burned over their whole body with glowing plates of iron, consummated a glorious contest. At Rome, of Saint Rufus, whom Diocletian, having punished him with all his household, made a martyr to Christ.

## The third day before the Kalends · 29 November

[0747]

The vigil of Saint Andrew the apostle. At Rome, on the Salarian Way, the birthday of Saint Saturninus, martyr, and of Sisinnius, deacon, under the emperor Maximian, whom, long wasted in prison, the prefect of the City ordered to be raised upon the rack, and stretched with sinews, and beaten with cudgels and scorpions. Then he ordered flames to be applied to their sides, and, taken down from the rack, their heads to be cut off. On the same day, at Toulouse, of blessed Saturninus, bishop, who, held by the pagans in the Capitol of the same city, and cast headlong from the highest summit of the Capitol down all the steps, his head crushed, his brain dashed out and his whole body torn apart, breathed out a soul worthy of Christ.

## The day before the Kalends · 30 November

[0751]

In the city of Patras, of the province of Achaia, the birthday of Saint Andrew the apostle, who also preached at Scithia. This most blessed man, seized by the proconsul Egea, was first shut up in prison, and then most grievously beaten, but at the last hung upon a cross, survived there two days. At Sanctonas, of Saint Trojanus, bishop, a man of great holiness, who, buried in the earth, shows by many virtues that he lives in heaven.
