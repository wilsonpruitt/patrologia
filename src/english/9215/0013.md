## DECEMBER HAS 31 DAYS.

[0755]

## On the Kalends · 1 December

[0755]

At Rome, the birthday of the holy martyrs Crisantus and Daria, virgin, who, after many passions which they had won for Christ, were ordered by the emperor Numerian to be put down into a sand-pit on the Salarian Way, and there, living, to be overwhelmed with earth and stones. Also at Rome, of the holy martyrs Diodorus, presbyter, and Marianus, deacon, with many others, who at the command of the aforesaid emperor celebrated the glory of martyrdom. At the city of Mainz, the birthday of blessed Albanus, martyr. Also in the city of Noyon, of Saint Eligius, bishop and confessor, whose admirable life a manifold number of signs commends. On this same day, of Saint Nathalia, wife of Saint Adrianus, martyr. In the city of Narni, of Saint Proculus, presbyter.

## The fourth day before the Nones · 2 December

[0759]

The birthday of Saints Verus and Securus, brothers, who were crowned with martyrdom at Africa. At Rome, the passion of Saint Viviana, martyr, who, at the command of Julian the sacrilegious emperor, was beaten with leaded scourges so long, until she gave back her spirit.

## The third day before the Nones · 3 December

[0761]

At Rome, the birthday of the holy martyrs Claudius, a tribune, and his wife Hilaria, and their sons Jason and Maurus, with seventy soldiers, of whom the emperor Numerian ordered Claudius, bound to a huge stone, to be given headlong into the middle of the sea, but the soldiers and the sons of Claudius himself to be punished by capital sentence. At Tingis, the metropolis, the passion of Saint Cassianus, who, long discharging the office of a recorder, at length inspired from heaven, held it execrable to serve, bloodstained, the slaughter of Christians; whence, renouncing that same office, under the Christian confession he merited to obtain the triumph of martyrdom.

## The day before the Nones · 4 December

[0765]

At Pontus, of blessed Meletius, bishop and confessor, who, though he was magnificent by the prerogative of learning, yet the virtue of his mind and the sincerity of his life stood out far more magnificent in him. Also, at Alexandria, of Saint Clement, presbyter, who flourished as greatly as could be in the schools of divine learning. [*Bouillart adds, in the text:* In the district of Bourges, of Saint Syranus [nt: The commemoration of Syranus is in the first hand, with no erasure, added nonetheless, but by Usuardus. That Syranus, or Sigirannus, is well known. Labbe in his Bibliotheca and Mabillon in the Benedictine Acts have made his deeds public. Bouillart.], abbot. *And in the margin:* At the monastery Floriacum, the translation of the most holy confessor of Christ, Benedict, abbot.]

## On the Nones · 5 December

[0769]

In Africa, at the colony *Thebestina*, the birthday of Saint Crispina, martyr, who, in the times of Diocletian and Maximian, when she would not sacrifice, was beheaded by order of the proconsul Anolinus. On the same day, in Italy, of Saint Dalmacius, martyr. In the city of Trier, of Saint Nicecius, bishop, a man of all holiness. Also in the city of Thagora, of Saints Julius and Potamia.

## The eighth day before the Ides · 6 December

[0771]

The birthday of blessed Nicholas, bishop of Myra in Lycia, of whom, among many marks of miracles, this memorable thing is related: that, being set far off, he turned the emperor Constantine from the destruction of certain men to mercy, both by admonitions through a vision and by threats. In Africa, of Saints Dionisia, Dativa, Leontia, and a religious man named Tertius, and Emilianus and Boniface, with three others, who all, for the confession of the Catholic faith, tortured with most grievous, in innumerable, punishments, merited to be joined to the number of the confessors of Christ.

## The seventh day before the Ides · 7 December

[0775]

At Alexandria, the birthday of blessed Agatho, martyr, under the emperor Decius, who, when he was a soldier and prevented certain people who wished to mock the dead corpses of the martyrs, a clamour of the whole populace is suddenly raised against him. Offered, moreover, to the judge, when he persisted in the confession, he was condemned to the loss of his head for piety. In the city Sanctonas, of Saint Martin, abbot, at whose tomb miracles very frequently come to pass by divine power. In the district *Meldicensis*, of Saint Fara, virgin.

## The sixth day before the Ides · 8 December

[0777]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Euticianus, pope, who ruled the Church one year, and under Aurelian was crowned with martyrdom, buried in the cemetery of Calistus, who also himself buried with his own hand, in various places, three hundred and forty-two martyrs. On this same day, at Alexandria, of Saint Macharius, martyr, who, in the time of Decius, when he was being urged by the judge [0779A] with many words to deny, and with so much the greater constancy professed his faith, is at the last ordered to be burned alive. [*Bouillart, at the edge of the page:* The Conception of the most blessed Mother of God, the Virgin Mary.]

## The fifth day before the Ides · 9 December

[0781]

The birthday of Saint Leochadia, virgin, who, by the prefect of the Spains Dacianus, at Toledo, worn out by the harsh [*Bouillart,* [0783] *dira*] custody of prison [nt: At this place a leaf has been torn out of the codex. But he who restored it, because he used larger letters, left this article unfinished and cut short by more than half. What was lacking Sollerius supplied from the Pratensis codex B. I praise the deed and imitate it. Bouillart.], when she had heard of the most grievous tortures of blessed Eulalia and of the rest of the martyrs, with her knees set in prayer, gave back to Christ an undefiled spirit. On this same day, of Saint Cyprian, abbot of Périgueux, a man of magnificent holiness and most renowned in miracles.

## The fourth day before the Ides · 10 December

[0785]

At the city *Hispolitana*, of the holy martyrs Carpoforus, presbyter, and Abundius, deacon, who, in the persecution of Diocletian, first beaten most cruelly with cudgels then thrust back into prison with food and drink denied them, again tortured on the rack, and after this long wasted in prison, were at last struck with the sword. In Spain, in the city of Emerita, the passion of Saint Eulalia, virgin, who, when she was thirteen years old, for the confession of Christ, by order of the governor Dacianus, suffered very many torments, at last hung upon the rack and torn with hooks, burning torches being applied on either side, drawing in the fire gave back her spirit. On the same day, at the aforesaid city, of Saint Julia, martyr, who was a fellow virgin of Saint Eulalia and an inseparable companion to her as she hastened to the passion.

## The third day before the Ides · 11 December

[0789]

At Rome, the birthday of blessed Damasus, pope, and the passion of Saint Traso, martyr, who, since he was most Christian, was seized at the command of Maximinus, and with two others, Pontianus and Praetextatus, crowned with martyrdom. In the city of Amiens, of the holy martyrs Victoricus and Fuscianus. Into the nostrils and ears of these the judge ordered iron spikes to be driven, and their heads to be pierced through with red-hot nails, then their eyes to be torn out, and last of all to shoot through their bodies; and so, together with Saint Gentianus their host, their heads cut off, they passed to the Lord. In the Spains, of Saint Euticius [nt: Eutychius was left out in the Dervensis codex. Bouillart.], whose acts are extant.

## The day before the Ides · 12 December

[0793]

At Narbonne, the birthday of Saint Paul, confessor, whom blessed Paul the apostle, having ordained him, appointed as bishop to that same city: and who, going with the same Apostle to the Spains for the sake of preaching, was left there, where, the office of preaching not slothfully fulfilled, illustrious for miracles, crowned, he rested. In Alexandria, of the holy martyrs Epimachius and Alexander, who, when they had been a long time in chains, and, worn out by various punishments, had persevered in the faith, are ordered at the last to be consumed by fires. Also at Alexandria, of the holy women Ammonaria and Mercuria, with two others, who, having endured and overcome unheard-of kinds of torments, receive the end of all, the sword striking. Also of the holy martyrs Hermogenes, Donatus and twenty-two others. In the district *Vinnacensis*, of Saint Walericus, presbyter and confessor.

## On the Ides · 13 December

[0797]

At Syracuse, a city of Sicily, the birthday of Saint Lucy, virgin and martyr, under the persecution of Diocletian, whom, when the panders wished, at the command of Paschasius the consular, to corrupt her, they could by no means move, nor with ropes added, or with very many yoke of oxen. Pitch, resin, boiling oil she received, harmed in nothing, but at length, a sword plunged into her vitals, she was struck. On this same day, of Saint Autbertus, bishop of Cambrai.

## The nineteenth day before the Kalends · 14 December

[0801]

At Cyprus, the birthday of blessed Spiridion, bishop, who was one of those confessors whom Maximian, their right eyes dug out and the left ham cut, had condemned to the mines; and who, since he led a prophetic life, was also renowned for the glory of signs. At Alexandria, of the holy martyrs Hero, Arsenius, Isidore and Dioscorus, a boy, whom the judge, under the Decian persecution, tearing them with various torments, when he saw them armed alike with the constancy of faith, orders to be given to the fires. But Dioscorus, scourged in many ways. by the divine will was released for the consolation of the faithful. In Antioch, the birthday of the holy martyrs Drusus, Zosimus, and Theodorus. Also at Reims, the passion of Saint Nicasius, bishop, and of Eutropia, virgin, his sister.

## The eighteenth day before the Kalends · 15 December

[0803]

At Africa, of blessed Valerianus, bishop and confessor, who, when he was more than eighty years old, was ordered to be driven out of the city alone, and it was so commanded that no one should let him dwell, neither in a house nor in a field; and thus, lying a long time naked under the open air on the public road, in the confession of Catholic truth and the defence of holiness, he completed the course of a blessed life. In the territory of Orléans, of Saint Maximinus, confessor.

## The seventeenth day before the Kalends · 16 December

[0807]

Of the three children, Ananias, Azarias and Misael, whose bodies are laid at Babylon under a certain cave. In Tuscia, the passion of Saint Barbara, virgin, under the emperor Maximian. She, after the dire wasting of prison and the cutting of the sinews, and the burning of lamps, and the cutting off of the breasts, and the torture of other torments, given at the last to the sword, consummated her martyrdom. At Ravenna, of Saints Valentinus. Naulis and Agricola.

## The sixteenth day before the Kalends · 17 December

[0809]

The translation of Saint Ignatius, bishop and martyr, who, brought from the city of Rome to Antioch, lies outside the gate *Damnitica*, in the cemetery of the church. On the same day, of blessed Lazarus, whom the Lord Jesus is read in the Gospel to have raised from the dead. Also of blessed Martha, his sister, whose venerable memory a church built not far from Bethany, where close by was their house, preserves. In the East, at the city of Eleutheropolis, of fifty holy martyrs, who suffered under the Saracens.
