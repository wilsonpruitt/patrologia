## The fifteenth day before the Kalends · 18 December

[0813]

At Macedonia, in the city of Philippi, the birthday of the blessed martyrs Rufus and Zosimus. These were of the number of the disciples through whom the primitive Church was founded among the Jews and the Greeks, of whose happy contest also Saint Policarpus writes in the epistle to the Philippians. In Africa, the passion of Saint Moyses, martyr, At Tours, of Saint Gatianus, bishop and confessor, who, sent from the city of Rome to that same city as its first bishop, illustrious for many miracles, rested in peace.

## The fourteenth day before the Kalends · 19 December

[0817]

At Egypt, of blessed Nemesius, martyr, who, tortured with redoubled punishments by the judge Emilianus, was ordered to be burned with robbers, bearing the likeness of the Saviour, who together with robbers endured the cross. In the city of Nicaea, of Saint Darius, martyr. Also at Auxerre, of Saint Gregory, bishop.

## The thirteenth day before the Kalends · 20 December

[0819]

At Alexandria, of the most blessed martyrs Ammon, Zeno, Ptolomeus, Ingenius and Theophilus, who, standing by the tribunals, when a certain Christian set in the punishments was trembling, and was now almost inclining to deny, were trying with face, eyes and nods to raise him up. And when for this cause the clamour of the whole populace leapt out against them, bursting forth into the midst, they testify that they are Christians. Through whose victory Christ at once most gloriously triumphed, and gave constancy of mind to his own. In Thrace, in the city of Gelduba, of Saint Julius, martyr. Also in Amphitrea, of Saint Liberatus.

## The twelfth day before the Kalends · 21 December

[0823]

The birthday of blessed Thomas the apostle, who, preaching the Gospel to the Parthians and Medes, suffered in India, and not long after was translated thence to the city of Edessa. On the same day, in Tuscia, of Saints John and Festus.

## The eleventh day before the Kalends · 22 December

[0825]

At Rome on the Lavican Way between the Two Laurels, the birthday of thirty martyrs, who all on one day, in the persecution of Diocletian, were crowned. At Alexandria, of blessed Ischyrion, martyr. He, when he was being forced to sacrifice by blandishments and injuries, and despised them; pierced through the vitals with a sharpened stake, is handed over to death. In the city of Ostia, of Saints Demetrius, Honoratus and Florus. [*Bouillart:* In the city of Ostia, of Saint Basileus.]

## The tenth day before the Kalends · 23 December

[0829]

At Nicomedia, of twenty holy martyrs, whom the Diocletianic persecution, tortured with most grievous torments, made martyrs to Christ, and joined them, blessed, to the number of the saints in the heavens. At Rome, the birthday of Saint Victoria, virgin, under the persecution of the emperor Decius, who, when she was betrothed to a certain pagan, and would neither marry nor sacrifice, after many miracles done, among which she had gathered very many virgins, was struck with the sword in the heart by the executioner, at the request of her betrothed. Also at Rome, of Saint Servulus, of whom blessed Gregory writes that from his earliest age to the end of his life he lay paralysed; at whose tomb miracles very frequently come to pass. At Paris, the dedication of the Basilica in honour of the holy cross and of Saint Vincent, martyr, and the deposition of the lord Childebert, the king.

## The ninth day before the Kalends [nt: (Here, in Bouillart's edition, the year begins.)] · 24 December

[0833]

The vigil of the birthday of the Lord. At Antioch of Syria, the birthday of forty holy virgins, who under the Decian persecution consummated martyrdom through various torments. At Spoleto, a city of Tuscia, of Saint Gregory, martyr and presbyter, in the times of the emperors Diocletian and Maximian, who, first beaten with knotted cudgels, and then, after the gridiron and prison, struck on the knees with iron cards, but also set on fire along his sides with burning lamps, was at length beheaded. In the city of Tripoli, of Saint Lucian.

## The eighth day before the Kalends · 25 December

[0835]

JESUS CHRIST THE SON OF GOD IS BORN in BETHLEHEM OF JUDAH in the forty-second year of Caesar Augustus [*Bouillart inserts:* in the sixty-sixth week, according to the prophecy of Daniel.], of the one hundred and ninety-third olympiad. On the same day, the birthday of Saint Anastasia under the emperor Diocletian, who, for the confession of the Lord worn out by long custody, then carried with two hundred men and seven hundred women to the Palmarian islands, she herself indeed by fire, but the rest by various killings, celebrated martyrdom. At Rome, in the cemetery of Apronianus, the passion of blessed Eugenia, virgin, who in the time of the emperor Gallienus, after the marks of virtues, after the sacred choirs of virgins, long contending for Christ, at the last had her throat cut with the sword.

## The seventh day before the Kalends · 26 December

[0839]

At Jerusalem, the birthday of Saint Stephen the protomartyr, who was stoned by the Jews not long after the passion of Christ, namely in the same year as his Ascension. The venerable body of this man was found by divine revelation in the time of the emperor Honorius. At Rome, on the Appian Way, the deposition of Saint Dionisius, pope, who shone out illustrious for the teachings of the faith under the emperor Claudius. On the same day, of Saint Marinus, a most illustrious man, who obtained the triumph of martyrdom at Rome under the emperor Marcian.

## The sixth day before the Kalends · 27 December

[0843]

At Ephesus, the birthday of blessed John, apostle and evangelist, who, after the banishment of exile, after the revelation of the divine Apocalypse, after the writing of the Gospel, persevering until the times of the emperor Trajan, founded and ruled the churches of all Asia. And, worn out with old age, in the sixty-eighth year after the passion of the Lord, but of his own age the ninety-ninth, he died, and was buried near the same city. At Alexandria, of Saint Maximus, bishop: who was made quite illustrious and distinguished by the title of confession.

## The fifth day before the Kalends · 28 December

[0847]

At Bethlehem, the birthday of the holy Innocents, who were slain by King Herod. At Ancyra of Galatia, of Saints Euticus, presbyter, and Domitianus, deacon.

## The fourth day before the Kalends · 29 December

[0849]

At Jerusalem, of David the king. At Arles, the birthday of Saint Trophimus, whom Paul mentions, writing to Timothy, who, ordained bishop by the same Apostle, was sent as the first to the aforesaid city to preach the Gospel of Christ. From whose fountain, as Pope Zosimus writes, all Gaul received the streams of faith. In the district *Oximensis*, of Saint Ebrulfus, confessor. [*Bouillart adds:* Also at Jerusalem, of Saint Melania, consecrated to God.]

## The third day before the Kalends · 30 December

[0853]

At Spoleto, the birthday of blessed Sabinus, bishop, of Exuperantius and Marcellus, deacons, and of Venustianus with his wife and sons, under the emperor Maximian. Of whom Marcellus and Exuperantius, first hung upon the rack, then heavily wasted with cudgels, afterwards scraped with hooks, and roasted by the burning of their sides, completed their martyrdom. Venustianus, however, not long after, together with his wife and sons, was slain with the sword. But Saint Sabinus, after the cutting off of his hands and a long binding in prison, was beaten even to death. Their martyrdom, therefore, although it took place at different times, is nevertheless kept on one day. At Alexandria, of Saint Mansuetus, martyr, with ten others.

## The day before the Kalends · 31 December

[0857]

At Rome, the birthday of Saint Silvester, pope, whose illustrious acts are extant. At Senones, of blessed Sabinianus, bishop, and Potentianus, who, sent by the Roman pontiff to preach, made that same metropolis illustrious by the confession of their martyrdom. And in the same city, of Saint Columba, virgin: who, having overcome the fire, was cut down with the sword [*Bouillart adds:* by order of the emperor Aurelian.] At Retiaria, of Saint Hermes, exorcist.

And at length the feasts close December, the last.

*TO THE GREATER GLORY OF GOD.*
