# Cruces — 9245, Hincmar of Reims, *Instructio ad Ludovicum Balbum*

- **col 0985A** — printed *ejus constitutionem vindicare sibi votuerunt*. Intended *voluerunt*. Rendered "wished to claim for themselves."
- **col 0984D** — the column sequence jumps 0983D → 0984D with no A/B/C columns in between. This is the printed sequence in the plate (the intervening columns belong to the preceding piece); anchors reproduced as found, not interpolated.
- **col 0985D** — *et ideo non se contra illos concrederent*: *concredere* here in a reflexive sense with *contra*. Rendered "would not entrust themselves against them"; sense is "would not commit themselves to opposing them." A read-through may want to loosen this.
- **col 0987C** — *per occasionabiles circadas*: *circada* is the Carolingian episcopal circuit-levy. Rendered "circuit-levies" per the working-equivalent rule rather than left in italic Latin; flag if the project prefers *circada* italicized as a technical institution-word.
- **col 0988C** — the sentence *sicut Dominus dicit,* qui injuste judicant, juste judicati condemnabuntur is given as dominical but corresponds to no gospel text verbatim (cf. Matth. VII, 2). Translated as printed, attribution untouched.
