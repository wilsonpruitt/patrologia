⟦p22⟧ ⟦s1⟧ I, Lambert, notary of the sacred palace and envoy of the lords Hugh and Lothair, most pious kings, together with the above-written lord archdeacon, have delivered [0899B] and subscribed it.

⟦p23⟧ ⟦s1⟧ I, Peter, who am called Rabbus, notary of the lord emperor Henry, have diligently collated the above-written adjudication of the lord Atto, bishop of Vercelli, pronounced by him in the synod of Milan and subscribed, as faithfully copied above by the below-written Bogerius Bonafides, notary, from the original of the aforesaid adjudication — which appears to have been secured with three seals, the first of which was that of the lords the kings, the second bearing Saints Peter and Paul, and the third bearing Saint Ambrose but almost consumed by age — with that original itself, for the most part decayed, so that it can scarcely and with difficulty be read; being asked so to do by [0900A] the venerable lord Ambrose, called of Vimercate, abbot of the monastery of Saint Ambrose, where his holy body rests; at the instance also of the lady Columba, abbess of the Monastero Maggiore of Milan; and this by mandate of the lord John, called of Rusca, honorable podestà of the city and commune of Milan, there being present the said Bonafides, T. Senago, Jo. ⟦s2⟧ Coalia, J. Canova, A. Lomatio, notaries, and Thomas called Crassus, and Rufinus Corbus, jurists, and Ossa de Ossis, Peter called Bellabucca, Comasinus de Cremis, John Marcellinus, Guido the viscount, as witnesses; and because I found it to agree, apart from the lettering more or less, I have subscribed with my own hand.
