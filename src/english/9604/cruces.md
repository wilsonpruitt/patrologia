# Cruces — 9604 (Auctor incertus, *De prandio monachorum*)

This text is written in strongly non-classical, late/Merovingian-Latin orthography and morphology (e.g. `sedeunt` for *sedent*, `mensa`/`cathedra` as nominatives for accusatives, `pisces...manducant`, `honeste vel ordinabiliter Deo conservantur`). These are rendered per the intended sense, not emended.

Migne prints a run of in-text bracketed/parenthetical variant readings — normalizations he supplies beside the manuscript's form. Reproduced in place as parentheticals, following the printed reading, not silently substituted:

- **1346C** — `Sic fit de omnia (omni) administrationem.` Migne's `(omni)` normalizes `omnia`. Rendered "with every (every)" — kept literally though the two English words coincide; the parenthetical marks Migne's editorial variant, not a translation choice.
- **1346D** — `paris suos (paribus suis)`: variant normalizing to correct case. Rendered "their fellows (their fellows)."
- **1349/1347A** — `conscripsimus (conscripsi)`: 1st pl. vs 1st sg. Rendered "we have written down (I have written down)."
- **1347B** — `Gelasius (Zelasius)`: spelling variant of the pope's name. Rendered "Gelasius (Zelasius)."
- **1347B** — `annalem cantum` etc.: `annalis cantus` = the year's cycle of chant; rendered "chant of the year."
- **1347C** — `moralia diptycha (tibica)`: garbled. `(tibica)` is Migne's flagged alternative to `diptycha`; neither yields clean sense for Gregory's *Moralia in Iob*. Rendered literally "the moral diptychs (tibica)"; not emended.
- **1347D/1348** — `Niceni (Nicaeni)`: spelling variant. Rendered "Nicaea (Nicaea)."

Scripture in this chunk (2 Tim. 3–4, Rom. 10, Matt. 16 at 1348D) is quoted loosely/conflated by the author; translated as the Latin prints, not conformed to a standard version.
