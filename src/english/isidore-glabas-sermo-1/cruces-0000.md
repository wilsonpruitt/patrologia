# Cruces — isidore-glabas-sermo-1, chunk 0000 (Fable anchor)

## Work-wide conventions (anchor session, 2026-07-31 — merge into the work-wide section)

- **ΛΟΓΟΣ as a work-division head** renders by the work's established English
  division term — here "SERMON" (registry + the plate's own Latin column: SERMO
  I) — consistent within the work. (Nicetas uses "Discourse" for the same word;
  per-work, not per-corpus.)
- **Εὐλόγησον, Πάτερ** — the liturgical formula before the sermon renders
  "Give the blessing, Father."
- **Mariological epithets — fixed equivalences, one per Greek epithet, never
  interchanged:** πάναγνος = all-pure · πανυπέραγνος = all-surpassingly pure ·
  Πανάχραντος = All-immaculate · Πάντιμος = All-honored · πολυύμνητος =
  much-hymned · Θεομήτωρ = Mother of God · Δέσποινα = Lady · ἀειπάρθενος =
  ever-virgin · ἡ μακαρία = the blessed one. Glabas builds arguments on the
  differences between epithets; collapsing two into one English word is the
  homily analogue of a lemma-conformation.
- **Maker-vocabulary distinctions, fixed within the work:** Κτίστης = Creator ·
  Δημιουργός = Maker · πλάστης = Fashioner · πλάσμα = moulded work ·
  δημιούργημα = created work.
- **Rhetorical pile-ups are structure, not padding:** doublets/triplets
  (γυμνὸς, ἐλεεινὸς, αἰχμάλωτος) render 1:1, never compressed; exclamations
  kept (οἴμοι! = alas!); rhetorical questions stay questions.
- **Bold coinages are rendered boldly, not normalized:** ταφόζωος = tomb-bound
  (existence) · βρεφουργηθέντες = wrought as babes · ἔξαλμα = outleaping ·
  προέτεκε = bore before. Allatius routinely flattens these (sepulcralem vitam,
  in quadam infantia); his paraphrase is NOT a license to flatten the Greek.
