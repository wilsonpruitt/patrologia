⟦p1⟧ ⟦s1⟧ [1424] *III. Hymn of supplication to the holy Trinity.*

⟦p2⟧ ⟦s1⟧ Remember me, of the root without beginning
⟦s2⟧ Ineffable shoot,
⟦s3⟧ And strange breath, to both
⟦s4⟧ Equal, to offspring and to source,
⟦s5⟧ My three-sunned form;
⟦s6⟧ When thou judgest me, a sinner,
⟦s7⟧ Save me, thy servant, from the fire,
⟦s8⟧ Thine own, and from every dread
⟦s9⟧ Tempest draw me up, Saviour;
⟦s10⟧ And loose at once the bond
⟦s11⟧ Of the swarm of my grievous evils.

⟦p3⟧ ⟦s1⟧ And write me in thy book,
⟦s2⟧ Escaped from this stream
⟦s3⟧ Unwounded and safe.

⟦p4⟧ ⟦s1⟧ And that I sing and honour,
⟦s2⟧ In times without measure,
⟦s3⟧ Thy might, grant by thy nod.
