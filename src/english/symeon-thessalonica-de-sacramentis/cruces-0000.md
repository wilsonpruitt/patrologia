## Genuine cruces

- **0180 κρειττόνω(ς).** Calfa `κρειττόνω,`; the plate's last letter is worn — reads as
  κρειττόνω with a possible final ς. Grammar wants the adverb κρειττόνως ("in a better
  manner"); Latin *nobiliores … modo* ("in a nobler manner") concurs with the adverbial sense.
  Rendered "in a better manner." Not a `[sic:]`: the plate is worn, not wrong, and the
  letter is undecidable on this copy. **Second-witness candidate** if another PG 155 scan
  is ever harvested.
- **0179 ἀναλαμβανόμενος ἀφ᾽ ἡμῶν.** Literal "taking up this power of his from us" — the sense
  is that he assumed it from our side, as man, and then bestowed it through his priesthood.
  Rendered "having taken up this power of his from among us." Latin here is OCR-broken
  (*pro nobis illa potentia … nobis eam sui ope exhibet*) and settles nothing.
- **0181 Clerk's question ends in a COMMA on the plate** (δι᾽ ἀγγέλου,) — Calfa agrees; the
  Latin prints a question mark. Pattern 8: the English keeps the comma. Not a `[lat:]`
  (punctuation is not a fact asserted).
- **0176 δι᾽ ὑμᾶς … ὑμῖν (plural) vs 0177 σοι/Σύ (singular)** inside one speech: Pattern 17
  carried as printed — "for your sake … to you", then "to thee … do not thou". The High
  Priest addresses the body of clergy, then the one asking. Not smoothed.

## Conventions set by this anchor (→ `translation-style.md`, "Catechetical-liturgical exposition")

title says τελεταί) · τελετή = **rite** · the fixed seven: βάπτισμα baptism · χρίσμα chrism ·
κοινωνία communion · χειροτονία ordination · γάμος marriage · μετάνοια repentance · ἔλαιον ἅγιον
holy oil · σφραγίς = seal · χάρισμα = gift · ἱερωσύνη = priesthood · Δεσπότης (of Christ) =
Master; δέσποτα (to the bishop) = master · Ἀρχιερεύς. / Κληρικός. speaker tags → *High Priest.*
/ *Cleric.* in italics as printed (ἀρχιερεύς = "high priest" per Wilson's 2026-09-24 ruling on
7091; ⚑ **flag for Wilson**: in Symeon the speaker is the bishop, and "Hierarch"/"Bishop" are
the scholarly alternatives — one word, work-wide, decide before chunk 0001) · ΚΕΦΑΛ. xʹ →
