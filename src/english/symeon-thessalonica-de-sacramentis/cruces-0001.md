### Genuine cruces

- **0181C ὅλω(ς), 0184C ἀναμαρτήτω(ς).** The plate's last letter is worn in both: ὅλω, (a
  comma-like mark) and ἀναμαρτήτω followed by a gap. Grammar wants the adverbs ὅλως ("at
  all") and ἀναμαρτήτως ("sinlessly"); Latin *nulli naturae … possibile* / *praeter peccatum*
  concurs. Same class as 0180 κρειττόνω(ς) in the anchor: worn, not wrong — no `[sic:]`.
- **0181D τῶν οἰκείων, ἐχόντων τὴν χάριν αὐτοῦ** — gender undecidable ("his own people" or
  "his own things"). Rendered neutrally, "those that are his own, having his grace." Latin
  *bonaque sua … continentia* takes it as neuter; it settles nothing about the Greek.
- **0184A μυρίων … ἱερουργῶν.** Read as the genitive plural of the noun ἱερουργός ("of
  celebrants"), not a participle: "though myriads of celebrants offer." Latin *sescenti
  offerant sacerdotes* agrees.
- **0184B Κατανοεῖ οὖν** — the plate's accent sits ambiguously (indicative κατανοεῖ or
  imperative κατανόει, same letters). The imperative fits the bishop's κατανόησον a few lines
  earlier and Latin *recogita*; rendered "Consider, then." Accent-only; not a word change.
- **0184B πρὸς αὐτήν** (feminine, plate and Calfa agree) — "akin toward it"; the antecedent
  (θεότης? φύσις?) is not expressed. Rendered "toward it". Latin *ad illud* neuter.
- **0184D καὶ διάκεινται.** The type on the last line of col 184 is damaged (δι·ά·κειν-ται);
  read διάκεινται, "and are disposed," rendered bare. Latin *et accedunt* is a looser sense
  (draw near) — whether the Greek is διϊκνοῦνται or similar cannot be settled on this copy.
  **Second-witness candidate.**
- **0184D τοῦ λόγου ψυχῆς** — lower-case λόγου on the plate (Calfa agrees). Context and Latin
  *Verbi anima* make it the Word; rendered "the Word". Capitalization is editorial.
- **0184C the long ἐπεὶ … λαμβάνει period** is anacoluthic (ἐπεὶ γὰρ ἀγαθός … οὐ γὰρ ἂν … τὸ
  ἄκρον γὰρ … ἐπεὶ Θεοῦ ἡ κτίσις … λαμβάνει) — carried as one sentence, comma-joined as
  printed; not repaired.
- **0181C ἐπειδήπερ after a question mark, lower case** (plate: ἄτρεπτος; ἐπειδήπερ) — Pattern 8,
  kept: "…not being by nature unchangeable? since some of them fell away". Same at 0185C
  πῶς γὰρ ὁ καθαρὸς μόνος; ἀλλ᾽ ὑπὲρ ἡμῶν — "…he who alone is pure? but for our sake".
- **0185D Peter, Acts 2:36,** printed without guillemets (Ὅτι καὶ Κύριον …): no quotation
  marks supplied (Pattern 6). Isaiah 61:1 is in « » on the plate and in the English.
