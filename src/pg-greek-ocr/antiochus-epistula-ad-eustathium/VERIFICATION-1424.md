## Content

The martyrdom narrative proper. `Ἐπελθόντων γὰρ τῶν Ἰσμαηλιτῶν τῇ ἡμετέρᾳ
Λαύρᾳ πρὸ μιᾶς ἑβδομάδος τοῦ παραληφθῆναι τὴν ἁγίαν πόλιν` — the raid falls
**one week before the taking of the Holy City**, dating it to the eve of the 614
sack. Named actors: **ἀββᾶς Νικομήδης**, who nearly died of grief at the sight,
and **Μόδεστος ὁ ὁσιώτατος** — the future locum tenens of Jerusalem — who
gathered, washed and entombed the bodies. Two scripture citations close the
column (Isa 57:1–2; Wisd 3:1–6) and need `[n:]` markers at chunking.
