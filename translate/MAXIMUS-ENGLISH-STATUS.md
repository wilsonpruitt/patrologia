# Maximus the Confessor (PG 90-91): English translation status

Compiled 2026-11-06 from memory of the secondary literature. **No live web verification was done**; every row carries a confidence grade. CPG numbers are the least certain column: treat any marked "?" as needing a check against Clavis Patrum Graecorum III (CPG 7688-7710 range for Maximus; 7735 area for the Vita/Acta dossier). "Not found" = I know of no published English, not proof none exists.

Abbreviations: Berthold = G. C. Berthold, *Maximus Confessor: Selected Writings* (CWS, Paulist, 1985). Sherwood = P. Sherwood, *The Ascetic Life; The Four Centuries on Charity* (ACW 21, Newman, 1955). Louth = A. Louth, *Maximus the Confessor* (Routledge Early Church Fathers, 1996). B-W = Blowers and Wilken, *On the Cosmic Mystery of Jesus Christ* (SVS, 2003). A-N 2002 = Allen and Neil, *Maximus the Confessor and his Companions: Documents from Exile* (OECT, OUP, 2002). Philokalia 2 = Palmer, Sherrard, Ware, *The Philokalia* vol. 2 (Faber, 1981).

## Notes

- Other Maximus works outside your list that have English: *Capita XV* (Opusc. section, partial), Letter to Marinus / Opusc. 15 (Louth, partial), Ep. 2 "On Love" (Louth), *Ep. 12* (in some selections). Also Maximus-in-Cooper: Adam Cooper, *The Body in St Maximus the Liturgist* (OUP, 2005) translates scattered passages.
- Where the Quaestiones/Ambigua have **two** published English versions (Constas vs. B-W / Louth), only Constas is complete. For Q. ad Thalassium, the pre-2018 English was only B-W.
- Opuscula is the largest known gap: no one-volume English for all 27. Louth's two (Opusc. 3, 7) plus a few journal translations are all I can name with confidence.
- Capita alia / Ad Theopemptum / Hymni / Ps 59 / Computus: treat as untranslated until checked.
