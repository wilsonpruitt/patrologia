Each item gives a crop of Migne's page (the Greek column, about 7 printed lines), our transcription of one line
in it, and the spot in question, with one or two candidate readings in random order (A/B).

For each item:
1. Find the line in the crop (our transcription is for orientation only; it may itself be wrong at the spot).
2. Transcribe **exactly what the plate prints** at the spot: the word(s) in question plus one word either side,
   letter by letter, accents and breathings included. Read it before you look at the candidates.
3. Then say which candidate the plate matches: `A`, `B`, or `neither` (if neither, your transcription is the answer).
4. Note any footnote mark (a raised number) or *(sic)* printed at or right beside the spot.
5. If the crop does not show the line, or the print is too damaged to read, say `unreadable` and why.

Do not judge which reading is right Greek: only what the plate prints. Migne's misprints are expected.

Write your results to ~/patrologia/translate/dorotheus-doctrinae/verify/result-2.tsv as a TSV with this header, one row per item, and nothing else in the file:

    id	plate	match	mark	note

(`plate` = your transcription; `match` = A / B / neither / unreadable; `mark` = the footnote number, sic, or empty.)
Read the images one at a time with the Read tool. Use no other files and no web. When done, report counts of
A/B/neither/unreadable in one line.
