Coordinator rulings (binding):
1. **ἐνέργεια = *activity*** where it is ordinary Greek (as the card says); only a clearly Christological use (the two operations)
   is *operation*, with a T note. **ἀνενέργητος = without activity.** Do not "correct" activity to operation in this work.
   **νοερός = noetic** (card), not "intellectual"; νοητός intelligible, αἰσθητός sensible, λογικός rational.
2. **Address.** The Proem and the closing exhortation address a group in places (ὑμῖν, ἠγαπημένοι, ὑμᾶς): *you*; a singular
   σοι / σε / σου / ἀπήτεις / ἔδειξας: *thou*. Follow the Greek's number, never mix inside one sentence; keep the T notes that say so.
   The general reader at ch. 23 ("Ἄθρει … ὅστις … καθέστηκας") is *thou*.
3. **Scripture and Fathers.** Migne's Greek prints no quotation marks around Scripture. Where a translator italicised a verbatim
   Scripture quotation and gave it an S note, keep italics as the single convention for **verbatim Scripture only** (no « » unless Migne
   prints them; a Father's or Dionysius's quotation gets « » only if Migne prints them, else S note only, no italics). Make all six
   chunks follow it; do not spend effort beyond that.
4. **Chapter headings.** Each chapter: `CHAPTER n` (a line on its own, n in Arabic), the title in italics beneath. Chapters run 1…24 in
   order across all chunks (the translators restored mangled numbers: check each is present once, in order, and that no number is
   skipped or doubled across a seam). The title is `THE MYSTAGOGIA` + the sub-title in italics, in my-001 only.
5. **Terms** (card § Terms + the translators' own choices; make them one): sanctuary / nave (ἱερατεῖον / ναός), altar (θυσιαστήριον), synaxis,
   the practical / the knowers, hierarch / high priest, Trisagion for Τρισάγιον ("the thrice-holy hymn" first time, Trisagion after: the
   translators' P note says it is the Sanctus here: check), the Creed, positing / removal (θέσις / ἀφαίρεσις), φρόνημα = way of thinking,
   ἀλάθητος / ἄληστος kept apart (never escaping notice / unforgetting), οἰκειότης / οἰκείωσις (two words, two renderings:
   rule and apply: *intimacy* for οἰκειότης, *kinship* for οἰκείωσις; affinity → intimacy unless the Greek is οἰκείωσις), καταργεῖν annul.
   For any other word that two chunks render differently, choose the better and apply it across; list it.
6. **Seams.** Read across every boundary (my-001|002|003|004|005|006): each chunk ends mid-sentence and the next opens in it. No sentence doubled
   or dropped, the join grammatical, the column anchor at each chunk start and new page. Check each join against the Greek of the last
   and first lines.
7. **[?] marks.** List every one left (chunk, page.line, note number): the coordinator reads them on the plate.
8. **Notes.** A P note on a person/rite (Dionysius, the elder, the Trisagion/Sanctus...) once in the WORK at its first appearance; a
   repeat in a later chunk becomes "(see note N, my-00M)" or is dropped with renumbering. S notes: book chapter:verse (LXX numbering for
   Psalms); Dionysius: work + chapter (+ section).
9. **Report** (short): every edit as `chunk · what · why`, counts per task, and anything you could not decide (with the Greek) for the
   coordinator.
